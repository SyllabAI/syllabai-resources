28 ESTERS

Alcohols, carboxylic acids and esters are organic compounds that contain oxygen as well as carbon and hydrogen. They
are closely related. Carboxylic acids are made from oxidation of alcohols. In this chapter, you will learn how we can make
esters by reacting alcohols with carboxylic acids. Esters are frequently used for perfumes and artificial flavours.
