varies with the temperature. But at each temperature, some particles will be
moving faster than the average and others more slowly.

Some very fast particles at the surface of the liquid will have enough energy to
overcome the forces of attraction between the particles — they will break away
to form a gas. This is evaporation. You don’t see any bubbling; the liquid just
slowly disappears if it is open to the air. If the liquid is in a closed container,
particles in the gas will also be colliding with particles at the surface of the
liquid. If they are moving slowly enough they will be held by the attractive
