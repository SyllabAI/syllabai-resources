CHEMICAL FORMULAE, EQUATIONS AND CALCULATIONS: PART 2

When doing calculations involving solutions you will most often be asked
to calculate a number of moles given the volume of the solution and its
concentration. The equation for doing this is:

number of moles = volume of solution (dm*) x concentration (mol/dm*)

Because concentration is usually given in units of mol/dm® but volumes of
solutions are often given in cm, we usually have to convert the volume in
cm to dm® by dividing by 1000.

EXAMPLE 3

Calculate the number of moles of NaOH in 50cm of 0.10 mol/dm® solution.
Converting the volume to dm* we get 50/1000 = 0.050dm*.

Number of moles = volume of solution (dm*) x concentration (mol/dm*).
Number of moles = 0.050 x 0.10 = 0.0050 mol.
