THE TRIPLE BOND IN A NITROGEN A nitrogen atom has 5 electrons in its outer shell and so if two nitrogen atoms

combine they will both share 3 electrons each; this means that each atom will
have 8 electrons in its outer shell. There are, therefore, three shared pairs of
electrons between the nitrogen atoms; this is called a triple covalent bond or,
usually, just a triple bond.
