MORE ACID-BASE REACTIONS

Acids in solution are acidic because they donate a proton (H*) to water to form
the hydroxonium ion. If we take a general acid (HA), this is what happens when
it is put into water:

HA(aq) + H2O(!) + HgO*(aq) + A-(aq)

HA donates a proton to H,O, which acts as a base. For nitric acid this would
be:

HNO,(aq) + H,O(!) + H,O*(aq) + NO,-(aq)
acid base

proton from water, for example:

(Na*),0-(s) + H,O(l) + 2Na*(aq) + 2OH=(aq)

an acid because it donates a proton.
