THE PERIODIC TABLE

The Periodic Table shows all the elements in the universe and is one of the most important
tools that a chemist has. The arrangement of the elements allows us to understand trends in
properties and make predictions. The modern Periodic Table was first presented in 1869 by a
famous Russian chemist, Dmitri Mendeleev (left). This chapter explores some of the features of

the Periodic Table.
