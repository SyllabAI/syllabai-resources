THE VOLUME OCCUPIED BY 1 MOLE OF A GAS

1 mole of any gas contains the same number of molecules and so occupies the
same volume as 1 mole of any other gas at the same temperature and pressure.

This is by far the most important
consequence of Avogadro's law. At room temperature and pressure, the volume occupied by 1 mole of any gas
is approximately 24 dm? (24000.cm*). The volume occupied by 1 mole of a gas
is often called the molar volume.
