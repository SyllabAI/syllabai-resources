NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) + NaCl(aq) + H,O(!)
We saw in Chapter 16 that the ionic equation for this reaction is:
OH-(aq) + H*(aq) + H2O0(!)

All neutralisation reactions for an acid reacting with an alkali have the same
ionic equation: they all involve the hydroxide ions from the alkali reacting with
the H* ions from the acid to form water.
