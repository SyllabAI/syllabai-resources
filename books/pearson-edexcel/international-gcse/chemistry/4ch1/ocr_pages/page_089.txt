DEDUCING THE FORMULA FOR AN IONIC COMPOUND

To find the formula for sodium oxide
Sodium is in Group 1, so the ion is Nat.
Oxygen is in Group 6, so the ion is O*.

To have equal numbers of positive and negative charges, you would need
two sodium ions to provide the two positive charges to cancel the two
negative charges on one oxide ion. In other words, you need:

Nat Nat OF
The formula is therefore Na,O.
