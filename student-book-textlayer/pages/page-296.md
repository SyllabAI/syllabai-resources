26ALCOHOLS

What we commonly refer to as 'alcohol' is just one member of a large family
(homologous series) of similar compounds. Alcohols all contain the functional
group - OH covalently bonded to a carbon chain.
The familiar alcohol in drinks is C2 H50H (or, better, showing the structure,
CH3CH20 H) and should properly be called ethanol.
