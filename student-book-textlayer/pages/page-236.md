EXPERIMENTS TO MEASURE THE RATE OF REACTION
The rate of a reaction is the speed at which the amount of reactants decreases
or the amount of products increases. It is measured as a change in the
concentration (or amount) of reactants or products per unit time (per second,
per minute etc.).
rate of reaction = change in concentration, volume or mass
