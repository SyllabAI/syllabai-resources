USES OF POLY(CHLOROETHENE)

Poly(chloroethene), known as PVC, has a lot of uses. It is quite strong and
rigid, and so can be used for water pipes or replacement windows. It can
also be made flexible by adding plasticisers. This makes it useful for sheet
floor coverings, and even clothing. These polymers don't conduct electricity,
therefore PVC can be used for electrical insulation.
