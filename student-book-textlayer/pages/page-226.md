Dissolving is a physical process rather
than a chemical reaction. When some

salts dissolve in water heat is given out
(exothermic), but when others dissolve
the process is endothermic.

The amount of heat absorbed when dissolving 1 mole of ammonium chloride
in water is therefore:
NH4Cl(s) --+ NH4 Cl(aq)

The positive sign shows that heat is absorbed and the dissolving of ammonium
chloride in water is an endothermic process (the temperature goes down).
