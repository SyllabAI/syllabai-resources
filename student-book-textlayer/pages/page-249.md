•

Know that the characteristics of a reaction at
dynamic equilibrium are:
• the forward and reverse reactions occur at the
same rate
• the concentrations of reactants and products
remain constant.

•

Understand why a catalyst does not affect the
position of equilibrium in a reversible reaction.

•

Know the effect of changing either temperature
or pressure on the position of equilibrium in a
reversible reaction:
•

an increase (or decrease) in temperature shifts
the position of equilibrium in the direction of
the endothermic (or exothermic) reaction

•

an increase (or decrease) in pressure shifts
the position of equilibrium in the direction that
produces fewer (or more) moles of gas.

REVERSIBILITY AND DYNAMIC EQUILIBRIA
DEHYDRATION OF COPPER(II) SULFATE CRYSTALS
If you heat blue copper(II) sulfate crystals gently, the blue crystals turn to
a white powder and water is driven off. Heating causes the crystals to lose
their water of crystallisation and white anhydrous copper(II) sulfate is formed.
'Anhydrous' simply means 'without water'.

HEATING AMMONIUM CHLORIDE
If you heat ammonium chloride, the white crystals disappear from the bottom
of the tube and reappear further up . Heating ammonium chloride splits it into
the colourless gases ammonia and hydrogen chloride.
