PRINCIPLES OF CHEMISTRY

HINT

We often use the term 'molar mass'
instead of 'mass of 1 mote'.
EXTENSION WORK

To be 100% correct 'molar mass' and
'mass of 1 mole' are not exactly the
same thing and have different units:
molar mass has units of g/mol and the
mass of 1 mole has units of g. You are
unlikely to come across the distinction
between these at International GCSE.
