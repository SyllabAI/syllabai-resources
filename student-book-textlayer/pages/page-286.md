The alkanes are the simplest family of hydrocarbons. Hydrocarbons are
compounds that contain carbon and hydrogen only.
Many of the alkanes are used as fuels. Methane is the major component of
natural gas. Ethane and propane are also present in small quantities in natural
gas, and are important constituents of liquefied petroleum gases (LPG) from
crude oil distillation (discussed in Chapter 23).
