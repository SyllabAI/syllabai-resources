Combustion is an exothermic reaction so the temperature of the water goes
up. As ethanol is burned, the total mass of the burner and ethanol goes down.
Using the above data we can determine how much heat is released when one
mole of an alcohol is burnt, that is, the molar enthalpy change of combustion.
