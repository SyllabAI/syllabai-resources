PRINCIPLES OF CHEMISTRY

5 CHEMICAL FORMULAE, EQUATIONS AND CALCULATIONS: PART 1

WRITING EQUATIONS
There are two types of chemical equation that you could be asked to write:
word equations and symbol equations. Symbol equations are usually called
chemical equations and you should only write a word equation if you are
specifically asked to. All chemical equations must be balanced.
