ISOMERS OF C4H8

An important application of optical isomerism is in the synthesis of
pharmaceutical drugs. You might have heard of the thalidomide tragedy. Back in
the 1960s in Germany, the molecule B shown in Figure 22.19 was found to have
anti-nausea effects and was therefore used to treat morning sickness in pregnant
woman. Unfortunately, molecule B was being manufactured together with its
optical isomer A and little was known about the molecule A until many children
born in that era were found to have deformed limbs. These days, many drug
companies devote a great deal of money to separating optical isomers of new
drugs just in case they have side effects.
