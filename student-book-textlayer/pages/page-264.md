22 INTRODUCTION TO ORGANIC CHEMISTRY

In the next few chapters we will be looking at organic compounds. The term
'organic' was originally used because it was believed that organic compounds
could only come from living things. Now it is used for any carbon compound
except for the very simplest (carbon dioxide, carbon monoxide, the carbonates
and the hydrogencarbonates).
Organic compounds can exist as chains, branched chains or rings of carbon
atoms with hydrogens attached.
When you start doing organic chemistry, you are suddenly faced with a whole
lot of new compounds with strange names and unfamiliar ways of drawing
them. It can be quite scary!
