KEV POINT

The 1- ions formed by the halogens
(Group 7) are collectively called halide
ions. Chloride, bromide and iodide ions
are all halide ions.

The acid is added to react with and remove other anions which might also
produce precipitates with silver nitrate solution, for example carbonate and
hydroxide ions. You must not use hydrochloric acid {or hydrobromic acid or
hydroiodic acid) as you will be adding halide ions and you are certain to obtain
a precipitate.
