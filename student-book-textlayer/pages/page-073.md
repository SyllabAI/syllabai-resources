Avogadro's law:

Equal volumes of gases at the same temperature and pressure contain
equal numbers of m olecules.
This means that if you have 100 cm3 of hydrogen at some temperature and
pressure, it contains exactly the same number of molecules as there are in
100 cm 3 of Cl2, or any other gas under those conditions, irrespective of the size
of the molecules.
