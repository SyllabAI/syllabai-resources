KEY POINT
How do we know when the reaction
has finished? We can generally
see when there is no more reaction
because nothing is happening in
the crucible. We can also check by
weighing the crucible and contents,
then heating it and weighing again. If
the mass is higher when we weigh it
again then this indicates that a reaction
has occurred. We keep heating and
weighing until the mass stays constant.
