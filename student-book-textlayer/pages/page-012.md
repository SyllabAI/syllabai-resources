STATES OF MATIER
Solids, liquids and gases are known as the three states of matter.

Think about these facts:
• You can't walk through a brick wall, but you can move (with some resistance
- it pushes against you) through water. Moving through air is easy.
• When you melt most solids their volume increases slightly. Most liquids are
less dense than the solid they come from.
• If you boil about 5 cm3 of water, the steam will fill an average bucket.
