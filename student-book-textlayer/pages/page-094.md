PRINCIPLES OF CHEMISTRY

8 COVALENT BONDING

COVALENT BONDING
WHAT IS A COVALENT BONO?

In any bond, particles are held together by electrostatic attractions between
something positively charged and something negatively charged. in a
covalent bond, a pair of electrons is shared between two atoms. What holds
the atoms together is the strong electrostatic attraction between the nuclei
(positively charged) of the atoms that make up the bond, and the shared pair of
electrons (negatively charged).
