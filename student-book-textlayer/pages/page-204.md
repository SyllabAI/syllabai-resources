Make a solution of your suspected sulfate in distilled (pure) water, add some

dilute hydrochloric acid and then add some barium chloride solution. A sulfate

You add acid to the solution to react with and remove other anions whic h
might also produce white precipitates when you add the bariu m chloride
solution. For example, if you didn't add acid, you would also obtain a white
precipitate if there were carbonate ions present. This is because barium
carbonate is also white and insoluble. The acid reacts with the carbonate ions
and removes them. It is important to remember that the acid must be added

You must never acidify the solution with sulfuric acid because sulfuric acid
contains sulfate ions. If you add those, you are bound to get a precip itate of
barium sulfate because you have added sulfate ions.
