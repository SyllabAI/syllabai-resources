DISPOSAL OF ADDITION POLYMERS
Recycling of plastics is important, not just to save raw materials, but
because it takes a very long time for addition polymers like poly(ethene) and
poly(chloroethene) to break down in the environment. They contain strong
covalent bonds, making them essentially inert at ordinary temperatures. They
are non-biodegradable, meaning they cannot be broken down by bacteria in
the environment.
One solution to the problem of their disposal is to bury them in landfill sites,
which are basically just big holes in the ground , where they will remain
unchanged for thousands of years.
