17 ACIDS, BASES AND SALT PREPARATIONS

SALTS
All acids contain hydrogen. When that hydrogen is replaced by a metal, the
compound formed is called a salt. Magnesium sulfate is a salt , and so is zinc
chloride, and so is potassium nitrate.
