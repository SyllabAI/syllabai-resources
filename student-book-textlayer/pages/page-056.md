In order to find out the empirical formula of a compound such as copper oxide,
we need to know how many atoms of copper combine with how many atoms
of oxygen. We can work out the number of atoms from the number of moles. If
w e know the ratio b etween the number of copper atoms and oxygen atoms in
the compound we know the formula.
