# Help and Support for Enterprise

## Sources of Help and Support

### Formal Sources of Help and Support:

* Government and Business Agencies

  * They can provide loans, grants, reduce tax rate, training for staff and etc.

* Consultants

  * Can aid by providing financial advice, helping conduct research or planning for enterprise, etc.

* Financial Institutions

  * Can provide start up loans, loans to expand business or micro loans.

* Charities

  * Can raise fund to help with enterprise projects that are benefit to the community.

    Teachers

    Can help provide support through out the planning and setting up of enterprise.

* Other Entrepreneurs

  * Can share ideas and gain knowledge to each other.

* Business Network

  * Can give opportunities to gain inspiration and advice from other entrepreneurs or meet potential investors.

### Informal Sources of Help and Support:

* Friends and Family

  * Can provide support throughout the set up of enterprise by either through encouragement or financial support.

### Choosing a Suitable Source of Help and Support:

* Source of help and support depends on the type of help needed and type of enterprise.
