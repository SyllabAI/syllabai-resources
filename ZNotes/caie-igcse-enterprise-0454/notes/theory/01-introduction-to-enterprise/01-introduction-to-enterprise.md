# Introduction to Enterprise

## What is Enterprise?

- Enterprise is a term that refers to a business set up and run by individuals willing to take **risks**.

  - Enterprise is also considered as an attribute, meaning the ability to take risks, be innovative and make decisions in a business context.

### **Type of Enterprises and their Aims**

There are two categories of Enterprises:

1. Business Enterprise

- The main aim is to generate profits, which are used to grow and develop the business.

2. Social Enterprise

- The main aim is to help the community.
  - While it also generates profits, those profits are mainly reinvested into ideas or projects that support social causes.
  - Some of the profits are also used to keep the enterprise running.

### **Enterprise Capability**

Entrepreneur:

- Someone who builds and runs a business or organization.
  - Entrepreneurs need to have many attributes in order to run their business well.

Key Attributes that entrepreneurs should have:

1. Risk-Taking:

- It is an essential trait that helps entrepreneurs navigate through uncertainty and allows them to bring in new opportunities.
  - It is not only considered a possible loss but also a lesson or idea to take away in the end.

2. Decision-Making:

- Effective decision-making helps enterprises succeed by ensuring timely and strategic actions to help them navigate complex situations.
  - Decision-making goes hand in hand with Risk-taking.

3. Innovation:

- Refers to being able to come up with creative ideas and solutions.

4. Positive Attitude:

- A vital characteristic within an entrepreneur that can help them tackle challenges and stay motivated, encouraging the business to succeed.

### **Stakeholders**

- Someone or a group of people or an organization interested in an enterprise's activities.
- There are internal and external stakeholders.
