## Ways to be Enterprising in Everyday Life

### **Ways of Being Enterprising at School**

1. Taking Responsibility - Example: Signing up for leadership positions

2. Being Organized - Example: completing homework and tasks on time or Reaching school on time.

3. Taking Initiatives - For example, Starting student clubs and activities or holding fundraisers to raise money.

4. Using technology - Examples: Using information online to conduct research or searching up revision notes.

5. Problem-solving or coming up with innovative ideas.

6. Learning in collaboration with other students.

7. Making reasoned evaluations

### **Ways of Being Enterprising at Home**

1. Taking Responsibility - E.g., Help with chores in the house

2. Earning Money - E.g., Walking pets or babysitting

3. Planning Activities - E.g., Grocery shopping or mowing the lawn

4. Developing new skills and using them in different situations. E.g., Learning to manage money or finances.
