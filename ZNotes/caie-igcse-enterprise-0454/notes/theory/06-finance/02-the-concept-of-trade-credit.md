## The Concept of Trade Credit

- This refers to an agreement  where customers can purchase goods on account, without paying cash, and pay the supplier at a later date.
  - It is give for a specific number of days
  - Cash is not immediately paid so delaying payment is a source of finance.

### Trade credit Between Enterprise and Suppliers:

- Trade Payable: Amount of money an enterprise owes the supplier for goods received but not yet paid for.

- supplier: A person or organization that provides goods and services to enterprises.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Enterprise may get discounts for early payment|Enterprise will have to pay a penalty in case of late payment|
|Enterprise can buy goods now and pay later|Suppliers have to chase enterprise for late payments which is time and cost consuming|
|Trade credit encourages repeat of business for suppliers||

### Trade Credit Between Enterprise and Customers:

- Trade Receivable: Amount of money customers owe an enterprise for products or services purchased for but not yet paid for.
- Customer: A person or organization that buys products or services from an enterprise.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Customers can buy goods now and pay later|Customers will face a penalty for late payments|
|Enterprise can encourage loyalty and repeat business by offering trade credit deals|chasing late payment or non paying customers can be time and cost consuming|
|Customers may get discounts for early payments|There is a risk of of having an impact on cash flow of an enterprise due to late payments or non paying customers|

- Cash flow: Money going into or out of an enterprise.
