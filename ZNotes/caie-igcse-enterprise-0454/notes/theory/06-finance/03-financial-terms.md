## Financial Terms

- **Cashflow Forecast:** Helps understand possible financial situation of an enterprise in the future using information on cash moving in and out of enterprise.

  - Cash inflow: cash coming into the enterprise.

  - Cash outflow: cash moving out of the enterprise.

  - Surplus: when cash moving into the enterprise is greater than cash moving out of the enterprise.

  - Deficit: when cash moving into the enterprise is less than cash moving out of the enterprise.

- **Break Even Point:** When enterprise income is able to cover for all the cots. (Total revenue =  Total cost).

  - Revenue: Total amount of money made after selling products or services.

  - Variable Cost: expenses that vary according to enterprises activities.

  - Fixed Cost: expenses that usually stay constant and do not vary according to enterprise activities.

  - Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost

  - Contribution = Variable Cost Per Unit - Sale Price Per Unit

- **Income Statement:** Helps identify whether an enterprise is having profits or losses.

  - Expenditure: Total amount of money that moves out of an enterprise.
  - Cost of Sales: money used to produce goods and services.
