# Finance

## Sources of Finance

### Sources of Finance for Start Ups

1. Personal Savings:

- Owners personal funds are used to start up enterprise.
- It is an internal source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Free to use money whenever needed|If enterprise fails to survive, then owner loses their money|
|Can get back money with an interest||

2. Loan from family and friends:

- Family or friends lending money as a form of investment, used to start up an enterprise.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Family would be more willing to give some funds|If enterprise fails to survive, then family loses their money|
|Family may not demand a great interest percentage||

3. Bank Loan:

- Bank providing a large sum of money that is paid back with interest
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Great amount of funds are available|High interest rates|
|Long term source of finance|A lot of documentations and legal work is required in order to get loan.|
||Failure to make payments back to the bank can result in the bank seizing enterprises assets|

4. Bank Overdraft:

- Provided when enterprise has no funds in their account.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Can be used to resolve a short term financial issue|High interest rate|
||short term source of finance|

5. Leasing:

- Renting of machinery for a period of time with a fee.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Cheaper than buying equipment|Long term it can be expensive, as cost of renting may sum up greater than cost of buying equipment|
|Access to new or updated equipment can easily be accessed after leasing period for old equipment is over||

6. Mortgages:

- Loan provided by bank to purchase properties.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Large amounts of funds available|Lot of documentation and paperwork is needed to be provided to receive funds|
|Usually has less interest rate|Failure to make payments back on time can result in property bought using mortgage to be seized|

7. Community Sources:

- Money contributed by local groups or communities.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Used for projects leading to betterment of the community|Money can only used on community projects agreed for|
|Usually doesn't have to be paid back|If funds are not used as agreed then it can be reclaimed.|

8. Grants:

- Funds provided for projects usually by governments.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Funds can be used for projects that are too costly for expensive|Money can only used on projects agreed for|
|Usually doesn't have to be paid back|If funds are not used as agreed then it can be reclaimed.|

9. Subsidies:

- Support for enterprises typically managing public welfare projects.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Can either get an enterprise funds or percentage decrease in tax bill|Enterprise needs to meet certain criteria's to get funds|
||Only some types of enterprise can access funds or benefits|

10. Crowdfunding:

- Raising funds using digital media and marketing and offering rewards to individual investors in return.
- External source of finance


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Small investments from large number of user can lead to great sum of money|Good technical and marketing skills are needed in order to raise large sum of money|
||Failure of enterprise to survive can result every investor loosing their money invested.|

11. Shares:

- Selling shares to people who invest in the company.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Large sum of money can be obtained|Shareholders would expect the right to give their inputs in the operations and decisions of the enterprise.|
||Selling too many shares can lead to owner loosing their ownership and control over enterprise.|

### Sources of Finance for Expansion and Trading:

1. Personal Savings:

- Owners own funds used for enhancement of enterprise.
- Internal source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Free to use money whenever needed|If enterprise fails to survive, then owner loses their money|
|No interest is to be paid||

2. Retained Profits:

- Part of enterprises profits that reinvested into the enterprise.
- Internal source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Free to use money whenever and however needed|If a business has shareholders, then they must be informed about how retained profits are utilized, and they may impose their own conditions or expectations.|
|No interest is to be paid back||

3. Private Institutions:

- Businesses that provide funds to entrepreneurs unable to access loans, on the condition that the entrepreneurs agree to take part in a course on financial management.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Anyone can access their loans regardless of how much they earn|Often have very high interest rates compared to banks|

4. Venture Capital:


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|A large sum of funds can be obtained|They would expect control over enterprise operations which could lead to conflicts with enterprise owner|
|They have a lot of knowledge and skills which can contribute to the growth of the enterprise||

5. Shares:

- Selling of shares to already existing shareholders.
- External source of finance.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Large sum of money can be obtained|Shareholders would expect their percentage of the profits|
||Selling too many shares can lead to owner loosing their ownership and control over enterprise.|
