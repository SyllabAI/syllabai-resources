# Communication

Communication involves the process of passing on information among individuals or groups in a work environment.

## Types of Communication

### Formal Communication

Methods of communication which is recognized and officially set up.

Examples:

* Emails

* Reports

* Monthly meetings

### Informal Communication

Method of communication that is casual and usually used when with friends or family

* Examples : Discussions with co-workers outside of work or  messaging friends.

### Choosing type of communication

When choosing a type of communication, it is vital to consider who you are interacting with and what is your aim.

An example is if you are approaching a bank for a loan, a formal tone would be more appropriate as opposed to an informal tone.

### Verbal Communication

* Can be in forms of interviews, meetings, reports letters, emails, negotiations.

* There should always be use respectful language and avoid slangs (use jargons wherever possible).

### Non-verbal Communication

* Relates more to how a person communicates and interacts rather than form of interaction.

  * Ex: A persons body language, looks, tone and volume of voice.

### Methods of Communication:

1. Written

2. Oral

3. Visual

4. Electronic

5. Non Verbal

### Use of different methods of communication with internal and external stakeholders

* Method of communication depends on: what is needed, urgency of matter and type of information to be discussed.

* Examples for Internal stakeholders include:

  * When some urgent feedback or guidance is need from a manager, verbal communication might be best.

  * Sending some documents such as financial records to managers, shareholders and owners using written communication, such as emails.

* Examples for external stakeholders:

  * Reaching out to customers or potential market using electronic communication (social media).

  * Using written communication (emails) to communicate with suppliers.
