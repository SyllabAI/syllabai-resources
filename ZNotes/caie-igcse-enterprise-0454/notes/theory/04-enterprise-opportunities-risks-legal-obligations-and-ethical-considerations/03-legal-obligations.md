## Legal Obligations

Laws and rules are legally put in place to protect stakeholders, such as:

**1. Marketing and selling products or services:** Laws safeguard customers from purchasing dangerous, defective products or items that don't align with what was advertised.

**2. Finance:** Laws and guidelines are to be followed when providing or requesting financing to ensure that the correct documents are used and all parties involved are protected.

**3. Production:** Laws are established to ensure that employees work in a safe environment.

**4. Employment:** Laws protect employees from discrimination, ensure they receive at least the minimum wage, and guarantee fair treatment in the workplace.
