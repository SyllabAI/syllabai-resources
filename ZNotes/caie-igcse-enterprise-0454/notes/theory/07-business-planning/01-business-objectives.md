# Business Planning

## Business Objectives

* Aim: The overall goal of a business. (Long Term)

* Objectives: Targets which help achieve the aim. (Short Term)

### Objectives should be : 

* **S** - Specific

* **M** - Measurable

* **A** - Achievable

* **R** - Realistic

* **T** - Time Based

### Types of Objectives:

* Profit Maximization

* Growth or Expansion

* Survival

* Cash Flow

* Being Ethical

* Legal Compliance

* Increasing Revenue

* Increasing Market Share

* Not for Profit

* Protecting the Environment

* Supporting Social Needs

### Influence of Aims on Enterprise Activities:

* Enterprises will choose objectives which would lead to them meeting their goals and then plan activities to work on the objectives. 

  * Ex: If the objectives in expansion, then the enterprise will looks for places where the demand of their services will be high and open up shops in more areas.

  * Ex: If objective is increasing sales then the enterprise may work on their marketing or they ay increase the costs of their services.
