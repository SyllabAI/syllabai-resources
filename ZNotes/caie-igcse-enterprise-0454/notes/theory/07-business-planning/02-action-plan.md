## Action Plan

* Action plan is a framework of activities an enterprise needs to complete.

  * It consists of the activities, when they need to be completed, by who they need to be completed and how progress is to be monitored.

### Importance of Action Plans:

* Helps identify what is to be prioritized and what can be set for later. This ensures all the work is done in a timely manner.

* Helps figure out potential problems or issues that may arise during an activity which allows for the planning to tackle them.

* It helps monitor progress made so far. 

* Keeps track of who is responsible for what and when.

### Methods of Monitoring Action Plans:

* Checklists

* Check progress of activities

* Gaining customer feedback

* Reviewing sales made

### Importance of Updating Action Plans:

* Situations can change or some issues may arise due to which the planned activities in the action plan may not be able to be completed.

  * Due to these reasons it is important to constantly monitor the activities being completed and modify the action plan whenever necessary.
