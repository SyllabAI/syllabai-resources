# Markets and Customers

## The Purpose of Marketing

**How marketing helps achieve enterprise aim?**

* Raises awareness of products and services

* Improves enterprise image.

* Increases sales, market share and profits.

* Increases and maintains brand loyalty.

* Helps enterprise reach target market.

**How does marketing help customers?**

* Provides knowledge and information on product.

* Helps customers make informed decisions so that they save time and money.

* Creates awareness of products they didn’t even know were available.
