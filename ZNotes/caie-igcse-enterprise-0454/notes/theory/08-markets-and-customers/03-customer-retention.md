## Customer Retention

Customer retention is measuring how loyal customers are to an enterprise and the likelihood of them buying products or services repeatedly.

### Reasons for retaining customers :

* Loyal customers tend to spend more per visit.

* Word of mouth can bring in more customers and so more sales and profits.

* Help gain new customers.

* Helps withstand competition as loyal customers will make repeated purchases.

### Methods of Measuring Customer Retention:

* Number of complaints.

* Number of sales

* Questionnaires and surveys

* Focus groups

* Suggestion boxes.

### Methods of Retaining Existing Customers:

* Offering good quality products.

* Effective customer service

* Resolving complaints.

* Asking for feedback.

* Offering wide and new range of products and services.

* Loyalty rewards.
