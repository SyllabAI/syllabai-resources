## Market Research

### Methods of Identifying Potential Customers

**Primary Research**: Collecting original data for a specific purpose.

Types of Primary Research :

* Questionnaires and Surveys:

  Asking people a set list of questions.

  Can be done face to face or sent by email/chat.

**Secondary Research**: Collection of already existing information.

Types of Secondary Research:

* Newspaper and articles.

* Government released data.

* Existing data from enterprises sales records.

### Effectiveness of Different Methods of Market Research:

A method is to be chosen by considering :

* Type of information needed.

* Time that available to conduct research.

* The cost and the budget.
