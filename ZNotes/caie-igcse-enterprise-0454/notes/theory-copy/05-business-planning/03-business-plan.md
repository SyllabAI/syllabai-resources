## Business Plan

### Purpose and Importance of Business Plans:

* Can be used as proof to show that an idea is viable.

* Helps entrepreneurs focus on their aims and goals, competitions, finances and the market.

* Helps identify and plan for the enterprises finances.

* Can be helpful when reaching out to banks or organizations for loans as the business plan shows how well the entrepreneur has planned and their commitment. 

* Helps identify potential risks and allows time to plan how the risk will be dealt with.

### Contents of a Business Plan:

* Preliminary Information

* Marketing Information

* Operations

* Human Resource 

* Financial

### Methods of Monitoring Business Plans:

* Getting feedback from customers

* Looking at the cashflow forecast and Income statement.

* Gathering on the employees , like how many hours they have worked.

### Reasons for Updating Action Plans:

* Change in management.

* Changes in market.

* New competition.

* Initial objects and aims planned are achieved.

* Additional sources of finance needed.

* New products of services are introduced.
