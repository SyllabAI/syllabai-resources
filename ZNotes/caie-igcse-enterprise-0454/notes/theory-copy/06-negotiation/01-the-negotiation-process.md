# Negotiation

## The Negotiation Process

**Negotiation** involves discussions with an aim of reaching a conclusion regarding a course of action or to resolve a dispute in a way that satisfies the interests of all parties involved.

### Negotiations Involve:

- Resolving disputes
- Bargaining for individuals or collective advantage
- Agreeing on process or course of actions

### Stages of the Negotiation Process:

**1. Planning**

- Setting Objectives
- Possible outcomes
- Strengths and weaknesses of proposal
- Figuring possible arguments or counter arguments
- Deciding which evidence can be used to back the proposal

**2. Conducting the Negotiation**

- Set the tone

  - Select time and location which suitable for all involved.
  - Try to get to know the other party involved.
  - Focus and be careful of language you use and what or how you say something
  - Be confident

- Presenting Proposal

  - Be simple, clear and specific.
  - Visual aids can help enhance presentation.

- Understanding others point of view

  - Offer and accept to reach an agreement
  - Bargain
  - Listen to the other side

- Summarize to check understanding

  - Be clear about what you are offering.
  - Ask more questions to confirm understanding

- Reach an agreement

  - Check if both sides are happy
  - Agreement should preferably be in writing

**3. Measuring Success**

- Consider:

  - Were objectives achieved and how were they achieved ?
  - Were both sides happy?
