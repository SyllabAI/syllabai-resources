# Setting Up a New Enterprise
## The Enterprise Process
### The Six Stages of an Enterprise

1. **Identifying the problem, need, or want**: Figuring out what problems an existing enterprise may be facing or could face.

2. **Creative Solutions**: coming up with innovative ideas to resolve the problem identified.

3. **Create an action plan**: plan all tasks or activities, keeping in mind the budget, employees' strengths or skills, and specific deadlines. 

4. **Implementing plan**: Follow the plan, and if issues arise, resolve them as soon as possible. 

5. **Monitor Progress**: Look over the progress made so far and how well the plan made is being followed. (If it's an existing company, consider the progress made).

6. **Evaluate success and failures**: Look back to what went well and what did not and how it can be improved next time. (Customer feedback can help in making better evaluations)
