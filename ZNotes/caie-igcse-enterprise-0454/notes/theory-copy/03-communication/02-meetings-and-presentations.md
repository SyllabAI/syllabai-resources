## Meetings and Presentations

### Steps of organizing a meeting

1. Send notice of meeting.

2. If meeting is accepted then send agenda of meeting.

### Reasons why meetings should be carefully planned:

* To ensure all key points and concerns are discussed.

* Everyone is able to give in their input.

* Ensure meeting does not run beyond the set duration.

### Important documents needed for meetings

* Notice: Includes details of meeting such as date, time duration, location and purpose.

* Agenda: Attendee list, objectives to be discussed.

* Minutes:  summary of points that have been discussed and actions to be taken before the next meeting.

### Important documents used during presentations:

* Visual aids

  * Meeting organizers should decide when to give out the visual aids and give attendees time to raise any questions or concerns they may have

* Handouts

  * Ensure the information in handouts are same as presentation.

  * Ensure information is short, concise and to the point.

### Evaluating a meeting or presentation

* Can be done by checking through agenda to see if all points were discussed as well if 

* Reviewing the outcomes and whether or not they were as expected.
