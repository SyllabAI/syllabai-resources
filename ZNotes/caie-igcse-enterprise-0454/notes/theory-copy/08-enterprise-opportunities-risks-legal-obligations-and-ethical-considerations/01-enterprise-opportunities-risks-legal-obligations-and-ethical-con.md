# Enterprise Opportunities, Risks, Legal Obligations, and Ethical Considerations

## Opportunities

**Opportunity** is a moment or situation that makes it possible for something to happen or be achieved.

### **Ways through which Opportunities Arise**

1. Changes in customers needs or wants in regards of products. Examples include: new fashion trends, change in income or populations size and structure.

2. Change in ability to fulfil needs and wants.

3. Advancements in technology.

4. Updates on governments laws and policies. For example: change in taxation rules or accessibility of grants and subsidies.

5. (**Note**: Opportunities can exist at local, national, international, or global levels.)
