## Ethical Considerations

**Ethics** refers to the values or principles that a person upholds, which guide and influence their decision-making.

- An entrepreneur should always consider the **positive** and **negative** impacts their business activities may have on the community.

  - A negative impact include: pollution from business activities, like air pollution, which can affect the health of people living nearby.

  - A positive impact can include: supporting community activities which helps enterprises strengthen bonds with the community and promote growth.

### **Ethical considerations that can arise within the enterprises**

1. Whether or not a percentage of the profits made should be given to charities.

2. Paying the suppliers fairly.

3. Should organic farming be implemented or should the use of chemicals and pesticides continue being used in growth of agriculture.

4. Is minimum wage enough to cover employees expenses.

### **Positive impact of ethical considerations on enterprises activities**

1. Better brand image which can lead to higher sales.

2. Suppliers can offer more flexible terms and conditions.

### **Negative impact of ethical considerations on enterprises activities**

1. If suppliers are being paid at fair price, this increases costs of enterprise and so enterprise may increase their sale price.

(Note: At times, ethics and profits can be at odds, as ethical practices may lead to reduced profitability.)
