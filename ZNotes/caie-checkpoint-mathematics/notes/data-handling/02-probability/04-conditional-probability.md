## Conditional Probability

### Formula:

**$P(A\|B)$ = $\frac{P(A,B)}{P(B)}$**

**Question:**

Susan took two tests. The probability of her passing the two tests is 0.6. The probability of her passing the first test is 0.8. Find the probability of her passing the second test.

**Solution**:

P(Both tests pass) = $0.6$

P(First test pass ) = $0.8$

P(Second test pass) = $\frac{0.6}{0.8} = 0.75$ <= Answer
