## Example Problems

1.  Paper: Maths Specimen Paper 1 #8

Question:

Martin is playing a game. The probability of winning is 0.3. What is the probability of not winning?

**Solution**:

P(Winning) = 0.3

P(Winning + Losing) = 1,

as it is the total P(Losing) = 1- 0.3 = 0.7 = Answer

 

2. Paper: Maths Paper 2 April 2018 #2

Complete these sentences.

* The probability that a football team wins a match is 0.6 and the probability it does not win is...

**Solution**:

P(Win) = 0.6 P(Win+Lose) = 1

P(Lose) = 1 - 0.6 = 0.4 = Answer

 

* The probability that a player scores a goal is... and the probability that the player does not score a goal is 3/8.

**Solution**:

P(No Score) = $\frac{3}{8}$

P(Score + No Score) = 1

P(Score) = 1 - $\frac{3}{8}$ = $\frac{5}{8}$

 

* The probability that a fan supports a team is 72% and the probability that the fan does not support the team is...%.

Theoretical and Experimental Probability:

P(Support) = 72%

P(Support + No Support) = 100% \n P(No Support) = 100%-72% = 28% = Answer
