## Multiplying Probabilities

- Multiply all the fractions which you get

- Multiply, as the events are dependent on each other or happen togeather, use AND 

- Example: Find the probability of getting 2 6’s while rolling two fair dies.

 

### Probability of getting a six on the first roll:

Total possibilities = 6 Number of ‘6’ appearing = 1 ,

So fraction = $\frac{1}{6}$

 

### Probability of getting a six on the second roll:

Total possibilities = 6 Number of ‘6’ appearing = 1,

So fraction = $\frac{1}{6}$

- Multiplying
  $\frac{1}{6}\times\frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{36}$

Here, the two events are dependent on each other, as if no 6 is rolled on the first roll, it is impossible to get 2 6’s in two rolls.
