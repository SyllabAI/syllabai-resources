## **Stage 9**

* Significant figures are numbers or digits required in mathematics in order to give a proper quantity.
* The usual number of significant figures is 3. More than 3 figures are also accepted.
* During intermediate working, we have to round off non-terminating decimals to 5 significant figures or more.
* There are 3 rules:
  * Zeros are not significant after decimal point before non zero digits

  * All non zero digits are significant

  * Zeros after non zero digits in a decimal are significant
* Double-checking the answer will help us to realise whether we made a mistake or not.

E.g. If we get an answer as -4:30 p.m., we can immediately interpret that our answer is wrong since time cannot be negative.

* It is important to **pay attention to the units**.
