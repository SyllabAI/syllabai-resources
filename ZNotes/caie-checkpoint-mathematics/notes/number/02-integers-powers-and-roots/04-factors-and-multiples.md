## Factors and Multiples

* A factor (also called a divisor) is a number which you can divide another number by

E.g. 2 is a factor of 6, because we can divide 6 by 2. 3 is also a factor of 6.

A multiple is the value which we get when we multiply another value by a number.

E.g. 12 is a multiple of 6, because we can get 12 from multiplying 2 into 6.

* The HCF (Hightest Common Factor) of two or more numbers is their largest common factor.

E.g. HCF(12, 14) = 2, because that is the largest number which we can divide both 12 and 14 by.

* LCM (Lowest Common Multpile), which is the lowest common multiple which two or more numbers have.

E.g. LCM(2,3) = 6 the lowest common multiple of 2 and 3 is 6.

* Prime numbers are the numbers which can only be divided by 2 numbers, which are 1 and itself.

A few prime numbers: 2,3,5,7,11, 13, 17,19…

* A number can be factored, which is the process of dividing a number by other prime numbers.
* This process is called prime factorisation.
