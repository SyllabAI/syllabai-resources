## Stage 9

* Factors are values that can divide another given value.

==> Factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8 (all these numbers can divide 8).

* Transforming to division can help us to divide by decimals and place the decimal point correctly.
* The effect of multiplying or dividing a number by another number between 0 and 1 will make it a decimal value.
* There are infinite decimals between two whole numbers.
