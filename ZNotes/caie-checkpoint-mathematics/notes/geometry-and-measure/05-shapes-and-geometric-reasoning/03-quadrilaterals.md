## Quadrilaterals

Quadrilaterals have four sides and their angle sum is 360°.

Examples of quadrilaterals are kites, rectangles, squares, and rhombuses.

* Quadrilaterals have an exterior angle sum of 360°. All quadrilaterals have 4 vertices, 4 sides and two diagonals.
* The value of each diagonal is equal to the square root of the sum of the two unlike sides of the quadrilaterals.
