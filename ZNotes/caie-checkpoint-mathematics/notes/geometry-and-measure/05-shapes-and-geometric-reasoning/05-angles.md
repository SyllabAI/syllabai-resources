## Angles

* Angles can be acute (< 90°), right (=90°) or obtuse (>90°).
* Reflex angles are more than 180°.
* Alternate angles occur on different sides of the transversal are equal.
* Corresponding angles are formed when a line passes two parallel transversals.
* Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.
