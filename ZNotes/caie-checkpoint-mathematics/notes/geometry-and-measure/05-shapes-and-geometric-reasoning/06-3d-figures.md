## 3D Figures

3D figures are formed when a 2D figure is extended from all points.

Examples of 3D figures:

* Cones

* Cubes

* Cylinders

* Cuboids.

  3D figures have volume (volume refers to which is the amount of substance that can fit into the figure.)

* All 3D figures have a net. 

  A net can be reasoned/created through the 3D view of the figure.
