**Question 1**

![](../../../assets/b3f1f0ea5a5f-62391e0d47c1806c8d791b352fb6104f1579f564c2f8.54)Solution:

It is known that the perimeter is 20cm.

This means that each side is $ \frac {20 } {4} $= 5cm.

Thus, area of each square = $5^2$ = 25 $cm^2$

There are a total of 8 squares, which makes the total area:

25 x 8 = 200 $cm^2$

**Question 2**

![](../../../assets/63f1bf09fb23-62391e0d47c1806c8d791b35f2989e7da6496ad71e48.55)

Solution:

Area of P = 17 $ \rightarrow $ Side of Square P = $ \sqrt {17} $

Area of R = 50 $ \rightarrow $ Side of square R = $ \sqrt {50} $

Side of Q = *x* $ \rightarrow $ Area of Q = $ x^2 $

Using Pythagoras’, $ ( \sqrt {17} )^2 + {x}^2 = ( \sqrt {50} )^2 $

$ x^2 $ = 50 - 17 = 33

Thus, the area of square Q is 33 $cm^2$.
