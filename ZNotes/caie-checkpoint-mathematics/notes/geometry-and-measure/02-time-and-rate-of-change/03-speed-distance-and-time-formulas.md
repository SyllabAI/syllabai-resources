## Speed, Distance and Time Formulas

Speed=$\frac{Distance}{Time}$

Distance = Speed x Time

Time =$\frac{Distance}{Speed}$

Average speed = $\frac{Total distance}{Total time}$

**Example**

Find the average speed of a vehicle if it travelled 120 km in 3 hours.

Total distance =120km

Total time = 3 hours

Average speed = $$\frac{120}{30}$$ = 40km/hr
