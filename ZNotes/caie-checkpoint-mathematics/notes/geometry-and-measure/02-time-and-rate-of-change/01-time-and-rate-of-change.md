# Time and Rate of Change

## Time

Time is a *dimension* by which us humans use to help measure the ongoing and continuous sequence of events.

Time is used as a quantity.

It is often measured by using  **seconds, minutes, or hours.**

### 12- hour clock and 24- hour clock

It can be measured by using a **12-hour clock** or a **24-hour clock**.

* A 12-hour clock has time restarting at 12:00.

* 24-hour clock has time restarting at 00:00.

* Time in 12-hour clocks are often ending with either AM(**Ante Meridiem**) or PM(**Post Meridiem**) .

* We can convert time from a 12-hour clock to a 24-hour clock by adding 12 hours to the 24 hour clock. Adding 12 hours is only for time in **PM**.

  For example:

  4:00 PM is equal to 4+12=16:00.

  ### Distance, Speed and Time

  Speed is **inversely proportiona**l to time.

  A travel graph usually shows the ratio between time and distance travelled, from which we can calculate speed.

  Speed=**$\frac{distance}{time}$**
