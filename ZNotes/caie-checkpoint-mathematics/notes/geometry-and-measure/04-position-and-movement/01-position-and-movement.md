# Position and Movement

Position refers to the location of an object, while movement refers to the distance of it after moving.

* In mathematics, we can find how far an object moved with the use of appropriate apparatus, such as rulers and measuring tape. 
* Another way to measure how far an object has moved is with the help of the cartesian plane (XY plane).

  The midpoint of a segment refers to the point bisecting the length of the segment.
