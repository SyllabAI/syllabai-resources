## Change in Shape

A shape can be rotated on a given point with a given angle.

A shape can be reflected along a given line.

A shape can be translated up/down/left/right on a cartesian plane with a given direction and magnitude.

Shapes can be enlarged by a scale factor, where all the sides are all multiplied or divided by the same ratio (called the scale factor).

### Tessellation

* Tessellation is a way of covering a space using the identical figures, without any overlaps.
* Regular polygons like triangles, squares, and hexagons are examples of shapes that tessellate, while others like pentagons, circles, and ovals do not. 

  ### Bearings 
* Bearings always measure clockwise from north on the compass and written with three digits like 53$^{\circ}$ as  053$^{\circ}$ .
* Distance on a coordinate plane can be measured by subtracting the difference between the number of the two points on either axis.
* The midpoint of a segment refers to the point bisecting the length of the segment.
