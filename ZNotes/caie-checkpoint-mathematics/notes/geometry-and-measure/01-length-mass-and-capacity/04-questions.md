## Questions

1. A water bottle has a capacity of 50ml. Find how many litres are needed to fill 80 of these bottles.

Solution:

80 x 50 = 4000ml

1 litre (l) = 1000ml

$$\frac{4000}{1000}$$= 4litres

2. Write these measurements in **ascending order:** 30cm, 0.35m, 320mm, 28cm.

First convert all to a common length which is centimetres (cm).

30cm = 30cm

28cm = 28cm

0\.35m = 35cm

320mm = 32cm

In ascending order: 28, 30, 32, 35

Final answer: 28cm, 30cm, 320mm, 0.35m

3. A land of area 3$$km^2$$ has 33% of it used for growing crops. Find the area of land used for growing crops in $$cm^2$$.

Solution:

3 x $$\frac{33}{100} = $$\frac{99}{100}$$km^2$$

1km = 100,000cm

$$1km^2$$ = 1km x 1km

1 $$km^2$$ = 100,000cm x 100,000cm = 10,000,000,000$$cm^2$$

$$\frac{99}{100}  $$km^2$$

$$\frac{99}{100} x 10,000,000,000$$cm^2$$ = **9,900,000,000$$cm^2$$**
