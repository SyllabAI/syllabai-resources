## Metric Conversion

Commonly used metric units:

### Length

* Centimetres (cm)
* Metres (m)
* Kilometres (km)

These units may be squared to calculate **area (eg $$cm^2$$), or** cubed to calculate **volume (eg $$cm^3$$).**

1 m = 100 cm (One hundred)

1 km = 1,000 m (One thousand)

1 km = 100,000 cm (One hundred thousand)

1 $$m^2$$ = 10,000 $$cm^2$$ (Ten thousand)

1 $$km^2$$ = 1,000,000 $$m^2$$ (One million)

1 $$km^2$$ = 10,000,000,000 $$cm^2$$ (Ten billion)

### Volume

Millilitre (ml or mL)

Litre (l or L)

Centimetres Cubed ($$cm^3$$)

Meters cubed ($$m^3$$)

Kilometres cubed ($$km^3$$)

1 $$m^3$$ = 1,000,000 $$cm^3$$ (One million)

1 $$km^3$$ = 1,000,000,000 $$m^3$$ (One billion)

1 $$km^3$$ = 1,000,000,000,000,000 $$cm^3$$ (One quadrillion)

1 ml = 1 $$cm^3$$

1 L = 1000 ml = 1000 $$cm^3$$
