## Practice Problems

Question 1:

A cube has side lengths 5 centimetres. Calculate the volume of the cube.

Solution:

A cube has all the sides equal: length, width and depth. One side is 5cm, so all sides are 5cm.

5cm x 5cm x 5cm = 125cm

Answer = 125$$cm^3$$

Question 2:

A cube has sides of length 6 cm. A cuboid has sides of lengths 6cm, 7cm and 5 cm. Calculate which has the **larger volume, the cube or the cuboid.**

Solution:

Volume of cube = 6 x 6 x 6 = 36 x 6 = 216

Volume of cuboid = 6 x 5 x 7 = 30 x 7 = 210

216 > 210, so the cube has a larger volume.
