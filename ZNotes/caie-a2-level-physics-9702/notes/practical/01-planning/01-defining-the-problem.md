## Defining the Problem (3 marks)

* Identify:
  * The independent variable in the experiment (1 mark)
  * The dependent variable in the experiment (1 mark)
  * The quantities to be controlled – kept constant (1 mark)
* The independent variable is the one you modify to observe its effect on the dependent variable.
* The quantity to be kept constant can vary, but choose the one that directly influences the variable being measured in a physical sense.
* Explain why it is important to keep a variable constant using scientific terminology
