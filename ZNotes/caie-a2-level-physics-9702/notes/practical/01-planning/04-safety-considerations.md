## Safety Considerations (1 Mark)

* Assess the risk of the experiment
* Describe precautions that be taken to keep risks to a minimum for errors in the experiment and your own safety as well. (1 Mark)
* Try to write as many as possible (if you have time) as you never know which one could be found in the marking scheme

**Examples of Safety Considerations**:

* Risk Assessment:
  * Light: Avoid direct eye exposure; use goggles.
  * Electricity: Risk of electrocution; wear insulated gloves.
  * Heat: Risk of burns; wear gloves and use holders for hot objects.
  * Heavy Objects: Risk of injury from falling objects; use sand buckets and gloves.
  * Sound: Conduct in a quiet room to prevent noise interference.
  * Lightroom: Conduct in a dark room to prevent external light interference.
  * Measurement: Take 3-4 readings and average for accuracy.
  * Stability: Ensure apparatus stability, especially with added weights.
  * Radioactive Substances: Store in lead containers.
  * Horizontal Surface: Use a spirit leveler.
  * Current Protection: Use protective resistors to reduce current.
  * Safety Shutdown: Switch off currents when not in use to prevent overheating.
  * Hall Probe: Keep at right angles to the magnetic field; calibrate beforehand.
  * Magnetic Fields: Avoid external magnetic field interference.
  * Wind Experiments: Close windows, and turn off fans and AC.
  * Oscillation Time Period: Conduct 10 oscillations and divide to find the period.
  * Fiducial Markers: Use for oscillation measurements.
  * Fast Motions: Record with a video camera and playback in slow motion.
  * Object Drop Experiment: Ensure the object is released from rest.
