# Capacitance

## Capacitors

* **Function:** storing energy
* **Usage:** Time delay, power smoothing, and protection against surges and spikes, temporary power supply
* **Dielectric:** an electrical insulator

**How a Capacitor Stores Energy:**

* On a capacitor, there is a separation of charge with \+ve on one plate and –ve on the other.
* To separate the charges, work must be done; hence, energy is released when charges come together
* Equal and opposite charges on the plates so **no resultant charge** (a capacitor stores energy not charge)
