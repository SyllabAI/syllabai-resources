## Capacitance and Farad

* **Capacitance ($C$):** The charge ($Q$) stored per unit potential difference ($V$) across the plates or conductor.
* **Unit:** **Farad** ($\text{F}$), defined as $1\text{ coulomb per volt}$ ($1\text{ F} = 1\text{ C V}^{-1}$). Commonly expressed in $\mu\text{F}$ or $\text{nF}$.

$$
C = \frac{Q}{V}
$$

* The capacitance of a capacitor is directly proportional to the area
  of the plates and inversely proportional to the distance between the
  plates

$$
C \propto \frac{A}{d}
$$
