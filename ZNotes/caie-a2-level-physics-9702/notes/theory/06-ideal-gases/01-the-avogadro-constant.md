## The Avogadro Constant

* **Amount of Substance ($n$):** An SI base quantity used to measure the quantity of elementary entities (atoms, molecules, ions, or electrons) in a sample.

* **The Mole ($\text{mol}$):** The fundamental SI unit for the amount of substance.

* **Avogadro Constant ($N_A$):** $6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$. The number of microscopic particles (atoms, molecules, or ions) contained in exactly one mole of any substance.

* **Definition:** the Avogadro constant/the mole equals the amount of substance containing the same number of atoms found in exactly $12\text{ g}$ of carbon-12
