## Kinetic Energy of a Molecule

### Deriving Average Kinetic Energy of a Molecule

By comparing the microscopic kinetic model with the macroscopic ideal gas equation ($pV = N k T$):

$$

\frac{1}{3} N m \langle c^2 \rangle = N k T

$$

Divide both sides by $N$ and re-arrange to isolate the translational kinetic energy ($E_k = \frac{1}{2} m \langle c^2 \rangle$):

$$

\frac{1}{2} m \langle c^2 \rangle = \frac{3}{2} k T

$$

**Conclusion:** The average translational kinetic energy of a single gas molecule is directly proportional to absolute thermodynamic temperature ($T$).
