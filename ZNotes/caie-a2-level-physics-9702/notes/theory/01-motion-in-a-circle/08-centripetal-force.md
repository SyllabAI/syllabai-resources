## **Centripetal Force:**

Because the object is accelerating, it is not in equilibrium. There must be a resultant force acting on the object to keep it in its circular path, known as the centripetal force ($$F_c$$).

An equation can be derived using Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$ and the formulae for acceleration above):

$$

F_c = mr\omega^2 = m\frac{v^2}{r}

$$

Characteristics of Centripetal Force:

* It is always directed exactly towards the center of the circle.

* It is constant in magnitude for uniform circular motion.
