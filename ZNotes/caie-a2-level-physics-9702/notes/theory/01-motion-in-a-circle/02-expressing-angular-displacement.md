## **Expressing Angular Displacement:**

**Definition:** The angle through which an object turns or rotates relative to a reference line, measured in radians.

You calculate angular displacement by dividing the arc length ($$s$$) by the radius ($$r$$):

Formula:
$$
\theta = \frac{s}{r}
$$

When the arc length ($$s$$) is equal to the radius ($$r$$) ($s = r$), then:

$$\theta = \frac{r}{r} = 1 \text{ rad}$$
