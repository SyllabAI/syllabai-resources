## **Angular Acceleration:**

The angular acceleration ($$\alpha$$) is defined as the rate of change of angular velocity with respect to time:

$$

\alpha = \frac{\Delta \omega}{\Delta t}

$$

The SI unit for angular acceleration is radians per second squared ($\text{rad s}^{-2}$).

### Key Relationships:

Just as with linear motion, these terms describe the behavior of rotational motion:

* The gradient of an angular velocity vs. time graph represents the angular acceleration.

* The area under an angular velocity vs. time graph represents the angular displacement ($$\theta$$).
