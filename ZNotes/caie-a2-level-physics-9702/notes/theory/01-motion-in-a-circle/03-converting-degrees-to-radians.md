## **Converting Degrees to Radians:**

A complete revolution corresponds to $360^\circ$ or $2\pi\text{ rad}$.

To convert between degrees and radians, use the formula:

$$

\text{angle in rad} = \text{angle in degrees} \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}

$$

**Quick reference values:**

* $1\text{ rad} \approx 57.3^\circ$

* $180^\circ \approx \pi \text{ rad}$

* $360^\circ \approx 2\pi \text{ rad}$
