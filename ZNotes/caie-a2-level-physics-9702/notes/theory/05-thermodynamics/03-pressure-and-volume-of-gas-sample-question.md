## Pressure and Volume of Gas (Sample Question):

Maximilian is performing a series of actions on a gas as follows and he completes a table to show the changes for each stage:
![Three-Stage Gas Thermodynamic Cycle](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/d5a84d2cbda843c8fc750db4.webp =784x)

![Thermodynamic Processes Energy Changes](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/667b6838d6371304471f40e9.webp =782x)

**Explaination:**

* **Stage 1: $A \rightarrow B$ (Constant Pressure Cooling / Isobaric Compression)**

  * **Process:** The gas is cooled at a constant pressure of $1.2 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}$ from an initial volume of $V_A = 0.24\text{ m}^3$ to $V_B = 0.08\text{ m}^3$.

  * **Thermal Energy ($q$):** Thermal energy is removed from the gas, so $q = -48.0\text{ kJ} = -48,000\text{ J}$.

  * **Work Done ($w$):** Work is done **on** the gas as it compresses:

    $$w = -P \Delta V = -P (V_B - V_A)$$

    $$w = -(1.2 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}) \times (0.08 - 0.24)\text{ m}^3 = +19,200\text{ J} = +19.2\text{ kJ}$$

  * **Internal Energy Change ($\Delta U$):** Using the First Law of Thermodynamics ($\Delta U = q + w$):

    $$\Delta U = -48.0\text{ kJ} + 19.2\text{ kJ} = -28.8\text{ kJ}$$

* **Stage 2: $B \rightarrow C$ (Constant Volume Heating / Isochoric Process)**

  * **Process:** The gas is heated at a fixed volume of $V = 0.08\text{ m}^3$, increasing pressure from $1.2 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}$ to $3.5 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}$.

  * **Work Done ($w$):** Because volume remains constant ($\Delta V = 0$), **no work is done on or by the gas**:

    $$w = 0\text{ kJ}$$

  * **Thermal Energy & Internal Energy:** Since $w = 0$, all thermal energy supplied goes directly into increasing internal energy ($\Delta U = q$).

* **Stage 3: $C \rightarrow A$ (Gas Return / Closed Cycle Completion)**

  * **Internal Energy Change ($\Delta U$):** Since internal energy is a state function depending only on temperature and state, returning to initial point $A$ means the total change over the full cycle is zero ($\Delta U_{\text{net}} = 0$).

  * **Net Work Done ($w_{\text{net}}$):** The net work done in the full cycle equals the **enclosed triangular area** on the $P\text{-}V$ graph:

    $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times (0.24 - 0.08)\text{ m}^3 \times (3.5 \times 10^5 - 1.2 \times 10^5)\text{ Pa}$$

    $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 0.16 \times 2.3 \times 10^5 = +18,400\text{ J} = +18.4\text{ kJ}$$
