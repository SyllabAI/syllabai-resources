# Thermodynamics

## Internal Energy

**Definition**: Internal energy is defined as the sum of the random distribution of kinetic and potential energies within a system of molecules

* It is determined by the state of the system (such as temperature, random motion of molecules, and phase of matter). Always include the word **"random"** when defining it in exams

* A rise in temperature directly corresponds to an increase in the average kinetic energy of the molecules, which increases the internal energy of the system

* For an ideal gas (which has zero potential energy as there are no intermolecular forces between molecules), internal energy is purely kinetic and directly proportional to thermodynamic temperature in Kelvin ($\Delta U \propto \Delta T$)
