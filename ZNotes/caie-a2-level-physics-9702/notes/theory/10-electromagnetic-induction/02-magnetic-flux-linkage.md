## Magnetic Flux Linkage

* **Definition:** Magnetic flux linkage is the product of the magnetic flux ($\Phi$) passing through a coil and the number of turns ($N$) in that coil.

* **SI Unit:** Weber ($\text{Wb}$) or Weber-turns ($\text{Wb}\cdot\text{turns}$).

* **Equation:**

  $$

  \text{Magnetic Flux Linkage} = N \Phi = N B A \cos\theta

  $$

* **Variables:**

  * $N$: Number of turns in the coil.

  * $\Phi$: Magnetic flux through a single turn ($\Phi = B A \cos\theta$).

  * $B$: Magnetic flux density.

  * $A$: Cross-sectional area of the coil.

  * $\theta$: Angle between the magnetic field lines and the normal to the coil's cross-sectional area.
