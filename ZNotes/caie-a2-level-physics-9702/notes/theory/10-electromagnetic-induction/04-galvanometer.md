## Galvanometer

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/92c2eb1cd1a1791ba81a091f.webp =621x)

A highly sensitive instrument used to detect and measure small electric currents, as well as determine their direction.

* **Zero-Center Scale:** The needle sits at the center ($0$). A deflection to the **right** indicates current flowing in one direction, while a deflection to the **left** indicates current flowing in the opposite direction.

* **Role in Induction Experiments:** When a magnet moves into a coil, the galvanometer needle deflects. When the magnet stops moving, the needle returns to $0$ (no current). When the magnet is pulled out, the needle deflects in the opposite direction.
