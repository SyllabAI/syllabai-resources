## Practical Examples of Damping

  - Car suspension:
    
      - **Oscillation:** Car oscillates due to spring like connection to
        wheels
    
      - **Damping:** Critical damping needed to stop oscillation as
        quickly as possible to avoid motion sickness – hydraulic in
        nature

  - Tall buildings:
    
      - **Oscillation:** During earthquakes
    
      - **Damping:** Large weight hung at the top of the building to
        supply a counter oscillation
