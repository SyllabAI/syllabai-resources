## Simple Harmonic Motion

**Simple Harmonic Motion:** Motion where the acceleration $a$ is directly proportional to the displacement $x$ from a fixed (equilibrium) point and is always directed in the opposite direction to the displacement.

Defining equation of SHM:

$$
a = - \omega^{2}x
$$

![](https://images.znotes.org/cie/a2/physics-9702/image011.png =300x300)

**Observations from graph:**

* The negative sign in the equation represents that $a$ and $x$
  are in opposite directions. $a$ is always directed towards the
  mean position.

* Straight line passing through the origin implies that $a$ is directly proportional to $x$.
