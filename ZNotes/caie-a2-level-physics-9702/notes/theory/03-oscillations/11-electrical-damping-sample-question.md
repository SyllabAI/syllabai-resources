## Electrical Damping (Sample Question):

Max is investigating a problem where a small magnet undergoes light damping as it oscillates vertically inside a copper coil due to electromagnetic induction. The initial amplitude of the oscillation is \\(4.0\\text\{ cm\}\\). During the first complete oscillation, the total energy of the system decreases by \\(15\\%\\). Calculate the amplitude of the oscillation at the end of this first cycle.

1. **Recall the core SHM energy proportional relationship.**
   \\(E\\propto (\\text\{Amplitude\})\^\{2\}\\implies E=kx\_\{0\}\^\{2\}\\)

2. **Determine the remaining energy after a \\(15\\%\\) decrease.**
   \\(E\_\{\\text\{new\}\}=100\\%-15\\%=85\\%\\text\{\\ of\\ \}E\_\{\\text\{initial\}\}\\)
   \\(\\frac\{E\_\{\\text\{new\}\}\}\{E\_\{\\text\{initial\}\}\}=0.85\\)

3. **Construct the geometric ratio using the amplitudes.**
   \\(\\frac\{E\_\{\\text\{new\}\}\}\{E\_\{\\text\{initial\}\}\}=\\left(\\frac\{x\_\{\\text\{new\}\}\}\{x\_\{\\text\{initial\}\}\}\\right)\^\{2\}\\)
   \\(0.85=\\left(\\frac\{x\_\{\\text\{new\}\}\}\{4.0\}\\right)\^\{2\}\\)

4. **Rearrange and calculate the final amplitude.**
   \\(x\_\{\\text\{new\}\}=4.0\\times \\sqrt\{0.85\}\\)
   \\(x\_\{\\text\{new\}\}=\\mathbf\{3.7\}\\text\{\\ cm\\ (to\\ 2\\ s.f.)\}\\)
