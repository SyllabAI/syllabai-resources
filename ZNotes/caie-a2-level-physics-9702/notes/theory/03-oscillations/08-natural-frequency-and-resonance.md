## Natural Frequency and Resonance

* **Free oscillation**: oscillatory motion not subjected to an external periodic driving force; oscillates at natural frequency. **e.g.**: Tuning fork, Pendulum
* **Natural frequency** $\mathbf{f}_{\mathbf{0}}$**:** the frequency of oscillation of a freely oscillating object when there is no external forces acting on it
* **Forced oscillation:** oscillation caused by an external driving force to alter amplitude; the driving force determines frequency
* **Resonance:** max amplitude of vibration of oscillating body when driving frequency equals the natural frequency of the system
