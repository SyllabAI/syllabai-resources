# Oscillations

## Oscillations:

### Key Definitions

* **Displacement** $\mathbf{(x}\mathbf{)}$**:** instantaneous shortest distance of the moving object from its equilibrium position

* **Amplitude** $\mathbf{(A}\mathbf{)}$**:** maximum displacement from the equilibrium position *(displacement at extreme positions)*

* **Period** $\mathbf{(T)}$**:** time taken for one complete oscillation

* **Frequency** $\mathbf{(f)}$**:** number of oscillations per unit of time such that $$f = \frac{1}{T}$$

* **Angular frequency** $\mathbf{(\omega)}$**:** rate of change of angular displacement which can be rearranged as $$T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$$

  *Note: This uses the same formulas as circular motions*

$$
\omega = 2\text{πf} = \frac{2\text{π}}{\text{T}}
$$

**Phase difference** $\mathbf{(\phi)}$**:** a measure of how much one wave is out of step with another wave $\phi=2\pi\frac{t}{T}$ where $T$ is the period and $t$ is the time lag between waves; phase difference is measured in radians
