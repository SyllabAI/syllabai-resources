## Comparing Medical Imaging Methods

* X-Ray:

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
|----|----|
| Sharp image | Equipment heavy and not portable |
| Improvable contrast | May cause ionization and damage tissues |
| Can form image where air is trapped (lung) |    |

* Ultrasound:

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
|----|----|
| Portable equipment | Cannot be used to form image where air is trapped e.g. lungs |
| Less harmful than X-ray | Image not sharp due to refraction |
| Can break kidney stones | Rapid movements in tissue – may damage |
| Used for treatment of sprained joints |    |

* CT/CAT:

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
|----|----|
| show metabolic activity of cells | Equipment heavy and not portable |
| can be used to detect cancer |    |
