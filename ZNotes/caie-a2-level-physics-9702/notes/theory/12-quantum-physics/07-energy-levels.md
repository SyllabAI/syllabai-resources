## Energy Levels

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/0c82f8acc693ce43b991c527.webp =492x)

* * **Discrete Levels**: Electrons in an atom possess specific, discrete electron energy levels. They are represented as stacked horizontal lines increasing in energy.

  * **Ground State**: The lowest available energy level that electrons normally occupy.

  * **Excitation**: Electrons gain energy and move up to an "excited state" via collisions, absorbing a photon, or heat.

  * **Ionisation**: Occurs when an electron gains sufficient energy to be completely removed from the atom ($0\text{ eV}$ level).

  * **De-excitation**: When an electron falls back to a lower energy state, it releases energy by emitting a photon

$$
hf = E_{2} - E_{1}
$$

where $f$ is the frequency, $E_{2}$ is the energy of the higher
level and $E_{1}$ is the energy of the lower level

* The frequencies of electromagnetic radiation emitted by electrons when they come down to ground state were found to be discrete, showing electrons can only absorb specific discrete values $\Rightarrow$ the **energy of an atom is quantised.**
