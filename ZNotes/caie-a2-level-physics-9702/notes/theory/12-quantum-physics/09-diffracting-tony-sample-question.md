## Diffracting Tony (Sample Question):

After failing his engineering and science admissions test, Tony Stank gives up his dream of building flying suits. Instead, Tony interns with Dr Pym and becomes a superhero. Tony Stank (An 80 kg human) is uniformly shrunken to a microscopic width of ($1.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ m}$) while maintaining his original mass. Estimate the de Broglie wavelength of this shrunken Tony when moving at a speed of ($7.5\text{ ms}^{-1}$), and use your calculation to explain whether Tony would exhibit any observable diffraction when passing through a physical gap of width ($2.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ m}$).

Solution:

**1. Calculate the de Broglie Wavelength**

* Use the de Broglie equation: $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$

* Substitute Planck's constant ($h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J s}$), mass ($m = 80\text{ kg}$), and velocity ($v = 7.5\text{ ms}^{-1}$):
  $$\lambda = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J s}}{80\text{ kg} \times 7.5\text{ ms}^{-1}}$$
  $$\lambda = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{600} = 1.105 \times 10^{-36}\text{ m}$$

* **Wavelength:** $\lambda = 1.1 \times 10^{-36}\text{ m}$ (rounded to **2 significant figures**).

**2. Evaluate the Condition for Diffraction**

* **Physical Law:** Observable diffraction requires the wavelength to be roughly equal to the gap size ($\lambda \approx D$).

* **Comparison:** The calculated wavelength ($1.1 \times 10^{-36}\text{ m}$) is vastly smaller than the gap width ($2.0 \times 10^{-12}\text{ m}$).

* **Conclusion:** Because $\lambda \lll D$, no diffraction pattern will form, and the human will travel strictly as a classical particle.
