## Wave-Particle Duality

* **Wave Behaviour**: electromagnetic radiation shows properties of diffraction and interference
* **Particle Behaviour**: photoelectric effect
* Thus, electromagnetic radiation has a dual nature and its particles are photons

### Photon Momentum

* Despite being massless, Einstein demonstrated that a photon traveling in a vacuum carries **momentum** ($p$).

* **Formula:**

  $$p = \frac{E}{c} = \frac{hf}{c} = \frac{h}{\lambda}$$

* Because photons possess momentum, they exert a physical **force** ($\text{Rate of change of momentum: } F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$) and **pressure** ($P = \frac{F}{A}$) when they hit and are absorbed by a surface.
