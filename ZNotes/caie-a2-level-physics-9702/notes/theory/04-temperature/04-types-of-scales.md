## Types of scales

### Empirical Scale

This is Celsius and not an SI unit

**Triple point of pure water:** temp. at which water exists as a vapour, liquid, and solid – at 273.16 Kelvin

### Thermodynamic Scale

* **Thermodynamic (Kelvin) Scale:** theoretical scale that is independent of properties of any particular substance.
* SI base unit

$$
K = ℃ + 273.15
$$

* The difference between two temperatures in Celsius and two temperatures in Kelvin **is the same**

  **Absolute zero:** the temperature at which a system has minimum internal energy (not zero) – impossible to remove any more energy – at 0 Kelvin
