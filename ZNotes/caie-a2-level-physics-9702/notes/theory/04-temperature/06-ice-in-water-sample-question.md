## Ice in Water (Sample Question)

Mr Leuw is making a cold beverage. He drops a $0.050\text{ kg}$ ice cube at $0^\circ\text{C}$ is dropped into $0.200\text{ kg}$ of water at $25.0^\circ\text{C}$ inside an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Determine the final equilibrium temperature $T_f$.

(Take $c_w = 4190\text{ J kg}^{-1}\text{ K}^{-1}$, $L_f = 3.34 \times 10^5\text{ J kg}^{-1}$)

* *Energy absorbed to melt ice:*

  $Q_1 = m_{\text{ice}} L_f = 0.050 \times (3.34 \times 10^5) = 16,700\text{ J}$

* *Energy absorbed to warm melted ice to $T_f$:*

  $Q_2 = m_{\text{ice}} c_w (T_f - 0) = 0.050 \times 4190 \times T_f = 209.5 , T_f$

* *Energy released by cooling water:*

  $Q_3 = m_w c_w (25.0 - T_f) = 0.200 \times 4190 \times (25.0 - T_f) = 20,950 - 838 , T_f$

* *Conservation of energy ($Q_1 + Q_2 = Q_3$):*

  $16,700 + 209.5 , T_f = 20,950 - 838 , T_f$

  $1047.5 , T_f = 4,250 \implies T_f \approx 4.06^\circ\text{C}$
