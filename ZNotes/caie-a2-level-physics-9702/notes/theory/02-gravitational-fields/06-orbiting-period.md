## Orbiting Period

In the section on centripetal acceleration, we have concluded that:

$$
v^{2}=\frac{G M}{r}
$$

and for the linear velocity

$$
v=\omega r \text{ where }\omega=\frac{2 \pi}{T}=2 \pi f
$$

If we combine and simplify them, then we will get:

$$

T^2 = \left\(\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}\right\)r^3

$$
