## Stealing the Moon (Sample Question):

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/ddaffc934381f6f2ead4628f.webp =620x)

The supervillain Gru plans to steal the Moon by using a tractor beam to pull it out of its stable circular orbit and bring it to a complete standstill resting directly on the Earth's surface.

Initially, the Moon has a total **orbital kinetic energy of $3.81\times 10^{28}\text{\ J}$**.

Calculate the total net energy that Gru’s machinery must safely absorb from the Earth-Moon system to bring the Moon to rest on the planet's surface.

**Data:**

* **Mass of Earth** = $$5.97\times 10^{24}\text{\ kg}$$

* **Mass of Moon** = $$7.35\times 10^{22}\text{\ kg}$$

* **Radius of Earth** = $$6.37\times 10^{6}\text{\ m}$$

* **Radius of Moon** = $$1.74\times 10^{6}\text{\ m}$$

* **Initial Orbital Radius** = $$3.84\times 10^{8}\text{\ m}$$

* **Gravitational Constant** = 6.67x10\^-11 N(m\^2)(kg\^-2)

Solution:

**Step 1: Calculate the final center-to-center distance (\\(r\_\{f\}\\)) when touching.**
$$

r_{f}=R_{E}+R_{M}=(6.37\times 10^{6})+(1.74\times 10^{6})=8.11\times 10^{6}\text{\ m}\

$$

**Step 2: Calculate the change in gravitational potential energy (\\(\\Delta E\_p\\)).**

$$\Delta E_p = -3.53 \times 10^{30}\text\\$$

**Step 3: Calculate total net energy to absorb ($\Delta E_{\text{total}}$).**

The total mechanical energy change accounts for dropping down the potential well ($\Delta E_p$) and losing all orbital kinetic energy ($\Delta E_k = -3.81 \times 10^{28}\text{ J}$):

$$\Delta E_{\text{total}} = \Delta E_p + \Delta E_k$$

$$\Delta E_{\text{total}} = (-3.53 \times 10^{30}\text{ J}) + (-0.0381 \times 10^{30}\text{ J}) = -3.57 \times 10^{30}\text{ J}$$

Gru's machinery must absorb **\*\*$$3.57 \times 10^{30}\text{ J}$$\*\*** (or $$3.6 \times 10^{30}\text{ J}$$).
