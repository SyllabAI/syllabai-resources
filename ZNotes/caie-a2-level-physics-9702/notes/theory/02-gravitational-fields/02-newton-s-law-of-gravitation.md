## Newton’s Law of Gravitation:

Newton’s Law of Gravitation states that “Any two point masses attract each other with a force that is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of their separation”

Formula:

$$
F = \frac{\text{GMm}}{r^{2}}
$$

Where:

- $F$ is the gravitational force between two masses ($\text{N}$)
- $$G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}   \text{m}^3 \text{kg}^{-1} \text{s}^{-2}$$, the gravitational constant
- $M$ & $m$ are point masses ($\text{kg}$)
- $r$ is the distance between the centres of the two masses ($\text{m}$)

**Key Notes:**

* For a point outside a uniform sphere, the mass of the sphere may be considered to be a point mass at its centre
* As $r$ is defined as the distance between the centres of each mass, if given the distance between the surfaces of planets, we must add the radius of each planet
