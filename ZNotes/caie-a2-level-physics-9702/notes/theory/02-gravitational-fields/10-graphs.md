### Gravitational Field Strength between two masses:

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/7fb7e0668b3e9815aab9c877.webp =458x)
The example added shows Earth and the Moon; it can be seen that there is a point with zero field strength between them

That point is where the field cancels each other out (any test mass will feel zero net force there due to equal forces on both ends)

This is possible because field from Earth and Moon are in opposite directions and equal in magnitude

***It is closer to the moon due to the moon having a lesser mass***
