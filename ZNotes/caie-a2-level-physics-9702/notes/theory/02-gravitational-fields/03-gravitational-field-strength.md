## Gravitational Field Strength

The gravitational field strength at a point is the gravitational force exerted per unit of mass

By equating $W = mg$ and Newton’s Law of Gravitation

$$
mg = \frac{\text{GM}m}{r^{2}}
$$

$$
\therefore g = \frac{\text{GM}}{r^{2}}
$$
