## Escape Velocity of a Satellite

By conservation of energy:

Initial K.E. \+ Initial G.P.E = Final = 0

$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^{2} - \frac{\text{GMm}}{r} = 0
$$

Thus escape velocity $= \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}$

Escape velocity is the minimum speed a satellite needs to escape the gravitational pull of a planet and get into orbit
