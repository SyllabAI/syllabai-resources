## Representing Magnetics Fields

**Magnetic Field due to Permanent Magnet**

- Magnetic field lines come out of the north pole and go into the
  south pole

- Tangent drawn to the field line at any point in the field shows the
  direction of the force that a ‘free’ magnetic north pole would
  experience

- Field is strongest where field lines closest together
