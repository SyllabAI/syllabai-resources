## Magnetic Flux Density

* **Magnetic flux (**$\mathbf{\Phi}$**):** product of (magnetic) flux density and area where the area is perpendicular to the (magnetic) field. Unit = weber ($\text{Wb}$)
* **Magnetic flux density (**$\mathbf{B}$**):** force acting per unit current on unit length of conductor placed at right angles to the magnetic field

$$
\Phi = BA
$$

* 1 Tesla ($\text{T}$) is the magnetic field producing a force of $1\text{ N}$ on a wire carrying a current of $1\text{ A}$ normal to the field over a length of $1\text{ m}$:

$$
1T = 1\ N\ A^{- 1}\ m^{- 1}
$$

* **The magnitude of the force on a current-carrying conductor with:**

$$
F = BIL\sin\theta
$$

* Find direction using Fleming’s Left Hand Rule
* If wire parallel to the field lines, $\theta = 0$ and $F = 0$
* If the wire is at right angles to field lines, $\theta = 90$ and
  force acting on wire maximum ($F = BIL$)
