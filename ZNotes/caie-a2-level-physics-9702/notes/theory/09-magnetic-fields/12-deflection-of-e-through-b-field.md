## Deflection of e through Magnetic Field

Circular motion

- Circular path

- Kinetic energy constant

$$BQv = \frac{mv^2}{r}\ \text{so}\ r = \frac{\text{mv}}{\text{BQ}}$$

- Faster moving particles move in bigger circles, $r \propto v$

- Heavier particle move in bigger circles, $r \propto m$

- Stronger field, particle moves in smaller circle,
  $r \propto \frac{1}{B}$

NOTE: In the case of the electric field by two parallel plates, the force is always in the same direction and therefore the trajectory us parabolic, not circular.
