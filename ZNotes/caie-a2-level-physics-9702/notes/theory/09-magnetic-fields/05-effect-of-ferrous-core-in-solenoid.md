## Effect of Ferrous Core in Solenoid

  - The strength of the generated magnetic field can be increased (by
    about 1000 times) by adding a ferrous (iron) core inside the
    solenoid.

**Two possible reasons to explain this effect**

  - Ferrous material has a higher permeability than air; stronger
    ability to support the formation of a magnetic field within itself

  - Ferrous material is magnetic and become magnetized when placed into
    the solenoid, thus contributing to overall magnetic field strength
    of the solenoid.
