## Radiant Flux Intensity \[F\]

* **Radiant Flux Intensity ($F$):** The observed power transmitted normally per unit surface area measured on Earth. Measured in $\text{W m}^{-2}$.

* **Formula:**

  $$F = \frac{L}{4\pi d^2}$$

  * $L = \text{Luminosity of the star } (\text{W})$

  * $d = \text{Distance between star and Earth } (\text{m})$

* **Key Assumptions:**

  * Power radiates uniformly in all directions through spherical space.

  * No radiation is absorbed or scattered by interstellar dust.
