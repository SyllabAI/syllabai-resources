## **Hubble’s law and the Big Bang Theory**

**Redshift in Astronomical Spectra:**

* **Observation:** Spectral lines (emission and absorption) observed from distant astronomical objects appear shifted toward longer wavelengths (the red end of the spectrum) compared to known laboratory values on Earth.

* **Cause:** The relative motion of the source moving away from the observer causes light waves to stretch.

**Redshift Equation (Doppler Effect):**

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/a346e9b5722458c8fcb94774.webp =487x)

* **Formula:**

  $$\frac{\Delta \lambda}{\lambda} \approx \frac{\Delta f}{f} \approx \frac{v}{c}$$

  * $\Delta \lambda = \text{Change in wavelength } (\text{m})$

  * $\lambda = \text{Reference wavelength emitted by source } (\text{m})$

  * $\Delta f = \text{Change in frequency } (\text{Hz})$

  * $f = \text{Reference frequency emitted by source } (\text{Hz})$

  * $v = \text{Recession speed of the source } (\text{m s}^{-1})$

  * $c = \text{Speed of light in a vacuum } (3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m s}^{-1})$

* **Condition:** Valid for non-relativistic speeds ($v \ll c$).

### **Expansion of the Universe:**

* Redshift indicates that almost all distant galaxies are receding from Earth.

* The amount of redshift increases with a galaxy's distance, meaning farther galaxies move away faster.

* Rather than galaxies moving through static space, space itself is expanding uniformly, stretching the wavelength of radiation as it travels through space.

### **Hubble’s Law & The Big Bang Theory:**

* **Hubble’s Law Statement:** The recession speed (v) of a galaxy is directly proportional to its distance (d) from Earth.

* **Equation:**

  $$v=H_0​ \cdot d$$

  * v=Recession speed ($ms^{−1}$)

  * d=Distance to galaxy ($m$)

  * $$H_0$$​=Hubble constant ($s^{−1}$)

* **Evidence for the Big Bang:**

  * If the universe is expanding today, running time backward implies all matter was originally concentrated at a single, extremely dense, and hot point (a singularity).

  * Extrapolating the expansion backward in time suggests a definitive moment of creation—the Big Bang.

* ### **Age of the Universe:** 

  Estimating the age of the universe ($T$) using SI units assuming a constant expansion rate of the universe:

  * **Hubble’s Law Equation:**

    $$v = H_0 \cdot d$$

  * **Distance & Speed Relation:**

    Assuming a constant recession speed $v$, the distance $d$ travelled by a galaxy moving away over time $T$ (the age of the universe) is:

    $$d = v \cdot T \implies T = \frac{d}{v}$$

  * **Substitute $v$ from Hubble’s Law:**

    $$T = \frac{d}{H_0 \cdot d} = \frac{1}{H_0}$$

  * **Calculate Age $T$ in seconds:**

    $$T = \frac{1}{2.27 \times 10^{-18}\text{ s}^{-1}} \approx 4.41 \times 10^{17}\text{ s}$$

  * **Convert $T$ to years:**

    $$T = \frac{4.41 \times 10^{17}\text{ s}}{3.15 \times 10^7\text{ s/year}} \approx \mathbf{1.4 \times 10^{10}\text{ years}} \quad (\approx 14\text{ billion years})$$
