## Coulomb’s Law

* **Point Charge Assumption:** For points outside a spherical conductor, the charge on the sphere can be treated as if it were a point charge concentrated at its center. This is because the field lines are radial and appear to originate at the center.

  ![Electric Field Lines Positive Charge](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/8f007f61e1cb8c820a8318de.webp =333x)

* **Coulomb’s Law:**

  * The electrostatic force $F$ between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of their charges ($Q_1 Q_2$) and inversely proportional to the square of the distance ($r^2$) between them:

    $$F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Q_1 Q_2}{r^2}$$

* **Permittivity of Free Space ($\varepsilon_0$):** A constant equal to $8.85 \times 10^{-12} \text{ F m}^{-1}$.

* **Note:**

  * **Newton's Third Law:** The forces acting on both charges are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction (an action-reaction pair), regardless of charge sizes.
