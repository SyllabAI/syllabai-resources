## Electric Field of a Point Charge

* **Radial Electric Field Strength ($E$):**

  * The electric field produced by a point charge $Q$ at a distance $r$ in free space is given by:

    $$E = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}$$

  * Independent of the magnitude of any test charge placed in the field.

  * Vector quantity directed radially outward from a positive charge and radially inward toward a negative charge

  * Note: Has the same inverse-square mathematical structure as radial gravitational fields ($g = \frac{GM}{r^2}$).
