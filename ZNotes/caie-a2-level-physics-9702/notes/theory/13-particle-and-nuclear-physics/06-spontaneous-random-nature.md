## **Spontaneous & Random Nature of Radioactive Decay**

* **Radioactive Decay:** The spontaneous disintegration of an unstable nucleus into a more stable nucleus with the emission of $\alpha$, $\beta$, or $\gamma$ radiation.

* **Spontaneous:** The rate of decay is **not affected by external/environmental factors** (e.g., temperature, pressure, chemical environment).

* **Random:** It is **impossible to predict which specific nucleus will decay or when** a particular nucleus will decay; there is a constant probability of decay per unit time.

* **Evidence:** Fluctuations in count rate detected by a Geiger-Müller (GM) tube near a radioactive source.
