## **Decay Constant ($\lambda$)**

* **Definition:** The **probability of decay of a nucleus per unit time**.

* **Unit:** $\text{s}^{-1}$, $\text{min}^{-1}$, $\text{h}^{-1}$, or $\text{year}^{-1}$

* **Differential Rate Equation:**

  $$\frac{\Delta N}{\Delta t} = -\lambda N$$

  *(The negative sign indicates $N$ decreases over time)*

### **Activity Equation ($A = \lambda N$)**

* **Activity ($A$):** The rate at which nuclei decay (number of decays per unit time).

* **Unit:** Becquerel ($\text{Bq}$), where $1\text{ Bq} = 1\text{ decay per second}$.

* **Formula:**

  $$A = \lambda N$$

  * $A$ = Activity ($\text{Bq}$)

  * $\lambda$ = Decay constant ($\text{s}^{-1}$)

  * $N$ = Number of undecayed radioactive nuclei remaining
