## **Mass Defect ($\Delta m$)**

* **Definition:** The difference between the total mass of the individual, separated nucleons and the actual mass of the nucleus.

* **Key Concept:** $\text{Mass of separated nucleons} > \text{Mass of bound nucleus}$.

* **Formula:**

  $$\Delta m = Zm_p + (A - Z)m_n - m_{\text{nucleus}}$$

  * $Z$ = proton number (atomic number)

  * $A$ = nucleon number (mass number)

  * $m_p$ = mass of a proton ($\text{kg}$ or $\text{u}$)

  * $m_n$ = mass of a neutron ($\text{kg}$ or $\text{u}$)

  * $m_{\text{nucleus}}$ = measured rest mass of the nucleus
