## Question 4: Narrative Writing

### **What you should aim for:**

* Clear story mountain.

* Slowly building up to the climax.

* 1-2 settings.

* 2-3 characters.

* More than 1 dialogue.

### **General Format:**

1. Opening:

   * Introduce setting, context, character(s).

   * What, when, where, who.

2. Build-up:

   * Introduce and build up conflict.

3. Climax/Dilemma.

4. Resolution.

5. Ending

**Structure**: Cyclical; Flashback; Cliffhanger; Original story mountain…

### **Character(s):**

* Appearance.

* Personality.

* Hobby.

* Social relationship.

* Strengths/weaknesses.

* Background.

### **Tips:**

* If the task is *‘tự sự’* instead of *‘tường thuật’*, you must write in first person.

* Create a clear story mountain in logical, chronological order.

* You must have a climax/dilemma to demonstrate a clear story mountain.

* The story must align with the task; it must include necessary lines or elements that the question asks for and circulate around a specific theme.

* Don’t go too deep into the characters’ emotion.

### **Unsuccessful story:**

* Too unrealistic.

* No development.

* Inappropriate language.

* Lack of logic/abrupt ending.

* Too much setting or character.

* Too much dialogue.
