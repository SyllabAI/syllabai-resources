## Question 1: Discursive Writing

* **Length:** 400-500 words.

### **General Format**

1. **Introduction**: acknowledges the topic being asked & how the topic has both sides.

2. **Second paragraph**: Agree/Disagree.

   * 3 ideas/points to support this side of the argument.

   * Each idea should be coupled with an evidence/example to strengthen.

3. **Third paragraph:** Agree/Disagree (Opposite to the second paragraph).

   * Starts with “However'“ or ”On the other hand”: “*Tuy nhiên*”; “*Tuy vậy*”; “*Trái lại”.*

   * 3 ideas/points to support.

   * Each idea should be coupled with an evidence/example to strengthen.

4. **Conclusion:**

   * Starts with “To conclude” or “Overall”: “Để tóm tắt”, “Tóm tắt lại”.

   * Summarize both viewpoints and ideas.

### **Exam Tips**

* Keep your introduction **short & precise**: 5 lines maximum.

* Keep your ideas and examples **brief**, avoid going into too much depth.

* Avoid personal pronouns.

* Use “some people believe” or “a majority believes”: “*Một vài người cho rằng*…”, “*Một số ý kiến cho rằng…*”

* Evidence is not statistics; don’t make up statistics.

* Give realistic scenarios that the topic applies to as examples.

* Pay attention to the length of your work.
