## Question 2: Argumentative Writing

* One-sided argument.

* **Length:** 400-500 words.

### **General Format**

1. **Introduction:** Acknowledges the topic & states your personal opinion.

2. **Body paragraphs**: 2 big paragraphs or 3 smaller paragraphs.

   * Each paragraph has a separate idea and examples to support.

   * Divide ideas into facets (physically, mentally, financially…): “*Về mặt tinh thần*”, “*Về khía cạnh thể chất*”.

   * Evidence: detailed, realistic; create real-life & relatable scenarios to demonstrate the topic and persuade the audience.

3. **Conclusion:** State your opinion & summarize the facets.

### **Tips:**

* Signpost using conjunctions: *Thứ nhất, Thứ hai, Ngoài ra, Hơn nữa, Không chỉ vậy.*

* Clear opinion; avoid being partial.

* Pay attention to the length of your work.
