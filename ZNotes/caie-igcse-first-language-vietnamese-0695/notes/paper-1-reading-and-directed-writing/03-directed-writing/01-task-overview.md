# Directed Writing

## Task Overview

Question 3 consists of **only one big question** and this question has a total of **25 marks.** In this question, you’ll pick out **relevant** information from **text B and C** to answer the bullet points in the **required text type.**

### **Syllabus Criteria:**

* **Develop, analyze, evaluate upon an issue in society.**

* Use material from **both texts** for **specific context.**

* Use vocabulary, grammar and punctuation suitably.

* Accurate spelling and sentence structures.

* **Be convincing.**
