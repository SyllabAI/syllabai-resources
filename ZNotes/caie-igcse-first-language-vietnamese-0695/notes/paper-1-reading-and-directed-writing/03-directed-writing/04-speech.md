## Speech

* **Tone & Style:**

  * Formal/casual.

  * First person.

  * Collective pronouns: we, us…

  * Confident tone to be convincing.

* **Language:**

  * Rhetorical question.

  * Hyperbole.

  * Repetition.

  * Metaphor.

  * Personification.

  * Ethos, pathos, logos.

  * Create hooks.

* **Format & Structure:**

  * Combine ideas with opinion.
