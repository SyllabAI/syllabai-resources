## **Report**

* **Tone & Style:**

  * Formal, objective, clear.

  * Factual: Facts, findings, observations.

  * Third person.

* **Language:**

  * Avoids overly complex/emotive language.

* **Format & Structure:**

  * Headings & sub-headings.

  * Introduction: States the purpose and what the report will cover.

  * Information should be logically grouped.

  * Conclusion:

    * Summarizes findings.

    * Includes suggestions/recommendations.
