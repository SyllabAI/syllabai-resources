## Article

* **Tone & Style:**

  * Factual/formal.

  * Third person.

  * Expert opinion.

* **Language:**

  * Emotive language if appropriate.

  * Create hooks.

* **Format & Structure:**

  * Headings & sub-headings.

  * Combine facts with opinion.

  * Register 5 W’s & how in the opening and elaborate more afterwards.

  * Opening:

    * Engage with the reader.

    * Outline the main point

  * Middle: Go into more details.

  * Ending: 

    * Conclude.

    * Summarize.
