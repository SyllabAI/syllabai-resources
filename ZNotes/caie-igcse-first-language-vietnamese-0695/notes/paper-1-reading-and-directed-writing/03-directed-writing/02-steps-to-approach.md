## Steps to Approach

1. **Find T.A.P.P.**

   * Text Type:

     * Article.

     * Letter.

     * Speech.

     * Report.

   * Audience.

   * Perspective.

   * Purpose: Argue, Persuade, Inform…

2. **Pick out suitable ideas from** **both texts.**

3. **Plan your answer:**

   * Divide ideas into groups **based on the bullet points** given.

   * **Use your own words** to jot down the ideas.

   * **Merge ideas** together if you find them in both texts.

4. **Write your answer:**

   * Implement ideas into TAPP.

   * Integrate diverse sentence structures and punctuation.

   * Demonstrate **accurate** uses of grammar and vocabulary.

   * **Evaluate** ideas.

   * Length: **450-500 words.**

   * Well-organized structure.
