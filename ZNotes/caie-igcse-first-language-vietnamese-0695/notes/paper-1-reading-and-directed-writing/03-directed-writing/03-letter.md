## Letter

* **Tone & Style:**

  * Formal/casual.

  * First person.

* **Language:**

  * Starts with Dear: *“Gửi…”.* or *“Kính gửi…”* (more formal).

  * Ends with: Signoffs: *“Từ…”.* or *“Trân trọng*”(more formal).

  * Avoid slangs or informal vocabulary.

* **Format & Structure:**

  * Establish T.A.P.P accurately from the introduction.

  * Opening:

    * Register the receiver & relationship.

    * Explain the purpose of the letter.

  * Middle: Elaborate.

  * Ending:

    * Summarize.

    * Conclude.

    * Signoffs.
