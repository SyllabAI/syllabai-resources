# Topics List

## Notes and Topics

### **Notes**

* The topic list is for all components.
* It is used during the course to help students develop their skills (in researching, reasoning, and questioning)
* Although students are not expected to have experience with each topic, you should familiarize yourself with some of the key issues/perspectives you may encounter regarding these global issues.
