## Question 1

### **Definitions**

* **Generalisation:** a statement suggesting that something is true all of the time when it is only true some of the time
* **Opinion:** an individual’s point of view or belief which is not necessarily shared by others or supported by evidence
* **Fact:** something which can be verified/proven and is true/accurate
* **Value judgment:** a statement, view or decision about what is right, wrong or important, morally and/or ethically, based on a particular set of standards, principles, or beliefs
* **Bias:** a tendency or prejudice for or against something; an unbalanced approach not prepared to consider counterarguments or other points of view
* **Vested interest:** a personal reason for involvement or interest, especially an expectation of a financial or other gain
* **Prediction:** a statement about the future or what will happen in times to come or following on from now/the present.
* **Inference:** an idea or conclusion reached based on evidence and reasoning
* May ask you to **identify** a certain aspect of an argument and **explain** why
  * Use the definitions above to aid in your explanation
  * Include examples from the source to support your explanation

### **Perspective**

* May ask you to describe a certain perspective towards the global issue
* **Personal perspective:** someone's own view about an issue
* **Local perspective:** perspective about a local area or the views of a local community
* **National perspective:** perspective about a particular country or common to a whole country
* **Global perspective:** perspective about the whole world
* You can approach this question by describing the following elements of the perspective:
  * Issue
  * Causes
  * Consequences
  * Values
  * Action
* Make sure to use/cite examples from the source **to describe your answer clearly**
