## Question 2

### **Evaluate the Research/Argument**

* May ask you to **explain** the strengths and weaknesses of the research/argument outlined in a source
* Provide **at least two strengths and two weaknesses**
* Include examples/evidence from the source to support your evaluation
* Strengthen your explanation by evaluating the way it is used to support the claim:
  * *Does it increase the credibility of the claim?*
  * *Does it increase the accuracy of the claim?*
  * *How does it affect the authority of the author/source?*
  * *Does it increase the reliability of the claim?*
  * *The validity it provides*

### **Testing a Claim**

* **Possible methods:**
  * Interviews of relevant experts
  * Observation
  * Review of secondary sources/literature/research/documents
  * Internet and media search
  * Questionnaires
  * Surveys
  * Case studies
* **Possible types of evidence:**
  * Compare statistics/information on the global issue
  * Individual testimony or personal experience
  * Material from pressure groups/charities/governments/international organisations
  * Primary and secondary research data and information
  * Quantitative and qualitative research data and information
  * Research reports
* You should write **at least two points** on each of the above two areas
* Ensure that your explanation is **clearly and explicitly** related to testing the claim being tested
