## Question 3

### **Evaluate the Effectiveness of Arguments**

* May ask you to compare two arguments and decide whose argument is more convincing
* Support your judgment by considering some of the following aspects of the arguments:
  * Strength of reasoning:
    * Logic
    * Structure
    * Balance
    * Claims
  * **Use of language:**
    * Tone – emotive, exaggerated, precise, measured
    * Clarity
  * **Evidence:**
    * Range of information and depth
    * Relevance
    * Sufficiency
    * Sample size
    * Source – media; internet
    * Date – how recent
    * Different types of information – fact, opinion, value, anecdote
    * Testimony – from experience and expert
  * **Sources of bias or vested interest:**
    * Local interest
    * Economic
    * Personal values
    * Experience
  * **Acceptability of their values to others:**
    * How likely other people are to agree with their perspective/view
* **Your response must consider both arguments** 
* **Discuss various aspects of the arguments (at least 2)** 
* **Use relevant material from the source to support each of your points**




* Example answer structure: 
  * **Introduction**: State which argument is more convincing 
  * **Paragraph 2:** Analyze the stronger argument 
    * Look into the **PEEL method** as a way to structure your paragraph
  * **Paragraph 3:** Analyze the weaker argument 
    * Repeat above
  * **Conclusion:** Clearly state who has the better argument and repeat the main points that you have looked at above
* Use linking devices:
  * However, in comparison, in contrast,...
  * Firstly, secondly, furthermore, finally,...
