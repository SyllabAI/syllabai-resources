# Written Examination

## Paper Outline

* **Source Material**: provide information and different perspectives about a global issue related to one of the topics __[from the topic list](https://znotes.org/caie/igcse/global-perspective-0457/theory/topics-list/)__
  * You should think about issues within each topic to familiarize yourself with the concepts and terminology you may encounter
* It contains **four main questions** with sub-questions
  * **Question 1:** students will read and analyze the information, arguments and perspectives presented about the global issue
  * **Question 2:** students will evaluate the research/evidence and suggest ways to test a claim related to the global issue
  * **Question 3:** students will evaluate the effectiveness of arguments, making a reasoned judgment about the most convincing.
  * **Question 4:** Students will assess actions in response to the global issue and explain their judgements with reasons and evidence
