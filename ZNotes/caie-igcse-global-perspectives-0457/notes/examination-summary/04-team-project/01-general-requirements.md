# Team Project

## General Requirements

* Students work in teams of two to five members
* The team selects a topic [from the topic list](https://znotes.org/caie/igcse/global-perspective-0457/examination-summary/topics-list/) and identifies a relevant local issue
* The Team Project has two elements:
  * **Team Element**
    * Explanation of Research and Planning
    * Evidence of Action
  * **Personal Element**
    * Reflective Paper
