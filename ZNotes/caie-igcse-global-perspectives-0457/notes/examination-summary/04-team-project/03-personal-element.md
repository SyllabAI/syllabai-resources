## Personal Element

### **Reflective Paper**

* The report must be 750–1000 words (excluding citations and bibliography)
* To gain full marks:
  * Present a summary of their research findings and how these were or were not used in the Team Project
  * Reflect on their learning from the whole experience of the Team Project including their learning about the issue and teamwork and what they learned about themselves
  * Evaluate how effective the action they carried out was and how effectively they worked with their team

### **Points to Adress**

* **Summary of their research findings**
  * Summary of research findings - *How did it influence the project?*
  * Include citations and references
* **Reflection on individual learning**
  * Learning about the issue
    * *How have your research findings impacted your understanding of the issue?*
    * *The different perspectives you explored*
  * Learning from the teamwork
    * Skills (e.g. *scriptwriting, video editing, researching…)*
    * Strengths
    * Weaknesses
  * Reflection on performance
    * *How could you have improved your performance **individually and as a team member?***
* **Reflection on teamwork**
  * Benefits and challenges of teamwork
  * Possible improvements for teamwork
    * *How could the team have worked more effectively in the past?*
    * **OR** *how could the team work more effectively in the future?*
* **Evaluation of the action and teamwork**
  * *How successfully did the action address the issue?*
  * *How successful were you in working with other team members?*
  * *How successful were you in managing your own tasks?*
    * e.g. by creating a timetable, a working plan,…

### **Tips**

* When discussing teamwork, here are a few examples you can talk about:
  * Clear communication
  * Sharing and developing ideas
  * Setting targets and deadlines
  * Solving problems/disagreements
  * Assigning roles to each team member
  * Asking for and supporting others when needed
* Always be clear and specific about your working processes
  * Include examples of things experienced while completing the team project
