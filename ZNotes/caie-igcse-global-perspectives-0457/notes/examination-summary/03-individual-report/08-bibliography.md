## Bibliography

* This section **takes up 5 marks**
* To score full marks:
  * Citation and referencing are complete.
  * A consistent method is used.
  * The links between citations and references are clear
* Make sure to have **a clear and consistent method** for citation/referencing
  * Possible methods: MLA, APA, Harvard, Chicago,…
  * Refer to this site to help you: <https://www.citethisforme.com/>
* When candidates quote sources within other sources, they must reference both
* Every quoted person or organization should have a reference in the essay (to avoid the risk of plagiarism)
* Sources must be in **alphabetical order**

### **Notes for citation and referencing**

* **In-text citation:**
  * Use bracketed citations, numbering (footnotes), or in-text referencing
* **References:**
  * References for books or magazines should include the author, date, and title of the publication.
  * References for online materials should include at least the full URL (leading to the document) and date of access.
  * Evidence of their primary research can be added to the reference list. It should not be included as part of the essay unless it is to be read and counted in the word count.
