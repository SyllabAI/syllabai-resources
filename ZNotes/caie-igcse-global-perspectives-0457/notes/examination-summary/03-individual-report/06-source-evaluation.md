## Source Evaluation

* This section **takes up 10 marks**
* **To score full marks:**
  * Make **four appropriate and developed points** of evaluation of the sources used
  * Explaining their impact **on the research** and **the argument**
* **Evaluate a source by looking at the following aspects:**
  * Authority (of the author)
  * Verified research/ Credibility
  * Research relevance to the topic
  * Bias
  * Cross-checked
  * Peer review
  * Accuracy
  * Reliability
