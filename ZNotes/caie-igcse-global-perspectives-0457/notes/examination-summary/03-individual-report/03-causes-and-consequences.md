## Causes and Consequences

* This section **takes up 5 marks**
* To score full marks:
  * Analyses a global issue, explaining causes and consequences **in depth**

### **Causes**

* You should compare different causes of the issue you are discussing by:
  * Explaining which were the more important
  * Explain how and why different causes existed for different groups of people, places, or situations.

### **Consequences**

* You should also compare different consequences (impacts/effects) by:
  * Explaining which consequences were the most serious
  * Explaining the different consequences for different groups of people, places, or situations.
