## Courses of Action

* This section **takes up 10 marks**
* To score full marks:
  * Presents two possible relevant courses of action
    * Give details of **implementation**
    * Evaluate their **practicality** and possible **impact**
  * Selects a preferred option and justifies that selection
* You can consider courses of action that have been successful elsewhere and explain how these solutions might be applied in other countries.
* Present your possible course of action by considering:
  * *Who would do it?*
  * *How would it be done?*
  * *Reference to the scale of impact*
  * *Reference to barriers to change*
  * *How likely is this course of action going to succeed? Why?*
    * i.e. If this course of action has been carried out before, evaluate its success by providing **statistics and data**. From there, consider its effectiveness if implemented now
