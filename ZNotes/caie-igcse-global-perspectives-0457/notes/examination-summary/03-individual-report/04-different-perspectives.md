## Different Perspectives

* This section **takes up ten marks**
* To score full marks:
  * Presents and explains **one global** and **one national/local perspective** on the selected global issue

### **Global Perspective**

* This is a **global** viewpoint/opinion/feeling/attitude towards the issue presented (by someone or some organizations)
* You must be clear about whose perspective is being presented, either by **paraphrasing** or **quoting** the person or organization with clear attribution.
* **Global organizations:** United Nations, UNICEF, **UNFPA**, WWF, WHO,...
* It should not just be information about the global situation
* A range of national perspectives does not make a global one

### **National and Local Perspective**

* This is a **national** viewpoint/opinion/feeling/attitude towards the issue presented (by someone or the government)
* Once again, you must be clear about whose perspective is being presented
* It should not just be information about the situation happening within the community/country
