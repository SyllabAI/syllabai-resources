# Introduction to 0547 Mandarin Chinese

## Summary

IGCSE Mandarin Foreign Language (0547) is like any other foreign language in the IGCSE language subjects; the framework is the same where four areas of the CEFR Language are tested:


1. Listening
2. Speaking
3. Writing
4. Reading

**These are the five topic areas which are tested in this course:**

A. Everyday activities

B. Personal and social life

C. The world around us

D. The world of work

E. The international world
