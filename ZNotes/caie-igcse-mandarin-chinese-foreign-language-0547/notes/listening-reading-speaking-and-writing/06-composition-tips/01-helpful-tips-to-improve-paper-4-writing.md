# Helpful Tips to Improve Paper 4: Writing

## Tips:

* Try to be familiar with as much vocabulary as possible; a vocabulary list is provided in the back.
* Listening to Chinese songs or watching Chinese movies is helpful; they will familiarise you with Chinese accents and sentence structure.
* Suppose you have trouble writing sentences. Try to use English (or any other language you are comfortable with), then translate it to Chinese. (NO Google Translating/AI)
* Use your heart (not literally, please) when writing/speaking/reading in Chinese.
* Try communicating with others in Chinese, like your friends or online friends.
* Learn how to spell and write accurate expressions for ideas such as expressing disappointment, being pleased about something, agreeing with somebody, apologising, and giving positive and negative opinions and preferences.
* Learn a set of more complex sentence structures to use when writing. Comparatives and superlatives can be good ways of expressing opinions. Aim to include linking words, such as 先, 然后/再, 最后 and structures, such as 好处是……, to express ideas.
* Try to stick to the word limits in Questions 2 and 3. What you write must be relevant to the set bullet points. Irrelevant material will not gain you credit.
* Make sure that you have enough time to check all of your answers carefully. Ensure that you have covered all of the set tasks. You could then check that the sentences you have written are in the correct word order, and you could also check the accuracy of the characters and agreement of measure words and adjectives with 的.
