## Useful Websites

Here are some websites Cambridge recommends for students to study this course effectively.

Learners and teachers often use the following sites and provide direct access to Internet resources.


1. www.quizlet.com

   This website is a good platform for learning vocabulary. There are ready-made vocabulary lists for this course that learners can find. In addition, learners can also start to comply with their vocabulary list. The site allows learners to generate interactive activities or flashcards.
2. www.memrise.com This is another helpful website for consolidating vocabulary learning.

   This website lets Learners find the course online and do the linked games and tests.
3. www.hello-world.com/games/conversations.php/?language=Mandarin&translate=English

   A beneficial website to practise listening. The website contains a vast range of topics if the learners are interested in other aspects of Mandarin Chinese and the countries where the language is spoken.
4. www.hskreading.com

   The website contains a collection of reading texts, which learners can practise according to their capability.
5. www.ctcfl.ox.ac.uk/Lang%20work/Grammar%20database/Grammar_database_content.htm

   The Oxford University website is beneficial for learning grammar. Though it’s a comprehensive list, it leads you to understand the grammar points, and there are linked exercises, too.
