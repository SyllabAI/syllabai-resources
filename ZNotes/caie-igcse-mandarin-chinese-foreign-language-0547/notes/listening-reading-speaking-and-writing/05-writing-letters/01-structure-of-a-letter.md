# Writing Letters

## Structure of a Letter

* **Greeting**
  * Start with “亲爱的 (Name): ” which means “Dear (Name) :”
  * Don’t forget to put the colon “ : ” after the name.
  * Under the “亲爱的 (Name): ”, it is good to follow up with

    “你好！你最近过得好吗？” which means “Hello! How are you doing?”
  * Leave a 2-word indent after writing that.
* **Body**
  * Start talking about your chosen topic; imagine that you are telling a story or describing it to a friend.
  * Try to expand and put as many adjectives as possible; this will increase your word count and marks.
  * Try to add additional details, too.
  * Don’t forget to write what they need you to write about.
* **Closing**
  * I recommend you to write “期待早日收到你来信。祝你 (Wishes)”, which means “I hope will receive your letter soon, wish you (Wishes).”
  * Here are the examples of wishes you can use:

    天天开心 (To be happy every day)

    身体健康 (To be healthy)

    心想事成 (Wishes/dreams to come true)

    出入平安 (Have peace wherever you go)

    吉祥如意 (Be lucky as you desire)

    一帆风顺 (to have a smooth sail)

  <!-- end list -->
* Write your name on the bottom right of the letter.
