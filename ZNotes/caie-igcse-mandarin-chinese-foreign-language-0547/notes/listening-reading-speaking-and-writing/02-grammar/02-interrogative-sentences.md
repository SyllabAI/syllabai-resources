## Interrogative Sentences

* **Using “吗?”**
  * E.g.

    “你看了<span class="underline">吗</span>？” (Did you see it?)

    “你想吃饭<span class="underline">吗</span>？” (Do you want to eat?)
* **Using “(Verb/Adj) 不 (Verb/Adj)”**
  * E.g.

    “我们一起回家，<span class= "underline">好不好</span>？” (Let's go home together, okay?”

    “你<span class="underline">要不要</span>做你的功课？” (Do you want to do
    your homework?)

    “你是一个医生，<span class="underline">对不对</span>？” (You are a doctor,
    right?”
  * Note: You can’t use “吗” if you’ve already used

    “__不__” in your sentence or vice versa.
