## Negative Sentences

* **Using 不**
  * E.g.

    “我今天晚上<span class="underline">不</span>喝酒” (Tonight I’m not going
    to drink wine)

    “我<span class="underline">不</span>吃肉” (I don’t eat meat)

    “你晚上<span class="underline">不</span>洗澡吗？” (Don’t you shower at
    night?)

    “我<span class="underline">不</span>饿” (I’m not hungry)

    “我家<span class="underline">不</span>远” (My house isn’t far)
* **Using 没**
  * E.g.

    “我<span class="underline">没有</span>钱” (I don’t have money)

    “我<span class="underline">没</span>办法” (I have no solutions/way)

    “你<span class="underline">没</span>去上班吗？” (You didn’t go to
    work?)

    “我昨天<span class="underline">没</span>喝酒” (I Didn’t drink wine
    yesterday)

    “妈妈<span class="underline">没有</span>做晚饭” (Mom didn’t cook
    dinner)
