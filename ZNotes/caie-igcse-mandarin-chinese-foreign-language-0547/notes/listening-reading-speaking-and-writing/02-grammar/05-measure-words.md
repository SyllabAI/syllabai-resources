## Measure Words

* **Number + Measure Word + Noun**

E.g.

* “这里有<span class="underline">三个人</span>” (There are 3 people here)

* “他有<span class="underline">两只猫</span>” (He has 2 cats)

* “我刚买了<span class="underline">一瓶水</span>” (I just bought a bottle of
  water)

* “我想吃一<span class= "underline">碗面条</span>" (I want to eat a bowl of noodles)

We use “两” if we want to say that there are two, not”. ““”
