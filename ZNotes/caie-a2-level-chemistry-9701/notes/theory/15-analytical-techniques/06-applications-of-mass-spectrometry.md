## Applications of Mass Spectrometry

**Finding number of carbon atoms using M and $\lbrack$M+1$\rbrack$ peak**

* **$\lbrack$M+1$\rbrack$ peak:** an ion that has a mass one unit greater than the
  molecular ion in a mass spectrum, due to presence of the
  <sup>13</sup>C isotope

$$n = \frac{100}{1.1}\times\frac{\text{abundance of }\lbrack M + 1\rbrack^+\text{ion}}{\text{abundance of }M^+\text{ ion}}$$
