## Neutrality of Amides

- The presence of the electron withdrawing oxygen atom means that the
  lone pair on the amide’s nitrogen atom is not available to be
  donated to e.g. H<sup>+</sup> ions

- In amines, the lone pair of electrons is localized around the nitrogen atom. However, in amides, it is delocalized into the C=O group through resonance

- Thus, the lone pair of electrons become less available for $H^+$

- Hence, amides are much weaker bases than amines and considered to be neutral.
