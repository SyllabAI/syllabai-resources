## Amino Acids in Acidic/Basic Conditions

- If an is acid added, the –COO<sup>-</sup> part of the zwitterion accepts
  an H<sup>+</sup> ion, reforming –COOH group, resulting in a +vely charged ion

- If an alkali is added, the –NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> part of the
  zwitterion donates an H<sup>+</sup> ion to the OH<sup>-</sup>,
  reforming –NH<sub>2</sub> group and H<sub>2</sub>O, resulting in a -vely
  charged ion
