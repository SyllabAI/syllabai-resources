## Hydrolysis of Amides‎

![](../../../assets/4137c4330e8d-f1dc9d8a5ca1673f9605a725.webp)

* If we react an unsubstituted amide ($RCONH_{2}$) with acid, a carboxylic acid and ammonia are formed

  * The ammonia in the solution reacts with excess acid forming an ammonium salt

* If we react $RCONH_{2}$ with an alkali, the salt of the carboxylic acid, and ammonia are formed

* Note: The solution formed from alkali hydrolysis should be acidified
