## Formation of Alkyl Amines

**Substitution to form primary amine**

* $R–Br$ + $NH_3$ $\rightarrow$ $R–NH_2$ + $HBr_{(g)}$

* Type of reaction:

  * nucleophilic substitution

* Reagent:

  * hot conc. NH<sub>3</sub> in ethanol

* Condition:

  * high pressure

  **Substitution to form secondary amine**

* R’-Br + R-NH<sub>2</sub> $\rightarrow$ R’-NRH + HBr<sub>(g)</sub>

  * Type of reaction: nucleophilic substitution

  * Reagent: primary amine in ethanol

  * Condition: heat in sealed tube under pressure

**Reduction of nitriles**

* Step 1. Halogenoalkane to nitrile:
  CH<sub>3</sub >Br + CN<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ CH<sub>3</sub >CN + Br<sup>-</sup>

* Type of reaction:

  * nucleophilic substitution

* Reagent:

  * KCN (to provide cyanide ions)

* Conditions:

  * heat under reflux, KCN is ethanolic

* Step 2. Nitrile to amine:
  R–C‎≡N + 4[H] ‎→‎ R–CH<sub>2</sub>NH<sub>2</sub>

* Type of reaction:

  * reduction of nitrile

* Reagent:

  * hydrogen gas

* Reducing agent:

  * LiAlH<sub>4</sub> in dry ether, or Nickel (Ni)

* Conditions:

  * high temp. and pressure

**Reduction of amides**

* R–CO­–NH<sub>2</sub> + 4[H] → R–CH<sub>2</sub>NH<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O

* Type of reaction:

  * reduction

* Reagent:

  * hydrogen gas

* Reducing agent:

  * LiAlH<sub>4</sub> in dry ether

* Conditions:

  * r.t.p.
