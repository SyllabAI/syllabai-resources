## Condensation Reactions

* Mechanism:

  * addition-elimination

Note: All reactions with acyl chlorides happen under r.t.p. because acyl chloride is reactive and doesn’t require catalyst or extreme conditions for reactions to place

**Forming Amides**

- <span class="underline">Primary amides:</span>

  - R—CO—Cl + NH3 →R—CO—NH2 + HCl

  - Reagent: ammonia

  - condition: r.t.p

    ![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1493681241550225498/1498289750132658277/image.png?ex=69f09e91&is=69ef4d11&hm=ba854e591df1ef752127cc17721577a439ae5ebc308894beaa2fce3c4b4fe0ee&=&format=webp&quality=lossless&width=929&height=143)

- <span class="underline">Secondary amides:</span>

  - R—CO—Cl + R’NH2 → R—CO—NHR’ + HCl

  - Reagent: alkyl amine

  - condition: r.t.p

    ![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1493681241550225498/1498290028428787723/image.png?ex=69f09ed3&is=69ef4d53&hm=faceb04cef2d7c17b92ba8952ebceb48f45361d4efcc0beae9f7371fad27872a&=&format=webp&quality=lossless&width=1005&height=153)

- <span class="underline">Note:</span> If excess NH<sub>3</sub> used,
  the HCl formed is neutralized to NH<sub>4</sub>Cl

**Esterification**

- R—CO—Cl + HO–R → R–CO–OR<sub>(aq)</sub> + HCl<sub>(aq)</sub>

- Reagent & Condition:

  - Aliphatic alcohol at r.t.p
