## Nucleophilic Substitution Reaction

**Hydrolysis**

  - C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>Cl<sub>(l)</sub> + NaOH<sub>(aq)</sub>
    $\rightarrow$ C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>OH<sub>(l)</sub> +
    NaCl<sub>(aq)</sub>

  - Type of reaction:
    
      - nucleophilic substitution

  - Reagent:
    
      - sodium hydroxide

  - Condition:
    
      - Temp.: 300-330<sup>o</sup>C
    
      - Press.: 150-300 atm

  - Rate of Hydrolysis: (slowest to fastest)
