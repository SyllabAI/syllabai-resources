## Entropy Change in Exo/Endo Reactions


|Exothermic Reactions|Endothermic Reactions|
|:---|:---|
|Energy released to surroundings|Energy absorbed from surroundings|
|Increases ways of arranging the energy of molecules in surrounding|Decreases ways of arranging the energy of molecules.|
|Entropy increases|Entropy decreases|
|Increased probability of chemical change occurring spontaneously|Decreased probability of chemical change occurring spontaneously|
