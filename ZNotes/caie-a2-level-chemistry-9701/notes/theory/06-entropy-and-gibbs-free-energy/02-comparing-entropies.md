## Comparing Entropies

* Disorder of gas > liquid > solid (eg. $CO_2 (g) > CaCO_3 (s)$)

* If the physical states of the substances are the same, then the substance with the greater number of atoms has greater entropy (eg. $CO_2 (g) > CO (g)$).

* The greater the temperature, the more the movement hence, the greater the entropy
