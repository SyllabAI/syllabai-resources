## Temperature Change & Reaction Spontaneity

- **For exothermic reactions:**

  If temperature is increased

  - $T\mathrm{\Delta}S$ value becomes more positive

  - $\mathrm{\Delta}G$ positive $\therefore$ less likely to be
    spontaneous

- **For endothermic reactions:**

  If temperature is increased

  - $T\mathrm{\Delta}S$ value become more negative

  - $\mathrm{\Delta}G$ nagetive $\therefore$ more likely to be
    spontaneous
