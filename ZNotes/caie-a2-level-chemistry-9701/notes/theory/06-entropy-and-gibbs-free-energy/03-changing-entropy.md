## Changing Entropy

- As a substance changes from solid to liquid to gas, its entropy increases as molecules move more and are more disordered.

- When an ionic salt is dissolved in water (s) $\rightarrow$ (aq);
  the entropy increases as as the crystals break up and the ions and find
  their way between the water molecules.

- Increasing the temperature of a substance (without changing state) as the K.E(kinetic energy) of its particles increases, so the particles move more, hence, entropy is greater.

- If a reaction increases the number of gas molecules, the entropy
  increases as gases have higher entropies than solids or liquids.

- Simpler substances with fewer atoms have lower entropy values than
  more complex substances.

- For similar substances, harder substances have lower entropy values.
