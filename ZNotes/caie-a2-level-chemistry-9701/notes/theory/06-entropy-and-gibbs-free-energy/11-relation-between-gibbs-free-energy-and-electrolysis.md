## Determining spontaneity of a reaction using $E^\theta$ 

$\Delta G = -nFE$

* $\Delta G$ is Gibb’s free energy change, $n$ is moles of electrons, $F$ is Faraday’s constant and $E$ is cell potential

* If E>0V (positive), $\Delta G$ is negative, then reaction is spontaneous

* If E<0V (negative), $\Delta G$ is positive, then reaction is non-spontaneous
