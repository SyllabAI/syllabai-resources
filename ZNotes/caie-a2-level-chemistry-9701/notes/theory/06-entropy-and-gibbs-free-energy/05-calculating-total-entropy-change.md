## Calculating Total Entropy Change

* Firstly, calculate the entropy change for a reaction:

  $\Delta S^\Theta_{system} = \Delta S^\Theta_{products} - \Delta S^\Theta_{reactants}$

* Next, calculate entropy change in the surroundings:

  $$
  \mathrm{\Delta}S_\text{surroundings}^{\theta} = \frac{- \mathrm{\Delta}H_\text{reaction}^\theta}T
  $$

  $T$ in Kelvin; standard temperature = 298K

  * If $\mathrm{\Delta}H_{\text{reaction}}^{\theta}$ in kJ
    mol$^{-1}$, change from KJ to J by $\times \ 1000$

  * Negative sign part of equation, always put it

  Use values calculated to find total entropy change:

  $\Delta S^\Theta_{total} = \Delta S^\Theta_{system} + \Delta S^\Theta_{surroundings}$

  If $\mathrm{\Delta}S_\text{total}^{\theta}$ positive, reaction
  is feasible
