## **Identifying Functional Groups:**


|**Functional Group**|**Reagent / Test**|**Positive Observation**|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**Alkene** ($\text{C=C}$)|Aqueous Bromine ($\text{Br}_2\text{(aq)}$)|Orange solution decolourises|
|**Haloalkane** ($-\text{X}$)|1. Warm with $\text{NaOH(aq)}$<br>2. Acidify with $\text{HNO}_3$<br>3. Add $\text{AgNO}_3\text{(aq)}$|Precipitate forms (White = $\text{Cl}$, Cream = $\text{Br}$, Yellow = $\text{I}$)|
|Alcohol (1° or 2°)|Acidified $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$ + heat|Orange solution turns green|
|Aldehyde / Ketone|2,4-DNPH (Brady's reagent)|Orange precipitate forms|
|**Aldehyde** (only)|Tollens' Reagent + heat|Silver mirror forms on the glass|
|Phenol|Aqueous Bromine ($\text{Br}_2\text{(aq)}$)|White precipitate forms, orange solution decolourises|
|**Methyl Carbonyl** ($\text{CH}_3\text{CO}-$)|Alkaline Iodine + heat (Iodoform test)|Pale yellow precipitate forms ($\text{CHI}_3$)|
