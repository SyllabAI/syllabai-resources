## Ring Activating and Deactivating Groups


|**Ring Activating**|**Ring Deactivating**|
|:---|:---|
|<div align="center"> Single-bond groups such as Alkyls, NH<sub>2</sub>, OH</div>|<div align="center">Groups with multiple such as CHO, COR, NO<sub>2</sub>, COOH</div>|
|Donates e<sup>-</sup> to the ring|Withdraws e<sup>-</sup> from ring|
|+ve inductive effect|-ve inductive effect|
|Increase e<sup>-</sup> density at position 2, 4 and 6, so substitution occurs at these positions|Decrease e<sup>-</sup> density at positions 2, 4, and 6, so substitution occurs at 3 and 5 positions|
|Thus, elec-substitution is faster and occurs under milder conditions|Thus, elec-substitution is slower and occurs under harsher conditions|

**Exception:** Chlorine is ring deactivating but it directs incoming groups to 2,4,6 position
