## Reactions with Arenes

**Hydrogenation**

* C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>6(l)</sub> + 3H<sub>2(g)</sub> $\xrightarrow{Nickel}$ C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>12</sub> (cyclohexane)

* **Type of reaction:**

  * Electrophilic addition

* **Conditions:**

  * Temperature: 150ºC

  * Catalyst: Ni (or Pt)

## Electrophilic Substitution Reactions

**Halogenation**

* C$_{6}$H$_6$ + X$_2$ $\xrightarrow{catalyst}$
  C$_6$H$_6$X + HX (where X is a halogen atom)
* **Reagent:**
  * dry Cl$_2$ gas / pure Br$_2$ liquid
* **Conditions:**
  * Temp./press.: r.t.p.
  * Catalyst: anhydrous AlCl$_{3}$ or AlBr$_3$
* **Generating electrophile** (E$^{+}$)**:**
  * catalysts are e$^{-}$ deficient halogen carriers, and form
    dative bond drawing e$^{-}$ from halogen molecule
    producing X$^{+}$
* AlCl$_3$ + Cl$_2$ $\rightarrow \lbrack$AlCl$_4\rbrack\ ^{-}$ +
  Cl$^{+}$
* **Regenerating catalyst:**
  * H$^{+}$ +  $\lbrack AICl_4 \rbrack^-$ $\rightarrow$ HCl + AlCl$_3$

**Nitration**

* C$_6$H$_6$ + HNO$_3$ $\rightarrow$
  C$_6$H$_5$NO$_2$ + H$_2$O

* **Reagent:**

  * nitrating mixture i.e. conc. HNO$_3$ and conc. H$_2$SO$_4$

* **Conditions:**

  * Temp.: 25-60$^o$C under reflux

* **Generating electrophile** (E$^{+}$)**:**

  * 2H$_2$SO$_4$ + HNO$_3$ $\rightarrow$ 2HSO$_4\ ^{-}$ + NO$_2\ ^{+}$ + H$_3$O$^{+}$

* **Regenerating catalyst:**

  * 2HSO$_4\ ^{-}$ + H$_3$O$^{+}$ + H$^{+}$ $\rightarrow$ 2H$_2$SO$_4$ + H$_2$O

  Note: when benzene is further reacted with conc. HNO$_3$, 1,3,5-trinitrobenzene is formed

**Alkylation / Acylation**

* C$_6$H$_6$ + RCl/RCOCl $\xrightarrow{AlCl_3}$
  C$_6$H$_5$R/C$_6$H$_5$RCO +
  HCl${\text{(g)}}$
* **Reagent:**
  * alkyl halide (RCl) / acyl chloride (RCOCl)
* **Conditions:**
  * Temp.: heat under reflux
  * Catalyst: anhydrous AlCl$_3$
* **Generating electrophile** (E$^{+}$)**:**
  * AlCl$_3$ + RCl / RCOCl $\rightarrow$ $\lbrack$AlCl$_4 \rbrack\ ^{-}$ + R$^{+}$ / RO$^{+}$
* **Regenerating catalyst:**
  * H$^{+}$ + $\lbrack$AlCl$_4 \rbrack\ ^{+}$ $\rightarrow$ HCl +
    AlCl$_3$
