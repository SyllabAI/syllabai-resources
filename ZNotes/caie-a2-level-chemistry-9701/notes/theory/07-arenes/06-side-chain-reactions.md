## Side Chain Reactions

**Chloromethyl Benzene**

* C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>CH<sub>3(l)</sub> + Cl<sub>2(g)</sub> $\rightarrow$
  C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>CH<sub>2</sub>Cl<sub>(l)</sub> + HCl
* Type of reaction:
  * free radical substitution
* Reagent:
  * Cl<sub>2(g)</sub>
* Condition:
  * U.V. light
* With excess Cl<sub>2</sub> a mixture of di, tri chloromethyl benzene
  can be obtained

  -> Without the presence of UV light, halogens will be substituted into the arene ring and under the presence of UV light, arenes will be substituted into the side alkyl chain

**Benzoic Acid**

* C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>CH<sub>3(l)</sub> + 3$\lbrack$O$\rbrack$ $\rightarrow$
  C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>COOH<sub>(l)</sub> + 2H<sub>2</sub>O
* Type of reaction:
  * oxidation
* Reagent:
  * KMnO<sub>4(aq)</sub> or
    K<sub>2</sub>Cr<sub>2</sub>O<sub>7(aq)</sub>
* Condition:
  * heat under reflux (2 to 3 hours)
* Alkyl gp. always oxidized regardless of chain length
* If more than one alkyl gp., each one oxidized
