## Solvents

  - **General solubility rule:** like dissolves like
    
      - Covalent substances dissolve/mix in other covalent substances
    
      - Ionic substances do not dissolve/mix in other covalent substance
        (exception is water)

  - Solubility in water
    
      - Although covalent, highly polar nature makes it excellent
        solvent for ionic substances
    
      - Dissolves some covalent substances because they either react or
        form H-bonds with water molecules
