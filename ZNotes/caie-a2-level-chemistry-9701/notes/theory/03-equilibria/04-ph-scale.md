## pH Scale 

  - Gives strength of acid depending on $$H^+$$

  - Use $–\log\ $of values as they are very small giving pH

  - Lower pH value, stronger the acid

$- \log\left\lbrack H^{+} \right\rbrack = pH$ $\Leftrightarrow$
$\left\lbrack H^{+} \right\rbrack = 10^{- pH}$

**Calculating pH of a strong acid:**

Find the pH of 3.6 $\times$ 10<sup>-5</sup> mol dm<sup>-3</sup>
HNO<sub>3</sub>

**<center>Solution</center>**

*It is a strong acid and dissociates completely the conc. of ions is
equal to conc. of the acid*

$$\left\lbrack H^{+} \right\rbrack = \left\lbrack H\text{NO}_{3} \right\rbrack = 3.6 \times 10^{- 5}$$

*Calculate the pH by logging the conc.*

$$pH = - \log{(3.6 \times 10^{- 5}}) = 4.44$$
