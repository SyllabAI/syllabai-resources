## Predicting Precipitation

* **Precipitation:** When two aq. Solutions of ionic substance are mixed, a ppt. will form provided the K$_{\text{sp}}$ of the insoluble solid is less than ionic product of ions

$$
\text{Ionic Product >} K_{\text{sp}}
$$

**Predicting if insoluble salt will precipitate:**

Will a ppt. BaCO$3$ (K${\text{(sp)}}$ = $8.1 \times 10^{- 9}$)
form from solution containing 0.1 mol dm$^{-3}$
Ba$^{2+}{\text{(aq)}}$ & 1.0 mol dm$^{-3}$
CO$\ ^{2-}\ _{\text{(aq)}}$

$$
\text{Solutions}
$$

*Calculate ionic product:*

$$
1 \times 0.1 = 0.1
$$

Compare to K$_{\text{sp}}$ value

$1.0 > 8.1 \times 10^{- 9}\therefore$ ppt. will form
