## Controlling pH of Blood

* Respiration in cells produces carbon dioxide and carbon dioxide
  combines with water in blood

CO<sub>2(aq)</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>(aq)</sub>
$\rightleftharpoons$ H<sup>+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub> +
HCO<sub>3</sub><sup>-</sup><sub>(aq)</sub>

* Carbonic anhydrase catalyses the formation of H2CO3 which dissociates to form HCO<sub>3</sub><sup>-</sup> ions and increase its concentration similar to acidic buffers
