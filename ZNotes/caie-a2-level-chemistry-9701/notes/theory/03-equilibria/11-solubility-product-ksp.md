## Solubility Product K<sub>sp</sub>

* When a sparingly soluble salt is shaken with distilled water and
  left to settle, the water contains aq. ions from the salt in very
  small conc. and a dynamic equilibrium is set up between ions and
  insoluble solid
* Solubility product (K$_\text{(sp)}$): product of conc. of each ion in a saturated solution of sparingly soluble salt at 298K raised to the power of moles in dissociation equation, e.g.

Fe<sub>2</sub>S<sub>3</sub><sub>(s)</sub> $\rightleftharpoons$ 2Fe$^{3+}\text{(aq)}$ + 3S$^{2-}\text{(aq)}$

K$_\text{(sp)}$ = 2Fe<sup>3+2</sup>,3S<sup>2-3</sup> units: mol<sup>5</sup> dm$^{-15}$

* The conc. of ions is independent of the amount of solid
* K$_\text{(sp)}$ values change only with temperature
* Concentration of ions = solubility of salt

**Calculating solubility product from solubility:**

A saturated solution of magnesium fluoride, MgF$_2$, has a
solubility of $1.22 \times 10^{- 3}$mol dm<$^{-3}$. Calculate the
solubility product of magnesium fluoride.

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

*Write down the equilibrium equation*

MgF<sub>2</sub><sub>(s)</sub> $\rightleftharpoons$ Mg<sup>2+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub>+2F<sup>-</sup><sub>(aq)</sub>

Write down the equilibrium expression

$$
K_{sp} = \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack \lbrack 2F^{-} \rbrack^{2}
$$

Solubility of salt = conc. of ions

$$
\therefore \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack=\lbrack F^-\rbrack=1.22 \times 10^{-3}
$$

Substitute into equilibrium expression
K<sub>sp</sub> = (1.22 $\times$ 10<sup>3-</sup>}) $\times$ {2 $\times$ (1.22 $\times$ 10<sup>-3</sup>)}

$$
K_{sp}= 7.26 \times 10^{- 9}mol^{3} dm^{-9}
$$
