## Conjugate pairs

* Conjugate acid-base pair: It consists of two chemical species that differ from each other by the presence or absence of a single proton (H⁺)

* Acid: Proton donor. It makes a conjugate base:

  * (acid) HCl $\leftarrow\rightarrow$ H<sup>+</sup> + Cl<sup>-</sup> (conjugate base)

  * This is a conjugate acid-base pair

* Base: Proton acceptor. It makes a conjugate acid:

  * (base) NH<sub>3</sub> + H<sup>+</sup> $\leftarrow\rightarrow$ NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup> (conjugate acid)

  * This also a conjugate acid-base pair

Example:

* CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O ⇌ CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> + OH<sup>-</sup>

  * CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub> (base) and CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> (acid) are a conjugate pair

  * H<sub>2</sub>O (acid) and OH<sup>-</sup> (base) are the other conjugate pair
