## Buffer Solutions

* **Buffer solution:** a solution in which pH doesn’t change
  significantly when small amounts of acids or alkali added
* A buffer solution can be:
  * Acid: Weak acid + its salt

    e.g. CH<sub>3</sub>COOH and CH<sub>3</sub>COONa
  * Alkali: Weak alkali + its salt

    e.g. NH<sub>3</sub> and NH<sub>4</sub>Cl
* In both types, the salt provides more of its conjugate pair, for
  example

  HA<sub>(aq)</sub> $\rightleftharpoons$
  H<sup>+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub> + A<sup>-</sup><sub>(aq)</sub> &
  MA<sub>(aq)</sub> $\rightarrow$ M<sup>+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub> +
  A<sup>-</sup><sub>(aq)</sub>
