## Calculating pH of Buffer Solutions

$$
pH = pK_a + \log\left( \frac{\left\lbrack \text{Salt} \right\rbrack}{\left\lbrack \text{Acid} \right\rbrack} \right)
$$

**Calculating pH of buffer solution:**

A buffer solution is made by adding 3.28g of CH$_3$COONa to 1
dm $^3$ of 0.01 mol dm$^{-3}$  CH$^3$COOH

1. What is the pH of this buffer (K$_{\text{a}}$
   $= 1.8  \times \ 10^{- 5}$)

What is the change in pH when 1 cm$^3$ of 1 mol
dm$^{-3}$ NaOH is added to 1 dm$^3$ of the buffer?

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

**Part (a):**

*Calculate the moles and concentration of the salt*

$$
\text{Moles}
$$

$$
= \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Molar Mass}} = \frac{3.28}{82} = 0.04
$$

$$
\text{Concentration}
$$

$$
= \frac{\text{Moles}}{\text{Volume}} = \frac{0.04}{1} = 0.04
$$

*Use formula to calculate pH:*

$$
pH = - \log{\left( 1.8 \times 10^{- 5} \right) + \log{\left( \frac{0.04}{0.01} \right) =}}5.35
$$

**Part (b):**

*NaOH reacts with acid:*

NaOH$_{\text{(aq)}}$ + CH$*3$COOH$*{\text{(aq)}}$CH$*3$COONa$*{\text{(aq)}}$ + H$_2$O

*Calculate no. of moles of NaOH used:*

$$
\text{Moles}
$$

$$
= Concentration  \times \ Volume = 1 \times \ 0.001 = 0.001
$$

*Hence that much of acid has been reduced and the same amount has
formed as salt. Calculate new concentrations of salt and acid.*

**Conc. of Acid**

$$
=  \frac{\left( 0.01  \times  1 \right)\ –\ 0.001}{1\  + \ \left( 1 \times \ 10^{- 3} \right)} = 8.9 \times \ 10^{- 3}
$$

**Conc. of Salt**

$$
=  \frac{0.04 + \ 0.001}{1\  + \ \left( 1\times \ 10^{- 3} \right)} = 0.0410
$$

*Use formula to calculate pH:*

$$
\text{pH} = - \log{\left( 1.8 \times 10^{- 5} \right) + \log{\left( \frac{0.0410}{8.99\  \times \ 10^{- 3}} \right) =}}5.40
$$

*Find the change in pH:*

$$
\mathrm{\Delta}pH = 5.40 - 5.35 = 0.05
$$

**Calculating pH of buffer solution with different volumes of acid and
salts:**

2. What is the pH of the buffer solution formed when 40cm$^3$ of
   1 mol dm$^{-3}$ of nitrous acid (pK$_{\text{a}}$ = 3.34) is
   added to 20 cm$^3$ of 1 mol dm$^{-3}$ of sodium hydroxide?

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

**Part (a):**

*Write out an equation for the reaction*

HNO$*{2\text{(aq)}}$ + NaOH$*{\text{aq}}$ NaNO$_{2\text{(aq)}}$ +
H$_2$O$_1$

*Calculate the moles of each reactant*

$$
\text{Moles of HNO}_2
$$

$$
= 1 \times \left( 40  \times \ 10^{- 3} \right) = 0.04
$$

$$
\text{Moles of NaOH}
$$

$$
= 1 \times \left( 20 \times {\ 10}^{- 3} \right) = 0.02
$$

*NaOH is limiting reagent therefore moles of product and reactants
are:*

NaNO$_2$ formed $= 0.02$

HNO$_2$  remaining = 0.04 – 0.02 = 0.02$

*Calculate concentration of salt and acid:*

Total Volume $\left( 20 + 40 \right) \times 10^{- 3} = 0.06$dm$^3$

Conc. of HNO$_2$ $= \frac{0.02}{0.06} = \frac{1}{3}$

Conc. of NaNO$_2$ $= \frac{0.02}{0.06} = \frac{1}{3}$

*Use formula to calculate pH:*

$$
\text{pH} = {3.34}{+ \log{\left( \frac{\frac{1}{3}}{\frac{1}{3}} \right) =}}3.34
$$
