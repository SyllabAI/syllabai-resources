## Reaction with Benzene Ring

![](../../../assets/df2383969fd6-image066.png)

### **Bromination**

![](../../../assets/c599957dbb23-allimagese252a016497e8db5daccab31.png)

- Type of reaction:

  - electrophilic substitution

- Reagent:

  - aq. Bromine

- Condition:

  - r.t.p. (no catalyst)

- Rapid reaction, bromine decolourises, and white ppt. (2,4,6-tribromophenol) and misty fumes (HBr) form

- No catalyst needed since Br<sub>2</sub> molecule easily polarized by activated benzene ring, with increased <sub>­</sub>e<sup>-</sup> density in ring

### **Nitration**

‎![](../../../assets/dffb109b5e92-image178.png)

- Type of reaction:

  - electrophilic substitution

- Reagent:

  - dilute nitric acid

- Condition:

  - r.t.p. (again, due to activated benzene ring)

- If concentrated nitric acid used, then trinitrophenol is produced:

![](../../../assets/1080161a9655-image071.png)

### **Reduction**

![](../../../assets/5da228545c5c-allimagesf9fa2bf3c0b081b07894ef5e.png)

* Type of reaction:

  * reduction

* Reagent:

  * Hydrogen

* Conditions:

  * Nickel catalyst and heat

* Product is cyclohexanol and it is a secondary alcohol
