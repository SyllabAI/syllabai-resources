## Reactivity of Benzene vs Phenol

### Phenol reacts more readily with electrophiles as compared to benzene. This is because:

* One of the lone pairs on the oxygen atom overlaps in phenol overlaps with the pi electron system of the benzene ring

* As a result, there is an increased electron density in the ring

* The electron donating group -OH in phenol activates the benzene ring and directs incoming groups towards the 2-,4-,6- position

* Due to the difference in their reactivity, the reagents and conditions for electrophilic substitution reactions will differ between phenol and benzene.
