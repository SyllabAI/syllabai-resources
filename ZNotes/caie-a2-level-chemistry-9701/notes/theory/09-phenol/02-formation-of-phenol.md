## Formation of Phenol

### 1st Step

![](../../../assets/1c83d8d32c14-4ffad079ea9e1ccbdef7efa6.webp)

* Type of reaction:

  - diazotization reaction (it is also the 1st step in the making of an azo dye)

- Reagents:

  - Phenylamine (C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>NH<sub>2</sub>) and nitrous acid (NaNO<sub>2</sub> and excess HCl<sub>(aq)</sub>)

  - *(HNO<sub>2</sub> is a weak, unstable acid, so it has to be produced in a test tube by the reaction of NaNO<sub>2</sub> with excess HCl)*

- Condition:

  - use ice to keep low temp. (less than 10${^\circ}$C)

### 2nd Step

* The diazonium salt formed will decompose easily when warmed with water, giving off nitrogen gas and forming phenol
