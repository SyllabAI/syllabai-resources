# Phenol

* Phenol ($C_{6}H_{5}Cl$) is an aromatic alcohol; a white crystalline solid that is
  both corrosive and anaesthetic
* OH group is ring-activating, so phenol is more reactive than benzene
* OH group is 2, 4, 6-directing, so electrophilic substitution occurs at 2, 4, 6- positions
* Phenol’s m.p. ($46 ^{\circ}C$) is relatively high for an arene because of hydrogen bonding between its molecules
