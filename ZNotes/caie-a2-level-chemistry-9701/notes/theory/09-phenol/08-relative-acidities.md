## Relative Acidities

‎![](../../../assets/69478e4be6c2-image072.png)

- The stronger the acid:

  - the higher the K<sub>a</sub> value

  - the more easily the H<sup>+</sup> is donated

  - the more stable the conjugate base is

 

**Ethanol:** C<sub>2</sub>H­<sub>5</sub>OH + H<sub>2</sub>O
$\rightleftharpoons$ C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub>O<sup>-</sup> +
H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup>

- <span class="underline">K<sub>a</sub>:</span> 10<sup>-18</sup> mol
  dm<sup>-3</sup>

- <span class="underline">Ability to donate H<sup>+</sup> ions:</span>

  - C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub> is an e<sup>-</sup> donating gp.
    which increases charge density on O of OH

‎![](../../../assets/4546a837b0d5-image179.png)

- More attraction between O–H so loss of H<sup>+</sup> difficult

<!-- end list -->

- <span class="underline">Stability of conjugate base:</span>

  - In ethoxide ion, C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub> gp. increases –ve
    charge

  - Makes ethoxide ion more basic than OH<sup>-</sup>

  - Ability to accept H<sup>+</sup> increases, moving equi. to left

‎![](../../../assets/989b2bc9e475-image180.png)

 

**Water:** H<sub>2</sub>O + H<sub>2</sub>O $\rightleftharpoons$
OH<sup>-</sup> + H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup>

- <span class="underline">K<sub>a</sub>:</span> 10<sup>-16</sup> mol
  dm<sup>-3</sup>

- No e<sup>-</sup> donating or withdrawing gp. is present, hence the ability
  to donate and accept H<sup>+</sup> ion is the same

 

**Phenol:** C<sub>6</sub>H­<sub>5</sub>OH + H<sub>2</sub>O
$\rightleftharpoons$ C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>5</sub>O<sup>-</sup> +
H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup>

- <span class="underline">K<sub>a</sub>:</span> 10<sup>-10</sup> mol
  dm<sup>-3</sup>

- <span class="underline">Ability to donate H<sup>+</sup> ions:</span>

  - OH is a ring activating gp. & the lone pair of e<sup>-</sup>s on
    O becomes part of delocalized e<sup>-</sup> system

  - Decreases e<sup>-</sup> density on O of OH, and attraction
    between O and H decreases, so H<sup>+</sup> is lost more easily

- <span class="underline">Stability of conjugate base:</span>

  - In phenoxide ion, the -ve charge on O is delocalized around the ring and has a
    reduced tendency to attract H<sup>+</sup>

  - Conjugate base is stable
