## Solubility of Gp-2 Hydroxides

* The radius of cation increases down group, therefore charge density
  decreases
* Attraction between cation and water molecules decreases, therefore
  value of $\mathrm{\Delta}H\\_{\text{hyd}}^{\ominus}$ becomes less
  exothermic
* $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{lat}$</sub> also decreases but decrease is more significant
* $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{sol}$</sub> becomes more exothermic
  because decrease in $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{hyd}$</sub> is
  less than decrease in ${\Delta}H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{lat}$</sub>
* Therefore, solubility of Hydroxides increases down the group
