## Thermal Stability of Gp.2 CO<sub>3</sub> and (NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>

* Down the group, polarizing power decreases, as size of cation increases
* Polarizing power of Mg<sup>2+</sup> > Ca<sup>2+</sup> > Sr<sup>2+</sup> >
  Ba<sup>2+</sup>
* The greater the polarization, the more distorted is the anion
* Therefore, the more polarized nitrate/carbonate will decompose at a lower temperature
* **Order of stability:** Ba<sup>2+</sup> > Sr<sup>2+</sup> >
  Ca<sup>2+</sup> > Mg<sup>2+</sup>
