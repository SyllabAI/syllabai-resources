## Dissolving Salts

* **Enthalpy change of solution
  (**$\mathbf{\mathrm{\Delta}}\mathbf{H}$<sub>${\mathbf{\text{sol}}}$</sub>$^{\mathbf{\ominus}}$**):**
  Enthalpy change measured when one mole of ionic solid is dissolved in
  sufficient water to form a very dilute solution under standard
  conditions (can be +ve or –ve)

* **e.g.** NaCl<sub>(s)</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O $\rightarrow$ NaCl<sub>(aq)</sub>

* **Enthalpy change of hydration
  (**$\mathbf{\mathrm{\Delta}}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{\text{hyd}}}^{\mathbf{\ominus}}$**):**
  Enthalpy change measured when one mole of gaseous ions dissolves in
  excess water to form a very dilute solution under standard
  conditions

  **e.g.** Na<sup>+</sup><sub>(g)</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O $\rightarrow$
  Na<sup>+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub>

* Factors affecting $\Delta$ $H^{\ominus}$<sub>$hyd$</sub> $=$
  factors affecting $\Delta$ $H^{\ominus}$<sub>$lat$</sub>

* The solubility of ionic salts depends on value of $\Delta H^\ominus$<sub>$sol$</sub>: more –ve = more
  soluble

* If $\Delta H^\ominus$<sub>$sol$</sub> is positive, then the salt is insoluble
