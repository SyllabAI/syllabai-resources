## Ion Polarisation

* **Ion Polarisation:** Distortion of the electron cloud on an anion by a neighbouring cation
* **Polarising power:** Ability of a cation to attract electrons and distort an anion
* Polarization will be increased if:
  * Cation has a higher charge density: high positive charge and
    small size
  * Anion has greater polarizability: high negative charge and large
    size
