## Solubility of Gp-2 Sulfates

* The radius of cation increases down group, therefore charge density decrease

* Attraction between cation and water molecules decreases, therefore
  value of $\mathrm{\Delta}H\\_{\text{hyd}}^{\ominus}$ becomes less
  exothermic

* $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{lat}$</sub> also decreases but decrease is less significant

* $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{sol}$</sub> becomes less exothermic
  because decrease in $\Delta H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{hyd}$</sub> is
  less than decrease in ${\Delta}H^{\ominus}$<sub>$\text{lat}$</sub>

* Therefore, solubility of Sulfates decreases down the group
