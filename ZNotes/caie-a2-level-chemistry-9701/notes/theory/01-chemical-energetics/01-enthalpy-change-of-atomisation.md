# Chemical Energetics

## Enthalpy Change of Atomisation

* **Enthalpy Change of Atomisation (** $\mathrm{\Delta H_{at} ^{\ominus}}$ **):** Enthalpy change measured when 1 mole of gaseous atoms is formed from its element under standard conditions.
* e.g.: Na (s) ${\rightarrow}$ Na (g)
* e.g: 1/2Cl$_2$ (g) ${\rightarrow}$ Cl (g)
* **Note:** When exact values are not given, you may have to use a combination of enthalpy changes instead:
