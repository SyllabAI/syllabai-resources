# Carboxylic Acids

* Carboxylic acids are weak acids and dissociate incompletely in
  aqueous solutions

<center>R–COOH + H<sub>2</sub>O 

$\rightleftharpoons$

 R–COO<sup>-</sup> +
H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup></center>

* The O–H bond in carboxylic acid is weakened by the carbonyl group
  C$=$O
* The carboxylate ion is stabilized by the delocalization of
  e<sup>-</sup>s around the –COO<sup>-</sup> group, spreading and
  reducing the density of -ve charge making it less likely to bond
  with H<sup>+</sup>
