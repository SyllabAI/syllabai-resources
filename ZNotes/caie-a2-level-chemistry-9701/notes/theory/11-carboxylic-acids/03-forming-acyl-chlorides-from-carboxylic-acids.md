## Reactions with Carboxylic Acids to form Acyl Chlorides

* Carboxylic acid can react with $PCl_3$ and heat, $PCl_5$ and, $SOCl_2$ to give acyl chloride

* $CH_3COOH$ + $PCl_3$ + $heat$ $\rightarrow$ $CH_3COCl$ + $H_3PO_3$

* $CH_3COOH$ + $PCl_5$ $\rightarrow$ $CH_3COCl$ + $POCl_3$ + $HCl$

* $CH_3COOH$ + $SOCl_2$ $\rightarrow$ $CH_3COCl$ + $SO_2$ + $HCl$
