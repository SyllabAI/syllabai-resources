## Heterogeneous Catalysis

* Catalysts and reactants in different physical states
* A catalyst provides a surface on which the reaction occurs
  * Reactant particles adsorbed on the surface on collision with the catalyst
  * Molecular rearrangement occurs - bonds in reactants break, and new bonds in the product are formed
  * Product molecules desorbed from the surface
* The rate of reaction is dependent on the surface area of the catalyst
* **Adsorption:** weak bonds formed between reactant molecules & surface of the catalyst (diffusion onto the surface)
* **Desorption:** releasing of product molecules from the surface of the catalyst (diffusion away from the surface)
