## Rate Equation

- **Rate equations:** states the relationship between the rate
    constant and the concentrations of those reactants

$$
xA\  + \ yB \rightarrow Products
$$

$$
\mathbf{R = k}\left\lbrack \mathbf{A} \right\rbrack^{\mathbf{m}}\left\lbrack \mathbf{B} \right\rbrack^{\mathbf{n}}
$$

- **Rate constant** ($\mathbf{k}$): proportionality constant in
    rate eqn.
  - Larger the rate constant, faster the reaction
  - Depends on temperature
  - Unit depends on overall order of reaction
- **Order of reaction** ($\mathbf{m,n)}$): power to which conc.
    of the reactant is raised in experimentally determined rate eqn.

**Overall order of reaction** $= m + n$

- **Half-life,** $\mathbf{t}_{\frac{\mathbf{1}}{\mathbf{2}}}$ of a
    reaction is the time taken for conc. of one of the reactants to fall
    by half
