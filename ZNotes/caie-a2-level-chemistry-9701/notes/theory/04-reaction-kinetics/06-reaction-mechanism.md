## Reaction Mechanism

* **Rate-determining step:** the slowest step in a reaction mechanism

**In a multistep reaction:**

* The rate of reaction is dependent on the slowest step that needs the
  highest activation energy.
* The rate equation includes only reactants that are present in the rate-determining step.
* The orders with respect to the reactants are the moles of the reactants in the rate-determining step

**Hydrolysis of Alkyl Halide**

* Primary alkyl halide mechanism:

$$
C_3H_7{CH}_2Br\ +\ OH^- \xrightarrow{slow} \lbrack C_3H_7BrOH\rbrack
$$

$$
\lbrack C_3H_7BrOH\rbrack \xrightarrow{fast}C_3H_7CH_2OH + Br^-
$$

$\therefore k\lbrack C_3H_7CH_2Br \rbrack \lbrack OH^-\rbrack$

* Tertiary alkyl halide mechanism:

$$
(CH_3)_3CBr \xrightarrow{slow} (CH_3)C^+\ +\ Br^-
$$

$$
(CH_3)C^+\ +\ OH^-\xrightarrow{fast} (CH_3)COH\ +\ Br^-
$$

$\therefore R=k\lbrack(CH_3)_3CBr\rbrack$


**Constructing rate equation for multistep reaction**

The mechanism for the production of NO$_2$F involves

$$
NO_2 + F_2 \xrightarrow{slow} NF_2 + NF_2 + NO_2 \xrightarrow{fast} NO_2F + NF
$$

$$
NF + O_2 \xrightarrow{fast} NO_2F
$$

**a.  What is the overall stoichiometric equation?**

**b.  Write a rate equation for the reaction.**

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

**Part (a)**

*List all the reactants and products together*

**Reactants:** $NO_2 + F_2 + NF_2 + NO_2 + N + O_2$ 

**Products:** $NF_2 + O_2 + NO_2F + NF + NO_2F$

*Cancel the common things and form an equation*

$2NO_2 + F_2 2NO_2F$

**Part (b)**

*The rate equation will include only reactants of the slowest step, therefore:*

$$
R=k \lbrack NO_2\rbrack \lbrack F_2\rbrack
