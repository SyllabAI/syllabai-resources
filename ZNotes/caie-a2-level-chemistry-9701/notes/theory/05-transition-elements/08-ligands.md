## Ligands

* **Monodentate ligands:** form only one co-ordinate bond with central
  metal ion (donates one pair of e<sup>-</sup>s)
* **Bidentate ligands:** form 2 co-ordinate bonds with central metal
  ion (donates 2 pairs of e<sup>-</sup>s per molecule)

* **Polydentate ligands:** form multiple co-ordinate bonds with the central metal ion in a complex

* $EDTA^{4-}$ is a hexadentate ligand, an example of a polydentate

  * It forms 6 co-ordinate bonds to form an octahedral complex
