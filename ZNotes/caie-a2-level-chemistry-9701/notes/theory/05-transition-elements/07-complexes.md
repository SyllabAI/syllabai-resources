## Complexes

**Complex ion:** an ion or molecule formed by a central metal
ion surrounded by one or more ligands, which are bonded to
the metal ion by dative(coordinate covalent bond).

**A complex consists of:**

- **A central transition metal ion** that can accept e$^{-}$s

- **Ligand(s):** a species that contains at least one lone pair of
  e$^{-}$s that forms a dative bond to a central metal
  atom/ion

- **Co-ordination number:** number of coordinate/dative bonds between the
  central metal atom/ion and ligands

- Different metal ions show different coordination number with same
  ligands

- Transition metals form complexes because of:

  - vacant d orbitals that are energetically accessible
