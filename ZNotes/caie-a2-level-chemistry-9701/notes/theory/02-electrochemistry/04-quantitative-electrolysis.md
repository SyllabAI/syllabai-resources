## Quantitative Electrolysis

* To calculate the quantity of charge passed during electrolysis:

$Q = It$

where Q=charge in coulombs (C), I=current in amperes (A), and t=time in seconds (S)

* (Note: The mass of a substance produced is proportional to the quantity of electricity which passes through electrolyte)
* **1 faraday (F):** Quantity of electric charge carried by 1 mol of
  electrons = 96,500 C
* Relationship: $F = Le$
  * $F$ = Faraday’s Constant
  * $L$ = Avogadro’s Constant
  * $e$ = charge on 1 electron
* Example:
  * To liberate 1 mol of H<sub>2(g)</sub>

2H<sup>+</sup><sub>(aq)</sub> + 2e<sup>­-</sup>$\rightarrow$ H<sub>2(g)</sub>

* Therefore, 2 faradays of electricity are required to form 1 mole­

### Calculating current passed during electrolysis

1. Dilute sulfuric acid is electrolysed. Water is split into hydrogen and oxygen:
   2H<sub>2</sub>O(l) $\rightarrow$ 2H<sub>2</sub>(g) + O<sub>2</sub>(g)\
   A current is passed though the solution for 14 minutes.
   462 cm<sup>+3</sup> of hydrogen are produced at the cathode (room conditions).
   Find the current passed.

* Step 1. No. of H<sub>2</sub> moles=?
  * no. of H<sub>2</sub> moles = (vol. of gas in cm<sup>3</sup>)/(24,000 cm<sup>3</sup>)
    no. of moles = 462/24000 = 0.01925
* Step 2. No. of H<sub>2</sub> molecules=?
  * No. of H<sub>2</sub> molecules = 0.019 x 6.02x10<sup>23</sup> = 1.16x10<sup>22</sup>
* Step 3. Total no. of electrons transferred to produce this no. of H<sub>2</sub> molecules=?
  * Using 2H<sup>+</sup>(aq)+ 2e­<sup>-</sup> → H<sub>2</sub>,
    we know that the ratio of electron-moles to hydrogen gas moles is 2:1.
  * Thus, total no. of electrons transferred= 1.16x10<sup>22</sup> x 2 = 2.32x10<sup>22</sup>
* Step 4. Charge of these electrons = 2.32x10<sup>22</sup> x 1.6x10<sup>-19</sup> = 3.71x10<sup>3</sup>C
* Step 5. Current passed = ?
  * Q=It
  * I = 3.71x10<sup>3</sup>/(14 x 60)
  * I = 4.4A

### Determining the Avogadro constant

1. Aqueous copper (II) sulfate is electrolysed using copper electrodes. A current of 1.5 A is passed for 3 hours. 5.09 g of copper is deposited on the cathode. Determine the value for the Avogadro constant, L. (charge on one electron= -1.6x10<sup>-19</sup>, relative atomic mass of Cu= 63.5)

* Step 1. No. of Cu moles=?

  * No. of Cu moles= mass/Mr

  * Cu moles = 5.09/63.5

  * Cu moles = 0.0802 (3 significant figures)

* Step 2. No. of electron moles=?

  * Looking at the data provided, the right electrode to use here would be:

    * Cu<sup>2+</sup> + 2e<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ Cu

  * The ratio of copper moles to electron moles here is 1:2

  * Hence, electron moles= 0.1603 (3 significant figures)

* Step 3. Charge =?

  * Q=It

  * Q= 1.5 x (3 x 60 x 60)

  * Q= 16200C

* Step 4. No. of electrons=?

  * No. of electrons= (total charge)/(charge on one electrons)

  * No. of electrons= 16200/1.6x10<sup>-19</sup>

  * No. of electrons= 1.0125x10<sup>23</sup>

* Step 5. Avogadro constant L (number of particles per mole)=?

  * L = 1.0125x10<sup>23</sup>/0.1603

  * L = 6.32x10<sup>23</sup>
