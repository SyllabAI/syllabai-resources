## Nernst Equation

* Equation relating voltage of chemical cell to its standard potential
  and to concentrations of reactants & products

$$E = E^{\theta} + \frac{\text{RT}}{\text{zF}}\ln\frac{\left\lbrack \text{oxidized form} \right\rbrack}{\lbrack reduced\ form\rbrack}\text{  }$$

* $z$ is number of electrons transferred in reaction
* $F$ is the value of the Faraday constant 96,500 C
  mol<sup>-1</sup>$\ $

**Simplified relationship** by substituting standard values

$$E = E^{\theta} + \frac{0.059}{z}\log\frac{\left\lbrack \text{oxidised form} \right\rbrack}{\lbrack reduced\ form\rbrack}\text{  }$$

* If conc. less than 1 mol dm<sup>-3</sup>,
  $\log{\lbrack oxidized\ form\rbrack}$ is –ve and $E$ is less
  than $E^{\theta}$
* If conc. more than 1 mol dm<sup>-3</sup>,
  $\log{\lbrack oxidized\ form\rbrack}$ is +ve and $E$ is more
  than $E^{\theta}$

### Example Question.

* (Relevant half-equations:)

  * Al<sup>3+</sup> + 3e<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ Al ($E^{\theta}$=-1.66V)

  * Sn<sup>4+</sup> + 2e<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ Sn<sup>2+</sup> ($E^{\theta}$=+0.15V)

Q. An electrochemical cell is constructed using an Sn<sup>4+</sup>/Sn<sup>2+</sup> half-cell and an Al<sup>3+</sup>/Al half-cell. The cell is operated at 298 K. The Al<sup>3+</sup>/Al half-cell has standard conc. The Sn<sup>4+</sup>/Sn<sup>2+</sup> half-cell has [Sn<sup>4+</sup>] = 0.300 mol/dm<sup>3</sup> and [Sn<sup>2+</sup>] = 0.150 mol/dm<sup>3</sup>.

(i) Use the Nernst equation to calculate the electrode potential (E) of the Sn<sup>4+</sup>/Sn<sup>2+</sup> half-cell.

* Through the relevant half-equation, we know that z=2, and $E^{\theta}$=0.15

* Using $$E = E^{\theta} + \frac{0.059}{z}\log\frac{\left\lbrack \text{oxidised form} \right\rbrack}{\lbrack reduced\ form\rbrack}\text{  }$$:

  * $$E = 0.15 + \frac{0.059}{2}\log\frac{\left\lbrack \text{0.300} \right\rbrack}{\lbrack 0.150\rbrack}\text{  }$$

* Hence, E=0.159 V (or 0.16 V)

(ii) Calculate the E<sub>cell</sub> under these conditions.

* E<sub>cell</sub>= 0.16 + (-1.66)

* E<sub>cell</sub>= 1.82 V

  * (The Sn<sup>4+</sup>/Sn<sup>2+</sup> half-cell is more positive and will hence undergo reduction while the Al<sup>3+</sup>/Al half-cell will undergo oxidation)

(iii) Write a reaction for the overall cell reaction that occurs.

* 2Al + 3Sn<sup>4+</sup> $\rightarrow$ 2Al<sup>3+</sup> + 3Sn<sup>2+</sup>

* This is because both half-equations must have the same number of electrons:

  * 2Al<sup>3+</sup> + 6e<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ 2Al

  * 3Sn<sup>2+</sup> $\rightarrow$ 6e<sup>-</sup> + 3Sn<sup>4+</sup>
