## Feasibility of a Reaction

* For a spontaneous (i.e. feasible) reaction,
  E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> must be positive.
* For example:


|(Finding the feasibility of a reaction) Will chlorine oxidize $Fe^{2+}$ ions to $Fe^{3+}$ ions?|
|:---|
|*Identify the reducing and oxidising agents from the question;* chlorine is the oxidising agent and is therefore reduced|
|*Identify relevant equations from data booklet and place in correct direction. If using opposite direction, switch sign for value of $E^{θ}$*|
|$Cl_2+2e^- →Cl^-$                       $ E^\theta=+1.36 $|
|$ Fe^{2+} → Fe^{3+}+e^- $                 $ E^\theta= - \left(+0.77 \right) $|
|*Add Eθs together. If positive, reaction occurs and is feasible otherwise, does not occur spontaneously.*|
|(If E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is zero, then the reaction is in equilibrium)|
|$ ‎1.36 - 0.77 = 0.59 $|
|Reaction occurs spontaneously and is feasible|

* (Note: The feasibility of a reaction based on $E^{\theta}$ values is no guarantee that a reaction will proceed quickly)
* If E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is about +0.1V there are more products than reactants
* If E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is about -0.1V there are more reactants than products
* If E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is more positive than +0.6V the reaction goes to completion
* If E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is more negative than -0.6V then there is little to no reaction
