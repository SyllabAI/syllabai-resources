## Oxidizing Ability of Halogens (Gp. 17)

* Electrode potential values can be used to determine ease of
  oxidation or reduction
* The more +ve $E^{\theta}$, the easier it is to reduce therefore
  stronger oxidizing agent (ability).
* Relative reactivity of halogens can be determined by ordering them
  in descending order of $E^{\theta}$, most reactive to least

| **Reaction** | $E^θ$ |
|----|----|
| F<sub>2</sub> + 2e<sup>-</sup>$\rightarrow$ 2F<sup>-</sup> | +2.87 |
| Cl<sub>2</sub> + 2e<sup>-</sup>$\rightarrow$ 2Cl<sup>-</sup> | +1.36 |
| Br<sub>2</sub> + 2e<sup>-</sup>$\rightarrow$ 2Br<sup>-</sup> | +1.07 |
| I<sub>2</sub> + 2e<sup>-</sup> $\rightarrow$ 2I<sup>-</sup> | +0.54 |
