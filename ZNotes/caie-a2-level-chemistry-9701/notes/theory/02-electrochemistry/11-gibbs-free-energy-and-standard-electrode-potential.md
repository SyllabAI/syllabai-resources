## Gibbs Free Energy and Standard Electrode Potential

* Since both E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> and ΔG$^{\theta}$ both tell us the feasibility of a reaction, there is a relationship between them:
* ΔG$^{\theta}$ = -nFE$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> where:
  * ΔG$^{\theta}$ is the standard Gibbs free energy in J/mol (not kJ/mol)

  * n is the number of moles of electrons transferred in the cell reaction

  * F is the Faraday, 96500 C/mol

  * E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> is the standard cell potential
