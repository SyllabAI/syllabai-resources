## Standard Electrode Potentials

* **Standard electrode (redox) potential**
  E$^{\theta}$**:** The voltage produced when a standard half-cell is connected to a standard hydrogen electrode under standard conditions
* **Standard cell potential**
  E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub>**:** The difference in standard electrode potential between two half-cells
* Standard conditions:
  * Temperature: 298 K or 25 Degrees Celsius
  * Pressure: 1 atm (101 kPa)
  * Concentration: 1.00 mol dm<sup>-3</sup>
* E$^{\theta}$<sub>cell</sub> = more positive E$^{\theta}$ (red) - more negative E$^{\theta}$ (oxid)
* Note: In the data booklet, reduction reactions are given. Since oxidation is the reverse of reduction, the sign will change for the respective oxidations
