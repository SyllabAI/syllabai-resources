## Standard Hydrogen Electrode (S.H.E.)

* It is a reference cell used to measure $E^{\theta}$of any other half-cell

* ‎Consists of:

  * H<sub>2(g)</sub> at 298K and 1atm pressure
  * Pt electrode in contact with an aq. solution of H<sup>+</sup> ions at
    1\.00 mol dm<sup>-3</sup> conc. (e.g. of HCl­<sub>(aq)</sub> or ½
    H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4(aq)</sub>)
    (Pt electrode is inert, allows conduction and is coated with finely divided Pt, serving as a catalyst)
