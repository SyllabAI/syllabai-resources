## Methods (8 Marks)

  - Describe the methods to be used to vary the independent variable,
    and the means that you propose to ensure that you have measured its
    values accurately

  - Describe how you will measure the dependent variable

  - Describe how you will control each of the other variables

  - Explain how you will use any control experiments to verify that it
    is the independent variable that is affecting the dependent variable
    and now some other factor

  - Describe the arrangement of apparatus and the steps in the procedure
    to be followed

  - Suggest appropriate volumes and conc. of reagents

  - Assess the risks of your proposed methods

  - Describe precautions that should be taken to keep risks to a minimum

  - Draw up tables for data that you might wish to record

  - Describe how the data might be used in order to reach a conclusion
