## Volumes of Apparatus

  - Always mention the volume of apparatus being used

  - Common volumes:
    
      - Test tube = 16cm<sup>3</sup>
    
      - Gas syringe: 100cm<sup>3</sup> up to 500cm<sup>3</sup>
    
      - Glass beaker = 250cm<sup>3</sup>
    
      - Polystyrene cup = 150cm<sup>3</sup>

  - Calculate quantities and show volume would not exceed the apparatus
    used
