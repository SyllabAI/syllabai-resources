## Potential Risks and Solutions

  - Oxygen: is an oxidant so remove any oxidisable material

  - Nitrogen dioxide: is poisonous so carry out an experiment in a fume
    cupboard

  - When collecting gas over water: potential suck back so remove
    delivery tube from water when heating stops

  - Solution boils over/sprays: use gloves, eye protection

  - Corrosive nature of reagents: use gloves
