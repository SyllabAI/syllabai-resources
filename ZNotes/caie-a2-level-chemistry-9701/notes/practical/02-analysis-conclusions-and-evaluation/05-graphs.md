## Graphs

  - Independent variable plotted on the x-axis and the dependent on the
    y-axis

  - Appropriate scale; 1 large box = 1, 2, 4 or 5.

  - The graph must cover at least half the grid in both directions

  - If experiment/relationship shows origin (0, 0) is a valid point,
    scaling must include origin & line should include it as it is a
    definite point not subject to experimental errors

  - When referring to an anomalous result, clearly define the point
    before stating the reason

  - When calculating gradient, show construction lines and hypotenuse
    must be greater than half the line
