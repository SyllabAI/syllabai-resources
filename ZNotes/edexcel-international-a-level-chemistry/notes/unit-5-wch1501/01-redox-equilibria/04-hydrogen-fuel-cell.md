## Hydrogen Fuel Cell

* In a disproportionation reaction, a species is both oxidised and reduced - indicated by an increase and decrease in oxidation number for that species, and if the E cell value is positive, then the disproportionation reaction is feasible.
* Fuel cells are electrochemical cells that generate an electrical current without recharging. A fuel undergoes combustion in oxygen, and the energy is used to generate a voltage. The most common type of fuel cell is the hydrogen fuel cell, which makes water as a product, so it is environmentally friendly. It uses hydrogen and oxygen to generate a continuous current. Methane and other gases with a lot of hydrogen can also be used in a fuel cell.
* Hydrogen is flammable and expensive, which makes fuel cells not that common to use
