## Reactions with NH3

Reactions with $NH_{3}$ - deprotonation: $NH_{3}$  removed $H^{+}$ ions from the water ligands to make $H_{2}O$, forming $NH_{4}^{+}$ ions. The two water molecules that lost $H^{+}$ are now $OH^{-}$ ligands. Or ligand exchange where the $H_{2}O$ ligands are replaced by $NH_{3}$ ligands, releasing $OH^{-}$ and $H_{2}O$ ligands
