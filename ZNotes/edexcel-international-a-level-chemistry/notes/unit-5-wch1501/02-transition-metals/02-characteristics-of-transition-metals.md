## Characteristics of Transition Metals

* Hard solids with high melting/boiling temperatures
* Can act as catalysts
* Forms coloured ions and compounds
* Forms stable ions with different oxidation numbers and incompletely filled d-orbitals
* Zinc and Scandinadium are not transition metals as they cannot form ions with completely filled d-orbitals (e.g. $Zn^{2+}$, $Sc^{3+}$ )
* Ligands form complexes with complex ions
* Number of dative covalent bonds around the metal ion is the coordination number.
* Transition metals with high oxidation numbers are uncommon, and they have an electronegative element attached to the metal ion, e.g. oxygen
