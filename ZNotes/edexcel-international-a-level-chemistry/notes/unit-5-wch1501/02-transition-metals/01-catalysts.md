# Transition Metals

## Catalysts

* Adsorption: reactants become attached to the surface of the heterogeneous catalyst and the reaction occurs
* Desorption: products become detached from the surface of the catalyst in heterogeneous catalysts
* Heterogeneous Catalyst: Catalyst in a different state to the reactants
* Homogeneous Catalyst: Catalyst in the same state as the reactants
* Autocatalysis: when a reaction product acts as its own catalyst for the reaction

$S_{2}O_{8}^{{2-}(aq}) + 2I^{-}(aq) \rightarrow 2SO_{4}^{{2-}(aq}) + I_{2} \text{ uses an } Fe^{2+}/Fe^ \text{ catalyst}$
