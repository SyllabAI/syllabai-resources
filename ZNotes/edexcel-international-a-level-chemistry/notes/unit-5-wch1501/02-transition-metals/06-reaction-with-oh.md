## Reaction with OH

Reactions with $OH^{-}$ - deprotonation: $OH^{-}$ ions removed $H^{+}$ ions from the water ligands to make $H_{2}O$. The two water molecules that lost $H^{+}$ are now $OH^{-}$ ligands.


|Ligand|Product|Colour change|
|:---|:---|:---|
|$[Cu(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Cu(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Blue solution → Blue precipitate|
|$[Cr(H_2O)_6]^{3+}$|$[Cr(H_2O)_3(OH)_3] + 3H_2O$; with excess $OH^-$: $[Cr(OH)_6]^{3-}$|Green solution → Green precipitate → Green solution|
|$[Co(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Co(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Pink solution → Blue precipitate → Pink precipitate (upon standing)|
|$[Fe(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Fe(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Pale green solution → Pale green ppt → Brown precipitate (as $Fe^{2+}→Fe^{3+}$)|
|$[Fe(H_2O)_6]^{3+}$|$[Fe(H_2O)_3(OH)_3] + 3H_2O$|Yellow-brown solution → Brown precipitate|
|$[Mn(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Mn(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Pale pink solution → Pale brown ppt → Dark brown precipitate  (via $[Mn(H_2O)_3(OH)_3]$ then $MnO_2 \cdot xH_2O$)|
|$[Ni(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Ni(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Green solution → Green precipitate|
|$[Zn(H_2O)_6]^{2+}$|$2H_2O + [Zn(H_2O)_4(OH)_2]$|Colourless solution → White ppt → Colourless solution (in excess $OH^-$)|
