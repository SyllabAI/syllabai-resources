## Ligands

* Monodentate ligands use one lone pair of electrons on one atom to form the dative bond with the metal ion, e.g. $H_{2}O$, $OH^{-}$, $NH_{3}$
* Bidentate ligands use 2 lone pairs of electrons to form dative covalent bonds with the metal ion, e.g. $NH_{2}CH_{2}CH_{2}NH_{2}$, 1,2-diaminoethane, (en), has 2 lone pairs of electrons on the nitrogen atoms that can attach to one metal ion.
* $EDTA^{4-}$ is a hexadentate ligand as it has 6 lone pairs that form 6 dative covalent bonds with one metal ion. The lone pairs are on the nitrogen atoms and the $COO^{-}$ ions. These bonds are depicted using dashed lines.
