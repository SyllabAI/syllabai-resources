# Formulae, Equations and Amount of Substance

## Atoms, Elements, and Molecules

**Element:**

* Chemically, the simplest substances that cannot be broken down using chemical reactions

**Atom:**

* The smallest part of an element that has the properties of that element

**Molecule:**

* A particle made of two or more atoms bonded together

**Molecule of an element:**

* A molecule that contains atoms of the same element

**Molecules of a compound:**

* A molecule that contains atoms of two or more different elements

**Compound:**

* a substance containing atoms of different elements combined together

**Ion**:

* A species consisting of one or more atoms joined together and having a positive or negative charge

**Monoatomic ion:**

* Ions that are made up of single atoms

**Diatomic ion:**

* Ions that are made up of 2 atoms

**Polyatomic ions:**

* Ions that are made up of 3 or more atoms

**Empirical formula:**

* The simplest whole-number ratio that shows the number of atoms present in a compound

**Molecular formula:**

* Shows the actual number of each type of atom in a molecule

**Parts per million:**

* Parts per million is a measure of the number of parts of solute in one million parts of solution
