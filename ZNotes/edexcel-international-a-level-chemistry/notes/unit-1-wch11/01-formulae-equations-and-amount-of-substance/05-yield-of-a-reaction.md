## Yield of a Reaction

### **Theoretical Yield**

* theoretical field is calculated using the equation for the reaction
* It is assumed that the reaction always go to completion with no losses

### **Actual Yield**

* the actual yield is found by weighing the product after a reaction

### **Percentage Yield**

* percentage yield is the percentage of actual yield that is obtained from the reaction compared to it’s theoretical yield
* $\frac{\text{actual yield} \times\ 100}{\text{theoretical yield}} = \text{Percentage yield}$
