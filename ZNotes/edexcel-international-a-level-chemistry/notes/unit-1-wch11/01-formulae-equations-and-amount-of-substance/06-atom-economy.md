## Atom Economy

* Atom economy is another method of comparing chemical reactions to one another
* A higher atom economy would mean that a larger number of atoms from the reactants end up in the desired product
* A lower atom economy would mean that a lower number of atoms from the reactants end up in the desired product
* If a reaction involves the addition of 2 reactants to form 1 product, which is the desired product, it has 100% atom economy
* Atom economy is calculated through the formula
  * $\text{Atom economy} = \frac{\text{molar mass of the desired product}}{\text{sum of the molar masses of all products}} \times\ 100$
* **Example:**
  * $Ca_3(PO_4)_2 + 3H_2SO_4 → 2H_3PO_4 \text{(desired product)} + 3CaSO_4$
  * this reaction has an atom economy of $\frac{(98.0\ \times\ 2)\ \times\ 100}{(98.0\ \times\ 2)\ +\ (136.2\ \times\ 3)}\ =\ 32.4%$
