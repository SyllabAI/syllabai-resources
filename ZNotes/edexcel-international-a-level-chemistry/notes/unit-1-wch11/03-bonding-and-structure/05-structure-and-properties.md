## Structure and Properties

The table below gives information about how the properties of a material depends on its structure:

|    | Giant Ionic | Giant Covalent | Giant Metallic | Simple Molecular |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| Bonds Present | Strong Ionic Bonds | Strong Covalent Bonds | Strong Metallic Bonds | Weak Covalent Bonds |
| Melting/Boiling Points | High | Very High | Fairly High | Low |
| Hardness | Hard but brittle | Very Hard | Hard but malleable | Soft |
| Electrical Conductivity | Conducts in molten or aqueous states | Non-conductor | Conductor | Non-conductor |
| Solubility in water | Often soluble | Insoluble | Insoluble | Generally insoluble |
| Solubility in non-polar solvents | Insoluble | Insoluble | Insoluble | Usually soluble |
