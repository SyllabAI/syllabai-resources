## Dealing With Polymer Waste

* Polymers are lightweight, unreactive, cheap and can be manufactured on a large scale. This is what makes them desirable.
* Incineration (burning) is one of the methods to get rid of polymer waste.
* Incineration, though widely used, leads to the release of toxic and greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide that contribute to global warming.
* Due to the increased awareness of the harmful effects of polymers, scientists are developing biodegradable polymers that can be broken down by microbes.
