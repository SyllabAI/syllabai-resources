## Chemical Reactions

Organic compounds undergo addition, oxidation, reduction and substitution reactions.

**Addition Reactions:**

* When two reactants come together to form a single product.
  * $C_{2}​H_{4}​ + Br_{2}​ \rightarrow C_{2}H_{4}B𝑟_{2}$
  * General equation: A + B $\rightarrow$ C
  * Alkanes do not undergo addition reactions due to the absence of multiple bonds in their structure.
* Addition polymerisation is a type of reaction where large numbers of monomers (reactants) form a polymer (product).

**Oxidation Reactions:**

* Organic compounds can be oxidised (gain oxygen or lose hydrogen) by reacting with inorganic reagents like certain oxidising agents [O].
* Common oxidising agents are $K_{2}Cr_{2}O_{7}$ and $KMnO_{4}$.
* Combustion of alkanes is an example of an oxidation reaction.

**Reduction Reactions:**

* Reduction is the opposite of oxidation.
* $C_{2}H_{4} + H_{2}​ \rightarrow C_{2}H_{6}$
* Alkanes do not usually undergo reduction reactions.
* Substitution Reactions:
  * When two reactants come together to form two products (by displacement).
  * $CH_{4} + Cl_{2} \rightarrow CH_{3}Cl + HCl$
  * Alkanes undergo **free radical substitution** [type: substitution, mechanism: free radical].
