## ΔG

* ΔG is an indicator of reaction feasibility, and the equation determines it: $\Delta G = -RT lnK$, where R is the gas constant, T is the temperature in Kelvins, and K is the equilibrium constant.
* When ΔG is less than or equal to 0, the reaction is feasible when the equilibrium constant is large.
* $\Delta S_{\text{total}} = RlnK$
