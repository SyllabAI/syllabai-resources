# Acid-Base Equilibria

## Key Terms

* Bronsted-Lowry Base: A proton acceptor
* Bronsted-Lowry Acid: A proton donor
* Strong acid: An Acid that completely dissociates in solution to form a high concentration of  $H^{+}$ ions, which gives a high pH (of 0-1)
* Weak acid: An acid that does not completely dissociate in solution; it forms a lower concentration of $H^{+}$ ions (pH 3-7)
* Weak base: A Base that does not completely dissociate in solution, forming a pH of 7-11
* Strong base: A base that completely dissociates in solution, forming a pH of 12-14
* Conjugate base: formed when an acid donates a proton
* Conjugate acid: formed when a base accepts a proton; these both include conjugate acid-base pairs
