# Kinetics

## Rate of Reactions

* **Rate of Reactions** could be expressed in **two** ways:

Rate =$\frac{\text{change in concentration of products}}{\text{time}} = \frac{\text{d[products]}}{\text{dt}}$

Or:

Rate =$\frac{\mathbf{-}\ \text{change in concentration of reactants}}{\text{time}} = \frac{- \text{d[reactants]}}{\text{dt}}$

* Note that if you want to look at the change in [reactants] to calculate the rate of reactions, remember to include **‘minus’** in
  your calculation.

* Unit of rate of reaction: mol dm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$
