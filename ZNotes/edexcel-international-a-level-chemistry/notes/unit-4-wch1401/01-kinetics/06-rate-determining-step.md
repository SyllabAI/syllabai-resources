## Rate Determining Step

* The overall rate of a reaction is determined by the rate-determining step, which is the slowest step in a multi-step reaction:
* The rate-determining step can be used to write the rate equation and to predict the mechanism for the reaction

$K = \frac{Ae^{-Ea}}{RT}$ / $lnk = lnA - \frac{Ea}{RT}$

* K is the rate constant, R is the gas constant, T is the temperature in Kelvins, A is the Arrhenius constant, and Ea is the activation energy.
* The Ink form of the equation can be used to plot a graph of y = mx + c so the activation energy can be found.
