## Rate Equations

A + B -> C

Rate = $k[A]^{m}[B]^{n}$

* k is the rate constant, m and n are the orders of the reaction
* [A] is the concentration of A
* The total order of the reaction is m + n
* The order of reaction can be zero order, first order or second order
* K has units that can be calculated by cancelling: 

$K = \frac{\text{rate}}{[A]^{m}[B]^{n}}$

* [A] and [B] are in $mol$ $dm^{-3}$ and rate is in $mol dm^{-3} s^{-1}$
* Solids and liquids are not included in the rate equation. Gaseous and aqueous substances are included in the rate equation.
