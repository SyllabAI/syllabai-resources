## The Equilibrium Constant of Water (Kw)

* $K_{w} = [OH^{-}][H^{+}] = 1 \times 10^{-14}$ at 25 degrees Celsius
* $H_{2}O \rightarrow H^{+} + OH^{-}$
* As temperature increases, the equilibrium will favour the forward reaction as it is endothermic, and water will become more acidic as the concentration of $H^{+}$ ions will increase
* $pK_{w} = -logK_{w}$
* $K_{w} = 10^{-pK_{w}}$
* pH of a strong base can be calculated using the concentration of the $OH^{-}$ ions and $K_{w}$ to find the [$H^{+}$] ions
