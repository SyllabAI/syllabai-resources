## Defintions to know for Born-Haber Cycle

* **Lattice Formation Enthalpy ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{lat}}$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of a solid ionic compound** is formed from its gaseous ions under standard conditio*Example*: $\text{Na}*{(s)} + \frac{1}{2}\text{Cl}* \rightarrow \text{NaCl}_{(s)}$ (exothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{Na}^+*{(g)} + \text{Cl}^-* \rightarrow \text{NaCl}_{(s)}$

* **Lattice Dissociation Enthalpy ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{lat diss}}$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of a solid ionic compound** is separated into gaseous ions under standard conditions (endothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{NaCl}*{(s)} \rightarrow \text{Na}^+* + \text{Cl}^-_{(g)}$

* **Enthalpy of Atomisation ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{atom}}$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of gaseous atoms** is formed from an element in its standard state (endothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{Na}*{(s)} \rightarrow \text{Na}*$ 

* **First Ionisation Energy ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{I}}$)**: The energy required to remove **one mole of electrons** from **one mole of gaseous atoms** (endothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{Na}*{(g)} \rightarrow \text{Na}^+* + \text{e}^-$ 

* **Bond Dissociation Enthalpy ($B.E$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of bonds is broken** in the gaseous state (endothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{Cl}*{2(g)} \rightarrow 2\text{Cl}*$ — *This is double the atomisation enthalpy of chlorine*.

* **First Electron Affinity ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{ea}}$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of electrons** is added to one mole of gaseous atoms (exothermic).

  * *Example*: $\text{Cl}*{(g)} + \text{e}^- \rightarrow \text{Cl}^-*$ *Note*: The **second electron affinity** is always **endothermic** because energy is required to overcome the repulsion between the incoming electron and the negatively charged ion.

* **Standard Enthalpy Change of Formation ($\Delta H^\ominus_{\text{f}}$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of a compound** is formed from its elements in their standard states under standard conditions.

  * *Example*: $\text{Na}*{(s)} + \frac{1}{2}\text{Cl}* \rightarrow \text{NaCl}_{(s)}$
