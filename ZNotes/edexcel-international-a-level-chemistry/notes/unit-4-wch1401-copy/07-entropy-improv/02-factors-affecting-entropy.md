## Factors Affecting Entropy

* **Physical States**:

  * Solid \< Liquid \< Gas

  * Gas particles are free to move randomly throughout a large volume resulting in significantly higher entropy than solids or liquids.

* **Temperature**:

  * Higher temperatures lead to higher entropy because particles have more kinetic energy allowing energy to be more dispersed.

* **Number of Particles (Moles)**:

  * An increase in the total number of moles during a reaction increases the number of possible microstates, thus increasing entropy.
