# Entropy and Energenetics

## Introduction to Entropy:

* **Definition**: Entropy is a measure of the dispersal of particles and energy among particles within a system. It is frequently interpreted as a measure of the disorder or randomness of a system

* **Symbol**: S

* **Units**: $\text{J K}^{-1} \text{ mol}^{-1}$

* **Microstates:**

  * A **microstate** is a possible arrangement of particles in a system.

  * The greater the number of possible microstates, the greater the entropy
