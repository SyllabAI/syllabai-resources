## Lattice Enthalpy ($\Delta_{\text{latt}}H^\circ$)

* **Definition**

  * The enthalpy change when **one mole of an ionic compound** is formed from its gaseous ions under standard conditions

  * *Equation*: $\text{M}^+*{(g)} + \text{X}^-* \rightarrow \text{MX}_{(s)}$ (Always **exothermic**, values are negative)

* Factors Affecting Lattice Enthalpy:

  * **Ionic Radius**: A smaller ion has ahigher charge densitiy. Leading to stronger electrostatic forces of attraction and **more exothermic** lattice enthalpies.

  * **Ionic Charge**: Higher charges result in stronger electrostatic attraction, making the lattice enthalpy **significantly more exothermic**.
