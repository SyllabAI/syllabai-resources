## Enthalpy Changes of Solution & Hydration

* **Enthalpy of Solution ($\Delta_{\text{sol}}H^\ominus$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of solute** dissolves completely in water under standard conditions.

* **Enthalpy of Hydration ($\Delta_{\text{hyd}}H^\ominus$)**: The enthalpy change when **one mole of gaseous ions** dissolves in water to form aqueous ions (always **exothermic**).

* Formula

  * $$\Delta_{\text{sol}}H^\ominus = \Delta H^\ominus_{\text{lat diss}} + \sum \Delta_{\text{hyd}}H^\ominus$$
