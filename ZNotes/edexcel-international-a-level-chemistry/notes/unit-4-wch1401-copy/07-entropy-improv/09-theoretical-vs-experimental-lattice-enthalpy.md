
## Theoretical vs. Experimental Lattice Enthalpy

* **Theoretical Model** assumes:

  * The ions are in contact

  * The ions are perfect spheres

  * The ions are interacting purely through electrostatic forces.

* **Experimental Value**: Calculated directly from a Born-Haber cycle.

* **Polarisation & Covalent Character (Fajans' Rules)**:

  * If the experimental value is **more exothermic** than the theoretical value, the ionic compound has **covalent character**.

  * Polarization occurs when a small, highly charged cation warps the electron cloud of a large, polarisable anion.

    * **Small cation** size with a **high charge** $\rightarrow$ high charge density $\rightarrow$ high polarising power.

    * **Large anion** size with a **high charge** $\rightarrow$ highly polarisable.
