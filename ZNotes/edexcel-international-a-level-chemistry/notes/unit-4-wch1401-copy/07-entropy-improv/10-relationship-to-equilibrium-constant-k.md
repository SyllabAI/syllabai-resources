## Relationship to Equilibrium Constant (K)

* For reversible processes, the total entropy change is linked directly to the equilibrium constant (K) and the gas constant.

  * $$\Delta S_{\text{total}} = R \ln K$$

* **Implications**:

  * If $\Delta S_{\text{total}} > 0$, then $K > 1$ (products are favoured).

  * If $\Delta S_{\text{total}} < 0$, then $K < 1$ (reactants are favoured).
