
## Born-Haber Cycle

* **Definition**: a thermochemical energy diagram that applies **Hess’s Law** to show the formation of an ionic compound from its constituent elements through a series of individual steps

* **Direction Rules**:

  * **Upward arrows**: Endothermic steps (energy put into the system, e.g., atomisation, ionisation, bond breaking).

  * **Downward arrows**: Exothermic steps (energy released, e.g., formation, first electron affinity, lattice formation).

* **Key Relationship**:

  * $$\Delta_f H^\ominus = \sum \Delta_{\text{atom}}H^\ominus + \sum \text{IE} + \sum \text{EA} + \Delta_{\text{lat}}H^\ominus$$
