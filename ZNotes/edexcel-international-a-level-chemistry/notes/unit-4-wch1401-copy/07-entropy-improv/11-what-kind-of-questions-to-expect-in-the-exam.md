## What kind of questions to expect in the exam?

* Predict sign of $\Delta S_{\text{system}}$ from physical states/moles of gas.

* Calculate $\Delta S_{\text{system}}$, $\Delta S_{\text{surroundings}}$, and $\Delta S_{\text{total}}$.

* Find temperature at which a reaction becomes feasible.

* Link $\Delta S_{\text{total}}$ to $K$ using $\Delta S_{\text{total}} = R \ln K$.

* Construct complete Born-Haber cycles (including $1^{\text{st}}$/$2^{\text{nd}}$ IE, EA, Atomisation, Lattice).

* Explain ionic vs. covalent character using Fajans' rules.

* Calculate $\Delta_{\text{sol}}H$ using Lattice Dissociation enthalpy and Hydration enthalpies.
