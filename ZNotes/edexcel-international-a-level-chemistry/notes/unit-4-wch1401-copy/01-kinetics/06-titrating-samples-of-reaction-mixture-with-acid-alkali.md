## Titrating samples of reaction mixture with acid/alkali

* Aliquots (samples) of the reaction mixture are withdrawn at specific time intervals

* The they will be rapidly cooled or diluted ("quenched") to arrest the reaction

* Then titrated to determine the remaining concentration of a reactant (e.g., acid or iodine).

* *++Example:++*

* $$\mathrm{HCOOCH_3(aq) + NaOH(aq) \rightarrow HCOONa(aq) + CH_3OH(aq)}$$

  * (The $\mathrm{NaOH}$ could be titrated with an acid)
