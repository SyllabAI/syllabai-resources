## Rate equation:

* Definition: The **rate equation** expresses the mathematical relationship between the rate of a chemical reaction and the concentrations of the reactants.

* For a general reaction: $A + B \rightarrow \text{Products}$

  * The equation would be $$\text{Rate} = k[A]^m[B]^n$$

  * **$[A]$ and $[B]$**: Concentrations of reactants in $\text{mol dm}^{-3}$.

  * **$m$ and $n$**: **Orders of reaction** with respect to reactants $A$ and $B$.

  * **$k$**: **Rate constant** (constant for a given reaction at a specific temperature).
