## Measurement of change of mass

* This is suitable for reactions generating a dense gas (such as $\text{CO}_2$) that escapes from an open system.

* The reduction in total mass is monitored continuously using a precision balance.

* A good example for this would be the reaction: $$\mathrm{HCOOH(aq) + Br_2(aq) \rightarrow 2H^+(aq) + 2Br^-(aq) + CO_2(g)}$$
