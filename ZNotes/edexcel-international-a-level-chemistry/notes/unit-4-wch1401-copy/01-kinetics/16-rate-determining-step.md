## Rate determining Step:

*++Definition:++* In a multi step reaction mechanism, the **Rate-Determining Step (RDS)** is the **slowest individual step** in the sequence.

* Because a reaction cannot proceed faster than its slowest step, the overall reaction rate is limited entirely by the RDS.

* $$\mathrm{Overall\ Reaction\ Rate} = \text{Rate of the Slowest Step (RDS)}$$
