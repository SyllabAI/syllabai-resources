## Case Study: Acid-Catalysed Iodination of Propanone

* Chemical equation:

  * $$\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3(aq) + I_2(aq) \xrightarrow{H^+} CH_3COCH_2I(aq) + H^+(aq) + I^-(aq)}$$

* Experimental Rate Equation:

  * $$\text{Rate} = k\mathrm{[CH_3COCH_3(aq)][H^+(aq)]}$$

* Deductions:

  * 1. $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$ and $\mathrm{H^+}$ are involved in or before the **RDS** (Order = $1$).

    2. $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$ and $\mathrm{H^+}$ are involved in or before the **RDS** (Order = $1$).

    3. $\mathrm{H^+}$ acts as a catalyst because it appears in the rate equation but is regenerated in the overall reaction.
