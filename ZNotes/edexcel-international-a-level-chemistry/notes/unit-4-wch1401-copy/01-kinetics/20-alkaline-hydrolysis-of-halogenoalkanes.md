## Alkaline Hydrolysis of Halogenoalkanes

*++Definition:++* Alkaline hydrolysis of a halogenoalkane involves nucleophilic substitution using an aqueous hydroxide ion (e.g., $\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}$ or $\mathrm{KOH(aq)}$) under reflux to produce an alcohol and a halide ion.

* $$\mathrm{R-X(aq) + OH^-(aq) \rightarrow R-OH(aq) + X^-(aq)}$$
  * (where $\mathrm{X = Cl, Br, I}$)
