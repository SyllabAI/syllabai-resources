* There is a way to calculate the Unit of K using the formula

*++Example++*:

* If r = k\[A\] then u could use the formula $$k = \frac{r}{[A]}$$ which would be $$\frac{\mathrm{mol \cdot dm^{-3} \cdot s^{-1}}}{\mathrm{mol \cdot dm^{-3}}} = \mathrm{s^{-1}}$$
  * This might seem unorthodox at first but after focusing and using basic power rules you learned in maths this will become much clearer
