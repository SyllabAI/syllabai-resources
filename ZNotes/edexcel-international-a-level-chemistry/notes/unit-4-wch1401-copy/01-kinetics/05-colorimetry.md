## Colorimetry:

* Tracks changes in light absorbance over time if a reactant or product undergoes a distinct color change (e.g., the depletion or formation of aqueous iodine, $\text{I}_2$)

* Example: $$\mathrm{H_2O_2(aq) + 2I^-(aq) + 2H^+(aq) \rightarrow 2H_2O(l) + I_2(aq)}$$

  * (The $\mathrm{I_2}$ produced is a brown solution)
