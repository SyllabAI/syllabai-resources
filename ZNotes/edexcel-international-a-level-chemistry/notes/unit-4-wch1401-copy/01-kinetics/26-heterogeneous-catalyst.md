## Heterogeneous Catalyst:

* The catalyst and reactants are in **different phases**

* There are 3 different stages in this reaction

  1. **Adsorption:** Reactant molecules form weak bonds with active sites on the solid catalyst surface, weakening internal bonds.

  2. **Reaction:** Weakened bonds allow reaction to proceed with lower $E_a$.

  3. **Desorption:** Product molecules leave the surface active sites free for new reactant molecules.

*++Example:++*

* $$\mathrm{2SO_{2(g)} + O_{2(g)} \xrightarrow[\text{(s)}]{V_2O_5} 2SO_{3(g)}}$$
