## Sn1 mechanism

* The intermediate carbocation is **trigonal planar** around the central carbon.

* Occurs in **two separate steps**.

* The nucleophile can attack with **equal probability from above or below** the plane.

* This forms equal amounts (50:50) of both optical enantiomers, resulting in a **racemic mixture** (which has **no net effect** on plane-polarized light).

* Rate=*k*\[R−X\] as the $[\mathrm{OH^-}]$ is not involved in the slow step (1st order overall)
