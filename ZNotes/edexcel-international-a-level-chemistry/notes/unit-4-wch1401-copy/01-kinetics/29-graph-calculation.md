## Graph calculation

* The value of k is only affected by the temperature

* ### $$\frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{\ln k_2 - \ln k_1}{\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}} = -\frac{E_a}{R}$$

  ### $$\therefore \frac{\ln\left\(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right\)}{\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}} = -\frac{E_a}{R}$$

  ### $$\therefore \ln\left\(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right\) = -\frac{E_a}{R} \left\( \frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1} \right\)$$

  * **Application:** This equation is used to calculate the $E_a$ at two different temperatures and two different $k$ values.
