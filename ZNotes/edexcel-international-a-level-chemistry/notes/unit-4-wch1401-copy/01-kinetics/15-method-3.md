* ## Method 3: Rate/Concentration Graphs:

  * **Zero Order:** Horizontal line ($\text{Rate}$ is independent of $[A]$).

  * **First Order:** Straight line through the origin ($\text{Gradient} = k$).

  * **Second Order:** Parabolic curve upwards ($\text{Rate} \propto [A]^2$). Plotting $\text{Rate}$ vs $[A]^2$ gives a straight line through the origin.
