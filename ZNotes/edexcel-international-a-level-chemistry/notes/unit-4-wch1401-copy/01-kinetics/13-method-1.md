## Method 1: Initial Rates (Comparing Runs)

1. Keep the concentration of all reactants constant except one.

2. Observe how changing that concentration alters the initial rate.

   * If rate is unchanged $\rightarrow$ **0th order**

   * If rate increases by the same factor $\rightarrow$ **1st order**

   * If rate increases by the factor squared $\rightarrow$ **2nd order**
