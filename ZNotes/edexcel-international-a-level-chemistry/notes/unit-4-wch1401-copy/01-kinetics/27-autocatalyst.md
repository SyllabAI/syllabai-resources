## Autocatalyst:

* In Autocatalysis reactions, the reaction catalysed by one of the products.

*++Example:++* oxidation of ethanedioic acid by acidified potassium manganate (VII):

* $$\mathrm{5 \underset{(aq)}{\begin{matrix} COOH \\\ \vert{} \\\ COOH \end{matrix}} + 2 \underset{(aq)}{MnO_4^-} + 6H^+ \rightarrow 2Mn^{2+} + 10CO_2 + 8H_2O}$$

* This reaction is slow at room temperature, it is slow in the beginning and when $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ is formed, the rate of reaction becomes fast as $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ behave as a catalyst for the reaction, this called auto catalyst.
