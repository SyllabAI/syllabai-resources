## Method 2: Concentration/Time Graphs (Continuous Monitoring)

* **Zero Order:** Straight line with a negative gradient

* **First Order:** Exponential decay curve with a **constant half life**

* **Second Order:** Steeper decay curve where the half-life **increases** progressively as concentration decreases.
