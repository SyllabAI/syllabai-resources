## Activation energy and catalysts

**Activation energy ($E_a$)** is the minimum amount of kinetic energy that colliding particles must possess in order for a chemical reaction to occur upon collision.

* ![](https://i.imgur.com/rtpXazW.png)

* **Catalyst:** "Substance increases the rate of reaction and not change chemically"

  * **How it works:** Catalyst works by providing an alternative route for the reaction which has a lower activation energy than the original route.

  * There are variuous types of catalysts and you will learn about some of them: Heterogeneous catalyst and Homogeneous catalyst
