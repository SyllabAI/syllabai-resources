## **Sn2 mechanism**

* The nucleophile attacks from the **opposite side** of the halogen atom.

* The product remains **optically active**, rotating plane-polarized light in a different direction than the reactant.

* Occurs in **one single, concerted step**.  No intermediate is formed, it passes through a single **five-coordinate transition state**.

* $1\text{st}$ order with respect to $[\mathrm{R-X}]$, $1\text{st}$ order with respect to $[\mathrm{OH^-}]$ ($2\text{nd}$ order overall)
