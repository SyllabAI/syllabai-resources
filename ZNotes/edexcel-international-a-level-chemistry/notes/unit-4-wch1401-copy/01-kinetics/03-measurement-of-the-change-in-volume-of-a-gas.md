## Measurement of the change in volume of a gas:

* Used when **a gaseous product** is evolved (e.g., $\text{H}_2$ or $\text{O}_2$).

* The volume of gas produced is recorded **at regular time intervals** to calculate the initial or continuous rate.

* Plot a graph for the volume of gases collected (y-axis) against time(x-axis) and draw a **tangent** to determine the reaction rate **at certain times**.

  ![](https://i.imgur.com/R7T74Gk.png =289x289)

* A good example would be using this in the reaction $$\mathrm{HCOOH(aq) + Br_2(aq) \rightarrow 2H^+(aq) + 2Br^-(aq) + CO_2(g)}$$

  As there is a change in the volume of gases involved in this reaction
