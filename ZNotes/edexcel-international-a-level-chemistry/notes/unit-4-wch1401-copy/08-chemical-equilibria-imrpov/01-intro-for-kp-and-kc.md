# **Chemical Equilibria**

## Introduction for Kc and Kp

* **$K_c$ (Concentration Equilibrium Constant)**: Writing expressions for reactions in solution or gas phase using molar concentrations and deriving their units.

* **$K_p$ (Gas-Phase Equilibrium Constant)**: Writing expressions using partial pressures.

* Pure solids ($\text{s}$) and pure liquids ($\text{l}$) are excluded from $K_{c}$ and $K_{p}$ expressions.
