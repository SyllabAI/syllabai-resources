## Example for calculating Units of $K_c$

* Example 1:

  * **Reaction:** $\text{H}_{2(g)} + \text{I} \rightleftharpoons 2\text{HI}$

    1. Write the $K_c$ expression:

       * $$K_c = \frac{[\text{HI}]^2}{[\text{H}_2][\text{I}_2]}$$

    2. Substitute units ($\text{mol dm}^{-3}$) into the expression:

       * $$K_c = \frac{(\text{mol dm}^{-3})^2}{(\text{mol dm}^{-3})(\text{mol dm}^{-3})}$$

    3. Simplify:

       The top expands to $(\text{mol dm}^{-3})^2$ and the bottom multiplies to $(\text{mol dm}^{-3})^2$. They cancel each other out completely.

       * **Unit:** **++No units++**

* Example 2:

  * **Reaction:** $\text{N}_{2}O \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NO}$

    Write the $K_c$ expression:

    1. * $$K_c = \frac{[\text{NO}_2]^2}{[\text{N}_2\text{O}_4]}$$

    2. Substitute units:

       * $$K_c = \frac{(\text{mol dm}^{-3})^2}{(\text{mol dm}^{-3})}$$

    3. Simplify:

       One $(\text{mol dm}^{-3})$ from the bottom cancels out one from the top, leaving one on top.

       * **Unit:** ++$\text{mol dm}^{-3}$++
