## Linking Equilibrium Constant ($K$) to Total Entropy ($\Delta S_{\text{total}}$)

* The equilibrium constant is mathematically tied to the total entropy change of the system via the gas constant (R) and temperature (T in Kelvin):

  * $$\Delta S_{\text{total}} = R \ln K$$

* **Key Interpretations**:

  * If **$\Delta S_{\text{total}} > 0$**, $\ln K$ is positive $\rightarrow$ **$K > 1$** (products are heavily favored at equilibrium).

  * If **$\Delta S_{\text{total}} < 0$**, $\ln K$ is negative $\rightarrow$ **$K < 1$** (reactants are heavily favored at equilibrium).
