* Detailed Rules for Temperature & $K$:

  * **Exothermic forward reaction ($\Delta H$ is negative)**:

    * Increasing temperature shifts equilibrium to the **left** $\rightarrow$ **$K$ decreases**.

    * Decreasing temperature shifts equilibrium to the **right** $\rightarrow$ **$K$ increases**.

  * **Endothermic forward reaction ($\Delta H$ is positive)**:

    * Increasing temperature shifts equilibrium to the **right** $\rightarrow$ **$K$ increases**.

    * Decreasing temperature shifts equilibrium to the **left** $\rightarrow$ **$K$ decreases**.
