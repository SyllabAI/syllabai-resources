## Calculating Partial Pressures for $K_p$

* To find the partial pressure of an individual gas in a mixture, follow these two steps:

  1. **Calculate the Mole Fraction ($x$)**:

     * $$\text{Mole fraction of gas } Z = \frac{\text{Moles of gas } Z}{\text{Total moles of all gases combined}}$$

       *(Note: The sum of all mole fractions in a mixture always equals 1)*

  2. **Calculate the Partial Pressure ($p$)**:

     * $$\text{Partial Pressure } (p_Z) = \text{Mole fraction } (x_Z) \times \text{Total Pressure } (P_{\text{total}})$$
