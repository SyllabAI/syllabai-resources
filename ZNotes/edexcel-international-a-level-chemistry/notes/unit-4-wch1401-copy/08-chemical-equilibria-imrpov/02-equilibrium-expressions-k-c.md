## Equilibrium Expressions ($K_c$ )

* For any reversible reaction at equilibrium:

  * $$aA + bB \rightleftharpoons cC + dD$$

* **The Golden Rule**: **Products always go on top, reactants always go on the bottom.** The stoichiometric balancing numbers become the **powers (indices)**.

* **$K_c$ (Concentration Equilibrium Constant)**:

* Uses molar concentrations measured in $\text{mol dm}^{-3}$,

* Formula:

  * ### $$K_c = \frac{[C]^c [D]^d}{[A]^a [B]^b}$$
