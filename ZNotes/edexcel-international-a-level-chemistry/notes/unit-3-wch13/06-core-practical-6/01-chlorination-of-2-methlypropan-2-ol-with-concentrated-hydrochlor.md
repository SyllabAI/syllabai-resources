# Chlorination of 2-methlypropan-2-ol with Concentrated Hydrochloric Acid

**Method**:

* Measure 8 $cm^3$ of 2-methylpropan-2-ol in a measuring cylinder and measure its mass.
* Pour the 2-methylpropan-2-ol into a separating funnel, and reweigh the measuring cylinder to find
  the mass of the 2-methylpropan-2-ol used.
* Using a fume cupboard, add 20 $cm^3$ of concentrated hydrochloric acid to the separating funnel, in portions of 3 $cm^3$.
* After each portion, stopper the flask and invert it several times. Open the tap when doing this to release the pressure.
* Allow the separating funnel to stand in the fume cupboard for about 20 minutes. Gently shake it at intervals.
* After 20 minutes, allow the layers to separate in the funnel. Open the tap and remove the lower aqueous layer. Dispose of this layer.
* Add sodium hydrogencarbonate solution in 2 $cm^3$ portions to the separating funnel (This neutralises any remaining acid).
* Shake the funnel after each addition to release the pressure. Continue this until no more bubbles of $CO_{2}$ are seen.
* Allow the layers to separate in the funnel. Again, remove and pour away the lower aqueous layer.
* Run off the organic layer into a clean conical flask and add two spatulas of anhydrous sodium sulfate. Stopper the flask, shake the contents and allow this to stand until the liquid becomes clear (This step dries the organic liquid).
* Decant the liquid into a weighed clean distillation flask.
* Distil the liquid by holding a 250ml beaker half-full of boiled water around the flask using standard distillation set up. Collect the liquid that distils in the range 47-53$^o$C.
* Measure the mass of the 2-chloro-2-methylpropane collected.
