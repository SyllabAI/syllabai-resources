# Moles, Yield and Percentage

## Moles, Concentration, and Volume

* **Moles** = $\frac{\text{Mass}}{M_{r} }$   (where $M_{r}$ is the relative molecular mass)
* **Moles** = c × v    (where c is the concentration and v is the volume)
  * c and v should be in the same units, usually in $dm^{-3}$
  * Volume is usually given in $cm^{-3}$
  * Conversion Factor: 1 $dm^{-3}$ = 1000 $cm^{-3}$
