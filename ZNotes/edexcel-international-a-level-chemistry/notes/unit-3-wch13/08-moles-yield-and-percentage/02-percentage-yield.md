## Percentage Yield

* The yield of a chemical reaction is the quantity of product obtained.
* Yield can be theoretical or actual.
  * The maximum amount of product that can be produced is the theoretical yield. This is determined through stoichiometric calculations.
  * The actual amount of product that is obtained is the actual yield. The actual yield is generally lesser than the theoretical yield.
* The percentage yield of a chemical reaction can be determined using:

$$

\frac{\text{Actual Yield}}{\text{Theoretical Yield}} \times 100

$$

This formula provides information about the efficiency of the reaction.
