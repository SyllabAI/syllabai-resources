## Tests For Gases

The table below shows the tests and positive results for gases:

| Gas | Test | Positive Result |
|----|----|----|
| Hydrogen | Lit splint | Burns with a pop sound |
| Oxygen | Glowing splint | Is relighted |
| Carbon Dioxide | Bubble through limewater | Turns milky |
| Ammonia | 1. Damp red litmus paper | Turns blue |
|    | 2. Concentrated HCL | White smoke (of ammonium chloride) |
| Hydrogen Halides: HCl, HBr, HI | Concentrated ammonia | White smoke |
