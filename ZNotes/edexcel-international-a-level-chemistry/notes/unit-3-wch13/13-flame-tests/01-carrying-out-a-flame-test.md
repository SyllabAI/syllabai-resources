# Flame Tests

Flame tests are used to identify the presence of metal cations.

## Carrying Out A Flame Test

* Dip a nichrome/platinum wire in concentrated hydrochloric acid.
  * Nichrome and platinum wires have a high melting point and are inert (will not react and produce a flame colour).
  * Hydrochloric acid cleans the wire.
  * HCl also converts the metal being tested into chloride, producing better results as chlorides are more volatile.
* Dip the wire in the solid you’re testing for.
* Place the wire in a bunsen flame.
