## Calculating Uncertainties

* To calculate the uncertainty of a measurement, divide the uncertainty of the apparatus used over the measurement in question:

$$

\pm \frac{\text{Uncertainty}}{\text{Measurement}}

$$

* To calculate the percentage uncertainty, multiply your calculated uncertainty by 100.

* Here's an example:

  Calculate the percentage uncertainty of titre 3:


||Rough Titration|Trial 1|Trial 2|Trial 3|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|Initial burette reading/cm^3|0.50|1.50|2.50|0.65|
|Final burette reading/cm^3|26.05|22.9|24.0|25.0|
|Titre/cm^3|25.55|21.4|21.5|24.35|

* $\frac{0.05 \times 2}{24.35} = 0.0041$
* Multiplying this with 100 will give 41.1%, which is the percentage uncertainty in measuring 24.35 cm$^{3}$
  * The uncertainty of the burette is multiplied with 2 because the mean titre is a result of **both** initial and final burette readings, each having an uncertainty of 0.05 cm$^{3}$
