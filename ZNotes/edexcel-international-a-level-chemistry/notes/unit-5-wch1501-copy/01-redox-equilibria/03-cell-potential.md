## Cell Potential

* Cell Potential is the more positive cell minus the more negative cell. If the overall cell potential value is positive, then the reaction is favorable and spontaneous, becoming more favorable as it becomes more positive.
* Ions in different oxidation states can also be combined to form a half cell, e.g. $Fe^{2+}$and $Fe^{3+}$ can form a half cell using the platinum electrodes as there are no solids in the reaction.
* Increasing the concentration of the solutions makes the emf more positive as there are fewer electrons in the reaction, while increasing the pressure makes the emf more negative as there are more electrons in the reaction.
* Emf and total entropy change and lnK are all directly proportional, as there is increased disorder, so a more positive entropy change and emf value.
* The reaction may be thermodynamically feasible, but it may not be kinetically feasible as the reactants may not have enough energy for the reaction to occur under standard conditions (the reaction rate is prolonged)
* Standard reduction potentials: Species that have very positive electrode potentials are good oxidising agents and will oxidise the species that have a more negative electrode potential than that one.
* Species with very negative electrode potentials are good reducing agents and will reduce the species with a more positive electrode potential than those.
