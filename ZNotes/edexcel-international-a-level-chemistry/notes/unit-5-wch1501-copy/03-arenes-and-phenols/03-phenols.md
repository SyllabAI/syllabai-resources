## Phenols

* Due to its high carbon content, benzene burns in oxygen with a smoky flame.
* Phenols can react with Bromine water via multiple substitution reactions to form a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol that smells like an antiseptic. It will decolourize the bromine water. Phenol can react with bromine water, but benzene cannot, as phenol has an -OH group that interacts with the ring of delocalized electrons, increasing the electron density and making it easier for electrophiles to attack it.
