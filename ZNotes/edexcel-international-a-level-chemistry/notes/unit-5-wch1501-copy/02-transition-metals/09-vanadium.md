## Vanadium

| Oxidation Number | Formula | Name | Colour |
|----|----|----|----|
| 2+ | $V^{2+}$ | vanadium (II) | purple |
| 3+ | $V^{3+}$ | vanadium (III) | green |
| 4+ | $VO^{2+}$ | oxovanadium (IV) | blue |
| 5+ | $VO_{2}^{+}$ | dioxovanadium (V) | yellow |

Zn can be used as a reducing agent as the $E_{cell}$ value is more negative than many of the half equations for V, so the reactions are thermodynamically feasible. Zn cannot make $V^{2+} → V$
