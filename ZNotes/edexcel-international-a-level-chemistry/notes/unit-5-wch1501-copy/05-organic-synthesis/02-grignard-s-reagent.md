## Grignard's Reagent

* Grignard’s Reagents are molecules that are RMgX, and they are prepared in dry ether.
* This is used to lengthen the carbon chain of a compound, and it is safer than using nitriles.
* When Grignard’s reagent is added to $CO_{2}$, dilute HCl, a Carboxylic acid and a halide are formed.
* When Grignard’s reagent is added to carbonyl compounds, i.e. aldehyde and ketone, and then dilute HCl is added, a primary or secondary alcohol is formed.
