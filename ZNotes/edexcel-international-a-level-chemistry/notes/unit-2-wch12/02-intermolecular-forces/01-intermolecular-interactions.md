# Intermolecular Forces

## Intermolecular Interactions

* Intermolecular forces/interactions are simply forces between molecules that do not involve sharing of electrons.
* They are relatively weaker than covalent and ionic forces.
* London forces, dipole-dipole forces, and hydrogen bonding are examples of intermolecular forces.
* Van Der Waals forces is an umbrella term for London forces and dipole-dipole forces.
* These forces explain the physical and chemical properties of molecules.
