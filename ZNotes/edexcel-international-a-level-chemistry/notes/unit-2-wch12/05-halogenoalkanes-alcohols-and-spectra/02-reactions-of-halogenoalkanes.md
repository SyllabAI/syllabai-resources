## Reactions of Halogenoalkanes

* Halogenoalkanes undergo various reactions including substitution, elimination and nucleophilic.

**Reaction with:**

* KOH (aq.) - $CH_{3}CH_{2}Cl + KOH \rightarrow CH_{3}CH_{2}OH + KCl$

  * **Condition**: heat under reflux

* KCN (ethanolic) - $CH_{3}CH_{2}Cl + KCN \rightarrow CH_{3}CH_{2}CN + KCl$

  * **Condition**: heat under reflux

* $NH_3$ (ethanolic) - $CH_{3}Cl + 2NH_{3} \rightarrow CH_{3}NH_{2} + NH_{4}Cl$

  * **Condition**: heat in a sealed tube, high temperature and pressure

* KOH (ethanolic) - $CH_{3}CH_{2}Cl + KOH \rightarrow C_{2}H_{4} + KCl + H_{2}O$

  * **Condition**: heat under reflux

* The first three reactions are nucleophilic substitution reactions, where a nucleophile (like -OH) is involved.

* The last reaction is an elimination reaction.

* When aqueous KOH is added, an alcohol is formed.

* When ethanolic KOH is added, an alkene is formed.
