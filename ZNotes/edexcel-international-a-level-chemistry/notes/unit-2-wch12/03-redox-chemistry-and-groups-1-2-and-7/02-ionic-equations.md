## Ionic Equations

$Cu + 2AgNO_{3} \rightarrow Cu(NO_{3})_{2} + 2Ag$

* The ionic equation for this is: $Cu + 2Ag^{+} \rightarrow 2Ag + Cu^{2+}$
* This ionic equation can be written from the half equations:
  * $Cu \rightarrow Cu^{2+} + 2e^{-}$

  * $Ag^{+} + e^{-} \rightarrow Ag$
* To make both sides the same, equation 2 is multiplied with 2: $2Ag^{+} + 2e^{-} \rightarrow 2Ag$
* So, $2e^{-}$ can be cancelled from both equations which will eventually give the final overall ionic equation.

* Balance the hydrogen and oxygen atoms by adding H2O and/or H+ (in acid solution).
* Balance the electric charges by adding electrons.
