## Reactions of Group 1 and 2 Elements

### **Group 1 Elements:**

1. Group 1 metals are very reactive (Li is the least reactive metal in group 1), and the reactivity increases down the group because (i) the size of metallic radii increases (ii) the shielding effect increases.
2. The reactions of group 1 metals with oxygen, chlorine, and water are extremely vigorous

**Reactant with:**

* Oxygen: $4M_{(s)} + O_{2(g)} \rightarrow  2M_{2}O_{(s)}$ (basic oxide)

* Chlorine: $2M_{(s)} + Cl_{2(g)} \rightarrow 2MCl_{(s)}$

* Water: $M_{(s)} + H_{2}O_{(l)} \rightarrow 2MOH_{(aq)} + H_{2(g)}$

### **Group 2 Elements:**

1. The reactions of group 2 elements become more vigorous down the group.

2. Magnesium with steam is a different reaction than with water:

   * $Mg_{(s)} + 2H_{2}O_{(g)} \rightarrow MgO_{(s)} + H_{2(g)}$

**Reactant with:**

* Oxygen: $2M_{(s)} + O_{2(g)} \rightarrow 2MO_{(s)}$ (basic oxide)

* Chlorine: $M_{(s)} + Cl_{2(g)} \rightarrow MCl_{2(s)}$

* Water: $M_{(s)} + 2H_{2}O_{(l)} \rightarrow M(OH)_{2(aq)} + H_{2(g)}$
