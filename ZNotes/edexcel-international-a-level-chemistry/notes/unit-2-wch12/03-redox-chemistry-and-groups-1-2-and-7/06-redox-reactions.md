## Redox Reactions

* Elements at the bottom of groups 1 and 2 and at the top of group 7 have the most vigorous reactions.
  * Reactivity of groups 1 & 2 increases down the group and decreases down the group for group 7
* Products of these reactions are usually ionic salts
* All these reactions are redox reactions where the halogen acts as an oxidising agent
  * Halogens act as oxidising agents in most reactions due to their high electronegativity
* Halogens are reduced from 0 to -1 and the metals are oxidised from 0 to +1/+2 depending on their group.

### Disproportionation of Chlorine

* Water: Cl$_2$ + H$_2$O → HClO + HCl

  * Application: Used to disinfect drinking water

* Cold Alkali: Cl$_2$ + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaClO + H$_2$O

  * Application: Active ingredient in bleach, also a disinfectant

* Hot Alkali: 3Cl$_2$ + 6NaOH → 5NaCl + NaClO $_3$+ 3H$_2$O

  * Used in bleaching, and as a weed killer
