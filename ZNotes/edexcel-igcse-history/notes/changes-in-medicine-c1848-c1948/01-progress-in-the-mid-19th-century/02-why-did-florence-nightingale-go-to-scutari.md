Florence Nightingale, a middle-class nurse, gained experience by visiting hospitals in the 1840s and underwent additional training in Germany.

Florence Nightingale and a team of nurses were sent to a military hospital in Scutari in *1847*. The hospital housed over *10,000 patients* in poor conditions with diseases like Cholera and Typhoid Fever prevalent.

Nightingale improved hospital sanitation by cleaning surfaces, washing equipment, and improving air quality. Funded by The Times, she invested in new supplies for patients.

Florence Nightingale, known as **'The Lady with the Lamp,'** faced resistance from army medical staff in Crimea due to perceived limited medical knowledge of women.
