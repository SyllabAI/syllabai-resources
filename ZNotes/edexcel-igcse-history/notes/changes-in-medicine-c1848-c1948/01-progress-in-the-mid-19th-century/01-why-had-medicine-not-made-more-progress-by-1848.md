1. Belief in illnesses caused by imbalances in the body's **Four Humours** led to treatment using the theory of opposites to restore balance.

2. The **Miasma Theory** linked disease spread to foul odors, leading people to counteract this by smelling poppies in public.

3. Scientists developed the **Spontaneous Generation** theory linking decaying matter to the creation of maggots, fleas, and diseases

4. **Lack of Medical Knowledge** as Doctors were only able to observe a few dissections during training as such procedures were only conducted on bodies of criminals who were set to be executed.

5. The **Government's Lack of Responsibility** and funding for disease research led hospitals to depend on charities and donations for funding, reflecting the connection between disease knowledge and technological advancements.
