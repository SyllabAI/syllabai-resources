## When answering…

* Follow the command words in each question

  * bagaimanakah: how?

  * apakah: what?

  * mengapakah: why?

  * di manakah: where?

  * terangkan: explain

  * berikan: provide

* Provide the exact examples asked by the question

* Spend 50-55 minutes on this section

* Keep your answer short !!
