## General Format

1. **Introduction**

   * Acknowledge the topic and give a brief overview of your content
   * Should be 3 sentences max

2. **2nd paragraph**

   * 3 main points

   * 1-2 elaborations per point

   * Use words like “*utama*” for your first point

   * e.g., “*punca utama"*, “*kesan utama*”

3. **3rd paragraph**

   * 2 main points

   * 1-2 elaborations per point

   * Use words like “*tambahan pula*”, “*di samping itu*” to introduce new ideas

4. **4th paragraph**

   * 3 main points

   * 1-2 elaborations per point

   * Use words like “*selain itu*”, “*seterusnya*” as connectives

5. **5th paragraph**

   * 2 main points

   * 1-2 elaborations per point

   * Use words like “*akhir sekali*” for your last point

6. **Conclusion**

   * Give a brief summary of your content

   * Should be 2 sentences max 

   * Use words like “*Kesimpulannya*”, “*Konklusinya*” to start this paragraph
