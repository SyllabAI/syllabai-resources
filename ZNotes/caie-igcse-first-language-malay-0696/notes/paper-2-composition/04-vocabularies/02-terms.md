## Phrases

Use these as your elaboration/strong verbs in your argumentative and discursive essay according to the topic/context

* ### Economy

  * merancakkan ekonomi tempatan *(stimulate the local economy)* 

  * menjana pendapatan negara *(generate profit for the country)* 

  * menambah peluang pekerjaan *(increase job opportunities）*

  * ketidakstabilan ekonomi *(economic instability)* 

* ### Agriculture

  * jaminan bekalan makanan *(guaranteed food supply)* 

  * mengurangkan kebergantungan terhadap import makanan *(reduces reliance towards food import)* 

  * meningkatkan hasil pertanian *(increase crop yield)* 

  * meningkatkan produktiviti *(increase productivity)*

* ### Education

  * melahirkan generasi berilmu dan berkemahiran *(produce a generation that is intelligent and skilled)*

  * membentuk modal insan yang berkualiti *(developing high-quality capabilities)*

  * membangunkan potensi individu *(cultivate an individual’s potential)* 

  * meningkatkan daya saing generasi muda *(increase the competitivenes of the younger generation)*

* ### Society

  * memperkukuh perpaduan masyarakat *(strengthen society’s unity)* 

  * membina masyarakat yang harmoni *(build a harmonious society)* 

  * menjamin kesejahteraan sosial *(guarantees social peace)* 

  * mengeratkan hubungan antara anggota masyarakat *(strengthens the bond between people of the society)* 

  * meningkatkan kesedaran masyarakat *(increase awareness among society)*
