## Peribahasa dan Simpulan Bahasa (Idioms and Proverbs)

Use these for any essay according to context

Add the phrase “*bak kata pepatah*” or “*bak kata mutiara*” before adding your idioms or proverbs

* ### Hardwork

  * belakang parang jika diasah juga akan menjadi tajam juga *(even the back of the machete will become sharp if honed)* 

  * jika tidak dipecahkan ruyung, mana mendapat sagunya *(achieving your goals or dreams comes with hard work and perseverance)* 

  * di mana ada kemahuan, di situ ada jalan *(when there’s a will, there’s a way)* 

* ### Unity

  * berat sama dipikul, ringan sama dijinjing *(supporting one another through thick and thin)* 

  * bagai aur dengan tebing *(helping one another)* 

  * bulat air kerana pembetung, bulat manusia kerana muafakat *(unity is achieved through mutual cooperation）* 

  * duduk sama rendah, berdiri sama tinggi *(to stand as one)*

  * bersama kita teguh, bercerai kita teguh *(united we stand, divided we fall)*

* ### Family

  * bagai melentur buluh biarlah dari rebungnya *(shaping a person’s value/character starts from when they’re young)* 

  * bapa borek, anak rintik *(like father, like son)* 

  * bagaimana acuan begitulah kuihnya *(the apple doesn’t fall far from the tree)* 

* ### Going to a new place

  * di mana bumi dipijak, di sana langit dijunjung *(you must respect the customs/traditions of the place you’re visiting)*

  * bagai rusa masuk ke kampung *(feeling lost in a new setting)* 

  * masuk kandang kambing mengembek, masuk kandang kerbau menguak *(When in Rome, do as the Romans do)*
