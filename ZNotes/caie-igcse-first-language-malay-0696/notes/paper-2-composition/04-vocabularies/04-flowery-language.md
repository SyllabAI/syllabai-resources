## Frasa Berbunga (Flowery Language)

Use these for your descriptive or narrative essay

* angin semilir menyapa pipiku *(the soft air kissed my cheeks)* 

* rasa kesejukan menusuk tulangku *(the cold feeling pierced through my bones)*

* rasa gelisah menjama hatiku *(nervousness took hold of my heart)* 

* aroma makanan berlari-lari ke rongga hidungku *(the food’s aroma ran through my nostrils)* 

* bunyi terngiang-ngiang di cuping telingaku *(the sound resonated near the cup of my ears)* 

* kecur air liurku menangkap bau lauk *(salivating at the sight of food)* 

* sinar matahari memancar ke wajahku *(the sunlight shone on my face)* 

* rasa gembira meruap di dadanya *(happiness bloomed in her/his chest)*

* bau busuk menusuk rongga hidungku *(pungent smell infiltrated my nose)* 

* keringat membasahi dahiku *(sweat drenched my forehead)* 

* senyumannya manampakkan barisan gigi yang putih bak mutiara *(he/she smiled ear to ear)*
