## Essay Types

The essay in this section is either a narrative or a descriptive essay.

### Narrative

* 5-6 paragraphs in total

* 5-6 sentences per paragraph

* Keep your plot simple

* Your story should only have 1-3 characters

* Your story MUST have a rising action, climax, then resolution

  * Rising action at the 2nd paragraph 

  * Climax at the 3rd/4th paragraph

  * Resolution at the 4th/last paragraph 

* Mix different types of sentences to bring out different effects

  * **Short sentences:** suspense/fast pace

  * **Long sentences:** description/slow pace

### Descriptive

* 5-6 paragraphs in total

* Use flowery language to express the “show don’t tell” technique

  * “*hati melonjakkan kegembiraan*” (I felt happy)

  * “*kicauan burung berlagu merdu seolah-olah menyambut kedatanganku*” (the birds sang in harmony as if welcoming me)

  * “*rasa sejuk menusuk tulangku*” (the cold feeling pierced through my bones)

* Describe your senses

  * touch, scent, sight, taste, hearing, feelings

* Your story should **not** have a plot; instead, focus on a different object/subject in each paragraph
