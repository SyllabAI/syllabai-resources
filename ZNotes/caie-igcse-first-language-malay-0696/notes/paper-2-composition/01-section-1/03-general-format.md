## General Format

Word count: 350-450 words

1. **Introduction**

   * Give a brief introduction to your topic

   * Start with words like “*dasawarsa ini*” and “*dalam era globalisasi ini*”

2. **2nd to 6th paragraph**

   * 1 main point followed by elaborations
   * Use connectives to start a new point
     * “*di samping itu*”, “*selain itu*”, “*seterusnya*”, “*selain daripada itu*”

3. **Conclusion**

   * Give a brief summary of your content

   * Use words like “*tuntasnya*”, “*konklusinya*” and “*kesimpulannya*”
