## General Tips

* Pay attention to the length of your work

* Spend \~10 minutes planning your draft

* Remember to write which topic you’ve chosen above the writing space 

* Spend \~1 hour on this section

* Use idioms and proverbs where appropriate

  * Some that you can ALWAYS use are:

    * ”*Sudah terang lagi bersuluh*“ (as we all know)

    * “*Seperti yang sedia maklum*” (as mentioned before)

    * ”*Sesal dahulu pendapatan, sesal kemudian tidak berguna*” (think before you do)
