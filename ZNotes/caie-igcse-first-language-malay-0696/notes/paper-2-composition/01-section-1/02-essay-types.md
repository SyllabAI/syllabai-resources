## Essay Types

The essays in this section are split into 2 types: discursive and argumentative.

### Discursive

* Must have 5 main points

* \~7 paragraphs in total

* Never use personal pronouns like “*saya*” or “*awak*”; use “*kita*” or directly address the subject (“*pihak kerajaan*”, “*pihak sekolah*”, “*ibu bapa*”)

* Keep your register formal

* Your essay must reflect a balanced viewpoint

### Argumentative

* Must give your **stand in the 1st paragraph**

* Must have 5 main points

* \~7 paragraphs in total

* You must give the pros AND cons of your topic

  * You should have 3 pros and 2 cons

### Elaborating the points

* Follow the ‘IMBAKUP’ concept to elaborate each point

  * **Isi** (main point)

  * **Mengapa** (why)

  * **Bagaimana** (how)

  * **Ayat contoh** (example)

  * **Kesan** (consequence)

  * **Ungkapan** (idiom/proverb)

  * **Penegasan** (affirm your point)

*“ungkapan” and “penegasan” are interchangeable*
