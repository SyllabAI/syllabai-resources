### Proton

* has charge of $+e$ so usually shown as $uud$

* $+\frac{2}{3}e + \frac{2}{3}e - \frac{1}{3}e = +e$

### Neutrons

* has no charge so usually shown as $udd$

* $+\frac{2}{3}e - \frac{1}{3}e - \frac{1}{3}e = 0$

### Change in quark composition

**β⁻ decay**

$\boxed{d \rightarrow u + \beta^- + \bar{\nu}_e}$

* A **down quark changes into an up quark**.

* Neutron: udd

* Proton: uud

$\boxed{udd \rightarrow uud + \beta^- + \bar{\nu}_e}$

**β⁺ decay**

$\boxed{u \rightarrow d + \beta^+ + \nu_e}$

* An **up quark changes into a down quark**.

* Proton: uud

* Neutron: udd

$\boxed{uud \rightarrow udd + \beta^+ + \nu_e}$

### Particle Family

* Hadrons: made up of quarks.

* Leptons: fundamental particles.

* Baryons: made up of 3 quarks

* Mesons: made up of 1 quark & 1 antiquark
