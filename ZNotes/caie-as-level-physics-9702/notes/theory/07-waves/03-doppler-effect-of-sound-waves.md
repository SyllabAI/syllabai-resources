## Doppler Effect of Sound Waves

The **Doppler effect** is the apparent change in the frequency of a wave due to relative motion between the **source** and the **observer**.

**For sound:**

![ChatGPT Image Sep 26, 2026, 10 29 49 PM](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/259b904950bef04e77585c38.webp =937x)

* If the source moves **towards** the stationary observer, the observed frequency **increases**. 

* If the source moves **away** from the stationary observer, the observed frequency **decreases**. 

* If both source and observer are stationary, the observed frequency equals the source frequency.

* Observed frequency and known frequency aren’t equal during doppler effect

* happens in all types of waves

* Formula - $f_{0} = \frac{f_{s}v}{v \pm v_{s}}$​

### **Worked example**

A police car is travelling along a straight road at a constant speed of $25 ms^{-1}$ towards a stationary observer. The siren on the car emits sound of frequency 750 Hz.

The speed of sound in air is $340 ms^{-1}$.

**a)** Calculate the frequency of the sound detected by the observer as the car approaches.

The police car passes the observer and continues travelling at the same speed.

**b)** Calculate the frequency detected by the observer as the car moves away.

**c)** Explain why the observed frequency decreases after the car passes the observer.

Ans :

a) $f_o=750\left\(\frac{340}{340-25}\right\)$

$=750\left\(\frac{340}{315}\right\)$

$=809.52\text{Hz}$

$\boxed{f_o=809.52\text{Hz}}$

b) $f_o=750\left\(\frac{340}{340+25}\right\)$

$=750\left\(\frac{340}{365}\right\)$

$=698.63\text{Hz}$

$\boxed{f_o=698.63\text{Hz}}$

c)When the car moves towards the observer, the wavefronts are closer together, so the wavelength decreases and the observed frequency increases.

When the car moves away, the wavefronts are spread further apart, so the wavelength increases and the observed frequency decreases.
