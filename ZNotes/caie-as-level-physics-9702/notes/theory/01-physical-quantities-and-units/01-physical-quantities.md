### **Example problems**

A student shouts across a lake that is 300m wide. Estimate the time taken for the sound to reach the opposite side through the air. (Use estimate speed of sound)

a)$0.09\mathit{s}$

b)$0.9\mathit{s}$

c)$9.0\mathit{s}$

d)$90\mathit{s}$

Correct Answer : $0.9\mathit{s}$

Solution : $\text{time} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$

$\text{time}=\frac{300}{330}=0.91\approx0.9\text{s}$
