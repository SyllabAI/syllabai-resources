### Homogenous nature of equation

* So when any equation is meaningful both the sides should be equal, so if it should be equal then **SI units on both sides should be equal**.

* If **SI units on both sides match then an equation is homogenous**

Let’s take :  $E = mgh$

$E = Fd$, so the SI unit of $E$ is $\mathrm{kgms^{-2}} \times \mathrm{m}$ = $\mathrm{kgm^2s^{-2}}$

For $mgh$, the SI unit is $\mathrm{kg} \times \mathrm{ms^{-2}} \times \mathrm{m}$. = $\mathrm{kgm^2s^{-2}}$

Therefore, $kgm^2s = kgm^{2}s$

Let’s look at another equation - $\boxed{T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}}$

$[T]=\mathrm{s}\qquad [l]=\mathrm{m}\qquad [g]=\mathrm{ms^{-2}}$

$\left\[\frac{l}{g}\right\] = \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{m,s^{-2}}} = \mathrm{s^2}$

$\sqrt{\mathrm{s^2}}=\mathrm{s}$

$\left\[2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}\right\] =\mathrm{s}$

$[T]=\mathrm{s}$

$\boxed{\therefore\ T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}\text{is dimensionally homogeneous.}}$

#### Example problem

* The drag force F acting on a sphere moving through a fluid is believed to depend on the radius r of the sphere, the speed v of the sphere, and the viscosity η of the fluid, according to the equation: $F = kr^{a}v^\eta$ where k is a dimensionless constant, and a and b are unknown powers. The SI base units of viscosity η are kg m⁻¹ s⁻¹.
  * (a) State the SI base units of force F.

  * (b) By considering the homogeneity of the equation, determine the values of a and b.

Ans : (a)  F = kg m s⁻²

Ans : (b) SI units of RHS: (m)ᵃ × (m s⁻¹)ᵇ × (kg m⁻¹ s⁻¹)

* = $kgm^{a+b-1}s$

* Equate to LHS: kg m s⁻²

* Mass: exponent of kg is 1 on both sides ✓ (consistent, no new info)

* Time: −b − 1 = −2 → b = 1

* Length: a \+ b − 1 = 1 → a \+ 1 − 1 = 1 → a = 1
