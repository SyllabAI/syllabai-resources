### Internal Resistance

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/f2a5acfdcbfed9a23e4af6db.webp =739x)

The internal resistance in a battery is due to the opposition of flow of electrons in the electrolyte

* symbol - $r$ (same units as resistance)

* So due to internal resistance the terminal p.d is lower than the e.m.f.

* When current starts to flow some of the e.m.f is lost to overcome the internal resistance in battery ($Ir$)

* Terminal p.d = E.M.F - $Ir$ $\rightarrow$ $V = E - Ir$

* Thus e.m.f.: $E = IR + Ir$

* $E = I(R + r)$

  * E - e.m.f.

  * I - current in circuit

  * R - Resistance

  * r - internal resistance
