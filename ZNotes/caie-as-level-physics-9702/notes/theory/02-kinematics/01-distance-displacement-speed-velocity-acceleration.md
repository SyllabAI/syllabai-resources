### Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity (unit - $\mathrm{ms^{-2}}$)

* Is a vector quantity

* Acceleration = $\frac{v-u}{t}$ = $\frac{\Delta v}{t}$

* There’s two types of acceleration : **Uniform & Non Uniform**

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/7293c922d8a739f9d80332fe.webp =776x)

  * Uniform - rate of change of velocity is constant

  * Non uniform - rate of change of velocity isn’t constant
