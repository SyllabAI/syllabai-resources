# Work, Energy and Power

## Energy Conservation

### Work Done

$\text{Work done} = \text{Force} \times \text{displacement in the direction of the force}$

* Work done = Energy

* SI unit : Joules ($J$)

* As mentioned in definition the formula is $W=Fs$

  * $W$ - Work done

  * $F$ - Force

  * $s$ - Displacement of object

* If displacement is at an angle then resolve it.

  * $$W = Fs\cos\theta$$

### Law of conservation of energy

The total energy of an isolated system cannot change—it is conserved over time. Energy can be neither created nor destroyed but can change form, e.g. from g.p.e to k.e.

* Usually all energy transfers aren’t useful such as heat from electrical appliances like TV

* Car - Chemical energy $\rightarrow$ Kinetic energy \+ Thermal energy

  * g.p.e - gravitational potential energy

  * k.e - kinetic energy

### Power

Work done per unit time

* SI unit - Watts ($W$) or $Js^{-1}$

* $Power = \frac{\text{Energy}}{\text{Time\ Taken}}$

  * Deriving it to form $P = Fv$

  $P = \frac{\text{W.d}}{T}$​ & $W.d. = Fs$

  $\therefore P = \frac{\text{Fs}}{T} = F ( \frac{\text{s}}{\text{t}} )$​

  $\therefore P = Fv$

### Efficiency

Efficiency of a system is the ratio of useful energy output from the system to the total energy input

* As all energy transfers aren’t useful, so the efficiency isn’t usually 100% in real life

  * $Efficiency = \frac{\text{Useful\ Energy\ Ouput}}{\text{Total\ Energy\ Input}} \times 100$

    $Efficiency = \frac{\text{Useful\ Power\ Ouput}}{\text{Total\ Power\ Input}} \times 100$
