# Deformation of Solids

## Stress & Strain

### Deformation

* Deformation is basically a change in a objects form, size or shape

* In order for this to occur a force should act on a object

* According to the syllabus $\rightarrow$ compressive forces or tensile forces

  * Compressive forces - Acts towards each other on the object essentially compressing it so the object decreases in length

  * Tensile forces - A pair of forces that act outward, away from each other, at each end of the object, causing it to stretch/extend.

### Hooke’s Law

So the deformation tends to be proportional to force applied on it until a certain limit known as the limit of proportionality

**Hooke’s Law**

The extension produced is proportional to the applied force (due to the load) as long as the limit of proportionality hasn't been exceeded.

* Load - Force that acts on a spring that causes deformation

* Extension - Increase in length due to load

* Limit of Proportionality - The point beyond which the extension is no longer proportional to the force

As in Hooke’s law Force ($F$) is proportional to extension ($x$) so a constant the spring constant is $k$ is introduced

Forming $\rightarrow$ $F$=$kx$ leading to $k = \frac{F}{x}$

* Unit of $k$ - $\mathrm{Nm^{-1}}$

### Stress

* Force applied per unit cross sectional area

* Formula - $\sigma = \frac{F}{A}$​

* Unit - ($\text{N m}^{-2}$​ or Pascals)

### Strain

* Ratio of extension by original length

* Formula - $\varepsilon = \frac{x}{l}$​

* Unit - (no units)

### Young’s Modulus

* The ratio of stress to strain

* Formula - $E = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon}$​

* Unit - $\text{N m}^{-2}$​  or Pascals (as strain has no units same unit as stress)
