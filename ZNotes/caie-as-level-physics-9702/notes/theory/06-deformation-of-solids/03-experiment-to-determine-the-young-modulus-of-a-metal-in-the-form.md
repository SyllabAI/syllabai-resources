## Experiment to determine the Young modulus of a metal in the form of a wire

![5cf120c0 a596 480c ab64 159d57a700a4](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/a4abe7522c4e5e43f471ecf5.webp =788x)

### Apparatus

* Test wire

* Bench

* Two clamps

* Ruler (mm scale)

* Pulley

* Weights and weight hanger

* Marker

### Method

1. The wire is fixed firmly at one end of the bench.

2. The wire is passed over a pulley at the other end and weights are hung.

3. A marker is placed on the wire. The initial position of the marker is noted on the ruler (zero mark).

4. A known load is added and the new position of the marker is noted. The extension is the change in reading.

5. Repeat for several loads within the elastic limit.

### Measurements

* Length of wire between clamps = **L**

* Diameter of wire = **d** (using micrometer screw gauge)

* Cross sectional area = **$\frac{πd^2}{4}$**

* Extension for load **= ΔL = $L_{final}$ - $L_{initial}$**

* Stress = **$\frac{F}{A}$**

* Strain = **$\frac{ΔL}{L}$**

* Young modulus = **$\frac{stress}{strain}$ = $\frac{FL}{AΔL}$**

**Note - Make sure that wire is within elastic region**
