# Forces, Density and Pressure

## Turning Effects of Forces

### Centre of Gravity

* Force is the rate of change of momentum

* The point in a object where all the weight of an object acts upon in simple terms

* **Centre of gravity**: Point where all the weight of the body is considered/seems to act

* Can be found using plumb line experiment

### Moment

* Product of force & perpendicular distance from pivot

* Moment = $\text{Force} \times \text{Perpendicular distance from pivot}$

* SI unit: $Nm$

* When at an angle resolve components

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/49e51ff61586989aa2c80b31.webp =1101x)

**Principle of Moments**: Sum of clockwise moments is equal to sum of anticlockwise moments in an isolated system (so resultant force & resultant moment is 0)

**Worked example**

A force of 50 N acts on a lever 0.40 m from the pivot at an angle of 30 degrees.

**(a)** Calculate the perpendicular component of the force.

**(b)** Calculate the moment of the force about the pivot.

Ans :

a) $F_{\perp}=F\sin\theta$

$=50\sin30^\circ$

$=50(0.5)$

$\boxed{F_{\perp}=25\mathrm{N}}$

b) $M=F_{\perp}d$

$=25(0.40)$

$\boxed{M=10\mathrm{Nm}}$

### Torque of a Couple

* Product of force and perpendicular distance between 2 forces

* **Couple**: Pair of forces that act to produce rotation (like the spanner in example)

* Conditions for Torque:

  * Resultant force is 0

  * Should be separated by distance $d$

  * Forces should have:

    * Same magnitude

    * Be parallel

    * In opposite directions

    * Should act through same point

* When there is no resultant force and no resultant torque, a system is in equilibrium
