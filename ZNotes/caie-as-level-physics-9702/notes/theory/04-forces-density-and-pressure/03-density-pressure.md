## Density & Pressure

### Density

* Mass per unit volume

* SI unit : $$\mathrm{kgm^{-3}}$$

* Formula : $$ \rho = \frac{m}{V} $$

### Pressure

* Perpendicular force per area

* SI unit : $$ \mathrm{Pa} $$ or $$ \mathrm{kgm^{-1}s^{-2}} $$

* Formula : $$P=\frac{F}{A} $$

**Pressure in fluids & Hydrostatic Pressure**

* Fluids refer to both liquids and gases

* Particles are free to move and have kinetic energy they collide with each other and the container. This exerts a small force over a small area causing pressure to form.

* Hydrostatic pressure is the pressure exerted by a fluid at equilibrium at a given point within the fluid, due to the force of gravity

* Volume of water $= A \times ∆h$

  Mass of Water $= density \times volume = \rho \times A \times ∆h$

  Weight of Water $= mass\ \times g = \rho \times A \times ∆h \times  g$

  Change in Pressure$\ = \frac{\mathrm{\text{Force}}}{\mathrm{\text{Area}}}  = \frac{\rho \times A \times ∆h \times g}{A}$​ $= \rho g∆h$

### Upthrust

An upward force exerted by a fluid on a submerged or floating object

* Caused by **difference in hydrostatic pressure**

**Origin of Upthrust**:

* Due to difference in depth, there is a difference in hydrostatic pressure between top and bottom of object.

* Pressure on bottom surface \> pressure on top surface

* Force on bottom of object is greater than downwards force on top of object so resultant force is upwards

* Which forms upthrust

### Archimedes Principle

Archimedes’ principle states that an object submerged in a fluid at rest has an upthrust equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by the object

* Upthrust=$\rho g V $

  Upthrust=Force and Force=Pressure x Area

  Upthrust is the total upwards force exerted on a body by a liquid. Force acting upwards-Force acting downwards= Upthrust

  U=$\rho g h_2 A - \rho g h_1 A$

  where $\rho$ is the density of the liquid the object is placed in, $g$ is acceleration due to gravity and $h_2$ and $h_1$ are depths the pressure acts on

  U=$\rho g A (h_2-h_1) $ and $(h_2- h_1)$ will give height, $h$ of the object ($h_2$ & $h_1$ are depths of two points along an object)

  U=$\rho g A h$ so U=$\rho g V$ as Area x height will give volume of the object

  $\therefore U=\rho g V$
