### Inertia

* Mass is the variable that measures inertia.

* Inertia is the ability to resist change in motion, so it is proportional to an object’s mass.

* The higher the mass, the higher the inertia.

* An object has inertia both when stationary and when moving (it resists any change to its state of rest or motion).

If we think about how inertia works in real life, we arrive at:

* **Newtons First Law: If a body is at rest, it remains at rest, or if it is in motion, it moves with a uniform velocity until it is acted on by resultant force.**
