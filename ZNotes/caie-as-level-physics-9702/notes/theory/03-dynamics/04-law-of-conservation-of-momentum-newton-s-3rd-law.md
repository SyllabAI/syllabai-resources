## Law of conservation of momentum & Newton's 3rd law

### Law of conservation of momentum

* Sum of momentum before collisions is equal to sum of momentum after collision, or total momentum is constant when there’s no external force or in an isolated system

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/0b87d9149908b26011114883.webp =670x)

* Now, according to the image, we can see that the forces exerted on both balls are equal and opposite—this acts as a simple real-life example of ***Newton’s Third Law***.

### **Newtons’s Third Law** 

* When a body A exerts a force on body B , body B will exert a force on body A of equal magnitude and opposite in direction.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/71386139a9bcd8df4bb03da1.webp =1067x)

### Examples

* Foot pushes ground back, ground pushes foot forward

* Rocket expels gas down, gas pushes rocket up
