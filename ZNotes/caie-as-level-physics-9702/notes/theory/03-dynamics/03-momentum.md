## Momentum

* Product of mass & velocity

$\boxed{p=mv}$

* $p$ - momentum

* $m$ - mass

* $v$ - velocity

* Momentum is a vector quantity, so it can change due to varying velocity. This change is known as ***impulse***.

  $m(v-u)$**=Impulse**

  * $m$ - mass

  * $v$ - final velocity

  * $u$ - initial velocity

* As mentioned before in the 2nd law, resultant force is proportional to the rate of change of momentum.

* Rate of change of momentum is basically $\frac{impulse}{time}$, so:

$$
F=\frac{\Delta p}{t}
=\frac{m(v-u)}{t}
=m\left\(\frac{v-u}{t}\right\)
=ma
$$

* So, rate of change of momentum = $ma$ = $F$

* Impulse = $\text{Force} \times \text{time}$

* This formula is applicable when force is constant

**a)** Calculate the impulse on the ball.
**b)** Calculate the average force acting on the ball

Ans : a) Δ$p=m(v-u)$

$=2.0(11-5.0)$

$=2.0(6.0)$

Δ$p=12$ $kgms^{-1}$

b)$J=Δp$

$Δt \times F=Δp$

$F$ = $\frac{\Delta p}{t}$

=$\frac{12}{0.60}$
