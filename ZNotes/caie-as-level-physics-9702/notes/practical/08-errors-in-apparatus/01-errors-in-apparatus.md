**Meter Ruler**

|


||
||

Parallax error

|

Put coloured paper behind / view at eye level perpendicular to the scale / extend mark to wood / use shadow projection

||
||

Difficult to hold ruler still

|

Mount ruler in stand

||
||

Difficult to measure, since the ruler moves

|

Clamp rule / ensure the rule is vertical using a set square

||
||

**Newton Meter**

|


||
||

Difficult to pull Newton meter parallel to ruler/bench

|

Ensure force is parallel to ruler, e.g. use a long string, pulley and weights

||
||

Difficult to judge reading on meter when detached: ruler moves suddenly / Force = 0 after detachment

|

Use a Newton meter with a "max hold" facility / video and playback or freeze-frame / use a force sensor and data logger

||
||

Difficult to zero Newton meter when horizontal

|

Use a system of pulleys and weights / use a force sensor with data logger

||
||

**Objects with an unfixed diameter (circular objects)**

|


||
||

Difficult to measure diameter since the object is flexible or not perfectly circular

|

Use Vernier calipers or a micrometer screw gauge to measure the average diameter

||
||

Difficult to form a perfect sphere / diameter varied

|

Use a method to make uniform spheres/discs, e.g. moulds

||
||

**Protractor**

|


||
||

Protractor "wobbles" / difficult to measure; container curved at the bottom / difficult to line up

|

Use a protractor with a horizontal line level to the tabletop / use a freestanding or clamped protractor

||
||

Parallax error in $\theta$

|

Use a mirror scale

||

* **General:**
  * **Error:** two readings are not enough to come to a valid conclusion
  * **Improvement:** take several readings and plot a graph
