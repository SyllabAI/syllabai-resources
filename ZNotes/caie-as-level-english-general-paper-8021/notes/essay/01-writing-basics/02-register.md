## **Register**

### **1. What is register?**

Register is the level of **formality** in your writing or speech. It’s how appropriate your language is for the context, purpose, and audience.

### **3. How to control register**

* **Vocabulary**: Use subject-specific and precise words for formal writing. Avoid slang or overly casual expressions unless it suits the task.

* **Sentence structure**:

  * Formal: Although this issue is often overlooked, it remains crucial.

  * Informal: People usually don’t talk about it, but it’s a big deal.

* **Tone**: Match the emotion and seriousness to the task. Avoid sarcasm and over-exaggeration in formal writing.

### **4. Shifting register**

Sometimes, shifting between registers helps make a point.

**Example (in a speech):** ”*We’ve discussed the statistics, the facts, and the science. But let’s be real, nobody wants to live like this.”*

**This slight drop to an informal line builds focus and connection to what you are trying to convey.**

### **5. Tips for exam use**

* The General Paper essay will always require you to use the formal register.

* **Avoid mixing registers**, don’t start formally then randomly drop into chatty slang. (Shifting registers is useful, but if you do it too often or are not mindful of it, it lowers your band level)

* **Practise editing** - take one paragraph and rewrite it in different registers. It’ll teach you control.
