## **Practice “Your Country” Questions**

Many General Paper essay questions ask you to refer to **your own country**. These usually begin with phrases like:

* “In your country…”

* “To what extent is this true in your society…”

* “How far does this apply where you live…”

**To be prepared, you should:**

* Practise adapting every major topic (education, law, politics, gender, etc.) to your own country

* Build examples, statistics, and trends specific to your country

* Know both strengths and problems your country faces in each area

* Stay updated with recent developments, reforms, or controversies


Generic global answers won’t score as highly; using real, local examples shows understanding, analysis, and awareness.
