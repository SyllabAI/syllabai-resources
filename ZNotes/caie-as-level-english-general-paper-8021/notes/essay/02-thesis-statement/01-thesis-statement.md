# **Thesis Statement**

### **1. What is a Thesis Statement?**

* The **main controlling idea** of an essay.

* An **arguable assertion**, not a fact.

* States your **opinion or position** clearly.

* Usually placed at the **end of the introduction**.

### **2. Purpose of a Thesis Statement**

* **For Writers**:

  * Organizes thoughts and ideas.

  * Keeps writing **focused** and **relevant**.

  * Helps build topic sentences and essay structure.

* **For Readers**:

  * Sets **expectations** for the essay.

  * Serves as a **roadmap** for arguments.

  * Helps readers follow your logic and evaluate your claim.

### **3. What Makes a Good Thesis Statement?**

* Makes an **arguable point**, not just a fact.

* **Summarizes** the entire essay in one or two sentences.

* **Predicts**, **controls**, and **limits** the scope of the essay.

* Provides a **clear framework** for the body paragraphs.

* Is **specific** and **focused**, not too broad or narrow.

### **4. Developing a Thesis Statement**

* Start with a **topic** -> Ask **a question** -> Your **answer** = your **thesis**.

* Use:

  * Class discussions

  * Preliminary research

  * Conversations with others

* Be open to **refining or changing** your thesis while writing.

* Expect a **tentative thesis** first, and a **final version** later.
