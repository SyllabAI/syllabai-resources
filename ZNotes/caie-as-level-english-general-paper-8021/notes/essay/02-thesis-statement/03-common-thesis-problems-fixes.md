## **Common Thesis Problems & Fixes**

Problem

|

Why It’s Bad

|

Better Version Tip

Too factual

|

No room for argument

|

Add an opinion or interpretation

||
||

Too broad

|

Hard to focus and support

|

Narrow the scope

||
||

Too narrow

|

Too little to write about

|

Widen slightly to include more discussion

||
||

Multiple main ideas

|

Confusing and scattered

|

Focus on one central idea

||
||

Sentence fragment

|

Incomplete thought

|

Make it a complete, grammatically correct sentence

||
||

A question

|

Doesn’t take a stand

|

Answer the question with a clear claim

||
||

Vague language

|

Weak and unengaging

|

Use specific, strong, clear words

||
||

Phrases like “I think” or “In my opinion”

|

Weakens authority

|

Drop them. State your opinion confidently
