## **Examples & Practice**

Example:
“Cell phone use while driving should be banned, for it creates an unsafe environment.”
**Topic**: Cell phone use while driving
**Opinion**: Should be banned
**Reason**: Creates danger for all drivers

Use this structure to break down others.

#### **Analyse & Evaluate**

Example:
“Drug use is a serious problem in today’s world.”
**Why it's weak**: Too vague and obvious; no arguable point.

Try to rework this as practice.

#### **Fix the Statement**

Bad: “Writing an essay can be fairly easy.”
Better: “With the right planning strategy, even beginner writers can produce well-structured essays.”

A **strong thesis** does the following:

* States a **clear opinion**

* Shows **why** the topic matters

* Helps both the **writer and reader** stay on track

* Can evolve as you write, but should always be **specific, assertive, and arguable**
