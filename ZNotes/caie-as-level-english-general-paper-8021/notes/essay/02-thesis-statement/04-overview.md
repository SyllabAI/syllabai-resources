## Overview:

**Thesis Statement =** your main claim in one clear sentence.

### It should:

* State the main point clearly.

* Present your position, essentially what side you are taking. (Mainly only for argumentative)

* Focus on relevant and important content, so that your ideas are manageable within the essay

* Be coherent, easy to follow, and use precise wording

* Directly address the essay question!!

* Be grounded and supported with good evidence/analysis

**In short:** A thesis statement = a clear, arguable, focused answer to the essay question that you can prove with evidence and evaluation throughout the rest of your essay.
