## **Linking Ideas**

### **Why Linking Matters**

* Creates flow and cohesion between ideas.

* Helps the reader follow your logic clearly.

* Makes the essay feel structured and professional.

### **Where to Use Linking**

* At the **start of a paragraph** to connect with the previous point.

* **Within paragraphs** to build reasoning or contrast ideas.

* Avoid overusing the same word.

* Don’t force linkers - make sure they suit the logic of your ideas.

* Keep your tone formal: avoid casual transitions like “anyway” or “by the way”.
