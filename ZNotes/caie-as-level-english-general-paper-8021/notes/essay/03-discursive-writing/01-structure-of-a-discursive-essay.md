# **Discursive Writing**

## **Structure of a Discursive Essay**

### **Typical Format:**

1. **Introduction**

   * Hooks the reader

   * Briefly introduces the topic

   * May state your position (optional in balanced essays)

2. **Body Paragraphs**

   * Each paragraph presents one argument/point 

   * Include supporting evidence or examples

   * Stay formal and well-structured

3. **Conclusion**

   * Reiterate your position

   * Summarise key arguments

   * End with a strong final thought
