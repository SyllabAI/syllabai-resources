## **Styles of Introductions**

Choose an introduction style that suits your tone:

#|
||

Style

|

Example

||
||

Provocative

|

“It is difficult to see how anyone can approve of fox hunting.”

||
||

Balanced

|

“Fox hunting is a subject about which people hold strongly contrasting views.”

||
||

Quotation

|

“Oscar Wilde once described fox hunting as ‘The unspeakable in pursuit of the uneatable.’”

||
||

Illustrative/Ideal

|

“On a glorious autumn morning, a terrified, exhausted animal is savaged to death...”

||
||

Anecdotal

|

“I have always detested fox hunting since I was almost physically sick...”

||
|#
