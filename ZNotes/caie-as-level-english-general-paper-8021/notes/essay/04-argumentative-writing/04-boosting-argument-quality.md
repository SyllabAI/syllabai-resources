## **Boosting Argument Quality**

**Use real-world examples**

* Mention case studies, news, and research

* Example, “According to a 2022 WHO report…”

**Define key terms if unclear**

* Especially in intros

* Example, “Freedom of speech refers to the legal right to express opinions without censorship…”

**Use powerful phrasing**

* “It is essential that…”

* “This clearly demonstrates…”

* “The evidence strongly suggests…”
