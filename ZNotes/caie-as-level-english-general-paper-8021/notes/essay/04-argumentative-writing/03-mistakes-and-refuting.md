## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

* Writing only **your side** without addressing the other

* Using **emotional** rather than logical arguments

* Being too **vague or broad**

* Forgetting a clear **topic sentence** in body paragraphs

* Introducing **new ideas** in the conclusion

### **How to Refute Opposing Views**

* Acknowledge the **other side** briefly

* Use phrases like:

  * “Some may argue that...”

  * “It could be said that...”

* Then refute with logic/evidence:

  * “However, this overlooks the fact that…”

  * “This argument fails to consider…”

**Example**:
”*Some claim social media improves communication. However, studies show it leads to increased loneliness among teens.”*
