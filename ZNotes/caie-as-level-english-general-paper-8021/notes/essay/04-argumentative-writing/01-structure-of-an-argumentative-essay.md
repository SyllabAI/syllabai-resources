# **Argumentative Writing**

## **Structure of an Argumentative Essay**

### Typical format:

**Introduction**

* Introduce the topic

* Give brief background

* End with a **thesis statement** (your main claim)

**Body Paragraphs (3–4 typical)**

* Start with a **topic sentence**

* Provide evidence: facts, stats, quotes

* Respond to opposing views (acknowledge & refute)

**Conclusion**

* Summarise key points

* Restate the thesis in new words

* Don’t add new arguments

### Purpose of an Argumentative Essay

* To **clearly present a claim (your point of view)**

* To **support the claim** with strong **evidence** (facts, examples, data)

* To **consider and respond to opposing views**

* To **persuade** the reader to accept your position
