# **Understanding and Approaching the Paper**

## Paper Format and Structure

Time: 1 hr 45 mins
Total Marks: 50
Format: 2 sections - A and B (25 marks each)

No choice of questions: **all must be attempted**.

### **Section A**

Typically, you’re given a scenario with characters, meetings, or proposals.

* All your answers come from the insert - respond to **specific prompts** based on the **extract** and **background information** (the 2 sources you will be provided).

#### **Question types:**

1. **Identification (1-2 marks each):**

   * Find evidence, e.g. “Identify two signs that X is controlling.”

   * **Requires** brief, precise phrases directly linked to the material.

2. **Explanation (2-3 marks each):**

   * Explain why someone acted in a certain way, using information from across sources.

   * Answer with clarity, accurate reference, and clear connection to the question.

3. **Justification of statements (2-3 marks):**

   * Prove or disprove a claim, e.g. show why something is exaggerated or inaccurate.

   * **Must** include direct references and logic. You would mainly find your body points in the background information.

4. **Comparisons/Contradictions (3 marks):**

   * Match up what’s claimed vs what’s observed, and usually you need to look at the 2 different sources.

5. **Evaluation questions (8-10 marks):**

   * You’ll be asked to explain flaws, analyse suitability, or give reasons for choices.

   * Written in continuous prose - requires planning, structure, and clear logic.

### **Section B**

#### **Question types:**

1. **Direct Identification (1 mark each):**

   * List facts/benefits as stated in specific lines, don’t waste time paraphrasing.

2. **Vocabulary in Context (1 mark each):**

   * Define a word based on how it’s used in the passage (**context is important** - no other dictionary meaning).

3. **Short explanation (2–4 marks):**

   * Explain reasons, effects, or actions - must be based strictly on the passage.

4. **Paraphrasing / Using Own Words (3–5 marks):**

   * Rewrite key details or explanations by rephrasing, but don’t change **keywords**.

5. **Word-Limited Summaries (30–60 words):**

   * Requires selecting only the most relevant points from a specified section.

   * Clear, concise, and accurate wording is critical.

6. **Opinion + Application (2–3 marks):**

   * Personal opinion questions - they must be logically reasoned and not repeat info from the passage, use the passage to your advantage and infer clues from the given context.
