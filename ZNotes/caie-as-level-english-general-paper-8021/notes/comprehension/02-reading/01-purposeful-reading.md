# **General Paper Reading**

## Purposeful reading

Avoid passive reading; focus on understanding the writer's aim, key points, and likely question areas.

* **Before** reading, glance at the questions to know what to focus on, keep in mind keywords, and ideas asked about in the questions.

* **While** reading each paragraph, ask:

  * Is this relevant to the questions?

  * What is this paragraph doing?

  * Is it introducing a view, giving evidence, or making a comparison?

* **After** reading, jot down any evaluation and analysis points that come to you, and begin answering the question using the insert simultaneously.

This initial read **(skim+scan)** helps identify argument flow, pick out answers efficiently, and avoid wasting time rereading irrelevant parts.

**Annotate your insert; this saves time and helps you write your answers without spending too much time on planning.**
