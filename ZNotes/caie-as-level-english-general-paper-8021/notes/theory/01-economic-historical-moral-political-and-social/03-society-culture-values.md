### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Modern society places increasing emphasis on individual responsibility and activism. Social media has amplified individual voices, allowing figures such as Greta Thunberg to influence global debate. However, critics argue that digital activism can be performative, raising questions about whether online action leads to meaningful social change.*
