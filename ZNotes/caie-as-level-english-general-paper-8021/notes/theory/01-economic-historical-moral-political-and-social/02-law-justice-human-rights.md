### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Justice systems aim to balance punishment and rehabilitation. While punitive systems such as those in the US prioritise deterrence, countries like Norway focus on rehabilitation through education and humane prison conditions. Evidence suggests lower reoffending rates in rehabilitative systems, raising questions about whether punishment alone effectively protects society.*
