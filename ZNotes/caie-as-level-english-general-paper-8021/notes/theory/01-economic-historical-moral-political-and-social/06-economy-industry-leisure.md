### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Industry and commerce are central to economic growth, providing employment and raising living standards. In recent decades, globalisation and digitalisation have transformed industry through automation and the rise of e-commerce, as seen in companies such as Amazon. While these developments increase efficiency and consumer access, they also contribute to environmental damage, job displacement, and market domination by large corporations. Similarly, sport and leisure play an important social role by promoting health and national unity, yet increasing commercialisation has raised concerns about corruption, inequality, and the prioritisation of profit over wellbeing.*
