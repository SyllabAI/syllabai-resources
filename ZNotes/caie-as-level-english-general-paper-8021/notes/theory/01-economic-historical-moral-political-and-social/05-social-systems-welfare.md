### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Social systems such as welfare, employment, and migration exist to support populations and maintain social stability. Welfare systems aim to reduce poverty and protect vulnerable groups, yet they are frequently criticised for encouraging dependency or placing financial strain on taxpayers. Migration can provide essential labour and economic growth, but it also generates political tension and fears over cultural integration. Additionally, changes in work, including automation and the gig economy, have increased job insecurity, particularly for young people, raising concerns about long-term economic inequality.*
