### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

### *A key issue in modern politics is the distribution of power within the state. In democratic systems such as the UK, power is separated between the executive, legislature, and judiciary to prevent abuse. However, critics argue that this separation is weaker in practice, particularly when executives dominate legislatures. This can be contrasted with authoritarian states such as Russia, where power is concentrated under strong leadership, often justified through nationalism.*
