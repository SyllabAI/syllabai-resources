### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH**

*History plays a crucial role in shaping national identity and collective memory. By studying past events, societies can learn from previous mistakes and develop informed citizenship. However, history is not always taught objectively, as governments may selectively emphasise or omit events to promote national pride or political agendas. This is evident in debates over colonial history and how different countries teach events such as World War II. Similarly, education systems aim to empower individuals economically and socially, but inequalities in access and curriculum relevance often limit their effectiveness.*
