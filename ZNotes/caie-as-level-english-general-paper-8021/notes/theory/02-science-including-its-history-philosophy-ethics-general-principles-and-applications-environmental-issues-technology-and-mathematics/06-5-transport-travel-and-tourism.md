### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Transport and tourism play a vital role in globalisation by supporting economic growth, cultural exchange, and mobility. However, mass tourism and frequent air travel have contributed to environmental degradation, overcrowding, and inequality. While sustainable transport and eco-tourism offer potential solutions, critics argue that meaningful change requires stronger government regulation and shifts in consumer behaviour.*
