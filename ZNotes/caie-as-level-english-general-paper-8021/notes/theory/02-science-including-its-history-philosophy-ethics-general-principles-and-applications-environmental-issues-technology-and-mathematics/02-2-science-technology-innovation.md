### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Scientific and technological innovation drives progress in areas such as space exploration, ICT, and applied mathematics. Space programs contribute to research, employment, and technological advancement, though critics argue resources could be better spent addressing problems on Earth. Similarly, ICT has transformed communication and education but has increased concerns over privacy, cybercrime, and inequality. Mathematics underpins these innovations, highlighting its essential role in modern society.*
