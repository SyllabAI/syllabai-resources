**IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Modern societies face growing tensions between security, privacy, and access to essential resources. Surveillance technologies can improve safety and governance, yet they risk eroding civil liberties and personal freedom, particularly in authoritarian states. At the same time, food and water security are increasingly threatened by climate change, population growth, and conflict, raising ethical questions over resource ownership, technological solutions, and global responsibility.*
