**IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Medical and scientific advances have significantly improved quality of life, yet they raise serious ethical concerns. Technologies such as gene editing and AI in healthcare offer the potential to prevent disease and improve diagnosis, but critics argue they risk inequality, misuse, and loss of human values. Drug testing and vaccine development further highlight tensions between profit, safety, and access, as seen during the COVID-19 pandemic. These debates raise the fundamental question of whether healthcare should be treated as a human right or a privilege.*
