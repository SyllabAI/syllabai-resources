### **IDEAL EXAM PARAGRAPH:**

*Environmental sustainability is one of the greatest challenges facing the modern world. Human activities such as industrialisation and deforestation have contributed to climate change, pollution, and biodiversity loss. While international agreements and renewable energy offer solutions, progress is often slow and unequal. Natural disasters further expose global inequalities, particularly between rural and urban areas and between developed and developing countries.*
