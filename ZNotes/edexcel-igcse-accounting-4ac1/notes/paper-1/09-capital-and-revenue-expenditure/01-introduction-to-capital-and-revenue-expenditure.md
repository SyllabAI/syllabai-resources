# Capital and Revenue Expenditure

## Introduction to Capital and Revenue Expenditure

A business organisation incurs expenditures for various purposes. Some of these expenditures are meant to bring in more profits in the long term, while some of these expenditures are for the short term.

* The main reason for incurring expenditure is to increase the efficiency of the business and drive in higher returns.

* The two types of expenditure incurred are :

  * Capital Expenditure

  * Revenue Expenditure
