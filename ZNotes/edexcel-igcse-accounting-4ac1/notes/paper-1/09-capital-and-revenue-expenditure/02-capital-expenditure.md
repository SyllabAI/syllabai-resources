## Capital Expenditure

Expenditures that are incurred by an organization for **long-term** benefits are known as **capital expenditure**.

* They increase the capacity or capabilities of the long-term assets by either enhancing or adding new assets to the organization.

* These expenditures are added to the asset side of the balance sheet.

  Examples of Capital expenditure :

  * Premise purchased

  * Cost of adding air conditioning to offices.

  * New machinery

  * Installation of machinery

  * Delivery charges
