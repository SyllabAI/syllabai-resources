## Revenue Expenditure

Expenditures which are incurred by an organization to manage the **day-to-day** functions of a business are called **revenue expenditure**.

* These expenditures don’t help in the creation of assets or the reducing the liabilities of a business.

* They are **recurring** in nature and are very essential to maintain the daily operations of a business of an organization.

  Examples of Revenue expenditure :

  * Rent of premises

  * Maintenance of assets

  * Repairs to Van

  * Carriage on purchases and revenue

  * Legal charges for debt collection

### The Two Categories of Revenue Expenditure

1. Expenditures for generating revenue for a business.

   * These are the expenditures that are essential for meeting the operational cost of a business, hence these are classified as operating expenses.

2. Expenditures for maintaining revenue-generating assets.

   * These are the expenditures incurred by the business towards repair and maintenance of the assets of an organization to keep them in working condition without enhancing their lifespan.

   * Therefore, such expenses can be towards repaired and repainting of assets.
