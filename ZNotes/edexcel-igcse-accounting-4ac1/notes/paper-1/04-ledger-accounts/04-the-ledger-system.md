## The Ledger System

 The system comprises of removing the transaction of the account and placing the other part of the duality on the opposite side of the ledger according to the golden rules of accounting, to put it to perspective, here’s an example :

You are making a **Rent** Ledger Account.

* Paid rent in cash $5000.

  * This transaction can be split into two parts using the duality concept :

* Rent as **debit**, 5000. Cash as **credit**, 5000.

  * Now strike off the account with the name of the transaction, in this case, rent :

* ~~Rent as **debit**, 5000~~. Cash as **credit** 5000.

Finally, when recording the remaining account in the ledger, **record it on the opposite side**, in this case, record cash on the debit side :

**Please note** : when writing down the dates for any ledger account, do not use the dd/mm/yy format, instead, specify the month of when the transaction took place.

### Summary

* Account name will be given on top of the ledger.

* Never record the transaction directly.

* Identify the debit and credit parts of each transaction.

* The name of the ledger account will be part of the transaction as either debit or credit, strike that part off.

* The part you are left with, write it on the opposite side.

  * Suppliers have an opening balance on the credit side – Trade payables.

  * Customers have an opening balance on the debit side – Trade receivables.

  * If a discount and an amount is given, both are to be written on different accounts but on the same side together.

* When calculating the closing balance, the new balance brought down should end up on the opposite side.
