## Types of Ledgers

It can be difficult for a business to record all ledger accounts in one book, so it is necessary to divide up the ledger. This enables the same types of accounts to be kept together.

There are three types of ledgers :

* Revenue Ledger or **Trade Receivables Ledger**.

  * Personal accounts of all credit customers.

* Purchases Ledger or **Trade Payables Ledger**.

  * Personal accounts of all credit suppliers.

* Nominal Ledger or **General Ledger**.

  * All other types of accounts.
