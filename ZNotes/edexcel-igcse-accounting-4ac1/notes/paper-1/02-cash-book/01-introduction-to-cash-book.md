# Cash Book

## Introduction to Cash Book

Cash book is a book of original entry; **it only includes transactions which include money or cash using the duality concep**t.

* It uses **receipts** as **debits** and **payments** as **credit**.

### Features of a cash book

* Acts as both a journal and a ledger.

* Can be used as an alternative to a cash account for recording transactions.

* It follows the dual entry system of accounting (i.e Debit and Credit transactions).

* The debit side should always be identical to the credit side.

* Cash book should always have a debit balance.
