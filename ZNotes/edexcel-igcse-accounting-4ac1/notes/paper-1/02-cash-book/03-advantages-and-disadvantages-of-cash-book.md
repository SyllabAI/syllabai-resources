## Advantages of Cash Book

#|
||

Advantages

|

Disadvantages

||
||

Using the cash book balance and cash in hand total, it is easier to identify and verify errors or mistakes in the entry.

|

Only cash and bank transactions are recorded. Credit transactions, such as credit sales and credit purchases, must be recorded in other books of original entry.

||
||

It creates a record of transactions that occur on a regular basis which is convenient for accounting purposes.

|

Errors can still occur. Mistakes in recording amounts, dates, or the wrong side of the cash book can slip through.

||
||

Cash fraud is more noticeable using a cash book.

|

Maintaining a cash book can be time-consuming.

||
||

Due to it being maintained date wise, transactions can be traced back to the cash book.

|

It does not prevent fraud completely. Although a cash book can help identify suspicious transactions, it cannot stop fraud or theft if entries are intentionally falsified or omitted.
