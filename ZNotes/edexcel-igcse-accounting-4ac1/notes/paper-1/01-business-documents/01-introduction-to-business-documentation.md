
# Business Documentation

## Introduction to Business Documentation

All businesses and organizations are involved in trading. Trading is the buying and selling of goods and services so that you can make a profit.

* The financial transaction of buying and selling involves some very important documents, both the buyer and the seller require these documents in order to be able to prepare their accounts.

There are 7 business documents relating to trading, namely purchase order, invoice, credit note, statement of account, remittance advice, receipts and petty cashier voucher.
