## How to Calculate Depreciation

### Straight Line Method

The formula for slm is (Original cost - Salvage value) / Life of the asset (in years)

* The depreciation of slm will always remain the same every year, therefore, as an example, if the depreciated value is $800, next year, it will remain that amount subtracted by the original value of the asset.

### Reducing Balance Method

The depreciation of rbm will always change every year, we’ll take an example to explain this.

* For instance, Original value of the asset is 50000, and the depreciation percentage is 5% on the reducing balance method. 3 years have passed.
* The first year disposal value will be calculated as 50000 times 5%, which is 2500, this amount subtracted from the original value of the asset is 47500, this is the new value.
* The next year disposal value will now be calculated using 47500 times 5% instead, which leads to a lower disposal of 2375, and this amount subtracted from the new value will be 45125, the newest depreciated value of the asset after 2 years.
* The cycle repeats every year until the asset has become unusable.
