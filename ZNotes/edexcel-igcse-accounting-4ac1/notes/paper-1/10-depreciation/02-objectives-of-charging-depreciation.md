## Objectives of Charges Depreciation

1. To ascertain the accurate amount of profit and loss.

   * By charging depreciation as an expense on the income statement, we are anticipating a future loss and therefore, profit is not overstated.

2. To show the fixed asset at it’s proper value in the balance sheet.

### Which assets should be depreciated?

All assets that have a finite useful lives should be depreciated. But mainly, **non current assets** will have depreciation noted down in the final accounts of a sole trader.

* Therefore, depreciation should be provided on all assets except freehold land, which does not have a finite useful life.

* Land that is used in an extractive industry, such as quarries and mines, will lose value as the minerals are extracted and should be depreciated.
