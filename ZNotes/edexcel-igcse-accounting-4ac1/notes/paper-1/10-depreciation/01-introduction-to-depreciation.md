# Depreciation

## Introduction to Depreciation

Depreciation is the reduction in the value of non-current assets due to the passage of time or due to their use in the business.

* It is the part of the cost of a fixed asset that is consumed during the period during which it is used by the business.

* It reduces the book value of the asset but not its market value.

### Causes of Depreciation

1. Wear and Tear

   * Assets become worn out through use.

2. Obsolescence

   * This means a decrease in the usefulness of an asset because it is becoming out of date, old-fashioned, etc.

3. Passage of Time

   * The asset’s useful life reduces over time.
