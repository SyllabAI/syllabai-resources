## The Accounting Rule of DEAD CLIC

In every account book or journal, we abide by the golden rules of accounting when classifying whether an account is debit or credit. However, in trial balance, we do not use these rules, instead we use **DEAD CLIC**.

* DEAD – Debit

  * D is for **Debit.**

  * E is for **Expenses.**

  * A is for **Assets.**

  * D is for **Drawings.**

* CLIC – Credit

  * C is for **Credit.**

  * L is for **Liabilities.**

  * I is for **Income.**

  * C is for **Capital.**

Therefore, when classifying any account into a debit or credit account we follow these rules.

* Only in the case where the account is either a purchase or sales account, you cannot apply the rules of DEAD CLIC and have to use the **golden rules of accounting**.

Examples :

1. Insurance.

   * Insurance is an expense, therefore debit.

2. Commission received.

   * Commission received is an income, therefore credit.

3. Trade payable.

   * Trade payable is a liability, therefore credit.

4. Purchases.

   * Purchases come into the business’s inventory, therefore according to the golden rules of accounting, it is classified as debit.
