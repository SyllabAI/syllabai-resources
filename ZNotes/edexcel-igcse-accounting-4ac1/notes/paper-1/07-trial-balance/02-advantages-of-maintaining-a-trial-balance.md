## Advantages of Maintaining a Trial Balance

The purpose of a trial balance is to verify whether the debit and credit balances of the ledgers are equal, but a trial balance is also useful to maintain for more reasons, such as follows :

* Arithmetical accuracy.

* All balances in the ledger accounts are in one place.

* Errors in trial balances can be rectified easily.

* The final accounts for a year utilizes the data collected from a trial balance.
