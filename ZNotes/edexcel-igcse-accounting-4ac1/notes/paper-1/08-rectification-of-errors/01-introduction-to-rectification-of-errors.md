# Rectification of Errors

## Introduction to Rectification of Errors

In a trial balance, both debit and credit sides must line up, sometimes it doesn’t balance due to errors in the accounts, but sometimes it does due to the errors compensating each side.

* The types of errors which can occur are :

  * Error of Omission

  * Error of Commission

  * Error of Principle

  * Error of Original Entry

  * Error of Complete Reversal

  * Compensation Error
