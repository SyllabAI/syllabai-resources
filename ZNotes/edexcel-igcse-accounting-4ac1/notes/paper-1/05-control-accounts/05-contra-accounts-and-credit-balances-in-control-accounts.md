## Contra Accounts and Credit Balances in Control Accounts

### Contra/Set off Accounts in Control Accounts

When a person is written in both the trade receivables control ledger a/c and the trade payables control ledger a/c, it is named as a **contra/set off account**. 

* At the end of the financial month, the lower balance is transferred to the higher balance, evening out both accounts, this is called setting off.

### Credit Balances in Control Accounts

If a trade receivables control ledger a/c’s closing balance ends up on the debit side or the opening balance ends up on the credit side, it could mean :

* When customers have returned goods and the accounts are settled.

* When the customer has made an overpayment by accident.

* There are some errors in the ledgers, such as a wrong amount or duplication.

If a trade payables control ledger a/c’s closing balance ends up on the credit side or the opening balance ends up on the debit side, it could mean :

* When suppliers have gotten returned goods and the accounts are settled.

* When the supplier has made an overpayment by accident.

* There are some errors in the ledgers, such as a wrong amount or duplication.
