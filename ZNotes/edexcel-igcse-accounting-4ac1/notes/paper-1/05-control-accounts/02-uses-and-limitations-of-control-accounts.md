## Uses and Limitations of Control Accounts

Uses of a control account :

* To find errors in revenue or purchase ledgers

* Frauds are easy to spot since the transfers are made under the scrutiny of an official.

* Amounts by which are due from trade receivables and which you are due from trade payables can be obtained easily.

* Reference and accounting purposes.

Limitations of a control account :

* Control accounts will itself contain errors.

* Control accounts cannot guarantee no errors in the individual accounts if they include a compensating error.
