## Petty Cash Book Guidelines

* A petty cash book differs from a cash book in the sense that it doesn’t have an opening balance per say, rather it has a **Float**.

* Every month, the float needs to be restored. The total of all payments that occurred in that month is the exact amount of money needed to restore the float. The **float** amount should be entered in the receipts column.

* Each transaction will have all the data spread out, the **date**, **particular**, **voucher number**, and **amount spent** will be explicitly shown.

* The details will specify what type of transaction it is and so you can use it to determine which column the amount spent should be sorted into. It is also important to note that the amount spent should always be entered into the payments column as well.

* Every **column** is to be totaled up.

* When totaling the payments column, the total amount should be entered into the particulars as cash and then brought to the receipts column. When the receipts column is totaled up (but not entered as a total yet), the closing balance is written in the payments column, it is found through the subtraction of the total receipts column and the total payments column and is always going to become the **original float value**.

* The new opening balance is always going to be the **original** float value.
