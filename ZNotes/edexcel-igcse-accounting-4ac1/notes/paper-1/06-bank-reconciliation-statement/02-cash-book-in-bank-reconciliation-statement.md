
## Cash Book in Bank Reconciliation Statement

When preparing a bank reconciliation statement, we also have to prepare a bank statement/updated cash book.

* **A bank statement is a copy of a customer’s account in the books of a bank**.

It has a ledger format but it’s rules are completely unique :

* Receiving money is credited

* Giving money is debited

  * If the updated cash book balance is on the debit side, it is negative.

  * If the updated cash book balance is on the credit side, it is positive.

The updated cash book is **prepared from the point of view of the bank**.

* When the bank receives money, it is a trade payable for them, therefore credited.

* When the bank gives money, it is a trade receivable for them, therefore debited.

1. Always assume the bank is right.

2. Identify the bank transactions that don’t appear in the cash book and pass book respectfully.

   * Direct debit : An individual allows an organization they are liable to, to directly collect varied amounts of debt from their bank account.

   * Credit transfer : An amount another individual paid directly into another person’s bank account.

   * Standing order : A specific set of instructions given to the bank to pay fixed amounts on specific dates.

3. Make the updated cash book with the missing transactions.

4. Take the closing balance and make the bank statement in accordance.

   * Balance as per pass book must match bank balance.
