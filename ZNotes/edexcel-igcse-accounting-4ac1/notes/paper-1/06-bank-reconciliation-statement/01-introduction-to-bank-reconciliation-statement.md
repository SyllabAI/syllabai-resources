# Bank Reconciliation Statement

## Introduction to Bank Reconciliation Statement

A bank reconciliation statement is a statement that **reconciles the balance as per the cash book and the balance as per the bank statement**.

* It shows the causes of the difference between the two.

The uses of a bank reconciliation statement are as follows :

* Locates errors in the cash book and bank statement.

* Reveals the correct amount of cash at bank.

* A delay in the clearance of the cheque will be shown.

* Discourages and reduces fraud.

### Reasons for the need for a Bank Reconciliation Statement

There are a multitude of reasons why there could be a difference between a bank statement’s balance and a cash book’s balance. Such as :

* Timing differences.

  1. There will be a delay between the items being entered into the cash book and their entry into the bank statement.

* Cheque lodgments.

  1. Cheques deposited but not collected by the bank.

* Unpresented cheques.

  1. Cheques issued for payment but have not yet been paid.
