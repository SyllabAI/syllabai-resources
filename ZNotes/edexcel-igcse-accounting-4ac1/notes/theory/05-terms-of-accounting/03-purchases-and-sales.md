## Purchases and Sales

### Purchases

* **Purchases** : The amount of goods bought by a business for resale or for use in production.

* Goods purchased by cash are called **cash purchases**, they are purchases in which you directly pay for on the spot.

* Goods purchased by credit are called **credit purchases**, they are purchases in which you are given the goods but are obligated to pay later.

  * **Total Purchase = Cash Purchase \+ Credit Purchase**

### Returns Outward

Goods returned to suppliers due to defective quality or not as per the terms of purchase are called **Purchase Return** or **Returns Outward**.

* To find net purchases, purchase return is deducted from the total purchases.

  * **Net Purchases = Total Purchases - Returns Outward**

### Trade Payables

Trade payables means the people whom you have brought from on credit, meaning you still owe them money for it and are liable to them since they can ask for, they money back at any time, making this is a **liability**.

### Sales

* **Sales** : The amount of goods sold that are already bought or manufactured by the business.

* Goods sold for cash are called **cash sales**, they are sales in which you receive the payment directly on the spot.

* Goods sold for credit are called **credit sales**, they are sales in which you give the goods away and the customer is obligated to pay later.

  * **Total Sales = Cash Sales \+ Credit Sales**

### Returns Inward

Goods returned by customers due to defective quality or not as per the terms of sale are called **Sales Return** or **Returns Inward**.

* To find net sales, sales return is deducted from the total sales.

  * **Net Sales = Total Sales - Returns Inward**

### Trade Receivables

Trade receivables means the people who have bought a good from you on credit, meaning they still owe you money for it and they are liable to you since you can ask for the money back at any time, making them your **asset**.
