## Expenses and Income

* **Expenses** : The money the business spends to use day to day goods and services, excluding money spent on acquiring assets. (Such as Interest, Bank charges, etc.)

* **Income** : The money the business earns from non-trading operations, excluding equity introduced by the owner. (Such as Rent received, Discount received, etc.)

* **Revenue** : The money earned from selling a product or providing a service to it’s customers. (Such as Sales revenue, Subscription received, etc.)

* **Owner’s Equity** : The amount invested by the proprietor/s in the business, can be either in cash or in the form of assets.

  * Amount is increased by more profit earned or additional capital introduced.

  * Amount is decreased by losses incurred or amounts withdrawn.

* **Drawings** : The amount of cash or value of goods withdrawn from the business by the proprietor for their own personal use and is deducted from the capital.

### Other Receivables and Other Payables

* When an income is pending, it is considered a **current asset**.

* When an income is paid in advance, it is considered a **current liability**.

* When an expense is pending, it is considered a **current liability**.

* When an expense is paid in advance, it is considered a **current asset**.
