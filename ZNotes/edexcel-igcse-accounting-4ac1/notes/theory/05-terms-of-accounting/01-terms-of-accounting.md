# Terms of Accounting

## Assets and Liabilities

### Assets

* **Assets** : Economic resources (things of value) owned by a business that are expected to benefit future operations.

* **Current assets** : Any asset that can be easily converted into cash within one calendar year. They are useful for the day to day operation of business. (Such as Cash, Trade receivables, etc.)

* **Non current assets** : Asset which is purchased with the intention of using the business and to generate regular revenue out of that asset is known as non current assets. (Such as Machinery, Motor vehicles, etc.)

* **Frozen assets** : An asset which has been legally blocked from the owner and therefore cannot be accessed, sold, spent or transferred.

### Liabilities

* **Liabilities** : All the debts and money owed to others by the business.

* **Current Liabilities** : Debts that are payable within one year of the balance sheet date and which will require the use of a current asset. (Such as Trade payables, Short-term loans, etc.)

* **Non Current Liabilities** : Debts and other non-debt financial obligations which are due after a period of at least one year from the date of the balance sheet. (Such as Long-term bank loans, Equity, etc.)

### Accounting Equation

This equation is the foundation of double entry accounting. The accounting equation displays that all assets are either financed by borrowing money or paying with the money of the company’s owner. It is also known as the Balance Sheet equation and uses the duality concept.
