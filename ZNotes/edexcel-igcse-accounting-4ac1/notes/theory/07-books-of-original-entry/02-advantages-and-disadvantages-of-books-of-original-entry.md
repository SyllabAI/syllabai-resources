## Advantages and Disadvantages of Books of Original Entry

#|
||

Advantages

|

Disadvantages

||
||

Daily transactions are recorded in the books, which reduces the chance of omissions.

|

Journals can be quite bulky which makes it difficult to manage the data.

||
||

Books of original entry records details as well as summary of transactions, which help in tracing any error in recording.

|

It takes a lot of time and effort to record transactions in a ledger from the journals.

||
||

Transactions are recorded in a chronological order which helps in organizing the transactions into separate ledger.

|

During manual transfer of entries from journal to the ledger, risk of transposition errors increases.
