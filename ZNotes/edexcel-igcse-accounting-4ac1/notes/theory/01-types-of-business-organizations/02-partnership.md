## Partnership

Unlike a sole trader, a partnership can have between 2 to 20 owners. A contract is drawn up to show how profits and losses are shared.

* In a partnership, all owners have **unlimited liability**. All of them share financial responsibility over the business.

* The contract which details the profits shared amongst all the owners is called a **deed of partnership**.

* **Limited partnerships** are partnerships where some of the owners have **limited liability**, in a partnership like this, there should always be one unlimited partner.

* Limited partners are not liable for the debts of the business.

* Partnerships do not have any legal obligation to publish their accounts to public, unlike the other types of business organizations.

### Deed of Partnership

A deed of partnership is a contract between partners which could either be verbal or written. While a deed of partnership is not required by law, it helps solve issues between partners when they arise.

A deed of partnership includes:

* The equity contributed by each partner

* The ratio at which profits and losses are shared

* Salaries to be paid to partners

* Interest, if required, which is to be paid on equity

* Interest, if required, which is to be charged on partners’ **drawings**.
