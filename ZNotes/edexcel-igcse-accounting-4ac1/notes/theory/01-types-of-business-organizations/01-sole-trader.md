# Types of Business Organizations

## Sole Trader

A sole trader is an individual trading alone under his or her own name, or under a trading name. This means the sole trader is responsible for running their own business, and are financially responsible for it.

* Therefore, in the eyes of the law, the sole trader has **unlimited liability**. Or in other words, the owner and the business are the same.

* If the business is unable to meet financial obligations and cannot pay its bills, a court has the authority to hand over the sole trader’s personal assets to cover the business’s debts.

* Sole trader businesses do not have any legal obligation to publish their final accounts to the public, unlike the other types of business organizations.
