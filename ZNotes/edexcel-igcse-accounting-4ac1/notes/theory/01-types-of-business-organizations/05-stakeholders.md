## Stakeholders

A stakeholder is defined as a person or business who is affected by the actions of a business. This effect can either be positive or negative.

* Owner(s)

  * Look at the overall profitability of the business.

  * Monitor how the business traded over the previous financial year.

  * Compare the business performance with the previous financial year to gauge whether they earn a return on investment and if the equity is safe.

  * Look at the income statement to assess whether they receive any share of profit.

* Manager(s)

  * Run the business on the behalf of the owners.

  * Monitor the accounts to see how the business is performing.

  * Make decisions based on the financial data available.

* Employees

  * May look at the accounts to judge business performance to gauge whether they have job security.

  * Looks at the income statement to assess the overall profitability of the business.

  * May use the accounts to secure a pay rise.

* Customers

  * Looks at how financially stable the company is.

  * Assess whether the supply of goods and services, and whether they should trade with the company

* Suppliers

  * Looks at the accounts to see how financially stable the business is.

  * Assesses what credit terms to give and how much interest to charge.

* Providers of External Finance

  * Assesses the company’s ability to pay back any money they lend the business, such as loans.

  * Looks at the statement of financial position to assess the liquidity of the business.

* Government

  * Looks at the profits of the business to monitor whether the business is paying enough tax.
