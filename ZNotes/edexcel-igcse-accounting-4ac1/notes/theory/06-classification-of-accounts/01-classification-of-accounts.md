# Classification of Accounts

## The 3 categories of transactions

When classifying accounts, transactions are to be divided into three categories.

* Transactions relating to individuals and firms.

* Transactions relating to properties like assets, goods or cash.

* Transactions relating to expenses or losses and incomes or gains.

Therefore, we are given three classes of accounts to sort such accounts into, known as **Real Accounts**, **Personal Accounts**, and **Nominal Accounts** respectively.
