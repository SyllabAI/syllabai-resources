## Real Accounts

Real accounts only relate to the possessions, properties, and assets which are owned by the business, i.e buildings, machinery, etc.

* This is then further separated into tangible and non-tangible accounts.
  * Tangible assets are like Cash, fixtures and fittings, computer equipment, inventory, etc.

  * Intangible assets are like Patents, trademarks, copyrights, goodwill, etc.

These accounts exist to **manage the assets that leave and enter the business’s possession**.
