## Nominal Accounts

Nominal accounts only relate to incomes and expenses for a business, i.e purchases and revenue.

* All of these accounts do not have any form, shape or physical existence. They instead exist as ledger entries in accounting books.

* Nominal accounts are temporary, they reset to zero at the end of each financial year.

* Their balances are transferred to the income statement at the end of the year to calculate whether the business made a net profit, or a net loss.

The nominal accounts are made to document **incomes, expenses, gains and losses**.
