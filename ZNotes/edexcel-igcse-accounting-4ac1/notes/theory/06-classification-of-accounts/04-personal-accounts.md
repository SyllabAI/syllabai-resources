## Personal Accounts

Personal accounts only relate to people or businesses, i.e the trade receivables and trade payables.

They include the following :

1. Natural persons

   * Accounts which relate to individuals. For an example, Leon’s a/c, Ada’s a/c, Chris’s a/c, etc.

2. Artificial persons

   * Accounts which relate to a group of persons or a firm. For an example, IP Corp, Belobog Ind, Northland Bank, etc.

   * Do note that the proprietor of the business has their own capital and drawings accounts, both of which fall under the classification of Personal.

In the simplest terms, personal accounts deal with **the secondary party in a transaction**.
