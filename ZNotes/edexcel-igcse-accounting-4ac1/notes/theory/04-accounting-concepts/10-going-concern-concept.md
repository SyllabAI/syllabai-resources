## Concept 9 : Going Concern

Accounting always **assumes that the business will continue to operate for an indefinite period of time**.

* The final accounts of a business are prepared on the basis that there is no intention to close down the business.

* Due to this concept, the non current assets of the business appear in the balance sheet at book value (Cost - Depreciation).

* Where it is expected the business will cease to trade in the near future, the value of assets in the balance sheet will have to be adjusted.
