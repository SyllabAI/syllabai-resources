Concepts

Definition

||
||

Business Entity

|

Business has a separate legal identity from that of its owner.

||
||

Money Measurement

|

Transactions which can be expressed in terms of money are recorded in the ledger accounts.

||
||

Consistency

|

Transactions should be recorded in the same way for all future accounting periods.

||
||

Accruals

|

Profit of the year = Revenue - Expenses.

||
||

Prudence

|

Profits and Losses should not be overstated in the final accounts.

||
||

Materiality

|

If the value of an item is relatively small, it is not significant enough to have a specific ledger.

||
||

Realization

|

Profit is only earned once the customer has agreed to pay or paid for the good or service.

||
||

Duality

|

Debit and credit aspects of a transaction cannot exist without each other and equal the same value.

||
||

Going Concern

|

The assumption that a business will operate for the indefinite future.
