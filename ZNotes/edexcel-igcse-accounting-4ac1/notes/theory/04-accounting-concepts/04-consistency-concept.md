## Concept 3 : Consistency

Transactions of a similar nature should be **recorded in the same way in the accounting period and in all future accounting periods**.

* This is important to ensure that the profits/losses of different periods, and balance sheets can be compared meaningfully.

* So if company X was using the straight line method to calculate their depreciation for the final accounts of the financial year, they cannot switch the depreciation method to reducing balance in order to remain consistent in their final accounts.
