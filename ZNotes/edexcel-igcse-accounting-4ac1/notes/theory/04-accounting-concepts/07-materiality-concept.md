## Concept 6 : Materiality

This concept applies **when the value of an item is relatively small and doesn’t warrant separate recording**.

* Purchases like a box of paper clips, a calculator, or a small clock for the office are considered small expenditures.

* Such purchases needn’t not be included in the main expense account, and have separate expense accounts to be recorded in.

* Materiality also relates to the classification of some items.

* Stationary such as staplers or hole punches, which typically last for more than one year, but would be incorrect to classify as a non-current asset.

* Therefore, they are recorded in expenses, and should not be included in the statement of financial position because of a lack of resale value.
