## Online Bank Accounts and Apps

Online accounting systems record all customer transactions, like customer receipts, supplier payments, other payments and receipts.

* Many organizations have an online bank account as this means their records are all automatically updated and are up to date always.

* All receipts and payments are linked to personal accounts of the **trade receivables** and **trade payables**.

* The system automatically updates these accounts with the newer transactions.

* Online banking apps are now available to download on many phones, making your bank account accessible anywhere, so long as you have internet access. Otherwise known as mobile banking.

* Accounting systems automatically update the ledger.

### Wages and Salaries

* Because of online banking, options such as combined computerised accounting and wages/salaries packages open up.

* Using separate payroll packages are also alternatives, such packages perform all the necessary payroll functions.

### Inventory Control

Inventory control includes raw materials and ordinary goods purchased for resale.

* This means that the inventory records are updated automatically after each **revenue invoice** or **purchase invoice** is entered into the system.

* This allows an accurate figure of inventory is held in the accounting system at all times.

* It is crucial that the inventory stays up to date for the end of financial year inventory checkup.

* Inventory lists are used in the physical inventory check to ensure variances in the data are identified and amended.
