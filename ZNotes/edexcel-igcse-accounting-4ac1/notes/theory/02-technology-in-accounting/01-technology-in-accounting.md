# Technology in Accounting

In the modern accounting sphere, businesses are using specially written IT software to write their accounts, even small businesses can afford computerised accounting systems to record financial information and process data digitally.

## Internet Access

Organizations now use their own websites to advertise their products and services online.

* Internet access enables online banking, making payments of suppliers and employees easier.

* Online websites offer online ordering systems and management.

* The internet is a useful source of information and data, which organization need access to.

* Allows customers to deal with the business directly, with all the transactions carried out online.

* Some businesses have shifted to online only plans, meaning that they do not have an in person store and officially operate only remotely.
