## Functions of Accounting Software

Accounting software allows financial statements to be produced more quickly than traditional hand written methods. Pre-programmed formulae and layouts allow for financial data to directly be entered once.

* The software uses copies of the data in order to utilize it for various purposes, and then stores the data in different ways.

* The software can use the data provided to complete all aspects of a transaction, like generating documentation and providing reporting functions.

### Revenue

During the preparation and printing of revenue invoices, credit notes and month end statements, data from the respective documents is entered, processed and recorded.

* Data is placed in customer accounts in the receivables ledger.

* Data is placed by automatic update of inventory records.

### Purchases

When the data from purchase invoices and credit notes are received, the data from the respective documents is entered, processed and recorded.

* Data is placed in supplier accounts in the payables ledger.

* Data is placed by automatic update of inventory records.

* Data is printed out of remittance.
