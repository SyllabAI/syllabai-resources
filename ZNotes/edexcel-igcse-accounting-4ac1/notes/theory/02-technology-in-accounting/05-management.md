## Management

A computerised accounting system allows the owners/managers of a business to access financial data and reports with ease. At the end of every month or specific accounting period like a financial year, month end/year end functions are carried out to provide overall reports, just as the following:

* Day books for customers and suppliers

* Nominal/General ledgers and bank account transactions

* Activity reports on all ledger transactions

* Audit trails

* Analysis report for aged trade receivables and trade payables

* Financial statements such as a trial balance, income statement and statement of financial position

* Ratio analysis

* Reports for human resources and payroll (such as staff attendance, etc.)
