Disadvantages

||

Introduction of a new system may cause conflicts among staff and disruptions in the business.

||

||

System downtime can be very disruptive.

||

|

Data back-up at regular intervals is necessary to protect data.

Fraudulent access can seriously affect the business’s operation and it’s profitability.

||

||
