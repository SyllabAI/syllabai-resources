## Accounting Roles and Functions

These are various roles in an accounting department, usually they are organized in different ways and operate differently, but they typically cover these five areas:

* Accounts payable

  * Responsible for keeping check on the money that goes out of the business to pay for bills and expenses.

  * Maintaining good relations with trade payables and checking the business is getting the best deals.

  * Ensures no costs are incurred for late payments and no interest is incurred.

* Accounts receivable

  * Responsible for checking that the business has received all the money it is entitled to.

  * Tracks invoices to make sure they are paid on time.

  * ‘Friendly’ reminder services are set up for customers to make sure no irrecoverable debts occur.

* Payroll

  * Ensuring that all staff are paid on time and that government receives the correct amount of tax.

* Reporting and financial statements

  * Preparing financial reports for internal and external use.

  * Budgets and forecasting to check the viability of a new product or store.

  * All financial statements and information which could be used for possible investors, banks and other professionals involved in the development of the company may also be provided.

* Checking the accounts.

  * Responsible for controlling the finances of the business.

  * Ensures that the correct procedures are followed and the correct accounting standards are adhered to.

  * Puts systems in place to show compliance and to protect against fraud and theft.

  * This part of the accounting department is otherwise known as the **internal audit**.
