## Principle 6 : Public Interest

This principle highlights that all accountants need to behave in the public interest, or in the manner so that the **public has confidence that profits stated are true and fair**.

* Professional accountants must not mislead the public with overstated figures when producing financial accounts for a firm.

### “Public”

For accountants, the term public refers to the following:

* Investors, owners and business owners of public sector and private sector organizations.

  * Require good financial information that is free from bias.

  * Base their actions and decisions on the most reliable data.

* Customers and competitors.

  * Rely on financial information as decisions based on this data could have significant impacts on the costs and benefits to them.

* Taxpayers

  * Any financial information which has a negative impact on taxpayers is of significant interest to them.

### “Interest”

All things that are valued by society is called interest.

* This may include the economic wellbeing and certainty in the business environment.

* Sound financial information to be given to stakeholders so that they are not misled.

* Comparing financial information from two different companies allows investors to make the best decisions.

* Sound financial data can improve the efficiency and lower costs of government or private organizations.

‘Interest’ also may include financial information that leads to the protection of the environment and reduces the depletion of natural resources.

### Public Interest

To act in the public interest, accountants must work towards the highest levels of ethical and professional standards.

* Professional accountants have to produce high quality financial statements that are audited to ensure accuracy.

* Professional accountants must adhere to the international code of conduct for all accountants across the globe.

* Professional accountants must guarantee there are rigorous checking systems.

* Professional accountants who behave in an unethical manner are dealt with through strict structures of disciplinary measures and sanctions.
