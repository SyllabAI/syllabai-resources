## Principle 1 : Integrity

This principle highlights **fair dealing and trustworthiness**.

* Professional accountants must remain straightforward and honest in all professional and business relationships.

* Professional accountants must not knowingly produce financial statements or financial reports that contain false or misleading information.

* Professional accountants must not produce financial statements or financial reports that deliberately omit any data which could lead to misunderstandings.

* Professional accountants must not partake in any bribes to alter or change any of the data they present in financial reports or statements.
