# Circular Measure

## Circle Theorems

These theorems **must** be known to solve circular measure questions.


|Circle|Theorem|
|:---|:---|
|![](../../../assets/70ec85854150-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae0664da6139ed896382e4c3c09f.png)|Radius, $OA$, forms a $90^\circ$ degree, or $\frac{\pi}{2}$ radian angle, at the point of contact with a **tangent**.|
|![](../../../assets/a687cb30b093-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae06c32ce4fed05f659b346db18f.28)|The **perpendicular bisector** of a chord, $AB$, of a circle passes through the centre of the circle, $O$.|
|![](../../../assets/9373e5ce9023-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae0697d128afc999498fba33136e.30)|The **angle in a semi circle** is always equal to $90^\circ$ degrees, or $\frac{\pi}{2}$ radians.|
|![](../../../assets/85d7b367ca6a-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae065aa05f99d4f58b641235c6f8.36)|Tangents which meet at the **same point** are **equal in** **length**, $TA = TB$, and angle $a = \text{angle} \sim b$.|
|![](../../../assets/a8a845b94429-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae065883b7b3a096ff725b1e5ef6.39)|The angle at the centre is **twice** the angle at the circumference.|
|![](../../../assets/96cb48d70282-66b4cb318965325f0f4dae06e2f9b275dc40c196b1fb441a.41)|Angles in the **same segment** are equal.|
