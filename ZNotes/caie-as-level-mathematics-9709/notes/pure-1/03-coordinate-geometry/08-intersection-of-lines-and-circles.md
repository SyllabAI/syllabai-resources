## Intersection of lines and circles

* when an equation in the form of $ax^2 +bx + c = 0$ is created from the equation of the line and the circle, the value of $b^2-4ac$ decides the number of intersection points
* if the value of $b^2-4ac$ is
  * $\gt$ 0 then two distinct points of intersection are formed
  * = 0 then a single point of intersection (line is a tangent to the circle) is formed
  * < 0 then there are no points of intersection
