## Translation applied to a Circle Equation

* A translation applied to a circle equation moves the coordinates of the centre and all the points on the circumference.
  * It does not change the radius, $r$.

Consider a circle equation

$$

r^2 = (x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 
$$

With coordinates of the centre as $(a, b)$. To apply a translation of ${c \choose d}$, we can add this to the coordinates of the centre.

$$

(a + c, b + d)
$$

So the equation of the transformed graph is

$$

r^2 = (x - [a + c])^2 + (y - [b + d])^2
$$
