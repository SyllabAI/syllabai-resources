## Stretches

* A stretch multiples all the $x$ or $y$ points on a curve by the stretch factor $a$.

| Function | Stretch factor and direction | Result | Geometric interpretation | New points |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| $f(x)$ | Stretch in the $x$-axis by factor of $\frac{1}{a}$ | $f(ax)$ | Stretch parallel to the $x$-axis by a scale factor of $\frac{1}{a}$ | $\left(\frac{x}{a}, y \right)$ |
| $f(x)$ | Stretch in the $x$-axis by factor of $a$ | $f \left(\frac{x}{a} \right)$ | Stretch parallel to the $x$-axis by a scale factor of $a$ | $(ax, y)$ |
| $f(x)$ | Stretch in the $y$-axis by a factor of $\frac{1}{a}$ | $\frac{f(x)}{a}$ | Stretch parallel to the $y$-axis by a scale factor of $\frac{1}{a}$ | $\left(x, \frac{y}{a} \right)$ |
| $f(x)$ | Stretch in the $y$-axis by a factor of $a$ | $af(x)$ | Stretch parallel to the $y$-axis by a scale factor of $a$ | $(x , ay)$ |

**<center>Example 1</center>**

The graph of $f(x) = 5x^2 + 2$ is stretched by a factor of 2 along the y-axis, find the resulting equation of the graph.

**<center>Answer</center>**

for this stretch factor

$$

 y = 2f(x)
$$

$$
y = 2(5x^2 + 2)
$$

$$

y = 10x^2 + 4
$$

$$
\phantom{invis}

**<center>Example 2</center>**

The graph of $y = x^2 + 3x + 2$ is stretched by a factor of $\frac{1}{3}$ along the x-axis, find the resulting equation of the graph.

**<center>Answer</center>**

For this stretch factor

$$
y = f(3x) 
$$

$$
y = (3x)^2 + 3(3x) + 2
$$

$$

y = 9x^2 + 9x + 2
$$
