## Translations

* A translation moves all the points on a curve in the $x$ or $y$ direction, depending on the translation.

| Function | Transformation | Result | Geometric interpretation | New points |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| $f(x)$ | ${a \choose 0}$ | $f(x - a)$ | Translation of $a$ units in the **rightward**, or **positive**, $x$ direction. | $(x + a, y)$ |
| $f(x)$ | ${-a \choose 0}$ | $f(x + a)$ | Translation of $a$ units in the **leftward**, or **negative**, $x$ direction. | $(x - a, y)$ |
| $f(x)$ | ${0 \choose b}$ | $f(x) + b$ | Translation of $b$ units in the **upwards**, or **positive**, $y$ direction. | $(x, y + b)$ |
| $f(x)$ | ${0 \choose -b}$ | $f(x) - b$ | Translation of $b$ units in the **downwards**, or **negative**, $y$ direction. | $(x, y - b)$ |

**<center>Example</center>**

The graph of $f(x) = x^2 + 3x + 2$ has been translated by the vector ${-1 \choose 3}$. Find the equation of the resulting graph.

**<center>Answer</center>**

Applying the translation to the function $f(x)$, we get:

$$
y = f(x+1) + 3
$$

$$
y = (x+1)^2 + 3(x+1) + 2 + 3
$$

$$
y = x^2 + 5x + 9
$$
