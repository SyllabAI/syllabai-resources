# Momentum

* Linear momentum is a vector quantity, having the same direction as the velocity.
* The units of momentum are $Ν\text{s}$

$$
{Momentum = mass \times velocity}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ {p = mv}
$$

* **Principle of conservation of linear momentum:** when bodies in a
  system interact, total momentum remains constant provided no
  external force acts on the system.
* When both particles A and B move in the same direction

$$
m_Au_A+m_Bu_B=m_Av_A+m_Bv_B
$$

* When both particles $A$ and $B$ move towards each other

$$
m_Au_A-m_Bu_B=m_Av_A+m_Bv_B
$$

* When both particles stick together, their velocity becomes the same after impact and can be considered as a single particle with a combined mass.

$$
m_Au_A+m_Bu_B=(m_A+m_B)v 
$$

**<span class="underline">{SP20-P04} Question 3:</span>**

Three small smooth spheres A, B and C of equal radii and of masses 4 kg,
2 kg and 3 kg respectively, lie in that order in a straight line on a
smooth horizontal plane. Initially, B and C are at rest and A is moving
towards B with speed 6ms<sup>-1</sup>. After the collision with B,
sphere A continues to move in the same direction but with speed
2ms<sup>-1</sup>

i)  Find the speed of B after this collision

ii)  Sphere B collides with C. In this collision these two spheres
coalesce to form an object D. Find the speed of D after this
collision

**<center>Solution:</center>**

**<span class="underline">Part (i)</span>**

Calculate momentum of system before collision:

$$
p = mv
$$

$p = \left( 4 \times 6 \right) + 2\left( 0 \right) + 3\left( 0 \right) = 24\ kgms^{- 1}$

Calculate momentum of system after collision:

$$
p = \left( 4 \times 2 \right) + 2v
$$

Apply conservation of momentum:

$$
total\ momentum\ before = \ total\ momentum\ after
$$

$24 = 8 + 2v$ $v = \frac{16}{2} = 8.0\ ms^{- 1}$

**<span class="underline">Part (ii)</span>**

Calculate momentum of system before collision:

$$
p = \left( 8 \times 2 \right) + \left( 3 \times 0 \right) = 16\ kgms^{- 1}
$$

Calculate momentum of system after collision:

<span class="underline">Note:</span> The masses B and C combine to form
D

$$
p = \left( 2 + 3 \right)v
$$

$$
p = 5v
$$

Apply conservation of momentum:

$$
16 = 5v
$$

$$
v = \frac{16}{5} = 3.2\ ms^{- 1}
$$
