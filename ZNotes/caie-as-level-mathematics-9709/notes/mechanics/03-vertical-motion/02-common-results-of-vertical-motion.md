## Common Results of Vertical Motion

**Finding time taken to reach maximum height by a projectile travelling
in vertical motion:**

$$
v = u + at
$$

* Let $v = 0$ and find $t$
* The time taken to go up and come back to original position would be
  double of this $t$

**Finding maximum height above a launch point use:**

* $v^{2} = u^{2} + 2as$
* Let $v = 0$ and find $s$

**Finding time interval for which a particle is above a given height:**

* Let the height be $H$ and use
* $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^{2}$
* Let $s = H$
* There will be a quadratic equation in$\text{ t}$
* Solve and find the difference between the 2 $t$’s to find the time
  interval

**{S04-P04} Question 7:**

Particle $P_1$ projected vertically upwards, from the horizontal ground, with speed $30ms^{- 1}$. At same instant $P_2$ projected
vertically upwards from tower height $25m$, with speed $10ms^{- 1}$

1. Find the time for which $P_1\ $is higher than the top of the
   tower
2. Find the velocities of the particles at the instant when they are the same height
3. Find the time for which $P_1$ is higher than $P_2$ and
   moving upwards

**<center>Solution</span></center>**

**<span class="underline">Part (i)</span>**

Substitute given values into displacement equation:

$$
25 = \left( 30 \right)t - \frac{1}{2}\left( 10 \right)t^{2}
$$

$$
5t^{2} - 30t + 25 = 0
$$

Solve quadratic for $t$

$$
t = 1s\ or\ 5s
$$

$P_1$ reaches tower at $t = 1$ then passes it again when coming
down at $t = 5s$

Therefore, time above tower $= 5 - 1 = 4$ seconds

**<span class="underline">Part (ii)</span>**

Displacement of $P_1$ is $S_1$, and of $P_2$ is $S_2$ &
relationship:

$$
S_1 = 25 + S_2
$$

Create equations for $S_1$ and $S_2$

$$
S_1 = 30t + \frac{1}{2}\left( - 10 \right)t^{2}\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ S_2 = 10t + \frac{1}{2}\left( - 10 \right)t^{2}
$$

Substitute back into the initial equation

$$
30t + \frac{1}{2}\left( - 10 \right)t^{2} = 25 + 10t + \frac{1}{2}\left( - 10 \right)t^{2}
$$

Simple cancelling

$$
t = 1.25s
$$

Find velocities

$$
v = u + at
$$

$$
V_1 = 30 - 10\left( 1.25 \right) = 17.5ms^{- 1}
$$

$$
V_2 = 10 - 10\left( 1.25 \right) = - 2.5ms^{- 1}
$$

**<span class="underline">Part (iii)</span>**

We know when $P_1$and $P_2$ at the same height $t = 1.25s$. Find the time taken to reach max height for $P_1$

$$
v = u + at
$$

$V$ is $0\ $at max height

$0 = 30 - 10t\ \ \ \ t = 3s$

Time for $P_1$ above $P_2$ $= 3 - 1.25 = 1.75$ seconds
