## Changes in Energy

$$
\varepsilon_f - \varepsilon_i = \left( \text{Work} \right)_{engine} - \left(Work\right)_{friction}
$$

* $\varepsilon_f$ is the final kinetic energy of the object
* $\varepsilon_i$ is the initial kinetic energy of the object
* $\left( \text{Work} \right)_{\text{engine}}$ is the energy caused
  by driving force acting on the object
* $\left( \text{Work} \right)_{\text{friction}}$ is the energy used
  up by frictional force or any resistive force

 

**<span class="underline">{S05-P04} Question 7:</span>**

Car travelling on horizontal straight road, mass 1200kg. Power of car
engine is $20kW$ and constant. Resistance to motion of car is $500N$
and constant. Car passes point $A$ with speed $10ms^{- 1}$. Car
passes point $B$ with speed $25ms^{- 1}$. Car takes $30.5s$ to
move from $A$ to $B$.


1. Find acceleration of the car at $A$
2. Find distance $\text{AB}$ by considering work & energy

**<center>Solution:</center>**

**<span class="underline">Part (i)</span>**

Use formula for power to find the force at $A$

$$
P = Fv
$$

$$
20000 = 10F\ \ \ \ \ \ \ Driving\ force = 2000N
$$

We must take into account the resistance to motion

$$
\therefore F = Driving\ Force - Resistance = 2000 - 500
$$

$$
F = 1500
$$

Use Newton’s Second Law to find acceleration:

$$
1500 = 1200a\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ a = \frac{1500}{1200} = 1.25ms^{- 2}
$$

**<span class="underline">Part (ii)</span>**

Use power formula to find work done by engine:

$$
P = \frac{\text{w.d.}}{t}
$$

$20000 = \frac{\text{w.d.}}{30.5}\ \ \ \ \  \ \ \ \ w.d. = 610000J$

There is change in kinetic energy of the car so that means some work
done by the engine was due to this:

$k.E.\ at\ A = \frac{1}{2}1200\left( 10 \right)^{2}$
$k.E.\ at\ B = \frac{1}{2}1200\left( 25 \right)^{2}$

$$
Change\ in\ k.E. = \ k.E.\ at\ B - k.E.\ at\ A
$$

$$
Change\ in\ k.E. = 375000 - 60000 = 315000
$$

There is also some work done against resistive force of $500N$; due to
law of conservation of energy, this leads us to the main equation:

$$
w.d.\ by\ engine = change\ in\ k.E + \ w.d.\ against\ resistance
$$

$$
610000 = 315000 + 500s
$$

$$
s = \frac{610000 - 315000}{500} = \frac{295000}{500} = 590m
$$
