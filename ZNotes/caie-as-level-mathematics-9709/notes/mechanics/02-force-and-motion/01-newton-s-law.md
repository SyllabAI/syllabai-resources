# Force and Motion

## Newton’s Law

**Newton’s $1^{st}$ Law of Motion:**

Object remains at rest or moves with constant velocity unless an
external force is applied

**Newton’s $2^{nd}$ Law of Motion:**

$$
F = ma
$$

**Newton’s $3^{rd}$ Law of Motion:**

If object A exerts a force on object B, then object B must exert a force of equal magnitude and opposite direction back on object A.
