## Independent Events

  - Events that are not connected to each other in any way, or the next
    event does not rely from the previous event.

  - Examples of Independent events:
    
      - Coin tosses
    
      - Dice rolling

  - Examples of *not* Independent events:
    
      - Picking a ball from a bag (The next probability will increase
        due to a decrease in the number of balls in the bag)

  - **Multiplication Law for IEs:**

$$P\left( \text{A and B} \right) = P(A) \times P(B)$$

In other words, the probability that events A and B occur is just by
multiplying them.
