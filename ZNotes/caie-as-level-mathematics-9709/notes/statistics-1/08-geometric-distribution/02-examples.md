## Examples

**{S20-P51}‎ Question 1:‎**

The score when two fair six-sided dice are thrown is the ‎sum of the two numbers on the upper faces.

a) Show that the probability that the score of $4$ is $\frac{1}{12}$‎

The two dice are thrown repeatedly until a score of 4 is ‎obtained. The number of throws taken is denoted by the ‎random variable $X$.‎

b) Find the mean of $X$.

c) Find the probability that a score of 4 is first ‎obtained on the 6th throw.‎

d) Find the probability of $x > 8$.
**<center>Solution:</center>**

**Part (a)** ‎ *To get a score of 4, there are three ways to do it:‎*

‎3+1

1+3‎

2+2

So now we calculate each probability and then add them up:‎

$$

\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)\left(\frac{1}{6}\right) = \frac{1}{12}
$$

**<span class="underline">Part (b)</span>**

*This is a geometric distribution; its mean must be* $\mu = \frac{1}{p}$

*Because* $p = \frac{1}{12}$

$$
\therefore\mu = 12
$$

**<span class="underline">Part (c)</span>**

*First obtained in the 6<sup>th</sup> throw means* $P\left( X = x \right)$*, using the formula for geometric mean:*

$$
P\left( X = 6 \right) = \left( \frac{1}{12} \right)\left( 1 - \frac{1}{12} \right)^{6 - 1}
$$

$$
P\left( X = x \right) \approx 0.0539
$$

**<span class="underline">Part (d)</span>**
To find $P(x > 8)$:
$$
\left(1 - \frac{1}{12} \right)^8 = 0.498
$$
