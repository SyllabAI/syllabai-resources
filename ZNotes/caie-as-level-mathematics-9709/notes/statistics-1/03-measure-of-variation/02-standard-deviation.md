## Standard Deviation

- The standard deviation is the measure of the spread of values from the mean.
    - It is denoted by $\sigma$.
* If coded mean and sums are given, use as it is, standard deviation not altered

$$
\text{Standard deviation} = \sqrt{\text{Variance}} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum{(x_i-\overline{x})^2}}{n}} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum{{x_i}^2}}{n}-{\overline{x}}^2}
$$

$$
\text{Standard deviation} = \sqrt{\text{Variance}} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum{(x_i-\overline{x})^2}f_i}{\sum{f_i}}} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum{{x_i}^2}f}{\sum{f_i}}-{\overline{x}}^2}
$$
