## Median

* Middle value when data is ordered
  * If $n$ odd, median
    $= \frac{1}{2}\left( n + 1 \right)^{\text{th}}$ value
  * If $n$ even, median $= \frac{1}{2}n^{\text{th}}$ value
* Not affected by extreme values

**<span class="underline">Estimating Median from Grouped Frequency
Table:</span>**

| $\mathbf{x}$ | **Frequency** $\mathbf{f}$ | **Cumulative Frequency** |
|----|----|----|
| $10 - 20$ | $4$ | $4$ |
| $20 - 25$ | $8$ | $12$ |
| $25 - 35$ | $5$ | $17$ |
| $35 - 50$ | $3$ | $20$ |

**<span class="underline">Solution:</span>**

*Use cumulative frequency to find the middle value i.e.*

$$
20 \div 2 = 10
$$

$\therefore$ *you are finding the 10<sup>th</sup> value*

*The 10<sup>th</sup> value lies between 20 and 25*

 ![](../../../assets/0b284459c6ae-image005.png)

$$
\left( 12 - 4 \right)\ :(25 - 20)
$$

$$
\left( 12 - 10 \right)\ :(25 - Median)
$$

$$
25 - Median = \frac{12 - 10}{12 - 4} \times \left( 25 - 20 \right)
$$

$$
Median = 23.75
$$
