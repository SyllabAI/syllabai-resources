## Summation notation

- The summation notation is used throughout the syllabus to denote the sum of data values or probabilities.


$$
\sum x_i = x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + \cdots + x_n
$$

- This is the sum of all $x$ values till the $n$th value.

It should also be known that:

$$
\sum a = a + a + a + a + \cdots + a = an \quad [\text{Where} ~ a ~ \text{is a constant}]
$$
- The sum of a constant number $n$ times is $a \times n$.

It is also possible to re-write a summation:
$$
\sum(x - a) = \sum x - \sum a = \sum x - an
$$
