* **Distance**: Total path length

* **Displacement**: Change in position with direction

  * s = v $\cdot$ t

* **Speed**: Rate of change of distance.

  * $v=\frac{d}{t}$

  * Applies when speed is constant.

* **Average Speed** $= \frac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$

* **Constant Speed:**

  * a = 0

* **Velocity**: Rate of change of displacement.

  * Velocity is zero when object is stationary.

* **Acceleration**: Rate of change of velocity.

  * $$a = \frac{v-u}{t}$$
  * Negative acceleration means object is slowing down.
  * Positive acceleration means object is speeding up.
  * Do not assume direction of motion of the object through sign of acceleration
  * Acceleration is **not** constant when an object has increasing velocity
  * **Deceleration** is a term that may be used to indicate decreasing velocity

* **Constant Acceleration:**

  Two cases:

  * Occurs when object is on an inclined plane or attached to a pulley.

  * Body must be falling through the air and the only force acting on the body must be weight.
