# **Descriptive Writing**

A well-written descriptive essay should be able to perfectly depict the essence of its surroundings. It should include the people around, the atmosphere, the place, and the situation of the surroundings.

* You should be able to successfully describe the setting, the surroundings, the time of the day, and the season (summer, winter, autumn, spring).

* Not only provide with greater details, but also with the small, unpretentious details.

* Create a dramatic emphasis on what you feel.

* Briefly explain the multiple scents, noises, and colours that you think will help with getting your message across to the reader.

* Create chaos, anomalies, and uncertainty.
