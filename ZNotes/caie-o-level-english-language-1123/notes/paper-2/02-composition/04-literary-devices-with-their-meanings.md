# Literary Devices

Here are some literary devices that you may use along with their meaning:

 1. **A Blank Canvas:** It is a narrative or novel about events and experiences in the life of the main character as they mature and become an adult.

 2. **Cliche:** Something or someone that is not at all original, surprising, or interesting because it has very often been seen before.

 3. **Euphemism:** A word or phrase used to avoid saying an unpleasant or offensive word.

 4. **Exposition:** The description or explanation of background information within a work of literature.

 5. **Figurative Language:** The use of non-literal phrases or words to elicit an emotional response from a reader or audience.

 6. **Idiom:** A short expression or phrase that means something more than just its literal meaning.

 7. **Monologue:** An extended speech uttered by one character, either to others or as if alone.

 8. **Perspective:** The narrator’s point of view in a story.

 9. **Foreshadowing:** A device used by a writer to provide hints or clues to the reader or audience about what will happen later on in the text.

10. **Tone:** The attitude that a character, narrator, or writer takes towards a given subject.
