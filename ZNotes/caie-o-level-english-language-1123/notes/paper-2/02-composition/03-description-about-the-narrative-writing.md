# Narrative Writing

Narrative writing can be fictional and based on reality, or both. It should portray a well-connected sequence of events. You will be critiqued on the following:

* Your ability to portray the setting, the place, and the general atmosphere.

* The way you provide the reader/examiner with the background of your story, and how you relate your story to the character’s background.

* The plot of your narrative, the starting, the climax, the twists and confusion, and the way you conclude your story.

* You may also add up to 5-6 characters for your story. You shouldn’t add more, as the story might get complicated to carry forward.

  * Your story should have a clear beginning, plot, and ending. Adding a lot of confusion and chaos in your writing would be helpful.

  * If your story has a shift between the present, the past, and the future, the shift should be clear and well organised. Make sure that you don’t mix it up.

* You should also add dialogue to make the story interesting. Just keep in mind that whatever dialogues you write are appropriate to use.
