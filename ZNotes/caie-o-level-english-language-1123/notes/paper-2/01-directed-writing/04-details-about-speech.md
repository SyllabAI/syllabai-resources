# Speech

**4. Speech**

A speech is when a person is directly talking to an audience regarding a specific problem or concern. Not only does one engage with their audience by addressing concerns or answering questions, but also by connecting with them on personal and emotional levels to create a greater impact. By adding quotes, statistics, polls, and asking rhetorical questions, one can alter the audience's opinion and hence improve one's argument and speech eminently.

Just like an article, you should maintain and set a persuasive and strong tone throughout, as it would help create a more notable outcome. You should clearly be only in support of one side.

---

The Speech should be written in the following format:

1. Ladies & gentlemen, (for general audience) / Honourable principal, respected teachers & dear fellow students, (for school audience)

2. Body (The main part of the speech, 2 – 3 paragraphs)

3. Ending: Thank you. (Optional)
