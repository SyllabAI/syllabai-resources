# Planning Your Response

* Make sure to go through both texts and underline/highlight all of the relevant information or ideas you could use in your letter.

* After reading, decide which view you are going to support based on which Text appeals more to you or which side has better and more straightforward arguments.

* Develop a rough structure in your mind of which points you are going to put in each paragraph and how you are going to connect them.

* For the side you aren't going to support, while you are noting their points, try to also develop counterarguments that you can use to oppose that point of view to strengthen your own argument.

* Ensure that you have a reasonable number of points from both texts before starting your writing. Keep notes in your mind of any synonyms of words you find in the texts to help you portray the points in your own words.
