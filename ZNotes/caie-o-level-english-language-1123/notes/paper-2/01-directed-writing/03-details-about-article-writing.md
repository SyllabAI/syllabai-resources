# Article Writing

**3. Article Writing**

After understanding the question and going through both the texts with contradicting views, one must highlight all the vital points that are to be included in the article. The use of a catchy heading will attract the readers to read the article; the heading can be controversial or anodyne. By adding quotes from significant figures, jokes, or good advice, one can really spruce up their article. An article usually shows a biased support towards one opinion more than the other, which is why a persuasive and strong tone is to be set throughout the article. It is essential for the article to not only be based solely on opinions but also upon factual reasoning.  A very common yet effective way to present factual reasoning is by providing statistics. Another way to keep the reader inclined towards reading the article is by asking rhetorical or vexed questions, creating a state of aggression or confusion. Even if the article itself has no controversy, the heading will force the reader to keep on reading to find the controversy. Keep in mind that there is no reason to state the purpose of writing the article. There should be at least 3-5 paragraphs without any sort of repetition.

---

The article should be written in the following format:

1. Heading / Title

2. Writer’s Name

3. Body (The main part of the article, 2 – 3 paragraphs)

4. Conclusion (Ending paragraph of the article with the opinion or recommendation)
