# Letter Writing

**1. Informal Letter**

Once you have read and highlighted everything you want to put in your response, you should roughly organise your answer. Once you start writing your response, you should follow the general format by first addressing your recipient with a warm greeting and catching up on general life updates or asking them about their life (Take care not to extend the introduction too much). After this, you should start the next paragraph by addressing the context of the letter, and by now, the first bullet should be addressed. After the closing of the first paragraph, do the same with the remaining paragraphs. Once you have addressed all the bullet points, close your response with a conclusive statement that addresses your ultimate stance on the topic. Then, sign off with regards and your name.

Keep in mind that in your answer, all the sentences and paragraphs can be and are appreciated if they all have varying lengths, and a wide range of vocabulary words with no repetition.

---

The Informal Letter should be written in the following format:

1. Dear Uncle, / Ali, (Write the relationship if the recipient is a family member, and the name in case of a friend.)

2. Body (The main part of the Letter, 2 – 3 paragraphs)

3. Love, / Best regards, / Best wishes,

4. Your First Name

**2. Formal Letter**

Unlike the informal letter, in a formal letter, one should only greet the recipient before immediately addressing the reason for writing the letter (No other introduction). One should be precise and to the point with their words. Instead of a warm greeting before starting off with the first paragraph, one should directly start by addressing the first bullet point. In a formal letter, one is usually trying to convince the recipient to agree with one's own opinion about a certain topic or subject, which is why it is highly essential to maintain a persuasive yet formal tone throughout your letter in support of your view, whilst silently disagreeing with the opposing opinion.

All the bullet points should be discussed in your response; you should have a minimum of 3 paragraphs, all with varying lengths and a wide range of vocabulary.

---

The Formal Letter should be written in the following format:

1. Dear Sir/Maam

2. Body (The main part of the Letter, 2 – 3 paragraphs)

3. Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully, / Yours truly,

4. Your Signature

5. Your Full Name

6. (Write your post here, if you are writing the letter in an official position, e.g.
   The Secretary,
   Drama Club
   ABC School )
