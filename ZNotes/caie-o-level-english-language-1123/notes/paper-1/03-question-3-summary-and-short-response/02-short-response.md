## Short Response

### **Details about this question**

- This is the second part of the first question of the ‘Section B’.
- In this question, candidates are expected to **give their opinion** about a question by using the information given in Text B. 
- This is an interview question, you will be provided with a statement which will be  provided to you by a ‘reporter’, you will have to answer the question as if you are giving an interview.
- This question is of **5 marks**

  **Assessment Objective:**

**The objective is to demonstrate an understanding of implicit meanings and attitudes (R2).**

In this question, you will be expected to know how to pick up implicit meanings from the text and what the writer is trying to say.

### **How to attempt this question:**

- The question statement will usually ask you to **act** like 'someone' (eg, the owner of a hotel) and to give your opinion in terms of that position or personality
- You must **adopt a relevant tone** according to who you are supposed to act as
- 1 mark *may* be given to start your answer with the statement, "As the owner of a hotel, I believe..."
- **Identify 5 points** related to what the question is asking
- Usually, more than 5 points will be given in the text; however, since the lines are limited, **refrain from writing excess information**
- In this question, **you can copy-paste the text**, and there is no word limit however, write your answer within the space provided
- Write your response in **first-person**
- Use phrases like "In my opinion", "I believe", "I think", "My thoughts are", "Our plan is" etc

### **Writing in first-person:**

First-person writing is used here to ensure that a personal tone is maintained throughout the answer as that is the question's demand.
**Do the following to write in first-person:**

1. Understand that you are writing about yourself/your views
2. **Use first-person pronouns**

- Examples: I, my, we, our, mine

### **What not to do?**

- Do not write your answer in bullet points
- Do not write more than the lines provided
- Do not write less than 5 points
- Do not write irrelevant information
