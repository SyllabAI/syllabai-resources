# Question 3: Summary and Short Response

## Summary

### **Details about this Question**

Summary is the first question of the ‘section B’ in paper 1. The purpose of this question to assess the ability of the student to pick and choose the important points of the text and comprise them into one paragraph in the form of a continuous writing. The text provided is either a factual text or an opinion based text. You are provided with a question statement which you have to address, the statement can be either for or against however all the points that should be mentioned in the summary will be provided to you in the text, so all you need to do is understand and choose the correct points. The **total marks that you can** **score in a summary are 20**. You should not exceed the word limit provided by the examiner which is **150 word max.**

Ideally you are not to spend more than about **40 - 45 minutes** on this question.

You will be critiqued on both your reading and writing skills.

### **Assessment Objectives**

### **Reading assessment objectives**

Both the following objectives will be tested for a total of **10 marks**

- **Objective 1 (R1):** demonstrate understanding of explicit meanings
- **Objective 2 (R5):** select and use information for specific purposes

### **Writing assessment objectives**

Both the following objectives will be tested for a total of **10 marks**

- **Objective 3 (W2):** Organise and structure ideas and opinions for deliberate effect
- **Objective 4 (W3):** Use a range of vocabulary and sentence structures appropriate to the context

### **How to attempt this question:**

### **Objective 1 (R1): Demonstrate understanding of explicit meanings**

- Here, the examiner is **testing your reading skills.**
- The goal is to understand what the text is saying.
- The **meaning and ideas will be written clearly**; no inferred or implicit ideas will be asked of you.

**Objective 2 (R5): Select and use information for specific purposes**

- After reading the text once, read the question given very carefully.
- You are supposed to write the summary of a specific theme from the text and **not the entire text.**
- **Identify at least 10 points** related to the information the question is asking and underline or mark them.
- **Identify relevant points**; only what the question is asking of you.

### **Objective 3 (W2): Organise and structure ideas and opinions for deliberate effect**

- Write your summary in continuous writing; only one long paragraph.
- **Do not make multiple paragraphs or write your answer in bullet points!**
- **Before writing, think of your answer structure**;

1. Either note it mentally or
2. On the side of the paper with a pencil which you can erase later or
3. **Number the points** in the order you want to write them to save time (this is most effective)

- **Merge ideas** that relate to each other to save words
- **Write your sentences deliberately**; choose words that allow you to convey your idea as briefly as possible
- Avoid elaborating points unnecessarily

### **Objective 4 (W3): Use a range of vocabulary and sentence structures appropriate to the context**

- **Do not copy-paste sentences** from the given text
- You must **use your own words or synonyms** to convey your points
- If you can't think of a synonym for a word, write the opposite of it for eg; instead of writing a synonym for "bad" you can say "not good"; however, this is **only for worst-case scenarios** and isn't an advisable approach

### **Summary of what not to do:**

- Never copy-paste words from the text
- Do not write irrelevant points
- Do not underwrite; you must give at least 10 points
- Never use bullet points; write in a continuous writing format
- Don't make multiple paragraphs
- Don't write over 150 words
