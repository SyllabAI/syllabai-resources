# Question 2: Use of Language

## Practical Details of Question 2

This is the second question of the section A of paper 2. The maximum marks awarded for this question are **9 marks.** You should ideally spend no more than 25-30 minutes on this question. The questions are typically of 1 or 2 marks and in some cases 3 marks. At the end of each question a line number is mentioned, which will help you identify and provide an answer to the question. Words like **"Explain"**, **"Suggest"**, **"Describe"** will be used in the question statement and they all require you to analyse the information and convey your thoughts on why the writer chose to use specific words.

### **Assessment objective:**

The objective is to **demonstrate an understanding of how writers achieve effects and influence readers (R4).**

In this question, you are supposed to focus on the writer's craft and how different linguistic devices have been used to create effect or meaning.

### **How to attempt these questions?**

- **Always write in paragraph form.**
- Read the lines or paragraph stated in the question carefully and **consider what the writer is trying to say.**
- **Think of the different connotations** of the words, examples and context given.
- When you are asked to support your answer with evidence from the text, simply write your answer and copy-paste the sentence from the text; preferably with quotation marks.
- When the number of examples or reasons you are required to mention is provided in the question, **adhere to the number specified.**
- Make sure to **distinctly state your points**; you can use words like firstly and secondly to separate your ideas
- For questions of **1 mark, write 1 point** in the answer, if it's of 2 marks, write 2 points
- If the last question is of 3 marks, the examiner will usually ask you to **give an example or evidence for 1 mark**, you should exactly mention the line from the text without paraphrasing it. However, in your explanation you are required to provide your understanding of the example you provided with and why you believe that it is the best suited for your answer. You should provide with two points for the explanation.  

### **What not to do?**

- **Do not use words from the text** unless you are quoting an example or evidence. 
- Never write your answer from elsewhere in the text when the line and paragraph number are specified.
- **Do not overwrite** and give more points than required.
- **Do not** underwrite by giving less points than the requirement.
- **Never use bullet point** to list your ideas; you must write in a continuous writing format.
