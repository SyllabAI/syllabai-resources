## Explicit Questions

### **What are Explicit Questions?**

Explicit questions demand a direct answer, which is clearly stated in the text. The question is very straightforward; you only have to pick up the answer from the text and state it.

### **How to Attempt these Questions:**

- Directly **lift** the sentence from the text and state it as your answer
- Usually, the **examiner will mention the line numbers or even paragraph numbers** in the question so that the answer is easy to identify
- Since answers are direct, **spend time on questions according to their marks** eg; if it's of 2 marks, spend a maximum of 2 minutes on it.
- Make sure that you answer according to the number of marks. For example, if the question is of 2 marks, then you only write 2 relevant points for it. 
- If a **question is challenging, skip it** for now and **come back to it later**.
- When words like **"Give"**, **"State"** and **" Identify"**, **"What"**, **"Summarise"** are written in the question, understand that the answer is explicitly mentioned in the text.

### **What not to do**

- There is **no need to paraphrase or use synonyms** unless stated in the question.
- **Do not overwrite**.
- **Do not write excessive detail** unless asked, it is better to write to the point answers.
- **Never answer from elsewhere in the text when the line or paragraph is specifically given.**
