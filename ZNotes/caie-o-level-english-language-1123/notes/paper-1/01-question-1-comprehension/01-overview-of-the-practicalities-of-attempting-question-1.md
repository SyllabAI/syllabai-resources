# Question 1: Comprehension

## Overview of comprehension:

This question is the first question of Paper 1 in Section A. The maximum score one can get in this question are **25 marks**, out of these 25 marks, **16 marks are rewarded for successfully attempting and fulling the criteria for R1 and R2**, while the rest **9 are awarded for your use of language**.  One should spend no more than 30 minutes on this question. One should throughly read and understand the text before answering the questions. While attempting the questions, one should always refer back to the text. At the end of every question, a line number is mentioned from where you need to extract out the answer. Make sure that your answers do not have any unnecessary information written, nor any grammatical errors. 

### **Assessment Objectives**

In this section, candidates will be tested on the following objectives:

- **Objective 1** Demonstrate understanding of explicit meanings (R1)
- **Objective 2** Demonstrate understanding of implicit meanings and attitude (R2)
