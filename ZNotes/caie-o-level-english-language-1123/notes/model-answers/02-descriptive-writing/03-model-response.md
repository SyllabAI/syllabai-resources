### **Describe the scene at an important cultural event both before it starts and during the event [25]**

Amidst the concrete jungle of Tokyo, where the melodies of street musicians intermingle with the noise of passing vehicles, exists a hidden gem, World Festival. The mere mention of its name exudes a sense of intrigue and mystery, beckoning people from across the globe to visit it.

---

A palpable sense of anticipation hung heavy in the air as the clock ticked away the minutes leading up to the start of the festival, like a curtain waiting to be drawn. Armed with pens and notepads, event organizers ran around in a flurry of activity, desperately trying to make last-minute adjustments. A cacophony of voices could be heard: the laughter of children darting across the street, the soft chatter of senior citizens reminiscing about previous festivals, and the annoying shouts of vendors preparing their stalls. Women clad in a vast tapestry of cultural dresses meandered through the crowd, their bracelets jiggling with each step. A unique scent flowed through the air: the scent of new clothes coupled with the aroma of fragrances. Truly a rejuvenating smell. In the backdrop, the light sizzle of barbecue could be heard, though overshadowed by the intermittent testing of the luxurious sound system.

---

As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting shadows that danced across the cobble paths like playful spirits, the sound of a bell echoed through the festival grounds; the wait was finally over. A feeling of excitement rushed through the crowd like a wave. Suddenly, a noise was heard from the stage as people from different countries, races, and backgrounds came together to witness the start of the festival. The dance performance started. From the Pashto Bhangra to the Indonesian Hartka, various dances were put on display. Each jump, each step, and even each breath was filled with determination. The differences in the cultures of the various participating countries were made quite apparent. Yet, the crowd was united in their thundering applause and appreciation for each dancer. Next came the singing performance. The crowd was left in complete awe of the spectacle unfolding before them. Every note seamlessly transitioned to the next as the singers represented their respective cultures to perfection with their rhythm and rhyme.

---

Soon enough, a tantalizing aroma filled the air as families gathered to indulge in culinary delights from around the world, their recipes passed down through generations. Friends engaged in animated conversations, their smiles as bright as the fairy lights and lamps adorning the paths. The festival continued strong deep into the night as the fireworks illuminated the sky with a vast array of colors: the very colors of life and hope. The festival serves as a testament to the fact that despite the differences between our cultures, we share many similarities, as is evident by the camaraderie developed between strangers through jokes and shared experiences.
