## Outlining of Descriptive Essay

The question will be something as follows:

● Write about 350 to 450 words on the topic of your choice.

● Up to 10 marks are available for the content and structure of your answer, and up to 15
marks for the style and accuracy of your writing.

---

### Plan:

### **Before starting:**

* Outline in your mind the different things that you want to describe or focus on in your
  essay

* Decide whether you want to have a still description or whether you will be describing the place or event as you are moving through it. (Most of the time, the latter is a better fit)

* Before writing, collect at least 2 to 3 descriptions that are relevant to each of the 5 senses: touch, taste, smell, sight, and hearing.

* Be sure to also describe your own feelings in that scene, whether it's excitement or
  anxiety or fear, try to portray it through action or words.

---

### **After Starting:**

* Introduce the setting of the description, i.e., a place, a city, a time, etc.

* Body paragraphs should focus on the descriptions using the 5 senses and your feelings

* Try to develop a clear structure, like dedicating each paragraph to a different sense or if it’s a moving description, then show the progression of time throughout the essay so it flows better.
