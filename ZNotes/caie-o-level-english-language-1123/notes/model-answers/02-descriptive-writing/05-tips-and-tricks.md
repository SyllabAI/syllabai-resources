## Tips and Tricks

* The best tip for descriptive essays is to prepare, prepare, and prepare.

* Try to learn new words and phrases that you can use in pretty much any descriptive topic that could be asked. Words like myriad or armed with are great examples of this.

* Create pre-planned introductions and conclusions that fit into any setting.

* E.g., starting with: Nestled in the heart of the The dash can be replaced with anything, and it could still work as a good starting point for your essay. The same can be done for a conclusion, e.g., in the game of life, it is these moments of and___ that leave the greatest impact. The dashes can be replaced by any emotion relevant to the setting, from joy to sadness.

* Finally, when writing, try to describe as many things as possible, even if they seem insignificant because even the chirping sounds of crickets add another layer of depth to your environment, and the way you portray the environment is what matters most in a description.
