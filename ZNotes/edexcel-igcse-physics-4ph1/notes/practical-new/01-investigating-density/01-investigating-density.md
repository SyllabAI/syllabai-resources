# Practical: Investigating Density

### Investigating Density

Aim

To determine the density of regular solids, irregular solids and liquids by measuring their mass and volume.

Apparatus

* Balance, ruler and measuring cylinder

* Displacement can, water and thread

* Solid samples and a liquid sample

Formula

$$\rho=\frac{m}{V}
$$

* $\rho$ = density ($\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ or $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$)

* $m$ = mass ($\mathrm{kg}$ or $\mathrm{g}$)

* $V$ = volume ($\mathrm{m^3}$ or $\mathrm{cm^3}$)

### Method 1 — Regular solid

1. Measure the mass using a balance.

2. Measure the length, width and height of a rectangular block using a ruler.

3. Calculate the volume:

V=l\\times w\\times h

4\. Calculate density using $$\rho=\frac{m}{V}$$.

### Method 2 — Irregular solid

1. Measure the mass using a balance.

2. Fill a displacement can with water until water flows from the spout. Allow the dripping to stop.

3. Place a measuring cylinder beneath the spout.

4. Using a thread, lower the object fully into the water, ensuring no air bubbles are trapped.

5. Collect the displaced water and measure its volume.

Volume of displaced water = volume of object

6\. Calculate density using $$\rho=\frac{m}{V}$$.

### Method 3 — Liquid

1. Measure the mass of an empty measuring cylinder.

2. Pour a known volume of liquid into the cylinder.

3. Read the volume at eye level, from the bottom of the meniscus.

![](https://znotes-static.s3.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/cie/igcse/physics-0625/668d8381f4e2583c5508106e3544aa95e4d5d2151ba3b681.png =432x)

1. Measure the mass of the cylinder containing the liquid.

2. Calculate:

Mass of liquid = total mass − mass of empty cylinder

6\. Calculate density using $$\rho=\frac{m}{V}$$.

### Worked example — Density of a liquid

A measuring cylinder containing 50 cm³ of liquid has a mass of 146 g. The empty cylinder has a mass of 100 g.

Mass of liquid = 146 − 100 = 46 g

m=146-100=46\\ \\mathrm\{g\}

\\rho=\\frac\{46\}\{50\}=\\boxed\{0.92\\ \\mathrm\{g/cm\^3\}\}

Converting to kg/m³: 0.92\\times1000=\\boxed\{920\\ \\mathrm\{kg/m\^3\}\}

* The smallest scale division limits the precision of a measurement.

* Repeat measurements where possible and calculate a mean to reduce random errors.

### Safety

* Handle glassware and heavy objects carefully.

* Clean up water spills immediately to prevent slipping.

* Lower solids gently to avoid breaking the glassware.
