### Worked Example

Suppose: $$s=1.2\text{ m}$$

and - $$t_1=2.4\text{ s},\quad t_2=2.5\text{ s},\quad t_3=2.3\text{ s}$$

Mean time:

$$t_{\text{mean}}=\frac{t_1+t_2+t_3}{3}$$

Average speed:

$$v=\frac{1.2}{2.4}=0.50\text{ m/s}$$

**Illustrative calculation only — not an actual experimental measurement.**\*
