# Investigating Extension Due to Applied Force

## Experimental Setup

### Aim

To investigate how the **extension** of a spring, metal wire or rubber band changes as the **applied force** increases.

### Apparatus

* clamp stand and clamp

* spring / metal wire / rubber band

* mass hanger and slotted masses

* half-metre rule

* set square

* safety goggles

### Variables

* **Independent variable:** applied force, $F$

* **Dependent variable:** extension, $x$

* **Control variables:** same material, same original length, same ruler position and same reference point
