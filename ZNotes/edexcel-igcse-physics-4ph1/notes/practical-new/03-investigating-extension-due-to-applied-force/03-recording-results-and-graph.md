### Graph and Expected Results

Plot **force against extension**:

* x-axis → extension, $x$

* y-axis → force,  $F$

* **Spring:** initially gives a straight line through the origin and obeys Hooke's law.

* **Metal wire:** may also show extension proportional to force up to a certain load.

* **Rubber band:** usually gives a curved graph, so extension is not directly proportional to force.

* Loading and unloading a rubber band may give different curves.

### Conclusion

For a spring, extension is directly proportional to the applied force while Hooke’s law is obeyed. A metal wire may also show a proportional relationship up to a certain force, while a rubber band does not show a directly proportional relationship. If the material returns to its original length when the force is removed, it has behaved elastically.
