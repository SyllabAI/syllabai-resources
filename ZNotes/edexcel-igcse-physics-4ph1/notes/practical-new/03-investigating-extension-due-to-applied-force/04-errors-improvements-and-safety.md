## Errors, Improvements and Safety

### Sources of Error and Improvements

* **Parallax error** when reading the ruler → use a set square and read the scale at eye level.

* Material may still be **oscillating** → wait until it becomes stationary.

* Ruler or clamp may move → secure the apparatus firmly.

* Small extensions may be difficult to measure → use a finer-scale measuring instrument where available.

* Repeat measurements where appropriate to improve reliability.

### Experimental Uncertainty

* Each length measurement has an uncertainty of about **half the smallest scale division** of the ruler.

* The extension, $x$, is calculated from two measured lengths:

  $$x=L-L_0$$

* Because both $L$ and $L_0$ have measurement uncertainty, their **absolute uncertainties are added** when finding the uncertainty in $x$

  $$\Delta x=\Delta L+\Delta L_0$$

* Using a ruler with **smaller scale divisions** reduces the uncertainty in the length measurements.

### Safety and Precautions

* Wear **safety goggles**, especially when using wires or rubber bands.

* Secure the clamp stand so it cannot fall.

* Keep hands and feet away from underneath the masses.

* Do not overload the material because it may snap.

* Add and remove masses carefully.
