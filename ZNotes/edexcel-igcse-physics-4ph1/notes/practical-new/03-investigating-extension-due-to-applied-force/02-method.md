## Experimental Method

### Method

1. Clamp the material securely and place a ruler vertically beside it.

2. Measure the original unstretched length, $L_0$

3. Add a known load.

4. Allow the material to stop moving, then measure its new length, $L$, using a set square.

5. Calculate the extension, $x$: $$x=L-L_0$$

6. Add further masses **one at a time** and repeat the measurements.

7. Record the applied force and corresponding extension for each load.

8. Remove the masses **one at a time** and repeat the measurements to check whether the material returns to its original length.

9. Repeat the investigation using a **metal wire and rubber band**.

### Why Important Steps Are Done

* Keep the ruler **vertical** → ensures length is measured correctly.

* Use a **set square** → reduces parallax error.

* Wait until the material stops moving → prevents inaccurate readings caused by oscillations.

* Add masses gradually → provides enough readings to identify the relationship.

* Take readings while unloading → checks whether the material returns to its original length.
