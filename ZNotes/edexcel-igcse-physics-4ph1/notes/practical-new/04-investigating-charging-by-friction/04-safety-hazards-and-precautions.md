### Experimental Precautions

* Use **clean, dry insulating materials**, as moisture can allow charge to leak away.

* Keep the rod at approximately the **same distance** from the paper during each test.

* Use paper pieces of **similar size and mass** for a fair comparison.

* Avoid touching the rubbed part of the rod, as this may reduce the charge.

* Carry out the investigation away from draughts, which could move the paper even when it is not attracted electrostatically.
