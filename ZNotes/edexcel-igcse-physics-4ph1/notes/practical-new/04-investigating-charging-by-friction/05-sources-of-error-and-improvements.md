### Experimental Uncertainty and Limitations

* **Measurement limitation:** The experiment does not directly measure the amount of electric charge. Attraction only provides evidence that charge is present.

* **Distance uncertainty:** The distance between the rod and paper may vary slightly between trials, affecting the strength of attraction.

* **Observation uncertainty:** It may be difficult to count paper pieces accurately if several move or stick together.

### Improving Reliability

* Repeat each test **at least three times** and compare the results.

* Use similar paper pieces and keep the testing distance constant.

* Check that the rod is initially uncharged before each trial.

* Keep the materials and environmental conditions as similar as possible.

### Evaluating the Investigation

* The experiment can show that an insulating material becomes charged after rubbing.

* However, it **cannot measure the exact amount of charge** or determine its sign using neutral paper alone.

* More controlled conditions and repeated observations improve confidence in the results.
