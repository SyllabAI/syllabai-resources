# Investigating Charging by Friction

## Experiment Setup

### Aim

To investigate how **insulating materials become electrically charged by friction** and how this charge can be detected using small pieces of paper.

### Apparatus

* Plastic insulating rod

* Dry woollen cloth

* Small, lightweight pieces of dry paper

* Ruler to help keep the testing distance constant

* Dry, flat surface

### Variables

#|
||

Variable

|

Description

||
||

Independent variable

|

||
||

|

||
||
