## Particle Model

* All matter is made of **particles that are always moving**.

* The **arrangement and movement** of particles determine whether a substance is a solid, liquid or gas.

* Heating gives particles more energy, so they move or vibrate faster.

  ### Heating a System

  * Heating transfers energy to a substance, increasing the energy stored in it.

  * This energy can:

    * increase the particles’ kinetic energy, so the **temperature rises**, or

    * overcome forces between particles, causing a **change of state**.

  * During a change of state, the temperature stays constant until the change is complete.

  ### Changes of State

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/d3fe913b9a3445b39d845b00.webp =800x)

* **Melting:** particles gain energy and can move out of their fixed positions, forming a liquid.

* **Boiling / evaporation:** particles gain enough energy to move apart and form a gas.

* During a **change of state**, energy is used to overcome forces between particles, so the **temperature stays constant until the change is complete**.
