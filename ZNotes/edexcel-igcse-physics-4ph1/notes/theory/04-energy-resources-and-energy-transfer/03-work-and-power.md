## Work and Power

* The gravitational potential energy of an object that has been raised
  to a height, *h,* above the ground is given by:
* Gravitational potential energy, GPE = mgh
* The kinetic energy of a moving object is calculated using the
  equation

$$

\text{K.E} = \frac{1}{2} mν^2

$$

* In some cases, work done of object = gain in GPE before hitting the
  ground
* Work transfers energy to an object:

$$

\text{Work = Force}  \times \text{Distance}

$$

* Power is the rate of transfer of energy or the rate of doing work.

$$

\text{Power} = \frac{\text{work done}}{\text{time taken}}

$$
