## Energy Resources and Electricity Generation

- Renewable sources are not exhaustible

- Non-renewable sources of energy are exhaustible

- Payback time (for example, of solar panels) = $\frac{\text{Initial\ cost}}{\text{Annual\ savings\ or\ earnings}}$

### **Types**

**Fuel**: burnt to make thermal energy, makes steam, turns turbine

* Advantages: Cheap, Plentiful, Low-tech

* Disadvantages: Harmful wastes, Greenhouse/ pollutant gas 

**Wave energy:** generators driven by up and down motion of waves at sea.

* Advantages: No greenhouse gases produced

* Disadvantages: Difficult to build

**Tidal energy:** dam built where river meets sea, lake fills when tides come in & empties when tide goes out; water flow runs generator

* Advantages: No greenhouse gases produced

* Disadvantages: Expensive, Can’t be built everywhere

**Hydroelectric:** river & rain fill up lake behind dam, water released, turns turbine ∴ generator

* Advantages: Low impact on environment

* Disadvantages: Energy produced at constant rate, Few areas of the world suitable

**Geothermal**: water pumped down to hot rocks rising as steam

* Advantages: No CO$_2$ produced

* Disadvantages: Deep drilling difficult and expensive

**Nuclear fission:** uranium atoms split by shooting neutrons at them

* Advantages: Produces a lot of energy with very little resources

* Disadvantages: Produces radioactive waste

**Wind:** windmills are moved by the breeze. They generate electricity from kinetic energy.

* Advantages: No CO$_2$ / Greenhouse gasses produced

* Disadvantages: Few areas of the world suitable

**Solar cells/ photovoltaic cells:** made of materials that deliver electrical current when it absorbs light

* Advantages: No CO$_2$ produced

* Disadvantages: Variable amount of sunshine in some countries
