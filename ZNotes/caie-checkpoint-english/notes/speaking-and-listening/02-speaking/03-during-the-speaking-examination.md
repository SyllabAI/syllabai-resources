## During the speaking examination 

* Politely greet the examiner when you enter. 
* Think before you speak. 
* Use correct words. 
* Use synonyms. 
* Listen to yourself while you speak and if you hear a mistake, correct it. 
* Do not hesitate in asking the examiner if you do not understand a question or activity. 
* Give a reason for your answer. 
* Speak at a regular pace. 
* Speak clearly using best language and pronunciation. 
* Use synonyms and varied vocabulary to enhance your presentation. 
* Modulate your voice according to the situation and if you are playing a character. 
* Answer according to the question asked. 
* Do not panic and take deep breaths if you do not know something. 
* Politely greet the examiner when you exit.
