# The Fundamentals of Accounting

## The Purpose of Accounting

* **Accounting:** Accounting is: uses bookkeeping records to
  * Collecting and recording financial data
  * Classifying financial data
  * Summarizing financial data
  * Analyzing financial data
  * Interpreting financial data
  * Communicating financial data
* It does this by using records from **Bookkeeping** (A process of detailed recording of all the financial transactions of a business).
* The business aims to make a profit. This is calculated in the financial statements, which are usually prepared at the end of a financial year.

### **Role of accounting in monitoring progress and decision making**

Useful for:

* Owners of a business
* Prospective investors
* Bank managers
* Creditors or suppliers
* The government

### **Purposes of Measuring Business Profit and Losses**

* **Profits = Income - Expenses**
* A business can also make a loss wherein the expenses exceed the income. Loss= negative profit.
* By measuring the profit and loss of a business, one can:
  * Understand the progress of the business
  * Use ratios and compare a business's profit to other figures in the financial statements to get a more comprehensive view of its progress.
  * Compare the progress of the business to the progress of similar businesses.
  * Comparing the business to itself from year to year
  * Base the business’ future and make decisions based on the profit/loss figure.
