## The Ledger

* Ledgers are divided based on the types of accounts they contain, this is done so that several people can bookkeep simultaneously, i.e. Work can be distributed (the same applies for books of prime entries).

| Ledger | General Description/Contents |
|----|----|
| Sales Ledger | Debtors Ledger |
| Purchases Ledger | Creditors Ledger |
| Nominal Ledger | Real A/C s (assets) & nominal A/C s (income, expenses & capital) |
| Cash book | MCB & PCB |
