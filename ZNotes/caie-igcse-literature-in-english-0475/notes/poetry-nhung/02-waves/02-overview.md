## **Overview**

* ### **Structure:**

  * 24 lines, 8 stanzas

* ### **Themes:**

  * Identity 

  * Racism

  * Social pressure

  * Cultural heritage

  * Betrayal of one's roots

* ### **Background:**

  * Kayo Chingonyi explores Black British identity and cultural experiences.

  * The poem refers to waves, a hairstyle in which tightly curled hair is brushed into a rippling pattern.

  * The hairstyle became popular among Black men, influenced by Black American and R&B culture, particularly around the late 1990s and early 2000s.

  * Hair becomes more than a physical feature: it represents cultural identity, social acceptance and pressure to follow popular beauty standards.

* ### **Poet’s purpose:**

  * Identity: To explore how appearance influences the way people understand and present themselves.

  * Fitting in: To show how the desire for acceptance can pressure people into changing their natural appearance.

  * Cultural heritage: To explore how attempts to follow beauty trends can cause people to distance themselves from their roots.

  * Racism: To question why natural Black hair may be treated as something that needs to be controlled or hidden.

  * Self-acceptance: To suggest that rejecting one's natural features can create an ongoing conflict between personal identity and social expectations.
