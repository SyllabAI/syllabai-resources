## Mood

* ### Warm, nostalgic mood:

  - “you're sweet like chocolate, boy” creates an affectionate, playful mood through the simile. The musical reference recalls the excitement of shared cultural trends $\rightarrow$ presenting the hairstyle as a source of enjoyment and belonging before its deeper consequences emerge.

* ### Pressured mood:

  - “everyone had a method” suggests that grooming has become a widespread social expectation. “Everyone” creates a sense of universality, implying that individuals may feel compelled to follow the trend to avoid being different from their peers.

* ### Insecure mood:

  - “ashamed of the hair’s natural grain” reveals embarrassment about natural features. The contrast between natural hair and the desired hairstyle suggests that repeated exposure to beauty standards can transform a harmless preference into dissatisfaction with one's identity.

* ### Conflicted mood:

  - “betray our roots” creates guilt and regret through the verb “betray”, which implies disloyalty. $\rightarrow$ suggesting an internal conflict between the desire to fit into society and the need to remain connected to one's cultural heritage.
