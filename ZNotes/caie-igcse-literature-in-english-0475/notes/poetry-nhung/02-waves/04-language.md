## Language

* ### Word choice:

  * “rebellious” suggests natural hair is difficult to control, presenting it as something that must be disciplined to meet beauty standards. Overall reflecting the pressure to suppress natural features to gain acceptance.

  * “slick” suggests smoothness and control, contrasting with “rebellious”. From that, it highlights how society favours carefully styled hair over its natural texture.

  * “deference” suggests submission to R&B stars, implying that admiration of celebrities can influence how young people present themselves.

  * “facade” suggests a false outer appearance that conceals one's true identity. This implies that fitting in requires hiding natural features to maintain an acceptable image.

  * “transcendent beauty” exaggerates the hairstyle's attractiveness, presenting it as an almost perfect ideal, connecting to the powerful influence of beauty standards.

  * “ashamed” reveals embarrassment about natural hair, suggesting that social expectations have become internalised and affect self-acceptance.

  * “unbidden” suggests natural hair returns without permission, emphasising that natural features persist despite attempts to control them.

  * “betray our roots” metaphorically presents conformity as disloyalty to one's heritage, highlighting the conflict between social acceptance and cultural identity.

* ### Semantic field of submission:

  * “deference”, “taming”, “hide”, “ashamed”, “betray” suggest submission and self-rejection. The progression reveals how beauty standards become internalised, causing the boys to view their natural features as flaws. “Betray” implies disloyalty to their heritage, suggesting that the desire for acceptance can come at the cost of cultural identity and self-acceptance.
