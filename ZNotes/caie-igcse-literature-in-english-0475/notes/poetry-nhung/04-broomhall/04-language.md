## Language

* ### Sensory imagery:

  * “the Arabic texture of my hair” “Texture” appeals to the sense of touch, drawing attention to the speaker's physical appearance.

  * The imagery suggests that cultural identity can be associated with visible features.

* ### Oxymoron:

  * “blue shameless blackness” “Shameless” suggests confidence and a refusal to feel embarrassed, while “blackness” emphasises racial identity.

  * The unusual combination highlights the complexity of expressing and accepting one's identity.

* ### Contrast:

  * “middle-class white boys in reggae bands” The contrast between the speaker and the white boys highlights differences in race and social background.

  * It raises the question of whether appreciating another culture means fully understanding its history and experiences.

* ### Repetition:

  * “love roots” and “love” The repetition emphasises the boys' appreciation of reggae music and its cultural associations.

  * However, it also questions whether loving a culture is enough to understand its deeper meaning.

* ### Metaphor:

  * “this shop / can be all the home I need” The shop is presented as a form of home rather than simply a physical location.

  * Suggesting that a place can provide emotional comfort and belonging.
