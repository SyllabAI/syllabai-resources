## Imagery

### Sensory imagery:

* “the smell of cassava roasted over open coals.” The smell imagery creates a vivid impression of food and its cultural associations.

* It connects the speaker's surroundings with familiar cultural experiences and memories.

### Sensory imagery:

* “the Arabic texture of my hair” The description makes the speaker's physical appearance vivid and tangible.

* Hair becomes a visible feature through which others may interpret his cultural identity.

### Visual imagery:

* “blue shameless blackness” The colour imagery draws attention to racial identity and the way Blackness is represented.

* “Shameless” adds a sense of confidence, potentially challenging negative attitudes towards Black identity.

### Symbolism:

* “this shop / can be all the home I need” The shop symbolises familiarity, comfort and acceptance.

* It suggests that belonging can be found through everyday places and relationships, rather than only through one's birthplace.
