## Mood

* Conversational tone:

  * “How much, auntie?” The everyday question creates a casual and familiar atmosphere.

  * Addressing the woman as “auntie” suggests respect and a sense of community.

* Reflective tone:

  * “if their love is enough to know the code.” The questioning tone introduces uncertainty about whether the boys genuinely understand the culture they appreciate.

  * It encourages readers to consider the difference between enjoying a culture and understanding its history.

* Confident tone:

  * “blue shameless blackness” The word “shameless” suggests confidence and a refusal to feel embarrassed about racial identity.

  * This creates a defiant tone that challenges the expectation that people should hide or feel ashamed of who they are.

* Comforting yet uncertain mood:

  * “I can be Abdi and this shop / can be all the home I need.” The reference to “home” creates a sense of comfort and acceptance.

  * However, the word “today” highlights that feeling of belonging may be temporary.
