## Structure

* First-person narration:

  * “I'm Abdi.” The first-person perspective presents the speaker's personal experience of identity.

  * It allows readers to understand how he navigates the way others perceive him.

* Third-person self-reference:

  * “At least these boys who call me / Abdi seem to be fond of Abdi.” Referring to himself as “Abdi” in the third person creates distance between the speaker and the identity others recognise.

  * This may suggest uncertainty about whether the identity others assign to him fully represents who he is.

* Enjambment:

  * “I can be Abdi and this shop / can be all the home I need.” The sentence continues across the line break, creating a flowing rhythm.

  * The separation draws attention to the relationship between the speaker's identity and his sense of home.

* Conversational rhythm:

  * “How much, auntie?” The short question resembles everyday speech and makes the interaction feel natural.

  * This grounds the poem's exploration of identity and belonging in an ordinary setting.
