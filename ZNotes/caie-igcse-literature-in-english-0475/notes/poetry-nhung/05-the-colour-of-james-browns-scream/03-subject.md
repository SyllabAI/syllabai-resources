## Subject

* ### Alliteration

  The combination of assonance and consonance references different genres of music =\> The poem moves through different representations of music, gradually developing the idea of its power. 

* ### **Allusion (Poetic and literary technique of referencing a famous person, place, event, artwork, or other text)**

  * “Legba” a Vodou gatekeeper spirit who opens the path for humans to speak with the divine.

  * “Larry Levan” Famous NYC DJ at Paradise Garage =\> Represents music’s power to unite and transform people 

  * “Puerto Rican” =\> Shows cultural diversity within club culture

  * “Machismo” =\> Traditional masculine toughness/pride

  * “When Doves Cry” =\> A famous song by Prince, released in 1984 =\> Connects poem to Black music history and emotional intensity

  * “James Brown's” =\> Influential funk/soul musician =\> Represents energy, rhythm, and Black musical heritage

  * “King of King Street” =\> Club/music reference =\> Shows music allowing emotional vulnerability

  * “Heartbroken” =\> A song associated with Larry Levan's Paradise Garage DJ sets. =\> Shows music allowing emotional vulnerability

  * “Willi Ninja” =\> American dancer/choreographer famous for voguing =\> Represents dance, self-expression and identity

* ### Metaphor:

  “Let us become like them”. “them”(Legba, Larry Levan, Willi Ninja,…) is a metaphor for the culture and history of black culture. The desire to connect to the past leads to the theme of identity and heritage

  “I have known you by many names / but today you are Larry Levan.” The shift from general identities to a specific person establishes music as connected to personal and cultural memory.
