## Imagery

* ### Image choice:

  “wields a cutthroat like a paintbrush” creates a vivid image connecting violence and art. Images of taste, movement, and art make music feel almost tangible

  “To fix a drink our lips/ will yearn for, a taste we've been/ trying to recreate ever since.” Uses sensory imagery to make music seem desirable and physical.

* ### Semantic field:

  “Platter”, “swing”, “feet”, “shuffle” and “shape-shift”  Semantic field of movement, dancing
  Emphasizes the physical power of music, showing how the beat makes people move and creates a sense of energy and freedom.

* ### Symbolism:

  * Legba:

    * Symbol of Black cultural beliefs

    * Connected to movement (Ability to shapeshift)

  * Singers/ Dancers/ DJ:

    * Black popular culture

* ### Similie

  “Sliding quick and slick as a skin-fade” Compares the dancer’s smooth movements to a fresh haircut, celebrating Black culture and identity through the appreciation of style, dance, and self-expression.

* ### Motif:

  * Music/ sound

  * Movement

  * Culture legacy
