## Language

* ### Word choice:

  “us” or “our” creates inclusion and community.

  Pronouns such as “us” and “I” show both individual and collective experiences.

  “We are still moving/ to the same sound” represents the lasting impact of the black culture and music

* ### Metaphor:

  “Paintbrush” presents music as artistic expression.

  Crossroads” presents us crossing into the spirit world =\> emphasizing how music connects a person to their soul.
