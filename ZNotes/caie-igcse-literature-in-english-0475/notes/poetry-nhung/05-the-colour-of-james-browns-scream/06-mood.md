## Mood

* “Though some of us don’t know it is your grave” Creates a bittersweet and mournful mood because the celebration takes place alongside awareness of death.

* References to cultural identity (Legba, Singers, Dancers, DJ) create a sense of pride and belonging.

* “To fix a drink our lips/ will yearn for” The poet uses sensory imagery to make music feel physical and desirable, specifically the culture of music presented in the past.

* “Our” The poem becomes increasingly powerful and celebratory as it presents music as something that unites people.
