## Structure

### Alliteration

* Sliding”, “skin”, “sweeping” and “slick” Sibilance, creating a fast-paced rhythm. The poet uses sound devices to recreate the musical quality of the subject.

* Alliteration, assonance, and consonance recreate the rhythm and musicality of the subject.

* Repetition of sounds helps the poem echo the qualities of music itself. The combination of assonance and consonance creates a flowing, musical quality.
