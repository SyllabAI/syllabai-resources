## Overview

* ### **Structure:** 

  * 25 lines, 1 stanza

* ### **Themes:**

  * Power of music

  * Community and connection

  * Identity and transformation

  * Memory and legacy

* ### **Background:**

* The single continuous stanza mirrors the continuous flow of music and dancing.

* The poem moves through different representations of music, gradually developing the idea of its power.

* References to DJs, songs, and dancers show how music connects people across different identities and generations.
