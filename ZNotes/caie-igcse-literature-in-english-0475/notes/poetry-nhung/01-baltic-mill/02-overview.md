## Overview

* ### Structure:

  * 21 lines, 3 stanzas

* ### Themes:

  * Identity

  * Belonging/Connections

  * Nostalgia

  * Cultural heritage

  * Racism

* ### Background:

  * Chingonyi was born in Zambia but grew up in Newcastle, which became an important part of his identity

  * In the poem, he walks through Newcastle and the Quayside with someone close to him, passing places connected to his childhood and the city’s industrial past.

  * He reflects on how the city has changed and how its history, including racism, is part of the place he grew up in

* ### Poet’s purpose:

  * Identity and belonging: To explore how the places we grow up in shape our identity, even when we have connections to different cultures.

  * Connections: To show how people from different backgrounds can overcome their differences and develop meaningful relationships.

  * Cultural heritage: To encourage readers to acknowledge both the achievements and the uncomfortable history of the places they call home.

  * Nostalgia: To explore how places change over time while memories preserve our connections to the past.
