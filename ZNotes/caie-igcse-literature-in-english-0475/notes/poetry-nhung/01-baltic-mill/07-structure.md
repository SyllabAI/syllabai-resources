## Structure

* ### **Septets**:

  * The poem is divided into three seven-line stanzas, creating a controlled and organised structure that reflects Newcastle’s buildings and industrial landscape. This contrasts with the poem’s personal reflections, suggesting how the speaker’s memories and emotions exist within the city’s physical environment.

* ### **Enjambment**:

  * “rain- / slick paving slabs” $\rightarrow$ the sentence continues across the line, creating a sense of continuous movement, like flowing water.

  * Used throughout the poem $\rightarrow$ creates a flowing pace and reflects the movement of the River Tyne and the speaker's thoughts. This links the physical movement through Newcastle with the speaker’s emotional reflections, suggesting that memories and personal connections flow together like the river itself.

* ### **Rhyme**:

  * “source” / “course”  $\rightarrow$ creates a connection between the river imagery and the idea of a journey, suggesting that the speaker and his companion are travelling through life together despite their different backgrounds.

  * “slick” / “slabs” $\rightarrow$ creates rhythm within the line and contributes to the poem's musical quality.

* ### **Sibilance**:

  * “slick”, “slabs”, “standing”, “still”, “stories”, “sources”, “sedimental selves” The repeated ‘s’ sound creates a soft, flowing effect that resembles the movement of water. This reinforces the River Tyne motif and reflects the gentle, intimate nature of the speaker’s connection with his companion.

* ### **Plosive alliteration**:

  * “past”, “paving”, “Biscuit”, “backstreet” the repeated ‘p’ and ‘b’ sounds create sharper, more abrupt sounds that contrast with the poem’s flowing water imagery. This may reflect the solid, built environment of Newcastle and draw attention to the city’s industrial landscape and history.

* ### **Asyndeton**:

  * “drawn from two sources, / offered up our flaws, / our sedimental selves” $\rightarrow$ the lack of conjunctions creates a continuous sequence of ideas, mirroring the flowing movement of the river. It also presents the sharing of personal flaws and experiences as a natural progression, emphasising the trust and intimacy between the speaker and his companion.
