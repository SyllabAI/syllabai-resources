## Mood

* ### **Gloomy imagery:**

  * “turbid mist” the adjective “turbid” suggests cloudiness and obscurity, creating a gloomy, melancholic atmosphere. This reflects the speaker’s thoughtful mood as he walks through Newcastle, looking back on its history and memories.

* ### **Nostalgic metaphor:**

  * “throwback to another world” presents the past as a completely different world, emphasising the speaker’s nostalgia for earlier experiences and how memories allow him to reconnect with a past that feels distant from his present.
  * The phrase could suggest that memories idealise the past, making it feel like a different world rather than an accurate reflection of how things were.

* ### **Historical allusion:**

  * “blackface minstrels” refers to minstrel shows in which performers, often white, darkened their faces to portray racist stereotypes of Black people. This exposes Britain's history of racism and shows how prejudice is embedded in the area's cultural past.

* ### **Inclusive pronouns:**

  * “we”, “our”, “us” create a sense of togetherness and intimacy, emphasising the shared experiences between the speaker and his companion. This creates a warm, connected mood and highlights their growing bond.

* ### **Simile:**

  * “like this river, drawn from two sources” compares two people from different backgrounds to a river formed from two sources, suggesting that their differences can merge to create a shared identity. This creates a hopeful and intimate mood, emphasising the strength of their connection.
