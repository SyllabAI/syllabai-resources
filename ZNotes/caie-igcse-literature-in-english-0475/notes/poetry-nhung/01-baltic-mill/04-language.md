## Language

* ### Alliteration:

  * “cobbled street past Castle Keep” The repeated ‘c’ sound creates rhythm and draws attention to the physical setting of Newcastle.

  * “Biscuit ... backstreet ... back” The repeated ‘b’ sound creates a rhythmic, almost musical quality.

  * “slick paving slabs” The repeated ‘s’ sound creates a smooth, flowing sound which links to the rain and river imagery.

* ### **Metaphor**:

  * “our sedimental selves” $\rightarrow$ compares people to layers of sediment, suggesting that their identities have been formed from layers of memories and past experiences.
  * The image could also suggest that some past experiences remain buried beneath the surface, influencing people even when they are not openly discussed.

* ### **Word choice**:

  * “vestige” $\rightarrow$ means a remaining trace of something from the past, reinforcing the idea of memory and things disappearing over time.
