## Subject

* ### **Semantic field of place:**

  * “Castle”, “Quayside”, “Baltic Mill”, “Tyne”, “Laing”, “Gateshead” $\rightarrow$ establishes a strong sense of place and belonging, showing how Newcastle shapes the speaker's identity and connects to the theme of Identity.

* ### **Allusion (Indirect reference to a famous person, place, event, or literary work)**:

  * “blackface/ minstrels from Gateshead mines and/ iron works.” $\rightarrow$ references Britain's racist cultural history, showing that the speaker's sense of place is connected to both its heritage and uncomfortable past; representing the theme of Racism, Cultural Heritage. As someone born in Zambia and raised in Newcastle, Chingonyi has connections to different cultural backgrounds. The allusion highlights how racism forms part of the history of the place where he grew up, complicating his sense of belonging.

* ### **Metaphor:**

  * “throw/ back to another world” presenting the past as a different world, emphasising nostalgia and the distance between past and present, connecting to the theme of Nostalgia

* ### **Personification:**

  * “the city of faces / cast in stories”, which gives the city a human quality, suggesting that the people and their experiences are an important part of its identity $\rightarrow$ Connects to the theme of Identity, Belonging.
