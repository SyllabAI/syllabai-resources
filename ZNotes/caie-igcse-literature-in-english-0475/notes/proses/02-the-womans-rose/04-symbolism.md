## Symbolism

* **Rose**

  * Emotional connection and sisterhood. The fact that the rose was still in the box even after years showcases how much she values that bond and connection. The rose is withered and still stands strong, this highlights the everlasting effect of a simple act and how much it has affected the writer.

* **Acacia flowers**

  * Given to the writer by a man, showcasing superficial admiration. The writer throws it away after a few years and showcases it does not mean to her that much and is easily removable from her memory.

* **Spring**

  * Showcases hope and how sisterhood and the connection between women never fails.

* **The townsmen**

  * The superficial society that creates rivalry amongst women.

* **Box**

  * Showcases the writer’s attempt to preserve the emotional connection and sisterhood she experienced. A way to store and hang onto the past.
