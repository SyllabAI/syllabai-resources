# **The Woman’s Rose**

## Analysis

* The Woman’s Rose follows an unnamed author as she reminisces about her past, namely when she was fifteen and had visited a small up-country town, where she met another young girl.

* The Prose explores various themes of jealousy, sisterhood, and the complexity of female relationships, as well as how women are pitted against each other by superficial societal expectations.

* The prose starts with the writer recounting an old brown box she has. The writer states that the box holds all her precious possessions.

* However, the writer states that her most prized possession is a ‘rose’, and no one has her rose.

* The writer ties the rose to a young girl she met on her trip to a town. The writer emphasizes how the girl was the epitome of beauty and had all the men’s attention.

* However, as soon as the writer entered the town, all the men started showering her with all the affection and attention, completely forgetting about the other girl, as she was ‘younger’ and ‘new’.

* The writer furthermore states that although she liked the attention, she despised the men for forgetting all about the other girl.

* Additionally, the writer was sure that the young girl despised her for stealing the spotlight and sparking the themes of jealousy and rivalry.

* However, towards the end of the prose, as the writer was leaving the town, it was revealed that the young girl had been the one who gave her the rose.

* Each woman admired the other, and their so-called rivalry was a mere assumption and was created by patriarchal expectations and superficial admiration.
