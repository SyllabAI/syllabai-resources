## Themes

* Superficial vs. genuine love

* Competition and rivalry

* Feminism

* Hope (SPRING)

* Emotional connection and sisterhood

* Memory and nostalgia.

* Female solidarity

  * How the writer and the girl chose to uplift each other instead of competing to be the center of attention.

* Female worth beyond appearances

  * The story mainly suggests how women are objectified, and that is more to the young girl and the writer than their appearances. This suggests that the true value lies in character and in kindness, which means more to the writer ( why she kept the rose) than materialistic connections ( why she threw the acacia flowers)
