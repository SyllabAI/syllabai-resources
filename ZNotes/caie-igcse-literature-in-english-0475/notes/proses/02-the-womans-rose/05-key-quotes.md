## Key Quotes

* ‘*... no one has my rose*.’

  * Showcases how much the rose means to her and foreshadows its importance and how it has affected her life.

* ‘*Spring never fails us*’

  * Spring comes after winter, thus showcases how hope is returning along with the colour. A reminder that no matter how hard a situation is something or how cold a winter is, spring will always show up as it’s a part of nature’s cycle.

* *‘ I have an old, brown carved box’*

  * Highlights the themes of nostalgia and memory.

* ‘ She and I never spoke to each other’

  * Showcases the duality of female relationships and how they are put against each other by society.

* *‘ … the rose is still there*’

  * showcases permanence and how much the writer values that act of kindness.

* *‘I was like a child with a new whip’*

  * Highlights the power she had over the men just because she was young and new, which implicitly suggests the materialistic mindset of the society and the men. ‘Child’ further connotes youth and innocence.
