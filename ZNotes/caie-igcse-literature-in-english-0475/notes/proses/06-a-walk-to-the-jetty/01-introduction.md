# A Walk To The Jetty

## Introduction

* **Author & Context:** Written by celebrated Antiguan-American author Jamaica Kincaid, "A Walk to the Jetty" serves as the poignant concluding chapter of her acclaimed 1985 semi-autobiographical novel *Annie John*, capturing post-colonial themes of displacement and the painful transition into adulthood.

* **Core Premise:** The narrative follows seventeen-year-old Annie John as she walks through her familiar hometown of St. John's, Antigua, alongside her parents to reach the jetty, where a ship waits to take her to England to pursue a career in nursing.

* **Key Focus:** The story acts as a profound psychological exploration of adolescence, independence, and ambivalence, focusing on the painful necessity of severing maternal and cultural bonds in order to establish an autonomous self.
