## Key Quotes

* ***"My heart now beat fast, and no matter how hard I tried, I couldn't keep my mouth from falling open and my nostrils from spreading to the ends of my face. My old fear of slipping between the boards of the jetty and falling into the dark-green water... came over me."***

  * Crucial because it uses heavy physical imagery to emphasize that her journey is not a temporary visit, but a permanent break from her past that causes her physical and mental anguish. The bodily reactions literalize her internal panic at crossing the threshold.

* ***"I did not want to go to England, I did not want to be a nurse, but I would have chosen going off to live in a cavern and keeping house for seven unruly men rather than go on with my life as it stood."*** 

  * Captures the central paradox of her development: Annie recognizes that while she is leaving behind everything familiar, she must fiercely and completely break away from her claustrophobic life on the island to form her own individual identity.

* **"As I passed them, I looked at this and I looked at that... I walked through the streets... I was memory."**

  * **Why It’s Important:** This detail highlights how nostalgia intensifies simple observations, turning everyday island landmarks into painful reminders of a childhood she can no longer hold onto. She actively realizes she is transforming into the sum of her past as she physically walks away from it.

* **"I went down into my cabin... I lay down on my bunk. The sound of the sea going *clic, clic, clic* against the side of the ship was the only sound I could hear..."**

  * **Why It’s Important:** This closing action marks her definitive transition into adulthood, demonstrating that moving forward requires a deliberate acceptance of the blank slate ahead, even when the future remains entirely uncertain and isolated from her home.
