## Analysis 

* **Genre & Form:** A coming-of-age narrative  short story/novel chapter written in the first person. It presents a psychological and emotional exploration of adolescence, independence, and post-colonial identity.

* **Narrative Perspective & Voice:** Narrated by Annie John in the first person. Her voice is deeply intimate, introspective, hypersensitive, and marked by intense emotional conflict—oscillating between childlike attachment and adult cynicism.

* **Structure:** The story is structured around a **physical and emotional journey**. The literal, linear walk from Annie’s childhood home to the jetty is punctuated by non-linear memory fragments, turning the physical route into a map of her past.

* **Post-Colonial Context:** Set in British-colonized Antigua, the text reflects the colonial practice where young Antiguans felt compelled to leave their island for England (the "mother country") to achieve professional advancement, leaving them caught between two worlds.
