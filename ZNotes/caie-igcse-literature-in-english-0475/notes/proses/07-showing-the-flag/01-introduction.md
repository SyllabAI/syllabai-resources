# Showing The Flag

## Introduction

* **Author & Context:** Written by acclaimed English author Jane Gardam  and first published in her 1989 collection *Showing the Flag*. It serves as an acutely observed social commentary on "Englishness”, examining middle-class emotional repression, structural custom, and communication barriers.

* **The Setting:** Set in post-WWII England and France. The narrative follows a solo cross-channel voyage via steamship and train from an English port to Paris, forcing a child into overwhelming, busy, and adult-dominated spaces.

* **Core Premise:** The story follows Philip, an almost 13-year-old schoolboy who has been attending boarding schools since the age of six. He is traveling entirely unaccompanied to France for a three-month educational exchange stay with a French-Canadian family, the Fosters. To identify himself to his host, Major Foster, he must pin a small paper **Union Jack flag** to his lapel upon arrival. When a sudden sea breeze blows the tiny flag away, Philip slips into an intense psychological spiral of panic, assuming his mother has deliberately abandoned him.
