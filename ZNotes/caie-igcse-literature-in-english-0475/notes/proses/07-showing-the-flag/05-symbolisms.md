## Symbolisms

* **The Union Jack Flag:** Literally used for port identification, it symbolically represents identity, connection to home, and emotional security. Its loss plunges Philip into psychological isolation, while the hidden spare flag represents the ultimate triumph of maternal love over accidental misfortune.

* **The Idiom "Showing the Flag":** Literally referencing naval ships displaying their national flags upon entering foreign harbors, the title metaphorically signifies maintaining a courageous, composed, and dutiful front despite feeling completely terrified inside.

* **The "Immense" Suitcases:** Symbolize the massive adult roles and emotional burdens the young boy is forced to heave up and carry entirely on his own. His defensive actions protecting them from thieves on the train literalize his forced, premature steps toward self-reliance.
