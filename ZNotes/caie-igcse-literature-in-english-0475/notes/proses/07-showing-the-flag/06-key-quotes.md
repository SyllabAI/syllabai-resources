## Key Quotes

* ***"The boy with big ears, whose father was dead, kissed his mother with a sliding away of the eyes, heaved up his two immense suitcases and loped up the gangplank."***

  * Instantly establishes the text's poignant tone. The sensory imagery of "big ears" frames Philip's raw, small vulnerability, while the "immense suitcases" and "sliding away of the eyes" signal his deep internal anxieties and emotional avoidance.

* ***"Since six. He's been away since he was six. Oh, boarding-schools. Oh, children—why does one have them?"*** 

  * Exposes Gwen's hidden heartbreak and resentment toward the rigid English boarding-school custom. The fragmented repetition and rhetorical question demonstrate that she views the separation not as a choice, but as an agonizing "renunciation" forced upon her by class expectations.

* ***"She did not love him, want him, know him and she never had… She wanted him lost."***

  * **Why It’s Important:** Showcases the extreme, dark heights of a child's psychological spiral when cut off from open communication. The loss of the paper flag distorts his reality completely, converting his mother's stoicism into an act of malicious abandonment.

* ***"Oh Philip, my darling, don't hate me for fussing, but I do so love you."*** 

  * The emotional resolution of the text. Written in his mother’s direct, uncensored handwriting, the note completely shatters Philip's defensive paranoia. It breaks through the cultural wall of English reserve, delivering the explicit validation of love and safety he desperately needed.
