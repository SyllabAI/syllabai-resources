
## Key Quotes

* ***"When it happens"***

  * Establishes the central premise of the story. The phrase "when" (rather than "if") indicates that Mrs. Burridge views disaster not as a possibility, but as an absolute certainty.

* ***"He can't protect me. They are all waiting, for whatever it is to happen."***

  * Marks a key shift in her mindset. She dismantles the myth of male protection and acknowledges that when societal systems fail, every individual is ultimately on their own.

* ***"She thinks of her girls now and hopes they will not have babies; it is no longer the year for it"***

  * Illustrates her stark, unvarnished pragmatism. Rather than harboring sentimental hopes for future generations, she views family life through the cold lens of survival and resource scarcity.

* ***"They have spotted her gun, their eyes have fastened on it, they want it."***

  * Highlights the collapse of social trust. In an emergency, basic possessions transform into targets for violence, cementing the dystopian reality where survival eclipses human community.

* ***"You never know how you will act in a thing like that until it actually happens."***

  * Underlines the story's core ambiguity. It reminds the reader that all her detailed preparation remains theoretical until tested by real catastrophe.
