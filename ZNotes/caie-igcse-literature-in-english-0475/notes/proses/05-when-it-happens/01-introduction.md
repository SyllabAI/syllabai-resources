# When It Happens

## Introduction

* **Author & Context:** Written by acclaimed Canadian author Margaret Atwood. "When It Happens" reflects Cold War-era anxieties surrounding sudden geopolitical collapse, nuclear dread, and domestic insecurity.

* **Core Premise:** Set on a quiet Canadian farm, the narrative centers on Mrs. Burridge, an aging, practical housewife who carries out her everyday domestic routines while obsessively planning for an impending, unstated societal collapse.

* **Key Focus:** The story acts as a psychological study of foreboding and gender roles, examining how women navigate survival, isolation, and the breakdown of traditional patriarchal protection when order fails.
