## Character Analysis

**Mrs. Burridge**

* **Role:** A pragmatic, highly observant 51-year-old farm woman.

* **Traits:** Practical, anxious, isolated, hyper-prepared, and emotionally distant.

* **Development & Function:** Mrs. Burridge represents survivalism rooted in domestic knowledge. Haunted by memories of past historical struggles, she sees through the illusion of societal stability. While her planning may appear paranoid, Atwood frames her actions as the rational response of a woman who knows she cannot rely on traditional patriarchal protection when order breaks down.

**Frank (Her Husband)**

* **Role:** Mrs. Burridge's husband.

* **Traits:** Complacent, outdoor-focused, dismissive, and traditional.

* **Function:** Serves as a direct foil to Mrs. Burridge. Frank represents the false security of normal routine. He ignores subtle signs of crisis, dismisses his wife's concerns, and leaves structural problems unfixed (e.g., the hole in the stairs), highlighting the emotional gap in their marriage and Mrs. Burridge's underlying isolation.
