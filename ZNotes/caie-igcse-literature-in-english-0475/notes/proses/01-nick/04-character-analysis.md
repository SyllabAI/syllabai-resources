## Character Analysis

**Nick (The Protagonist)**

* **Initial State:** Bitter, ungrateful, and envious. Despite owning a snug home, healthy livestock, and thriving crops, he cannot enjoy his wealth because he is fixated on what others have.

* **Flaw:** Selfishness and spite. When given magic, his immediate impulse is to ruin things for his neighbours.

* **Development:** Through physical suffering in non-human forms, Nick undergoes a moral awakening. He realizes that envy is self-destructive and ends the story humble, repentant, and contented.

**The Fairy** 

* **Role:** Supernatural catalyst.

* **Function:** She is not a benevolent fairy godmother; she acts as a trickster figure who gives Nick enough rope to hang himself. By turning his wishes into immediate reality, she forces him to confront the absurd, destructive consequences of his own bitter thoughts.

**Giles Hodge & The Villagers**

* **Role:** Plot foils and representatives of normal community life.

* **Function:** Giles Hodge represents hard-working, honest prosperity. The villagers highlight the contrast between Nick’s inner malice and the peaceful, innocent town around him.
