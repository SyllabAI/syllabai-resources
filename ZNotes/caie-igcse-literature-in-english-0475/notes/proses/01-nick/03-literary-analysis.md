## Literary Analysis 

* **Genre & Form:** "Nick" is structured as a **cautionary fairy tale and moral allegory**. It uses fantasy and transformation to explore real human psychology and morality.

* **Narrative Arc & Poetic Justice:** Rossetti structures the narrative around cause-and-effect loops. Every time Nick acts out of malice, his transformation backfires. This creates **poetic justice**—his attempts to inflict suffering on others directly inflict physical pain on himself.

* **Circular Plot Structure:** The narrative begins with Nick as a bitter man in his village and ends with him as a cured, peaceful man in the same village. The physical landscape remains constant, but Nick undergoes an internal moral transformation.

* **Victorian Context:** Written in the 19th century, the story highlights Victorian values of **gratitude, community harmony, humility, and moral accountability**. It serves as a critique against greed, relentless comparison, and societal discontentment.
