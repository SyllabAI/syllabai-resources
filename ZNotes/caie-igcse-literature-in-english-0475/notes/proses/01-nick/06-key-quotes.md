## Key Quotes

* ***"I call him poor because, with all these, he was discontented and envious."***

  *  This quote establishes the central thesis of the story. It defines "poverty" not as a lack of material goods, but as a state of mind driven by relentless discontentment and envy.

* ***"Every other man's riches were his poverty."***

  * Highlights how envy distorts reality. Nick cannot measure his worth by what he owns; he measures it by what others possess, turning their happiness into his own source of suffering.

* ***"Henceforward you shall straightway become everything you wish; only mind, you must remain under one form for at least an hour."***

  *  Set by the fairy, this rule introduces the core narrative constraint. The "one hour" rule prevents Nick from making quick escapes, forcing him to experience the full, painful consequences of his reactionary wishes.

* ***"It was provocation enough to be burned; but to contribute by his misfortune to the well-being of his tormentors was still more aggravating."***

  *  Demonstrates situational irony and Nick's initial refusal to learn. Even while suffering as wood/fire, his main frustration is that his suffering accidentally helps others, emphasizing how deep-seated his spite is before his ultimate epiphany.

* ***"*Nick felt quite discouraged by his ill success, and now entertained only one wish—that he were himself again*."***

  * Marks Nick’s moment of epiphany. He realizes the futility of malice, leading directly to his decision to seek redemption, repair relationships, and live peacefully as himself.
