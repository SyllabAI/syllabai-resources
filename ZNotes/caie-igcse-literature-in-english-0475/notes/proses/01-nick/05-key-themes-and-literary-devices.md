
## Key Themes and Literary Devices

**Key Themes**

1. **The Destructive Nature of Envy:** Jealousy distorts perception. Nick is materially rich, yet his internal bitterness makes him feel poor. Rossetti demonstrates that envy harms the envious person far more than its intended targets.

2. **True Contentment Comes From Within:** External riches or status cannot bring peace if the mind is filled with bitterness. Nick only finds true happiness once he stops wishing for more and accepts who he is.

3. **Repentance and Redemption:** The story offers a message of hope. No matter how spiteful or flawed someone has been, genuine self-reflection and actions to make amends can bring about moral redemption.

**Literary Devices**

* **Irony (Situational Irony):** Every wish Nick makes to gain an advantage or cause harm results in immediate harm to himself. For instance, becoming fire to escape being burned leads to him being drenched and nearly extinguished.

* **Symbolism of Transformations:**

  * **Sparrows:** Represent petty, pest-like greed.

  * **Mad Dog / Stick:** Represent blind hostility and anger turning into an object of violence.

  * **Fire:** Represents uncontrolled rage and envy consuming everything, including itself.

* **Allegory:** The entire narrative operates as a symbolic lesson on morality and the spiritual dangers of envy.

* **Contrast / Juxtaposition:** The idyllic, cozy setting of the village is sharply contrasted with Nick’s ugly, agitated inner thoughts.
