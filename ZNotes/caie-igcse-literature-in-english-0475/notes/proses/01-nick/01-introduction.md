# Nick

## Introduction

* **Author & Context:** Written by Victorian poet and author Christina Rossetti, "Nick" is a Victorian moral tale and cautionary fairy tale that uses supernatural elements to explore human morality.

* **Core Premise:** Set in a small village near Fairyland, the story centers on Nick, a prosperous, healthy farmer who has everything he needs yet suffers from deep discontent, envy, and spite toward his neighbours.

* **Key Focus:** The text functions as a moral allegory, highlighting the self-destructive nature of envy, the importance of inner contentment, and the possibility of redemption through repentance and making amends.
