# Fluke

## Introduction

* **Author & Context:** Written by acclaimed Sri Lankan author Romesh Gunesekera, "Fluke" is featured in the *Stories of Ourselves* anthology. It provides a sharp post-colonial critique of modern corporate capitalism and post-civil-war Sri Lanka's shift toward superficial commercialism.

* **Core Premise:** The narrative centers on Vasantha, a pragmatic, elderly courier and taxi driver hired to transport Mr. Weerakoon—a high-strung, brand-obsessed corporate marketing consultant—to a beach resort for a seminar titled *"Marketing: The Secret of Success."*

* **Key Focus:** The story contrasts Vasantha's grounded, authentic simplicity against Mr. Weerakoon's fragile, performative corporate facade, exploring how true success lies in self-awareness, inner peace, and a connection to nature rather than commercial buzzwords and status symbols.
