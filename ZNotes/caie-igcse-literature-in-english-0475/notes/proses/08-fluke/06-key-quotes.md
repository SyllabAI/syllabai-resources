## Key Quotes

* ***"I do whatever my customer wants me to do. That's the key. It is no big secret."***

  * Demystifies the corporate hype. While Mr. Weerakoon charges money to present "The Secret of Success," Vasantha encapsulates genuine, practical business ethics in a single, simple sentence.

* ***"His blue suit is tight so that he looks like he is bursting with energy... a dark blue presentation folder... made of pretend leather..."***

  * Exposes the illusion of modern corporate image. Vasantha notices the cheap, artificial reality ("pretend leather") behind Mr. Weerakoon's polished appearance.

* ***"I don't give a toss about social standing or net worth."***

  * Highlights Vasantha’s strong personal identity. Unlike the conference attendees obsessed with rank and modern prosperity, Vasantha measures worth by peace of mind and self-reliance.

* ***"The sea keep\[s\] the same soft time..."***

  * Uses sibilance and gentle rhythm to highlight the contrast between nature's eternal peace and humanity’s stressful, self-imposed deadlines.

* ***"Whatever your foibles, your wanton misdeeds, you can dream of new ventures and be a success if you can sleep easy."***

  * Expresses the story’s core moral message: true success is not found in status, devices, or marketing, but in conscience, peace of mind, and the ability to "sleep easy."
