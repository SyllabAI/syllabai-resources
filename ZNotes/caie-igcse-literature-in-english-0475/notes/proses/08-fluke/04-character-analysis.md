
## Character Analysis

**Vasantha (The Narrator)**

* **Role:** An elderly, independent driver/courier running a modest business.

* **Traits:** Unassuming, observant, practical, witty, spiritually grounded, and contented.

* **Development & Function:** Vasantha serves as the moral anchor of the story. He does not care about social status or net worth. Because he does "what he loves" and keeps life simple, he possesses an inner calm ("good chi") that allows him to navigate modern disruptions smoothly.

**Mr. Weerakoon**

* **Role:** A frantic, modern marketing consultant and presenter.

* **Traits:** Anxious, performative, brand-obsessed, insecure, and clumsy ("waddles").

* **Development & Function:** He represents the modern corporate class in Sri Lanka's remodelled economy. Despite his sharp blue suit, tight fitting, and expensive gadgets, he is easily overwhelmed by minor setbacks (a lost phone, a broken projector), exposing the illusion of his expertise and authority.
