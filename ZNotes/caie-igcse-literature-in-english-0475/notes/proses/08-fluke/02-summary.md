## Summary 

Set in post-Civil War Sri Lanka, "Fluke" is narrated by Vasantha, an elderly, modest courier and taxi driver who runs his own small transportation business. Vasantha is hired to transport Mr. Weerakoon—a self-proclaimed "modern" corporate marketing consultant bursting with performative energy—to a coastal hotel for a presentation titled "Marketing: The Secret of Success."

Upon arriving at the resort, Mr. Weerakoon acts flustered, anxious, and overly reliant on technology. He struggles with his equipment and suffers a meltdown when he misplaces his brand-new smartphone. Vasantha, remaining calm and observant, assists with moving the presentation boxes. However, when Vasantha finds the misplaced smartphone, he looks through it and discovers Mr. Weerakoon's private corporate files, realizing the man's presentation is an empty sham. This creates an internal ethical dilemma for Vasantha, who realizes he holds the upper hand but cannot easily offer advice without admitting he invaded the man's privacy. Throughout the seminar, Vasantha steps away to appreciate the ocean breeze and reflect on the changing face of his country. He realizes that behind Mr. Weerakoon’s loud, corporate facade lies deep insecurity. By the end of the day, Vasantha holds the upper hand, recognizing that true success and inner peace come from self-awareness and simplicity, not superficial corporate buzzwords.
