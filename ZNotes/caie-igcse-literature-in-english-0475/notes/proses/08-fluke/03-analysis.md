## Analysis 

* **Genre & Form:** A contemporary short story (featured in *Stories of Ourselves*) written in the first-person narrative perspective. It functions as a social critique of modern capitalism and corporate posturing in post-war Sri Lanka.

* **Narrative Voice:** Told through Vasantha’s wry, observant, and humorous first-person voice. His pragmatic, unpretentious tone acts as a filter that exposes the absurdity and emptiness of modern corporate culture.

* **Contrast and Juxtaposition:** Gunesekera structures the narrative around sharp contrasts:

  * **The Modern Corporate World vs. The Natural World:** The claustrophobic, anxious environment of the conference room versus the timeless, calming presence of the sea outside.

  * **Vasantha vs. Mr. Weerakoon:** Authentic self-assurance versus performative, fragile confidence.

* **Double Meaning of the Title:** The title *"Fluke"* serves a dual purpose:

  1. It refers to a **stroke of luck or chance**, highlighting the unpredictable nature of success and modern business encounters.

  2. It refers to the **tail fin of a whale**, linking directly to the themes of nature, timelessness, and ocean life that contrast with human corporate schemes.
