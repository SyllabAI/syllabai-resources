# **The Black Ball**

## **Introduction**

* **Author & Context:** Written by renowned African American author Ralph Ellison and originally drafted in the 1940s, "The Black Ball" is a work of social realism that addresses the racial hierarchies, economic segregation, and systemic inequalities of Jim Crow-era America.

* **Core Premise:** The narrative centers on John, an African American single father working as a janitor in a luxury white apartment building, who struggles to protect his young son from prejudice while trying to keep his job under a harsh white manager.

* **Key Focus:** The story explores the intersection of racial oppression, fatherhood, and economic vulnerability, tracing a hardworking father's awakening to the necessity of collective labor solidarity over individual submission.
