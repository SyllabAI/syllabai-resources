## Key Themes

1. **Systemic Racism and Economic Vulnerability:** Illustrates how racial prejudice is tied to economic oppression, leaving Black workers constantly at risk of losing their livelihoods over unfair accusations.

2. **Fatherhood and the Loss of Innocence:** Highlights the painful burden of a Black parent who must shield their child from discrimination while gradually preparing them to survive in an unequal world.

3. **Solidarity vs. Individual Compliance:** Demonstrates that individual hard work and subservience cannot overcome systemic prejudice, highlighting the necessity of collective union organization.

4. **Dignity and Resilience:** Explores how marginalized individuals maintain internal strength and self-respect despite oppressive social conditions.

## Literary Devices

* **Idiom & Metaphor:** The phrase "behind the black ball" is used as a double-meaning metaphor representing both economic termination and societal disenfranchisement.

* **Juxtaposition:** The high-gloss, pristine brass in the luxury lobby is juxtaposed with the cramped, hidden garage living quarters, underscoring racial and class inequality.

* **Irony:** Situational irony occurs when John’s son is blamed and punished for a white child’s destructive behavior.

## Symbolism 

* **The "Black Ball" (Idiom & Toy):** Symbolizes **racial exclusion, social disadvantage, and the systemic 'game' of prejudice**. To be "behind the black ball" means to be at an insurmountable disadvantage in society.

* **The Brass Door Knobs/Fixtures:** Symbolize **the superficiality of wealth and white corporate expectations**. John must keep them polished to project an illusion of perfection for white tenants while living in obscurity.

* **The Union Organizer’s Burn Scars:** Symbolize **the physical cost of fighting oppression and the shared struggle for justice**. They serve as proof of sacrifice and authenticity.

* **The Garage Quarters:** Symbolize **marginalization and spatial segregation**, representing how Black workers are kept hidden behind the scenes of white luxury.
