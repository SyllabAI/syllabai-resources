## Summary 

Set in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, the story is narrated by an unnamed, disillusioned journalist who lives in a cramped apartment with his nagging wife and mother. Frustrated by his stagnant career and suffocating home life, he encounters Commander Scranton at a beachside café in Copacabana. Scranton is a shabby, destitute American man who claims to be a former NASA Apollo astronaut. Although a newspaper exposé previously unmasked Scranton as a fraud and a failed pilot, tourists still approach him, buying him drinks and paying for photos and stories of his supposed lunar voyage.

Fascinated by Scranton’s complete emotional detachment from reality, the narrator visits his filthy apartment above the local Luxor Cinema. Scranton shows him "lunar photographs," which are merely torn magazine cutouts. Rather than feeling cheated, the narrator finds peace in Scranton’s worldview. He abandons his job and family to care for Scranton as his health deteriorates, escorting the ailing man to cafés to profit off the tourists. After Scranton dies, the narrator moves into Scranton's empty room, adopts his identity, and takes over his role, telling tourists that he, too, walked on the moon.
