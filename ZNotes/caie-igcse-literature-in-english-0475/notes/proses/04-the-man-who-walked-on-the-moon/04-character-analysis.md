## Character Analysis

* **The Narrator:**

  * **Role:** A failed Brazilian journalist who abdicates his real life to step into another man's myth.

  * **Traits:** Cynical, passive, alienated, imaginative, and obsessed with escape.

  * **Function & Arc:** Represents the psychological desire to break free from societal burdens (family, employment). He transitions from an observer of Scranton’s fraud to his caretaker, and ultimately transforms into Scranton himself.

* **Commander Scranton:**

  * **Role:** A destitute American imposter pretending to be an Apollo astronaut.

  * **Traits:** Frail, delusional, opportunistic, yet strangely serene.

  * **Function:** Serves as the catalyst for the narrator's transformation. He embodies the tragic emptiness that lies behind grand human ambition and public celebrity.
