
## Key Quotes

* ***"you probably assume that I am a man of few achievements... ready to seize the first offer of a free drink."***

  * When reflecting on his unfulfilling career and the expectations of society, the narrator addresses the reader directly, saying that he is a man of few achievements. This quote establishes the narrator's self-awareness regarding his perceived status as a societal failure, setting up his eventual rejection of conventional standards of success.

* ***"around me were the million faces of the city. People strode past, so occupied with their own lives that they almost pushed me from the pavement."***

  * This illustrates the profound urban isolation that drives both the narrator and Scranton toward retreat into an alternate reality.

* **"*I, too, was once an astronaut,"*** and concluding with total conviction when approached by tourists: ***"Yes, I am the astronaut."*** This frames the cyclical structure of the story, proving that the narrator has fully traded his real-world identity for the liberating fantasy of the moonwalker.
