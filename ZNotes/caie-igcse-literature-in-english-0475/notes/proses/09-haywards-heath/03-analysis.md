## Analysis 

* **Genre & Form:** A contemporary short story framed as a psychological, introspective drama exploring memory, post-colonial identity, and aging.

* **Narrative Perspective:** Written in the third-person limited omniscient point of view, closely focalized through Attila. This perspective allows the reader to experience his sharp internal reflections and nostalgia while observing Rosie with respectful distance.

* **Tone & Mood:** Bittersweet, melancholic, tender, and reflective. Forna balances the painful reality of cognitive decline with quiet moments of beauty and grace.

* **Structural Arc:** Follows a journey toward emotional closure. Attila shifts from seeking a sentimental reunion with his past to quietly accepting the reality of loss and finding peace in Rosie's present happiness.
