# Haywards Heath

## Introduction

**Author & Context:** Written by Sierra Leonean-Scottish author Aminatta Forna, "Haywards Heath" explores themes of memory, aging, love, loss, and race in post-colonial Britain.

**Core Premise:** The narrative follows Attila, a now-successful West African trauma doctor visiting England, as he travels south to the town of Haywards Heath to visit his former university lover, Rosie. Upon arriving at her residential care home, he discovers that Rosie suffers from dementia and no longer recognizes him.

**Key Focus:** The story acts as a tender exploration of acceptance, contrasting Attila’s sharp memories and professional success against Rosie’s mental decline, ultimately examining what it means to let go of the past.
