# The Gold Watch

## Introduction

**Author & Context:** Written by renowned Indian novelist and short story writer Mulk Raj Anand, "The Gold Watch" is a classic piece of post-colonial realism that examines the harsh socio-economic realities of colonial India and the dehumanizing nature of corporate labor systems.

**Core Premise:** The narrative follows Srijut Sudarshan Sharma, a devoted and long-serving clerk working for a British distribution company, who receives a gold watch from his employer—an object he initially mistakes for a high honor, only to realize it is a veiled dismissal marking his sudden forced retirement.

**Key Focus:** The story acts as a poignant critique of British imperialism and industrial capitalism, contrasting a dedicated worker's human loyalty against an uncaring corporate machine that discards aging labor without compassion.
