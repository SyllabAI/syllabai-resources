## Analysis 

* **Genre & Form:** A post-colonial realism short story that functions as a socio-political critique of colonial labor exploitation, economic subordination, and human vulnerability.

* **Narrative Perspective:** Third-person limited omniscient, focalized primarily through Mr. Sharma’s internal thoughts, mounting dread, and physical reactions.

* **Setting & Tone:** Set in an office in colonial India. The atmosphere is tense, oppressive, and heavy with quiet despair, contrasting the formal, polite veneer of the British management with the internal panic of the protagonist.

* **Structural Arc:** Structured around the presentation of the gold watch—moving from deceptive anticipation and formal honor to sudden psychological devastation and quiet resignation.
