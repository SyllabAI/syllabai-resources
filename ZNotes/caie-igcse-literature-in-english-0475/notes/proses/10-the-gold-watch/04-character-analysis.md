
## Character Analysis

**Mr. Sharma**

* **Role:** The central protagonist; a veteran Indian clerk working for a British firm.

* **Traits:** Obedient, hard-working, humble, traditional, and emotionally vulnerable.

* **Development & Function:** **Srijut Sharma** represents the exploited colonial worker who believes that loyalty and hard work inside a corporate system will guarantee long-term security. His trajectory exposes the tragic disillusionment of the colonized subject when confronted with imperial capitalism.

**Mr. Acton**

* **Role:** The British manager/employer at Henry King & Co.

* **Traits:** Polite, detached, authoritative, paternalistic, and corporate-minded.

* **Development & Function:** Serves as the antagonist and an embodiment of British colonial rule and corporate coldness. He masks the cruelty of unfair dismissal behind a facade of Western etiquette, generosity, and formal praise.
