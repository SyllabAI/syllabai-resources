## Plot

* **Setting**: Oyo, Nigeria (around World War 2)


* Based on the real-life story of a horseman in Nigeria, the story revolves around the obstacles that Elesin Oba, the king’s horseman, faces on the night he is supposed to commit a ritualistic suicide in order to follow the king into the afterlife
* **Subplots**:
  * Conflicts between tradition and modernity
  * The Yoruba community
  * Elesin’s personal journey 
  * Colonial interference
