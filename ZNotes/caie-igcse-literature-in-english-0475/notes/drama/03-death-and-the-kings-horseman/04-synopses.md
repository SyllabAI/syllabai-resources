## Synopses

### **Act 1:**

* Elesin Oba, the late king’s horseman, is entering the market with his drummers and praise singers; he banters with the Praise Singer, and reassures him that he will not try to evade or escape death.
* Some women arrive and talk about how honorable Elesin is, and he acts as if he is offended, but they realize that he is only joking.
* A beautiful girl walks in and Elesin learns that she in engaged to Iyaloja’s (mother of the market) son
* Elesin states that he wants to marry this girl, and Iyaloja decides to let him do so, since he will die soon.
* Iyaloja sends the women off to inform the girl, and she appears worried that Elesin might bail on his death, much like the Praise Singer.
* The women return with the girl, and she kneels in front of Elesin.


### **Act 2:**

* Simon Pilkings (a district officer) and his wife Jane are preparing for a big party, by wearing costumes confiscated from local ‘Egungun’ men; these costumes were used for masquerades, representing the reincarnated spirits of the people’s ancestors.
* Amusa (native administration policeman) comes in to inform Simon of something but is astounded by the couple’s costumes; though he is a recent convert to Islam, he still respects the local people’s cultures.
* Amusa ends up writing what he meant to say on a pad of paper, and Simon reads it with Jane.
* With some help from Joseph, their servant, Simon finds that a local chief will be sacrificing himself tonight.
* Simon is disappointed by this news, as he realizes the chief involved is Elesin, whom he clashed with when Simon helped the chief’s son get into medical school in England.
* Joseph asks to leave but he is soon back, and interprets the music in the back as either wedding drums or the kind used to herald a chief’s death.
* While talking, Simon offends Joseph but apologizes, since Jane worries that Joseph may resign over this.
* Jane appears to decide on not going to the ball due to this incident but Simon tells her to put her costume back on, as he’s sent a note to Amusa, through Joseph, telling him to arrest Elesin and lock him up.
* It is also revealed that the Prince is in town and will be attending the ball.



### **Act 3:**

* Amusa and some constables have arrived in the market to stop Elesin’s suicide, but are met with some women that taunt Amusa and tell him to leave.
* Iyaloja appears disappointed with the women’s behavior, yet also tells Amusa to leave, who now backs off.
* Elesin steps out of a stall with a piece of cloth, appearing to have come from consummating the marriage.
* He instructs his wife on what to do after his death and wishes to die with the women in the market, and begins to dance.
* The Praise Singer talks back and forth with Elesin, though he seems to be in some kind of trance; the Praise Singer breaks down and hopes that Elesin is treated properly by the ancestors on the other side, saying that if not, he can return.




### **Act 4:**

* At the masquerade, the Pilkings’ and the Prince are talking, when a note arrives for Simon.
* The Resident snatches it before the note’s carrier can interrupt Simon, and reads it.
* The Resident carefully takes Simon away (while giving an order to his aide-de-camp) and asks Simon of the ongoing matters.
* Simon informs the Resident of Elesin, and since the Resident has sent for Amusa, Simon sends his wife to follow up and speed them back.
* After criticizing Simon for his handling of the situation, and commanding him to keep things controlled as well as sign a report, the Resident returns to the party.
* Simon dismisses the aide-de-camp, as well as Amusa since he still cannot look at Simon; Simon then heads out to intervene, leaving Jane at the party.
* Olunde (Elesin’s son) enters, looking for Simon, and he is glad to see Jane at first.
* However, they soon begin to clash, as Jane finds out that Olunde has gotten word of the king’s death, and has come to fulfill his duty of burying his father, rather than to stop him.
* Hearing the drums, Olunde states that Elesin must be dead; hearing Jane get upset, the aide-de-camp runs in, but Jane eventually tells him to leave, assuring him that she is better now.
* Simon comes back and Olunde tells him that though he has no hard feelings about Simon’s actions, he is glad that Simon didn’t succeed.
* Olunde tries to go see his father’s body before it turns cold, but Simon tells him it is guarded, so he’ll send Olunde down to “the place” with the aide-de-camp.
* Privately, Simon talks to the aide-de-camp about going down to the cellar, and says that he’ll take Elesin down to the cellar himself.
* Simon returns to Jane and asks her to stay with Olunde, while dismissing his requests to see Elesin.
* After Simon marches off, Jane and Olunde discuss the current situation, and Olunde remarks that the riots would occur only if Simon succeeded in stopping Elesin.
* From a distance they hear Elesin yelling, who then rush in with constables following him.
* Jane yells at them to leave him and Elesin collapses at Olunde; however, Olunde tells Elesin he doesn’t have a father anymore, leaving Elesin upset.


### **Act 5:**

* Elesin and his bride are in a cell when Simon enters.
* The two men talk, with Elesin lamenting the damage Simon created, but they hear Jane shouting so Simon leaves to go find her.
* While Simon is gone, Elesin reflects on what may have prevented his death from happening, such as the gods failing him, his marriage, etc.
* Jane and Simon come back with Iyaloja, who is eventually allowed to talk with Elesin.
* She taunts and criticizes him for his failure to complete his duty.
* After some discussion, some women bring in a bolt covering something unidentifiable, though Elesin refers to it as a “courier”, insisting they must talk so that he can fulfill what he can of his remaining duty.
* Iyaloja removes the cover, revealing Olunde’s dead body: he had sacrificed himself to complete his father’s mission.
* Elesin then strangles himself with his own chain.
* Iyaloja shames Simon for not understanding their customs, and also for trying to close Elesin’s eyes.
* She then sends with the bride to close Elesin’s eyes and put dirt on his eyelids.
