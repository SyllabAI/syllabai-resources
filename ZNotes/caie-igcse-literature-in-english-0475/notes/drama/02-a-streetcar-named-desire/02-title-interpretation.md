## Title Interpretation

* Characters’ actions are often driven by desire
* Streetcar symbolizes the journey and arrival of Blanche DuBois into the lives of her sister Stella and her husband Stanley in New Orleans
* Suggests both a literal place (Desire Street in New Orleans) and a metaphorical desire for a better life or escape
* Can represent each of the characters’ individual desires \[ex. Blanche’s desire for love and stability, Stanley’s desire for power and authority\]
* Can serve as a representation of Blanche’s struggle to adapt to a new life and her displacement from her past self
