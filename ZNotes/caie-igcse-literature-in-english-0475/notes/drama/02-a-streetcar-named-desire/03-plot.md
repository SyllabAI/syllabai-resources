## Plot

* **Setting**: 632, Elysian Fields, New Orleans, USA
  * Elysian Fields was the final resting place of the victorious and heroic in Greek mythology
* Blanche DuBois leaves her once-fabulous lifestyle after a series of losses and moves into a shabby apartment rented by her sister and brother-in-law
* **Subplots**:

  
  1. Blanche and Stanley’s rivalry within the household
  2. Stella and Stanley’s relationship
  3. Mitch and Blanche’s relationship
  4. Blanche navigating her past troubles and her current mental state
  5. Stanley’s power dynamics
