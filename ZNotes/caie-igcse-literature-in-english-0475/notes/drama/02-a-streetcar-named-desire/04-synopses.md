## Synopses

### **Scene 1**

* Stella watches Stanley go bowling.
* Blanche arrives at Elysian Fields and waits for her sister.
* The sisters greet each other when Stella returns.
* Blanche talks of various family deaths and loosing Belle Reve.
* The scene makes it clear that Blanche and Stanley are opposites.
* It is revealed that Blanche is a widow.



### **Scene 2**

* Stanley demands more information about Belle Reve from Stella, while Blanche bathes.
* Stella defiantly defends Blanche, then leaves.
* Stanley confronts Blanche when she finishes bathing, and she tries to divert the conversation by flirting.
* Stella’s pregnancy is revealed to Blanche.



### **Scene 3**

* It is poker night - Stanley and his friends are drinking and playing.
* Stella and Blanche return home from their outing.
* Blanche meets Mitch and views him as a potential suitor.
* Stanley turns violent with Stella after she calls him a “drunk animal”.
* Blanche then takes Stella upstairs to Eunice, their neighbor.
* However, Stella goes back to Stanley and they make love.



### **Scene 4**

* Blanche and Stella discuss the previous night, and Blanche is astounded at her sister’s acceptance of Stanley’s behavior.
* Blanche criticizes Stanley in a long, soliloquy-like speech.
* Stanley secretly listens to Blanche’s criticism of himself.



### **Scene 5**

* Eunice and Steve fight upstairs, while Blanche writes a fabricated story to a man named Shep.
* Stanley hints at knowledge of Blanche’s promiscuous past.
* Blanche flirts with a young man collecting newspaper subscriptions.
* Mitch arrives and they go on their date.



### **Scene 6**

* Blanche and Mitch return from their date.
* They have a conversation highlighting their differences.
* Blanche tells the story of how her husband committed suicide after she found out about his homosexuality.
* Mitch comforts her and they discuss marriage.



### **Scene 7**

* Few months pass, it is now mid-September and Blanche’s birthday.
* Stella is told of Blanche’s past by Stanley, though she outright refuses to believe him.
* Blanche takes note of the sudden change in the atmosphere.



### **Scene 8**

* Stella, Stanley, and Blanche celebrate the latter’s birthday without Mitch.
* Stanley turns violent and gives Blanche a bus ticket back home as a ‘gift’.
* Stella criticizes this but suddenly goes into labor, and Stanley takes her to the hospital.



### **Scene 9**

* Blanche has been drinking.
* Mitch, who has been clearly drinking as well, visits her and tells her that he knows of her stained past.
* Though Blanche attempts to explain herself, he dismisses this and tries to assault her.
* Blanche, however, shouts “Fire!” and Mitch then leaves.



### **Scene 10**

* Blanche’s drunken state is evident through her unstable and erratic dialogue, as well as actions.
* Stanley returns from the hospital and celebrates the birth of his child.
* Stanley unveils Blanche’s true nature and sexually assaults her.


### **Scene 11**

* Stella packs Blanche’s suitcase for what the latter believes is a trip with an admirer; Stella does not believe the assault story.
* Stella and Mitch are distressed as the Doctor and Matron arrive.
* Blanche is taken away, possibly to a mental institution.
