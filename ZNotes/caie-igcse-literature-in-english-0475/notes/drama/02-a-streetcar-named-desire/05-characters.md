## Characters

### 1. **Blanche DuBois**

* Around 30 years old, sister to Stella Kowalski
* Forced to leave her teaching position in Mississippi, ends up living with her sister in New Orleans
* Nervous and constantly appears to be on edge
* An alcoholic, promiscuous, widowed
* Avoids her reality
* Repulsed yet attracted to Stanley



### 2. **Stella Kowalski**

* Around 25, married to Stanley Kowalski, sister to Blanche DuBois
* Shares a robust and physical relationship with Stanley
* Has a mild disposition that makes her stand out
* Her denial of reality shows her similarity to Blanche, yet is more tolerant than her
* Left Mississippi for New Orleans



### 3. **Stanley Kowalski**

* Polish, yet sees himself as American
* Husband to Stella Kowalski
* Almost animalistic, as described by Blanche
* Lacks imagination and ideals
* Goes to large extents to unveil Blanche’s past
* Disturbing and degenerate
* Down-to-earth initially, but turns out to be brutish and crude



### 4. **Harold “Mitch” Mitchell**

* Lives with his dying mother and hopes to marry before her death
* Gentlemanly, compared to the other male characters in the play
* Drawn to Blanche by their mutual need for support, though he doesn’t fit her ideal type
* Clumsy and unrefined in a way
* Noticeably more sensitive
