## Plot

* Set in Athens, Greece, as well as the surrounding forest -→ shows the two ideas of rationality vs. chaos
* 4 Athenians run away to the forest, and a fairy obstructs their pursuits of each other
* **Subplots**:

  
  1. The lovers’ quarrels (4 Athenians)
  2. The fairy world (Oberon, Titania, Puck)
  3. The mechanicals’ play
