## Title Interpretation

* **Midsummer** = The longest summer day, often associated with magic and enchantment in folklore.
* **Night** = All that occurred may have been while they were asleep, resulting in everything being categorized as a dream or illusion
* **Dream** = All the events of the play were seen as fake, fantastical events that challenge their sense of reality and reason
* The play is structured around contrasting worlds: the human world and the fairy world, and the title symbolizes the clash of the two.
