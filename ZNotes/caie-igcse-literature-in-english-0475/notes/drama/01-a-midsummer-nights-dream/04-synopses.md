## Synopses

### Act 1

#### **Scene 1 (I.I)**

* Egeus complains to Theseus about Hermia’s disobedience.
* Hermia pleads for her love with Lysander.
* Theseus gives her an ultimatum: either marry Demetrius, face the punishment of marrying Lysander, or live as a nun.
* Lysander proposes to run away outside of Athens.
* Hermia and Lysander plan to meet in the woods the next night and escape.
* Helena laments her unrequited love for Demetrius to Hermia and Lysander and then runs to Demetrius to tell him of the couple’s plan, hoping to spoil it.

#### **Scene 2 (I.II)**

* 6 local craftsmen meet to rehearse and perform “The Most Lamentable Comedy and Most Cruel Death of Pyramus and Thisbe”.
* Quince assigns parts, and Bottom is designated as Pyramus, the lead character, a lover who kills himself.
* Bottom desires to play Thisbe and the lion, but Quince insists that only Bottom can play Pyramus.
* Quince suggests the cast meet in the woods for their next rehearsal to ensure privacy.

### Act 2

#### **Scene 1 (II.I)**

* Puck warns another fairy serving Titania that Oberon is angry with the Queen and to avoid Oberon’s sight.
* Titania and Oberon arrive, and she accuses him of loving other women as well as constantly arguing with her, which affects nature.
* Oberon refuses to stop arguing unless Titania gives him a servant boy.
* Titania refuses, citing a promise to the boy’s mother.
* Puck is instructed to find the “love-in-idleness” flower so that Oberon can make Titania fall in love with the first thing she sees.
* Oberon, invisible, observes Demetrius and Helena in the woods, and deicides to help Helena by telling Puck to put the juice in Demetrius’ eyes.

#### **Scene 2 (II.II)**

* Titania instructs the fairies to sing to her to sleep in her bower.
* Oberon puts the flower’s juice on her eyes once asleep.
* Hermia and Lysander get lost and decide to sleep, and Lysander sleeps away from Hermia for modesty.
* Puck mistakes Lysander for Demetrius and applies the juice to him.
* After chasing Demetrius, Helena discovers Lysander, who wakes up and instantly falls in love with Helena.
* Helena believes that she is mocking her and runs, yet Lysander pursues.
* Hermia wakes up shocked to find Lysander gone and searches for him.

### Act 3

#### **Scene 1 (III.I)**

* Quince finds a quiet place for the mechanical rehearsal, so they discuss play problems and plan creative solutions.
* Puck stumbles upon them and decides to use magic to give Bottom the head of an ass.
* The mechanicals run away from Bottom’s new appearance.
* Bottom, unaware, thinks it’s a joke and starts singing, awakening Titania.
* Due to the flower’s juice, Titania falls in love with Bottom.
* Titania commands Bottom to stay and calls for her fairies, who lead him to her fairy bower.

#### **Scene 2 (III.II)**

* Oberon wonders what Titania has seen, and Puck arrives with the news.
* Hermia arrives with Demetrius, fearing harm to Lysander; Demetrius denies any role in Lysander’s plight, so Hermia runs off frustrated.
* Oberon realizes the mistake with Lysander and orders Puck to bring Helena and put the juice on Demetrius’ eyes.
* Demetrius awakens and declares love for Helena, who thinks they are mocking her; the 2 men argue over Helena.
* Hermia arrives confused, and Helena accuses her of conspiring, leading to a physical fight.
* Lysander and Demetrius protect Helena and leave to fight elsewhere.
* Helena runs away, chased by Hermia.
* Oberon confronts Puck, but Puck insists it was a mistake because of their attire.
* Oberon instructs Puck to ‘overcast the night’ and administer the antidote.

### Act 4

#### **Scene 1 (IV.I)**

* Bottom commands the fairies around, and Titania sends them away for privacy.
* Oberon and Puck arrive, and Oberon gives Titania the antidote.
* Titania awakens, shocked by Bottom, and Puck removes his head.
* Theseus, Hippolyta and Egeus find the lovers in the woods, who wake, and Egeus raises concerns about the law.
* Theseus overrules Egeus when Demetrius announces his feelings for Helena, and Theseus decides on simultaneous weddings with a feast.
* Bottom reflects on a bizarre dream and plans to document it.

#### **Scene 2 (IV.II)**

* Mechanicals worry about Bottom’s absence and the play’s fate and discuss the money they would’ve earned, as well as Bottom’s share.
* Bottom appears, promising to disclose wonders, but instead instructs them to prepare for a performance at the palace.

### Act 5

#### **Scene 1 (V.I)**

* Hippolyta questions the truth of the lovers’ story, but Theseus dismisses it as a dream.
* Lovers arrive joyous for the celebrations.
* Theseus considers entertainment options suggested by Philostrate.
* Theseus insists on watching the mechanicals play.
* Guests mock and interrupt the performance, so Theseus replaces the play’s epilogue with a dance.
* Lovers retire to bed; Puck, Oberon, and Titania bless the marriages.
* Puck, left alone, addresses the audience and asks for forgiveness if the fantastical elements caused the offence.
