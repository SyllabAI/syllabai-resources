## Symbolism

### Love Juice

* A **symbol** of the play’s vision of love being irrational, unpredictable, and fickle
* It can overwhelm and transform people without their will, like love
  * This is seen by how Demetrius and Lysander act differently when Puck applies the love juice
* Acts as a **plot device**, setting off a series of comedic misunderstandings and romantic entanglements among the characters
* Showcases how love can blind individuals to external appearances and social status
  * Titania falls in love with Bottom, despite him having the head of an ass and being a human rather than a fairy like her
  * Lysander suddenly falls in love with Helena, despite his seemingly resilient love for Helena
* Helps contribute to themes like dreams, love as a force, and reality vs. appearance
  * Love as a force: Much of the characters’ actions are performed in the name of love as they attempt to gain the attention of their lovers; the love juice helps to propel such actions, almost like a drug or a truth serum
  * Reality vs. appearance: The characters of the play, when anointed with the love juice, are suddenly blinded, acting by the will of the flower’s juice; they cast off previous assumptions and are put into a trance that they cannot escape
* Contributes to **comedic elements** in the play by causing misunderstandings and conflict among the characters$\implies$signature feature of Shakespeare’s romantic plays
* It is used to illustrate how love can be manipulated, misunderstood, and misdirected

### Forest

* Though the play is set in Athens, Greece, most of its conflicts and climaxes occur in the surrounding woods.
* Inhabited by mainly fairies, it becomes a place in which they control all happenings
* An area where reality and fantasy are confused, as fairies begin to interfere in the human characters’ conflicts
  * Titania falls in love with Bottom in the woods and instructs him to lay with her.
  * Puck applies the love juice from the forest to three of the human characters’ eyes, confusing them.
* Becomes a symbol of transformation and change, as many of the characters go through changes that eventually define their personalities
  * Demetrius begins to love Helena in the forest.
  * Oberon begins to feel guilt for what he does to Titania and becomes less arrogant.
* Moreover, this setting becomes a form of escape for the play's human characters.
  * Hermia and Lysander plan to run away into the forest
  * Societal norms are absent, and the characters attempt to do all that they cannot in the city of Athens
  * The characters explore their needs and desires in the forest, mainly in the context of romantic relationships
