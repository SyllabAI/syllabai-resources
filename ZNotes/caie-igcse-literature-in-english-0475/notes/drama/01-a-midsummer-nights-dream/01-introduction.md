# A Midsummer Night’s Dream

## Introduction

### **Author**

* William Shakespeare (1564-1616)
* The play was mostly likely written around 1594-1596, around the same time Shakespeare wrote “Love's Labor's Lost” and “Romeo and Juliet”
* First performed around 1596, supposedly for an aristocratic wedding (the theory, however, does not have any conclusive evidence)
* The play was first published in 1600
