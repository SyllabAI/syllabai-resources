## Stanza 3

* The third stanza begins with the caller saying that the speaker’s feelings are ‘*unusual*’. However, they urge them to ‘*Go on*’, which, once again, shows their insistence and wish to find out as much as possible about how the speaker is feeling.
* The speaker, still oblivious and without suspicion, goes on to explain the impact with the shock and surprise has had on them physically, saying that their throat has ‘*gone dry*’, their ‘*nose is tingling*’.
* Their throat going dry is metaphorically symbolic of how people are often unable to speak up for themselves when they are filled with emotion.
* Nose tingling is usually a sensation that is typically associated with the cold, showing how the speaker feels like they have gone cold with shock.
* The speaker continues, saying that they are unsure whether thay are ‘*going to sneeze - or cry*’, which shows the confusion of their mental state. The word ‘*sneeze*’ furthers the ‘*cold*’ image given off my the reference to nose tingling in the previous line.
* The caller encourages this emotion of the speaker’s, telling them to not ‘*be ashamed of giving way to your emotions*’. This shows the core beliefs of the caller - that experiences and memories are more important than actual wealth.
* The caller continues by saying ‘*It isn’t every day you hear you’re going to get a million pounds*’. This continues the deception that the caller has been attempting to propagate from the beginning of the poem, as instead of saying that they are going to be giving the speaker a million pounds, they merely say that the speaker has heard that they are going to get a million pounds.
* This shows the human mind’s ability to manipulate reality into seeing and hearing only what people want to see or hear, no matter what was actually said.
