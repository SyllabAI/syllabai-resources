## Stanza 5

* They explain what this ‘*retrospective Chances Module is*’, saying that ‘*Nearly everyone’s bought a ticket in some lottery or another, once at least*’. The way in which this statement, which is clearly an assumption, is presented as a fact, shows the nature of the caller.
* The interesting thing here is that all lotteries operate using the fact that when people buy tickets, most people will not earn anything, and so the company will make enough money to pay the winners and still make a profit.
* With a business model like this, there are no revenue sources, and only costs. This is a definite marker that the caller is not who they claim to be, and, at the very least, they do not actually intend to give the speaker any money.
* Them calling the winner the ‘*lucky person*’ shows how some of the best experiences of life often come out of luck.
* The speaker starts believing the claims of the caller, as they say, ‘*Well, that’s incredible*’ and call the system ‘*marvellous*’.
* However, they are still in disbelief, and they do not completely believe the caller, which is why they say that they will believe the caller ‘*what \[they\] see the cheque*’.
