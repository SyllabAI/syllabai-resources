## Stanza 6

* This is the point where the caller pulls the curtain down on their scheme, and finally states that ‘*there’s no cheque*’.
* The speaker is astonished, and in their confusion, asks the caller what would happen of ‘*the money*’.
* The caller explains that they ‘*don’t deal in money*’ and that ‘*experiences are what \[they\] deal in*’. This show the caller’s opinion that memorable experiences are more important than money.
* They hope that the speaker has had a ‘*great*’ and ‘*__exciting__*’ experience, and say that their real ‘*prize*’ is this memory.
* They congratulate them, and then, for the first time in the poem, they show what seems to be proper respect, telling the speaker to ‘*have a nice day*’.
* The speaker describes in the last line of the stanza that the ‘*line went dead*’. The word ‘*dead*’ here is a metaphor for how fast their fascination fell apart.
