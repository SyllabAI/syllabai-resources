## Stanza 4

* The caller’s patronising words continue in the fourth stanza, with, ‘*Relax, now, have a little cry; we’ll give you a moment…*’. The way in which the caller says that they will ‘*give*’ the speaker a moment insinuates that the caller is doing the speaker a favour by allowing them to sit and enjoy the moment.
* This once again reinforces the idea that the caller’s intent is to give the speaker a new experience, and not actual money.
* The speaker starts snapping out of their shocked state, as they start questioning the motives of the caller. They say that they ‘*haven’t bought a lottery ticket for years and years*’.
* This is one of the first definite clues to the reader that the caller is not what they claim to be. All of the hints thus far have been subtle.
* The caller laughs again, showing their sarcastic and mocking tone. They attempt to cover their tracks, telling the speaker to not ‘*worry about a ticket*’.
* They say that they are ‘*Universal*’, which is a double entendre based on the fact that their company is called Universal, and the fact that they are trying to say that they are universally accepted.
* They use a long and fancy name, the ‘*retrospective Chances Module*’ in an attempt to sound more genuine and confuse the speaker.
