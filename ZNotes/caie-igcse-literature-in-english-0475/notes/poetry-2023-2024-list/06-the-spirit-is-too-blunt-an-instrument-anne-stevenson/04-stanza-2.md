## Stanza 2

* The second stanza is also completely based on just describing parts of the body and its anatomy.
* The poem focuses on giving the reader an idea of just how complex and beautiful human anatomy really is, and how its minute details are beyond the scope of our imagination.
* The speaker mentions the ‘*distinct eyelashes*’. The usage of the word ‘*distinct*’ emphasises how each eyelash is perfectly shaped in a similar manner, despite there being so many of them.
* They then mention the ‘*sharp crescent fingernails*’, with the emphasis here being on how the shape of the fingernails is the exact same for each one.
* They describe the ‘*shell-like complexity of the ear*’, and emphasise just how intricate and exact it is by saying that ‘*its firm involutions \[are\] concentric in miniature to minute ossicles*’.
* Here, the word involutions has two meanings - first, the shrinkage of an organ, so to show how the body has been designed even on a minute scale, and second, the state of being complete, which is what the speaker believes the human body to be.
* The words ‘*infinitesimal*’ and ‘*flawless*’ emphasise, once again, the level of perfection that the body possesses.
* The word ‘*imagine*’ makes it clear that none of these features are visible, they are not for anyone’s viewing pleasure, but they are there just for the sake of being perfectly in sync with one another.
* The word ‘*completed*’ is used to describe the body, which sums up the speaker’s opinion of the human body completely - it is like a perfect finished product.
