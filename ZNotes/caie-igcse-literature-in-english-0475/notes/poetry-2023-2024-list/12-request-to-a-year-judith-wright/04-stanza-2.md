## Stanza 2

* The second stanza begins with enjambment, as its first line continues the sentence that was still incomplete in the last line of the first stanza. This shows the fluidity of the speaker’s thoughts, and how she has clearly thought about this before, and knows what to say.
* She says that her great-great-grandmother ‘*had eight children*’. This introduces another theme of the poem, which is motherhood.
* By stating that the grandmother had so many children, the speaker sets up the next line of the poem, which informs the reader that the great-great-grandmother had, therefore, ‘*little opportunity for painting pictures*’. This shows how she had the will to paint, but did not have the time.
* It is a representation of how societal obligations often tend to oppress artistic tendencies.
* The speaker then describes the setting of the events which she is going to describe in the rest of the poem, by saying that her great-great-grandmother ‘*sat one day on a high rock / beside a river in Switzerland*’. This allows the reader to visualise the scene better.
