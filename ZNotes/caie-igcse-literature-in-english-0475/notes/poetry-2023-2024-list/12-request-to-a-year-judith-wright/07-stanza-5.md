## Stanza 5

* The fifth stanza is back to the great-great-grandmother, and we are informed that it was ‘*evident*’ that ‘*nothing*’ ‘*could be done*’. This shows the rationality that the great-great-grandmother, and this is furthered by the fact that instead of panicking, she remained calm.
* She instead, ‘*with the artist’s isolating eye*’ (which brings back the theme of art), ‘*sketched the scene*’. It shows how the great-great-grandmother took every opportunity possible to pursue her art, and shows the power that art holds.
* The speaker ends this stanza by saying that ‘*the sketch survives to prove the story by*’. This returns back to the fact that art is eternal and has a longer legacy than anything else.
