## Stanza 1

* The poem begins with the speaker directly addressing ‘*the year*’, asking if it ‘*is meditating a suitable gift*’. This is the speaker using the literary technique known as ‘*apostrophe*’, which is when a speaker directly addresses or attempts to converse with someone, or something, which does not exist or is not present there.
* The reason why the speaker believes that the year may be meditating on a gift for her is left ambiguous for the time being, and it draws the reader in, intriguing them.
* This also acts as personification and metaphor.
* The speaker then states that she ‘*should like it to be the attitude of \[her\] great-great-grandmother*’. The phrase ‘*should like it*’ indicates a firmness of will, and indicates how much the speaker strives to be like her great-great-grandmother.
* It is interesting that the speaker seems to admire and know much about a woman that she has never actually met.
* She goes on to describe her great-great-grandmother as a ‘*legendary devotee of the art*’. This, and the previous line, is indicative of one of the biggest themes of this poem - the importance and eternal nature of art.
* Despite having died a very long time ago, the great-great-grandmother’s legacy still lives on in her art.
