## Stanza 3

* The speaker continues with enjambement in the third stanza, stating that her great-great-grandmother ‘*from a difficult distance viewed / her second son*’. The word ‘*difficult*’ shows how the distance was very large, and also shows how the distance made her feel very helpless.
* The speaker continues by saying that the son was ‘*balanced on a small ice-floe*’ and that he was lifting ‘*down the current towards a waterfall / that struck rock-bottom eighty feet below*’. Here, the speaker creates a feeling of urgency and fear, by clearly outlining the peril which the son faced.
* Also, the detailed way in which the speaker is able to describe this scene once again re-iterates the fact that art is eternal, and leaves a very long legacy behind.
