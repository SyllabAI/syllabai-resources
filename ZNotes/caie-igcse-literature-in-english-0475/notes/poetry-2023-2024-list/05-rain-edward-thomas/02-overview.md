## Overview

* Structure - 18 lines, 1 stanza
* Themes:
  * Inevitability of death
  * The comfort of solitude
* Lack of rhyme structure is reminiscent of the chaos of nature and the war
* Metrical pattern of poem reminiscent of the sound or rain
* Poem is in present tense, which helps the reader better visualise the scene
* Present tense gives effect that speaker is still in the war
* Speaker’s fate is left ambiguous, making the poem feel unfinished
