## Overview

* Structure - 28 lines, 1 stanza
* Themes:
  * Anguish
  * Frustration
  * Power of love
* The entirety of the poem takes place over a single stanza. The lack of demarcation indicates the frenzied nature of the speaker’s thoughts, and their lack of organisation
* It can be broken down, however, into two sonnets condensed into a single stanza, the first being a Shakespearean sonnet, and the other being a Petrarchan sonnet
* This combination of two different styles once again shows how the speaker’s thoughts are not very organised and how he is unable to focus in only one direction at once.
