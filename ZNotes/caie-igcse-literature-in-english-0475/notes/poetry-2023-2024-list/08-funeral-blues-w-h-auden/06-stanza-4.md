## Stanza 4

* The final stanza returns back to the topic of the speaker’s demands. However, as per the trend set in the earlier stanzas, the outlandishness of the demands has increased even further.
* The speaker states that ‘*the stars are not wanted now*’ and asks that someone ‘*put out every one*’. The declaration of the speaker’s that ‘*the stars are not wanted*’ shows how to them, it feels like nothing matters anymore in life. It is a representation of the apathy that comes with grief, and is an accurate depiction of how many people tend to react to such scenarios.
* The speaker’s demands, which up till now, had been humanly possible to carry out, have now escalated to the point of being simply impossible.
* The next demand of the speaker is to ‘*Pack up the moon and dismantle the sun*’. The speaker wishes for time to stop, and does not want the days to keep passing. It shows how they have lost all interest in moving forward with their lives, and how they feel like they will be stuck in this moment of grieving forever.
* They ask that someone ‘*Pour away the ocean and sweep up the wood*’. These elements of nature, that are usually associated with beauty and positive experiences, have lost all meaning in the speaker’s life, and all they want is for all the visual distractions in their life to go away.
* They justify all of these demands by stating that ‘*nothing now can ever come to any good*’. This shows how hopeless the speaker, and by extension, most mourners, feel, and shows how they feel like the world has come to an end for them.
* It is an extremely impactful line, and does a good job at getting across to the reader how the speaker feels.
