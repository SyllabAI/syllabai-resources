## Overview

* Structure - 16 lines, 4 stanzas
* Themes
  * Grief
  * Death


* The poem is made up of four quatrains, each following an AABB rhyme scheme.
* The orderly manner in which the poem is structured is juxtaposed with the speaker’s grief and fractured state of mind.
* The stanzas are structured as stairs, which each stanza acting as a further step down towards irrationality and grief-struck madness.
