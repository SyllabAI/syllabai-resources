## Stanza 2

* The second stanza opens with the lines, ‘*Let aeroplanes circle moaning overhead / Scribbling on the sky the message He Is Dead*’. This is the beginning of a trend that becomes more and more clear as the poem goes on - the speaker’s demands escalating in scale with every stanza.
* The sound ‘*moaning*’ is typically associated with mourning or sadness, and its use illustrates the fact that the speaker wishes for even inanimate objects to show grief for the death of the speaker’s loved one.
* It is clear that the deceased meant a lot to the speaker, and that is the reason for their demands.
* The next request of the speaker’s reads ‘*Put crêpe bows round the white necks of the public doves*’. This is a highly difficult, but yet not outside the bounds of reality, demand, and it once again, acts as the speaker’s despair and grief put into words.
* Doves are also typically symbols of peace and equality, and the speaker’s wish to put bows round their necks is indicative of their wish for peace and calm.
* The speaker’s final demand is that ‘*the traffic policemen wear black cotton gloves*’. The colour black is often associated with mourning in many cultures, so wanting policemen to wear black gloves shows the speaker’s wish for everyone and everything around them to mourn for their lost loved one.
* Policemen, especially traffic policemen, are tasked with maintaining the normal and safe flow of traffic, and by extension, people’s lives. The wish for traffic policemen to wear black gloves is also representative of how the speaker wishes for their life to also be brought back into its normal course, and how they wish for their loved one to be back with them.
