## Stanza 4

* The fourth stanza is once again only two lines long, much like the second stanza. 
* This stanza reads ‘*A world of wonders in / Every challenge to the skin*’. The phrase ‘*a world of wonders’* shows just how much the speaker enjoyed experimenting and challenging themselves. It shows the reckless lifestyle which the speaker once led, and the risks they took
* Even though the reader cannot help but feel sorry for the speaker, lines like this one make the reader completely aware of the fact that the speaker did live a dangerous and reckless lifestyle.
* ‘*Every challenge to the skin*’ once again reminds the reader of just how strong the speaker’s skin used to be, and how they used to get pleasure from challenging themselves and experimenting.
* Every event and act of intimacy would change him, and would make him develop as a person.
