## Stanza 7

* The seventh stanza starts with ‘*Stopped upright where I am*’, which shows how he is completely stationary.
* He knows by now that he is about to experience pain, something which is reinforced by his statements in the last line of this stanza, which reads, ‘*The pains that will go through me*’.
* This gives an idea to the reader of how painful and chaotic his life is now and how his normal function is impaired by the constant cycle of pain that he goes through.
* The speaker states he stands there, ‘*Hugging \[his\] body to \[him\] / As if to shield it*’. This shows his subconscious instinct to protect himself.
* Once again, the word ‘*shield*’ is brought into play, but unlike the earlier descriptions of his body as a shield, the writer understands that his hands are useless as a shield. This can be seen from his utilisation of ‘*As if*’.
