## Stanza 6

* The sixth stanza is once again a couplet, and it reads, ‘*I have to change the bed, / But catch myself instead*’.
* Him stating that he has to ‘*change the bed*’ is a reminder of the fact that the bedsheets are soaked with sweat, and this can be interpreted as a metaphorical representation of the impact which his disease has on the people around him.
* However, he is unable to do so, and instead ends up stopping in his tracks and standing still, showing his inability to reverse the damage which the disease has had on him and the people around him.
* It also shows how he has gotten used to the disease by now, and knows that something bad or painful is about to happen soon.
