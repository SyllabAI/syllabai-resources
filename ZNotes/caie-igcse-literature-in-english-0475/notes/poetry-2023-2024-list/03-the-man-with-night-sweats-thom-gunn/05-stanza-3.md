## Stanza 3

* The third stanza is still in past tense.
* It begins with ‘*I grew as I explored*’. The words ‘*I grew*’ shows how this is the same body which the speaker grew up in, and the same body with which the speaker has experienced everything which he has done up to the present.
* The word ‘*explored*’ indicates that the speaker has experimented and taken many risks in his life, and it leads the reader to wonder whether it was one of these such mistakes that led to him having lost his ‘*shield*’ of protection.
* The speaker describes their body as ‘*The body I could trust*’, and the fact that this is in past tense really shows the extent of the effect of AIDS on the reader - he can no longer put his trust in his own body. This line evokes a sense of sympathy for the speaker in the reader’s mind.
* The speaker states that they ‘*adored / The risk that made robust*’. This shows how the speaker once used to live a life of taking risks, and their body allowed them to sustain their lifestyle.
* The usage of the word ‘robust’ once again re-iterates how strong the speaker’s body was at that point in time.
