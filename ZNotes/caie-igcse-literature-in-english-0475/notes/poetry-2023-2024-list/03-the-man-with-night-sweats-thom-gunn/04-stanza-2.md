## Stanza 2

* The second stanza introduces the aforementioned shield metaphor, with ‘*My flesh was its own shield: / Where it was gashed, it healed*’.
* This stanza being only two lines long can be interpreted as a subtle reference to the finality of this statement, and how the speaker’s body no longer supports them like it did.
* The word ‘*was*’ indicates usage of past tense, which is different from the tense used in the first stanza of the poem. This indicates that the speaker is no longer in the present, and how he is reminiscing back to the past. This indicates that what the speaker is talking about is what used to hold true in the past, and that his situation is different in the present.
* The word ‘*gashed*’ is a much harsher word than other similar words such as ‘*cut*’ or ‘*scratch*’. The intent of using ‘*gashed*’ seems to be to show the great extent to which the speaker could, in the past, tolerate physical damage.
* It also shows just how strong the ‘*shield*’ of the speaker’s ‘*flesh*’ was, and sets up the descriptions of the speaker’s present weakness which follow in the poem by contextualising the change and allowing the reader to understand the type of metamorphosis AIDS has put the speaker through.
