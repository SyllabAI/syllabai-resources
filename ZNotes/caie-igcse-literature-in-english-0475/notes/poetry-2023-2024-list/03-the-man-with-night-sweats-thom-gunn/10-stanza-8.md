## Stanza 8

* The final lines of the poem read ‘*As if hands were enough / To hold an avalanche off*’. This shows how the speaker is aware of the futility of his gesture, of wrapping his arms around himself.
* However, he still does it anyways, subconsciously, and it demonstrates a sort of regret of his, and a wish of his to have his old, strong, healthy body back.
* The word ‘*avalanche*’ here is a metaphorical reference to the speaker’s pain.
* The poem ends on a melancholy and sombre note, by giving the reader a visualisation of just how bad the pain and suffering of the speaker is, along with leaving them with an important message - to not take their ‘given shield’ for granted, and to take care of their body properly.
