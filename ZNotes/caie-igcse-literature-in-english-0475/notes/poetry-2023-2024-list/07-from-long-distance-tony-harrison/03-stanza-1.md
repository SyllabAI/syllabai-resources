## Stanza 1

* The poem begins with the speaker very frankly stating, ‘*Though my mother was already two years dead*’. This plain statement directly brings up the theme of the poem, which is death and the impact it has on people around it.
* The bluntness of the statement shows the speaker’s straightforward nature when it comes to death and his loved ones - he is clearly not one to sugarcoat things.
* The usage of ‘*mother*’ as opposed to something less formal like ‘*Mom*’ is impactful, as it shows how much the speaker has been affected by her death, even though he does not wish to show it. It seems to imply that he cannot bear to refer to her in an informal context.
* The word ‘*already*’ makes the time period that the poem is taking place in very clear.
* The second line of the first stanza introduces the reader to one of the most important recurring themes of this poem - the way in which the speaker’s father reacts to his wife’s death, and the way in which he copes with his grief.
* Despite him knowing that his wife is dead, he is in a sort of denial - he keeps ‘*her slippers warming by the gas*’.
* The word ‘*warming*’ creates a contrast with the feeling of coldness that is typically associated with death and grief.
* Slippers are also something often associated with day-to-day movement, and it shows how he is living life as if she is about to come home at any minute.
* The father also ‘*put hot water bottles her side of the bed*’. Once again, the feeling of warmth implied with ‘*hot water bottles*’ is at contrast with the coldness of death and grief.
* The phrase ‘*her side of the bed*’ implies that the father still keeps his dead wife’s side of the bed empty and ready for her to come and sleep in it again, and he has not moved on to sleeping in a smaller bed, or anything such.
* Lastly, the reader is informed that the speaker’s father also goes to ‘*renew \[his dead wife’s\] transport pass*’. It is clear that the father is putting in actual work just to maintain this illusion in his mind - he not only fills up warm water bottles and keeps her slippers by the gas, but he actually takes time out of his routine on a regular basis to go and get the transport pass of his wife renewed.
* This shows the commitment that he has to her, and the depth of his love for her - how despite clearly acknowledging that she is gone, he still tried to have some part of her stay with him.
