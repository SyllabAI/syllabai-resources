## Stanza 6

* The sixth stanza is another very short paragraph, just like the third and fourth stanzas before it. These short paragraphs, and the abrupt ways in which they begin with enjambement, are indicative of the rushed manner in which the speaker’s thoughts are flowing, and the gravity of what is being said.
* This paragraph describes what the Planners are doing in their territories. The speaker is informed that they are ‘*guessing directions*’, which shows how they do not truly know what they are doing despite the magnitude of the perfection that these Planners are capable of creating.
* The lines that they sketch are told to be *‘rigid as wooden borders*’. The rigid lines being made on snow show how they are bound to disappear as the snow melts, once again reiterating the theme that the Planners’ illusions of perfection are temporary in nature.
