## Stanza 3

* The third stanza begins with enjambement, as the sentence from the second stanza continues on without any punctuation demarcating the two stanzas.
* The speaker explains that these imperfections give ‘*momentary access to / the landscape behind of under / the future cracks in the plaster*’. The word ‘*landscape*’ shows the massive scale of the world that the perfections of the suburbs have hidden away.
* The phrase ‘*future cracks*’ shows how the temporary illusion of perfection that the suburbs exude is not bound to last for a very long time.
* The word ‘*plaster*’ refers to the fact that plaster is often used to cover up cracks and imperfections on walls and other structures.
