## Stanza 5

* The fifth stanza finally introduces the reader to the titular City Planners, described as having the ‘*insane faces of political conspirators*’. This shows how the speaker believes these Planners to all be together in a political and immoral plot - the plot to create these false perfections.
* These City Planners are ‘*scattered over unsurveyed territories*’, showing how the Planners wish to take over every part of the world they can.
* They are described as ‘*concealed from each other*’, showing how despite each of them acting independently, they all share the same final motive of creating mathematical precision and perfection.
* The word ‘*private blizzard*’ seems to show nature’s attempts to fight against the Planners and their perfectionist regime.
