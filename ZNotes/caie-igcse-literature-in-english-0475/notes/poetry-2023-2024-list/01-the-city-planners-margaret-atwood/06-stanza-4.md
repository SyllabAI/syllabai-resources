## Stanza 4

* The fourth stanza continues with the same enjambement as the third.
* It says that the ‘*houses*’, which are representative of the false perfection that the suburbs have attempted to create, will slide into the ‘*clay seas*’, which represent the natural world.
* The speaker describes this motion as being ‘*gradual and glaciers*’ and states how ‘*right now nobody notices*’. These two phrases can be interpreted as references to the slow impact of climate change and environmental destruction, and how it is often ignored and played down by people and the media.
