## Stanza 7

* The final stanza is a short couplet, which summarizes what the Planners are trying to do.
* The word ‘*panic*’ shows how the suburbs exude a concealed air of madness and hysteria despite seeming orderly and controlled.
* The words ‘*panic*’ and ‘*order*’ are juxtaposed from one line to the next, showing the contrasting views of the Planners and the speaker.
* The couplet ending with the word ‘*snows*’ is significant as well - ‘*snows*’ is not a grammatically correct word, and this grammatical imperfection seems to be a sort of last stand of the speaker’s and comes off as an attempt of the speaker’s to safeguard the imperfection and randomness of their surroundings.
