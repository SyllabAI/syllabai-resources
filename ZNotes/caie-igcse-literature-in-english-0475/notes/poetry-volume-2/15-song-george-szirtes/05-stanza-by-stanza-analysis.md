## Stanza-by-stanza analysis

#|
||

Stanza

|

Points

||
||

1

|

* It opens with confident and declarative statements

* Suggest social and political systems remain unchanged unless people actively challenges them

* This creates anticipations and suggests change is already beginning beneath the surface

* There is musical unity reinforces the chant like tone of collective resistance

* The rather creates an energetic and uplifting and opening mood

||
||

2

|

* Introduces the importance of collective effort

* Suggests ordinary individuals working together can challenge powerful structures

* Emphasises the scale of oppression and the difficulty of resistance

* Idea that unity creates strength is evident

* The example of a single “voice” breaking glass highlights the power of individual protest

* The sestet structure slows the pace and encourages reflection on the process of change

||
||

3

|

* The persona explains that the same tune has been sung by oppressed people throughout history

* Suggests resistance is universal and timeless

* Oppression is presented as a heavy burden

* Suffering shapes people’s lives

* Tyranny is shown to silence voices

* Parallelism reinforces the physical and emotional pressure created by injustice

* Systems of oppression attempt to silence hope and protest

||
||

4

|

* Introduces the role of courageous leaders; to inspire change

* Leadership multiplies the strength of ordinary people

* Change becomes possible when people unite behind visionary leadership

* No system of oppression is permanent

* The tone is energetic and conflicting

* Change is not accidental but created through effort and conviction

* Leaders help transform hope into action

||
||

5

|

* Unity between past suffering and future change

* Sense of continuity and collective suffering

* Change is finally happening

* There is emotional relief after oppression

* Freedom improves both political conditions and personal wellbeing

* Change is gradual but meaningful

||
||

6

|

* The certainty of change always arrives eventually

* There is an increase in confidence and optimism in the end

* Resistance movements always lead to transformation over time

* The chant like structure of the poem is a shared political song

* The tone is triumphant and hopeful

* Oppression cannot permanently silence people
