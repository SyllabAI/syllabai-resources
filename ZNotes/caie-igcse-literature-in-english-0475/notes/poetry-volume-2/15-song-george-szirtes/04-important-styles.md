Repetition

Parallelism 

|

14-16

|

* This mirrors the heaviness of oppression through repeated structure

* This creates a rhythmic weight that reflects the pressure described

Assonance 

|

1-4

|

* Creates harmony and musical rhythm suitable for a chant or song 

* Links key words together through repeated vowel sounds 

* This produces a grounded and steady tone supporting the poem’s message 

* Hence this reinforces unity between ideas of stillness and change 

* Supports the poem’s optimistic and encouraging atmosphere
