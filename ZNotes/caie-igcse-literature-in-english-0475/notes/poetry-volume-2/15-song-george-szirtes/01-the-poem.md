# **Song: George Szirtes**

## The poem

1. *For Helen Suzman*

### Stanza 1

2. *Nothing happens until something does.*

3. *Everything remains just as it was*

4. *And all you hear is the distant buzz*

5. *Of nothing happening until something does.*

### Stanza 2

 6. *A lot of small hands in a monstrous hall*

 7. *can make the air vibrate*

 8. *and even shake the wall;*

 9. *a voice can break a plate*

10. *or glass, and one pale feather tip*

11. *the balance on a sinking ship.*

### Stanza 3

12. *It’s the very same tune that has been sung*

13. *time and again by those*

14. *whose heavy fate has hung*

15. *on the weight that they oppose,*

16. *the weight by which are crushed*

17. *the broken voices of the hushed.*

### Stanza 4

18. *But give certain people a place to stand*

19. *a lever, a fulcrum, a weight,*

20. *however small the hand*

21. *the object however great,*

22. *it is possible to prove*

23. *that even Earth may be made to move.*

### Stanza 5

24. *Nothing happens until something does,*

25. *and hands, however small,*

26. *fill the air so the buzz*

27. *of the broken fills the hall*

28. *as levers and fulcrums shift*

29. *and the heart, like a weight, begins to lift.*

### Stanza 6

30. *Nothing happens until something does.*

31. *Everything remains just as it was*

32. *And all you can hear is the distant buzz*

33. *Of nothing happening. Then something does.*
