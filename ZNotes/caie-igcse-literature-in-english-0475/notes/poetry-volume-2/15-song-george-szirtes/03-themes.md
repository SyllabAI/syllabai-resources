Courage, change & collaboration

|

* Change only happens when people take action

* Oppression is presented as a heavy weight that crushes people

* Tyranny and injustice is evident

* Even powerful systems can be challenged and moved

* Ordinary people can work together to create change

* Collective strength multiplies the impact

* This allows communities to overcome injustice

* Hence, change is always achievable through action

* Unity is a powerful force against tyranny

* Resistance is both necessary and hopeful

||
||

The power of the individual

|

* one person can bring major change

* Even the smallest actions can shift outcomes

* Leadership multiplies collective strength

* Real individuals can challenge oppressive systems

* This in turn encourages the audience to believe their own actions matter

* Hence, change often begins with one determined person
