Small hands, the voices and the feather

|

* This conveys the power of ordinary individuals to influence large systems 

* This represents collective action and unity among common people 

* Hence, suggests that cooperation can challenge strong institutions or authority

* Even one person speaking out can create a social or political impact  

* The smallest action can lead to a major outcome 

* Small actions matter especially during moments of crisis or instability 

* This acts as a call to action, encouraging responsibility, courage, and participation 

* Reinforces the poem’s message that change does not depend on power or status, but on action
