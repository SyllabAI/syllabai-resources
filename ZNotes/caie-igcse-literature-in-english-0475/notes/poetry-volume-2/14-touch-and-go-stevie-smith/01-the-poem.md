# **Touch and go: Stevie Smith**

## The poem

### Stanza 1

1. *Man is coming out of the mountains*

2. *But his tail is caught in the pass.*

3. *Why does he not free himself*

4. *Is he not an ass?*

### Stanza 2

5. *Do not be impatient with him*

6. *He is bowed with passion and fret*

7. *He is not out of the mountains*

8. *He is not half out yet.*

### Stanza 3

 9. *Look at his sorrowful eyes*

10. *His torn cheeks, his brow*

11. *He lies with his head in the dust.*

12. *Is there no one to help him now?*

### Stanza 4

13. *No, there is no one to help him*

14. *Let him get on with it*

15. *Cry the ancient enemies of man*

16. *As they cough and spit.*

### Stanza 5

17. *The enemies of man are like trees*

18. *They stand with the sun in the branches*

19. *Is there no one to help my creature*

20. *Where he languishes?*

### Stanza 6

21. *Ah, the delicate creature*

22. *He lies with his head in the rubble*

23. *Pray that the moment pass*

24. *And the trouble.*

### Stanza 7

25. *Look, he moves, that is more than a prayer,*

26. *But he is so slow*

27. *Will he come out of the mountains?*

28. *It is touch and go.*
