Repetition

|

1, 6-8, 12-13, 19 & 27

|

* This supports the poem’s anxious tone
* Emphasising how far the man’s struggle feels
* This shows the poet’s uncertainty about his success
* Hence, highlighting that the man is not progressing quickly
* This creates suspense about whether he will succeed

||
||

Anaphora

|

6-8

|

* This suggests the man is going nowhere as fast as he should be

* This shows the poet is repeatedly reassessing his progress

||
||

Simile

|

17-18

|

* This shows that the enemies are comfortable in the mountains

* This suggests they are rooted in place and not interested in change

* Hence they will not help the crawling man

* They are passive rather than active enemies

||
||

Alliteration

|

1,12,13,18,23,25 & 26

|

* This gives emphasis to key moments in the poem

* This Emphasises the persona’s plea for help

* This highlights the man’s movement

* Reinforcing how slow his progress is
