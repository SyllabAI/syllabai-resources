Humanity’s struggle to progress

|

* This represents all of humanity trying to become more civilised

* The mountains represent primitive life and survival instincts

* The tail shows humans are still partly animalistic and unevolved

* Being “caught in the pass” suggests humanity is stuck between past and future

* Progress toward civilization is shown as slow and difficult

* The man’s movement is described as “touch and go” = success is uncertain

* Hence, this suggests humans are still struggle with violence, selfishness, and instinct

* This raises question whether humanity will ever fully escape its primitive nature

* The poem presents civilization as fragile and incomplete

||
||

Religious feelings and doubt

|

* The poet watches the man struggle but cannot help

* Hence the poet turns to prayer as a last resort

* This shows human desire to believe in divine support during hardships

* This raises uncertainty about whether spirituality is helping or not

* The man moves after the poet prays = there are possible suggestion of divine intervention

* Yet, the movement is still slow, so success remains uncertain

* This suggests faith does not always provide a clear answer

* This reflects human uncertainty about whether a higher power exists

* Hence this shows tension between hope and doubt in religion
