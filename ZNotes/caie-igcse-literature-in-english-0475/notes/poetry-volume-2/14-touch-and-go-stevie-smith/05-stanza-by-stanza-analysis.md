## Stanza-by-stanza analysis

#|
||

Stanza

|

Points

||
||

1

|

* The man represents all humanity

* humanity is attempting to leave its primitive existence behind

* Animal instincts are preventing progress

* This Shows humanity is partly civilised but still partly primitive

* This suggests human progress is difficult, slow, and incomplete

||
||

2

|

* Speaker shifts from criticism to sympathy and reflection

* humans are emotionally burdened by fear and anxiety

* There is suspense about whether progress is possible

* This suggests humanity remains far from escaping its primitive condition

* The tone becomes more compassionate and concerned

||
||

3

|

* The poet invites the audience to observe the man closely, increasing emotional engagement

* This suggests humanity’s struggle is painful but meaningful

* Man is presented as a suffering figure

* Suggests possible comparison between human struggle and spiritual sacrifice

||
||

4

|

* Introduction of the mysterious ancient enemies of man, this creates tension and conflict

* The enemies represent forces resisting progress, though their identity remains ambiguous

* Humanity cannot rely on external assistance

* Reinforcing the theme that progress must be self-driven

||
||

5

|

* Presents the enemies as rooted and resistant to change

* They are comfortable in primitive conditions

* Their passivity becomes a symbol of dangerous complacency

* Raises possibility that remaining in the mountains may feel seductively easy

||
||

6

|

* The poet’s tone becomes fully compassionate and protective

* Vulnerability of humanity during its struggle for progress is evident

* Reinforcement of continued difficulty

* Prayer introduced as a last resort, highlighting human helplessness

* Hope now depends on faith rather than action

||
||

7

|

* Emphasises on renewed attention to progress

* There is persistence despite difficulty

* Prioritising human effort over passive hope

* Emphasise on uncertainty about humanity’s future

* The poet concludes with ambiguity rather than resolution
