# A Long Journey: Musaemura Zimunya

## The poem

### Stanza 1

1. Through decades that ran like rivers

2. endless rivers of endless woes

3. through pick and shovel sjambok and jail

4. O such a long long journey

### Stanza 2

5. When the motor-car came

6. the sledge and the ox-cart began to die

7. but for a while the bicycle made in Britain

8. was the dream of every village boy

### Stanza 3

 9. With the arrival of the bus

10. the city was brought into the village

11. and we began to yearn for the place behind the horizons

### Stanza 4

12. Such a long travail it was

13. a long journey from bush to concrete

### Stanza 5

14. And now I am haunted by the cave dwelling

15. hidden behind eighteen ninety

16. threatening my newfound luxury

17. in this the capital city of my mother country

18. I fight in nightmarish vain

19. but my road runs and turns into dusty gravel

20. into over beaten foot tracks that lead

21. to a plastic hut and soon to a mud grass dwelling

22. threatened by wind and rain and cold

### Stanza 6

23. We have fled from witches and wizards

24. on a long long road to the city

25. but behind the halo of tower lights

26. I hear the cry from human blood

27. and wicked bones rattling around me

### Stanza 7

28. We moved into the lights

29. but from the dark periphery behind

30. an almighty hand reaches for our shirts.
