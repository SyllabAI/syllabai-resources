The complicated legacy of colonialism

|

* colonialism is conveyed through development and suffering simultaneously

* The landscape is reshaped during the colonial rule

* The modern infrastructure represent industrial progress

* The Zimbabweans’s built the infrastructure themselves

* This is accomplished through violence and forced control by the colonisers

* The “new found luxury” presents feelings of uneasiness for the persona when looking back at its origins

* Since memories of war and colonial suffering linger in the air

* The past continues to haunt the people in the present even after independence

* Colonialism left progress, but also instability and trauma for the people

||
||

The cost of development 

|

* industrialisation = permanent change form village life 

* Traditional transports have vanished 

* modern transports have completely replaced them

* Hence the villagers desire urban lifestyles 

* The “bicycle” conveys new dreams influenced by the Brits (British people)

* Hence there is loss of tradition and identity 

* The persona enjoys the modern comforts 

* Yet feels conflicted and uncertain 

* The persona feels a distance between him and his cultural heritage 

* Old beliefs are constantly rejected in the present 

* There is constant tension between modern life and their ancestral roots
