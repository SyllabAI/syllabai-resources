# The Capital: W.H Auden

## The poem

### Stanza 1

1. *Quarter of pleasures where the rich are always waiting,*

2. *Waiting expensively for miracles to happen,*

3. *O little restaurant where the lovers eat each other*

4. *Café where exiles have established a malicious village;*

### Stanza 2

5. *You with your charm and apparatus have abolished*

6. *The strictness of winter and the spring’s compulsion*

7. *Far from your lights the outraged punitive father,*

8. *The dullness of mere obedience here is apparent.*

### Stanza 3

 9. *Yet with orchestras and glances, O, you betray us*

10. *To belief in our infinite powers; and the innocent*

11. *Unobservant offender falls in a moment*

12. *Victim to the heart’s invisible furies.*

### Stanza 4

13. *In unlighted streets you hide away the appalling;*

14. *Factories where lives are made for a temporary use*

15. *Like collars or chairs, rooms where the lonely are battered*

16. *Slowly like pebbles into fortuitous shapes.*

### Stanza 5

17. *But the sky you illumine, your glow is visible far*

18. *Into the dark countryside, the enormous, the frozen,*

19. *Where, hinting at the forbidden like a wicked uncle,*

20. *Night after night to the farmer’s children you beckon.*
