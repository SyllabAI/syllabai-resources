Darkness & light

|

* When the city is illuminated, the unlit streets hide poverty

* This suggests that the cities glitter is a calculated lie

* The light isn’t a beacon of hope

* Rather a deceptive tool used to trap the vulnerable rural people

* It traps them into a life of isolation

* The city’s lights symbolize the temptation of a “black sheep” life style

* Luring people away from their strict rural traditions

* The darkness depicts a world of forced obedience

* This makes the city’s lights as a temporary escape

* Yet it is the real trap

* Light and darkness simply replace each other in form of oppression
