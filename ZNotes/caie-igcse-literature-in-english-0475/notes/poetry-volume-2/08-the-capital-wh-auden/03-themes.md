The big city false promise

|

* The city is depicted as a factory that abolishes seasons

* Hence making the city unnatural/ artificial space

* This place hides the grim reality of time

* The city promises power yet that is a lie

* This is a lure for people in the villages

* The city replaces their family rules with a more mechanical form of control

* The city resembles a predator

* The predator is harsh & a manipulative deceiver that exploits people’s excitement

||
||

Urban vs rural life

|

* The country life is characterized by mere obedience

* To a strict traditional authority

* The poem presents 2 choices either get compressed by conventions in the country or get compressed by loneliness in the city

* Human connection is replaced with exploitation

* Both the country and the city don’t provide an option for growth in life

||
||

Inequality, class & wealth

|

* The poet depicts the rich as waiting “expensively” for miracles to happen

* This paints the upper class as jaded, bored and utterly disconnected from the labour that supports them

* In the unlighted streets, laborers are treated as objects of temporary use

* The capitalist city strips humans of their identity

* Hence reducing them to a mere commodity

* The city grinds down the human spirit

* Poverty doesn’t kill quickly

* It is like a slow poison that eats away at a person until they are unrecognizable
