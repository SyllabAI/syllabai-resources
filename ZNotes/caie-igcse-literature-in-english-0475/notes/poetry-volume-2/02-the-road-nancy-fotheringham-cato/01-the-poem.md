# The Road: Nancy Fotheringham Cato

## The Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *I made the rising moon go back*

2. *behind the shouldering hill*

3. *I raced along the eastern track*

4. *till time itself stood still.*

### Stanza 2

5. *The stars swarmed on behind the trees,*

6. *but I sped fast as they*

7. *I could have made the sun arise*

8. *and night turn back to day.*

### Stanza 3

 9. *And like a long black carpet*

10. *behind the wheels, the night*

11. *unrolled across the countryside,*

12. *but all ahead was bright.*

### Stanza 4

13. *The fence-posts whizzed along wires*

14. *like days that fly too fast*

15. *and telephone-poles loomed up like years,*

16. *and slipped into the past*

### Stanza 5

17. *And light and movement, sky and road*

18. *And life and time were one,*

19. *While through the night I rushed and sped,*

20. *I drove towards the sun.*
