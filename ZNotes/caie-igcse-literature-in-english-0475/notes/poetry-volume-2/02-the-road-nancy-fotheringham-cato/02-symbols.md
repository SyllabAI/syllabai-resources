## Symbols 

#|
||

**Symbol**

|

**Points**

||
||

Night

|

* Death, despair

* The fear of the unknown

* Contrasts against the sun (juxtapostion)

* The reader wants to have the dark behind, in the past and not hinder with the present

* And to avoid being consumed by the dark, the speaker must not stop moving

* The speaker strives to outrun the darkness of the night

||
||

The road

|

* Represents the path of life

* Life is the forward movement on this road

* Transition form birth to death

* Life and time are interlinked

* The fence posts in lines 13, stanza 4 are compared to the days and years as if they are static

* While the speaker moves on

||
||

Sun

|

* Hope, life and joy

* A guiding guardian

* A God like figure

* The speaker strives to enter the light
