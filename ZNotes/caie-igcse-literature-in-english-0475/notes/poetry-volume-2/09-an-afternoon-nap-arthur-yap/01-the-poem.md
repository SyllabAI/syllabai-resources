# An afternoon nap: Arthur Yap

## The poem

### Stanza 1

1. *the ambitious mother across the road*

2. *is at it again. proclaiming her goodness*

3. *she beats the boy. shouting out his wrongs, with raps*

4. *she begins with his mediocre report grades.*

### Stanza 2

5. *she strikes chords for the afternoon piano lesson,*

6. *her voice stridently imitates 2nd. lang. tuition,*

7. *all the while circling the cowering boy*

8. *in a manner apt for the most strenuous p.e. ploy.*

### Stanza 3

 9. *swift are all her contorted movements,*

10. *ape for every need; no soft gradient*

11. *of a consonant-vowel figure, she lumbers*

12. *& shrieks, a hit for every two notes missed.*

### Stanza 4

13. *his tears are dear. each monday,*

14. *wednesday, friday, miss low & madam lim*

15. *appear and take away $90 from the kitty*

16. *leaving him an adagio, clause analysis, little*

17. *pocket money.*

### Stanza 5

18. *the embittered boy across the road*

19. *is at it again. proclaiming his bewilderment*

20. *he yells at her. shouting out her wrongs, with tears*

21. *he begins her expensive taste for education.*
