The high cost of parental pressure for success

|

* The nap usually depicts rest, peace and tranquillity

* However the author uses it as a situational irony

* Instead of sleep, the afternoon is filled with the violence of lessons and scolding

* It illustrates that in a high-pressure society, even the time meant for rest is taken by the need to succeed

* the poem reveals the appalling reality behind closed doors

* The home is seen as a domestic space where the mother’s monstrous ambition is hidden from the public eye

* The mother is a symbol of the societal pressures

* The piano is traditionally a symbol of high culture

* The verb choice transforms an instrument of beauty into an instrument of physical and psychological brutality

* The child’s spirit is the currency spent for the mother’s vision of success
