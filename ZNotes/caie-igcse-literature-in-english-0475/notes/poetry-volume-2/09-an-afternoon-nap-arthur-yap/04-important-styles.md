Consonance

|

1-9 & 11-21

|

* These sounds are percussive

* They powerfully mimics the raps and strikes the mother gives to the boy

* The harsh sounds suggest there is nothing soft or nurturing about this mother

* She is as aggressive as her actions

* The mother is characterized as a vicious predator stalking its prey

* Hence making her anger cold and calculated

* Not as sudden loss of temper

||
||

Metaphor

|

5-6 & 10-11

|

* The poem’s metaphors suggest that the son’s identity has been entirely swallowed by his lessons

* She has rejected the natural softness of motherhood

* She doesn’t sound like a human

* Rather a machine

* She treats the boy’s childhood as an investment rather than a life

||
||

Parallelism

|

1-4 & 18-21

|

* This is shown between stanza 1 & 5

* This parallelism illustrates that the son is literally learning his mother's behavior

* Just as how the mother complains about the boy’s quirks

* The boy does the same

* The mother is berating, ranting and harsh
