## Symbols

Symbol

Points

||

Sunday morning

|

* Religion & moral judgement

* Religion = tool to control the poor

* Highlights hypocrisy

* Since survival = sin in the eyes of the rich

||
||

Painted windows

|

* Concealment and privilege

* Rich can use money to hide thier bad behaviours form the public

* Hence avoiding judgment

* Wealth = protected reputation

* Contrasts with the poor’s life

* Their lives are always visible

||
||

Carriage 

|

* statues and protection 

* Private movement 

* One without any criticism

* This shows how wealth creates freedom

* Freedom from moral scrutiny    

||
||

Yachting vs fourpenny boat

|

* unequal freedom between classes

* The rich escape judgment

* While the poor are watched even at leisure

* Shows how the same action is judged differently depending on class 

||
||

Children of light

|

* religious or moral authorities 

* This ironically suggests that those who claim wisdom fail to see injustice 

* Represents society’s false moral superiority
