Points

Anaphora

|

6, 11, 16 & 21

|

* Creates repeated contrast between classes

* Shows inequality is systematic not accidental

* Makes the poem sound like  a structured evidence against society

||
||

Juxtaposition

|

11 - 15, 16 - 20

21 - 25

31 - 35

|

* Shows the same actions are judged differently depending on class

* Highlights the hypocrisy of  the society

* Reinforces injustice

||
||

Parallelism 

|

11 - 15, 16 - 20, 21 - 25, 31-35 & 36- 40

|

* Each stanza compares the rich with the poor in same pattern 

* This suggests inequality is predictable and normalised 

* Hence it strengthens the persuasive tone

||
||

Hyperbole

|

30

|

* Exaggerates society

* Disgust toward the poor people’s happiness 

* Shows harshness of judgement
