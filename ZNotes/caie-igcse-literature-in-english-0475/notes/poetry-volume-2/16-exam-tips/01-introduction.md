# Exam tips & Essay writing guide

## Introduction

* Make the first paragraph most powerful by using the best styles and techniques, this creates a positive bias in the examiner.

* For the first line in the paragraph of your essay, make sure to use the key words from the question and link it to a style as if the style is the answer to the question. Ex: Auden employs the use of the theme; inequality, wealth and class, to convey their distaste for the city.

* In the exam, quickly write down all the styles you can remember before starting the paper, this reduces stress when you have run out of points.
