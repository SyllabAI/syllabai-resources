Symbols

Points

The caste mark

|

* Metaphoric journey into an alien life

* Transitioning into religion

* The persona’s sincere pilgrimage

* Shows an Indian person’s religion and faith

* A symbol of religious faith that the persona doesn’t have yet

||
||

Reflection in the glasses

|

* The persona is aging and sees himself in the old man’s shoes

* Its a splitting reflection

* Hence, the persona has divided feelings:

* 1) Excited, optimistic and hopeful

* 2) Anxious, uncertain and nervous

||
||

The bus

|

* symbolizes time passing

* The human lifecycle

* Life just takes you, all you know is that there is an end

* Certain mortality at an uncertain moment

* The persona is feeling discomfort, this shows that the journey to embrace spirituality is going to be rigorous

* Forces the fact that the persona doesn’t have control over the journey

* The decision to do it is what the persona has control over

||
||

The covered windows & sun beams 

|

* The persona feels trapped, discomfort and anxiety 

* The persona is trapped with their thoughts which is taunting him

* Emphasizes that fact that humanity is trapped under a similar tarpaulin flap thats stoping them from indulging into the spiritual realm 

* The persona lacks the faith and purpose that the old man has 

* The light is seen as bullets or beams form a “sawed off shotgun”

* This type of shotgun is really powerful, it unleashes all its raw power and incredibly lethal 

* Such a weapon is symbolized as light

* This shows that the persona is highly skeptical about that whole journey and has a deep fear for regret, lack of hope and persistence
