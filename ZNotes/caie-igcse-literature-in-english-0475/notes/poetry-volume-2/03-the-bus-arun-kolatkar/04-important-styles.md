Enjambment

|

1-2 & 4-6,

|

* It mimics the jerkiness and jolting of the mountain bus ride

* Its the marking point in the shift of the narrative

* The sentences are split across stanzas to create a transition

* It creates suspense in the audience

||
||

Imagery

|

4-6 & 16-20

|

* This powerfully displays the persona’s inner conflicts

* The persona’s inner frustrations and hopes are brought out

* Shows the claustrophobic situation as the persona cant see the road and is trapped

* Establishes a threatening tone

||
||

Consonance 

|

Every where 

|

* The “d” and the “p” sounds in the “cold winds keeps whipping“ shows the thudding and popping of the tarpaulin

* The “r” sound mimics the whir of wheels and the engine’s struggle on the long drive 

* The “s” sound in the “sunlight” makes it more menacing and strange
