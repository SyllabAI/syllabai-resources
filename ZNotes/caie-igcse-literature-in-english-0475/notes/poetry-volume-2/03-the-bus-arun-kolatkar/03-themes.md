## Themes

#|
||

Themes

|

Points

||
||

Faith, uncertainty & doubt

|

* The persona is going to Jejuri India to visit a temple/ shrine

* The persona is trapped under his thoughts and can’t see beyond into the spiritual realm like the old man

* He is hopping to get some clarity into life after this trip

* But this trip is not going to be easy

* The persona is interested and excited to see the world form the eyes of a pilgrim

* There are mixed feelings, curiosity and hope vs discomfort and boredom

* The persona is uneasy with the idea of faith itself

* The violent sun rays are quite threatening to the persona, especially since the sun is seen as enlightenment.

* This proves that the persona is highly skeptical over the whole journey

||
||

Mortality & the aging of time

|

* The persona is constantly reminded about his age and the aspect of inevitable death

* So he is trying to seek guidance form spirituality to overcome this fear of death and to accept reality

* The journey also reminds the reader that as the bus gets closer to Jejuri, the persona is closer to becoming the old man himself self

* The light is seen as a weapon aimed at the old man and the driver

* Hence the fact that every break of dawn draws a human closer and closer to mortality/ the grave

||
||

Isolation, perspective & division

|

* The persona is revealed to the fact that age, religion and social norms are what has separate the persona form the rest on board the bus

* Hence, stands as the cause to why the persona is finding difficulties into understanding the old man, shifting out of his own thought bubble

* The more the persona looks deep into the old man, the more he feels separated himself

* There is a lot of work to be done to reach the old man’s state of mind

* The caste mark on the old man’s head identifies himself to belong to a specific class of people and a specific religious belief

* This is something that the persona lacks

* The age difference between the persona and the man is quite significant

* Hence making the persona feel more left-out

* This sharply depicts the differences in cultural beliefs between the young and the old generation
