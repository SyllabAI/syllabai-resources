# The Bus: Arun Kolatkar

## The Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *the tarpaulin flaps are buttoned down*

2. *on the windows of the state transport bus.*

3. *all the way up to jejuri.*

### Stanza 2

4. *a cold wind keeps whipping*

5. *and slapping a corner of tarpaulin at your elbow.*

### Stanza 3

6. *you look down to the roaring road.*

7. *you search for the signs of daybreak in what little light spills out of bus.*

### Stanza 4

 8. *your own divided face in the pair of glasses*

 9. *on an old man’s nose*

10. *is all the countryside you get to see.*

### Stanza 5

11. *you seem to move continually forward.*

12. *toward a destination*

13. *just beyond the castemark beyond his eyebrows.*

### Stanza 6

14. *outside, the sun has risen quietly*

15. *it aims through an eyelet in the tarpaulin.*

16. *and shoots at the old man’s glasses.*

### Stanza 7

17. *a sawed off sunbeam comes to rest gently against the*

18. *driver’s right temple.*

19. *the bus seems to change direction.*

### Stanza 8

20. *at the end of bumpy ride with your own face on the either side* 

21. *when you get off the bus.*

22. *you don’t step inside the old man’s head.*
