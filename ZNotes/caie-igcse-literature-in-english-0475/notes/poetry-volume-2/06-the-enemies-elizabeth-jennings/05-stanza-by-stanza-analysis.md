## Stanza-by-stanza analysis

Stanza

|

Points

||
||

1

|

* The poem starts in the middle of the action

* This creates mystery and prompts gossip in the reader

* The women provide their instinctive hospitality

* The tension builds up

* There are traces of fear amongst the people

||
||

2

|

* The mood gets more tense

* There is more suspense in the air

* The people feel they have been invaded

* The people persist not to ask the strangers for clarification

* Peace is still existent

* And the people’s fear is just an imagination

||
||

3

|

* The city becomes a haunted place

* There is dead silence

* The arrival of the strangers has turned friends into “enemies”

* The people are more cautions and reserved

* The strangers have entered the people’s head space

||
||

4

|

* The poem ends with the fact that the strangers have moved form the streets to the people’s thoughts

* This illustrates utter disturbance and loss of a sense peace

* Fear is apparent

* Once open hearts are now closed and almost forbidden as a result of resistance to change

* The people have seemed to not accept the change

* The mood is panicky and unstoppable
