Extended metaphor

|

14 - 15 & 17 - 20

|

* A person’s face, mind and heart are like houses

* They are private spaces that can either be open and welcoming

* Or locked and shut

* “Candid looks” depicts an open door

* Or a window without shutters

* It is an open invitation to a friend to enter

||
||

Parallelism

|

4 - 6 & 14 - 19

|

* Allows the poet to shift the mood of the poem

* Between anxiousness and nervousness

* This highlights the list of unasked questions

* While the women are being kind

* These are the exact questions that will cause fear and panic the next day

* The human connection is breaking down

||
||

Enjambment

|

1 - 3, 7-11, & 14-19

|

* The frequent breaks creates panic amongst the readers

* Creates the general suspense that echoes through out the poem

* This mimics the people’s internal paranoia

* This foreshadows the collapse of the social trust the exists within the people

* It represents the people’s frantic movement towards isolation

* It also symbolizes the social breakdown that’s happening
