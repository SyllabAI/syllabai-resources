# The Enemies: Elizabeth Jennings

## Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *Last night they came across the river and*

2. *Entered the city. Women were awake*

3. *With lights and food. They entertained the band,*

4. *Not asking what the men had come to take*

5. *Or what strange tongue they spoke*

6. *Or why they came suddenly through the land.*

### Stanza 2

 7. *Now in the morning all the town is filled*

 8. *With stories of the swift and dark invasion;*

 9. *The women say that not one stranger told*

10. *A reason for his coming. The intrusion*

11. *Was not for devastation:*

12. *Peace is apparent still on hearth and field.*

### Stanza 3

13. *Yet all the city is a haunted place.*

14. *Man meeting man speaks cautiously. Old friends*

15. *Close up the candid looks upon their face.*

16. *There is no warmth in hands accepting hands;*

17. *Each ponders, ‘Better hide myself in case*

18. *Those strangers have set up their homes in minds*

19. *I used to walk in. Better draw the blinds*

20. *Even if the strangers haunt in my own house.’*
