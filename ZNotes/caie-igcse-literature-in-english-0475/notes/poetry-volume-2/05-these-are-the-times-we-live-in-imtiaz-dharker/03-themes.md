The cruelty and irrationality of discrimination

|

* Through the use of the second person “you” the persona invites the reader to step in to the shoes at that moment

* The persona is muslim

* The official is viewing the persona through an Islamophobic set of eyes

* There is clear signs of racism

* This prejudice is dehumanizing, reductive and absurd

* The persona is not shocked by this incident suggesting that this discrimination has happened over and over

* Hence the persona is seen to have out grown this intimidation

* This is seen as utterly ridiculous

* The agent can only see one type of prejudice in the persona just because the persona has their name starting with a “z”

* This letter is not part of a typical english name

* The situation becomes more insulting to the persona when the official deeply examines the passport

* In the eyes of the official, the persona is measly just a passport

* Prejudice is illogical, painful and reductive

||
||

The burden of prejudice

|

* The persona is familiar with Islamophobia and racism

* Through this the person's sense of self, safety, identity, and community is destroyed and value less

* The persona feels terribly overwhelmed

* Hence he “shrinks to the size of the passport”

* The official is extremely xenophobic

* And is using his job as a tool against the persona

* Humanity and individuality is almost non existent here

* The persona feels that a huge part of them is not seen

* The official sees the persona as a iconic terrorist figure that would make their way onto the newspaper
