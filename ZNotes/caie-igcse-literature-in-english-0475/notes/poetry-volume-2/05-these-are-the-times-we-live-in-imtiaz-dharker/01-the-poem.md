# These are the times we live in: Imtiaz Dharker

## Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *You hand over your passport. He*

2. *looks at your face and starts*

3. *reading you backwards from the last page.*

### Stanza 2

4. *You could be offended,*

5. *It makes as much sense*

6. *As anything else,*

7. *given the times we live in.*

### Stanza 3

 8. *You shrink to the size*

 9. *of the book in his hand.*

10. *You can see his mind working:*

11. *Keep an eye on that name.*

12. *It contains a Z, and it just moved house.*

13. *The birthmark shifted recently*

14. *to another arm or leg.*

15. *Nothing is quite the same*

16. *as it should be.*

17. *But what do you expect?*

18. *It’s a sign of the times we live in.*

### Stanza 4

19. *In front of you,*

20. *he flicks to the photograph,*

21. *and looks at you suspiciously.*

22. *That’s when you really have to laugh.*

23. *While you were flying,*

24. *up in the air*

25. *they changed your chin*

26. *and redid your hair.*

27. *They scrubbed out your mouth*

28. *and rubbed out your eyes.*

29. *They made you over completely.*

### Stanza 5

30. *And all that’s left is his look of surprise,*

31. *because you don’t match your photograph.*

32. *Even that is coming apart.*

### Stanza 6

33. *The pieces are there*

34. *But they missed out your heart.*

35. *Half your face splits away,*

36. *drifts onto the page of a newspaper*

37. *that’s dated today.*

38. *It rustles as it lands.*
