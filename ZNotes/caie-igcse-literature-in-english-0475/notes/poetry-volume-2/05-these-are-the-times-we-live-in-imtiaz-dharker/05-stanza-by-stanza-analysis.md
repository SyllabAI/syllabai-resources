## Stanza-by-stanza analysis

Stanza

|

Points

||
||

1

|

* The use of the second person “you”

* This invites the reader to experience the humiliation from the official

* The persona is immediately suspected

* The persona’s identity is ignored

* The persona becomes just a travel history or a bureaucratic record

* There is evidence of Islamophobia

* Terrorism and racism have become normal in this world

* The persona doesn’t fight back, he rather resigns

* Such treatment is quite familiar and absurd

||
||

2

|

* There is loss of humanity

* The persona is replaced by a suspicious document

* The “Z” highlights further alienation and social isolation

* The official is extremely xenophobic

* Hence the official thinks that a whole birth mark has moved places

* The official is stripping the persona of their humanity

* The persona is hopeless and the official is very racist

||
||

3

|

* The official is unhurried

* Hence showing that he has all the power to waste the persona’s time

* The official makes no effort to hide his bias

* He is rude

* He asserts his dominance over the persona

* He is misusing his position

||
||

4

|

* The musicality in the ending suggests the persona’s bitter and sarcastic humor

* The persona imagines begin made over during the flight

* The persona is being silenced

* His perspective is being ignored

* The world has changed how they look

* Turning human faces into a suspicious object

||
||

5

|

* The mirror of prejudice

* The persona’s identity is being ripped apart to shreds by discrimination

* The racist gaze sees features, names and birthmarks only

* But completely fails to acknowledge the humanity and self-hood of the persona

||
||

6-7

|

* There is a dream like horror

* The persona is literally falling apart under the pressure of being profiled

* The persona is being associated with a terrorist

* A threat to society seen in news paper headings

* There is a tense and hostile silence
