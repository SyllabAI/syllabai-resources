## **Practice question**

How does Julius convey such a striking impressions of the “bus” in this poem?

* Marks: 25

* Time: 45min

* Words: 600-800

Exam tip: Every paragraph should answer these 3 questions

1) What is the specific theme, emotion, or message being conveyed in this part of the poem?

2) What literary devices or word choices is the author using to get that point across? You must provide evidence.

3) Why did the author choose this specific word or technique instead of another? What is the what is the effect on the reader? How does it relate to the bigger picture of the poem?
