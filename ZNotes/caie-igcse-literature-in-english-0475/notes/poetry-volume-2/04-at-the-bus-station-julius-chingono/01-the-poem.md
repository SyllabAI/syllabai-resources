# At the Bus Station: Julius Chingono

## The Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *When you arrive*

2. *at the bus station*

3. *pull down your tie*

4. *or remove the tie*

5. *to prevent strangulation.*

### Stanza 2

 6. *During the fight*

 7. *to board the bus,*

 8. *unfasten all buttons*

 9. *of the shirt and jacket*

10. *to avoid losing the buttons.*

### Stanza 3

11. *During the battle*

12. *to gain entry*

13. *to the bus,*

14. *tighten both shoelaces*

15. *for, when you are hauled*

16. *into the bus,*

### Stanza 4

17. *you hang in the air*

18. *and the shoes may come off,*

19. *tighten your belt*

20. *to avoid being undressed*

### Stanza 5

21. *during the scrambling*

22. *at the door,*

23. *remove your spectacles*

24. *and hold tight to someone*

25. *until you are in the bus.*

### Stanza 6

26. *During the climb*

27. *pay no attention to human sounds,*

28. *also bear in mind*

29. *words lose meaning*

30. *until you are inside the bus.*
