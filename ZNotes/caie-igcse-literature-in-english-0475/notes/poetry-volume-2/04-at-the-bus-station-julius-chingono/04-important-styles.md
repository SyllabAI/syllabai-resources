Anaphora

|

6,11& 26

(“During”)

|

* Instructions to board the bus amid jostling the rambunctious crowd
* Depicts the timeframe / the movement of time during the battle
* It depicts the struggle as prolonged and almost never ending
* Its shows that the persona’s and the passenger’s endurance are put to test

||
||

Alliteration

|

7-8, 10-11, 13-14, 16 & 19-20

|

* The “b” sound is repeated a lot

* This mimics the violence of the battle

* The reader can experience the situation with the persona

* Wrestling through the crowd

* This further depicts the intensity of the action

* Specifically the word “bus” shows the reader that this is the only goal all the passengers have, nothing else!

||
||

Imagery

|

3-27

|

* Startling, violent and chaotic imagery

* This allows the reader to imagine the horrendous chaos that happens at the bus stop

* Frenzied crowd going wild

* The reader can imagine people in pain, crying and suffering in agony

* Most of the poem is left to the reader to fill in the gaps through the use of imagination

||
||

Repetition

|

3-4 & 8-10 (“bus”, “button”,etc)

|

* All involve tightening

* Tying or fastening

* Action packed

* The list of instructions is given here

* This creates emphasis that belongings may be lost

* Belongings in the sense that humanity may be lost

* The crowd holds the power to crush, rob and tear the persona’s attire

* The mob like crowd is fixated to the bus, only the bus, nothing else
