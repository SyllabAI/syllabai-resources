## Practice question

How does Sarah Jackson convey ideas about death and mortality in the poem ‘The Instant of my death’?

* Marks: 25

* Time: 45min

* Words: 600-800

Exam tip: Use PEEL to construct each paragraph

1) Point: any style of theme

2) Evidence: exact lines form the poem to back up the point

3) Evaluate: talk about what the author is trying to convey

4) link: connect it back to the question, like kind of answer it!
