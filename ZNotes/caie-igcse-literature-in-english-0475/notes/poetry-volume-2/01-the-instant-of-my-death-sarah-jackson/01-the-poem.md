# The Instant of My Death: Sarah Jackson

## The Poem

### Stanza 1

1. *The bus was crammed and the fat man rubbed against*

2. *my leg like a damp cat*

3. *while you read The Jataka Tales three rows from the back*

### Stanza 2

4. *and we all stumbled on; wheels and hours grinding, tripping*

5. *as Spiti rose up around us, sky propped open by its peaks.*

### Stanza 3

6. *I traced the rockline on the window with my finger,*

7. *counted cows and gompas, felt my eyes glaze over*

### Stanza 4

8. *until we reached Gramphoo. There, where the road divided,*

9. *I saw a thin boy in red flannel squat between two dhabas;*

### Stanza 5

10. *a black-eyed bean, slipped in between two crags, he was so small*

11. *that I almost missed him, until he turned, gap-toothed, and shot me*

### Stanza 6

12. *with a toy gun. And a piece of me stopped then, though the bus moved on,*

13. *and the fat man cracked open an apple with his thumb.*
