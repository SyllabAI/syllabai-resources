The orchid flower

|

* The orchid lacks a stable foundation

* This mimics the women’s scenario with economic and physical instability

* This is shown through her living conditions, “too-cramped”

* The orchid doesn’t originate form the persona’s city rather some where else

* This apply’s to the persona since she is also an emigrant

* She has been stripped of her ancestral identity and forced to embrace an new one

* The city life is seemed to suffocate the persona’s potential just like the orchid in a “duffle bag”

* Life is a constant battle against the punishing environment thats alien and hostile

* Yet the orchid still survives and refuses to die, this powerfully depicts the persona’s mindset
