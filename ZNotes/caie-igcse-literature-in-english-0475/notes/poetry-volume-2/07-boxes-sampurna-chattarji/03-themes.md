* The flat being only “nine steps” depicts a sense of entrapment

* This further conveys the fact that human needs or space are much less important than urban density in the modern society

* The issue escalates so much that the people you love become an obstacle to hurdle in the house

* This conveys a meaning that loved ones become an annoyance in that flat

* The cramped architecture restricts easy human connection

* The persona wishes for a better life

* But to get one, she has to step into the outside, inhuman environment

* The rapid growth of urbanization limits the natural world at a physical and mental state
