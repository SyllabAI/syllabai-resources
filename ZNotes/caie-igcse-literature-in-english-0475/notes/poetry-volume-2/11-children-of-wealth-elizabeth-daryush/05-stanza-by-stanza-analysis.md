## Stanza-by-stanza analysis

#|
||

Stanza

|

Points

||
||

1

|

* there is warmth and comfort

* Safety in the “nursery”

* There is an establishment of social privilege

* Safety is bought not earned

* There are ideas of softness, over comfort and excessive shielding form reality

* Protection does not only block suffering

* Rather it blocks understanding of life as a whole

* The children are protected form the winter

* This is symbolic of hardships, poverty & life’s general difficulties

* The poet emphasizes an ironic and critical tone

* There is evident ignorance of reality

||
||

2

|

* Calamity is building right into this apparently secure structure

* The children lack experience and in depth knowledge about survival

* Wealth is used as a shield against danger

* Emotional numbness leads to ignorance

* Which leads to suffering, immaturity & lack of wisdom

* The poet addresses the false comfort of wealth

* The children are significantly damaged form the over protection

||
||

3

|

* the tone now becomes more urgent and commanding 

* Hence causing humility, loss of the privilege & experiencing the real world 

* Wealth, comfort & privilege stunt children’s growth by cutting them off form life’s harsher realities

* While money might create the illusion of perpetual safety

* It can’t actually keep trouble away 

* Weather rich or poor, you will have to face pain & trouble

* discomfort is just part of being alive
