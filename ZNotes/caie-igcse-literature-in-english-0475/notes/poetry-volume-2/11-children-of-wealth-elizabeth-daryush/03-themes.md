The value of experience

|

* The poet urges the children to step out of their “nursery”

* And they should face the world raw (without help)

* Staying in comfort guards them from not only pain

* But also knowledge and feeling

* knowledge and feeling are inseparable

* To understand life, one must experience both fully

* Suffering is a fundamental aspect of life and can’t be skipped

* Avoiding this doesn’t delete it from existence

* It rather just delays the time of confrontation.

||
||

The false security of wealth

|

* The poet criticizes the belief that money can protect people from sufferin

* This wealth allows the children to watch suffering from a distance

* without ever feeling it

* This distance creates ignorance

* Not security

* Comfort without experience is dangerous

* Wealth temporarily hides pain

* It’s not a remedy!

* This makes the reader feel uncomfortable

* Privilege is fragile rather than powerful
