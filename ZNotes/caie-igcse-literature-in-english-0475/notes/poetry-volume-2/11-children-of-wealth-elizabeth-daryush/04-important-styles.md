Imagery

|

1-5 &6-7

|

* Sensory imagery

* There are feelings of softness

* Padding and insulation

* The children are protected form physical pain

* And emotional and intellectual growth form experience

* This makes the children’s life over protected and pampered

* And this over comfort is depicted as limiting the childrens potential

* Rather than nurturing them

* The children observe suffering without experiencing it

* Reality is merely a tv show for the children

* Hence leading to an imbalance between danger and comfort

||
||

Metaphor

|

7-8, 11 & 13

|

* the house is described as a “citadel”

* This is a fortress built for defense

* Wealth is used as protection against reality

* Life is treated as something hostile

* This fortress mentally isolates the children

* There is knowing about suffering without experiencing it or understanding it

* Ignorance is shown as fragile and deceptive
