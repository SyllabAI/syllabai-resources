The wiring & the house

|

* The comfortable house shows human life that appears safe & protected

* It has material comfort

* privilege

* The house is a barrier against reality

* Hence creating an emotional and physical distance form life’s hardships

* Tho this is temporary & unreliable

* Danger could exist inside the house

* Suffering & disaster is part of life

* Wealth can’t provide permanent protection

||
||

Snow & winter

|

* This represents death

* Suffering & the harsh reality of life

* There are hardships and emotional coldness

* This conveys the inevitable suffering every human must face

* The winter is sheltered away form the children

* So they grow up without learning the hardships of life

* This portrays the their ignorance towards life

||
||

The nursery

|

* there is protected childhood

* & protected ignorance

* A safe space

* Without the interference of discomfort and danger

* This presents an illusion of eternal safety

* The children are shielded form knowledge & experience

* This raises immature adults

* This could result in over helplessness in life

* This makes the reader claustrophobic & deceptive

* There is realization of the danger of living without exposure to reality
