Cooperation, collaboration & equality 

|

* The poem begins with a structural imbalance

* Because the hair is parted to one side, the hair is distributed unevenly

* The heavy and scanty plaits represent the natural or social differences in human capacity, wealth, or power

* Smither acknowledges that a perfectly equal starting point is often impossible

* cooperation is not a forced burden, but a willing partnership

* The poem argues that when the strong and weak unite

* The result is not that the strong are weakened

* But rather that the entire structure becomes stronger

* The weak part contributes to the structural integrity of the strong part
