Metaphor

|

2-6 & 11-12

|

* The metaphors evolve from nature snakes and rivers to the human body, signaling a shift from raw state to functional unit

* When the two plaits join, they become a muscle

* A muscle represents work, movement, and intentional strength

* This metaphor turns the hairstyle into an ethical foundation

* Because it hangs down her back

* It implies that justice is something that supports the individual's backbone or moral uprightness.

||
||

Repetition 

|

3, 5-6, 8 & 10-13

|

* This creates a sense of balance 

* The word thin is repeated to emphasize the vulnerability of the smaller plait

* While wide is used for both the ribbon and the resulting combined plait

* This illustrates that the width = the justice is what overcomes the thinness = the inequality

* “Strong, Strong, Strong Together” 

* This triple repetition reinforces the idea that strength is not lost when shared

* It is multiplied
