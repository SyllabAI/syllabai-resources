The plaits

|

* The persona describes a side parting that results in two plaits of different thicknesses

* One heavy and sturdy, the other little and thin

* This symbolizes the inherent inequality of life

* In any society there are those who are strong, privileged & wealthy

* And those who are weak

* By using a child’s hair, the poet suggests that inequality is not a grand distant theory

* But rather something felt on the body from a young age

* This is the poem’s definition of justice

* Justice is not about making everyone identical

* It is about collaboration

* The weak and the strong plait work together providing the structure for shared strength
