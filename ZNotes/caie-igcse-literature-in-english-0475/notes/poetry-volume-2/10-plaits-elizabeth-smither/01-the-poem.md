# Plaits: Elizabeth Smither

## The poem

### Stanza 1

1. I had two plaits: one thick

2. an anaconda plait and the other

3. more like a thin grass snake.

### Stanza 2

4. My parting was on one side

5. a harvest, a rich waterfall

6. and a thin trickling river

### Stanza 3

7. but they were companionably joined

8. and tied with a wide ribbon

9. whose loops and bows were equal.

### Stanza 4

10. The weak and the strong

11. were strong together, the raised segments

12. of hair, a wide and thin muscle

### Stanza 5

13. a lesson that hung down my back

14. so though I could not see justice

15. I could feel how it was distributed.
