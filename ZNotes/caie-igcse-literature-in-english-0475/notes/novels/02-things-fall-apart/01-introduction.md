# Things Fall Apart

## Introduction

- **Author**: Chinua Achebe.

- **Title interpretation**: The Igbo community began to disintegrate due to the arrival of the colonialists.

  - ‘Things’: Lack of identity, insignificant.

  - ‘Fall’: Slow, gradual process.

  - ‘Apart’: No redemption, impossible to rebuild.

- **Setting**: Lower Nigerian villages: Umuofia, Mbanta, Mbaino, Iguedo…

- **Place & Time written**: Nigeria, 1959.

- **Plot**: Okonkwo was a respected man who believed in rigid masculinity and wanted to pass the trait to his sons. The rigid masculinity was a response to his cowardly father; it led to Okonkwo’s distorted view and destructive actions towards Igbo traditions. The arrival of colonialists catalyzed the gradual collapse of the community.

### **Themes:**

* Clash of cultures/Hybridity.
- Interpretations of masculinity.
- Consequences of colonialism (change vs tradition).
- Fate vs Free Will.
- The importance of cultural identity is evident through language and traditions.
- Gender roles.
- Family and Kinship.
