## Proverbs

* Offer a glimpse into the social norms and lifestyle of the Igbo.

* Unfold characterization and depth for the culture.

* Emphasize the importance of oral tradition and communication within the community.

* Condemn the barbaric stereotype in the past, Western literature.

* It is the bridge of understanding between generations and preserves history.

***Chi***: Emphasizes the importance of spiritual guidance for Ibo people.

***Kola nuts*** and ***palm oil:*** Reinforce the uses of language and oral tradition. People do not use violence to deal with everything like the stereotype.
