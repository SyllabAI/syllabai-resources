## Characters


 1. **Gogol (Nikhil) Ganguli**:
    * Son of Ashoke and Ashima Ganguli, brother to Sonia Ganguli
    * Obedient, inquisitive, and sensitive
    * Grows up to pursue a career in architecture
    * Struggles with his identity, in relation to both culture and name
    * Has 3 important relationships within the novel
 2. **Ashoke (Mithu) Ganguli**:
    * Ashima’s husband, father of Gogol and Sonia Ganguli
    * Grew up in Calcutta, India
    * Suffers a traumatic train accident, after which he travels abroad
    * PhD student at MIT, later a professor of engineering
 3. **Ashima (Monu) Ganguli**:
    * Ashoke’s husband, mother of Gogol and Sonia Ganguli
    * Moves to Cambridge after her arranged marriage
    * Becomes a locus of Bengali immigrant activities
    * Most attached and homesick for India in the family
    * Struggles to adapt to American life, yet finds comfort and purpose in her children
 4. **Sonali (Sonia) Ganguli**:
    * Daughter of Ashoke and Ashima Ganguli, sister to Gogol Ganguli
    * Also struggles with her cultural identity
    * Moves to California for college but moves back home once her father passes
    * Engaged to a Jewish-Chinese journalist (Ben) at the end of the book
 5. **Moushumi Mazoomdar**:
    * Bengali woman, marries Gogol
    * Grew up in London
    * Was a family friend of the Gangulis
    * Had a failed engagement with an American banker, then married Gogol
    * Has an affair with Dimitri Desjardins
    * PhD student at NYU
 6. **Maxine Ratliff**:
    * Gogol’s second significant girlfriend
    * Art history graduate from Barnard
    * Lives with her parents in New York
    * Elegant
    * Breaks up with Gogol after his father’s death when he pushes her away
 7. **Ruth**:
    * Gogol’s first girlfriend
    * An English major at Yale
    * Meets Gogol on a train to Boston
    * Dates Gogol for a little more than a year and breaks up with him after her semester at Oxford
 8. **Graham**:
    * An investment banker
    * Moushumi’s first fiancé
    * Met Moushumi’s in Paris
    * Lived in NYC
 9. **Dimitri Desjardins**:
    * Moushumi’s old crush and affair partner
    * Professor of literature
10. **Ben**:
    * Sonia’s fiancé
    * Jewish - Chinese
    * An editor at the 'Boston Globe'
