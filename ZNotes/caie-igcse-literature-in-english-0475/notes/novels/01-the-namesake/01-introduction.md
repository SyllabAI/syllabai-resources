# The Namesake

## Introduction

* **Author**: Jhumpa Lahiri
* **Title interpretation**: Protagonist was named after his father’s favorite Russian writer
* **Setting**: Calcutta, India and Massachusetts, New York, USA
* **Plot**: Ashima and Ashoke, immigrant Bengali parents, try to adjust to life in America while Gogol, their son, tries to find his identity and choose between two worlds
* **Themes**: Immigration, Identity, Family and tradition, Love and marriage
