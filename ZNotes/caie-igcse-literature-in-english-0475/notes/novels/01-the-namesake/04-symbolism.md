## Symbolism

### **Trains**:

* Train accident which Ashoke barely survived
* Unknown person commits suicide on the train tracks Gogol is travelling by
* Reminder of the constant and inevitable forward motion of life
* Place where Gogol meets Ruth and discovers Moushumi’s affair
* Represent motion, travel, and distance
* Shows how the characters are divided between homes, constantly unsettled

### **Books**:

* Ashoke’s love for them + how one saved him
* Novel’s ending shows Gogol’s discovery of the book his father gave him
* Act as records of the past$\implies$Moushumi’s books from Dimitri and Graham
* Are also carriers of names$\implies$Ashima’s book of addresses + Gogol is named after an author
* Gogol’s first gift was a book from Ashoke’s friend at MIT
