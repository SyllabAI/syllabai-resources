## Themes

### Environmental Destruction

Environmental destruction is the central theme of the poem. Boey present a world where nature has been steadily damaged by industrialisation and urban expansion. Unlike the peaceful landscapes celebrated by William Wordsworth, the modern environment is much **more polluted, mechanised, and controlled.**

* Cities replace forests and open land.

* Development appears aggressive and unstoppable

* Nature is reduced to something controlled and exploited.

Through this, Boey questions whether modern “progress“ has come at the cost of **irreversible environmental damage.**

### Romanticism vs Modern Reality

The poem creates a powerful contrast between Romantic ideals and contemporary reality. Woodsworth believed **nature nurtured the soul and shaped moral character.** In Boey’s modern world, that **harmony no longer exists.**

* Direct address to Wordsworth highlights the time gap.

* The poem feels sort of like an apology for failing to preserve nature.

* Romantic hope is replaced by modern disillusionment.

This contrast deepens the poem’s sense of regret and irony.

### Loss of Spiritual Connection

Boey suggests that humanity has lost its emotional and spiritual bond with nature. Instead of awe and reflection, nature is treated as a resource for economic gain.

* Nature is commodified rather than respected.

* Technology and urban life distance humans from the natural world.

* Spiritual reflection is replaced by material ambition.

The loss is not only environmental but moral and emotional.

### Criticism of Industrialisation

Industrialisation is presented as relentless and indifferent. The poem implies that **economic growth has now become society’s priority,** often at the expense of environmental preservation.

* Development appears mechanical and lifeless.

* Profit is valued over sustainability.

* Progress feels rather destructive than beneficial.

Boey ultimately challenges the reader to question **whether modern advancement truly deserves to be called progress.**
