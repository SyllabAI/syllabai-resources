## **Context**

The poem directly addresses William Wordsworth, one of the major Romantic poets of the 19th century.

Wordsworth believed:

* Nature is pure and healing

* Nature shapes moral and spiritual growth

* Humans should live in harmony with the natural world

Boey writes from the modern era to “report“ what has happened to nature since Wordsworth’s time, and the report is not good.

## **Overview**

The speaker addresses Wordsworth and describes how:

* Nature has been destroyed by industrialisation

* Cities have expanded aggressively

* The environment has been polluted and exploited

* Humans have prioritised progress and profit over preservation

The poem suggests that Wordsworth’s Romantic vision of nature no longer exists in the modern world.
