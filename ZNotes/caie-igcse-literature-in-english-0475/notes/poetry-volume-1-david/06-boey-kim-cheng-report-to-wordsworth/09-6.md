## Conclusion

Report to Wordsworth is a reflective and morally charged poem that examines the consequences of **modern industrialisation on nature.** By directly addressing **Wordsworth,** Boey Kim Cheng highlights the contrast between Romantic ideals, where nature is **spiritual, restorative, and morally instructive,** and contemporary reality, where **urbanisation, pollution, and mechanisation** have destroyed much of the natural world.

Ultimately, the poem serves as both a warning and a **moral reflection:** it reminds readers that true progress is not measured by urban growth or material development, but by preservation of nature and the values that sustain both the environment and the human spirit. Boey urges modern society to acknowledge its responsibility and reconsider its relationship with the natural world.
