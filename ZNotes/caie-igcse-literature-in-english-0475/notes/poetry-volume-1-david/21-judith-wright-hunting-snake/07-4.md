## Key Literary Devices

#|
||

**Device**

|

**Example/Use**

|

**Effect**

||
||

Personification

|

"he quested"

|

Gives the snake purposeful, almost human determination

||
||

Metaphor

|

"diamond scale"

|

Makes the snake’s scales appear precious and beautiful.

||
||

Contrast

|

"Cold, dark and splendid"

|

Combines threatening qualities with admiration.

||
||

Alliteration

|

“fierce intent”

|

Gives emphasis to the snake’s determination and predatory nature.

||
||

Imagery

|

"sun glazed his curves"

|

Creates vivid usual imagery and makes the snake appear beautiful.

||
||

Enjambment

|

Lines flow into one another

|

Mirrors the snake’s continuous, fluid movement

||
||

Sibilance

|

Repeated soft **s** sounds in descriptions of the snake

|

Can imitate the snake’s smooth movement and create a subtle sense of danger
