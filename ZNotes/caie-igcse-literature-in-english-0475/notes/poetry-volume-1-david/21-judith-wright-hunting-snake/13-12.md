## Conclusion

Wright presents *Hunting Snake* as a powerful exploration of **humanity's encounter with the natural world**. The snake is simultaneously threatening and beautiful, creating a mixture of fear and fascination in the speakers. Through **contrasting imagery, vivid description and the controlled structure of the sonnet**, Wright shows that nature cannot be easily categorised as either beautiful or dangerous. Ultimately, the poem suggests that humans should **respect and admire nature's power**, recognising that the natural world exists independently of human control.
