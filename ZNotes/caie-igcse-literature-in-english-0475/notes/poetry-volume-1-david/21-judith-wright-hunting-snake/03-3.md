## About the Poet

Judith Wright (1915–2000) was an **Australian poet, environmentalist and activist**. Her poetry often explores **nature, humanity's relationship with the natural world, mortality and the passing of time**.

## Overview

In *Hunting Snake*, Wright describes an encounter between the speaker and another person who suddenly see a large snake moving through the grass. Although the snake initially creates **fear and tension**, the speakers become fascinated by its **beauty, power and mystery**.
