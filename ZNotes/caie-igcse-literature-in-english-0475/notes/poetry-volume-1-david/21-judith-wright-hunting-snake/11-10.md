## Exam Tip

## This is a strong thesis statement:

> **Wright presents the snake as both threatening and beautiful, using contrasting imagery and the speakers' changing reactions to show the power and mystery of the natural world.**

For strong analysis, link:

**snake \+ fear \+ beauty \+ nature \+ human response**

### Don't just say:

> Wright uses contrast in **"Cold, dark and splendid."**

### Instead:

> Wright uses contrasting adjectives in **"Cold, dark and splendid"** to combine the snake's threatening qualities with the speaker's admiration of its beauty. The contrast reflects the poem's wider presentation of nature as something that can inspire both fear and wonder.
