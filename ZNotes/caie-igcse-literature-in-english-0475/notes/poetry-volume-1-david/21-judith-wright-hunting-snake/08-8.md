## Important Ideas / Quotes to Remember

* **"Sun-warmed"** — establishes a peaceful, pleasant natural setting.

* **"we froze half-through a pace"** — shows the immediate fear and power of the snake.

* **"The great black snake"** — establishes its size, darkness and threatening appearance.

* **"Head-down, tongue-flickering"** — creates vivid imagery of the snake's movement.

* **"sun glazed his curves of diamond scale"** — presents the snake as beautiful and precious.

* **"we lost breath to watch him pass"** — shows the humans' fear and fascination.

* **"fierce intent"** — reminds us that the snake is a predator.

* **"we scarcely thought"** — shows how completely the humans are absorbed by the encounter.

* **"Cold, dark and splendid"** — combines danger with beauty.

* **"grass that hid his prey"** — shows nature continuing independently of humans.

* **"We took a deeper breath of day"** — suggests relief after the danger passes.

* **"looked at each other, and went on"** — returns the humans to ordinary life after their extraordinary encounter.
