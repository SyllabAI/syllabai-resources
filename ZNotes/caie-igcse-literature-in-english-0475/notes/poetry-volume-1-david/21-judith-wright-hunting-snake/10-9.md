## Main Message

Wright presents nature as **powerful, unpredictable, beautiful and sometimes dangerous**.

The snake initially frightens the humans, but its beauty and purposeful movement soon fascinate them. The encounter makes them temporarily stop and observe a world that exists independently of them.

Ultimately, the poem suggests that **nature deserves both respect and admiration**, because beauty and danger can exist together within the natural world.

> **The poem shows that an encounter with nature can temporarily interrupt human life and reveal the power, beauty and mystery of the world beyond ourselves.**
