## **Structure & Form**

* Free verse- no regular rhyme or rhythm, reflecting natural thought and reflection.

* Two-part structure:

* Part 1: Spiritual, cultural, and educational reverence

* Part 2: Political reflection on language and colonialism

* The shift marks a move from c**elebration of culture to questioning historical power.**

## Important Ideas/ Quotes to Remember

* **Pan emigrating-** India as a living home of ancient spirituality

* **“Sin to shove a book aside“**- Sacred respect for learning

* **“Oppressor’s tongue“**- Language linked to colonial power

* Idea that **future generations may love the coloniser’s language**

## Main Message

Bhatt presents a “different history“ where:

* Culture preserves **spiritual and intellectual values**

* But colonialism leaves a deep mark through **language**

The poem shows that identity is shaped by both pride in cultural traditions and the lasting effects of historical power.

## Exam Tip

This is a strong thesis statement:

> *Bhatt contrasts India’s spiritual respect for knowledge with the political history of language to show that cultural identity is shaped by both tradition and colonial influence.*

Link **culture \+ language \+ history** in every paragraph for top marks.
