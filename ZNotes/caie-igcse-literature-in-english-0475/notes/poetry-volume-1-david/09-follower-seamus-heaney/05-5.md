## Model Question and Answer

**How does Heaney present the relationship between father and son in *“Follower”*?**

**Model Answer:**

Heaney presents the father-son relationship as one that changes over time. At the beginning of the poem, the father is shown as skilled and powerful, with precise movements that make him appear almost heroic. The speaker admires him deeply and wishes to follow in his footsteps. However, the speaker also reveals his own clumsiness, which highlights the gap between them and suggests feelings of inadequacy. In the final lines, there is a clear role reversal as the father “stumbles behind” the son. This shift reflects the passage of time and the effects of aging. The tone also becomes more complex, as admiration is replaced by frustration. Overall, Heaney presents the relationship as evolving, showing how childhood admiration can change into a more complicated adult perspective.
