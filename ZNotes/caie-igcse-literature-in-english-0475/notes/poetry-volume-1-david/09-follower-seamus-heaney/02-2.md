## About the Poet

Seamus Heaney was an Irish poet, playwright and a translator. He received the 1995 Nobel Prize in Literature. Heaney wrote about rural Irish life, nature, childhood memories, and the political turmoil of Northern Ireland. His work usually explored the themes of **identity, memory, ancestry, and the intersection of myth with daily life.**

## Context

* Heaney grew up on a farm, where his father worked as a **skilled ploughman.**

* Farming required **precision, strength, and experience.**

* The poem reflects a **personal memory,** but also explores universal ideas about **growing up and changing relationships.**

## Overview

*“Follower“* is a poem about a father-son relationship and a coming-of-age poem. The speaker remembers admiring his father as a child, but the poem ends with their roles reversed.

## Structure & Form

* Written in **one continuous stanza (no breaks).**

* Regular structure with **clear, steady rhythm**, mirroring the controlled movemement of ploughing,

* Uses **enjabment,** reflecting ongoing action and movement.
