**Alliteration**

|

"blustering husbands"

|

Draws attention to husbands as intimidating or overbearing.

||
||

**Hyperbole**

|

“The best of husbands are so hard to please."

|

Exaggerates husbands' demands to emphasise the difficulties of marriage.

||
||

**Imagery**

|

"Children's cries" / "careful faces"

|

Creates vivid pictures of the emotional and domestic pressures of marriage.

||
||

**Direct Address**

|

Thus, Madam, be advised by me."

|

Makes the poem feel like sincere, personal advice.

||
||

**Contrast**

|

"A virgin state..." vs "A married state..."

|

Highlights the difference between peaceful single life and stressful married life.

||
||

**Imperatives**

|

"Turn, turn..." "Suppress..."

|

Shows the speaker strongly urging women to reject romantic temptation.

||
