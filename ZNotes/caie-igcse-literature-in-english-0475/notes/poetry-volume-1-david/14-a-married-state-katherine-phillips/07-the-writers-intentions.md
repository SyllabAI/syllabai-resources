## Structure & Form

* **Form & Genre:** A 16-line, single-stanza **didactic lyric poem** that serves as a direct warning and piece of advice to women regarding the hardships of marriage.

* **Rhyme Scheme & Meter:** Written entirely in **rhyming couplets** ($AABBCC...$) primarily using **iambic pentameter**. This controlled, regular meter creates a measured, persuasive rhythm that reinforces the poet's logical argument.

* **Syntactical Structure (Anaphora):** Employs **anaphora** with the repetition of *"No"* (*"No blustering husbands..."*, *"No pangs of childbirth..."*, *"No children's cries..."*) across lines 7–9. This systematic listing visually and rhythmically builds a cumulative case for the peace of singlehood.

* **Line Enjambment & Flow:** Uses enjambment between lines 11 and 12 (*"freed from all the cares that do / Attend on matrimony..."*) to emphasize the overwhelming weight of marital burdens before delivering the concluding advice.
