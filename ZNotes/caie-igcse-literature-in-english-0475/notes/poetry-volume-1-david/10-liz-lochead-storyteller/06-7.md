## **Conclusion**

Overall, *Storyteller* presents storytelling as a powerful and transformative act that shapes perception, strengthens community, and preserves memory. Liz Lochhead elevates storytelling beyond simple entertainment, portraying it as a form of creative labour that subtly challenges traditional gender roles. Although the stories themselves are temporary, their emotional and cultural impact is lasting, reinforcing the idea that imagination can outlive the moment of telling and continue to influence those who hear it.
