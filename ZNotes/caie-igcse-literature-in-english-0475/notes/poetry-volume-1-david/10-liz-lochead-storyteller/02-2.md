## About the Poet

Liz Lochhead is a Scottish poet and a playwright who’s work usually focuses on women’s voices, identity & culture and oral traditions. She often writes in a **conversational, natural tone** and interested in how **ordinary people carry culture.**

## Context

* Set in a **working-class / rural setting**

* Storytelling happens during **domestic work** (sewing, cooking, etc.)

* Reflects **oral tradition**- stories passed down by memory, not books

The poem presents a woman whose role is to **tell stories while others work.** Though her work may seem “useless“, it actually preserves culture and imagination. The stories live on in children, showing **storytelling as powerful and lasting.**
