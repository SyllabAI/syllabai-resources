## Important Ideas / Quotes to Remember

* **"loom of another world"** — separates the children's reality from ordinary childhood.

* **"their dresses bright"** — colourful imagery creates beauty and youth.

* **"like television"** — suggests the children are fascinated by the weaving, while also implying that work has replaced normal childhood entertainment.

* **"garden of Islam"** — connects the carpet with Moroccan Islamic culture and spirituality.

* **"merchant's truck"** — introduces the commercial side of the children's labour.

* **"servants of the mosque"** — connects their work to religion.

* **"school of days"** — metaphor for life and the passage of time.

* **"hard at work"** — reminds us that these are children engaged in labour.

* **"colours of all-that-will-be"** — suggests creativity, possibility and the future.

* **"freeze into the frame of all-that-was"** — suggests that the children's future and childhood are being fixed into the past.
