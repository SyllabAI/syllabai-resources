## About the Poet

Carol Rumens is a British poet whose work often explores place, identity, culture, memory, and human relationships. Her poetry frequently examines ordinary experiences while revealing deeper social and emotional issues.

## Overview

*Carpet Weavers, Morocco* focuses on children working at carpet looms, but Rumens uses the weaving process to explore ideas about childhood, labour, time, poverty, religion, and the contrast between innocence and hardship.
