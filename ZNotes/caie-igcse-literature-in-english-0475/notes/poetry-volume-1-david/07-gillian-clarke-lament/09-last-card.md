
## Conclusion

In *Lament*, Gillian Clarke presents war as a devastating force that harms wildlife, destroys natural habitats and causes widespread human suffering. Through repetition, vivid imagery, metaphor and an elegiac tone, she creates a memorial for the victims of conflict and exposes the environmental consequences of warfare.

By placing animals and people within the same lament, Clarke emphasises the interconnectedness of humanity and nature. The poem also condemns vengeance and suggests that conflict can destroy the possibility of meaningful communication.

Ultimately, *Lament* is both an environmental protest and a humanitarian warning. Clarke encourages readers to recognise the full cost of war and consider the urgent need to protect both human life and the natural world.
