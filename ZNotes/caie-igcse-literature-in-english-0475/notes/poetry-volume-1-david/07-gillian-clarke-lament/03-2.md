## About the Poet

Gillian Clarke is a Welsh poet and playwright known for writing about:

* **Nature and the environment**

* Welsh identity and landscape

* War and political conflict

* Human relationships

She often writes about the fragile relationship between humans and the natural world. Clarke later became the National Poet of Wale (2008-2016).

In “*Lament*“, she responds to a global crisis rather than a personal or local issue.
