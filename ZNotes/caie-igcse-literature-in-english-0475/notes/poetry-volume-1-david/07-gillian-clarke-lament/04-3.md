
## Context

The poem was inspired by the environmental destruction during the **Gulf War,** when massive oil spills polluted the sea and killed wildlife.

Instead of focusing on soldiers or politics, Clarke presents **nature as the true victim of war.**

## Overview

“*Lament*“ is:

* An **elegy** (a poem of mourning)

* An **environmental protest poem**

* A poem using **dramatic monologue** (different voices speak)

The poem gives voices to:

* A heron

* The earth

* Oil

This creates a layered expression of grief and irony.

## Structure & Form

* Written in **free verse** (no regular rhyme scheme).

* Divided into sections with shifting speakers.

* Uses **anaphora** (“For the…“, “I am…“) to create a chant-like mourning rhythm.

* Accumulative listing emphasizes the scale of devastation.

The repetition mirrors both prayer and accusation.
