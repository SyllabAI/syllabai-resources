
## Themes

**Human Uncertainty and Indecision**

One of the most important themes in the poem is the idea that people often feel uncertain about the direction their lives should take. The cockroach moves in a zigzag pattern, turning left and right without a clear destination. This behaviour mirrors how humans sometimes act when they are unsure about their choices.

The poet suggests that many people go through moments where they:

* hesitate before making decisions

* question their actions

* feel unsure about the future.

By comparing human behaviour to the movement of a cockroach, Halligan shows that uncertainty is a normal part of life. The insect’s movement becomes a metaphor for the **confusing paths people take while trying to figure things out.**

**Inner Conflict and Overthinking**

The cockroach appears to pause and reconsider its movement, which leads the speaker to imagine that it might be thinking deeply. This creates the impression of an internal struggle, as if the insect cannot decide what to do next.

This reflects how humans often experience inner conflict when faced with difficult choices.

People sometimes:

* change their minds

* doubt themselves

* feel mentally overwhelmed.

The poem highlights how the human mind can become trapped in cycles of thoughts, just like the cockroach that keeps changing direction.

Halligan uses this comparison to suggest that **overthinking can make people feel stuck or lost.**

**The Complexity of the Human Mind**

Although the poem begins with a simple observation, it gradually becomes a reflection on how complex human thinking can be. The speaker projects human emotions and thoughts onto the cockroach, which shows how easily people interpret behaviour in psychological terms.

The poem suggests that humans often:

* analyse situations deeply

* imagine behind actions

* try to understand behaviour in complex ways.

This themes also shows how the mind **can turn small experiences into deep reflections about life.**
