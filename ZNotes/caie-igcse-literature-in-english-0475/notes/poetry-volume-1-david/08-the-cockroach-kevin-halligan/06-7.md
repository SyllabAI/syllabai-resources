## Conclusion

Overall, *The Cockroach* shows how a simple everyday observation can reveal deeper truths about human nature. By watching the cockroach move in an uncertain and unpredictable way, Halligan reflects on how **people often struggle with decisions and experience moments of confusion in life.**

The cockroach becomes more than just an insect; it represent the **human mind when it is unsure, conflicted, or overthinking.** Through techniques such as simile, personification, and careful description of movement, the poet connects the insect’s behaviour to human psychology.

Ultimately, the poem suggests that **uncertainty is a normal part of life.** Just like the **cockroach that changes direction many times, people also move through life searching for the right path.** This makes the poem thoughtful and relatable, encouraging readers to reflect on their own experiences and decisions.
