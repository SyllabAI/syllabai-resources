## **Overview of the Poem**

The speaker watches a cockroach moving across the floor in an unusual and unpredictable way. Instead of walking straight, the insect changes direction repeatedly, hesitates, and seems unsure where it is going.

As the speaker continues observing it, he begins to imagine the cockroach as if it were human, someone who is struggling to make decisions or dealing with confusion in life.

By the end of the poem, the cockroach is no longer just an insect; it becomes **a symbol of human indecision and psychological conflict.**
