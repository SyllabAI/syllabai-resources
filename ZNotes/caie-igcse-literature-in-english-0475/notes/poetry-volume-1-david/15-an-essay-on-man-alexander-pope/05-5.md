## Important Ideas / Quotes to Remember

* **"Know then thyself"** — Humans should understand themselves and their limitations.

* **"presume not God to scan"** — Humans should not arrogantly attempt to understand everything about God.

* **"The proper study of mankind is man"** — Humanity should focus on understanding itself.

* **"middle state"** — Humans exist between opposing extremes.

* **"He hangs between"** — Humanity is suspended between different possibilities.

* **"god or beast"** — Humans are uncertain about their position between the divine and animal worlds.

* **"Born but to die, and reasoning but to err"** — Both human life and human intelligence are limited.

* **"Created half to rise and half to fall"** — Humans possess both greatness and weakness.
