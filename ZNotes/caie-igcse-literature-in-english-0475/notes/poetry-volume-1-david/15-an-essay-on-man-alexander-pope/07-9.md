## Conclusion 

Pope presents humanity as a **complex and contradictory "middle state"**. Humans possess reason, power and the potential for greatness, but they are also limited by ignorance, weakness, error and mortality. Through **contrast, paradox, repetition and heroic couplets**, Pope emphasises the tension between humanity's abilities and its limitations. Ultimately, the poem encourages **self-knowledge and humility**, reminding humans that they should understand their own place in creation rather than presume to understand everything about God and the universe.
