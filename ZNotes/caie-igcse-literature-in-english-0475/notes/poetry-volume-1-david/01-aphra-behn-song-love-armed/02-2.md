## **About the Poet**

Apra Behn (1640-1689) was a Restoration writer and one of the first English women to earn a living through writing. Her work is universally known for being bold, honest about desire, and critical of traditional ideas about love and power. While writing in a male-dominated literary world, Behn often exposed how love can be unequal and painful, especially for women.

## **Form and Structure**

* A lyric poem, written as a song

* Two stanzas with regular rhythm and rhyme

* Musical and light structure contrasts sharply with the apparent dark message about love

* This contrast reinforces the idea that love can appear beautiful while causing suffering.

In Song: Love Arm’d, Love (Cupid) is personified as a violent, triumphant God who sits surrounded by bleeding hearts. Love gains his power from two people in this poem:

* the speaker (‘‘me’’) who gives love emotional depth through pain and desire

* the beloved (“thee’’), who gives love cruelty, pride, and power

Although both contribute to Cupid’s strength, only the speaker suffers, while the beloved remains untouched and victorious.
