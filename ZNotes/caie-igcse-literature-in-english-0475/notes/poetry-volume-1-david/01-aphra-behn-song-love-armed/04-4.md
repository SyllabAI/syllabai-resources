## **Language and Literary Devices**

* **Imagery-**”the bleeding hearts”, “fires,“ “sighs and tears“

* **Metaphor-** Love compared to war and conquest

* **Contrast-** speaker’s suffering vs beloved’s freedom

* **Irony-** a poem called a ‘‘song and expressing pain and bitterness

## **Tone**

The tone is **bitter, ironic, and reflective.** Although the poem sounds playful and musical, it carries a strong sense of emotional injustice and quiet resentment.

## **Message**

Apra Behn challenges the romantic idea of love. She presents it as a **destructive and unequal force,** especially harmful to the person who loves more deeply. Love is powerful not because it is “divine“, but because we humans give it power through desire, pride and emotional vulnerability
