## About the Poet

**Elizabeth Brewster (1922-2012)** was a Canadian poet known for writing about identity, belonging, place and displacement, and memory. She often explored how migration and geography shape personal identity. Her poetry is reflective and thoughtful rather than dramatic or emotional.

## Overview

This interesting poem explores how a **person’s identity is shaped by where they come from.**

The speaker reflects on her homeland and suggests that even if she were to move elsewhere, her origins would always remain part of her identity. Through this poem, Brewster shows that **home is not just a physical place, it is something much more internal and permanent.**
