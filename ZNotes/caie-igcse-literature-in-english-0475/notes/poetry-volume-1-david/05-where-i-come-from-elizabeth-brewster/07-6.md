## **Conclusion**

In *Where I Come From,* Elizabeth Brewster ultimately suggests that identity is inseparable from origin. Throughout the poem, the repeated declaration “Where I come from“ builds the idea that a person’s environment, memories, and early experiences quietly shape who they become. The homeland is not presented as a simple geographical location, but as a psychological and emotional foundation that continue to influence the speaker even if she moves elsewhere.

The poem leaves readers reflecting on their own beginnings, suggesting that understanding where we come from is essential to understanding who we are.
