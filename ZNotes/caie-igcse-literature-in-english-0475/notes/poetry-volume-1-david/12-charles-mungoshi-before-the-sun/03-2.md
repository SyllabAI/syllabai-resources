## About the Poet

Charles Mungoshi was one of Zimbabwe's most celebrated writers. He wrote poetry, novels, short stories, and plays in both English and Shona. Growing up in a farming family, much of his work reflects rural African life, nature, culture, and human experiences. He was an important voice in post-colonial African literature.

## Context

* The poem was published in *The Milkman Doesn't Only Deliver Milk* (1988).

* Zimbabwe had gained independence from British colonial rule only a few years earlier (1980).

* Mungoshi often wrote about ordinary rural life and the relationship between people and nature.
