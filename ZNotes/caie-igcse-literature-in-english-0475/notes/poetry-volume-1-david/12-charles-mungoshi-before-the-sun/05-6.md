## Language and Literary Devices

#|
||

**Device**

|

**Example**

|

**Effect**

||
||

Imagery

|

"Intense blue morning"

|

Creates vivid visual picture

||
||

Personification

|

"the sun just winks" 

|

Makes nature seem friendly and alive

||
||

Simile

|

"like some latecomer to a feast"

|

Makes the sun feel human and playful

||
||

Sensory Imagery

|

"sweet nose-cleansing odour"

|

Appeals to smell and immerses the reader

||
||

Symbolism

|

Sun, maize, smoke

|

Adds deeper meaning

||
||

Free Verse

|

No regular rhyme scheme

|

Creates a natural conversational tone

||
||

Enjambment

|

Ideas continue across lines

|

Reflects flowing movement of nature
