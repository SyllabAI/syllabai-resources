## About the Poet

Charles Mungoshi is a Zimbabwean writer and poet known for short stories, novels, and poetry. He often writes about rural African life, family, and everyday experiences. He normally focuses on how ordinary moments can carry deep meaning and symbolism.

## Overview

A poem about a young boy doing early morning rural work. He chops wood, makes a fire, roasts maize, and enjoys the peaceful morning. The poem shows how simple rural life becomes meaningful and almost spiritual.

## Context

The poem reflects rural life and traditional practices in Zimbabwe. It shows harmony between humans and nature and is influenced by postcolonial African writing, focusing on identity and everyday life.
