## Themes

**Harmony with Nature**

The boy works closely with the natural environment. Nature is shown as friendly and alive, not threatening. 

The sun is personified as a living presence.

The boy interacts with fire, wood, and maize naturally, suggesting that everything feels connected and balanced.

**Childhood and Innocence**

The speaker is a young boy experiencing freedom, and work is seen as fun, not a burden. His playfulness is shown in "sharing" maize with the sun. There’s innocence displayed before adult responsibility.
