**Exam Tip**

This is a strong thesis statement:

**Shakespeare contrasts the temporary beauty of nature with the lasting power of poetry to suggest that although physical beauty inevitably fades, art can preserve love and beauty beyond time and death.**

For strong analysis, link:

**beauty \+ time \+ nature \+ death \+ poetry**

**Don’t just say:**

Shakespeare uses personification in **“death brag.”**

**Instead:**

Shakespeare personifies **“death”** as an opponent capable of **“bragging”**, presenting death as something the speaker actively challenges. This creates a defiant tone and suggests that poetry can defeat death by preserving the beloved’s beauty beyond physical life.
