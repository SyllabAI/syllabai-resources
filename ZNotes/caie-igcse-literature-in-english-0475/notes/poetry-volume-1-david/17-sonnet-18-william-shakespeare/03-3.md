## About the Poet

William Shakespeare (1564–1616) was an **English poet, playwright and actor** and one of the most influential writers in English literature.

His poetry often explores **love, beauty, time, death and human relationships**.

## Overview

*Sonnet 18* is one of Shakespeare’s most famous sonnets. The speaker considers whether to compare the person he loves to a **summer’s day**, but ultimately argues that the beloved is **more beautiful and more lasting than summer**.
