**Structure & Form**

* A **Shakespearean sonnet** consisting of **14 lines**.

* Written mainly in **iambic pentameter**.

* Uses the traditional rhyme scheme:
  **ABAB CDCD EFEF GG**

* The poem is divided into:

  * **Three quatrains** — develop the argument.

  * **A final rhyming couplet** — provides the conclusion.

* The poem uses a **volta**, or turning point, at **“But”** in line 9.

* The first three quatrains focus mainly on the **limitations of summer and physical beauty**.

* The final section shifts towards the idea that **poetry can overcome time and death**.

**Key structural idea:**

**Temporary nature → Eternal poetry**

The poem begins with a natural comparison but ends by suggesting that **art can achieve what nature cannot: permanence**.
