## Tone

#|
||

**Section**

|

**Tone**

|

**Effect**

||
||

Opening

|

Admiring, affectionate

|

Establishes the speakers praise of the beloved

||
||

First quatrain

|

Appreciative but critical

|

Begins to reveal the imperfections of summer

||
||

Second quatrain

|

Reflective, serious

|

Emphasises the temporary nature of beauty

||
||

Third quatrain

|

Defiant, confident

|

Challenges the power of death and time

||
||

Final couplet

|

Triumphant, certain

|

Claims poetry will give the beloved eternal life

||
|#

The tone shifts from **romantic admiration to confident defiance**, as the speaker moves from praising the beloved’s beauty to claiming that poetry will make it immortal.
