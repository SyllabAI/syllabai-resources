## Key Literary Devices

#|
||

**Device**

|

**Example/Use**

|

**Effect**

||
||

Rhetorical question

|

"Shall I compare thee to a summer's day?"

|

Introduces the central comparison and draws the reader into the speaker's argument.

||
||

Personification

|

"eye of heaven"

|

Gives the sun human qualities and elevates its importance.

||
||

Metaphor

|

“summer’s lease”

|

Presents summer as having a temporary contract or lifespan.

||
||

Contrast

|

"summer's... short a date" / "eternal"

|

Contrasts temporary natural beauty with the permanence of poetry.

||
||

Alliteration

|

"summer's lease"

|

Gives the phrase rhythm and emphasises summer's limited duration.

||
||

Imagery

|

“darling’s buds of May”

|

Creates delicate natural imagery and represents youthful beauty.

||
||

Enjambment

|

Lines flow into the following lines

|

Creates continuity and reflects the ongoing passage of time.
