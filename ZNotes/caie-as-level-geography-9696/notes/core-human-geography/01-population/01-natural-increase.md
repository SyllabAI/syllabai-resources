# Population

## Natural Increase

### **Natural Increase Rate**

* **NIR:** when birth rates exceed death rates.
* **Birth Rate:** live births per 1000 people per year.
* **Death Rate:** deaths per 1000 people per year.
* **Fertility Rate:** average number of children a woman has during her lifetime. There will be population growth if FR > 2.1 (replacement level).
* **Infant Mortality Rate:** number of children under the age of 1 who die per 1000 live births per year.
* **Life Expectancy:** the average number of years at birth a person is expected to live.

$$
\text{Overall}\ \text{population}\ \text{change}\  = \ (\text{BR} - \text{DR})\ \\ \pm \ \text{net}\ \text{migration}.
$$
