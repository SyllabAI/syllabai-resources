## Youthful Populations

* **Causes:** high BR (lack of family planning and contraception, high
  infant mortality), migration, tradition and a lack of care for old
  dependents leads to high DR amongst that age group.
* **Advantages**
  * Lower DR, higher demand in the country.
  * IT literate and educated population.
  * Large future market.
* **Issues**
  * Abundance of future workers may result in unemployment.
  * The cost of childcare and child benefit is high.
  * Short-term worker shortage.
  * High healthcare costs.
  * Spending diverted from other uses.
* **Solutions**
  * Anti-natalist policies, reduced BR/IM
  * Privatised education/healthcare
  * Increased immigration of middle-aged, restricted immigration
  * Removed child benefits
  * Greater care of old dependents to reduce the death rate in the elderly.
