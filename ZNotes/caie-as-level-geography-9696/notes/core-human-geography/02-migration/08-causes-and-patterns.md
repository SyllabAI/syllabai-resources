## Causes and Patterns

* **Causes:** population pressure, economic differences, civil war,
  natural disasters.
* **Barriers:** cost of moving, distance to move, cultural/information
  barriers, immigration laws (visa requirements, border walls,
  intercepting illegal entries, checking return tickets for tourists
  etc…)
* **Patterns:** LICs HICs; HICs HICs; South North. May involve gender,
  distance, level of development, former colonies of a nation.
