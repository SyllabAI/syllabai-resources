## Outputs of the Drainage Basin System

* **Evapotranspiration (ET)**
  * **Evaporation:** liquid changes into water vapour, from puddles
    and streams. Rate of evaporation increases in hot, dry and windy
    conditions and with larger soil surface area.
  * **Transpiration:** water is drawn up from the soil by the plant and leaves the plant as water vapour through tiny pores on the underside of the leaves’ known as stomata.

<!-- end list -->

* **Potential Evapotranspiration (PET):** the amount of evaporation that would occur if an unlimited water source were available.
* **River Discharge:** water that flows into the sea, or that moves within channels (streams/rivers). Water enters the channel as direct channel precipitation or other flows.
  * $\mathbf{Q = A}\mathbf{V}$ (Q = Discharge, A = Cross Sectional Area, V = Velocity). Measured in m<sup>3</sup>/second – Cumecs.
