# Use of Technology in Design and Making

## CAD and CAM

### **CAD (Computer Aided Design)**

* It allows you to **change your design quickly** without having to manually re-draw it every time.
* It is much more **precise** with **measurements** (no human error).
* A simple way to **create 3D models** (refer to them as XYZ models to remember what they are).
* It allows **perfect simulations** of the product before actual production takes place. As a result, key faults can be identified and fixed beforehand.
* Precise **runtime, key details, multiple viewing angles.**

**CAD is used a lot in the industry. Almost all parts of modern-day objects were designed using CAD!**

### **CAM**

* CAM is **Computer Aided Manufacturing**.
* It transforms CAD products into actual **physical products.**
* Very common when it comes to design and technology.
* It involves the **use of machinery and automated manufacturing**.
