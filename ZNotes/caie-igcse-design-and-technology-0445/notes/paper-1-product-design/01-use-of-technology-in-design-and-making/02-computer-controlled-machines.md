## Computer-Controlled Machines

### **Machinery Computer-Control**

* Machines can be controlled by a computer, which can **monitor how different machines function with the help of sensors**.
* Examples of machines that a computer can control are **laser cutters**, **CNC machines** and **router machines.**
* Computers can enhance **quality control** by detecting **problematic samples** more efficiently due to their built-in mechanisms. Furthermore, they **evaluate each sample** from a consistent perspective.

### **Stock Control**

* Stock Control is the **“upper” and “lower” bounds of how much output a company can produce.**
* **Upper** means the **maximum product output** a company can create.
* **Lower** means how much the **smallest product outpu**t a company can create without having problems (this isn’t zero!)
* Stock Control is highly important; without **proper stock control, a company could run out of money.**
