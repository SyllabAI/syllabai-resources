## Effects of Design and Technology Activities

* Design Technology has a massive **impact on the environment**. Everything was once designed, which means that the materials were carefully selected. The production process and the logistics were also planned. Materials are finite, meaning they are not renewable and eventually will run out; designers tend to search for materials that would damage the environment as little as possible through design and technology. 
* Since pollution also has a massive environmental impact, the extraction of materials and the formation of new materials (such as concrete and polymers) can produce a large carbon footprint. These materials have to be chosen and innovated to reduce our carbon footprint. 
* An example of reducing pollution (more precisely, reducing carbon footprint) would be using renewable energy (statistically, burning oil for electricity is the most polluting industry), obtaining materials through certified areas, and making the structure last longer.
  * Another example would be that material x was chosen over material z. This would change the environmental impact.
* Design technology also has an economic impact, depending on the chosen materials and manufacturing methods, making the product more expensive (or less), meaning that it will appeal to a smaller audience (or bigger, depending on factors). This would change demand, therefore changing the economic impact.
* Design Technology also creates a **social impact**. Depending on what social/class group the product is made for or what generation the product is targeted at. (Ex. Sports cars tend to be targeted towards male consumers.) Another social impact could be on the group and class that manufactures the product, such as the illegal use of children in harsh labour. This affects outside factors such as education and impacts future careers.
* Another social impact is the illegal extraction of materials in a given area (a good example would be Nigerian oil); illegally extracted would mean that the company or group doing the extraction has not been certified or inspected, and this could mean that they could be a threat to the local communities by threats like pollution and illegal labour. This would affect the living conditions and environment of the population living there.
