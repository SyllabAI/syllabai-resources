# Design Technology in Society

## Design Technology in Society

* Design Technology is everywhere around you, from door handles to mechanical ballpoint pens. All of this was designed in CAD.
* Humans need products, and these products have to be designed by people:
  * **Designers** design the product prototype, mainly in CAD and through drawing.
  * **Artisans** manufacture the product using the materials given.
  * **Technologists** are experts in their field of design and production.
