## Joints

### **Introduction**

* Joints can be very helpful for multiple reasons:
* They can join structural members together
* Allow loads to be mobile
* Joints can be different depending on how they are attached

| Material | Joint |
|----|----|
| Wood | screws, adhesive, coach bolts |
| Steel | welding, brazing, riveting, nuts, bolts |
| Plastic | welding, nuts, bolts, screws |
| Pre-Cast Concrete | mechanical connections |
| Cast Concrete | monolithic connections |

### **Short Explanation to Joining**

**Brazing**

* A material with a lower melting point is melted
* It is placed between 2 other material
* Once the material hardens, it sticks the other 2 materials together

**Soldering**

* A soft solder, either copper or brass, is melted
* It is placed into the hole that needs to be covered (soldered)

**Welding**

* Parts of material are melted under great temperature
* They are then fused together and placed in cold water to harden

**Screws (Machine)**

* These are almost normal screws that are placed into a hole
* They are then blocked inside the hole by either an adhesive or something at the end (similar to a nut)

**Riveting**

* A rivet is placed into the desired space
* A rivet gun is then used to snap the end.
* The rivet is pressed, and a joint is created.
