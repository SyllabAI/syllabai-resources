## Materials

### **Introduction**

* It is important to consider the correct material for any project
* Different materials have different properties, so they react differently to corrosion or weathering
* Some materials are both durable and aesthetically pleasing

### **Mechanical Properties**

| Properties | Definitions |
|----|----|
| **Tensile Strength** | **Ability to withstand pulling forces (tension)** |
| **Compression Strength** | **Ability to withstand compression forces** |
| **Shear Strength** | **Ability to withstand forces that tear a material apart** |
| Torsional Strength | Ability to withstand twisting forces |
| Bending Strength | Ability to withstand bending forces |
|    |    |
| Hardness | Ability to withstand wear |
| Elasticity | A measure of how flexible a material is |
| Toughness | Ability to absorb energy and deform without fracturing |
| Resistance to corrosion | Ability to withstand rusting (or weathering) |
| Thermal conductivity | How well a material conducts thermal energy |
| Optical properties | Transparent, Translucent or Opaque |

~~(Please note that the bits in bold are the 3 main definitions you need to know)~~
