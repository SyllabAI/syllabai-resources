# Time Management

* The total allocated time for the paper is **1 hour 15 minutes.**
* According to my experience, **the hardest part of the paper is time management!**
* Here is the recommended time allocation (one that I use):

| Question/Part: | Time (minutes): |
|----|----|
| Part A | 5 |
| Part B | 5 |
| Part C | 20 |
| Part D | 10 |
| Part E | 20 |
| Part F | 3 |
| Part G | 7 |
| Proofreading | 5 |
| **Total Time:** | **75** |

* The longer (drawing + annotations) questions require more time as they have the greatest weightage!
* **It is important to spend a lot of time drawing and annotating** (these are the crucial questions).

**Important Note**: This is not a definitive guide, just the timings I recommended.
