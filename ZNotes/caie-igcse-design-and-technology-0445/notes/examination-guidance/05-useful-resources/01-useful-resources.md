# Useful Resources

In this section, I will provide you with some of the best available resources for P1.

Please note that there aren’t many, due to D&T being a relatively niche subject.


1. <https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/papers/caie/igcse-design-and-technology-0445> (a website I used for past papers; it is completely free and contains both papers and mark schemes)
2. <https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-design-and-technology-0445/> (the official Cambridge sub-section for 0445 D&T containing syllabus content and changes)
3. <https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mSnbyzqe2MAOUkS2p6htCsq_ygr2dZVb/view> (a more in-depth PDF guide to paper 1 with answers)
4. Good Luck!
