# Materials

* Materials are everywhere; things you use must be made from something.
* These materials are carefully selected for the purpose! Materials range from expensive to cheap, however, they all play a crucial role in everyday activities.

## Properties of Materials

### **Plastic**

* New Material (Common production of objects began in the 1950s)
* Extremely cheap
* Very common
* Heat-Resistant (Fairly well)
* Usually does not require treatment against weathering
* Low density
* Moderately strong against compression
* High tensile capabilities
* Requires processing

### **Wood**

* Flammable
* Range of pricing (some woods are expensive)
* Durable
* Exposed to weathering (unless treated)
* Moderately strength Resistant to Compression and Tension forces
* Split into 2 types (Hardwoods, Softwoods)
* Low Density (Usually floats)
* Very common in everyday life
* Natural

### **Metal**

* Shiny, Sonorous, Ductile, Malleable
* Range of pricing
* Very Durable
* Most require treatment against weathering
* Strong Compression and Tension
* Very common in everyday life
* Natural
