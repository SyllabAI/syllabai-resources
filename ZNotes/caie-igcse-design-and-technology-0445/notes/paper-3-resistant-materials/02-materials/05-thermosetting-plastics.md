## Thermosetting Plastics

### **Polyester Resin (GRP)**

* May be **reformed by remelting**
* High density
* Excellent strength
* High water resistance
* Cheap (Relatively)
* Withstands repeated recycling
* Shatter resistant
* Generally comes in small pellets
* Uses:
  * Automotive Interiors
  * Packaging Material
  * Ropes/Carpeting

### **Melamine Formaldehyde (MF)**

* Smooth surface
* Hygienic qualities
* Heat-resistant
* Chemical Resistant
* White!
* Uses:
  * Toilet Seats
  * Pan knobs/handles
  * Cups, Bowls etc.

### **Urea Formaldehyde (UF)**

* High hardness, High toughness
* Scratch resistant
* Very good electrical insulator
* High density
* High tensile strength
* Low water absorption
* Uses:
  * Electric Equipment
  * Foam insulator
  * Agricultural Use

### **Phenol-formaldehyde (PF)**

* Commonly used in **moulding**
* Low impact strength brittle
* Very simple to mould
* Can be both transparent and colorless
* Good moisture resistance
* Good Electrical Insulator
* It is produced in an exothermic reaction between Phenol and Formaldehyde
* Uses:
  * Wood Adhesive
  * Brake lining
  * Appliance handles

### **Epoxy Resin**

* Epoxy resin is **extremely common**, if you take DT you might come across it in the workshop
* It is produced in an Exothermic reaction between Bisphenol A and Epichlorohydrin (ECH)
* The substances are mixed in a ratio of 1:1 or 2:1
* It is toxic in contact with skin (when in liquid form)
* You need to wait a few hours to let it set
* Low cost
* High strength
* Electrical Insulator
* Chemical solvent
* At base it appears to be transparent, however, it is dyed to achieve a colour
* **Uses:**
  * Adhesive
  * Commonly used for coatings
  * Composite materials
