# **Metals**

* Metals are another very **common element**, they are extracted through the process of mining the Earth’s core
* Metal usually come from **ore**
* They are **first mined then crushed and refined** so that they become pure (eg. Blast Furnace for the Refining of Iron ore)
* Metals have some specific properties (they are usually measured on a scale):
  * Sonorous
  * Ductile
  * Hard
  * Malleable
  * Toughness
  * Shiny (Lustrous)
* Metals are also split into 2 categories; Ferrous (contains IRON) and Non-ferrous (DOES NOT contain iron)

### **Ferrous Metals**

* Ferrous metal all contain IRON this means that they have some common properties.
* They all **conduct heat and electricit**y
* They all **react with oxygen** (Because of the Iron) and rust
* Have **high melting points**
* Here is a list of Ferrous metals and they properties:

| Name | Melting Point (Degrees) | Properties | Uses |
|----|----|----|----|
| Cast Iron | 1200 | It is brittle and easily corrodes by rusting. | Casting and base metal for alloys, car brake discs. |
| Stainless Steel (Alloy) | 1400 | It is Hard and wear resistant. It is an alloy containing 18% chromium, 8% magnesium and 8% nickel. | It is used for Cutlery, kitchen equipment, surgical equipment. |
| High Speed Steel (HSS) (Alloy) | 1400 | It contains a high content of tungsten, chromium and vanadium. Very brittle but also resistant to wear. | It is used for Drill bits, Lathe tools and milling cutlery. |
| Mild Steel (Alloy) | 1600 | It is though, ductile, malleable and has good tensile strength. It has very poor resistant to corrosion. 30% Carbon. | General purpose material. \n Car bodies and bolts. |
| High Carbon Steel (Alloy) | 1800 | It is hard and though but the high concentration of carbon makes it brittle. It can be heat treated. | It is used in cutting tools, hand tools and ball bearings. |

### **Non-Ferrous Metals**

* These metals/alloys do not contain IRON.

| Name | Melting Point \n (Degrees) | Properties | Uses |
|----|----|----|----|
| Lead | 160 | It is very soft and malleable, it is one of the lowest melting points that metals have. | Roofing, Construction, Casting and lead batteries. |
| Tin | 230 | It is very ductile and malleable. It appears to be shiny and water resistant. | Often used for metal coating, tin foil (for the kitchen). |
| Zinc | 420 | It is very weak, however it is very corrosion resistant. | Used for coating. Used for waterproofing steel. |
| Duraluminium (Alloy) | 630 | Very strong, hard and lightweight. It is an alloy of copper (3%), manganese (1%) and Magnesium (1.6%). In normal state it is soft and workable. | Aircraft construction and Boat building. |
| Aluminium | 660 | Light in color, It is very light and also the most common metal in Earth. | Saucepans, Cooking foil, bicycles, ect… |
| Brass (Alloy) | 925 | It is a mixture of Copper (65%) and Zine (35%), It is typically shiny and of a nice yellow color. | Used for decorations, musical instruments. |
| Bronze (Alloy) | 950 | It is a brown in color and is usually cast into shapes. It darkens with exposure to elements. | Bearings, Sculptural Pieces and Hand bells. |
| Copper | 1080 | It is a very ductile and malleable metal. It goes green when it corrodes. Perfect conductor of heat and electricity. | Piping, cookware and electrical fittings. |

* These are the main non-ferrous metals that you are required to know!
