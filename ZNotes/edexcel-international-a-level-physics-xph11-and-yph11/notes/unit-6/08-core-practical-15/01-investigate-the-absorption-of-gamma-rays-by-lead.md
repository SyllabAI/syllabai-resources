**Investigate the Absorption of Gamma Rays by Lead**

**Introduction:**

* Different types of radiation have different penetrating powers and can be stopped by different materials of various lengths. The absorption of any type of radiation is modelled as an exponential curve, and the equation 

$$

N = N_0e^{-\mu x}

$$

Procedure:

1. The thickness of each of the discs is measured with Vernier callipers or a micrometre.
2. The background count rate is recorded for 5 minutes. This is repeated, and the average value is subtracted from subsequent readings.
3. Once this has been done, the source is put in position, and the count N$_0$ with no lead discs in place is recorded.
4. The count N is then taken for several thicknesses of $x$ of lead using the discs singly or combined as absorbers. Care must be taken to keep the distance between the source and the G-M tube constant.
5. A graph of the count rate N against absorber thickness $x$ is initially plotted.
6. A graph of ln N against $x$ should be plotted to test whether the graph is an exponential curve.

**Calculations:**

1. You can convert the equation into logarithmic form, giving  $lnN = -\mu x + ln0$

2. This can be compared to $Y = mX + C$

3. Since the value of the gradient will be $–\mu$, which is a constant, the line should be straight with a negative constant gradient.

   **Safety:**

   * A sealed source should always be used.
   * The source must be kept in a lead-lined box in a special locked metal cupboard labelled with the radioactivity symbol in a separate store room.
   * Protective gloves should be worn.
