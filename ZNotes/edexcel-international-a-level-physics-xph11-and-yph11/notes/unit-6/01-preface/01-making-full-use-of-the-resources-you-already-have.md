# Preface

## Making Full Use of the Resources You Already Have

* If you do not take A Level Maths, or even if you do, it is essential that you go through the Maths Skills required in the course. You can do this by checking out the specification.
* Additionally, you can go through some of the general notes for Unit 3 to remind yourself of how practical questions are structured.
* Remember also to do a lot of past paper questions. If you have finished all the papers in the new specification, go through the old specification papers as well if there is extra time.
* There are also many other websites, such as YouTube, where you can access videos visually demonstrating the experiments. This will give you a clearer understanding of how the procedure is carried out.
