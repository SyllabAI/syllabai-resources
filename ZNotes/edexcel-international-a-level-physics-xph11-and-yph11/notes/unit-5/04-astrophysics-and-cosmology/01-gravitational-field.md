## Gravitational Field

* **The Gravitational Field** is a type of force field. This means that it is a region whereby

  a mass experiences a force
* Gravitational force is always attractive.
* The force that a body will feel is the strength of the gravitational field (g) multiplied by the amount of mass (m), as given by the equation:
  * $F = mg$
  * where $F$ is the force that a body will experience
  * $m$ is the mass
  * $g$ is the gravitational field strength

We have learnt the formula of Newton’s Second Law $F = ma$ previously in AS Level. We can equate these two force equations to give:

* $F = mg = ma$
* Thus $a = \frac{mg}{m}$
* So $a = g$


* For this reason, the acceleration due to gravity has the same value of gravitational field strength, which is 9.81
