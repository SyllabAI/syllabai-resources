## Stefan-Boltzmann Law

* **Luminosity** describes the total output power of a star, unit W. For example, the luminosity of the Sun is L = 3.90 x 1026 W.
* Luminosity is a more accurate way of determining the brightness of a star because it is impossible for us to determine how bright a star is by using our naked eyes
* **Luminosity depends on two factors:**
  * Surface Area of the object, $A$
  * Surface temperature in Kelvin, $T$
* Therefore, the formula for luminosity is $L = \sigma AT^4$ for all black body radiators
* The **Stefan-Boltzmann Law** states that the luminosity is proportional to its surface area and the fourth power of its temperature in Kelvin
* $σ$ is the **Stefan-Boltzman constant**, which is 5.67 x $10^{-8} Wm^{-2} K^{-4}$
* If the object is a sphere, $A = 4\pi r^2$
