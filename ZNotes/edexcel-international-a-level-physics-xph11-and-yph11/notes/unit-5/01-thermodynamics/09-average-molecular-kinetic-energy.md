## Average Molecular Kinetic Energy

* The equation linking pressure and mean square speed of the molecules:

$$

pV = \frac{1}{3}Nm{c\\_{rms}}^2

$$$

**The ideal gas equation in terms of number of molecules:**

$$

pV = NkT

$$

$$

NkT = \frac{1}{3}Nm{c\\_{rms}}^2

$$

$$

m(crms)^2 = 3kT

$$

* Kinetic energy = $\frac{1}{2}mv^2$

* Instead of $v^2$ for the velocity of one particle, $(crms)^2$ is the average speed of all molecules

* Multiplying both sides of the equation by $\frac{1}{2}$ obtains the **average molecular kinetic energy** of the molecules of an ideal gas:

$$

Ek = \frac{1}{2}m(c)^2 = \frac{3}{2}kT

$$

* $E\\_k$ = kinetic energy of a molecule (J)
* $m$ = mass of one molecule (kg)
* $(crms)^2$ = mean square speed of a molecule ($m^2 s^{-2}$)
* $k$ = Boltzmann constant
* $T$= temperature of the gas (K)
