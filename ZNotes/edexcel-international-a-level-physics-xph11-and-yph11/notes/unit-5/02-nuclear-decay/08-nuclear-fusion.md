## Nuclear Fusion

* Nuclear fusion is the process whereby small nuclei join together to form large nuclei.
* If we take some light nuclei and force them to join together, the mass of the new, heavier nucleus will be less than the mass of the constituent parts, as some mass is converted into energy.
* However, not all of this energy is used as binding energy for the new larger nucleus so that energy will be released from this reaction. The binding energy per nucleon afterwards is higher than at the start. This is the process of nuclear fusion and is what provides the energy to make stars shine.
* This is the process of nuclear fusion, which you MUST know for your exam:

### **Some Limitations of Nuclear Fusion**

* Very high temperatures are needed
  * To overcome electrostatic repulsion
  * Nuclei come close enough to fuse
* Very high densities are needed
  * This gives a sufficient collision rate
* (Very high) temperatures lead to confinement problems
* Contact with the container causes the temperature to fall (and fusion to cease)
