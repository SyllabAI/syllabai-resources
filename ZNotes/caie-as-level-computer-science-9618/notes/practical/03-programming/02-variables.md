* **Declaring a variable:**
  * Pseudocode: ‘’’DECLARE <identifier> : <data type> ‘’’
  * Python: no need to declare however must write above as a comment
    (‘’’python #...‘’’)
* **Assigning variables:**

> ‘’’python <identifier> ← <value>‘’’ or ‘’’python <expression>‘’’
