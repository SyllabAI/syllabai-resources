## Selections




* “IF” Statement
  * Pseudocode: `IF…THEN…ELSE…ENDIF`
  * Python: `if (expression): (statements) else: (statements)`
* “CASE” Statement
  * Pseudocode: `CASE OF variable:  … … … OTHERWISE: … ENDCASE`
  * Python: `if (expression): (statement) elif (expression): statement) … else: (statement)`
