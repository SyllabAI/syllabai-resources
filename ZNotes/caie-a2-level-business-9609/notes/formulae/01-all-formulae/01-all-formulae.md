# Formulae for A2 Business Theory

### Absenteeism (%)

Absenteeism = (number of days absent / total workdays) x 100

### Accounting Rate of Return (ARR)

ARR = (average annual profit / average investment) x 100

### Acid Test Ratio

Acid Test Ratio = liquid assets / current liabilities

### Annual Depreciation

Annual Depreciation = (original cost - residual value) / useful life

### Average Inventory

Average Inventory = (inventory start + inventory end) / 2

### Average Investment

Average Investment = (initial capital cost + residual capital value) / 2

### Cost of Sales

Cost of Sales = (opening inventories + purchases) - closing inventories

### Current Ratio

Current Ratio = current assets / current liabilities

### Dividend Cover Ratio (x)

Dividend Cover Ratio = profit for year / annual dividends

### Dividend per Share

Dividend Per Share = total annual dividends / total issued shares

### Dividend Yield Ratio (%)

Dividend Yield Ratio = (dividend per share / market share price) x 100

### Earnings Per Share

(EPS) Earnings Per Share = profit for year / number of shares issued

### Free Float

Free Float = estimated next activity - duration - estimated this activity

### Gearing Ratio (%)

Gearing Ratio = (non current liabilities / capital employed) x 100

### Gross Profit

Gross Profit = revenue - cost of sales

### Gross Profit Margin Ratio (%)

Gross Profit Margin Ratio = (gross profit / revenue) x 100

### Income Elasticity of Demand (YED)

Income Elasticity of Demand = percentage change in demand / percentage change in income

### Liquid Assets

Liquid Assets = current assets - inventories

### Market Share (%)

Market Share = (sales of business / total market sales) x 100

### Net Book Value

Net Book Value = original cost - accumulated depreciation

### Net Realisable Value

Net Realisable Value = existing inventory value - cost of selling

### Operating Profit Margin Ratio (%)

Operating Profit Margin Ratio = (operating profit / revenue) x 100

### Payback Period - Additional Months

Additional Months to Payback = (additional net cash flow needed / annual cash flow) x 12

### Price/Earnings (P/E) Ratio

Price Earnings Ratio = market share price / earnings per share

### Price Elasticity of Demand (PED)

Price Elasticity of Demand = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price

### Promotional Elasticity of Demand (PED)

Promotional Elasticity of Demand = % change in demand for a product / % change in promotional spending

### Rate of Inventory Turnover

Rate of Inventory Turnover = cost of sales / average inventory

### Reserve

Reserve = Shareholders' equity - equity capital

### Return on Capital Employed Ratio (ROCE)

Return on Capital Employed Ratio (ROCE) = (operating profit / capital employed) x 100

### Total Float

Total Float = Latest Finishing time (LFT) - Duration - Earliest Starting Time (EST)

### Total Revenue

Total Revenue = selling price x quantity sold

### Trade Payable Turnover (Days)

Trade Payable Turnover = (Trade payables / credit purchases) x 365

### Working Capital

Working Capital = Current assets - Current Liabilities.
