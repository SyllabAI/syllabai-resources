# Financial statements

## Introduction

* The finance department prepares financial statements at every year-end
* For limited companies, these go into the annual report sent to all shareholders

### **Why keep financial (accounting) records**

* Keeping financial records of transactions is a must
* It is useful to ask: **Why is it necessary to keep accounting records?**
* The table below shows questions that cannot be answered without accurate, detailed records of all financial transactions


|**Question**|**Why this is important?**|
|:---|:---|
|**Did we make a profit or loss last year?**|Profit or loss measures business performance and is essential for managers, shareholders, and government (for taxes)|
|**How much do we owe?**|Timely payment to suppliers is necessary or they may refuse to send further deliveries|
|**How much do customers owe us?**|Cashflow crisis may arise from late payments by customers|
|**Are we able to repay bank loans?**|Loan repayment on time is essential. Failure can make further loan finance difficult to obtain|
|**Did we pay wages to workers last week?**|Delayed or unrecorded worker payments can damage manager-worker relations|
|**Are we able to finance a dividend payment to shareholders?**|Shareholders may sell shares if dividends are not paid due to financial uncertainty|
