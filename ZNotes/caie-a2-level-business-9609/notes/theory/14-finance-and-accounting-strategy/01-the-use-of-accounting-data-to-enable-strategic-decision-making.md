# Finance and Accounting Strategy

## The use of Accounting data to enable Strategic decision making

### **The use of financial statements in developing strategies**

* **A business cannot decide on a new strategy without:**
  * Analysis of current profitability and financial performance of the business.
  * Assessing availability of finance sources.
  * Comparing the success of current strategies with similar businesses.

### **The contents of an annual report**

* **Financial statements**
* **Chairman's statement:** Summarises major achievements, future prospects, and impacts of the political and economic environment.
* **Chief executive’s report:** Analyses the last financial year by area or product divisions, highlighting major projects, mergers, takeovers, closures, or rationalisations.
* **Auditors’ report:** Provides an independent review of account accuracy and methods, confirming a 'true and fair view' unless concerns are raised.
* **Notes to the accounts:** Detail fixed assets, long-term loans, depreciation methods, and other specifics beyond the main accounts.

### **Usefulness of an annual report**


|**Stakeholders**|**Information in annual reports is used to…**|
|:---|:---|
|**Managers**|Measure performance against targets, past results, and competitors. Support strategic decisions. Control and monitor departments and divisions. Set and review budgets against actual performance.|
|**Banks**|Decide on lending money, increasing overdraft facilities, or continuing an overdraft.|
|**Suppliers**|Assess liquidity, credit risk, and the need for early debt repayment.|
|**Customers**|Evaluate business security, future supply assurance, and access to spare parts and services.|
|**Government, tax authorities**|Calculate tax owed, assess potential for expansion or closure, and ensure legal compliance with accounting regulations.|
|**Shareholders, potential investors**|Assess business value and investment, profitability trends, shareholder profits, growth potential, and compare performance with competitors before deciding to buy or sell shares.|
|**Employees**|Assess the business's ability to pay wages, potential for expansion or reduction, job security, affordability of wage increases, and compare average wages with director salaries.|
|**Local community**|Assess profitability, potential expansion benefits for the local economy, and whether losses could lead to closure.|
