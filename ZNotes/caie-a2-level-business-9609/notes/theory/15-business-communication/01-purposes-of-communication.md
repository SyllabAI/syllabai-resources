# Business Communication

## Purposes of Communication

### **Effective communication**

* The exchange of information and instructions between groups or people, with feedback.
* Key features –
  * Sender
  * Clear message
  * Appropriate medium
  * Receiver
  * Feedback
* Internal communication is between different people or groups within the organization
* External communication is when the communication occurs with outside parties – government, customers, and suppliers

### **Why is effective communication important?**

* Higher staff motivation, labour productivity

* Improve in the number and quality of ideas generated by the staff

* Quicker decision making

* Quicker response to market changes

* Reduced risks of errors

* Effective coordination between departments

### **Adequate External Communication is important because:**

* **Customers:** The employee can share updates on new products to customers, as well as safety info, special offers, and ask for feedback.

* **Suppliers:** Business needs to properly discuss orders, deliveries, discounts, and fix any mistakes.

* **Shareholders:** Firm is required to provide updates on meetings, payments, new directors, and financial reports, only if they are an incorporated business.

### **Adequate Internal Communication is important because:**

* **Employees:** To discuss their employment contract, training, pay raises, performance reviews, new schedules, and solving workplace issues together.

* **Managers:** It makes it easier for the organization to coordinate tasks, share reports, pass on decisions, and allow managers to share their ideas.
