## Control, Authority and Trust

### **Authority and Responsibility:**

* Authority refers to the power to give orders and make decisions.

* Delegation grants authority to employees for tasks and decisions.

* Responsibility remains with the manager, who is accountable for outcomes.

### **Managers' key responsibilities:**

* Selecting employees for tasks.

* Allocating necessary resources.

* Arranging training.

* If poor performance occurs, the manager is responsible for these factors.

### **Employees remain accountable through:**

* Setting and agreeing on targets.

* Regular appraisals.

* Monitoring performance against targets.

**Control and Trust in Delegation:**

* Control is to measure and supervise an employee's performance.

* Delegation requires trust, reducing direct control over employees.

* Some managers resist delegation due to fear of losing importance or taking risks.

* Effective delegation gradually reduces control, increasing employee motivation.

* Empowerment goes beyond delegation, allowing employees to choose how to complete tasks.

* Greater empowerment boosts creativity but requires even more trust from managers.
