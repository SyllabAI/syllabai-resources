## Line & Staff

### **Line & Staff Management**

**Line Managers:** managers who have direct authority over people, decisions and resources within the hierarchy of an organization. Line managers carry out core activities of a firm.

**Staff Managers:** managers who, as specialists, provide support, information and assistance to senior line managers. They do not have direct authority over others.

### **Potential Conflicts**

* Staff managers are highly paid and may switch jobs frequently.

* Line managers may resent staff managers as they are considered “experts” and might think they are interfering in their work.

* Staff managers may have direct access to directors, causing jealousy to line manager.
