## Centralization & Decentralization

**Centralization:** keeping all the important decision-making powers within head office or the centre of the organization.

**Functions:**

* Minimal delegation; major decisions made by senior managers at head office.

* It ensures uniformity, consistency, and strong control over operations.

* It helps maintain a consistent brand image and reduce costs.

**Decentralization:** decision-making powers are passed down the hierarchy to empower subordinates and area or product managers.

**Functions:**

* Delegates decision-making to lower-level managers.

* It empowers employees, showing trust in them.

* It allows adaptation to local markets (e.g., multinationals adjusting products to regional preferences).

  It enables faster decision-making closer to market needs.
