## Emotional Intelligence (El)

The ability to understand one's own emotions, and those of others, to achieve better business performance.

### **Key Features of EI:**

* Self-awareness: Understanding your own emotions, goals, and behavior.

* Empathy: Recognizing and understanding others’ feelings.

**Why EI is essential for great leadership:**

* It helps leaders connect better with people.

* It improves decision-making and team management.

* Higher emotional quotient (EQ) is linked to better business performance.

* Leaders with high EI can be more effective than those with just high IQ.

**Emotional Quotient (EQ):** the level of a person's emotional intelligence as recorded in a standardized test.

### **Goleman’s 4 main competencies for development of Emotional Intelligence:**

* **Self-Awareness:** Understanding one's emotions, abilities, and self-confidence.

* **Self-Management:** Managing stress, staying trustworthy, showing initiative, and maintaining self-control.

* **Social Awareness:** Recognizing others' emotions, considering their views, and interacting well with diverse people.

* **Social Skills:** Handling emotions in relationships, understanding social dynamics, and using skills to persuade, negotiate, and lead.

### **Challenges of Managers with Low EI:**

* Taking tasks beyond their abilities: Lack confidence to succeed.

* Struggle with stress: Become unapproachable and untrustworthy.

* Autocratic: Make decisions without considering different perspectives.

* Poor social skills: Face difficulties in communication, negotiation, and team-building.

**The ultimate impact of such managers with low EI is low motivation, poor performance, and weak leadership.**
