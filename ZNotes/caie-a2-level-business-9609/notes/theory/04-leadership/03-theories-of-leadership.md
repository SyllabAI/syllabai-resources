## Theories of Leadership

### **Great Person Theory**

* Leadership is a natural ability that can not be taught.

* Some people are born with traits like confidence, intelligence, charisma, and strong communication skills that make them good leaders.

* Leaders are seen as smart, heroic, and naturally ready to take charge when needed.

---

### **Trait Theory**

* It believes people are born with leadership traits.

* Some people naturally have the right personality and skills to become leaders.

* Strong supporters of this theory think leadership traits cannot be

  learned, they are part of a person’s nature.

**Common Leadership Traits:**

* Taking initiative

* Determination and high energy

* Good judgment and decision-making skills

* Flexibility and adaptability

* Creativity and innovative thinking

* Charisma and strong personal presence

* Emotional intelligence and understanding others

* Motivation and drive to succeed

* Confidence, honesty, and integrity

**How can this theory help?**

This theory is often used to predict if someone will be a good leader.

Potential leaders are compared to successful ones to see if they have the same traits.

**Modern society view on Trait Theory:**

* Some leadership traits can be natural, while others can be learned.

* Skills like flexibility and decision-making can be developed over time.

* Learning and improving these traits can enhance leadership potential.

---

### **Behavioral Theory**

* It believes leadership skills can be learned, not just inherited.

* It focuses on studying leaders’ actions rather than their inborn traits.

* Leadership effectiveness is measured by observing behaviors and their impact on success.

* A leader’s behavior directly affects business performance.

* The best leaders adapt their style to fit different situations.

**There are three sets of skills for leadership:**

* Technical skills – Understanding business processes and techniques.

* Human skills – Interacting effectively with people.

* Conceptual skills – Developing new ideas and solving problems.

**Effective Leadership Behavior:**

* Setting clear goals.

* Motivating employees to achieve objectives.

* Communicating effectively.

* Building strong relationships and teamwork.

---

### **Contingency Theory:**

* Leaders must adapt their leadership style to different situations.

* A style that worked before may not be effective in a new situation.

* Some leaders struggle to change their approach, which can lead to business failure.

**Factors Influencing Leadership Approach:**

* Level of experience and maturity of subordinates: Skilled and mature employees require a more relaxed leadership style.

* Leader-Follower Relationship: A positive relationship makes teamwork and collaboration easier.

* Time constraint for task completion: Tight deadlines may require a more directive leadership approach.

* Leader’s Authority Level: Higher authority can motivate employees to meet expectations.

---

### **Power and Influence Theories**

* Leadership depends on how leaders use power and influence to guide subordinates.

* There are two types of theory which focuses on Power and Influence:

### **French & Raven’s Theory**

### **It identifies two sources of personal power:**

* Expert Knowledge: Leaders with strong expertise gain more influence.

* Charm of The Leader: Leaders with strong appeal attract willing followers.

* Additionally, A leader’s formal position in a company also gives them authority over employees.

### **Transactional Leadership**

* Employees work in exchange for rewards (e.g., salary, bonuses).

* Leaders give clear instructions and manage through rewards & punishments.

* Successful task completion leads to rewards, while failure results in penalties.

* Used when deadlines are tight and meeting objectives is critical.

* Most leaders use this approach when necessary, even if they prefer other styles.

---

### **Transformational Leadership**

* It focuses on leading rather than just managing workers.

* Most effective during times of significant change in a business.

* It aims to transform the company culture for better performance.

* Transformational Leadership puts emphasis on employee satisfaction, motivation, and productivity.

**Key Features:**

* Charisma: It influences and inspires subordinates.

* Vision: Leaders motivates employees to achieve long-term goals.

* Stimulation: It creates a dynamic work environment with new challenges.

* Personal Support: An understanding leader who ensures to meet individual employee needs.

### **Summary of Leadership Theories:**

* Leaders should explore various leadership theories to shape their own approach.

* Leaders should set a positive example through integrity, effective communication, and emotional understanding.

* They Combine emotional intelligence with the ability to make informed decisions.

* They must be flexible/adaptable and adjust leadership style to different situations.

**By applying these behaviors, any leader can become great.**
