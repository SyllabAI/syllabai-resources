## **What is a Informal Leader?**

A person who has no formal authority but has the respect of colleagues due to experience, personality or expertise and has greater support than formal leaders.

### **Consequences of Different Reaction of Management to Informal Leaders:**

* **Appointing Them as Leaders**: It can be effective. However, the informal leader risks losing worker support if seen as siding with management.

* **Ignoring Them:** It can lead to disruptions or industrial action due to their strong employee backing.

* **Working with Informal Leaders**: It might help the firm because the informal leader’s influence can ensure that employees’ work aligns with business goals better. It fosters cooperation between management and staff.

* **Avoiding Isolation**: Managers should not diminish their influence by reassigning them or cutting them off from colleagues.
