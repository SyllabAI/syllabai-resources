## Management By Objectives (MBO)

Establishing a management information system to compare actual performance and achievements against pre-set objectives for each department or employee.

**Key Aspects of MBO:**

* Involves discussion and agreement between managers and employees.

* Encourages job enrichment and delegation of authority.

* Supportive of Theory Y approach.

* Believes that Theory X approach reduces motivation.

**How are targets set?**

* Individual performance targets set during annual appraisals.

* Targets should be agreed upon, not imposed.

### **The Hierarchy of Objectives and Objectives in Action:**

* **Aim:** To increase market share of the company.

* **Corporate Objectives:** Increase revenue by 15% of all regions per year.

* **Divisional Objectives:** In a region, to increase sales by 5% and raise market presence.

* **Departmental Objectives:** For R&D department, it can be to create a product with lower cost of production method with same quality.

* **Individual Targets:** For HR department, it can be to reduce labor turnover by 10% and conducting a certain set of training per year.
