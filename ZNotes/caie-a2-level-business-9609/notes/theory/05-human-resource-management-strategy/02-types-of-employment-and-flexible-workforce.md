## Types of Employment and Flexible Workforce

### **Flexible Workforces**

A workforce with a higher proportion of temporary or part-time workers than full-time employees. Companies increasingly hire part-time, temporary, home-based, and variable-hour workers for flexibility and cost efficiency.

### **Types of Flexible Workforces:**

**Temporary Workers**

* Hired for a fixed period, often during peak seasons.

* It helps businesses stay competitive by adjusting staff levels to sales.

* Example: Tourism industry, theme park employees.

**Part-Time Workers**

* Work fewer hours than full-time employees (e.g., 20 hours per week).

* It allows businesses to manage labor costs effectively.
