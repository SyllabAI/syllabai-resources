## Uses of IT and AI in HRM

**Common Role of IT in HRM:**

* **Employee Records Management:** Stored in databases for easy access.

* **Work Schedule Tracking:** Planning software for flexible work arrangements.

* **Holiday Planning:** Software for employees to select preferred leave dates.

### **Evaluation of Increased IT Use in HRM**

* Frees up HRM time for strategic tasks, boosting efficiency.

* Less personal contact may make HR managers seem distant as it decreases human interaction.

* IT-based methods reduce discussions/two-way communication unless conferencing tools are used.

* Constant monitoring may create a sense of being watched.
