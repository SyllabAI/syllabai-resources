# Human Resource Management (HRM) Strategy

## Soft and Hard HRM

A human resource strategy is a medium to long-term plan aimed at achieving the business's HR objectives. It is an essential part of the company's overall HR management.

**Hard HR Strategy:**
Hard HRM follows a strict approach to managing human resources. It can be considered that this strategy focuses on cutting cost.

**Key features:**

* Treating personnel as resources to be used as efficiently as possible.

* Offering the minimum possible pay.

* Hiring employees at the lowest cost.

* Controlling workers and making them redundant when necessary.

* Often associated with autocratic leadership.

* Negative impact on motivation of employees.

**Soft HR Strategy:**
Soft HRM adopts a more considerate and supportive approach to managing human resources. It tries to ensure they focus on their development and reach self-actualization, be motivated and stay loyal to the business.

**Key Features:**

* Views employees as valuable assets to the business.

* Prioritizes employee growth and development to boost long-term success.

* Typically associated with democratic or laissez-faire leadership styles.

* It encourages higher motivation and a strong sense of community within the organization.
