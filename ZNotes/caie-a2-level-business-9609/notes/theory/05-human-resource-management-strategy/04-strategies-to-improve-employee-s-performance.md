## What happens due to subpar employee’s performance?

* Damages business prospects.

* It leads to lower efficiency and customer dissatisfaction.

* If an employee has shown improvements in any aspect, it should be acknowledged and praised.

### What are the causes that lead to such occurrences?

* Low motivation

* Family issues

* Poor management

* Inadequate resources

**Strategies To Improve Employee's Performance:**

To improve employee’s performance, managers need to identify and analyze reasons for bad performance of the business, which can be:

* Low productivity

* Falling behind competitors.

* Unexplained absenteeism.

* Increased customer complaints.

**What Are Hard HRM Strategies?**

* It is giving strict warnings and discipline for poor performance.

* Moreover, giving Flexible contracts to employees for easier dismissal.

* It can cause a fall in short-term costs.

* However, it is probable that it will reduce motivation and worsen performance.

**What Are Soft HRM Strategies:**

* Regular performance appraisals with two-way discussions.

* Giving additional training to improve skills and efficiency.

* Doing quality circles, which are small groups identifying and solving problems.

* Autonomous workgroups, where there are multi-skilled teams managing work independently.

* Giving financial incentives such as profit-sharing or employee ownership schemes.

* Giving clear goals and tracking performance.

* It can lead to higher motivation, reduced absenteeism and better quality output.

* However, it would require training and appraisal, which would increase cost.
