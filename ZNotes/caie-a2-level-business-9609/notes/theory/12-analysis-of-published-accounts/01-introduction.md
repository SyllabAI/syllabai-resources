# Analysis of Published Accounts
## Introduction
* Published accounts reveal some details into a company’s performance, such as yearly changes in profits, revenue, current assets, liabilities, and shareholders’ equity. Comparisons can also be made between companies.
* However, individual accounting figures alone provide limited insight into performance or efficiency.
* Accountants use ratios - comparing two figures - to create meaningful assessments.
* These ratios fall into five main categories, starting with liquidity ratios.
