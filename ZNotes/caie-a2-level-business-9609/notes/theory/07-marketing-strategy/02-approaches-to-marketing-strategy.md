## Approaches to marketing strategy

* Marketing managers should consider some key issues when developing an effective strategy.

### **Consistency**

* **A marketing strategy must align in quality, quantity, and messaging. Specifically, it should be consistent with:**

  * **The business**: Reflect the brand's identity and avoid conflicting with customers’ perceptions.

  * **The product**: Support the product's nature and intended image through suitable promotional methods.

  * **The market**: Tailor to the target market, whether industrial vs. consumer or niche vs. mass market.

### **Coordination**

* **A marketing strategy must be fully coordinated, ensuring all activities deliver a consistent message. Key examples include:**

  * Aligning the marketing mix with objectives.

  * Coordinating promotion tactics with the budget.

  * Ensuring the 4Ps are aligned with each other.

### **The changing role of IT and AI in marketing**

* Marketing has fundamentally changed due to IT, with AI accelerating decision-making in numerous ways.

### IT applications in marketing

* **Widely used IT applications in marketing include:**

  * **Internet**: Websites are essential for product promotion, sharing details, and selling globally. Online services and media drive traffic.

  * **Email**: Opt-in email lists allow businesses to engage customers with product announcements, discounts, and updates.

  * **Mobile**: Mobile marketing reaches customers via text messages and apps, offering deals and promotions.

  * **In-store**: Digital signage and advanced point-of-sale systems help businesses market products and track inventory.

  * **Social Media**: A powerful tool for product marketing, allowing businesses to engage customers, share updates, and encourage reviews.

### **Potential AI applications in marketing**

* **AI's ability to analyse vast data is revolutionising marketing. Here are four ways AI aids marketing managers:**

  * **Consumer insights**: AI tracks consumer behavior across social media, helping adjust messages or offers to meet preferences.

  * **Digital advertising**: AI optimizes campaigns by analyzing consumer searches, profiles, and online data for targeted promotions.

  * **Personalized messaging**: AI creates detailed consumer profiles to deliver tailored messages at the right time and through the right medium.

  * **Real-time interaction**: AI identifies trends and enables brand marketing to engage consumers at the moment of decision-making, influencing purchases.

* **Limitations of AI in marketing include:**

  * Consumer resistance to data collection.

  * Ongoing need for management supervision.

  * High investment in data, IT, and computing.

  * Lack of human creativity in AI systems.
