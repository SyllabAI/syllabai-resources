# Operations Strategy

## Operational Decisions

* Expanding or reducing capacity

* Locating a business or relocating it

* Offshoring or reshoring

* Outsourcing

* Changing operations (production) methods

* Application of IT and AI

### The influence of human, marketing and finance resource availability on operational decisions

* Strategic operations decisions require assessment of human, marketing, and financial resources.

* If resources are insufficient, decisions may need to be revised or abandoned.

### **Role of IT & AI in Operations Management**

* IT has significantly expanded for service providers and product manufacturers.

* It enhances efficiency, automation, and decision-making.

* Examples: CAD (Computer Aided Design) & CAM (Computer Aided Manufacturing).

### **AI Applications in Business Operations:**

* **Automation & Productivity:**

  * AI automates repetitive tasks, increasing efficiency.

  * AI-powered customer service can answer calls 24/7 and resolve 90% of issues on the first call.

* **Data Processing & Decision-Making:**

  * AI reduces search time and processes large amounts of data.

  * It enhances decision-making and boosts productivity.

* **Employee Efficiency:**

  * AI frees up employees for higher-level, creative tasks.

  * Improves operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

* **Banking:**

  * Faster processing of loan requests.

* **Online Payments:**

  * AI detects and prevents fraud.

* **Legal Sector:**

  * AI identifies relevant past court cases.

* **Manufacturing:**

  * AI schedules maintenance for optimal efficiency.

* **Pharmaceuticals:**

  * AI predicts the success of drug development.

* **Cybersecurity:**

  * AI monitors behavior, detects threats, and responds to cyber risks.

### **Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM):**

The use of computer software to control machine tools and related equipment in the manufacturing of components or complete products.

* CAM automates factory production using robotic tools (e.g., lathes, milling, welding machines).

* Computers control each step, ensuring precision and consistency beyond human-operated machinery.
