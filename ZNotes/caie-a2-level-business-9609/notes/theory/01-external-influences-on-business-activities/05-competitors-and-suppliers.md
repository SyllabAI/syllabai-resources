## Competitors and suppliers

### Impact of Competitors and Suppliers

* The more competitors and their market share, the less market power individual businesses have, leading to price decisions being influenced by competitors unless product differentiation is achieved

* Even with fewer competitors, if it is easy for new businesses to join an industry, an individual business's market power will be low

* Fewer suppliers reduce a business's ability to influence prices, while many competing suppliers allow businesses to push prices down and demand better credit terms
