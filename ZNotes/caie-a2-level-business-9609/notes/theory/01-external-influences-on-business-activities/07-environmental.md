## Environmental

### **Environmental factors affecting business activity**

* ### Business activities can greatly impact the environment

* Air and noise pollution, road congestion, expansion into rural areas, greenhouse gas emissions, and depletion of non-renewable resources are all important environmental issues

* Global concern about these issues is growing

### **How environmental issues impacts business behaviour**

Some analysts believe there is a strong business case for corporate social responsibility (CSR) due to environmental impacts of business activity. Evidence shows that consumers increasingly support businesses with green policies and boycott those harming the environment.

### **Environmental audits**

* An audit is an independent check, commonly associated with company accounts which have to be verified.

* Recently, some businesses have used audits to assess environmental performance, despite challenges in measuring these factors financially and no legal requirement to include them in published accounts.

* However, stakeholders are calling for environmental audits to become mandatory, allowing comparisons of businesses' environmental impacts.

* These audits are voluntary, and companies with strong environmental records tend to publish them, while those with poor records avoid doing so unless audits become compulsory.

### **How stakeholders may use environmental audits**

* **Businesses** use environmental audits to report on pollution, waste, energy, transport use, and recycling, comparing results with past data, targets, and similar businesses.

* **Managers** set sustainability targets and report progress in the next audit.

* **Consumer groups** may use audits to influence buying behavior, leading to increased sales and positive media coverage.

* **Investors**, particularly ethical ones, use audits to guide investment or lending decisions.

* **Employees** take pride in companies with strong environmental records, encouraging teamwork and attracting qualified applicants who may want to join a company with a good reputation and team spirit.

### **Evaluation of environmental audits**

* Some observers will not take environmental audits seriously until they are compulsory with clear standards and verification

* Companies have been accused of using audits as a publicity stunt or as a smokescreen to hide possibly damaging practices

* Audits can be time-consuming and costly, limiting their value for small businesses or those with limited finance

### **Paying incentives for the award of contracts**

* **paying incentives for the award of contracts** can be illegal, depending on the context and jurisdiction.

* Here are some ways businesses are granted contracts through payment of incentives:

  * **Bribery & Corruption Risks** – Paying incentives for contract awards can lead to unethical or illegal business practices.

  * **Unfair Competition** – Such incentives may create an uneven playing field, where contracts go to those who offer the highest bribe rather than the most qualified bidder.

  * **Corporate Governance Issues** – This practice undermines transparency and accountability in business dealings.

  * **Legal Consequences** – Many jurisdictions have strict laws against bribery, such as the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) in the U.S. and the UK Bribery Act.

### **Ways Governments Constrain Business Activities & Why**

* **Regulations & Laws:** Ensure ethical practices (e.g., labour, environment, consumer rights).

* **Taxation:** Discourage harmful activities and fund public services.

* **Price Controls:** Prevent overpricing ( price ceilings/maximum pricing) and ensure fair wages (price floors/minimum pricing).

* **Competition Laws:** Prevent monopolies and promote fair competition.

* **Trade Restrictions:** Tariffs, quotas to protect local industries.

* **Subsidy Control:** Avoid market distortions and ensure fair competition.

* **Licensing & Permits:** Maintain quality and safety standards.

* **Environmental Policies:** Reduce pollution, promote sustainability.

### **Sustainability and business decisions**

* Taking environmentally friendly decisions shows a company's commitment to sustainability

* Sustainable business decisions help maintain current living standards globally. Protecting the environment ensures future generations can enjoy a similar quality of life

* Pressure groups, government laws, and green consumerism are pushing businesses to make more sustainable decisions
