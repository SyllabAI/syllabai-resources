## Technological

### Technological Influences on Business Activities

* Technology, in its simplest form, refers to the use of tools, machines, and science in industry.
* Recent developments in technology have transformed how most businesses operate, including:
  * How products demanded by consumers
  * How products are produced
  * How businesses communicate
  * How businesses gather, store, and use information.

### **The Effect of Technological Change on Businesses**

* **Opportunities**

  * New products

  * New processes

  * Lower costs

  * Better communication

  * Increased information

* **New technology also results in possible threats to business:**

  * **Costs:** Capital costs can be substantial, and recurring labour training expenses are necessary with ongoing technological advancements. Redundancy costs may arise when employees are replaced by technology

  * **Workforce relations:** These can be threatened if technological changes are not explained properly and are justified to workers. Job losses can reduce job security for remaining employees, lowering motivation. Trade unions may oppose changes if they threaten too many jobs

  * **Reliability:** Breakdowns in automated systems can halt production. New systems may have teething problems, and efficiency gains may take longer than expected to achieve

  * **Data protection:** National laws regulate the right to hold staff and customer data, so businesses must stay updated on legal constraints for IT use

  * **Management:** Some managers do not support new technology. Recognizing and managing change requires good management skills.

  * **Competition** Rival companies may adopt technology more quickly, potentially making a business less competitive despite its investments.

### **Provision of data for decision-making**

* **Management information systems use IT to supply managers with extensive data on business operations. The benefits include:**

  * Managers can quickly access data from all departments and regional divisions, improving overall control

  * Computers can be used to quickly analyze and process data, enabling managers to interpret and make decisions faster

  * Management information systems speed up decision communication to those in the organisation that need to know

* **The drawbacks include:**

  * The ease of transferring data electronically can cause information overload, making it harder to identify key information and areas needing action

  * The power that information brings to central managers may reduce the authority of work teams and middle managers, leading to oppressive control and decreased job enrichment and motivation

### **Introducing Technology Effectively**

* Analyse – the use of IT 

* Involve – managers and other staff 

* Evaluate – different systems and programs (cost, efficiency, budget)

* Plan – introduction of new system, training 

* Monitor – introduction and effectiveness of the system
