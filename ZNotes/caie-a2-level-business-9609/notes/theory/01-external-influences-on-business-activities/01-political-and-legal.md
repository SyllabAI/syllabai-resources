### **Legal Constraints on Business Activity**

* Laws on employment practices, working conditions and wage levels

* Laws on marketing and consumer rights

* Laws on business competition

* Laws on the location of business

### **The Law and Employment Practices**

* Prevents exploitation of workers (minimum wage, working conditions)

* Controls the use of trade union action

* **Legal Constraints generally cover these areas of employment practices**

  * Recruitment, employment contracts and termination of employment

  * Health and safety at work

  * Minimum wages

### **Recruitment, Employment Contracts and Termination of Employment**

* **Legally protecting the rights of workers includes**

  * A written contract of employment must be signed by the employee and employer

  * Minimum age

  * Minimum wages, working conditions, and working hours being controlled

  * Holidays and pension entitlements

  * Ensuring no discrimination takes place

  * Protection against unfair dismissal

### **Health and Safety Laws**

* **These laws aim to protect workers from injury and discomfort at work and usually require businesses to**

  * Equip factories with safety equipment

  * Provide adequate facilities

  * Provide protection & training from dangerous machines

  * Give adequate breaks

### **The Law, Consumer Rights and Marketing Behaviour**

* **Reasons to protect consumers**

  * Influential advertising is making it difficult for consumers to make rational decisions

  * Products are becoming more scientific and technological, difficult for consumers to understand

  * More pressurized selling techniques

  * Increased globalization

  * Increased competition leading to businesses trying to exploit consumers

### **Laws for Consumer Protection**

* **Sale of Goods Act**

  * Goods should be fit to sell

  * Suitable for the purpose

  * Perform in the way described

* **Trade Descriptions Act**

  * No misleading descriptions or claims

* **Consumer Protection Act**

  * Firms providing dangerous or defective products are liable for the cost of any damage

  * Illegal to quote misleading prices

### **The Law and Business Competition**

* **Benefits of free and fair competition to consumers**

  * Wider choice

  * Lower prices

  * Improve the quality, design and performance of the product

  * International competition will help strengthen the domestic economy

* **Laws on competition**

  * Investigate and control monopolies

  * Limit uncompetitive practices

### **Evaluating the Impact of Consumer Protection Laws on Business**

* **Following these laws results in business costs rising due to**

  * Redesigning products to meet consumer health and safety laws

  * Redesigning ads to more accurately describe the product

  * Improving quality control

* **Following these laws requires a change of strategy and culture; however, the benefits are**

  * Reduces the chance of bad brand image through consumer injury from using a product

  * Reduces chances of legal action

  * Better brand image and loyalty

  * A better reputation resulting from dealing with consumers fairly and honestly
