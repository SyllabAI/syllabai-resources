## Marketing Strategy:

* **Marketing plan**: A detailed and researched report outlining marketing objectives and the strategy to achieve them.

* **Economic collaboration**: Countries working together to achieve common goals, such as promoting free international trade.

* **Free-trade agreements**: Agreements between countries or trading blocs to reduce or eliminate trade barriers like tariffs and quotas.

* **International marketing**: Selling products in markets outside the original domestic market.

* **BRICS**: Acronym for five rapidly developing economies with significant market opportunities: Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa.

* **Pan-global marketing**: Marketing a standardised product worldwide as if the entire world were a single market, using the same strategies everywhere.

* **Global localisation**: Adjusting the marketing mix, including product differentiation, to suit local and regional tastes and cultures while maintaining local market differences.
