## Human Resource Management Strategy:

* **Human resource management (HRM) strategy**: A long-term plan for managing an organisation's human resources.

* **Hard HRM**: An approach to managing employees focused on cutting costs.

* **Soft HRM**: An approach to managing employees focused on their development, self-fulfillment, and motivation.

* **Full-time employment contract**: A contract for a complete working week.

* **Permanent employment contract**: A contract where the worker is employed indefinitely unless dismissed, made redundant, or they decide to leave.

* **Temporary contract**: A contract offered for a fixed period, such as six months, with no obligation for renewal.

* **Part-time contract**: A contract where workers are only required to work a certain number of hours each week (less than full-time).

* **Zero-hours contract**: A contract with no guaranteed minimum hours per week, requiring availability when called by the employer.

* **Gig economy**: A labor market with temporary, flexible jobs, where workers are often independent contractors or freelancers.

* **Compressed working hours**: A full-time workweek completed in fewer days, such as four 10-hour workdays.

* **Shift work**: Work scheduled outside the traditional 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., including evening, night, early morning, or rotating shifts.

* **Absenteeism**: The rate of workforce absence as a proportion of the total number of employees.

* **Management by objectives (MBO)**: A system comparing actual performance and achievements against pre-set objectives for each department or employee.
