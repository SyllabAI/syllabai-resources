## Leadership:

* **Leadership**: The art of motivating a group of people to achieve a common objective.

* **Informal leader**: A person without formal authority but who has the respect of colleagues and some power over them.

* **Emotional intelligence (EI)**: The ability to understand and manage one's own emotions and those of others to improve business performance.

* **Emotional quotient (EQ)**: The measurement of a person's emotional intelligence as assessed through a standardized test.
