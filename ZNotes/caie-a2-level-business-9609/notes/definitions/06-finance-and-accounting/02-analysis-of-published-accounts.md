## Analysis of Published Accounts:

* **Current ratio**: A ratio comparing current assets with current liabilities.

* **Acid test ratio**: A ratio comparing liquid assets with current liabilities.

* **Profitability**: A measure of a business's ability to make a profit from sales or capital investment.

* **Gross profit margin ratio**: A ratio comparing gross profit (before overhead expenses) with revenue.

* **Operating profit margin**: A ratio comparing operating profit for the year with revenue.

* **Return on capital employed (ROCE) ratio**: A ratio comparing operating profit (profit from operations) with the capital employed in the business.

* **Capital employed**: The total value of long-term finance invested in a business.

* **Rate of inventory turnover**: The number of times inventory is bought and sold within a year.

* **Trade receivables turnover (days)**: The average time (in days) to receive payment from customers who bought on credit.

* **Trade payables turnover (days)**: The average time (in days) to pay suppliers for credit purchases.

* **Credit purchases**: The value of materials and supplies bought on credit (over one year).

* **Gearing ratio**: A ratio that measures the proportion of capital employed that is financed by long-term borrowing (non-current liabilities).

* **Share price**: The quoted price of one share on the stock exchange.

* **Dividend**: The share of company profits paid to shareholders.

* **Dividend yield ratio**: A ratio measuring the annual return from dividends as a percentage of the current market share price.

* **Dividend per share**: The financial amount received in dividends on one share.

* **Dividend cover ratio**: A ratio measuring how many times dividends could be paid from profit for the year.

* **Price/earnings ratio**: The number of years it would take, at the current earnings per share, to purchase one share at the current market price.

* **Earnings per share**: The amount of profit after tax and interest earned per share.
