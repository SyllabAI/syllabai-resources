## Corporate Planning and Implementation: 

* **Corporate plan**: A plan detailing a business's central objectives and the strategies to achieve them.

* **Corporate planning**: The process used by companies to set long-term plans to meet objectives such as growth and higher returns on capital.

* **Corporate culture**: The values, attitudes, and beliefs of an organisation’s people that influence internal and external interactions.

* **Power culture**: A culture concentrating power among a few individuals.

* **Role culture**: A culture where each staff member has a clearly defined job title and role.

* **Task culture**: A culture emphasizing cooperation and teamwork.

* **Person culture**: A culture giving individuals freedom to express themselves and make their own decisions.

* **Entrepreneurial culture**: A culture encouraging risk-taking, innovation, and testing new ventures.

* **Transformational leadership**: A leadership style where the leader works with teams to identify the need for change, create a vision, and implement change collaboratively.

* **Change management**: Planning, implementing, controlling, and reviewing the transition of an organisation from its current state to a new one.

* **Business process re-engineering**: Rethinking and redesigning business processes to achieve significant performance improvements.

* **Project champion**: An individual supporting a project, driving it forward by explaining its benefits, and assisting the team in implementing changes.

* **Project groups**: Teams formed within an organisation to solve problems requiring input from various specialists.

* **Continuity planning**: Preparing resources to ensure the business can continue operations after a major crisis.
