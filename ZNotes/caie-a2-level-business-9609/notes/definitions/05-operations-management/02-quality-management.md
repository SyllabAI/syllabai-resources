## Quality Management:

* **Quality product**: A good or service that meets customers' expectations and fulfils its intended purpose.

* **Quality standards**: Minimum acceptable production or service standards based on customer expectations.

* **Quality control**: Inspecting the product or a sample of products to check for defects.

* **Quality assurance**: Ensuring agreed quality standards are met at each production stage to satisfy customers.

* **ISO 9000**: An internationally recognised standard for quality assurance methods meeting specific criteria.

* **Total Quality Management (TQM)**: A quality approach involving all employees in continuous improvement.

* **Internal customers**: People within the organisation relying on the quality of others' work.

* **Zero defects**: A mindset focused on ensuring products consistently meet customer expectations.

* **Benchmarking**: Comparing a business's performance against the best companies in the same industry.

* **Benchmark performance indicators (BPI)**: Key areas of business performance measured and compared with competitors.
