# Operations Management

## Location and Scale:

* **Optimal location**: A business location that offers the best combination of quantitative and qualitative factors.

* **Quantitative factors**: Measurable issues in financial or numerical terms that managers consider in decision-making.

* **Qualitative factors**: Non-measurable issues that managers consider, such as employee satisfaction or brand image.

* **Offshoring**: Relocating a business process from one country to another.

* **Trade barriers**: Taxes (tariffs) or other restrictions on the free international movement of goods and services.

* **Reshoring**: Moving a previously offshored business operation back to its original country.

* **Scale of operations**: The maximum output achievable with the available resources.

* **Internal economies of scale**: Cost reductions per unit of production achieved as a business expands its operations.

* **Internal diseconomies of scale**: Increases in unit production costs that occur when a business grows too large.

* **Demerger**: Separating one business unit from another, often reversing a prior merger.

* **External economies of scale**: Cost reductions per unit that benefit a business as the industry grows in a region.

* **External diseconomies of scale**: Increases in unit costs resulting from industry expansion, particularly in one region.
