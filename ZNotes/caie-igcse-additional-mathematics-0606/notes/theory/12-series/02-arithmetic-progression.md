## Arithmetic Progression

* A sequence formed by adding the same value each time is called an **arithmetic sequence**.

* A common difference $d$ is added (or subtracted) $(n−1)$ times to the first term.

* The **general term** (the $n$-th term) is given by: $U_n = a + \left( n - 1 \right)d$

* Where $n$ is the number of the term, a ($U_1$) is the first term and $d$ is the common difference

Formula for the sum of the first $n$ terms between first term $a$ to last term $l$  or with first term $a$ and common difference $d$

$$
S_n = \frac{n}{2}\left( a + l  \right)
$$

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2} \left( 2a + (n - 1)d \right)
$$

**Example 1:** Find the $n$-th term of the arithmetic sequence given the sequence $3,7,11,15…$

* Step 1: Identify the first term $a$ and common difference $d$

$a=3$, $d=7-3=4$

* Step 2: Use the formula of $n$-th term of an arithmetic sequence

$U_n = a + (n - 1)d$

* Step 3: Substitute the values of $a$ and $d$:

$$
U_n = 3 + (r - 1) \times 4 = 3 + 4n - 4 = 4n - 1
$$

**Answer**: The $n$-th term is: $U_n = 4n-1$

**Example 2**: Find the sum of the first $n$ terms given the sequence $3,7,11,15…$

* Step 1: Substitute values of $a$ and $d$ into the Sum Formula:

$S_n = \frac{n}{2} \left( 2a + (n - 1)d \right)$

* Step 2: $a=3$ and $d=4$, so

$$
S_n = \frac{n}{2} \left[ 2 \times 3 + (n - 1) \times 4 \right]
$$

$$
= \frac{n}{2} \left[ 6 + 4n - 4 \right] = \frac{n}{2} (4n + 2)
$$

* Step 3: Simplify

$$
S_n = \frac{n}{2} \times (4n + 2) = n(2n + 1)
$$

$$
S_n = n(2n + 1)
$$
