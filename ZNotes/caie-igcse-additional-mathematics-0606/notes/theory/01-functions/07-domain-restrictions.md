## Domain Restrictions

1. Division: Denominator can’t be zero.

   Restriction to domain: Set denominator > 0.

2. Even roots (like √x): Can’t take square root of negative numbers (in real numbers).

   Restriction to domain: Set radicand ≥ 0.

3. Logarithms: You can’t take log of zero or negatives.

   Restriction to domain: Set argument > 0
