# Simultaneous Equations

## Substitution Method

### Steps -

1. Make one variable the subject in one equation (e.g $y=x+3$)

2. Substitute into the second equation

3. Solve for one variable

4. Plug back in to find the other

**Example**

Given the equations, solve for x and y

$y=x+3$ (first equation)

$x^2-3xy+y^2+19=0$ (second equation)

Substitute $y=x+3$ into the second equation

Therefore

1. $x^2-3(x)(x+3)+(x+3)^2+19=0$

2. $x^2-3x^2-9x+x^2+6x+9+19=0$

3. $-x^2-3x+28=0$

4. $x^2+3x-28=0$

5. $x^2+7x-4x-28=0$

6. $(x+7)(x-4)=0$

7. $x=-7,x=-4$
