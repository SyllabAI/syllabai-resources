## Tangents to a Circle

The gradient of the radius from the center $(a,b)$ to the point $(x_1,y_1)$ is:

$$
m_{\text{radius}} = \frac{y_1-b}{x_1-a}
$$

The gradient of the tangent at that point is the negative reciprocal of the radius gradient (because the tangent is perpendicular to the radius):

$$
m = -\frac{1}{\frac{y_1 - b}{x_1 - a}} = -\frac{x_1 - a}{y_1 - b}
$$

Equation of tangent at point $(x_1, y_1)$:

$$
y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)
$$
