# Circular Measure

## Formula

### **Radian measure:**

$$

\pi = 180º\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ 2\pi = 360º

$$

Degree to Rad  = multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$

Rad to Degree = multiply by $\frac{180}{\pi}$

### **Arc length:**

$$

s = r\theta

$$

### **Area of a sector:**

$$

A = \frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta

$$
