## Solving Absolute Value Inequalities

**1.Inequality of the form:**
$$
k\|ax + b\| > c \quad (c \geq 0)
$$

Divide both sides by $ k > 0 $:

$$
\|ax + b\| > \frac{c}{k}
$$

Solve:
$$
ax + b > \frac{c}{k}, \quad ax + b < -\frac{c}{k}
$$

**Example**: $2\|3x - 1\| > 4 , \|3x - 1\| > 2$

Solve both cases:

* $3x - 1 > 2 \Rightarrow x > 1$

* $3x - 1 < -2 \Rightarrow x < -\frac{1}{3}$

Final answer: $x < -\frac{1}{3}, \quad x > 1$

---

**2.Inequality of the form:**
$$
k\|ax + b\| \leq c \quad (c > 0)
$$

Divide both sides by $ k > 0 $:
$$
\|ax + b\| \leq \frac{c}{k}
$$

Which gives:
$$
-\frac{c}{k} \leq ax + b \leq \frac{c}{k}
$$

**Example**: $\|2x + 5\| \leq 3$

Solve:
$-3 \leq 2x + 5 \leq 3$

Subtract 5:
$-8 \leq 2x \leq -2$

Divide by 2:
$-4 \leq x \leq -1$

---

**3.Inequality of the form:**
$$
k\|ax + b\| \leq \|cx + d\| \quad (k > 0)
$$

Split into two inequalities:
$$
k\|ax + b\| \leq cx + d
$$
$$
k\|ax + b\| \leq -(cx + d)
$$

Example: $2\|3x - 1\| \leq \|x + 4\|$

Split into:

* $2\|3x - 1\| \leq x + 4$

* $2\|3x - 1\| \leq -(x + 4)$

---

**4.Inequality of the form:**
$$
\|ax + b\| \leq cx + d
$$

Split into two cases:
$$
ax + b \leq cx + d
$$
$$
-(ax + b) \leq cx + d
$$

Also include domain restriction: $cx + d \geq 0$

**Example**: $\|2x - 3\| \leq 4x + 1$

Split:

* $2x - 3 \leq 4x + 1$

* $-2x + 3 \leq 4x + 1$

Also: $4x + 1 \geq 0$

---

### **Quadratic absolute inequality:**

**Type 1:**

$$
\|ax^2 + bx + c\| > d
$$

Split into:
$$
ax^2 + bx + c > d
$$

$$
ax^2 + bx + c < -d
$$

**Example**: $\|x^2 - 4x + 1\| > 2$

Split:

* $x^2 - 4x + 1 > 2 \Rightarrow x^2 - 4x - 1 > 0$

* $x^2 - 4x + 1 < -2 \Rightarrow x^2 - 4x + 3 < 0$

---

**Type 2:**
$$
\|ax^2 + bx + c\| \leq d
$$

Equivalent to:
$$
-d \leq ax^2 + bx + c \leq d
$$

Example: $\|x^2 - 4x + 3\| \leq 5$

Rewrite as:$-5 \leq x^2 - 4x + 3 \leq 5$

Split into:

* $x^2 - 4x + 3 \geq -5$

* $x^2 - 4x + 3 \leq 5$

---

### **Graphical Method:**

Plot:
$$
y = \|f(x)\|, \quad y = g(x)
$$

Use the graph to visually identify where the inequality holds by comparing which curve lies above or below the other.
