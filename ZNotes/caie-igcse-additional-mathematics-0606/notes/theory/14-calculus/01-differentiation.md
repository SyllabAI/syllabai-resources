# Calculus

## Differentiation

* Function:

$$

y = x^n
$$

* First derivative:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = nx^{n - 1}
$$

* Second derivative:

$$
\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{d}{dx} \left( \frac{dy}{dx} \right)
$$

### Conditions for increasing and decreasing functions:

* Increasing function:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} > 0
$$

* Decreasing function:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} < 0
$$

A derived function (also called the derivative) represents the rate of change or gradient of a function.

If $y = f(x)$, then the derived function is written as $f'(x)$ or $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$

NOTE: $f''(x) = \frac{d}{dx} \left( f'(x) \right)$
