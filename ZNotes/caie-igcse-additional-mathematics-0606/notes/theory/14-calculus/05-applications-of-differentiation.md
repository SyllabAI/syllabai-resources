## Applications Of Differentiation

**Gradient at a Point**

The gradient of the curve at a point is the value of the derivative at that point:
$$\text{Gradient at } x = a \Rightarrow f'(a)$$

**Equation of Tangent**

The equation of the tangent to the curve at $ x = a $ is:
$$y - f(a) = f'(a)(x - a)$$

**Equation of Normal**

The equation of the normal to the curve at $ x = a $ is:
$$y - f(a) = -\frac{1}{f'(a)}(x - a)$$

**Stationary Points**

Stationary Points occur when the derivative is zero.

$$f\`(x)=0$$
