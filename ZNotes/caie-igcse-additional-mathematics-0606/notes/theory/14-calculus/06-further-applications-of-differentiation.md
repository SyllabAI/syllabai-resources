## Further Applications Of Differentiation

### **Related Rates Of Change**

If $x$ and $y$ are related by the equation $y = f(x)$,
then the rates of change $\frac{\text{dx}}{\text{dt}}$ and
$\frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dt}}$ are related by:

$$

\frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dt}} = \frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dx}} \times \frac{\text{dx}}{\text{dt}}

$$

### **Small Changes:**

### If $y = f(x)$ and small change $\text{δx}$ in $x$ causes a small change $\text{δy}$ in $y$, then

$$
\delta y \approx \left( \frac{dy}{dx} \right)_{x = k} \times \delta x
$$
