## Position Vector Dividing a Line Segment in a Given Ratio

If point $C$  lies on the line segment $AB$ and divides it in the ratio $m:n$, then the position vector of $C$, denoted $\vec{OC}$, is given by:

$$
\vec{OC} = \frac{n \vec{a} + m \vec{b}}{m + n}
$$

Where:

- $\vec{a} = \vec{OA}$ is the position vector of point $A$
- $\vec{b} = \vec{OB}$ is the position vector of point $B$
- $m:n$ is the ratio in which point $C$ divides $AB$

**Example:**

Let $\vec{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \ 1 \end{pmatrix} $, $ \vec{OB} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \ 7 \end{pmatrix} $, and suppose point $C$ divides $A$ in the ratio $2:1$. Then:

$$
\vec{OC} = \frac{1 \cdot \vec{OA} + 2 \cdot \vec{OB}}{2 + 1}
= \frac{1 \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 2 \ 1 \end{pmatrix} + 2 \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 8 \ 7 \end{pmatrix}}{3}
$$

$$
= \frac{\begin{pmatrix} 2 \ 1 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 16 \ 14 \end{pmatrix}}{3}
= \frac{\begin{pmatrix} 18 \ 15 \end{pmatrix}}{3}
= \begin{pmatrix} 6 \ 5 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Therefore, the position vector of point $C$ is
$$
\vec{OC} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \ 5 \end{pmatrix}
$$
