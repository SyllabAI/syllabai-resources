## Equating Like Vectors

* If two vectors are equal, their corresponding components must also be equal.

**Example:**\
Let $\vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix}x + 2 \ 3\end{pmatrix}$ and $\vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix}5 \ y - 1\end{pmatrix}$.\
If $\vec{a} = \vec{b}$, then:\
$x + 2 = 5 \Rightarrow x = 3$\
$3 = y - 1 \Rightarrow y = 4$

### **Vector Geometry Example**

In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ lies on $BC$ such that $BD : DC = 2 : 1$. Let $\vec{AB} = \vec{b}$ and $\vec{AC} = \vec{c}$. Find $\vec{AD}$ in terms of $\vec{b}$ and $\vec{c}$.

* Step 1: Find $\vec{BC} = \vec{AC} - \vec{AB} = \vec{c} - \vec{b}$

* Step 2: Since $BD : DC = 2 : 1$, $D$ divides $BC$ in the ratio $2:1$\
  So, $\vec{BD} = \frac{2}{3}(\vec{c} - \vec{b})$

* Step 3: Then,

$$
\vec{AD} = \vec{AB} + \vec{BD} = \vec{b} + \frac{2}{3}(\vec{c} - \vec{b})
= \vec{b} + \frac{2}{3}\vec{c} - \frac{2}{3}\vec{b}
$$
$$
= \left(1 - \frac{2}{3}\right)\vec{b} + \frac{2}{3}\vec{c}
= \frac{1}{3}\vec{b} + \frac{2}{3}\vec{c}
$$

So, $\vec{AD} = \frac{1}{3}\vec{b} + \frac{2}{3}\vec{c}$
