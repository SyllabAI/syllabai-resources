## Scalar Product

Definition: The scalar product (also known as the dot product) of two vectors $ \vec{a} $ and $ \vec{b} $ is defined as:

$$
\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \|\vec{a}\| , \|\vec{b}\| \cos \theta
$$

Where:

- $ \|\vec{a}\|$  and  $\|\vec{b}\| $ are the magnitudes of the vectors
- $ \theta $ is the angle between them

Let $ \vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \ 3 \end{pmatrix} $, and $ \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \ 1 \end{pmatrix} $

Then:
$$
\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = (2)(4) + (3)(1) = 8 + 3 = 11
$$
