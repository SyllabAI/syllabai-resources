## Collinearity of Vectors

Two vectors $ \vec{a} $ and $ \vec{b} $ are collinear if one is a scalar multiple of the other. That is:

$$
\vec{a} = k \vec{b} \quad \text{for some scalar } k
$$

**Example:**

Let $\vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \ 6 \end{pmatrix} $, and $ \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \ 3 \end{pmatrix} $

Then:
$$
\vec{a} = 2 \cdot \vec{b}
$$

Conclusion:\
Since one vector is a scalar multiple of the other, the vectors $ \vec{a} $ and $ \vec{b} $ are collinear.
