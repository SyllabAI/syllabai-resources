## Identifying When To Use Permutations and Combinations

### Permutations

* Order **matters**

* Used for arranging or ordering objects (letters, people, numbers)

* Note: $AB \neq BA$

* Keywords: *arrange*, *order*, *code*, *number*

### Combinations

* Order **does not matter**

* Used for selecting groups or teams

* Note: $AB=BA$

* Keywords: *choose*, *select*, *team*, *group*
