## Combinations

The number of ways of selecting $\text{\~r}$ objects
from $\text{\~n}$ unlike objects is:

$$
_\ ^{n}{C_r = \frac{n!}{r!\left( n - r \right)!}}
$$

Order **>does not** matter

Example: How many ways to choose 3 people from 10?

Number of ways = ${}_{10}C_3 = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 120$
