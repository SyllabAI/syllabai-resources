## Permutations

The number of ordered arrangements of r objects taken from n
unlike objects is:

$$
_\ ^{n}{P_r = \frac{n!}{\left( n - r \right)!}}
$$

Order **matters**

Example: How many 3-letter codes can be formed from 5 different letters?

Number of codes =
${}^5P_3 = \frac{5!}{(5 - 3)!} = \frac{5!}{2!} = \frac{120}{2} = 60$
