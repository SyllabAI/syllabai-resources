# Permutations & Combinations

## Introduction

### Factorial

* The product of all positive integers up to a given number.

* It is used to count arrangements and calculate permutations and combinations.

**General formula** $\rightarrow$ $n! = n \times \left( n - 1 \right) \times \left( n - 2 \right)\ldots \times 3 \times 2 \times 1$

For example $\rightarrow 5! = 5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 120$

**Note:**

* $0! = 1$
* Factorial is defined only for non-negative integers.
