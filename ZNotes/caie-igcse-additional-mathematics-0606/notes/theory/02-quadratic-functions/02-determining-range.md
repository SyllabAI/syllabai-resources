## Determining Range

**Case 1**: When $a > 0$ (Parabola opens upwards)

Vertex: The minimum point of the parabola.

Range: All real numbers greater than or equal to the y-coordinate of the vertex.

Inequality Form: $f(x) \geq k$

**Case 2**: When $a < 0$ (Parabola opens downwards)

Vertex: The maximum point of the parabola.

Range: All real numbers less than or equal to the y-coordinate of the vertex.

Inequality Form: $f(x) \leq k$
