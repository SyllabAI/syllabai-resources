# The Digital Divide

* **Digital Divide:** It’s the technology divide between countries that includes the divide of:
  * **Demographic groups:** Different sections of populations.
  * **Economic areas:** An area considered in terms of production and consumption of goods and services.
* **Different groups affected by the digital divide:**
  * People living in cities versus people living in rural areas
  * People educated in using technology versus people uneducated in using technology
  * Older people versus younger people
  * Areas that are more industrially developed versus areas that are less industrially developed
  * Different socio-economic groups.
* **City versus rural living:** The access to technology depends on whether they live in a city or rural area.
  * Reducing the digital divide in rural areas:
    * Improving the quality of infrastructure
    * Using satellite technologies
* **Technology educated versus technology uneducated:** The education of technology differs among people.
  * Reducing the digital divide with the technologically uneducated:
    * Providing accessible technology courses in local communities
    * The courses are offered for free or low-cost
* **Older people versus young people:** Some people have grown up with technology as an integral part of life, and some prefer old methods of communication.
  * Reducing the digital divide between younger and older people:
    * Incentives to get older people to see the advantages of technology.
* **More industrially developed areas versus less industrially developed areas:** Access to technology is greater in the USA, Europe and northern Asia, while it’s restricted in parts of Africa, India and southern Asia.
  * Reducing the digital divide in less industrially developed areas:
    * Promoting business and products that they could offer if they had better infrastructure
    * Using satellite technologies
* **Different socio-economic groups:** Richer people can afford high-speed technology, and the lack of information impacts the poor.
  * Reducing the digital divide in different socio-economic groups:
    * Providing initiatives for technology for free to the poor
    * Large organizations work together to provide the internet.
