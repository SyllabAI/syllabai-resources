# Sound and Video editing

## Sound Editing

* **Track:** A specific recording which can be edited or combined.
* **Splice:** Joining together two or more sound or video clips.
* **Stereo clip:** A clip that has multiple tracks
* **Mono clip:** A clip that has a single track
* **Sample rate:** Number of times sound is sampled in a second
* **Sample resolution:** Number of bits that are used to represent each sound sample
