## Calibration

* **Calibration:** Testing and modifying a device to make sure it takes correct readings.
* **Characteristic curve of sensors:**
  * Offset curve: The readings are higher or lower than the ideal reference.
  * Sensitivity curve: The readings change over a range at a different rate to the ideal reference.
  * Linearity curve: The readings are directly proportional to the ideal reference.
* **Types of Calibrations:**
  * **One-point calibration:** Used to correct offset calibration curve.
  * **Two-point calibration:** Used to correct both offset and sensitivity curves.
  * **Multipoint calibration:** Performed with multiple readings to increase the accuracy.
