## Advantages and Disadvantages of an Expert System

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|----|----|
| Provides answers to questions that require knowledge you don’t have | Responses are always logical but can’t be useful every time |
| Aids professional people by prompting them and guiding them to look at areas of knowledge they may not have considered or remembered | If there are any errors in the rules and databases, they can generate the wrong solution. |
| The responses that are produced are always consistent | It is very expensive and requires a lot of consultants from expert humans |
| It can be used at any time |    |
| Arrives at a solution faster than an expert human. |    |
