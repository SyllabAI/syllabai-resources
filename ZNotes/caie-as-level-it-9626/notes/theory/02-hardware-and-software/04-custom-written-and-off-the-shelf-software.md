## Custom-Written and Off-the-Shelf Software

* **Custom-written:** Software that is written to meet the requirements of a client.
  * It is expensive because the client covers the entire development cost.
  * Takes a longer time to develop.
  * The requirements of the client can be met precisely.
  * Developers will ensure compatibility with the client’s hardware, software and data.
  * Bugs are likely since it is not used widely.
  * The client will have access to support from the developer company.
* **Off-the-shelf:** General-purpose software available to a large market.
  * Development cost is spread between all the customers who purchase it at a specific price, reducing overall cost.
  * Immediately available.
  * User requirements may not be precise/unnecessary features.
  * It may have compatibility issues.
  * Many users mean fewer bugs due to patches released after identifying bugs.
  * Wide range of support both from the developing company and externally.
