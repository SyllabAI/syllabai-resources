## Sources of Data

**Direct Data Source:** Data collected from the primary source is used for its intended purpose.

* **Questionnaires**
  * Ideal for gathering specific opinions, especially when dealing with many respondents.
  * Questions should be structured to gather the right data, ensure easy analysis, and not overwhelm respondents.
  * Online questionnaires are efficient because they directly input data into databases, saving time on manual entry.
* **Interviews**
  * Involve asking respondents questions directly, allowing for follow-up queries to gain more detailed responses.

**Indirect Data Source:** Data collected from the secondary source and used for a different purpose.

* **Electoral Register**
  * Governments keep a register of voters, including names and addresses.
  * Originally for elections, it’s also used by credit reference agencies to verify addresses or by marketing agencies for direct mail.
  * People can choose to keep their electoral register details hidden from public view.
* **Businesses and Personal Data**
  * Businesses often buy lists of contact information (emails, phone numbers) collected by third parties.
  * This data is often collected by third parties through interactions like online forms, subscriptions, or loyalty programmes.
  * This indirect data is then used for targeted marketing campaigns.

### Advantages and disadvantages of direct and indirect data


|Direct data|Indirect data|
|:---|:---|
|Only needed data is collected, making the data relevant.|Large samples are easy to acquire.|
|The data can be trusted because the original source is known.|Data is immediately available.|
|The data is up to date|Additional sorting of irrelevant data will be required.|
|The data is collected and presented in the format required.|The source is not reliable as the original source is unknown.|
|The collection of original data can take longer than using data that is available.|The data may be out of date.|
|A large sample of data can be difficult to collect.|The data may not be available in the required format.|
