## Expectation & Variance of Sample Mean

$E\left( \overline{X} \right) = \mu$,
$\text{Var}\left( \overline{X} \right) = \frac{\sigma^{2}}{n}$

**<span class="underline">{IS Ex 6c} Question 5:</span>**

The mean weight of a soldier may be taken to be 90kg, and
$\sigma = 10$kg. 250 soldiers are on board an aircraft, find the
expectation and variance of their weight. Hence find the $\mu$ and
$\sigma$ of the total weight of soldiers.

**<span class="underline">Solution:</span>**

*Let* $X$ *be the average weight, therefore*

$$E\left( \overline{X} \right) = \mu = 90$$

$\text{Var}\left( \overline{X} \right) = \frac{\sigma^{2}}{n} = \frac{10^{2}}{250} = 0.4\ $kg<sup>2</sup>

*To find* $\mu$ *of total weight, you are calculating*

$E\left( X_{1} \right) + E\left( X_{2} \right)\ldots + E\left( X_{250} \right) = 250E\left( X \right) = 22\ 500$kg

*To find* $\sigma$*, first find* $Var(X)$

$\text{Var}\left( X_{1} \right)\ldots + Var\left( X_{250} \right) = 250Var\left( X \right) = 2500$kg

$$\text{Var}\left( X \right) = \sigma^{2} = 25000$$

$\therefore\sigma = \sqrt{25000} = 158.1$kg
