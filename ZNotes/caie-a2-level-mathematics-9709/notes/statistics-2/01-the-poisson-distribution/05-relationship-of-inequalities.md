## Relationship of Inequalities

  - $P\left( X < r \right) = P\left( X \leq r - 1 \right)$

  - $P\left( X = r \right) = P\left( X \leq r \right) - P\left( X \leq r - 1 \right)$

  - $P\left( X > r \right) = 1 - P\left( X \leq r \right)$

  - $P\left( X \geq r \right) = 1 - P\left( X \leq r - 1 \right)$
