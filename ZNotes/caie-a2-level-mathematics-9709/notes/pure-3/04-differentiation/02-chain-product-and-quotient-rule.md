## Chain, Product and Quotient Rule 

- **Chain Rule:**

$$
\frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dx}} = \frac{\text{dy}}{\text{du}} \times \frac{\text{du}}{\text{dx}}
$$

- **Product Rule:**

$$
\frac{d}{\text{dx}}\left( \text{uv} \right) = u\frac{\text{dv}}{\text{dx}} + v\frac{\text{du}}{\text{dx}}
$$

- **Quotient Rule:**

$$
\frac{d}{\text{dx}}\left( \frac{u}{v} \right) = \frac{v\frac{\text{du}}{\text{dx}} - u\frac{\text{dv}}{\text{dx}}}{v^{2}}
$$
