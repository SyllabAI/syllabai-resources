## Implicit Functions

- These represent circles or lines with circular curves, on a
    Cartesian plane

- Difficult to rearrange in form $y=\ \therefore\text{ }$differentiate as is

- Differentiate $x$ terms as usual

- For $y$ terms, differentiate the same as you would $x$ but
    multiply with $\frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dx}}$

- Then make $\frac{\text{dy}}{\text{dx}}$ the subject of formula for
    derivative
