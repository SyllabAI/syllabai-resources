## Multiplication and Division in Polar Form
  
- To find **product** of two complex numbers in polar form:
  - Multiply their magnitudes
  - Add their arguments

$$
z_{1}z_{2} = \left| z_{1} \right|\left| z_{2} \right|\left( \arg z_{1} + \arg z_{2} \right)
$$

**<span class="underline">Example:</span>**

Find $z_{1}z_{2}$ in polar form given,

$$
z_{1} = 2\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{4} \right) \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ z_{2} = 4\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{8} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{8} \right)
$$

**<center>Solution:</center>**

$$
z_{1}z_{2} = \left( 2 \times 4 \right)\left( \cos\left( \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{8} \right) + i\sin\left( \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{8} \right) \right)
$$

$$
z_{1}z_{2} = 8\left( \cos\frac{3\pi}{8} + i\sin\frac{3\pi}{8} \right)
$$

- To find **quotient** of two complex numbers in polar form:
  - Divide their magnitudes
  - Subtract their arguments

$$
\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}\  = \frac{\left| z_{1} \right|}{\left| z_{2} \right|}\left( \arg z_{1} - \arg z_{2} \right)
$$

**<span class="underline">Example:</span>**

Find $\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}$ in polar form given,

$$
z_{1} = 2\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{4} \right) \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ z_{2} = 4\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{8} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{8} \right)
$$

**<center>Solution:</center>**

$$
\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}} = \left( \frac{2}{4} \right)\left( \cos\left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{8} \right) + i\sin\left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{8} \right) \right)
$$

$$
\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{8} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{8} \right)
$$
