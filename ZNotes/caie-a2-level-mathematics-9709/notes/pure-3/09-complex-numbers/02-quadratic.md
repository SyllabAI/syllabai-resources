## Quadratic

  - Use the quadratic formula:
    
      - $b^{2} - 4ac$ is a negative value
    
      - Pull out a negative and replace with $i^{2}$
    
      - Simplify to general form

  - Use sum of 2 squares: consider the example

&nbsp;

**<span class="underline">Example:</span>**

Solve: $z^{2} + 4z + 13 = 0$

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*Convert to completed square form:*

$$\left( z + 2 \right)^{2} + 9 = 0$$

*Utilize* $i^{2}$ *as* $- 1$ *to make it difference of 2 squares:*

$$\left( z + 2 \right)^{2} - 9i^{2} = 0$$

*Proceed with general difference of 2 squares method:*

$$\left( z + 2 + 3i \right)\left( z + 2 - 3i \right) = 0$$

$$z = - 2 + 3i\ \ \ \ \ and\ \ \ \ \ z = - 2 - 3i$$
