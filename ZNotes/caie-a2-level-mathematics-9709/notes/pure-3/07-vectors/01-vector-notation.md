# Vectors 

## Vector Notation

- A vector can be represented as $\overrightarrow{\text{AB}}$ or
    $\mathbf{a}$

- The column vector form:

$$
\overrightarrow{\text{AB}} = \ \begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

- The linear vector form:

$$
\overrightarrow{\text{AB}} = x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j} + z\mathbf{k}
$$
