## Equation of a Line

  - The equation of a straight line is expressed in the form:
    
    **r = a** $+ \ t$**b**

  - For example:
    
    The column vector form:

$$
r = \ \begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
3 \\
 - 2 \\
\end{pmatrix} + \ t\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
1 \\
3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The linear vector form:

$$
r = \mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k} + t\left( \mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k} \right)
$$
