## Determining the initial rate of reaction

* This is an important skill that can be easily learnt as questions that ask to calculate the initial rate of reaction comes up very often.
* To determine the initial rate of reaction (gradient), the following formula is used:

$$

\text{Gradient}=\frac{\text{Change in y}}{\text{Change in x}}=\frac{y\\_2 - y\\_1}{x\\_2 - x\\_1}

$$

* A tangent is drawn where the graph is at the start of the reaction.
* Two points on the tangent are selected and placed into the gradient formula to find the initial rate of reaction.
