## Tables

* You may be asked to draw tables where you have to collect data from a graph. 
* Read the instructions correctly as there may be multiple graphs and they will only ask you to draw a specific one.
* Draw the appropriate amount of cells required—do not leave extra unless the question requires it.
* The table must be drawn with a ruler with straight lines. 
* The table’s border must be drawn completely.
* The heading must contain the units of measurement.
