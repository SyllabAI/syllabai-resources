## Sustainability

* Items that we currently use very often, which are made from unsustainable materials and methods can be replaced with plant-based substitutes as they are sustainable and renewable.
* Carbon neutrality: The balance between the emmission of carbon into the atmosphere and removal of carbon from the atmosphere.
* Plants are carbon neutral as the rate at which they are grown are equal to the rate at which they are being used.
* Fossil fuels like oil, which are used to make plastics, are not sustainable and non-renewable, so in the future it will run out, which is why scientists are now looking into plant-based substitutes. 
* Plant-based substitutes are also much more biodegrable than other materials, which would help solve the problem of waste management. 
* Plants are inexpensive and quick to grow, so they will be cheaper than the non-renewable alternatives.
* Plants can be turned into many useful items that we use often:
  * Biofuel—Bioethanol which can replace a part of fossil fuels, made from the fermentation of starch. 
  * Bioplastics which can replace oil-based plastics and can biodegrade by microorganisms.
  * Ropes and fabrics can be made from plant fibres like the sclerenchyma which makes it more durable.
  * Plants have chemicals in them that have provided further research into its antimicrobial and therapeutic properties.
