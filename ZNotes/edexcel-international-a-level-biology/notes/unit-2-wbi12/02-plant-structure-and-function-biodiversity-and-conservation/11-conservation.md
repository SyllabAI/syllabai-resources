## Conservation

* Many living organisms on Earth either animals, plants or others are being affected drastically by the activity of humans.
* This is why there is much needed importance on their conservation as a species on Earth as well as its genetic diversity.
* Scientists and environmentalists around the world have agreed that the utilisation of places like zoos and seed banks are one of the best chances of conservation of species and its genetic diversity.

### **Zoos**

* **Zoos** use an array of methods to help in the conservation of many endangered species and its genetic diversity such as:
  * **Captive Breeding Programmes** - endangered animals are captured and bred in zoos to increase their population and genetic diversity.
  * **Reintroduction Programmes** - endangered animals with a large enough population are returned back to their natural habitat.
  * **Educational Programmes** - people can be educated about the programmes ran by the zoos, the importances of preserving endangered species and the harmful human activities that we are doing, leading to the extinction of organisms.

### **Seeds banks**

* Seed banks store plant seeds in order to conserve the endangered species and its genetic diversity.
  * The seeds are check via X-ray to see if they contain a developed embryo.
  * The seeds are cautiously stored in a dry and cool condition (between -20 to -40°C) to maximise their storage time and viability
  * **Viability**: ability to survive or live successfully.
