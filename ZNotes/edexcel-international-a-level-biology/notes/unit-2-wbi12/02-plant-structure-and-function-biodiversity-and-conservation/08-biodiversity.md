## Biodiversity

* **Endemism**: when a species of organism is found only within a particular geographical region.
* **Why Biodiversity matters**?
  * **Moral and ethical:** some people/cultures believe we have no
    right to cause extinction of other species.
  * **Ecological**: ecosystems are more stable when biodiversity is
    high. They also add direct value to us eg medicinal herbs and
    must be protected so that species beneficial to us are not lost.
  * Nutrient cycles and food chains are disrupted when biodiversity
    is harmed.
  * **Aesthetic:** many people enjoy the variety of organisms and
    habitats on earth, which provide inspiration for creative
    people.
* The number of species in a community is known as **species
  richness**
* Species diversity takes species richness into account, but also
  includes evenness of abundance of each species
* In species diversity there are two points that need to be found:
  ***distribution*** and ***abundance*** of species.
* **Species abundance** is the measure of the number of species in a
  given area.

### **Measuring Biodiversity**:

* **Diversity Index:**

$$

D=\frac{N(N - 1)}{\sum\\_{}^{}{n(n - 1)}}

$$

* The formula will be provided to you in the exam
* D = diversity index
* N = The total number of organisms
* n = The total number of organisms in each species
* **Heterozygosity Index:**

$$

\frac{\text{Number of Heterozygotes}}{\text{Number of Individuals in the Population}}

$$

* The greater the heterozygosity index, the greater the diversity due
  to high genetic variation
