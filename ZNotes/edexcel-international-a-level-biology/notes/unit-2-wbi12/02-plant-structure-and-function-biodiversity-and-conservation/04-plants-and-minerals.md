## Plants and Minerals

  - Plants use water for many functions, including:
    
      - Photosynthesis: Plants use water, in addition to carbon dioxide,
        to form glucose.
    
      - Water is essential in keeping plant cells turgid, providing
        support.
    
      - Water can act as a transport medium for minerals and sucrose in
        the plant.
    
      - Water helps regulate temperature, preventing loss of water.

| Minerals  |                                                                                |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Nitrates  | Used to produce proteins, nucleic acids, chlorophyll.                          |
| Magnesium | Produce chlorophyll, activation of enzymes, and preventing yellowing of leaves |
| Calcium   | Form the middle lamella and cell membranes                                     |

  - Plant fibres are extracted from plants through various methods like
    retting. The use of plant fibres have many advantages like being
    biodegradable, carbon neutral, and being a sustainable resource.

  - Starch has other uses like producing bioplastics, which could be
    burned without polluting the environment.
