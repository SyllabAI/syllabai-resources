* photosynthometer
* spectrophotometer

### **Statistical tests:**


1. Student’s t-test - compares the means of two groups. If the calculated value>critical value, there is a significant difference.
2. Spearman rank correlation coefficient - checks whether there is a significant correlation between two data sets.
3. Chi-squared test

For the tests, we generally use 0.05 degrees of freedom.
