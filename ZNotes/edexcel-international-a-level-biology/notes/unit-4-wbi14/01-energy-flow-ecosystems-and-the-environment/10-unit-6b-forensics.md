## Forensics

**The role of microorganisms in the decomposition of organic matter and the
recycling of carbon:**

* Polysaccharides are broken down into monosaccharides, which are sent into the soil and absorbed by plants [e.g., nitrates and mineral ions] to form new carbon compounds
* These are absorbed into decomposers and used for growth/reproduction
* During decomposition, CO2 is released and sent into the atmosphere, after which producers can absorb it

### **DNA Profiling**

Involves comparing and analysing non-coding introns, usually for forensics

### Amplification of DNA using the PCR [polymerase chain reaction] process

* DNA sample mixed with buffer solution, TAQ polymerase, nucleotides and primers
* Heated at 90-95C to break H bonds between DNA strand
* Heated at 50-60C for the 20s so primers bind to the beginning of the DNA strand [annealing]
* Heated at 75C for at least the 60s so TAQ polymerase builds up complementary strands
* This process is repeated until a sufficient amount of DNA is obtained

### Use of gel electrophoresis to separate DNA fragments of different lengths

* extract DNA [e.g., extract haemoglobin from blood]
* cut into fragments using restriction enzymes
* load into wells in agarose gel
* an electric field applied to the gel
* shortest DNA fragments move furthest in a given period
* peptide bands are stained/made visible
* compare the size and position of bands to determine relationships

### **Determining the time of death of a cadaver**

* Body temperature: metabolic reactions slow and stop, so heat production slows down and heat energy is lost through radiation, conduction and evaporation —> is affected by surface area to volume ratio, ambient temperature, clothing, and covering, method of death, and fat level


|<3 hours|warm|not stiff|aerobic respiration slows and stops|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|3-8 hours|warm|stiff|anaerobic respiration produces lactic acid, which decreases the pH|
|8-36 hours|cold|stiff|no energy for respiration, no ATP for muscle contraction|
|>36 hours|cold|stiff|decomposition takes place; small muscles stiffen first and unstiffen last|

Rigor mortis: degree of muscle contraction

*  Forensic etymology: collect insects on the body, hatch eggs in the lab and observe the life cycle to work backwards and find how long they were on the body

* Extent of decomposition: identify the stage of succession of organisms around the cadaver
