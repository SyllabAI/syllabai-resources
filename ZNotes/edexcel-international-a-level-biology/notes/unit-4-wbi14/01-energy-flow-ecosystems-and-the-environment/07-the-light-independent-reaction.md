## **The Light-Independent Reaction**

* This is also called the Calvin Cycle and can occur in darkness and light.
* It relies on the products of the light-dependent reaction.

**Steps:**

1. The 5-carbon compound RUBP joins with atmospheric CO2 through carbon fixation in the presence of the RUBSICO enzyme [this enzyme is the rate-limiting factor], forming an unstable 6-carbon compound
2. This breaks down to produce 2 GP molecules [each contains three carbon atoms]
3. This is reduced to GALP using released electrons from reduced NADP and
   energy from ATP.
4. 1/3 of GALP is used, and 2/3 of GALP is converted to RUBP, continuing the cycle.
5. The GALP produced can:
   → combine with nitrates to make amino acids
   → be used for cellular respiration
   → produce glucose through glucogenesis
   → can convert into a variety of double sugars or polysaccharides
