# Energy Flow, Ecosystems and the Environment

## Photosynthesis

Overall reaction: 6CO2 + 6H2O —> C6H12O6 + 6O2 + energy

* Autotropic organisms produce their nutrients through photosynthesis, while heterotrophic organisms consume autotrophs to gain nutrients.
* ATP [adenosine triphosphate] is used as energy currency for photosynthesis.
* Photosynthesis occurs in two stages: light-dependent reaction and light-independent reaction.
