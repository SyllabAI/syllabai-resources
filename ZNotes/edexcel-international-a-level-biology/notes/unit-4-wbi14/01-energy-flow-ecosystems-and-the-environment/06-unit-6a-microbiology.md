## Microbiology

### Aseptic technique

1. sterilising workbench with alcohol

2. working next to the Bunsen burner [two on either side] to create airflow

3. sterilising/autoclaving equipment

4. opening Petri dishes of bacteria for the minimum amount of time possible

5. wearing gloves and goggles

Bacteria can be either simple or complex and come in various shapes, including: Cocci [spherical], bacilli [rod], spiri/spiro, vibrio [comma]

### Growth of microorganisms

**Methods of measuring growth:**

1. Cell counts- from the liquid sample using a haemocytometer [cells touching the top and right should be counted]
2. dilution plating-prepare microorganism broth by mixing known volumes of a culture with a sterile liquid at different ratios to produce a series of dilutions, diluting to different levels and cultures in Petri dishes
3. mass-known volume centrifuged until only solid mass remains, multiplied to find the total mass of culture
4. turbidity-measure light passing through using a spectrophotometer
