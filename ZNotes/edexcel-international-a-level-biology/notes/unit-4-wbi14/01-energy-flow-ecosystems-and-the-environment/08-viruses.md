## Viruses

### **HIV**

* retrovirus that converts RNA to DNA and inserts through reverse transcriptase and integrase enzymes
* destroys T helper cells
* prevents the activation of T killer and B memory cells
* weakens the immune system/weakens the immune response
* body susceptible to infections, incredibly opportunistic

### **Tuberculosis [TB]**

* bacteria infect phagocytes in the lungs
* these are sealed inside tubercles which are covered with thick waxy mucus, preventing them from being destroyed
* when the immune system is weakened, bacteria become active and multiply
* lung tissue is damaged and alveoli break down leaving large air spaces with reduced surface area for gas exchange
* insufficient oxygen for aerobic respiration
* organ failure
* symptoms: fever, night sweats, loss of appetite and weight, produces sputum
* treatment: a mixture of antibiotics [for the 1st 2 months] because bacteria may be resistant to some commonly used drugs but may be affected by others

### **Ebola virus**

* The virus is enclosed in an RNA-containing package.

* Glycoproteins on the membrane of the virus bind to cell surface receptors

* The cell membrane engulfs the virus

* Lytic and lysogenic cycles take place

* Symptoms: high fever, headaches, aching joints and muscles, muscle weakness, diarrhoea, internal and external bleeding
