One advantage of JJ employing part time workers is that they may be more flexible in their working hours. JJ’s 25 part time employees may be more willing to come in during peak seasons of high demand- as demand for the jewelry is forecasted to increase- compared to the 60 full time employees. This can promote greater productivity during these times and ensure that JJ is able to meet customer needs.

Another advantage of JJ hiring part-time employees is that they may be paid lower wages than full-time employees. Since JJ’s 25 part-time employees have a more flexible schedule of when they prefer working, they may be subject to lower overall wages in comparison to the 60 full-time employees. This allows JJ to reduce its variable costs of producing its jewelry.

One disadvantage of JJ employing part time workers is a lack of commitment to the job. Since JJ’s 25 part-time workers only work half of the usual working hours compared to the full-time workers, they may be more ignorant of the quality of their work as they may view the job as temporary. This can lower their productivity and efficiency when making the jewelry, as they will be less committed to the business.

Another disadvantage of JJ hiring part-time workers is that they may not undergo training. As the part time workers will be producing jewelry for less hours compared to full time workers, they may not undergo the same training that full time workers undergo to produce the jewelry. This may result in inconsistent work across both groups.

 

**2(b)**

Advertising in fashion magazines: JJ can use above-the-line marketing to promote the new items of jewelry. Fashion magazine adverts can make it more likely that the advert is seen by JJ’s target market – increasing the likelihood of sales as more potential customers are seeing the advert. Furthermore, photographs used in the magazine advert may display the jewelry in a more attractive way, showing off its colors and pattern. This can allow it to appeal to potential customers more than if they are just told about the jewelry/ read about it.  However, if JJ’s target market is not readers of fashion magazines, and are instead, middle aged working women, they may miss the promotion and there may be a loss of potential sales.

Handing out leaflets in Main City: This is an affordable method of promoting the new jewelry items. Leaflets can allow JJ to keep marketing costs low as they bulk print, which can allow for lower unit costs. Leaflets can also allow the promotion of the new jewelry items to reach a wider and more mainstream audience as the receivers of the leaflet will be anyone in the main city. JJ may also be able to incentivize the purchasing of the latest item by offering discounts with the leaflet.

Point-of-sale displays in shops: This method of promotion can allow customers to physically see the new items of jewelry. This will make it more likely that they may be attracted to the piece and wish to purchase it. This can also help JJ stand out in the jewelry market as there may be few firms displaying jewellery at a point of sale. However, many customers may dismiss the latest items and not purchase them if they do not properly see it or if they do not wish to add more to their carts after already choosing items.

Recommendation: JJ should choose fashion magazines as a method of promoting the latest item because it makes it more likely that JJ’s target customers will buy it. Leaflets may be useless if customers throw them away or ignore them when being passed out. Furthermore, the chance of customers purchasing a jewelry item through a point of sales display may be low, as JJ cannot guarantee that customers see the promotion and acknowledge it.
