Environmental issues are of increasing importance for most businesses nowadays. Governments, customers and pressure groups may choose to focus more on JJ’s impact on the environment- specifically, whether their use of scarce resources is beneficial, or harmful.

Using coal for fuel in its factory: The release of coal emissions increases the level of air pollution present – which may affect JJ’s reputation, as more pressure groups become conscious of how air pollution can worsen global warming. This activity can therefore lead to pressure groups creating bad publicity for JJ, tarnishing the name of their bracelets and rings. Additionally, the price of coal is increasing, raising JJ’s fixed costs of powering the factory and machines. Increasing fixed costs will lead to higher total costs which must be spread out over production and may make the jewellery more expensive for customers.

Buying from suppliers that damage the environment: if JJ’s customers become aware of their use of materials from unethical suppliers, they may be less willing to buy their jewellery, and this may cause a loss of business for JJ. Furthermore, pressure groups may try to force JJ to switch to alternative suppliers by creating bad publicity - this may force JJ to increase their transport costs by switching to new suppliers who are located further away.

Conclusion: JJ should solve the issues relating to using coal to power the factory as the price of coal increasing will directly and heavily impact JJ’s costs. JJ may also benefit by choosing to switch to solar panels as the government is providing a 25% grant to businesses who invest in solar energy. This can allow JJ to reduce its costs, after which it may be easier to implement new suppliers or materials. Customers may also be unaware of the damage caused by JJ’s suppliers, which may therefore cause no effect to sales or publicity, thus making the issue more secondary in nature.
