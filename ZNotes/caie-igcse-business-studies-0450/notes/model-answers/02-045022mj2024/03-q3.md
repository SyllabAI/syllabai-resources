**3(a)**

Reason 1: Profit is a source of finance to businesses. Retained profit that is left over after all costs and tax have been paid can be reinvested into the business in order to increase the firm’s scale of production/to buy noncurrent assets or fulfil other business objectives.

Reason 2: Profit is an indicator of success. If a business is sustainably increasing its annual profits, this may be an indicator to both the business itself and to external stakeholders (such as banks or investors) that the business is safe to continue lending to, investing in, etc.

Reason 3: Profit is a reward to entrepreneurs. Entrepreneurs must be rewarded for the risk and efforts they take to organise production. Therefore, profit acts as an incentive to get more individuals to become entrepreneurs and start business ventures.

Reason 4: To attract investors. Profit is a sign that investors look for in a successful business, and therefore, profit can incentivise more investors to raise capital and buy shares in the business.

 

**3(b)**

Ecommerce refers to the buying and selling of JJ’s jewellery over the internet. Online retail can pose a number of opportunities and threats to JJ.

Opportunities: JJ can use a website to promote their brand in a way that can reach a worldwide audience. Websites are also cost effective, making them economical compared to other forms of promotion. Using online retail can also cut out the need for intermediaries such as jewelry shops that sell JJ’s rings and bracelets- which helps to make the products cheaper for customers, as well as increasing JJ’s profit margins as less revenue is paid to retailers. JJ may also be able to save on menu costs, as changing prices of jewellery is more conveniently done online compared to reprinting new labels and brochures in physical stores.

Threats: Online retail may increase the level of competition for JJ, as there may be many jewellery manufacturers on the web who have similar websites and prices. This increases consumer choice and therefore makes it harder for customers to choose to buy JJ’s jewellery. Maintaining websites is also a costly task, as there will be initial design costs to create the website, as well as to maintain the website and update it. Another threat to JJ is the lack of the physical experience for customers – since they will shop by looking at pictures and will be unable to try on rings and bracelets, there may be a loss of business, as some customers may prefer to try on their jewellery before buying to ensure that they are satisfied.  Furthermore, using online retail may require JJ to invest in an expensive security framework to ensure that customers can conduct safe transactions without any risks.

Justification: JJ will benefit from the opportunities of ecommerce, due to having 30% of its customer base located outside country X. A website therefore allows JJ to reach these customers in a cost-effective way. Strategic marketing and online offers can allow JJ to bring in customers, reducing the effect of high competition, and thus, the gains from ecommerce.
