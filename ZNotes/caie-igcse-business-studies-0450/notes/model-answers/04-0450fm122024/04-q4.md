One method of sales promotion that Suliman can use is “BOGOF”, or buy one get one free. This promotion can be put on t-shirts, so that customers can get a t-shirt free from the purchase of another t-shirt. This can incentivize more sales, as customers may feel as though they are getting their money's worth by receiving more than what they paid for. 

 

Another method of sales promotion that Suliman can use is loyalty cards. These can be cards given to loyal customers who have bought over 10 t-shirts on different occasions and can avail discounts on future purchases. Maintaining existing customers can create a loyal customer base and is also cheaper than trying to attract new customers with other promotional methods.

One factor that Suliman must consider is the rent of the new location. Since Suliman’s t-shirt business is small, the rent of the new location must be in budget and affordable, since it is unlikely that Suliman will be able to secure loans with a bank to pay for the rent. A very expensive new location may raise fixed costs very high, and if Suliman is unable to pay this, he will have to find alternate options or will have to stick to his current location. 

 

Another factor that is important to consider is the distance of the new location to Suliman’s suppliers. If the new location is very far from suppliers, it can lead to high transport costs to move cloth and materials needed for the t-shirts. This will lead to higher variable costs and will lower profit margins for Suliman. If the new location is closer to suppliers, it can lead to a fall in transport and total costs, which can raise profit margins. 

 

**4(e)** 

For small businesses, being a sole trader may be the best choice. 

This is because sole traders are their own boss, and in complete control of the business. They can make quicker decisions, and do not have to agree or disagree with others. This can lead to smoother operations as a sole trader need not to consult others for important objectives or decision making. Sole traders are also able to keep the total of the profits they make, and do not have to share the rewards of the business. Furthermore, small businesses do not have to publish their accounts or financial information–which allows them to keep such information private from competitors, who may try and use it. 

While sole traders experience many benefits when running a small business, there are also reasons why it is not always the best form. Sole traders have unlimited liability, which leaves them at risk if the business fails and is forced to liquidate its assets. This causes sole traders to be fully vulnerable and increases the risk they take on when starting the business. 

However, being a sole trader is the best form of organization for a small business. This is because most small firms do not have plans to expand or grow, due to the owners’ preferences. Therefore, there is less of a need for finance from other partners or investors. Sole traders will also benefit from being able to make their own decisions without consulting anyone–which saves time, money and effort–and allows them to keep their best interests at the heart of the business.
