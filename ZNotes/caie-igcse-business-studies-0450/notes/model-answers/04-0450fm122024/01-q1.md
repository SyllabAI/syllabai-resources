**1(e)** 

External recruitment refers to a business using external sources and agencies to recruit candidates for a job vacancy. 

One way of recruiting candidates externally is through specialist magazines. These magazines allow for businesses to target people who are suited to the job by being specific to certain industries and types of work. This can save time by filtering candidates to only those who are best for the job and have the required experience and qualifications. 

Another way to externally recruit is through online recruitment sites, such as LinkedIn or Indeed. These sites are easily accessible to anyone with the internet, which allows businesses to scope a larger applicant pool for the vacancy. They can also have detailed information about the job, which allows for suited candidates to apply based on if they fit the profile required.

An online recruitment site is likely to be the best method of external recruitment. This is because it will not only open the business to a wider selection of candidates but also decrease the need for future recruitments if the new candidate is fit for the role. Screening through online algorithms can also save more time by ruling out candidates who do not pass the algorithm tests/interviews. It is also cheap to advertise on these sites and reduces the administration costs of filling the vacancy.
