**2(b)** 

A pressure group is a group of individuals who join with the common interest to act against a business or government to force them to change their actions or policies. An example of a pressure group is a trade union, which may try to force a business to increase the wages or health and safety of its workers.

An advantage to PLY of using email to communicate with customers is that it can be sent to many people at once. Mail merge and other features of emailing can allow for PLY to send out mass emails, contacting thousands of customers at a time. This can help save time and allows the business to focus on the new legal controls over business activity. 

 

A disadvantage to PLY of using emails may be that not all customers have emails. If people in PLY’s customer base do not have email addresses or internet connection, they will be unable to reach PLY to ask inquiries about their hats or to receive promotional offers from PLY. This can result in lost business, and additionally, users may become alienated if they receive spam emails from PLY. Furthermore, this can lead to lower sales if more customers stop using email. 

 

**2(e)** 

Market research is the process of identifying, gathering and collating data pertaining to a market so that it can be studied by a business. 

One reason why businesses may gather market research is to identify customer wants or needs. If a business can correctly identify that their target market needs a specific product, it helps them to try and curate something that fits the needs and wants of the target market, instead of producing a good or service which is not required or wanted by customers. An example of this is Netflix, which leverages data analytics extensively to understand viewer behaviour and personalize recommendations.

Another reason why businesses might conduct market research is to identify what competitors are doing. Businesses may want to scope what the competing businesses in the market are doing to differentiate their own products from those, as well as to know what the current price for a product is in the market and to make pricing decisions based on that. This can allow the business to remain competitive by not pricing a product much higher or much lower in comparison to others selling the same product. 

 

Identifying customer needs is likely to be the most important reason to conduct market research. Its insights businesses on what is trending, and what is going to sell. Without identifying what customers want, the product may be priced low but will not get any sales as it is irrelevant. This is why producers will first have to identify consumer needs through market research before touching on other aspects of the market.
