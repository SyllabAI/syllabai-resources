**3(d)** 

A multinational company is a business which has operations in more than one country and generates revenue beyond its home country’s borders. 

One advantage of FR being a multinational is access to cheap labor. In many developing nations, wage rates are low as workers are unskilled and looking to work. This can allow FR to have access to cheap labor, which can reduce its total costs and increase profit margins for the hotel and its owners. 

Another reason why being a multinational company is advantageous to FR is because it may give it access to government grants. Since MNCs create employment for their host country, governments may be incentivized to help FR with grants, as its hotels will create activity in the economy. These grants can be used to fund the expansion or may help improve the hotel's cash flow. 

 

**3(e)** 

One way a cash flow forecast may be used is in the process of loan applications. To apply for loans, businesses must show banks that they are in a good position to repay the loans in the future, and that they are equipped with sufficient cash. A cashflow forecast will help prove to the bank that the business has a stable flow of cash entering the business daily. 

 

A cashflow forecast will also allow managers to see if the business is likely or not to run out of cash. If the cashflow forecast for a certain period shows that outflows are much higher than inflows, it can help managers to take necessary decisions to improve the cashflow and increase the cash in the business to avoid becoming illiquid. 

The most important function of cashflow forecasts is to show if the business is likely to run out of cash. If there is not enough cash in the business, managers will be unable to pay day to day expenses such as wages. This situation must be prevented beforehand and is only possible through monitoring the cashflow forecast.
