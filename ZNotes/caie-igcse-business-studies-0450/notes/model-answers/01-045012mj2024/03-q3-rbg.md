**(e)**

One way a pressure group may try to influence business activity is through customer boycotts. This is when customers stop purchasing a business’s product or service, in order to persuade them into stopping their activity. Boycotts can lead to little to no sales, and falls in revenue, and thus, may pressurize a business to stop committing the activity.

Another way a pressure group may try to influence business activity is through raising awareness on the effect of the business activity. This may be through publicly speaking on it and creating negative publicity for the business, in an attempt to make customers turn against the business activity. This can ruin a business’s reputation and therefore may make them more willing to reduce their harmful activity.

A boycott may be the most effective method of influencing business activity as it directly reduces the business’s sales and revenue. This can lead to business failure and thus, may be more impactful than raising awareness on the business’s activity, especially if customers do not see the negative publicity/are unaffected by it.
