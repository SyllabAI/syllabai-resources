Manufacturing businesses may benefit a great deal from using flow production lines. This is because flow production can allow a business to experience economies of scale, where the average costs fall due to fixed costs being more spread out over production. This can allow for a higher profit margin for manufacturing businesses.

Furthermore, production can be more continuous using flow production, allowing manufacturing businesses to better maximize their productivity.

However, flow production may also cause problems for manufacturing businesses. Flow production may lead to demotivated employees as most of the production process is automated. This may lead to higher levels of absenteeism as employees get bored of managing the flow production lines.

Overall, the advantages of flow production are greater than the disadvantages to a manufacturing business. The incidence of economies of scale can help boost the manufacturing business’s sales if unit costs lead to price reductions. This can increase demand for the manufactured products and increase revenues.
