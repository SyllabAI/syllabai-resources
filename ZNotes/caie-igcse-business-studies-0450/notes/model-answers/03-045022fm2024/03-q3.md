**3(a)** 

Way 1: Decreases the cost of imports 

Explanation: the appreciation of the exchange rate will allow country X to buy more goods from abroad at a lower price. This makes AF’s imported materials, which makes up 90% of its ingredients, cheaper. This can reduce AF’s variable costs significantly, and increase the profit margin as the costs of production fall. AF’s meals may also become more competitive in comparison to other competitors' street stall meals, as they can price lower than other street food vendors and attract sales.

 

Way 2: More income to spend on other projects 

Explanation: As AF’s costs fall due to the fall in price of its imported ingredients, it may have more income from selling meals left over to invest into other products or projects. Therefore, in the long run, there will be more income left to spend on investing in the new technology, buying more equipment, and developing the new range of meals. 

 

**3(b)** 

Investing in new technology: investing in new technology can allow AF to greatly increase its productivity, and produce more meals each day. This can increase sales if all the output is sold, which is possible considering the high demand in the Main city for AF’s meals. The 10 employees may also be more motivated to work using machines as they can develop their training to use them, and boring jobs can be automated. Furthermore, the meals may be more consistent in nature, with exact ingredients and portions due to being automated, which may lead to higher returns on investment if the better-quality meals bring in more sales. Nevertheless, investing in technology is highly expensive– and would require AF to finance the 50,000$ through external sources, which may rack up debts and interest on the business. 

 

Buying more of their existing equipment: existing equipment may help to maintain the traditional taste of the meals enjoyed by people from country A– therefore it can help keep sales stable as the meals may not change in taste in the eyes of the customer. Additionally, buying existing equipment is much cheaper than investing in new technology, which makes it easier to pay back in case a loan is taken, and lower interest will have to be paid. However, existing technology may not be as efficient or productive as new technology–which may result in AF not experiencing any gains from purchasing it.

 

Recommendation: AF should buy its existing equipment. This will ensure that the traditional aspect of its meals is not changed– which can better guarantee that the 100,000 people from country A are more likely to continue purchasing and enjoying it. It can also ensure that employees do not feel discouraged from high automation, which they may feel might replace their jobs.
