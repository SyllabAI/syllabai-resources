## Joint Venture

* **Joint Venture**: is when two or more businesses work together on a project, sharing costs, risks, and profits, while staying independent.


|**Advantages**|**Disadvantages**|
|:---|:---|
|Sharing of costs|Profits have to be shared if the project is successful|
|Knowledge and experience can be shared|Conflict in decision-making|
|Risks shared|Different methods of running a business can create conflict|
