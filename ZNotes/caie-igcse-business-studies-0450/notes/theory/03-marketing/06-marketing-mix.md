## Marketing Mix

* **Marketing Mix**: a term used to describe all the activities that go into marketing a product or service.
* The marketing mix can be summed up as the 4 Ps:
  * **P**roduct - applies to the product or service. Design, features and quality.
  * **P**rice - the price at which the product is sold, comparisons between prices of competitors.
  * **P**lace - channel of distribution that is selected.
  * **P**romotion - how the productivity is advertised and promoted.
* **You should always mention the 4 Ps when answering questions about Marketing Mix!**
