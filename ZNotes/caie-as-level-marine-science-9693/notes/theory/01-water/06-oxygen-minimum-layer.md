## Oxygen Minimum Layer

![PPT - Chapter 16 The Ocean Depths PowerPoint Presentation, free ...](https://tse2.mm.bing.net/th/id/OIP.TWK1P6mWljaE8gfcwahM5wHaFj?r=0&pid=Api =436x)

* Oxygen minimum layer is layer of the ocean where the concentration of dissolved oxygen changes dramatically.

* This typically occurs at a depth of around 500m (usually between 100m to 1000m).

* The reason for this layer is due to the lack of photosynthesis

  * since little to no producers are there due to lack of sunlight passing through,

  * paired with high respiration rate from decomposition and existing organisms.

* After passing this O2 minimum layer, DO conc starts to increase because..

  * Decomposition has been completed & there is less respiration carried out by bacteria.

  * Organisms below that layer have adapted to respire less.

  * Low temperature makes O2 stay dissolved.

  * High increasing pressure increases oxygen solubility.
