## **Effect of Salinity on Density**

![Satellite Applications for Geoscience Education](https://cimss.ssec.wisc.edu/sage/oceanography/lesson4/images/sm\_salinity\_depth.jpg =423x)

* As the salinity of water increases, the density increases.

* So fresh water sits on saltier water.

* There is an area where salinity changes significantly with depth called the halocline.

* In non-tropical oceans, the halocline can be in the reverse direction,

* since a greater rate of precipitation than evaporation can result in lower salinities at the surface
