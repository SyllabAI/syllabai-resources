## **The Solubility of Gases In Water**

* Gases in the atmosphere (nitrogen, O2, CO2) are in a state of equilibrium with the gasses dissolved in ocean water,

* if the concentration of gas in air is changed so will it in ocean water.

* Turbulence and wave action helps mix and maintain the equilibrium.

* More turbulence = easier dissolution of gas.

### Gas Solubility

* CO2 is very soluble in seawater due to its ability to form carbonic acid (a weak acid) when introduced to water.

* While O2 has low solubility since it doesn’t chemically combine with the water molecules. So, the level of CO2 in seawater is higher than O2.

### Water Temperature

* Cold water can dissolve more gas than warm water due to warmer water having more kinetic energy, allowing dissolved gas to escape by evaporation much more easily at a quicker pace from the water surface. 

* So, water found near the poles will dissolve more O2 than water in the tropics.

### Atmospheric Pressure

* The solubility of gases increases with increasing pressure 

* there is greater concentration of gas in the atmosphere

* This pushes more of those gas molecules to dissolve in the seawater and stay dissolved

### Water pressure due to depth

* The solubility of gases increases the deeper we dive into the ocean

*  because the pressure of water above you continues to increase.

### Salinity of Seawater

* The solubility of gases increases with lower levels of salinity 

* since there is more space for gas molecules to interact with water and dissolve in them.
