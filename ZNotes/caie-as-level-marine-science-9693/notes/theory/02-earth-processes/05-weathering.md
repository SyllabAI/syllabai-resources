## Weathering

* The breaking down of rocks into smaller pieces over time due to exposure to the atmosphere, moving water, and living organisms.

* Chemical weathering

  * occurs when the chemical composition of rock changes through exposure to water or oxygen, especially when it rains, the water can dissolve ions from the rock, particularly Calcium and transport them to the ocean as run-off

* Physical Weathering

  * when the rocks are broken into smaller pieces without changing their chemical composition. 

  * The most prominent method is temperature change where its changes can cause the rocks to expand  and crack. Then these cracks can be filled with water which further breaks the rock when it freezes as it would expand the cracks. 

  * Another way is through ocean waves.

* Organic (biological) weathering

  * Combination of physical weathering and chemical weathering but done by living organisms.

  * **Lichen →** Attaches to basaltic rock and releases a special compound that breaks down the rocks into smaller particles.

  * **Roots →** of a tree can grow into the cracks of rocks which then slowly forces itself deeper to the rocks, eventually breaking it apart.
