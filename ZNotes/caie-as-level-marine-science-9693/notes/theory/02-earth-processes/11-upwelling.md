## Upwelling

* **Upwelling**

  * The movement of cold, nutrient-rich water from the seabed vertically to the ocean’s surface

* **Process**

  * Winds blow offshore from the coasts.

  * Warmer surface water is pushed offshore by the wind.

  * Colder water moves from the seabed to the surface.

  * This deeper water is nutrient-rich due to organic material sinking to the bottom.

  * This process brings the nutrients up to the surface where they can be used by producers.

* A mid-ocean ridge or a sea mount can deflect a cold water current upwards, causing upwelling too.
