## Sedimentation

* When minerals reach their new location and are left there to accumulate through the process of *deposition* 

* Particle size and water speed have the greatest impacts on the rates of sedimentation. 

* In general, the faster the water is moving, the larger the size of the particle it can carry. 

* As water begins to slow, larger particles begin to fall out of suspension and deposit themselves. 

* water’s ability to carry sediments and how it deposits them is what gives it the ability to help shape coastlines.
