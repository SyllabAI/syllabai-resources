## Ocean Features

* **Trenches** → Long, narrow, deep canyons in the seabed 

  * only form at the subduction zones within convergent boundaries due to these processes.

  * Other common features along the subduction zones are volcanoes and tsunamis.

* **Volcanoes** → …

  * Can be formed at a convergent boundary when pressure builds up between the oceanic and continental plate which creates a pressure point that thins out the Earth's crust then allowing gasses and magma to rise through the opening creating an eruption.

  * Can also form at divergent boundary when plates separates, creating new seafloor and the magma builds up as solidified volcano from continued build up.

* **Earthquakes** → …

  * Occurs after there has been a sudden release of energy from the movement of the Earth’s crust.

  * When two plates are moving past each other, they can get stuck, building up pressure for them to move until there is a sudden slippage of one of the plates, releasing the stored potential energy in a sudden burst that releases seismic waves through the lithosphere, shaking everything.

* **Tsunamis** → …

  * Long-wavelength, high energy waves, created by seismic activity, happen due to sudden release of energy on the seabed, either through an earthquake or volcanic eruption.

  * At first, an earthquake happens, releasing energy to the seabed.

  * Then the energy displaces a large volume of water lying about the seafloor.

  * The water holds on to the energy and then begins to move very quickly but noticeably through deep water.

  * However, as the tsunami reaches shallow, coastal waters, the wave slows and grows in height exponentially.

* **Mid-Ocean Ridges** → Underwater mountain ranges 

  * built over time as magma pushes through the weakened crust at these boundaries, and solidifies, creating new rocks at the seafloor.

* **Hydrothermal Vents** → Structure formed in deep ocean water, within a mid-ocean ridges system, that provides nutrients at deep depths of the ocean as sunlight can’t pass through making the absence of producers.

  * Hydrothermal vents are formed when cold ocean water seeps through cracks in the thin crust surrounding divergent boundaries.

  * As the water moves through the crust to an area directly over a magma chamber, getting superheated, it dissolves minerals from the rocks, turning it black.

  * Once heated by the magma, the water reaches temperatures well over 100 °C but never boils because of the extreme pressure from above.

  * This superheated water then escapes the crust through a fissure.

  * As the superheated water meets the near-freezing water on the ocean floor, it begins to cool.

  * As the water cools, minerals including ions of copper, sulfides, zinc, and iron, precipitate out of the solution, forming solids, settling near the fissure, piling on top of each other due to their lack of solubility.

  * This forms a chimney.

  * Organisms, particularly worm larvae, rely on locating the unique chemical signatures of a vent plume when attempting to settle in a new colony.

  * Hydrothermal vents can be classified as “black smokers” white smokers” based on the color of their plume.

  * Also the area surrounding the hydrothermal vent is highly acidic due to the high presence of minerals.

* **Abyssal Plains** → Flat deep areas of the ocean floor that are essentially feature-less

  * found at transform boundaries or in the wide expanse of ocean floor between boundaries.

  * They are a result of ocean floor spreading.

  * Basically the ocean floor is covered by sedimentation which leads to the smooth flat appearance of the abyssal plain.
