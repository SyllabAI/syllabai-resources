## Erosion

* The movement and transportation of rocks and minerals by natural agents to new locations

* Ice Erosion

  * closely associated with glaciers. When glaciers move over land, its weight crushes and breaks the rocks below, weathering them. However the sediments that are formed are carried within the bottom of the glaciers as it moves, depositing them as it drifts.

* Gravity erosion

  * When chunks of rocks of a cliff drop down to the ravine or ocean below due to gravitational forces

* Wind erosion

  * When wind blows, picking up small pieces of sand or sediment, carrying it to new locations.

* Water erosion

  * It's able to pick up sediments and carry them through runoff or down rivers on the way to the ocean. An example is a canyon. The water works both as an erosion agent and a weathering agent.
