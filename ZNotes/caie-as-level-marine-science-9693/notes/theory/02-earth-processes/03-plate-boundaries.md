## Plate Boundaries

* Lithosperic tectonic plates are only able to move if the mantle is moving as well. 

* The movement of the mantle is caused by convection currents within the mantle where the molten rocks move because of density changes caused by temperature differences. 

* Magma gets heated → less dense so it rises, magma cools down → more dense it sinks. Then as the magma sinks, it will get heated as the centre of the Earth is really hot.

* Types of plate boundaries:

  * **Convergent Plate Boundaries (Destructive)** → Forms when two tectonic plates move towards each other. 

    * When the two plates are of varying densities, a subduction zone will form and usually happens due to when a denser oceanic plate is forced underneath the other, less dense, continental plate. 

    * This can create trenches.

  * **Divergent Plate Boundaries (Constructive)** → Forms when two tectonic plates are moving away from each other, allowing magma from the mantle to push through to the crust, creating a new seafloor as it solidifies from the cold ocean water. T

    * he new rocks eventually build up and form underwater mountain ranges called mid-ocean ridges which contain volcanoes and hydrothermal vents.

  * **Transform Plate Boundaries** → Forms when two plates slide laterally next to each other. 

    * This neither constructs nor destroys, but it has high seismic activity due to the increased friction between plates, causing small cracks and pressure build ups.
