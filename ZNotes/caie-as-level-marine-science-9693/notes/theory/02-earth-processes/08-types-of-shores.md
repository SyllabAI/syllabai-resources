## Types of Shores

* Rocky shores

  * Characterized by the presence of a rocky substrate that has variants of slopes from nearly vertical cliff faces to wide flat expanses of rocks.

  * The size of the rocks also varies from very large boulders to much smaller pieces of gravel and pebbles.

  * They’re usually made up of granite or igneous rocks, which are incredibly resistant to weathering and take much longer time to break down than softer rocks such as sandstone.

  * The largest rocks usually sit farthest from the water at the high-water mark and the smallest rocks are nearer the low- water mark.

  * Erosion rate → High

  * Sedimentation → very little

* Sandy shores

  * Made up of loose deposits of sand, small pieces of gravel. and shells.

  * Sandy shores are formed by the erosion of sandstone and sedimentation of sand particles by waves.

  * Sandy shores are constantly in motion as the wind and ocean moves the sand up and down the beach.

  * These shores tend to have a gradual slope towards the ocean.

* Muddy shores

  * Found in protected regions and are least exposed to weathering and erosion, allowing deposition of very fine silt particles and organic materials.

  * Usually a shallow slope or no slope at all, that’s why they’re called mud-flats.

  * tHe biggest factor that forms this shore is sedimentation.

* Estuaries

  * It forms in partially enclosed areas where fresh water and salt water meets (the mix is called brackish).

  * Since they’re sheltered from weathering and erosion action of waves, the bottom is usually made of fine sand and silt that falls out of suspension.

  * the water in estuaries are murky with high turbidity

* Deltas

  * Form at the mouth of a river where it meets the sea, forming a triangular shape as at its mouth, it picks up sediments along the way through erosion.

  * THe sediments the deposits and forms landmasses.
