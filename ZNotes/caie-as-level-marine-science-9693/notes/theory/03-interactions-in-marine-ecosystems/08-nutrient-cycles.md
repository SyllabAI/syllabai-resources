
## Nutrient Cycles

* Nutrient cycle show the essential movement and recycling of the elements that are necessary for organisms to live and grow.

* All nutrient cycles have a biotic and an abiotic phase like what is shown.

### The Biotic Phase 

* In the carbon cycle, CO2 is fixed during photosynthesis into glucose. This glucose can then  be made into starch by producers.

* Carbon exists as CO2 in abiotic phase but is in the form of carbohydrate, lipids, and proteins when in biotic phase.

### The Abiotic Phase 

* After death, organisms must be broken down by decomposers, which results in nutrients returning to their inorganic form and the abiotic part of the cycle. They can be found as:

  * Ions dissolved in water (Mg2\+, CO32-, PO43-, NO3-)

  * Gases in the atmosphere (CO2)

  * Forming sediment that can later become rocks
