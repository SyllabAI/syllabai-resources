## Productivity

* → Rate of production of new biomass per unit area by autotrophic organisms by either photosynthesis or chemosynthesis

### Photosynthesis

* Photoautotrophic organisms that uses light energy to produce biomass/their own food by photosynthesis under the equation:

  * Carbon Dioxide \+ Water → Glucose \+ Oxygen

  * With the help of Chlorophyll & light energy

* Factors Affecting Photosynthesis

  * Temperature and carbon dioxide.
    * There’s always water in marine environments and they contain dissolved CO2

    * Although the reactions of photosynthesis are affected by the temperature, the temperature in each area in the ocean is very stable and so has little effect of the rate of photosynthesis.
  * Nutrients
    * A lack of nutrients will cause a ↓ rate of productivity as it ↓ the rate of growth.
  * Light
    * Photosynthesis only takes place in the photic layer where light can penetrate, so most biomass is in this layer.

    * Waves affect the light penetration since it can reflect and focus it.

    * Additionally, light travels slower in water and the solid particles within the water can also scatter or focus the light.

* The sunlight can also increase temperature, ↑ kinetic energy in water and also ↓ density, thus it would lay on top of cold dense water allowing thermocline or pycnocline which causes lack of mixing between the two layers. The buoyant water also allows the phytoplankton to stay on the photic layer. However, this thermocline prevents nutrients from going up, so there’s upwelling to help with that. Basically longer day — ↑ productivity, longer night — ↓ productivity.

### **Chemosynthesis**

* A process where carbon dioxide is turned into usable organic molecules using the energy stored in dissolved chemicals such as hydrogen sulfide by chemoautotrophs.

* Chemicals dissolve in heated water under sea crust as it makes its way back to the surface to  emerge from the vents.

* Up to 75% of organisms near the hydrothermal vents depend on mutualistic relationships with chemosynthetic bacteria to get food.

### **Respiration**

* Respiration is the process of living things releasing chemical energy stored in organic molecules such as carbohydrates. 

* The equation is: Glucose \+ Oxygen → Carbon Dioxide \+ Water

### The link between photosynthesis and respiration

* The cycle of cellular respiration and photosynthesis is essential for the survival of humans, animals, and plants. 

* Humans and other animals breathe in oxygen that plants make during photosynthesis so that the blood may carry it to the cells where it is used for respiration. 

* Plants absorb the carbon dioxide that is emitted from the body during respiration and use it to assist fuel their growth and development.

* Secondary production is the rate of production of new biomass by consumers, using the energy gained by eating producers.

* More energy from primary productivity = longer food chain
