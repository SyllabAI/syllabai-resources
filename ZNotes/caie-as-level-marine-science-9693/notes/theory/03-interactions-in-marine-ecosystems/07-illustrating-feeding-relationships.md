## Illustrating Feeding Relationships

* Producers are always at the bottom, followed by the primary consumer, up until the apex consumer.

### Pyramid of Numbers

* Shows the number of organisms present in each trophic level at a particular moment in time.

### Pyramid of Biomass

* Measurement of each organism’s biomass changes a lot especially when phytoplankton get eaten but they also reproduce very quickly.

### Pyramid of Energy

* Shows the rate of production of biomass rather than the standing crop, so it's always pyramid shaped as energy decreases up the trophic level.

### Pyramid of Number, Biomass, and Energy During an Algal Bloom

* At first, the producer's numbers, biomass and energy would increase, so does the next trophic level follows, until it reaches hypoxic conditions which kills animals, decreasing the animals number, energy, and biomass.

* During algal bloom, the phytoplankton mostly grows inedible parts called cyanobacteria, thus passing along less available energy This causes a shorter food chain
