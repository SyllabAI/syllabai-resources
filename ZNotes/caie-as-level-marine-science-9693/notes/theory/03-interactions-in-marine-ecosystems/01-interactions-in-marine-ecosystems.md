# **INTERACTIONS IN MARINE ECOSYSTEMS**

## Interactions

**Parasitism**

* Where the symbiont benefits but the host is harmed

* Copepods And Marine Fish

  * Copepods are the symbiont whereas marine fish is the host, thus the marine fish is harmed by the copepods.

  * One of the most popular line of copepods are sea lice, and the most prominent types are Lepeoptheirus salmonis and Caligus elongatus.

  * These sea lice are ectoparasites which means they live on the outside of their host while endoparasites (tapeworms) are found inside the host.

  * These sea lice feed on mucus, tissues, and blood that are essential to keep marine fish alive, thus it would lead to the deaths of hosts, especially when they are attached at a juvenile host’s gills in abundant amounts.

* Even at small amounts, the sea lice can cause infections that cause other diseases such as salmon anemia which cause big losses to fish farming.

  ### Commensalism

* Where the symbiont benefits and the host remains unaffected

* Manta Rays and Remora

  * Remora fish (suckerfish) has a modified dorsal fin that allows it to create suction and attach itself to smooth surfaces such as manta ray’s skin.

  * This relationship is usually known as phoresis where the symbiont uses the host for transport.

  * In this case, the Manta ray is unaffected but the remora is benefitted as it uses less energy to travel.

* It can become mutualistic as remora might eat some parasites off of Manta ray, but it can also be parasitic as it makes Manta rays exert more energy and be more vulnerable to predators due to decreased camouflage.

  **Mutualism**

* Where both organisms benefit from the relationship

* Boxer Crabs and Anemones

  * Anemones have stinging cells called cnidocytes on their tentacles which are then put in use by the crab as a self defence mechanism.

  * Then the anemone also gains easy access to food by being able to pick up debris and food from the crab’s hiding space as well as being fed by the crab.

  * If a crab doesn’t have an anemone, it will try to steal it from another crab.
