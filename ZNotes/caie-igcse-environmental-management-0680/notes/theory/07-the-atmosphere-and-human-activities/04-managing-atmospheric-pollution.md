## Managing Atmospheric Pollution

### **Reduction of carbon footprint:**

* **Carbon footprint:** a measure of the impact of our activities on
  the environment.

### **Reduced use of fossil fuels:**

* Low-sulfur coal can be used
* Increased use of renewable energy

### **Energy efficiency:**

* ### Using energy-efficient appliances

### **Carbon capture and storage:**

* Waste carbon dioxide from power stations can be transported via
  pipelines to storage sites

### **Transport policies:**

* Creation of cycle lanes, bus lanes, metro systems and trams

* Electric or hybrid cars can be encouraged

* Biofuels can be used

* Vehicles can be banned from certain parts of the city by pedestrianisation

* Public transport and residential parking can be made free

* Particularly effective in Singapore to reduce cars on the road:

  * A combination of higher vehicle tax

  * Electronic road pricing (ERP)

  * An efficient, cheap mass rapid transport (MRT) system

### **International agreement and policies:**

* Policies such as the Montreal Protocol, Kyoto Protocol and Paris Climate The conference can be passed on worldwide.

* An international cooperation is required.

#### Montreal Protocol:

* A global agreement to protect the stratospheric ozone layer by agreeing to limit the production and use of ozone-depleting substances

* The treaty was originally signed in 1987 in Montreal, Canada.

* The protocol has proven innovative and successful.

#### Kyoto protocol:

* An international agreement that called for industrialized nations to significantly reduce their greenhouse gas emissions.

* Adopted in Kyoto, Japan in 1997, and entered into force in 2005.

* Laid the groundwork for future climate agreements like the Paris Agreement

#### Paris Agreement:

In 2016, 117 countries committed to the following:

* Limit global temperature increase to below 2˚C above pre-industrial levels.

* Increases global adaptation to a changing climate and to lower greenhouse gas emissions.

* Finance low greenhouse gas development

* Cap greenhouse gas emissions as soon as possible

### **CFC replacement:**

* Reduction in the use of CFCs
* Hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs) can be used as an alternative
* Safe disposal of items containing CFCs

### **Taxation:**

* Higher road tax to decrease car ownership

### **Catalytic converters:**

* Catalytic converters in vehicles reduce sulfur dioxide emissions
* They also convert nitrogen dioxide and carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide and nitrogen
* Selective catalytic reduction (SCR) - converting nitrogen oxides with the help of a catalyst and urea or ammonia into diatomic nitrogen and water.
* Low-sulfur vehicle fuels can also be used.

### **Flue-gas desulfurisation:**

* Scrubbers can be used to remove 95% of sulfur dioxide emissions
* Lining chimneys by using $CaCO_3$ and water, which converts $SO_2$ into $CaSO_4$

### **Reforestation and afforestation:**

* **Reforestation:** replanting an area with trees
* **Afforestation:** planting trees in a barren land
