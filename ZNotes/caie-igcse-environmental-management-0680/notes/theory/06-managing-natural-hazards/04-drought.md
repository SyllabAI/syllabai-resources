## Drought

* Drought is when there is a lack of rain or less rain than normal over a long period of time.

* Most droughts occur when regular weather patterns are interrupted and evaporation exceeds precipitation.

**Causes of Drought**

* Changes in atmospheric circulation patterns altering storm tracks and wind patterns

* Air in a high-pressure system sinks and doesn’t form rain clouds.

* **Effect of El Niño Southern Oscillation and La Niña:**

  * **El Niño** occurs when Pacific trade winds weaken, shifting warm surface water east toward South America, where it causes heavy rainfall and flooding, while leaving cooler water near Australia, causing high air pressure, sinking air, and severe droughts for about 9 to 12 months.
  * **La** **Niña** is an intensification of normal conditions where stronger trade winds push warm water far west, bringing torrential rains and flooding to Australia, while leaving unusually cold water along South America that suppresses cloud formation and causes prolonged droughts lasting 1 to 3 years.

* **Human activity can influence the effect of the drought in a region:**

  * Agriculture practices can make **land vulnerable to drought**. Irrigation causes a dependency on water, **soil compaction** (ability to hold water) is effect by overcultivation and overgrazing. **Makes soil drier, vulnerable to erosion and eventually desertification.**
  * **Deforestation**, decreases soil infiltration and increases soil erosion.
  * Building of dams on larger rivers, causes drought downstream due to reduction of water flow.
