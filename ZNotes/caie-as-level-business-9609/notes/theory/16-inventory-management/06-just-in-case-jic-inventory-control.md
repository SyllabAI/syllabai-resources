## **Just-in-Case (JIC) Inventory Control**

This management system targets to lower the risk of running out of inventory to the minimum by holding high buffer inventory levels.




| Benefits of JIC inventory management | Drawbacks of JIC inventory management |
|----|----|
| JIC makes it highly unlikely to run out of inventory, so production can continue even with major supply delays.  | High capital cost as finance is required to invest in inventories |
| Lower requirement to forecast sales as accurately as with JIT due to high inventory level | Other costs such as space, holding and other variables associated with inventory, increasing overall expense of business |
| Takes advantage of purchasing economics of scale by ordering supplies in large quantities, lowering average supply cost | Inventories are highly likely to lose its value due to potential change in fashion or technology |
