## How Market Research assists NPD

| New Product Development | Correlation to Market research |
|----|----|
| Identify consumer needs and likings | Primary and Secondary research into consumer needs and potential competition |
| Product vision and packaging design | Experimenting the product and packaging with consumer group |
| Brand positioning and trial of advertisement | Pre-testing the product image and promotion |
| Product launch and after-launch period | Overviewing sales and consumer feedback |
