## The Market Research Process

1. Identify the problem and define the objective 
   * Identify the purpose of the research to ensure unnecessary data is not collected. 
   * After identifying the problem, research objectives are set. These are always in the form of questions.
2. Determine research design
   * Deciding whether to use primary research secondary research or a mix of both
3. Design and prepare the research instrument 
   * Identifying the most suitable method of data collection in terms of cost and time.
4. Sampling and collecting data 
   * Choosing a sample size and method
5. Analyse data 
6. Visualise and communicate results 
   * Representing the data in the forms of bar graphs, pie charts, line graphs, etc.
