## Outsourcing

**Outsourcing**: Using another (a third party) to undertake a part of the production process rather than doing it within the business using the firm’s own employees.


|Advantages|Disadvantages|
|:---|:---|
|Outsourcing is usually cheaper than hiring full-time specialists and leverages economies of scale.|Outsourcing can negatively impact employee motivation and public image might be questioned due to possible redundancies and ethical standards as business might from low-wage economies.|
|It turns fixed costs to variable costs and allows for flexibility for the business depending on demand.|Due to hiring workers from low-wage economies, can undermine CSR commitments regarding workers and the environment.|
|This method can provide high-quality services or resources which might not be available internally.|Outsourced IT functions can pose security risks and accountability issues for lost data.|
|They helps the management to concentrate on core activities by outsourcing function such as HR or finance.|Customers may have concerns about foreign call centers and outsourced components affecting quality of the service/good they purchase, lowering their loyalty.|
|It frees up resources which can be used for other business improvements.|It is difficult to monitor outsourced quality. Therefore, the business need quality control on-site to ensure quality being up to the highest standards.|
