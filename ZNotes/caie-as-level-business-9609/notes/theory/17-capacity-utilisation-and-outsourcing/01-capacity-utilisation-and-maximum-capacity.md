# Capacity Utilisation and Outsourcing

## Capacity Utilisation and Maximum Capacity

* Maximum capacity refers to the total possible level of output that can be undertaken/sustained by a business in a given time period.
* Capacity utilisation refers to the proportion of maximum output capacity currently being achieved. It measures the efficiency of the business usage of its resources.

The following formula rate of capacity utilisation measures exactly that:

$ \text{Capacity Utilization Formula} = \frac{\text{Current Output Level}}{\text{Maximum Output Level}} \times 100$
