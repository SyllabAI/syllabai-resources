## Cashflow forecast

Estimation of future cash inflow and outflow of a firm.

### Why do business requires them for their business plan?

* Acquiring finance: Shows well thought out planning done by firm, thus firms are more lenient to give loan to starts up.
* It makes the banks/investor thinks the chances of success are higher than they would think without forecast.
* Their credit given by supplier is shorter so forecasting helps gather the sum for payment.

Closing balance: cash held by a business at the end of a month after cash flow calculation.

It becomes next month’s opening balance such as 5,800$ for May.

Opening balance: cash kept by the business at the beginning of the month.

### Benefit and Drawback of Cashflow Forecast:


|Benefits|Limitations|
|:---|:---|
|They show possible negative net cash flow, thus allowing business to find reason of such to fix the issue or gather additional finance.|Unexpected cost increase due to changes in variables, leading to inaccuracies in the forecast.|
|By indicating possible negative cash flow in a time period, business can plan to minimize the negative net flow.|Incorrect assumption regarding sales/cost of sales by the business can occur.|
|They are essential for all business plans, without it, acquiring finance by lenders are exhaustingly hard.|Mistakes can be made by the person making the forecast, leading to inaccuracies.|
