## Cause of Cashflow Problems




* Lack of planning in cases of business not efficiently forecasting out cash flow management, it leads to higher unexpected cost and other negative factors.
* If a business keeps loosely/ill sufficient record of cash flow, then they might not chase trade receivable and can receive trouble for late trade payable.
*  Many firms allow trade credit to be competitive in the market. This is beneficial although if the credit goes for example, 2 month long, it leads to lower short-term cash inflow and increases the likelihood of bad debt.
* Overtrading/expanding a business rapidly without obtaining all of the necessary finance thus a cash flow shortage develops.
*  Unforeseen cost due to variable factor such as demand varying too much.
