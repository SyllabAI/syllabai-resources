## How new Merger/Takeover can achieve their objectives:

* Better results due to sharing of research and ideas between the now integrated business.
* Achievement of economies of scale, reducing average cost.
* Usage of same sale outlet and team increasing effectiveness of cost-saving and decreasing need of high budget for marketing team.
* It helps decrease expenses through means of rationalisation of  assets.

### Why new Merger/Takeover can fail to achieve their objectives:

* The combined business experiences diseconomies of scale because of becoming too big thus way harder to manage.
* The custom and culture of integrated business may vary significantly which can hamper its coordination.
* Small advantages might be seen from the research and marketing facilities if business originally produced products different from the integrating business.
* Directors might find rapid growth rate too hard to operate effectively.
