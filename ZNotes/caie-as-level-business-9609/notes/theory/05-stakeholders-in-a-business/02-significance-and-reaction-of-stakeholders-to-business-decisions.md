## Significance and Reaction of Stakeholders to Business Decisions:


| Business Decision | Stakeholder Response | Employees | Local Community | Customers |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Build new factory to expand business** | **Impact** | Job opportunities | More employment for local residents and rise in spending in local businesses | Greater efficiency leading to lower prices |
|    |    | New working method in new factory requiring new skills | Possible disruption  due to increased pollution and traffic levels | Concern about whether the focus will shift from quality to quantity. |
|    | **Reaction** | Increased number of job seekers | May seek to deny planning permission | Will purchase more products if prices decrease while maintaining the same quality |
|    |    | Trade unions might push for higher wages for more skilled work | Might impose restrictions on large trucks |    |
|    |    |    | May organize a petition or boycott |    |
| **Horizon Integration/Takeover** | **Impact** | The merged company could become more stable and provide increased career advancement opportunities | Expansion on the existing site could lead to more local jobs and higher incomes | Economies of scale could result in lower prices |
|    |    | Rationalization aimed at reducing waste and cutting costs might lead to job losses | Rationalization of overlapping facilities might cause closures and job losses | Reduced competition might lead to higher prices and fewer choices |
|    | **Reaction** | There may be industrial action if job security is compromised | The community might urge the government to block the takeover if it threatens job losses and facility closures | Customers could respond with a boycott if prices increase due to decreased competition |
| **Purchase of IT-controlled automated machines** | **Impact** | Training and advancement opportunities may be provided | Local IT service providers might see increased orders | More efficient and adaptable production processes could lead to improved quality and a wider range of products |
|    |    | Fewer untrained workers will be needed; those unable to acquire new skills could face redundancy | There will be a need for skilled workers, reducing the number of unskilled job opportunities | Issues with IT reliability might result in supply delays |
|    | **Reaction** | Workers facing redundancy might take industrial action | There may be calls for retraining programs for the unemployed who lack skills | Increased demand may follow if the product quality remains high |
