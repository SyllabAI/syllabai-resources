# Stakeholders in a business

## Introduction

* Stakeholders: People or groups of people that are affected by, and therefore have an interest in the activities of a business.

* Stakeholder concept: The view that businesses and their managers have responsibilities to a wide range of groups, not just their shareholders.

### Customers

* Roles:

  * **To buy goods and services**

  * **To give revenue from sales**, which allows the business to function and expand

* Rights:

  * **Obtaining goods and services** that meet local laws regarding health and safety, design and performance

  * **To be offered replacements, repairs or compensation** if the product or service fails the minimum levels laid down by law

* Responsibilities:

  * **Honesty** – Payment for goods bought or services received when requested

  * **Not committing theft**

  * **Not to make false claims** about poor service, underperforming goods or failed items, to tarnish business's reputation out of personal spite

### Suppliers

* Roles: **To supply goods and services** to allow the business to offer its products to its own customers

* Rights:

  * **On-time payment** as discussed or written in the service agreement between the business and suppliers

  * **Fair treatment** and not to be exploited by the customer business

* Responsibilities: **Supply goods and services ordered** by the business in the time and condition agreed upon by the purchase contract or supplier’s service agreements

### Employees

* Roles:

  * **Provide manual and other labor services** to the business, in accordance with the employment contract, to help in production of goods and services to be supplied to customers

* Rights:

  * **Having an employment contract** that meets legal standards, e.g. minimum wage rate

  * **Treatment and payment** in accordance with agreement on the employment contract

  * In most countries, **to be allowed to join a trade union** if desired without repercussions

* Responsibilities:

  * **Meeting the conditions and responsibilities** of the employment contract

  * **Ability to cooperate with management** in all reasonable requests

  * **Observance the ethical code of conduct** of the business

### Local Community

* Roles: **Observance the ethical code of conduct** of the business; provides labor for the business.

* Rights:

  * **To be consulted** about major changes that affect it

  * E.g. expansion plans or changing methods of production

* Responsibilities: **To cooperate with the business**, where reasonable to do so, on expansion and other plans

### Local Government

* Roles: **To provide local services and infrastructure** to the business to allow it to operate, produce and sell within legal limits

* Rights: **Not to have the community’s lives badly affected** by the business’s activities

* Responsibilities: **To meet reasonable requests** from the business for local services such as public transport for employees and waste disposal

### Government

* Roles:

  * **To pass laws** that restrain many aspects of business activity

  * **To provide law and order** and economic stability to allow business activity to take place

* Rights: **To expect the business to meet all legal constraints**, such as producing only legal goods and to pay taxes on time

* Responsibilities:

  * **To treat businesses equally** under the law

  * **To prevent unfair competition** that could damage chances of business survival

  * **To establish good trading links** with other countries to allow international trade

### Lenders

* Roles: **To provide finance** to the business in different forms

* Rights:

  * **To be repaid on the agreed date**

  * **To be paid finance charges**, e.g. interest on loans

* Responsibilities: **To provide the agreed amount of finance** on the agreed date for the agreed time period

### Managers:

* Roles: **To control, command and direct resources**

* Rights:

  * To have a contract of employment

  * To have sufficient authority to fulfill roles

* Responsibilities:

  * To report to stakeholders

  * To act legally and ethically

### Owners/Shareholders

* Roles: **To give business access to sufficient finance**

* Rights:

  * To receive a share of the profits

  * To receive accurate reports on business performance

* Responsibilities:

  * **Set targets for managers**, ideally on the basis of SMART criteria

  * To give managers adequate time and resources to meet targets
