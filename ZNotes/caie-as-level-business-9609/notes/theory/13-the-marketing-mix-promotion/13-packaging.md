## Packaging 

* The quality, design and colour of packaging play an important role in promotion 
* Cheap and nasty packaging may destroy the brand image 
* Unique packaging can help the brand form necessary basis of promotional theme, such as Audi cars are identified through their 4 ring on the front of a car
* Wasteful expenditure on packaging alongside rise in environmental concern and creation of pressure groups might lead to negative publicity if it's packaging is deemed too pretentious or polluting. This will even reduce the product’s competitiveness 
* Packaging decisions must be blended in with the overall objectives of the business 
* Functions –
  * Protect the product 
  * Give important information 
  * Support the brand image
  * Aid customer recognition
