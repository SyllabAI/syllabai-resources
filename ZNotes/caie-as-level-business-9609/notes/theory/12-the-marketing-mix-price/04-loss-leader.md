## Loss Leader

* Retailers use the loss leader method by setting very low prices on some products, sometimes below their cost.
* The aim/goal is to attract customers with these low priced items, hoping they will also buy other products that are more profitable.
* The business expects that the extra profit from these other products will cover the losses from the low priced items, resulting in a positive contribution.
* Loss leaders often promote the sale of related/complementary products. For example, selling printers at a low price might lead customers to buy more expensive printer ink.
