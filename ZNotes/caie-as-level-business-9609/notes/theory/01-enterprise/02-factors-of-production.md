## Factors of Production

1. **Land** - all natural resources (minerals) used in production.
   * Return for land is “rent”
2. **Labour** - both manual and skilled work
   * Return for labour is “salary” or “wages.”
3. **Capital** - finance is needed to set up and run the business, as well as man-made goods used in production, e.g., capital goods like machinery.
   * Return for capital is “interest”
4. **Enterprise** - the driving force that arranges all other factors of production and takes the risk of the new business venture
   * Return for enterprise is “profit”
