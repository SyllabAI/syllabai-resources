## Making the decisions – factors influencing finance choice

Choosing the right finance source is utmost important for long-term success. Costly or inflexible sources, or those that can be withdrawn quickly, can harm the business harshly. Finance managers must carefully evaluate options before making decisions. The factors influencing such decisions are the following:

| Why finance is required and time period when it is needed | Costs |
|----|----|
| Avoid using long-term finance for short-term needs to minimize risk and cost.  | No finance source is free to obtain, including internal finance, due to opportunity costs.   |
| Use permanent capital, like issuing shares, for long-term expansion or research projects for long term finance. | Rising interest rates can increase the cost of loans. |
| Go for short-term finances to address immediate needs such as increasing inventory or paying creditors. | Listing a new public company in the Stock Exchange can incur high fees and promotional costs. |
|    | Once equity finance has been raised. Dividends are not tax-deductible, unlike loan interest |
