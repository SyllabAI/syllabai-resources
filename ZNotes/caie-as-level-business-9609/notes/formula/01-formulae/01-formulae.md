## Formulae:


* **<p>Added Value** = Price - Total Costs</p>


* **<p>Sales Growth (%)** = (Original Sales - New Sales / Original Sales) × 100</p>


* **<p>Revenue** = Selling price × Quantity sold</p>


* **<p>Market Capitalisation** = Current share price × Total number of shares issued</p>


* **<p>Labour Turnover (%)** = (Number of workers who left /  Average Number of Workers) × 100</p>


* **<p>Time-based Salary** = Wage rate × Hours Worked</p>


* **<p>Piece-Rates** = Units Produced × Rate per Unit</p>


* **<p>Commission** = Sales Revenue × Commission Rate</p>


* **<p>Profit Share** = Total Profit × Share of Profit</p>


* **<p>Market Share (%)** = (Business Sales of The Business / Total Revenue of The Industry) × 100</p>

* OR

* **<p>Market Share (%)** = (Units Sold by Business / Total Market Units Sold) × 100</p>


* **<p>Market Growth (%)** = (New Market Size - Original Market Size / Original Market Size) × 100</p>


* **<p>Median Value** = (Number of values + 1) / 2</p>


* **<p>Range** = Highest result - Lowest result</p>


* **<p>Angle of Section (Pie Chart)** = (Value of one section / Total value of all sections) × 360 degrees</p>


* **<p>Labour Productivity** = [Output Produced / Number of Workers (per hour)]</p>


* **<p>Capacity Utilisation (%)** = (Current Capacity / Total Capacity) × 100</p>


* **<p>Profit** = Price - Total Costs (per unit)</p>

* OR

* **<p>Profit** = Total Revenue - Total Costs (total)</p>
* OR

* **<p>Profit** = Contribution - Overheads</p> 


* **<p>Working Capital** = Current Assets - Current Liabilities</p>


* **<p>Opening Cash Flow** = Closing Cash Flow - Net Cash Flow</p>


* **<p>Closing Cash Flow** = Opening Cash Flow + Net Cash Flow</p>


* **<p>Net Cash Flow** = Cash Inflow - Cash Outflow</p>


* **<p>Closing Cash Balance** = Opening cash balance + (Cash inflows - Cash outflows)</p>


* **<p>Total Variable Costs** = Variable Costs per Unit × Output</p>


* **<p>Average Fixed Costs** = Total Fixed Costs / Output</p>


* **<p>Contribution** = Price - Variable Costs per Unit (per unit)</p>

*    OR

* **<p>Contribution** = Revenue - Total Variable Costs (total)</p>


* **<p>Unit Contribution** = Price of unit - Direct (variable) cost of unit</p>


* **<p>Average Total Cost** = Total Costs / Output</p>


* **<p>Marginal Cost** = Change in Cost / Change in Output</p>


* **<p>Total Cost** = Total Variable Costs + Total Fixed Costs</p>
*   OR
* **<p>Total Cost** = (Variable Cost per Unit × Output) + Fixed Costs</p>


* **<p>Break-Even Level of Output** = Fixed Costs / Contribution</p>
* OR
* **<p>Break-Even Level of Output** = Fixed Costs / (Price - Variable Costs per Unit)</p>

* **<p>Production Over Break-Even Point** = (Margin of safety / Break-even output) * 100</p>


* **<p>Margin of Safety** = Current Level of Output - Break-Even Level of Output</p>


* **<p>Adverse Budget Variance** = Actual Cost - Budgeted Cost</p>


* **<p>Favourable Budget Variance** = Budgeted Cost - Actual Cost</p>
