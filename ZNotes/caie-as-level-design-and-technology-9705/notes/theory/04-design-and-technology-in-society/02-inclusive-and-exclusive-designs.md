## Inclusive and exclusive designs

#|
||

**Note**

|

Inclusive Design

|

Exclusive Design

||
||

Underlying Principle

|

Design for human diversity

|

Design for a specific user.

||
||

Examples of Features

|

* large, clear fonts

* high-contrast colours

* voice controls

* captions and subtitles in movies

* ergonomic grips

* and simple, predictable layouts

* durable, simple designs for children

* intuitive interfaces with larger text for the elderly

|

* complex software or professional-grade cameras that require a high level of technical skill.

* gaming controllers with small buttons

* dresses for tall girls (above 95th percentile)

||
||

Outcome

|

Creates a more accessible and user-friendly experience for everyone (in the 5th to the 95th percentile of a certain physical measured attribute), promoting equality and a broader user base

|

Limits accessibility and can make a product unusable for people outside of the target group
