# Technology

## Developments in Technology

* **Invention:** The creation of a completely new idea or device that did not exist before.

* **Innovation:** The practical improvement of an existing idea or product to solve a problem better.

* **Evolution:** The gradual transformation and refinement of a product or system over time.

* **Impact on Design and Manufacture:** Technological developments radically shift industrial workflows, cutting lead times and unlocking complex geometries.

  * *Example:* In the automotive industry, the evolution from manual 2D drawing boards to integrated CAD/CAM systems allows engineers to simulate crash tests digitally before building physical prototypes, reducing vehicle development cycles from years to months and drastically reducing material waste.
