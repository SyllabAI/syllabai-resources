## Impacts of technology

#|
||

Technology

|

Impact on design and making

||
||

3D Printing

|

Enables quick design iterations

Reduces lead times

||
||

Robotics

|

Designers have to consider how robots will interact with their design

Reduces labor cost

Makes the workspace safer

Accurate, repeated production, fewer mistakes

||
||

Artificial intelligence, Augmented Reality/Virtual reality (AR/VR)

|

Allows 3D visualization and testing of designs.

Reduces the chances of making a faulty product ( the manufacturer can understand the design as if it’s already manufactured)
