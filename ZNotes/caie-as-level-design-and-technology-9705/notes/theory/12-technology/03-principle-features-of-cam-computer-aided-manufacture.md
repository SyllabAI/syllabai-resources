## Principal features of CAM (computer-aided manufacturing)

* CAM is the automated control of manufacturing machinery through computer software.

* **Control and Operation of Machines:** CAM software translates CAD vector geometries into machine-readable instruction codes (such as G-code). It directly controls and operates physical machines by calculating optimal toolpaths, regulating spindle rotational speeds, setting feed rates, managing automatic tool changers, and directing coolant flow to eliminate human error and ensure high-precision, repeatable production.

* It calculates the most efficient cutting paths to shape materials and minimize waste.

* Simulates the manufacturing process virtually to monitor performance and detect tool collisions before physical cutting begins.

* CAD and CAM work as an integrated team: CAD is the brain that designs the object, while CAM is the muscle that operates the machines to build it.
