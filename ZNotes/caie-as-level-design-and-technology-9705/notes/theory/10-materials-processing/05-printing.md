## Printing

* ### Digital Printing

  * A modern method that transfers digital images directly onto a surface without the need for printing plates.

  * It is fast, cost-effective for small runs, and allows for customization and a wide range of colours.

* ### Screen Printing

  * A stencil-based technique where ink is pushed through a fine mesh screen onto the material below.

  * It is very cost-effective for large-volume production.
