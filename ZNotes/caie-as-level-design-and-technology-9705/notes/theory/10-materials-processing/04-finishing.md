## Finishing

* Paints: Liquid coatings applied to wood and metals to provide aesthetic colour and a protective barrier against moisture, UV rays, and corrosion.

* Polymer coatings: Thick protective layers applied via methods like fluidized bed dip coating (using thermoplastic powder) to provide a durable, tactile, and electrically insulated finish (commonly used on wire products like dish racks and tool handles).

* Varnishes: Transparent or translucent liquid finishes applied to wood to seal the porous surface against moisture and wear while accentuating the natural grain.

* **UV Varnishing:** A clear liquid coating applied to paper and card that is cured instantly using ultraviolet (UV) light, producing a high-gloss, highly durable finish.

* **Spot Varnishing:** A technique where varnish is applied selectively to specific areas ("spots") of a printed surface (such as a company logo or photograph) to create a striking visual contrast between matte and gloss areas.
