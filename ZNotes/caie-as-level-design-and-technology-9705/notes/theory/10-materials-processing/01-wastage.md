# Materials Processing

## Wastage

* ### Cutting

  * Hand tools (hacksaws, tenon saws, and metal shears) are used to cut materials like wood and metal.

  * Machine tools (power saws and CNC machines) offer greater speed, accuracy, and automation.

  * A laser cutter uses a high-powered laser to cut or engrave materials with extreme precision, ideal for intricate designs on wood, plastic, or acrylic.

  * A vinyl cutting machine uses a small blade to cut shapes and letters from sheets of vinyl, often used for stickers and signs.

* ### Drilling

  * Hand drills are portable and manual, suitable for quick, low-precision holes.

  * Powered drills (cordless/electric hand drills) - Offer high torque and portability for general drilling tasks across various locations.

  * A press drill/pillar drill - A stationary machine that provides high accuracy, strict vertical alignment control, and adjustable spindle speeds for precision depth drilling in workshop environments.

* ### Turning

  * A process used to create cylindrical or conical shapes.

  * A workpiece is rotated on a lathe while a cutting tool is moved along its axis to remove material.

  * A wood lathe is used for shaping wooden items.

  * A centre lathe is used for precision metalworking.

* ### Milling and Routing

  * Milling is a precision process for metals, used to create flat surfaces, slots, and complex shapes.

  * Routing is the equivalent for wood and plastics, often used to create decorative edges, grooves, and intricate designs.

* ### Use of Abrasives

  * Abrasives, such as sandpaper, files, or grinding wheels, are used to remove small amounts of material to shape, smooth, or polish a surface.

  * This gives a clean finish to a product.
