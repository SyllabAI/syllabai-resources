## Addition

* Adhesives: glues or epoxy to bond materials together.

* Welding: A permanent fabrication process that joins metals by applying intense heat to melt the base materials, often fusing them together with a molten filler metal. Common types include arc welding, MIG, and TIG welding, which are widely used for heavy structural frames, metal chassis, and permanent fabrications.

* Mechanical Fasteners: Using screws, bolts, nuts, and rivets to hold parts together.
