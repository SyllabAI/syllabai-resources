## Aesthetics and ergonomics 

* By understanding both aesthetics and ergonomics, a designer can design products that are visually appealing and comfortable, making them loved products/ popular products.

* An excellent chair is not only functional, but is attractive and allows a user to sit on it for hours without feeling pain or discomfort.

* Ergonomic features can blend in with aesthetic features, such as the grip and curves on a toothbrush that might seem to be part of the style but effectively allow the product to be used comfortably and effectively.
