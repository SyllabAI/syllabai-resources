# Health and Safety

## Standard Risk Assessment Procedures

* The first step is to identify all potential hazards associated with a product.

* Once a hazard is identified, the risk needs to be estimated.

* The estimated risk is then compared against established safety standards and regulations to determine if it is acceptable.

* If the risk is unacceptable, it must be mitigated.

* Designers and manufacturers must implement strategies to reduce or eliminate the identified risks, this involves:

  * Elimination, like using a non-toxic material instead of a toxic one.

  * Substituting a hazardous process or material with a less hazardous one.

  * Engineering controls, like adding a safety guard on a moving part.

  * Providing warnings, training, or instructions on safe use.

  * Providing Personal protective equipment to the user.

* The risk should be continuously monitored.
