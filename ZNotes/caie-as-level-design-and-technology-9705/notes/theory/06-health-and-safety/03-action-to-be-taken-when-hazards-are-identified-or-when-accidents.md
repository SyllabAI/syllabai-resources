## Action to be taken when hazards are identified or when accidents occur in a school workshop or practical area

* When a hazard is identified in a school workshop or practical area, you should first stop what you are doing.

* Immediately report the hazard to a teacher or supervisor.

* If it is safe and you are trained, you can attempt to remove or clean up the hazard.

* Otherwise, wait for a qualified person and put up a sign or barrier to warn others of the danger.

* When an accident occurs, you must first alert the teacher or supervisor.

* If you are trained and it is safe, you should administer immediate first aid.

* It is crucial to secure the area and turn off any machinery to prevent further injury.

* Finally, you must report the accident to the teacher or supervisor.
