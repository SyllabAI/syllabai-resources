## Safe working practices

* PPE:

  * Wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment for the task, such as goggles, a face mask, gloves, or a lab coat.

  * For specific tasks, use specialized PPE, such as **welding goggles for welding** and **gauntlets and spats for hot metal working**.

* Tool and Equipment Use:

  * Always use the correct tool for the job.

  * Ensure safety guards are in place on all machinery and are used correctly.

  * Stop work immediately to address any potential hazards.

  * Apply proper manual handling precautions when lifting heavy objects to prevent injury.

* Training and Supervision:

  * Receive proper safety training on the use of all tools, equipment, and machinery.

  * Be trained on the properties of materials, especially corrosive or toxic substances.

  * Work under supervision as required, especially with new or complex equipment.

* Securely tie back long hair

* Roll up sleeves to prevent them from getting caught in machinery.

* Maintain a clean workspace to avoid trips, slips, and other accidents.
