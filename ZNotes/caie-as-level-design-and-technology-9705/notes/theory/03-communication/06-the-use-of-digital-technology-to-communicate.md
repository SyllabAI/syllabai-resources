## The use of digital technology to communicate

* ### **Software Packages:**

  * Microsoft Word is used to create reports, design specifications, and client letters.

  * Microsoft Excel manages numerical data, costing calculations, and material lists.

  * Microsoft PowerPoint builds presentations for design proposals and project reviews.

  * Adobe® Acrobat Pro manages and secures PDF documents as a universal file-sharing format.

* **Email -** Serves as a primary tool for sending formal text messages and digital files where immediate replies are not required, maintaining a written audit trail.

* **Collaboration Software:**

  * Microsoft Teams combines chat, video meetings, and file sharing in a single collaborative workspace.

  * Miro provides a digital whiteboard for real-time brainstorming, flowcharts, and mood boarding.

* **Video Conferencing -** Enables face-to-face remote meetings regardless of physical location, facilitating client pitches, design reviews, and interactive team communication.
