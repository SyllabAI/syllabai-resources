## Enhancement techniques

* **Tone and colour -** Varying shades (tone) and hues (colour) are applied to drawings to create light and shadow effects. This makes objects appear three-dimensional by clearly defining form and volume.

* **Material representation -** Specific drawing techniques, textures, and patterns are employed to simulate the visual appearance of materials like wood grain, metal, or glass. This gives viewers a clearer understanding of the intended final product.

* **Shadows -** Cast shadows (shadows an object projects onto a surrounding surface) and form shadows (shadows on the object itself caused by its form) are added. These elements ground the product in its environment and enhance realism and depth.
