## Planning drawings

### **Flowcharts**

* Diagrams that outline the sequence of steps and decisions in a process using standardized symbols. They clarify process logic and system workflows![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/5a1248f362d4ae690a645f0f.webp =825x)

### Gantt Charts

* Bar charts that display project schedules by mapping out the start and finish dates of individual activities. They assist in tracking progress and identifying task dependencies for project management.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/f429127c2583a0e5c47c09bf.webp =767x)

### Materials or Cutting Lists

* A materials list (Bill of Materials or BOM) details all raw materials, components, and sub-assemblies needed to build a product to help calculate costs. A cutting list specifies exact sizes and quantities to be cut from stock material to minimize waste.![Standardized Cutting List for Furniture](../../../assets/a5b24ad880e4-f1609d508bd47558452bbc4b.webp)
