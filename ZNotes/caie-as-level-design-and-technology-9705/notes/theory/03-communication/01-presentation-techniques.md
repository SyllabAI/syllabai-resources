# Communication

## Presentation techniques

* **Freehand sketching -** Quick drawings are produced without drawing instruments to communicate design ideas rapidly. Exploded and sectional (cut-away) views are utilized when ideas need to be recorded quickly without requiring absolute precision.

* **Accurate isometric drawings -** These provide a three-dimensional view with axes set at 120 degrees. Circles and curves appear as ellipses in this projection method.

  * *Construction of Arcs and Circles:* Because true circles are distorted into ellipses in isometric views, they are constructed using the **four-centre method**. An isometric square (rhombus) is first drawn to represent the bounding box of the circular feature. Midpoints of all four sides are identified, and construction lines are drawn from the obtuse corners to these midpoints to locate four arc centres. Using these precise centres, four connecting arcs are drawn to complete an accurate isometric ellipse.

* **Estimated one- and two-point perspective drawings -** These techniques show realistic depth by making parallel lines converge toward one or two vanishing points on a horizon line.

* **Accurate planometric drawings (45 degrees/45 degrees) -** This style presents a three-dimensional view starting from an unskewed 2D plan (such as an interior kitchen layout) with vertical lines projected at specific angles.
