## Stages in design thinking

* **Empathise -** Conduct qualitative and quantitative research to understand users' physical, emotional, and psychological needs.

* **Define -** State the core users' needs, pain points, and problems clearly based on gathered insights.

* **Ideate -** Challenge assumptions, brainstorm broadly, and create a diverse range of conceptual ideas.

* **Refine -** Develop and modify promising ideas through iterative testing and analysis.

* **Realise -** Transform refined concepts into tangible models, prototypes, or operational solutions.

* **Test -** Try out solutions with real users in real-world contexts to gather performance data and feedback.
