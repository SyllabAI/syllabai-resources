## The influence of design movements on the design of products

#|
||

Design movement

|

Influence on design of products

||
||

Arts and Crafts

|

* Rejection of industrial mass production, emphasis on handicraft and natural materials like wood and stone.

* Products were valued for their honest, simple forms and the visible skill of the artisan

* Ex: William Morris wallpapers

||
||

Art Deco

|

* Embraced modernity with streamlined, geometric forms and luxurious materials such as chrome, plastics, and exotic woods.

* The style promoted a sense of glamour, speed, and sophistication.

* Ex: Chrysler Building, Empire State Building

||
||

Bauhaus

|

* Pioneered the concept of "form follows function". Designs were simplified to their core geometric shapes, favoring a rational and functional aesthetic.

* The movement promoted mass production and the use of new materials like tubular steel.

* Ex: The Barcelona chair, The Wassily chair

||
||

Scandinavian

|

* Focused on simplicity, functionality, and accessibility.

* Products featured clean lines and the prominent use of light-colored natural materials.

* The design aimed to improve everyday life for all.

* Ex: IKEA designs, Monochromatic artwork

||
||

Minimalism

|

* Advocated for extreme simplicity and the complete removal of non-essential elements.

* The core principle of "less is more" led to designs with clean lines, a simple color palette, and a focus on essential form and function.

* Ex: Apple products and packaging, Nike logo (and most rebrands)

||
||

Modernism

|

* Rejected historical decoration in favor of functionality and mass production.

* Designs were unadorned, rational, and clean, using new materials like steel and concrete.

* The goal was to create affordable, accessible products for the masses.

* Ex: Sydney Opera house, Barcelona chair

||
||

Post-modernism

|

* Reacted against the strict rules of Modernism by reintroducing ornamentation, historical references, and bright colors.

* Designs were often playful and humorous, combining different styles and materials to challenge a single, unified aesthetic.

* Ex: Juicy Salif, AT&T Building
