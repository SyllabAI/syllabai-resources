## Factors that affect the design of new products

#|
||

Factor

|

Impact

||
||

Scale of production (individual (one-off), batch and mass production)

|

* Dictates materials and manufacturing processes.

* Mass production requires design for automation and cost-efficiency etc

||
||

Production processes

|

* Directly influences the shape, form, and material choices based on what is feasible.

* 3D printing materials differ from injection moulding materials etc

||
||

Costs (material costs and production costs)

|

* Cost constraints dictate material selection, design complexity, and manufacturing methods.

||
||

Changing customer requirements

|

* Drives the need for new features, improved functionality, or a completely new product concept.

||
||

Social and cultural changes

|

* Influences lifestyle and values, leading to new product categories (products for health and wellness for example).

||
||

Fashion and trends

|

* Affects the visual ideal or likable style, color, and aesthetic of a product to ensure it is modern and appealing.

||
||

The availability of materials and resources

|

* Requires designers to find alternative, sustainable, or more readily available materials, which can alter the design.

||
||

New manufacturing technologies

|

* Enables new possibilities for complex shapes and customization.

||
||

Changing legal and environmental requirements.

|

* Forces designers to consider safety, recyclability, and reduced environmental impact in their choices of materials and processes.

||
