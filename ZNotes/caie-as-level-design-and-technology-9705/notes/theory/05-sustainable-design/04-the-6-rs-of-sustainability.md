## The 6 Rs of Sustainability

* **Refuse -** Say no to unnecessary products, single-use plastics, and excess packaging that harm the environment.

* **Rethink -** Question consumption habits and design paradigms; evaluate whether a physical product is truly needed or if a service/digital solution can suffice.

* **Reduce -** Cut down on the overall consumption of raw materials, energy, and goods during production and use.

* **Reuse -** Find creative ways to utilise items multiple times for their original purpose or secondary applications rather than discarding them.

* **Repair -** Fix broken appliances, furniture, and clothing using accessible spare parts and modular maintenance rather than replacing them.

* **Recycle -** Process and transform waste materials into new, usable secondary raw materials as a final measure when items can no longer be kept in active circulation.
