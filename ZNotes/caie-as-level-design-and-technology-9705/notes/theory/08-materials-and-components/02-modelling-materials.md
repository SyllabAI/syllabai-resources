* Modeling materials are substances that are used to create 3D objects, often as representations of an idea ( a model) or art.

Balsa Wood

|

Balsa is an exceptionally lightweight and soft wood, making it easy to cut, shape, and sand with simple hand tools like a craft knife. Despite its softness, it has a high strength-to-weight ratio. It glues well but has poor screw and nail-holding power.

|

Model aircraft and boats, architectural models, and various hobby and craft projects. It is also used in the construction of wind turbine blades and as a core material in composite structures.

|

Balsa is a fast-growing, highly sustainable, and renewable material. It is biodegradable and has a low environmental impact compared to many other materials.

|

Strips, sheets, blocks, and dowels in various sizes.

Polymorph

|

Polymorph is a thermoplastic that is supplied as small pellets. When heated to around 62°C (144°F) in hot water, the pellets fuse into a soft, mouldable, putty-like mass that can be shaped by hand. As it cools, it becomes a hard, tough, and durable plastic, similar to nylon. It is reusable; it can be reheated and reshaped multiple times without losing its properties.

|

Creating custom ergonomic handles, prototyping mechanical parts, temporary repairs, and making small molds or jigs. It's popular for a wide range of DIY and design projects.

|

As a thermoplastic, it can be melted down and reused, which reduces waste. However, like other plastics, it is not biodegradable and is made from non-renewable resources.

|

Small granules or pellets.

||

Plaster of Paris

|

Plaster of Paris is a fine white powder (calcium sulfate hemihydrate) that, when mixed with water, forms a smooth, pourable paste. It has a fast setting time, hardening into a solid, rigid form. It is dimensionally stable and does not shrink or crack as it dries. Once set, it can be sanded, carved, and painted. It can be brittle.

|

Casting and molding, sculpting, creating decorative plasterwork for ceilings and walls, and in medicine for making orthopedic casts to set broken bones.

|

It is made from gypsum, a natural mineral, and is not toxic. However, the quarrying of gypsum can impact local habitats. Set plaster of Paris is a durable, non-biodegradable waste product that fills up landfills. While it can be recycled under controlled conditions, it's not a common practice.

|

Powder, sold in bags or tubs.
