oleophobic coating

|

A very thin, almost invisible layer that repels oils and fats. It gives surfaces a slippery feel, making them easy to clean and resistant to fingerprint smudges

|

touchscreen devices like smartphones and tablets, but also on camera lenses and spectacles.

|

might contain poisonous chemicals

|

liquid solution or spray that is applied to a surface

||
||

hydrophobic material

|

A surface that strongly repels water, causing it to bead up and roll off

|

water-resistant fabrics, protective coatings for buildings, and self-cleaning surfaces.

|

poor disposal can lead to pollution

|

coating or spray to be applied to a surface

sugar-based

|

Derived from sources like sugarcane, these bioplastics can be chemically identical to traditional plastics like PET

|

Plastic drink bottles, food containers, and packaging.

|

They reduce reliance on fossil fuels and can be recycled through existing recycling systems.

|

Granules, pellets, and preforms for bottle moulding.

||
||

cellulose-based

|

Made from cellulose, the main component of plant cell walls. They have high tensile strength and are rigid, but can be brittle. They are biodegradable and have good water resistance

|

Packaging films, food wrappers, and some textile fibres

|

biodegradable and provide a sustainable alternative to petroleum-based plastics.

|

Sheets, films, and granules.

metal foam

|

A cellular structure with a large percentage of gas-filled pores. It is incredibly lightweight yet has high compressive strength and excellent energy absorption. It's also a good thermal and acoustic insulator

|

crash-absorbers for cars, lightweight structural panels for aerospace, and heat exchangers

|

Forged parts, rods, bars, sheets, and powders.

|

Panels, sheets, and custom-moulded parts.

||
||

super alloy

|

These are alloys based on nickel, cobalt, or iron that exhibit superior mechanical strength, creep resistance, and corrosion resistance at very high temperatures. They are perfect for extreme environments.

|

jet engines, gas turbines, and rocket engines.

|

The production of superalloys is complex and energy-intensive

|

Forged parts, rods, bars, sheets, and powders.
