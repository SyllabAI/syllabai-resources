## **Comparison of the main energy sources**

* Fossil fuels such as oil, natural gas, and coal are finite, and using them a lot is not a sustainable practice since they can’t be replenished.

* Using them also contributes to greenhouse gas emissions, which lead to global warming.

* Water, wind, and solar energy are renewable sources of energy and therefore are more sustainable options for energy production.

* There are fewer greenhouse gas emissions when these sources are used.
