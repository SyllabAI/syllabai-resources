## Forms of energy

* **Kinetic:** Energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

* **Potential:** Stored energy an object has due to its position, state, or configuration (e.g., gravitational or elastic).

* **Thermal:** Heat energy associated with the temperature and internal kinetic motion of particles within matter.

* **Electrical:** Energy resulting from the flow of electric charge (electrons) through a conductor.

* **Chemical:** Energy stored within the atomic bonds of chemical substances, released during chemical reactions or combustion.
