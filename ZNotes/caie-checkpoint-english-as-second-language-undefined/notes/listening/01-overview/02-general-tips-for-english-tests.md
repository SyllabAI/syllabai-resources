## **General tips for English tests**

* When practicing past papers before the exam, **make sure to underline** the words that you do not understand and use a dictionary to find out the meaning. This will help to strengthen your English-language skills.

* You may refer to the [*Online Cambridge English Dictionary*](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/) or the *Oxford English Dictionary* (any edition) for reference. However, you **will not be able to use** a dictionary in the Checkpoint test.

* When following the guide in the next chapter, make sure to **keep a copy of a past exam paper**. This will help you understand the exam format properly.
