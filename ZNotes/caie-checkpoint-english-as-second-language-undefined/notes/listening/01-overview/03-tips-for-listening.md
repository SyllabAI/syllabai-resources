
## Tips for Listening

* It is important to be attentive during the test to not miss certain information being spoken, which may be important to know when answering.

* Regular practice is also something that needs to be done to perfect your listening skills.

* When doing past papers before the test, there is a **Tape Script (TS)** document, which may be available. This tapescript is a transcript of the dialogues (conversations) spoken in the recording played during the test.

* If you do not understand certain words when practising for your test, you can check with the Tape Script and find those words and check them with a dictionary. This will help to perfect your listening skills as well.

* During the test, recordings will usually be in an American or British accent^, so it is important to get used to both accents to be familiar with them.

* Getting to know how people with these accents speak will help you in the test.

### ^accent:

a way of pronouncing words shared by the people of a particular country
