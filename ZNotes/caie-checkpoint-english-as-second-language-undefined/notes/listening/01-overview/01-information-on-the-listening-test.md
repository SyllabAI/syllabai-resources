# Overview

## Information on the Listening test

To do well in Paper 3 (Listening), you should be able to understand the main points being spoken and specific information mentioned in an extended talk on a wide range of topics. Using this information, you will answer the questions that follow.

### **Information on the Listening test:**

* The total mark for this paper is **30**.

* You may not use dictionaries.

* There are **six** parts to this test, and each part will be played **twice**.

* There will be time for you to look through the questions and check your answers during the test.

* Write your answers while you are listening.

* In all of the parts of this paper, you will be provided with an example.

  * This example is provided to demonstrate how to answer the question provided properly.
