## Sample response (Story writing)

*Question taken from a past paper*

**[1110/02/O/N/2017]**

Your English teacher wants you to write a story. Your story must begin with the sentence:

* *As the competition started, Stef felt very excited.*

Write your story in **100-120 words** in an appropriate style below.

$ \quad $

It was her first time participating in a Geograbee competition. The Geograbee is an annual Geography spelling bee held in different parts of the world each year. Stef’s mother had heard about this competition and was interested. She told her daughter, who agreed to participate in the Geograbee. The Geograbee is divided into two segments. The first segment is where participants have to spell the names of all 193 countries correctly. Only Stef and another girl managed to finish successfully.

The second segment was the Grand Final. The final two participants were asked to spell difficult geographical words. The first participant to reach 12 points wins. The other girl misspelt the last word, unlike Stef who correctly spelt all of the words correctly. She was overjoyed as she received the winner’s trophy from the host of the competition.

$ \quad $
