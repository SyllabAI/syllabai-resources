## **Format for Essay writing**

* You will have to write an essay based on your thoughts to your English teacher on a topic discussed in class.
* You will be given a sentence from which you’ll have to write an essay in your own opinion.
* You should start the essay by writing your opinion about the topic given in the question.
* You should then go on to explain why you think that way about the topic given.
* End your essay with your conclusion, and summarise what you said in the entire response at the end.
* The completed essay must make sense and must be relevant to the topic given in the question.
* You do not have to include word count as part of your response
