## **Sample response (Essay)**

$ \quad $

In your English class, you have been talking about reading. Your teacher has asked you to write this essay:

* *Why should young people read more than play with their handphones or iPads?*

Write your essay in 1**00-120 words** in an appropriate style below

$ \quad $

In my opinion, young people should read more and spend less time on their handphones or iPads. There are many reasons for this, and some of which I would like to discuss below.

Reading helps young people, especially people whose first language is not English, to learn new vocabulary and how to use grammar in sentences properly. It can also help to improve the reader’s speaking ability and to converse in proper English with others, especially native speakers.

On the other hand, playing with handphones or iPads has no educational benefit; it wastes time and is only meant as a time-pass and young people are better off reading books.

In conclusion, reading has much more benefits than wasting your time playing on your handphone or iPad.

$ \quad $

Marks given for the response above: **20/20 marks**
