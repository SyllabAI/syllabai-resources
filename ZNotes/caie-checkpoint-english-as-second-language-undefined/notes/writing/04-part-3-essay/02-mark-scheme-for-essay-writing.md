## **Mark scheme for Essay writing**

*Simplified*


|Marks (Band)|CONTENT|COMMUNICATION|ORGANISATION|LANGUAGE|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|5|All content is related to the task. The reader fully understands the content.|Candidate holds the reader’s attention, and ideas are easy to understand|Text is generally well organised and flows, using a wide range of vocabulary|Candidate uses a range of simple everyday vocabulary and grammar. Errors may be present. However, meaning is understood|
|4|*Performance shares*|*features of*|*Band 3*|*and Band 5*|
|3|Some parts are not fully covered/do not make sense. The reader does not fully understand the content.|Candidate uses easy to understand ideas in generally appropriate ways|Text is connected and makes sense, using basic linking words|Candidate uses everyday vocabulary appropriately, sometimes overusing certain words. Uses simple grammatical forms. Errors may be noticeable, however meaning is still understood|
|2|*Performance shares*|*features of*|*Band 1*|*and Band 3*|
|1|The candidate does not understand the task fully / some parts of the answer not related to the task.|Candidate uses simple ideas in simple ways|Text is connected using basic and frequently uses the same linking words|Candidate uses basic vocabulary appropriately \n Uses simple grammatical forms|
|0|Content is not related to the task at all. The reader does not understand the content.|*Performance*|*below*|*Band 1*|
