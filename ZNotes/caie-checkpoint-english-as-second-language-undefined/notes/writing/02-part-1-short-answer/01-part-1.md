# Short Answer

## **Part 1**

### (Questions **1-5**)

* In this question, you will be given **two** sentences for each question.
* The first sentence will already be completed for you.
* Your task is to complete the second sentence so that it means the same as the first sentence.
* An example will be provided for you in this part to demonstrate how to answer the question.
* Remember to use **no more than three words** when answering the question.
  * This means that if your answer is grammatically correct but more than three words in length, it will be marked wrong by the examiner as you didn’t follow the question’s requirements.
* Ideally, you should spend **no more than 5 minutes** here.
