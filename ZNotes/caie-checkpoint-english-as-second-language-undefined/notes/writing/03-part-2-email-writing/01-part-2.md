# Email

## **Part 2**

(Question **6**)

* Here, you will be given an email-writing task.
* You will be asked to write an email to your English-speaking friend, and you’ll be given **three** points to include.
* Ideally, you should write **35-45 words** for this task, and it is essential not to write more than that.
* You should spend **about 15 minutes** in this section.
* The total mark for this task is **10**, which consists of:
  * up to **5** marks for content
  * up to **5** marks for language
* When marking your email, an examiner will refer to a table like the one in the next card to find out how well you did.
