## **Mark scheme for Email writing**

*Simplified*


|Marks (Band)|CONTENT|LANGUAGE|
|:---|:---|:---|
|**5**|All three points have been clearly covered.|The reader is clearly informed properly.  Language is accurate for the message to be understood clearly.|
|**4**|All three points are covered, but there might be some lack of clarity (not so clear)|*Response shares features of both Bands 3 and 5*|
|**3**|All three points are attempted  **OR**  Two points are clearly covered, and one part is unattempted.|The reader is informed in a generally appropriate way.  Language errors may sometimes make communication of the message difficult.|
|**2**|Only two parts of the message are attempted.|*Response shares features of both Bands 1 and 3*|
|**1**|Only one part of the message is attempted.|The reader is informed in a basic way, which needs a lot of explanation to understand the message.  Language errors make the message difficult to understand|
|**0**|Email not attempted or not understandable by the examiner|Email not attempted or not understandable by the examiner|
