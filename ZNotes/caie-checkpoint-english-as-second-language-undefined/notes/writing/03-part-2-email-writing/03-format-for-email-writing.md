## **Format for Email writing**

When writing your response, you should follow the format below.

* Start with “Dear …. (your friend)”
* Cover all three bullet points asked in the question
* Be clear and keep the email short, as you can only write about **35-45 words**.
* Finish your email with “Yours sincerely” or “Your friend”.
* Keep the complete response in 1 paragraph only, as the length of the email is short.
* You do not have to include word count as part of your response.
