## General Tips for English Tests

* When practicing past papers before the exam, underline the words you do not understand and use a dictionary to find the meaning. This will help to strengthen your English language skills. You may refer to the [*Online Cambridge English Dictionary*](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/) or the *Oxford English Dictionary* (any edition) for reference. However, you cannot use a dictionary in the Checkpoint test.
* Keep a copy of a past exam paper following the guide in the next chapter. This will help you understand the exam format properly.
