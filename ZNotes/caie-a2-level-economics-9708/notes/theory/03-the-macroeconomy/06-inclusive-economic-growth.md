## Inclusive Economic Growth

* **Inclusive Economic Growth**: economic growth is distributed relatively across society and creates opportunities for all.
* **It provides benefits to the economy in 2 ways:**
  * Monetary, with everyone experiencing a rise in income.
  * It is non-monetary, with everyone experiencing improved living standards and better education facilities.

**Impact of Economic Growth on Equity and Equality:**

* Economic growth has positively affected equity and income distribution in some countries.
* However, this is only if countries are willing to change to accommodate that growth. This means that some industries would close and others would be created; this could increase unemployment if workers are occupationally and geographically immobile.

**Policies to Promote Inclusive Economic Growth:**

* These government policies aim to evenly distribute the opportunities and benefits created by economic growth.
* Redistribute income through transfer payments and Progressive tax.
* Availability of high-quality education to all, which should raise opportunities for pay and employment.
* A relatively high national minimum wage to ensure leisure time for all workforce.
* Anti-discrimination legislation to prevent discrimination and ensure benefits and opportunities for all.
* Improvements in transport and communication links to reduce geographical immobility of labour and regional differences in income.
* Trade union legislation allows for the protection of workers’ rights.
* Legislation against slavery and child labour to ensure that vulnerable groups are not exploited.
