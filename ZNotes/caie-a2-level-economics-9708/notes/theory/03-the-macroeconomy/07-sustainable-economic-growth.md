## Sustainable Economic Growth

* **Sustainable Economic Growth**: economic growth that can continue over time.
* To achieve sustainability, a deliberate and determined effort must be made to balance economic, social and environmental objectives.
* Using natural resources can lead to **economic growth in the short run**; however, **conserving resources allows them to be beneficial in the long run**.
  * Enables **future generations to benefit** from them and may mean that the **resources can be sold at higher prices**.
 
**The Factors that Influence the Conservation of Resources:**

- Demand for the product in the long run.
- The comparative advantage of producing that product and whether it may arise.

**Impact of Economic Growth on the Environment and Climate Change**:

- Economic growth may harm the environment and increase the speed of climate change.
- The **primary sector’s constant usage of natural resources** can cause these resources to be **exploited and depleted,** causing them to be **non-renewable in the long run**.
- The **higher rate of manufacturing** in the secondary sector causes **emissions**.
- The **growth of the tertiary sectors**, like tourism, can **damage the environment**, destroying **natural sites and ecosystems**.
- Economic growth can improve environmental conditions and reduce the pace of climate change if done through sustainable means such as clean energy and resources.

**Policies to Reduce the Impact of Economic Growth on the Environment and Climate Change**:

- **Subsidise cleaner energy sources**, provide incentives and reduce costs to firms to be more sustainable.
  - An opportunity cost may not always encourage efficient use of the money.
- **Provision of Information** about the effects of certain activities and the usage of nudge theory to influence consumer/firm behaviour.
- **Pass legislation to ban or restrict the production** **or consumption** of certain products, like banning plastic pages or fines for littering.
  - It can be costly to enforce.
- **Using taxes to restrict firms' polluting activities** makes external costs become private. The income from this tax can be reinvested into compensation.
  - A risky tax rate may influence economic growth negatively.
