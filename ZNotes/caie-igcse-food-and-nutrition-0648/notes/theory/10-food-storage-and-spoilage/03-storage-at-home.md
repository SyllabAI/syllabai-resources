## Storage at home

* Label containers.
* Separate raw food from cooked.
* Store foods at required temperatures.
* Use airtight containers to store products like flour, sugar etc.
* Use a bread box/container to store bread safely.
* Bread should be stored at room temperature.
* Use the kitchen cabinets to store canned foods.
* Tighten lids of bottles or any liquid container to avoid drips in the fridge or counter.
* Store eggs in the fridge.
* Foods like onions and potatoes should be stored in dry places.
* Don’t store any food items under the sink area.
