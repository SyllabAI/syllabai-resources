# Food storage and spoilage

## __Action of enzymes, bacteria, yeast & moulds__

* **Enzymes:**
  * Oxidation: oxidase enzymes cause the destruction of certain nutrients.
  * Browning: if foods get cut or bruised, the surface will discolour due to the activity of enzymes.
  * Ripening: change in colour by the presence of enzymes.
* **Bacteria:**
  * Reproduce under suitable conditions of:
  * Temperature.
  * Moisture.
  * Food supply.
* **Moulds:**
  * Grow best in warm moist conditions & will grow slowly in cool places.
  * Spores are released into the atmosphere and land on food to germinate.
* **Yeasts:**
  * Can grow in acidic, salty, and sugary concentrations and can grow without oxygen but they need water to reproduce.
