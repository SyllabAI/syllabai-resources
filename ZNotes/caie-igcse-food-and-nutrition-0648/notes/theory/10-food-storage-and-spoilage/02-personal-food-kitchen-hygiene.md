## Personal food & kitchen hygiene

* Food and kitchen hygiene needs to be regularly maintained and obeyed in both personal and public kitchens.
* Always keep hair tied or covered way before cooking.
* Cut your nails short.
* Wear a clean apron to avoid stains.
* Don’t wear jewellery such as rings.
* Clean the cutting boards before using.
* Wash dishes instead of letting them pile up.
* Wash hands and kitchen equipment before using.
* Use a decent sized dustbin or reuse plastic shopping bags to save money.
* Separate raw meats from the cooked to avoid cross-contamination.
* Use separate cutting boards for meat and fruits and vegetables.
* Clean the kitchen sink frequently to avoid bad odour spreading.
* Keep a clean and organized fridge.
* Keep electrical appliances away from the sink or water.
* Wear gloves when washing sharp blades or knives to avoid cuts.
* Disinfect or clean the kitchen counters.
* In shops, especially when maintaining ice, use an ice scoop to transfer or change ice.
* Change ice on regular basis.
