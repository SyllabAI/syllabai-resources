# Nutritive Value of Foods

## Proteins


|<i>Sources</i>|<i>Functions</i>|
|:---|:---|
|HBV: <i>(All essential amino acids.)E.g., Milk, Meat, Fish, Eggs, Cheese.</i>|Growth (hair, nails, skin)|
|<i>LBV:</i>(Lacks some essential amino acids.) E.g., Cereals, pulses, nuts, and some vegetables.|Growth (hair, nails, skin)|
|Chemistry: Oxygen, Carbon, Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Sulphur, Phosphorus.|Body maintenance|
|<b><i>Deficiency:</i></b> Kwashiorkor, Marasmus, and Kidney disease.|Providing energy|
|<b><i>Symptoms:</i></b> Kwashiorkor: Diarrhoea, Fatigue, Enlarged Belly.|Repair|
|<b>Marasmus:</b> Growth failure, sunken eyes, “old man’s” face.|Repair|
