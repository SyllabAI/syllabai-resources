## Effect of Heat on Proteins, Fats, Oils, Sugar, Starches, and Vitamins

|    | ***__Dry heat__*** | ***__Moist heat__*** |
|----|----|----|
| **Proteins** | Denature & digestibility may reduce. | Coagulates. Hydrolysis of bacterial proteins. |
| **Fats** | Breaks to glycerol & fatty acids. | - |
| **Sugar** | Melts – caramelizes - burns – leaves black residue. | Dissolves – caramelizes – burns when water has evaporated. |
| **Starch** | Changes to dextrin | For rice: Grains soften- absorb water- swell – rupture- dissolves to form a paste. |
| **Vitamins** | Baking/roasting, have very little effect on loss of vitamins. | Water-based cooking causes greater loss of vitamins in food |
