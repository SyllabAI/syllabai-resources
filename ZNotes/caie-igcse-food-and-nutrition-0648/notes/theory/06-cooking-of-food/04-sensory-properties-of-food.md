## Sensory properties of food

* Sensory properties of food include properties apart from taste.

E.g.: Smell, texture, appearance etc.

* **Appearance:**
  * Consistency.
  * Presentation.
  * Portion.
* **Texture:**
  * Smooth.
  * Lumpy.
  * Graininess.
* **Smell:** The aroma of the food while it’s being cooled and after

it’s cooked.

* **Flavour:** Combination of tastes and takes other sensory properties into account.
* **Taste:** Detects if food is spicy, sweet, tangy, bitter, sweet etc.
