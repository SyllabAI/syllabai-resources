## Economical Use of Food, Equipment, Fuel, and Labour

* Buy small equipment that last long, e.g.: microwave.
* Buy proper sized pots and pans that trap heat well to prevent wastage of heat from fuel.
* Switch of electrical appliances that are not in use.
* Switch of lights in the kitchen when not in use.
* Buy ingredients in bulk.
* Meal prep to save time and labour.
* Cook in batches
* Buy foods that have longer shelf life.
* Buy foods that are easy to store and don’t take up much space in the kitchen.
