## Organisation of cooking area

* Keep all cleaning equipment under the sink area.
* Keep a drying rack for dishes near the sink area.
* Keep all spices in closed containers to avoid moisture.
* Store pots and pans in a cabinet stacked or hung.
* Store utensils in a drawer neatly with spoons, knives and forks organised.
* Keep all electrical equipment together on one side of the kitchen next to their plugs.
