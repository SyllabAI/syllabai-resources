## Ventilation

* Maintains a balance between air that’s removed and fresh air that’s returned to the kitchen.
* Types of common ventilation:
  * Natural ventilation.
  * Exhaust ventilation.
* Natural ventilation requires no mechanical energy and involves movement of air.
* Exhaust ventilation creates airflow by forcing air out using a fan.
* Advantage to using an exhaust fan is that its efficient in increasing the airflow.
* Disadvantage to using exhaust fan is it uses mechanical energy so high costs and additional maintenance costs.
