## Use of Herbs and Spices

* Herbs:
  * Garlic - Stews, salads, soups.
  * Mint - Drinks, salads, ices.
  * Oregano - Pizza’s, pasta.
  * Parsley - Good source of vitamin C.
  * Thyme - Used for stuffing.
  * Basil - For salads
* Spices:
  * Chilies - Spicy, used in curries.
  * Cinnamon - In cakes, pies, biscuits.
  * Vanilla - For essence, in cakes.
  * Turmeric - In curries, for coloring.
  * Paprika - Not very spicy, used to season dishes.
  * Ginger - In cakes, meat dishes.
