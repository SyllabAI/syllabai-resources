## Methods of Thickening Sauces

* By **coagulation** of protein (e.g.: Egg custard sauce):
  * Mix egg & milk.
  * Add sugar.
  * Pour in a bowl over a pan of boiling water.
  * Stir all the time till egg coagulates.
  * Remove from heat.
* By **emulsification** of fat (e.g.: Mayonnaise):
  * Beat fry ingredients into egg yolk to stabilise emulsion.
  * Add oil drop by drop.
  * Continue adding oil till thickening starts.
  * Add vinegar drop by drop for consistency.
  * Serve.
* **Pureeing** (e.g.: apple sauce):
  * Peel and slice apples.
  * Stew in water & butter till soft.
  * Puree and add sugar.
