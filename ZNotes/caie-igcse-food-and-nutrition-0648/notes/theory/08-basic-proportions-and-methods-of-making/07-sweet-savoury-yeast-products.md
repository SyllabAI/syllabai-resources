## Sweet & Savoury Yeast Products

* **Savoury:** flavourful and tasty. Opposite to salty and sweet.
* **Sweet:** food made with sugar like biscuits or cakes.
* Changes in bread making:
  * Dough rises as CO2 expands in heat.
  * Yeast activity decreases as temperatures rises.
  * Fermentation stops as yeast is killed at 55C.
  * Water is absorbed by starch granules in the flour.
  * Gluten starts to coagulate at 70 C.
  * H2O, CO2 & alcohol escape during baking.
  * Sugars caramelize.
