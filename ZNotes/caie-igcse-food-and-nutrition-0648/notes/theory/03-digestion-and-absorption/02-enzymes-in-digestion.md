## Enzymes in Digestion

* **Amylase:** breaks **starch** to **maltose.**
* **Erepsin:** digests **peptones** to **amino acids** (found in intestinal juices).
* **Invertase:** breaks **sucrose** to **glucose + fructose**.
* **Lactase** breaks **lactose** into **glucose + galactose**.
* **Lipase:** breaks **fats** (found in pancreatic juices/intestines).
* **Maltase:** breaks **maltose** to **glucose**.
* **Pepsin:** breaks **proteins** into **peptides,** then to **peptones,** then **polypeptides**.
* **Renin:** clots milk.
* **Trypsinogen:** breaks ***proteins*** into ***peptones***.
