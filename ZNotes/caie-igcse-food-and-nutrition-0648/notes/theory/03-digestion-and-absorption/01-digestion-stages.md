# Digestion and Absorption

## Digestion Stages


|Physical breakdown|Chemical breakdown|
|:---|:---|
|<b>Teeth</b> rip, tear & grind food to smaller pieces.|Protein & fat <b>DO NOT</b> break down here.|
|<b>The tongue</b> pushes food down the oesophagus.|Carbohydrate: Salivary amylase converts starch to maltose.|
|<b>Salivary</b> glands produce saliva and amylase to moisten food for easy swallowing.||


|Physical breakdown|Chemical breakdown|
|:---|:---|
|Peristalsis moves food around and helps to break it down.|Protein & fat DO NOT break down here.|
|Broken food pieces mix with gastric juices produced in the stomach.|Carbohydrate: Salivary amylase converts starch to maltose.|
|Chyme is a term used to refer to broken-down food.||


|Chemical breakdown only|
|:---|
|<b>Chyme</b> mixed with bile (Bile is produced by the liver and stored in the gall bladder) and pancreatic juice (from the pancreas).|
|<b>Protein:</b> Trypsinogen (produced by the pancreas) mixes with enterokinase to form trypsin; this <i>continues to <b>break down proteins</b> into <b>peptones</b>.</i>|
|<b><i>__Trypsinogen + Enterokinase -> Trypsin__</i></b>|
|Fat: Bile emulsifies fats to disperse them into small droplets. Pancreatic lipase breaks fat into glycerol and fatty acids.|
|<b>Carbohydrate:</b> Amylase breaks down starch to maltose.|


|Chemical breakdown only|
|:---|
|<b>Protein:</b> Erepsin converts peptones to amino acids.|
|<b>Fat:</b> is broken by lipase further.|
|<b>Carbohydrate: Maltase</b> breaks down <b>maltose</b> to <b>glucose. Invertase</b> breaks sucrose to <b>glucose</b> + <b>fructose. Lactase</b> breaks lactose into <b>glucose</b> + <b>galactose.</b>|
