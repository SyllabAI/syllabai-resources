## **Periodic Trends**

* Periodic trends are **specific patterns** that are present in the periodic table.
* The **horizontal rows** are called **periods.**
* The number of occupied electron shells determine the **period** of an element.
* The **vertical columns** are called **groups.**
* The number of outer shell electron determines the **group** of an element.
* It illustrates different aspects of a certain element, including its size and its electronic properties.

**Melting Point in metals and nonmetals:**

* Metal has a high melting point.
  * Examples: Aluminum, Iron, Copper, Tin, Gold, Lead, Titanium, Uranium, and Zinc.
* Mostly, nonmetal has a low melting point.
  * Examples: Nitrogen, Oxygen, Sulphur, Chlorine, Neon.
* Non-metal carbon has **the highest melting point** of all the elements.
* Also, semi-metal boron has a high melting point.

**Metallic Characteristics:**

* Metallic characteristics **decrease from left to right across a period**.
* The decrease in the radius of the atom allows the outer electrons to ionize more readily.
* Metallic characteristics **increase down a group.**
  * The shielding of electrons causes the atomic radius to increase.
