## Elements, Compounds, and Mixture

### What are Pure Substances?

A sample of matter that has definite chemical and physical properties.

* Example: elements, atoms, molecules, compounds.

**Atoms**

* Atoms are the building material of matter.
* An atom consists of a nucleus:
  * Protons (positively charged particles), electrons (negatively charged particles), and protons (neutral particles)
  * Electrons orbit around the nucleus.
* Atom is the **smallest** unit of an element that has all of the same properties of that element.

**Elements**

* Element is a pure substance that cannot be separated into simpler substances by physical or chemical means.

* Elements consist of only one type of atom.

* They can be found in the periodic table of the elements.

  Example:

  * Diamond is made of carbon (C)
  * Copper is an element (Cu)

**Compounds**

* Atoms of two or more **different** **elements** joined together by chemical bonds.

* Made of elements in a specific ratio that is always the same

* They have their own physical and chemical properties.

* They can only be separated by chemical means, not physically.

* The smallest particle is called a molecule.

  Example:

  * Water ($H_2O$) is a compound made of molecule hydrogen (H) and oxygen (O)

  * Sand ($SiO_2$) is a compound made of silicon (Si) and molecule oxygen (O)

  * Table sugar ($C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}$) is a compound made of molecule carbon (C), molecule hydrogen (H), and molecule oxygen (O)

  * Potassium Chloride (KCl) is a metal halide salt composed of potassium and chlorine.

  * Sodium Sulfate ($Na_2SO_4$) is an inorganic compound mainly used for **the manufacture of detergents and in the Kraft process of paper pulping**

  * Calcium Carbonate ($CaCO_3$) is a common substance found in rocks as the minerals calcite and aragonite and is the main component of eggshells, snail shells, seashells, and pearls.

**Molecules**

* A molecule is a group of two or more atoms chemically bonded together. These atoms can be of the **same element** (like $O_2$ or $N_2$) or **different elements** (like $H_2O$).

  * Example: Two nitrogen atoms are joined together ($N+N=N_2$) to make one nitrogen molecule.

* Molecules are held together by bonds: ionic bonds and covalent bonds.

* They exist as molecules of elements or molecules of compounds

**Mixtures**

* A mixture contains two or more different substances that are **physically combined**.

* Substances are **held together by physical forces**, not chemicals.

* There is **no chemical change** that takes place

* Each item retains its properties in the mixture

* They **can be separated physically**.

* There are 2 types of mixture: Homogeneous and Heterogeneous

  ***Homogeneous***: The substance that is uniformly mixed

  * Example: sugar and water

  * Solutions: Solutions are groups of molecules that are mixed up in a completely even distribution.

    * Solute: the substance to be dissolved. In this case, sugar is the solute
    * Solvent: the one doing the dissolving. In this case, water is the solvent

    ***Heterogeneous***: The substance is **not** uniformly mixed

  * Example: Sand in a glass of water or the ingredients in salad dressing

  * Suspensions: is a heterogeneous mixture consisting of parts that are visible to the naked eye
