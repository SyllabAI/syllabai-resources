## Types Of Reaction

### Exothermic Reaction

* Heat is **released** during the reaction
* The reaction **gives** energy to its surroundings
* As a result, the temperature is **increased**
* Also known as a hot reaction
* Examples: Burning, oxidation
  * $2H_2 (g) + O_2 (g) \rightleftharpoons 2H_2O(l) + energy$
  * $H_2O(g)\rightleftharpoons H_2O(l) + energy$

### Endothermic Reaction

* Heat is **absorbed** during the reaction
* The reaction **gets** the energy from its surrounding
* We have to provide energy, which can be **absorbed** during the reaction for the chemical process that occurs
* The result of  ΔH is positive (\+)
* Examples: Melting of ice
  * $Energy + 2HgO (s)\rightleftharpoons 2Hg(aq) + O2(g)$
  * $Energy +H_2O (s)\rightleftharpoons H_2O (l)$
