## Diffusion

* The net movement of molecules from a region of high concentration to a region of lower concentration as a result of their random motion.
  * Example: When a bottle of perfume is opened, the perfume particles diffuse from an area of high concentration (near the bottle) to an area of low concentration. 
* Diffusion forms a homogeneous mixture.

### Factor Affecting The Rate of Diffusion

* **Concentration**
  * The greater the difference in concentration, the faster rate of diffusion.
  * The closer it gets to equilibrium, the slower the rate of diffusion.
* **Mass of Molecules**
  * The heavier it gets, the diffusion will be slower
  * The lighter the molecules, the diffusion will be faster.
* **Temperature**
  * The high temperature will increase the energy.  It will increase the rate of diffusion.
  * Low temperature will decrease the energy of the molecules, it will decrease the rate of diffusion.
* **Solubility**
  * Liquid materials or non-polar will pass through the plasma membrane, the rate of diffusion more than polar substances.
