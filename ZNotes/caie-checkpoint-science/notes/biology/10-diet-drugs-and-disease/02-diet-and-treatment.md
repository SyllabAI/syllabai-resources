## Diet and treatment

### Malnutrition and starvation

* Aid workers from NGOs give **malnourished** children in areas of famine powdered milk mixed water.
* First, they are given **F75** (a form of powdered milk) which prepares their body to start digesting food again.
* After a few days, they are fed **F100** which contains more proteins and energy to help the body build up.
* **Plumpynut** is made more peanut paste, powdered milk, minerals, oil, sugar and vitamins. It is given as replacement to F100.
* **Unimix** is a flour made from maize and soya beans. To this powdered milk, minerals, oil, sugar and vitamins are added. This is mixed with water to make a porridge and added to their diet.
* Children are fed on this diet for up to a month until they can move onto other foods.

### Treating obesity

* Treatment plans with certified professionals help the person shift to a **balanced** diet.
* Another part is to build up an exercise routine so more energy is used up and the body is strengthened.
* In obese adults, the aim is to reduce body mass.
* In kids, the aim is to help the bodies grow in the right proportions so that over a few years the body mass and appearance adjust to normal.
