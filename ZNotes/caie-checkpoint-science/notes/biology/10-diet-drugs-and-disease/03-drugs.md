## Drugs

### Development in the womb

* Alcohol reduces number of sperm produced in males.
* Smoking makes the ovaries less efficient.
* Non medicinal drugs can make the menstrual cycle irregular, or even stop in people with uteruses. They can also slow down sperm production and cause sperms to develop abnormally (eg. two tailed sperm)

| Drug | Effect on foetus |
|----|----|
| Alcohol | - drinking even small amounts can cause nerve damage \n - large amounts can cause the head to be smaller, abnormal development of eyes, nose and lips \n - foetal alcohol syndrome (FAS) |
| Tobacco | - slows down growth \n - organs are smaller than normal \n - lungs work less efficiently |
| Non-medicinal drugs | - slows down growth \n - less resistant to diseases \n - baby’s body can become dependant on the drug the mother was taking |

### Sniffing solvents

* Children take up sniffing solvents as those used in glues, as they are easier and cheaper to get than drugs.
* Solvents cause loss of appetite, resulting in loss of body mass.
* Can cause headaches, sickness, coughing and harm to the brain that results in memory loss.
* Also harms the lungs, affects how the nerves control breathing and damage the structure of the liver, kidneys and bone marrow.
* After sniffing the solvent, a person may lose control of themselves and may suffocate on the plastic bag from which they inhaled the solvent.
* May also become unconscious and if they are sick, their vomit may block their windpipe causing death by suffocation.

### Alcohol

* Alcohol affects the nervous system and slows down the speed at which nerve cells carry signals.
* Small amounts make the person feel more relaxed, but makes the nerves work slightly slower.
* Makes the drinker react more slowly.
* Louder voice, increased aggression, recklessness, slurred speech, difficulty in thinking clearly.
* Too much alcohol can cause uncoordinated movement, or inability to walk. They may fall asleep and become unconscious.
* In extreme cases, they may be sick while unconscious and can choke on their own vomit, causing death by **suffocation**.
* Alcohol is a poison, and the liver collects alcohol from the blood and breaks it down to make it harmless.
* Too much alcohol in the blood can cause intoxication, and the liver cannot handle this level. Medical intervention is needed to remove excess alcohol from the body.
* Alcohol addiction over the years causes *inflammation* of the liver, and development of a disease called **hepatitis**.
* Part of the liver may turn into *scar* *tissue* and leads to **cirrhosis** of the liver. Reduces the liver’s ability to deal with *toxins* and may be fatal.

### Drugs and behaviour

* People may take non-medicinal drugs because of peer pressure, and trying to fit in.
* They may think it is exciting to use substances that are illegal.
* Older people may take them to cope with their feelings of loneliness, sadness.
* Non-medicinal drugs affect the nervous system, and can cause memory loss, mental illness, hallucinations in the long term.
* If a person continues to take drugs over a long period of time (eg. throughout their teenage years) their body becomes more tolerant to the drug, so larger amounts are required for the person to feel it’s effects.
* The drug-taker’s brain becomes so used to the drug that the person becomes dependant on it.
* If they suddenly stop taking the drug, they may suffer severe withdrawal symptoms.

| Non-medicinal drug | How it is used | Effects |
|----|----|----|
| Cannabis (marijuana) | Smoked | - hallucinations, mental illness |
| Cocaine and crack | Sniffed (called snorting), smoked or injected | - overconfidence and carelessness \n - sickness, sleeplessness, mental illness |
| Heroin | Smoked or injected | - can slow down body reactions to the point of death \n - needle users may catch hepatitis and AIDS if needles are shared |
| Ecstasy | Swallowed in tablet form | - speeds up body reactions \n - produces confusion, can be fatal |
| Amphetamines | Swallowed in tablet form or sniffed (Snorted) in powder form | - increases activity at first, then produces feelings of sadness (lasts up to 2 days) \n - heart and brain damage |
| LSD | Swallowed in tablet / liquid form / small piece of paper | - hallucinations, which can be terrifying and to which the brain ‘flashback’ weeks/months later |
