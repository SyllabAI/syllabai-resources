## Disease

### Overview

* **Sexually Transmitted Diseases** **(STDs)** are transmitted through sexual intercourse.
* Eg: chlamydia, gonorrhoea, syphilis
* Malaria is caused by a microorganism from the Protoctista kingdom.
* Malaria is spread by female ***Anopheles*** mosquitos.
* **Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS)** is caused by a virus called **Human Immunodeficiency virus** **(HIV)** which damages the immune system, and makes it nearly impossible for the person to protect themselves from diseases.
* It is spread by sexual intercourse, sharing unsterilized needles, unmonitored blood transfusions, and breastfeeding.
* **Tuberculosis** is a disease caused by a microorganism from the bacteria group.
* Spread by droplets produced by coughing, sneezing and spitting.
* Infects the lungs, can spread to brain, kidneys and bones.

### Development in the womb

* Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID) is the most common disease preventing conception in females.

* It can be caused by a wide range of infections and affects the ovaries, fallopian tubes, and the uterus.

* When the fallopian tubes are infected, they become inflamed and cause infertility.

* Main cause of PID is STDs.

* Infertility can also have other causes like diabetes and kidney disease.

* When males are affected with an STD, activity of the sperm is reduced so that they are less able to swim and find the egg.

* When the person recovers, the sperm goes back to normal. But if the person has contracted the diseases multiple times, tubes carrying the sperm may be scarred due to the infection, and block the path of the sperm.

* Tuberculosis can affect both reproductive systems, blocking the path of sperm in males and causing infections in the oviduct and uterus in females.

* If the mother has malaria, it can kill the foetus as it grows, or slow down its development causing the baby to be abnormally small.

* Tuberculosis during pregnancy may also cause foetal death.

* German measles (Rubella) is a disease which is transmitted by droplets which have been coughed or sneezed into the air.

* It can damage the nervous system of the foetus, produce deafness, blindness, also can damage the heart.

* If a female has an STD during pregnancy, it can cause blindness, deafness, swollen liver and skin sores in her baby.

* A female with AIDS may pass on the virus during pregnancy or at birth, and this will damage the immune system of the baby.

### Development in young people

* Diseases can slow down or stop growth. As the person recovers, growth can begin again.
* Tuberculosis has an active stage when the lungs are attacked and the person begins to cough frequently, develop fevers and lose body mass.
* The bacteria then becomes inactive, and the person recovers. However the person still carries the disease and it may start again and enter the blood, spreading to other organs. This can be fatal unless treatment is provided.
* Virus causing AIDS can be passed to infant through breastfeeding.
* Malaria can damage the nervous system of a growing child, and if untreated it can be fatal.

### Behaviour and health

* AIDS patients may suffer from nervous system disorders which make them think slowly, have trouble with memory, concentration, and lose some coordination.
* They may also suffer from disease of the bones, in which they lose minerals and become weaker. The danger of them breaking increases.
* They may also suffer from kidney disease, heart disease affecting circulation, and may have increased risk of developing cancer.
* Microorganisms that cause Malaria invade the liver and red blood cells. As number of microorganisms increase, the person may face chills and fever every few days.
* In one fatal type of malaria, it can damage the brain, kidneys and lungs.
* STDs affects the reproductive health of a person.
* **Chlamydia** can also infect the eyes causing blindness.
* **Syphilis** can cause sores and rashes on the skin.
