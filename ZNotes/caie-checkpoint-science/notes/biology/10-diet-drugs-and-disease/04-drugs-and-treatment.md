## Drugs and treatment

### Addiction and prevention

* Smokers can receive advice and support from health services.
* **Nicotine** gum, or nicotine skin patches can be used so that they still receive smaller amounts of the drug while not smoking.
* This reduces the effect of the **withdrawal** **symptoms** and helps them break their dependency on cigarettes.
* Alcoholics can take medicine, join support groups, and stay in rehabilitation centres- where there is medical supervision and course of activities to improve health.
* People addicted to non-medicinal drugs may also attend **rehabilitation** centres but can also get into therapy and help from health services to help with their transition.
