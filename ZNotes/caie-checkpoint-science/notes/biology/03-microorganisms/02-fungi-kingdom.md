## Fungi kingdom

* Fungi can be seen without a microscope when they are in large numbers.
* But when there is a stage in their life cycle where they behave like other micro-organisms.

### Fungal reproduction :


1. Fungal **spores** are released from the parent.
2. They are carried by air and water currents and can travel long distances.
3. They have a protective coat (spore coat) that helps them survive in heat, cold, and dry conditions.
4. This **spore** **coat** only opens when the spores land in a place with favourable conditions.
5. A thin thread called **hypha** grows out and begins to digest any food around it.
6. As it feeds, many **hyphae** (plural) are produced.
7. As they grow many **hyphae** form a larger structure called **mycelium**.
8. Which in time produces **fruiting** **bodies** (example - mushroom).

### More about fungi

* Moulds produce **mycelium** that can be seen growing on food like bread. Their fruiting bodies are tiny black globes called sporangia.
* Yeasts are micro-organisms in all stages of their life cycle. They do not produce **hyphae**, but just split into identical copies of themselves.
* A few types of fungi are *parasites*- they feed on other organisms.
* Athlete’s Foot is a fungal infection cause by fungi that feeds on damp spots between the toes.
