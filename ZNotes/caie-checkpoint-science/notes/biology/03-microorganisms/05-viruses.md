## Viruses

* Do not have a cell structure.
* Basically, RNA stores inside a protein coat.
* Can be stored for years without changing - like mineral specimens.
* When they are in their *dormant* state, they do not feed, respire, or excrete.
* When they are placed inside living tissues - they come out of their dormant state.
* Viruses try to take over the host’s cells by making multiple copies of themselves.
* They reproduce at high rates and destroy the host’s cells in the process.
* Each type of virus attacks a certain set of cells in the body.
* Cold virus- attacks cilia and cells in the lining of the nose. This leads to excess mucus, and a stuffy nose.
* Some examples: Influenza, Corona Virus, Chicken Pox, Measles, and rabies.
* Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) can lead to the development of AIDS.
