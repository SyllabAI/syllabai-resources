## Protoctista kingdom

* All living things under this kingdom have microscopic bodies
* Made from one cell (unicellular)
* Contains nucleus (eukaryote)
* Outside of their cell bodies may contain a single long haired structure (flagellum/flagellates)
* The flagellum is covered with a coating of tiny hairs called **cilia** - to help move around in water.
* The flagellates lash their long hair like a whip to move, and the *ciliates* move to and fro.
* **Protophyta**- unicellular ‘plants’ that make their own food from photosynthesis.
* **Protozoa**- unicellular ‘animals’ that consume other organisms like animals do.
* **Amoeba** is a type of *protozoan* which doesn’t have flagellum, or cillia - but makes projections from its body wall called **pseudopodia** or ‘false feet’.
* This helps it move along and find food.
* Some members of the Protoctista live in the bodies of other organisms.
* Some protoctists live in humans that cause diseases like malaria, dysentery.
