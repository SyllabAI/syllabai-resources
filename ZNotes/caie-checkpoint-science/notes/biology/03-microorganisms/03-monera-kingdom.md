## Monera kingdom

* Living things under this kingdom are **unicellular** (single celled organism)
* They are **prokaryotes** (doesn’t contain a nucleus)
* They consist of Bacteria, and Blue-green algae.

### Bacteria

* They are found everywhere - air, water, surfaces of plants, animals, rocks and inside living things too.
* Spherical, spiral or rod shaped.
* Some bacteria feed on the insides of living things - where they cause disease.
* *Diphtheria*, *Whooping Cough*, *Cholera*, *Typhoid, Tuberculosis* and *Food Poisoning* are all diseases caused by different kinds of bacteria.
* Some bacteria are useful.
* Yoghurt is made by introducing certain bacteria to milk and making it sour.
* Vinegar is made by allowing bacteria to feed on ethanol and change it into *acetic* acid.

Bacterial reproduction


1. They reproduce by a process called **binary fission** - where each *bacterium* (singular) divides into two.
2. Under perfect conditions of warmth, moisture and food - bacteria can reproduce by fission once every 20 minutes!
3. When conditions become dry, hot, and unsuitable for feeding - some bacteria form spores.

   They can survive inside these spores for a long time and break out once favourable conditions return.

### Blue-green algae

* They live in seas, oceans and lakes.
* They grow and damp, wet rocks at the sides of rivers and streams.
* You can see them if they occur in large numbers.
