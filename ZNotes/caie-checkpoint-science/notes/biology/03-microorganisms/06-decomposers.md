## Decomposers

* These microorganisms feed on the dead bodies and rot them down.
* When animals and plants die - their bodies would remain unchanged if there were no bacteria and fungi.
* Minerals from the body tissues are released into the soil in this process - and are available to plants to take them up in their roots.
* The plants build up the minerals in their own bodies as they grow, and then they are passed into the animal’s bodies when they are consumed.
* The minerals that are now in the animal’s body, help it grow and keep them alive.
* Without micro-organisms - these minerals would be locked up inside of the plants and animals and would never be available for new living things.
* Decomposers ensure that the minerals that living organisms need are recycled (e.g. maggots, earthworms).
