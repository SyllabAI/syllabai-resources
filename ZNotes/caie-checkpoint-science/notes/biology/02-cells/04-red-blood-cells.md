## Red Blood Cells

- Found in the blood stream

- Haemoglobin combines with oxygen to form *oxyhaemoglobin*.

- Red Blood Cells carry this substance to all parts of the body.

- When the Red blood cells reach their destination, they release the oxygen, so the organs can use it.

- Haemoglobin forms again, for another trip to the lungs.

- The haemoglobin gives the red blood cells their pigment.

### Adaptation

- Mature red blood cells lose their nucleus - to make more space for haemoglobin.

- Red blood cells have a disc-like structure. The bi-concave shape gives a larger surface are for the cells.

- Both these adaptations maximize the amount of haemoglobin the red blood cell can carry. This means more oxygen can get diffused, and can be transported around.
