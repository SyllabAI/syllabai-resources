## Root hair cells

* Found in roots of plants.
* They grow a short distance after the tip of each root.

### Adaptation

* The tiny extensions in the cell allow them to grow easily between soil particles.
* The shape of the extensions gives the root hair cells a larger surface area.
* Both these features help increase the amount of water and mineral salts absorbed from the soil.
* The cell membrane controls which dissolved substances can enter the cell, so no foreign substance is allowed in.
