## Nerve cells

* Found throughout the body.
* Makes up the nervous system.
* Nerves are made from neurones.
* They conduct electrical impulses to and from the brain and Spinal cord.

### Adaptations

* Has long fibres called **dendrites** - which connect distant parts of
  the body to the brain and spinal cord, like your feet!
* Most have a **myelin sheath**. The impulses jump between the gaps of the sheath, making the travel time faster, rather than traveling the whole length of the neuron.
