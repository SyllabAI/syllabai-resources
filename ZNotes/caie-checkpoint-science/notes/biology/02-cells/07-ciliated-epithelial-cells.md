## Ciliated epithelial cells

* Found in the lining of the nose, and the **trachea** (windpipe).
* These are epithelial cells.
* Cilia are microscopic hair-like structures that are extensions of the cytoplasm.
* Cells that have cilia are *ciliated*.

### Adaptation

* Air entering the throat and nose contain foreign substances and dust. This is not good for the lungs.
* These particles get trapped by the mucus secreted by **goblet cells**.
* The cilia wave to and fro, and carries the dust away from the lungs. This is why we sneeze!
