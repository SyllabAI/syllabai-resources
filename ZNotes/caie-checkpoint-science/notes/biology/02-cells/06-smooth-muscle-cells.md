## Smooth muscle cells

* Found in the **oesophagus** (food pipe) and in the intestines.
* Moves the bolus as you swallow to the stomach.

### Adaptation

* Smooth muscle cells are arranged in layers at right angles to each other.
* They contract and relax, creating wave-like motions - moving the food. This motion is known as **peristalsis**.
