## White Blood Cells

* Found in the blood stream.
* White blood cells have an irregular shape. They keep changing as the cytoplasm flows inside.
* White blood cells are a part of the body's immune system.
* They help you stay healthy by attacking harmful bacteria and micro-organisms that may make you ill.

### Adaptation

* **Lymphocytes** (a type of WBC) produces **antibodies**, which attack harmful substances in the blood, like bacteria.
* **Phagocytes** (another type of WBC) *engulfs*, and destroys these harmful substances in the blood.
