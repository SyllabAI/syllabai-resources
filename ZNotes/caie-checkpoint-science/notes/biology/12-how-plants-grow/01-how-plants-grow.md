# **How Plants Grow**

* Soil contains substances called mineral salts.
* Plants need different mineral salts in different amounts to survive.
* The minerals dissolve in the water in the soil and are taken up by the plants via the roots.
* They move through the plant to where they’re needed.
* Some of the important mineral ions include:
