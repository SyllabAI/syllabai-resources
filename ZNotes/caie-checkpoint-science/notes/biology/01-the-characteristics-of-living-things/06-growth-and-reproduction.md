## Growth and Reproduction

* Food is essential to grow, repair damaged body parts, and live.
* Reproduction is important to keep a plant/animal species in existence.
* If the elephants in a herd stopped producing offspring, the herd would eventually disappear (become extinct), as the old elephants died.
* Every single species in the world is essential to maintain the balance.
