## Natural selection

Just as humans select individuals for further breeding, selection in a habitat takes place naturally.

* Those individuals of a species which have the most suitable features adapted to survive will continue to live and breed- passing on their desirable features.

  Individuals which have features that don’t suit them for survival will perish, so that they don’t pass on their features to the next generation.
* This form of selection is known as **the survival of the fittest**, is used to explain the theory of evolution, where one species change in time until another species is produced.
