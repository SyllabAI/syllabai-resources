## **Variation**

### **Continuous variation**

* Features that vary in small amounts from one individual to the next of the same species

* Features that have a wide range of characterization.

* Examples: height, body mass

* Examples: A healthy human new-born can weigh between 2.5 to 3.6 kg. This is a range, and new-borns can fall anywhere between these values.

* Environmental factors like nutrition, exercise and healthcare can affect these features.

* Example: A child growing in poverty in a under-developed country will be malnourished and will have a lesser body mass.

### **Discontinuous variation**

* Distinct features that have no in-between values.

* Examples: blood group, sex

* Example: A human can have only one blood group. For example, they can either be O or AB. They can’t change this.

* Environmental factors cannot affect these features.

* Example: A child’s blood group will remain the same since birth no matter where they grow up.
