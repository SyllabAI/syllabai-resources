## Genetically Modified Organism

In the past selective breeding was the only way to improve crops and farm animals to provide more food.

* In the early 1990s the first genetically modified organism was produced, a tomato with a certain gene that made the fruit soften- turned off. The idea was that the tomato could be stored for longer.
* The quality of the crop wasn’t as good as expected, but work continues to make tomato crops that are disease resistant.
* Genes from other organisms such as wild relatives or bacteria are used now to develop genetically modified organisms.
* It aims to help crops grow better, produce a higher yield or help lower the production costs (e.g. some tomato plants were modified to produce thick tomato puree more cheaply)
* Genetically modified organisms are new organisms. Scientists disagree on their use because they can enter a natural habitat and breed with the native organisms, to produce a more unusual organism- which may have a negative impact on the ecosystem.
