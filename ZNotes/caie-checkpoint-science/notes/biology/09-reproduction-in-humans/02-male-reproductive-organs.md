## Male reproductive organs

* **Testicles** is the male gonad- sperm cells are developed here.
* Testicles develop inside the body of the unborn child and usually move down to the outside before the baby is born.
* **Scrotum** is a bag of muscle and skin that holds the testicles.
* They are positioned outside the body because the conditions are cooler, more optimal for sperm production.
* **Seminiferous tubules** are long microscopic pipes found inside each testicle. Male gametes/sex cells are made here.
* **Epididymis** is a long-coiled tube where the sperm collect, found on top of the testicle.
* Sperms cells travel to the outside of the body along the **sperm duct** and the **urethra**.
* Glands along this path add liquid to the sperm cells, called **seminal fluid**/**semen**.
* Urethra runs through the middle of the penis. Also connected to the **bladder** and is the tube through which urine flows.
* Semen and urine do not flow down the urethra at the same time.
* The penis contains spongy tissue along its length that can fill with blood to make it hard stiff and erect.
* **Glans** **penis** (tip of the penis) has a large number of receptors and is very sensitive.
* The glans is covered with a fold of skin called the foreskin. If circumcision has taken place, the foreskin will have been removed.
