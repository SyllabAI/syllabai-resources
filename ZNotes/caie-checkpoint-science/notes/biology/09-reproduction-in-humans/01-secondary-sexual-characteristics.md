# Reproduction in Humans

## Secondary sexual characteristics

* Up until 20 years of age, sex of the child can only be determined by looking at genitalia, until further changes begin.
* The pituitary gland beneath the brain (behind the nose) is an organ that secretes hormones into the blood.
* Hormones are chemicals that circulate all around the body via the blood stream but have effect on specific target organs/parts.
* At around the age of 10-13 in females and 12-14 in males, the gland secretes increased amounts of a growth hormone and **gonadotrophin**.
* Growth hormone travels across the body, stimulates growth in hands, feet, hips, chest, and legs.
* Gonadotrophin stimulates production of sex hormones.
* In females sex hormones are produced in the **ovaries** and are called **oestrogen** and **progesterone**.
* In males they are produced in the **testes** or **testicles** and the hormone is called **testosterone**
* Sex hormones cause the development of body features known as secondary sexual characteristics.
* These changes take place in the first half of adolescence and is called **puberty**.
* It takes about 3 years in girls, may take longer in boys. It varies person to person.
* When the changes are complete, each person is capable of reproduction.

| Males | Females |
|----|----|
| Facial hair + growth of hair in armpits and pubic region | Growth of hair in armpits and pubic region |
| Voice becomes lower and deeper | Breasts develop |
| Growth of penis and testicles | Growth of vagina and uterus |
| Shoulder broadens | Pelvis widens |
