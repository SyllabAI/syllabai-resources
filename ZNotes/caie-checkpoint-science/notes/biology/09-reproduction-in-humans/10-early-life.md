## Early life

### Breast feeding

* The baby starts to suck as soon as its born.
* During pregnancy some of the hormones produced by the mother cause the **mammary** **glands** (milk-secreting) in her breasts to develop.
* The milk that the mother provides for her baby contains all the nutrients the baby needs.
* It also contains antibodies to protect the baby from the disease.
* Some mothers cannot provide enough milk for their babies and use special powdered milk made up into liquid instead.
* This is delivered in a sterile bottle with a **teat**.

### Milestones and development

* The end of the **umbilical** **cord** attached to the baby withers away and falls off leaving the navel.
* The baby continues to grow rapidly, and the organ system become more coordinated.
* At the end of the first month the baby can lift its head up for a few seconds.
* In the following months the baby can lift its head more steadily, kick and push itself up on their hands.
* It will listen and look for sources of sounds by moving its eyes and turning its head.
* By 6 months the baby’s first set of teeth start to push through its gums.
* The baby’s diet is gradually changed to solid foods.
* When this happens, usually before 12 months the baby is said to be **weaned**.
* All parts of the body continue to grow but at about 4 years of age the head has reached almost its full size.
* The sex organs do not develop fully until puberty.
