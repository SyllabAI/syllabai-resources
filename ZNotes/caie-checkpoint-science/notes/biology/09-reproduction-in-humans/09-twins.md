## Twins

* About 1 in hundred pregnancies produce twins.
* The twins may be identical or non-identical.
* Identical twins form from the same fertilised egg which splits into two during cell division to form two nearly identical embryos.
* Non identical twins form from two eggs which were released into the oviduct at the same time. Each egg is fertilised by a different sperm.
* Identical twins share the same genetic material, non-identical twins don’t.
