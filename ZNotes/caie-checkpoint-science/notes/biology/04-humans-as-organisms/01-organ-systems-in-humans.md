# Humans as Organisms

## Organ Systems in Humans

1. **Support and movement systems:** 

   * Skeletal System. 

   * Muscle System.

2. **Control, regulation, and communication systems:** 

   * Endocrine System. 

   * Nervous System.

3. **Environmental sensing systems:** 

   * Sensory System.

4. **Waste elimination systems:** 

   * Excretory System.

5. **Gas exchange system:** 

   * Respiratory System.

6. **Internal transport and distribution system:** 

   * Circulatory System.

7. **Energy intake and processing system:** 

   * Digestive System.
