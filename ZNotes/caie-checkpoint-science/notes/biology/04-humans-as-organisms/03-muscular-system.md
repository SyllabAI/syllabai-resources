## Muscular System

* Muscle is a tissue that can move. 

* It can contract and expand. 

* A muscle is attached to two bones across a joint with the help of **Tendons** . 

* The action of one muscle produces an opposite effect to the other muscle and causes movement in the opposite direction. 

* These 2 muscles are called **Antagonistic Muscle Pairs.**

* **Smooth Muscle** - Found in other organ systems, such as the digestive system, where it moves food down the alimentary canal. 

* **Cardiac Muscle** - The heart is made of this muscle. Its movement pumps blood throughout the body. The nervous system controls the movement of these 2 muscles so that you don't have to act voluntarily.
