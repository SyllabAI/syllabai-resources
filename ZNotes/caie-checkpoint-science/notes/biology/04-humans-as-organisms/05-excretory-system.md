## Excretory system

* Waste produced by the body in chemical reactions collect in the
  blood.

* As the blood passes through the **kidneys**, a waste product called
  **urea** is filtered from the blood with water.

* The mixture of urea and water is called as **urine**.

* On a hot day, little urea and water are released from the blood - through the skin. This is your sweat. As the sweat evaporates, your skin cools down.

* As the blood passes through the lungs, carbon dioxide is removed and passed into the air.
