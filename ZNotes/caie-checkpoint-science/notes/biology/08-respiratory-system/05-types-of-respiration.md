## Types of respiration

### Aerobic respiration

* Energy is released when sugar molecules take part in a chemical reaction with oxygen.
* Glucose is broken down by oxygen, producing carbon dioxide and water as waste products.
* Word equation: **$\text{Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy}$**
* Symbol equation: **$C_6​H_{12}​O_6​+6O_2​→6CO_2​+6H_2​O$**

### Anaerobic respiration

* Occurs when body doesn’t get enough oxygen for aerobic respiration to take place (e.g. when you exercise, and you cannot breathe fast enough to get the oxygen to release energy for your muscles)
* Body responds by releasing the energy anyway by breaking down glucose and producing lactic acid as a by-product.
* Lactic acid is toxic to the body if it builds up in the muscles for too long, it can be broken down by aerobic respiration.
* That’s why after exercising you take deep breaths to increase oxygen intake, allowing the body to respire aerobically and break down the lactic acid.
