## Smoking and health

### Cigarette smoke

* **Nicotine** is a chemical that is the addictive component of smoking.
* Other chemicals in the smoke swirl around the air passages and the linings
* The heat from the smoke and the chemicals damages the cilia lining, so the dust trapped in the mucus are unable to be moved away.
* More **mucus** is produced and is coughed up by a jet of air as the lungs exhale strongly (smoker’s cough), instead of the cilia carrying it up.

### Impacts on the body

* During smoker’s cough, the amount of dirty mucus reaching the throat may be too much to swallow.
* *Chronic* *bronchitis* develops, the lining of the bronchus becomes inflamed and open to infections from microorganisms.
* Inflammation of the air passages makes breathing more difficult and the smoker develops a permanent cough.
* Coughing causes the already weakened walls of the alveoli to burst - reducing the surface area of the lungs to respire.
* This leads to a disease called **emphysema**, where the alveoli is greatly reduced, causing breathing issues and leading to formation of air pockets in the lungs

### Cancer

* Cells lining the air passages are killed by smoke.
* They are replaced by cells below them as they divide and grow.
* The chemicals in the smoke can alter the DNA of the new cells, causing mutations.
* The new cells containing mutations divide uncontrollably and are called cancer cells.
* These cells replace the normal cells in the tissues around them, but do not perform the functions of the cells they’ve replaced.
* The cancer cells continue to divide until they form a lump called a **tumour**.
* Tumours block the airway or break up and spread to other parts of the lung where more tumours can develop.

**Effects of smoking and drug intake during pregnancy:**

Smoking results in less oxygen being available to the fetus, and leads to many negative effects during and after pregnancy such as:

* Premature birth

* Birth Defects

* Low birth weight

* Increase the risk of infant death

* Drug intake and alcohol consumption can lead to permanent growth problems and intellectual disabilities. Alcohol consumption can lead to fetal alcohol syndrome risks.
