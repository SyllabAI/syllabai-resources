## Biological Pest Control

* It is the use of one animal species to control the numbers of a pest.
* In 300 CE, the Chinese filled sheep bladder with fat and placed them next to ant nests. When the bladder was teeming with ants, they were collected and placed in orange trees. Bamboo poles were placed so that ants could move freely from one tree to the next. These ants didn’t eat the orange, but rather another pest that attacked the fruit.
* In Australia, the prickly pear was introduced as a ornamental plant, but quickly started growing in the wild, turning into a weed. A moth that has a larva that feeds on prickly pear was released, and in time their numbers were reduced.
