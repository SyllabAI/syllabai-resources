## Water pollution

### Fresh water

* Most harmful pollutants in water are PCBs (*Polychlorinated Biphenyls*) and heavy metals such as Cadmium, Chromium, Nickel and Lead.

  In large concentrations they can damage many of the organs in the body, can **cause cancer.**

  PCBs are used to make plastic and along with mercury compounds are taken by organisms in the beginning of the food chain. They are passed up in the food chain as each organism is eaten by another.

* The apex predator has large amounts of toxic chemicals, which can cause permanent damage or death. This is known as **bio-accumulation**.

* Unmonitored use of fertilizers allows them to leach from the soil into rivers and lakes, leading to the overgrowth of water plants including algae. This is called **algal bloom.**

* When these plants die, large number of decomposing bacteria take oxygen from the water.

* Reduction in oxygen levels kills many animals. The whole process is known as **eutrophication**.

* **Phosphates** in detergents also create the same effect.

### Sea water

Pollutants of fresh water are washed into the sea as rivers reach the coast.

* They cause damage to the plants and animals that live in the sea, making them unfit to be collected for human food. Large amounts of oil are transported by tankers in ships.
* Oil released from the ship, or oil that spills out forms a film on the water surface, preventing oxygen from entering the water.
* Also reduces the amount of light that could pass through the upper waters of the sea to reach the phytoplankton (tiny floating plants) affecting the food web.
* When tankers get wrecked, large amounts of oil spill out in the water, and are washed up onto the shore.
* Causes catastrophic damages to the habitat which may take years to recover.

### Oil spills

* If there is a break in oil pipe at sea, or if an oil tanker has a leak - an oil spillage occurs.
* Removing oil spills is very tough, and the damage can last for years.
* Oil coats the seagull’s wings, not allowing them to fly.
* Oil covers the gills of the fish and chokes them.
* This ends up killing many aquatic organisms in a short amount of time, destroying the balance of the ecosystem.
