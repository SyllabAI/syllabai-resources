## Indicators of pollution

Some living things are sensitive to pollution, and can be used as biological indicators.

* Lichens are sensitive to air pollution:


|Level of Pollution|Type of Lichen|
|:---|:---|
|Very high|No lichen|
|High|Crusty|
|Moderate|Leafy|
|Clean air|Bushy|

Some freshwater invertebrates can be used to estimate the amount of pollution in streams:


|Level of Water pollution|Invertebrate|
|:---|:---|
|Very high|No|
|High|Rat tailed maggots|
|Moderate|Bloodworms|
|Moderate to Low|Freshwater shrimp|
|Clean water|Stonefly nymph|
