## Fertilizers

* During harvesting, plants are taken out of the soil, and the minerals they have taken from the soil go with them to the market. Therefore, fewer minerals are left in the soil for the next crop.
* Fertilizers are added to the soil to replace the minerals.
* They are added in quantities that will make sure the plants grow as healthy as possible, and produce a large yield.
* Amount of fertilizer that should be added must be carefully calculated.
* If too little is added, yield is small. If too much is added, plants will not use all the minerals and they maybe leached into streams/rivers and cause eutrophication and pollution.

| Factor | Inorganic | Organic |
|----|----|----|
| Composition | Manufactured chemical compounds (e.g. ammonium nitrate) | Animal and human waste (e.g. manure, sewer sludge) |
| Release | Dissolves in soil water instantly, can be taken up by the roots immediately | Minerals are released more slowly, as decomposers in the soil break it down |
| Application | Light weight; can be spread by aeroplanes and helicopters flying over the field | Heavier; must be spread before the crop is grown, into the soil directly |

### Soil structure

* Good soil has particles of rock bound together with humus (decomposed plants and animals) to form lumps called crumbs.
* Crumbs don’t interlock but settle on each other loosely, with air spaces between them.
* Air provides a source of oxygen for the roots and organisms growing in the soil.
* Air spaces also allow roots to grow easily and settle through the soil.
* Humus acts a s sponge, and holds some of the water passed through the soil.
* Plant roots draw on the water stored in the humus.
* In time, humus rots away and in natural habitats, it is replaced by the decaying bodies of other organisms.
* Manure adds humus to the soil, and helps bind the rock particles together.
* If the soil only receives inorganic fertilizers, humus is gradually lost, and the soil crumbs break down.
* Rocky fragments that remain form dust, which can easily be blown away by the wind.
