## Pests and pesticides

* Fertilizers are used to make the crop yield as high as possible.
* Crop plants maybe affected by other organisms who compete with them for resources, or feed on them.
* These organisms are known as pests.
* Chemicals known as pesticides have been developed to kill them.

### Herbicides

* Seeds are planted so that the plants grow a fixed distance apart from each other.
* This gap allows each plant to receive all the sunlight, water and minerals it needs to grow healthily, producing a large yield.
* A weed is a plant growing in the wrong place. They grow in the spaces between the desired crop plants, and compete for light, water and minerals.
* This means that crop plants may receive lesser light as the weeds shade them and absorb most of the water and minerals.
* Weeds can also be infested with microorganisms that can cause disease in the crop plant (e.g. cereals maybe attacked by fungi that live on grass plants growing as weeds in the crop)
* Weeds are killed by herbicides.
* **Non-selective herbicides** kill any plant. Can be used to clear areas of all plant life, so that crops can be grown in soil later.
* **Selective herbicides** kill only certain plants, the weeds alone. Can be used while the crop is growing, to eradicate just the targeted weeds.
* Herbicides may be sprayed onto crops from the air.
* Some of the herbicide may drift away from the field and into surrounding natural habitat, killing the plants there.

### Fungicides

* Chemical that kills fungi
* Fungal spores may be in the soil of a crop field, floating in the air, or on the seeds before they are sown.
* Seeds maybe coated with fungicides to protect them when they germinate.
* They are also sprayed on crop plants to give a protective coat against fungal spores in the air.

### Insecticides

* When a large number of plants of the same kind are grown together (ref. monoculture farming) they can be a huge feeding area for insects.
* Large populations of insects can build up on the plants, causing irreparable damage.
* A swarm of locusts can eat entire fields of plants.
* Insecticides are used to kill these insect pests.
* **Narrow-spectrum insecticides** kill only certain insects, and leave others unharmed.
* **Boad-spectrum insecticides** kill a wide range of insects, not only those that feed on crop, but also predatory insects that may prey on them.
* If insecticides drift away from the fields after spraying, they can kill insects in their natural habitats.
