## Decomposers

Organisms that break down dead matter, and gain energy from doing so.

When they have finished feeding, bodies of plants and animals reduce into simpler substances from which they were made.

Example- carbohydrates in plants are broken down into carbon dioxide and water as the decomposers respire.

* Other substances are released from the plant’s body as minerals and return to the soil.
* Decomposers are essentially **recyclers**; recycle the substance from which living things are made so that they can be used again.
