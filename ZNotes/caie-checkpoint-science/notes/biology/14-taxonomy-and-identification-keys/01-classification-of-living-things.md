# **Classification of Living Things**

Living things are divided into 6 Kingdoms:

* **Animalia (Animals):** Multicellular organisms that eat other living things and can usually move.

* **Plantae (Plants):** Multicellular organisms that make their own food through photosynthesis and cannot move on their own.

* **Fungi:** Organisms like mushrooms, molds, and yeasts that absorb nutrients from other matter.

* **Protista (Protists):** A diverse group of mostly single-celled microscopic organisms, such as algae and amoebas.

* **Bacteria (Eubacteria):** True bacteria that are microscopic and single-celled.

* **Archaea (Archaebacteria):** Ancient single-celled prokaryotes
