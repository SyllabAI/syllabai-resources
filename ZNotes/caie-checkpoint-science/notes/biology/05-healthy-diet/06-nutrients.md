Protein

|

Builds and repairs body tissues, makes muscles, and supports the immune system

|

Causes slow growth in children, muscle loss, swelling, and a weak immune system, also known as Kwashiorkor

|

Chicken, turkey, fish, beef, eggs, and dairy products like milk, yogurt, and cheese, Beans, lentils, chickpeas, tofu, tempeh, quinoa, nuts, and seeds

||
||

Carbohydrates (Carbs)

|

Provides energy quickly for daily activities

|

Causes low energy, constant tiredness, dizziness, and muscle weakness

|

Oats, brown rice, whole-wheat bread, whole-grain pasta, quinoa, and barley., Potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn, and fruits like bananas, apples, and berries.

||
||

Fats and Oils

|

Provides long-term stored energy, keeps the body warm, and protects organs

|

Causes lack of essential fatty acids

|

Avocados, olive oil, extra virgin coconut oil, and ghee, Almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds, and natural peanut butter. Salmon, mackerel, and sardines.
