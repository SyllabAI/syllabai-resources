## Proteins

### Structure

* Made form atoms of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen and sometimes sulfur.
* Atoms of these elements join to make molecules of amino acids.
* Amino acids link together to form protein molecules.

### Uses in the body

* Are the building blocks of the body.
* Needed for building structures inside cells and the formations of tissues and organs.
* Helps in growth, repair of damaged parts, and replaces tissues that are constantly worn out (eg: lining of the mouth)
* Enzymes, hormones, and antibodies are made from proteins.
