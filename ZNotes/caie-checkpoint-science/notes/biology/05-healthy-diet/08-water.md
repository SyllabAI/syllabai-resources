## Water

Water is essential because it acts as the primary delivery system and environment for every chemical reaction in the body.

* It is an ideal solvent which allows all the chemical reactions in the body to take place properly.

* **Nutrient Absorption:** Water breaks down the food you eat so your body can absorb vitamins and minerals.

* **Nutrient Transport:** It forms the basis of your blood, which carries oxygen and nutrients to your cells.

* **Waste Removal:** Water filters out toxins through your kidneys, liver, and sweat glands, and prevents constipation.

* **Temperature Control:** It keeps your body at a safe temperature by allowing you to sweat when you get too hot.

* **Joint and Tissue Protection:** Water acts as a lubricant and cushion for your joints, spinal cord, and brain.

* **Energy Levels:** Even mild dehydration can cause fatigue, brain fog, headaches, and a drop in physical performance.

* Inadequate amount of water in the diet can cause dehydration, dry mouth, extreme thirst, headaches, and dark urine.
