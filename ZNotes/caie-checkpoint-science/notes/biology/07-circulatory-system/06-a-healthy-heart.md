## A healthy heart

**Heart problems and disease**

* Walls of the arteries are elastic, and in young people they are free from obstruction and their diameters are large enough to let the blood flow with ease. 

* As the body ages, the wall become less elastic. 

* Fatty substances such as cholesterol stick to the walls. Calcium settles in the fatty layer and forms a raised patch called an **atheroma**. 

* Blood has lesser space to pass along the artery, and its pressure rises as it pushes through the narrow tubes. 

* Other components of the blood such as **platelets** settle on the atheroma and make it larger. 

* This may cause a blood clot (which narrows the artery even more) or can completely block it, causing **thrombosis** . Artery cannot supply oxygen and nutrients to the relevant organ. 

* Thrombosis in coronary artery (blood vessel that supplies blood to the heart) causes **heart attack** . 

* Thrombosis in a brain artery causes **stroke**. 

* Features that develop in the body that cause heart disease can be inherited. People who have relatives with heart disease should take care to keep their own heart and circulatory system healthy.

 **Keeping the heart** **healthy** 

* Heart muscles require exercise to remain strong, just like the rest of our muscles. 

* Heart muscles contract more quickly and strongly during exercise that it does it at rest, so that more can be pumped to your muscles that need extra oxygen. 

* Regular exercise keeps the organs healthy and uses up large amounts of fat building up in the body.

* To prevent cholesterol blocking the arteries leading to the heart, a lower fat diet can be adopted.

* People who have high-fat diets and are obese will cause strain to their hearts as it has to push the blood to the enlarged body. The extra strain on the heart and the buildup of fatty substances in the coronary artery can cause the heart muscle to fail, leading to heart attack.
