# Listening

## General Tips

* Keep vigilant and an active mind when taking this exam.
* All the parts of the audio are repeated twice.
* Mark all the answers in pencil when the audio is played once during the exam.
* When the audio plays for the second time, recheck along with the audio to see if you marked the answers correctly, and then fill it out with a pen.
* Read the questions thoroughly and underline the keywords.
* If a question asks for more than one detail, write the details in 2 separate sentences.
* This paper also tests your spelling, so having a vast vocabulary and correct spelling.
  * some words you can learn, which are commonly repeated, are
    * Days of the week, name of the months, time of the day, etc.
    * Spellings of numbers
    * Daily life words
* The best way to master this exam is to do as many listening papers as you can to improve your Urdu language hearing skills and also help in time management practice, which is required for this paper.
