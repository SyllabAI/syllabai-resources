## Note-Making

* Time to allocate: **12 - 15 minutes**

### **What it will include:**

* The question requires one to read the passage and make notes under the headings given
* Make sure to write the correct points under each heading

### **Tips that might help:**

* Before reading the passage, go through the headings
* This gives one an idea of what information to find from the passage.
* Usually, answers are found in chronological order.
* The number of points required for each part corresponds to the marks given for the heading, so if the question is for two marks, 2 points are needed in the form of bullets.
* If bullets are not given in answer space, MAKE SURE to put bullets, as this part of the paper wants points taken from the passage.
