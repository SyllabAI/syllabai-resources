## Summary

* Time to allocate: **14 - 15 minutes**
* Just write a summary using the points you made in exercise 3,
* The summary needs to be in **your own words,**
* The summary format depends on what they ask for in the question. It can be written in chronological order, discussing advantages or disadvantages.

### **Tips that might help:**

* Write the summary in continuous prose (no bullet points)
* The best way to give a summary a structure is to use connectives
* List of connectives which may be used in writing:

| First / firstly, second / secondly, third / thirdly | پہلے / پہلے ، دوسرے / دوسرے ، تیسرے / تیسرے |
|:---|---:|
| Next, last, finally | اگلا ، آخری ، آخر میں |
| In addition, moreover | اس کے علاوہ ، اس کے علاوہ |
| Further / furthermore | مزید / اس کے علاوہ |
| Another | ایک اور |
| Also | بھی |
| In conclusion | آخر میں |
| To summarize | مختصر کرنے کے لئے |

* Remember to stick to the word limit, Don’t write too much and remove unnecessary points.
* Practice past paper questions and polish your vocabulary to write a summary in your own words quickly.
