# Reading

## Short Answer Questions

* Time to allocate: **8 -12 minutes**

* The text in this part of the paper can be anything from an advertisement, brochure, leaflet, guide, report, manual, instructions, or a newspaper/magazine article.

### **Tips that Might Help:**

* You can copy phrases directly from the passage as your answer
* It is essential to look at the keywords in the question and any noun/verb.
