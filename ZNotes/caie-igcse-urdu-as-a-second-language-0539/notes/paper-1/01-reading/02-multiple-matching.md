## Multiple Matching

* Time to allocate: **10 - 12 minutes**

### **What it will include:**

* A series of short paragraphs followed by sentences to match its corresponding paragraph
* You will have to examine each paragraph closely and choose which paragraph the given point matches.

### **Tips that might help:**

* Just write the person/thing the point corresponds to, given in the text.
* Some questions are direct, so the answers are easy to find. For others, you will have to read between the lines. You have to look at what the author implies and what they mean.
* Some questions will also use synonyms of the words used in the passage to confuse you!
* Tick boxes using a pencil and once done selecting all options then fill them out with a pen, to avoid ticking a wrong box.
