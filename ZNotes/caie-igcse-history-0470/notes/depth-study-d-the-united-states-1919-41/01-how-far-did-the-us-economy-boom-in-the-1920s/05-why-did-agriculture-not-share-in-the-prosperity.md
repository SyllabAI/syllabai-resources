## Why did agriculture not share in the prosperity?

### **Overproduction**

* During WW1, demand for crops was high and large exports were sold to Europe
* After the war, Europe was able to meet its own needs and leftover crops were left as a surplus in America
* Fruit growers shipped their products in large quantities, but demand for this decreased as economies affected by the war recovered
* Low demand and high production meant grain prices collapsed

### **Technology**

* Farm labourers were laid off and replaced by machinery
* The industry became mechanised
* This new machinery made farming more efficient, but with such low demand, it resulted in overproduction.
* Largely mechanised farms turned all the profits while still losing money because of overproduction.
* Small farmers suffered, and most were barely able to make money at all
* Many farmers had to sell their farms or were evicted because of low income
* Between 1920 and 1930, the number of farms declined for the first time in history

### **Synthetic production**

* Rayon became a popularly produced material used in clothing
* Synthetics soon replaced raw materials like cotton and wool
* Cotton and wool farmers suffered, and mass-producing factories took over the material aspect of the agriculture industry
* In some cotton mills, workers were paid $10 a week
