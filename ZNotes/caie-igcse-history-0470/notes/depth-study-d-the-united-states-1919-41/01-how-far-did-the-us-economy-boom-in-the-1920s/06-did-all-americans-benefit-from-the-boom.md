## Did all Americans benefit from the boom?

### **Who benefited from the boom?**

* **Old Immigrants**
  * They had a lot of old money, and many were factory owners.
  * Few worked jobs where they were at higher risk of being laid off
* **Assembly Line Workers**
  * As the assembly line gained traction, those working in mass production gained higher wages and were in higher demand
  * This included those working in automobiles and other commercial appliances
* **Existing Construction Workers**
  * With expansion across the West Coast and more factories being built, those working in construction were also in high demand.
  * A lot of these workers were Asians that had immigrated from East Asia
* **Booming Industry Workers**
  * Some old industry workers benefited from the boom, like those in steel and glass, which were in high demand because of technological development.
  * New industries like synthetic materials were cheap and fast to produce, were mass advertised, and were durable, meaning they were also in high demand.
  * Workers in these new industries also benefited

### **Why didn’t everyone benefit from the boom?**

* **Farmers**
  * Most farmers were replaced by new machinery.
  * Overproduction meant there was very low demand for crops, and a surplus of farmers meant many lost their work.
  * There was competition from Canadian farmers.
  * Farm labourers were laid off since farmers could not afford to pay their wages
  * Large farms became mechanised and did not need workers to harvest crops
* **Black People**
  * Most lived in the South and worked as labourers or sharecroppers
  * 75% lost their farm jobs
  * Many migrated north for more opportunities but faced much more discrimination
  * 60% of black women worked as low-paid domestic workers
  * Car factories and other booming industries hired low numbers of black people
    * Some were all-white
  * Living conditions in black communities were poor.
* **New Immigrants**
  * Many arrived just before or after the war.
  * Unemployment rates were high because of saturation in most industries and nearly 4 million unemployed troops.
  * Often took whatever work they could find and were usually less educated
  * Mass immigration meant labour was cheap, and they were not paid well
  * Labour became increasingly mechanised, and it was difficult to find jobs
* **Old Industry Workers**
  * Almost every industry was undergoing mechanisation, and workers were being let go
  * While some industries, like steel, benefited, most older industries suffered
  * Overproduction caused prices to drop and wages to fall
    * Markets for old industry products were shrinking
    * Raw materials were losing demand and being replaced by newer synthetics
    * Coal was being replaced by electricity, oil, and gas
      * Mines closed
      * Safety standards dropped
  * 600,000 workers went on strike in 1922 but to no avail
  * Unions were broken since non-union mines charged less and profited more
