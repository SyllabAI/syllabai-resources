## Why did some industries prosper while others did not?

* **Industries that prospered included technology, advertisement, entertainment, metals, and automobiles.** <br>
* Mechanization made mass production considerably easier in these industries
* With many of them being new developments and with mass advertising, products appealed to consumers and were in high demand
* Mass production also resulted in low prices and high availability, so products were bought in millions per year
* Old industries like metal boomed because of the demand due to automobile expansion
* The assembly line pioneered the expansion of the automobile industry and made cars affordable for most households
* Many entertainment activities became popular leisure activities, and cinema, media, and sports turned millions in profit <br>
* **Industries that did not prosper included agriculture, coal, textiles, and factory work.** <br>

### **Unemployment**

* After the war ended, millions of troops were left without jobs
* While some found work in growing industries and factory work, many did not
* Production rates decreased as exports did, so the need for workers was lower <br>

### **Technology**

* The development of new technology meant many workers were left without a job.
* Mass production and the assembly line also resulted in many workers being laid off as their needs decreased <br>

### **New materials**

* New synthetic materials, like nylon and rayon, became popular since they were cheap to manufacture and durable.
* This reduced the demand for natural materials like cotton and wool
* Though synthetic manufacturing boomed, older industries that grew these natural materials went into crisis as there was decreasing demand <br>

### **Electricity**

* The development of electricity reduced the demand for previously used fuels like coal.
* While coal still powered many homes and factories, electricity was a cheaper option, and so as America turned to electricity, the coal industry began to fail.
