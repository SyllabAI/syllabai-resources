# How far did the US Economy Boom in the 1920s?

## The US System Of Government

* **The Federal System:** Individual states take care of their own internal affairs and anything that concerns the whole country is the job of the Congress.
* **The Constitution:** a set of rules that every individual in a country must follow.
* **The President:** The most important politician. Is elected every 4 years. The congress and the supreme court stop the president from being too powerful.
* **Congress:** Made up of the senate and the House of Representatives. Congress and the president run the country.
* **The Supreme Court:** Made up of judges and makes sure the government doesn't misuse their power and can have the power to say that a law is unconstitutional.
* **Parties:** Two main political parties,the republicans and the democrats. In the 1920s and 1930s, republicans were stronger in the north and the democrats were stronger in the south. The republicans wanted the government to not intervene in everyday life of the people but the democrats were more willing to intervene!

### **The Republican Policies**

* **Laissez-faire:** They believed that if the government leaves businesses alone to private properties they will prosper.
* **Protective tariffs:** Made It more expensive to import foreign goods and therefore protected businesses against foreign competition.
* **Low taxation:** They believed that if people made their own money they would spend it on American goods and reinvest their money in industries.
* **Powerful trusts:** The republicans allowed the trusts to do what they wanted because they believed that they knew better than politicians about what's good for the USA, the Democrats disagreed.
