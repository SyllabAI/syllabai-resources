## Who were the people of “The Melting Pot?”

### **Who were the Americans?**

* Immigration has made America a very mixed society
* By 1920, over 103 different nationalities lived there
* America became known as “the melting pot” because everyone became assimilated and “stirred together” to lose their old identity and become Americans
* **Old Immigrants**
  * The first European settlers mostly came from northern and western Europe
    * The British
    * The Germans
    * The Scandinavians
  * Their descendants usually had the best jobs, the most money, and the most political power.
  * They were often known as **WASPS**: White Anglo-Saxon Protestants 
* **Native Americans**
  * The original inhabitants of the land
  * They were affected by waves of immigration and forced off their land from 1850 to 1890
  * Many lived in reservations across America.
    * Some stayed traditional and maintained their ways of living, remaining mostly isolated from the rest of society.
    * Most of them assimilated amongst others 
* **Southern and Eastern Europeans**
  * Most immigrated to America in the late 19th century
    * The Polish
    * The Russians
    * The Italians
  * Many were escaping persecution and poverty in Europe
  * Many were Jews or Catholics
  * They were often treated very badly
* **Asians**
  * Lived throughout the USA but were heavily concentrated on the West Coast
  * Most were Chinese, Japanese, or Korean
  * Most were low-income labourers, especially construction workers
    * Helped build the West Coast cities
    * Constructed railroads
* **Hispanics**
  * People from South America and Mexico also wanted a better life in the USA
  * Shared the common language of Spanish
  * Many of them, especially Mexicans, worked as cowboys on cattle ranches in the West.

### **Why did people want to immigrate to the “Land of Opportunity?”**

* **Pull Factors**
* What did people find attractive in America?
  * **Space**
    * America had wide plains with lots of farm settlements
    * Growing cities had room for expansion 
  * **Natural resources**
    * Land was cheap
    * America had large stores of oil, timber, and minerals
  * **Economic opportunity**
    * American industry and business led the world
    * The USA had industrialized rapidly after 1850
    * The economy was growing fast
    * There were many opportunities to set up new businesses
  * **Wages**
    * Wages for skilled trades and factory labour were higher than in Europe
    * Farm workers were able to buy land at the time.
  * **Land of the Free**
    * People believed they would be free to practice their religion
    * The 1791 American Bill of rights guaranteed freedom of religion, speech, and the press
* **Push Factors**
* What made people want to leave their countries?
  * **Overcrowding**
    * Populations were increasing
    * There was a shortage of land, especially in Europe 
  * **Lack of opportunity**
    * Europe was still a class-dominated society
    * The upper classes had the best land and housing and ran the businesses
    * Lower classes could not improve their situation 
  * **Unemployment**
    * Many workers were being laid off
    * Wages were meagre due to a lack of demand
