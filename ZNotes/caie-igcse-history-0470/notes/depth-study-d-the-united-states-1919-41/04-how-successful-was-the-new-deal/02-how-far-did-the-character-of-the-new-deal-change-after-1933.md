## How far did the character of the New Deal change after 1933?

More work had to be done

**Second New Deal introduced between 1935 and 1937**

Emphasis on victims of depression and creating a fairer American society

**The Wagner Act, 1935**

* Replaced the NRA, introduced by Senator Wagner
* Supported the rights of employees to form trade unions and outlawed unfair practices used by employers to prevent union employment, such as the dismissal of trade union member workers.
* National Labor Board – referee between trade unions and employers.

**Social Security Act, 1935**

* Provided people with unemployment insurance, old age pensions, and help for the sick and disabled.
* Funded by taxes levied on employers and workers.

**Work Progress Administration (WPA), 1935**

* Addressed the problem of unemployment
* Provided a range of projects and work programs – building schools, hospitals, and highways.
* Schemes to employ artists, actors, and writers.
* National Youth Administration – part-time work and vocational training for young people.

**Resettlement Administration (RA), 1935**

* Focused on the plight of farm labourers, sharecroppers, and tenant farmers who were made unemployed.
* Aimed to move 500,000 families to areas with better land.
