## What were the reasons to Roosevelt's success in the election of 1932?

### **The experiences of ordinary people (1929 - 1930)**

* The conditions the ordinary people faced were horrifying. 
* There were people digging through garbage cans because they couldn't afford food and people would close their eyes so they couldn’t see the maggots and worms in their “food”.
* Whereas on the other hand millions of pounds of food were being thrown away to make sure prices weren’t rising.
* Many people were losing their jobs and they had to be homeless because the government claimed their farms / houses / land.
* Many Americans had to live in shanty towns called “Hoovervilles”.
* The ordinary people were tired of such a living so when the 1932 election came around they wanted someone who would end the great depression and improve the lives of ordinary people.

### **The policies of the Republicans**

* The Republicans followed four specific policies: the laissez-faire, import tariffs, low taxations and powerful trusts. 
* The one that, in particular, didn't help and the great depression was the laissez-faire.
* While the laissez-faire had helped America reach the prosperity of the 1920s, it was a reason why the flaws in the American economy were showing 
* Hoover had believed that ‘prosperity was right around the corner’ and did very little to change that policy
* This led to the American public regard Hoover as a “do nothing” president.
* Once there was Roosevelt who had promised the American public that he would be doing a lot to help solve this, varying from farming issues to unemployment to bank security and more!
* This was a lot more roosevelt was offering to the American public than hoover had done in his entire presidential race

### **The actions taken by the Republicans**

* The republicans did very little to prove against the “doing nothing” allegations.
* Hoover had tried to restart the economy with tax cuts and tried to persuade leaders to not cut wages and even started the reconstruction finance company to stop banks from going bankrupt.
* But all of this was in vain as most republicans were very hesitant towards changing their policies and did not want to interfere too much.
* He created the President's Emergency Committee for Employment (PECE) to coordinate state and local relief programs and develop methods for increasing employment in the private sector, 
* But with no direct control of funding for relief or jobs, he requested that the Federal Farm Board give the Red Cross for distribution to relief agencies.
* He believed that “prosperity was just around the corner”. By the time Hoover started to take measures, it was already too late to provide relief.

### **Roosevelt’s election campaign and personality.**

* Roosevelt and his campaign was an extreme contrast to hoover's. he wanted an active government to improve the lives of ordinary people.
* He had plans on getting people back to work and he already started them in his own state. He was also not afraid to ask for advice from experts of the industries.
* All these characteristics helped show the people exactly what they wanted; action.
* He made many speeches promising a “new deal" and he radiated confidence.
* He made personal contact with the American people and seemed to offer hope and a way out of the terrible situation they were in. That is why he won the election.
