|Vilna 1920-1929|Poland seizes Lithuania’s capital, Vilna.|The league asks Poland to withdraw, but Poland refuses. France will not go against Poland as they see it as a potential ally, so Vilna is awarded to Poland.|Failure|The league was unable to stop polish aggression and France’s national interests overpowered international need for justice|The power imbalance was clear; permanent council members were using the league rulings in favor of national interests rather than international stability.|
|Aaland Islands, 1920-21|Sweden and Finland had a dispute over which nation had ownership over the Aaland Islands. The islands had swedish population but belonged to finland.|The league decided that the islands would remain with finland, but the swedish population must be protected and given autonomy.|Success|Both nations agreed to league decisions, and no conflict ensued|Showcasing the league’s successful decision making abilities, HOWEVER success was also due to the fact that nations were tired of war and so were willing to give the league a chance.|

### How important was the League’s humanitarian work?

**Refugees**

- After the war, millions of people were displaced in Europe and Asia.
- Refugee commission introduces Nansen Passport, allowing stateless refugees to travel and resettle.
- Resettled around 400,000 prisoners of war.
- Provided millions of refugees with food, shelter, and employment

**Health**

- Conducted campaigns to fight malaria, yellow fever, leprosy, smallpox, and tuberculosis.
- Promoted vaccine campaigns
- Stopped the outbreak of typhus in Eastern Europe

**Working Conditions**

- The ILO promoted worker rights, fair wages, and decent working hours.
- Placed limitation on child labour
- Banned poisonous lead paint
- Established over 60 international labor conventions, such as 8 hour workday and better working conditions for women and children.

**Slavery**

- Supported abolition of slavery in Ethiopia and Liberia
- Strived to end forced labor in colonial territories
- Reduced trafficking of women and children, especially in prostitution rings.
- Put pressure on colonial powers to act on the issue
