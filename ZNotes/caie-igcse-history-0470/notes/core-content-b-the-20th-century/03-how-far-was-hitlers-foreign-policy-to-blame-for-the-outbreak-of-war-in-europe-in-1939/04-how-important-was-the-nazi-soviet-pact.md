## How important was the Nazi-Soviet pact?
### End of Appeasement 
- March 1939: Hitler marched troops into the rest of Czechoslovakia; allies realized Hitler’s next step would be Poland; threatened war if he tried invading.
- While he did not fear Britain and France, he did fear the USSR entering the war.

### Nazi-Soviet Pact
- August 1939: Nazi-soviet pact signed.
- A 10 year non-aggression pact between the two nations, seen as highly surprising as Hitler was very vocal in his hate of communism.
- Through the pact, Stalin and Hitler secretly divided up Polish territories. 
- Hitler avoided a 2 front war through the pact. 
- Why did Stalin agree? The pact gave stalin much needed time to prepare for war against Hitler; resources from poland and baltic states would help gow soviet economy; poland would be a good buffer zone against German attacks; Stalin did not trust britain and france following the munich agreement, felt they were powerless against germany, and believed they supported Hitler due to his anti-communist ideals.

### Hitler invades Poland
- 1st September 1939: Hitler invades Poland
- Allies give Hitler an ultimatum: withdraw or war.
- 3rd September: Britain and France declare war on Germany.
