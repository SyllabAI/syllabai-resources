- War would be avoided by conceding to some demands of aggressive dictators (axis powers), essentially appeasing them.

### Invasion of sudetenland.
- Hitler sought the Sudetenland for its strategic geographic position, economic resources, military defenses, and large population of ethnic Germans to strengthen and protect Germany.
- Britain, France and the USSR had promised to protect Czechoslovakia against a German invasion.
- 15 September 1938: Chamberlain and Hitler meet in Bavaria, agreeing that certain parts of sudetenland would be given to German if governments of Britain, Czechoslovakia and France agreed; HOWEVER Hitler claims that germans in sudetenland are being mistreated by Czech government, demands all of sudetenland, Chamberlain tells British government to prepare for war,
- BUT 29th september: Munich Conference is held to discuss the issue with the presence of Italy, Germany, Britain, and France, but neither the USSR OR Czechoslovakia.
Creation of the Munich Agreement; Sudetenland would do to Germany, borders of Czechoslovakia would be secured; Czech government had no choice but to agree to prevent war.
- 1st October: German troops march into sudetenland; Poland and Hungary take over Czech territory where they claim their citizens live.
Chamberlain returned to Britain declaring war had been delayed; the Czech leader resigned; trust in hitler and his promises had been discarded.
