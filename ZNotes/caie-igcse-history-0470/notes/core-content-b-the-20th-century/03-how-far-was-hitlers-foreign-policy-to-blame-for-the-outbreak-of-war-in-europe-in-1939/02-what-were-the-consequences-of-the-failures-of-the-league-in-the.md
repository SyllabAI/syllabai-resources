## What were the consequences of the failures of the League in the 1930s?
### Great Depression hindered the league’s work
- Member states like Britain and France became more focused on national interests than collective security and international interests. 
- League was supposed to uphold ToV, but Britain disagreed; league took no action when - - German troops reoccupied the Rhineland in 1936.
- League could not stop German Rearmament in 1935, so all permanent members began rearmament.
- Manchuria and Abyssinia crises showed league could not deal with aggressive major powers, encouraging Germany, Italy, and Japan to take further actions.

### The Saar plebiscite, 1935
- As stated in ToV, 15 years after the Saar was under league mandate, a plebiscite took place to decide whether it would remain under league control, be a part of Germany, or a part of France.
- Over 90% voted for Germany; Hitler reclaimed one of Germany’s land, and while following the treaty; great propaganda for Hitler.

### Hitler’s foreign policy
- Destroy ToV- Rearm, Reintroduce conscription, Build up armed forces, Take back lost territory.
- Destroy communism
- Reunite Germany- Anschluss with Austria, taking back the Polish corridor and absorbing Sudetenland.
- Living space/ Lebensraum- expand eastwards toward Poland and the Soviet union.

### Foreign policy is seen as “Reasonable”
- No one stopped rearmament rallies and conscription as other countries were doing it too.
- BUT not everything was achieved easily- Mussolini objected to annexation of Austria in 1934, putting a stop to that for a while.

### The occupation of the Rhine
- Hitler uses 1935 alliance between France and Russia as a reason to reoccupy “Germany is surrounded by enemies”
- Why was this not stopped? League was busy with the Abbysinian crisis; Britain believed Germany had a right to the Rhine; France was undergoing elections so did not want to be in a conflict with Germany.

### Spanish Civil War
- Fought between left wing republican government and right wing rebels led by General Franco.
- The Soviet Union sent weapons in support of the republican government; Mussolini and Hitler declared for Franco.
- Britain and France did not get directly involved.
- Spain became a testing ground for the new German Luftwaffe (air craft)
- Relations between Musolini and Hitler strengthened; Britain and France sped up rearmament. 
- 1936: Germany and Japan sign the Anti-Comintern Pact against communism.
- 1937: Germany and Italy form the Rome-Berlin Axis alliance.
Germany, Italy, and Japan became known as the “Axis Powers”.

### Anschluss with Austria, 1938
- Many in Austria supported Anschluss, Hitler tells the Austrian Nazi party to stir up trouble and demand anschluss.
- Austrian chancellor appeals to Britain and France for aid; none offered; decides to hold a plebiscite; hitler fears losing plebiscite, so demands Chancellor’s resignation; replaced by Nazi leader Seyss-Inquart, who asks Germany for help in restoring order.
- March 1938: german troops march in unopposed; plebiscite held, Germany wins with 99% votes.
- British prime minister Chamberlain supported the union.
