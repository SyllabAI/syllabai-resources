## Causes of the second world war
- ToV led to political and economic problems for Germany, alongside resentment 
- Failures of the league showed Hitler that it was powerless against a powerful nation like Germany, especially without support of the USA; other nations started prioritizing national interests over league interest.
- Appeasement gave hitler the Rhine, Austria, and Czechoslovakia without any opposition, making hitler believe his actions would never be reprimanded.
- Nazi-Soviet pact allowed hitler to avoid a two front war when he invaded Poland.
- Hitler’s Foreign policy pushed all of his actions leading up to the invasion of Poland.
