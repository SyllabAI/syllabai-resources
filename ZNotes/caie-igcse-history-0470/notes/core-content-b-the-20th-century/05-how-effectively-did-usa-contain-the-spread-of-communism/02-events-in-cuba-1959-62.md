## Events in Cuba, 1959-62

### Cuban Revolution

- Cuba had long been an American ally. There was a US naval base on the island and many American-owned businesses.
- The USA supported the dictator, Batista, as he was anti-communist. In 1959, communist - Fidel Castro overthrew Batista. 
- Thousands of anti-communists flee to USA, demanding amarican action to Castro
American industry, land, etc were all nationalized.

### Breakdown of US-Cuba relations

- Eisenower asks CIA to devise plans to overthrow Castro, economic trade between US and Cuba stops after an economic sanctions placed by US on Cuba
- US media criticizes Castro
- Plans to use cuban exiles for an invasion are developed by the CIA
- Castro allies with soviet union, receives $100 million in economic aid
- Cuba also receives arms from USSR to defend itself from USA

### Bay of Pigs invasion

- January 1961: diplomatic relations with cuba broken down
- Kennedy approves of eisenhower’s plans to deploy 1400 US troops and Cuban exiles to Bay of Pigs beach
- Plan failed. 20,000 cuban troops with air force stopped the invasion.
- Failure leads to khrushchev undermining kennedy as young and naive.

### Khrushchev arms Cuba- WHY??

- To defend Cuba: Castro had allied Cuba with the communist USSR. Khrushchev wanted to ensure Cuba could defend itself against a US invasion after the Bay of Pigs.
- To close the missile gap: US had missiles in Turkey “USSR’s backyard”. Missiles in Cuba= “US’s backyard”
- To strengthen his position: missiles in cuba= detteret= showing other soviet leaders that he is a strong leader.
- To bargain: viewed Kennedy as weak after bay of bigs. Wanted to force US to make concessions.

### Cuban Missile Crisis

- US discovers the missiles and 13 days of crisis follow
- 16 October: Kennedy was informed of the missiles and Ex Comm formed.
- 20–22 October: Kennedy decided on the use of a blockade and demanded the USSR withdraw its missiles.
- 23 October: Khrushchev replied saying the blockade would be ignored.
- 24 October: 20 Soviet ships approached but then received orders to turn around.
- 26 October: Khrushchev sent a first letter to Kennedy asking him to promise not to invade Cuba. In return, the missiles would be removed.
- 27 October: Khrushchev sent a second letter demanding that US missiles needed to be removed from Turkey as well. A U2 spy plane was shot down over Cuba.
- 27–28 October: Kennedy accepted the terms of the first letter and Khrushchev agreed to dismantle the missiles.

### Consequences 

- Last direct confrontation between USA and USSR during cold war
- Kruschev succeeded in getting US to remove missiles in Turkey, but was forced to keep the deal a secret.
- Other soviet leaders thought he looked weak, removed from power in 1964
- Missile gap had not been closes
- Kennedy successfully avoided nuclear war, kept peace, and got rid of soviet missiles in cuba= seen as the winner of the crisis
- Cuba remained a soviet ally, left alone by US, but isolated in the west as the only communist state.
