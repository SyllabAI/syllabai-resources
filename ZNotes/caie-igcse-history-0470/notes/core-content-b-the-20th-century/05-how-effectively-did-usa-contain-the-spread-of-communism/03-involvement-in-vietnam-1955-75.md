## Involvement in Vietnam, 1955-75

### Background

- Indochina ruled by France pre world war 2, japanese occupation during the war, communist Viet Minh formed in 1941 in opposition to japanese forces, led by Ho Chi Minh, declare vietnamese independence after defeat of japan in 1945.
- 1945-1954: Indochina war; fought between French controlled south and Viet Minh controlled north for control of Vietnam; USA supports France as per containment; in 1954 France pulls out of war and signs the Geneva Peace Accord that splits Vietnam into North and South with plans of nationwide free elections.

### Domino Theory

- US believed in Domino Theory- if one country falls to communism, neighboring countries will, too.
- Eisenhower endorses anti-communist, catholic government of Diem, helps set up Republic of South Vietnam out of fear of communists winning free elections; Diem is unpopular with Vietnamese peasants, who were buddhists.

### Vietcong and Kennedy’s involvement 

- Viet Cong: communist party in the south, comprised of south vietnamese peasants, taking orders from Ho Chi Minh(1960); Kennedy responds by sending Diem more funds and advisers to build up South’s army (1961).
- 1962; viet cong launches a guerilla warfare campaign against south government; Kennedy responds by sending 12,000 military advisors and US helicopters to the South
- Ho Chi Minh trail used by north to send viet cong supplies; Diem murdered in 1963 and his government is overthrown.

### Johnson and escalation of involvement 

- 1964: communist patrol boats open fire on US ships in the Gulf of Tonkin, President Johnson uses this to get congress to pass the Gulf of Tonkin Resolution, US troops land on Vietnam, US enters the War.
- 1965: Operation Rolling Thunder, US begins bombing North vietnam cities and factories; over half a million US troops in Vietnam by 1968.

### War tactics- Vietcong

- Guerrilla Warfare: ambushes, traps, and hit-and-run tactics. Tunnel complexes constructed to ambush US forces, blitz attacks.
- Civilian Help: aided south vietnamese citizens to gain support and help
- Ho Chi Minh Trail: bombed constantly, but US could not stop the supplies.

### War tactics- US

- Bombing: Operation Rolling Thunder
- Chemical weapons: Agent Orange to destroy crops and trees used by Viet cong for camouflage, and Napalm to neutralize guerrilla troops, ended up killing a lot of civilians.
- Conscription: “draft” letters sent to men as young as fresh high school graduates, average age of soldiers 19, BUT most were inexperienced, low morale, and drug use.
- Search and destroy missions: US troops deployed to villages to search for and execute viet cong troops; many walked into traps, ended up killing more innocent villagers than actual vietcong, turning vietnamese public opinion against the US.

### Public opinion turns against the war, 1968-70

- Tet Offensive- early 1968, launched by viet cong, attacking over 100 cities and military targets in the south, including american embassy in Saigon (the capital)
- Failed attempt, BUT a turning point in media and public opinion over the war; question of whether the expense and casualties were truly worth it.
- My Lai Massacre of 1968: one of the US search and destroy missions, troops go to village of My Lai, end up killing innocent civilians, including women and children; news leaked to american press; anti-war protests peak during 1968-70 period.

### Nixon and Vietnamisation

- Nixon enters office in 1968, wants to withdraw BUT not make it look like containment failed. By 1973, nixon reduces US troops in vietnam to 30,000 under his policy of “Vietnamisation”= handing reigns of war over to the vietnamese people.
- Nixon signs a peace treaty with North Vietnam, “Peace with Honour”
- All of vietnam, cambodia, AND Laos become communist states by 1975. 
- Failure in containment policies, economic, political, propaganda, and social disaster, atrocities of war had been revealed.
