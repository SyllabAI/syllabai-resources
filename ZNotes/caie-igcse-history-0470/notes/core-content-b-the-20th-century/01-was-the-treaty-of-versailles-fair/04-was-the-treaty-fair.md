## Was the Treaty fair?

### Main Clashes

- Harsh terms v. Treaty of Brest- Litovsk: Germany claimed treaty is harsh, but when Russia left war in 1917, the German treaty of Brest-Litovsk was arguably just as harsh, and this suggests that had Germany won, they’d have enforced equally harsh treaties on the allies.
- “Wrongful guilt” v. Schiffen Plan: The German plan to cut through Belgium to invade France in 1919 was used by allies to prove that Germany was aggressive from the start, and started WW1. But Germany claimed that all countries were planning for war, eg. Russian army mobilization of 1914, and hence blame should be shared.
- Harsh reparations v. German economy: Germany claimed the reparation price was too high, and the Treaty of Versailles took away resources that could’ve been used to pay the reparations; however the price was only a fraction of Germany's annual economic output.
- League of Nations Hypocrisy: The League of Nations looked like a “victor's club” because it did not invite or allow any losing countries to join; League of Nations still helped the losing countries settle disputes and crises as fairly as possible.
- Disarmament issue: Only losing countries were made to disarm, leaving them unable to protect themselves; global disarmament talks were in progress and were a League of Nations aim.
