## Why was the Berlin Wall built in 1961?

### The East to West emigration

- Vast differences in standard of living between east and west berlin; east berlin= low standards of living; west berlin= showpiece of success of capitalism: freedom, democracy, luxury; appealed to east berliners, who could travel within berlin to see the stark difference; 
- Many migrated west and didn't return; most were well educated professionals who got better paying jobs and more freedom in the west; negative propaganda for soviet russia and a brain drain for east berlin.

### The Berlin Wall 

- Khrushchev's ultimatum to Kennedy to withdraw troops and make Berlin a free city; kennedy refuses; Berlin Wall built to stop East to West emigration; began as barbed wire on 13 October 1961, became concrete with one crossing point, Checkpoint Charlie.
- Brief standoff at Checkpoint Charlie on 27 October; highlighted East-West tension.
- Kennedy accepted wall's existence; West saw it as a prison for East Germans, while communists viewed it as protection against capitalism.
