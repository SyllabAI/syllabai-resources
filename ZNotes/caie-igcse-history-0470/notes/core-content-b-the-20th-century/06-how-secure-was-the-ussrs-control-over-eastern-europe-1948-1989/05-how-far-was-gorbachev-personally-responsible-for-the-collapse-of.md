## How far was Gorbachev personally responsible for the collapse of Soviet control over eastern Europe?

### Gorbachev’s reforms

- Introduced Glasnost (openness) for social and political reform, promoting open debate on issues.
- Launched Perestroika (restructuring) to allow market forces in the Soviet economy.
- Reduced defense spending, decreasing military presence in Eastern Europe.
- Improved relations with the West, signaling a reduced Cold War tension and promoting diplomacy over confrontation.
- Announced Soviet troops would no longer enforce communist rule in Eastern Europe, encouraging democratization.

### Freedom from communist governments in East Europe

- Gorbachev’s stance inspired many Eastern European countries to pursue independence:
- Hungary, Poland, East Germany, and Czechoslovakia held free elections or opened borders.
- Romania and Bulgaria saw anti-communist uprisings.
- Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia declared independence from the USSR.

### Role of the USA

- Under President Ronald Reagan, the USA intensified the arms race and defense spending, straining the Soviet economy.
- Reagan and Gorbachev signed treaties reducing nuclear arsenals, which eased superpower tensions.
- By the late 1980s, US pressure and military escalation accelerated Soviet economic decline.

### Other factors

- The Soviet Union could no longer economically or militarily support Eastern Europe.
- Rising nationalism in Soviet republics, such as Ukraine and Lithuania, led to demands for independence.
- Internal Soviet challenges, including economic hardships and Yeltsin’s push for independence of Soviet republics, destabilized the USSR further.
