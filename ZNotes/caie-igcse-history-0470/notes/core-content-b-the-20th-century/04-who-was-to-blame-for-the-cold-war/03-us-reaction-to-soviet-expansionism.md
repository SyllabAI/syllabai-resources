## US reaction to Soviet expansionism

### Policy of Containment

- Since 1946, there had been a civil war in Greece between monarchists and communists. 
Britain supported Monarchists and Stalin protested this to the UN, which took no action.
- 1947: Britain announces withdrawal from greek civil war, Truman took action to stop spread of communism by funding british troops.
- Policy of containment/ containing communism was known as the Truman Doctrine.
US committed to  help any country threatened by communism take-over by sending money, supplies, weapons and advice.
- Turman believed that communism was only a threat to european nations until the economies were stabilized.
- George Marshall, secretary of state, is sent to assess financial situation of europe, and his reports make Truman propose $17 Billion in economic aid= approved after soviet control of Czechoslovakia.
- Any nation could receive loans from US in “Marshall aid” provided they would create a strong market for US goods.
- Stalin forbade EE nations from accepting Marshall aid.
