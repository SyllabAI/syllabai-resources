## USSR’s control of East Europe by 1948

### Control of Eastern European states following Potsdam conference

- Cominform was set up in 1947 (Communist information Bureau) to coordinate east European communist parties under the rule of the USSR, with only Yugoslavia resisting rule.
- East Germany, 1945: The Red Army remained in eastern Germany, leading to the formation of the GDR (German Democratic Republic)  under communist control in 1949.
- Poland, 1947: The Red Army stayed, and the communists took over through a coalition government, murder, and rigged elections.
- Romania, 1947: The Red Army stayed, rigged elections put communists in power, they controlled security forces, and abolished the monarchy.
- Bulgaria, 1947: The Red Army stayed, a left-wing coalition won, and communists eliminated other leaders, destroying democracy.
- Hungary, 1948: The Red Army stayed, and communists used the Ministry of Interior to eliminate rivals, becoming the largest party in 1948.
- Czechoslovakia, 1948: The Red Army left, and Czechoslovakia became a one-party communist state after communists removed non-communist leaders.
- Albania, 1948: Communists took control of Albania with Yugoslav and Soviet support, later aligning with the USSR under a Stalinist regime.

### Churchill’s Reaction

- Western powers alarmed, agreed to EE being soviet sphere of influence, BUT surprised by complete domination. 
- March 1946, churchill gives his “Iron Curtain speech”
- Stated that Europe was divided by an iron curtain, with soviet controlled east and liberal west; liberation of west was threatened by soviet domination of east.
- Stalin viewed soviet domination of east as necessary for defense in case of potential attacks from west.
