## Consequences of Berlin Blockade 
### Unison of western zones of Germany
- Germany would be divided into 4 zones : France, USSR, USA, Britain. Berlin was in the soviet zone BUT would be divided into 4 zones too. Allied Control Council would decide matters for germany.
- By 1947, Truman decided that strong Germany= useful ally following soviet domination of the East.
1947: Bizonia formed when USA and Britain merged their zones, France follows in 1948 and - Stalin’s suspicions grow.
- Western zones introduce their own currency, inraging Stalin.

### Stalin’s Response
- Creates his own currency for his zone, BLOCKS west berlin, cutting off supplies by road, rail, and canal from western zones.
- USA destroying the blockade= act of war.
- Aggressive move, BUT IF Truman withdrew from berlin= HUGE Propaganda win for Stalin

### Truman’s Response to Berlin Blockade
- If they withdrew from berlin, west germany would be next.
- BERLIN AIRLIFT: operation by USA, Britain, and France to fly supplies to west berliners.
- For 11 months, nearly 300,000 flights took place from West Germany and dropped over 2 million tons of fuel, food and other supplies to the 2 million West Berliners.
- Stalin lifts the blockade in May 1949.
- COMECON (Council for Mutual economic assistance) created in 1949 for EE communist states. Response to Marshall aid by tying economies of all soviet states to Moscow.

### Consequences of Berlin Blockade
- Creation of NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization) in 1949, a military alliance for the west, where one nation being attacked would provoke a response from all members. Stalin forms Warsaw Pact in response to West Germany joining NATO in 1955.
- West Germany becomes known as Federal Republic of Germany in May 2949.
- East Germany is named German Republic of Germany as a response.
- Proxy Wars would be the primary means of fighting in this “Cold War” Rather than direct conflict.
