# Who was to Blame for Cold War?

## Breakdown of US-Soviet alliance, 1945.

### US-Soviet alliance during WWII

- Allied against Nazi Germany, despite ideological differences. 
- The Grand Alliance was made up of Winston Churchill (PM, Britain), F. D. Roosevelt (US President), and Joseph Stalin (USSR Leader). 
- SEVERE DIFFERENCES between USA, Britain and USSR:


| Factor | USA | USSR |
| -------- | -------- | -------- |
| Government and elections| Multi-party state (democracy)| One party state|
| Economy | Capitalist society with private ownership and economic classes (rich and poor)| Communist, classless society with government ownership.|
| People | Law guaranteed individual freedom and rights     | Every aspect of life was state controlled.|
| Ideology | Wanted spread of democracy and capitalist economy, against communism.    |Expansionist ideology where each state would be a communist leadership.|

- Defeated Hitler by May 1945.

### Cracks in alliance during wartime 

- Mistrust from US and Britain and fear of spread of communism
- Stalin’s mistrust of Roosevelt and Churchill, which started before the war, during the period of appeasement where Stalin was not consulted in issues such as the Munich conference and Germany was appeased as a buffer for the west against communism.
- Stalin did not share his wartime strategies with the US and Britain and Churchill didn’t share his knowledge of German troop movements with Stalin.
- Public opinion supported a seemingly unified alliance.

### Yalta conference

- February, 1945.
- AGREEMENTS; Germany and Berlin to be divided into 4 zones, each under mandate of one of the big 4 (US, USSR, Britain, France)
- War criminals to be brought to trial
- Stalin agreed to join the US in the war against Japan.
- Agreement to form the UN
- Free elections to be held in occupied nations after the war, East Europe to be a soviet sphere of influence.
- DISAGREEMENTS; Poland; Stalin wanted to absorb east Poland into the USSR, Roosevelt and Churchill wanted free elections; compromise reached that some of Stalin’s demands would be adhered to provided the USSR does not help communists in Greece.

### Potsdam conference, 1945.

- Roosevelt died and was replaced by Truman, who was more openly anti communist and prepared to stand up against Stalin's aggressive expansion in east europe; USSR’s Red Army, most of eastern europe, Stalin began to set up pro-soviet communist governments.
- Truman informs Stalin of USA’s successful testing of the first atomic bomb.
- Stalin was already aware because of spies.
- Churchill is replaced by clement attlee halfway through the conference.
In churchill’s absence, the conference was essentially a rivalry between Truman and Stalin:
1. Stalin wanted to incapacitate Germany and gain ruhr resources, BUT Truman didn't want a repeat of ToV.
2. Truman was reluctant to impose harsh reparations BUT Stalin wanted compensation for the war.
3. Truman did not want Soviet rule in East Europe BUT this was agreed at yalta.
- Denazification, trials for war crimes, and splitting Germany into four zones governed by an allied control council were all agreements from yalta that were upheld.
