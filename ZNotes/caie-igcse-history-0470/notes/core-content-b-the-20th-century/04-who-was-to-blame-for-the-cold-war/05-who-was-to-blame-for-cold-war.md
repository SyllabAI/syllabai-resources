## Who was to blame for Cold War?
### USSR was to blame
- Broke yalta agreement over Poland
- Dominated east europe after world war two
- Cominform created, mean communist nations took orders from Moscow
- Attempted absorption of west germany during berlin blockade.

### BUT
- USSR suffered during WWII and Stalin was trying to defend communism
- It was agreed that east europe would be soviet sphere of influence
- Stalin couldn’t trust western leaders following Iron Curtain speech and truman's announcement of Atomic Bomb
- Truman was openly hostile to communism 

### USA was to blame
- Truman was more anti communist than Roosevelt
- Effect of announcement of atomic bomb by Truman at Potsdam.
- Doctrine of containment viewed as attempt to isolate USSR in Europe.
- US supported anti-communist forces in 1947 Greek Civil War.
- Marshall Aid was an attempt to extend US control over European markets and spread capitalist ideology. 
- Creation of Bizonia in 1947. 
- Creation of new currency in Bizonia in 1948.

### BUT
- Containment policy was developed AFTER aggressive soviet domination of east europe
- Marshall aid was also a policy to help war-torn countries of europe
- Stalin had broken agreements made at Yalta and Potsdam.
