# Hitler’s Rise to Power: How was Hitler able to become Chancellor in 1933?

## How did Hitler become the leader of the Nazis?

* **Hitler and the First World War**
  * When the First World War broke out, Hitler was in Munich and immediately enlisted in the army.
  * During the war, he won the Iron Cross for his bravery and was given the job of countering enemy propaganda in German trenches due to his good speaking skills.
  * In 1918, he was badly gassed and was in hospital when Germany announced they had signed the armistice, and it left him extremely bitter.
  * He blamed the loss of Germany on Jews and Communists.
  * Once recovered, he became an informant for the German army.
* **Hitler Joins the Nazis**
  * In September 1919, he was sent to observe a meeting of a right-wing extremist party called the “German Workers’ Party”.
  * Hitler agreed with many of their ideas and joined the Party.
* **Hitler and the Development of the Nazis**
  * In 1920, the Party was renamed to the National Socialist German Workers’ Party (Nazis)
  * They made their intentions and what they stand for very clear and made no secret of it some of them were:
    * Destroy Marxism
    * Remove Jews from all positions of leadership in Germany
    * No non–Germans to be newspaper editors
    * Challenge terror or violence with your own terror or violence
    * Educate gifted children at the state’s expense
    * Increase old–age pensions
    * Strong central government
    * Nationalise important industries
    * Conquer Lebensraum (Living Space)
    * Rearm Germany
    * Abolish the Treaty of Versailles
    * Destroy the Weimar Republic
  * Hitler set up the party’s private army, called the Stormtroopers or SA, and comprised former members of Friekorps, Former members of the Army, and members of the Party.
  * They were formed to protect speakers at Nazi meetings from intimidation by left–wing opponents.
  * However, they started the violence by breaking up opposition meetings.
  * In 1922, he became the leader of the party
* **Hitler and his Henchmen**
  * *Joseph Goebbels*
    * He was a son of an office worker and was not able to fight in WWI due to a crippled foot, but he was very intelligent, well-educated, and a good public speaker.
    * He joined the party in 1922 and become head of Nazi propaganda during the Depression. He used posters, rallies, and the Nazi newspaper *“Volkischer Beobatcher”*
  * *Hermann Goering*
    * Came from a middle-class background, fought in the Air Force during WW1, and won the highest medal for bravery under fire.
    * Joined the party in 1922 and a year later became Second in charge of the SA
  * *Rudolf Hess*
    * He was a pilot and soldier during WW1 and a soft, sensitive, and humourless man.
    * He was Hitler’s private secretary at first and was later responsible for matters of party administration.
  * *Heinrich Himmler*
    * Fought briefly in WW1 and was an agriculture student. He was frail, timid, and clumsy, hard-working, and precise.

    * He joined the party in 1923, He persuaded Hitler to separate the SS from the SA, in 1927. He became the leader of the SS, and expanded it to over 50,000 memebers by 1933.

      *Ernst Rohm*

    * He was a captain during WW1 and joined the Freikorps in crushing the Spartacists, and was a founding member of the Nazi Party.

    * In 1921, he became the leader of the SA.
