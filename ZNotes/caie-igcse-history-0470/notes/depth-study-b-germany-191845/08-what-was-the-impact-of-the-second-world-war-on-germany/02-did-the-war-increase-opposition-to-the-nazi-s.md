## Did the war increase opposition to the Nazi’s?

* **Yes – but only to a limited extent**

* War led to:

  * More hardship (bombing, shortages, deaths)

  * More awareness of Nazi failures and crimes

* However:

  * Opposition remained small, uncoordinated, and suppressed

  * No serious threat to Nazi control

**Youth Opposition**

### **Edelweiss Pirates**

* **Type of people:** Working-class teenagers

* **Goals:**

  * Reject Hitler Youth control

  * Gain freedom and independence

* **Actions:**

  * Skipped Hitler Youth, wore different clothes

  * Helped deserters and escaped prisoners

  * Some sabotage and attacks

* **War impact:**

  * Became more active after 1943 due to chaos and weaker control

### **Swing Youth**

* **Type of people:** Middle-class teenagers

* **Goals:**

  * Reject Nazi cultural control

  * Enjoy freedom (music, fashion)

* **Actions:**

  * Listened to jazz (banned music)

  * Copied American/British styles

* **War impact:**

  * War restrictions increased their desire to rebel

* **Limit:**

  * Cultural opposition only (not political)

**Intellectual Opposition**

### **White Rose Group**

* **Type of people:** University students \+ professor

* **Goals:**

  * End Nazi rule

  * Expose Nazi crimes

* **Actions:**

  * Distributed anti-Nazi leaflets

  * Encouraged passive resistance

* **War impact:**

  * Formed after witnessing war atrocities (1942–43)

* **Outcome:**

  * Leaders executed (1943)

* **Importance:**

  * Shows moral opposition increased during war

**Military & Elite Opposition**

### **July Bomb Plot (1944)**

* **Type of people:** Army officers \+ conservative elites

* **Goals:**

  * Kill Hitler

  * End the war

* **Actions:**

  * Bomb placed at Hitler’s Head Quaters 

* **War impact:**

  * Triggered by military defeat (e.g. after 1943)

* **Outcome:**

  * Failed →  about 5,000 executed

* **Importance:**

  * Most serious attempt to remove Hitler

**Church Opposition**

### **Religious Resistance**

* **Type of people:** Priests, pastors, church members

* **Goals:**

  * Protect religion

  * Oppose Nazi policies (e.g. euthanasia)

* **Actions:**

  * Sermons against Nazis

  * Some secret help to victims

* **War impact:**

  * War policies increased criticism

* **Limit:**

  * Not trying to overthrow Hitler

**Civilian Opposition**

* **Type of people:** Ordinary Germans

* **Causes (war-related):**

  * Bombing

  * Food shortages

  * Deaths of soldiers

* **Actions:**

  * Complaining, low morale

  * Listening to foreign radio

* **Limit:**

  * Fear prevented open rebellion

**Final Conclusion**

* War **increased opposition** because:

  * Conditions worsened

  * Defeats reduced confidence in Hitler

* BUT:

  * Opposition was disorganised and limited

  * Gestapo repression prevented serious threat
