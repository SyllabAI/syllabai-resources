## How important was Belgium’s reaction to the Schlieffen Plan?

* As Belgium was neutral, Germany did not expect them to fight back.
* The Belgian army slowed down the Germans and did not reach Paris in the expected six weeks
* It took 2 weeks to capture Brussels and the fortress town of Liège.
  This had a great effect as the plan relied on speed.
* German soldiers weakened due to exhaustion, supply problems and
  casualties.
* Belgian defence gave time for British forces (the BEF) to arrive to
  help.

### **The Schlieffen Plan failed because:**

* Belgium refused to let the German army through to attack France
  and slowed down the Germans.
* Russia mobilised quicker than the Germans had expected.
* Germany had to transfer troops to the east.
* They did not expect this as Russia was going through a crisis and
  had poor transportation.
* This was a miscalculation on the German part.
* Britain had signed a treaty with Belgium to protect it as a neutral country, sending its forces to stop German advances.
