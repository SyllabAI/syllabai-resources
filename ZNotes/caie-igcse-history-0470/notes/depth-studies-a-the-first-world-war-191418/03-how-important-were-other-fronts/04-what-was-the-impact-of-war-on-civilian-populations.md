## What was the impact of war on civilian populations?

### **DORA (Defence of the Realm ACT) - Britain**

* Press censorship
* Daylight Saving Time (DST) / British Summer Time
* Food rationing
* Reduced licensing hours for pubs
* Imprisonment without trial
* 1917 Espionage act - USA
* Sedition act - USA
* All countries except Britain introduced conscription in 1914
* Conscription was introduced for all single men between ages 18 - 41
  in Jan 1916
* Extended to all men aged 18 - 41 in May 1916.

### **Cause of food shortages:**

- Conscription of farm workers

- Submarine warfare

* Germany had *Ersatz*, substitute food, e.g.: acorns and
  beechnuts instead of coffee

* Turnips used in place of potatoes after drought in 1916 -
  Germany.

* Food rationing:

  * Britain: started in Jan 1918 with sugar and meat, extended later
    in the year
  * Germany: limited rationing - 1914, most food rationed in 1916

### **Employment opportunities for women**

* Farms : Women's Land Army - 1915
* Munitions factories
* Public transport
* Russia - all women's battalion - 'Amazon'
* Right to vote after the war ended
* Not many joined the workforce, most women in lowly paid jobs moved
  up into better paid jobs
* Public pressure forced women out of new jobs back into old ones
  after war ended
* Lloyd George had to ensure men higher pay after war

### **Deaths on Home Front:**

* 940,000 civilians died due to military action
* 5.9 million died due to malnutrition, disease and accidents
* Arrival of Spanish flu at last stages increased the death toll by 20 million people
