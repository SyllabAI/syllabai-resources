## Finding the LCM

### How to find the LCM of two small numbers

* The product of the two numbers will always be a multiple, but not always the LCM.

* The LCM for smaller numbers can be found by writing down the list of multiples for both numbers and identifying the smallest number that appears in both lists.

  The multiples of $4$ are: $4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, …$

  The multiples of $6$ are: $6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, …$

  So the LCM of $4$ and $6$ is $12$.

* The multiples of the LCM of two numbers are also multiples of the two numbers.

  The multiples of $12$ are: $12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, …$ (these are all also multiples of $4$ and $6$).

### How to find the LCM of any two numbers

* The LCM of any two numbers can be found by writing each number as a product of its prime factors.

  $120 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$

  $150 = 2 \times 3 \times 5^2$

* For each list, identify the highest power that appears for each term.

  The highest power of $2$ that appears in both lists is $2^3$.

  The highest power of $3$ that appears in both lists is $3^1$.

  The highest power of $5$ that appears in both lists is $5^2$.

* The LCM will be the product of these terms (note that they do not have to common terms).

  $2^3 \times 3 \times 5^2 = 600$

  Therefore, the LCM of $120$ and $150$ is $600$

### How to find the LCM using the Venn diagram method

* First write the two numbers as a product of its prime factors.

  $360 = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5$

  $450 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$

* Draw a Venn diagram and put the terms that appear in both products in the centre.

* Put the rest of the factors into their respective sections.

  $2$ appears once in both lists so it will go once in the centre.

  $3$ appears twice in both lists so it will go twice in the centre.

  $5$ appears once in both lists so it will go once in the centre.

* The LCM of the two numbers will be the product of all the numbers present in the Venn Diagram.

  $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 1800$

  Therefore, the LCM of $360$ and $450$ is $1800$.
