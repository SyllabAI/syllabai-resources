## Index Laws Extended

*This card contains the index laws involving integer, fractional and negative powers. These laws are part of the higher tier mathematics course. You do not need to know them if you are taking the foundation tier mathematics course.*

---

Here are the remainder of the laws of indices from earlier:

6. $(\frac{a}{b})^x = \frac{a^x}{b^x}$

   When a fraction is raised to a power, both the numerator and denominator are raised to that power.

   $(\frac{3}{8})^2 = \frac{3^2}{8^2} = \frac{9}{64}$

7. $a^{-x} = \frac{1}{a^x}$

   When a term is raised to a negative power, it is equal to the reciprocal of that term raised to the positive power.

   $3^{-3} = \frac{1}{3^3} = \frac{1}{27}$

   $4^{-1} = \frac{1}{4}$

8. $a^{\frac{x}{y}} = \sqrt[y]{a^x}$ or $(\sqrt[y]{a})^x$

   Fractional powers represent roots. The denominator is the type of root (ex. square or cube root), and the term is raised to the power of the numerator.

   $27^{\frac{3}{2}} = (\sqrt[3]{27})^2 = 3^2 = 9$

   $64^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{64} = 8$

   $125^{\frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{125} = 5$
