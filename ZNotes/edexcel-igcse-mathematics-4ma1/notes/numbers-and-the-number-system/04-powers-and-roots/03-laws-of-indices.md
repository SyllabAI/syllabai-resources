## Laws of Indices

*This card only contains the index laws for multiplication and division of positive and negative integer powers including zero. The rest of the index laws are covered later within this chapter and are part of the higher tier mathematics course. You only need to know the index laws in this card if you are taking the foundation tier mathematics course.*

---

The first couple laws of indices are:

1. $a^x \times a^y = a^{x + y}$

   When you multiply two terms with the **same** base, keep the base the same and add the powers together.

   $2^3 \times 2^2 = 2^{3 + 2} = 2^5$

2. $a^x \div a^y = a^{x - y}$

   When you divide a term by another with the **same** base, keep the base the same and subtract the power from the other.

   $5^6 \div 5^4 = 5^{6-4} = 5^2$

3. $(a^x)^y = a^{xy}$

   When you raise a term with a power to another power, keep the base and multiply the two powers together.

   $(15^4)^2 = 15^{4 \times 2} = 15^8$

4. $a^1 = a$

   Any number raised to the power of $1$ is itself.

   $5^1 = 5$

5. $a^0 = 1$

   Any number raised to the power of $0$ is $1$.

   $6^0 = 1$
