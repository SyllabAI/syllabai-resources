## Calculating Squares, Square Roots, Cubes & Cube Roots

* To calculate squares, simply multiply the number by itself once.

  $7^2 = 7 \times 7 = 49$

* The square root of a number is the value you square to get the number.

  $5^2 = 25$ so $5$ is the square root of $25$.

  * This is written as:

    $\sqrt{25} = 5$

* To calculate cubes, simply multiply the number by itself thrice.

  $4^3 = 4 \times 4 \times 4 = 64$

* The cube root of a number is the value you cube to get the number.

  $3^3 = 27$ so $3$ is the cube root of $27$.

  * This is written as:

    $\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$
