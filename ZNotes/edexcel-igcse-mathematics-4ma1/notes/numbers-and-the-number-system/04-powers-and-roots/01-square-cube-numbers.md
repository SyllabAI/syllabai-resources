# Powers & Roots

## Square & Cube Numbers

* A number multiplied by itself once is known as a square number.

* Square numbers are denoted as the number raised to the power of $2$.

  $6^2 = 6 \times 6 = 36$ so $36$ is a square number.

* A number multiplied by itself twice is a cube number.

* Cube numbers are denoted as the number raised to the power of $3$.

  $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$ so $8$ is a cube number.

* If you raise a number to the power of $x$, you find the product of $x$ numbers.

  $5^5 = 5 \times 5 \times 5 \times 5 \times 5 = 3125$

* Any number raised to the power of $1$ is itself.

  $12^1 = 12$

* Any number raised to the power of $0$ will equal $1$.

  $9^0 = 1$

* $1$ raised to the power of any number will always be $1$.

  $1^4 = 1 \times 1 \times 1 \times 1 = 1$

* You should be familiar with the square numbers up to 12.

  $1^2 = 1$

  $2^2 = 4$

  $3^2 = 9$

  $4^2 = 16$

  $5^2 = 25$

  $6^2 = 36$

  $7^2 = 49$

  $8^2 = 64$

  $9^2 = 81$

  $10^2 = 100$

  $11^2 = 121$

  $12^2 = 144$

* You should be familiar with the cube numbers up to 5.

  $1^3 = 1$

  $2^3 = 8$

  $3^3 = 27$

  $4^3 = 64$

  $5^3 = 125$
