## Finding the HCF

### How to find the HCF of two small numbers

* The HCF for smaller numbers can be found by writing down the list of factors for both numbers and identifying the largest number that appears in both lists.

  Factors of $20 = 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20$

  Factors of $36 = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 18, 36$

  So the HCF of $20$ and $36$ is $4$.

* If there are no common factors between two numbers, the HCF is $1$.

### How to find the HCF of any two numbers

* The HCF of any two numbers can be found by writing each number as a product of its prime factors.

  $180 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5$

  $168 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 7$

* Identify the terms that appear in both products.

  $2$ and $3$ appear in both lists.

* Choose the highest power for each term that appears in **both** lists.

  $180 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5$

  $168 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$

  $2^2$ is the highest power of $2$ that appears in **both** lists.

  $3^1$ is the highest power of $3$ that appears in **both** lists.

* The HCF will the product of these prime factors.

  $2^2 \times 3 = 12$

  Therefore, $12$ is the HCF of $180$ and $168$.

### How to find the HCF using the Venn diagram method

* First write the two numbers as a product of its prime factors.

  $180 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5$

  $168 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 7$

* Draw a Venn diagram and put the terms that appear in both products in the centre.

* Put the rest of the factors into their respective sections.

  $2$ appears twice in both lists so it will go twice in the centre.

  $3$ appears only once in both lists so it will go once in the centre.

* The HCF of the two numbers will be the product of the numbers in the centre.

  $2 \times 2 \times 3 = 12$

  Therefore, the HCF of $180$ and $168$ is $12$.

* Remember if there are no common factors the HCF is $1$.
