## Expressing Integers as a Product of Powers of Prime Factors

* Prime factors are the factors of a number that are also prime numbers.

  $2, 3$ and $7$ are the prime factors of $42$.

* The product of all the prime factors of a number will equal the number.

  $2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$

### Prime Factor Decomposition

* The common method of finding prime factors is by using a factor tree.

  * Split the number into the largest factors you know.

    $20 \rightarrow 2 \times 10$

  * Note the prime factors for later and continue to split the other factors until you are left with only prime factors.

    $10 \rightarrow 2 \times 5$

    So the prime factors of $20$ are $2, 2,$ and $5$.

  * If a prime factor is repeated multiple times, write it as a product of its powers.

    Therefore, $20 = 2^2 \times 5$

* A more efficient way to split the number is to divide each value by the smallest prime factor you know (such as $2$ or $3$).

### How to use prime factor decomposition (PFD)

* PFD can be used to identify whether a number is a square number.

  * If all the powers of the prime factors are multiples of $2$, it is a square number.

    $3600 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$

    $3600$ is a square number since $2^4 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$ can be written as $(2^2 \times 3 \times 5)^2$

* PFD can be used to identify whether a number is a cube number.

  * If all the powers of the prime factors are multiples of $3$, it is a cube number.

    $9,261,000 = 2^6 \times 3^3 \times 5^3 \times 7^3$

    $9,261,000$ is a cube number since $2^6 \times 3^3 \times 7^3$ can be written as $(2^2 \times 3 \times \times 5 \times 7)^3$

* PFD can be used to calculate the square root of a square number.

  * Find the square root of the product of the prime factors, i.e. divide the powers by 2.

    $400 = 2^4 \times 5^2$

    $\sqrt{2^4 \times 5^2} = 2^2 \times 5$

  * This will give you the prime factor decomposition of the square root, the product will equal the square root.

    $2^2 \times 5 = 20$

    Therefore, the square root of $400$ is $20$.

* PFD can be used to calculate the square root of a non-square number.

  * Split the terms with odd powers into terms with one even and one odd power.

    $121,500 = 2^2 \times 3^5 \times 5^3 \rightarrow 2^2 \times 3^4 \times 3^1 \times 5^2 \times 5^1$

  * Collect all the terms with even powers at the start.

    $2^2 \times 3^4 \times 3^1 \times 5^2 \times 5^1 \rightarrow 2^2 \times 3^4 \times 5^2 \times 3 \times 5$

  * Square root the product of the prime factors.

    $\sqrt{2^2 \times 3^4 \times 5^2 \times 3 \times 5}$

  * Split the terms with even powers into a separate root.

    $\sqrt{2^2 \times 3^4 \times 5^2 \times 3 \times 5} \rightarrow \sqrt{2^2 \times 3^4 \times 5^2}\sqrt{3 \times 5} = 2 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times \sqrt{15} = 90\sqrt{15}$

    Therefore, the exact value for the square root of $121,500$ is $90\sqrt{15}$
