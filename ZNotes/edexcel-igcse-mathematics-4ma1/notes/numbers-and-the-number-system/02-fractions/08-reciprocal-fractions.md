## Reciprocal Fractions

* Reciprocal fractions are the inverse of a fraction (the numerator and denominator are switched).

  The reciprocal of $\frac{2}{3}$ is $\frac{3}{2}$

* A fraction is equivalent to its numerator multiplied by the reciprocal of its denominator.

  $\frac{3}{5} = 3 \times \frac{1}{5}$
