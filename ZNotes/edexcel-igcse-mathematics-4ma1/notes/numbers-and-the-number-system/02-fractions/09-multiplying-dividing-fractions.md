## Multiplying & Dividing Fractions

### Multiplying Fractions

* To multiply two fractions together, simply multiply the numerators together and the denominators together i.e. write the product of the numerators as the new numerator, and the product of the denominators as the new denominator.

  $\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{2 \times 8}{5 \times 9} = \frac{16}{45}$

### Dividing Fractions

* To divide a fraction by another fraction, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction i.e. flip the second fraction and then multiply.

  $\frac{6}{11} \div \frac{3}{8} = \frac{6}{11} \times \frac{8}{3} = \frac{48}{33}$

* Always make sure to flip the **second** fraction (the divisor) only, and not the first fraction (the dividend).

### Multiplying & Dividing Mixed Fractions

* To perform multiplication or division with two mixed numbers, convert them to improper fractions first and then perform the calculations as you would usually.

* If the final result is an improper fraction, you can convert this to a mixed fraction again.
