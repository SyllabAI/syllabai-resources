# Fractions

## Simplifying Fractions

* Simplifying fractions converts a fraction to an equivalent fraction with the smallest possible numerator and denominator values (the ratio/value of the fraction is kept constant).

  $\frac{7}{14} \rightarrow \frac{1}{2}$

* To simplify a fraction, divide both the numerator and denominator by the highest common factor.
