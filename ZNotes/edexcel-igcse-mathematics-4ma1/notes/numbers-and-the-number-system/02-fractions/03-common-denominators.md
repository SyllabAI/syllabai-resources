## Common Denominators

To perform addition and subtraction with fractions, it is required that the denominators of the fractions are the same.

### How to make the denominators of two different fractions the same

* Find the lowest common multiple of both denominators.

* Multiply both the numerator and denominator of both fractions by the value needed to make the denominator the LCM.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/6b3a008edeef3caab1422445.webp =400x200)

* You can also simply multiply the fractions by the other denominator.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/fdab4314c21488c8c05c23ce.webp =400x200)

* However, this is less efficient as you may need to simplify your final results, which requires finding the HCF.
