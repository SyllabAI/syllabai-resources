## Adding & Subtracting Fractions

### Adding Fractions

* To add two fractions together, make all the denominators the same and then add the numerators.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/6906eb1346687b332c7c6e37.webp =400x200)

### Subtracting Fractions

* To subtract a fraction from another, make all the denominators the same and then perform the calculation on the numerators.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/1c09edac50fbbee35422146a.webp =400x200)

### Adding & Subtracting Mixed Fractions

* To perform addition or subtraction on two mixed numbers, convert them to improper fractions and then perform the calculation as usual.

* If the result is an improper fraction, you can convert this back into a mixed fraction.
