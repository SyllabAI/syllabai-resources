## Mixed Numbers & Vulgar Fractions

* Vulgar fractions are fractions with integer values for the numerator and denominator.

  $\frac{3}{5}$

* Proper fractions are fractions where the numerator is smaller than the denominator.

  $\frac{7}{11}$

* Improper fractions are fractions where the numerator is larger than the denominator.

  $\frac{15}{4}$

* Mixed numbers or mixed fractions are fractions with a whole number and a proper fraction combined.

  $5\frac{19}{24}$

### How to convert an improper fraction to a mixed number

* Divide the numerator by the denominator.

* The denominator will remain the same.

* The remainder will be the numerator.

* The quotient will be the whole number next to the fractional part.

### How to convert a mixed number to an improper fraction

* Multiply the denominator by the whole number part of the mixed number.

* Add the numerator to this calculated value.

* The final value is the numerator.

* Keep the denominator the same.
