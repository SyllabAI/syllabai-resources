## Ordering & Applying Fractions

### How to order fractions

* To order fractions, first make all the denominators the same if they are not, then sort by the size of the numerator.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/c348b8399806605d35156587.webp =450x200)

* You can also order fractions by converting them to decimals to compare the values.

### Finding a fraction of a value

* To find the fraction of a given number, simply multiply the fraction by the number.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/9e501ac02111d763df1e3954.webp =450x300)
