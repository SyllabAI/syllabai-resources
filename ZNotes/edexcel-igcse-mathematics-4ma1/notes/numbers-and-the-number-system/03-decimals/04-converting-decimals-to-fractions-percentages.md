## Converting Decimals to Fractions & Percentages

### Decimals to Fractions

* To convert a decimal to a fraction, first write the decimal number over $1$.

  $0.25 = \frac{0.25}{1}$

* Then multiply the numerator and denominator by the value needed to make the numerator a whole integer value i.e. the last place value.

  $0.25 \rightarrow \frac{0.25}{1} \times \frac{100}{100} = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}$

  $0.03405 \rightarrow \frac{0.03405}{1} \times \frac{100,000}{100,000} = \frac{3405}{100,000} = \frac{681}{20,000}$

### Decimals to Percentages

* To convert a decimal to a fraction, multiply the decimal value by $100$.

  $0.6 \times 100 = 60$%
