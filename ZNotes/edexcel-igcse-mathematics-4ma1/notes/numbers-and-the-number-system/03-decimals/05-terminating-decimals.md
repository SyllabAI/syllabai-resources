## Terminating Decimals

* Terminating decimals are numbers that have a finite (countable) number of digits after the decimal point i.e. the decimal terminates.
  * $4.5582$ is a terminating decimal, $3.333333…$ is not a terminating decimal.
* Terminating decimals have fractional values.

  $1.5 = \frac{3}{2}$

  $0.2 = \frac{1}{5}$
