## Ordering Decimals

* To order decimals, compare the sizes of the values starting from the largest place value first.

* If the whole number values are the same, compare the sizes of the numbers from the first decimal place onwards.

  * $10.3 > 9.3$ because $10 > 9$
  * $3.44 > 3.34$ because $0.4 > 0.3$
  * $89.89 > 89.88$ because $0.09 > 0.08$
