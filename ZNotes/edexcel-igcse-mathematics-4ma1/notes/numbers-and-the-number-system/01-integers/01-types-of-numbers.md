# Integers

## Types of Numbers

* Integers are whole numbers that do not contain any fractional or decimal component.

  $…, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …$

* The symbol ℤ is used to represent integer values.

* Rational numbers can be expressed as a fraction with whole numbers as the numerator and denominator, where the denominator does not equal $0$. They may have infinite decimal digits that show a repeating pattern of digits (recurring decimals).

  $3.333333… = \frac{10}{3}$

  $3.142857142857… = \frac{22}{7}$

* Irrational numbers cannot be expressed as a fraction with integer values as the numerator and denominator, where the denominator does not equal $0$. They have infinite decimal digits that do not repeat in a pattern.

  $\pi = 3.14159265…$

  $\sqrt{2} = 1.41421356…$

  $e = 2.718281828459…$

### Types of Integers

* Positive integers (whole numbers greater than $0$)

  $1, 2, 3, 4, …$

* Negative integers (whole numbers less than $0$)

  $-1, -2, -3, -4, …$

* Zero (not positive nor negative)

The following are **not** integer values:

* Decimals

  $2.785, 3.14$

* Proper fractions

  $\frac{1}{2}$, $\frac{3}{4}$

* Improper fractions or mixed numbers

  $\frac{17}{4}$, $2\frac{3}{4}$, $6\frac{7}{8}$

* Irrational numbers

  $\pi$, $\sqrt{2}$
