## Rules of Operations

### Addition and Subtraction

* When adding or subtracting two numbers with the same sign, the sign becomes a plus, i.e. the operation becomes addition.

  $++$ $\rightarrow$ $+$

  $--$ $\rightarrow$ $+$

* When adding or subtracting two numbers with different signs, the sign becomes a minus, i.e. the operation becomes subtraction.

  $+-$ $\rightarrow$ $-$

  $-+$ $\rightarrow$ $-$

* The answer will take the sign of the **bigger** number.

  $8 - (-5) = 8 + 5 = 13$

  $(-10) - (-30) = (-10) + 30 = 20$

  $(-3) - 5 = -8$

  $16 + (-9) = 16 - 9 = 7$

### Multiplication and Division

* Perform the calculations ignoring the signs first, and then apply the sign rules.

* Same signs will give a positive result.

  $(+) \times (+) = +$

  $(-) \times (-) = +$

  $(-3) \times (-5) = 15$

  $(-26) \div (-2) = 13$

* Different signs will give a negative result.

  $(+) \times (-) = -$

  $(-) \times (+) = -$

  $6 \times (-8) = -48$

  $30 \div (-6) = -5$
