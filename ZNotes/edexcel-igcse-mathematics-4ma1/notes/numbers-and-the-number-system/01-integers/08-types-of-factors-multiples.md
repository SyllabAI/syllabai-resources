## Types of Factors & Multiples

* Prime factor: a factor of an integer that is also a prime number.

  $3$ is a prime factor of $12$.

* Highest common factor (HCF): the largest shared factor of $2$ different integers (that divides them without a remainder).

  The HCF of $12$ and $8$ is $4$.

* Lowest common multiple (LCM): the smallest shared multiple of $2$ different integers.

  The LCM of $3$ and $5$ is $15$.

The method to find the HCF and LCM is covered in the Powers & Roots chapter.
