## Ordering Integers

Integers can be ordered off their size:

* Ascending order (starting with the smallest integer and ending with the largest integer).

* Descending order (starting with the largest integer and ending with the smallest integer).

* $<$ means smaller or less than.

  $5 < 8$

* $>$ means larger or greater than.

  $100 > 97$

* $\le$ means less than or equal to.

  $x \le 4$ so $x$ could be $4, 3, 2, …$

* $\ge$ means greater than or equal to.

  $x \ge 5$ so $x$ could be $5, 6, 7, …$

* The larger the digit following a negative sign, the smaller the negative number.

  $-4 < -2$

* $0$ is greater than every negative integer, and less than every positive integer.
