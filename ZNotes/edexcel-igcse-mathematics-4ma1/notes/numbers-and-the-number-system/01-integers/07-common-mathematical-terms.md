## Common Mathematical Terms

### Odd and Even Integers

* Even integers are divisible by $2$ and give no remainder.

  * The last digit is always either $2, 4, 6$ or $8$.

    $-10, -6, -4, 0, 2, 4, 20, 1348,$ etc.

* Odd integers are integers are not divisible by $2$ without producing a remainder.

  * The last digit is always either $1, 3, 5, 7$ or $9$.

    $-33, -21, -7, 1, 3, 9, 225,$ etc.

### Factors

* Factors are integers that divide another integer without leaving a remainder.

  The factors of $12$ are: $1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12$

### Multiples

* Multiples are integers that have been multiplied by another integer.

  The multiples of $5$ are: $5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, …$

* The times table of an integer is a list of its multiples.

### Prime Numbers

* Prime numbers are positive integers greater than $1$, that only have $2$ factors: $1$ and itself.

  $2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, …$

  $1$ is **not** a prime number as it has only $1$ factor.

  $2$ is the only even prime number.
