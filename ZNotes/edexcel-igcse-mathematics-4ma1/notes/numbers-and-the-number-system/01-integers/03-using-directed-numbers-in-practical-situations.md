## Using Directed Numbers in Practical Situations

Directed numbers have both:

* Magnitude (size)

* Direction or position relative to zero, indicated by a sign ($+ \text{ or } -$)

Common examples of directed numbers include:

* Temperature

  $+37.6°\text{C}$, $-5°\text{C}$

* Transactions

  $+$£$50$, $-$£$25$

* Distance (ex. altitude)

  $+250 \text{ m }$, $-30 \text{ m }$
