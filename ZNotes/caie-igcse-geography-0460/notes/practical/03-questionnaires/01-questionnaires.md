# Questionnaires

### **Open-ended questions:**

* Questions that have infinite numbers of answers. The respondent has no ‎restriction on how they might answer.


|**Pros:**|Cons|
|:---|:---|
|You are getting respondents’ personal opinions. |Relatively expensive.|
|They are not limited in their response.‎|No user-anonymity, which may affect the response|
||Time-consuming.|

### **Closed questions:**

* When there is a limited number of responses. These questions are often multiple-choice in style.


|**Pros:**|**Cons:**|
|:---|:---|
|All answers relevant to your research|Results lack personal opinion; they are very generalised|
|Results are easy to analyse using graphs and tables|If the "other" box is ticked, you don't know what the respondent thinks|
||Your personal opinion has been placed on the questions (subjective).‎|
