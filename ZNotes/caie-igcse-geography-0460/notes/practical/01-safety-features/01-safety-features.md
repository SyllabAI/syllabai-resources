# Safety Features

* Protection from the weather (waterproof jacket, umbrella, hat, sun
  cream)
* Always carry out coursework in groups
* Always tell an adult or teacher where you are carrying out coursework
* Always carry a mobile phone with you
* Never do coursework near a river or the sea without an adult or
  teacher and without them checking that it is safe
* Carry out coursework in daylight and wear reflective clothes
* Check that your study area is safe.
* Don't display valuables making you more vulnerable to crime, e.g. if you have a camera or a phone, keep it out of sight
