## Stratified Sampling

* **Advantages:**

  * Can be used alongside random or systematic sampling, and with point, line or area techniques.

  * Can generate results which are more representative of the whole population.

  * Flexible and applicable to many geographical enquiries and investigations.

  * Correlations and comparisons can be made between subpopulations.

* **Disadvantages:**

  * Proportions of the subpopulations must be the same; up-to-date data may not be available which makes the division into subpopulations difficult.
