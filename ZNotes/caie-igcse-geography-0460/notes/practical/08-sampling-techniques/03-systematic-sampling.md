## Systematic Sampling

Done in regular intervals or pattern that is followed by every nth time.

* **Advantages**:

  * More straightforward than random sampling.

  * Good coverage of the study area can be more easily achieved - better representation of the total population.

* **Disadvantages**:

  * More biased as not all members have an equal chance of being selected.

  * May lead to over or under representation of a particular pattern/subpopulation.
