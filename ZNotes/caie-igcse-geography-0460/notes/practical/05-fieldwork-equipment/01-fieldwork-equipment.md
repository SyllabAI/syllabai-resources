# Fieldwork Equipment

### **Quadrat:**

* Used for measuring vegetation cover. Usually 50cm<sup>2</sup> divided into 100 small squares. Placing a quadrat over an area of vegetation can calculate the area covered in vegetation or % of ‎different vegetation types.         ![](../../../assets/21142e6c09af-image058.png)

### **Callipers:**

* measure the width, depth or length of small objects like a load.

  ![](../../../assets/efc39f50ffd8-image059.png)

### **Clinometer:**

* used for measuring slope angle (gradient).

![](../../../assets/b64c894dded7-image060.png)

### **Flowmeter:**

* used for measuring the velocity of rivers. Have a small propeller, which you place just under ‎the water's surface. Depending on the speed of the propeller, a small digital readout gives the speed ‎of the river.‎

![](../../../assets/ca2393a4c57e-image061.png)

### **Pebbleometer**:

* A fundamental device for measuring the size and shape of material found on a beach or river.

  ![](../../../assets/f47f6be900fb-image062.png)

### **Ranging Poles**:

* They have a pointy end to properly anchor them into the soil/sand.          ![](../../../assets/8674149d53e5-6489ce6c1e036968c6dfa4b58fb2c610453d54c621ee002c.png)
