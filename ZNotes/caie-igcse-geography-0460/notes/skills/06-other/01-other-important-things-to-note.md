# **Other important things to note**:

* **Settlement** **patterns**: dispersed, nucleated, linear.
* **Natural advantages of a settlement**: flat land, near water source, on the coast, near farmland, near natural resources, defensive location.
* **Human locational factors**: good road links, schools, hospitals, jobs, leisure facilities
* tourism can be promoted by: beaches, sea, mountain, waterfalls, historical buildings, water parks, golf courses, hotels and restaurants.

---

* **Describing photos:** structure, type of vegetation, geographical features.
* **Describing** **relief**: average height of relief and height of highest point
  * mountainous or hilly or part of a plateau
  * state locations (grid reference) of any relief features that stands out
  * **slope**: concave, convex, uniform, steep
* **Describing landscape**: ridge, plateau (elevated land with flat top and steep sides), scarp, floodplain
  * broad, flat, steep-sided, deeply cut, gently sloping

---

* **Drainage**:
  * density of drainage: the greater the number of streams, the higher the density
  * stream patterns: dendritic, trellis or radial
  * area drained – many tributaries joining the main river etc.
  * direction of flow
  * course of river: straight or meandering, braiding, width
  * presence of river features e.g rapids, waterfall
* **Choropleth map**: maps which are shaded according to a pre-arranged key.
