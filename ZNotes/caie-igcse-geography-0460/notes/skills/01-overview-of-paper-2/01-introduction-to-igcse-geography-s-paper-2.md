# Overview of Paper 2

## Introduction to IGCSE Geography’s Paper 2

### **This paper is assessed in main 2 sections**

1. Mapwork Question (Question 1); 20/60 marks,
2. short answer, evidence-based questions (Questions 2–6); 8 marks each, 40/60 marks.

### **You may use the following equipment in Paper 2:**

* Pencil, rubber, ruler, protractor, calculator and string or piece of paper.
* A sheet of plain paper will be provided to you in the exam room for measuring distances or assisting with cross-sections on the large-scale maps.
