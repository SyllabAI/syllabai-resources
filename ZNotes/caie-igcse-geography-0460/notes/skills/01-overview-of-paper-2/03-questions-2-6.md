## Questions 2–6

These questions are based on the themes you have studied.

### They may draw on:

* maps
* graphs and diagrams
* photos and sketches
* small paragraphs
* etc.

### They may ask you to:

* identify features described,
* describe what is shown in the figures,
* explain why something occurs,
* recall facts from the themes,
* compare and contrast the two resources given.

---

### **The Structure of the Questions**

Usually, there are at least one of each of the following types of questions:

* map-based questions that ask you to describe, explain and predict,
* photo-based resource that asks you to identify and explain,
* long answer compare and contrast questions,
* diagram and graph-based questions that ask you to describe changes, distributions and patterns

**These questions are less common:**

* describing field sketches, or drawing field sketches,
* plotting data on a diagram (usually examined in Paper 4).
