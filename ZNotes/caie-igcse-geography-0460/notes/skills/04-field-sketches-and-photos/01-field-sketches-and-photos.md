# Field Sketches and Photos

### **Reasons you would make a field sketch:‎**

* No map of your study area exists
* The map of your study area contains too much detail or not
  enough detail
* You do not have a camera to take a photograph
* You want to remove some detail from the view you are drawing
* You want to labels or annotations to the view you are drawing

### **Describing photographs:‎**

* Describe the structure (building) shown in the photo.‎
* Describe the type of vegetation
* Describe the geographical features shown
