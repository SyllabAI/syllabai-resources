## Content

### Introduction (3 parts in the order)

* Broad background statement

* Rewrite the question

* Thesis statement

* Include the aspects though which you are going to assess this topic

### Body 1 and Body 2 (4 parts in this order)

* Topic sentence (state your point)

* Reason (must be relevant to the topic sentence)

* Example (must be relevant to the topic sentence)

* Summarize the point

### Conclusion (2-3 sentences)

* No new ideas

* Repeat the ideas used in the body paragraphs

### Thesis statement (one sentence)

* Show what you think on the topic

* Can use “I”

* Clearly show stance on the topic

* Must connect to the previous sentences in the intro
