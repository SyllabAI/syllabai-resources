## Sample

### *Sample Question*

*Are smart phones good or bad? Give your arguments to support you position.*

### Sample essay

*The 21st century has ushered in rapid technological progress. Until the COVID-19 pandemic, people weren’t as screen-dependent—especially on smartphones. Whether they are a boon or bane depends on their influence on health, economics, education, and social life.*

*Firstly, smartphones affect eye health. Long exposure to screens strains vision, leading to headaches or the need for glasses. Additionally, humans are social creatures, yet texting is replacing meaningful face-to-face interaction, weakening connections. Economically, older phones were used mostly for calls and cost less, while today’s data packages appear affordable but often drain money.*

*However, during the pandemic, smartphones played a vital role. They supported remote learning for two years and helped maintain relationships across borders when physical contact was impossible.*

*In conclusion, smartphones are double-edged swords. Their impact depends entirely on how users engage with them. Used mindfully, they’re powerful tools for progress. Used carelessly, they may isolate and harm.*
