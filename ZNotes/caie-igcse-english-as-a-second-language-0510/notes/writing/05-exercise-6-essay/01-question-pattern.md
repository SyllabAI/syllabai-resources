# Essay

## Question Pattern

* Mark distribution: content - 6, language - 9 (refer language section)

* Word count - 120-160 words (can extend to 180 if it fits the page and if it is ABSOLUTELY necessary)

You will be given a statement and asked to give your opinion on it

### Route 1

* Choose one side and argue your points. Back them up with evidence.

### Route 2

* Argue effectively for **both** sides and settle for one in the **conclusion**
