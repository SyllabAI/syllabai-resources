## Sample

### Sample Question

*Your class recently went to a career day to get information about different types of jobs. Now, your teacher wants you to write a report about the career’s day.*

*In your report, say what you found useful about careers day and how you think the event could be improved for next year.*

### Sample report

++Careers Day: Highlights and Improvements++

*The purpose of this report is to brief on the Careers Day conducted on Tuesday, July 17,* *in our school from 11am to 3pm and to suggest possible improvements.*

*To start off with, students agree that the people were friendly especially when we asked questions. In addition, we were given a clear idea on the entrance exams to clear and the best universities for that specific career. This proved useful for us. Subsequently, the insights, which focused mostly on work-life balance with professions and the future career prospects, provided me with a multi-faceted perspective.*

*Nevertheless, it must be noted that all jobs were similar, primarily in the technology sector. Next time, the diversity of careers could be expanded. Moreover, students mentioned that the salary expectations were not covered. That could be touched upon next year. Also, the duration could be cut to 2 hours instead of 4. I felt it was too long.*

*All in all, this Careers Day did its job by introducing new viewpoints on building a self-sustaining career. With the improvements mentioned above, next year’s Careers Day would be an even bigger success. I’d definitely go again.*
