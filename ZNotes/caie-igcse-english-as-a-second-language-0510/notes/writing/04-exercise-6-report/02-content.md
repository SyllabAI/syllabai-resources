## Content structure

* Introduction

* Body 1

* Body 2

* Conclusion

### Introduction

* State what happened (where, when, what, who, why)

* State **purpose** of report

Ex.

*The purpose of this report is to brief on the Careers Day conducted on Tuesday, September 3 rd in our school from 11am to 3pm and to suggest on possible improvements.*

### Body 1

* Address the first highlighted point

* Approx. 3 points

* State and describe your points (this can be short)

### Body 2

* Address the second highlighted point

* Approx. 3 points

* *Elaborate* and *explain* your arguments (this can be short)

### Conclusion

* Signal the end (using linking words)

* Summarize your body 1 and body 2

* In about 3 sentences
