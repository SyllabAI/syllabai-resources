## Sample

### Sample question

*Recently, you and some of the other students from your school took part in a quiz.*

*Write an email to a friend about the quiz. In your email, you should,*

* *Explain why you took part in the quiz*

* *Describe how you prepared for the quiz*

* *Say how you felt about taking part in the quiz*

### **Sample email**

*Hi Eve,*

*I hear you’re under the weather lately. Is that why you haven’t come back to school after summer break? School feels different without you. Anyway, guess what? I’m now part of the school’s quiz team, and we have a good shot at winning regionals, too!*

*It all began when I was still buzzing with energy over the astronomy camp we attended over summer break, and then it all fell into place perfectly! You see, word’s been around that the quiz team needs someone with an astronomy and space niche to compete in the regionals. And I signed up without a second thought!*

*I know it isn’t like me to be impulsive, but the opportunity was too good to pass up! We had two weeks until round 2, so we burnt the midnight oil working through our most difficult-to-outsmart tactics and strategies since we were in the dark about our competitors. And we did our best to keep our noses to the grindstone. While I didn’t have many resources beyond camp notes, the YouTube channel you recommended helped me go the extra mile. Thanks a million for that one!*

*When the day came, I was a nervous wreck. We were against the hardest team, but we didn’t let the worries wash us away. After multiple fiery rounds, we bested them by the skin of our teeth! We’re officially in for Round 2!*

*Come by if you’re up for it,*

*Ava*
