# **Exercise 5-Email Writing (15 marks)**

## Structure

* Mark distribution- *6 marks for content and 9 marks for language* (refer to language module).

* Word count- **120-160 words** (though you could extend it to 180 as long as the page looks presentable and you do not go beyond the last line of the page in the exam)

**Structure** -There’d be 3 bullet points, address them in that order.

Dear (name),

* *Paragraph 1*- Use this to ‘catch up’ on things, think personalization, and deliberately connect it to the first prompt without making it too sudden. (Make this short.)

* *Paragraph 2*- Address the first prompt, and towards the end, flow toward the second prompt.

* *Paragraph 3*- Address the second prompt and flow toward the third one

* *Paragraph 4*- Address the third prompt and connect it to the recipient. End with brief plans or a friendly farewell.

Your friend (or something similar),

(your name)

* Notice the command words (usually describe, explain, and say)

* *Describe*-longest paragraph, try using more figurative language.

* *Explain*- shorter (not by much) paragraph.

* *Say*-shortest
