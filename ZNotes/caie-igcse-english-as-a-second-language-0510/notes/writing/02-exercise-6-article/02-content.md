### **Body** (3 points on average per paragraph)

* State your point

* Bring discussion- add connection, evidence, reason, and depth to your arguments instead of plainly stating facts

Ex. Instead of writing,

*“Teenagers have lots of opportunities to have a good education in our town.”*

Try writing:

*“Being a teenager means starting high school and building a future and out local high school was, and still is, one of the best educational institutes for teens.*”

* If, possible add examples. You do not need to include examples for all your points. Add them if, even after adding discussion, the point is not clear.

* Repeat this for all points.

### **Conclusion** (2 sentences, max 3)

* Summarize your viewpoint

* Can link it to the intro

* Start by using linking words like ‘overall’ or ‘considering the above arguments’

  (Avoid cliches like ‘in conclusion’, ‘in a nutshell’ or ‘in summary’. Not using these can make your writing stand out)
