
## Sample

### **Sample question**:

*In your article, write about whether you think your town is a good place for teenagers to live.*

*Now write an article for the local newspaper giving your views.*

### **Sample article**

*After our town’s been getting rave reviews about being one of the best places for adults, there’s been debate on if it’s the same for teenagers. So, is our town a good place for teenagers?*

*To begin with, being a teenager means starting high school and building a future. To aid with that, our local Rockport High was, and still is, one of the best educational institutes for teens. Moreover, most firms in the area offer internships and part-time jobs for ambitious students. Additionally, if teenagers just want to hang out, the local cinema showcases a wide selection of movies that appeal to teens. Also, the arcade is a top hangout spot too.*

*However, a part of teenagers’ lives includes operating on a small budget which is still something our town is yet to offer, especially when it comes to clothes and food. Furthermore, figuring out the bus routes is difficult along with calculating fares. And there aren’t many modes of transport either, so teenagers are confused or lost when travelling within town.*

*Overall, our town is a stable environment for teenagers to build their academic and personal lives. With the shopping options being more affordable, teenagers could truly thrive here.*
