## Sample

### Question:

*A movie theatre has recently opened in your town. You have decided to write a review of the movie theatre for your school magazine.*

### Sample review

*Have you ever heard of a movie theatre that only shows horror movies at night? That’s right, the newly opened Blackout Blockbuster is the new hot topic of the town. Here’s what I think of it.*

*What makes it stand out from its competitors is the show timings; nothing before 9 PM and after 6 AM. With the added fact that they just show one kind of movie, horror. The Blackout Blockbuster boasts of elaborate infrastructure, immersive sound systems, and great snacks, all for a reasonable fare. Furthermore, discounts are available for larger groups.*

*When I visited, there wasn’t a great range of movies to choose from in terms of language. They only seem to show English horror films with limited options for regional languages. However, the staff were friendly. Moreover, the sound systems could have been executed better since, in some areas of the theatre, sound clarity was missing. Additionally, the timings were, in my opinion, rather odd; they would need to be adjusted to accommodate a larger customer base.*

*Overall, I would recommend this theatre for those who like horror movies with a suitable ambience, though I suggest young children avoid such theatres. As for teenagers, if they went in a group, it’d be fun. I’d rate this 3.5/5.*
