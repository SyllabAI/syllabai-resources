## Structure review

### Introduction

* Open with a hook (a question, mostly)

* Give the place/thing/incident/experience/service a name

* Hint that this **purely** your opinion

### Body 1

* Describe the subject (special features, what makes it stand out from its competitors)

* Give about 3 points

### Body 2

* Mention and explain what you think about the subject

* Can be positive or negative

* Give 3-4 opinions

* **Never** make all the reviews positive or vice versa

* Possible organization of points:

  * 3 positives+1 negative (for a positive review)

  * 2 positives+2 negatives (for a balanced review)

  * 1 positive+3 negatives (for a negative review)

### Conclusion

* Indicate the end

* Mention to what kind of people you would recommend this to (and to whom you won’t)

* Give a rating
