# Review Writing

## Purpose and Structure

* Mark distribution; content - 6, language - 9

* Word count - 120-160 words (can extend to 180 if it fits the page and if it is ABSOLUTELY necessary)

### Purpose

* To inform reads of a place/thing/incident/experience/service (subject)

* Give your experience there

* Say what you think about it (the good and the bad)

### Structure

* Introduction

* Body 1

* Body 2

* Conclusion
