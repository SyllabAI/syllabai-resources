## Plan

This is NOT graded, but it is very useful and makes the writing process easier

* before writing, **always** have a **written** plan.

Your plan should include (for Ex-5): 

* What you will say in your intro (inquire about you friend)

* How will you connect paragraphs (flow)

* What figurative language will you use (have a rough idea on what similes/metaphors to use)

* **How** will you address all the 3 bulletin point and to **what extent** you will address them

* How will you end it

Remember this is a rough one, jot down ideas. No need to write full paragraphs. Use this as a reference to write your email. You will have enough space to plan in the question paper.

(for Ex-6)

* your intro (this should be a little detailed)

* what points you are going to include in your body paragraphs

* if examples are needed, what examples would you include

* what explanations you are going to give for your reasons (this should be brief in your plan but detailed in your actual answer)
