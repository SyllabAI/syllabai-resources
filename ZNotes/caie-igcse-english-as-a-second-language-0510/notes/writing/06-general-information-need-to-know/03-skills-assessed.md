## Skills assessed

* Understanding the task and purpose

* Writing in appropriate formats (e.g., article, email, report, review)

* Using the correct tone and register (formal/informal depending on the audience)

* Organising ideas clearly with paragraphs

* Developing ideas with detail, explanation, or examples

* Using a wide range of vocabulary appropriately

* Using a variety of sentence structures

* Spelling words accurately

* Using correct punctuation and grammar

* Linking ideas logically with connectives (e.g., however, therefore, although) (refer language module for this)

* Writing with coherence and cohesion

* Staying within the word limit (usually around 150–200 words)

* Addressing all bullet points or prompts in the task

* Using accurate verb tenses and subject-verb agreement

* Editing and proofreading for clarity and correctness
