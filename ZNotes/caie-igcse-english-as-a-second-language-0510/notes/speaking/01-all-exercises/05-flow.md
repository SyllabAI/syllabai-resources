## Flow

* You are **not** required to answer immediately after the question is asked.

* Take a few seconds to gather hints on what to say.

* Then start speaking, stringing together your elaborated hints.

* If you speak too fast, your teacher my not understand what you’re saying, and they may not give your answer the marks it deserves

* Also, if you talk too fast, you run out of points sooner

* So, take you time and give sufficient details for your answer, your teacher will not interrupt you.
