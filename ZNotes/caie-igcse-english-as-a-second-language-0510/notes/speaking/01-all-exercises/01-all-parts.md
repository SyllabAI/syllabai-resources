# Speaking

## All parts

* 4 parts

* 10-12 minutes long.

* Assessed twice, once by your teacher and once by your examiner. The one by your examiner is the final grade

* Only parts 2,3, and 4 are **assessed** (given marks)

### Part 1 (warm up)

* Intro, basic details are shared (welcome, syllabus)

* For 2-3 mins

* Basic questions about you

* This for the examiner to understand you and for you to warm up

  *“Can you tell me about your friends?”*

  *“What would you like to do when you finish school?”*

  *“What kind of music do you like?” -* [*from*](https://learning.cambridgeinternational.org/classroom/pluginfile.php/210484/mod_resource/content/1/0510_y24_sn_3.pdf) 

### Part 2

* Interview

  *“Can you tell me about the town or village where you live? “*

  *“What can you do in your free time in your area? “*

  *“Do you think it is healthier to live in a town or the country? Why? Why not?” -* [*from*](https://learning.cambridgeinternational.org/classroom/pluginfile.php/210484/mod_resource/content/1/0510_y24_sn_3.pdf) 

### Part 3

* Based on the 2nd part you will be given a speaking card.

* **One minute** preparation time (you cannot make notes here)

* You can **keep** the card during the talk

* The card will have the topic, usually a choice to make between 2 options.

* Assess each option in detail and conclude, give your opinion.

* **Tip**- treat this part as reading out an article writing assignment. Speak in the way you would write it.

* 3-4 minutes

### **Part 4**

* Discussion (conversation) between you and the examiner

* Based on the short talk

* Answer each question until you are satisfied with the answer (don’t overdo it though)

* 3-4 minutes
