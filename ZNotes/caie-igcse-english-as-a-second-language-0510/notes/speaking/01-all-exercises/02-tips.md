## Tips

* You can ask the examiner to **clarify** the question before you answer it

* During the test, ask any questions you have about the test

* Your answers do not have to sound scripted. Casual tones are appreciated (but not too informal)

* Be casual and open

* You will not be interrupted so take you time say what you honestly think.
