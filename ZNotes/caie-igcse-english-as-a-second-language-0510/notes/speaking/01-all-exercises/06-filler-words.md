## Filler words

* Marks are **not** directly reduced because you use filler words.

* But, if using **too much** filler words affects the actual rubric (Grammar, Vocabulary, Development and Pronunciation), then your mark in **that** category would be reduced. This **will** affect your overall score.

* Try imagining that the speaking exam is a normal casual conversation in English.

* As long your fillers aren’t noticeable and they do not affect you overall answer, then, it is fine.
