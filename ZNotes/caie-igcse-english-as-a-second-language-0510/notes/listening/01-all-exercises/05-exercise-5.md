## Exercise 5

* This is an interview recording about a specific aspect about the person who is being interviewed (usually their job or hobby)

* There would be 8 multiple choice questions

* Before the recording starts you will be given time to read the questions

* While reading the questions, mark/ underline the key words. This will make it easier to spot answers quicker

* When the recording plays for the first time, eliminate incorrect options

* When the recording is replayed, cross-check your answer. Confirm your answer

* If you are confused between 2 options, use the replay to narrow down the correct answer

### Places for confusion

* When all options are mentioned in the talk:

  * If the question is “How does X person feel about…” find which option the speaker **connects with**

    Ex: look for phrases like *“…nothing beats it for me.”* or *“…it was exhausting…”*

  * Usually the audio format is:

    * The question is asked

    * All options are talked about

    * A phrase is said that confirms the answer

  * You will be able to figure this out when you listen to audios

  * Pay attention to the last sentence/phrase of audio **before** the next question. This is you biggest clue to find your answer

  * You can also use this sentence/phrase to **narrow down your answer** between 2 options you are confused about
