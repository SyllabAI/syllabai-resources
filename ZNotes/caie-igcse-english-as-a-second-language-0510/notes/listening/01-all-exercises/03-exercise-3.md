## Exercise 3

* You will have 8 questions

* One person would give a talk about a specific topic

* Before the recording starts you will be given time to read the questions

* While reading the questions, underline the key words. This will make it easier to spot answers quicker

* When the recording plays for the first time, eliminate incorrect options

* When the recording is replayed, cross-check your answer. Confirm your answer

* If you are confused between 2 options, use the replay to narrow down the correct answer

### Places for confusion

* When all answers are mentioned

  * Answers would be **indirectly** stated

    Ex: ‘at sea level’- flat land

  * Pay attention to words that narrow down the answer: ‘last’, ‘first’, ‘now’…

  * So, before the recording starts, assume all the options are correct.

  * Then use the information in the audio to find evidence that an option is wrong

  * Once the incorrect options are eliminated, you will have your answer
