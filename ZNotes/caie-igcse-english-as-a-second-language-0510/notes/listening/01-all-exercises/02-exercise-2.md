## Exercise 2

* There would be 5 recordings and there would be 2 questions per recording

* When the recording plays for the first time, eliminate incorrect options

* When the recording is replayed, cross-check your answer. Confirm your answer.

* If you are confused between 2 options, use the replay to narrow down the correct answer

### **Places for confusion:**

* When none of the answers are **directly** given:

  * Before the recording starts, assume all the options are correct.

  * Then use the information in the audio to find evidence that an option is wrong

  * Once the incorrect options are eliminated, you will have your answer

  * This method is useful since most answers are **implicit**
