# Listening

## Exercise 1

* You will have 8 questions (each question is for 1 mark)

* There will be 4 options, each option will be a picture

* When the recording plays for the first time, eliminate incorrect options

* When the recording is replayed, cross-check your answer. Confirm your answer.

* If you are confused between 2 options, use the replay to narrow down the correct answer

* Underline the key words in the question in the time given to read the question

* Try making mental sequences of events, this makes finding the final answer easier

### Places for confusion:

* When every answer is mentioned in the recording:

  * Identify a condition in the question, such as ‘now’ or ‘yesterday’ or ‘next, (OR)

  * Identify what option the characters agree on; that will most likely be your answer
