## Tips

* There would be different accents in the audio. (British, American and Australian only).

* Watch movies and tv series with these accents (without subtitles).

* Practice **a lot of** past papers from websites.

* Listen to the audio **without** headphones; you will **not** have headphones in the exam.

* If you are unsure about an answer, look for **possible synonyms** for the words in the audio and match it to the options
