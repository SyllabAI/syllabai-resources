# Exercise 4

## What you can expect

* One text (550 -600 words)

* Six questions (each for one mark) of 2 types

  * Main ideas and details from the text (5 marks)

  * Author’s opinion (one mark)

* The text could come from a blog, article, or website

### About the paper

* You **cannot** find all the answers directly in the text.

* Most answers would be **implicit**

* So read the text and understand the **“main idea” of each paragraph**

  *Step 1*- read the text fully

  *Step 2*- read all the questions and mark the key words in the question and the options given

  *Step 3*-narrow down the line where the answer is present and compare it to the options

  S*tep 4*- find the option the **best** resembles the line in the text
