## Sample questions and sample answers

**1.What is one main problem caused by roads in natural areas? (easy) (C)**

A. They are too expensive to build.

B. They prevent cars from moving quickly.

C. They are dangerous for animals crossing them.

**2.How are wildlife bridges made to be suitable for animals? (easy) (B)**

A. They are built high above the trees.

B. They are covered with natural vegetation.

C. They are painted in bright colors.

**3.Which country built the first wildlife bridges? (easy) (B)**

A. Canada

B. France

C. Australia

**4.What has been the result of wildlife bridges in Banff National Park? (medium) (C)**

A. More animals are using the roads.

B. Animal-vehicle accidents have increased.

C. There are far fewer accidents involving animals

**5.How does Australia help tree-dwelling animals cross roads? (medium) (C)**

A. By cutting down trees near roads

B. By building tunnels only for large animals

C. By constructing rope bridges and tunnels

**6.Why do some people support the high cost of building wildlife bridges? (medium) (B)**

A. They look attractive in cities.

B. They can reduce other expenses like healthcare and road repairs.

C. They increase the speed of traffic flow

**7.What is the main reason wildlife bridges are covered with plants? (medium) (B)**

A. To keep animals hidden from drivers

B. To help animals feel at ease while crossing

C. To improve the appearance of roads

**8. What does the text suggest about the effectiveness of the bridges in Canada’s Banff National Park? (hard) (C)**

A. They have almost eliminated accidents involving animals.

B. They are mostly used by larger species like wolves.

C. They have significantly improved safety for both animals and drivers.

**9. Why does the text mention fencing along the roads? (medium) (C)**

A. To prevent people from entering dangerous wildlife areas

B. To stop animals from using the roads entirely

C. To lead animals toward safer crossing points

**10. What does the text imply about the use of rope bridges in Queensland? (hard) (A)**

A. They are intended for species that live mainly in trees.

B. They are more effective than tunnels in all cases.

C. They are used because other bridges are too expensive

**11.Which of the following best summarizes the writer’s opinion on the cost of wildlife bridges? (medium-hard) (B)**

A. They are too expensive to be built widely.

B. Their advantages make them worth the expense.

C. Governments should spend the money elsewhere

**12. What is the writer’s overall attitude toward wildlife bridges? (hard) (C)**

A. Cautiously optimistic about their long-term success

B. Neutral but informative about both sides

C. Supportive of them as a necessary solution
