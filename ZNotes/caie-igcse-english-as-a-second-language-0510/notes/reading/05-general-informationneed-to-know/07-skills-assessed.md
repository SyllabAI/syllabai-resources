## Skills assessed

* Skimming – reading quickly to get the general idea (gist).

* Scanning – looking for specific information

* Reading for detail – understanding precise factual content

* Identifying main ideas

* Identifying supporting details

* Understanding implied meaning and inference

* Understanding opinion, attitude, and purpose

* Understanding vocabulary in context

* Recognizing tone and style

* Distinguishing fact from opinion

* Identifying sequence of events

* Making logical connections within a text

* Summarising key points from a text

* Extracting relevant information efficiently

* Understanding overall text structure and organization

Note- don’t pay too much attention to this list. You will gain the skills as you read more and practice past papers. Use this a checklist instead.
