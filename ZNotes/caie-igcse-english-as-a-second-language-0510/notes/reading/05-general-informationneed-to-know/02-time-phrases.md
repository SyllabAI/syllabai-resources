Future

|

Used to talk about something that will happen later.

|

tomorrow

next week / next month / next year

in two days / in a week

soon / later

in the future

the day after tomorrow

someday / eventually

by [time] (e.g. "by next Friday"

||
||

Past

|

Used to describe something that happened before now.

|

yesterday

last night / last week / last year

two days ago / a week ago /

a long time ago

in 1990 / in July

the day before yesterday

earlier

previously

once

back then

||
||

Present

|

Used to describe something happening now or regularly.

|

today

now

at the moment

these days

every day / every week

usually / normally / often

sometimes / occasionally / rarely

currently

at present
