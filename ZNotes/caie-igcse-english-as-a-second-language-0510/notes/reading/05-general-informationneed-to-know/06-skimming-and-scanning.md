## Skimming and Scanning

**Skimming**

* Used to get the ‘General idea’ of the entire text

* Read the **first** and **last** sentence of all paragraphs

* Use this technique for ‘quick reads’

**Scanning**

* Deep reading

* **underline** or mark the **important lines** (details, dates, overall opinions etc.)

* note the **main idea of each paragraph** (this will help narrow down where your answer this)

* Use this technique to **read questions** and **all the texts**

### **Knowing what to mark**

**In the texts:**

* Do **not** mark anything while reading the text for the first time

* After reading the questions, mark anything that gives clues about the answer

**In the questions:**

* The key words (words that tell you what to look for in the text)

* The ‘Wh’ words (who, where, why, what, when, how)
