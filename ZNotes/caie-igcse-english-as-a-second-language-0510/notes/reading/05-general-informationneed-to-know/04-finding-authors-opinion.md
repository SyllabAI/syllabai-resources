## Finding authors opinion

* Try asking yourself “Why has the author included this idea?” after reading any text in general as practice

* You will instinctively understand this with **continuous** practice

Phrases

|

Examples

||
||

Direct opinion

|

I think, I believe, I feel, In my opinion

||
||

Evaluative words

|

Amazing, unnecessary, essential, ridiculous, effective, pointless, important, disappointing, successful

||
||

Judging phrases

|

It’s clear that, There’s no doubt that, It seems that, It’s obvious that, It’s surprising that

||
||

Modals suggesting opinions

|

Should, must, could have, ought to, might not be

||
||

Adverbs showing bias

|

Unfortunately, fortunately, clearly, hopefully, surprisingly

||
||

Loaded comparisons

|

Better than, worse than, more important than
