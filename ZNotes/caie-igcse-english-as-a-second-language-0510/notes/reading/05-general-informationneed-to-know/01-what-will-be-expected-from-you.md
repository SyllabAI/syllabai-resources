# General information (need-to-know)

## **What will be expected from you**

* To be able to pick out information (arguments, ideas, and facts) from a text under a theme having different purposes. The texts will have:

  * Different purposes (filter out specific information, the author’s perspective, or a specific objective)

  * Different themes (narrative, persuasive, discursive, descriptive, informational, argumentative)

  * Different types (notices, guidelines, advertisements, pamphlets, blogs, articles, reviews, and websites)

* Figure out patterns and connecting between texts

  * Time phrases

  * Phrases/words the add information

  * Phrases/words that connect opposite ideas

  * Phrases/words that highlight examples

  * ‘Cause and effect’ phrases

  * Pronoun use

* To be able to identify what is not directly said (implicit information)

  * Understand the overall concept and opinion of the text

  * Pick out where the author’s opinion is inserted

  * Figure out the meaning of unknown words or phrases using context (this will come with practice)

* Use reading techniques such as skimming and scanning a text

  * Narrow down where the answer might be

  * Identify **exactly** what the question asks you to do
