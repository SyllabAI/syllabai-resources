   To ask about people.

   * *Who is your teacher?*

   * *Who called me?*

   Where

   |
   
   To ask about places or locations.

   * *Where do you live?*

   * *Where is my phone?*

   ||
   ||
   
   Why

   |
   
   To ask about reasons.

   * *Why are you late?*

   * *Why is the sky blue?*

   ||
   ||
   
   When

   |
   
   To ask about time.

   * *When is your birthday?*

   * *When will the movie start?*

   ||
   ||
   
   What

   |
   
   To ask about things, information, or actions.

   * *What is your name?*

   * *What are you doing?*

   ||

   |
   
   To refer to ‘in what way’

   * *How do you make this?* (In what way do you make this?)

   |
