## Sample Text

When December arrives, I know the cold mornings are coming. The alarm clock rings before the sun has risen, and I slowly leave the comfort of my bed. Even though the air feels freezing, I’ve started to enjoy the quiet and beauty of winter mornings.

Frost covers the ground, the windows, and even things left outside overnight. Everything looks different—almost as if it has been covered in glitter. There is a deep silence, as if the cold air has made the world quieter. I can even see my breath hanging in the air like smoke.

Sometimes, I step outside just as the sun starts to rise. The sky changes colour, turning pink and orange, and the rooftops begin to glow in the sunlight. There are no cars or people around, and the peaceful atmosphere makes me feel calm and grateful.

Winter has a special smell. I notice the scent of smoke from chimneys, the wet smell of the ground, and the fresh smell of pine trees. These smells are much stronger in winter than in other seasons. I often stop to breathe in the cold air, just to enjoy it.

Of course, winter mornings can be uncomfortable. My gloves don’t always keep my fingers warm, scarves feel itchy, and the pavements are sometimes slippery. But even with these small problems, I still look forward to winter mornings.

The best thing is how they make me notice small details. I see birds calling in the quiet, frozen spider webs on fences, and the way sunlight shines on the frost. These are things I usually don’t see or think about in warmer months.

Waking up early in the cold is not easy. But if you take a moment to look and listen, winter mornings can be full of quiet beauty.
