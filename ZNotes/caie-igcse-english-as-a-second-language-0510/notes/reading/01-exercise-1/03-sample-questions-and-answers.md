## Sample questions and answers

### 1. What time of day does the alarm clock ring?

Before the sun has risen.

### 2. How does frost change the appearance of things?

Covered in glitter

### 3. Give a phrase that describes how the rooftops look at sunrise.

Begin to glow in the sunlight

### 4. What shows that the writer has started to enjoy winter mornings?

"I’ve started to enjoy the quiet and beauty"

### 5. According to the writer, how do the smells in winter compare with other seasons?

They are stronger / more noticeable

### 6. What problem do the writer’s gloves have?

They don’t keep fingers warm

### 7. What sound is mentioned as being heard during winter mornings?

Birds calling

### 8. What does the writer say about waking up early in winter?

It is not easy

### 9. What three small details does the writer notice more in winter mornings?

Any three of:

* Birds calling in the quiet

* Frozen spider webs

* Sunlight shining on the frost

(These are only for practice. In the exam you will get 6 questions)
