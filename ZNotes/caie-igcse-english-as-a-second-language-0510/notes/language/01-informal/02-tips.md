## **Tips**

* Using similes and metaphors to connect to the email recipient, if possible. For instance, *‘The oak tree next to my new house is like the one outside our old school where we used to spend evenings reading and talking about everything under the sun.’* (simile)

* You can create your own figurative language, too — it doesn’t have to be a common phrase, as long as it fits.

* Focus on theme-related vocabulary (don’t use them too often, it ruins the informal tone).
