# Informal language

## Tone

Purely informal **without** slang or abbreviations. To make the email informal, use:

#|
||

|

Description

|

Example

||
||

Simile

|

comparing something directly to another using ‘like’ or ‘as’

|

* *‘*As busy as a bee’

* ‘Her hands were calloused like leather’

||
||

Metaphors

|

comparing something with another **without** ‘like’ or ‘as’

|

* ‘a walk in the park’

* ‘visiting the carnival was a lucid dream’

||
||

Personalization

|

using certain phrases and instances to establish that you and the person you’re writing the email to are close

|

* Asking about a sports game they played in

* Inquiring about an illness they had the last time you’d seen them

* Make plans together in the email

* Asking about other friends you two share

||
||

Idiom

|

phrase that has a figurative meaning

|

* *turn a blind eye* (to ignore something)

* *at the drop of a hat* (sudden action)
