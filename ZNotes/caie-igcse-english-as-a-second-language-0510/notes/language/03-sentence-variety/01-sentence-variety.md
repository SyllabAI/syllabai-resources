# **Sentence variety**

Changes sentence structure, length to give variation

1. **Active voice**

   * Subject **does** the action.

   * *Example: he caught the ball*

2. **Passive voice**

   * Subject **receives** the action

   * *Example: the ball was caught (by him)*

3. Alternate between short sentences with longer, compound (or complex) ones

4. Add questions and exclamations (do **not** do this in formal writing. Try this in exercise 5)

   * “I knew you would be excited about it!”

   * “How is you new neighborhood? Have you made any new friends yet?”

5. Include different sentence types (imperative, declarative, interrogative, exclamatory)

6. Experiment with ways to start sentences (words or phrases)
