## Sentence Types

#|
||

Sentence type

|

Description

|

Example

||
||

Imperative

|

* gives instruction, command or request

* may end with a full stop **or** an exclamation mark

|

* “Pass the salt please.”

* “Get back here!”

* “I wonder what time it is.”

||
||

Declarative

|

Makes a statement (states a fact)

|

* “I know where we’re going”

* “This is the longest holiday of the semester.”

||
||

Interrogative

|

* Asks a question

* Ends with a question mark (?)

|

* “Where have you been?”

* “Who is you new friend?”

||
||

Exclamatory

|

* Shows excitement or emotions

* Always ends with an exclamation mark (!)

|

* “It’s good to have you here!”

* “The buildings in the city are so tall! You should’ve seen them!”

||
|#

### Note:

* **Never** include interrogative and exclamatory sentences in **formal/semiformal writing** (exercise 6)

* You are encouraged to include **all** sentence types in **email writing** (exercise 5)
