## Starting Sentences

#|
||

Methods

|

Examples

||
||

Using linking words

|

* *Moreover*, the weather was perfect for a hike.

* *As a result*, she left the meeting early.

||
||

Using time markers (Indicate when something happens)

|

* *After she left*, everyone in the room panicked.

* *At sundown*, the entire town buzzed with newfound energy.

||
||

Using prepositional phrases (Set the scene or give detail)

|

* *Under the bed*, she found her lost bracelet.

* *By the fireplace,* the dog slept peacefully.

||
||

Using subordinal clauses -A group of words with a subject and verb that doesn't form a full sentence. (Add complexity or conditions)

|

* *Although it was raining,* they went for a walk.

* *Because he studied every night,* he passed the exam.

||
||

Add facts (Start with confidence and clarity)

|

* *Practice makes perfect.*

* *Change is never easy.*

||
||

Adding -ed or -ing phrases (Provide action or description)

|

* *Walking down the street,* she noticed a new café.

* *Surprised by the news,* he dropped his phone.

||
||

Adding adverbs (Describe how something is done)

|

* *Quietly*, he slipped out of the room.

* I*nterestingly*, no one disagreed with her idea.

||
