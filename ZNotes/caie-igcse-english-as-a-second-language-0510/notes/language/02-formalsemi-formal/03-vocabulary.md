## Vocabulary

* Do **not** use personal pronouns (except reviews, reports and emails)

* Use **theme vocabulary-** words that connect directly to the concept of the question.

  * **Ex**- if a question asks for an article on small businesses:

    * Revenue

    * Profit

    * Customer service

    * Marketing

    * Funding

    * Local economy

    * Sustainability

* Read fiction and newspapers to expand ideas

* Gather knowledge from other subjects you have taken in school (economics, computer science etc.)

* Use **synonyms** instead of repeating words.

* Explore past papers to get an idea on what topics you would need to know about to attend exercise 5 and 6
