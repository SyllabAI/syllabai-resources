## Sentence variety

* Change up the length and structure of your sentences.

Example:\
**Instead of**

*To begin with, sports aids in the overall development of a student. They learn strategic thinking; teamwork and they socialize with similar-minded peers. This is a vital platform to grow into better versions of themselves and learn real-world skills*.

**Try:**

*To begin with, real-world skills such as teamwork and strategic thinking could be learnt via sports. Also, students could socialize with like-minded peers.*

(the overall length of the content may or may not change)
