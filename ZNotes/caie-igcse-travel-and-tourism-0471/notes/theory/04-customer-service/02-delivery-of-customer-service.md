## Delivery of customer service

* ### Procedures for handling complaints

  1. Listen carefully to the complaint:
     Allow the customer to explain without interruption.

  2. Stay calm and show empathy:
     Be polite and patient, and acknowledge the customer’s feelings.

  3. Apologize sincerely:
     Offer a genuine apology for the inconvenience caused.

  4. Clarify and confirm the problem:
     Repeat the issue to ensure full understanding.

  5. Explain the situation if necessary:
     Give clear and honest information.

  6. Offer an appropriate solution:
     Resolve the issue (e.g., refund, replacement, alternative).

  7. Take action and keep promises:
     Ensure the solution is carried out properly.

  8. Follow up if needed:
     Check that the customer is satisfied with the outcome.

  9. Record the complaint:
     Keep details for future improvement.

* ### Making reservations

1. Collect customer details:
   Record name, contact details, number of people, dates, and preferences

2. Check availability:
   Confirm if the service (hotel, flight, tour) is available

3. Provide suitable options:
   Suggest choices based on budget and customer needs

4. Confirm the booking:
   Finalise the reservation with the customer

5. Process payment:
   Take payment or deposit if required

6. Issue confirmation:
   Provide tickets, receipts, or booking reference details

7. Explain terms and conditions:
   Inform about cancellation policies, timings, and rules

8. Update records:
   Keep accurate booking details for future reference

* ### Taking payments

1. Confirm the total amount to be paid (including any extra charges)

2. Offer different payment methods (e.g., cash, card, online)

3. Process the payment accurately

4. Provide a receipt as proof of payment

5. Explain payment details (e.g., deposits, refunds, additional charges)

6. Ensure security of customer payment information

7. Keep accurate records of transactions

* ### Having good product knowledge

  * The knowledge that staff have about the products and services offered by the organization.

    * knowledge of products and services offered (e.g., hotels, tours, transport)

    * knowledge of prices and packages

    * knowledge of locations and destinations

    * knowledge of facilities and features (e.g., hotel amenities, transport options)

    * knowledge of timings and schedules

    * knowledge of health and safety procedures

    * knowledge of customer needs and preferences

  * ### Staff can gain good product knowledge by

    * staff training programmes (induction and ongoing training)

    * brochures and catalogues provided by the organization

    * websites and online platforms (company or partner sites)

    * familiarisation trips (FAM trips) to experience products directly

    * WTM (World Travel Mart), which is a global series of events for the travel industry to connect, learn, and do business

    * manuals and internal documents (guidelines, handbooks)
