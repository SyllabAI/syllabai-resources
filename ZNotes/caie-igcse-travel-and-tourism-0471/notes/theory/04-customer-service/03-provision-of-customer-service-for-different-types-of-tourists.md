## Provision of customer service for different types of tourists

#|
||

Provision

|

Explanation

||
||

Access

|

Ensuring services are accessible to all tourists (e.g., ramps, lifts, accessible transport)

||
||

Information

|

Providing clear and accurate information (e.g. directions, signage, multilingual information)

||
||

Providing for tourists with specific needs

|

* Visually impaired: Provide audio guides, braille signage, and staff assistance for navigation.

* Hearing impaired: Use visual displays, written communication, and sign language support.

* Wheelchair users: Provide ramps, lifts, accessible rooms, and transport.

* Dietary needs: Offer special meal options (e.g., vegetarian, halal, allergy-free)

||
||

Facilities

|

Providing appropriate facilities (e.g., accessible rooms, recycling systems, energy-efficient facilities)

||
||

Ancillary services

|

Additional services that support tourists (e.g., travel insurance, currency exchange, Wi-Fi)
