* ### Customer service standards

  * These are the expected levels of service that staff must follow when dealing with customers to ensure consistency and quality.

  * Types of Customer Service Standards

    #|
    ||
    
    Type

    |
    
    Explanation

    |
    
    Example

    ||
    ||
    
    Operational standards

    |
    
    Specify what staff must do or say when serving customers

    |
    
    Greeting customers politely, responding within a set time, following procedures

    ||
    ||
    
    Competence standards

    |
    
    Specify the skills, behaviour, and attitude staff must show

    |
    
    Being friendly, professional, knowledgeable, and helpful

    |#
    

* ### Sustainability Standards in Customer Service

  * Guidelines followed by organizations to ensure their services are environmentally, socially, and economically responsible.

  * Examples of Sustainability Standards

    * Paperless transactions:
      Using e-tickets, emails, and digital receipts to reduce paper waste

    * Recommending local providers:
      Encouraging tourists to use local hotels, guides, and businesses to support the local economy

    * Promoting sustainable options:
      Suggesting eco-friendly transport, accommodation, and activities

    * Reducing waste and resources:
      Minimising plastic use, encouraging recycling, and saving water and energy

    * Supporting local communities:
      Promoting local culture, products, and employment opportunities

  * Why Sustainability Standards are Important

    * Protect the environment

    * Support local communities and economies

    * Improve the organisation’s image and reputation

    * Meet the needs of responsible tourists
