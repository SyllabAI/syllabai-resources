## Skills required when working in the travel and tourism industry

* ### Types and importance of training

  * ### Types of training

    #|
    ||
    
    Type

    |
    
    Explanation

    |
    
    Examples

    ||
    ||
    
    On-the-job training

    |
    
    Learning while working (e.g. shadowing experienced staff)

    |
    
    A new employee shadows a receptionist and learns how to check in guests

    ||
    ||
    
    Off-the-job training

    |
    
    Training away from the workplace (e.g., courses, workshops)

    |
    
    Staff attend a customer service workshop or language course

    ||
    ||
    
    Job rotation

    |
    
    Moving between roles to gain wider experience

    |
    
    An employee works in reception, then reservations, then customer service

    ||
    ||
    
    Monitoring

    |
    
    Observing staff performance and providing feedback

    |
    
    A manager watches staff interact with customers and provides feedback

    ||
    |#
    

  * ### Importance of training

    * Enables staff to provide high-quality customer service

    * Improves staff knowledge and skills

    * Helps staff deal with customers effectively

    * Ensures consistent service standards

    * Increases customer satisfaction

* ### Ability to speak other languages

  * Helps staff communicate with international customers more effectively, reducing misunderstandings and improving customer satisfaction.

* ### Ability to address the needs of responsible tourists

  * Helps staff provide environmentally and socially responsible options, such as eco-friendly services and support for local communities, improving customer satisfaction.
