# Global Tourism

## The scale of travel and tourism

### Factors affecting tourism demand and how they are managed

* **Economic** (money-related):

  * Disposable income: money left after tax and necessities → more income = more travel

  * Government investment: marketing and infrastructure improves tourism

* **Social** (people & society):

  * Demographics: age, population, income levels

  * Health awareness: demand for outdoor/health tourism

  * Social consciousness: tourists prefer ethical/sustainable options

  * Fashion and trends: places become popular due to trends

* **Technological**:

  * Improved transport (faster, cheaper travel)

  * Enhanced information technology and social media increase awareness and ease of booking

* **Political**:

  * War, terrorism → reduce tourism

  * Stability and safety → increase tourism

  * Travel restrictions/visa rules affect demand

* **Environmental**:

  * Climate, extreme weather

  * Natural disasters can reduce demand

* **Health**

  * Diseases/pandemics reduce travel demand

### Reasons for growth of sustainable tourism

* **Changing consumer attitudes**:

  * Tourists are becoming more aware of the negative impacts of tourism.

  * Many now prefer environmentally friendly holidays and want to protect local cultures and environments.

* **Media** (e.g., social media influence):

  * Media spreads awareness about sustainable tourism and environmental issues.

  * Social media allows travelers to share experiences and promote eco-friendly destinations and practices.

* **Availability and promotion of sustainable tourism products/services**:

  * Increased demand has led to more eco-friendly hotels, tours, and activities.

  * Promotion and marketing make these options more visible and attractive to tourists.

* **Government policies**:

  * Governments support sustainable tourism because it creates jobs and income.

  * They introduce policies and initiatives to protect the environment and local communities (e.g., conservation laws and eco-tourism projects).

### Shocks Which Have Disrupted the Growth of Tourism

* Economic crises/recession: Reduced disposable income lowers demand for travel.

* 9/11 attacks: Led to a major decline in air travel due to safety fears.

* Malaysia Airlines Flight 17 crash and AirAsia Flight QZ8501 crash: Increased fear of flying, reducing tourism demand.
