### Management activities of these organisations

* Encouraging sustainable tourism: Promoting eco-friendly practices to protect the environment and local communities

  * E.g., eco-tourism initiatives in Costa Rica

* Managing tourism demand: Controlling visitor numbers to prevent overcrowding and over-tourism

  * E.g., visitor limits at Machu Picchu

* Ensuring resilience and managing risks: Responding to crises and ensuring tourist safety

  * E.g., safety measures during COVID-19

* Planning and development: Long-term planning to ensure tourism grows sustainably

  * E.g., tourism development plans in Dubai

### Services provided by these organisations

* Promoting the destination: Marketing through campaigns and media.

  * E.g., VisitBritain campaigns

* Maintaining standards and quality: Setting regulations (e.g., hotel ratings) to ensure good service and encourage repeat visits.

  * E.g., hotel classification systems in France

* Providing information: Offering maps, websites, and guidance to tourists and providers

  * E.g., tourist information services in Singapore

* Supporting local businesses: Helping small tourism businesses grow through funding and training.

  * E.g., local tourism support programs in India

* Improving infrastructure: Developing transport and facilities to enhance the tourist experience.

  * E.g., metro and airport development in Dubai
