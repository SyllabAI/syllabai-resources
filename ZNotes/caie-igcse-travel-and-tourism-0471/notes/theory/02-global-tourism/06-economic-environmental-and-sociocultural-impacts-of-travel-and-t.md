## Economic, environmental and sociocultural impacts of travel and tourism

### Economic Impacts

#|
||

Positive

|

Negative

||
||

Contribution to GDP, tax revenue and wealth creation: increases national income and improves living standards

|

Inflation: prices of goods and services rise due to tourist demand

||
||

Multiplier effect: tourists' spending circulates, creating more income

|

Leakage: money leaves the country through imports and foreign businesses (profit repatriation)

||
||

Employment: creates direct and indirect jobs

|

Seasonal employment: jobs are unstable and only available at certain times

||
||

Infrastructure development: improves transport, communication and services

|

Opportunity cost: money spent on tourism instead of sectors like healthcare or education

||
||

‎

|

Overdependence: the economy relies too much on tourism, which is risky during crises
