## Managing destinations sustainably

#|
||

Aim

|

Actions

||
||

Protect the natural and built environment

|

* Preservation: Limit tourism to keep areas unchanged

* Conservation: Manage tourism to avoid damage

* Regeneration: Restore damaged areas

* Maintain biodiversity

* Manage visitor numbers and behavior

* Use resources sustainably

* Limit pollution

||
||

Combat climate change

|

* Empowering communities and visitors through environmental education

* Championing a circular eco**nomy** with active recycling and upcycling initiatives

* Minimizing our footprint by actively preventing waste and overconsumption

* Encourage low-carbon transport

* Promote eco-friendly accommodation

||
||

Empower local and indigenous communities

|

* Protect culture and traditions

* Fostering civic ownership by putting communities in charge of their own spaces

* Provide training in sustainable practices

* Ensure locals benefit economically (jobs and income)

||
||

Support social enterprise development

|

* Support local economy and wealth creation

* Develop sustainable infrastructure

* Promote community tourism

* Create jobs

* Support small/local businesses

* Community tourism (tourism managed and run by local people for their benefit)
