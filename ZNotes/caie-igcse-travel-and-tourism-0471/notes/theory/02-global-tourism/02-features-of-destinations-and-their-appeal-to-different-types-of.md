## Features of destinations and their appeal to different types of tourists

#|
||

Features

|

Explanation

|

Example

||
||

Location

|

Where the destination is affects accessibility, climate and popularity

|

Dubai is easily accessible and a global tourist hub

||
||

Weather & seasonality

|

Climate at different times of the year influences when tourists visit

|

Switzerland attracts skiers in winter and hikers in summer.

||
||

Topography

|

Physical features like mountains, beaches, deserts

|

Himalayas attract adventure tourists

||
||

Accessibility

|

Ease of reaching the destination (transport links, cost, visa rules)

|

Singapore is well-connected with major international flights

||
||

Gateways & hubs

|

Gateways: entry points (airports)

Hubs: transport interchanges (train stations, bus terminals)

|

Heathrow Airport (gateway) and Grand Central Terminal (hub)

||
||

Infrastructure

|

Basic facilities like roads, airports, public transport

|

Japan has advanced transport systems (bullet trains)

||
||

Attractions (natural & built)

|

Places tourists visit, such as landmarks, parks, theme parks

|

Taj Mahal attracts cultural tourists

||
||

Culture & traditions

|

Local way of life, language, festivals, customs

|

Diwali attracts cultural tourism in India

||
||

Expected behaviour

|

Rules tourists must follow (dress codes, customs, laws)

|

Visitors in Saudi Arabia follow modest dress codes

||
||

Tourism organisations/providers

|

Availability of services like hotels, tour guides, agencies

|

Thomas Cook provides travel services and packages

||
||

Sustainable provisions

|

Eco-friendly practices and facilities to reduce impact

|

Costa Rica promotes eco-tourism and conservation
