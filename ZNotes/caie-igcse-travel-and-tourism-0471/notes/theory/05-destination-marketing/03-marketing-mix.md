## Marketing mix

* ### The marketing mix is the combination of the four Ps, product, price, place, and promotion, used by an organization to market products and services effectively to its target market.

* ### Product

  * ### The product/service mix is the combination of travel and tourism products and services offered by an organization to meet the needs of different target markets

  * Examples of Tourism Products and Services

    * Accommodation

    * Transport

    * Attractions

    * Guided tours

    * Food and beverage services

    * Entertainment

  * ### Sustainable Tourism products and services help protect the environment, support local communities, improve the destination’s image, and attract responsible tourists

    * Eco-friendly accommodation

    * Recycling programmes

    * Renewable energy use

    * Promoting local products and culture

    * Low-carbon transport options

  * ### Why Organizations Change Their Product / Service Mix

    * Product innovation, by introducing new products/services keeps existing customers interested and attracts new customers

    * Product diversification, offering a wider range of products helps extend the product life cycle and reduce risk

    * Adaptation to market trends, enables keeping the mix dynamic, helping organizations remain relevant as customer needs and travel trends change

    * To gain recognition as an innovator in the market

    * To expand operations into new destinations or markets

    * To defend market share when challenged by competitors

* ### Promotion

  * ### Promotion refers to the activities used to create awareness, increase visibility, stimulate demand, and increase sales of a product/service

  * ### Objectives of Promotion

    * Persuade customers to purchase products/services

    * Create awareness of products, services, or destinations

    * Encourage repeat visits and brand loyalty

    * Inform customers about offers, features, and new products

    * Improve brand image and reputation

  * ### AIDA principle

    * Attention: using bold headlines, colors, or images to attract attention

    * Interest: using videos, pictures, detailed information, or virtual tours

    * Desire: using emotional language and USP (unique selling point) to create desire

    * Action: encouraging customers to take action through contact details or booking links

  * ### Stages of a Promotional Campaign

    1. Choosing the target audience

    2. Establishing the message

    3. Setting the timing

    4. Deciding the advertising budget

    5. Measuring results and effectiveness

  * ### Above-the-Line and Below-the-Line Promotion

    * Above-the-line promotion: promotional activities that are clearly visible to customers (e.g., TV, radio, newspapers)

    * Below-the-line promotion: more subtle promotional activities aimed at targeted groups (e.g., direct marketing, sponsorships, sales promotions)

  * ### Factors Affecting the Choice of Promotion Method

    * Costs

    * Target market segment

    * Timing

    * Brand image

    * AIDA principle

    * Size and location of target market

    * Available budget

    * Type of product/service being promoted

* ### Price

  * ### Price is the amount of money a customer has to pay in order to purchase a product or service

  * ### Importance of pricing

    * Affects customer demand

    * Influences profitability

    * Helps create brand image

    * Affects competitiveness in the market

    * Influences customer perceptions of quality

  * ### Pricing strategy/policies

  * ### Factors Affecting Physical Location Choice

    * Cost: affordability of land, rent, or operating expenses

    * Availability of suitable premises: whether appropriate buildings or facilities are available

    * Character of the area: image, safety, and attractiveness of the location

    * Local and transient population: availability of local customers and tourists

    * Adjacent facilities: nearby attractions, restaurants, shopping areas, or hotels

    * Accessibility and transport links: availability of roads, airports, railways, and public transport

    * Availability of staff/human resources: access to skilled and suitable employees
