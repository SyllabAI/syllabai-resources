## Market research and analysis

* ### Market research

  * ### Market research is the collection and analysis of information about consumers and the market to help organizations make marketing decisions.

  * ### Stages of Market Research

    1. Set objectives

    2. Design the research

    3. Collect data

    4. Analyze data

    5. Report results

  * ### Importance of Market Research

    * Identifies customer needs and wants

    * Helps create suitable product portfolios

    * Identifies and analyzes competitors

    * Helps organizations understand their market position

    * Supports marketing plans and decision-making

    * Helps identify suitable budgets

    * Reduces business risks and uncertainty

* ### Types of market research

  * ### Primary Market Research

    * Primary market research involves collecting new data directly from customers or the market

  * ### Types of Primary Market Research

    * Random sample: participants are chosen randomly without predetermined conditions

    * Non-random sample: participants are selected based on specific conditions; cheaper but may be biased

  * ### Qualitative and Quantitative Research

    * Qualitative research: Research based on opinions, feelings, and experiences.

    * **Examples**

      * Interviews

      * Focus groups

      * Open-ended questions

    * Quantitative research: Research based on numbers, statistics, and measurable data.

    * Examples

      * Surveys with numerical results

      * Charts and graphs

      * Questionnaires with closed questions

* ### Reasons for conducting market research

  * Understanding position in the market

    * Helps organizations identify how well they are performing compared to competitors and understand their strengths and weaknesses in the market

  * Identifying customer needs and wants

    * Helps organizations understand customer preferences, expectations, and buying behavior so suitable products and services can be developed

  * Competitor analysis

    * Helps organizations study competitor products, prices, promotional activities, and market strategies to remain competitive

  * Creating marketing plans

    * Helps organizations develop effective marketing strategies, choose suitable target markets, and decide appropriate promotional methods

  * Reducing business risks

    * Market research helps organizations make informed decisions and reduces the risk of product failure or financial loss

  * Forecasting future trends

    * Helps organizations identify changes in tourism trends and customer demand so they can adapt products and services accordingly
