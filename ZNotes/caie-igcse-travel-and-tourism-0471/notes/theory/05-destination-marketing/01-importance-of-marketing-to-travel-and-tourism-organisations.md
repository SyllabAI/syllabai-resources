# Destination marketing

## Importance of marketing to travel and tourism organisations

#|
||

Aspect

|

Explanation

||
||

Increased sales/usage/profitability/market share/customer base

|

Creating a product portfolio to meet the needs of the target market and introducing new tourism products can attract more customers, increasing sales, market share, and customer base.

||
||

Competitive advantage

|

Promotional techniques can create a positive image that makes the destination appear better than competitors and helps establish a USP (unique selling point).

||
||

Positive image and reputation for sustainable practices

|

Publicity, positive customer reviews, and word of mouth can increase awareness and improve the reputation of the destination or organization.

||
||

Customer satisfaction/brand loyalty/repeat business

|

Products developed through accurate market research and regular updates through direct marketing help meet customer needs and encourage repeat business.
