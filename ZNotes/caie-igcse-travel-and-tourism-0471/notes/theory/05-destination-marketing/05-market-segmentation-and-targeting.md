## Market segmentation and targeting

* ### Market segmentation is the process of dividing a market into smaller groups of customers with similar needs, characteristics, or buying behavior.

* ### Importance of Market Segmentation

  * Helps organizations identify target markets

  * Improves customer satisfaction

  * Supports effective marketing strategies

  * Helps organizations compete more effectively

  * Increases sales and profitability

  * Helps develop suitable products and services for different customer groups

  * ### The relationship with market segments

    * ### Type of customers: different customers have different expectations and needs.

      Examples

      * Business tourists may require Wi-Fi and conference facilities

      * Families may prefer child-friendly attractions and larger rooms

      * Ecotourists may prefer sustainable accommodation and nature-based activities

    * ### Quality/economy/value for money: Products and services are developed according to customer budgets and expectations.

      Examples

      * Luxury resorts for high-income tourists

      * Budget hotels for economy travelers

      * All-inclusive packages offering value for money

  * ### Accessibility

    * Tourism products and services should be accessible to different customer groups.

    Examples

    * Wheelchair access

    * Braille signage

    * Multilingual information

    * Accessible transport facilities

  * ### Sustainability

    * Organizations may develop sustainable products and services to attract responsible tourists.

    Examples

    * Eco-friendly accommodation

    * Recycling programmes

    * Use of renewable energy

    * Promoting local culture and products
