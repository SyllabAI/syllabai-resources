## Sustainable Travel and Tourism

* ### Sustainability: Our ability to sustain or conserve the environment and people’s way of life into the future.

* ### Sustainable travel and tourism: Visiting destinations in ways that help protect and conserve the environment while supporting the local people and their way of life.

Environmental sustainability: Tourism should protect natural environments.

|

* Reduce, reuse, and recycle

* Conserve wildlife and ecosystems

* Use resources carefully

|

On the Galápagos Islands, there are strict rules on where tourists can go; licensed guides are required to protect wildlife

||
||

Economic sustainability: Tourism should benefit local economies long-term.

|

* Provide jobs for local people

* Support local businesses

* Reduce leakage of money

|

In Kerala, Tourists buy local products and use local guides, so money stays in the community

||
||

Socio-cultural sustainability: Tourism should respect local people and culture.

|

* Protect traditions and heritage

* Avoid overcrowding and cultural damage

* Improve quality of life for locals

|

In Bali, there are guidelines for tourists about temple behavior and dress codes.

||
|#

* ### Ecotourism: Visiting a destination to enjoy and appreciate the natural environment while minimizing damage and supporting conservation efforts.

* ### Responsible tourism: Visiting destinations in ways that show respect for the environment and local people.
