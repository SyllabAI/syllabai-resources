##  Types Of Destination

#|
||

Type of destination

|

Description

|

Example

||
||

Beach and coastal

|

Destinations located along coastlines with beaches

|

* Bondi Beach (Australia)

* Goa (India)

||
||

Rural (countryside)

|

Destinations located outside towns and cities, often with natural landscapes

|

* Swiss Alps

* Lake District (UK)

||
||

Resorts

|

Destinations designed for leisure and relaxation, including purpose-built resorts (built for tourism), integrated resorts (all facilities in one place), and eco-resorts (environmentally sustainable).

|

* Disneyland Resort (USA)

* Club Med resorts

||
||

Urban (towns and cities)

|

Destinations located in cities or towns offering cultural and entertainment attractions

|

* New York City

* Tokyo

||
||

Island destinations

|

Destinations surrounded by water, attracting tourists for their natural beauty

|

* Bali (Indonesia)

* Seychelles

||
|#
