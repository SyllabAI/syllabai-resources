## Main Reasons Why People Travel

* ### Leisure: Travel for the purpose of recreation, relaxation, and entertainment.

  * The most common types of tourism are sun, sea, and sand.

* ### Business: Travel for the purpose of work, MICE-related activities

  * Meeting

    * Business tourists meet for the purpose of exchanging information, making sales, or making future plans.

  * Incentive trips

    * A trip given to an employee as a reward for good work.

  * Conference

    * Business tourists meet for the purpose of learning about and discussing a topic of common interest along with guest speakers.

  * Exhibition

    * Business tourists attend to network and stay updated with latest developments in their industry

* ### Visiting friends and relatives (VFR): Travel for the purpose of meeting other people.

  For example,

  * Birthdays/Weddings/Funerals

  * Returning to the community where family originated

  * To attend religious and cultural holidays

  * People working in foreign countries returning home

* ### Special interest tourism: Travel motivated by a specific reason

  For example,

  * Educational

  * Health

  * Sports

  * Pilgrimage/Religious
