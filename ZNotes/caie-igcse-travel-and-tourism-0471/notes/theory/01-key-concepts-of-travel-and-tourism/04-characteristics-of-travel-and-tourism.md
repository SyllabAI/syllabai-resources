## Characteristics Of Travel and Tourism

* ### Product

  * The things tourists buy as part of their trip; tourism products are usually a combination of different elements that create the overall experience.

    * Holiday packages

    * Attraction tickets (theme parks, museums)

    * Souvenirs

    * Cruise packages

* ### Service

  * The actions or facilities provided to tourists: services are not physical; they are experiences or help given to tourists.

    * Hotel accommodation

    * Transport (flights, taxis, trains)

    * Tour guides

    * Customer service at airports/restaurants

* ### Intangible

  * Travel and tourism services cannot be seen or touched before purchase, making it difficult for organizations to represent or demonstrate them to tourists in advance.

    * E.g., a tourist cannot experience a flight journey before purchasing it.

  * Ways to overcome intangibility:

    * Using brochures and leaflets to provide detailed information and visuals

    * Using websites to showcase services through images, videos, and customer reviews

* ### Perishable

  * Travel and tourism services cannot be stored for later use; if they are not sold at the time they are available, they are lost.

    * E.g., a ferry operates daily with 200 tickets to a destination, but only 150 are sold; the remaining 50 unsold tickets are lost and cannot be used later

* ### Seasonal

  * Travel and tourism services are seasonal, meaning demand for them varies at different times of the year.

    * E.g., theme parks are usually busy on weekends but less crowded during weekdays.

  * Factors causing seasonality:

    * Weather and climate

    * Religious events and festivals

    * School holidays and national holidays

  * Ways to overcome seasonality

    * Offering price reductions and discounts during off-peak periods

    * Targeting different markets (e.g. business travellers, elderly tourists)

    * Organising events and festivals to attract visitors

* ### Dynamic

  * Travel and tourism products are constantly changing and evolving as the industry develops to meet changing customer needs and preferences.

    * E.g., a current trend in travel and tourism is sustainability, with more tourists choosing eco-friendly options.

  * Why it is important for travel and tourism organizations to be dynamic

    * Meeting evolving traveler expectations

    * Risk management and resilience

    * Benefit from technological innovation

* ### Increasingly sustainable

  * Travel and tourism products are becoming more sustainable, with greater awareness of the impacts of tourism on the environment, economy, and local communities.

    * E.g., visitor attractions offer educational visits for students, benefiting both the learners and the attraction.

* ### Resilient

  * Travel and tourism organizations are resilient, meaning they can survive and recover after difficult situations or challenges.

    * E.g., the tourism industry recovered after the COVID-19 pandemic.
