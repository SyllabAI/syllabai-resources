## Types Of Tourists

* Tourists can be classified according to the following

  * Reasons for travel

  * Preferences

  * Needs

#|
||

Type of tourist

|

Description

|

Characteristics

||
||

Leisure tourists

|

Travel for enjoyment, relaxation, or recreation

|

* Price-sensitive

* Travel during the holidays

* Contribute to seasonal demand

||
||

Business tourists

|

Travel for work purposes such as meetings and conferences

|

* Time-sensitive

* Fixed schedule

* Require Wi-Fi and business facilities

* Should be located near amenities

||
||

Special interest tourists

|

Travel for a specific purpose or interest

|

* Focused on one activity (e.g. sports, culture, nature)

* Willing to spend more on their interest

||
||

Independent tourists

|

Plan and book their own travel

|

* Prefer flexibility and control over itinerary

* Do not use package holidays

||
||

Responsible tourists

|

Travel while respecting environment and local communities

|

* Environmentally and socially aware

* Minimise negative impacts

||
||

Ecotourists

|

Travel to natural areas with minimal environmental impact

|

* Respect nature and culture

* Support conservation

* Environmentally aware

||
||

Families

|

Groups of different age groups travelling together

|

* Varied needs

* Require family-friendly facilities

* May travel for leisure or VFR

||
||

Couples

|

Two people travelling together

|

* Seek relaxation/romantic experiences

* Prefer comfort and privacy

||
||

Groups

|

People travelling together on organised trips

|

* Follow fixed itineraries

* Often use packages

* May get group discounts

||
||

Individuals

|

Travel alone

|

* Prefer independence

* Flexible plans

* May seek solitude

||
||

Tourists with specific needs

|

Tourists with particular requirements

|

* Need special facilities (e.g. wheelchair access, dietary needs, medical support)

||
