# Key Concepts Of Travel and Tourism

## Main Types of Tourism

* ### Domestic tourism: Residents travel within their own country for the purpose of business, leisure, or other purposes.

  * For example, a South Korean family living in Busan travels to Seoul to attend a wedding.

* ### International tourism: people traveling to and staying in countries outside their usual environment for leisure, business, or other purposes for less than one year.

  How to prepare for an international trip:

  * Currency exchange

  * Transport

  * Passport documents should be up-to-date

  * Visas must be obtained

  * Preparing for experiencing a new language and culture

* ### Day trip: a form of tourism lasting less than 24 hours.

  * For example, a family returns home the same day as their visit to the zoo.

* ### Short break: a form of tourism lasting 4 nights or less: suitable for those who cannot stay away for long periods due to commitments such as work, school, or health.

  * For example, a family takes a short break by staying in a resort for 3 nights.

  Short breaks have increased in popularity because:

  * More and easier access to different transport methods

  * Increased wealth in some countries

  * Increasing demand for tourism
