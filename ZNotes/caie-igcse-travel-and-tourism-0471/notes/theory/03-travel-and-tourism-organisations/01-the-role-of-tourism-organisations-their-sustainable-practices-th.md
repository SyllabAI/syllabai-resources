# Travel and tourism organisations

## The role of tourism organizations, their sustainable practices, the products and services they provide and their appeal

1. ### Travel agents: organizations that sell travel and tourism products and services on behalf of other providers, along with additional (ancillary) services

* * ### Types of travel agents

  * ### Independent Tours / Packages: Travel where tourists book transport, accommodation, and activities separately, allowing flexibility and choice

  * ### Ancillary Services Offered: Extra services provided in addition to main bookings

    * Travel insurance

    * Currency exchange

    * Visa assistance

    * Car hire / bike hire

    * Guided tours

  * ### Roles of travel agents

    * Sell holidays and tourism products

    * Provide information and advice

    * Book tickets and accommodation

    * Arrange travel documents and services

    * Collect deposits and final payments

    * Use computer reservation systems (CRS)

  3. ### Accommodation providers: Organizations that provide places for tourists to stay during their visit to a destination

  * ### Occupancy rate: The percentage of rooms or spaces occupied in accommodation (75 out of 100 rooms occupied = 75% occupancy rate)

  * Accommodation can be categorized by:

    * Luxury

    * Budget

    * Eco-friendly
