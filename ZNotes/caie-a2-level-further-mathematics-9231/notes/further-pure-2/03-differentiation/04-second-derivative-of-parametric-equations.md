## Second Derivative of Parametric Equations

* Find first derivative simply by differentiating each parameter and dividing:

  $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\frac{dy}{dt}}{\frac{dx}{dt}}$
* Find second derivative by differentiating $\frac{dy}{dx}$ and dividing by $\frac{dx}{dt}$

  $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{\frac{d}{dt}\frac{dy}{dx}}{\frac{dx}{dt}}$
