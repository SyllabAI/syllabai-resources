## 2nd Order DE: Homogeneous Case

* These type of differential equations will be of the form:

$a \frac{d^2y}{dy^2} + b \frac{dy}{dx} + cy = 0$ 

* First step to solving them is to find the complementary function by forming an auxiliary equation:

$a \lambda ^ 2 + b \lambda + c = 0$ 

* The complementary function formed depends on the discriminant of the auxiliary function
