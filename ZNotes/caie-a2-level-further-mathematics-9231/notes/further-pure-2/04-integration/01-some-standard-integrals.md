# Integration

## Some Standard Integrals

* $\int \frac{1}{a^2+x^2}dx $ can be solved with $x=atan \theta$ to give $\frac {1}{a} tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}+c$
* $\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}dx$ can be solved with $x = asin \theta$ to give $sin^{-1}(\frac{x}{a}) + c$
* $\int \frac{1}{a^2-x^2}dx$ can be solved with $x = tanh \theta$ to give $\frac{1}{a} tanh^{-1} \frac{x}{a} + c$
