## Final Velocity

 ![](../../../assets/61b27d53532d-636c10b7c80fc62ad61ad987a62cafbf1e46dd8f4796badf.43)

* This defines the velocity of the projectile after seconds of travel.
* Final velocity, according to the SUVAT equations, is also dependent on the initial velocity and any acceleration acting on the projectile:

  $ v = u + at$
* Above is a scalar equation which can be written in each component as:

  $ v_x = u cos\theta$  $v_y = u sin\theta - gt$

  Note that only $v_y$ has a $-gt$ term because gravity only affects the $y$-component, which is negative due to gravity going downwards.
* The final speed of the projectile can be achieved by taking the magnitude:

  $v = \sqrt{v^2_x + v^2_y} = \sqrt{(u cos\theta)^2 + (u sin\theta - gt)^2}$
