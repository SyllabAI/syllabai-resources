## Acceleration with respect to displacement

* Not all the time we can solve differential equations with respect to $t$ 
* If the question gives an acceleration function in terms of $x$ or $v$, then we need to use an alternative form of acceleration:

  $a = v\frac{dv}{dx}$

Derivation:

$a = \frac{dv}{dt}$

By the chain rule:

$\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{dv}{dx} \times \frac{dx}{dt}$ 

But $v = \frac{dx}{dt}$

$\therefore a = v \frac{dv}{dx}$
