## Finding Centre of Mass

* We can find the centre of mass by taking moments

* If each mass $m_i$ has position vector, $r_i$ then the position vector of the centre of mass $\overline{r}$is: $\overline{r} = \frac{Σ m_ir_i}{Σ m_i}$

$$

(m_1 + m_2 + \dots)
\begin{pmatrix}
\overline{x} \\
\overline{y}
\end{pmatrix}

=

m_1
\begin{pmatrix}
x_1 \\
y_1
\end{pmatrix}
+
m_2
\begin{pmatrix}
x_2 \\
y_2
\end{pmatrix}

+ \dots

$$

* We can solve for $\overline{x}$ & $\overline {y}$ separately instead of using vectors:

  $(m_1 + m_2 + …) \overline{x} = m_1x_1 + m_2x_2 + …$

  And

  $(m_1 + m_2 + …) \overline{y} = m_1y_1 + m_2y_2 + …$
