## Comparing the $\mathbf{\chi}^{\mathbf{2}}$ Value 

  - Once you have calculated the $\chi^{2}$ value of the data given,
    you must then compare it to the critical values of the $\chi^{2}$
    distribution

  - To test 5 classes at a 5% significance level, find the critical
    value of the $\chi^{2}$ distribution at 95% with 4 degrees of
    freedom

  - If the distribution fits, the calculate value should be less than
    the critical value, accepting $H_{0}$
