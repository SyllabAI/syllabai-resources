## Difference in means: Normal distribution

* Assume:
  * Underlying distributions are normal
  * Large sample sizes
  * Populations are independent
  * Population variance of the two populations is the same
* The test statistic is:

$$
Z = \frac{\left( \overline{X} - \overline{Y} \right) - \left( \mu_{x} - \mu_{y} \right)}{\sqrt{\left( \frac{\sigma_{x}^{2}}{n_{x}} + \frac{\sigma_{y}^{2}}{n_{y}} \right)}}
$$

Critical value:

| **Confidence level** | 90% | 95% | 98% | 99% |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| $\mathbf{z}$**-value** | 1.645 | 1.960 | 2.326 | 2.576 |
