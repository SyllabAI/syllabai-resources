## Difference in means: paired sample t-tests

  - Assume:
    
      - Differences are normally distributed
    
      - Populations are independent
    
      - Population variance of the two populations is the same (but may
        be unknown)

  - The test statistic is
    $\frac{\overline{d} - \mu_{d}}{s_{d}/\sqrt{n}}$

  - Critical value is $t_{n - 1}$
