## **Costs**

### **Key Vocabulary**

* **Costs**: expenses that must while running the business
* **Fixed cost**: the costs that remain constant
* **Variable cost**: the costs that vary with the level of output
* **Total cost**: the total of the fixed cost with the variable costs
* **Total revenue**: money generated from the sale of outputs


1. **Fixed costs**

   a. Fixed costs do not vary with the level of output. They don’t increase when output increases neither do they decrease when the output decreases. However, they are to be met even if no output is produced.

   b. They form a straight horizontal line in a graph. Examples: rent, wages, etc.
2. **Variable costs**

   a. Variable costs are the costs that vary with the level of output. When output increases they increase and the vice versa. Examples: raw materials, packaging.

   b. $\text{Total \ variable \ costs} = \text{Variable \ cost \ per \ unit} \ * \ \text{number \ of \ units}$
3. **Total costs**

   a. This is the addition of the Variable and fixed costs.

   b. $\text{Total \ cost} \ = \ \text{Fixed \ cost} \ + \ \text{Total \ Variable \ costs}$
4. **Average costs**

   a. This is the average cost of producing one single unit.

   b. $\text{Average \ costs} \ = \ \frac{\text{Total \ cost}}{ \text{total \ quantity \ produced}}$
5. **Total revenue**

   a. The amount of money the firm receives after selling its outputs

   b. $\text{Total \ revenues} \ = \ \text{Price} \ * \ \text{Quantity}$
