## To investigate the effects of different concentration of solution on the number of cells being plasmolysed

  - Cut strips of sample using tweezers/scalpel

  - Standardize sizes of strips (measure length using callipers/ruler
    mass using electronic balance)

  - Same number of strips in all different concentrations

  - Leave for same period of time

  - Mount on slide in sucrose solution (with coverslip); (see slide
    preparation technique)

  - Count large number of cells, count more than once and in more than
    one field view, take mean

  - Record number of plasmolysed and non-plasmolysed cells
