## To investigate the effects of different concentrations of enzymes on the activity of enzymes

* Make at least 5 new enzyme solutions (of 5 different concentrations)
  by diluting (serial or simple) the stock enzyme solution
* Keep the volume of all the solutions the same
* Measure volume using a graduated pipette/burette/measuring cylinder
* Repeat the experiment at least three times with each solution and take mean
* Keep the pH value most suitable for the enzyme to work (add acid/alkali/buffer to keep pH constant)
* Incubate enzyme at a suitable working temp of the enzyme (incubator, thermostatically controlled water bath)
* Use the same size/shape of sample/strips on which the enzyme is acting
* Continue experiments for the same period of time
* Immerse the sample/strip and start the stopwatch simultaneously
