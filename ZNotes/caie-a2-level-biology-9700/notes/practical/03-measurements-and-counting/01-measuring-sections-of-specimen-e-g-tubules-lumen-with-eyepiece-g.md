# Measurements and counting

## Measuring sections of specimen (e.g. tubules/lumen) with eyepiece graticule

  - Set magnification at x40 – x400

  - Use stage micrometre and eyepiece graticule

  - Calculate the number of eyepiece graticule units per stage
    micrometre unit/calibrate the eyepiece graticule with the stage
    micrometre

  - Convert from eyepiece units/mm to micrometre

  - Use the formula:

$$\frac{\text{number\ of\ stage\ micrometer\ divisions}}{\text{number\ of\ eyepiece\ graticule\ units}} \times value\ of\ 1\ micrometer\ division$$
