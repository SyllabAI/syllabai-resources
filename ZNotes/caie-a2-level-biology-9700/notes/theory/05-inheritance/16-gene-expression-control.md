## Gene Expression Control

* **Gene expression**: When a gene is transcribed to mRNA and then translated into a protein

* Only a few genes present in the nucleus of a cell are actually expressed at a time

  * For example, the gene for melanin production won’t be present in heart muscle cells

* **Structural Gene**: A gene that codes for a protein that has a function within the cell

  * e.g. enzymes, rRNA, proteins that form part of the cellular structure etc

* **Regulatory Gene**: A gene that codes for a protein that helps control the gene expression

  * e.g. transcription factors

* **Inducible Enzyme:** An enzyme that is synthesised only when substrate is present

* **Repressible Enzyme:** An enzyme that is normally produced, and whose synthesis is prevented by an effector
