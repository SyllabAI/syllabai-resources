## Epistasis

* A type of **gene interaction** where one gene affects the expression of another gene

* Example: Feather color in chickens

  * **G**: colored feathers and **g**: white feathers (absence of pigment)

  * **F**: prevents production of pigment even if G is present, and **f**: does not prevent production of pigment
