## Haemophilia

* A **sex-linked**, **recessive** disorder where blood does not clot normally, leading to excessive bleeding even from a small injury

* Caused by a mutation in the **F8** gene, which codes for a protein called **factor** **VIII**

* Factor VIII is synthesised in the liver and secreted into the blood plasma, playing an important role in blood clotting

* The F8 gene is found on a non-homologous region of the X-chromosome, which means males are more likely to be affected

* Abnormal alleles result in abnormal factor VIII, less of it, or none at all, preventing proper blood clot formation
