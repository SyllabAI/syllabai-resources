## Dihybrid Inheritance

- The inheritance of 2 genes, each with 2 alleles (so 4 alleles total)

- The two genes control different traits and are located on separate chromosomes, so they are not linked

- They assort independently of each other during gamete formation

### Example: Pea plants seed shape and seed color

* Gene 1, seed shape: **R** = Round, dominant and **r** = Wrinkled, recessive

* Gene 2, seed color: **Y** = Yellow, dominant and **y** = green, recessive

1) Cross between **RrYy** (round, yellow) and **Rryy** (round, green)

* RrYy gametes: **RY**, **Ry**, **rY**, and **ry**

* Rryy gametes: **Ry** and **ry**
