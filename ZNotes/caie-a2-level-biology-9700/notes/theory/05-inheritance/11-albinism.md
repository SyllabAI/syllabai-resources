## Albinism

- The dark pigment **melanin** is totally or partially missing from eyes, skin and hair

- Affected individuals have pale skin and hair, and light blue or pink eyes

- They tend to avoid bright light, they have poor vision and increased susceptibility to sunburn, and their eyes move in rapid, jerky movements

- Caused by a faulty **recessive** allele

- The ***TYR*** gene is found on the long arm of chromosome **11**, and codes for the enzyme **tyrosinase**

- Tyrosinase is involved in the metabolic pathway that produces melanin

![](../../../assets/44a3c868d758-allimages83e081271c39a59819c7e0b9.png)

* A mutated recessive allele of the gene for tyrosinase results in absent/inactive tyrosinase, preventing melanin synthesis and causing albinism
* Tyrosinases are found in plants too, their action seen in the blackening of a potato slice exposed to air
