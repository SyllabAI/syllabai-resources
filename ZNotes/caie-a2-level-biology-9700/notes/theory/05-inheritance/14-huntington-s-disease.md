## Huntington’s Disease

* A fatal neurological disorder which causes involuntary movements, progressive mental deterioration, loss of brain cells, mood changes etc.

* Usual onset between 28-65 years old, symptoms showing at 30-40 years old

* The ***HTT*** gene, found on **chromosome 4**, codes for a protein called **huntingtin**, which is important for the development of neurones (particularly in the brain)

* The nucleotide sequence of this gene contains a large number of repeated **CAG** triplets, called a stutter

  * If the number of repeats is over **40**, Huntington’s is developed

  * If it is between **36-39**, the disease may or may not develop

* The faulty allele is **dominant**, and affected individuals may have already passed it down before symptoms occur and they become aware they have it
