## Stem Elongation and the *Le* gene

* Plant genes have a 2 letter abbreviation, also in italics

* The height of some plants is partly controlled by their genes

  * If the dominant allele ***Le*** is present, the plant can grow tall

  * If the plant is homozygous recessive (***le le***), it will always remain short

* The dominant allele regulates the synthesis of the last enzyme in a pathway that produces active **gibberellin**

* Gibberellin stimulates cell division and elongation in the stem, so the plant grows tall

* A **recessive** allele has one different nucleotide, so it codes for **alanine** instead of **threonine** at a position in the primary structure of the enzyme

  * This produces a non-functional enzyme, preventing gibberellin synthesis

* Applying active gibberellin to plants that would usually remain short can stimulate them to grow tall
