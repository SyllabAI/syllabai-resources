## Sickle Cell Anaemia

- Inherited **recessive** blood disorder where haemoglobin structure is affected

- Caused by **base substitution** in the ***HBB*** gene, which is found on the short arm of chromosome **11**

- This gene codes for the amino acid sequence in the $\beta$-globin polypeptide of haemoglobin

- The base sequence CTT is replaced by CAT (T is substituted by A)

- **Glutamic acid** (polar) is replaced by **valine** (non-polar) at the **6th** position in the polypeptide chain

- This makes the haemoglobin less soluble, changing its structure to form long fibers within the RBCs, which become sickle-shaped

- The distorted cells carry less oxygen and get stuck in capillaries, stopping unaffected cells from getting through and therefore blocking blood flow

- Sickled RBCs break down faster, resulting in a lack of red blood cells (anaemia)

- However, people with the sickle cell anaemia mutation have an advantage against malaria
