## Multiple Alleles

- This means a gene has more than 2 possible alleles
- Example: the ABO blood group system
- The **I** (immunoglobin) gene has 3 alleles: **Iᴬ** (blood type A), **Iᴮ** (blood type B), and **Iᴼ** or **i** (blood type O)
- This means there are 4 possible phenotypes: A, B, AB and O
- **Iᴬ** and **Iᴮ** alleles are codominant, so if they are inherited together, both alleles are expressed fully, resulting in blood type AB
- **Iᴼ** is recessive, meaning it is only expressed if two copies are inherited (**IᴼIᴼ**)

### Codominance:

- Codominance means both alleles are equally and fully expressed in the phenotype if they are both inherited
- For codominance, use a capital letter to represent the gene, and superscripts to represent different alleles
- e.g. In the ABO blood group system, use capital **I** for the gene
- The alleles are written as superscripts **ᴬ** and **ᴮ** (capital) and **ᴼ** (lowercase)
