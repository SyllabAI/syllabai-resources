## F1, F2 and Test Cross

### F1 generation:

* Crossing a homozygous dominant organism with a homozygous recessive

* All offspring are heterozygous

### F2 generation:

* Crossing two F1 (heterozygous) organisms
* Produces a 3:1 phenotypic ratio

### Test Cross:

* A method to determine the genotype of an organism showing dominant phenotype

* Cross the unknown dominant phenotype with a homozygous recessive

Example: Cross a tall plant with a homozygous dwarf plant (tt)

* If the tall plant is homozygous, all offspring will be tall:
