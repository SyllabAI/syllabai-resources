## Energy

* All energy originates from the Sun, then plants convert its light energy to chemical energy in carbohydrates and other substrates, which are respired to form ATP

* Energy is used for:

1. **Active Transport**: e.g. sodium-potassium pump, endocytosis and exocytosis

2. **Movement:** Can be intracellular movement e.g. transporting proteins from ribosomes to Golgi apparatus, or whole tissues and organs e.g. muscle contraction

3. **Anabolic Reactions:** These reactions synthesize larger molecules from smaller ones e.g. protein synthesis, DNA replication, and polymerization

4. **Bioluminescence:** e.g. in jellyfish

5. **Maintaining body temperature**
