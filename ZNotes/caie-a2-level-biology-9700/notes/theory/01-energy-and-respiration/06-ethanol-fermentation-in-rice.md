## Ethanol fermentation in rice

* Rice is grown in intentionally flooded fields to kill weeds through lack of oxygen

* The rice crops survive as they have adapted

### Adaptations:

1. Rice crops grow taller

* Leaves and flowers are held above water surface, allowing gas exchange through stomata

2. Presence of **aerenchyma**

* Aerenchyma is a tissue with many air spaces between cells, through which oxygen can diffuse into deprived areas

* It also allows buoyancy, helping parts of the plant float

3. Tolerance to high alcohol levels

* They produce alcohol dehydrogenase to break down ethanol, allowing them to respire anaerobically longer

4. Hydrophobic, corrugated surfaces in underwater leaves

* Air is trapped between ridges in this surface

* This reduces chances of waterlogging and allows gas exchange
