## Respiratory Substrates

* Glucose is the first substrate to be respired, in all 4 stages

* When glucose runs out, lipids are converted to acetyl CoA then enter Krebs Cycle to be respired

* Amino acids are used last because they have more important, specialized functions. They are either:

  * Converted to pyruvate and enter link reaction, or

  * Converted to acetyl CoA and enter Krebs Cycle

* Glucose can be broken down both aerobically and anaerobically for ATP synthesis, while lipids & amino acids can only be used aerobically

* The more C-H bonds a molecule has, the more energy it can provide

  * More hydrogen atoms mean more NADH & FADH₂ produced

  * More electrons form from hydrogen splitting, which are passed along the ETC

  * More protons pumped so more ATP formed

* Energy values are determined using a **calorimeter**

  * A known mass of substance is burned with oxygen

  * The rise in temperature of a known mass of water is measured

### Respiratory Quotients:

* RQ: The ratio of volume of CO₂ produced to O₂ used per unit time

* RQ = volume of CO₂ given out in unit time/volume of O₂ used in unit time
