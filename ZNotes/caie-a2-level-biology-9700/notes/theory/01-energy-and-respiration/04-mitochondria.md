## Mitochondria

* Site of aerobic respiration in eukaryotic cells

* 0.5 to 1 µm in diameter

* Has two membranes: Outer membrane is smooth, while the inner one is folded into cristae which increase surface area

* The electron transport chain (ETC) is found in the inner membrane/cristae

* Active cells can have longer cristae, for even larger surface area to synthesize more ATP

* The outer membrane is permeable, while the inner membrane is impermeable to H⁺ ions to maintain the proton gradient

* The proton gradient across the inner membrane is formed during oxidative phosphorylation

* Intermembrane space between the double membrane has low pH due to high concentration of protons

* The matrix has:

1. **Mitochondrial DNA** for transcription to make mRNA

2. **70S ribosomes** for synthesis of mitochondrial proteins like enzymes

3. **Many enzymes** for stages 2 and 3 of aerobic respiration
