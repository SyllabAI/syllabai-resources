## Bioinformatics

* **Bioinformatics:** The collection, processing and analysing of biological information & data using computer software

* **Databases** store biological information, such as gene sequences, complete genomes, amino acid sequences and protein structures

* **DNA sequencing** is determining the exact nucleotide sequence of a gene, allowing genomes of many species to be published

  * e.g. The **Human Genome Project** sequenced all 23 human chromosomes

### Database Examples:

1. **Ensembl:** data on genomes of eukaryotic organisms

2. **UniProt:** data on primary sequence and functions of proteins

3. **Protein Data Bank:** amino acid sequence and protein structure details

4. **BLAST search tool:** an algorithm to search databases to compare primary sequences of different proteins or nucleotide sequences of genes

### Uses of bioinformatics:

1. Identifying evolutionary relationships:

* Researchers can compare sequences/genomes to find similarities

* Close similarities and matching sequences indicate common ancestry

2. Comparing genes of humans and model organisms

* Human genes may be found in other organisms like *Drosophila*

* If a gene in the model organism is similar to the one in humans, the organism is used in research to investigate when and where the gene has an effect on humans

* *Caenorhabditis elegans* was the first multicellular organism to have its genome fully sequenced, and was used as a model organism because of its simplicity (which lies in the fact that it has less than 1000 cells, and is conveniently transparent)

3. Identifying genome sequences of pathogens

* The information on the pathogens can be used in vaccine and drug development
