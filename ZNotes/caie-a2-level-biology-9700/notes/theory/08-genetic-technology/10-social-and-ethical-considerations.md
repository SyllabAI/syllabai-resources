## Social and Ethical Considerations

### For Genetic Screening:

**Advantages:**

1. Preventative treatment and lifestyle advice can be given before symptoms even appear e.g. screening for breast cancer, or newborn screening

2. Allows people to make informed decisions, such as not having children so that the gene is not passed on

3. Reduces long-term treatment cost, as early treatment can prevent severe symptoms

4. Removes any anxiety a person may have if they are tested negative e.g. if the person’s family has a history of Huntington’s disease

**Disadvantages:**

1. Resources spent on expensive genetic technology for the rich could be better used on basic health care for the poor

2. Some disagree with pregnancy termination under any circumstance, regardless of what genetic screening reveals

3. The knowledge that a person has a disease-causing gene may cause emotional stress

* The stress may not even be valid as not all tests are 100% accurate, and sometimes Huntington’s for example does not even develop

4. About pre-implantation genetic diagnosis (PGD) in particular, people believe selecting specific embryos is unethical

* Parents have used PGD to select their desired baby gender (sex pre-selection), which is not only unethical, but it may unbalance the population’s sex ratio

### For Gene Therapy:

**Advantages:**

1. Increased life expectancy and quality of life

2. Germline gene therapy prevents disease-causing genes from being inherited by future generations

3. Reduces the need for long-term treatment and dependency on medication

**Disadvantages:**

1. People argue that changing the genes we inherit is just unethical

2. High cost and limited success rate

3. Some gene therapies have led to unfortunate side effects e.g. the immune system may respond to the vector

4. Genetically modified cells have a short lifespan, so gene therapies may be temporary

5. Only recessive conditions like LCA and hemophilia can be treated at the moment

6. It is difficult to direct vectors to specific cells where the allele is to be expressed, and even then, the allele is not always expressed if inserted into the genome
