## Viruses

* Not classified in the 3-domain system because they:

  * Are non-cellular (lack organelles and cytoplasm)

  * Do not have their own independent metabolism

  * Cannot replicate on their own

* Instead, they are classified based on genetic material

  * Whether they contain RNA or DNA

  * Whether the nucleic acid is single or double stranded

* In cellular organisms, DNA is double stranded and RNA is single stranded, but in viruses both can be either single or double
