# Classification, Biodiversity and Conservation

## Taxonomic Hierarchy

- Classification is arranging organisms into groups based on shared characteristics

- Taxonomy: The study and practice of naming, defining and classifying organisms

- Hierarchal system:

  - Domain > Kingdom > Phylum > Class > Order > Family > Genus > Species

  - **D**umb **K**ids **P**refer **C**heese **O**ver **F**ried **G**reen **S**pinach (mnemonic)

- Each rank in the hierarchy is called a taxon

- Organisms are named using binomial nomenclature, which consists of *Genus* (capitalized) and *species* (lowercase) e.g. *Homo sapiens*
