## Reasons to maintain biodiversity

### Moral & ethical reasons:

1. It is **unethical** for humans to drive species to extinction, regardless of its usefulness or lack thereof to humans

2. **Indigenous communities** depend on biodiversity for their way of life, so they can conserve their traditions and culture

3. People believe in the **duty** to conserve beauty for future generations

### Ecological reasons:

1. Higher biodiversity means ecosystems are **more resilient** to environmental changes, diseases, and threats such as pollution

2. Maintains **genetic diversity**: Avoids inbreeding depression, promotes hybrid vigor, decreases homozygosity, and increases adaptability

3. Maintains the **food web** so different species can survive as the loss of one species disrupts the entire chain and causes other species to die off too

4. Allows **nutrient cycling**, which consists of processes like nitrogen fixation, decomposition and soil formation, ensuring plant growth

5. Plants depend on **pollinators**, so without these pollinator species, food production would be affected

6. Atmospheric gases are regulated by forests, promoting **climate stability** so natural disasters are avoided

### Economic reasons:

1. **Medicine:** Making new drugs e.g. isolating antibiotics from fungi, extracting useful compounds from species used in traditional medicine. If species are lost, we may lose potentially beneficial substances that we do not know about

2. **Improving crops using genes** from their wild strains, especially if diseases affect crops that are currently not genetically diverse so using genes, we can widen their genetic diversity so that they can adapt

3. **Ecotourism** provides employment and generates revenue for many countries

4. Species provide different **resource materials**, such as wood for building, medicinal herbs, food, fuel, etc.

###  Aesthetic Reasons:

1. **Inspiration** for artists, photographers, poets etc.

2. Healthy ecosystems promote **mental health** in humans, who naturally enjoy the outdoors

3. Allows **recreational activities** such as diving, hiking, birdwatching etc.
