## International Conservation Organizations

### IUCN: International Union for Conservation of Nature

* Global authority on the status of the natural world and the measures needed to safeguard it

* Provides info about the conservation status of species

* They monitor and categorize threatened and endangered species

* The **IUCN Red List of Threatened Species** evaluates the conservation status of a species and how close is to extinction, indicated by 9 categories

### CITES: Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Flora and Fauna

* Restricts/prevents international trade of endangered species

* This includes any products from them such as furs, skins and ivory

* It has **appendices** which list endangered species according to set criteria

* Species do not always benefit from the trade restrictions, because when a product becomes illegal, its price rises, which makes it more worthwhile to break trade laws

* IUCN informs key decisions at CITES conferences
