## Extinction

* An endangered species is one threatened with extinction, where their numbers have decreased so low that reproduction is affected
* Smaller populations and populations with less genetic diversity are at greater risk of extinction

### Reasons for species extinction and biodiversity loss:

1. **Climate change:**

* Greenhouse gas emissions come from industrial activities, burning fossil fuels, deforestation, organic waste etc.

* These gases trap heat in the atmosphere, leading to global warming

  * Ice melts so the total area of ice decreases, destroying polar habitats and contributing to rising sea levels

  * Warmer oceans reduce plankton populations, disrupting the food web

  * Increased sea levels and temperatures damage coral reefs

  * Some species change migration patterns, but then starve as prey don’t shift accordingly

  * Organisms at high altitudes find it difficult to adapt

2. **Habitat destruction:**

* Caused by deforestation, urbanization, pollution and draining wetlands

  * Habitat fragmentation isolates populations and makes it harder to find food/prey and mates

  * Breeding sites are lost

3. **Pollution:**

* Mining causes air pollution, leading to acid rain which destroys habitats

* Oil spills cause suffocation and poisoning in marine animals

* Eutrophication is caused by fertilizers draining into rivers, killing species in water

* Non-biodegradable waste kills animals if they ingest it when mistaken for prey e.g. turtles could eat plastic

* Soil acidification from excessive pesticide use or acid rain harms plant growth

4. **Overexploitation:**

* Overuse of fishing, hunting, poaching etc. for resources decreases numbers
* Species struggle to increase numbers, and predators lose prey as well

5. **Alien species:**

* Non-native species enter an ecosystem and disrupt it

* They could introduce diseases, or become a new predator in the area and decrease numbers of organisms
