## Biodiversity

* **Biodiversity:** The variation of life forms, including species diversity, genetic diversity, and ecosystem diversity

* **Ecosystem:** A self-contained system of living organisms (biotic factors) interacting with each other and with the environment (abiotic factors), in an area

* **Niche:** The role of an organism in an ecosystem

  * Includes its habitat, how it obtains energy, and how it interacts

### Species diversity:

* The number of different species in an ecosystem and their relative abundance

* The more species there are and the more evenly they are distributed, the greater the species diversity

* Distribution and abundance of a species for a **uniform** area is found by **random sampling**, such as:

  * Quadrats: for plants or slow-moving organisms

  * Mark-release-recapture: for estimating population size of mobile animals

  * Simpson’s index of biodiversity: calculates species diversity

* Distribution and abundance of a species for a **varying** area where physical conditions change is found by **systematic sampling**, such as:

  * Transects: line and belt transects

  * Pearson’s linear correlation: testing linear correlations e.g. soil pH vs plant height

  * Spearman’s rank correlation: testing correlations between variables that are not normally distributed e.g. abundance of two species

### Genetic diversity:

* The variation of alleles in the genome of a species

  * e.g. different breeds of dogs

* Found using how many alleles there are of each gene and the proportion of genes that have different alleles

* Genetic differences exist because the populations adapted slightly differently to different biotic and abiotic factors
