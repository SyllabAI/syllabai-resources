## Alien Species

* Non-native species that are introduced into an ecosystem

* This invasion could be accidental e.g. from trade, or intentional e.g. to control pests

* Effects on ecosystems:

1. Disrupting the food web by preying on native species

2. Competing with native species for resources like food, water and space

3. Introducing diseases

4. Affecting natural habitats, such as coral reefs
