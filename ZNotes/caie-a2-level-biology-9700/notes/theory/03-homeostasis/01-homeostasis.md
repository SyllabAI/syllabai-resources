# Homeostasis

## Homeostasis

* Homeostasis is the maintenance of a constant internal environment

* The tissue fluid is the immediate environment of a cell, influencing its activity

* The physiological features controlled in mammals by tissue fluid are:

1. Temperature:

* Low temperature slows metabolic reactions, and too high temperatures denature enzymes

2. Water Potential of Blood:

* Low water potential **outside** causes water to leave the cell by osmosis so the cell shrinks

* High water potential outside causes water to enter so the cell swells and then bursts

3. Blood Glucose Concentration:

* If it is low, respiration slows or stops, resulting in low energy

* If it is high, it decreases the water potential **inside** the cell, so water moves out of the cell

4. Blood pH:

* Enzymes denature at extreme pH values (either too high or too low)
