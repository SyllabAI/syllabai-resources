## Homeostasis in Plants

* Stomata control the diffusion of gases into and out of the leaf cells

* The lower epidermis of leaves has the highest density of stomata

* They open when light intensity, temperature and humidity is high and CO2 concentration inside the leaf is low

* They close when there is no light, temperature is too high, water is less, or CO2 concentration inside the leaf is high (important for photosynthesis)

* Each stomatal pore is surrounded by 2 **guard cells**, which are elliptical shaped cells

* Guard cells have many chloroplasts and mitochondria, thick cell walls, waxy cuticle on the outside, no plasmodesmata, and several small vacuoles

* They control the opening and closing of stomata:

  * When turgid (full of water) stomata open

  * When flaccid (less water) stomata close
