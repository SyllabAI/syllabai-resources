## Biosensors

* Urine tests do not show the *current* blood glucose concentration

* Biosensors are reusable and more precise

1. Glucose oxidase immobilized on a recognition layer

2. A small sample of blood is tested

3. If glucose is present, it is oxidized to gluconic acid and hydrogen peroxide

4. An electrode oxidizes hydrogen peroxide, generating a current

5. The current is proportional to glucose concentration, and is processed into a digital reading
