## Urine Analysis

- Much easier to collect than blood samples

- The presence of glucose higher than a certain value (renal threshold) indicates diabetes

### Test Strips/Dipsticks:

* Used to test urine for glucose, ketones, proteins and pH

1. The enzymes **glucose oxidase** and **peroxidase** are immobilized on a pad at one end

2. The stick is immersed in urine

3. If glucose is present, it is oxidized into **gluconic acid** and **hydrogen** **peroxide** (catalyzed by glucose oxidase)

* glucose ---glucose oxidase--> gluconic acid + hydrogen peroxide

4. Peroxidase catalyzes a reaction between hydrogen peroxide and **chromogen** (which is colorless) to form a brown compound

* hydrogen peroxide + chromogen (colorless) ---peroxidase--> oxidized chromogen (colored) + water

5. The resulting color of the pad is compared with a color chart

* The more glucose present, the darker the color
