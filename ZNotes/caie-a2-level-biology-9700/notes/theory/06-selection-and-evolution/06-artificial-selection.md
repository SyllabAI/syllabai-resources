## Artificial Selection

* Artificial selection is used by humans to select desirable features in a species

* How it works:

1. Variation exists in a population, so some individuals have ideal characteristics

2. Humans select these individuals with desired traits and breed them

3. Offspring that inherit these desired traits are chosen and bred

4. This is repeated for many generations, which over time increases allele frequency for ideal characteristics

* Artificial selection is a form of directional selection, as humans act as a selection pressure and favor one extreme genotype

* Examples:

  * Disease-resistant maize

  * Dairy cows that produce more milk

  * One wheat variety used for bread is ideally rich in gluten, another variety used for other food products ideally has less gluten

### Disadvantages

1. **Loss of genetic variation**

* This means the organisms have reduced ability to adapt

2. **Increase in homozygosity**

* Harmful recessive alleles have a higher chance of coming together and being expressed

3. **Inbreeding depression**

* This is the loss of ability to survive as individuals are smaller and weaker due to breeding between close relatives

* Higher risk of genetic disorders

* Outbreeding (breeding individuals that are not closely related) produces heterozygous plants that show **hybrid vigor**

  * Hybrid vigor is an increased ability to survive
