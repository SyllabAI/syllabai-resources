## Genetic Drift

* **Genetic drift:** Random changes in allele frequency that occur by chance

* More likely to affect small populations where alleles can be lost easily

  * The **gene pool** (all genes/alleles present in a population) is smaller for these

### Founder Effect:

* Occurs when a few individuals colonize a new area and become geographically isolated

* The new population has reduced genetic diversity as they carry only a fraction of the original population’s alleles

* They evolve differently due to new selection pressures, eventually becoming genetically distinct from the original population

* The population experiences genetic drift and allele frequencies change

* Results in evolution and **speciation** (formation of a new species)

* Example: A population of white and yellow flowers with the same gene pool: 

  * By chance, only yellow flower seeds fall on distant ground and begin growing there, while the white flower seeds fall on unsuitable ground

  * Over time, the new population loses the alleles for white flowers

### Bottleneck Effect:

* A large decrease in genetic diversity due to drastic reduction in population numbers

* Many alleles are lost, reducing the specie’s gene pool

* Occurs due to natural disasters, disease, overhunting etc

* Survivors do not represent the original population’s genetic diversity

* Example: Cheetah population numbers decreased due to climate changes in the Ice Age, so now they have much less genetic diversity compared to other animals
