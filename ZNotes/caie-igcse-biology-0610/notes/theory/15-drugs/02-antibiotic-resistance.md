## Antibiotic Resistance

Antibiotic-resistant bacteria can be reproduced through the theory of **natural selection**:

* Mutation, giving rise to variation.
* Antibiotics kill bacteria without changing genes
* Competition for food space, among others
* Reproduce via binary fission
* Then, alleles are passed on to offspring to reproduce.

An example of antibiotic-resistant bacteria is **Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA)**
