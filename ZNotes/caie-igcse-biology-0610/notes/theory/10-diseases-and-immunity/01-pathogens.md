# Diseases and Immunity

## Pathogens

* **Pathogen:** a disease-causing organism.
* **Transmissible Disease:** a disease in which the pathogen can be passed from one host to another.
* The pathogen for a transmissible disease may be transmitted either:
  * **Direct Contact:** the pathogen is passed directly from one host to another through blood, body fluids, semen, etc.
  * **Indirect Contact:** the pathogen leaves the host and is carried in some way to another e.g., contaminated surfaces/food, from animals, from air
