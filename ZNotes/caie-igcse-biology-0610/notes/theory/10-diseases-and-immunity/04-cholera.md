## Cholera

* **Diarrhoea:** loss of water from watery faeces
* **Oral rehydration therapy and antibiotics** are used to cure this.
* The bacterium, “***Vibrio cholerae***”, causes Cholera.
* The bacterium is transmitted through contaminated water.
* The cholera bacterium **produces a toxin that causes the secretion of chloride ions** into the small intestine, **decreasing water potential** in the gut, water moves into the gut **down the water potential gradient**, causing diarrhoea, dehydration, and loss of salts and water from the blood.
