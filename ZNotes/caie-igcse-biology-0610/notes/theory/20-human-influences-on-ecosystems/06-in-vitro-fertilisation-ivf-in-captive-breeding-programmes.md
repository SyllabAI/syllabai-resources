## *in vitro* Fertilisation (IVF) in Captive Breeding Programmes

- It involves fertilisation of an egg outside the mother's body
- *Method*
  - Eggs are extracted from the ovaries of the mother using a needle
  - Eggs are kept in a culture medium.
  - Male semen is mixed with the eggs so fertilisation takes place
  - Zygotes form which develop into embryos
  - These embryos are checked after being left in the culture medium for several days
  - The embryos are then transferred into the mother or a surrogate.
