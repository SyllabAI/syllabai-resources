## Nervous and Hormonal Control

| Comparison | Nervous system | Endocrine system |
|----|----|----|
| Speed of action | Very rapid | Can be slow |
| Nature of message | Electrical impulses travelling along nerves | Chemical messengers (hormones) travelling in the bloodstream |
| Duration of response | Usually within seconds | It may take years (puberty) |
| Area of response | Localized response (only one area usually) | Widespread response (in many organs) |
| Example of process-controlled | Reflexes such as blinking | Development of the reproductive system |
