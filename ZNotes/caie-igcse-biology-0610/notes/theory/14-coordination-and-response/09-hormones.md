## Hormones

**Hormones:** A chemical substance produced by a gland and carried by the blood, altering the activity of one or more specific target organs.

### **Endocrine Glands**

* adrenal glands and adrenaline
* pancreas and insulin
* testes and testosterone
* ovaries and oestrogen

### **Adrenaline**

* A hormone secreted by the adrenal gland.
* It increases pulse rate, heart rate and pupil diameter.
* Increases blood glucose concentration for respiration.
* Adrenaline is secreted, for example, bungee jumping or riding a rollercoaster.

| Gland | Hormone | Function |
|----|----|----|
| Adrenal gland | Adrenaline | Prepares the body for vigorous action |
| Pancreas | Insulin | Reduces the concentration of glucose in the blood |
| Testes | Testosterone | Causes the development of male sexual characteristics |
| Ovary | Oestrogen | Causes the development of female sexual characteristics |
| Pancreas | Glucagon | Increases concentration of glucose in the blood |
