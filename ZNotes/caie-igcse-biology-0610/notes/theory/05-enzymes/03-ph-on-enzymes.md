## pH on Enzymes

* Enzymes are **sensitive to pH.**
* Some enzymes work best in an acid, and others in an alkaline.
* Enzymes work best at their optimum pH.
* If the pH changes,  the hydrogen bond is broken, denaturing the enzyme, changing the shape of its active site. Substrate can no longer bind with the enzyme, therefore, no reaction occurs.
* Pepsin is used in acidic conditions, Amylase is used in neutral conditions, and trypsin is used in alkaline conditions.
