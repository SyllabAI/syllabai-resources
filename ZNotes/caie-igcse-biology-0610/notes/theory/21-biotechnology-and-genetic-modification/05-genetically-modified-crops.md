## Genetically Modified Crops

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|----|----|
| Uniform in shape – easy to transport/appeal to consumers | Natural species may die |
| Growing season shorter | Decrease biodiversity/genetic diversity |
| Drought resistant – less water | Led to the development of superweeds – stronger than GM |
| Higher yields | No one knows the long-term effects on humans |
| Solve global hunger | Expensive seeds |
