## Enzymes in Digestion

* **Amylase:** breaks down starch into maltose; it is produced in the pancreas (secreted into the duodenum) and salivary glands.

![Screenshot 2024-09-25 094029.png](../../../assets/a0aee601087f-62519fa10628d955a6622f4af3b1d95010e1f2837670a1c3.png)

* **Maltase**: breaks down maltose into glucose in the membrane of the epithelium lining in small intestines.

![image1-1-3.png](../../../assets/6e63177c39b1-62519fa10628d955a6622f4a53ad52f001f0e75c0a089d1d.png)

* **Protease:** breaks down proteins into peptides and amino acids. Pepsin comes from the stomach (acidic), and trypsin comes from the pancreas (alkali).

![eb34a2a670077be85de3000b88ed2da8cd2d55fb.gif](../../../assets/ba49976de7f5-62519fa10628d955a6622f4a10b8002be8542f8bc0eb0962.gif)

* **Lipase:** breaks down lipids into fatty acids and glycerol,
  produced by the pancreas.

![Lipid-is-broken-down-to-free-fatty-acids-and-glycerol-by-the-lipase-enzyme.png](../../../assets/a499b03341ff-62519fa10628d955a6622f4a959f39205c29ce9306a4898a.png)
