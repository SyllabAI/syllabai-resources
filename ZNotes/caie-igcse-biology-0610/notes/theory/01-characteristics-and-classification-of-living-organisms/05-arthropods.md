## Arthropods

* Arthropods are the largest group under **invertebrates** (Organisms that do not have a backbone).
* All arthropods have **three standard features**:
  1. Exoskeleton

  2. Jointed legs

  3. Segmented body


|Type of Arthropod|Number of Legs|Body parts|Wings|Antennae|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|Crustacean|5 pairs (1 modified as claws)|All body segments tucked under carapace|None|2 pairs|
|Myriapods|Many|Many|None|1 pair|
|Insects|3 pairs|3 (head, thorax, abdomen)|2 pairs (1 or both may be vestigial)|1 pair|
|Arachnids|4 pairs|2 (cephalothorax and abdomen)|None|None|
