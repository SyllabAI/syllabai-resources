# Gas Exchange in Humans

## Gas Exchange Surfaces

| Properties | Reasons |
|----|----|
| Thin surface | Short distance to diffuse (one cell thick) |
| Large surface area | Many molecules can diffuse at once/More alveoli |
| Good ventilation | Regular fresh air supplies keep up concentration gradients for oxygen and carbon dioxide. |
| Good blood supply | Gases can be carried to/from the cells that need/produce them |
| Moist | Allow gases to dissolve, ready for diffusion |
