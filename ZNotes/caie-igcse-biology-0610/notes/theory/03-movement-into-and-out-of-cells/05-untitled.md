## Difference between Diffusion, Osmosis, and Active Transport


|Feature|Diffusion|Osmosis|Active Transport|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|Type of movement|Passive (no energy required)|Passive (no energy needed)|Active (requires energy-ATP)|
|Direction|High → Low concentration|High → Low water potential|Low → High (against gradient)|
|Substance moved|Gases or solutes (e.g., oxygen, $CO_2$)|Water only|glucose, ions, etc.|
|Membrane needed?|Not always|A partially permeable membrane is required|Requires membrane and carrier proteins|
