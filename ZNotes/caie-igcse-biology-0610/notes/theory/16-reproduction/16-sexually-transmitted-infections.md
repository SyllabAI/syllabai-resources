## Sexually Transmitted Infections

**Human Immunodeficiency virus** (**HIV)** is one example of a sexually transmitted infection.

* **Transmission:** Intercourse, blood transfusion, organ transplant or sharing a needle with an infected person
* **How it affects the immune system:**
  * Infects and destroys lymphocytes
  * Decreases the efficiency of the immune system
  * The body becomes liable to infection by other pathogens
  * This may lead to AIDS and death from infection
