
## Sexual Hormones in Humans

**Testosterone**: Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone which is produced in the testes.

* Primary characteristics- Growth of the testes and the penis. Production of sperm is stimulated.

* Secondary characteristics-Growth of facial and body hair. Deepening of voice as the larynx enlarges. Increased muscle mass and broadening of shoulders.

**Oestrogen**: Oestrogen is the primary female sex hormone which is produced in the ovaries.

* Primary characteristics- Development and maturation of ovaries and uterus. Regulation of the menstrual cycle. Release of egg is simulated.

* Secondary characteristics- Growth of body hair. Breast development and growth of mammary glands. Widening of hips to prepare the pelvis for childbirth. Increased fat deposition under the skin.
