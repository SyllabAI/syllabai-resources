## Hormones in Menstrual Cycle

* **Oestrogen**: Secreted by the ovaries. It stops FSH from being produced - so that only one egg matures in a cycle, and it stimulates the pituitary gland to release the luteinizing hormone(LH).
* **Progesterone:** Secreted by the **placenta** during pregnancy, while during the menstrual cycle it is by the **ovaries**. It’s primary function is maintaining the uterus lining during the middle of the menstrual cycle and pregnancy.
* **Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH):** Secreted by the pituitary gland. It causes an egg to mature in an ovary and stimulates ovaries to release the oestrogen hormone.
* **Luteinizing hormone (LH):** Secreted by the pituitary gland and causes mature eggs to be released from the ovary.
