## Germination

**Germination**: A process controlled by enzymes

* **Water:** activates enzymes to turn insoluble food stores into
  soluble substances, and makes tissues swell so that the testa splits
* **Oxygen:** enters through the gaps in the testa (along with water),
  and is used in aerobic respiration.
* **Temperature:** must be suitable for enzymes to work (at optimum
  temperature).
