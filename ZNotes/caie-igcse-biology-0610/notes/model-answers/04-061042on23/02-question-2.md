i) A tomato has more stomata on its lower epidermis than its upper epidermis, while a water lily has stomata only on its upper epidermis and no stomata on the lower epidermis. The water lily also has more stomata on its upper epidermis than the tomato, and overall more stomata per mm² than the tomato. In tomatoes, most stomata are on the lower epidermis, which is not facing the sun, helping to reduce water loss through transpiration or evaporation. In contrast, the water lily floats on water, so only its upper surface is exposed to air, and therefore its stomata are concentrated on the upper epidermis.
