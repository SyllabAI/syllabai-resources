
## Question 6

a. 

i) **The roots show positive gravitropism** – they grow downwards, with gravity. **The shoots show negative gravitropism** – they grow upwards, against gravity.

ii) Gravitropism

iii) Auxin is made in the shoot tip and diffuses to where it stimulates cell elongation. In seedling B (Petri dish 1), auxin accumulates at a higher concentration on the lower surface of the shoot, causing greater elongation of cells on the lower side. As a result, the shoot curves and grows upwards, away from the pull of gravity. In seedling E (Petri dish 2), auxin is distributed equally throughout the shoot, so cell elongation occurs evenly on all sides. This means the shoot does not bend but instead grows straight, as the effect of gravity is constantly countered by the rotation of the Petri dish.
