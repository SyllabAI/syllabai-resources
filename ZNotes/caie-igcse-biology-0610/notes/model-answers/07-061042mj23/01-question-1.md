## Question 1

a.) A leaf is made up of multiple tissues that work together for a specific function – photosynthesis.

b.) B is the palisade mesophyll. The cells are tightly packed below the upper epidermis; with the large vacuoles in the cells pushing chloroplasts towards the edges of the cells. This maximises light exposure to the chloroplasts for photosynthesis. C contains spongy mesophyll and vascular bundles (xylem and phloem). The mesophyll has loosely packed cells with air spaces to allow gas exchange and evaporation of water from cell surfaces into the air spaces. The xylem has thick, lignified, waterproofed, pitted cell walls and forms long continuous hollow tubes with no cytoplasm or end walls, allowing transport of water and mineral ions. Phloem allows translocation of sucrose, sugars, and amino acids.

iii) It reduces the water lost from the leaf by transpiration, this helps to prevent the leaf from wilting.

d.) At high humidity, the stomata will take longer to close because the water vapour concentration gradient between the air spaces and the outside will reduce. This means the guard cells will not lose water as quickly, so they stay turgid for longer.
