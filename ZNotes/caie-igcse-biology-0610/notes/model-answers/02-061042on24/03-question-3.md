ii) Protein is only present in fluid A, and not in fluids B or C, because proteins are too large to pass through the filtration membrane in the nephron. Filtration occurs from fluid A to B in the nephron. Glucose is present in fluids A and B, but not in fluid C, because all the glucose is reabsorbed in the nephron. Water, ions, and urea are present in all three fluids, but their concentrations are highest in fluid C, because only some water and ions are reabsorbed in the nephron, and excess water and ions are transferred to fluid C.

b.)Blood glucose concentration is controlled by negative feedback to maintain homeostasis.
A change in blood glucose levels is detected by the pancreas. The pancreas secretes insulin when blood glucose is high, and glucagon when it is low. Insulin lowers blood glucose levels by stimulating the conversion of glucose to glycogen. This conversion takes place in the liver, where glycogen is stored. Insulin also promotes cellular respiration, using up more glucose. Glucagon increases blood glucose levels by stimulating the conversion of glycogen back into glucose.
