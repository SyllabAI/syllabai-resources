An **entrepreneur is a person who organizes, operates and takes risks for a new business venture**. The entrepreneur brings together the various factors of production to produce goods or services.

**Characteristics of successful entrepreneurs:**

* Risk taker

* Creative

* Optimistic

* Self-confident

* Innovative

* Independent

* Effective communicator

* Hard working

**Benefits of being an Entrepreneur:**

* Independance

* Able to put own ideas into practice

* May become famous and successful if the business grows

* Access to all profit

* Ability to use personal skills and interests

**Disadvantages of being an entrepreneur:**

* High risk involved

* Capital needed

* Lack of knowledge and experience

* Opportunity cost
