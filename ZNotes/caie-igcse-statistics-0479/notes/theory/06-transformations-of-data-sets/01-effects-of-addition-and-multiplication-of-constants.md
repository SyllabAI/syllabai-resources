# Transformations of Data Sets

## Effects of Addition and Multiplication of Constants

### Mean:

* Addition:

  * Adding a constant to the dataset increases the Mean by the said constant

  * For example, the Mean is $3$, and the number $2$ is added to the dataset

    * $3+2$

    * $= 5$

* Multiplication:

  * Multiplying the dataset by a constant scales the Mean by the said constant

    * For example, the Mean is $3$ and the dataset is multiplied by $2$

      * $3 \times 2$

      * $= 6$

### Standard Deviation:

* Addition:

  * Adding a constant to the dataset does not affect the Standard Deviation

  * For example, the Standard Deviation is $3$, and the number $2$ is added to the dataset

    * The Standard Deviation will remain 3

* Multiplication:

  * Multiplying the dataset by a constant scales the Standard Deviation by the said constant

  * For example, the Standard Deviation is $3$, and the dataset is multiplied by $5$

    * $3 \times 5$

    * $= 15$

### Median:

* Addition:

  * Adding a constant to the dataset increases the Median by the said constant

  * For example, the Median is $4$, and the number $2$ is added to the dataset

    * $4+2$

    * $= 6$

* Multiplication:

  * Multiplying the dataset by a constant scales the Median by the said constant

  * For example, the Median is $4$ and the dataset is multiplied by $2$

    * $4 \times 2$

    * $= 8$

### InterQuartile Range (IQR):

* Addition:

  * Adding a constant to the dataset does not affect the IQR

  * For example, the IQR is $11$, and the number $5$ is added to the dataset

    * The IQR will remain 11

* Multiplication:

  * Multiplying the dataset by a constant scales the IQR by the said constant

  * For example, the Standard Deviation is $11$, and the dataset is multiplied by $4$

    * $11 \times 4$

    * $= 44$
