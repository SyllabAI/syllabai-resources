## Making Comparisons between Datasets

* Once we are given the mean and standard deviation of a dataset, we can infer how the values are distributed throughout it.

* This also enables us to make logical comparisons between two or more datasets.

* If the Median/Mean of a dataset is high/low, we can infer that the values in the dataset are also respectively high/low.

* If the Standard Deviation/Range of a dataset has a large/small value, we can infer that the values in the dataset are respectively more/less consistent or spread out.
