## Types Of Sampling

1. ### Simple Random Sampling:

   * A method of sampling in which the sample is chosen at random, so all members of the population have an equal chance of being selected.

   * For example:

     * Picking a random person using computer software to win the lottery.

     * Writing names on pieces of paper and randomly selecting 5 pieces of paper.

     * Using random sampling numbers to select from a sampling frame.

2. ### Systematic Sampling:

   * A method of sampling in which the sample is obtained by selecting members of a population at regular intervals from a sampling frame.

   * The sampling frame is divided into equal-sized groups and one member is chosen from each group.

3. ### Stratified Sampling:

   * A method of sampling in which the population is divided into strata or groups according to their proportion in the population.

   * For example:

     * A sample from a neighborhood stratified by gender.

       * The population is divided according to gender, and a sample is taken from each group according to the number of members in each group

4. ### Quota Sampling:

   * A method of sampling in which the interviewer chooses the members of the population selected for the sample.

   * The interviewer may be given set groups to choose from.

   * For Example:

     * Interviewing 10 males above the age of 40 and 10 males below the age of 40

A sampling frame is **NOT** required for quota samples; however, it is necessary for all other types of sampling. This is because the members of the population interviewed are selected by the interviewer and not with the help of a number table.
