## Bias in Sampling

* Showing prejudice towards or against a certain type of person, item, or group.

* In a biased sample, some members of the population are more likely to be selected than others; as a result, the sample may not be representative.

* For example:

  * All members of the population selected for the sample are male.

  * Selecting the first 10 people to arrive at school while investigating the most common modes of transport.

  * (As the time to arrive is affected by mode of transportation, the sample may be unrepresentative and is hence biased)

* Random and systematic samples may accidentally produce biased samples.

* Stratified sampling is fairer; however, it is difficult to account for every group in a population.

* Quota sampling is often most biased due to the data collector’s own prejudices.
