# Data and Its Collection

## Key Terms

* ### Population:

  * The whole from which a sample is selected.

  * Can refer to:

    * All the people living in a city.

    * All the pencils produced at a factory.

* ### Sample:

  * A part of the population.

  * There are 4 types of sampling methods.

* ### Census:

  * A method of data collection in which data is collected from every member within a defined population

  * For example, all students in a class were asked about the quality of chairs.

* ### Representative Sample:

  * A sample that accurately represents the characteristics of a population.

  * Does not have bias.
