## Surveys

* Surveys are a method of data collection

* There are 2 types of surveys.

  * A “**Census Survey”** is a survey in which data is collected from the entire population

  * A “**Sample Survey”** is a survey in which data is collected from a part of the population.

* The list of questions asked in a survey is called a “Questionnaire”.

* ### A questionnaire is preferably :

  * Straightforward

  * Short

  * Easy to understand

* A questionnaire includes either **“Open”** or **“Closed”** questions.

* ### Open question

  * Requires a detailed answer and encourages the interviewee to share their point of view.

  * For example: How do you feel about the changes made to the timetable?

  * Answers to open questions are difficult to compile and interpret. Oftentimes they may even get misinterpreted.

* ### Closed question

  * Limit the interviewee to restricted answers.

  * For example: Answer “True” or “False” to the following statements.

  * Interviewees may feel that none of the options are suitable

  * It is easier to compile and interpret answers to closed questions
