# Frequency Distributions

## Types of Frequency Distributions

* Frequency distributions are a method of organizing raw data in which values are arranged according to their frequencies.

* There are 2 main types of frequency Distributions: **Grouped Frequency Distributions** and **Ungrouped Frequency Distributions**.

### Ungrouped Frequency Distributions:

* Individual values are shown alongside their corresponding frequencies

### Grouped Frequency Distributions:

* Data is organized within class intervals, and each class interval is displayed alongside its corresponding frequency.

* Class intervals may be defined as:

  * $0-10$

  * $0 \leq x < 10$

### Advantages of Grouped Frequency Distributions:

* Useful for organizing large amounts of data

* Easier to analyse and spot trends

* Speeds up calculations

### Disadvantages of Grouped Frequency Distributions:

* Original data is lost.

* All calculations (mean, median, etc.) are estimates and not exact values.

* Extreme values cannot be recognised in large class intervals.
