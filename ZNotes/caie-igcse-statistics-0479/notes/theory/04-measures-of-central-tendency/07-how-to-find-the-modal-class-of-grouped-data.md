## How to Find the Modal Class of Grouped Data

* To find the modal class of grouped data, we must find the class with the highest frequency density.

* To find the frequency density, we must divide the frequency by the class width

* For example:

#|
||

Mass

|

No of Pouches

|

Frequency Density

||
||

10-20

|

7

|

$\frac{7}{10}   = 0.7$

||
||

20-40

|

13

|

$\frac{13}{20} = 0.65$

||
||

40-80

|

34

|

$\frac{34}{40} = 0.85$

||
|#

* As the class 40-80 has the highest frequency density, it is our modal class.
