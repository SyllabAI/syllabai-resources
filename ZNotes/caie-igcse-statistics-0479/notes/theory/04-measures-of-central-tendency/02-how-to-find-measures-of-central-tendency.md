## How to Find Measures of Central Tendency

* For example, we are given the following data regarding the ages of applicants for a job:

  21, 24, 24, 25, 27, 28, 31, 32, 32, 34, 35, 36, 36, 36, 36, 37

### Mean:

* To find the mean, we will add all the values and divide them by the number of values present.

* Total =

  * $21+24+24+25+27+28+31+32+32+34+35+36+36+36+36+37 = 494$

* Total No. of Values: 16

  * Mean = $\frac{Total}{No\ of\ Values}$

  * Mean = $\frac{494}{16}$

  * Mean = 30.875

  * Mean $\approx$ 30.9

### Median:

* To find the median, we first need the middle value in the dataset.

* There are a total of 16 values in the dataset

* When there is an odd number of values in the dataset, we use the following formula to find the median.

$$

\frac{n}{2}

$$

(where n = total number of values)

* When there is an even number of values in a dataset, we take the average of the two middle values instead to obtain the median.

* Take x and y as the 2 middle values.

  * $x = \frac{n}{2}$

  * $y = \frac{n}{2} + 1$

  * Where n is the total number of values.

* $x = \frac{16}{2} = 8$

* $y = \frac{16}{2} + 1 = 9$

* So our Median would be the average of the 8th and 9th values of the dataset.

* $\frac{32+32}{2}$

* $= 32$

### Mode:

* To find the mode, we must simply identify the most repeated value or the value with the greatest frequency *f* in the dataset.

* The value 36 is repeated 4 times; hence, it has the greatest frequency and is the most common value.

* Therefore, the Mode is 36.
