## How to Solve Problems Where the Mean is Given

* The mean of 6 numbers is 26. The numbers are 20, 24, 26, 27, 28, $x$. Find the Value of $x$

  * To solve this, we will first find the total sum of the 6 numbers

    * $6 \times 26 = 156$

  * Then we will make an equation to find the value of $x$

    * $20+24+26+27+28+x = 156$

    * $x = 31$
