* The Table above is a frequency distribution table regarding the number of days students came to school in a month.

* It is a more efficient and organised way of displaying data rather than listing each value individually.

### Finding the Mean:

* To find the mean number of days students came to school, we can multiply each class with the frequency and total the products to obtain the number of total number of days attended by the class.

* Then, we can simply divide the total number of days attended by the class by the total number of students.

  * $\frac{
    (14\times3)+(15\times5)+(16\times1)+(17\times5)+(18\times3)+(19\times0)+(20\times1)}{3+5+1+5+3+0+1}
    $
  * $\frac{292}{18}$
  * $= 16.2$

### Finding the Median:

* To find the Median, we need the middle value of the data.

* As we know, there are 18 total values; we need to find the 2 middle values.

  * $\frac{18}{2} = 9$

  * $\frac{18}{2} + 1 = 10$

* The Median will be the average of these 2 values.

* We can figure out the values by counting the frequency in the frequency distribution.

* The values are 16 and 17.

* So, our median would be:

  * $\frac{16+17}{2}$

  * $= 16.5$

### Finding the Mode:

* To find the mode, we can simply look at the class with the highest frequency.

* The highest frequency is 5, which occurs for both 15 and 17 days attended.

* Hence, we have 2 modes: 15 and 17.
