## Finding Crude Rates

* We have previously learned that Crude Rates are simple rates that give us the number of events occurring in a population.

* Crude Rates may be found per hundred, thousand, million, etc.

* For example, Birth Rate is usually calculated per thousand.

* The rate you need to measure the Crude Rate will usually be provided to you in the question.

### Example Question:

A city had a population of 300,000 at the start of 2025. There were 3756 births during the year. Find the Crude Birth Rate for the year 2025

* Although the rate we need to measure the Birth Rate in is not given, we will measure it in “per thousand“ as Birth and Death rates are measured per thousand.

  $$

  \text{Crude Rate} = \frac{Number\ of\ Births}{Total\ Population} \times 1000

  $$

  * We will divide the total deaths by the total population to find the Crude Rate.

  * We are multiplying our rate by 1000 to find the Birth Rate per thousand.

* $\text{Crude Rate} = \frac{3756}{300000} \times 1000$

* $\text{Crude Rate} = 12.52$

Hence, the Crude Birth Rate per thousand of the population is 12.52.

### Finding the Number of Events:

* You may also be asked to find the number of Events using the Crude Rate.

* For Example:

  * The Crude Birth Rate in a town of 10,000 people is 53 per thousand.

* $\text{Crude Rate} = \frac{Number\ of\ Births}{Total\ Population} \times 1000$

* $53 = \frac{x}{10000} \times 1000$

* $x = 530$

### Important:

* Questions may also ask you to calculate other types of Crude rates, such as Death/Accident rate; the syllabus is not confined to Birth Rates only.

* The same method would be used.
