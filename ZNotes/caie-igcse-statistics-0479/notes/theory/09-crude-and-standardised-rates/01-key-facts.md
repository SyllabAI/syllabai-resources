# Crude and Standardised Rates

## Key Facts

* A rate is a measure of 2 or more quantities.

* There are 2 Main Types of Rates:

  * Crude Rates

  * Standardised Rates

### Crude Rates:

* Crude Rates are simple rates that give the total number of events occurring in a population.

* Crude Rates do **NOT** account for any subgroups within the population.

### Standardised Rates:

* Standardised Rates are Rates whose values have been weighted.

* Standardised Rates account for the subgroups within the population.
