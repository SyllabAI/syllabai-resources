* A small hotel records the number of guests they receive every 3 months for 3 years.

* To make a Time Series Graph, we will first label our $x$ and $y$ axes appropriately according to the intervals given in the data.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/0309b661426f9925ddd10734.webp =737x)

* Now that we have labelled our axes, we can plot out each data point and join them together in straight lines in order to get a Time Series graph.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/91f0fd47df40608e79e47290.webp =789x)

* Here we have obtained the Time Series graph for the number of guests the hotel receives every 3 months.

* From this graph, we can interpret that:

  * Oct-Dec is usually the busiest time of the year.

  * Jul-Sep sees the least number of guests every year.
