## Seasonal Variation and Components

* Variation is classified as seasonal variation if there is a recurring trend of the variation repeating at regular time intervals of a year or less.

* For Example:

  * A trend in the number of chocolate sales that repeats every year.

  * A trend in the number of gym-goers that repeats every month.

  * A trend in the temperature which repeats every day.

* A Seasonal Component is the mean of the variations compared to the general trend values (found from the trend line) on a Time Series Graph.

* For Example:

  * The graph we made above had 4 quarterly variations.

  * There will be a total of 4 seasonal components, one seasonal component for every quarter.

### How to Find and Use Seasonal Components:

Example Scenario:

* Over a time period of 4 years, the readings for time interval 1 were:

  * 25, 28, 22, 26

* For time interval 1, the trend values were

  * 19, 21, 23, 25

* As the seasonal component is the mean of the variation, the seasonal component for Time Interval 1 would be:

  * $\frac{11}{4}$

  * $= 2.75$

* We can also find Seasonal Components from the Time Series graph.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/8a724d255f06ec6d181c49e4.webp =771x)

* To find the Seasonal Component, we need to find the deviations.

* The deviations can be found by calculating the difference between the actual line (blue) and the trend line (orange).

* The deviations would be:

  * Year 1 = $+6$

  * Year 2 = $+7$

  * Year 3 = $−1$

  * Year 4 = $+1$

* We can take the average of all the deviations to find the Seasonal Component.

  * $\frac{6+7-1-1}{4}$

  * $= 2.75$

* We can also use the Seasonal Component to predict future values.

* If the Trend line suggests that the reading for a future year may be 30, we can add the seasonal component to 30 to gain a more accurate prediction for the reading.

  * $30 + 3.25$

  * $= 33.25$

* Hence, our prediction for that specific future year would be 33.25.

**It is also important to note that the sum of a set of seasonal components will be equal to $0$**

### Assumptions and Limitations:

* While predicting future values, we assume that the seasonal patterns remain consistent.

* There are linear changes in the trend over time.

* We must understand that predicting future data with trend values and seasonal components does not account for any anomalies.

  * For example, a natural disaster that causes a significant increase in monthly rainfall.

* Predictions would be inaccurate in case of a huge change

  * For example, the sales of X company if the company completely shut down.
