## Types of Probability Events

* There are 3 main types of probability events:

  * Mutually Exclusive Events

  * Dependant Events

  * Independent Events

### Mutually Exclusive Events:

* Events that are Exclusive of each other, meaning they cannot occur at the same time.

* For example:

  * Getting Heads and Tails on a coin flip.

  * Rolling a 1 and a 4 on a die on the same turn.

### Dependent Events:

* A series of events in which the outcomes are dependent on a previous outcome.

* For example:

  * 2 cards are chosen one after the other without replacement.

### Independent Events:

* A series of events in which the outcomes are independent of each other.

* For example:

  * A coin is flipped, and a die is rolled.
