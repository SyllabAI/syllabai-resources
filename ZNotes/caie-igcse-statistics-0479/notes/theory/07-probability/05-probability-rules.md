## Probability Rules

* There are a few set rules that can be followed to help solve problems relating to probability.

### Addition Rule:

* The addition rule for probability is:

  * $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$
  * It refers to the probability of obtaining A or B, or both.

* ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/ff24df05d5f504f769a5b3f5.webp =563x)

  * For example, the probability of drawing a Heart or an Ace from a deck of cards:
    * $P(A \cup B) = \frac{13}{52} + \frac{4}{52} - (\frac{1}{52})$

    * $P(A \cup B) = \frac{13}{52} + \frac{4}{52} - \frac{1}{52}$

    * $P(A \cup B) = \frac{4}{13}$

* In the case of Mutually Exclusive Events, the rule is:

  * $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)$
  * For example, the probability of rolling a 2 or a 6 on a fair 6-sided die:
    * $P(A \cup B) = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{6}$

    * $P(A \cup B) = \frac{1}{3}$

### Multiplication Rule:

* The multiplication rule for Independant Events is:

  * $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$
  * It refers to the probability of events A and B occurring at the same time.

* ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/c21c31598648fbad4c65cd56.webp =577x)

  * For example, the probability of rolling a 3 on a fair six-sided die and getting tails on a coin flip:
    * $P(A \cap B) = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{2}$

    * $P(A \cap B) = \frac{1}{12}$
