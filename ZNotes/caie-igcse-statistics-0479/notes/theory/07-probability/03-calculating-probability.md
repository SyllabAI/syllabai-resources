## Calculating Probability

* The basic formula for probability is:

  * $P = \frac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{number of total outcomes}}$

### Calculating Basic Probability:

A small pouch contains 3 Red marbles, 4 Green marbles, and 1 Blue marble.

Sam reaches in and grabs 1 random marble from the pouch.

Find the Probability that the marble he grabbed is:

* Red:

  * $P = \frac{number\ of\ favourable\ outcomes}{number\ of\ total\ outcomes}$

    * As there are 3 Red marbles, there are 3 Favourable outcomes of the event that a Red marble is chosen.

  * $P = \frac{3}{3+4+1}$

  * $P = \frac{3}{8}$

* Green:

  * $P = \frac{4}{3+4+1}$

  * $P = \frac{4}{8}$

  * $P = \frac{1}{2}$

* Blue:

  * $P = \frac{1}{3+4+1}$

  * $P = \frac{1}{8}$

### Probability of an Event NOT Happening:

* The probability of an event not happening is defined by a “ **‘** “ or a complement.

* For example:

  * The probability of Event A not happening/ The complement of Event A is defined as:

    * $P(A’)$

* To find the complement of any event, we use the following rule:

  * $P(A’) = 1 - P(A)$

* If the Probability of Event A is $\frac{3}{8}$

  * $P(A’) = 1 - P(A)$

  * $P(A’) = 1 - \frac{3}{8}$

  * $P(A’) = \frac{5}{8}$

### Calculating Probability without Replacement:

A small pouch contains 3 Red marbles, 4 Green marbles, and 1 Blue marble.

Sam reaches in and grabs 1 random marble from the pouch.

Suppose he grabbed a Red marble

Without Replacement, he grabs a second marble from the pouch

* In this case, without replacement means that he did not replace the marble he previously took out of the pouch.

* This means that there is 1 less red marble in the pouch than there was before

* Hence, the pouch now contains 2 Red marbles, 4 Green marbles, and 1 Blue marble.

* So the probability that he gets a red marble would be:

  * $P = \frac{number\ of\ favourable\ outcomes}{number\ of\ total\ outcomes}$

  * $P = \frac{2}{7}$

### Calculating Probability with Replacement:

A small pouch contains 3 Red marbles, 4 Green marbles, and 1 Blue marble.

Sam reaches in and grabs 1 random marble from the pouch.

Suppose he grabbed a Red marble

He replaces the red marble,

He grabs a second marble from the pouch

* Now he has replaced the red marble which he had taken out of the pouch.

* This means that the number of marbles in the pouch remains the same

* Hence, the pouch contains 3 Red marbles, 4 Green marbles, and 1 Blue marble

* So the probability that he gets a red marble would be:

  * $P = \frac{number\ of\ favourable\ outcomes}{number\ of\ total\ outcomes}$

  * $P = \frac{3}{8}$
