## Probability Notation

* Probability Notation is a way of writing down the Probability of an event.

### For Example:

There are 2 events, A and B.

* The Probability of A = $P(A)$

* The Probability of **NOT** A = $P(A’)$

* The Probability of A **and** B = $P(A \cap B)$

* The Probability of A **or** B = $P(A \cup B)$
