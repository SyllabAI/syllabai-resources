# Probability

## Key Facts

* Probability is the measure of how likely an event is to happen.

* Probability is measured from 0 to 1

* Flipping a coin is an **Experiment**

* Getting Tails is an **Event**

* There is 1 favourable **Outcome** of the event.

* Probability may be expressed as a Percentage, Decimal or Fraction.

* If outcomes of an experiment have the same chance of happening, they are known as **Equally Likely Outcomes.**

* The probability that an event occurs is the sum of its **Favourable Outcomes**.
