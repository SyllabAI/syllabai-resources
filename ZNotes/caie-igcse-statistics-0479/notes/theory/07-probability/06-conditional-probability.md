## Conditional Probability

* Conditional Probability is the measure of how the outcome of an event affects the probability of another event.

* How the probability of event A is affected, given that event B has occurred **OR** not occurred.

* Event A may or may not be affected by the outcome of event B.

* These are also further categorised as Dependant and Independant events.

### How to Calculate Conditional Probability:

* In case of Dependant Events, the probability varies according to the circumstances.

* These circumstances usually include whether or not the items are replaced, if the order of selection matters, etc.

* We have already discussed how to solve problems with and without replacement.

### How to Solve Problems where Order of Selection Matters:

* We flip 2 coins and need to find the probability of getting exactly 1 Head and 1 Tail.

* The possibilities of getting 1 Head and 1 Tail are:

  * Heads, Tails, OR Tails, Heads.

* As the possibilities can be arranged in 2 separate ways, we will multiply our probability by $2!$ or $2$.

* So, our probability would be:

  * $2!(0.5 \times 0.5)$

  * $2!(0.25)$

  * $2(0.25)$

  * $0.5$

Another example is:

* We select 3 toothbrushes from a pack of 18. There are 6 red, 6 blue, and 6 green toothbrushes.

* We need to find the probability of getting 1 toothbrush of each colour.

* The possibilities of getting 1 toothbrush of each colour are:

  * Blue Red Green, Blue Green Red, Green Blue Red, Green Red Blue, Red Green Blue, Red Blue Green.

* There are $6$ possible orders, or $3!$

* So we will multiply our probability by $3!$

* The Probability would be:

  * $3!(\frac{6}{18} \times \frac{6}{17} \times \frac{6}{16})$

  * $6(\frac{216}{4896})$

  * $\frac{9}{34}$
