* In the table above, we are given data regarding the age and gender of a population.

* The following is a Venn Diagram for the data given above.

* The number 10 represents the number of People who are Male and under 18.

* The number 12 represents the number of People who are Male and above 18

* The number 9 represents the number of People who are **NOT** Male and above 18

* The number 14 represents the number of People who are **NOT** Male and **NOT** above 18.

### Advantages of Venn Diagrams:

* Easy and Quick to interpret.

### Disadvantages of Venn Diagrams:

* Not suitable for representing data across many categories.

* Relationships between data are not highlighted.
