## Two-Way Tables

* A table used to display data that is categorized by (usually) 2 variables.

* The table shown above categorizes Fries and Nachos by their size.

* The Rows/Columns can be added to find the desired totals.

* For example,

  * To find the number of boxes of fries ordered

    * Add the values in the row labelled “Fries”

    * $13+35+24 = 72$ boxes of fries.

* ### Advantages of Two-Way Tables:

  * Easy to comprehend.

  * Compresses large datasets.

* ### Disadvantages of Two-Way Tables:

  * Can only analyze a limited number of variables.

  * Not ideal for continuous data.
