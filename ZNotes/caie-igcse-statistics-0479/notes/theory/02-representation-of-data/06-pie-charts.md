## Pie Charts

* A method of representing data in which a “Pie” is divided to show proportions of members in a population.

* There are 2 types of Pie Charts: “Simple Pie Charts” and “Comparative Pie Charts”

### Comparative Pie Charts:

* They are used to display and compare two or more sets of related data.

* The area of the 2 charts must be proportional to their totals.

* The radii of the 2 pie charts differ according to the size of the population.

**If a pie chart of radius 6 cm represents 50 Devices, a pie chart of what radius should be used to represent 80 Devices?**

* $6^2 \pi : x^2\pi$

* $6^2 : x^2$

* The ratio of areas and the ratio of totals must be equal.

* $6^2 : x^2$

  50 : 80

* Cross-multiply and solve for $x$

* $\frac{36 \times 80}{50} = x^2$

* $x= 7.6$

* Hence, the second pie chart should use a radius of 7.6 cm.

### Advantages of Pie Charts:

* Easy and Quick to interpret

* Proportions of each category in the population are highlighted

* Visually Appealing

### Disadvantages of Pie Charts:

* Not suitable when data is divided into many categories

* Difficult to find out exact numerical values unless labelled.
