## Bar Charts

* A method of representing data in which data is represented through “Bars” of different sizes.

* There are 4 types of Bar Charts.

### Simple Bar Charts:

* Are used to compare discrete categories of data.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/25c291e2b156f6fd8cdafdc1.webp =547x)

### Sectional Bar Charts:

* Used to compare proportional contributions within a category.

* Each Bar represents a total and is divided into sections

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/fec663f4866f6820af40ea10.webp =443x)

### Sectional Percentage Bar Charts:

* Each Bar represents a total and is drawn to a height of 100%.

* The Bar is divided into sections, and each section represents a set percentage of the total.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/955c89a69e2edc9b355fafe9.webp =477x)

### Multiple Bar Charts:

* Used to compare 2 or more sets of related data.

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/c7a999ed07fab9e83d6e363e.webp =446x)

### Advantages of Bar Charts:

* Easy to interpret

* Frequencies can easily be seen and compared.

### Disadvantages of Bar Charts:

* Not suitable for comparing large amounts of data.

* Not suitable for continuous data.
