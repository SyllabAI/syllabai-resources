## Box and Whisker Diagrams

* A method of representing data in which data is displayed through measures of Dispersion and Central Tendency.

* Drawn on graph paper.

* The smallest and largest values in the data are placed at the initial and final points of the diagram.

### Advantages of Box and Whisker Diagram:

* Measures of central tendency and dispersion are highlighted.

* Side-by-side comparison is easy when two diagrams are made on top of each other

### Disadvantages of Box and Whisker Diagram:

* Not visually appealing

* Comparatively harder to interpret.
