## Standard Deviation

* The Standard Deviation is a measure of how spread out data is compared to its Mean.

* The formulae for Standard Deviation are:

  * $\sqrt{\frac{fx^2}{f} - (\frac{fx}{f})^2}$
  * Where:
    * $x$ is the value of the data

    * $f$ is the corresponding frequency
  * **AND**
  * $\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x-\bar{x})^2}{f}}$
  * Where:
    * $x$ is the value of the data

    * $\bar{x}$ is the mean of all the data

    * $f$ is the corresponding frequency

* To find the Standard Deviation, we also need to find the Mean of the dataset.
