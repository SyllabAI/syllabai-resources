# Quartiles, Percentiles and Measures of Dispersion

## Key Facts

### Quartiles:

* Values that divide data into 4 equal parts

* The three quartiles are:

  * First Quartile / Lower Quartile

    * 25th Percentile of a dataset

  * Second Quartile / Median

    * 50th Percentile of a dataset

  * Third Quartile / Upper Quartile

    * 75th Percentile of a dataset

### Percentile:

* The value below which a set percentage of values in a dataset fall.
* For example:
  * If there are 100 people in line for a show.

  * The 60th person in line is at the 60th percentile.

  * This means that 59 people are behind him and 40 people are in front of him.

### Measures of Dispersion:

* Characteristics of data that show how spread out / scattered the data is.

* There are 3 measures of dispersion:

  * Range

  * Standard Deviation

  * Interquartile Range (IQR)
