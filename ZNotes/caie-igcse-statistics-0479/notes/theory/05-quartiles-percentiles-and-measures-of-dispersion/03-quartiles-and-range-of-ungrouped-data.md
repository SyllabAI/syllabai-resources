### Finding the Quartiles:

* Lower Quartile:

  * $Q_1 = \frac{n+1}{4}$

  * $ = \frac{19+1}{4}$

  * $Q_1 = 5$

    * The lower quartile is the 5th value in the dataset.

* Upper Quartile:

  * $Q_3 = \frac{3(n+1)}{4}$

  * $ = \frac{3(19+1)}{4}$

  * $Q_1 = 15$

    * The lower quartile is the 15th value in the dataset.

* Median:

  * $Q_2 = \frac{2(n+1)}{4}$

  * $ = \frac{2(19+1)}{4}$

  * $Q_1 = 10$

    * The lower quartile is the 10th value in the dataset.

* Now that we have found the location of each quartile, we can locate the answer using the cumulative frequency calculated in the table.

* The 5th value corresponds to 1; hence, 1 stuffed animal is our Lower Quartile.

* The 15th value corresponds to 4; hence, 4 stuffed animals are our Upper Quartile.

* The 10th value corresponds to 2; hence, our Median is 2 stuffed animals.

### Finding the Range:

* To find the range, we will simply subtract the minimum value from the maximum value

* Range

  * $\text{max} - \text{min}$

  * $5 - 0$

  * $= 5$

* Hence, our range is 5.
