## Calculating Price Relatives

* To calculate the Price Relative of an item, A Base Year is needed.

* A Base Year is an Index Number assigned a value of 100, and each future/past year has a value relative to the Base Year.

* The formula for Price Relative is:

$$

\frac{Price\ in\ Year\ x}{Price\ Relative\ in\ Year\ x} = \frac{Price\ in\ Base\ Year}{Price\ Relative\ in\ Base\ Year}

$$

### For Example:

* A Box of Tissues cost $€1.50$ in 2024. The Next year, The Price had increased to $€1.75$. Find the Price Relative for 2025 based on the Price in 2024.

  * We are given the prices of the Box of Tissues in 2 different years, and 2024 has been defined as the base year.

  * We can input these values into the formula

  * $\frac{Price\ in\ Year\ x}{Price\ Relative\ in\ Year\ x} = \frac{Price\ in\ Base\ Year}{Price\ Relative\ in\ Base\ Year}$

  * $\frac{1.75}{x} = \frac{1.50}{100}$

  * $x = 116.67$

* Hence, the price relative in 2025 is 116.67.

* We can also say that the price in 2025 is 16.67% greater than the price in 2024.
