## Example Scenario

* Probability Distributions and Expected Prizes are not just used to calculate earnings, but they may also be used in other scenarios.

### Example:

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/161f5d94e7f0f06ca669799b.webp =463x)

* Above, we are given a circular card with the probability of landing on each segment and the score for landing on each segment.

* We need to find the probability $x$ and the expected score for spinning the card 15 times.

* To find $x$:

  * We know that $\Sigma P(x) = 1$

  * $1 = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{3} + x$

  * $x = \frac{1}{6}$

* Now that we have every probability, we can find the expected score for 1 turn:

  * $\Sigma [x.(P)] = (\frac{1}{6} \times 10) + (\frac{1}{4} \times 5) + (\frac{1}{4} \times 5) + (\frac{1}{3} \times 3)$

  * $\Sigma [x.(P)] = 5.17$

* We have found the expected score for 1 turn. To find the expected score for 15 turns, we can multiply $\Sigma [x.(P)]$ by 15

  * $\Sigma [x.(P)] \times 15$

  * $5.17 \times 15$

  * $77.55$

* Hence, the expected score for 15 turns is 77.55
