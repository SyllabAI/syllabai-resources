# Bivariate Distributions

## Key Facts

* A Bivariate Distribution is a statistical measure that displays the relationship between 2 variables.

* The variables may or may not be related to each other.

### Types of Variables:

* Independent Variables:

  * Usually placed on the $x$ axis.

  * Is the variable that acts as the “Cause” and triggers a change in the value of the dependent variable.

* Dependent Variable:

  * Usually placed on the $y$ axis.

  * Is the variable that is affected by the “Cause” or the independent variable.
