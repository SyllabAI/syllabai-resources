* A teacher decides to plot a Bivariate Distribution to find out the correlation between Physics and Maths exam scores.

* To plot Bivariate data, we need to decide the $x$ and $y$ axes.

* For this example, we will take Maths on the $y$ axis and Physics on the $x$ axis.

  * For example, the plot of Student A will be at (54, 66)

* Then, we will use a suitable scale on our graph and plot all the points.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/46da37cdacfdeba07816d1da.webp =785x)

* Although it is possible, it is difficult to decipher the correlation between Math and Physics scores using this diagram.

* To make it easier, a line of best fit is drawn.

* A line of best fit can be drawn by eye; however, it is more accurate to draw it using semi-averages.

* There are 3 averages that need to be found to draw the line of best fit.

* They are the Arithmetic Mean and the two Semi-Averages

### Finding the Arithmetic Mean:

* We will find the Arithmetic Mean of $x$ and $y$ (represented by $\bar{x}$ and $\bar{y}$)  to get the average point.

  * $\bar{x} = \frac{54+92+67+78+57+93+76+56+100}{9}$

  * $\bar{x} = 74.8$

  * $\bar{y} = \frac{66+98+75+62+45+84+68+65+92}{9}$

  * $\bar{y} = 72.8$

* Hence, the average point is (74.8, 72.8)

### Finding the Semi-Averages:

* To find the semi-averages, we must first arrange the pairs in ascending order of $x$.
* To find the semi-averages, we **must** have an even number of pairs.

* Now that we have divided the data into 2 halves, we will find the arithmetic mean of both halves.

  * $\bar{x_1} = \frac{54+56+57+67}{4}$

  * $\bar{x_1} = 58.5$

  * $\bar{y_1} =\frac{66+65+45+75}{4}$

  * $\bar{y_1} = 62.75$

* The first semi-average is (58.5, 62.75)

  * $\bar{x_2} = \frac{78+92+93+100}{4}$

  * $\bar{x_2} = 90.75$

  * $\bar{y_2} = \frac{62+98+84+92}{4}$

  * $\bar{y_2} = 84$

* The second semi-average is (90.25, 84)

### Drawing the Line of Best Fit:

* To draw the line of best fit, we will first mark our 3 averages on the Bivariate Plot we made earlier as concentric circle plots.

* Our 3 averages were:

  * (58.5, 62.75)

  * (74.8, 72.8)

  * (90.25, 84)

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/f3b0ffaf3d0c2e55e119dab7.webp =804x)

* Now we can draw a line of best fit through these points.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/d8827811f6acab30b6235605.webp =819x)

* Now, using this line of best fit, we can decipher that the correlation between physics and maths scores is positive and strong.
