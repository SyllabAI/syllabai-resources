## Correlation

* Correlation is defined as the relationship between variables.

* There are 5 main types of correlations:

  * Positive Correlation

  * Negative Correlation

  * Weak Correlation

  * Strong Correlation

  * No Correlation

### Positive Correlation:

* A positive correlation is shown when both variables are directly proportional to each other.

* Meaning that when the variable on the $x$ axis increases/decreases, so does the variable on the $y$ axis.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/82525cbb24b43dd4c22d3837.webp =367x)

* The scatter plot above is an example of a positive correlation, as when the values of the variables on the $x$ axis increase, so do the values of the variables on the $y$ axis.

* The positive gradient of the line of best fit also indicates that the correlation is positive.

### Negative Correlation:

* A negative correlation is shown when 2 variables are inversely proportional to each other.

* Meaning that when the variable on the $x$ axis increases/decreases, the variable on the $y$ axis decreases/increases.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/66c53a765f505ad3e1a0ac4d.webp =374x)

* The scatter plot above is an example of a negative correlation, as when the values on the $x$ axis increase, there is an overall decrease in the values on the $y$ axis.

* The gradient of the line of best fit is also negative, hence once again suggesting that the correlation between the $x$ and $y$ axes is negative.

### Strong Correlation:

* A strong correlation means that the 2 variables have a strong relation to one another, and that they are greatly affected by one another.

* A clear indication of a strong correlation is that most of the points are usually clustered together near the line of best fit.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/f461b384185e75941837dacb.webp =365x)

* The Scatter Plot above is an example of a Strong Correlation, as most of the points are found close together near the line of best fit.

### Weak Correlation:

* A Weak Correlation means that the 2 variables have a weak relation to one another, and that they are not greatly affected by one another.

* A clear indication of a Weak Correlation is that most of the points are usually spread far away from each other and the line of best fit.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/88683addee79a2404efb7982.webp =371x)

* The Scatter Plot above is an example of a Weak Correlation as most of the points are spread far apart from each other and the line of best fit.

### No Correlation:

* No correlation means that the variables have no relation to one another.

* The line of best fit is usually straight, and there is no trend/pattern visible.
