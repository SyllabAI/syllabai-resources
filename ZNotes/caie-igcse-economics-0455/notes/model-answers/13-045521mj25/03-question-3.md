## Question 3

**3(a)** The reward to capital is **interest** and the reward to land is **rent**.

**3(b)** Enterprise is the factor of production involving decision making and the ability to take risks. Enterprise may decrease in an economy as the **quality of education** decreases and entrepreneurial skills are not taught properly and students are discouraged to become entrepreneurs. Entrepreneurs may **move to other countries** as there may be better economic prospects and higher profits in other countries.

**3(c)** If the cost of production increases due to an increase in the cost of raw materials, firms will raise the price of products to keep making a profit. Natural disasters and changes in weather conditions can create a shortage. For example, bad weather can create a shortage of agricultural crops. Prices would rise and demand for crops would decrease.

**3(d)** National minimum wage (NMW) is the minimum wage rate employers must pay workers, set by the government above the equilibrium wage rate.

* Raising the national minimum wage may cause firms to fire workers as they cannot afford to pay so many workers the mandated wage, ultimately increasing unemployment. This higher unemployment would reduce total demand as unemployed people do not have an income to spend on goods and services. Firms will reduce their output as consumers may be unemployed and unable to afford their products. As unemployment increases, Income tax revenue will fall and the government will have to spend more on unemployment benefits.

* People who receive low incomes will benefit from a rise in the national minimum wage as they will be able to afford more basic necessities, reducing absolute poverty. More people will be encouraged to enter the labour force as the reward for labour, wages, is higher. Higher wages may motivate workers and make them more productive.
