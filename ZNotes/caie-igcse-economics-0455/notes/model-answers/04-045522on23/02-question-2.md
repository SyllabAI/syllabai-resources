**2(a)** A capital good is a good used to produce another good or service, for example: a sewing machine

**2(b)** *The inflation rate is the percentage change in price levels.* A low inflation rate might make the country’s products more internationally competitive (cheaper compared to to products of other countries), and more exports may be sold. It can also create more certainty and encourage investment.

**2(c)** Protectionism is the restriction of international trade to protect domestic industries from foreign competition. To reduce protectionism, **tariffs** (taxes imposed on imports) can be removed, reducing the price of imports. Domestic firms face more competition, which only the most efficient firms will be able to overcome so more resources are devoted to them. The **quota** limit, limit of goods that can imported, can be increased and **embargoes**, bans on importing goods, can be removed, increasing the quantity of imports. Additionally, other countries may reduce their protectionism measures, allowing exports to be sold with less restrictions.
