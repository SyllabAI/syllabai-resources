## Question 4

**4(a)** Two costs of unemployment are high government spending on unemployment benefits and loss of income for unemployed people.

**4(b)** The private sector uses the price mechanism (interaction of demand and supply to determine prices) and employs the methods of production that have the least cost as they aim to make high profits. The public sector impose regulations, taxation, and provision of public goods. They are influenced by the social costs and benefits (total costs and benefits to society).

**4(c)** Demand for a foreign currency may increase if there is higher demand by other countries for that country’s products. Higher incomes in the buying country could cause increased demand for the foreign currency, as they have more purchasing power so higher demand for imports. Additionally, there may have been speculation that the currency will rise in price. The government may buy the currency to increase the exchange rate, as the currency will be in less supply and hold more value. More people may migrate to the country to, for example, buy a house and they would demand the foreign currency.

**4(d)**

* Inflation, which is a rise in the price level, might harm a country’s firms as the price of raw materials and capital goods may increase, increasing costs of production. Firms may lose profits or even go out of business. Workers may press for higher wages to be able to maintain their living standard and take industrial action, like strikes. It will be difficult for firms to plan ahead due to inflation, causing prices to fluctuate. Due to additional costs, like menu costs (the cost of changing the prices of products) and shoe leather costs (the costs involved in moving money around to gain higher interest rates), profits may fall.

* However, if the inflation is demand-pull inflation (increase in price level due to increased demand), sales may increase. If the inflation rates in other countries are higher, firms in the country could make products more internationally competitive. This means that even people in other countries will buy from firms abroad as the inflated prices are cheaper. Additionally, inflation may reduce the real value of debts, so less money (in real value) will have to be paid.
