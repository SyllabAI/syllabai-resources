## Question 2

**2(a)** The opportunity cost of producing primary sector goods include services and manufactured goods.

**2(b)** The national minimum wage is the minimum wage that employers must pay their workers. Increasing the national minimum wage can raise income of workers who are getting paid less. Providing education and training can improve the employment opportunities of workers.

**2(c)** Closure of firms reduces competition to remaining firms and can lead to a monopoly (single seller) being formed. Firms may become inefficient and begin to charge high prices, as consumers do not alternatives to switch to. Consumers may have to travel further to purchase products. Additionally, there may be less variety in products for customers to choose from.

**2(d)**

* The government should encourage people to walk to school and work as walking more may improve the health of the population because it is a physical activity. It may increase productivity and reduce healthcare costs. External costs (costs incurred by third parties) such as pollution and traffic congestion may decrease as vehicles are used less often.

* However, walking may not be as safe as other modes of transport. More people may be late if they walk to their office and job choice may be limited due to distance from home. Demand for bus and train travel may reduce, leading to bus and train drivers losing their jobs.
