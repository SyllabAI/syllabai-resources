## Question 4

**4(a)** There is lower spending and lower incomes during a recession.

**4(b)** Money must **portable**, meaning that it is easy to carry, and **limited in supply** so that it maintains its value.

**4(c)** Workers living in other countries may send money to their family to support them, improving their living standards as their families will be able to buy more goods and services. Workers may receive training abroad and become aware of new technologies which they can bring back to their country, increasing the quality of output. If there are a shortage of jobs at home, unemployment may reduce when some of the country’s unemployed people leave to work in other countries.

**4(d)**

* Consumers might benefit from firms buying new capital equipment as it may increase efficiency, decreasing the cost of production and allowing firms to charge lower prices. Less mistakes may be made if production is capital intensive (rather than labour intensive), and the quality of output may increase.

* However, prices may increase in the short term to cover the cost of purchasing capital equipment. Quality of output may decrease in the short term as the new capital equipment may be unfamiliar to workers. Additionally, products may become more standardised (identical).
