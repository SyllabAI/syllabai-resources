## Question 5

**5(a)** Rich households spend more because they may wish to buy **luxury goods and services**, which are expensive. Additionally, rich people may have **more confidence** in future prospects and think that they will be able to earn back the money they have spent.

**5(b)** A point *inside* the PPC means that resources like labour are not being used efficiently, and therefore output is lower than the maximum possible output achievable with the current resources. A point *on* the PPC means that all resources are being used efficiently and the output is the maximum possible output that can be produced with the resources currently available.

**5(c)** People who have been unemployed for more than one year may become **less skilled** as they have not been putting their skills to practice for a long time. There may be new skills that employers look for, like operating technologically advanced machines, that unemployed are not familiar with which makes them less attractive to hire. Qualifications that unemployed people have received may become **out of date** and many jobs require workers to have valid qualifications. Unemployed people may have to be **re-trained** which is expensive, so firms prefer not to hire people who have been unemployed for a long time. Additionally, if people are unemployed for a long time, it may indicate that the economy is in recession and industries are in decline. This means that demand for products is low and firms are unwilling to employ people because it will cost firms more and reduce their profits.

**5(d)** A merger occurs when the owners of two or more firms agree to join their firms to create one big firm.

* The large firm created can produce more a higher total output. It can take advantage of economies of scale (like purchasing economies of scale - buying raw materials from suppliers in bulk to receive discounts) and therefore have lower average costs of production. Lower costs of productions allows firms to charge lower prices without losing profit. The quality of products may also improve as the large firm can spend more on research and development of products.

* However, a merger can also result in a large firm facing diseconomies of scale. This includes communication diseconomies in which it takes a long time to communicate with managers and come to decisions because the firm is too large. This leads to increased average costs and firms may begin to charge higher prices to continue making a profit. Additionally, mergers result in less firms being in the market and increases the monopoly power of the firm created as a result of a merger. This means that the large firm can charge low prices and less innovative products because they know customer will still buy from them as there are not many other firms to buy from.
