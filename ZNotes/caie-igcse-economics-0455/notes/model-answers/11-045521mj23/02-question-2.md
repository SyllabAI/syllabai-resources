## Question 2

**2(a)** Money acts as a **medium of exchange** (it can exchanged to get goods and services) and **unit of account** (it can be used to compare the relative values of products).

**2(b)** If education improves, people may **learn more about the harmful effects** of cigarettes and discourage them from buying cigarettes. This reduces the demand for cigarettes. People may **learn more about the health benefits** of consuming fruit, encouraging them to buy more fruit and increasing the demand for fruit.

**2(c)** Tax revenue is government income, which can be spent on, for example, education or healthcare. This can increase if more people are employed as more people will be earning income. From this income, some amount will be paid to the government as **income tax**, increasing tax revenue. Additionally, corporation tax is the direct tax on profits of a firm. If firms earn more profit then more **corporate tax** revenue will be received by the government.

**2(d)** Unemployment benefits are monetary benefits given to unemployed people.

* Giving unemployment benefits increases the income of those who earn little or no income. They can use this income to buy more products and enjoy a better living standard, reducing relative poverty. Absolute poverty (the state in where individuals cannot afford the minimum necessities for survival) is therefore reduced by giving unemployment benefits. People who receive unemployment benefits are now willing to and able to buy more products, so total demand increases. Firms will expand and hire more workers to increase their supply to match total demand, so employment increases. Previously unemployed people may be hired so they receive income to buy basic necessities and poverty decreases.

* However, unemployed people may not wish to be employed because then they would stop receiving unemployment benefits. Providing unemployment benefits may not be help all the unemployed as they may not claim the benefits. Other groups who receive low incomes, like retired people and sick people will not get unemployment benefits. Additionally, instead of spending on unemployment benefits, the government can spend on improving education and healthcare, which may help people more.
