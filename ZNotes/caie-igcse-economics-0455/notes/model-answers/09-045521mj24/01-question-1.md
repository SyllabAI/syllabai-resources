**1(g)**

* Small firms are likely to be able to offer more personalised services, as they have a smaller number of customers and will know their **individual requirements** better. Additionally, in a market economy especially, a large number of small firms are likely to exist, so each firm has more competition. This competition encourages small firms to keep **prices low** and produce **high quality products** to prevent customers from buying from other firms.

* Small firms usually earn lower profits because they do not have enough **financial resources** as large firms to make many products. As a result, they may not be able to invest their profit and create high quality products.
