## Question 4

**4(a)** The tertiary sector is the sector covers the provision of services. An example is banking.

**4(b)** A private benefit is enjoyed by the people consuming or producing the product. For example, a person who receives healthcare services will enjoy better health. An external benefit is enjoyed someone who is not directly involved in consuming or producing the product. For example, if other people receive healthcare services and get vaccinated, a person who is not vaccinated is less likely to catch a virus.

**4(c)** A horizontal merger happens when two firms in the **same industry and same stage of production** combine. For example, an coffee shop merging with a competing coffee shop to make one, bigger coffee shop. A government may prevent a merger if it thinks that the new firm will become a **monopoly** (a single firm that dominates the market). The government may think that the new firm will abuse their power, charging high prices because they know that there are no other firms consumers can buy from. As a result, resources will not be allocated efficiently and market failure will occur. The government may think that a horizontal merger will result in a firm becoming too big. A firm that is too big may experience **diseconomies of scale**, where the average cost of production increases. An example is managerial diseconomies which is when a firm is too large that it is difficult to manage and decision making is slowed down.
