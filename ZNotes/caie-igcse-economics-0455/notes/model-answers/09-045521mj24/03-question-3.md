## Question 3

**3(a)** In a mixed economic system, there is private ownership of firms as well as government ownership. This means that both the private sector and public sector allocate resources in a mixed economic system.

**3(b)** A larger population means that there are more consumers with wants and needs. As the size of the population of a country increases, **total demand** increases. As population size increases, the number of people **willing and able to work** (labour force) increases. Firms can easily recruit workers and with more workers, more output can be produced.

**3(c)** One of the governments macroeconomic aims is **stable prices**, which is a result of a low inflation rate. When prices are stable, consumers know that prices will not increase greatly and continue to be affordable, so they will feel more confident. Inflation can create menu costs, which is the cost of changing prices, and shoe leather costs, which is the cost of shopping around for lowest prices.

**3(d)**

* An increase in air travel means there is an increase in demand for air travel, encouraging the air travel industry to expand and take on more workers to provide services to more customers. As a result of an increase in air travel, other industries may experience an increase in output. For example, there will be more tourism with more air travel. This will reduce unemployment in the global economy. The geographical mobility of workers, which is the ability of a worker to move to other locations for their work, will increase as air travel makes it easier for people to travel.

* However, air travel causes noise and air pollution, which can contribute to climate change. If demand for air travel increases so much, new airports will have to be built. This leaves less land for other uses, like agriculture. People may prefer air travel over other forms of transport. If demand for other forms of transport, like sea travel, decreases then the industry may become smaller and workers in these industries may become unemployed.
