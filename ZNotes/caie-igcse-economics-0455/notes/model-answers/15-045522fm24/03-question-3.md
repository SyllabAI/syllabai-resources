## Question 3

**3(a)** External growth is the increase in the size of a firm due to a **merger** (two or more firms combining to create one large firm).

**3(b)** The low-skilled workers may be in high demand due to **expansion of the industry** they are a part of. For example, if the construction industry expands, demand for unskilled construction workers will also increase.The job may be **dangerous** so a high wage is needed to attract workers, such as window cleaners of tall buildings.

**3(c)** Increased government spending can increase **total demand** (government spending is a component of total demand), causing firms to expand and take up more workers, **reducing cyclical unemployment**. Additionally, a cut in corporate tax will allow firms to retain more of their profit, which may encourage them to **invest their profits** and expand. Increased government spending on infrastructure may attract **MNCs**, who can provide job opportunities.

**3(d)**

* A government should provide free fruit as it is a merit good, and the social benefit of consuming fruit is greater than the private benefit. It may be under consumed if the government does not intervene as people do not realise the full benefits of consuming fruit. Consuming fruit could improve workers’ health, increasing productivity. Also, by providing it for free, poor people will be able to consume fruit.

* However, by providing fruit for free, it may be over consumed, leading to a shortage. Some fruit may even be wasted. There is also an opportunity cost that comes with this, as government spending on education or healthcare might have to be reduced.
