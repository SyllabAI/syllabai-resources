## Question 4

**4(a)** A shortage occurs when demand exceeds supply.

**4(b)** A firm can buy **raw materials for a cheaper price**, reducing the cost of production. It can also train workers to increase their **efficiency**, so that costs are minimised.

**4(c)** The brand of luxury chocolate is likely to have **substitutes**. If price increases, customers can switch to buying from other brands. Chocolate is **not a necessity** and its **purchase can be postponed**. Considering these factors, demand for the brand of chocolate will be price elastic. Salt has **no substitutes**, may be considered a **necessity**, and its purchase takes up a **small proportion of income**. Considering these factors, demand for salt will be price inelastic.

**4(d)**

* A country should import most of the food it consumes as it may not have the resources to produce food. By importing food, the country will be able to focus on producing other goods and services. Wages may increase and working conditions may improve in the secondary and tertiary sectors. If food were to be produced in a country, the output could be affected by weather.

* However, by importing most of the food it consumes, a country may become dependent on other countries for food, which is a basic necessity. Foreign producers could increase the price to high levels, which could make food inaccessible to the poor. There may be also disruptions in supply due to restriction on trade.
