## Question 5

**5(a)** The unemployment rate is the number of unemployed divided by the labour force, multiplied by 100.

**5(b)** Cigarettes are a **demerit** (cause more harm than consumers realise) good, they are over consumed and overproduced. Smoking harms the health of smokers. Discouraging smoking could improve health of the population, as passive smoking will reduce.

**5(c)** The price of exports may rise due to increases in **costs of production**, causing a fall in demand for exports. Other countries may impose **trade restrictions** such as tariffs, which would reduce the demand for exports. Additionally, **demand for products in the home country** may increase, and firms may sell more of their output in their home country, rather than other countries.

**5(d)**

* Discover of oil in a country can increase the output of oil, so more oil can be **exported** and less oil will be imported. The discovery may generate **more jobs** related to oil extraction, increasing employment and incomes. This will increase the **country’s GDP**.

* The discovery of oil can create **external costs** (costs incurred by third parties) like pollution and damage the habitats of animals. **Renewable resources** may be in high demand, replacing oil so selling oil may not be profitable. Oil may be depleted quickly and **working conditions** during extraction may be poor, so workers may be fall ill.
