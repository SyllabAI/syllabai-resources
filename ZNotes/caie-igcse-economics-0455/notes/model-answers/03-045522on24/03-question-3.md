* A high level of poverty might exist in a market economic system because there may be high levels of unemployment without the existence of public sector jobs. As the government does not intervene, welfare benefits will not be given to people with low/no incomes, increasing poverty. Merit goods such as healthcare and education may be under-consumer and so underproduced. Monopolies may develop, restricting output and setting high prices, making products unaffordable. 

* However, with a high level of competition, firms will be incentivised to keep prices low and make a profit.
