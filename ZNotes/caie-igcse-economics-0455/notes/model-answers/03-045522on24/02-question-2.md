* Free trade might increase economic growth because it allows countries to specialise in what they are good at producing. Due to the increased levels of competition from firms in other countries, productivity may increase and firms will be encouraged to keep costs of production low, which will increase demand for their products. Firms may be able to buy cheaper raw materials and capital goods, due to free trade so costs are lowered. Free trade allows for countries to export more to other industries, improving the trade position. 

* However, free trade may lead to the closure of infant industries (developing industries) and speed up the closure of industries in decline. Imports may replace domestic output. Free trade may cause firms to carry out unfair competition, such as dumping where exports are sold for a very cheap price, as a result demand for domestic products decreases, harming domestic firms.
