## Question 5

**5(a)** Influences on household spending include **income** and **interest rate**.

**5(b)** Older workers are likely to have **more experience** and require less training, having more skills than younger workers. Older workers may have different attitudes to work than younger workers, and may be **more reliable** and punctual to work.

**5(c)**

* Total demand is the sum of consumer spending (household spending), investment, government spending, and net exports. This means that as household spending increases, total demand increases.

* Total demand may increase so much that it exceeds supply. A high level of demand would cause firms to increase prices to make a greater profit. This increase in price level is **demand-pull inflation**. Additionally, higher demand encourages firms to expand and increase their output. They may hire new workers, increasing the cost of production as firms have to pay more wages. Firms will increase the price of their products to cover the higher cost of production. This increase in price level is **cost-push inflation**.

**5(d)**

* A government should aim to increase its imports because it may have scarce resources, making it difficult to produce goods and services in the country. If the country is facing a shortage of a product on the home market, importing the product would reduce the shortage and make it available to more people. Government revenue can increase with imports, as more import tariff (tax on imported goods) revenue is made. This revenue can be spent on education or healthcare, improving living standards. Firms could reduce their cost of production as cheaper raw materials and capital goods can be imported.

* However, more imports could worsen the current account balance, even causing a trade deficit (import expenditure exceeding export revenue). Aiming to increase imports would cause people to switch from buying on the domestic market to buying imports. This may cause domestic firms to go out of business due to a lack of demand for their products. Workers at these firms would become unemployed, increasing total unemployment.
