## Question 4

**4(a)** Two types of unemployment are **structural** and **cyclical** unemployment.

**4(b)** Receiving money from relatives working abroad gives families more money to spend on food and other basic necessities, reducing poverty. Additionally, the money received could be spent on quality healthcare which could greatly increase life expectancy.

**4(c)** An increase in **corporation tax**, a type of direct tax, **reduces the amount of profit** a firm can keep. Firms may have to reduce their output as they have less money. This may lead to a **recession** if the level output falls too much. Additionally, with reduced profit, **investment** made by firms may fall which lowers total demand. A lack of demand leads to **cyclical unemployment**, when there are more job vacancies than the number of unemployed. When faced with increased tax, firms may reduce employment to **cut the total cost** and maintain profit.
