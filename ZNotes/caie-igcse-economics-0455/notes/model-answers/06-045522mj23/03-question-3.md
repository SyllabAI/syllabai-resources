## Question 3

**3(a)** Objectives of private sector firms include **profit maximisation** and **growth**.

**3(b)** Quantity of land (natural resources) may increase due to discovery of natural resources, such as oil. Quality of land may increase due to reduction of pollution, preventing damage to land.

**3(c)** Spending on capital goods will increase demand, encouraging firms to expand their output. More workers may be employed to work with the capital equipment, reducing cyclical unemployment. Investment in training and education can improve productivity and mobility of workers, reducing structural unemployment (unemployment due to a mismatch between the jobs and skills of the unemployment). However, increase in investment could cause technological unemployment as capital goods may replace workers.

**3(d)**

* Preventing a rise in unemployment could result in economic growth as more workers contribute to increased output. Tax revenue may increase as more people stay employed and more income tax revenue is collected. Government spending on unemployment benefits may decrease and living standards may improve as people can afford more goods and services.

* However, unemployment may already be low, and a reduction in unemployment could lead to inflation. Workers will have more bargaining power and may press for higher wages, increasing costs of production and firms will increase their prices (cost-push inflation). Higher incomes may cause a current account deficit due to increased import expenditure. Additionally, the government policy measures have an opportunity cost and the government may have other objectives to focus on.
