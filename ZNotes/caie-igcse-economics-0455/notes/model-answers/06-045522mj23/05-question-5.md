## Question 5

**5(a)** The opportunity cost of households spending their income is saving and the opportunity cost of Chinese firms exporting their goods and services is selling their products at home.

**5(b)** Top sportspeople are in high demand, as large crowds watch the events top sportspeople participate in. Top sportspeople are highly skilled and undergo years of training, so are difficult to replace.

**5(c)** Tariffs (taxes on imports) may be imposed on imports to reduce demand for imports, by making them more expensive, and improve the current account balance. They may also be imposed to protect infant industries and declining industries, encouraging people to switch from buying imports to buying domestically produced products.

**5(d)**

* A country with a high economic growth rate might have a deficit on the current account of its balance of payments because incomes will rise and consumers may buy more imports. Firms may switch from selling products in foreign markets to selling products in the growing home market, reducing export revenue. Firms may import more raw materials and capital goods. The country may experience inflation, reducing its international price competitiveness.

* However, the high economic growth rate may have been led by exports. Economic growth may encourage investment, so the quality of product made domestically may increase and prices may be lowered. The higher tax revenue from economic growth could be spent on healthcare and education, improving productivity. The country may impose restrictions on trade, reducing import expenditure.
