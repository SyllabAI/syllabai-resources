## Question 4

**4(a)** A government may set a NMW (national minimum wage) to raise wages, reducing income inequality and reduce poverty.

**4(b)** Increase in demand for the currency due to higher incomes abroad may increase the value of a country’s floating foreign exchange rate. Additionally, speculation that the currency will rise in value may increase the value of a country’s floating foreign exchange rate as more people will buy the currency, decreasing its supply.

**4(c)** An increase in the price of milk will cause demand for milk to fall. If demand for milk is price elastic, revenue of milk producers will fall as quantity demanded will decrease by a greater percentage than rise in price. However if demand is price inelastic, revenue will increase as quantity demanded will fall by a smaller percentage than rise in price. Demand for soft drinks will likely rise and revenue of soft drink producers will increase. This is because soft drinks and milk are substitutes.

* Small firms may be more likely to go out of business than large firms because they may not be able to take advantage of economies of scale (like purchasing economies - buying in bulk), and therefore small firms may face high average costs. Small firms are also less likely to experience financial economies of scale and may face difficulty in obtaining a bank loan. Small firms may be new and have inexperienced entrepreneurs who may not be able to make the best decisions for the firm.

* However, large firms are more likely to experience diseconomies of scale than small firms. Small firms may receive financial support from the government, like subsidies. They may also have customer loyalty and be more flexible than large firms, allowing them to stay in business for longer.
