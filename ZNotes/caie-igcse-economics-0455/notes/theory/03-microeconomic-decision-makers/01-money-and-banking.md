Without money, economies would rely on **barter**, which is:

* **Inefficient**, because it requires a **double coincidence of wants** (you must find someone who has what you want *and* wants what you have).

* Hard to **store value or compare prices**.

### Characteristics of Money

* **Acceptability**: Everyone in the economy must trust and willingly accept it as payment for goods, services, and debts

* **Durability**: It must resist physical wear and tear so it can last a long time in circulation without getting easily damaged

* **Divisibility**: It should be easily broken down into smaller, precise denominations or units to buy items of varying prices

* **Portability**: It must be lightweight and easy to carry around for everyday transactions

* **Uniformity** (or Homogeneity): Identical units or denominations must look the same and hold the exact same value

* **Scarcity**: Its supply must be limited and controlled by a monetary authority like a **Central Bank** so it doesn't lose its purchasing power

* **Recognizability**: It must be easily identified as genuine currency to prevent counterfeiting and confusion

### **Why Banks are Important to the Economy**:

* Encourage **saving**

* Allow **borrowing for investment**

* Enable **efficient transactions**

* Support **economic growth**

* Control **inflation and money supply** (through the central bank)
