## Trade Unions

### Definition

* A **trade union** is an organisation of workers formed to protect and promote their interests (e.g., wages, working conditions).

  ### Objectives

  * Raise **wages**.

  * Improve **working conditions**.

  * Protect workers from **exploitation**.

  * Provide **legal/financial support** to members.

### Methods of Action

* **Collective bargaining**: union negotiates with employers on behalf of workers.

* **Industrial action**: strikes, go-slows, work-to-rule to pressure employers.

* **Advisory role**: giving training and legal advice.

### Factors Affecting Union Power

* **Membership size**: larger unions = stronger bargaining power.

* **Legal framework**: government laws may restrict/allow union action.

* **Elasticity of demand for labour**: if demand is inelastic, unions can push wages up without much job loss.

* **Economic conditions**: in recessions, unions are weaker; in booms, stronger.

### Impact of Trade Unions

* **On workers**: higher wages, better conditions, job security.

* **On employers**: higher labour costs, possible strikes → lower profits.

* **On economy**: can improve productivity (happier workers) or reduce efficiency (if wage rises \> productivity).
