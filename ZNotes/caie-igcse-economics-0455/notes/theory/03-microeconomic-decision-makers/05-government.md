## Government

### Role of Government

The government is an important economic decision maker. It influences the economy by allocating resources, correcting market failures, and promoting economic welfare.

### Functions of Government -

**1. Taxation**

* The government charges direct and indirect taxes from consumers to raise revenue for public expenditure.

* Consumer and producer behaviour can also be influenced by taxes (for example - higher taxes on cigarettes reduce consumption).

**2. Regulation**

* The government sets laws and regulations to protect consumers, workers, and the environment.

* The government also promotes fair competition by preventing unfair business practices.

**3. Provision of Goods and Services**

* The government provides public goods (e.g. national defence, street lighting) and merit goods (e.g. education and healthcare) that may not be adequately supplied by the private sector.

**4. Redistribution of Income**

* The government uses taxation and welfare benefits to reduce income inequality and improve living standards.

**5. Promoting Economic Stability**

* The government aims to achieve low unemployment, stable prices, and sustainable economic growth through fiscal and monetary policies.
