## Market Economic System

* A market economy is an economy where most economic decisions are made by consumers and firms through the price mechanism, with minimal government intervention.
* Private firms and individuals do resource allocation
* This is also called a free market economy due to
  * Economic decisions being made by individuals and businesses.

  * Private ownership of resources like land, capital, and labor being owned by private individuals or firms, not the government.

  * Consumers having the freedom of choosing which products they want to buy and which they don’t.
* There is no purely free market economy in the world, but some countries have less government intervention than others

However, market failures can lead to the misallocation of scarce resources, resulting in outcomes that are wasteful, inefficient, or even damaging to both people and the environment.

For example (Further explanation of a disadvantage) -

Public goods are **non-excludable** (people cannot be prevented from using them) and **non-rival** (one person's use does not reduce another person's ability to use them). Because firms cannot easily charge consumers for these goods, private businesses have little incentive to provide them, so governments often provide them instead. Examples include **street lighting, national defence, and lighthouses**.

Examples of how the Market Economic System works in variety of different countries -

1. United States - High demand for electric vehicles raises prices and profits, encouraging firms like Tesla to increase production.

2. United Kingdom - Higher wages in the financial sector attract more labour to banking and finance.

3. Singapore - Housing rents rise with demand, encouraging developers to build more private housing.

4. Hong Kong - High property prices signal scarcity, encouraging private developers to invest in construction.
