## Market Failure

**Market failure** occurs when the **price mechanism (discussed earlier) fails to allocate resources efficiently**, leading to **over or under allocation of resources** and a loss of economic welfare.

There are many causes of market failure. Here are a few -

1. **Negative Externalities (Overproduction)**

   * Social cost \> private cost

   * Too much of the good is produced and consumed

2. **Positive Externalities (Underproduction)**

   * Social benefit \> private benefit

   * Too little of the good is produced and consumed

3. **Information failure,** which occurs when consumers or producers **do not have full or accurate information** (Like Consumers unaware of health risks, Workers unaware of job dangers, or an advertisment being misleading)

4. Merit goods being under consumed or demerit goods being overconsumed. (Education, healthcare, public transport being underconsumed, cigarettes and alcohol being overconsumed, typically lead to market failure).

5. **Factor Immobility**

   * Factors of production (labour and capital for example) cannot always move easily between industries or locations.

   * As a result, resources may remain unemployed or be used inefficiently, leading to **market failure**.

### Consequences of Market Failure -

* Misallocation of resources

* Overproduction or underproduction

* Welfare loss

* Negative effects on society

### Governments methods of correcting -

* Indirect taxes (reduce demerit goods)

* Subsidies (encourage merit goods)

* Regulations and laws

* Provision of public goods

* Information campaigns
