# The Basic Economic Problem

## The Nature of the Economic Problem

* The basic economic problem arises because **resources available are scarce in relation to people’s unlimited wants.**
  * **Example** - A country has limited land for growing crops. Therefore, its government needs to make a choice about what crops to grow and what not to.
* **Economic goods** - Scarce and limited compared to their requirement, which makes them unique and valuable. Any product with a price tag on it is considered an economic good. 
  * **Example** - Watch, headphones, a new gaming laptop, car etc.
* **Free Goods** - Goods that are abundant, and not scarce. Example - Sunlight, Oxygen etc. Due to their abundant supply, it is not possible to make a profit from supplying free goods.
* Water used to be a **free good**, but due to population increases, its demand has
  increased significantly, making it an **economic good** now.
