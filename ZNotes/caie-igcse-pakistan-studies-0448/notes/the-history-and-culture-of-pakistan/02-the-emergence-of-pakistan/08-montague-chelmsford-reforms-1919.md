## Montague Chelmsford Reforms (1919)

### Key Points

* Separate electorates were granted to some communities
* Council of Princely States was formed
* British introduced a diarchy in provinces
  * Certain powers in provinces were ‘reserved’ (under British, i.e, Finance, Irrigation, etc) and some ‘transferred’ (given to Indians i.e, Agriculture)
  * Elections were introduced for provincial representation
* Faced disliking from Indians as their powers remained advisory
  * Important powers remained under British control, so no real authority of the Indians
  * Several restrictions on qualification caused a very small percentage of Indians to be eligible to vote
  * The princely states’ council had no real purpose or power
