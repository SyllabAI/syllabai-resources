## Allahabad Address (1930)

### Key Points

* Delivered by Muhammad Iqbal at the All India Muslim League session in Allahabad

* Emphasized that Muslims in India were a distinct nation with their own culture, religion, and way of life

* Advocated for the creation of a separate Muslim state in north-western India

* Stressed the need for political unity among Muslims to protect their rights

* Highlighted that Hindus and Muslims had different social, cultural, and political interests

* Called on Muslims to seek autonomy in Muslim-majority areas

* Considered a key ideological foundation for Pakistan
