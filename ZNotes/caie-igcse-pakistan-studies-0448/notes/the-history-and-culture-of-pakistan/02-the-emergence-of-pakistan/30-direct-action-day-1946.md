## Direct Action Day (1946)

### Key Points

* Declared by the All India Muslim League on 16 August 1946

* Called to press the demand for Pakistan and show Muslim solidarity

* Aimed to demonstrate Muslim strength after failure of negotiations with Congress

* Led to widespread communal riots, particularly in Calcutta

* Violence resulted in thousands of deaths and property destruction

* Marked a turning point in Hindu-Muslim relations, increasing urgency for partition

* British authorities struggled to control the unrest, highlighting the need for a solution to the India problem
