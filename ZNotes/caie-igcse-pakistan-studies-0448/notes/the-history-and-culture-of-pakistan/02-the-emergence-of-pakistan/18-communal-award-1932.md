## Communal Award (1932)

### Key Points

* Leaders in India did not come up with solution to communal issues, hence British presented their own solution known as “Communal Award”
  * Was presented by Ramsay MacDonald (PM of Britain at the time)
* Separate electorates granted to Muslims
  * Seats were reduced from Muslim-Majority areas (e.g Bengal)
* Muslim League and Congress did not approve much of the Communal Award but Muslim League still accepted
