## Delhi Proposals (1927)

### Key Points

* It was an attempt by the Muslim League and Congress to reach an understanding
  * This was to be able to present a set of common aims infront of the British
* Jinnah served as a key member of these talks
* Jinnah stood firm asking for separate electorates for the Muslims to increase representation, but came to a compromise
* Jinnah stated they would end this demand if:
  * Muslims were given a third of representation in the Central Legislature
* Sindh was to be separated from Bombay
* Seats granted based on population in Punjab and Bengal
  * Some leaders of the Muslim League disagreed with this stance of Jinnah’s
* Specifically, those in Punjab
