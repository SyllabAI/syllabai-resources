## Quit India Movement (1942)

### Causes

* Congress knew this was the best time to exploit British and make demands due to their position post WW2
* Congress alarmed by Cripps Mission’s proposal for separation of provinces
* Presence of British caused looming threat of Japanese invasion in India

### Failures

* British arrested prominent participants of Quit India Movement
  * Gandhi and Nehru were also arrested
* Jinnah did not support it, called it unethical and blackmail
  * Improved British-Muslim relations
