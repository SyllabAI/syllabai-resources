## Simon Commission (1927)

### Key Points

* Set up in 1927 by the British government to review the Government of India Act 1919.

* All members were British; no Indian members were included.

* Aimed to suggest constitutional reforms for India.\

* The absence of Indian representation led to widespread protests across India.

* Slogan used by Indians: “Simon Go Back.”

* Political parties, students, and leaders including Congress, Muslims, and Sikhs opposed it.

* Demonstrations and strikes took place in many cities, often facing British repression.

* Led to the formation of the Nehru Report (1928) as an Indian alternative proposal.

* Highlighted the growing Indian demand for self-rule and end of British autocracy.
