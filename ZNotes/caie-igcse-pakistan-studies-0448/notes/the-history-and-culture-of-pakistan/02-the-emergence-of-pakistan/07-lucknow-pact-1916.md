## Lucknow Pact (1916)

### Causes

* Signed between the Muslim League and Congress to improve relations
* Improve Hindu-Muslim unity to pressure the British into accepting common demands
* Combat theories of British strategies of dividing India to maintain influence
* Form a list of shared demands for the next British reforms
* Muslims were dissatisfied with the British and no longer cared for loyalty to them after the reversal of the Partition of Bengal
* Jinnah (from the Muslim League) and Mahajan (from Congress) put in an effort to persuade their respective parties to be more cooperative with the other

### Key Points

* Congress and the Muslim League are finally able to cooperate
  * Congress accepted that there was a lack of concessions for Muslims
* The rights of minorities acknowledged
  * Proposed that no law should be passed until ¾ of the community agrees to it
* Congress accepted the demands of the Muslim League
  * Separate electorates’ demand for Muslims, reserved seats in councils
  * Boycott of British goods as a form of economic protest
