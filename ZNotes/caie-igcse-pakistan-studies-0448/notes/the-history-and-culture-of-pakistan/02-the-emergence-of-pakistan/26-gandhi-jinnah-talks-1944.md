## Gandhi Jinnah Talks (1944)

### Cause and Failure

* Gandhi wished to discuss the potential partition of India and the Muslims’ strong advocation for the Two Nation Theory with Jinnah to negotiate
* Gandhi wished for central government’s control over Defense whereas Jinnah wished for provincial government’s control over it
* Jinnah wished for Bengal and Assam to be granted to Muslims but Gandhi wished to divide them
* Gandhi did not appreciate the Two Nation Theory entirely
  * Two Nation Theory was not negotiable by Jinnah as it was now the main focus of the Muslim League

### Success

* Gandhi acknowledged possibility of partition
* Jinnah made aims of Muslim League clear and final
  * For formation of Pakistan
* Gandhi realized that now was the time for them to work on independence from the British
