## Simla Conference 1945

### Failure

* Aim was to establish a council granting equal power and authority to the Muslims and Hindus
* Jinnah disagreed when Congress wished to nominate Muslim representatives
  * Stood firm and implied that Muslim League was sole representative of Muslims in India
* Jinnah did not wish to cooperate with other parties to nominate Muslims
* Jinnah unhappy with seats granted to Sikhs because they would side with Hindus so indirectly Hindus would remain a majority

### Impact

* British now knew that partition was necessary and not negotiable
* Muslim League established as sole representative of Muslims
* It was now implied that British were ready to leave India soon so Muslims and Hindus began gearing up for partition
