## Simla Deputation (1906)

### Key Point

* Separate electorates granted to Muslims
* Growing influence of Sir Syed Ahmed’s Two-Nation Theory seen in action
* Growing confidence and political strength of Muslims through confirmed spots in the constitution
* Improving relations between Muslims and British
* Eventual establishment of Muslim League due to this growing influence
### Causes

* Growing Hindu nationalism and extremism
	* Push for Hindi to be made national language
* Lack of representation and influence of Muslim minority became concerning
* Growing influence and belief in Sir Syed’s Two-Nation Theory
