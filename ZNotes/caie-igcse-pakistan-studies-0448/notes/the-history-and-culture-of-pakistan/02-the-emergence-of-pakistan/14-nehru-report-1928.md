## Nehru Report (1928)

### Key Points

* Prepared by a committee headed by Motilal Nehru in response to the Simon Commission.

* Proposed dominion status for India within the British Empire.

* Recommended one uniform constitution for all of India.

* Rejected separate electorates; suggested joint electorates with reserved seats for minorities.

* Aimed to protect minority rights but Muslims were dissatisfied with the proposals.

* Recommended freedom of religion and protection of language and culture of minorities.
