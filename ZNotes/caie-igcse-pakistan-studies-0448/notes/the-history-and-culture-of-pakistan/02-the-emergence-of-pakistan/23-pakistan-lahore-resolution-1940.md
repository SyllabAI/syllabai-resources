## Pakistan/Lahore Resolution (1940)

### Key Points

* Passed by the All India Muslim League at its session in Lahore on 23 March 1940

* Called for independent states in Muslim-majority areas of India

* Emphasized the protection of Muslim political, social, and economic rights

* Recognized that Hindus and Muslims were distinct nations with separate interests

* Advocated for autonomy in Muslim-majority regions

* Marked a turning point in the Pakistan Movement

* Laid the ideological foundation for the creation of Pakistan in 1947
