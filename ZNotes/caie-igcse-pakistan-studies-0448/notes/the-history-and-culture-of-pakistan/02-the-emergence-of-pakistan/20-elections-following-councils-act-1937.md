## Elections following Councils Act (1937)

### Key Points

* Held under the Government of India Act 1935 which introduced provincial autonomy

* Elections conducted in provincial legislatures across British India

* Indian National Congress won majority in most provinces (Bengal, Madras, United Provinces, Bihar, Orissa, Bombay)

* Muslim League performed poorly, winning very few seats

* Hindu-Muslim political divide became more evident

* Congress initially hesitant to form coalition with Muslim League

* British retained defense, finance, and external affairs under their control

* Elections marked a shift towards self-governing provinces while ultimate power remained with the British
