## Reversal of the Partition of Bengal (1911)

### Reasons

* Strong Hindu Opposition:

  * Widespread protests, strikes, and boycotts (Swadeshi Movement) made administration difficult.

* Nationalist Pressure:

  * Extremist groups and Congress leaders demanded reunification, pressuring the British.

* Administrative Problems:

  * Partition failed to improve governance as intended; managing two provinces created new difficulties.\

* Economic Disruption:

  * Swadeshi boycott affected trade and revenue, harming British economic interests.

* Political Strategy of the British:

  * Reversal aimed to appease the Hindu majority while still protecting Muslim interests.

* Royal Declaration at Delhi Durbar (1911):

  * King George V announced the reunification of Bengal and the transfer of the capital from Calcutta to Delhi to ease tensions.
