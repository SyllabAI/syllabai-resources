## Jinnah’s 14 Points (1929)

### Key Points

* Drafted by Muhammad Ali Jinnah in response to the Nehru Report.

* Aimed to protect political rights of Muslims in a future self-governing India.

* Key points included:

  * Separate electorates for Muslims at both central and provincial levels.

  * Reserved seats for Muslims in provinces and central government.

  * Proportional representation for Muslims where they were a minority.

  * Protection of Muslim culture, religion, and education.

  * No constitutional changes without Muslim consent.

  * Served as a basis for future Muslim political demands leading to Pakistan Movement.
