## Cripps Mission (1942)

### Key Points

* British attempt at gaining cooperation of Gandhi and Jinnah with them
* Led by Sir Stratford Cripps to gain support during World War 2
* Suggested granting of Dominion Status to India

### Causes of Failure

* It proposed Dominion status of India but Congress wanted a finalized, immediate transfer of all power and authority
* Congress opposed idea of separation of provinces to prevent formation of separate Muslim majority
* Jinnah rejected it due to lack of finality and wish for Pakistan’s formation
