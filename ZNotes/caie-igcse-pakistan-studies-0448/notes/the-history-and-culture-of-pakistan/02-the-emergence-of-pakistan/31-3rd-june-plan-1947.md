## 3rd June Plan (1947)

### Key Points

* Announced by Lord Mountbatten, the last Viceroy of India, on 3 June 1947
* Proposed the partition of British India into two independent dominions: India and Pakistan
* Punjab and Bengal were to be divided based on religious majority
* Provided freedom for princely states to join either dominion or remain independent
* Proposed transfer of power to the Indian and Pakistani governments by 15 August 1947
* Included provisions for the protection of minorities in both countries
* Aimed to end British rule quickly while preventing further communal violence
* Accepted by Indian National Congress and Muslim League, leading to independence
