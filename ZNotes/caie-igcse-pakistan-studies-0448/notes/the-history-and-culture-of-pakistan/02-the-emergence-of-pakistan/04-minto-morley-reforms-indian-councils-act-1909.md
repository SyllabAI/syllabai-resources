## Minto Morley Reforms/Indian Councils Act (1909)

### Key Points

* Introduced limited diarchy in provinces.
* Enlarged Central and Provincial Legislative Councils with more Indian members.
* Introduced separate electorates for Muslims to protect their political interests.
* Voting rights were limited to a small, elite section of society.
* Legislative councils had limited powers; Viceroy and Governors retained ultimate authority.
* Recognised communal representation, boosting Muslim political confidence.
* First step towards constitutional reforms in India and future nationalist movements.

### Criticism/Controversy

  * Power of Viceroy: Retained ultimate authority, limiting real Indian participation.
  * Unfair property qualification: Only wealthy elites could vote; majority of Indians excluded.
  * No real self-rule: Reforms gave limited powers, mostly advisory, not legislative.
  * Divide and Rule policy evident: Introduction of separate Muslim electorates created communal divisions.
