## Cabinet Mission Plan (1946)

### Key Points

* British proposed an interim government while the British prepared to withdraw
* Proposed that India be divided into 3 parts (2 Muslim majority, one Hindu majority)
* Suggest All India Commission to establish whether partition would be granted or not
* Aimed to reach common ground amongst Muslims, Hindus, Sikhs, etc but failed

### Failure

* Jinnah accepted proposal of division initially but rejected when Hindus claimed they would not be obliged to follow through once British left
* The Muslim League and Congress did not want an All-India Commission
