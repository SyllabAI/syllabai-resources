## Amritsar Massacre (1919)

* Key Points:
  * Peaceful protest being held in Jallianwala Bagh by Indians
  * General Dyer arrived with troops and opened fire
    * 400 Indians killed and over 1200 injured
  * Investigation into matter done through ‘Hunter’s Committee’
    * Certain powers in provinces were ‘reserved’ (under British i.e Finance, Irrigation, etc) and some ‘transferred’ (given to Indians i.e Agriculture)
    * Elections introduced for provincial representation
