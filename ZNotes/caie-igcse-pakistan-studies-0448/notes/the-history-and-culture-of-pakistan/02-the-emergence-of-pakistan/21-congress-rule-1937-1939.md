## Congress Rule (1937-1939)

### Key Points

* Hindi made the official language
  * Muslims struggled with this as they did not know it, nor did they wish for this to happen.
* Growing nationalism by Hindus and urged to push Muslims out of India
  * “Bande Mataram” was a song that promoted Hindu nationalism
* Cow Slaughter banned
* Anti-Muslim riots/protest
* Warda Scheme established
  * Students would have to bow down to a photo of Gandhi in their schools at the beginning of the day; the curriculum now involved hand-spinning cotton, and religious education was removed.
* Widya Mandar Scheme established
  * Children (including Muslim ones) were taught Hindu heroes’ histories, and Hindu temples were made centres of education.
* Large gap made between Congress and Muslims, which would be impossible to recover from
