## The First World War and its Impact on Subcontinent (1914-1918)

* Key Points:
  * World War (1914-1918) led the British to acknowledge possibility of self-government in India
  * British now agreed that Indians should be given more authority regarding how their country was run
  * British needed to stay on good side of Indians due to their contribution in World War 1
    * British cooperated with Congress to do so (1913)
    * Indians supported British in World War 1 hence expected cooperation in return
