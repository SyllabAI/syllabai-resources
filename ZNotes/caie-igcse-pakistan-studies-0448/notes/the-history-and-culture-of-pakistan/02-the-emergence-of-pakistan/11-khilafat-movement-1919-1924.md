## Khilafat Movement (1919-1924)

### Causes

* Muslims were concerned for the fate of the Turkish Empire
  * They feared the British would do the same to them as they did to Austria and Germany
  * They wished to preserve the Caliphate
* Muslims did not want to fight other Muslims in the wars
* Muslims feared mistreatment in the next reforms
* Hindus fiercely wished for self-rule

### Causes of Failure

* Congress and Muslims worked together, but did not have similar aims, so limited cooperation
* The Moplah Uprising increased violence/anger among Muslims
* Prominent Muslim leaders of the movement were imprisoned (Maulana Muhammad Ali), so crumbling leadership
* Kamal Attaturk abolished the Caliphate himself, so he lost the main aim of Muslims
* Hijrat Movement failed
  * Muslims migrating to Afghanistan were sent back and were not welcome
* Gandhi’s Non-Cooperation Movement/Swaraj led to the Chauri Chara Incident/violence
  * Aimed to influence the British through the non-cooperation movement
  * Loss of support due to Chari Chaura (21 policemen killed in Chauri Chara
  * Eventually withdrew from the Khilafat movement entirely, much to the dismay of Muslims who felt betrayed.

### Impact of Failure

* Muslims returning from the failed Hijra gave up all possessions initially and returned to a horrible economic state
* Muslim-British relations were significantly ruined
* Muslims lost land, wealth
