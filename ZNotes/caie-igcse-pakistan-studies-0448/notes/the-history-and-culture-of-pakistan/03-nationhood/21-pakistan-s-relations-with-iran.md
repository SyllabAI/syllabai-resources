## Pakistan’s Relations with Iran

### Positives

* Iran is the first nation to acknowledge Pakistan’s existence (1947)

* Pakistan PM visits Iran (1949)

* Shah Muhammad Reza, the last king of imperial Iran, was the first head of any state to come on an official state visit to Pakistan (1950)

* Treaty of Friendship between Liaquat Ali Khan and Shah Muhammad Reza (1950)

* Iraq and Iran join Pakistan in the Baghdad Pact (1955)

* Iran and Pakistan join in RCD (Regional Cooperation Development) (1964)

* Iran supported Pakistan in the war (1965)

* Iran supported Pakistan in the war (1971)

* Iran sent aid to Balochistan (1973)

* Bhutto’s visit to Iran (1977)

* Pakistan supported Iranian Revolution (1979)

* Foreign Minister of Pakistan Agha Shahi undertook a state visit to Tehran (1979)

* RCD restructured (1985)

* Zia-ul-Haq’s visit to Iran (1987)

* Benzair’s visit to Iran (1995)

* Nawaz Sharif's visit to Tehran for OIC 8th Summit Conference (1997)

* Pervaiz Musharraf's visit to Iran (1999)

* Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council Hassan Rowhani paid a state visit to Pakistan (2001)

* Iranian Foreign Minister Kamal Kharazi paid a two-day visit to Islamabad (2001)

* President of Iran, Mohammad Khatami, paid a three-day state visit to Pakistan (2002)

* Prime Minister Zafarullah Khan Jamali's visit to Iran (2003)

### Negatives

* Iran’s new government became suspicious of Pakistan due to Afghan War (1979)

* Iran became anti-american (1979)

* Stayed neutral in Iran-Iraq War (1980-88)

* Nuclear Issue (1998)

* American pressure on Pakistan to turn against Iran (1999-07)
