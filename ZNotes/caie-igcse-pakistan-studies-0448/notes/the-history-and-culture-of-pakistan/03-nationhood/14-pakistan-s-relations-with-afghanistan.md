## Pakistan's Relations with Afghanistan

### Positives

* Iskander Mirza's visit to Afghanistan (1956)

* Afghanis supported Pakistan in the war (1965)

* Ayub Khan visited Afghanistan (1966)

* Afghanis supported Pakistan in the war (1971)

* Bhutto's visit to Afghanistan (1974)

* Pakistan aided Afghanistan after an earthquake (1976)

* Daud and Bhutto visited each other’s countries several times (1976)

* Zia’s visit to Afghanistan (1977)

* Daud visited Pakistan (1978)

* Pakistan aided Afghanistan in the Afghan War (1979-88)

* Rehabilitation of Refugees (1979-88)

* Pakistan helps in the formation of the Mujahideen Government (1888-92)

* Islamabad Accord (1993)

* Pakistan aided Taliban (1994-96)

* Better relations than Musharaf’s government and many pacts have been signed (2008-11)

### Negatives

* Border dispute over Pakhtoonistan (1947)

* Afghanistan voted against Pakistan joining the UN (1947)

* Official Talks in Karachi (1947)

* Afghans attacked Kabul Embassy (1955)

* Afghanistan rejected membership of RCD because Pakistan was a member (1964)

* Birth of Kalashnikov Culture (1989)

* Talibanization promoted (1990’s)

* Relations deteriorated due to Musharaf’s American Support on the 9/11 incident (2000-01)
