## Khwaja Nazimuddin (1948-1951)

### Key Points

* Was appointed Chief Minister of Bengal (1947)
* Became Governor General following death of Jinnah
  * Became Prime Minister instead in 1951, when asked to by Ghulam Muhammad (Finance Minister at the time)
* Revised Objectives Resolution
  * Would appoint a board of religious scholars for legislation advice (both central and provincial)
  * People now feared that this board would have veto power over decisions
  * Proposals hence withdrawn
* Was unsuccessful as Prime Minister
  * Economy suffered during his time
  * Bengali-Punjabi rivalry led to internal issues within Pakistan
  * Lack of firm stance and charisma as a leader
  * Was removed by Ghulam Muhammad and Muhammad Ali Bogra replaced him
