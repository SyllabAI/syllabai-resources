## Pakistan’s Relations with China

### Positives

* Pakistan recognised the communist government of China and supported it in gaining UNO membership (1950)

* Pakistan aided China in Indo-China War (1962)

* Border Settlement with China (1963)

* Trade Agreements (1963)

* $60 Million long Term interest-free loan regarding the import of machinery (1963)

* China developed Heavy Mechanical Complex at Taxila (1963)

* Regular flights by PIA to China (1963)

* Supported Pakistan’s policy in Kashmir (1964)

* Pakistan supported China’s entry into the UN (1964)

* China pressurized India in war (1965)

* Pakistan arranged the China-US agreement (1971)

* Bhutto’s visit to China (1972)

* Karakoram Highway opened (1978)

* Nuclear Cooperation Treaty (1986)

* Pakistani President Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq visited China (1986)

* Developmental Projects→ Initial stages of CPEC (1999)

* Opening of Gwadar Port (1999)

* China gave the Chashma Nuclear Power Plant to Pakistan (1999)

* Good Relations with Musharaf’s Government, many treaties signed (1999-2008)

### Negatives

* India acknowledged China more enthusiastically than Pakistan (1950)

* Pakistan signed SEATO (1954)

* Pakistan signed CENTO (1955)

* Pakistan condemned military action in Tibet (1959)

* Couldn’t aid Pakistan in the war due to fear of Russian invasion (1971)
