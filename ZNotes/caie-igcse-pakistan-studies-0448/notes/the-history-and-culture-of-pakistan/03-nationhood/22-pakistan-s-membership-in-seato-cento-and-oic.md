## Pakistan’s Membership in SEATO, CENTO and OIC

### Pakistan as a member of SEATO

* Pakistan joined SEATO for support against India (1955)
* Joined in 1955 despite creation of SEATO in 1954 as the government faced opposition and uncertainties present within the country
* Following US’s intervention in Vietnam, Pakistan withdrew/left SEATO
  * Pakistan realized they would not receive much help/aid (rejected previously as well) hence they left

### Pakistan as a member of CENTO

* Pakistan joined CENTO with enthusiasm because other members were Muslim states as well
* CENTO lacked proper structure or system for mutual defense
  * The US supported but did not join CENTO
* CENTO eventually dissolved (1979)

### Pakistan as a member of OIC

* Pakistan has been part of all summits and conferences held by OIC
* A Pakistani (Sharif-uddin Pirzada, former minister) has been Secretary General of the OIC
* Pakistan received aid from fellow Muslim countries (eg Iran)
