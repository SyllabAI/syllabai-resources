## Zia ul Haq (1977-1988)

### Reforms (Islamization and Constitutional Changes)

* Placed great emphasis on the need to reinforce Islamic teachings and laws to create a proper Islamic society
* Introduced mandatory Zakat
  * 2.5% balance was to be deducted from bank accounts for mandatory Zakat
* Introduced Ushr
  * 10% of agricultural yield came under the Ushr and had to be given (in cash)
* Established ‘Federal Shariat Court’
  * Purpose was to evaluate current laws and announce Islamic punishments for any offenses
  * Blasphemy was now to be punished with death (instead of the former life imprisonment sentence)
* Introduced Hudood Ordinance
  * Islamic punishments (according to the Quran and Sunah) given to those involved in unIslamic practices that were against the law
* Promoted Islamic practices and teachings through education
  * Extra marks awarded to those giving CSS exams if they were Hafiz-e-Quran (had memorized the Quran)
  * Pakistan Studies and Islamiat made compulsory subjects
* Established the Majlis e Shura
  * A nominated assembly that had limited real power/authority
* Established 8th Amendment
  * Granted the President power to dismiss the Prime Minister whenever
* Held Elections in 1985
  * Due to 8th Amendment, Zia remained President
  * Muhammad Khan Junejo became Prime Minister

### Reforms

* Industries that were nationalized during Bhutto’s time were now denationalized
* Encouraged foreign investment by granting security
* Industrialization encouraged
  * Karakoram Highway was completed
  * Import of machinery
  * Pakistan Steel Mills launched
* Popularized more conventional, ‘national’ dressing
  * Women were to wear headscarves if appearing on TV (news anchors/reporters especially)
* Women were discouraged from working
  * Workforce now dominated by men
* Foreign Policy:
  * Following the Soviet Invasion in Afghanistan, USA worked with Pakistan (provided military and financial aid)
    * Anti-soviet activities hence carried out in Pakistan
  * Clarified stance on nuclear power in the Havana Conference
  * Following the war between Russia and Afghan Resistance forces, Pakistan took in Afghani refugees
    * Led to burden for the country, as well as rapid-spreading drug problem and Kalashinkov Culture (due to availability of weapons amongst common public)
* Following Elections (1985):
  * Muhammad Khan Junejo became Prime Minister
  * Attempted to work on foreign policy himself
  * Consulted several party leaders for said policy
  * Attempted to investigate Ojhri Camp Incident
    * ‘Ojhri Camp’ had been a military storage center which blew up in 1988
    * Many civilians dead/injured
  * Zia ul Haq dismissed Junejo’s government in 1988 due to authority to do so from 8th Amendment
    * Claimed Junejo was corrupt
    * Did not like the lack of focus on Islamization from Junejo
    * Junejo began investigating the Ojhri Camp incident
  * Promised re-elections but died in a plane crash (1988)
