## Zulfikar Ali Bhutto (1971-1977)

### Successful Reforms

* Held aim of minimizing the army’s power/influence, especially in politics
  * Removed several important army officials
  * Set up a new head post, ‘Chief of Army Staff’, and appointed someone himself (General Tikka Khan)
* Set up the Federal Security Force
  * This was meant to help the police force at the time and ran under the government’s orders
* Set up a new constitution
  * Made sure different parties were involved in setting up this constitution
  * Constitution established officially in 1973
  * Introduced new conditions such as the Prime Minister and President having to be Muslim, Pakistan was officially an Islamic Republic, political leadership could only be changed by a majority vote, and the President would mostly just be a figurehead
* Brought many industries under government control and attempted to change conditions
* Wished to improve and modernize the agricultural industry
  * Reduced maximum land size as he believed smaller areas led to more quality by landowners and better yield due to modern machinery/techniques
  * Size reduced from 500 acres to 250 acres of irrigated land, and then 1000 acres was cut to 500 acres for non-irrigated land
  * Wished to improve sense of security for small landowners so banned the selling of land to third party
* Wished to improve state of education sector
  * Nationalized schools and colleges
  * Wished to improve literacy rate by improving standards of education and eliminate differences previously present in private vs public schooling
* Introduced reforms for the Health Sector
  * Introduced Rural Health Centers to improve state of Health in rural areas
  * Basic Health Units started in urban areas
  * Made it mandatory for fresh medical graduates to work wherever government assigned, often rural areas
  * Cost of various medicines reduced as brand-names and such monopoly was banned
* Nationalized major industries of Pakistan
  * Major agriculture-based industries as well as banking were nationalized
  * Improved standard of living of workers and allowed formation of workers’ unions
  * Boosted industrialization, brought many units under direct government control
* Signed the Simla Agreement in 1972
* Signed with Indira Gandhi
* Agreed to only discuss the Kashmir issue with India if the prisoners of the 1971 War would be returned to Pakistan

### Failures of Reforms

* Reforms related to agriculture (limitation of land) were not working
  * Influential landowners simply transferred their land to trusted people, hence still had control
  * Law only followed on paper/for show
  * Corrupt landlords still held majority lands
* Education Sector’s reforms were limited
  * Little to no capable teachers
  * Insufficient budget allocation for any significant work
  * Despite education itself being free, poor families could not afford to send a child to school instead of letting the same child earning for them
  * Had a tough time implementing reforms in rural areas
  * Standard of education was low because of large number of students (hard to accommodate)
* Health and Social reforms despite lowering prices, had some negative affect
  * Many international medicine companies stopped operating in Pakistan due to banning of name-brands
  * Shortage of people in the medical field (Doctors, nurses, etc) hence key component for improved and wide-spread healthcare was missing
* Nationalization was also not entirely successful
  * There was a decreased demand for certain products due to general recession, and those products fell under the nationalized industry hence loss was faced
  * The private companies had to close due to this
  * Factory owners replaced with civil servants who did not understand how to operate things efficiently and were inexperienced
  * Lack of sufficiently trained, experienced people to take over managerial positions
* Bhutto’s Downfall:
  * General Election was announced in 1977
    * Opposition parties had a joint dislike for PPP and wished to get rid of them, formed a joint alliance (Pakistan National Alliance)
    * PPP won nonetheless, met with accusations of rigging and foul play
    * Mass protesting from PNA
    * Bhutto attempted to settle situation by pleasing religious communities and gaining some support (banned gambling, drinking, etc)
  * Eventually declared Martial Law
    * Leaders and supporters of the PNA were imprisoned
    * A coup was carried out by the military
    * Zia ul Haq (Chief of Army Staff) dissolved constitution and assemblies were dissolved
    * Bhutto was tried and executed for involvement in the assassination of Ahmed Raza Kasuri
