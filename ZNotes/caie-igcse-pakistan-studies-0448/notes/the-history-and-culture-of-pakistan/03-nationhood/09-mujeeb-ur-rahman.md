## Mujeeb ur Rahman

### Key Points

* Was part of the Awami League Secretariat
* Proposed the 6 Points:
  * Wished for provincial autonomy to counter dominance of West Pakistan
  * Wished for taxation and collection at provincial level too
  * Claimed federal government should only be concerned with Foreign Affairs and Defense
* These 6 points were rejected by Ayub Khan
  * Mujeeb ur Rahman was then imprisoned
  * Resentment grew in hearts of East Pakistanis and clear bias was shown by West Pakistan and by Ayub Khan at the time
  * These 6 points and Mujeeb ur Rahman’s leadership were a turning point in the formation of Bangladesh later, after the 1971 war
