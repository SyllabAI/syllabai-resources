## Yahya Khan (1969-1971)

### Successes

* Got rid of the One Unit Scheme
* Stated that the number of seats would now be proportional to population size
  * This was the first time the East was satisfied with the number of seats
* Organized fair elections
  * One man held the authority of one vote hence fair

### Failures

* Despite ‘fair elections’ he kept pushing the handover of power to Sheikh Mujeeb ur Rahman (who had been victorious in elections)
  * East Pakistan very unhappy, began proper rebellion
  * Military action taken against them, civil war began (1971)
* Made his troops surrender
  * East Pakistan now became ‘Bangladesh’
