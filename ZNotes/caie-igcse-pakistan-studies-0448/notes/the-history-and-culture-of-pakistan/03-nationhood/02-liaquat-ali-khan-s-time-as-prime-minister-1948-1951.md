## Liaquat Ali Khan’s time as Prime Minister (1948-1951)

### Successes

* Took over after the death of Jinnah
  * Was relatively popular
* Proposed the ‘Objectives Resolution’
  * Promised a proper, Islamic constitution which satisfied the religious communities and general public
* Signed pact with Nehru in 1951
  * Accepted that both states were responsible for the protection of minorities
* Took down Rawalpindi Conspiracy
  * An attempted coup against the government by some army officials due to the Kashmir policies
  * Said officials were discovered and arrested
* Focused on economic development
  * Pakistan got surplus budgets hence economy flourished

### Failures

* Government officials given far too much power after PRODA was passed
  * Led to corruption and civil servants forgot true purpose that Jinnah had in mind (to serve the country honestly/solely)
* Objectives’ Resolution issues:
  * Promises of a proper constitution and Islamic state were delayed
  * Eventual draft of constitution not deemed Islamic enough
  * Urdu, solely, was not accepted as national language hence increased divide between East and West
  * East Pakistan unhappy with representation and amount of seats
* Muslim League showed excellent performance due to their improved image thanks to the work of Jinnah
* Muslims had one united, clear aim of Pakistan ever since Lahore Resolution was passed
  * Muslim League now had undivided support by all Muslims in India
