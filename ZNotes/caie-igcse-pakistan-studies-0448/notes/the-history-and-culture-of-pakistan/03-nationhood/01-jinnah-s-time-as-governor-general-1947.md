# Nationhood

## Jinnah’s time as Governor General (1947)

### Problems

* East and West Pakistan combined to form Pakistan
  * Great distance made it hard to run both parts
  * An internal divide as they were completely different in terms of culture and language
* Canal Water Dispute
  * Water supply through rivers cut off by India
* Pakistan was very poor
  * Lack of development of any infrastructure due to lack of funds/administration
  * India refused to give complete finances offered initially (200 Million instead of 800 Million)
* Administration/Constitutional Issues
  * Lack of awareness of responsibilities by government officials/civil servants
  * Delayed establishment
* Social Issues
  * Pakistan consisted of multiple different culture with different languages
  * Felt hard to connect all of them despite Jinnah’s attempts to push Urdu as the national language
* Radcliff Award
  * Conflict arose when certain states given to India despite Muslim population majority (Ferozepur, Kashmir, Hyderabad, etc)
  * Led to the Kashmir issue, which has still not been resolved
* Lack of Resources
  * Pakistan did not have any ordinance factories
  * Were sent supplies/weapons which were inefficient and old
  * Pakistan did not have enough military officers, hence needed extra help from the British
