## Ghulam Muhammad (1951-1955)

### Successes

* Took over following assassination of Liaquat Ali Khan in 1951
* Introduced a 6 year plan to improve industrial sectors (agriculture, transport, minerals, etc)
  * Major jute mill started in East Pakistan
  * Large gas deposit discovered in Sui, Balochistan

### Failure

* Could not handle situation following drought (hence food shortage) properly
  * Rioting began
  * Ulemas wished to declare Ahmedis as Non-Muslims and remove them from office
* Dismissed Khwaja Nazimuddin, replaced him with Muhammad Ali Bogra
  * Limited the power of the Prime Minister to avoid replacement
  * Fell ill in 1955 and was replaced
