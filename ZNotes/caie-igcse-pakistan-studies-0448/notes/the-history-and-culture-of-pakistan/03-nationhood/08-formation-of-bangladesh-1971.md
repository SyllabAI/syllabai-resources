## Formation of Bangladesh (1971)

### Causes

* Social differences amongst the East and West Pakistan
  * East Pakistan had a different language (Bengali)
  * Different cultures
  * Geographic distance
* Economic reasons
  * Major jute production in East Pakistan dominantly, yet that surplus was never spent in East Pakistan and instead spent on West Pakistan (especially border with India)
  * Of the 22 wealthy asset-possessing families in Pakistan, almost all were from West Pakistan
* Civil Unrest/Military:
  * East Pakistanis were disappointed after movement by Awami League for Bengali to be declared national language resulted in the death of many students (1951) so relations weakened
  * All parties (including Awami league) banned in 1958 by Ayub Khan
  * East Pakistanis unhappy with delayed power-handover to Sheikh Mujeeb ur Rahman
  * Began to riot, now no amends could be made
  * East Pakistan also suffered in flood with little to no relief given on time
  * Relation permanently soured as military ordered to kill all involved in the civil war
  * Sheikh Mujeeb ur Rahman arrested and his 6 points were also previously rejected
  * Indian troops helped East Pakistanis (trained troops), 1971 War resulted in creation of Bangladesh and disgrace of Pakistan’s leadership
