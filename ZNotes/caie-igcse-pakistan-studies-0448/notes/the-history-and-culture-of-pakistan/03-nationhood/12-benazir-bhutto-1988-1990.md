## Benazir Bhutto (1988-1990)

### Reforms

* Won the elections held following death of Zia ul Haq in 1988
* Hosted the 4th SAARC Conference in Islamabad
* Lifted ban on Student Unions, Trade Unions
* Pushed for research and missile development
* Encouraged women by setting up a women’s bank
  * First female head of government

### Conflict/Opposition

* Unemployment and inflation were still largely present
  * Pakistani citizens lost faith, disappointed
* Constant disagreement and lack of common vision with President Ghulam Ishaq Khan
  * He wished to appoint the chiefs of the army himself, but so did she hence disagreement
  * Same issue with judicial appointment
* Coalition government in Sindh with MQM was not working out
  * Violence increased, PPP members in Sindh unhappy and MQM members were settled in Sindh
* Faced strong opposition (led by Nawaz Sharif)
  * He was Chief Minister Punjab + Led the united opposition
  * MQM eventually also joined opposition due to conflict with PPP members in Sindh
* ‘No Confidence Movement’ by opposition
  * Despite movement failing, it still highlighted issues within the government and showed strong presence of opposition against it
  * Was focused by Nawaz Sharif in Punjab
* Was blamed for corruption and nepotism
  * Her husband Zardari (known as Mr 10%) was accused of corruption and various other illegal deeds (bribery, coercion, etc)
  * She was accused of keeping her close family members in essential positions in the government, so nepotism accusations
* Her government was then dismissed due to uprest and lack of order in 1990
