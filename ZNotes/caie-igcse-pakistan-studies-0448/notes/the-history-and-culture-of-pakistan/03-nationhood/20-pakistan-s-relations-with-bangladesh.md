## Pakistan’s Relations with Bangladesh

### Positives

* Rehabilitation of Refugees (1971)

* Pakistan invited Bangladesh to the OIC summit in Lahore (1974)

* Bhutto visited Bangladesh (1974)

* Ambassadors exchanged (1975)

* Bangladesh remained a strong trading partner (1980’s)

* Zia-ul-Haq visited Bangladesh (1985)

* Bangladesh was hit by a severe storm, and Pakistan was the first country to aid them (1985)

* Bangladeshi President Ershad visited Islamabad (1986)

* Bangladesh was hit by a severe storm, and Pakistan was the first country to aid them (1988)

* Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina visited Pakistan (1998)

* Pakistani General Pervez Musharraf visited Bangladesh (2002)

### Negatives

* Creation of Bangladesh was the result of civil war in Pakistan hence relations already bitter
* Pakistan refused to acknowledge Bangladesh as an independent country for 2 years
* Unfair division of assets (1971)
