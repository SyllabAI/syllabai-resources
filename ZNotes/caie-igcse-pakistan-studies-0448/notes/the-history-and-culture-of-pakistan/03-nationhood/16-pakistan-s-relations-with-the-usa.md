## Pakistan’s Relations with the USA

### Positives

* Pakistan joined UNO and became a Pro-American country (1947)

* LAK visit to USA (1950)

* Pakistan Embassy built in the USA (1950)

* Pakistan signed Mutual Defence Assistance Agreement (1954)

* Pakistan signed SEATO (1954) and CENTO (1955) (Anti-communist treaties)

* Pak supported the USA in the U2 incident (1960)

* Peshawar Base (1960)

* Indus Water Treaty (1960)

* Ayub’s visit to the USA (1961)

* Ayub’s visit to the USA (1965)

* Pakistan arranged the China-US agreement (1971)

* Pak fought for the USA in Afghan War (1979)

* Zia-ul-Haq visit to USA (1982)

* Muhammad Khan Junejo's visit to the USA (1986)

* Woman PM (1988)

* Benazir Bhutto’s visit to the USA (1989)

* Benazir Bhutto’s visit to the USA (1995)

* Brown Amendment (1995)

* Nawaz Sharif’s visit to the USA (1998)

* Excellent relations with the Musharaf government (1999-2008)

* Musharaf’s visit to the USA (2002)

* U.S. Secretary of State Hillary Clinton's visit to Pakistan (2009, 2011)

### Negatives

* USA invite was late (1950)

* Pak supported China in Indo-China War while the USA aided India (1962)

* Ayub Khan’s visit to China (1962)

* USA didn’t support Pakistan in War (1965)

* Weapon Embargo (1965)

* Pakistan refused to sign Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (1970)

* USA didn’t support Pakistan in War (1971)

* Bhutto left SEATO and CENTO (1972)

* Nuclear Development (1972)

* Nationalisation Policies (1972)

* Pakistan improving relations with USSR, China and Iran (1971-73)

* Dictatorship (1977)

* Burning of US embassy (1979)

* Pressler Amendment (1985)

* USA refused to sell F-16 jets Pak had paid for (1988)

* Consequences of the Afghan War (1989)

* Economic Sanctions (1992)

* Nuclear Testings (1998)

* Kargil War (1999)
