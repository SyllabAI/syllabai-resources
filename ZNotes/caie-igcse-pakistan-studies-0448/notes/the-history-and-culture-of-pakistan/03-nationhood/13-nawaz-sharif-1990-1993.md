## Nawaz Sharif (1990-1993)

### Reforms

* Won the elections held following Benazir’s dismissal in 1990
* Privatized industries which ZAB had nationalized during his time and encouraged development
* Yellow cabs were imported to promote employment in the country
* Cheap loans were being provided
* Strengthened ties with Central Asian Muslim Republic
* Began the Motorway Project
* Began Nuclear Program
* Failures:
  * May have granted more loans but they were seldom repaid
  * Lack of funding from the US follow Afghan-Soviet war
  * Cooperative Societies Scandal came up
    * Direct loss of Nawaz Sharif’s reputation as they often loaned to the Sharifs’ family business (Itefaaq Group)
    * Many savings of people were lost hence imagine damaged in eyes of general public
  * BCCI Bank collapsed, causing major losses of investment
  * Lack of funding from the US follow Afghan-Soviet war
  * A lot of issues stemming from after math of Soviet-Afghan war
    * Widespread availability of guns (Kalashinkov Culture)
    * Drug usage spread
    * Crime rates rose
    * Terrorism rose

### Dismissal

* Attempted to blame Pervez Musharraf (Chief of Army Staff at the time) for the Kashmir Retreat but was unsuccessful and looked down upon
* Wished to replace Musharraf but was unsuccessful
  * President often conflicted over this issue with Nawaz Sharif
* Refused to let Musharaff’s plane land in the country
  * Led to a coup, with Musharraf taking over and Martial Law once again
