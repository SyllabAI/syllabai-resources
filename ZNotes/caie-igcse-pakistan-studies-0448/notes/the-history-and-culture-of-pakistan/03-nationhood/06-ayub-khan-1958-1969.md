## Ayub Khan (1958-1969)

### Reforms

* Introduced ‘Basic Democracies Order’
  * Commoners could now be elect members of council
  * Common people now could not vote for President
* Formed a Constitution Commission to form a new Constitution
  * Bengali and Urdu were still both considered the national languages
  * East still not pleased with number of seats
* Introduced a “Green Revolution”
  * Land reforms which improved efficiency and agricultural development
  * Attempted to preserve rights of small farmers (unsuccessful)
* Great economic development
* Named Pakistan the ‘Islamic Republic of Pakistan’
* Signed the Indus Water Treaty
  * The UN oversaw this
* Signed the Tashkent Accord
  * Effectively ended the Indo-Pak War
* Began construction of RCD Highway
  * Aimed to increase trade and improve relations with nearby/neighbouring countries
* Attempted to improve refugee problem
  * Appointed a rehabilitation minister (General Azam Khan)
* Increased awareness on overpopulation
  * Starting a family planning program despite criticism by Ulemas
