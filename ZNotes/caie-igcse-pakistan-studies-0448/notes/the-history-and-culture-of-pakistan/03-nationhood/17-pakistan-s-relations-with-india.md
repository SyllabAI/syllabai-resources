## Pakistan’s Relations with India

### Positives

* Agreement over Bengal and Assam border (1949)

* Establishment of LOC in Kashmir (1949)

* Liaquat Nehru Pact (1950)

* Indus Water Treaty (1960)

* Tashkent Agreement (1966)

* Simla Agreement (1972)

* Aide from the US led to peace in Kashmir (1979)

* Cricket Diplomacy (1987)

* Lahore Declaration (1999)

* Good Relations with Musharaf’s Government (1999-2008)

* Excellent relations with Zardari’s government (2008-2013)

### Negatives

* Initial Difficulties

* Kashmir Issue (1947)

* Jungardh Issue (1947)

* Canal Water Dispute (1947)

* Kashmir War (1948)

* Hyderabad Issue (1948)

* Indo-China War (1962)

* Indo-Pak War (1965)

* Mukhti Bahini was aided and trained by Indian forces (1969)

* Civil War→ Creation of Bangladesh (1971)

* Pakistan refused to sign Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (1970)

* Nuclear Development (1972)

* Indian Nuclear Tests (1974)

* Sikh Movement (1980)

* Assasination of Indra Gandhi (1984)

* Indian Army exercise in Rajasthan Desert (1987)

* Threat of War from India (1987)

* Siachen Glacier Issue (1989)

* Nuclear Testings from both sides (1998)

* Kargil War (1999)

* Samjhota Express Crash (2007)
