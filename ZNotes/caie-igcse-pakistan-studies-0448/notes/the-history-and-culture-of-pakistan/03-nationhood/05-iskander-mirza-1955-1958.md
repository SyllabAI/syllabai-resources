## Iskander Mirza (1955-1958)

### Successes

* Replaced Ghulam Muhammad as Governor General
* Started the One Unit System
  * Provinces and princely states joined as one ‘unit’
* Developed industries
  * Railway/telephone system expanded and modernized
  * Airport set up in Karachi
* Introduced new constitution
  * Became President
  * Solidified the fact that Muslims and Hindus could not get along
  * Pleased Ulemas by making it necessary for President to be Muslim
  * Made Urdu and Bengali national languages, so attempted to improve tensions between West and Eas*t*

### Failures

* Disappointed minorities with new constitution
* East Pakistan unhappy with number of seats
* NWFP, Balochistan and Sindh feared dominance of Punjab following one unit policy
* Could not efficiently handle things
  * Changed 4 PMs
  * Delayed elections
  * Eventually declared Martial Law and brought in Ayub Khan as Prime Minister who then forced him to resign
