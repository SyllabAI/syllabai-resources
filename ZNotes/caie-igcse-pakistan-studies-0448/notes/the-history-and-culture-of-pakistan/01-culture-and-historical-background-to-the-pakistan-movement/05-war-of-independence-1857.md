## War of Independence (1857)

* The War of Independence began on **10 May 1857** in Meerut.

* Indian soldiers refused to use greased cartridges in Meerut

* The revolt spread, including the murder of British officers

* Revolt moved to Delhi

* Indians succeeded in taking over Delhi, and Bahadur Shah Zafar was declared Emperor.

* Meanwhile, in Kanpur, Nana Sahib led a revolt and captured/eventually killed the British.

  * Nana Sahib failed once the British regained control by defeating both Tatya Topi and Nana Sahib.

* Bahadur Shah Zafar was eventually captured and exiled to Rangoon.
