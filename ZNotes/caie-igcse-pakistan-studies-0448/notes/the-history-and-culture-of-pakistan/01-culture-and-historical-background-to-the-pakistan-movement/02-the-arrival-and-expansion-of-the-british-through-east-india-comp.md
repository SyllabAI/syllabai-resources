## The Arrival and Expansion of the British through East India Company

* A British trading company, ‘East India Company’ was established
  * British were attracted to the subcontinent due to:
    * Spices, cotton, silk and other raw materials
    * Oust other countries in terms of trade and establish a monopoly in India
    * Were technologically ahead due to the Industrial Revolution, so the subcontinent was an easy target
  * Landed at Surat in 1608 and gained permission to trade in 1612
  * Upon refusal to pay taxes (1666), they were defeated in battle with Aurangzeb’s forces and their progress was haltered
  * Suffered defeat once again in 1756 by the Nawab of Bengal (Siraj ud Daulah)
  * Retaliated by defeating Siraj ud Daulah after secretly partnering with Mir Jafar, hence influence grew
  * Fought and won the Battle of Buxar (1764)
    * Defeated Mir Qasim, Nawabs of Oudh and Shah Alam II
    * Took control over Bengal, Bihar and Orissa
    * This led to the establishment of Robert Clive as Governor General of Bengal
  * Signed a peace treaty with the Marathas in 1782
  * Introduced ‘Permanent Settlement’ in 1793 (by Lord Cornwallis)
    * Landowners in Bengal were forced to pay a fixed sum to the British (nearly 10% of their total sum)
  * Subsidiary Alliances were introduced in 1798 (by Governor General Wellesley)
    * Local rulers paid for British protection, and an advisor
  * Defeated Tipu Sultan of Mysore in 1799 (Under G.G Lord Wellesley)
  * Took over Delhi administration by the 18th century
  * Grew in power during the 18th century and developed headquarters in Bombay, Calcutta and Madras
* The British eventually took over the EIC to avoid corruption and regulate the growing authority they held, as well as their foreign policies.
