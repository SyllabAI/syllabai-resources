## Languages

* Urdu
  * Served as a form of unity amongst different regions as it was spoken by many
  * Combination of Persian and Turkish, as well as other languages
  * Spoken from the Mughal Era to the present
  * Had rich poetry and literary history
  * Promoted by Bahadur Shah Zafar
  * Studied at Aligarh Muslim University
  * Had extreme support from Sir Syed Ahmed Khan
  * Translation of the Holy Quran was also done in Urdu
  * Involved in some controversy:
    * Was the National Language of Pakistan despite East Pakistan preferring Bengali, which eventually led to a greater rift and the formation of Bangladesh
    * The Hindi-Urdu Controversy in 1867 involved Hindu demand for Hindi to be made the official language of India against Muslim demand for Urdu to be made the official language of India
* Punjabi:
  * Spoken throughout Punjab
  * Promoted through drama, film, radio, etc
  * Quran’s translation was also present in Punjabi
  * Taught as a subject in universities
  * Punjabi folktales were very popular
  * Famous Sufi poets, i.e Baba Farid Ganjshakar, wrote Punjabi poetry
* Sindhi:
  * Sarmast Academy, after ‘Sachal Sarmast’ published his work to promote Sindhi
  * Compulsory subject in Sindh
  * Numerous magazines, dramas, literature etc. were present in the Sindhi language
  * Spoken since the 12th century
  * Bazm-E-Talib-Al-Muala promoted Sindhi Literature
  * The Sindhiology Department was established in Jamshoro’s Sindh University
* Pashto:
  * Promoted through Peshawar University, where it is taught as a university course
  * Pashto media (songs, dramas, etc) promoted the language
  * Taught in Islamia College
  * Dictionary of Pashto vocabulary prepared by an academy
    * Efforts led by Maulana Abdul Qadir
  * This can be traced back to the 13th Century
    * The golden age of Pashto Literature involved poetry and literary work by Sufi poets such as Rahman Baba and Khushal Khan Khattak
* Balochi:
  * The least developed of the regional languages
  * Traced back and first reported by a British traveller in his journal
  * Language brought by tribes
  * Poetry/literature by Ishaq Shamin, Atta Shad, etc
  * Taught as a university course
  * Literary Association established for the language after 1947 to help promote it.
