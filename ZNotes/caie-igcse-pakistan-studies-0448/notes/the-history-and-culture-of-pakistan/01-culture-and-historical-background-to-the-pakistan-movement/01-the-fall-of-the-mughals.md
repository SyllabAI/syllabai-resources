# Culture and Historical Background to the Pakistan Movement

## The Fall of the Mughals

* Reforms and Changes carried out by ***Aurangzeb***:
  * Greater emphasis on luxurious spending, hence the negative economic effect
  * Strict religious reforms
    * Jizya reimposition (tax on non-Muslims), banning of alcohol/court dances, strict emphasis on Islamic laws, destruction of Hindu temples, hence souring relations with Hindus and minorities
  * Lack of control and administration over the vast empire (that was still expanding into the North)
    * ‘Mansabdar’ system (officers appointed over regions to report activity, rebellion, and supervise certain areas) failed because of a lack of transport/communication and corruption
  * No proper succession system for children, hence internal conflict through wars fought between each other for the throne
  * Fighting costly wars, which drained wealth further
    * with Marathas, Rajputs, Pathans, Sikhs
  * Foreign Invasions caused further consumption of their wealth to cover the costs of war.
    * Persian Invasion (led by Nadir Shah in 1739), Afghani Invasion (led by Ahmed Shah Durrani in 1749, causing the Mughals to lose control over Punjab, Kashmir, and Multan), and Marathas (Skilled Hindu guerrilla fighters from the Deccan who occupied Delhi in 1737)
    * Arrival of the British
  * Protect trading interests in the subcontinent and the Far East
  * Oust the Dutch and Portuguese in terms of trading in the subcontinent, and establish influence early on
  * The Industrial Revolution allowed greater technological advancement for the British, hence an edge in terms of communication, weaponry, military, etc
