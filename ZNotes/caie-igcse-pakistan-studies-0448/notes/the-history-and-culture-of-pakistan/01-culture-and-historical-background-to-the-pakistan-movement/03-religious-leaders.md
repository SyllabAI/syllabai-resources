## Religious Leaders

### Shah Walliullah

* **Aims:**
  * Believed that Muslims in India fell astray from the path of Islam
  * Wished to bridge the divide due to sects amongst Muslims (notably Shia-Sunni conflicts)
  * Disappointed in the corrupt state of the Mughals
  * Believed Muslims did not truly understand Islam anymore due to the language barrier (not many translations of the Quran for the average public)
* **Work:**
  * Translated Quran into Persian
    * His sons also translated it into Urdu, eventually
  * Focused on teaching the fundamentals of Islam to the youth through his Madrassa Rahimyah
  * Implemented practical Islamic teachings by pushing merchants into adopting Islamic ways of trade
  * Wrote a lot of literature (51 books) concerning Islam and its various aspects
  * Bridged the Shia-Sunni divide by writing detailed literary accounts on the Caliphs that were agreed upon by both sects

### Syed Ahmed Barelvi

* **Aims:**
  * Believed that Muslims in India fell astray from the path of Islam (similar to Shah Walliullah)
  * Disliked influence of non-Muslims in the subcontinent
  * Felt the Sikhs were cruel and unjust towards Muslims in Punjab
    * Azaan was banned
    * Praying in mosques was limited/forbidden
  * Held strong belief in ‘Jihad’ and armed resistance against injustice in matters of religion
* **Work:**
  * Launched his “Jihad Movement” (armed battle against Sikhs/forces of Ranjit Singh)
    * Recruited men in his forces throughout Punjab and North-West Frontier, hence forming “Muhajideens”
* **Jihad Movement:**
  * Meant to liberate Punjab and NWF from the tyranny of the non-Muslims
  * It was not very successful because:
    * Disunity among each other due to starkly different backgrounds
    * Lack of support from Pathan tribes
    * Lack of finances for proper equipment for forces
    * Lack of armed training
    * Traitors amongst forces sabotaged them by informing the Sikhs of the route back to Balakot early on, leading to a surprise attack by Sikhs and the death of Syed Ahmed Barelvi

### Haji Shariatullah

* **Aims:**
  * Believed that Muslims began to practice Hindu practices
  * Wished to make Muslims aware of their rights, especially in Bengal
    * Hindu landlords were mistreating the Muslims in Bengal
  * Believed Muslims needed to be reminded of basic Islamic “Faraiz” (religious obligations)
  * Started “Faraizi Movement”
    * Reminded and encouraged Muslims to follow basic Islamic principles and fulfil duties (praying, fasting, etc.)
    * Discouraged following of Hindu customs
    * United Muslims against exploitation practices by Hindu landlords in Bengal
