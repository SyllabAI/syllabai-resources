## British Rule and Changes leading to War of Independence (1857)

* Made the official language English
* Introduced co education
  * Disliked by Muslims and Hindus due to opposing views to this because of religion
* Developed a railway system
  * Instilled fear in the hearts of Indians who thought their way of life was being tampered with
* Banned religious practices such as Suttee
* Arrival of Christian Missionaries
  * Muslims and Hindus were paranoid and feared forceful conversions to Christianity
* British discouraged production of Indian cotton goods to increase own monopoly
* All these factors led to growing resentment in the hearts of the Indians for the British
* Doctrine of Lapse introduction by Lord Dalhousie
  * Annexation of Indian lands
* British discouraged production of Indian cotton and cotton industry to develop their own monopoly
* Lack of employment of Indians due to language barrier
* Indians occupied lower posts in the military as well, whereas high ranking officials in military and civil services were almost always British
* ‘Greased Cartridge Incident’
  * Rumours of new cartridges being greased with pig and cow fat which went against Muslims’ and Hindus’ religion
* Mughal Emperor moved to Qutab Sahib in 1857
  * Moving emperor marked a lack of respect and mistreatment in the eyes of the Indians
