## Reasons for the Failure of the War of Independence (1857)

* There was no unity among the Indians due to regional, religious, and political differences.
* The rebels lacked common aims; some wanted to restore old rulers, while others fought only local battles.
* The revolt had no central leadership or coordination, making it poorly organised.
* The uprising did not receive support from Punjab, which remained loyal to the British.
* The ruler of Kashmir sent 2,000 troops to assist the British.
* Many Sikhs and some Hindu groups did not support the revolt due to past conflicts with the Mughal rulers.
* The rebels lacked modern weapons, training, and resources and mainly used outdated arms.
* The British army was disciplined, well-trained, and centrally commanded.
* The British possessed superior weapons and military techniques, giving them a clear advantage.
* The British controlled railways and telegraph lines, allowing quick movement of troops and better communication.
* British forces received reinforcements from Britain, strengthening their military position.
* The revolt was confined mainly to northern and central India, so it did not become a nationwide uprising.
