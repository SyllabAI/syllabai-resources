## Sir Syed Ahmed Khan

* Beliefs:
  * Was disappointed in low status/image of Muslims in the eyes of the British and Hindus
  * Believed that best way to prosper was for Muslims to cooperate with British rather than look down upon them
  * Studied and appreciated British educational system while in England
  * Wished Muslims moved towards modern education and science
    * Believed Muslims should take advantage of modern and scientific educational developments made by British
* Work (Aligarh Movement + British-Muslim Relation Improvement):
  * Established multiple schools
    * In Muradabad and Ghazipur
  * Established a Scientific society in Ghazipur
  * Established Institute Gazette and Tehzib ul Akhlaq
  * Held an annual Muhammadan Educational Conference where Muslims met up and discussed issues involving education
  * Allowed many Muslims to gain proper, advanced education
    * These Muslims later went on to be key components and political leaders in the Muslims’ politics (i.e Liaquat Ali Khan, Ayub Khan, etc)
  * Wrote a pamphlet underlining causes and explaining Muslim point of view of the events of the War of Independence in 1857 to help clear misunderstandings
  * Wrote “Loyal Mohammadens of Indian” highlighting Muslims that supported and cooperated with British, especially during and after the War of Independence
  * Wrote work pointing out similarities between Christians and Muslims
  * Cleared the misunderstanding over the term “Nadarath”
  * Established “Two Nation Theory” following Hindi-Urdu Controversy
    * Was the first to point out that Hindus and Muslims were two separate communities who could not live together in harmony
    * Disliked system of parliament as Hindus outnumbered Muslims
    * Believed Hindus were far ahead because of their cooperation with the British
