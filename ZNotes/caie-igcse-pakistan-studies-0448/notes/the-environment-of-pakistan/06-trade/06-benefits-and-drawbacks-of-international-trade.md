## Benefits and Drawbacks of International Trade

### Benefits of Trade for Pakistan

* **Foreign exchange earnings:** Selling goods abroad brings in strong currencies (like US Dollars and Euros), which helps pay for essential foreign goods and services.

* **Job creation:** Expanding export industries like textiles, sports goods, and surgical tools creates millions of jobs in manufacturing, logistics, and farming.

* **Industrial and infrastructure growth:** Trade encourages factories to scale up production, which drives investment in dry ports, seaports, motorways, and power grids.

* **Access to technology and modern goods:** Importing machinery and capital goods allows domestic industries to modernize and produce higher-quality items.

* **Consumer choice and lower prices:** Importing goods creates local competition, giving consumers access to a wider variety of products at competitive prices.

### Drawbacks and Risks of Trade for Pakistan

* **Risk of economic vulnerability:** Relying heavily on foreign markets leaves the economy exposed to global shocks, such as sudden drops in worldwide demand or international price hikes in crude oil.

* **Local business vulnerability (Dumping):** Cheap foreign imports can undercut local manufacturers, forcing domestic businesses out of market share.

* **Environmental degradation:** Rapid exportidriven production can cause water pollution from textile dyes, deforestation, and heavy air pollution from industrial emissions.

* **Depletion of natural resources:** Overexporting raw agricultural goods or minerals can deplete local soils and natural water supplies.
