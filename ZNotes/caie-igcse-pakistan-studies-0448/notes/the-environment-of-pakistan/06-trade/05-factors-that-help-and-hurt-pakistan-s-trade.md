## Factors That Help and Hurt Pakistan's Trade

* ### Factors That Help Trade:

  * EU GSP\+ Status: Zero or reduced import tariffs on textiles and clothing entering European markets.

  * Free Trade Agreements (FTAs): Bilateral tax-reduction pacts with China, Sri Lanka, and Malaysia.

  * Better Infrastructure: Seaports (Karachi, Port Qasim, Gwadar) and inland dry ports (Sialkot, Lahore, Faisalabad).

  * Government Support: Tax rates and subsidized credit for export industries.

* ### Factors That Hurt Trade:

  * Over-dependence on Textiles: Vulnerability to weather shocks, pests, and cotton crop failures.

  * High Production Costs: Expensive electricity, gas shortages, and high interest rates compared to regional competitors (Vietnam, Bangladesh).

  * Strict Quality Standards: Inability of small businesses to afford child-labour bans, safety, and environmental compliance audits.

  * Unstable Currency Rates: Devaluation makes imported raw materials and energy much more expensive.

  * Selling Low-Value Items: Exporting raw or semi-finished goods instead of high-margin finished products.
