## **Case Study: Pakistan's Textile Sector and EU GSP\+**

* ### **How GSP\+ has benefited Pakistan's trade:**

  * GSP\+ status eliminated EU import tariffs on Pakistani garments and textiles, giving exporters a direct price advantage over global competitors like India and Vietnam.

  * This policy catalyst helped scale textile exports past $10$ billion to the European market, driving foreign exchange earnings and creating hundreds of thousands of manufacturing jobs.

* ### **The challenges posed by rising environmental standards:**

  * The EU Green Deal mandates strict carbon footprint reductions, wastewater treatment certifications, and sustainable supply chains.

  * Many small-and-medium Pakistani textile mills utilize outdated, dirty coal-fired boilers and dump untreated toxic dyes into waterways, risking compliance failures and export bans.

* ### **Recommendations to sustain trade advantages:**

  * Pakistani textile producers must urgently upgrade to energy-efficient machinery, install Combined Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs) to purify wastewater, adopt solar power to cut carbon emissions, and secure international sustainability certifications to retain EU market access.
