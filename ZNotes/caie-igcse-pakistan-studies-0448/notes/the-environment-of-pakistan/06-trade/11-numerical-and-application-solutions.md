## Numerical and Application Solutions

### **1. Trade Balance Calculation:**

* **Total Exports**: $16$ billion (textiles) \+ $2.5$ billion (rice) \+ $0.8$ billion (sports goods) = $19.3$ billion.

* **Total Imports:** $18$ billion (oil) \+ $6$ billion (machinery) \+ $4$ billion (chemicals) = $28$ billion.

* **Balance of Trade**: $19.3$ billion - $28$ billion = -$8.7$ billion.

* **Verdict:** It is a trade deficit of $8.7$ billion because imports exceed exports.

**2. Currency Devaluation Calculation:**

* **Daily Volume:** $50,000$ barrels x $80$ per barrel = $4,000,000$ daily.

* **Cost at $160$ PKR/USD:** $4,000,000$ x $160$ = $640,000,000$ PKR ($640$ million PKR).

* **Cost at $285$ PKR/USD:** $4,000,000$ x $285$ = $1,140,000,000$ PKR ($1.14$ billion PKR).

* **Increase in Cost:** $1,140,000,000$ - $640,000,000$ = $500,000,000$ PKR ($500$ million PKR increase per day).

**3. Multiplier Effect Calculation:**

* **Initial Injection**: $500$ million.

* **Marginal Propensity to Save/Consume adjustments**:

  * With a marginal propensity to import (MPM) of $20$% ($0.2$) and assuming standard domestic spending behavior where workers spend $60$% of wages locally, leakages equal MPM plus domestic savings.

  * Using the standard multiplier formula where the multiplier equals $1$ divided by leakages, total national income expansion reaches approximately $750$-$1000$ million ($1.5-2$x multiplier due to leakage constraints).
