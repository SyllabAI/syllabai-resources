# Trade

## Important Economic Terms and National Income

* **Gross Domestic Product (GDP):** The total financial value of all final goods and services produced within the geographical boundaries of a country during a given year, regardless of whether produced by domestic or foreign-owned companies.

* **Gross National Product (GNP):** The total economic output produced by a nation's citizens and businesses, both domestically and abroad, in a given year.

  * $\text{GNP} = \text{GDP} + \text{net factor income from abroad}$

* Net Income from Abroad: Money sent home by workers overseas plus profits earned abroad minus profits sent back home by foreign companies operating locally. Pakistan's GNP receives a substantial boost from remittances sent by millions of overseas workers in the Middle East, Europe, and North America.

### Trade Terms

* **Visible Trade:** Buying and selling physical items you can touch, such as raw cotton, crude oil, machinery, or rice.

* **Invisible** **Trade**: Buying and selling services you cannot touch, such as IT support, call centres, shipping, banking, or tourism.

* **Balance of Trade:** The financial difference between physical exports and physical imports.

  * $\text{Balance of trade} = \text{value of physical exports} - \text{value of physical imports}$

* **Trade Surplus:** When a country sells more goods abroad than it buys (exports \> imports)

* **Trade Deficit:** When a country buys more goods from abroad than it sells (imports \> exports)

* **Balance of Payments:** The complete financial record of every dollar entering or leaving the country. This includes physical goods, services, foreign loans, and investments
