## Practice Questions and Answers

### **1. Distinguish between GDP and GNP. How does overseas remittances affect Pakistan's GNP?**

* **GDP:** Measures the total financial value of all goods and services produced within a country's physical borders during a year, regardless of company ownership.

* **GNP:** Measures the total economic output produced by a nation's citizens and businesses both domestically and abroad.

* **Remittances Impact:** Pakistan's GNP is significantly higher than its GDP because millions of overseas workers send home billions of dollars in remittances, which are added to domestic production via net factor income from abroad.

### **2. Define balance of trade and balance of payments. Why does Pakistan run a persistent trade deficit?**

* **Balance of Trade:** The financial difference specifically between physical visible exports and physical visible imports.

* **Balance of Payments:** The comprehensive financial record of all economic transactions entering or leaving a country, including physical goods, services, loans, and investments.

* **Trade Deficit Reasons:** Pakistan spends heavily on imported petroleum, machinery, chemicals, and food while exporting a narrow range of low-value agricultural items and textiles, resulting in imports perpetually exceeding exports.

### **3. Name Pakistan's top five trading partners and describe two main goods traded with each.**

* **China:** Exports cotton yarn and Basmati rice; imports factory machinery and steel.

* **European Union:** Exports ready-made clothes and bedsheets; imports industrial machinery and high-tech vehicles.

* **United States:** Exports cotton textiles and surgical instruments; imports high-tech machines and aircraft parts.

* **United Arab Emirates (Middle East):** Exports rice and fresh meat; imports crude oil and refined petroleum products.

* **Afghanistan:** Exports cement and wheat flour; imports fresh fruits, nuts, and coal.

### **4. What is GSP\+ status? How does it benefit Pakistan's textile exports?**

* **GSP\+ Status:** A preferential trade arrangement granted by the European Union that allows eligible developing nations to export products into EU markets with zero or heavily reduced import tariffs.

* **Benefits:** It provides Pakistani textile and clothing manufacturers a massive competitive price advantage against non-GSP\+ countries, helping expand European export earnings significantly.

### **5. Explain three trade barriers used by the government to protect local industries.**

* **Tariffs:** Taxes placed on imported foreign goods to artificially raise their retail price, making local consumers less likely to buy luxury foreign products.

* **Quotas:** Strict physical volume limits placed on specific products that can be imported during a given period to safeguard domestic producers from oversupply.

* **Embargoes:** Complete government bans on importing or exporting specific products or trading with designated nations due to health, safety, or political reasons.

### **6. Distinguish between visible and invisible trade, giving two examples of each from Pakistan.**

* **Visible Trade:** The buying and selling of physical, tangible goods that you can touch. Examples: Exporting raw cotton and importing crude oil.

* **Invisible Trade:** The exchange of non-physical services where no physical item crosses customs. Examples: Exporting IT software services and earning revenue from international tourism.
