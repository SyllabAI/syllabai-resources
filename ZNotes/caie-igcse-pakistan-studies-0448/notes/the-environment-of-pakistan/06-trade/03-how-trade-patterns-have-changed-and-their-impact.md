## How Trade Patterns Have Changed and Their Impact

### Recent Shifts in Trade

* Export Changes: In the past, Pakistan mainly exported raw materials like raw cotton. Today, it exports semifinished goods like yarn and finished products like ready-made clothes

* Import Changes: The import bill has grown quickly because oil and gas prices keep rising globally, local factories need more machines, and a growing population needs more imported cooking oil and food

### The Impact of Running a Trade Deficit

Pakistan constantly spends more on imports (like oil and machinery) than it earns from exports (like cotton and rice). This creates a steady trade deficit, which causes several problems:

* Drains cash reserves: Paying for high import bills eats up the foreign currency stored in the central bank

* Weakens the rupee: When the trade deficit grows, the Pakistani rupee loses value against foreign currencies, making future imports even more expensive

* Increases national debt: To fill the money gap, the government has to borrow from international lenders like the IMF, World Bank, or foreign banks

* Causes inflation: As the currency drops and fuel prices rise, prices for everyday consumer goods inside the country go up
