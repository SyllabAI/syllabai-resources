## Practice Questions and Answers

### **1. Explain why Punjab has the highest road density in Pakistan while Balochistan has the lowest.**

* **Punjab:** Features extensive flat terrain (Indus Plain) making construction cheap and easy, combined with high population density, major industrial/commercial cities, and intensive agricultural trade that generates massive daily traffic demand.

* **Balochistan:** Pakistan's largest province by area, characterized by vast barren deserts, rugged plateaus, and sparse population settlements where building and maintaining long paved roads across thousands of square kilometres yields very low economic returns.

### **2. Describe the role of air transport in connecting northern mountainous regions of Pakistan.**

* **Lifeline Services:** Serves as an essential lifeline for northern mountainous areas like Gilgit, Skardu, and Chitral, which are frequently cut off from the rest of the country for months during winter due to heavy snow, avalanches, and landslides blocking mountain roads.

* **Emergency Mobility:** Transports high-priority passengers, fresh medical supplies, and critical food rations to isolated communities when land routes are completely impassable.

### **3. Name three mountain passes used for cross-border trade and state which country each connects to.**

* **Khunjerab Pass:** Connects Pakistan (Gilgit-Baltistan) to China via the Karakoram Highway.

* **Khyber Pass:** Connects Pakistan (Peshawar) to Afghanistan (Kabul).

* **Khojak Pass:** Features road and rail tunnels connecting Pakistan (Quetta-Chaman) to Afghanistan (Kandahar).

### **4. What is a dry port? Explain two reasons why dry ports have been developed in Pakistan.**

* **Definition:** An inland intermodal terminal directly connected by road or rail to a seaport, allowing regional cargo to be processed, stored, and cleared by customs away from the coast.

* **Reason 1 (Relieving Port Congestion):** They take immense pressure off overloaded coastal gateways like Karachi Port and Port Qasim by shifting container processing inland.

* **Reason 2 (Reducing Transport Costs and Time):** Importers and exporters in manufacturing hubs can complete customs documentation locally, cutting transit delays and avoiding the high cost of sending uncleared containers to the coast.

### **5. List four problems associated with providing telecommunications in rural Pakistan.**

* **Geographical Obstacles:** Mountainous terrain blocks wireless signals and makes laying underground fibre-optic cables physically treacherous and expensive.

* **Power Shortages:** Frequent electricity blackouts and unreliable rural power grids disrupt cell towers and internet routers.

* **High Cost and Affordability:** Low-income rural households frequently struggle to afford smartphones, computers, and monthly broadband bills.

* **Digital and Literacy Divide:** Widespread illiteracy and lack of digital skills slow down technology adoption in remote communities.
