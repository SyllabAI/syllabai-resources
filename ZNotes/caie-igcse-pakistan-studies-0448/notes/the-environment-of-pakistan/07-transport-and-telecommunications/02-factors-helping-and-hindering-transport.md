### Recent Improvements and Feasibility Evaluation

* **Roads:**

  * Improvements: Construction of the Motorway Network (M-1 to M-5, M-9) has revolutionized travel time and freight movement. The Lowari Tunnel provides year-round all-weather road access to Chitral. The Karakoram Highway (KKH) upgrade and Hazara Expressway improved northern access.

  * Evaluation: Motorways reduced Lahore-Islamabad travel time from $6$ hours to $3.5$ hours, cutting freight costs by \~$20$%. However, initial capital costs are extremely high ($2$ to $3$ billion per $100$ km), toll revenues cover only \~$40$–$50$% of maintenance, and security concerns have delayed some projects. Motorways are highly feasible in Punjab/Sindh but marginal in Balochistan without subsidies.

* **Railways:**

  * Improvements: Upgrading main trunk lines, replacing wooden sleepers with concrete, introducing faster express trains (Green Line, Karakoram Express), and modernizing booking systems. The ML-1 project aims to upgrade the Karachi-Peshawar mainline to $160$ km/h.

  * Evaluation: Express trains improved passenger experience, but Pakistan Railways operates at a loss (\~$300$ million annually) due to aging rolling stock and financial constraints, delaying the ML-1 upgrade. Rail upgrades require foreign investment and institutional reform.

* **Airways:**

  * Improvements: Modernizing major international airports (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi) and building Sialkot International Airport as an export cargo model.

  * Evaluation: New Islamabad airport expanded capacity to $15$ million passengers/year. Sialkot handles \~$500,000$ passengers/year profitably. Conversely, PIA runs at a loss, and Gwadar airport lacks significant traffic. Airport infrastructure is commercially viable primarily where economic demand exists.
