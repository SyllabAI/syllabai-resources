## Formal vs Informal Sector

* **Formal Sector**: Government registered, legally regulated businesses that pay taxes, keep official records, offer fixed wages, structured working hours and job security (e.g., bank employees, registered textile mill workers).

* **Informal Sector:** Unregistered, unregulated, self-employed or casual employment operating outside official government oversight and taxation (e.g., street vendors, auto repair mechanics, rickshaw drivers, home tutors).

* **Informal Sector Services**: Small-scale street retail, casual construction labour, roadside vehicle repairs, domestic household help, recycling collection, artisan craft making.

### Evaluation of the Informal Sector

* **Advantages to Development**:

  * Provides vital employment and income for millions of poor, illiterate, or unskilled workers; requires minimal startup capital.

  * It also provides cheap, accessible services and low-cost goods for low-income citizens.

* **Disadvantages to Development:**

  * The government loses billions in uncollected tax revenue; no employment security, medical benefits, or safety (high risk of exploitation and child labour).

  * The unregistered activities affect official economic planning, GDP, and GNP statistics.

### Site for each industry 

(Site refers to the physical characteristics of the land itself - such as topography, drainage, and soil conditions.)

* **Cotton/Sugar:** Require flat land for large factory footprints. Cotton requires access to soft canal water and well-drained soil, while sugar mills must be located close to farms on well-drained land to prevent monsoon waterlogging.

* **Cement/Fertilizer:** Cement plants locate at the foot of limestone hills with flat areas for kilns and storage. Fertilizer plants require flat land near gas pipelines with good drainage to prevent chemical runoff.

* **Iron/Steel:** Requires large, flat coastal terrain (such as Port Qasim) with a deep-water channel to accommodate heavy import/export shipping.

* **Bricks/Oil Refining:** Bricks need flat alluvial plains with accessible local clay and water. Oil refineries require flat coastal land in Karachi or inland flat land along pipelines (PARCO, Attock).

* **Sports Goods, Surgical Instruments and Crafts:** Located on flat urban land in Sialkot operating out of small workshops, requiring no specialized physical site features. Crafts are largely home-based.
