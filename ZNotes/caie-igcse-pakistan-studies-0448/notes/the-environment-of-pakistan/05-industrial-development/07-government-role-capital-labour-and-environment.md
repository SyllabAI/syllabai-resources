## Government Role, Capital, Labour and Environment

* ### Sources of Capital and Labour:

  * Capital is sourced from commercial bank loans, the Industrial Development Bank of Pakistan (IDBP), Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), and overseas worker remittances. Labour is supplied by abundant unskilled rural-urban migrants and technical institutes like NAVTTC.

* ### Government Promotional Measures: 

  * Granting tax holidays, reducing import duties on manufacturing machinery, constructing infrastructure corridors (CPEC motorways), and establishing inland dry ports for local customs clearance.

* ### Environmental Solutions:

  * Water Pollution: Untreated industrial toxic chemical waste dumped into rivers and the Arabian Sea is mitigated by mandating Combined Effluent Treatment Plants in industrial zones.

  * Air Pollution: Smoke emissions from traditional brick kilns and steel furnaces contributing to winter smog are mitigated by converting brick kilns to fuel-efficient Zig-Zag Technology and installing factory smoke scrubbers
