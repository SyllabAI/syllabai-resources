## Export Processing Zones, Industrial Estates and Feasibility

* **Export Processing Zones (EPZs):** Enclaves created to attract foreign investment and increase exports by offering tax-free operations, $100$% foreign ownership, zero customs duty on imported machinery, and uninterrupted power (e.g., Karachi EPZ, Risalpur EPZ).
* **Industrial Estates:** Zones developed by provincial governments (e.g., Sundar Industrial Estate Lahore, M3 Faisalabad) equipped with roads, water, gas, and electricity to prevent haphazard industrial sprawl inside residential areas.
* **The Feasibility Failures of Hub and Nooriabad**: Built near Karachi to encourage regional growth, both suffered severe economic setbacks because they lacked an adequate, sustainable supply of industrial water.
* **Key Feasibility Logic:** A comprehensive feasibility study must evaluate all physical and human factors. If water or power is missing, delay the project; if water is strictly limited, scale down the zone; if basic utilities are impossible, reject the project entirely.

### Sources of labour

* **Unskilled/Manual Labour:**

  * Source: Driven by rural-urban migration as mechanized farming reduces rural labor demand, pushing millions annually toward urban hubs like Karachi, Lahore, and Faisalabad for construction, transport, and domestic work.

  * Challenge: Low literacy and lack of formal training lead to low productivity and low wages.

* **Skilled/Technical Labour:**

  * Source: Universities, technical institutes, and NAVTTC designed to support industrial modernization.

  * Challenge: Despite generating around $500,000$ graduates annually, a severe brain drain causes skilled professionals to emigrate. A skills mismatch forces industries to import foreign experts.

* **Semi-Skilled Labour:**

  * Source: Vocational training centers and on-the-job training for machine operators, electricians, and welders.

  * Challenge: Highly limited availability - only about $5$% of youth have formal vocational training due to a scarcity of quality institutes.

* **Overall Evaluation:** Pakistan has abundant manual labor but a sharp shortage of skilled labor, alongside a brain drain of $10$ million overseas Pakistanis. Expanding NAVTTC and linking training directly to industry requirements is essential.
