# The Land of Pakistan

## Location of Pakistan

### **Location**

* Covers an area of $796096$ sq. km.

* Between latitudes $23.5°$N (tropic of cancer), $30°$N and $36°$N.

* Between longitudes $64°$E, $70°$E and $76°$E.

### River Connections:

* The left-bank tributaries (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej) flow through Punjab.

  * It meets the Indus River at Mithankot.

* The right-bank tributary (Kabul River) comes from the west.

  * It meets the Indus near Attock.
