## **The Energy Crisis (Load Shedding)**

![](../../../assets/993a6e789991-17b02ff204f890eda9d3b573.webp)

### Causes of the crisis

* **Circular debt:**

  * The government cannot pay fuel supply companies because electricity distributors fail to collect bills from consumers, creating a massive debt chain that halts power generation.

* **High fuel import costs:**

  * Over $60$% of Pakistan's electricity is thermal, relying on imported oil and coal, which drain foreign reserves.

* **Transmission losses and theft:**

  * Ancient, poorly maintained power lines lose vast amounts of electricity - mainly through heat (technical losses) and illegal hookups (kunda system).

* **Seasonal HEP drop:**

  * During winter, dry weather and silting drastically reduce the water level in reservoirs (Tarbela & Mangla), causing hydroelectric power output to crash.

### **Consequences of load shedding**

* **Industrial shutdowns:**

  * Factories (especially the textile sector) face frequent blackouts, leading to missed export deadlines, loss of international clients, and worker layoffs.

* **Agricultural failure**:

  * Electric tube wells cannot run, depriving crops of water during critical growth periods, which lowers overall agricultural yields.

### Solutions to the crisis

* **Invest in renewable energy:**

  * Build large-scale solar parks (like the Quaid-e-Azam Solar Park) and wind farms (in the Jhimpir wind corridor in Sindh) to reduce fuel import bills.

* **Grid modernization:**

  * Replace old transmission lines and install smart meters to stop electricity theft and reduce technical distribution losses.
