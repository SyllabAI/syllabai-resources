## Users of Electricity

* **Domestic (Household) Sector (Largest Consumer):**

  * Used heavily for lighting, ventilation, and running home appliances (refrigerators, washing machines).

  * In summer, electricity usage spikes dramatically due to the widespread use of air conditioners (ACs).

* **Industrial Sector:**

  * Used to power heavy machinery, automated assembly lines, and high-heat industrial ovens.

  * (especially in textiles, cement, and chemical plants).

* **Agricultural Sector:**

  * Primarily used to power electric tube wells to pump groundwater for crop irrigation.

  * Also used to run crop-processing mills.

* **Commercial Sector:**

  * Powers lighting, computers, and air conditioning in offices, retail shops, streetlights, and shopping malls.
