## **Factors promoting or hindering availability of electricity**

### Promoting factors

* **Abundant renewable resources**: Solar, wind, and hydel potential is enormous.

* **Government initiatives**: CPEC energy projects, Thar coal development, and renewable energy policies.

* **Private sector involvement**: IPPs bring investment and expertise.

* **Foreign aid and loans**: Asian Development Bank, World Bank fund large projects.

### Hindering factors

* **Circular debt:** Government owes billions to IPPs and fuel suppliers, halting generation.

* **Transmission losses:** Old infrastructure loses approximately $18$-$20$% of generated electricity.

* **Electricity theft**: Illegal hookups (kundas) in rural and urban slums.

* **Political instability**: Provincial disputes over water distribution affect hydel output.

* **Seasonal variation**: Winter reduces hydel output; summer peak demand exceeds supply.

* **Fuel import dependency**: High costs drain foreign reserves.

* **Mismanagement**: Lack of planning and delayed maintenance.
