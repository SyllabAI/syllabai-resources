### **Advantages & Disadvantages of Energy Sources**

#|
||

**Energy Source**

|

**Advantages**

|

**Disadvantages**

||
||

**Coal**

|

Abundant reserves (Thar); cheap; supports brick/cement industry

|

Low-grade lignite; high sulfur; air pollution; not for steel

||
||

**Natural Gas**

|

Cleaner than oil/coal; cheap; easy transport; fertilizer feedstock

|

Reserves depleting ($15$-$20$ yrs); winter load shedding; explosion risk

||
||

**Oil**

|

High energy density; easy transport; supports transport/industry

|

Import-dependent (approximately $80$%); expensive; price volatility; pollutes

||
||

**Solar**

|

Safe; low running cost; efficient; no fuel needed

|

High construction cost; skilled labour; fails on cloudy days

||
||

**Wind**

|

Eco-friendly; no running cost; consistent medium/long term

|

Difficult maintenance; kills birds/bats; not always reliable

||
||

**Biogas**

|

Safe; no skilled labour; less deforestation; uses waste

|

Causes some pollution; limited gas; not for city areas

||
||

**Geothermal**

|

Reliable; large energy; jobs for locals

|

High installation cost; harmful gases; can destroy plants

||
||

**Wave**

|

Large energy; no greenhouse gases; safe

|

High installation; difficult maintenance; needs coastal conditions

||
||

**Nuclear**

|

Less space than dam; continuous supply; eco-friendly

|

Very high initial cost; parts not easily available; radioactive waste; skilled workers

||
||

**Hydel (HEP)**

|

Renewable; low running cost; eco-friendly; multipurpose

|

High construction cost; needs specific terrain; seasonal drop in winter

||
