## **Data Interpretation Question: Population Pyramid Analysis**

* **Birth Rate Trend:** A broad base ($40$% under $14$ years) indicates a very high birth rate, though a slight inward curve at the base points toward the beginning of a gradual downward trend over time.

* **Dependency Burden:** The massive youth cohort creates a heavy dependency burden, placing immense short-term pressure on pediatric healthcare, vaccination programs, and primary schooling.

* **Potential Demographic Dividend:** If this large youth cohort is successfully educated, healthy, and trained with modern technical skills, they will enter the workforce and drive accelerated economic output.

* **Future Challenges:** If the economy fails to generate millions of formal jobs annually, the youth bulge will morph into widespread unemployment, rising poverty, social unrest, and the expansion of urban slums.
