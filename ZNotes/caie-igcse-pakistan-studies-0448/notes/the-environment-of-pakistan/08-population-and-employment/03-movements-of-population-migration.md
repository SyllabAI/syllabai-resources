## **Movements of Population (Migration)**

### **Push and Pull Factors**

* **Push factors (rural areas):** Lack of secure employment, farm mechanization reducing manual labor demand, poor basic facilities (electricity, schools, healthcare), and environmental degradation/flooding.

* **Pull factors (urban centers):** Better-paying jobs, superior educational/medical institutions, modern amenities, entertainment ("bright lights"), and a more culturally free environment.

**Types of Population Movements**

* **Rural-urban migration:** Mass movement of villagers to major metropolitan areas (Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad).

* **Seasonal migration:** Movement tied to harvest seasons or pastoral transhumance in northern mountains.

* **Emigration:** Citizens leaving for the Middle East, Europe, or North America, generating vital remittances.

* **Immigration and refugees:** Historical influxes from neighboring conflicts (e.g., Afghanistan).

**Effects of Population Movements**

* **In urban areas:** Proliferation of katchi abadis (slums), massive strain on municipal services, traffic congestion, and rising crime.

* **In rural areas:** Depopulation of villages, aging demographic profiles, acute seasonal agricultural labor shortages, and shifting of state developmental funds away from rural zones.

**Solutions and Mitigation Measures**

* **Self-help schemes:** Providing slum inhabitants with land titles, building materials, and basic infrastructure to upgrade homes.

* **Infrastructure provision:** Expanding water, sanitation, and electricity to urban slums and rural feeder towns.

* **Decentralization:** Industrial estates and employment hubs created away from primary cities.
