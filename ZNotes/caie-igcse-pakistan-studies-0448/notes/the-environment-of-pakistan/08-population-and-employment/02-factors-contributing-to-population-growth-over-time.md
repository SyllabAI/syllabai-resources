## **Factors Contributing to Population Growth Over Time**

* * **Social factors:** Cultural norms favor large families, and sons are often desired to carry on the family name or work family lands. Low female social status limits control over family planning.

  * **Educational factors:** Low literacy rates, particularly among females, correlate with a lack of awareness regarding family planning and reproductive health.

  * **Economic factors:** Children are often viewed as economic assets who start working early. High infant mortality rates historically encouraged families to have more children to ensure survival to adulthood.

  * **Political/Religious factors:**

    * Conservative interpretations regarding family planning and historical gaps in government population welfare policies.

    * Belief that God provides 'Rizq' to everyone, eliminating the need for population control.

    * Influx of large numbers of refugees from Afghanistan after $1979$ due to war, instability, and drought.

    * Frequent changes in government lead to shifting population control projects, diverting resources.

  **Problems for Development, Sustainability, and Overpopulation**

  * **Resource strain & Infrastructure:** Rapid growth outpaces food production, putting intense pressure on clean water, electricity grids, housing, and transport.

  * **Environmental & Economic degradation:** Natural habitats are lost (due to alternative wood-cutting income); inflation rises due to high demand and low production; uneven distribution of wealth occurs.

  * **External debt & Overstaffing:** The country imports goods when local demand is unmet, reducing foreign exchange and driving external debt; governments may resort to overstaffing to manage unemployment, increasing inefficiency.

  **Possible Solutions to Evaluate**

  * Expanding female education and nationwide literacy campaigns.

  * Improving rural healthcare accessibility and local family planning clinics.

  * Running widespread public awareness media campaigns and educational schemes (e.g., Sabz Sittara).

  * Ensuring job creation outpaces population growth.

  * Providing tax reliefs to smaller families to encourage birth control.
