## **Employment and the Workforce**

### **Occupational Sectors**

* **Primary sector:** Extracting raw materials directly from nature (farming, mining, fishing).

* **Secondary sector:** Processing raw materials into finished goods (textiles, manufacturing).

* **Tertiary sector:** Providing commercial services (retail, banking, IT services, tourism).
