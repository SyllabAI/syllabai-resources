## **Unemployment and GNP**

### **Unemployment and Underemployment**

* **Unemployment:** People of working age, actively looking for work, but unable to find a job (driven by mechanization and capital-intensive production).

* **Underemployment:** People working below their skill level or part-time/seasonally when full-time work is desired.

* **Macroeconomic link:** Widespread unemployment depresses domestic output (**GDP**) and limits national wealth generation including remittances (**GNP**).

**Key Workforce Data**

* Pakistan produces approximately $500,000$ university graduates annually, but approximately $30$ - $40$ % face unemployment/underemployment due to a skill mismatch.

* Only approximately $5$ % of youth have formal vocational training (vs. $20$ - $30$ % in East Asian economies).

* The IT sector faces a shortage of approximately $100,000$ skilled professionals despite high demand.

### **Link to Macroeconomic Indicators: GNP and GDP**

* **Gross Domestic Product (GDP):** The total monetary value of all finished goods and services produced within a country's borders in a specific time period.

* **Gross National Product (GNP):** The total value of all finished goods and services produced by a country's residents and businesses, both domestically and abroad (including foreign remittances).

* **The Connection:** Widespread unemployment and underemployment mean human resources are under-utilised. This directly depresses domestic output (GDP) and limits national wealth generation (GNP).

### **Key Workforce Data**

* Pakistan produces approximately $500,000$ university graduates annually, but approximately $30$ - $40$ % face unemployment/underemployment due to a skill mismatch.

* Only approximately $5$ % of youth have formal vocational training (vs. $20$ - $30$ % in East Asian economies).

* The IT sector faces a shortage of approximately $100,000$ skilled professionals despite high demand.

**Importance of Literacy, Education, and Training**

* Formal education and technical training for both males and females are prerequisites for national progress.

* Economic benefits include attracting Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), boosting manufacturing efficiency, and fostering high-value tertiary sectors like IT and software development.
