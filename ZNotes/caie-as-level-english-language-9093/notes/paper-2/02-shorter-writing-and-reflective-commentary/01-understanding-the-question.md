# Shorter Writing and Reflective Commentary

## Understanding the Question

Shorter writing in the context of our syllabus means:

* selecting the most appropriate language for the task

* not going ‘off-task’ but sticking to the purpose

* making each word or phrase count and not writing any more than you really need to

Shorter writing tasks usually require **tight control of language**, **clear audience awareness**, and **efficient structure**. Since space is limited, **every choice matters**.
