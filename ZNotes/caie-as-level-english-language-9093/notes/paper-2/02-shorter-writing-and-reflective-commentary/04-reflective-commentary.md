## Reflective Commentary

The reflective commentary tests your ability to **explain and justify your writing decisions** using appropriate language terminology. Once you have completed your own piece of writing, you will be asked to review it and evaluate how well it met the brief. Like your own piece of writing, your commentary should be concise and to the point.

**Purpose of the Reflective Commentary**

You are expected to:

* Demonstrate **conscious control of language**

* Explain how choices fulfill the **audience and purpose**

* Show awareness of **form and conventions**

This mainly addresses **AO3**.
