## Audience and Purpose in Shorter Writing

You must establish **audience and purpose** immediately, often in the **first sentence**. Effective strategies include:

* Direct address, for example, “As students, we…”

* Clear stance or intent early

* Tone set from the outset, for example, formal, conversational or persuasive

You will be given a question that requires you to write in a particular form or use a particular text type. This could be a **descriptive or narrative text, or a transactional text such as a letter or a magazine article.**

**transactional writing:** non-fiction texts that communicate information between individuals or groups
