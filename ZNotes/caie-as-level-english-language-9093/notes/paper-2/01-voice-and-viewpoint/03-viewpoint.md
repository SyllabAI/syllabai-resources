## Viewpoint

Viewpoint is closely linked to voice. For example, it would be difficult to gauge what someone’s viewpoint is, whether as narrator or as a character in a text, without identifying what is distinctive about their voice.

Viewpoint can also be expressed more subtly, requiring you as a reader to **infer** meaning to work out what is being **implied**.

* **Infer:** to read between the lines; to work out meaning from clues in a text

* **Imply:** to suggest without stating directly
