# Voice and Viewpoint

## Voice

**Texts** have their own voices. These may or may not be those of the writer; they could also be the distinctive voice of a character or **narrator**. The voice also helps us to understand the **viewpoint** of the writer or character - their **perspective** on what happens.

* **Text:** any asset that can be read, anything that conveys meaning can be a text (from the shortest conversation to the longest novel)

* **Narrator**: a person (not always identified) who tells or recounts events

* **Viewpoint**: the way the writer/narrator looks at the world or the opinions they express

* **Perspective**: the particular angle by which a writer views or experiences events
