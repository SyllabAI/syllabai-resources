## What is Descriptive Writing?

Descriptive writing is also a form of creative expression that focuses on creating vivid images, emotions and experiences in the reader’s mind. However, it concentrates on detailed sensory imagery, carefully selecting language to paint a clear picture of a person, place, object or moment. It relies heavily on precise lexis, imagery, and literary devices such as similes, metaphors and personification to evoke atmosphere and mood. Furthermore, it deepens the reader’s experience by appealing to the senses and emotions.

Strong descriptive writing does more than list details; it selects and organises details purposefully to reflect a mood, theme or perspective. 

**For example,** describing a city through images of rust, smoke and noise may suggest decay or chaos, whereas focusing on light, colour and movement may suggest energy and hope. Ultimately, descriptive writing is about precision, atmosphere and emotional impact — it transforms ordinary observation into an engaging and vivid experience.
