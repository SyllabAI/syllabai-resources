# Writing a Reflective Commentary

## Skills for Writing a Commentary

The purpose of the commentary you write in response to your own work is to explain how you have met the brief set in the question. When reviewing your own work, you should try to match the points you make in your commentary to the key focuses in the original question. For example:

* In what way did I write in an appropriate style and format?

* Did I include the details requested? How did I show this?

* In what way did I demonstrate my outlook (my viewpoint)? What linguistic choices (words, phrases) helped me achieve this?

* What mood did I create, and how did I achieve this?
