## Responding to a narrative style question and transactional/descriptive style response

### Step 1: Decoding the task

1) What is the form of text required? What is its purpose?

2) Do you have to write a part or all of the text?

3) What is the text about? What is the background information?

4) What sorts of effects do you need to create?

### Step 2: Check the conventions

What core features would you be expected to include?

### Step 3: Generate ideas and create a plan

1) What details of a particular bus or car will you include?

2) What is the weather / time of day / season?

3) What people will you focus on? What will they be doing?

4) What structure will your article have?

### Step 4: Get writing!

Write your article/description, sticking closely to the task.
