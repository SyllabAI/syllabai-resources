## **Essays**

Essays are structured written arguments or reflections, commonly used in academic settings. They are formal, analytical and designed to communicate ideas.

**Purpose:** To argue or explore an idea logically

**Audience:** Educated and formal readership

**Tone:** Objective, formal and reasoned

**Conventions:**

* Introduction – body – conclusion

* Thesis statement

* Paragraphs with topic sentences

* Evidence and analysis

**How to write:**

1. Open with a thesis or guiding question

2. Use a structured argument (one point per paragraph)

3. Include evidence and commentary

4. Use signposting (however, furthermore, therefore, etc.)

5. End with a synthesised conclusion

**Tips:**

* Plan before you write

* Keep tone analytical, not emotional
