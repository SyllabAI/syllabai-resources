## **(Auto) biographies**

Biographies and (auto) biographies are life stories, either written by the person (auto) or by someone else (bio). They generally focus on events, relationships and reflections across a person’s life.

**Purpose:** To narrate and reflect on a life

**Audience:** General readers

**Tone:** Reflective, descriptive and personal

**Conventions:**

* First or third person

* Chronological or thematic structure

* Reflection and analysis

* Descriptive detail

**How to write:**

1. Decide on key life event(s)

2. Use vivid descriptions and character detail

3. Include feelings and reflection

4. Maintain a clear structure

5. End with a sense of growth or insight

**Tips:**

* Use the past tense but include reflective present

* Build atmosphere, not just facts
