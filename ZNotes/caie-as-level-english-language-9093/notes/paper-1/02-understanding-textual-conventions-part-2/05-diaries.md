## **Diaries**

Diaries are private, introspective writings that explore a person’s thoughts, feelings and daily experiences or occurrences. They are often emotional, informal and reflective.

**Purpose:** To reflect and record personal experience

**Audience:** One’s self (private)

**Tone:** Honest, emotional and introspective

**Conventions:**

* First person

* Informal

* Dates and times

* Reflective and emotional detail

**How to write:**

1. Use a realistic date

2. Begin with ‘Dear Diary’

3. Describe events, feelings or thoughts

4. Reflect on lessons or questions

5. End naturally (a final comment or plan)

**Tips:**

* Let your inner voice guide the tone

* Include emotional reaction
