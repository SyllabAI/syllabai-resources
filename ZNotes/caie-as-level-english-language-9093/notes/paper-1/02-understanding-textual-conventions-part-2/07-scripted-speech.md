## **Scripted Speech**

Scripted speeches are written to be delivered aloud to a specific audience. They aim to persuade, inspire or inform by using techniques that appeal to both the ear and the heart.

**Purpose**: To persuade or inform an audience

**Audience:** Listening audience (formal or informal)

**Tone:** Engaging, rhetorical and emotive

**Conventions:**

* Rhetorical devices (triplets, repetition, etc.)

* Personal connection

* Clear structure

* Audience address

**How to write:**

1. Greet the audience and introduce your topic

2. Use emotive language, anecdotes

3. Ask rhetorical questions, repeat key ideas

4. Use build-ups, contrast and call to action

5. Finish with a strong closing message

**Tips:**

* Practice reading aloud to ensure smooth flow

* Use the rule of three (make it more satisfying, effective and memorable) and inclusive pronouns (we, us, etc.)
