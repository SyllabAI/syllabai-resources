## **Descriptive Writing**

Descriptive writing creates a vivid mental image using sensory detail and figurative language. There may be no plot as it focuses on atmosphere and imagery rather than action.

**Purpose:** To create a vivid picture of anything specific by using descriptive words

**Audience:** General reader

**Tone:** Evocative and immersive

**Conventions:**

* Figurative language (simile, metaphor, personification, etc.)

* Sensory imagery

* No plot or minimal action

* Focus on detail and atmosphere

**How to write:**

1. Choose a moment or scene to focus on

2. Use all five senses

3. Build mood and atmosphere

4. Avoid telling rather show everything

5. Use varied sentence structures

**Tips:**

* Use metaphors and unusual comparisons

* Focus deeply on one place or moment
