## **Podcasts**

Podcasts are spoken texts written in advance, often in the style of informal conversation. They aim to inform, entertain or reflect and are written to sound natural when spoken aloud.

**Purpose**: To entertain, inform or reflect through spoken word

**Audience:** Listeners (often informal)

**Tone:** Conversational, lively and reflective

**Conventions:**

* Introduction of speaker or topic

* Clear structure or theme

* Spoken features (pauses, fillers and emphasis)

* Rhetorical questions and anecdotes

**How to write:**

1. Start with a friendly greeting and purpose

2. Use a personal and engaging tone

3. Keep ideas organised and flowing

4. Add pauses, emphasis and humour

5. End with a sign-off or summary

**Tips:**

* Read aloud while writing – ask yourself, ‘‘does it sound natural?’’

* Use repetition and voice shifts to maintain interest
