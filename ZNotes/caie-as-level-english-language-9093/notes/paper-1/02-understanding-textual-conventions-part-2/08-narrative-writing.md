## **Narrative Writing**

Narrative writing is storytelling, which is a structured account of characters, events and emotions. It draws the reader into a fictional or personal world through vivid description and pacing.

**Purpose:** To tell a story

**Audience:** General readers

**Tone:** Depends on genre (mysterious, emotional or light)

**Conventions:**

* Character, setting, conflict and resolution

* Dialogue

* Descriptive detail

* Structured plot

**How to write:**

1. Open with a strong hook

2. Introduce character + setting quickly

3. Build to a climax or conflict

4. Resolve or leave a twist

5. Use show, do not just tell

**Tips:**

* Use sensory description to bring scenes alive

* Vary sentence length for pace and impact
