# Understanding Textual Conventions (Part-2)

## Introduction

Paper 1 tests your understanding of a wide range of textual forms. You will need to:

* Analyse language, audience, tone, style and purpose

* Understand the conventions and structure of various written forms

* Write in different styles based on context and audience

* Compare texts and write commentaries

This guide covers key textual forms you must know, their features, how to write them and tips to master each of them. However, these chapters are tested but not often, apart from 15 and 16 (heavily tested).
