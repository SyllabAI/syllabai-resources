## **Travel Writing**

Travel writing focuses on exploring places, cultures and experiences through descriptive language and personal perspective. It merges narrative, reflection and rich imagery.

**Purpose:** To describe and reflect on places

**Audience:** Curious or armchair travellers

**Tone:** Evocative and observational

**Conventions:**

* Descriptive and sensory language

* Anecdotes and local detail

* First person

* Contrast and comparison

**How to write:**

1. Start with a memorable setting or encounter

2. Use vivid sensory detail

3. Blend personal reaction with description

4. Include cultural or people observations

5. End with a concluding thought

**Tips:**

* Think beyond setting — what did you learn from the place?

* Use metaphors or similes for fresh imagery
