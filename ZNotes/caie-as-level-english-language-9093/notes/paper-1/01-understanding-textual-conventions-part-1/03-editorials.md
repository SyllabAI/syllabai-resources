## **Editorials**

Editorials are opinion pieces written by editors or columnists in newspapers or magazines. They tend to argue a viewpoint on a current issue, often trying to influence public opinion or spark debate.

**Purpose:** To present a strong opinion on a current issue

**Audience:** General newspaper readers

**Tone:** Formal, assertive and persuasive

**Conventions:**

* Clear opinion from the start

* Argument structured logically

* Counter-arguments acknowledged and refuted

* Persuasive techniques (ethos, pathos and logos)

* Formal style but emotionally engaged

**How to write:**

1. Open with a hook and a clear thesis statement

2. Structure ideas into clear, argumentative paragraphs

3. Use evidence and statistics to back claims

4. Include rhetorical questions and anaphora

5. Conclude with a strong restatement of your view

**Tips:**

* Think about audience values (readers of a liberal vs conservative paper)

* Use connectives to build an argument: moreover, consequently, however, etc.
