## **Advertisements, Brochures and Leaflets**

These are promotional or informative texts designed to attract attention, inform an audience or encourage actions like buying, signing up or visiting. They are usually short, visually structured and highly persuasive.

**Purpose:** To persuade, promote and inform

**Audience:** General or targeted consumers

**Tone:** Persuasive, engaging and direct

**Conventions:**

* Headings and sub-headings

* Emotive and persuasive language

* Short and punchy sentences

* Slogans and brand language

* Bullet points for key features

* Contact information, pricing and offers

**How to write:**

1. Start with an attention-grabbing heading or slogan

2. Present key benefits or offers

3. Use persuasive devices (repetition and direct address)

4. Organise with sub-headings or bullet points

5. End with a clear call-to-action

**Tips:**

* Focus on benefits, not just features

* Use visual imagination (how would it look as a real leaflet?)

* Use parallelism for rhythm
