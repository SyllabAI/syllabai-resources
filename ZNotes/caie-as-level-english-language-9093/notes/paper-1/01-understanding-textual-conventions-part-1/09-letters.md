## **Letters**

Letters are written forms of communication addressed to a specific individual or group. They can be formal or informal, and are used to inform, persuade, complain, request or simply communicate.

**Purpose:** To communicate or persuade

**Audience:** Depends on the recipient (editor, friend, official, etc.)

**Tone:** Formal or informal depending on the audience

**Conventions:**

* Address and date (for formal)

* Salutation and sign-off

* Paragraph structure

* Clear tone (respectful, assertive, etc.)

**How to write:**

1. Choose an appropriate salutation (Dear Sir/Madam, Hi Jamie)

2. State the purpose in the opening paragraph

3. Use a structured argument in the body

4. Conclude with a clear closing line

5. Sign off appropriately (Yours sincerely for a formal letter, Best wishes for an informal letter)

**Tips:**

* Know when to be formal or informal

* Stay polite even when critical
