## **News Stories**

News stories are factual, objective reports about current, trendy events. They are structured to deliver essential information quickly, followed by supporting details and background.

**Purpose:** To inform objectively

**Audience:** General public

**Tone**: Neutral and factual

**Conventions:**

* Inverted pyramid structure (most important information first)

* 5Ws and 1H (Who, What, When, Where, Why, How) in the first paragraph

* Short paragraphs

* Third person and passive voice

* No opinion or bias

**How to write:**

1. Start with the lead (summary of key information)

2. Add details in descending order of importance

3. Include quotes if relevant

4. Keep paragraphs short and informative

**Tips:**

* Stick to facts only

* Use clear and uncluttered sentences

* Make sure the 5Ws are answered early
