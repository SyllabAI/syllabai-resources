## **Reviews**

Reviews are evaluative texts that offer a critical opinion of something, such as a book, film, play, restaurant or product. They aim to help readers decide whether it is worth their time, money or interest.

**Purpose:** To evaluate and inform

**Audience:** Readers deciding whether to engage with the product (film, book, etc.)

**Tone:** Subjective yet balanced

**Conventions:**

* Title and reference to subject

* Brief summary of content

* Personal evaluation

* Specific evidence (scenes, quotes or features)

* Recommendation or rating

**How to write:**

1. Begin with basic information (title, creator or genre)

2. Summarise the content briefly

3. Provide a critical evaluation

4. Support opinions with examples

5. Conclude with an overall impression and recommendation

**Tips:**

* Avoid spoilers (or signal them clearly)

* Use descriptive adjectives to reflect tone (haunting, uninspired, etc.)

* Be fair: mention strengths and weaknesses
