## **Blogs**

Blogs are informal, often personal pieces of writing published online. They might reflect on personal experiences, comment on social issues or give advice by always using a direct, conversational tone.

**Purpose:** To reflect, narrate, inform or persuade

**Audience**: Often informal, online readership

**Tone**: Conversational and personal

**Conventions:**

* First person

* Anecdotes and opinions

* Headings and sub-headings or informal layout

* Interactive tone (questions or comments)

**How to write:**

1. Choose a clear focus or theme

2. Write in a friendly and engaging voice

3. Use short paragraphs and rhetorical questions

4. Include reflection or humour

5. End with personal insight or takeaway

**Tips:**

* Be yourself since voice matters more than formality

* Keep it visually digestible (short and varied sentences)
