## **Investigate Journalism**

This is a deep, research-based form of journalism that seeks to uncover hidden truths, often about controversial topics or injustices. It relies on factual evidence, interviews and strong structure.

**Purpose:** To uncover the truth and inform

**Audience:** Serious readers interested in social issues

**Tone:** Formal, factual and analytical

**Conventions:**

* Complex structure with background and findings

* Quotes and sources

* Formal tone; no personal voice

* Factual and evidence-based

**How to write:**

1. Start with a summary of the issue

2. Present background context

3. Introduce key findings or revelations

4. Include quotes and data

5. Conclude with implications or ongoing concerns

**Tips:**

* Do thorough research; simulate a realistic investigation

* Use real or invented credible sources for effect
