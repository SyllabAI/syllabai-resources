## **Articles**

Articles are typically found in magazines, blogs or newspapers, and aim to inform, entertain or reflect on a topic. They balance information with a clear writer’s voice.

**Purpose:** To inform, explain or comment

**Audience:** Readers of a magazine, blog or online platform

**Tone:** Engaging and semi-formal

**Conventions:**

* Headline and subheading

* Introduction to hook the reader

* Developed argument or theme

* Anecdotes or examples

* Clear conclusion or reflection

**How to write**:

1. Create a catchy headline

2. Write an intro that hooks (question, statistic or anecdote)

3. Use topic sentences for each paragraph

4. Maintain a consistent tone

5. End with a memorable reflection or insight

**Tips:**

* Know your intended audience

* Keep paragraphs focused with strong topic sentences

* Blend fact and opinion smoothly
