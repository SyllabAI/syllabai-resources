## Figurative Language

**Introduction**: Language that goes beyond the literal to create imagery or emotional depth.

**Examples**:

* Simile — ‘as brave as a lion’

* Metaphor — ‘time is a thief’

* Personification — ‘the trees whispered’

* Hyperbole — ‘a million times’

**Effect**:

* Engages imagination and senses.

* Makes text memorable and impactful.

* Strong persuasive or creative effect.

**Tip**: Use sparingly since too much figurative language weakens clarity in non-creative tasks.
