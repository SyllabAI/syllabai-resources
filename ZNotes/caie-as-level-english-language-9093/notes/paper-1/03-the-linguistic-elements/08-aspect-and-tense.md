## Aspect and Tense

**Introduction**: How time is expressed in writing.

**Examples**:

* Present — ‘She sings.’

* Past — ‘She sang.’

* Future — ‘She will sing.’

* Continuous — ‘She is singing.’

* Perfect — ‘She has sung.’

**Effect**:

* Present — immediacy, urgency.

* Past — reflection, storytelling.

* Continuous — vividness, ongoing action.

* Perfect — completion, authority.

**Tips**: Keep tense consistent unless deliberately shifting for effect, for instance, flashbacks.
