## **V**ocabulary

**Introduction**: The actual words chosen; determines tone and precision.

**Examples**:

* Formal: legislation, therefore.

* Informal: mate, chill.

* Abstract: freedom, justice.

**Effect**:

* Formal — authority, seriousness.

* Informal — intimacy, relatability.

* Abstract — reflective, intellectual.

**Tips**: Build a rich vocabulary bank with the help of past papers. Switch registers smoothly when adapting to new genres (blog ≠ editorial).
