## Modality

**Introduction**: Degree of certainty/obligation/possibility in language.

**Examples**:

* High — must, will, definitely.

* Medium — should, probably.

* Low — might, could, perhaps.

**Effect**:

* High — confident, authoritative, persuasive.

* Medium — balanced, reasonable.

* Low — uncertain, polite, reflective.

**Tips**: In persuasive writing, use high modality. For reflective or cautious writing, use lower modality.
