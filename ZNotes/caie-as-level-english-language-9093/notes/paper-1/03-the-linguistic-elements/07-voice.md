## Voice

**Introduction**: The personality and perspective of writing.

**Examples**:

* Active — ‘The cat chased the mouse.’

* Passive — ‘The mouse was chased.’

* First person — (I/we), second person (you) and third person (he/they).

**Effect**:

* Active — clarity, energy.

* Passive — formal, detached.

* First person — personal, intimate.

* Second person — direct, engaging.

* Third person — objective, authoritative.

**Tips**: Match voice to task. A reflective diary ≠ a formal report.
