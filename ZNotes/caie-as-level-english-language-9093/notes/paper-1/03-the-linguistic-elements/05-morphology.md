## Morphology

**Introduction**: The structure and formation of words.

**Examples**:

* Prefixes — un-, pre-, re-

* Suffixes — -ness, -ly, -tion

* Roots — act, play, form

* Compound words — football, notebook

**Effect**:

* Latin/Greek roots — formal, technical feel.

* Anglo-Saxon roots — plain, direct.

* Morphology shifts tone (happy → unhappiness).

**Tips**: Spot where word formality changes meaning. In Paper 2, deliberately choose ‘start’ (simple) vs ‘commence’ (formal).
