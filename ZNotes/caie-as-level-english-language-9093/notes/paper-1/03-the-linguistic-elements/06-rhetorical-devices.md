## Rhetorical Devices

**Introduction**: Persuasive or impactful techniques in writing.

**Examples**:

* Rule of three — ‘life, liberty, happiness’

* Repetition — ‘We must fight. We must win.’

* Rhetorical questions — ‘Who would not want peace?’

* Parallelism/Antithesis — ‘It was the best of times, it was the worst of times.’

**Effect**:

* Persuades and engages reader.

* Creates rhythm, drama, emphasis.

* Makes ideas memorable.

**Tip**: Especially powerful in speeches and editorials but do not overuse.
