## Phonology

**Introduction**: The sound patterns of language.

**Examples**: Alliteration (‘wild winds’), assonance (‘smooth blue’), onomatopoeia (‘buzz’), rhythm and rhyme.

**Effect**:

* Creates musicality and emphasis.

* Increases memorability (ads, slogans).

* Engages emotions through sound.

**Tips**: Great in speeches or advertisements, but subtle in essays. Read aloud to check flow.
