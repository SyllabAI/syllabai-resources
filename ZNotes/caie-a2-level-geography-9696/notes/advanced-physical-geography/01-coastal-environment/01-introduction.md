# Coastal Environment

## Introduction

* The Coast is a narrow zone where the land and sea overlap and directly interact.

* Coastal environments are the most varied and rapidly changing in the world, and vary due to:

  * **Marine factors:** Waves, Tides, and Marine Life

  * **Human factors:** Urbanisation, Settlements, Tourism and Management

  * **Atmospheric factors:** Climate, Climate Change and Gravity

  * **Terrestrial factors:** Geology, Tectonic Activity, Erosion and Weathering and Biotic Factors
