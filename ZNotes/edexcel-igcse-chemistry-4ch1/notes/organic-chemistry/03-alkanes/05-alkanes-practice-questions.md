## Alkanes Practice Questions

1. State the general formula of the alkanes and explain why alkanes are described as saturated.
2. Give the molecular formula of the alkane with $6$ carbon atoms.
3. Name the two structural isomers of $C_4H_{10}$.
4. Write a balanced equation for the complete combustion of propane.
5. Write the equation for the reaction between methane and chlorine. State the condition needed and the type of reaction.
6. Ethane reacts with bromine in UV light. Name the organic product formed by mono-substitution.

**Answers**

1. $C_nH_{2n+2}$. They contain only single carbon–carbon bonds, so no more atoms can be added.
2. $C_6H_{14}$
3. Butane and 2-methylpropane
4. $C_3H_8 + 5O_2 \rightarrow 3CO_2 + 4H_2O$
5. $CH_4 + Cl_2 \rightarrow CH_3Cl + HCl$. UV light is needed. It is a substitution reaction.
6. Bromoethane, $C_2H_5Br$
