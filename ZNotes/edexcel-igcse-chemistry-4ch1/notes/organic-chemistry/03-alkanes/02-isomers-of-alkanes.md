## Isomers of Alkanes

* Alkanes are a homologous series: each member differs from the next by a $CH_2$ unit, and they all have the general formula $C_nH_{2n+2}$.
* Alkanes with $4$ or more carbon atoms can have **structural isomers**: the same molecular formula but a different arrangement of the carbon chain (straight or branched).

* **Naming**: Find the longest carbon chain, then number it so that the methyl side chains get the lowest numbers. For example, in 2-methylbutane, the longest chain has $4$ carbons (butane) and a methyl group is on carbon $2$.
* Branched isomers usually have **lower boiling points** than their straight-chain isomers, as the molecules cannot pack as closely together, so the intermolecular forces are weaker.
