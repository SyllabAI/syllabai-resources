## Combustion of Alkanes

* Alkanes are used as fuels because they release a lot of heat energy when they burn.

### Complete Combustion

* In plenty of oxygen, alkanes burn to form **carbon dioxide** and **water**.

$$
CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

$$
C_3H_8(g) + 5O_2(g) \rightarrow 3CO_2(g) + 4H_2O(l)
$$

$$
2C_2H_6(g) + 7O_2(g) \rightarrow 4CO_2(g) + 6H_2O(l)
$$

### Incomplete Combustion

* In a limited supply of oxygen, alkanes form **carbon monoxide** and/or **carbon (soot)**, and water. Less energy is released.

$$
2C_2H_6(g) + 5O_2(g) \rightarrow 4CO(g) + 6H_2O(l)
$$

$$
2C_2H_6(g) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 4C(s) + 6H_2O(l)
$$

* Carbon monoxide is toxic, as it binds to haemoglobin in red blood cells and stops them carrying oxygen.
