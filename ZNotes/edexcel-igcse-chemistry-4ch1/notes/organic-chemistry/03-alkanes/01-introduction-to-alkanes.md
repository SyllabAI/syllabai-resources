# Alkanes

## Introduction to Alkanes

* Alkanes are the simplest hydrocarbons. They contain **only single** carbon–carbon covalent bonds.
* General formula: $C_nH_{2n+2}$
* Alkanes are **saturated** hydrocarbons: every carbon atom forms single bonds only, so no more atoms can be added to the molecule.
* Alkanes are mainly used as **fuels**, as they release a lot of energy when they burn.
* Alkanes are generally **unreactive**, but they:
  * Burn in oxygen (combustion)
  * React with halogens in the presence of UV light (substitution)
  * Can be cracked into smaller molecules

### Physical Properties

* The first four alkanes are gases at room temperature. Larger alkanes are liquids, and very large alkanes (from about $C_{18}H_{38}$) are solids.
* As the molecules get bigger, the **boiling point increases**. The intermolecular forces of attraction between molecules get stronger as the molecules get larger, so more energy is needed to overcome them.

**Worked example**

* Use the general formula to find the molecular formula of the alkane with $8$ carbon atoms.
* $n = 8$, so the number of hydrogen atoms $= 2(8) + 2 = 18$.
* Molecular formula $= C_8H_{18}$ (octane)
