# Carboxylic Acids

## Introduction to Carboxylic Acids

* Carboxylic acids are a homologous series that contain the **carboxyl** functional group, $-COOH$.
* Their names end in **-anoic acid**.
* Carboxylic acids are formed by the **oxidation** of the corresponding alcohols, e.g. ethanol is oxidised to ethanoic acid.
* The carbon atom in the $-COOH$ group is counted in the name, e.g. ethanoic acid ($CH_3COOH$) has $2$ carbon atoms.

#|
|| **Name** | **Molecular formula** | **Structural formula** ||
|| Methanoic acid | $CH_2O_2$ | $HCOOH$ ||

|| Propanoic acid | $C_3H_6O_2$ | $CH_3CH_2COOH$ ||
|| Butanoic acid | $C_4H_8O_2$ | $CH_3CH_2CH_2COOH$ ||

### Vinegar

* **Vinegar** is an aqueous solution containing **ethanoic acid**.
* It is made by the microbial oxidation of ethanol (e.g. in wine) by bacteria in the air.
* It is used to flavour and preserve food.

**Practice question**

* Name the carboxylic acid formed when propan-1-ol is oxidised, and give its structural formula.

**Answer**

* Propanoic acid, $CH_3CH_2COOH$
