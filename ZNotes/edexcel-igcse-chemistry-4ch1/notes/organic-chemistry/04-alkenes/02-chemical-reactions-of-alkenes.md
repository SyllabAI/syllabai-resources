## Chemical Reactions of Alkenes

### Combustion

$$
C_2H_4(g) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 2CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

* Alkenes are not usually burned as fuels, as they are more useful for making polymers and other chemicals.

### Addition Reactions

* In an addition reaction, one of the two bonds in the $C=C$ double bond breaks, leaving a $C-C$ single bond. The electrons from the broken bond are used to join new atoms onto the two carbon atoms.
* Only one product is formed.

### Addition of Bromine

* Alkenes react with bromine to form a **dibromoalkane**.

$$
CH_2=CH_2(g) + Br_2(aq) \rightarrow CH_2BrCH_2Br(l)
$$

* ethene + bromine → 1,2-dibromoethane
* The bromine atoms add onto the two carbon atoms that were joined by the $C=C$ bond, so the product is named 1,2-dibromoethane.

$$
CH_2=CHCH_3(g) + Br_2(aq) \rightarrow CH_2BrCHBrCH_3(l)
$$

* propene + bromine → 1,2-dibromopropane

### Addition of Steam (Hydration)

* Alkenes react with steam to form **alcohols**. This is used to make ethanol from ethene (see the Alcohols chapter).

$$
C_2H_4(g) + H_2O(g) \rightarrow C_2H_5OH(g)
$$

* Conditions: $300 \ ^\circ C$, $60-70$ atmospheres, phosphoric acid catalyst

### Practice Questions

1. State the general formula of the alkenes.
2. Explain why alkenes are described as unsaturated.
3. Name the product of the reaction between ethene and bromine, and write the equation.
4. Describe a test to show that a gas is an alkene and not an alkane.
5. Write the structural formulae of two isomers of $C_4H_8$ that are alkenes.

**Answers**

1. $C_nH_{2n}$
2. They contain a $C=C$ double bond, so more atoms can be added to the molecule.
3. 1,2-dibromoethane: $C_2H_4 + Br_2 \rightarrow C_2H_4Br_2$
4. Bubble the gas through bromine water. An alkene turns it from orange to colourless; an alkane leaves it orange.
5. But-1-ene ($CH_2=CHCH_2CH_3$) and but-2-ene ($CH_3CH=CHCH_3$)
