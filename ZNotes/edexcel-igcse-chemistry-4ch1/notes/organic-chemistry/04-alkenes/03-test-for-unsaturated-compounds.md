## Test for Unsaturated Compounds

* **Bromine water** is used to test for a $C=C$ double bond (to distinguish alkenes from alkanes).
* **Method**:
  * For a liquid: add a few drops of ***bromine water*** to the compound and shake.
  * For a gas: bubble the gas through bromine water.
* **Results**:

#|
||

**Compound**

|

**Observation**

|

**Reason**

||
||

Alkene (unsaturated)

|

Bromine water ***decolourises***: changes from **orange to colourless**

|

Bromine adds across the $C=C$ bond to form a colourless dibromoalkane

||
||

Alkane (saturated)

|

Bromine water **stays orange**

|

There is no $C=C$ bond, so no addition reaction happens
