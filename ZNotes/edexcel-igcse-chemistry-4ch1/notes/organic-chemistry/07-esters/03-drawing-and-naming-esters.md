## Drawing and Naming Esters

### Naming

* The **alcohol** gives the first part of the name (**-yl**), and the **acid** gives the second part (**-oate**).
* An ester can be drawn either way around, but the name is always written with the alcohol part (-yl) first, followed by the acid part (-anoate).
* **Example**: ethanol \+ ethanoic acid → **ethyl ethanoate**. If methanol and propanoic acid were used, **methyl propanoate** would form.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/6f52aeece494dfb25beb31f4.webp =730x)

*Formation of ethyl ethanoate: the ethyl part comes from ethanol and the ethanoate part comes from ethanoic acid.*

### Drawing an Ester

1. Draw the carboxylic acid and the alcohol side by side, with the $-COOH$ group facing the $-OH$ group.
2. Remove $-OH$ from the **acid** and $-H$ from the **alcohol**. These form a molecule of **water**.
3. Join the remaining parts together through the oxygen atom to form the $-COO-$ ester link.

$$R-COOH + HO-R' \rightarrow R-COO-R' + H_2O
$$

* Here $R$ is the carbon chain of the acid and $R'$ is the carbon chain of the alcohol.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/4a341939726a27218e873f21.webp =664x)

*Forming an ester:* $-OH$ is removed from the acid and $-H$ from the alcohol to form water, and the remaining parts join to form the ester.

**Worked example**

* Name and write the structural formula of the ester formed from methanol and propanoic acid.

$$CH_3CH_2COOH + CH_3OH \rightleftharpoons CH_3CH_2COOCH_3 + H_2O
$$

* The alcohol (methanol) gives **methyl** and the acid (propanoic acid) gives **propanoate**: the ester is **methyl propanoate**.

**Practice questions**

1. Name the ester formed from ethanol and methanoic acid.
2. Name the alcohol and carboxylic acid used to make propyl ethanoate.
3. Write the structural formula of ethyl propanoate.

**Answers**

1. Ethyl methanoate, $HCOOCH_2CH_3$
2. Propan-1-ol and ethanoic acid
3. $CH_3CH_2COOCH_2CH_3$
