## Making an Ester

* Esters are made by reacting a carboxylic acid with an alcohol. This reaction is called **esterification**.
* It is a **condensation reaction**: two molecules join together and a small molecule (water) is removed.
* **Conditions**: a few drops of **concentrated sulfuric acid** as a catalyst, and **heat**.

$$
CH_3COOH(l) + CH_3CH_2OH(l) \rightleftharpoons CH_3COOCH_2CH_3(l) + H_2O(l)
$$

* ethanoic acid + ethanol ⇌ ethyl ethanoate + water
* The reaction is **reversible**, so to maximise the yield of ester, pure (water-free) ethanol and ethanoic acid are used.

### Practical: Preparing an Ester

1. Add about $1 \ cm^3$ of ethanoic acid and $1 \ cm^3$ of ethanol to a test tube.
2. Carefully add a few drops of concentrated sulfuric acid (catalyst).
3. Warm the test tube gently in a hot water bath for a few minutes. Ethanol is flammable, so a water bath is used instead of a naked flame.
4. Pour the mixture into a beaker of sodium carbonate solution. This neutralises the remaining acids, and the ester forms a layer on the surface.
5. Carefully waft the smell towards your nose: a sweet, fruity smell shows that an ester has formed.

* **Safety**: Concentrated sulfuric acid is corrosive, so wear eye protection and gloves. Keep ethanol away from naked flames.

### Hydrolysis of Esters

* The reverse reaction is called **hydrolysis**: the ester reacts with water to form the carboxylic acid and alcohol again.

$$
CH_3COOCH_2CH_3 + H_2O \rightarrow CH_3COOH + CH_3CH_2OH
$$

**Practice question**

* Write the equation for the reaction between methanol and ethanoic acid, name the ester formed, and state the catalyst needed.

**Answer**

* $CH_3COOH + CH_3OH \rightleftharpoons CH_3COOCH_3 + H_2O$. Methyl ethanoate. Concentrated sulfuric acid.
