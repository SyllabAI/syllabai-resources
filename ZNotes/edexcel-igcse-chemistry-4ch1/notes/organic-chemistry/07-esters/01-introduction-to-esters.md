# Esters

## Introduction to Esters

* Esters are formed by the reaction between a **carboxylic acid** and an **alcohol**.
* They contain the **ester** functional group, $-COO-$ (also written $-C(=O)O-$).
* Esters are **volatile** liquids (they evaporate easily) with distinctive, often fruity, **smells**.

### Uses of Esters

* **Food flavourings**, e.g. fruity flavours in sweets
* **Perfumes**, as they are volatile and have pleasant smells
* Small esters are also used as **solvents** (e.g. in glues and nail varnish remover)

### Naming Esters

* The name has two parts: the **alcohol** gives the first part (**-yl**), and the **carboxylic acid** gives the second part (**-anoate**).
