## Cracking

* **Cracking**: The process of breaking down long-chain alkanes into shorter-chain alkanes and alkenes.
* Cracking is an example of **thermal decomposition**.

### Why Cracking Is Needed

* Fractional distillation produces more long-chain hydrocarbons (e.g. fuel oil) than are needed, and not enough short-chain hydrocarbons (e.g. gasoline).
* Cracking helps to **match supply with demand**, by turning less useful long-chain fractions into more useful shorter-chain fuels such as petrol.
* It also produces **alkenes**, which are used to make polymers and other chemicals such as ethanol.

### Catalytic Cracking

* The long-chain hydrocarbons (e.g. from the fuel oil or diesel fraction) are heated to vaporise them.
* The vapour is passed over a hot catalyst of **silica** ($SiO_2$) or **alumina** ($Al_2O_3$) at about $600-700 \ ^\circ C$.
* At higher temperatures, cracking can also take place without a catalyst (thermal cracking).

$$
C_{14}H_{30} \rightarrow C_{10}H_{22} + C_4H_8
$$

* The same molecule can split in different ways:

$$
C_{13}H_{28}(l) \rightarrow 2C_2H_4(g) + C_9H_{20}(l)
$$

$$
C_{13}H_{28}(l) \rightarrow 2C_2H_4(g) + C_3H_6(g) + C_6H_{14}(l)
$$

**Worked example**

* Complete the equation: $C_{12}H_{26} \rightarrow C_8H_{18} + \ ?$
* Carbon: $12 - 8 = 4$. Hydrogen: $26 - 18 = 8$. The missing product is $C_4H_8$ (butene, an alkene).

**Practice questions**

1. Name the catalysts used in catalytic cracking and state the temperature.
2. Give two reasons why cracking is carried out.
3. Complete the equation: $C_{10}H_{22} \rightarrow C_7H_{16} + \ ?$

**Answers**

1. Silica or alumina, at about $600-700 \ ^\circ C$.
2. To produce more of the shorter-chain fractions (such as petrol) that are in high demand, and to produce alkenes for making polymers.
3. $C_3H_6$ (propene)
