# Crude Oil

## Crude Oil and Its Fractions

* Crude oil is a **mixture of hydrocarbons** of different chain lengths, mainly alkanes.
* It is a **finite** and **non-renewable** resource (it formed over millions of years and is being used up much faster than it forms).
* Crude oil is separated into **fractions**. Each fraction is a group of hydrocarbons with a similar number of carbon atoms, so they have similar boiling points.

### Trends in Physical Properties

* As the number of carbon atoms (chain length) increases:
  * **Boiling point increases**: larger molecules have stronger intermolecular forces of attraction between them, so more energy is needed to overcome these forces.
  * **Viscosity increases**: the liquids become thicker and flow less easily, as the stronger forces of attraction hold the molecules together.
  * **Colour becomes darker**.
  * **Volatility decreases**: larger hydrocarbons evaporate more slowly and don’t turn into a gas easily.
  * **Flammability decreases**: larger hydrocarbons don’t burn easily, so shorter-chain hydrocarbons are used as fuels.
