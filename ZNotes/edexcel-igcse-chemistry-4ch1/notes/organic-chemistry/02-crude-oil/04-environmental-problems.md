## Environmental Problems

### Oxides of Nitrogen

* In car engines, the high temperatures (and sparks in petrol engines) provide enough energy for **nitrogen and oxygen from the air** to react, forming oxides of nitrogen ($NO_x$).

$$
N_2(g) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2NO(g)
$$

$$
2NO(g) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2NO_2(g)
$$

* **Catalytic converters** in cars convert harmful gases such as carbon monoxide and oxides of nitrogen into less harmful gases (carbon dioxide and nitrogen).

### Sulfur Dioxide

* Fossil fuels contain sulfur impurities. When the fuel is burned, the sulfur is oxidised to **sulfur dioxide**:

$$
S(s) + O_2(g) \rightarrow SO_2(g)
$$

### Acid Rain

* Sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen dissolve in water in the atmosphere and react with oxygen to form **sulfuric acid** and **nitric acid**. This falls as **acid rain**.

$$
2SO_2(g) + O_2(g) + 2H_2O(l) \rightarrow 2H_2SO_4(aq)
$$

$$
4NO_2(g) + O_2(g) + 2H_2O(l) \rightarrow 4HNO_3(aq)
$$

* **Effects of acid rain**:
  * Kills trees and damages plants
  * Makes lakes and rivers acidic, killing fish and other aquatic life
  * Corrodes metal structures and damages buildings and statues made of limestone:

$$
CaCO_3(s) + H_2SO_4(aq) \rightarrow CaSO_4(s) + H_2O(l) + CO_2(g)
$$

### Carbon Dioxide

* Burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas, which contributes to global warming and climate change.
