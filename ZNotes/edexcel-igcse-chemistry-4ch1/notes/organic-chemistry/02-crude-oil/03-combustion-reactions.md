## Combustion Reactions

* A fuel is a substance that releases heat energy when it burns.
* Hydrocarbon fuels release heat energy when they react with oxygen (combustion). Combustion is exothermic.

### Complete Combustion

* Occurs when there is **plenty of oxygen**. The products are **carbon dioxide** and **water**.

$$
CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

$$
2C_8H_{18}(l) + 25O_2(g) \rightarrow 16CO_2(g) + 18H_2O(l)
$$

### Incomplete Combustion

* Occurs when there is **not enough oxygen**. The products are **carbon monoxide** and/or **carbon (soot)**, and water.

$$
2CH_4(g) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 2CO(g) + 4H_2O(l)
$$

$$
CH_4(g) + O_2(g) \rightarrow C(s) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

* Incomplete combustion releases **less energy** than complete combustion.
* Soot (carbon) blackens buildings and can cause breathing problems.

### Why Carbon Monoxide Is Poisonous

* Carbon monoxide is a colourless, odourless, toxic gas.
* It combines with **haemoglobin** in red blood cells more strongly than oxygen does.
* This reduces the ability of the blood to carry oxygen around the body, which can lead to unconsciousness and death.

**Worked example**

* Balance the equation for the complete combustion of propane, $C_3H_8$.
* $3$ carbon atoms → $3CO_2$. $8$ hydrogen atoms → $4H_2O$.
* Oxygen atoms needed $= (3 \times 2) + (4 \times 1) = 10$, so $5O_2$.

$$
C_3H_8(g) + 5O_2(g) \rightarrow 3CO_2(g) + 4H_2O(l)
$$
