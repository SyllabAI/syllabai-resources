## Oxidation of Ethanol

* Ethanol can be oxidised in three ways: combustion, microbial oxidation, and heating with an oxidising agent.

### Combustion

* All alcohols burn in oxygen to form carbon dioxide and water, releasing energy.

$$
C_2H_5OH(l) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 2CO_2(g) + 3H_2O(l)
$$

* Ethanol is used as a **biofuel**. It is renewable when it is made from biological sources such as sugar cane.

### Microbial Oxidation

* If ethanol (e.g. in wine) is left open to the air, microorganisms such as bacteria use oxygen from the air to oxidise it to **ethanoic acid**. This is how **vinegar** is made.

$$
C_2H_5OH(aq) + O_2(g) \xrightarrow{\text{microbes}} CH_3COOH(aq) + H_2O(l)
$$

### Heating with Potassium Dichromate(VI)

* Add ethanol to a test tube containing **potassium dichromate(VI)** solution and dilute **sulfuric acid**, then heat the mixture.
* The ethanol is oxidised to **ethanoic acid**.
* The solution changes colour from **orange to green**. The green colour is due to $Cr^{3+}$ ions, formed when the dichromate(VI) ions are reduced.

$$
CH_3CH_2OH + 2[O] \rightarrow CH_3COOH + H_2O
$$

* $[O]$ represents oxygen from the oxidising agent.

|| Microbial oxidation | Air (oxygen) and microorganisms | Ethanoic acid (vinegar) ||
