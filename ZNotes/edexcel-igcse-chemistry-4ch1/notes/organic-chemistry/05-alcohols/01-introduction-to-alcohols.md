# Alcohols

## Introduction to Alcohols

* Alcohols are a homologous series that contain the **hydroxyl** functional group, $-OH$, covalently bonded to a carbon atom.
* General formula: $C_nH_{2n+1}OH$
* Their names end in **-ol**.
* The number in the name (e.g. the $1$ in propan-1-ol or butan-1-ol) tells you which carbon atom the $-OH$ group is attached to.

#|
|| **Name** | **Molecular formula** | **Structural formula** ||
|| Methanol | $CH_4O$ | $CH_3OH$ ||
|| Ethanol | $C_2H_6O$ | $CH_3CH_2OH$ ||
|| Propan-1-ol | $C_3H_8O$ | $CH_3CH_2CH_2OH$ ||
|| Butan-1-ol | $C_4H_{10}O$ | $CH_3CH_2CH_2CH_2OH$ ||
|#

*The first four alcohols.*

*Displayed formulae of the first four alcohols. The $-OH$ group is on carbon $1$ in each.*

* Ethanol is often written as $C_2H_5OH$.
* Propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol ($CH_3CH(OH)CH_3$) are isomers: the $-OH$ group is on a different carbon atom.

**Practice question**

* Give the molecular formula and structural formula of the alcohol with $5$ carbon atoms and the $-OH$ group on carbon $1$, and name it.

**Answer**

* $C_5H_{12}O$, $CH_3CH_2CH_2CH_2CH_2OH$, pentan-1-ol
