## Naming Organic Compounds

* The name of an organic compound has two parts:
  * The **stem (prefix)** tells you how many carbon atoms are in the longest continuous carbon chain.
  * The **suffix (ending)** tells you which homologous series (functional group) the compound belongs to.

#|
|| **Stem** | **Number of carbon atoms in the longest chain** ||
|| Meth- | $1$ ||
|| Eth- | $2$ ||
|| Prop- | $3$ ||
|| But- | $4$ ||
|| Pent- | $5$ ||
|| Hex- | $6$ ||
|#

*Stems used to name organic compounds.*

#|
|| **Suffix** | **Homologous series** | **Example** ||
|| -ane | Alkane | Propane, $C_3H_8$ ||
|| -ene | Alkene | Propene, $C_3H_6$ ||
|| -anol | Alcohol | Propanol, $C_3H_7OH$ ||
|| -anoic acid | Carboxylic acid | Propanoic acid, $C_2H_5COOH$ ||
|| -yl -anoate | Ester | Methyl propanoate, $C_2H_5COOCH_3$ ||
|#

*Suffixes used to name organic compounds.*

### Naming Branched Alkanes and Alkenes

* Side chains (branches) are named from the number of carbon atoms they contain:
  * $CH_3-$ is a **methyl** group (1 carbon)
  * $CH_3CH_2-$ is an **ethyl** group (2 carbons)
* **Rules for naming**:
  1. Find the longest continuous chain of carbon atoms (for an alkene, it must contain the $C=C$ bond).
  2. Identify the side chains (e.g. methyl).
  3. Number the carbon atoms in the chain so that the side chains (or the $C=C$ bond) get the **lowest** possible numbers.
  4. Use di- (2) or tri- (3) if the same side chain appears more than once, and list different side chains in alphabetical order.
  5. Write the name as: position of side chain + side chain name + stem + suffix.

**Worked examples**

* $CH_3CH(CH_3)CH_2CH_3$: The longest chain has $4$ carbons (but-), there is a methyl group on carbon $2$, and there are only single bonds. Name: **2-methylbutane**.
* $CH_2=CHCH_2CH_3$: The longest chain has $4$ carbons, and the $C=C$ is between carbons $1$ and $2$. Name: **but-1-ene**.
* $(CH_3)_2C=C(CH_3)_2$: The longest chain containing the $C=C$ has $4$ carbons, the $C=C$ starts at carbon $2$, and there are methyl groups on carbons $2$ and $3$. Name: **2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene**.
