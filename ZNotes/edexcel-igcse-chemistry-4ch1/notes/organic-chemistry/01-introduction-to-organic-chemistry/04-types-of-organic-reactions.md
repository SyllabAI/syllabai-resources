## Types of Organic Reactions

### Combustion

* A reaction of a compound with oxygen. Complete combustion of a hydrocarbon in excess oxygen produces carbon dioxide, water and a large amount of energy.

$$
C_3H_8(g) + 5O_2(g) \rightarrow 3CO_2(g) + 4H_2O(l)
$$

### Substitution

* A reaction in which an atom or group of atoms is replaced by a different atom or group of atoms.
* Alkanes undergo substitution with halogens in the presence of ultraviolet (UV) light:

$$
C_2H_6(g) + Br_2(g) \xrightarrow{UV} C_2H_5Br(g) + HBr(g)
$$

### Addition

* A reaction in which two or more molecules join together to form a single, larger product, with nothing else formed.
* Alkenes undergo addition reactions across the $C=C$ bond:

$$
C_2H_4(g) + Br_2(aq) \rightarrow C_2H_4Br_2(l)
$$

**Practice question**

* Classify each reaction as substitution, addition or combustion:
  1. $CH_4 + Cl_2 \rightarrow CH_3Cl + HCl$
  2. $C_3H_6 + Br_2 \rightarrow C_3H_6Br_2$
  3. $C_2H_5OH + 3O_2 \rightarrow 2CO_2 + 3H_2O$

**Answers**

1. Substitution
2. Addition
3. Combustion
