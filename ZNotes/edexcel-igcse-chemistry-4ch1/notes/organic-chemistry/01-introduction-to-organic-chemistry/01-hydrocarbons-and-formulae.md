# Introduction to Organic Chemistry

## Hydrocarbons and Formulae

### Organic Compounds and Hydrocarbons

* **Organic compounds** are compounds that contain carbon.
* **Hydrocarbons** are compounds that contain hydrogen and carbon **only**.
* The carbon atoms can be joined together by single or double covalent bonds.
* Carbon–hydrogen bonds are always single bonds.
* Each carbon atom forms **4** covalent bonds, and each hydrogen atom forms **1** covalent bond.

### Ways of Representing Organic Molecules

* **Empirical formula**: The simplest whole-number ratio of atoms of each element in the compound.
* **Molecular formula**: The actual number of atoms of each element in one molecule of the compound.
* **General formula**: A formula that all members of the same homologous series follow, written in terms of $n$ (the number of carbon atoms), e.g. $C_nH_{2n+2}$ for alkanes.
* **Structural formula**: Shows how the atoms are arranged in the molecule without drawing every bond (a condensed structural formula), e.g. $CH_3CH_2CH_3$.
* **Displayed formula**: Shows **all** the atoms and **all** the bonds in the molecule.

**Worked example**

* Hexane has the molecular formula $C_6H_{14}$. Find its empirical formula.
* The ratio $C:H = 6:14$. Both numbers divide by $2$, giving $3:7$.
* Empirical formula $= C_3H_7$

### Homologous Series and Functional Groups

* **Homologous series**: A family of organic compounds that have the same functional group and similar chemical properties.
* All members of a homologous series:
  * Have the same **general formula**
  * Have the same **functional group**
  * Have **similar chemical properties**
  * Show a **gradual change (trend) in physical properties**, e.g. boiling point increases as the chain gets longer
  * Differ from the next member by a $CH_2$ unit
* **Functional group**: An atom or group of atoms that determines the chemical properties of a compound.
