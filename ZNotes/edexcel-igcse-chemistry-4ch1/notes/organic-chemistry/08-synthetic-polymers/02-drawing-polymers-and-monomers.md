## Drawing Polymers and Monomers

### From Monomer to Polymer

1. Draw the alkene monomer with the $C=C$ double bond in the middle and the other groups above and below.
2. Change the $C=C$ double bond into a $C-C$ single bond.
3. Draw **continuation bonds** (lines going out through the brackets) on each side.
4. Add **square brackets** around the repeating unit and write $n$ at the bottom right, showing that the unit is repeated many times.

### From Polymer to Monomer

1. Identify the **repeating unit** in the polymer chain (two carbon atoms in the main chain).
2. Remove the brackets, $n$ and the continuation bonds.
3. Change the $C-C$ single bond between the two carbons into a $C=C$ double bond.

**Worked example**

* Polystyrene has the repeating unit $-[CH_2-CH(C_6H_5)]-$. Deduce its monomer.
* Remove the continuation bonds and change $C-C$ to $C=C$: the monomer is $CH_2=CH(C_6H_5)$ (styrene).

**Practice questions**

1. Write the repeating unit of the polymer made from propene, $CH_2=CHCH_3$.
2. A polymer has the repeating unit $-[CH_2-CHF]-$. Deduce the formula of its monomer.
3. Name the polymer made from chloroethene.

**Answers**

1. $-[CH_2-CH(CH_3)]-$
2. $CH_2=CHF$ (fluoroethene)
3. Poly(chloroethene), PVC
