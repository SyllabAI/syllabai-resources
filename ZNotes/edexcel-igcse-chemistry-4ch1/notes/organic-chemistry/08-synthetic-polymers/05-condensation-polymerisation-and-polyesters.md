## Condensation Polymerisation and Polyesters

* **Condensation polymerisation**: Two **different** monomers, each with **two functional groups**, join together alternately, and a **small molecule (usually water)** is removed for every link formed.

### Polyesters

* Polyesters are made from:
  * A **dicarboxylic acid**: a molecule with a $-COOH$ group at each end, e.g. $HOOC-C_6H_4-COOH$
  * A **diol**: an alcohol with an $-OH$ group at each end, e.g. $HO-CH_2CH_2-OH$
* The $-OH$ from the acid group and the $-H$ from the alcohol group are removed to form water, and an **ester link** ($-COO-$) forms between the monomers.
* One molecule of water is formed for each ester link, so $2n$ water molecules form when $n$ of each monomer react.

$$nHOOC-C_6H_4-COOH + nHO-CH_2CH_2-OH \rightarrow -[OC-C_6H_4-COO-CH_2CH_2-O]_n- + 2nH_2O
$$

### Drawing the Repeating Unit of a Polyester

* The carbon chains of the monomers are often shown as boxes, because they can be any carbon group.
* The repeating unit contains **one** acid part and **one** diol part, joined by an ester link:

$$-[OC-\square-COO-\square-O]-
$$

### Deducing the Monomers from a Polyester

1. Find the ester links ($-COO-$) in the polymer chain.
2. Break the $C-O$ bond in each ester link to separate the acid part and the diol part.
3. Add $-OH$ to the acid part to complete the $-COOH$ groups, and $-H$ to the diol part to complete the $-OH$ groups.

* Adding water to break a polyester back down into its monomers is called **hydrolysis**.

### Biopolyesters

* **Biopolyesters** are polyesters made from renewable sources such as plant sugars and oils, often using microorganisms.
* They are **biodegradable**: microorganisms can break down the ester links, so they don’t remain in the environment like addition polymers.

**Practice questions**

1. State two differences between addition and condensation polymerisation.
2. Name the two types of monomer used to make a polyester.
3. How many molecules of water are formed when $n$ molecules of a dicarboxylic acid react with $n$ molecules of a diol?
4. Why are biopolyesters better for the environment than poly(ethene)?

**Answers**

1. Addition uses one type of monomer with a $C=C$ bond and forms only the polymer; condensation usually uses two different monomers and also forms a small molecule (water).
2. A dicarboxylic acid and a diol
3. $2n$
4. They are biodegradable, so they are broken down by microorganisms instead of remaining in landfill.
