## Properties and Uses of Some Polymers

**Poly(ethene)**

|

Ethene

|

Low-density poly(ethene) (LDPE) is flexible and not very strong; high-density poly(ethene) (HDPE) is strong and rigid

|

LDPE: plastic bags and cling film; HDPE: plastic bottles

||
||

**Poly(propene)**

|

Propene

|

Slightly stronger and tougher than poly(ethene)

|

Crates, ropes

||
||

**Poly(chloroethene)** (PVC)

|

Chloroethene

|

Strong and rigid; does not conduct electricity; can be made flexible

|

Water pipes, replacement windows, insulation for electrical wires

||
||

**Poly(tetrafluoroethene)** (PTFE)

|

Tetrafluoroethene

|

Very unreactive (strong $C-F$ bonds) and gives a non-stick surface

|

Non-stick coatings for pots and pans; linings for containers of corrosive chemicals
