## Disposal of Addition Polymers

### Why Disposal Is a Problem

* Addition polymers such as poly(ethene) and poly(chloroethene) are **inert**: they contain strong covalent bonds ($C-C$ and $C-H$), so they do not react at ordinary temperatures.
* They are **non-biodegradable**: bacteria and other microorganisms cannot break them down, so they remain unchanged in the environment for hundreds or thousands of years.
* **Burning** them can produce toxic gases, e.g. carbon monoxide from incomplete combustion, and **hydrogen chloride** from burning PVC. Carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas, is also released.

* Explain why poly(ethene) bags cause a long-term waste problem when buried in landfill.

**Answer**
