## Evaporation vs Boiling

### Evaporation

* Evaporation is the process of liquid converting into gas

* Occurs at the surface only

* No fixed temperature (happens over a range)

* Dependent on surface area; high energy particles escape from the surface

### Boiling

* Boiling is the process of liquid converting into gas

* Occurs throughout the liquid

* Happens at a fixed temperature (the boiling point of the liquid)
