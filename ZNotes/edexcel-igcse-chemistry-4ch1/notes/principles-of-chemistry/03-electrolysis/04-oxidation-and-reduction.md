## Oxidation and Reduction

* **Reduction** is the gain of electrons or the loss of oxygen.

* **Oxidation** is the loss of electrons or the gain of oxygen.

* Reduction always occurs at the cathode, while oxidation always occurs at the anode.

* Not all ionic compounds can be electrolysed, as some break up into other chemical compounds before their boiling point, making it impossible to melt them.

### Classifying Half-Equations

* If electrons are on the **left** of the arrow (being gained), the reaction is **reduction**.

* If electrons are on the **right** of the arrow (being lost), the reaction is **oxidation**.

### Mnemonics

* **OILRIG**: Oxidation Is Loss (of electrons), Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)

* **ANOX**: ANode = OXidation

* **REDCAT**: REDuction at the CAThode

* **CCRG**: Cathode, Cation, Reduction (is) Gain of electrons, Reducing Agent

* **AAOL**: Anode, Anion, Oxidation (is) Loss of electrons, Oxidising Agent
