## Reacting Mass Calculations

**Method - always 3 steps:**

1. Convert the given mass to moles ($\text{mass} \div M_r$).

2. Use the mole ratio from the balanced equation.

3. Convert moles back to mass ($\text{moles} \times M_r$).

**Worked example:**

6.0 g of $Mg$ burns completely. Find the mass of $MgO$ formed.

$$
2Mg + O_2 \rightarrow 2MgO
$$

* Moles of $Mg = \frac{6.0}{24} = 0.25$ mol

* Ratio $Mg : MgO = 2 : 2 = 1 : 1$, so 0.25 mol of $MgO$ forms

* Mass of $MgO = 0.25 \times (24 + 16) = 10$ g
