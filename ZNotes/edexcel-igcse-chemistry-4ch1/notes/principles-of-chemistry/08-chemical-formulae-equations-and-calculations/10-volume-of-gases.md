## Volume of Gases

* **Avogadro's law**: At the same temperature and pressure, equal amounts (moles) of any gas occupy the same volume.

* **Molar gas volume at RTP** = $24\ dm^3$ = $24\,000\ cm^3$

* **RTP** = Room temperature and pressure = 20 °C and 1 atm.

### Key Formulae

$$
\text{volume (dm}^3) = \text{moles} \times 24
$$

$$
\text{moles} = \frac{\text{volume (dm}^3)}{24}
$$

* Here 24 is the molar volume in $dm^3$/mol at RTP. If the volume is given in $cm^3$, use 24 000 instead of 24.

**Worked example:**

What is the volume of 154 g of $N_2$ at RTP?

* $M_r(N_2) = 28$

* Moles $= \frac{154}{28} = 5.5$

* Volume $= 5.5 \times 24 = 132\ dm^3$

### Reacting Gas Volumes

* By Avogadro's law, the coefficients in a balanced equation give the volume ratio directly, so there is no need to convert to moles.

**Worked example:**

150 $cm^3$ of propane burns completely. Find the volume of oxygen needed and the total volume of gaseous products.

$$
C_3H_8(g) + 5O_2(g) \rightarrow 3CO_2(g) + 4H_2O(g)
$$

* Oxygen needed $= 5 \times 150 = 750\ cm^3$

* Total volume of gaseous products $= (3 + 4) \times 150 = 1050\ cm^3$
