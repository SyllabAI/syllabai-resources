## Relative Masses

* $1\ amu = \frac{1}{12} \times$ the mass of a carbon-12 atom.

* **Relative atomic mass ($A_r$)** - Average mass of an element's isotopes, compared to $\frac{1}{12}$ of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. (The larger number on the Periodic Table.)

* **Relative molecular mass ($M_r$)** - Total mass of a molecule.

* **Relative formula mass ($M_r$)** - Total mass of an ionic compound (e.g. $NaCl$), but usable for molecules too.

* $M_r$ = sum of all of the $A_r$ values in the formula

|| $H_2O$ | $(2 \times 1) + (1 \times 16)$ | $18$ ||
|| $K_2CO_3$ | $(2 \times 39) + 12 + (3 \times 16)$ | $138$ ||
