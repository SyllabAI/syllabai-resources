## Percentage Yield

* **Actual yield** - The amount of product actually obtained (found by experiment only).

* **Theoretical yield** - The amount expected under perfect conditions, calculated from the balanced equation.

$$
\text{percentage yield} = \frac{\text{actual yield}}{\text{theoretical yield}} \times 100
$$

### Why Yield Is Never 100%

* Reactants are left behind in the equipment

* The reaction is reversible, so products turn back into reactants

* Products are lost during separation/purification (filtration, distillation)

* Side reactions occur, or a reactant contains an impurity

* Products are lost during transfer between containers
