### Measuring pH Using Universal Indicator

* Universal indicator is a mixture of indicators that shows a different colour at each pH value, so it can be used to measure the **approximate pH** of a solution.
* **Method**:
  1. Add a few drops of universal indicator solution to a small sample of the solution being tested (or dip a piece of universal indicator paper into it).
  2. Observe the colour produced.
  3. Compare the colour with a pH colour chart to find the approximate pH value.
* Typical colours: red (pH $0-3$), orange/yellow (pH $4-6$), green (pH $7$), blue (pH $8-10$), purple (pH $11-14$).
* Universal indicator only gives an approximate pH (to the nearest whole number). A pH meter gives a more accurate value.
