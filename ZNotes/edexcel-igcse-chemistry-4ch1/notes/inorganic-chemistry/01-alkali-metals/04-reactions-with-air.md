## Reactions With Air

$$
\text{alkali metal} + \text{oxygen} \rightarrow \text{alkali metal oxide}
$$

$$
4Na(s) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2Na_2O(s)
$$

* Freshly cut pieces of alkali metals tarnish very quickly when exposed to the air, as they react with oxygen to form a layer of oxide.

* A freshly cut piece of potassium tarnishes faster than sodium, which tarnishes faster than lithium. This shows that reactivity increases down the group.

* If heated with a Bunsen burner, all alkali metals react vigorously with oxygen:

  * Lithium burns with a red flame

  * Sodium burns with a yellow flame

  * Potassium burns with a lilac flame
