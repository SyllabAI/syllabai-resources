## Reactions with Bases

* Metal oxides, metal hydroxides and ammonia are bases.

* Bases react with acids to produce a salt and water. This is a neutralisation reaction.

$$
\text{base} + \text{acid} \rightarrow \text{salt} + \text{water}
$$

### Acids and Metal Oxides

* Copper(II) oxide reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid to produce a blue solution of copper(II) chloride.

$$
CuO(s) + 2HCl(aq) \rightarrow CuCl_2(aq) + H_2O(l)
$$

* With dilute sulfuric acid, copper(II) oxide forms a blue solution of copper(II) sulfate.

$$
CuO(s) + H_2SO_4(aq) \rightarrow CuSO_4(aq) + H_2O(l)
$$

* With dilute nitric acid, magnesium oxide forms a colourless solution of magnesium nitrate.

$$
MgO(s) + 2HNO_3(aq) \rightarrow Mg(NO_3)_2(aq) + H_2O(l)
$$

### Acids and Metal Hydroxides (Alkalis)

$$
NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) \rightarrow NaCl(aq) + H_2O(l)
$$

* A colourless solution is formed and gets warmer.

$$
2KOH(aq) + H_2SO_4(aq) \rightarrow K_2SO_4(aq) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

### Acids and Ammonia

* Ammonia solution neutralises acids to form ammonium salts:

$$
NH_3(aq) + HNO_3(aq) \rightarrow NH_4NO_3(aq)
$$
