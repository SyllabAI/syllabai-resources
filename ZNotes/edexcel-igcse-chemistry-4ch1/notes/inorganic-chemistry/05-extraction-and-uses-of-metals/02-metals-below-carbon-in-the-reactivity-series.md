## Metals Below Carbon in the Reactivity Series

* The cheapest method of extraction is to heat the metal oxide with carbon.

* Carbon is higher in the reactivity series than the metal, so it takes oxygen away from the metal oxide. The carbon is oxidised (to carbon dioxide or carbon monoxide), and the metal oxide is reduced to the metal.

* The extraction of iron is carried out in a blast furnace.

$$Fe_2O_3(s) + 3C(s) \rightarrow 2Fe(l) + 3CO(g)
$$
