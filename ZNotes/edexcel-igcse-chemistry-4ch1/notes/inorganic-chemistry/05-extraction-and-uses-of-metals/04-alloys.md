## Alloys

* **Alloy**: A mixture of a metal and one or more elements, usually other metals or carbon
* Alloys are harder than individual pure metals as in alloys the atoms are differently sized, which breaks up the regular arrangement of atoms in the lattice, making it more difficult for the layers of atoms to slide over each other

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/917e0d64628cbff30c35d692.webp =987x)

*In a pure metal, the atoms are all the same size and arranged in regular layers that can slide easily. In an alloy, atoms of different sizes disrupt the layers, so they cannot slide as easily.*

* **Examples of alloys**: Bronze, stainless steel, cupronickel
