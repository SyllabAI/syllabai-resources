## Combustion of Elements With Oxygen

* When elements burn in oxygen, they form **oxides**. Metal oxides are basic, and non-metal oxides are usually acidic.

### Magnesium

* Magnesium burns in oxygen with a bright white flame to form a white powder (magnesium oxide). Magnesium oxide is not very soluble, but the small amount that dissolves forms an alkaline solution.

$$
2Mg(s) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2MgO(s)
$$

### Sulfur

* Sulfur burns in oxygen with a blue flame, producing poisonous **sulfur dioxide** gas. When sulfur dioxide dissolves in water, it forms sulfurous acid, so the solution is acidic.

$$
S(s) + O_2(g) \rightarrow SO_2(g)
$$

$$
SO_2(g) + H_2O(l) \rightarrow H_2SO_3(aq)
$$

### Hydrogen

* Hydrogen burns in oxygen with a pale blue flame, producing water.

$$
2H_2(g) + O_2(g) \rightarrow 2H_2O(l)
$$
