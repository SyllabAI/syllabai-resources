# Chemical Tests

## Testing of Gases

### Hydrogen

* A lighted splint is held to the end of the test tube, causing the hydrogen to explode with a **squeaky pop sound**.

### Oxygen

* A glowing splint is held to the end of the tube. Oxygen would **relight the glowing splint**.

### Carbon Dioxide

* Carbon dioxide gas is bubbled through limewater, causing the limewater to turn cloudy/milky.

### Chlorine

* A piece of ***damp*** blue litmus paper held at the end of the test tube would turn **red** and then **bleach** (turn white).

### Ammonia

* A piece of ***damp*** red litmus paper held at the end of the test tube would turn **blue**.

#|
|| **Gas** | **Test** | **Positive result** ||
|| Hydrogen ($H_2$) | Lighted splint | Squeaky pop ||
