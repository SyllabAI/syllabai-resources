## Physical and Chemical Test for Water

### Chemical Test

* Water turns white anhydrous copper(II) sulfate **blue**. Anhydrous copper(II) sulfate lacks the water of crystallisation, so adding water turns it back into blue hydrated copper(II) sulfate.

$$CuSO_4(s) + 5H_2O(l) \rightarrow CuSO_4 \cdot 5H_2O(s)
$$

* This test shows that water is **present**, but not whether it is pure.

### Physical Test

* The melting and boiling points of water can be used to show if it is ***pure or not***.
* Pure water boils at exactly $100 \ ^\circ C$.
* Pure water freezes (melts) at exactly $0 \ ^\circ C$.
* Impurities raise the boiling point and lower the freezing point.
