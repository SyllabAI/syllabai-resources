### Test for Ammonium Ions ($NH_4^+$)

* Add a few drops of sodium hydroxide solution to the solution being tested and **warm** the mixture gently.
* If ammonium ions are present, ammonia gas is given off.
* Identify the gas by holding a piece of **damp red litmus paper** at the mouth of the test tube. Ammonia turns it **blue**.
* Ammonia also has a sharp, pungent smell (but do not smell it directly).

$$NH_4^+(aq) + OH^-(aq) \rightarrow NH_3(g) + H_2O(l)
$$
