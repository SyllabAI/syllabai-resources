## Testing for Sulfates

* Add dilute hydrochloric acid and then barium chloride solution. This would produce a **white precipitate** of barium sulfate if sulfate ions ($SO_4^{2-}$) are present.

$$
Ba^{2+}(aq) + SO_4^{2-}(aq) \rightarrow BaSO_4(s)
$$

* The acid is added to remove any other ions present in the solution (such as carbonate ions, which would also give a white precipitate).
* Never add sulfuric acid to this solution for the test, as sulfuric acid contains sulfate ions, so you are bound to get a positive result.
