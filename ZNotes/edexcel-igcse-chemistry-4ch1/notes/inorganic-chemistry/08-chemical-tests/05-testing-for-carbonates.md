## Testing for Carbonates

* Add dilute hydrochloric acid and look for fizzing/effervescence as a gas is given off.
* Test whether this gas is carbon dioxide by bubbling it through limewater. If the limewater turns cloudy/milky, the gas is carbon dioxide, so carbonate ions ($CO_3^{2-}$) are present.

$$
CO_3^{2-} + 2H^+ \rightarrow H_2O + CO_2
$$

$$
CO_2(g) + Ca(OH)_2(aq) \rightarrow CaCO_3(s) + H_2O(l)
$$

* The limewater turns milky because insoluble calcium carbonate forms.
