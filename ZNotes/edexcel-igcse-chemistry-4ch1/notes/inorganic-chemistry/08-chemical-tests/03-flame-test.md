## Flame Test

* A platinum or nichrome wire is dipped in concentrated hydrochloric acid to remove any impurities left behind.
* It is then dipped into the salt and held in the hot, **non-luminous** (blue) Bunsen burner flame.
* The colour of the flame shows which metal ion is present.
