## Movement of Substances Into and Out of Cells

* **Diffusion**: Movement of substances from an area of high concentration to an area of low concentration, down a concentration gradient
* **Osmosis**: Movement of free water molecules from a region of high water potential (more dilute solution) to a region of low water potential (More concentrated solution) across a semi-permeable membrane
* Both osmosis and diffusion are passive processes.
* **Active Transport**: Movement of molecules from a region of low concentration to a region of high concentration against a concentration gradient, using ATP

### **Factors that affect the Rate of Movement in and out of Cells**

* **Temperature**: The higher the temperature, the higher the kinetic energy of the molecules, which increases the rate of movement.
* **Concentration Gradient:** The higher the difference between the concentrations of the two regions, the faster the rate of movement.
* **Surface area to Volume ratio:** The higher the surface area to volume ratio, the greater the movement of substances.
* **Distance:** The shorter the distance, the greater the movement of substances.
