## Respiration

* Respiration is a process that releases energy (ATP) from foods, such as glucose, to be used for metabolic reactions.

| Aerobic respiration | Anaerobic respiration |
|----|----|
| Requires energy | Does not require energy |
| Produces huge levels of energy | Doesn’t produce as much energy |
| Oxidation of glucose in the presence of oxygen | Glucose is broken down without oxygen |
| The end products are carbon dioxide and water | The end products are carbon dioxide, along with lactic acid or ethanol |

* **Word equation for aerobic respiration in plants and animals:**
  * Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water (+ ATP)


  * **C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O**
* **Word equation for anaerobic respiration in plants:**
  * Glucose → Ethanol + Carbon dioxide (+ ATP)
  * **C6H12O6 → 2C2H5OH + 2CO2**
* **Word equation for anaerobic respiration in animals:**
  * Glucose → Lactic acid (+ ATP)
  * **C6H12O6 → 2C3H6O3**
