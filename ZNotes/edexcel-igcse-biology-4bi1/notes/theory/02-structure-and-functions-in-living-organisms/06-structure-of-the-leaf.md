## Structure of the Leaf

![](../../../assets/5126d6f4d528-652fc600a961454bd12eff5d11e4d87c98e466b1753c6fa4.gif)

* **Waxy Cuticle:**
  * Thin outer layer
  * Prevents water loss by evaporation from the leaf
  * Prevents the entry of pathogens, like bacteria, into the plant cell
* **Upper Epidermis**:
  * Transparent to allow light to pass through and reach the palisade mesophyll
* **Palisade Mesophyll**:
  * Situated below the upper epidermis
  * It is packed with chlorophyll, so main site of photosynthesis
  * Long, narrow cells
  * Densely packed together
* **Spongy layer**:
  * Rounded and loosely packed
  * Has air spaces between cells, so the main site of gas exchange
    * The air spaces allow the diffusion of oxygen and carbon dioxide into and out of the cell
  * Has fewer chloroplasts than the palisade mesophyll layer
* **Lower epidermis**:
  * Has many pores called stomata
* **Stomata**:
  * Allows carbon dioxide to diffuse into the leaf for photosynthesis
* **Guards Cells:**
  * Change their shape to open or close the stoma (singular of stomata)
