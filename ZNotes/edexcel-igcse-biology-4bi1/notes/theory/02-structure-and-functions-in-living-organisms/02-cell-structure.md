## Cell Structure

* All of the following structures belong to both animal and plant cells:
  * **Nucleus**: Controls the activities of the cell and contains its genetic material
  * **Cytoplasm**: This is where all the chemical reactions take place
  * **Cell Membrane**: Forms a barrier between the inside of the cell and the outside environment. It is selectively permeable, which means it can control the movement of some substances into and out of the cell.
  * **Mitochondria**: This is where aerobic respiration occurs
  * **Ribosomes:** This is the site where protein synthesis takes place
* Three structures belong to only plant cells, and **NOT** animal cells: cell wall, permanent cell vacuole and chloroplasts
  * **Cell Wall**: It is made up of a complex carbohydrate, cellulose, which is rigid to keep the shape of the cell and strengthens and supports the cell
  * **Chloroplasts**: Contain chlorophyll, which traps energy from sunlight to be used for photosynthesis
  * **Vacuole**: Contains the cell sap

| Feature | Plant cells | Animal cells |
|----|----|----|
| **Nucleus** | Present | Present |
| **Cell membrane** | Present | Present |
| **Cytoplasm** | Present | Present |
| **Mitochondria** | Present | Present |
| **Cell wall** | Present | Absent |
| **Chloroplasts** | Present | Absent |
| **Vacuole** | Present | Absent |

### **Cell Differentiation**

* **Differentiation** is the process by which a cell specializes under the control of genes.
* Different types of cells develop depending on their location.
  * Examples include:
    * **Nerve Cells**: They have an elongated axon to carry nerve impulses
    * **Leaf Palisade Cells**: Have a lot of chloroplasts for photosynthesis
    * **Sperm Cell:** Has a tail for swimming, and the head contains the father’s genes.
* **Stem Cell**: A cell that can divide many times by mitosis while remaining undifferentiated.
* There are two types of stem cells:
  * **Embryonic Stem Cells**: These are found in the early development of the embryo and can differentiate into any cell.
  * **~~Adult Stem Cells~~**: They are found in specific adult tissue; they cannot differentiate into any type of cell but can specialize into certain cell types:
    * Bone marrow can specialize into different blood cells (red blood cells and white blood cells)
* **Stem Cell Therapy**: Using stem cells to treat or repair damaged tissues.
  * Bone marrow transplants treat patients with conditions like leukemia, as the healthy cells killed during chemotherapy can be replaced by stem cells from the bone marrow.
  * Embryonic stem cells can be cultured in a fertility clinic. We may be able to use them to treat diseases like diabetes and Parkinson’s disease and repair damaged nerve tissues.
* There are ethical concerns with the use of stem cells from embryos, and many people object morally to their use in medical research.
