# Reproduction and Inheritance

## Introduction

* **There are two types of reproduction:**
  * Sexual reproduction
  * Asexual reproduction

| Sexual Reproduction | Asexual Reproduction |
|----|----|
| Requires two parents | Requires one parent only |
| Offsprings are not genetically identical | Offsprings are genetically identical |
| Gametes are produced | No gametes are produced |
| Offsprings show genetic variation | Offsprings do not show genetic variation |
| Fertilisation takes place | Fertilisation does not take place |

* **Fertilisation:** The fusion of a male and female gamete to produce a zygote that undergoes cell division and develops into an embryo
