## Genetic Modification

* **~~Genetic Modification~~**: Scientifically altering an organism’s DNA by introducing a gene from a different organism to give a desired trait.
* In genetic modification, **restriction enzymes** are used to cut DNA at specific sites and **ligase enzymes** are used to join pieces of DNA together.
* **Recombinant DNA:** DNA that has been artificially modified by combining genes from different organisms
* **Vector**: These are either plasmids or viruses, and they’re used to carry the gene of a different organism
* **Transgenic**: The transfer of genetic material from one species to a different species
* Large amounts of human insulin can be manufactured from genetically modified bacteria grown in a fermenter.
* **Production of human insulin via genetic modification**: People with type I diabetes are unable to produce their insulin; scientists genetically modified bacteria to be able to produce human insulin.
  * Cut open the plasmid of the bacteria using restriction enzymes
  * Cut out the insulin gene of humans that produces insulin using the same restriction enzyme
  * Insert the human gene into the plasmid
  * Ligase enzymes then join the exposed ends of the insulin gene and the plasmid.
  * The recombinant plasmid is inserted back into the bacteria
  * These bacteria are grown in large amounts in a fermenter to produce enough amount of insulin
* **Genetically Modified plants**
  * These are plants that had their DNA altered to give them a desired characteristic, such as:
    * Increased crop yield
    * Resistant to drought and disease
    * Longer shelf life
