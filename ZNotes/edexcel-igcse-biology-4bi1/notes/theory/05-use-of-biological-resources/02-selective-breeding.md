## Selective Breeding

* **Selective Breeding** involves breeding individuals with desirable features so their offspring also inherit those characteristics.
* **Desired characteristics in plants include:**
  * Produces high crop yield
  * Resistant to disease
  * Resistant to drought, flood, etc
  * High growth rate
* **Process of selective breeding in plants**
  * Two plants with the desired characteristics are chosen
  * They’re cross-bred: one pollen of one plant placed on the stigma of the other plant
  * The seeds are collected and germinated
  * The offspring with the desired traits are then selected and bred together
  * The process continues for generations until all offspring show the desired characteristics
* Desired characteristics in **animals** include:
  * Higher yield (meat, milk, eggs, etc)
  * Resistant to disease
  * Produces more offspring
  * Produce more fur or better quality fur
* **Process of selective breeding in animals**
  * Parents with the desired characteristics are selected and bred together
  * The offspring that show the desired trait are then chosen and bred together
  * The process continues for generations until all offspring show the trait
