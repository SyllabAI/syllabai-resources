# Roleplay

* A random card is given to candidates with the scenario. Do not write anything on this card, or make notes anywhere.

* This part is worth 10 marks, and will last about 2 minutes. Each response is worth 2 marks.

* 10 minutes are given to prepare for the examination

* During these 10 minutes, imagine the kind of questions that may be asked.

  Examples (for the topic in the Candidate Card below):

  * بكم تريد الدراجة؟ - How much do you want the bike for?

  * ما المواصفات التي تريدها (اللون، الحجم) - What are the descriptions you want (color, size, etc.)

  * هل تملك دراجة؟ كيف كانت أول دراجة لديك؟ - Do you own a bike? How was your first bike?

  * لماذا تريد دراجة جديدة؟ ماذا ستفعل بها؟ الى أين ستذهب - Why do you want a new bike? What are you going to do with it? Where are you going to go?

* You are allowed to be creative with your response, as long as you try to answer the question correctly

* Be mindful of the tenses (masculine and feminine / past, present, future, order / singular, plural / harakat) by keeping them as close to the question as possible (If the question uses the future tense, talk about the future)

* You will not be able to see the questions, you can only hear them on the spot. If you’d like the examiner to repeat, inform them to do so (do not say ‘لا أفهم’ ‘I do not understand’ as the examiner may move on to the next question)

* Stick to ‘Modern Standard Arabic’ (فصحة) by avoiding slangs and dialects

* Be simple when answering the question. Do not give very long answers.

* Minor errors are tolerated (Adjective endings, prepositions, etc.)

* Try to include parts of the question in your answer, so that you can form a full sentence that is grammatically correct
