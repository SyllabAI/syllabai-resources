# Listening

## Tips & Tricks

* Stay vigilant and focused while listening

* Read the questions before the recording starts and underline the keyword(s), which you will try to spot while listening

* You will only hear the recording twice, so try to get the answers in the first time, and finallize in the second time

* Synonyms or keywords are often hints to the answer, so be aware when you hear a part of a question in the recording

* When writing down answers, make sure you spell them correctly

* Do as many listening practices as possible to get used to the format

* Manage your time properly, use short pauses to correct yourself or to underline any places that you should return to later

* Make sure you have answered all the questions, and that you are writing your answer for the correct question
