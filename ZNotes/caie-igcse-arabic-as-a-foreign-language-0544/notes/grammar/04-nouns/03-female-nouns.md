## Female Terms

Words that usually end with a ‘ة’ are considered female terms. There are other words without ‘ة’ that are female too.

An example of a female term is the word table, ‘طاولة’ (sometimes also translated as ’مائدة’ or ‘منضدة’, sometimes the male term ‘مكتب’ is used \[although it means desk\]). It ends with a ‘ة’, which is a strong sign that it is a female term.

* Example: هذه طاولة، وهي خفيفة لأنها مصنوعة من البلاستيك
* Translation: This is a table, and it is light because it’s made from plastic.
* Word-to-word: This is a table, and **she** is light because **she** is made from plastic.
* Example: تذهب مريم الى بيت حنان كل يوم، لكن الشمس ساطعة اليوم والجو حار جدا، فلم تزورها اليوم. وهي مشغولة قليلا
* Translation: Maryam goes to Hanan’s house every day, but the sun is bright today, and the weather is very hot, so she didn't visit her today. She is a bit busy.


* Word-to-word: Maryam goes to Hanan’s house every day, but the sun is bright today, and the weather is very hot, so she didn't visit her today. She is a bit busy.

The words ‘مريم’, ‘حنان’ and ‘الشمس’ are all female despite not having a ‘ة’ at the end. These words are usually people’s names but can be other nouns (places, objects, and items).
