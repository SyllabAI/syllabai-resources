## Male Nouns

Words are usually classified as male or female (and sometimes have nothing to do with the object itself.

For example, the word book, ‘كتاب’ is considered male. So, if you want to talk about the book, you’ll say the following:

* هذا كتاب. أحمله معي دائما، لكنه ثقيل. هو مفيد جدا
* Translation: This is a book. I carry it with me all the time, but it is heavy. It’s very useful.
* Word-by-word: This is a book. I carry **him** with me all the time, but **he** is heavy. **He** is very useful.
* Example: محمد يذهب مع علي الى المقهى بعد المدرسة، وهو مقهى جميل جدا، وأود زيارته
* Translation: Muhammad goes with Ali to the cafe after school. and it is a very beautiful cafe. and I would like to visit it
* Word-to-word: Muhammad goes with Ali to the cafe after school, and it is a very beautiful cafe, and I would like to visit him
* In the above examples, you can see the book and cafe being given male characteristics, as there aren’t any ways to describe a gender-neutral item with gender-neutral characteristics. Muhammad and Ali are male names, so using male characteristics is quite obvious.
* Other male terms include: احمد، دفتر، كرسي، علي، حاسوب، قلم
