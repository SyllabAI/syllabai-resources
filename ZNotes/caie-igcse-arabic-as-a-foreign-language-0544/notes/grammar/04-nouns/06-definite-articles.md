## Definite Articles

In English, we use ‘the’ to express a definite object.

In Arabic, we use ‘الـ’ to discuss a specific object. This is called ‘ال التعريف’. When omitted, you are talking about an item in general

**Example:**

| Definite \ معرف | Indefinite \ نكرة |
|----|----|
| الباب | باب |
| السيارة | سيارة |
| الدراجة | دراجة |

 - هذا الباب ضخم
 - This door is large 
 
 - هذا باب كبير
 - This is a big door
