## Possessive Pronouns
 - To indicate something belongs to someone
 - A suffix is added after the noun


| English                       | Arabic Suffix | Example - Translation                                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mine                          | ـي            | هذه أغراضي - These are my things                              |
| Yours (Male Singular)         | ـكَ           | أليست هذه كتبكَ - Arent they your books?                          |
| Yours (Female Singular)       | ـكِ           | فستانكِ جميل - Your dress is pretty                           |
| His \\ Its (Male Singular)    | ـهُ           | درجة حرارة جسده منخفضة - His \\ Its body temperature is low       |
| Hers \\ Its (Female Singular) | ـها           | شعرها طويل - Her \\ Its hair is long                          |
| Ours                          | ـنا           | إنها معلمتنا - It's our teacher!                              |
| Yours (Male & Female Dual)    | ـكما          | $^1$هذا لكما - This is for you (two)                          |
| Theirs (Male & Female Dual)   | ـهما          | $^1$دفاترهما في الفصل - Their notebooks are in class          |
| Yours (Male Plural)           | ـكم           | كلوا فطوركم قبل الامتحان - Eat your breakfast before the exam |
| Yours (Female Plural)         | ـكنَّ \\ ـكم  | طاولاتكنَّ مرتبة - Your desks are tidy                        |
| Theirs (Male Plural)          | ـهم           | حقائبهم ثقيلة - Their bags are heavy                          |
| Theirs (Female Plural)        | ـهنَّ \\ ـهم  | تلك الجوائز لهنَّ - Those prizes are theirs                   |

$^1$ You can use this regardless of what the gender is (Male+Male, Female+Female, or Male+Female)
