## Relative Pronouns - الأسماء الموصولة
 - relative pronouns link two parts of a sentence (the independent and dependent clauses) together
 - An example is هو الذي فاز بالبطولة - He is the one who won the tournament
 - The word 'الذي' (who) is a relative pronoun

| **Usage (English)**                          | **Arabic**           | **Example - Translation**                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who is / That is - Male (Singular)           | الذي                 | الكتاب الذي أخَذتُهُ كان مملا - The book that I took was boring                                                        |
| Who is / That is - Female (Singular)         | التي                 | هذه المدرسة التي تخَرَّجتِ منها - This is the school that you (female) graduated from                                  |
| Who are / That are - Male (Dual)             | اللَذَان \\ اللَذَين | هما اللذان أخذاها - They (two males or male + female) are the ones who took it                                         |
| Who are / That are - Female (Dual)           | اللَتَان \\ اللَتَين | لم تسجل النادلتان اللتان أخذتا طلبنا الأرز المقلي - The two waitresses who took our order didn't record the fried rice |
| Who are / Those / That are - Male (Plural)   | $^1$الذِين            | همُ الذين فازوا في البطولة - They are the ones who won the tournament                                                  |
| Who are / Those / That are - Female (Plural) | $^1$اللاتي \\ اللواتي | اللاتي أخذن قلما ومسطرة - Those who took a pen and a ruler                                                             |

$^1$ These arent used for non-living objects, and you would use 'الذي' or 'التي' for plural items (dual works for both living and non-living)
