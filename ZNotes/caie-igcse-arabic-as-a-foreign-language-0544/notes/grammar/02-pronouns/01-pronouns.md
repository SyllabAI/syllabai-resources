# Pronouns

Different pronouns are used depending on the subject/doer, and they have their rules in conjugation (as shown below).

**The pronouns are**

* I  → أنا
* You (Male, Singular) → أنتَ
* You (Female, Singular) → أنتِ
* He → هو
* She → هي
* It (Terms may be male or female) → هو \ هي
* You (Male & Female, Dual) → أنتما
* They (Male & Female, Dual) → هما
* You (Male, Plural) → أنتم
* You (Female, Plural) → أنتن
* They (Male, Plural) → هم
* They (Female, Plural) → هن
* Us \ We → نحن
