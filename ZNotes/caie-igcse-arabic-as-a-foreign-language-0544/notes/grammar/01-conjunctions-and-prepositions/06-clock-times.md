In general, we use ‘و’ (meaning ‘and’) if it is less than XX:30 and ‘إلا’ (meaning ‘except’) if it’s after XX:30 (however you can choose not to use them or to use ‘و’ all the time).

24-hour and 12-hour time formats are the same as in English (you speak it as if it were a 12-hour format).
