## Numbers and Time

### Numbers

The numbers from 0 - 10 should be memorized, as they are the basis for whatever comes after, 11 - 20 all follow the same general form while numbers above 20 all follow a different general form.

Negative numbers (below zero) have the word ‘سالب’ (or ‘ناقص’) before them.

¹ put the number before ‘عشر’. For example: ‘أربعة عشر’ is fourteen

² For multiples of 10 (excluding 10 itself), delete the ‘ة’ at the end then add ‘ون’. There are two exceptions to this; for 20, it is ‘عشرون’ (not ‘اثنانون’) and for 80, you would delete the ‘ية’ then add ‘ون’ to get ‘ثمانون’

³ For multiples of 100 (excluding 100 itself), delete the ‘ة’ then add the word ‘مئة’ afterwards. For 800, its ‘ثمان مئة’, which follows the same rule as above

⁴  After 1000, everything else follows the same way as english:

10,000 -> Ten Thousand -> عشرة ألاف

200,000 -> Two Hundred Thousand ->مئتي ألف (This one deletes the ‘ن’ at the end)

1,000,000 -> Million -> مليون

215, 375, 104 -> مئتين وخمسة عشر مليون، ثلاث مئة وخمسة وسبعون ألف، مئة وأربعة
