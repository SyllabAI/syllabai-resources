## General Rule with Plural Adjectives

For singular adjectives, you simply use the root term.. and you generally add a ‘ة’ at the end of the root word to make it female.

The case for plurals is quite different depending on the subject:

For humans:

* You usually end with ‘ون’, ‘ين’, ‘ان’, ‘ات’, but there are cases in which you end with ‘اء’

  Example: هن جميلات، أنتم رائعون، هم فائزون، أنتما جيدتان، نحن فقراء، هم اذكياء

For non-humans:

* Almost always ends with a ‘ة’ after the root word (same as singular female) for both male and female, with some exceptions (like duals)

  Example: سيارات سريعة، أقلام كثيرة، حقيبتان ثقيلتان، قطط رشيقة، كلبان تعبان
