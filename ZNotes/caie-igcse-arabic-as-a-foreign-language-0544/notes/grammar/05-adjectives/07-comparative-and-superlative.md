## Comparative and Superlative
If you dont know what comparative and superlative adjectives are (or your english is as bad as mine), then the following are examples of such:
- Adjective: Fast - سريع
- Comparative: Faster - أسرع
- Superlative: Fastest - الأسرع

### The General Rule
If your adjective is on the same weight (sounds same) as 'فعيل' (like 'سريع', 'بطيئ', 'جميل') then the comparative is on the weight of 'أفعل' while the superlative is 'الأفعل'.
There are exceptions to this rule, for adjectives that dont sound similar to 'فعيل'.

Examples of the rules:


| English | Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
| -------- | -------- | -------- | -------- |
| Amazing\Great | **رائع \ جيد** | **أروَع \ أفضّل** | **الأروَع \ الأفضَل**|
| Sensitive | **حسَّاس** | **أكثَر تحسّسا** | **الأكثر تحسسا** |
| Fast | **سريع** | **أسرع** | **الأسرع** |

Examples of sentences:
- كان هذا رائعا... لا، بل الأروع - That was great... no, the greatest
- البوتاسيوم أكثر تفاعلا من الصوديوم - Potassium is more reactive than Sodium
