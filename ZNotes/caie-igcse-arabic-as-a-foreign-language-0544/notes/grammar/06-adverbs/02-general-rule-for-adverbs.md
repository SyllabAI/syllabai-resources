## General Rule for Adverbs
In English, many adverbs end with 'ly', such as 'happily', 'awkwardly', and 'strangely'. Of course, some adjectives don't follow this rule, like a few ones in the examples below ('always', 'sometimes', etc.)

In Arabic, there isn't a general rule which you can remember. However, there are quite a few adverbs that end with 'ا', such as 'قليلا' or 'سريعا' or others. Furthermore, some adverbs have a preposition before them, like 'بـ'

### The Types of Adverbs
There are two main types of adverbs, in Arabic...
1. ظرف زمان - Adverbs of time
 - These adverbs describe the time, whether it is absolute or relative
 - Examples of these are: 
   - غدا، مساءً، سنويا - tomorrow, at night \ nightly, yearly \ annually
   - دائما، عادةًَ، في بعض الاحيان - Always, usually, sometimes
2. ظرف مكان - Adverbs of place
 - Used to describe a place where an action is happening
 - Examples include:
   - هنا، هناك - Here, there
   - يمين، يسار - (To the) right, (To the) left
