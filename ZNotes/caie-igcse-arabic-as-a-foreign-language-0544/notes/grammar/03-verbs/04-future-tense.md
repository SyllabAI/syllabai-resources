## Future Tense

If you want to talk about the future, you dont need to use a special form of tense.

Add ‘سـ’, ‘قد’, ‘لن’ or other terms that indicate the future before the present tense of the verb. 'لن' negates something ('I will not').

**Example:**

| Verb (In Present Tense) | Future |
|----|----|
| استطيع - (I can) | سأستطيع - (I will be able to) |
| نجري - (We run) | قد نجري - (We might run) |
| تمسح - (You wipe) | من الممكن ان تمسح - (You may wipe) |
| يأكلان - (They eat) | لن يأكلا - (They will not eat)\* |

* If you have ‘لن’, the ‘ن’ at the end is deleted. There are other similar exceptions, but they are deeper within the Arabic grammar and are uncovered here.
