# Forces and Their Effects
## Power

- Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred over time. It essentially describes how quickly energy is being used or produced.
- The formula for power is $\text{work done} * \text{time}$.
- The formula for work done is $\text{force} * \text{distance}$.
- In a system the energy can be altered with a change in mass or when heat is transferred.
