## Fields

### Gravitational

- Gravitational fields exert a force on objects with mass.
- Objects within a gravitational field experience a pull towards the source of gravity (e.g., planets pulling objects towards their center).
- The strength of a gravitational field decreases with distance from the source of gravity.


### Magnetic 

- **Friction:** Opposes the motion of an object sliding over a surface.
- **Normal Force:** Perpendicular to the surface, supports the weight of an object.
- **Tension:** A pulling force exerted by a string, rope, or cable when it is taut.
- **Compression:** A pushing force that occurs when an object is squished.

### Electrostatic

- Electrostatic fields affect charged particles, with like charges repelling and opposite charges attracting.
- The strength of the field increases with the charge and decreases with distance from the charge.
- Objects can become electrically charged through friction, conduction, or induction.
