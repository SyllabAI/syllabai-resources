# **Radioactivity**

## **Atomic Model**

### **Atomic Structure:**

* Atoms contains a nucleus, which contains neutrons and protons.
* Most of the mass of an atom is in the nucleus.
* Orbiting the nucleus are shells of electrons.
* A proton has an atomic mass of 1 and a charge of +1
* A neutron has an atomic mass of 1 and a charge of 0
* Lastly an electron has an atomic mass of $\frac{1}{1837}$ or negligible and a charge of -1
* Elements have an equal number of protons and electrons therefore they are neutral.
* On the periodic table elements are represented like this $^{6}_{12}\text{C}$ (Carbon), the number on the top is the atomic number(the number of protons) and the number on the bottom is the number of protons + neutrons,

### **Isotopes:**

* Isotopes are elements that have the same number of protons, but a different number of neutrons and atomic mass.

### **Ions:**

* An ion is an atom or molecule that has gained or lost one or more electrons, resulting in a net electrical charge. 
* Ionization is the process by which an atom or molecule acquires a charge by gaining or losing electrons.




### **Rutherford’s gold foil experiment:**

* Rutherford’s gold foil experiment proves that most of the mass in an atom is in the nucleus.
* Rutherford fired positively charged alpha particles at a thin sheet of gold foil.
* Most alpha particles passed straight through the foil without being deflected.
* A small number of alpha particles were deflected at large angles.
* Some alpha particles even bounced back towards the source.
* This showed that atoms have a small, dense, positively charged nucleus in the center.
* The experiment revealed that most of the atom is empty space.
* It led to the development of the nuclear model of the atom, replacing the previous plum pudding model.
