## Conversion of Units

* Units can be converted by multiplying or dividing by powers of ten or known time conversions.

  **Time conversions**:

* 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3600 seconds

* Example: 2 hours = 2 × 3600 = 7200 seconds. 

  **Length conversions**:

* 1 kilometre (km) = 1000 metres (m)

* 1 metre (m) = 0.001 km

* Example: 2500 m = 2500 ÷ 1000 = 2.5 km.
