## **Significant Figures**

* Significant figures are the digits in a number that represent its precision.

* For example, 0.004560 has 4 significant figures (4560).

* They show how accurate a measurement is.

## **Standard form**

* Standard form (also called scientific notation) is a way of writing very large or very small numbers using powers of ten.

* For example, 3,600,000 can be written as 3.6 × 10^6.

* This makes numbers easier to read and work with in calculations.
