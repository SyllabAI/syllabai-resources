## Determining the speeds of objects

### Light gates

* A light gate consists of a beam of light and a detector. When an object passes through, it interrupts the beam.

* If the object has a **known length**, the light gate records the time it takes to pass through, and speed is calculated as:

$$
v=\frac{\text{length of object​}}{\text{time taken}}
$$

* If two light gates are placed a known distance apart, the system can measure the time between interruptions to find average speed.
