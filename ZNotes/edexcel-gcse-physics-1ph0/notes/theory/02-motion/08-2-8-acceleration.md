## **Acceleration**

### **What is acceleration?**

* Acceleration is the change of speed in a given time period, or the rate of change of speed
* $\text{Acceleration (metre per second squared, m/s}^2) = \frac{\text{change in velocity (metre per second, m/s)}}{\text{time taken (second, s) (should be memorised)}}$
* $\boxed{a = \frac{(v-u)}{t}}$
* In freefall the acceleration is 10$m$/$s^2$ due to the force of gravity acting upon the object if we ignore air resistance. However if we include air resistance this will impact the acceleration causing it to be less than 10$m$/$s^2$.
