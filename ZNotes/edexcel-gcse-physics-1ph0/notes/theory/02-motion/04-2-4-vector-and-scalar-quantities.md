## Vector and scalar quantities


|Scalar Quantity|Vector Quantity|Examples|
|:---|:---|:---|
|Speed|Velocity|A car travels at a speed of 30 m/s and has a velocity of 30 m/s north|
|Distance|Displacement|A person walks 1 km in total, but his displacement is 200 m east.|
|Mass|Weight|A box’ mass is 20 g but its weight is 200 N down to the Earth|
|Energy|Force|Pushing a box with 10 N east using 50 J of energy|
|Acceleration (magnitude only)|Acceleration (with direction)|A car increases its speed by 5 m/s², while its acceleration is 5 m/s² east.|
|Momentum (magnitude only)|Momentum (with direction)|A ball has momentum of 10 kg·m/s, while its momentum is kg·m/s forward. |
