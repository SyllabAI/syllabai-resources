## Typical speeds encountered in everyday experience

**Natural Phenomena**

* Sound in air: \~ 340 m/s

* Wind: \~ 5–20 m/s (gentle breeze to strong wind)

**Human Movement**

* Walking: \~ 1.5 m/s (≈ 5 km/h)

* Running: \~ 3–5 m/s (≈ 10–18 km/h; sprinting can be faster)

* Cycling: \~ 5–10 m/s (≈ 18–36 km/h; varies with terrain/fitness)

**Transport Systems**

* Car (city): \~ 15 m/s (≈ 50 km/h)

* Car (highway): \~ 30 m/s (≈ 100 km/h)

* Train: \~ 55 m/s (≈ 200 km/h; high-speed trains faster)

* Commercial aircraft (cruise): \~ 250 m/s (≈ 900 km/h)

* Rocket leaving Earth: > 7,000 m/s (orbital speed)
