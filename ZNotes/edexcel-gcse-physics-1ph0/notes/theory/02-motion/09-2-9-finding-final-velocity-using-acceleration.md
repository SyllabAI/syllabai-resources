## Equation combining initial and final velocities, acceleration, and distance travelled

$(\text{final velocity})^2 ((\text{metre/second})^2, (m/s)^2)  -  (\text{initial velocity})^2  ((\text{metre/second})^2, (m/s)^2)  =  2 \times \text{acceleration} ; (\text{metre per second squared}, m/s^2) \times \text{distance}  (\text{metre}, m)$

$\boxed{v^2 - u^2 = 2\times a \times x} (\text{given in booklet})$
