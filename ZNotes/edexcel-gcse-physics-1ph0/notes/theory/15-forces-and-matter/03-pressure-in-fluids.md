## Pressure in Fluids

### Atmospheric Pressure and Altitude:

- Atmospheric pressure decreases with height due to the reduced weight of the air above as altitude increases.
Fluid Pressure:

- Pressure in fluids results from both fluid weight and atmospheric pressure, with more depth resulting in greater pressure.
### Force, Pressure, and Area:

- Pressure is defined as force per unit area ($P = \frac{F}{A}$), with pressure increasing as the force on a surface increases or as the area decreases.
### Depth and Density in Fluids:

- In a fluid, pressure increases with depth and density due to the increased weight of fluid above.
