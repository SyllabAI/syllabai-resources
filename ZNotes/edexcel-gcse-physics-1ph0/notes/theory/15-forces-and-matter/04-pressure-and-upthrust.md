## Pressure and Upthrust

### Density, Depth, and Fluid Pressure:

- Fluid pressure depends on both depth and density. Greater depth and higher density result in more pressure.
### Upthrust:

- Upthrust is the upward force exerted by a fluid on an object, causing objects to float or sink based on their density compared to the fluid.
### Upthrust and Displacement:

- Upthrust equals the weight of the displaced fluid. An object floats if its weight is equal to the upthrust and sinks if its weight is greater.
