# Forces and Matter
## Forces and distortion
### Forces and Distortion
- Objects require more than one force to distort. For example, squeezing or pulling on opposite sides is needed to compress or stretch an object.
### Elastic vs. Inelastic Distortion:

- Elastic distortion: The object returns to its original shape once the forces are removed (like a spring).
- Inelastic distortion: The object does not return to its original shape (like modeling clay).
### Force-Extension Relationship for a Spring:
- For a spring, force is directly proportional to extension until it reaches its elastic limit (Hooke's Law).
### Force-Extension for a Rubber Band:
- A rubber band’s force-extension graph isn’t linear; it stretches unevenly, and the proportionality is less predictable than a spring.
