## Dangers of Static Electricity

- Earthing is the process of transferring excess charge from a conductor to the ground, preventing the buildup of static electricity.
- By connecting a charged object to the Earth with a conductor, excess charge flows safely into the ground, neutralizing the object.
- Lightning occurs when a large charge builds up in clouds and is released as a discharge between the cloud and the ground or another cloud. Everyday shocks happen when there is a sudden transfer of electrons between two objects due to differences in static charge.
- Discharging static electricity can cause sparks, which might ignite flammable materials. Earthing prevents these discharges by allowing charge to safely flow into the ground.
