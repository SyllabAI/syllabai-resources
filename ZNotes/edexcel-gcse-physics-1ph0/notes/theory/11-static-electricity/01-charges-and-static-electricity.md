# Static Electricity

## Charges and Static Electricity

- When two insulators are rubbed together, electrons from one material are transferred to the other. The material that gains electrons becomes negatively charged, while the one that loses electrons becomes positively charged.
- Atoms contain protons (positively charged), neutrons (neutral), and electrons (negatively charged).
- Due to differences in electron affinity, some materials tend to gain electrons while others lose them. This results in one object being negatively charged and the other positively charged.
- Like charges repel, and opposite charges attract.
- When a charged object is brought near a neutral object, it causes a redistribution of charges within the neutral object, creating an attraction without direct contact.
