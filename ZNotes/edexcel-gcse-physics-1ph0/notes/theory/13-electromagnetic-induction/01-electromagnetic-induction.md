# Electromagnetic induction


### Producing Electric Current by Induction:
- Move a conductor (like a wire coil) through a magnetic field, or move a magnet near a stationary conductor. This movement changes the magnetic flux, inducing a current.
- Alternators and dynamos generate AC and DC currents, respectively. They use rotating coils in magnetic fields to produce currents by changing the magnetic flux
- Increasing the coil's speed, the magnetic field strength, or the number of coil turns can increase the current. The direction of motion and the magnetic field affect the current direction.
- By Lenz’s Law, the induced current’s magnetic field opposes the change in magnetic flux that created it, preventing sudden changes in current.
