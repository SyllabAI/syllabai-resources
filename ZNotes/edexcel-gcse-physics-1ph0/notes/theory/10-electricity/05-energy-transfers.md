## Energy Transfers

- When a current passes through a resistor, electrical energy is transferred to thermal energy (heat). This is known as Joule heating.

- Using the electron-ion model, the electrons moving through the conductor collide with ions in the lattice. These collisions transfer energy to the ions, increasing their vibrations, and the energy is dissipated as heat.

- Unwanted energy transfers in wires (such as heat loss) can be reduced by using materials with lower resistivity or by minimizing the length of wires. Insulation can also help to reduce energy dissipation.
- Advantages and Disadvantages of the Heating Effect
  - Advantages:
    -  Useful in applications like electric heaters, toasters, and electric stoves.
  - Disadvantages:
    - Wasted energy in electrical circuits, where heat loss reduces the efficiency of the system.
