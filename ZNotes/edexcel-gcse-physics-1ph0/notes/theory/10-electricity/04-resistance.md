## Resistance

- Resistance (R) opposes the flow of electric current in a circuit.
- The higher the resistance, the smaller the current for a given potential difference.
- The relationship between resistance, current, and voltage is given by: 
- 𝑅=𝑉𝐼
- R = Resistance (Ohms, Ω)
- V = Potential difference (Volts, V)
- I = Current (Amperes, A)

- The potential difference across each resistor is the same.
- The total current increases as more resistors are added, but the total resistance decreases.
- In a series circuit:

- The total current is the same through each resistor.
- The potential differences across individual components add up to the total potential difference of the power source.
- The total resistance is the sum of the individual resistances.
