## Power

- Power is defined as the rate of energy transfer or the amount of energy transferred per unit of time.
- The unit of power is the watt (W), where 1 watt equals 1 joule per second (1 W = 1 J/s).
- $\text{Power} = \frac{\text{Energy}}{\text{Time}}$
- $\text{Power} = \text{Current} * \text{Voltage}$
- $\text{Power} = \text{Current}^{2} * \text{Resistance}$
