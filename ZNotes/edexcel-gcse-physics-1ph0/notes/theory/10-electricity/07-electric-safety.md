## Electric Safety

- The live wire carries the voltage from the power supply to the appliance.
- The neutral wire completes the circuit by returning the current to the power supply.
- Circuit breakers are devices that automatically disconnect the circuit if too much current flows, preventing overheating and fires. They are more reliable and faster than fuses.
- The earth wire provides a path for the current to safely return to the ground if there is a fault in the system, preventing electric shocks.
- The fuse protects the appliance by breaking the circuit if the current becomes too high, which prevents overheating or damage.
- The live wire has a potential difference of around 230 V (in the UK).
- The neutral wire is at 0 V.
- The earth wire also has a potential difference of 0 V and is connected to the ground for safety.
- If the live wire accidentally connects to the earth, it can cause a large current to flow, potentially leading to electric shocks or fires due to the high energy transferred to the ground.
