## Current and Potential Difference

- Voltage is measured using a voltmeter.
- The voltmeter is connected in parallel across the component you are measuring.
- Ensure the circuit is complete and the power source is active when taking a measurement.

- Potential difference (often called voltage) is the difference in electric potential between two points in a circuit.
- It is the energy per unit charge provided by the power source or lost as charge passes through a component.
- Measured in volts (V).
- The formula is $\text{Potential Difference} = \frac{\text{Energy Transferred(J)}}{\text{Charge(C)}}$

- Current is measured using an ammeter.
- The ammeter must be connected in series with the component you are measuring the current through.
- Current is measured in amperes (A).
