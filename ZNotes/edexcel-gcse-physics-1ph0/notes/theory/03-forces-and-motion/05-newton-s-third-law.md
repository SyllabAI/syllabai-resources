## **Newton’s Third Law:**

### **Definition:**

* Newton’s third law states that suppose if Object A exerts a force on Object B, Object B exerts an equal and opposite force on Object A.
* For example if you were to punch the wall with a force of 20N, your fist would exert a force of 20 N on the wall and the wall would exert a force of 20 N on your fist.

### **Action-Reaction Pairs:**

* Action-reaction pairs are the forces two objects exert on each other as described by Newton's Third Law.
* The difference between an action-reaction pair and balanced forces is that in action-reaction pairs there is two forces and two objects however with balanced forces that there is more than one force reacts on one object.
