## **Newton’s Second Law**

* The second law states that the acceleration of the object is directly proportional to the  force acting on it, but inversely proportional to the mass of the object.
* For example, if a ball with a mass of 10kg and another ball with a mass of 5kg both have 5N of force applied on them, the ball with the lower mass would accelerate faster hence the formula is **Force(N) = Mass(kg) x Acceleration($m/s^{2}$).**
* Inertial mass is a measure of an object's resistance to changes in its state of motion.The larger an object's inertial mass, the more force it is required to change its velocity by a given amount. This is why it's much harder to push a heavy car than to push a bicycle.
