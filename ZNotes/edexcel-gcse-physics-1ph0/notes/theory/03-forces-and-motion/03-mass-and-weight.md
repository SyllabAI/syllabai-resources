## **Mass and Weight**

### **Difference between mass and weight:**

* The mass of an object is the amount of matter in an object, however the weight is the force exerted on an object due to gravity. Mass is measured in kilograms(kg) using a balance and weight is measured in newton(N) using a force meter.

### **Formula for weight:**

* The formula for weight is mass x gravity, and in calculations involving Earth we use g as 10. However if the object had a different force of gravity being exerted on it you would change the gravity.
* For example, if you had a ball with a mass of 10kg the weight would be 100N (10 x 10) assuming the ball is on Earth.
