## **Momentum:**

### **Definition:**

* Momentum measures how difficult it is to stop something that is moving.

* Therefore the equation is $\text{momentum} = \text{mass} * \text{velocity}$.

* For example if an object was traveling at a speed of 20 m/s with a mass of 50kg the momentum of the car would be 1000 kg m/s

### **Conservation of momentum:**

* The law of conservation of momentum states that the momentum before a collision and the momentum after a collision are equal providing that no external force is applied.

### **Change in momentum:**

* **Change in momentum = mass(final velocity - initial velocity)**
* p = m(v-u)
* Rate of change of momentum:
* $Force = \frac{mass(final velocity - initial velocity)}{time}$

### **Calculating velocities of objects after collisions:**

* For example two cars collide. Car A has a mass of 1000 kg and is moving at 20 m/s. Car B has a mass of 1500 kg and is stationary. After the collision the two cars stick together.

* Change in momentum of Car A:

  p = mv - mu

  p = 1000*2*0 -* 1000***0 = 20,000 kg m/s

* Change in momentum of Car B

  p = 1500*0=0 kgm/s

* Total momentum before = 20000 kg m/s = Total momentum after

* Their combined mass is 1000 kg +1500 kg = 2500 kg

* To calculate the combined velocity 20000 kg⋅m/s = 2500kg × v

* Which means v = $\frac{20000}{2500}$ which gives you 8 m/s
