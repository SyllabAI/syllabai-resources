## **Refraction**

### **What is Refraction:**

* Refraction is the bending of a wave as it passes from one medium to another where its speed changes. This change in speed causes the wave to change direction.
* When a wave moves from one material to another, it changes direction depending on the difference in wave speed between the two materials
* If the new material is denser the previous one, it moves towards the normal and slows down. However if the new material is less dense then the previous one it moves away from the normal and speeds up.




### **Angles:**

* Angle of refraction - the angle between the refracted ray and the normal at the point where the ray enters the new medium.
* Angle of incidence - the angle between the incoming ray  and the normal at the point of contact with the surface.
* Reflection occurs when the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.

### Snells Law

- Snell's Law describes how light (or any wave) bends, or refracts, when it passes from one medium to another. 
- This bending happens because light travels at different speeds in different materials
- It's mathematically expressed by $n_1 \sin(\theta_1) = n_2 \sin(\theta_2)]$
- Where $n_1$ and $n_2$ are the refractive indices of the first and second media, respectively.
- $\theta_1$ is the angle of incidence
- $\theta_2$ is the angle of refraction
