# The Universe
## Movement of a wave source

- Doppler Effect - The movement of a wave source alters the observed frequency and wavelength.
  - Moving towards: Frequency increases, wavelength decreases (blue-shift).
  - Moving away: Frequency decreases, wavelength increases (red-shift).

 

- Red Shift is a phenomenon where the light from an object, such as a galaxy, shifts towards the red end of the spectrum. 
  - Distant galaxies show greater red-shift.
  - Closer galaxies show less red-shift.
  - The further away a galaxy is, the faster it is moving away from Earth.
  - Indicates galaxies are moving away.
  - Red-shift supports the idea that the universe is expanding.
  

## Origin Of the Universe

- Big Bang theory:
  - The universe began from a single point and has been expanding ever since.
  - Suggests an expanding universe.
  - Red Shift proves Big Bang Theory
  - So does left over radiation from the Big Bang.
 
- Steady State Theory:
  - The universe has always existed and looks the same everywhere.
  - Matter is continuously created as the universe expands.
