# **Light and the Electromagnetic Spectrum**

## **Color**

### **How are different colors made:**

* White light is a combination of all colors of light (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet). 
* When white light strikes an object, the object may absorb certain colors of light and reflect others.
* The color of an object is determined by the wavelengths of light it reflects. 
* For example, a red apple appears red because it absorbs all other colors of light and reflects only the red wavelength.
* Different materials and pigments have unique properties that cause them to absorb certain wavelengths of light and reflect others.

### **Filters:**

* A filter is a material that only allows certain colors of light to pass through while absorbing the rest, which allows you to only get a certain color of light to pass through.
