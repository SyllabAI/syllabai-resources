# Particle Model
## Particles and density

- Particles in solids are tightly packed in a fixed arrangement, vibrating in place.
- Particles in liquids are close together but can move around each other, allowing liquids to flow.
- Particles in gases are far apart and move freely, filling the container they are in.
- Solids have fixed shapes and volumes because particles are locked in place.
- Liquids take the shape of their container and have a fixed volume because particles can move around each other.
- Gases have no fixed shape or volume, as particles move freely and spread out to fill their container.
- $\text{Density} = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}$
