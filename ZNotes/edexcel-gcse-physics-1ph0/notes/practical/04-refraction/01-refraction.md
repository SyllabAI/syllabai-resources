# Refraction

Apparatus:
- Ray box, 
- rectangular glass block, 
- plain paper, 
- pencil.

Procedure:

- Place the plain paper beneath the rectangular glass block.
- Shine a light ray from the ray box towards the glass block.
- Mark two points along the incident ray, two points along the refracted ray inside the glass, and two points along the emergent ray as it exits.
- Remove the glass block, connect the points to draw the path of the light, and measure the angles of incidence and refraction.
- Calculate the refractive index using Snell’s law.
- Repeat the experiment with different angles of incidence to demonstrate Snell's law.
