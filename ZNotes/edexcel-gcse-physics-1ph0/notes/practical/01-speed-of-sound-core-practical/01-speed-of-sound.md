# Speed of Sound

Apparatus:
- Two observers, 
- stopwatch or timer, 
- something that can make a loud noise.

Method:
- Two observers are set apart at a known distance
- First observer creates the noise the other observer has the stopwatch
- Observer creates the noise and the second observer starts the stopwatch as soon as he sees the motion and stops it when he can hear the noise.
- Use $\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$ to calculate speed of sound
- To improve accuracy increase distance to decrease the effect of reaction time on the final result
