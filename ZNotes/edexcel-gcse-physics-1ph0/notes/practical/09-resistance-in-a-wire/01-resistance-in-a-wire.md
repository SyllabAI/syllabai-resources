# Resistance in a wire
Apparatus:
- Ammeter
- Voltmeter
- Power source
- Wires of different length

Method:
- Set up a circuit with a power source, ammeter, voltmeter, and a wire of known length.
- Measure the voltage and current across different lengths of the same type of wire.
- Use $\text{resistance} = \frac{\text{voltage}}{\text{current}}$ to calculate different resistances
