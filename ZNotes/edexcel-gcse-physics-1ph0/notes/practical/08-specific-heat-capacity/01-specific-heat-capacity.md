# Specific Heat Capacity

Apparatus: 
- Solid block, 
- Drill,
- Thermometer, 
- Heater (of known power), 
- Cotton wool.

Method: 
- Drill two holes in the solid block.
- Measure the mass of the block.
- Insert the heater into one hole and the thermometer into the other.
- Wrap the block with cotton wool to insulate it.
- Record the initial temperature of the block, then turn on the heater for a specified time
- Calculate the heat energy supplied by the heater using Q = pt
- Record the final temperature of the block.
