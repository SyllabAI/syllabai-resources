# Density of regular and irregular objects

Apparatus: 
- Eureka can
- Weighing Scale
- Object
- Measuring cylinder

Method:

- Get the mass of the object using the weighing scale
- Fill the eureka can with water until full - wait till water stops leaking out
- Put measuring cylinder under eureka can
- Drop object lightly in the can
- Measure water displaced which is the volume
- Use $\text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$
