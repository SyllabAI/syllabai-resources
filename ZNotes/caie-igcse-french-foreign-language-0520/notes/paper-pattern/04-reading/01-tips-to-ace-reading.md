# Reading

## Tips to Ace Reading

### **1. Build Your Vocabulary**

* **Common Themes**: Focus on topics like **travel, education, health, environment, technology, and daily life**.

* **Synonyms & Antonyms**: The exam often paraphrases questions, so knowing different ways to say the same thing helps.

* **False Friends**: Be aware of words that look similar in English but mean something different (e.g., *actuellement* ≠ *actually*, it means *currently*).

* **Flashcards & Apps**: Use **Quizlet, Anki, or Duolingo** to memorize key words.

### **2. Master Reading Techniques**

* **Skim & Scan**: First, skim the passage to get the main idea. Then, scan for key words related to the question.

* **Identify Context Clues**: If you don’t know a word, look at the words around it to guess the meaning.

* **Recognize Cognates**: Many French words resemble English ones (*hôpital* = hospital, *différent* = different).

### **3. Understand Question Types**

* **MCQs (Multiple Choice Questions)**: Read all options carefully—sometimes, they include *distractors* to trick you.

* **True/False/Not Given**: Be careful with *Not Given*—if the information isn't explicitly in the text, it's **Not Given**, not False.

* **Gap-Filling**: Ensure the word you choose fits grammatically and contextually.

### **4. Use Grammar to Your Advantage**

* **Tenses**: Pay attention to past, present, and future tenses to understand the timeline of events.

* **Pronouns & References**: Know what words like *il, elle, cela, qui, que* refer to in the text.

* **Negations**: Words like *ne…pas*, *ne…plus*, and *ne…jamais* can change the meaning significantly.

### **5. Manage Your Time Well**

* **Don't Get Stuck**: If a question is too hard, move on and come back later.

* **Divide Your Time**: Spend **5-7 minutes per passage** and leave 5 minutes for review.

* **Answer Everything**: Even if unsure, make an educated guess—there’s no negative marking!

### **6. Practice with Past Papers**

* Download IGCSE past papers and time yourself.

* Review mistakes to identify weak areas.

* Try **listening** to French audio (news, podcasts) to get used to reading at a fast pace.

### **7. Stay Calm & Confident**

* **Don’t Panic** if you don’t know a word—use context!

* **Trust Your Instincts**—overthinking often leads to mistakes.

* **Stay Positive**—a confident mindset improves performance.

**Note :** also remember that if you are stuck on a question but have the answer before and after, the unused lines between the answers of the previous and next question is the answer of the question you are stuck on! Filter and find the perfect sentence of the answer! Also remember that usually easier comprehensions will have the same used verb, just find the keyword or verb in the comprehension!
