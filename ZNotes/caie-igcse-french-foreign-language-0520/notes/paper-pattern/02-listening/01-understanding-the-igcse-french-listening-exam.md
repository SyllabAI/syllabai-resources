# Listening

## **Understanding the IGCSE French Listening Exam**

### **Exam Format:**

* The listening exam consists of **multiple sections** with increasing difficulty.

* You will hear **different types of recordings**, such as:

  * **Conversations** (between friends, family, or in a shop)

  * **Announcements** (at stations, airports, schools)

  * **Interviews** (people talking about their experiences)

  * **News reports** (short stories about current events)

* **Answer types** include multiple-choice, gap-fill, matching, and short answers.

### **What Skills are Tested?**

* **Comprehension:** Understanding general and specific information.

* **Recognizing Opinions:** Identifying emotions and viewpoints.

* **Understanding Context:** Knowing where and why a conversation is happening.

* **Inferring Meaning:** Figuring out unfamiliar words using context.
