### **Gender**

* Nouns in French are either **masculine** or **feminine**.

* Most nouns ending in **-e** are **feminine**: *la table, la voiture*.

* Most nouns ending in other letters are **masculine**: *le livre, le stylo*.

* Some exceptions: *le problème (m), la main (f)*.

### **Plural Formation**

* **Regular:** Add *-s* to the singular form: *un chien → des chiens*.

* **Ending in -eau, -au, -eu:** Add *-x*: *un bateau → des bateaux*.

* **Ending in -al:** Change to *-aux*: *un journal → des journaux*.
