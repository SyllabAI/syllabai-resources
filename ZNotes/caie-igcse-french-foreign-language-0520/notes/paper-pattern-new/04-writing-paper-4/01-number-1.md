## Number 1

* Fill in the blank with a word that fits in with the topic (shown in the first column) of that particular blank.

* 5 blanks to fill, **5 marks.**

* Tests knowledge of basic vocabulary.

  * **Example 1:** In the paper 0520/43 Winter 2024, one of the topics is “Adresse” as shown in the first column and the second column’s contents are: “12 ___________ des Lilas, Bordeaux.” As is widely known, addresses are given by road, region, and city. So the ideal answer in the blank would be “route” or “rue” or “avenue.”
  * **Example 2:** In the paper 0520/43 Winter 2024, one of the topics is “Boisson” as shown in the first column where the answer can be anything as long as it is a drink.

* Sometimes, there can be **two** blanks concerning **one** topic.

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/43 Winter 2024, one of the topics is “Nourriture” as shown in the first column where any **two** types of food can be written as answers here in the **two** blanks.

* Recommended to spend a maximum of 3 minutes on this question.
