## **The Entire Paper in General: Information & Tips**

* The time limit for the paper is 1 hour.

* The paper has 45 marks in total.

* Recommended to do the paper in this order: ***1,3,2***

* Do **not** write beyond the lines given in the paper.

* Cross-check answers in case of any spelling mistakes or incorrect use of grammar (for numbers 2 and 3, preferably writing one paragraph and cross-checking it right after; for each paragraph).

* Always annotate (numbers 2 and 3) next to each bullet point the tense of the question and make sure to answer that question in the same tense.

* Answer the bullet point questions in chronological order when writing your answer (numbers 2 and 3).

* Try to use advanced vocabulary and complex structures for better marks (numbers 2 and 3).
