## **The Entire Paper in General: Information & Tips**

* The time limit for this paper is approximately 45 minutes, with approximately 5 minutes of transfer time.

* The paper has 40 marks in total.

* In general, the sections get progressively harder the further the paper proceeds. 

* It is fine to cross and/or write on the question paper as this paper is neither seen nor marked. It is **essential** not to make a mistake however, in the paper that is used for the transfer of answers.

* While instructions are being read in the different groups of numbers, have a brief look at the questions going to be asked and their options to get a general idea of what could be said (describing pictures/synonyms).

* In numbers 1-14, if one of the pictures is not known and/or the word for it is not known in French, use the method of elimination to decide on the answer basing on what has been said.

* Highlight the numbers where there was difficulty in hearing the answer by putting a symbol next to it. While transferring answers from the question paper to the final multiple choice paper, quickly shade the options without the symbol, and; before shading the answer of a number with a symbol on it, briefly (be extremely quick, other answers should not be risked unshaded due to time constraints) think about it and make a decision on which answer to shade.

* When the answer has been stated the first time, write down the **key** word (quickly) that describes one of the options and tick its box. Confirm if what was heard is right when the sentence is repeated. If the answer is not heard the first time, write down the **key** word heard from the second time that describes one of the options and tick its box. However, this time, there is no certainty in the correctness of the answer selected.

* When the last question, number 37 is done and already repeated, don’t waste time listening to the instructions after to transfer your answers. Immediately start transferring your answers after the last part has been repeated to spare a little bit of extra time for thinking and reconsideration.

* Learn the pronunciations of different French words so that the difficulty in hearing and understanding the audio in the exam is minimized.

* Throughout the paper, each group of numbers has its own storyline to create a chronological audio and set of questions.

* When practicing, work rigorously to improve speed of reading through the phrases and options in the paper as; from number 20 onwards, (excluding the time taken for the audio to read out the instructions), during the pauses, students are required to read, understand, and interpret an entire phrase in the space of about 2 seconds to keep up with the speed of the paper, which is one of the key components in this exam.

* When practicing, after the paper is finished, replay parts of the audio you did not understand and parts where you may have gotten a question wrong. If you still do not understand, then read through the transcript and listen again. Also, go through the transcript and write down new words and their translations to widen your vocabulary and reduce the chance of coming across new words during the exam.
