* Nearly every **relevant** sentence in the text has an answer.

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, an example of an irrelevant sentence would be: “Je m’appelle Carole.”

* Type of Activity

* Family Members

  * **Tip:** A commonly examined synonym here is ami - copain (Masculine) (friend) / amie -   copine (Feminine) (friend).

* Incorporate aspects of common sense when answering this question. All parts of the question do **not** necessarily examine one’s knowledge of synonyms.

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, the second sentence in the text says, “Aujourd’hui, je ne vais pas au collège car c’est dimanche.” Looking at part (a), the answer would be B as Carole did not go to college because it is the weekend. Relations like these have to be linked with each other like “Dimanche” which means “Sunday” to “week-end.” This example is **not** about synonyms as “Sunday” and “week-end” are not synonyms.
