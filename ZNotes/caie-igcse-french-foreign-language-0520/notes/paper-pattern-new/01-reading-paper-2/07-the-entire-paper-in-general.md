## The Entire Paper in General: Information & Tips

* The time limit for the paper is 1 hour.

* The paper has 45 marks in total.

* Recommended to do the paper in the following order: ***1,2,3,4,6,5***

* Do **not** write beyond the lines given in the paper.

* Cross-check answers in case of any spelling mistakes, silly mistakes, or incorrect use of grammar.

* If finding the answers to one of the parts in number 3, 4, or 6 proves difficult, use the previous and next part’s answers to figure out the approximate range of where the answer to the current part could be as the answers are all in chronological order of the text.

* The form of the verb is generally not changed in most cases in Passé Composé. This is extremely common in this tense and for the regular verbs in present tense. However, in all other tenses, the form of the verb usually changes. Remember this when answering questions 4 and 6.

* Underline key words relevant to the question after reading it.
