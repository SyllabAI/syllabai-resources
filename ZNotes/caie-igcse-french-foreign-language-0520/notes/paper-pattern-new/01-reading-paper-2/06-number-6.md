## Number 6

* Write short answers in sentence format (necessary to answer in this format most times) to the questions given.

* In this number, the sentence format is required to answer the question more often than in number 4.

* 1 line given to get 1 mark, 9 questions, **11 marks.**

* Tests the knowledge of a variety of vocabulary and synonyms.

* The sentence format of answering should be used in questions starting with “Que / Qu’est-ce que (What),” “Comment (How),” “Pourquoi (Why),” “Pour quelle raison (For what reason).”

* Sentence structuring is **not** required in questions starting with “Où (Where),” “Qui (Who), “Quand (When),” “Combien (How).”

* Two of the parts in this question (one of the parts **frequently** part ***(b)***) have 2 marks and **both** the answers to this part are **almost** **always** found in the *same* sentence in the text. Otherwise, both of the answers follow each other consecutively in order of the text.

* Generally, this text is written in third person. However, a handful of remarks by the character are displayed in first person using **speech marks** **(<<_______>>).** The answers to the parts found in third person can be copy pasted onto the lines. On the other hand, the answers to the parts found in first person have to be changed into third person.

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, part (c) asks: “**Qu’est-ce que** Magali a fait quand elle a réalisé qu’elle avait du talent ?” which translates to: “What did Magali do when she realised she had talent?” The text says, “j’ai commencé à écrire des histoires courtes…” The phrase is written in first person and past tense. However, it must be converted to third person while answering. So the answer must be written as: “Elle a commencé à écrire des histoires courtes.”
  * **Example 2:** In the paper 0520/22 Winter 2024, part (i) asks: “**Qu’est-ce que** Manu a fait pour Thomas ?” which translates to: “What did Manu do for Thomas?” The text says, “Il m’a dédié la chanson…” which is in first person and in past tense. The structure of the answer should be: “Manu lui a dédié la chanson.”

* **Tip:** Majority of answers to the parts in this question are in the **speech marks** **(<<_______>>)** where the character is recounting the past.

* Recommended to spend a maximum of 17 minutes on this question.
