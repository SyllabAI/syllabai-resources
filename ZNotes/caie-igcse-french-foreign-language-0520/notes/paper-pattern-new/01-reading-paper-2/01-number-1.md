# Reading

## Number 1

* Match the picture with the sentence.

* 6 pictures, 5 sentences: **5 marks.**

* Tests knowledge of basic vocabulary.

* Ensure that the picture is interpreted well to avoid confusion.

* Look out for key **nouns** in the sentences to relate to the pictures directly.

* If there is no **relevant** noun in the sentence, look out for a verb that will relate with the picture.

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, all sentences (a) - (e) have nouns that relate to the pictures such as: “télé,” “guitare,” “tablette,” “gâteaux.” However, sentence (b) is an exception. None of the nouns in the sentence “mère” or “week-end” relate to any of the pictures. Hence, the attention should be shifted to verbs. Of the two verbs “adore” and “peindre,” “peindre” which means “to paint” applies to picture A making it the answer.

* If unsure of what a certain picture may mean or what nouns to expect from it, complete all the other answers and choose which picture you think is most suitable for the remaining sentence using process of elimination.

* Recommended to spend a maximum of 2 minutes on this question.
