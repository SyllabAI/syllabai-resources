## Number 4

* Write short answers in sentence format (not necessary to answer in this format sometimes) to the questions given.

* 1 line given to get 1 mark, 11 questions, **12 marks**

* Tests the knowledge of a variety of vocabulary (synonyms rarely examined here).

* The sentence format of answering should be used in questions starting with “Que / Qu’est-ce que (What),” “Comment (How),” “Pourquoi (Why),” “Pour quelle raison (For what reason).”

* Sentence structuring is **not** required in questions starting with “Où (Where),” “Qui (Who), “Quand (When),” “Combien (How).”

* One of the parts in this question (**frequently** part ***(c)***) has 2 marks and **both** the answers to this part are **almost** **always** found in the *same* sentence in the text. Otherwise, both of the answers follow each other consecutively in order of the text.

* Generally, this text is written in first person. This means that; while answering in sentence format, the verb needs to be conjugated to suit third person (il /elle or vous / ils/ elles ).

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, part (c) asks: “**Qu’est-ce que** Vincent a fait dans le parc ?” This translates to “What did Vincent do in the park?“ The text says, “j’ai  mis des affiches dans le parc.” This phrase is in first person and in simple past tense. It has to be converted to third person when answering. So the answer should be stuctured as the following: “Il a mis des affiches dans le parc.” Also, ensure to change “mon,” “ma,” or “mes” from the text to “son,” ”sa,” or “ses” respectively when answering.
  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, part (e) asks: “Pourquoi Vincent veut-il sortir ?” which translates to “**Why** does Vincent want to go out?” The text says, “Maintenant, j’ai envie de sortir pour voir mes amis…” which means “Now, I want to go out to see my friends…” In this case, the words from the text cannot be directly copy pasted as an answer to this part. The “mes” has to be changed to “ses” and should be written as ”Pour voir ses amis.”

* Recommended to spend a maximum of 10 minutes on this question.
