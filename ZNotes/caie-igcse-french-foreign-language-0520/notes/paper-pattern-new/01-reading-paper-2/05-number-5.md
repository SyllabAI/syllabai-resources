## Number 5

* Write down the number that matches the letter’s description best.

* *8 numbers:* Each of them with a long paragraph of information, *5 letters:* Each of them with a short paragraph of information, **5 marks.**

* Tests the knowledge of a specific section of vocabulary and synonyms.

* In this question, synonyms are used extremely frequently to cause confusion and create **major** problems with interpretation and understanding of the paragraphs.

* Every sentence in each of the 8 paragraphs has a detail that is essential in answering the question.

* The best method to answer this question is to read all the shorter paragraphs first, then read the longer paragraphs to find a match.

* When reading both types of paragraphs, underline (with pencil) the **relevant** words/phrases. This can help save time so that irrelevant words/phrases are not read repeatedly when trying to find a match.

* Learn to highlight the **most** important detail out of the 2 or 3 given.

  * **Example 1:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, the most important details when choosing a school is the child’s age and the type of school (a school specializing in a specific thing (établissement spécialisé), a boarding school (internat/pensionnat) or a primary/middle/high school (école primaire/collège/lycée))

  * **Example 2:** In the paper 0520/22 Winter 2024, the most important details when choosing a technology-related job/jobs in general is the work hours (temps partiel/35 heures par semaine(full-time)) and experience/qualification (l’expérience/diplôme (degree in context).

* A common trick used by examiners is; having a few details in the long paragraph relating to one short paragraph, but having an **anomalous** detail which opposes what the short paragraph demands, hence making it the **wrong** answer.

  * **Example 1:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, read paragraphs (e) and 1. The matching details are:

    1\.The school provides a range of team sports (une gamme de sports collectifs).

    2\.The school accepts children from the ages of 11-15 (Nous acceptons les filles de 11 à 15 ans).

    * However, the fact that the school has a good, balanced education system (une bonne éducation équilibrée) **disagrees** with what the short paragraph (e) specifies; (s’intéresse beaucoup aux sciences) which means that their child is very interested in the sciences. They are not looking for a balanced education system, but more of a science-based education system, hence making number 1 the **wrong** answer for (e).

  * **Example 2:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, read paragraphs (c) and 3. Everything matches very well where the school offers a warm environment for shy children (une ambiance chaleureuse pour aider les enfants timides) and children who are bad at making friends (enfant a du mal à se faire des amis); except for the one **anomalous** detail where the children leave school at 11 years old (ils nous quittent à onze ans), while the short paragraph (c) specifies that he wants his child to be at the school until he is 18 years old (jusqu’à ses dix-huit ans) and preferably never change the school he is in (préférerais qu’il ne change jamais d’établissement). This makes 3 the **wrong** answer for (c).

* In addition to that, a long paragraph may have 1 or 2 details matching with one short paragraph, and have an **extra** detail that matches with another. However, this extra detail is ***usually*** **irrelevant** to the short paragraph where 1 or 2 details are already matching. It is merely added to create **confusion.**

  * **Example:** In the paper 0520/23 Winter 2024, read paragraphs (c) and 4. The campus of Claude Monet has a primary school, middle school, and high school in the same place (une école primaire, un collège et un lycée sur le même site) and this matches with what (c) demands (préférerais qu’il ne change jamais d’établissement). This is further emphasised by the sentence that they offer perfect continuity to students (Nous offrons une continuité parfaite aux élèves). At the end of the long paragraph 3, an **extra** detail (Complexe sportif, club de théâtre, orchestre et chorale) is added that does not relate to the short paragraph (c) at all. This creates **confusion** as this detail can be matched with the short paragraph (d) where she is looking for a special establishment specializing in singing and dancing.

* Recommended to spend a maximum of 23 minutes on this question.
