## **4. Use Grammar to Your Advantage**

* **Tenses**: Pay attention to past, present, and future tenses to understand the timeline of events.

* **Pronouns & References**: Know what words like *il, elle, cela, qui, que* refer to in the text.

* **Negations**: Words like *ne…pas*, *ne…plus*, and *ne…jamais* can change the meaning significantly.
