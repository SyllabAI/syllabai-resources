# Negative

## Negatives

* *Ne… pas* is the most common negative. It means ‘not’.
* Most negatives form a sandwich around the main verb.

  **Example:**

  *Vous **ne** mangez **pas** de viande* (You do not eat
  meat).
* **Remember**: *ne* becomes *n’* before a vowel or silent h
