## Immediate Future Tense

* Used to express actions that are going to occur in the near future

**Formation:**

$$\text{Subject} + \text{Aller} + \text{The}\ \text{infinitive}$$

**Example:** Apprendre (to learn)

Vous + (aller in present tense) + (infinitive)

= Vouz allez apprendre (You are going to learn)
