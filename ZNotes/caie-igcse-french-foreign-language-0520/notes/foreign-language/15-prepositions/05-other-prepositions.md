## Other Prepositions

| Preposition | Meaning |
|----|----|
| Contre | Against |
| Malgré | Despite |
| Sans | Without |
| Sauf | Except |
| Selon | According to |
| Sur/À propos de | About |
| Grâce à | Thanks to/Because of |
| À cause de | Due to/Because of |
| En plus/De plus | On top of that |
| Au sein de | Within |
| Parmi | Among |
| Au lieu de | Instead of |
| Pour | In order to |

* **Note:** *Grâce à* has a more positive connotation, whereas *À cause de* has a more negative connotation.
