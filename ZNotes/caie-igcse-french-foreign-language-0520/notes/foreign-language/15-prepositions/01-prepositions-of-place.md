# Prepositions

## Prepositions of Place

| Prepositions | Meaning |
|----|----|
| Sur | On |
| Sous | Under |
| Devant | In front of |
| Derrière | Behind |
| Avec | With |
| Dans | In |
| En | In (Abstract sense) |
| Chez | At the home of |
| Près de | Near |
| Loin (de) | Far (from) |
| À côté (de) | Nearby; Next to |
| Entre | Between |
| Autour de | Around |
| À gauche (de) | Left; To/On the left (of) |
| À droite (de) | Right; To/On the right (of) |
| Au centre/milieu de | In the centre/middle of |

* Some prepositions agree with the gender and number of the noun.
* These translate to ‘at’, ‘in’, or ‘to’ depending on circumstances

| Au | Masculine singular noun |
|----|----|
| À la | Feminine singular nouns |
| À l’ | Singular nouns starting with a vowel or h |
| Aux | Plural nouns |
