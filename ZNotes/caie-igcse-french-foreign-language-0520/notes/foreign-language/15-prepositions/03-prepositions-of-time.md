## Prepositions of Time

| Prepositions | Meaning |
|----|----|
| En | In (months; seasons;) |
| Dans | In |
| Depuis | Since/For |
| Pendant | For/During |
| Pour | For (duration in the future) |
| Il y a | Ago |
| Avant | Before |
| Après | After |

* **Note:** *En,* when used to talk about seasons, turns to *Au* when the word starts with a consonant. This does not apply to months.
