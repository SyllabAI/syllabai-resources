## Continuous Structure

* A structure exists that can express and emphasizes continuity in different tenses, notably the present and imperfect tenses.

**Formation:**

$$\text{Subject}\ + \text{Être}\ \text{en}\ \text{train}\ \text{de} + \text{Infinitive}$$

**Examples:**

Je suis en train de manger une pomme. (I’m in the process of eating an apple. *or* I’m eating an apple.)

Elle était en train de jouer au basket. (She was in the process of playing basketball. *or* She was playing basketball.)
