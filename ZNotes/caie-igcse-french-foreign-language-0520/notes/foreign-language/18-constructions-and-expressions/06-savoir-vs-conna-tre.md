## Savoir vs. Connaître

* Both simply mean *“to know”* but have very distincts uses
* **Savoir** is most of the time followed by an infinitive and a subordinating clause
  * When Savoir is followed by an infinitive, it means “to know how to”
    * Ex. Je sais parler français. (I know how to speak French.
  * When Savoir is followed by a subordination clause, it express a knowledge of an action or a fact
    * Ex. Il sait que tu y es allé hier. (He knows that you went there yesterday.)
* **Connaître** must always be followed a direct object (object, person, place etc.)
  * Ex. Je connais Louis. (I know Louis.)
  * Ex. Il connait bien le français. (He knows French well.)
