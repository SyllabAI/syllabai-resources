## Si Clauses

* Equivalent to if-then sentences in English
* Describes conditions to be met in order for results to occur

| Possibility | Condition clause | Result clause | Examples |
|----|----|----|----|
| Likely | Present | Present | S’il pleut, je ne sors pas. *ou* Je ne sors pas s’il pleut. <br> (If it rains, I don’t go out. *or* I don’t go out if it rains.) |
| Likely | Present | Future | S’il pleut, je ne sortirai pas. *ou* Je ne sortirai pas s’il pleut. <br> (If it’s raining, I won’t go out. *or* I won’t go out if it’s raining.) |
| Likely | Present | Imperative | S’il pleut, ne sortez pas. *ou* Ne sortez pas, s’il pleut. <br> (If it’s raining, then don’t go out. *or* Don’t go out if it’s raining) |
| Unlikely | Imperfect | Conditional | S’il pleuvait, je ne sortirais pas. *ou* Je ne sortirais pas s’il pleuvait. <br>(If it was raining, I wouldn’t go out. *or* I wouldn’t go out if it was raining.) |
