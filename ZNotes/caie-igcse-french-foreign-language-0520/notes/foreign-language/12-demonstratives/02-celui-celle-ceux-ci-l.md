## celui, celle, ceux, –ci, -là

* Celui and celle are used to specify one particular thing from a
  group.

<table> <thead> <tr> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr> <td>Celui</td> <td>Masculine singular</td> </tr> <tr> <td>Celle</td> <td>Feminine singular</td> </tr> <tr> <td>Ceux</td> <td>Plural</td> </tr> </tbody> </table>

* Forms ending in –ci indicate something close to the speaker (here/this). Example: celui-ci → this one
* Forms ending in –là indicate something that’s farther away from the speaker (there/that). Example: celui-là → that one
