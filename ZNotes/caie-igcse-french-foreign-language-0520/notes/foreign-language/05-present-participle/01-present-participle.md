# Present Participle

## Present Participle

* Used to express the -ing form of the verb
* Used to express an action that is happening simultaneously with another action
* Never takes a subject (je, tu, il…)

**Formation:**

$$
En + Verb\ in\ nous\ form\ (\ without - ons) + ( - ant)
$$

**Example:**

manger → mangeons →mange

= En mangeant (while eating)

Je regarde la télé en mangeant du gâteau (I'm watching the TV while eating cake)

**Note:** 

* Do not confuse it with the present tense. Present tense can also be translated like this: Je parle → I am talking.
