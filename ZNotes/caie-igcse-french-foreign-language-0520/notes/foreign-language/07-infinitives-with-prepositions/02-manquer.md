## Manquer à

* It is a regular *-er* verb meaning *to miss*
* Has a special property when used with the preposition *à*
* The subject and the object inverts their position in the sentence
* Formed by placing what you or someone misses as the subject of the sentence followed by the subject as the indirect object

**Examples:**

* Tu me manques. (I miss you.)
  * The translation *You miss me* for this example is incorrect
* Je te manque. (You miss me.)
  * The translation *I miss you* for this example is incorrect
* Paris me manque. (I miss Paris.)
* Vous allez nous manquer. (We are going to miss you.)
* Antoine manque à Dominique. (Dominique misses Antoine.)
