## Y

* *Y* means ‘there’ or ‘it’.
  * It replaces a thing (but never a person), a place and words including and comes after *à* or a preposition followed by a place
* It comes before all parts of the verb.

  **Example**:

  Il **y** est allé l’année dernière. (I went there last year.)

  Je ne m’**y** attendais pas. (I was not expecting that.)
* **Remember:** *S’attendre* has the preposition *à*. The example replaces *à ça*
