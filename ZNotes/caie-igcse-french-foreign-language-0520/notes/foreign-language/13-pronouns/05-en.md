## En

* En means ‘of them’, ‘of it’, ‘about it’ or ‘some’.
* It replaces words of quantity and words including and what comes after *de*.
* It comes before all parts of the verbs except the imperative

  **Example**: Tu veux du fromage ? Oui, j’**en** veux bien. (Do you want some cheese? Yes, I’d like some)

  Tu as combien de sœurs? J’**en** ai trois. (How many sisters do you have? I have 3 (of them))

  J’**en** ai parlé hier. (I spoke about it yesterday)
* **Remember:** *Parler* with *de* is used if we’re talking about something. The example replaces *de ça.*
