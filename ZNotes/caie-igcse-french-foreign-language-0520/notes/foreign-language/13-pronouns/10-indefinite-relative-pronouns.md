## Indefinite Relative Pronouns

* Link relative clauses to main clauses, but do not have an aforementioned specific object 


* **Ce qui/Ce que**
  * Means ‘that’ or ‘what’ — not to be confused the interrogative ‘what’ 
  * *Ce qui* replaces the subject
  * Ce que replaces the direct object
  * **Examples:**
    * *Je ne sais pas ce qui se passe.* (I don’t know what is happening.)
    * *Je fais ce que je veux.* (I do what I want.)
