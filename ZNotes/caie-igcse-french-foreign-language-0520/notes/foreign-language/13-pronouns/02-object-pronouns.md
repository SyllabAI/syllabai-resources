## Object Pronouns

### Direct Object Pronouns

* These replace a noun in a phrase.
* They agree with the noun in gender and number
* They are placed before the verb
* Example: Je le mange. (I am eating it)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|----|----|----|
| **1**<sup>**st**</sup> | Me | Nous |
| **2**<sup>**nd**</sup> | Te | Vous |
| **3**<sup>**rd**</sup> | Le/La | Les |

### Indirect Object Pronouns

* These replace the names of people that come after the preposition à.
* They agree with the noun in gender and number.
* They go before the verb.
* Example: Il écrit à nous. → Il nous écrit. (He writes to us)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|----|----|----|
| **1**<sup>**st**</sup> | Me | Nous |
| **2**<sup>**nd**</sup> | Te | Vous |
| **3**<sup>**rd**</sup> | Lui | Leur |
