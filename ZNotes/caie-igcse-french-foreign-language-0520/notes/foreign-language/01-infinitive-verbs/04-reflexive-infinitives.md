## Reflexive Infinitives

* When a reflexive verb is in its infinitive form, it retains its ending *(er, ir, re)*
* An additional se is placed before the infinitive
* **Note:** *se* becomes *s*’ when the infinitive starts with a vowel and a silent h

Common Reflexive Verbs in their infinitive form include:

| Verb | Meaning |
|----|----|
| Se réveiller | To wake up (To wake oneself up) |
| Se lever | To get out of bed (To get oneself out of bed) |
| Se coucher | To go to bed (To put oneself to bed) |
| S’endormir | To fall asleep |
| Se doucher; Se baigner | To take a shower (To shower oneself); To take a bath (To bath oneself) |
| Se promener | To take a walk (To walk oneself) |
| S’habiller | To dress up (To dress oneself) |
| Se brosser | To brush |
| Se dépêcher | To hurry up (To hurry oneself) |
