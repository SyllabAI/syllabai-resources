## Negative Infinitives

**Formation:**

$$
\text{Negation}\ + \text{Infinitive}
$$

* **Note:** Both negation words must come before the infinitive

**Examples:**

Ne…pas + fumer → Ne pas fumer (Not to smoke)

Je t’ai dit de ne pas fumer. (I told you not to smoke.) — Saying *ne fumer pas* is incorrect and could be interpreted as the imperative *ne fumez pas*.
