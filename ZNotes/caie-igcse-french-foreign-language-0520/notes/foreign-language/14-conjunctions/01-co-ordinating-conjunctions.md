# Conjunctions

## Co-ordinating Conjunctions

* They connect two grammatically equivalent words or phrases (e.g. two
  phrases, two words)
* **Example**: *Jean et Pierre sont intelligents.* (Jean and Pierre
  are intelligent)

|    |    |
|----|----|
| Car | For, because |
| Donc | So |
| Ensuite | Next |
| Et | And |
| Mais | But |
| Ni | Nor |
| Ou | Or |
| Ou bien | Or else |
| Puis | Then |
