## Negating the Imperative

**Formation:**

$$
\text{Ne} + \text{Verb}\ \text{in}\ \text{the}\ \text{imperative}\ + \text{2nd}\ \text{part}\ \text{of}\ \text{the}\ \text{negative}
$$

**Example:**

Ne parle pas! (Don’t talk !)

Ne parlons pas! (Let’s not talk!)

Ne parlez pas! (Don’t talk!)

* When negating the imperative with reflexive verbs, the reflexive pronouns *te*, *nous* and *vous* replace *toi*, *nous* and *vous*
* The reflexive pronouns are placed in between the **Ne** and the verb in the imperative
* Informal singular: add *te*
* First person plural: add *nous*
* Formal singular or Informal plural: add *vous*

**Example:**

Ne **t**'inquiète pas ! (Don’t worry!)

Ne **nous** inquiétons pas ! (Let’s not worry!)

Ne **vous** inquiétez pas ! (Don’t worry!)

* Note: *te* contracts to *t’*  when there is a vowel or a silent h after it.
