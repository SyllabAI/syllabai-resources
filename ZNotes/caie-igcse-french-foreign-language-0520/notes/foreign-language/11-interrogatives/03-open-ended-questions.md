## Open-ended Questions

* Open-ended questions are questions whose answers are not limited to a fixed response.
* They can be formed by adding the question words mentioned:
  * In front of inverted questions
    * Ex. Que fais-tu ? (What are you doing?)
  * In front of questions using *est-ce que*
    * Qu’est-ce que tu fais ? (What are you doing?)
    * **Note:** *Que* becomes *Qu’* when met with a vowel or y.
* They can also be formed by adding the question words after a sentence

**Example:**

Tu vas où? (Where are you going?)

* **Note**: *Que* at the end of the sentence changes to *Quoi*

**Example:**

Tu fais quoi ? (What are you doing?) — *Tu fais que* is incorrect
