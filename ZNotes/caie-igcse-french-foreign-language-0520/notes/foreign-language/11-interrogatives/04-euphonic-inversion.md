## Euphonic Inversion

* Inversions can result in two vowels being next to each other
* The letter *t* is added for euphony both in the written and spoken language
* This applies to only the subject pronouns *il*, *elle* and *on*

Example:

il parle → parle-t-il (parle-il is wrong)

elle a → a-t-il (a-il is wrong)

on arrive → arrive-t-on (arrive-on is wrong)

* **Note:** This does not apply to conjugated verbs that end with consonants
