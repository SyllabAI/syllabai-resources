# Complex Numbers
## Imaginary and complex numbers

- When solving a quadratic equation, 3 possible solutions can occur:  
  - there are 2 different real roots
  - there are 2 equal roots
  - there are no real roots


- there are no roots when the solution contains $\sqrt{-1}$,
this is labelled as $i$ and is called an imaginary number.
Manipulation of imaginary numbers use the same rules as surds

$$ i^2 = (\sqrt{-1})^2 = -1 $$

- example: 
  - $\sqrt{-5} = 5i$
  - $\sqrt{-21} = 21i$

---

- complex numbers are expressions that have an real part and an imaginary part, the conjugate of a complex number ($z*$) has   the sign of the imaginary part flipped
  - complex number = real part + imaginary part
  - $z = a + bi$
  - $z^* = a - bi$
  


- complex numbers can be added together by adding their real and imaginary parts separately, as well as being able to multiply with real numbers by multiplying out brackets
  - $ 2(7+5i) = 14 + 10i $
  - $(5 + 3i)(6 - 8i)$
    - $30 - 40i + 18i -24i^2$
    - $30 - 22i - 24(-1)$
    - $54 - 22i$
