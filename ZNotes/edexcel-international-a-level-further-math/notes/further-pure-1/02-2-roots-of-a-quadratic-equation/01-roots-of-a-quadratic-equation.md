# Quadratic Roots

* In a quadratic equation in the form $ax^2 + bx + c$, a relationship can be formed between its solutions ($\alpha$ and  $\beta$) and the coefficients of the quadratic equation.

  * $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$     (sum of roots)
  * $\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$     (product of roots)
