# Coordinate systems

## parametric equations

* parametric equations are where $x$ and $y$ are represented using an independent variable, typically $t$, called a parameter. Represented in the form of:

$$

x = f(t), y = g(t)

$$

* questions can ask to turn these parametric equations back into their Cartesian form (an equation in the form of $x$ and $y$ only)

### example question

* The equation, $H$, has parametric equations $x = 5t, y = \frac{5}{t}$. Write the Cartesian equation of $H$ in the form $xy = c^2$
  * $t = \frac{x}{5}$, we can now substitute this into y

  * $y = 5/ \frac{x}{5}$

  * $y = \frac{25}{x}$

  * $xy = 25$

  * $xy = 5^2$
