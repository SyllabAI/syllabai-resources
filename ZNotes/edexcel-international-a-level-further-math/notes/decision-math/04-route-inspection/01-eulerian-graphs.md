# Understanding Eulerian Graphs

Eulerian graphs are those that contain a trail that includes every edge of the graph and that can start and finish at the same vertex, for a graph to be Eulerian it must have an even number of even vertices

Semi- Eulerian graphs are those that contain a trail of every vertex but doesn’t have the same start and finish point

An even number of odd vertices will create a Semi-Eulerian graph example 1 pair of 2 even vertices

Non-Eulerian graphs are those that do not contain a trail, opposite of Eulerian Graphs
