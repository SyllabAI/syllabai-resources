# Formatting Problems using linear programming

Terms : 

Decision variables- Represent quantities you can choose or adjust eg- x,y,z

Objective of the function - Either maximizing profit or minimizing cost

Constraints- Problems that must be dealt with in order to match the objective of the problem

Feasible solution- Values for decision variable that satisfy each constraint

Feasible region- Region in a graphical linear programming problem that satisfies all constraints.

Example  : 

A company buys two types of water bottles to sell: Large and Small.

* They must place a minimum order of 150 Large bottles and 100 Small bottles.

* They require at least twice as many Small bottles as Large bottles.

* They need a total of at least 500 bottles.

* Each Large bottle costs $5 and each Small bottle costs $3.
  The company wants to minimize total cost.
  Formulate and solve this as a linear programming problem.

Solution-

Let L- number of larger bottles

Let S- number of small bottles

L,S≥0

Formulating Constraints

Minimum order: L≥150, S≥100

S≥2L

Total quantity : L + S ≥500

Minimizing cost-

C=5L+3S

Final Solution-

c=5L+3S

subject to: - 

L≥150

S≥100

S≥2L

L+S≥500
