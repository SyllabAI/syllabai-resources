# Classical vs Practical travelling salesman problem

To solve the travelling salesman problem we make use of upper bounds and lower bounds, this give us a boundary that tells us a range of where the optimal solution is located. The closer the upper bound and lower bound is the more narrow the interval is, bringing us closer to the optimal result.

Classical travelling salesman problem requires each vertex to be visited ‘exactly once’.

Practical travelling salesman problem requires each vertex to be visited ‘at least once’.
