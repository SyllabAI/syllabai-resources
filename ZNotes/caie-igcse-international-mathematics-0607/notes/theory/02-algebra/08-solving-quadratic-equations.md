## Solving Quadratic Equations

**Completing square method**

* Make the coefficient of $x^2$ equal to 1

  $x^2 +6x +5$

* Move the constant term to the other side

  $x^2 + 6x = -5$

* Take **half of the coefficient of $x$**, square it, and add to both sides

  $x^2 + 6x + 9 =4$

* Write the left side as a square

  $(x+3)^2 = 4$

* Solve for $x$

  $x + 3 = \pm 2$

  $x = -1$ $x=-5$

**Quadratic Formula**

$$

\boxed{\dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}

$$

* Write the equation in the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$

* Identify $a$, $b$, and $c$

* Substitute into the formula

* Simplify carefully

* Example: $x^2 +6x +5$

  * $a = 1$ , $b = 6$, $c=5$

    $x= \dfrac{-6\pm \sqrt{6^2 -4(1)(5)}}{2(1)}$

    $\quad = \dfrac{-6\pm \sqrt{36 -20}}{2}$

    $ \quad = \dfrac{-6\pm \sqrt{16}}{2}$

    $ \quad = \dfrac{-6\pm 4}{2}$

    $x = \dfrac{-6 + 4}{2}$

    $\quad= -1$

    $x = \dfrac{-6 - 4}{2}$

    $\quad =-5$

**Solving using Equation / Solver mode in GDC**

* Open the **Equation / Solver** mode on the GDC.

* Select **Polynomial** → **Degree 2** (for quadratics) 

* Enter the coefficients $a$, $b$, and $c$

* Press **Solve**.

* Read off the solutions $x1$, $x2​$

**Solving using Graph**

* Go to Graph mode

* Enter equation

* Plot the graph

* Use **CALC → Zero / Root** (or trace) to find x-intercepts

* The x-coordinates are the solutions

* Result Interpretation  :

  * Two roots means  two real solutions for the equation

  * One root means the graph is touching x-axis

  * No roots means there are no real solutions
