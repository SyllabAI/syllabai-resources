## Proportion

**Direct Proportion**

* Two quantities are in direct proportion if they increase or decrease at the same rate

* $y \propto x$ , which means $y = kx$ where $k$ is a constant

* This can be linear, square or cube

* Example : If $y \propto x$ and $y=12$, when $x=4$

  * $12 = k(4)$

  * $k = 3$

  * So the formula is $y = 3x$

**Indirect Proportion**

* Two quantities are in inverse proportion if one increases while the other decreases and vice versa

* $y \propto \frac {1}{x}$ , which means $y = \frac {k}{x}$, where k is a constant

* This can be linear, square or cube

* Example : If $y \propto \frac{1}{x}$​and $y=5$, when $x=2$

  * $5=\dfrac{k}{2}$

  * $k=10$

  * So the formula is $y = \dfrac{10}{x}$

Remember:

* Proportion questions are commonly asked involving speed, time, distance, mass, volume, work, and rate of change

* Always find the constant k and form the equation before answering the question
