## Algebraic Fractions

* A fraction that contains variables in the numerator, denominator or both

* Example: $\dfrac{3x}{2x+1}$ , $\dfrac{5}{x-3}$

### **Solving questions involving Algebraic Fractions**

**Addition and Subtraction**

* Find a common denominator by taking LCM of the denominators

* Rewrite each fraction with the common denominator

* Add or subtract the numerators only

* If quadratic equation formed , equate the equation to 0 and solve

* Solve fully by factorising or cancelling common factors

* Example: $\dfrac{1}{x-2} + \dfrac{x+1}{x-3}$

  * LCM is $(x-2)(x-3)$ so multiply 1 and $(x-3)$ in the 1st fraction and multiply $(x+1)$ with $(x-2)$

  * $(x - 3) + (x + 1)(x - 2) = 0$

  * Expand : $(x + 1)(x - 2) = x^2 - x - 2$

  * $(x - 3) + (x^2 - x - 2) = 0$

  * Simplify to $x^2 - 5 = 0$

  * Now solve , $x^2 = 5$

  * $x = \pm \sqrt{5}$

**Multiplication and Division**

* Cancel out common factor

* Multiply or divide

* If quadratic equation formed , equate the equation to 0 and solve

* Factorise if you can

  * Example: $\dfrac{3x}{4} \times \dfrac{8}{x}$

    * $= \dfrac{24x}{4x}$

    * $=6$

### **Simplifying Algebraic Fractions**

* Factorise

* Cancel common factors

* Example: $\dfrac{x^2 - 2x}{x^2 - 5x + 6}$

  1. Factorise the numerator and denominator

     $= \dfrac{x(x - 2)}{(x - 2)(x - 3)}$

  2. Cancel the common factor $(x - 2)$

     $= \dfrac{x}{x - 3}$
