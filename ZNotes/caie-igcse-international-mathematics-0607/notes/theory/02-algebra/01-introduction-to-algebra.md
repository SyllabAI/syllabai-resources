# Algebra

## Introduction to Algebra

### **Variables and Expressions**

* Variable: A symbol that represents an unknown or changing quantity

  * Example: $a,y,x$

* Expression: A combination of numbers (constants), letters (variables), and operation symbols (like +, -, ×, ÷) that

  represents a value

  * Remember that an expression does **NOT** contain a equal sign

  * Example: $3x−5y+2$

### **Substitution**

* A process of replacing variables with numbers

  * Example: Evaluate $3x+4$ when $x=5$

    * $3(5)+4=15+4 =19$
