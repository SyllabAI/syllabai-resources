## Linear Equations

### **Constructing Expressions, Equations, and Formulae**

* **Expressions:**

  * An expression uses numbers, variables, and operations but no equal sign

  * Examples:

    * Three more than a number $x$ → $x +3$

    * Twice a number $y$ decreased by 5 → $2y - 5$

* Equations:

  * An equation includes an equals sign and shows two expressions are equal

  * Example:

    * A number increased by 4 is 15 → $x +4 = 15$

    * “The sum of two consecutive numbers is 21” → $x + (x+1) = 21$

* Formulae:

  * A formula shows a relationship between variables

  * Example:

    * Area of a rectangle: $A = l × w$

    * Mole: $mol = concentration ×volume$

### **Solving  Linear Equations with One Unknown**

* Expand brackets (if any)

* Collect like terms

* Isolate the variable

* Solve and check

* Example : $3x +7 = 22$

  * $3x = 15$

  * $x= 5$
