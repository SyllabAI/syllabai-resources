## Sequences

A sequence is an ordered list of numbers that follows a definite rule or pattern

* The $n th$ term of a sequence is a formula that allows you to find the term at any position $n$ in the sequence

  $n th$ term represented by *$T_n$* where its the nth term of sequence *T*

* The term- to- term rule tells us how to get the next term from the previous term 

Types of Sequences:

1. Arithmetic

   * Linear

   * Quadratic

   * Cubic

2. Geometric

### **Arithmetic Sequence**

**Linear Sequence**

* Has a constant difference between consecutive terms

* *$T_n$* = $a+(n-1) d$ , where  $a$ = first term , $d$ = common difference

* Example: Find nth term of : 5, 8, 11, …

  * $a$ = 5 , $d$= 3

  * Term-to-term rule : +3

  * *$T_n$ =* $5 + 3(n -1)d$ = $3n+2$

**Quadratic Sequence**

* A quadratic sequence does not have a constant first difference, but the second difference is constant

* *$T_n$*= $an^2+bn+c$

* First Term = $a+b+c$

* First difference = $3a+b$

* Second difference = $2a$

* Example:

  * Sequence: 2, 7, 14, 23, ...

  * First differences: 5, 7, 9, ...

  * Second differences: 2

  * nth term = $n^2+2n-1$

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/cbdf3165036057f6fb1d5116.webp =500x)

### **Cubic Sequence**

* *$T_n$* = $an^3+bn^2+cn+d$

* First Term = $a+b+c+d$

* First difference = $7a+3b+c$

* Second difference = $12a+2b$

* Third difference = $6a$

* Example:

  * Sequence: 1, 8, 27, 64, ...

  * First differences: 7, 19, 37, ...

  * Second differences: 12, 18, ...

  * Third differences: 6, …..

  * *$T_n$*= $n^3$

  ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/523d3cd9378045c1183d401a.webp =630x)

**Geometric Sequence**

* A geometric sequence is a sequence where each term after the first is found by multiplying the previous term by a constant ratio $r$

* $r$ = $\dfrac{\text{next term}} {\text{previous term}}$

* *$T_n$ =* $ a \times r^{n-1}$, where $a$ = first term, $r$ = common ratio, $n$ = term number

* Example:

  * Sequence: 2, 6, 18, 54, ...

  * $r = 3$

  * *$T_n$ =* $2 \times 3^{n-1}$
