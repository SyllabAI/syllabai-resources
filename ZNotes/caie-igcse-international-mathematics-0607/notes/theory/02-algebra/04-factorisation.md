## Factorisation

### **Common Factor**

* Identify the highest common factor (HCF) of the coefficients

* Find the common variable factor(s) with the lowest exponent

* Factor out the HCF from each term

  Example: $(12x + 8)$

  * $= 12x + 8$ = $4(3x + 2)$

### **Trinomial**

* A polynomial with three terms, typically of the form $ax^2+bx+c$

* Find two numbers that multiply to $c$ and add up to $b$

* Rewrite the trinomial in factored form

* Example:

  * $x^2 +5x+6$

    ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/29d1d8979f2ad3cc272ef4f5.webp =500x)

    * $=(x+2)(x+3)$

**Points to remember:**

* For trinomials with $a$ not equal to 1, Multiply $a$ and $c$

* Find two numbers that multiply to $a\times c$ and add to $b$

* Rewrite the middle term using these two numbers and factor by grouping

* Example: $2x^2 + 7x + 3$

  * Multiply 2 and 3  which gives us 6

  * Numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 7 are: 6 and 1

  * So write in form $2x^2 + 6x + x + 3$

  * Factor the first 2 variable then the last 2

    * $2x^2 +6x +x+3$

    * $=2x(x+3)+(x+3)$

    * $=(x+3)(2x+1)$

**Four Term Polynomial**

* Group the terms into pairs

* Factor out the common factor from each pair

* Look for a common binomial factor and factor it out

* Example: $x^3 +3x^2 +x+3$

  * $(x^3 +3x^2)+(x+3)$

  * $x^2(x+3)+(x+3)$

  * $(x+3)(x^2 +1)$
