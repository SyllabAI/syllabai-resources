# Probability

## Introduction to probability

* Probability is a measure of  how likely an event will occur

* Probability values lie between $0$ and $1$

  * $0$ means the event is impossible

  * $1$ means the event is certain (will definitely happen)

$$

P(E) =\dfrac{\text{number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{ total number of outcomes}}

$$

* Values of probability can be given in decimals, percentage and fraction forms

* Example: The probability of rolling a 4 on a fair die is $\frac{1}{6}$

  * It can be represented as a decimal $= 0.1667$

  * It can be represented as a percentage by just multiplying the fraction by 100

    $\quad =\frac{1}{6} \times 100$

    $\quad = 16.67%$

* $P(A)$ is the probability of $A$

* $P(A')$ is the probability of $A$ not happening

* $P(A’)=1-P(A)$

* Problems may require using information from tables, graphs or Venn diagrams, so be mindful and choose appropriate values
