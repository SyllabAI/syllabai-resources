## Averages on a calculator

**Discrete data**

### **Steps to enter data**

* Press **MODE** → select **STAT**

* Choose **1-variable statistics**

* Enter data values one by one

* Press **STAT / SHIFT + STAT**

* Choose **CALC / VAR**

### Finding values in GDC

* Mean represented by $\overline{x}$

* Median represented by $Med$

* Lower Quartile represented by $Q_1$

* Upper Quartile represented by $Q_3$

**Grouped data** 

### **Steps to enter and find data**

* Find midpoint of the class interval 

* Go to **STAT mode**

* Choose **1-variable statistics**

* Enter **midpoints** in the **x-column**

* Enter **frequencies** in the **f-column**

* Press **CALC / VAR** and find the respective data needed
