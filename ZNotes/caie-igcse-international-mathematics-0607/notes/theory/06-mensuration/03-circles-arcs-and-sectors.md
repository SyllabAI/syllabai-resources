## Circles, arcs, and sectors

* Circumference : 2$\pi r$

* Area : $\pi r^2$

### **Sector Area**

$
\boxed{\text{Sector Area} = \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2}
$

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/1ba51e38819187cd80f272cc.webp =500x)

### **Arc Length**

$
\boxed {\text {Arc Length} = \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times 2 \pi r}
$
