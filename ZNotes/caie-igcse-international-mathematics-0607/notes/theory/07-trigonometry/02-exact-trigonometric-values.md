### **Method to remember:**

**Placeholder:**

* For sin take the values as $\frac{0}{4}$ to$\frac{4}{4}$  and whole root the values

* For cos write the values of sin from backwards

* For tan just divide sin by cos

**Hand method**:

Thumb - 0°

Index - 30°

Middle - 45°

Ring - 60°

Little - 90°

* To find $\sin(\theta)$

  * Bend the finger of the required angle.

  * Count fingers below the bent finger

    $$
    \sin(\theta) = \frac{\sqrt{\text{number of fingers below}}}{2}
    $$

  * Example: $\sin(60°)= \text{3 fingers below} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

* To find $\cos(\theta)$

  * Bend the finger of the required angle.

  * Count fingers above the bent finger

    $$
    \cos(\theta) = \frac{\sqrt{\text{Fingers above}}}{2}
    $$

  * Example: $\cos(60°)= \text{3 fingers above} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

* To find $\tan(\theta)$

  * Divide $\sin(\theta)$ by $\cos(\theta)$
