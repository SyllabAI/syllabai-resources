## Ratio and Proportion

### **Ratio**

* A method for comparing two or more quantities of the same type by showing their relative size to one another,  written as $a:b$ or $\dfrac{a}{b}$

  * Example: In a class there are 7 boys and 4 girls then the ratio of boys : girls become 7 : 4

* Ratio can be simplified by dividing the respective quantities by their HCF

  * Example: 20 : 30 : 40 can be divided by their HCF 10 to give the simplest ratio of 2 : 3 : 4

* Ratio in exams may be given as scale for maps, whose unit is cm unless stated otherwise

### **Proportion**

Usually means two ratios are equal and share a similar trend, a : b = c : d

Proportions are solved by cross multiplication

* Direct Proportion

  * The two quantities increase or decrease by the same ratio, $a\propto b$, a = kb, where k is a constant

  * Example: To print 50 pages, a printer uses 10 ml of ink. How much ink is needed to print 125 pages?

    * $\dfrac{50}{125} = \dfrac{10}{x}$

    * $50x = 1250$

    * $x = 25$

* Indirect Proportion

  * When one quantity increase the other decrease and vice versa, written as $a\propto \dfrac{1}{b}$, $a=\dfrac{k}{b}$

  * Example: 4 taps can fill a tank in 9 hours. How long will 6 taps take to fill the same tank?

    * More taps = less time

    * $\dfrac{4}{6} \times 9 = x$

    * $x= 6$
