## Exponential growth and decay

### Exponential growth

* A quantity increases by a constant percentage over equal time intervals

* General form :

  $$
  y= a(1+ r )^x
  $$

  * Where :

    * $Y =$ Final amount

    * $a=$ Initial amount

    * $r=$ Rate in decimal

    * $n=$ Number of time periods

  * If rate is $5%$ then $r = 0.05$

* This occurs when the population increases, investments grow 

**Example:**

* A town has $12,000$ people. It grows by $3%$ annually.
  Find the population after 5 years (correct to nearest whole number)

  * $12000$ in the initial value

  * $0.03$ is $r$

  * Since its exponential growth we add  $0.03$ with 1 $\rightarrow$ $1+ 0.03 = 1.03$

  * $\therefore$ $12000(1.03)^5$ $\approx$ $13,911$

### Exponential decay

* A quantity decrease by a constant percentage over equal time intervals

* General form :

  $$
  y= a(1- r )^x
  $$

  * Where :

    * $Y =$ Final amount

    * $a=$ Initial amount

    * $r=$ Rate in decimal

    * $n=$ Number of time periods

  * If rate is $5%$ then $r = 0.05$

*  This occurs when population decreases, currency value depreciates

**Example :**

* A substance loses 20% of its mass each day. If the initial mass is 500g, find the mass after 4 days

  * $500$ is the initial

  * $0.20$ is $r$

  * Since its exponential  decay we subtract $0.20$ from 1 $\rightarrow$ $1- 0.20=0.80$

  * $\therefore$ $500(0.80)^4$ $\approx$ $204.8g$
