## **Highest Common Factor (HCF)**

* The HCF of two or more numbers is the largest number that divides all of them exactly without leaving a remainder

### Methods to find HCF

**Prime Factorization Method**

* Write each number as a product of its prime factor (prime factorization)

  * Examples: $20 = 2^2 \times 5 \qquad 30 = 2 \times 3 \times 5$

* Identify and multiply the **common** prime factors with the **lowest powers**

  * HCF = $2 \times 5 = 10$

**Ladder Method**

* Write the number you want to factor on the right

* Divide by the smallest common prime number (a number that divides *all* the given numbers exactly)

* Continue dividing until no common prime factor is left

* Identify and multiply the **common** prime factors with the **lowest powers**
