## Ordering and Estimation

### **Ordering**

* Arranging numbers in **ascending** (smallest → largest) or **descending** (largest → smallest) order

* Comparison symbols :


|Name|Symbol|
|:---|:---|
|Greater than|$>$|
|Greater than or equal to|$\ge$|
|Less than|$<$|
|Less than or equal to|$\le$|
|Not equal to|$\neq$|

* Remember, while arranging negative numbers, the value closer to 0 is larger: For example $-2 > -5$

### **Estimation**

* Finding an **approximate value** by rounding numbers to make calculations easier.

* General rules :

  * If the next digit is **5 or more → round up**

  * If the next digit is **less than 5 → round down**

**Significant Figure  v/s  Decimal Place**


|Significant Figures|Rounding to decimal place|
|:---|:---|
|Significant figures start from the first non-zero digit|Decimal places count digits after the decimal point only.|
|Count from left to right, starting at the first non-zero number|Always count from the decimal point to the right|
|Example:|Example:|
|$0.004567 \approx 0.0046 ; (2\text{ s.f.})$|$3.4567 \approx 3.46 ; (2\text{ d.p.})$|
|$305.049 \approx 305 ; (3\text{ s.f.})$|$0.0499 \approx 0.05 ; (2\text{ d.p.})$|

Examples :

* $47.6 \approx 48$

* $392 \approx 400 \text{ (1 s.f.)}$

* $0.0632 \approx 0.06 \text{ (2 s.f.)}$
