## Standard Form

* Represented as **$A \times 10^n$**, where $n$ is a positive or negative integer and **$1\le A < 10$**
* $5590000$ can be written as $5.559 \times 10^6$

### **Operations using standard form**

* **Multiplication**

  1. Multiply the significant figures first

  2. Add the powers

  * Example: $ (2.0 \times 10^3)(4.0 \times 10^2 )$
    1. $= (2.0 \times 4.0) \times 10^{3+2} $

    2. $ = 8.0 \times 10^5 $

* **Division**

  1. Divide the significant figures first

  2. Subtract the powers

  * Example : $ \dfrac{6.0 \times 10^8}{3.0 \times 10^4} $

    1. $= \dfrac{6.0}{3.0} \times 10^{8-4} $

    2. $ = 2.0 \times 10^4 $

* **Addition and Subtraction**

  1. Do normal addition and subtraction of significant figures

     *Do NOT add or subtract the powers*

  2. Make sure the power in both the values are the same, if not adjust one number

  3. Adjust the smaller number by shifting the decimal and changing the exponent

  * Example: $2.5 \times 10^6 + 3.6 \times 10^5$

    1. $= 2.5 \times 10^6 + 0.36 \times 10^6 $

    2. $= (2.5 + 0.36) \times 10^6 $

    3. $= 2.86 \times 10^6 $
