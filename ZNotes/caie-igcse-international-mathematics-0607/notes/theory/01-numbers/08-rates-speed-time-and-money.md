## Rates, Speed, Time and Money

### Rate

* Comparison between two quantities with different units

* Example: Speed, Exchange rates, Hourly rates of pay, Flow rates, Fuel consumption, etc.

### Speed

* Rate of change in distance per unit time , usually measured in m/s or km/h

$$ \boxed{\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}} $$

* Conversion of units from **km/h to m/s**: multiply by **$\frac{5}{18}$**

* Conversion of units from **m/s to km/h**: multiply by **$\frac{18}{5}$**

### Time

* 1 year = 365 days

* 1 hour = 60 min

* 1 min = 60 sec

* Remember time may be given in terms of either 12 hour or 24 hour clock so be careful in the conversions

### Money

* Always rounded to 2 d.p. (pence) unless otherwise specified by the question

* Can convert from one currency to another using direct proportion
