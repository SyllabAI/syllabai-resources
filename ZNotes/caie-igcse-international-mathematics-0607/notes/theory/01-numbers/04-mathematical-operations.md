## Mathematical Operations

### **Basic Operations**

* Addition (+), Subtraction (-), Multiplication ($\times$), Division ($\div$)

### **Order of Operations**

Order of operation is a fixed sequence of steps that will allow us to solve a mathematical expression correctly, this is often simplified into the acronym **BIDMAS** or **BODMAS**

* **B**rackets

* **I**ndices or **O**rders (meaning powers and roots, like squares and square roots)

* **D**ivision

* **M**ultiplication

* **A**ddition

* **S**ubtraction

### **Tips to follow when solving a expression:**

* Always follow BIDMAS or BODMAS

* Always solve from left to right when operations are of the same priority (i.e., Addition and Subtraction or Multiplication and Division)

* Wherever possible simplify brackets

* If fractions are given, simplify numerator and denominator separately before dividing

* Make sure to write all the steps

### **Example**:

Solve the expression $3^2 \times (4+2) - 5$

* Simplify brackets first : $4+2= 6$

* Indices : $3^2 = 9$

* Multiplication : $9 \times 6 = 54$

* Subtraction : $54 - 5 = 49$
