## Fraction, decimals, and percentages

**Types of fraction**

* Proper fraction: A fraction where numerator is smaller than denominator

  * Example: $\dfrac{3}{5}$

* Improper fraction: A fraction where the numerator is grater than or equal to denominator

  * Example: $\dfrac{7}{4}$  ,   $\dfrac{5}{5}$

* Mixed fraction: A fraction which combines a whole number and a proper fraction, also known as mixed numbers

  * Example: $1\dfrac{3}{4}$

Remember, fractions should ALWAYS be in the **simplest form**

**Fraction and decimal**

* A fraction usually has its own decimal equivalent that can be written in either proper, improper or mixed forms

* Any decimal number (terminating or recurring) can be written as a fraction

* A terminating decimal number represents a fraction whose denominator is a power 10

**Fraction, decimal, percentage**

* Any fraction and decimal can be converted into percentage by multiplying by 100

  * Example: $\frac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25 %$

* Any percentage can be converted to decimal or fraction by dividing by 100

  * Example: $75 % \div 100 = 0.75 = \frac{3}{4}$
