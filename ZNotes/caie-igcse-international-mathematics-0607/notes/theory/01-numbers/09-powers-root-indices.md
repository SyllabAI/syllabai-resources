## Powers, Roots, Indices

### **Powers**

* Tells us how many times a number is multiplied by itself

**Example:**

* $2^2=4$

* $2^3= 8$

* $2^4 = 16$

* The base is 2 and the number on top is the exponent

### **Roots**

* Inverse operation of power

**Square root**

* $\sqrt{a}$ is the number which can be squared to give $a$

* Example : $\sqrt{4} = 2$ as $2^2 = 4$

**Cube root**

* $\sqrt[3]{a}$ is the number which can be cubed to give $a$

* Example : $\sqrt[3]{8} = 2$ as $2^3 = 8$

**Properties**

* $a^{\frac{1}{b}} = \sqrt[b]{a}$

* $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{a \times b}$

* $\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}$

### **Indices**

**Laws of Indices**

1. $ a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n} $

2. $ \dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n} $

3. $ (a^m)^n = a^{mn} $

4. $ (ab)^n = a^n b^n $

5. $ \left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^n = \dfrac{a^n}{b^n} $

6. $ a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n} $

7. $ a^0 = 1;  a \neq 0 $

8. $ a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m} $
