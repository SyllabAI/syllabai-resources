## Magnitude of vector

* Magnitude is the length of the vector

* Magnitude has the same sign as absolute value

* The formula for finding the magnitude is Pythagoras theorem

  vector **s** = $$\begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \end{pmatrix}$$

  **$\left\| s \right\|$** = $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$

* Example: find magnitude of vector $A$ $$\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$$

  * **$\left\| A \right\|$** = $\sqrt{5^2 + 4^2}$ = $\sqrt{41}$ = $6.4$
