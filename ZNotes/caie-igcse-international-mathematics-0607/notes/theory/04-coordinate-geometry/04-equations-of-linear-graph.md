## Equations of linear graph

* General Form: $y = mx +c$

* $m$ is the gradient, $c$ is the $y$-intercept (the point where the line crosses $y$-axis)

* Line equation can also be written in $ax +by = c$

* Example: Find gradient $(m)$ and y-intercept $(c)$ of graph with equation $5x +4y = 8$

  First convert to $y = mx +c$ form

  $4y = 8 - 5x$

  $y=\dfrac{8-5x}{4}$

  $y = 2-\frac{5x}{4}$

  $\therefore m=\frac{-5}{4}$ and $c= 2$
