## Length and Midpoint

**Length of line segment**

* Distance between 2 points in a graph

* Formula:

$$

\boxed {d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}}

$$

* Example: Find distance between (2, 6) and (8, 10)

  $=\sqrt{(8-2)^2 + (10-6)^2}$

  $=\sqrt{(6^2) + (4^2)}$

  $=\sqrt{36+4}$

  $=\sqrt{40}$

**Midpoint**

* Center of 2 points

* Formula:

$$

\boxed {\left( \dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)}

$$

* Example : Find center of (2,6) and (8,10)

  $=\biggl(\dfrac{8+2}{2}$ , $\dfrac{10+6}{2} \biggl)$

  $=\biggl(\dfrac{10}{2}$ , $\dfrac{16}{2}\biggl)$

  $=(5, 8)$
