## Logarithmic functions

* Inverse of exponential function

* $y=a^x$ is equivalent to  $x=\log_a  y$

* All logs are base 10 unless stated otherwise

* Log can used as easy way to solve questions involving exponential growth and compound interest

* $a^x = b$ can be written as $ x = \dfrac{log \thinspace b}{log \thinspace a}$

* Laws of Logarithm

  * $\log a + \log b = \log(ab)$

  * $\log a - \log b = \log\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)$

  * $\log a^n = n\log a$

  * $log_a  1 = 0$

* $y=\log_a x$ is the **reflection** of $y=a^x$ in the line $y=x$
