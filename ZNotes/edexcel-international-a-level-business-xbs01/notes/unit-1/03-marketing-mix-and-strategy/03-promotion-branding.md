## Promotion & branding

Promotion involves a business drawing attention to a product, service, or company. Some aims to be achieved are:

* consumers of an existing product

* retain existing customers over a wide area

* reassure consumers about products

* show consumers that rival products are worse

* improve and develop the image of a business

**Above-the-line Promotion**

* **Informative advertising**: adverts are designed to increase consumer awareness of products. They may give clear info about product features, e.g., adverts in newspapers.

* **Persuasive advertising**: adverts designed to put pressure on consumers to buy a product. They usually try to convince people to buy from a particular brand rather than a rival.

* **Reassuring advertising**: adverts designed to be comforting and suggest to consumers that they were 'right' to purchase the product.

**Below-the-line Promotion**

* Promotion that does not involve direct advertising.

* **Sales promotion**: incentives used to encourage people to buy products.

  * They could be used to boost sales or enter a new market. Examples include:

    * Free gifts

    * Competitions

    * Coupons/vouchers

    * Money-off deals

* **Public relations and publicity:** communication with stakeholders to increase sales by enhancing the image.

* **Press release**: information about the business is given to the media to include in TV/radio/magazines

* **Press conferences**: personal meetings to allow questions and answers

* **Sponsorship**: gain publicity by linking to sports events. Brand names are also displayed generally without paying for TV expenses.

* **Merchandising and packaging:**

  * product layout (careful to make it attractive to encourage following of one  product and force customers to look at certain goods).

  * Display material (posters/leaflets may be used to display products in order to encourage purchase. Lighting and window displays help draw consumers and improve the shopping environment).

* **Direct mailing**: sending product info/price changes, etc. to regularly.

* **Direct/personal selling**: communicate with consumers.

* **Exhibitions and trade fairs:**

  * Product testing before launch

  * Overseas exhibitions allow entry into the foreign market

  * Physical demonstration and answering questions face-to-face

  * Customer Service: speaking to trained personnel face to face

  * Attracting media.

### Factors Affecting Promotion

* **Cost/method of promotion**:  Small firms cannot conduct expensive TV or newspaper advertising.  market types. Local firms rely on local newspapers, while mass market firms rely on globally displayed ads like TV.

* **Product types**: Certain products, like raw materials, may not use sales promotion, and similarly, supermarkets will not use personal selling.

* **Stage in the product life cycle**: Promotional methods change as the product gets older.

* **Competition**: Businesses tend to copy successful methods of promotion used by rivals.

* **Legal factor**: Laws are implemented to protect consumers, which can affect the style of promotion.

### Types of Brands

* **Manufacturers' Brands**: brands created by the producers of goods and services. Manufacturers are involved in the production, pricing, distribution and promotion.

* **Own-labelled Brands**: products which are manufactured for wholesalers or retailers by other firms. However, the wholesalers and retailers sell products under their own name.

* **Generic Brand**: products that only contain the name of the actual product category, rather than the company or product name. These products are sold at a lower price than the branded product. They tend to account for a small percentage of all sales.

**Benefits of Strong Branding**

* Adding value.

* Ability to charge a premium price.

* Reduced price elasticity of demand.

### Promotion and branding to reflect social trends

* Emotional Branding: A marketing approach where businesses appeal to customers’ emotions, values, or feelings to build a strong relationship with the brand.

* Viral Marketing: A marketing strategy where consumers themselves share a company’s promotional message electronically, causing it to spread quickly to a large audience.

  * Utilises social media, messaging apps, email etc
