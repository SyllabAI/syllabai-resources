## Distribution

* **Distribution of products** refers to the location where consumers can buy products from.

* **A distribution channel** is the route taken by a product from a producer to a consumer. Examples are:

  * Producers → Wholesalers → Retailers → Consumers
  * Producers → Retailers → Consumers
  * Producers → Consumers

**Producer**

* A producer is an individual or organization that manufactures and creates products or services that will be sold

* Start of the production process

**Wholesalers**

* Wholesalers are those that buy in bulk quantities and may:

  * Break bulk, re-pack items, store goods, and provide delivery services to sell to retailers.

  * Stock goods are produced by many manufacturers.

**Retailers**

* Businesses that buy goods to be sold directly to consumers.

* Break bulk by buying in large quantities and selling to customers in small quantities.

* Sell in the most convenient locations for consumers.

* Add value by providing additional services.

* **Types of retailers:**

  * Independents

  * Supermarkets

  * Department stores

  * Multiples: store chains

  * Online retailers

  * Superstore

  * Kiosk

  * Market traders

### Two-Stage Distribution

* **Use of direct selling:**

  * Internet.

  * Direct mail.

  * Door-to-door sales.

  * Mail order catalogues.

  * Shopping parties.

  * Telephone selling.

**Customer**

* A customer is an individual or household to who purchases a goods or services for a given price.

* A customer can be intermediate which is when a member of the distribution channel purchases goods and services form the manufacturer. For example, retailer buying goods from the manufacturer

* Alternatively, it can be the end customer, which is the final stage of a distribution channel

### Choosing an Appropriate Channel

* **Nature of product:**

  * Services are sold directly to consumers.

  * Fast-moving consumer goods need a three or four-stage distribution.

  * High-quality producers sell to specific outlets only.

  * Products needing explanation are sold by skilled salespeople.

* **Cost**: Cheaper options preferred.

* **Market**: Mass market sellers use intermediaries, whilst niche market sellers use direct selling.

* **Control**: Some businesses require complete control over distribution, such as technology or electronics stores.

### E-commerce

* E-commerce is the buying and selling of goods and services over the internet
