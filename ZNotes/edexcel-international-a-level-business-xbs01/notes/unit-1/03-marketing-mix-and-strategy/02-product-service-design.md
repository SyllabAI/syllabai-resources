## Product/Service Design

Product design is the creation of a new product or service. This process occurs after a need has been identified. Product design considers:

* Shape and appearance.

* Adapting to the need.

* Ease of production from design.

* Cost-effectiveness.

* Dimensions and preferred material.

* Image when displayed.

* Whether the product should create a company image.

### Design Mix

* **Function**: A product must fit the purpose it is made for. Manufacturers of consumer durable goods offer warranties to show confidence in their reliability. The product/service should also be convenient for the consumer to use.

  * Ergonomic products are designed so that people can interact with the product without unnecessary effort and safety concerns.

  * A business with superior design functionality can generate a USP (Unique Selling Proposition).

* **Aesthetics**: Products and services should stimulate people's senses in addition to functionality. This is the aesthetic appeal of the product.

  * Designers consider size, appearance, smell, taste, or presentation of a service.

  * As the cost of resources and manufacturing decreases over time, aesthetics are likely to become more important in the design mix.

* **Cost/economic manufacture**: A well-designed product or service has to be economically viable. This implies that the product should be sold to make a profit. Producers have to minimize material costs. Businesses may have to compromise on cost and design.

### Design Mix and Social Trends

* Social trends like increasing awareness of the environment.

* Design for waste minimization.

* Products that require more energy should be made more durable to last a long time. This could be achieved by inputting replaceable components.

* Products can be designed to be smaller to save materials used in production. The cost of packaging materials will also be reduced as less area is occupied.

* Discouraging the use and production of one-use products.

* Menus can be designed to reduce food waste in the restaurant industry.

**Design for Re-use**

* Businesses could design reusable packaging. Design products with reusable components.

**Design for Recycling**

* Production methods are being adapted to allow newly designed products to be produced using recycled materials.

* Some firms specialize in the sole use of recycling materials for manufacturing.

* Some firms use materials/waste discarded by other firms in their design.

* It may be possible to recycle material to save time and effort.

**Ethical Sourcing**

* Using materials, components, and services from suppliers that respect the environment, pay fair wages, provide a safe working environment, and generally trade with honesty.

* Only a small number of firms adopt an ethical approach to supplying products, whilst most are focused on lowering costs. This could be because people are focused on lower prices than ethical products.

### Benefits of Adapting to Social Trends

* Lower costs are incurred if waste is reduced, as fewer resources are used.

* Products may sell in larger quantities if they follow social trends, hence improving revenue.

* Some businesses use design features to develop a USP to help market products more effectively.

* Firms emphasizing corporate social responsibility in their marketing are likely to be viewed as good corporate citizens. This can help increase sales revenue and avoid criticism for unethical trading.
