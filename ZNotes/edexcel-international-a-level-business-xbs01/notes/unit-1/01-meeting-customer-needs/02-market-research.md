## Market Research

* **Market research** is the gathering, presenting, and analyzing information about marketing and consumption of goods and services, this helps reduce the risk of failure. Benefit from market research:

  * Understanding needs and wants of consumers. It helps outline branding, design, and wanted/expected features.

* Data gathered from market research is qualitative and quantitative to identify needs and wants.

  * **Qualitative research:** involves data about attitudes, beliefs, and intentions. Focus groups are a common method to collect qualitative data. Info is usually open-ended ended which can lead to disagreements about its significance and importance.

  * **Quantitative research:** collection of data that can be measured. Surveys and government reports are common methods to collect this research type. Collected info is less open for interpretation.

* Identifying an insight into consumer behavior helps meet consumer needs more effectively since the product can be adapted likewise, leading to higher sales and customer loyalty.

### Primary Research

* Primary research involves the direct collection of data specifically for the project at hand.

* It can be carried out by the company or by a market research agency, depending on the budget/size of the business.

* Involves dealing with a sample of people that represent the target market.

* **Methods of Primary Research**

* **Questionnaires/Surveys:** A list of questions that record the opinions of the respondents.

  * A good questionnaire contains a balance of closed and open-ended questions to allow limited and unlimited types of responses.

  * They contain simple and clear questions to avoid use of jargon and bad grammar, no leading questions (Results will be too unreliable if a question has a suggested answer), and are usually short.

* **Focus groups/Consumer panels:**

  * Focus groups consist of a small group of people that represent the target audience/market to answer question in a moderated setting.

  * A group of customers is selected for feedback over a period of time. This shows how customers react to changes in a product.

* **Face-to-face/telephone interviews:**

  * face-to-face interview allows an interviewer to clear any confusion about the questions and obtain detailed answers; however, some people may be uncomfortable being approached for an interview.

  * A telephone interview allows a wider geographical coverage and may be cheaper than a face-to-face interview. However, some people may not answer the call, especially if no appointment is made.

* **Product Trial or Test Marketing**

  * Product testing consists of encouraging the consumer to examine or use the product before a full launch. This helps analyse the market and make adjustments to the product(s) as per the reviews before full launch.

  * Some people could be offered money to use the product and provide feedback.

  * Test marketing is the launch of a product in areas to test before a wide launch. Feedback is then gathered from consumers to make modifications after some time.

**Methods of Secondary Research**

* **Websites:** Research can be conducted by analyzing the competitor websites, helps gather info like prices, product names, delivery terms, store locations, etc. Websites also help identify the cheapest suppliers in the market to reduce costs.

* **Social Media:** Social media is a cost-effective and in-depth tool to gain insight into a firm's customers, market, brand appearance, and other important aspects. Latest posts on social media help identify current trends, consumer opinions, and consumer tastes. Some advantages of social media are:

  * It can reach millions of people.

  * Social media helps target specific groups.

  * Cheaper to use.

  * Allows personal communication.

  * Data was collected quickly from a large number of people.

  * High IT skills and complex equipment are not needed.

* **Newspaper/Magazine:** Market research can be done by analyzing the ads of a competitor or by the strength of competition in a market.

* **Television/Radio:** A business may gain knowledge from a business documentary and specific channels providing specific content, like a shopping channel. Showcasing competing products/adverts of competitors also helps gain information about rival products and marketing methods

* **Reports:** A government is likely to publish data for business use, like the size of the sectors, people in age groups, income levels, spending patterns, output value of other industries, transport methods, etc. Monetary banks like the World Bank may also publish a wide range of reports for business analysis.

* **Databases:** A company uses databases to organize, update, and recall details when required. The collection of data is known as a file, which consists of related records that have info sorted by headings (called fields). A database is a large data storage having a function that can be specified and varied by the  user. Searching options are present to find a specific piece of info within the file, the sorting option to arrange the data, calculations on fields, and include in reports.

### Sampling

* A sample is a group people who represent the target audience.

* A larger sample yields a more reliable result, but is difficult to manage and can be expensive.

**Sampling methods:**

* **Random Sampling:**

  * Gives each member an equal chance of being chosen (Has no bias)

  * However, it assumes all members are the same, which is not always true. Therefore, a small sample would not have all the characteristics of the population. It would also be expensive to list and contact every sample from the large group for an interview.

* **Quota Sampling:** the population is separated into a group that shares specific characteristics.

  * The interviewers are then assigned a quota to interview some targeted people.

  * However, the results may not precisely represent the population and are not random.

* **Stratified sampling**: The sample is divided based on previous knowledge. Interviewers choose a random sample from each of the groups, making sure they are in some proportion to the population as a whole. The sample is more representative due to this method.
