## Business Objectives

Objectives are set as:

* Employees need something to work towards, and these objectives help motivate people.

* Businesses may not have the motivation needed to keep the business going without goals. Hence, owners may lose control and allow their business to become passive.

* Objectives help owners decide where to take a business and craft steps.

### SMART  Objectives

* S - Specific - Stating clearly what is trying to be achieved

* M - Measurable - Can be measured quantitatively

* A - Agreed - Have everyone's approval

* R - Realistic - Able to be achieved with current resources

* T - Time-specific - Have a stated time in which to be completed

### Business Objectives

* **Survival**: could be a primary objective if there is a shortage of resources and little to no sales. Survival usually becomes the main goal during recession and inflation.

* **Profit Maximization**: occurs when a firm heavily focuses on profit. This can be more likely if businesses are owned by investors or shareholders. These businesses focus on keeping costs as low as possible and prices as high as possible. However, customer loyalty could be damaged, and this is also seen as being very competitive.

* **Sales Volume Maximization:** Involves a business selling as much as possible, which is a sign of growth. More output would lead to more revenue. It could also be used to gain a larger market share.

* **Market Share**: A larger market share allows rivals to dominate the market. This could lead to an increase in revenue and profit.

* **Cost Efficiency**: If cost is lowered, profit margins will rise. Businesses with lower costs may also gain a competitive edge. However, product quality or customer care, and service may be affected.

* **Employee Welfare**: Businesses improve employee welfare. Workers will be happier, more productive, more cooperative, and more flexible. This can be done by improving the work environment, frequent breaks, good equipment, training, etc. This could also reduce staff absenteeism and improve brand image.

* **Customer Satisfaction**: Customers are more likely to return if good customer service is provided. This can count as a USP and increases customer loyalty. Businesses aim to increase customer satisfaction by training staff for good service, providing platforms for feedback, dealing with customer complaints, and interacting with consumers using social media. Businesses must know what customers want and provide it.

* **Social Objectives**: A business could aim to promote prosperity and develop a strong sense with the local community. This could involve investing in local areas, employment for locals, etc.
