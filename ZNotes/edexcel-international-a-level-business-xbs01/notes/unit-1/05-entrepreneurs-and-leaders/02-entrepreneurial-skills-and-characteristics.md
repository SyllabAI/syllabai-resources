### Reasons Why People Become Entrepreneurs

* **Profit**: possibility of benefitting from a large profit if the business is successful.

  * **Profit maximisation:** an attempt to make as much profit as possible during a given time period.

  * **Profit satisficing:** making just enough profit to satisfy the needs of the business owner.

* **Independence**: motivated to work for oneself rather than another organization. Along with freedom in decision-making and planning

  * **Flexible working:** entrepreneurs can work remotely

* **Social enterprise**: Entrepreneurs engage in business to improve their interest in the social, environmental, and trade.

  * **Ethical stance:** provide goods and services complying with moral standards. Eg: producing with fair wages
