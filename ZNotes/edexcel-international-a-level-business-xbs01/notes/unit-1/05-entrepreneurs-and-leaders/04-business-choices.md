## Business Choices

* **Opportunity cost** means benefits forgone or lost from the next best alternative.

### Non-monetary Opportunity Cost

* Non-monetary opportunity cost can also be implicit spending, like assets

* Non-monetary opportunity costs can include feelings of satisfaction due to purchasing or investing in the best option.

* Productivity and efficiency could also be improved.

* Opportunity costs could be hard to quantify, as the future could be hard to predict.

* Non-monetary opportunity costs could also include intangible benefits.

### Business Choices and Trade-offs

* This means opting for one choice involves compromising another.

* One extremely important trade-off is between risk and reward, implying that higher risks give higher rewards. However, higher risks could result in high losses.

### Benefits of Running a Business

* **Independence**: an entrepreneur can make their own decisions and does not have to be instructed to do or plan by a senior.

* **Flexibility:** an entrepreneur can operate their business according to their convenient timing.

* **Profit**: an entrepreneur can retain all profit earned from sales.

* **Job satisfaction**: running a business gives an entrepreneur motivation either due to profit or other goals.

### Challenges of Running a Business

* **Day-to-day operations management**: an entrepreneur needs to manage operations such as labour and handle the daily costs incurred.

* **Lack of free time**: an owner has to be dedicated to their business hence they must dedicate a majority of their time towards the business and its operation.

* **A variety of skills are needed**: an entrepreneur needs skills like communication, leadership etc to successfully run a business.

* **Unlimited liability as a sole trader**: this implies that the owner(s) have to repay any liabilities using their own assets incase of business failure.

### Weighing of Trade-off

The following actions may help balance the trade-offs:

* **Obtain Info**: Listing the advantages and disadvantages of each choice to determine the heaviest weight.

* **Balance**: Determine what might be given up in the long term for short-term gains and vice versa.

* **Measure Support**: When weighing alternatives, it would be appropriate to think which key staff will support or oppose a particular suggestion.
