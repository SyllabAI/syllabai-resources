## Recruitment, Selection, and Training

* A large business usually hires new staff by using the human resource (HR) department.

* New employees are hired because of:

  * Business expansion

  * Labour turnover

  * Vacancies due to promotion

  * Cover for temporary staff absence due to paternity leave.

### Sources of Recruitment

* **Internal recruitment** occurs from within the business and has many advantages like:

  * Cheaper as no adverts are needed.

  * Recruits (internal) are already familiar with work culture and environment. Hence, less induction training is needed.

  * Qualities, abilities, and potential of the candidates is better known to the employer.

  * Can motivate existing staff as they see a scope for career advancements.

* **External recruitment** requires the employer to contact any potential employee after reviewing applications. Some advantages are:

  * Someone with new ideas and skills enters the business.

  * Larger number of applicants.

**Specific External Methods**

* Word of mouth involves a person hearing from a vacancy from another person.

* Direct application.

* Advertising.

* Private employment agencies are good for temporary workers.

* Head hunting (the head/executive) draws up a list of candidates who may decline the vacancy or hint at better terms of employment.

* Job centres.

### Recruitment and Selection Costs and Process

**Costs of recruitment and selection**

* HR department selection incurs cost while identifying the type and number of staff needed.

* Administrative costs when checking and updating the job description and person specification.

* Advertising.

* Time spent on handling and sorting applications.

* Interviewing could be expensive due to the involvement of highly paid staff to evaluate interviewers' performance.

* Higher salary or more benefits to the newly recruited.

**Recruitment Process Steps**

* Identifying the type of and number of staff needed: overall business plan helps choose between part/full-time and temporary/permanent

* Prepare job description and person specification

* Advertise using appropriate media

* Evaluate application, curriculum vitae (CV) and shortlist for interviews

* Conduct interviews with selected candidates

* Evaluate interviews, decide who to appoint by evaluating outcomes and make appointments for interviews. Conduct a reference check. Evaluate performance.

* Provide feedback/send a reply to unsuccessful

**Job Description and Person Specification**

* **Job Description**

  * States the title and outlines the tasks, duties, and responsibilities.

  * Helps outline expectations from an employee. It can be used in adverts and appraisals to see if performance is correct.

* **Person Specification**

  * Provides details of the qualification, experience, skills, attitudes, and any other characteristic expected from a person at a particular position.

  * Helps assess applicants.

  * Also states if a quality is essential or desirable.

### Training Costs and Reasons

**Training costs**

* Training courses and other resources like trainers, equipment.

* Loss of output.

* Employees leaving: employees may get a job elsewhere after training, hence time lost on previously trained staff.

**Reasons for training**

* Increased productivity.

* Skills to use tech and equipment.

* Learn new methods of working.

* Improve flexibility (do more than one role).

* Preparing to move to new areas of work.

* Improve quality and minimize waste.

* Implement health and safety.

* Increase job satisfaction and motivation.

* Assist in recruitment and retainment of high-quality staff.

**Induction training**

* Designed to help new employees settle quickly into a business.

* Most businesses tend to introduce workers to the nature of the business, and work practices, including health and safety.

**On the job training**

* Learning from colleagues.

* Mentoring.

* Job rotation.

* Traditional apprenticeships: workers trained for a period of time and are then accepted as full-time workers.

* Graduate training: for new university or school graduates.

**Benefits of Training**

* **Managers**

  * Workers could be more motivated, cooperative, flexible which will help the manager

  * Training also puts on a good brand image which makes it easier to attract and retain quality staff.

* **Owners**

  * Productivity causes increased output and lowered costs which helps improve financial performance of the business.

  * A competitive edge may also be gained in the market.

* **Employees**

  * Training helps increase job satisfaction and motivation while lowering anxiety about effective work.

  * Employees may also feel valued if the employer pays for the training.

  * A range of skills are also developed which could help the worker get a promotion or a better job.

* **Customers**

  * Consumers get better quality products as training improves quality.

  * Customer service may also be better due to training, e.g., better outcomes from complaints.
