### The Characteristics of a Leader

* Positive self-image, genuine ability, and realistic ambition.

* Support, vision, and commitment to suggest radical solutions.

* Experts in particular fields.

* Creative and innovative sense.

* Sense of change and deal with it.

### Leadership Styles

**Autocratic Leadership**

* A leadership style where a manager makes all the decisions without consultation.

* Where managers set objectives, allocate tasks, and give close supervision.

* This results in subordinate dissatisfaction, dependence, little teamwork, high supervision needs, and poor motivation.

**Paternalistic Leadership**

* Management makes all decisions and expects all employees to follow them, yet places great importance on employee welfare, unlike autocratic leaders.

**Democratic Leadership**

* A leadership style where managers allow employees to participate in decision-making.
* Encourages involvement in decision-making and requires good communication.
* **Persuasive Democratic Leadership**
  * A leader makes a decision and persuades others that it is a good idea
* **Consultative Democratic Leadership**
  * A leader consults others for their views before making decisions.

  * This is effective because:

    * Increased social or political movement.

    * People expect greater freedom

    * More commitment, motivation, and trust.

**Laissez-faire**

* A leadership style where employees are encouraged to make their own decisions, within certain limits.

* Provide resources and tools, and move away.

* The leader gives little or no guidance or feedback and allows freedom to make decisions.

* Only effective if workers are highly skilled.

* However, workers may lack the expertise and motivation to complete their duties, leading to missed deadlines and financial losses for the business.

### The Transition from Entrepreneur to Leader

* There is likely to be growth in sales, size of financial transactions, customers, regulations, and quantity of resources that need to be changed.

  Thus, the entrepreneur's behavior has to change to a leader's. Reason for the change (Growth leads to):

  * Increased need for formality (soft decisions and official communication).

  * Increased need for channels (detailed documentation).

  * Greater ownership (e.g., funding through shares).

  * Greater responsibility to others (more responsibility due to an increasing number of employees).

  * The need for motivation and innovative inspiration.

  * Need for strategy and vision.

**Difficulties in the Transition of an Entrepreneur to a Leader**

* Adapting management style.

* Stress.

* Sharing ownership and profit.

* Trusting others within the business.

* Lack of inherent leadership qualities.

**Overcoming the Difficulties**

* Delegation and trust in employees.

* Working to earn respect from the team.

* Gaining maturity and experience over time.

* Finding ways to achieve stress reduction.

* Pursuing education.
