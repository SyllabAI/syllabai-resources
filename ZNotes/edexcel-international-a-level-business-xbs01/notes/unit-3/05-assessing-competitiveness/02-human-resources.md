## Human Resources 

### **Labour Productivity**

* **Labour productivity:**

  * Labour productivity = $\frac{\text{Total output (per period of time)}}{\text{Avg number of employees (per period of time)}}$

  * Businesses find that they become less competitive despite increasing their labour productivity due to:

    * Rival firm may increase their productivity faster

    * New rivals may set up and pay lower wages

### **Labour Turnover and Retention**

**Labour turnover**

* The proportion of staff leaving a business compared to the number of staff staying over a period of time.

* **Retention rate** = $\frac{\text{Number of staff staying}}{\text{Avg number of staff in post}}$ X 100

**High labor turnover occurs due to:**

* Low pay.

* Poor training and promotion opportunities.

* Poor working conditions, low satisfaction, and harassment.

* Poor recruitment and selection.

* Vacancies rise in the boom phase.

### **Absenteeism and Limitations**

**Labour Retention**

* Labour retention = $\frac{\text{No. of staff staying}}{\text{Avg no. of staff in post}}$

**Absenteeism**

* Absenteeism is an issue because:

  * Sick pay.

  * Staff cost if temporary employees are needed or staff work overtime.

  * Output may suffer due to the low productivity of temporary staff.

  * Absence may be an issue if the worker is very important to a particular area.

  * Loss of a customer due to a delay.

  * Demotivate staff.

* **Rate of Absenteeism** = 

$$

\frac{\text{Number of staff absent on a day}}{\text{Total number of staff employed}} X 100

$$

* **Annual rate of absenteeism:** differences in the rate of absenteeism can occur due to:

  * **Size:** Small businesses have more commitment and teamwork.

  * Health and safety.

  * Nature of tasks.

  * Culture of a workspace.

  * Stress-related illnesses.

  * Workers may feel that they are not paid enough.

### **Limitations**

* Limitations of labour productivity calculation

* **Labour productivity:**

  * It is difficult to measure output accurately

  * Ignores the quality of work

* **Labour turnover and retention:**

  * Labour turnover may be higher for part-time workers

* **Absenteeism rate:**

  * Small firms may have a low rate, but a long-term leave can raise it

### **Strategies, Rewards, and Consultation**

* Strategies to increase productivity and retention and to reduce turnover and absenteeism

  * **Productivity**: increased output per worker esulting in higher revenue and profit

  * Increased moral

**Financial rewards**

* F. W. Taylor's piece rate

* Rewards productive workers

* Motivates

**Other ways of improving worker performance:**

* Performance related pay

* Bonus systems

* Profit related pay

* Commission systems

* Help increase staff loyalty

**Employee share ownership**

* **Sharesave scheme**: employees save monthly pay for a fixed number of years. At the end, employees can use the savings to buy shares at a price that was fixed at the start or at a discount.

* Employees can incur a capital gain if share prices increase over time

* Increased motivation and loyalty

* Fall in absenteeism

**Consultation strategies**

* Employees are likely to be more motivated if they are involved in decision making.

* **Three types of consultation are:**

  * **Pseudo-consultation:** management makes a decision and inform employees. Workers have no power to influence the decision.

  * **Classical consultation:** involving employees through representatives in discussions that affect them. This allows them to have influence.

  * **Integrative consultation:** management and unions discuss and explores matters which are common concern. The 2 groups come to joint decision using problem solving techniques. Used in quality circle.

### **Empowerment and Management by Objectives**

**Empowerment strategies**

* Training

* Providing the necessary resources

* Hand over authority (give authority to make decisions)

* Inspire confidence

* Provide feedback

* Management by objectives = increased motivation by setting targets
