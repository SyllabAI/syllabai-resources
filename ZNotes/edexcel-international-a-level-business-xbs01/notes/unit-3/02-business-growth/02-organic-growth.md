## Organic Growth

### **Methods of growing organically**

* New customers

* New products

* New markets

* New business model

* Franchising

#|
||

Advantages

|

Disadvantages

||
||

**Less risky**: uses existing business resources and experience

|

**Slow**: growth over a longer period as compared to integration

||
||

**Relatively cheaper**: does not require a large on-time investment

|

**Lack selective access to resources of other businesses:** cannot access internal business data of other businesses

||
||

**Keep control**: do not need to discuss decisions with the stakeholders of another business

|

**Unable to compete with firms growing by acquisitions**: acquisitions in the same industry lower the amount of firms in the overall market as a result of takeovers/mergers

||
||

**Better protection**: less strain on financial resources

|

**Unable to fully exploit economies of scale**: gradual growth may inhibit maximum exploitation

||
||

**Avoid diseconomies of scale**: gradual growth allows a business to scale operations while increasing efficiency and is likely to prevent major diseconomies like miscommunication

|

**May be inappropriate in fast growing markets**: dynamic markets are subject to change and fluctuations
