## Inorganic Growth

## Reasons for Merger and Takeover

* Exploit **synergies** (the whole is greater than the sum of its parts, forming a more powerful, larger, and efficient organization than by merging). This can arise due to diversification, removing assets, gains by management, etc.

* Quick and easy to expand the business.

* Buying could be cheaper than internal growth.

* Some businesses may have available cash.

* Consolidate position.

* Response to economic changes.

* Grow into a foreign market by merging with a firm in another country.

* Encouragement from market globalization.

* Gain economies of scale.

* Asset stripper firms sell profitable parts, close down unprofitable parts, and integrate activities into existing businesses.

* Increase the size of the firm.

* Increased financial rewards.

### **Mergers**

* Where two or more companies join together and operate as one.

* There is an agreement between both the companies.

* Helps cut costs and deal with overcapacity.

### **Takeovers (Acquisition)**

* This can be done with **PLC's** as their shares are traded openly. More than 51% needs to be bought to acquire a PLC.

* The company bought loses its identity and becomes a part of the predator firms.

* Result in sharp rise in share price (speculation).

### **Financial Risks and Rewards**

* **Financial rewards**:

  * **Stakeholder benefits**: dividends to stockholder, wage to employee.

  * **Stronger balance sheet**: greater revenue and asset addition.

  * **Lower costs**: exploit economies of scale.

  * **Lower taxes**: acquire a firm in a 'low-tax' country.

* **Risks**:

  * **Integration costs**.

  * **Overpayment**.

  * **Bidding wars** (if more than 1 buyer).

### **Horizontal and Vertical Integration**

**Integration**

* Businesses join together to form one business.

**Horizontal Integration**

* Joining of two firms at the same stage of production or line.

* *E.g.*, 2 cement firms.

* **Pros are**:

  * Common knowledge of markets.

  * Less likely to fail.

  * Similar skills of employees.

  * Less disruption.

  * Guaranteed outlets

**Vertical Integration**

* Joining of two firms at different stages of production.

* **Forward**: joining with a firm in the next stage of production.

* **Backward**: joining with a firm in the previous stage of production.

### **Conglomerates**

* A very large business organisation that owns a number of other businesses.

* Each business has its own BOD (Board of Directors).

* These businesses are usually acquired.
