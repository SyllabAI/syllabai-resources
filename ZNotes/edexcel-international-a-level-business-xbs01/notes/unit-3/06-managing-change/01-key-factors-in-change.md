# Managing change

## Key Factors in Change

**Possible Causes of Change in Business**

* Business Size: the main causes of change could be growth (externally).

* Poor Business Performance: new leadership brings changes to try to regain reputation.

* Market Changes and Other External Factors (**PESTLE**).

* Changes in Ownership.

**Managing Change**

* Process of organising and introducing new methods of working within a business.

**Organisational Culture**

* Resistance to change may be found in the culture.

* Customs and practices are fixed, which gives organisational stability, but it can be inflexible during change. A business could avoid culture clashes by:

  * Identify and analyse cultural differences before merging.

  * Communicate with employees to explain the purpose and effects of the merger.

  * Define and implement a new culture by clarifying behavioural norms, organisational structure, and strategy.

  * Celebrate and analyse acceptance of change by emphasising the benefits and opportunities that staff might enjoy.

**Size of the Organization**

* The larger the firm gets, the less flexible it becomes because of managing difficulties, longer decision-making (longer chain of command).

* Subcultures develop in large organisations, which are more difficult to manage.

**Time/Speed of Change**

* Firms may go through rapid change (product development (sic)).

* A crisis can lead to rapid change.

### **Managing Resistance to Change**

**Resistance occurs due to:**

* **Fear of the unknown.**

  * Employees and managers may fear that they will be unable to carry out tasks, may be made redundant, or face a fall in wages.

  * Individual workers might be worried that they will no longer work with their preferred (sic) colleagues or move to a job they dislike.

  * Owners: fear of unknown market conditions and not adjusting to new situations.

  * Customers and suppliers: unwilling to change their practices when a firm is changing.

**Generally, stakeholders may resist due to:**

* Disagreement with Reason to Change.

* Fear of Impact.

* Lack of understanding.

* Disagreement with the Process of Delivering Change.

* Lack of Involvement.

* Satisfaction with the Current Way of Working.

**Transformative Leadership**

* Where new leadership, such as a new CEO, brings about change with the purpose of improving business performance.

* Positive impact of new leaders.

* Inspire the whole organisation.
