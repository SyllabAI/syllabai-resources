## Business Ethics

* Business ethics considers the moral aspect of a decision. It focuses more on a strategic level.

**Ethical strategic decisions**:

* Strategic decision affects the long-run operations. Ethical decisions are made by senior management.

* **Issues regarding ethics requiring strategic decisions include:**

  * Environment

  * Animal rights (animal testing, habitats)

  * Workers in developing countries (low cost in LEDCs)

  * Corruption (bribes)

  * New technology (operations causing negative effects, e.g., Nuclear plants)

  * Product availability (unable to afford needs like medicine)

  * Trade issues (decide to trade with countries with sanctions or embargoes)

* **Trade-off between profit and ethics**

  * Ethical operation raises costs, which leads to reduced profits

  * Reduce Revenue

* **However, an ethical stance can be used for marketing.**

  * Promote positive beliefs to prevent penalties and problems from unethical or illegal behaviour.

* **Codes of practice**

  * How employees should respond in circumstances where they experience ethical issues. They usually contain statements about:

    * Environmental responsibility

    * Dealing with customers and suppliers honestly and fairly

    * Competing fairly, i.e., no collusion or predatory pricing

    * Responding to workforce needs fairly

### **Pay and Rewards**

* **Remuneration:** Reward for work, e.g., pay, benefits, wages.

* **Pay and rewards are used by firms to:**

  * Attract employees with the right skills

  * Reward and motivate existing staff (SO)

  * Maximise productivity

### **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**

* CSR is self-regulation where the interests of all stakeholders are considered.

* Social and environmental audits are carried out by the business on its own measures and include the following indicators:

  * Employment Indicators (union, benefits, treatment, wage, training, equal opportunities)

  * Human rights indicators (work council, child labour used, discrimination)

  * Operations in local communities (impact on life of locals)

  * Business integrity and ethics (violations, political contributions, unfair competition)

  * Product responsibility (Social impact of product sold, e.g., health & safety, true adverts)

* **Environment:** Separate audit consisting of the following indicators:

  * Use of energy and raw materials

  * Waste produced

  * Emission

  * Percentage of materials recycled

  * Impact on biodiversity

  * Fines for failure to comply with environmental regulations
