## Stakeholder Model vs. Shareholder Model

### **Stakeholders**

**Internal Stakeholders**

* Business owner: gain or lose financially

* Employees

* Managers and directors

**External Stakeholders**

* Shareholders

* Customers

* Creditors

* Suppliers

* Pressure groups

* Local community

  * **Positive impact:** employing locals

  * **Negative impact:** criticism due to pollution or noise

* Government

* Environment

**Stakeholder Influence (Stakeholder Model)**

* Taking other stakeholder objectives into consideration while making decisions.

  * According to Clarkson's principle, a firm should:

    * Recognise the interests of stakeholders

    * Maintain open communication channels to consult

    * Recognize the mutual dependence—meaning the distribution of benefits after considering the effort and risk each group contributes.

    * Minimize or remove the negative effects of business activity.

**Stakeholder Influence – Shareholder Model**

* The objectives of **shareholders** have **more influence on decision-making** than those of other stakeholders.

### **Potential for Conflicts Between Stakeholders**

**Shareholders and Employees**

* Higher wages = increase in costs = reduced profit and dividends

* Employee pressure for their own objective = closure

**Shareholders and Customers**

* Reduced Price = reduced dividends

* Poor service, investment in R&D, waste = increased dividends

**Shareholders and Managerial**

* Maximising benefits = reduced dividends

* Divorce of ownership and control

* Conflict with paying dividends and retaining profits for investment

**Shareholder and Environment**

* Profit maximisation = forget responsibilities = Global warming

**Shareholders and Government**

* Likely if the law is broken

* Fall in tax paid/avoid tax = increased profits and dividends = government is criticised and possible action
