# Business Objectives and Strategy

## Corporate Objectives

The aims of a business are less specific than objectives and are communicated using a **mission statement**.

### **Mission Statement**

A mission statement is a short qualitative statement about:

* Main purpose of business.

* Goals and values.

* Reference info, including:

  * Current market

  * Key commercial objectives

  * Ways it values stakeholders

  * Ethics

**Main Elements of a Mission Statement**

* **Purpose:** Why the business exists, what the business does for its stakeholders and why.

* **Values:** Integrity, innovation, sustainability, etc.

* **Standards and behaviour:** Communicates commitment to high standards and ethics.

* **Strategy:** How aims will be achieved.

**Why a Mission Statement is Made**

* Promise to customers to show what they can expect.

* Align the workforce with one goal.

### **Departmental and Functional Objectives**

These are the objectives of a department within a business, such as marketing or HR.

### **Corporate Objectives**

These focus on the **performance of the business** and **results over time**. They **must** be according to **SMART** criteria:

* **S:** Specific

* **M:** Measurable

* **A:** Agreed upon by everyone

* **R:** **Realistic** to available resources and market condition (if not realistic, may be ignored)

* **T:** Time specific

### **The Objective Hierarchy**

* Aims

* Mission

* Corporate Objectives

* Departmental/Functional Objectives

* Individual Objectives

### **Critical Appraisal of Mission Statements and Corporate Aims**

**Potential Issues**

* Mission statements could be too optimistic and may have a negative effect on employees.

* May demotivate as they are unachievable.

* Useless if unrealistic.

* May appear insincere.

* Mission statements must be assessed to ensure that they are relevant.

**Critical Reassessment Must Judge**

* Purpose of the statement.

* Intended audience.

* How does strategy fit?

* Are aims realistic?
