# Decision-making techniques

## Quantitative Sales Forecasting

Quantitative Sales Forecasting uses a business's **past sales data** to predict future levels. Its main components are:

* **Trend**

* **Seasonal fluctuations**

* **Cyclical fluctuations**

* **Random fluctuations**

### **Calculating the Trend**

* **A succession of moving averages** is calculated.

* **A moving average** is derived from **successive averages** (usually of a constant size and overlapping) of a series of figures.

* **A line of best fit** (crossing from the between) may use **a line of best fit that** is drawn. (Line that matches the general trend of sales.)

**Calculating Seasonal Variation**

* **Residual sales figures** are calculated.

* **Average variation** can be calculated afterward.

* **Seasonal variation** (written below the line for average variation)

### **Limitations of Quantitative Sales Forecasts**

* More reliable when:

  * in the short term.

  * Revised and updated frequently with new data.

  * The market is slowly changing

  * The market research data is available.

  * Those preparing the forecast have a good understanding of the market and can make adjustments.

* No forecaster is accurate all the time, which leads to having to produce a forecast range to account for it.

### **Qualitative Forecasting**

* Use **people's opinions or judgment** rather than numerical data.

* May employ **experts to express their views/opinions**.

* Often used if there is **insufficient data** or if **figures age quickly** due to rapid market change.
