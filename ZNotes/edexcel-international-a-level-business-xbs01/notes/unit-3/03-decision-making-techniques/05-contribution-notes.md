## Contribution Notes

### **Contribution**

**Unit contribution**

* Selling price - variable cost

* Amount to cover Fixed costs that can be covered by each unit of a good or amount of money left over from a single unit after covering variable costs.

**Total contribution**

* Total revenue - Total Variable Costs = Total Contribution

* Unit contribution * No. of units sold = Total contribution

**Uses**

* Calculate break-even output

  * Break even = $\frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{contribution}}$

* Calculate profit made

  * Profit = Total contribution - Fixed costs

* Calculate the amount that needs to be sold to get a particular profit goal

  * Output = $\frac{\text{Fixed Costs} + \text{Profit target}}{\text{contribution}}$

* Help decide which order to accept

* Help decide what price to be charged

### **Interpreting Contribution**

* Contribution margin ratio: Assess profitability, pricing, resource allocation

$$

\frac{\text{Unit contribution}}{\text{Price}} \times 100

$$

### **Contribution and Decision Making**

* **Contribution cost:** Unit contribution and total contribution can be calculated to decide between orders.

* **Contribution pricing:** Select a price exceeding VC, allowing a sale to contribute. This is used when the fixed cost is low.
