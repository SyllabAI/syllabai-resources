# Planning a Business and Raising Finance

## Planning

Setting up a business can be very complex and time-consuming. However, with careful planning, the process is easier, less expensive, and less stressful.

### **Contents of a Business Plan**

A business plan is a plan of how a business will develop over a future period of time. It helps identify and outline the strategy of the business and clarifies what is needed to achieve the goals and helps consider opportunity costs. Business plans should be professionally documented and brief.

* **Executive Summary:** It is an overview of the business start-up. It. describes the business opportunity to be exploited, the marketing and sales strategy, operations, and even finance. It is written at the end and read by potential investors/banks.

* **Elevator pitch:** A summary that can be explained in 2 minutes about the business. It has the name, what the business does, aims, what differentiates it from competitors etc. Written after the business plan.

* **Business and objectives:** Contains name, trading address, legal structure, and objectives. The best business must work towards an objective and survive.

* **Business opportunity:** Explains what the business sells. Visual material can be shown to allow viewers to understand, especially if the product is unknown to viewers. It can be graphs or charts.

* **Owner’s background:** Suppliers, investors, and lenders use this to ensure that the owner is competent and trustworthy and to analyse their skills and experience. This can include motives for start-up, level of commitment, nature of training, work experience, education & qualifications, interests, etc. It may also include a CV

* **The market:** Size of potential market, identify customer profile (characteristics of target consumer), nature of demand (inelastic or elastic), competition, and marketing priorities. May also include market research. Could also show the role of advertising and promotion, the role played by social media, business websites, business literature (leaflet), the role of word of mouth advertising, etc

* **Personnel:** Clarify the number of employees needed and the skills, qualifications, and experience that are required.

* **Premises and equipment:** Most businesses need a place to trade and a wide range of resources. This lists all required resources like the factory, machines etc

* **Casting and finance:** Owners must work out how much money will be needed to be borrowed from the investor. They must ensure that they are not undercapitalized and show that they are realistic about the amount needed to be successful.

* **Financial forecasts:** Might include a sales forecast (showing how the volume of sales will change over a future period), a cashflow forecast (how money flows in and out of the firm based on likely monthly profit), loss forecast (how business moves from loss to profit), and a break-even analysis (which shows at what point revenue is equal to costs).

* **SWOT analysis:** looks at internal strengths, internal weaknesses, external opportunities, and external threats to the business.

### **Purpose and Relevance of a Business Plan**

* Must include an application for funding while starting and running, as investors won't invest unless a clear vision is provided.

* They must also include how money will be repaid and the plans to raise money by other means.

* Force owners to take an objective, critical, and unemotional look at the whole business idea.

* Provide a strategy for the development of the business.

* Provides an action plan that identifies key tasks that must be undertaken and goals that must be met to improve chances of success.

* Highlight potential problems in advance so solutions can be found.

* Shows investors/money lenders that the owner is realistic, responsible, serious, and trustworthy.
