**Limited partnerships:**

* where some partners provide capital but take no part in management. This partner has limited liability (only loses the amount invested) and is called a sleeping partner. One partner with unlimited liability has to remain.

**Limited companies:**

* have separate legal identities from their owners. Capital is raised by selling shares. Each shareholder is an owner of the company.

* Limited liability

* Run by the BOD elected by shareholders in the Annual General Meeting

* pay corporation tax

* Require the making of a memorandum of association and the articles of association

**Private limited company:**

* Companies that sell shares privately. Their names end with Ltd. Directors of Ltd tend to be shareholders involved in running the business.

### **Social enterprise:**

* an organization that trades with the aim of human and environmental well-being. They generate income through donations and/or trade and reinvest profits. They can be linked to the government and are accountable and transparent.

  * **Cooperatives:** consumer/retail cooperatives are owned and controlled by members who are allowed to vote in meetings (AGM). Members elect a BOD and managers to run day-to-day operations. Surplus profits are distributed to members according to spending.

  * **Workers cooperative:** jointly owned by the employees. The employees contribute to decision making & production, share profits, and provide capital when buying shares.

  * **Mutual organizations:** they are owned by customers (members) who offer financial services like mortgages and savings products. Profits are returned to members in the form of better and cheaper products.

  * **Charities:** raise money for various causes and draw attention to the needs of the disadvantaged group. Most charities rely on donations for revenue and also conduct fundraising events. Some also run charity shops

**Lifestyle businesses:**

* aim to make enough money and provide the flexibility needed to pay for a particular lifestyle without having to sacrifice time for personal life. They can be similar to sole traders

### **Online businesses:**

* Businesses that use the internet to trade and have features like:

  * Consumers access businesses via the internet (a website that has product & price info)

  * Collect payment electronically (credit, PayPal).

  * No formal procedures to follow or legal requirements when starting an online business.

  * Traders must have secure sites with protections against technical breakdown and fraud

  * Low set-up costs

### **Forms of Business (PLC)**

* A private equity company is a business usually owned by private individuals backed by financial institutions.

* It is owned by shareholders who purchase shares on the stock exchange or in another country.

* An initial public offering (IPO) occurs when a company goes public. This occurs when shares are offered to the public for the first time. The first job when undertaking an IPO is producing a prospectus (a document that advertises the company to potential investors and invites them to buy shares before flotation). The prospectus may contain:

  * History of business

  * List of directors and key personnel

  * Description of operations

  * An outline of how money is raised and spent

  * Future strategies

  * Financial accounts and details

  * Details of unresolved action

  * Gives info about how to buy shares

  * An application form to buy shares.

**Going public is expensive since:**

* Lawyers needed to ensure the prospectus is legally correct

* Expensive publications have to be made available

* Paying an investment bank to process share applications

* Share issue has to be underwritten (insured against the possibility that some shares may remain unsold), and a fee is paid to the underwriter to buy unsold shares.

* Advertising and administrative expense

* Minimum of £50,000 share capital.
