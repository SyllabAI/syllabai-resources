## Legislation

### **Consumer rights:**

* Firms may restrict consumers by raising prices or offering low-quality goods.

* **Legislation influences:**

  * Product quality

  * Product and age restrictions

  * Health and safety

  * Promotion and advertising

  * Prices and payment methods

* **Effects of consumer legislation on a business:**

  * **Increased costs:** have to ensure the accuracy of measuring equipment and provide safety.

  * **Quality control:** improve the procedure or face legal action.

  * **Customer complaints:** A customer service department should be present to deal with problems before the consumer turns to the legal system.

  * **Changes in business practices:** Ensuring fair treatment may pressure a business to become market-oriented.

### **Employee Protection**

* Legal agreement between employee and employer (contract covers job title, hours, workplace, termination, grievance procedures).

* Prevents discrimination, unfair dismissal, and ensures equal pay.

**Effects on businesses:**

* Compliance costs

* Extra resources for employee wellbeing

* Tax compliance requirements

* Higher labour costs (e.g., minimum wage)

* New systems for HR and compliance

* Reduced flexibility (e.g., part-time or remote work requests)

* Penalties for breaking rules

**Positive effects:**

* Prevents exploitation

* Improves motivation and welfare

* Enhances company image (helps attract/retain staff)

### **Environmental Protection**

* Issues include pollution, habitat destruction, traffic congestion, and resource depletion.
* **Effects on businesses:**
  * Stronger brand/marketing through sustainability

  * Changes in materials, production, storage, and after-sales

  * HR training and audits to ensure compliance

### **Competition Policy**

**Restrictive practices can:**

* Prevent price rises

* Limit consumer choice

* Raise entry barriers

* Encourage collusion (firms fixing prices or dividing markets)

**Possible outcome:** May still cause price increases.

**Effects on businesses:**

* **Positive**:

  * Firms comply if illegal practices are banned

  * More innovation and efficiency

  * New products, lower costs, more progress

  * Higher employment and income

* **Negative**:

  * Mergers/takeovers slowed down by investigations or conditions

### **Health and Safety**

* Involves safety equipment, hygienic workplaces, proper space, temperature, noise control, and protection from hazardous substances.

**Effects on businesses:**

* Penalties for unsafe practices or accidents

**Benefits**:

* Better company image

* Easier to attract/retain skilled staff

* Workers feel safer and more loyal

### **Intellectual Property Rights**

* Awarded only if unique and meeting copyright/design/patent rules.

* Can be purchased from creators.

* Automatic protection prevents unauthorized use.

**Benefits**:

* Legal action against trademark misuse

* Encourages creativity

* More consumer choice, lower prices

* Supports R&D, giving firms a competitive edge

* Important in industries like pharmaceuticals

* Patents reward investment by excluding competitors

**Disadvantages**:

* Only protect new inventions, not modifications.
