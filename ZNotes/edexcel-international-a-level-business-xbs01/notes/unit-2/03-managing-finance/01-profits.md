# Managing Finance

## Profits

* **The statement of comprehensive income** is the income and expenses of a business in a financial year.

  * **Gross profit** is the difference between revenue/turnover and cost of sales

    * **Gross profit =** Revenue - Cost of sales

  * **Operating profit:** the difference between gross profit and business overheads (selling and administrative expenses)

    * **Operating profit =** gross profit - Operating expense

  * **Net profit:** difference between operating profit and finance costs (loan payments) and any other exceptional costs. It is calculated at the end of the year and after tax (usually).

    * Net profit is shown in a comprehensive income statement (interest paid - interest gained).

    * **Net profit =** Operating profit - finance and exceptional costs.

### **Ways to increase profit**

* Adjust the marketing strategy

* Find new markets

* Diversify (extend product lines)

* Mergers and takeovers

* Disposal of non-profitable activities

### **Measuring profitability**

* **Gross profit margin**

  * $\text{Gross Profit Ratio} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales (Revenue)}} \times 100$

  * Higher margins are preferred.

  * Gross profit margin shows the production efficiency and can be useful for analysing the pricing strategy of a business.

* **Operating profit margin**

  * $$\text{Operating Profit Ratio} = \frac{\text{Operating Profit}}{\text{Net Sales (Revenue)}} \times 100$$

  * Measures a company's pricing strategies and operating efficiency

  * Shows core business performance and helps evaluate operational efficiency

  * Excludes other revenue, losses, exceptional items, finance costs, and income tax.

* **Net profit margin**

  * $$\text{Net Profit Ratio} = \frac{\text{Net Profit (Net Income)}}{\text{Net Sales (Revenue)}} \times 100$$

  * accounts for all business costs (finance costs, non-operating costs, and exceptional items). It is calculated before tax deductions.

  * It measures the overall profitability of a company and is used by many stakeholders to evaluate the financial health of a company

  * High margins are preferred as they focus on the profit left after all deductions (last line in the statement of comprehensive income).

  * It is the final amount left to the owners.

### **Ways to improve profitability**

* Raising prices

* Lowering costs

* Buying cheaper resources

* Using existing resources more efficiently
