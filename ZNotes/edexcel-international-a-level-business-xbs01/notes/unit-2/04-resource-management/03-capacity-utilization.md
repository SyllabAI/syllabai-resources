## Capacity Utilization

* **Capacity utilization** is the maximum use that a business can make of its resources.

* **Full capacity** is when a business is not able to increase its output.

* **Surplus/spare capacity** occurs when a business operates below full capacity.

$$

\text{Capacity Utilization Rate} = \frac{\text{Actual Output}}{\text{Potential Output (or Maximum Capacity)}} \times 100

$$

* **Dealing with under-utilization:**

  * Reduce capacity (reduce excess, re-use unused machinery (mothball)).

  * Increase sales.

  * Increase wages during peak demand.

  * Outsourcing.

  * Redeployment (use resources elsewhere).
