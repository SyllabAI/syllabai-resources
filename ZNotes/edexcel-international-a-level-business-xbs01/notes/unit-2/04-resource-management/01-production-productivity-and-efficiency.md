* **Efficiency** means making the best possible use of all business resources.

* **Production** means when resources such as raw materials are changed into products, land, labour, capital, and enterprise.

* **Job production** is the production of a single product at a time. This is a type of labour-intensive production.

* **Cell production** produces a family of products in a small, self-contained unit within a factory.

  * Floor space is reduced as cells use less space than the flow production line.

  * Product flexibility improved

  * Lead times are cut.

  * Assessment of resources and handling time is reduced.

  * Less work in progress

  * Encourages team-work

  * Safer working environment

  * More efficient maintenance

### **Productivity**

* **Productivity** is the amount of output that can be produced with a given input of resources.

* **Labour productivity:** Output per worker per period.

* **Capital productivity:** dividing output by the total amount of capital employed in a given time period.

### **Factors influencing productivity**

* Specialisation and division of labour (concentrate on a limited range of tasks)

* Education and training

* Motivation of the worker

* Working practices

* Labour productivity - flexibility

* capital productivity

### **Ways to improve productivity**

* Improving labour productivity

* Increased specialisation

* Improved motivation

* Improve training

* Increase labour flexibility

* Improving capital productivity

* Improve service and maintenance

* Upgrade and replace with new technology

* Ensure operatives are well-trained

### **Factors affecting efficiency and how it could be introduced**

* **Introducing standardisation**

  * Outsourcing

  * Relocating

  * Downsizing

  * Focused operation

  * Removes inefficient parts

  * Re-skilling unprofitable aspects.

* **Delayering**

* **Investing in new tech**

* **Lean production:** aims to use fewer resources in production

  * Stresses productivity

  * Reduces cost and lead times

  * Reduces faulty products

  * Improves reliability and speeds up product design

* **Kaizen:** small improvements made by workers to improve quality, reduce waste, and increase efficiency. They have a huge impact in the long run.

* **Just-in-time production:** minimising/eliminating stocks held by the business.

### **Production**

* **Labour-intensive:** production methods that make more use of labour relative to machinery.

  * **Advantages**

    * more flexible

    * cheaper if small-scale

    * Workers may contribute to problem-solving and find improvements

  * **Disadvantages**

    * Need motivation

    * cannot work without breaks

    * difficult to manage

* **Capital-intensive:** production methods that make more use of machinery relative to labour.

  * **Advantages**

  * Cost-effective in large quantities

  * More precise and reliable

  * Operate 24/7

  * Easier to manage than workers

  * **Disadvantages**

    * High set-up costs

    * Delay production due to a breakdown. They are also costly to repair.

    * Machines are specialised, hence inflexible

    * May have lower motivation due to monotonous work

* **Factors affecting the method of production chosen**

  * **Nature of product:** Machines are likely to be used during times of high demand

  * **Price of labour and capital/machinery:** Firms will shift to machinery if labour is costly

  * **Size of firm:** Firms become more capital-intensive as they grow
