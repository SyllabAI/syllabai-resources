## Budgets

**A budget** is a financial plan that is agreed upon in advance. It shows how much money will be needed for everything and how it will be obtained.

* **Control and monitoring:** The success of the business can be compared by checking the amount spent and the yearly budget. Reasons for failing to spend within the budget could be analysed, and action can be taken.

* **Planning:** Forces management to think further than a daily basis

* **Coordination:** Managers can control and coordinate business activities by allocating budgets in various areas of the business.

* **Communication:** Managers and workers will have a clear framework within which to operate. Budgeting may help with decision-making, highlighting the priorities and emphasising that costs need to be under control.

* **Efficiency:** Managers can empower staff by delegating decision-making duties, showing financial control to lower levels.

* **Motivation:** The budget provides the workforce with targets, and the fear of not reaching the targets may act as an incentive. Improving the budget position could indicate success.

**Historical figures:** quantitative info based on past trading record. This can be used for sales revenue budgets, cost budgets, etc.

**Zero-based budgeting:** No money is allocated for future spending or costs if they cannot be justified by the fund holder. Must show that the cost will result in a benefit for the business.

* Main advantages include:

  * Improves resource allocation

  * A questioning attitude is developed, which helps eliminate unnecessary costs and inefficient practices.

  * Increased motivation due to more knowledge of operations

  * Encourages managers to look for alternatives

  * Encourages evaluation of cost regularly and helps minimise unnecessary purchases.

* Disadvantages are:

  * Time-consuming as it involves looking at a large number of details to create the budget plan.

  * Skilful decision-making skills may be unavailable in a firm.

  * Threatens existing methods of business operations, leading to an adverse effect on motivation

  * No preparation to justify leads to no spending being allocated to benefiting spending.

### **Using Budgets**

* Budgetary control: use of budgets to look into the future

* Preparation of plans

* Comparison of plans with actual results

* Analysis of variances: Analysis of variances involves finding reasons for the difference between actual and expected financial outcomes.

### **Variances**

* The difference between the figure that the business has budgeted for and the actual figure

* Favourable variances occur when actual figures are better than the budget figures. If they are worse, it is known as adverse/unfavourable variances.

* Managers analyse variances to improve business performance potentially

* Variances may focus on a firm's expenditure, income, volume, or profit.

* Profit is the most important, as any changes affect the profit.

* The number of variances equals the number of factors that can influence business costs and revenue.

* ### Variances for decision making:

  * Analysing the reason could help avoid it in the future.

  * Favourable variance analysis helps understand positive practices to continue.

  * Strategies can be implemented to correct or improve.

  * It is helpful for decision-makers as it provides information regarding financial outcomes and their causes.

### **Difficulties of Budgeting**

* **Setting budgets:**

  * Figures are only plans based on historical data.

  * If sales data is inaccurate, budgets will be inexact.

  * Limited funds lead to conflicts between departments.

  * Time spent on fixing budgets could be used to do other tasks.

  * Over-ambitious targets make budgeting useless.

* **Motivation:** Workers may not be motivated if they are not involved in the budget-setting process or if the budget is too unrealistic.

* **Rigidity:** Budgets could restrict business activity.

* **Short-termism:** Some managers may focus too much on current decisions and may take actions that could damage future business performance just to meet budget targets.
