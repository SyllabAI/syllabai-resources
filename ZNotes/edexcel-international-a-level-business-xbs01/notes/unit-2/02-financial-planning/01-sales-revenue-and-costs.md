# Financial Planning

## Sales, Revenue and Costs

* **Sales revenue**: value of output sold by business in a specific time period.

  * **Sales Revenue**= Quantity of Products Sold x Price

* **Sales volume**: number of units sold by a business

  * **Sales Volume** = Sales Revenue / Selling Price

### **Business Costs**

* **Short-run**: period of time when at least one factor of production is fixed.

* **Long-run**: period of time when all factors may vary.

* **Fixed cost**

  * Costs that stay the same in the short-run

  * They remain the same if the business produces at full capacity or at zero capacity.

* **Variable costs**

  * Costs of productions increase directly as output rises.

  * This is a linear relationship.

* **Total cost**

  * Cost of producing at any given level of output

  * **Total cost** = Fixed cost + Variable costs

* **Average cost**

  * Cost per unit of production.

  * **Average cost** = total cost/output

* **Profit and loss**

  * Difference between Revenue and cost

  * Profit = total revenue - total cost

### **Ways of Improving Sales Volume**

* **Advertising and promotion**

  * Improved targeting: aiming at people who are likely to buy

  * Extend product range

  * Extend distribution networks

  * Develop relationship with consumer (attract and retain)

* **Improving sales revenue**

  * Changing prices

  * Raising price if demand is inelastic

  * Lowering price if demand is elastic

* **Adding complementary services or products**

  * Increased revenue if consumers are persuaded to buy related products.
