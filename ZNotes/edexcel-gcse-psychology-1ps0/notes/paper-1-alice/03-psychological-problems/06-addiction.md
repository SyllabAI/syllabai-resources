## Addiction

* **Addiction:** characterised by the compelling need for a specific substance or engagement in a particular activity to maintain one's daily routine. Examples include substance dependencies like drugs or alcohol, as well as behavioural addictions like gambling or shopping.
* **Withdrawal:** unpleasant physical or psychological symptoms experienced when attempting to quit or being unable to satisfy an addiction.

### **Prevalence of Addiction**

* In 2015/16, 8,621 individuals were admitted to the hospital with drug-related issues.
* There were 15,074 hospital admissions for overdoses.
* There were 2,479 registered deaths related to drug misuse, marking a 10% increase from 2014.
* About 8.4% of adults admitted to taking illicit drugs.
* 6% of 11-year-olds acknowledged trying drugs at least once, compared to 24% of 15-year-olds.

### Addiction manifests in two primary forms:

1. **Dependence disorder:** reliance on substances such as alcohol and cocaine.
2. **Behavioural addiction:** dependence on activities like gambling.

### **Symptoms of Dependence Disorder**

* Compelling urge to consume a substance
* Gradual increase in tolerance for the substance
* Difficulty in stopping or reducing substance use
* Experience of physical withdrawal symptoms (e.g., shaking, sweating) during periods without substance use
* Substitution of regular activities with substance use or recovery from it
* Disregard for evidence indicating harm from substance use

### **Symptoms of Behavioural Addiction**

* Compelling need to engage in the activity regularly
* Difficulty in stopping or reducing the frequency of the activity
* Requirement to increase the frequency or engage in riskier variations for the same level of satisfaction
* Substitution of regular activities with the favoured activity
* Disregard for arguments highlighting the harmful nature of the activity
