## Obedience

* **Obedience:** Following orders from a higher authority

* **Blind Obedience:** Unquestioningly following orders of an authority figure

* **Milgram's Obedience Experiment (1963):**

  1. A study on obedience was conducted at Yale University.
  2. Revealed the alarming extent to which individuals obeyed authority figures, administering perceived electric shocks despite the harm it implied.
