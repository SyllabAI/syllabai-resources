## Conformity

* **Definition:** Adjusting behaviour or beliefs to align with others, either to fit in or due to uncertainty in unfamiliar situations.
* **Compliance:** Agreeing with the majority despite personal disagreement.
* **Normative Social Influence:** Compliance driven by the desire to belong to a group.
* **Internalisation:** Adopting group beliefs due to uncertainty about proper behaviour.
* **Informational Social Influence:** Conforming when unsure, relying on others for behavioural cues.

### **Types of Conformity**

1. Identification
2. Compliance
3. Internalisation
