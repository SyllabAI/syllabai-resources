## Factors Affecting Obedience

* **Factors Influencing Obedience in Milgram's Experiment:**

  1. **Proximity of the Victim:**
     * Mr Wallace's situated elsewhere led to easier continuity.
     * Same-room scenarios or forcing hands decreased obedience (40% and 30%, respectively).
  2. **Proximity of the Authority Figure:**
     * Mr. William's present in the room resulted in 65% obedience.
     * Instructions via telephone reduced obedience to 20%.
  3. **Authority Figure Characteristics:**
     * Mr. William's lab coat and legitimacy correlated with 65% obedience.
     * Replacement by an ordinary person decreased obedience by 20%.
  4. **Legitimacy of Context:**
     * Conducted in Yale: 65% obedience.
     * Replicated in a run-down office: 47.5% obedience.
  5. **Personal Responsibility:**
     * Participants asking another to administer shocks increased obedience to over 90%.
  6. **Support of Others:**
     * Confederates refusing shocks at 150 and 210 volts provided social support.
     * Only 10% continued to 450 volts.

* **Preventing Blind Obedience:**

  1. **Social Support:**
     * Presence of supportive individuals.
  2. **Familiarity with the Situation:**
     * Creating a known and comfortable environment.
  3. **Distance:**
     * Physical or psychological distance from the authority figure.
  4. **Education:**
     * Providing knowledge on the consequences and ethical implications.
