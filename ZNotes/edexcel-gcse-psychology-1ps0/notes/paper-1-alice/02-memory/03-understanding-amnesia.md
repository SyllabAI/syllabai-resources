## Understanding amnesia

* **Amnesia:** memory loss caused by a traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  * TBI = accident, disease, neurological conditions that are acquired in life
* **Anterograde amnesia:** inability to form new long-term memories
  * intact short-term memory so the person can process the information at that time, but cannot transfer to long-term memory
* **Retrograde amnesia:** impaired recall of memories before the brain injury
  * may be limited to a traumatic incident or specific time frame

  * in severe cases, patients may forget personal identity and origin

  * it is possible for these patients to regain some or all of their memories that were lost

### ***Beyond the specification:* Henry Molaison's (H.M.) case**

* Underwent brain surgery for epilepsy-related seizures
* Damage to the hippocampus resulted in both anterograde and retrograde amnesia
* Could recall childhood memories but not experiences a few years before surgery
* He was studied until his death in 2008
* His case has contributed to our understanding of:
  * how amnesia and its types affects individuals

  * the functioning of the hippocampus
