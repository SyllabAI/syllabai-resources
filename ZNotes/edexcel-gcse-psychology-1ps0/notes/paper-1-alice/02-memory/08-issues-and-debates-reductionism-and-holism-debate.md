## Issues and debates: Reductionism and holism debate

**Reductionism:**

* The theory explains phenomena by breaking it down into basic constituent parts
  * study each part, then see how they interact
* Associated with scientific methods, allowing isolation and control of factors affecting behavior
* It is desirable for scientific practice but may result in overly simplistic conclusions by ignoring other potential causes (e.g., attributing aggression solely to an inherited gene without considering upbringing or gene interactions)

**Holism:**

* Theory explaining phenomena as a whole
* Linked to qualitative methods for in-depth understanding of individual behavior causes
* Regarded as unscientific and challenging to achieve
* Involves studying many variables, which may make it difficult to point out exactly the influential variable/factor
* Findings are specific to an individual and lack generalisability

**Atkinson and Shiffrin’s Multi-store Model of Memory:**

* Reductionist as it describes memory in distinct parts with specific functions
* It also describes memory recall only improves due to rehearsal, ignoring factors like personal meaning given to the memory

**Bartlett's Work:**

* A holistic approach using qualitative analysis
* Considerable emphasis on establishing participant character and background to understand schema formation
  * so focuses on the interactions of multiple factors
