## Crowd Behaviour

* **Crowd Behavior:** Collective behaviour of a large group of individuals (mob mentality).
* **Personal Responsibility Reduction:** Decrease in the individual sense of responsibility.
* **Deindividuation:** Anonymity within the crowd, leading to reduced individual identity.

### **Types of Crowds:**

1. **Active Crowds:**
   * Participation in protests or demonstrations.
2. **Passive Crowds:**
   * Engagement in activities like listening at a religious service.

### **Behaviour Classifications:**

1. **Anti-Social Behavior:**
   * Unhelpful, destructive, and aggressive actions.
2. **Pro-Social Behavior:**
   * Acts perceived as kind, helpful, cooperative, and peaceful.
