## Issues and Debates: Individualistic vs Collectivistic Cultures

* **Society**: Refers to a community of people.
* **Social Issue**: Denotes a problem or conflict that impacts a community.
* **Culture**: Encompasses a collection of traditions, beliefs, and values a group shares.
* **Individualistic Culture:** Highlights independence, autonomy, and individuality, leading to less obedience.
* **Collectivist Culture**: Stresses group membership, interdependence, and cooperation, resulting in more obedience.

### **Examples**

* **Nazi Germany:**
  * ***Milgram's Obedience Experiment***: Demonstrates how individuals may follow authority figures, contributing to understanding the obedience seen in Nazi Germany.
  * ***Deindividuation***: Explains how a large group may lead to the loss of personal identity, fostering an environment where individuals may commit harmful acts with reduced accountability.
* **Black Friday (Consumer Behaviour):**
  * ***Crowd Psychology***: Describes the chaotic and aggressive behaviour during Black Friday sales, emphasizing the reduction in personal responsibility and deindividuation within a crowd.
  * ***Normative Social Influence***: Highlights how consumers may conform to aggressive shopping behaviours influenced by the social norm during sales events.
* **Rioting Behaviour:**
  * ***Social Identity Theory***: Analyzes rioting through the lens of social identity theory, where strong identification with a group leads to increased intergroup conflicts.
  * ***Collective Behavior***: Views rioting as a form of collective behaviour, where individuals act collectively beyond normal behaviour due to shared emotions and goals.
