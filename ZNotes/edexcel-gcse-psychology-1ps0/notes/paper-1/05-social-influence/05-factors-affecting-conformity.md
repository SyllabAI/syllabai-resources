## Factors Affecting Conformity

* **Locus of Control:**
  * Internal locus of control: belief in personal control over behaviour
  * External locus of control: belief in external factors controlling behaviour (e.g., fate/religion)
* **Age:**
  * Adolescents are more prone to conformity than older individuals (Steinberg and Monahan, 2007)
* **Personality Factors:**
  * Larger groups increase the likelihood of conformity
  * Unanimity among the majority enhances conformity by reducing social support for non-conformity
  * More challenging tasks lead to higher conformity as individuals seek guidance from others for the correct answer
