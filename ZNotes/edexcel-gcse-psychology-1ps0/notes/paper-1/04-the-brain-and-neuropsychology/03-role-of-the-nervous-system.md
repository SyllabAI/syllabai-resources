## Role of the Nervous System

* **CNS (Central Nervous System):**
  * Includes the brain and spinal cord.
  * Relays messages from the brain to the rest of the body, providing instructions.
* **PNS (Peripheral Nervous System):**
  * Comprises nerves connecting the CNS, especially the spinal cord, to the body's skin, muscles, and organs.
* **Neurotransmitters:**
  * Chemicals are present in the nervous system.
  * Transmit messages from one neuron to another.

### **Synaptic Transmission**

1. Electrical impulse starts within the neuron's cell body.
2. Small impulse travels along the axon (a long structure connecting the neuron's cell body to the terminal button at the end).
3. Reaches terminal buttons filled with vesicles containing neurotransmitters.
4. Vesicles release neurotransmitters into the synaptic gap.
5. Neurotransmitters are received by receptors on the next nerve cell.
6. This process continues to transmit the message.
