## Issues & Debate: Nature vs. Nurture

* **Caspi et al. (2003)** identified a specific version of the 5-HTT gene influencing the likelihood of depression after a stressful life event.
* **Nativist theories** suggest genetic inheritance contributes to psychological problems, making behaviour change challenging.
  * Patients with the short 5-HTT gene version may face a higher risk of depression relapse even after treatment.
* The **nature vs. nurture debate** involves considering genetic predispositions (nature) and environmental influences (nurture) in shaping psychological traits and behaviours.
* **Cognitive explanation of depression and learning theory of addiction** propose learned behaviours that can be replaced through treatment, supporting the effectiveness of CBT.
  * The assumption that CBT works for both addiction and depression implies nurture as the cause, not nature.
* Explanations considering an interaction between nature and nurture posit that a combination of the short 5-HTT gene and a stressful event increases depression risk.
  * Genes may predispose individuals to problems, and environmental factors trigger symptoms.
