## Cognitive Explanation for Depression

Cognitive theory examines the processing of information in the brain.

### **Aaron Beck's Cognitive Triad:**

* Comprises three negative thought patterns known as cognitive biases.
* Originates from negative experiences in an individual's past.
* This results in a negative self-schema, where the entire belief system is saturated with negativity, causing individuals to perceive every situation as more adverse than it is.
