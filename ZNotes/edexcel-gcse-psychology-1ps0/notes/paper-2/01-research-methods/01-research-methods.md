# Research Methods

## Variables

* Something that can be changed or measured in an experiment, like a characteristic or value.
* Used to determine if changes in one aspect result in changes in another.

### **Independent Variable (IV)**

* Controlled and manipulated by the experimenter.
* Example: In a study on sleep deprivation's impact on test performance, sleep deprivation is the independent variable.

### **Dependent Variable (DV)**

* Measured as a result of manipulating the independent variable.
* Example: Test performance is the dependent variable in the sleep deprivation study.

### **Control Variable**

* A control variable in scientific experimentation is an experimental element constant and unchanged throughout the investigation.

### **Extraneous Variable**

* Unwanted variables that can affect the relationship between IV and DV.
* **Types:**
  * Participant variables (e.g., mood, anxiety)
  * Situational variables (e.g., temperature, noise).

### **How to Reduce Extraneous Variable**

* **Standardisation:**
  * Keeping parts of the procedure consistent for all participants.
* **Random Allocation:**
  * Participants are randomly assigned to conditions to avoid experimenter bias.
* **Single/Double Blind Technique:**
  * Test conductors are unaware of the participant's condition, reducing bias.
