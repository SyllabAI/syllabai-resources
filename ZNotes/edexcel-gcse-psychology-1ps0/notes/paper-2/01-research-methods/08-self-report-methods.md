## Self-Report Methods

* **Interviews:**
  * *Strengths:*
    * Yields detailed information.
  * *Weaknesses:*
    * Structured interviews may result in superficial information.
    * Unstructured interviews can be time-consuming.
* **Questionnaires:**
  * *Strengths:*
    * Cost-effective and closed questions provide quantitative data.
  * *Weaknesses:*
    * Potential for socially desirable responses.
    * Lack of depth in quantitative data.
