## Research Process Overview

1. **Research Aims and Topic Selection:**
   * Identify the problem to be studied.
2. **Reviewing Existing Evidence:**
   * Conduct a literature review to understand prior knowledge.
   * Draw on ideas from other sociologists for clarity and decision-making.
3. **Hypothesis Setting:**
   * Formulate a theory or explanation to be tested.
4. **Choosing a Method:**
   * Evaluate the desired data type for hypothesis testing.
   * Select a method providing evidence for proving or disproving the hypothesis.
5. **Pilot Study:**
   * Conduct a small-scale test to identify and improve research methodology.
   * Save costs and time for future larger-scale research.
6. **Sampling:**
   * Select members from the survey population.
   * Ensure representativeness for generalizable findings.
7. **Data Collection and Analysis:**
   * Carry out the research and collect data for subsequent analysis.
8. **Hypothesis Testing and Publication:**
   * Prove or disprove the hypothesis based on findings.
   * Publish research outcomes.
