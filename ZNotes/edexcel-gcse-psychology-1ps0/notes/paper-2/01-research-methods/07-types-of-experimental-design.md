## Types of Experimental Design

1. **Independent Groups Design:**
   * *Strengths:*
     * Eliminates order effects, enhancing result validity.
   * *Weaknesses:*
     * Potential individual differences may affect validity.
     * Requires more participants, making data collection time-consuming.
2. **Repeated Measures:**
   * *Strengths:*
     * Compares the same participants, providing insights into real changes.
     * Requires fewer participants, expediting the process.
   * *Weaknesses:*
     * Susceptible to order effects impacting validity.
     * Potential for practice or boredom effects.
3. **Matched Pairs Design:**
   * *Strengths:*
     * Controls for individual differences, increasing result validity.
   * *Weaknesses:*
     * Time-consuming to match participants.
