## Differences in Communication Between Humans and Animals

### **Features of Human Language**

* **Semanticity:**
  * Use of words or symbols to represent objects or actions understood by others.
  * Example: Words represent specific meanings comprehensible to others.
* **Displacement:**
  * Language can discuss what is not present beyond current time and space.
  * Example: Ability to talk about past or future events.
* **Structure Dependence:**
  * Language follows patterns and rules involving word order and structure.
  * Example: Different meanings in sentences with the same words but different structures (e.g., 'the girl pats the dog' vs. 'the dog pats the girl').
* **Productivity/Creativity:**
  * Ability to produce new sentences or use words in novel ways.
  * Example: Forming new, understandable sentences, like 'the mat sat on the cat.'

### **Animal Communication**

* **Language Features (Aitchison, 1983):**
  * Communication is considered language if it satisfies the four criteria: semanticity, displacement, structure dependence, and productivity/creativity.
* **Semanticity in Animals:**
  * Examples: Bees signalling distances, vervet monkeys using warning calls, and ants using chemical signals.
  * Animal communication lacks displacement and productivity/creativity.
* **Similarities and Differences:**
  * Humans use language; animals use communication.
  * Semanticity: Some animals, like Kanzi, the Bonobo chimpanzee, exhibit symbolic communication.
  * Human language is more complex, involving discussions about work, social activities, and self-awareness.
* **Displacement in Animals:**
  * Example: Kanzi understands instructions about objects not present.
  * Steven Pinker suggests learned responses, while others argue for genuine understanding.
* **Structure Dependence in Animals:**
  * Example: Animals have structured signals (e.g., vervet monkey calls).
  * Animal signals often resemble what they represent, unlike human symbols.
* **Creativity/Productivity in Animals:**
  * Example: Kanzi's limited ability to generate new ideas using symbols.
  * Humans exhibit greater creativity in language, producing a wide range of new sentences.

### **Differences in the Brain and Physical Features**

* **Broca’s Area:**
  * Humans have Broca's area for speech, while other species have equivalent areas for non-verbal mouth and arm movements.
* **Vocal Areas:**
  * Humans and animals have differences in vocal areas, affecting speech capabilities despite genetic similarities.
