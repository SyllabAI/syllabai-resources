## Operant Conditioning

* **Positive Reinforcement:** Receipt of a desired outcome encouraging repetition (e.g., Ian receiving a sticker for tidying his room).
* **Negative Reinforcement**: Removal of an undesired outcome, leading to repeated actions (e.g., Ian is not allowed to play on his computer game when not tidying his room).
* **Positive Punishment**: Receipt of an undesired outcome (e.g., scolding) to discourage behaviour.
* **Negative Punishment:** Removal of a desired outcome to discourage behaviour.
  * **Primary Reinforcers:** Satisfy basic human needs (e.g., food).
  * **Secondary Reinforcers:**  Indirectly satisfy needs (e.g., tokens in a token economy).
* **Strength:** Applicable to various crimes; explains deviant behaviour in peer groups.
* **Weakness:** It doesn't cover all criminal motivations, such as genetic factors.
