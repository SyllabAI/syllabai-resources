## Factors Affecting Perceptual Set

* Perceptual set is the tendency to perceive some visual information in a particular way based on some information we have available to ourselves.

1. **Motivations:**
   * *Explanation:* Personal desires, needs, and motivations significantly impact perceptual sets.
   * *Example:* A person motivated by fitness may perceive a gym environment more positively than someone with no interest in fitness.
2. **Culture:**
   * *Explanation:* Cultural background and societal norms shape the way individuals perceive stimuli.
   * *Example:* Cultural differences may influence how individuals interpret facial expressions or body language.
3. **Past Experiences:**
   * *Explanation:* Previous encounters and experiences create mental frameworks influencing current perceptions.
   * *Example:* Someone with a positive past experience with dogs may view all dogs more favourably.
4. **Peers:**
   * *Explanation:* Social influences from friends, colleagues, or peers impact how individuals interpret and respond to stimuli.
   * *Example:* Group dynamics may shape perceptions, such as shared opinions about a particular event or person.
5. **Beliefs and Expectations:**
   * *Explanation:* Personal beliefs, values, and expectations act as filters, shaping how individuals interpret sensory information.
   * *Example:* A person with a positive expectation about a new movie may perceive it more favourably than someone with low expectations.
