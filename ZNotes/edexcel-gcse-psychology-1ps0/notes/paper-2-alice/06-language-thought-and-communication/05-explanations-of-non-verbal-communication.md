## Explanations of Non-Verbal Communication

* **Darwin's Theory of Evolution (1872):**
  * **Interest in Body Language:**
    * Charles Darwin was the first to study body language in both humans and animals.
  * **Facial Expressions and Survival:**
    * Wide eyes signalling fear and bared teeth indicating anger were considered survival mechanisms.
    * The original purposes of these signals may have changed, but they persist due to their communicative function.
* **Biological Purpose of Facial Expressions:**
  * **Robert Zajonc's Theory (1985):**
    * Facial expressions are tied to survival.
    * Emotional experiences triggered by blood flow changes in the brain.
    * Expressions, even formed for other reasons, lead to biological actions, influencing emotional states.
    * Example: Fear expression triggering flight from a predator.
* **Social Purpose of Eye Contact:**
  * **Lawrence Brunner's Theory (1979):**
    * Eye contact serves a social purpose in communication.
    * Ensures understanding between individuals.
    * Non-verbal cues, including eye contact, enhance honesty in verbal communication (Wacewicz and Żywiczyński, 2012).
    * Social living is an evolved trait aiding survival.
* **Role of Environment and Cultural Influences:**
  * **Cultural Differences:**
    * Cultural influences from birth shape experiences, leading to variations in non-verbal communication.
    * Suggests that aspects of non-verbal communication are learned and influenced by the environment.
