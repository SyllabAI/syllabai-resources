## Ethical Issues

### **Informed Consent**

* Participants should be informed about the study's purpose, procedures, and risks.
* Consent must be voluntary, with the option to withdraw without consequences.
* Clear, comprehensible consent forms and an opportunity for participant questions.

### **Deception**

* Avoid deception unless crucial for study objectives.
* If used, justify and follow with debriefing to explain the true nature of the study.
* Prevent unnecessary stress or harm due to deception.

### **Debriefing**

* Post-study session explaining the true purpose, addressing misconceptions, and answering questions.
* Mitigates potential negative effects of the study on participants.

### **Protection from Harm**

* Prevent physical or psychological harm to participants.
* Evaluate and minimize risks in studies involving potential harm.
* Prioritize participants' well-being throughout the research.

### **Right to Withdraw**

* Participants have the right to withdraw without consequences.
* Emphasize this right during informed consent.
* Promotes autonomy and protects participants from unwanted involvement.

### **Confidentiality**

* Ensure confidentiality of participants' information.
* Anonymise data and protect identities.
* Fosters trust and encourages honest responses.
