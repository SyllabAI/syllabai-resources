## Types of Experiments

* **Laboratory Experiments:**
  * Controlled environment with manipulated variables.
  * *Strengths:*
    * Strict controls allow easy replication.
    * High control over extraneous variables.
  * *Weaknesses:*
    * Artificial setting reduces ecological validity.
* **Field Experiments:**
  * Conducted in real-life settings outside a lab.
  * *Strengths:*
    * Higher ecological validity.
    * Less sampling bias.
  * *Weaknesses:*
    * Reduced control over the environment.
    * More time-consuming and expensive.
* **Natural Experiments:**
  * Change occurs naturally, not induced by the researcher.
  * *Strengths:*
    * Great ecological validity.
    * High generalisability.
  * *Weaknesses:*
    * Affected by confounding variables.
    * Lack of random allocation.
