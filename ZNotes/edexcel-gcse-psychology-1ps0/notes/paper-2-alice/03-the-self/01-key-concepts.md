# The Self

## Key Concepts

* **Self:** our thoughts and emotions about who we are
* **Self–concept**: how we see ourselves, based on how others see us and our own beliefs
* **Existential Self:** an awareness that we have an existence that is separate from others
* **Categorical self-awareness**: that we are seen by others by means of categories such as age.
* **Self-image**: How we see ourselves
* **Self-esteem**: How we value ourselves
* **Ideal Self**: How we would like to be
