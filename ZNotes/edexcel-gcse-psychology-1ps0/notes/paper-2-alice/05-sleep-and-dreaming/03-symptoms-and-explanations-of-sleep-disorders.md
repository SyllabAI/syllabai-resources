## Symptoms and Explanations of Sleep Disorders

### **Insomnia**

Difficulty falling or staying asleep, leading to inadequate sleep.

* **Symptoms:**
  * Persistent trouble initiating or maintaining sleep.
  * Waking up too early and struggling to go back to sleep.
  * Poor quality of sleep, resulting in daytime fatigue.
* **Explanations:**
  * **Psychological Factors:** Stress, anxiety, depression.
  * **Physical Factors:** Pain, medical conditions.
  * **Environmental Factors:** Noise, light, uncomfortable sleep environment.
  * **Lifestyle Factors:** Irregular sleep schedule, caffeine, nicotine.

### **Narcolepsy**

Neurological disorder causing sudden and uncontrollable episodes of falling asleep.

* **Symptoms:**
  * Excessive daytime sleepiness.
  * Cataplexy: Sudden muscle weakness or paralysis.
  * Hallucinations during sleep onset or waking.
  * Sleep paralysis: Inability to move or speak when falling asleep or waking up.
  * Disrupted nighttime sleep.
* **Explanations:**
  * **Genetic Factors:** Family history of narcolepsy.
  * **Neurotransmitter Imbalance:** Low levels of hypocretin, a neurotransmitter regulating wakefulness.
