## Treatments

### **Token Economy**

* Behavior modification is based on operant conditioning.
* Reinforcement through tokens for prosocial behaviour in prison.

**Strengths:**

* Short-term increase in prosocial behaviour observed.
* Economical and manageable by prison staff.

**Weaknesses:**

* Limited impact on preventing reoffending.
* Potential for abuse by prison staff.

### **Anger Management**

* Rehabilitation for offenders with violent crimes, focusing on anger control.

**Strengths:**

* Offenders can apply skills outside prison.
* High-risk offenders are less likely to re-offend.

**Weaknesses:**

* Not all violent crimes stem from anger.
* Potential abuse of the program by offenders.
* Mixed findings on effectiveness in reducing recidivism.
