## Eysenck Personality Types

1. Criminality may run in families.
2. Twin studies show higher concordance in identical twins.
3. Eysenck's Theory - PEN Scores:
   * **Extraversion**: Lively, sociable, pleasure-seeking.
   * **Introversion:** Shy, quiet.
   * **Neuroticism**: Continuum from stability to instability.
   * **Psychoticism**: Reckless, disregard for conventions.
4. Individuals with high E scores seek external stimulation due to low arousal levels, leading to outgoing and sociable behaviours. Conversely, those with low E scores avoid thrill-seeking activities to dampen their high arousal levels.
5. High N scores indicate an unstable nervous system, causing quick and rash reactions to stress. In contrast, low N scores suggest an unreactive nervous system, leading to calmness under stressful conditions.
6. Individuals with high P scores are characterized as cold, antisocial, and lacking compassion.

**Strengths and Weaknesses of Eysenck's Theory:**

* *Strengths:*
  * Evidence linking high PEN scores to criminal behaviour.
  * Holistic approach considering both biology and environment.
* *Weakness:*
  * Presumes fixed traits; ignores behavioural change.
