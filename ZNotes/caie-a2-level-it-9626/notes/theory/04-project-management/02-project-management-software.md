### Uses of Project Management Software

1. **Planning**

   * Define scope

   * Create schedules

   * Build task lists

   * Create WBS structures

2. **Resource allocation**

   * To allow appropriate resources to be allocated for each task, such as employees, equipment, materials, and finances.

   * Ensures staff skills and equipment are used effectively while preventing both idle downtime and overallocation.

   * Helps maintain tight control over financials, minimising waste and preventing expensive cost overruns.

   * Forecasts future capacity needs to navigate competing project demands and resolve scheduling conflicts before they derail a timeline.

   * Balances workloads fairly across team members, safeguarding human resources against mental fatigue or stress.

3. **Cost estimation**

   * Including various methods that may be used for cost estimation

   * Analogous; uses similar, older projects to compare and estimate the current costs

   * Parametric; uses historical statistical data

   * Bottom-up: estimates required cost for each task individually

   * Three-point; uses optimistic, likely, and pessimistic estimates

   * PERT estimates; includes uncertainty calculations
