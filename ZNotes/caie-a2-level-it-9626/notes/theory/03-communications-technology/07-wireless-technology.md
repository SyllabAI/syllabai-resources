## **Wireless Technology**

### **Wi-Fi**

* **Operation**

  * Uses radio waves to transmit data wirelessly.

  * Devices connect through a wireless access point (WAP) or a wireless router.

  * Uses IEEE 802.11 standards.

  * Data is transmitted in packets/frames over radio frequencies.

* **Uses**

  * Home and business networks.

  * Internet access for laptops, smartphones and tablets.

  * Connecting IoT devices.

### **Bluetooth**

* **Operation**

  * Uses short-range radio waves in the 2.4 GHz band.

  * Devices pair using Bluetooth protocols.

  * Creates a Personal Area Network (PAN).

* **Uses**

  * Wireless headphones and speakers.

  * Keyboards, mice and printers.

  * File transfer between nearby devices.

  * IoT and wearable devices.

### **Infrared (IR)**

* **Operation**

  * Uses infrared light to transmit data.

  * Requires line-of-sight between devices.

  * Signals cannot pass through walls.

* **Uses**

  * TV remote controls.

  * Short-range device communication.

### NFC (Near Field Communication)

* **Operation**

  * Very short-range wireless communication (typically ≤ 10 cm).

  * Uses electromagnetic induction.

  * Devices exchange data when brought close together.

* **Uses**

  * Contactless payments.

  * Electronic ticketing.

  * Access control systems.

  * Data exchange between mobile devices.

### Wireless Power Transfer

* **Operation**

  * Transfers electrical energy without wires.

  * Uses electromagnetic induction between coils.

  * Device receives energy when placed near a charging source.

* **Uses**

  * Wireless phone charging.

  * Wearable devices.

  * Medical implants.

### **Wireless Protocols**

* **Wi-Fi**

  * IEEE 802.11 standards.

* **Bluetooth**

  * Bluetooth protocol suite.

* **NFC**

  * NFC communication standards.

* **Mobile Networks**

  * 3G, 4G, and 5G wireless communication protocols.

### Uses of Wireless Transmission

* **Data Exchange**

  * File transfer between devices.

  * Sharing documents and media.

* **Mobile Communications**

  * Mobile phones and messaging.

  * Voice and video calls.

  * Internet access while moving.

* **Internet of Things (IoT)**

  * Smart home devices.

  * Smart sensors.

  * Wearables.

  * Industrial monitoring systems.

### Security Issues

* **Risks**

  * Unauthorised access.

  * Eavesdropping/interception.

  * Data theft.

  * Spoofing and impersonation.

  * Rogue access points.

* **Prevention Methods**

  * Strong passwords.

  * Authentication of users/devices.

  * Encryption.

  * MAC address filtering.

  * Disable unused wireless services.

  * Keep firmware updated.

### Wireless Security Protocols

* **WEP (Wired Equivalent Privacy)**

  * Early Wi-Fi security protocol.

  * Uses encryption.

  * Weak and easily cracked.

  * Not recommended.

* **WPA (Wi-Fi Protected Access)**

  * Improved security over WEP.

  * Stronger encryption and authentication.

* **WPA2**

  * Strong encryption (AES).

  * Widely used.

  * More secure than WPA.

* **WPA3**

  * Latest version.

  * Strongest authentication and encryption.

  * Better protection against password attacks.
