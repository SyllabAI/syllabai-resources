## The Impact of IT

### The Impact of IT on Society

 1. **Sports:**

    * IT improves sports equipment design and performance (e.g., running shoes).

    * High-definition scoreboards and video screens show real-time updates and replays.

    * Wireless devices record players’ performance data for detailed analysis.

    * Cameras provide more accurate scoring and help judges resolve disputes.

 2. **Manufacturing:**

    * IT supports automation and precision in production processes.

    * Use of robotics and sensors increases efficiency and reduces human error.

    * Computer-aided design (CAD) and computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) improve accuracy and product quality.

 3. **Healthcare:**

    * Medical information about new procedures or issues can be shared quickly and globally among healthcare professionals.

    * Enables collaboration on ideas and projects across different regions.

    * A cost-effective way to distribute information and communicate with patients who cannot travel.

    * Familiar platform for patients already using social media.

    * Risks include privacy breaches, inaccurate advice, misdiagnosis, and reluctance to discuss personal symptoms online.

    * Training is needed to use collaborative tools effectively.

 4. **Education:**

    * Social networking and blogs support collaboration among students and teachers.

    * Enables sharing of resources, quick feedback, and communication beyond the classroom.

    * Promotes peer learning and global educational exchange.

    * Risk of misinformation if sources are unreliable.

 5. **Banking:**

    * Online banking allows transactions anytime, removing dependency on branch hours.

    * Transactions are faster and can be done on mobile devices.

    * Easier tracking of financial activities.

    * Increased demand for internet access and technology.

    * Fewer physical branches and less paper usage.

    * Higher risks of phishing and other online security threats.

 6. **E-business and Finance**

    * Convenient for shoppers as it saves travel time and costs.

    * Reduces traffic and increases access to a wider range of goods.

    * Easier price comparison and product research.

    * Creates new employment opportunities but also causes job losses in traditional retail.

    * Risk of fraud and reduced personal contact with suppliers.

 7. **News:**

    * Social media allows news and videos to be posted anytime and anywhere.

    * Reaches local, specialised, and global audiences quickly.

    * Acts as a major source of news for many users.

    * Enables user-generated content and rapid sharing.

    * News on social media influences mainstream reporting.

    * Supports targeted content and easy access to full articles via links.

 8. **Entertainment and Media:**

    * Provides platforms for interaction, creativity, and sharing media.

    * Offers entertainment, stress relief, and engagement with communities.

    * Can promote healthy lifestyles through group challenges or discussions.

    * Risks include addiction, social isolation, and exposure to cyberbullying.

 9. **Family and Home:**

    * Social media helps maintain family contact across distances.

    * Reduces isolation among older individuals.

    * Increases happiness and sense of involvement.

    * Overuse may cause jealousy, lower self-esteem, or sedentary habits.

10. **Government:**

    * Social media helps governments engage with citizens and improve transparency.

    * Enables quick communication of policies and real-time public discussions.

    * Reduces communication costs and allows data-driven decision-making.

    * Makes governments more attentive to public concerns.

11. **Politics:**

    * Empowers citizens to organise movements and express opinions.

    * Gives marginalised groups a voice in political spaces.

    * Promotes government accountability through public visibility.

    * Can also create ideological polarisation or echo chambers reinforcing single viewpoints.

12. **Individuals and Organisations:**

    * IT fosters belonging, well-being, and global connection.

    * Enables collaboration, sharing of knowledge, and flexible work environments.

    * Risks include stress, comparison, misinformation, and over-reliance on technology.

### The impact of IT on Monitoring and Surveillance in Society

* Remote video cameras observe public areas for criminal activity.

* Facial and voice recognition identify and track individuals.

* Telephone and internet communications can be intercepted.

* Internet and social media activities are monitored for suspicious patterns.

* GPS devices track movements and alert authorities.

* Data mining detects trends that help identify criminal activities.

* Drones and listening devices extend surveillance capabilities.
