## Why use mail merge?‎

* Mail merge saves time and increases accuracy as:‎
  * Source data may already exist. So time isn’t wasted re-entering data for each document.‎
  * Accuracy improved as source data usually validated and verified. ‎
  * Letters are personalised rather than being generic easily.‎
  * Quicker to proof-read on master document than several individual documents.‎
  * Master documents can be reused in future.‎
  * Filters applied so documents only made for a select category. ‎
