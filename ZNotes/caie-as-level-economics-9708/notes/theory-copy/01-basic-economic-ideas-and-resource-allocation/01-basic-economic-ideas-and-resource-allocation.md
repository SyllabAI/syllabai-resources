# Basic economic ideas and resource allocations

## Scarcity, choice and opportunity cost

### 1.1.1The Fundamental economic problem

* The fundamental economic problem arise because resources are scarce while human wants are unlimited

* example: A hospital cannot hire more doctors because there are only a limited number of qualified doctors available.

* individuals (e.g., what to buy), firms (e.g., what to produce), and governments (e.g., how to allocate the budget) — each facing scarcity in their own way.

### Key Terms

* **Economic Problem:** The situation of the relative scarcity of resources in relation to the unlimited needs and wants of people.

* **Scarcity:** It is a condition where there are insufficient resources to satisfy all the needs and wants of the people

* **Choice:** The need to make decisions about the possible alternative uses of scarce resources

* **Need:** The demand for something that is essential, such as food or shelter

* **Wants:** Wants: desires for goods and services that are not essential for survival but improve quality of life for example a new television

* **Consumption:** The use of goods and services to satisfy human wants

* **Production:** The process of making goods and services using resources (factors of production) to satisfy human wants.

* **Factors of production:** Land, labour, capital and enterprise

### 1.1.2Opportunity Cost

* The value of the next best alternative given up when a choice is made

* Example: A student chooses to study for an exam instead of watching a movie.

  Opportunity cost: Watching the movie.

## 1.1.3The Basic questions of what will be produced, how and for whom

* What will be produced: decisions will need to be taken about what is going to be produced with the economic resources and how much will be produced

* How will it be produced? Production involves the combination of the 4 factors of production- land, labour, capital and enterprise and how will these FOPS will be used to produce the required output

* For whom will it be produced? Determining who will receive the goods and services produced.
