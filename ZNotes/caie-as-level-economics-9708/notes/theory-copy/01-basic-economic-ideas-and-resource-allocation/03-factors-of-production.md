# Basic economic ideas and resourse allocation

## Factors of Production

### Economic Resources

Definition:  The inputs used to produce goods and services; they are divided into 4 factors of production (FOPS)

### Land

Defination: land is the natural resource available in the economy 

Example: land, water, forest and oil 

### Labour

Definition: it is the human resource available in the economy 

Key point: both quantity and quality of labour matters 

### Capital 

Definition: is a type of physical resources including anything that can be regarded as made by humans to aid production 

Example: machines, factories and computers 

Don't confuse: in economic capital mainly refers to productive assets and not money.

###  Specilisation 

1. ###  occurs when firms, workers or countries concentrate on producing a particular good or service. 

2. It allows workers to focus on producing what they are best at, increasing : overall production 

3. Example: A farmer specialises in growing wheat instead of producing many different crops 

4. Benefits: 

   . Greater efficiency 

   . Workers become more     skilled 

   . Increased productivity 

   . More output and less wastage of resources 

### Division of labour 

Definition: The division of labour is when the production is divided into multiple tasks, with workers specialising in specific parts. 

Benefits 

* Increase efficiency and output per person 

* Reduce production cost 

* Faster production workers become more skilled
