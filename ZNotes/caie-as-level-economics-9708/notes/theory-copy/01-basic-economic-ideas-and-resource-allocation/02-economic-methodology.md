# Basic economic ideas and resourse allocation

## Economic Methodology

### 2.1 What is Economics

1. Economics is the study of how scarce resourses are allocated to satisfy unlimited wants

2. Economics studies how **individuals, firms and governments make choices** when resources are scarce.

### 2.2 Economics as social science

Economics is a **social science** because it studies **human behaviour and decision-making**.

Economists study:

* How people behave

* How consumers make decisions

* How firms make decisions

* How governments make decisions

* How resources are allocated

### 2.4 Ceteris Paribus

Meaning: is a Latin term widely used by economists to refer to a situation where ‘other things remain equal’ or are unchanged

Purpose: To isolate the effect of one variable on another.

### 2.5 Time periods

* Short Run: a time period in which it is possible to change only some FOPS. Typically, it is labour

* Long Run: it is possible for all factors of production or resources to change.

* Very Long Run: A period in which **all factors of production can change and other major conditions can also change**.

  For example:

  * Technology

  * Government regulations

  * Social conditions

### 2. What does ceteris paribus mean?

Answer:

Other things remaining equal; assuming other relevant factors remain unchanged.

---

### 3. Why do economists use models?

Answer:
To simplify complex economic situations and allow economic relationships to be analysed.

---

### 4. What is the difference between microeconomics and macroeconomics?

Answer:
Microeconomics studies individual markets and decision-makers, while macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole.

---

### 5. What is the difference between the short run and long run?

Answer:
In the short run, at least one factor of production cannot be changed. In the long run, all factors of production can be changed.
