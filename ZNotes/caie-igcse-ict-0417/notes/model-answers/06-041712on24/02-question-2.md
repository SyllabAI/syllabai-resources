## Question 2

**(a)**

A mailing list is a stored list of contacts used to send the same email to multiple recipients at the same time.

**(b)**

CC (Carbon Copy) sends a copy of the email to other recipients at the same time as the original is sent, and all recipients can see who received the email.

**(c)**

A mailing list is a stored list of email addresses saved for group emailing.

**(d)**

BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) sends a copy of the email to other recipients at the same time as the original is sent without other recipients knowing.
