## Question 11

**(a)**

* Anyone can make a website, so it’s important to check credibility.

* Compare information on different sites to see if it’s consistent.

* Look for reliable domain endings like “.gov”, “.edu”, or “.org”.

* Check the date when the website was last updated.

* See if the website links to trusted or official sources.

* Read product reviews carefully – fake reviews often sound overly positive.

* Check for author details, sources, and references.

* Visit comparison or review websites to cross-check facts.

**(b)**

* Compress the document or save it as a PDF without removing any text or images.

**(c)**

(i) A section break

* Splits the document into separate sections so each can have different formatting (e.g., landscape/portrait, headers, footers).

* Helps keep a document well organised.

(ii) Gutter Margin

* Adds extra space near the binding edge of printed pages.

* Ensures text isn’t hidden or cut off when the document is bound or stapled.
