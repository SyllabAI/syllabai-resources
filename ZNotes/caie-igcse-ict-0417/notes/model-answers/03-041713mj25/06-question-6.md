## Question 6

**(a)** 

Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI)

1. Use a wrist rest.

2. Take regular breaks.

3. Use ergonomic keyboard or mouse / voice control software.

**(b)**

 Headaches

1. Use an anti-glare screen or blue light glasses.

2. Look away from the screen regularly.

3. Adjust screen brightness and keep it clean.
