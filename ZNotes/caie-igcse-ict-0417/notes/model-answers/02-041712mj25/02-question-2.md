## Question 2

**(a)**

(i) *Computer Aided Design (CAD)*

* Computer Aided Design (CAD) software helps users create **2D drawings** and **3D models** of objects.

* It allows digital representation of products before they are manufactured and supports **design modification**, making the design process efficient.

(ii) *Measurement software*

* Measurement software allows the computer to **interact with sensors** to record physical quantities like temperature or pressure.

* It can also **visualise data** and help in **analysing sensor results**, ensuring accurate monitoring.

(iii) *Video editing software*

* Video editing software enables the user to **add effects and features** to a video. It also allows exporting videos in different **file formats** and resolutions.

* This makes it possible to modify and share videos professionally.

**(b)**

*Purpose of application software*

* The purpose of application software is to provide the services needed by the iser to perform a specific task, e.g., creating documents, editing videos, or designing products.
