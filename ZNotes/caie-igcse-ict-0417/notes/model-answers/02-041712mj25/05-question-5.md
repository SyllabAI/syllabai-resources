## Question 5

**(a)** 

Electrocution

1\.    Use **Residual Circuit Breakers (RCDs)** to cut off power if faults occur.

2\.    Regularly check **insulation of wires**.

3\.    Do not touch wires with **wet hands** or allow drinks near equipment.

**(b)**

 Trailing leads

1\.    Use **cable ducts** to keep wires organised.

2\.    Place cables under **carpets/false ceilings**.

3\.    Use proper **cable management** or replace with **wireless technology**.
