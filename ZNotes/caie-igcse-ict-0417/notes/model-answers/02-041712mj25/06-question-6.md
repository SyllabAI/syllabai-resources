## Question 6

**(a)**

*Phishing and pharming*

**Similarities**

* Both are **threats to data**.

* Use the **internet**.

* Trick users into giving **personal details**, leading to **identity theft**.

**Differences:**

* **Phishing**: A fake email is sent, with a link to a fake website. The user is aware of clicking the link.

* **Pharming**: A malicious code redirects the user automatically to a fake website, without them knowing.
