## Question 7

**(a)**

 **Avoiding copyright issues:**

• Ask **permission from copyright holders**

• **Acknowledge sources** used on the website

**(b)** 

**Principles of software copyright:**

• Software must not be **copied or shared illegally**

• Users must not **modify or plagiarise** the software

• Software must not be **installed on multiple systems** without permission

**(c)** 

**Why copyright legislation is needed:**

• Protects **intellectual property**

• Prevents **plagiarism and theft**

• Ensures creators are **rewarded for their work**
