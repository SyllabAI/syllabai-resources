## Question 12

**(a)**

*What is netiquette.*

* Netiquette means **internet etiquette**: the correct, polite, and acceptable way of communicating online.

* It shows **respect for others** and uses common courtesy when sending messages.

**(b)**

*Three rules of netiquette other than spaming*

* **Do not use all CAPITAL letters** → this looks like shouting. Example: “I AM ANGRY” may be seen as rude.

* **Respect people’s privacy** → do not share personal details of others. Example: not posting someone’s phone number online.

* **Be clear and concise** → avoid overly long or confusing messages. Example: writing a short, polite email instead of a large block of text.
