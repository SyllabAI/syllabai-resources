## Question 11

**(a)**

*How does the spell check software work?*

* The software **scans each word** typed.

* It compares each word against its **built-in dictionary**.

* If the word is not found, it **highlights** the word as an error.

**(b)**

*Why do some words or phrases get highlighted or underlined?*

* The word may not be in the dictionary (e.g. names, scientific terms).

* The dictionary may be set to another language (e.g. US English).

* Software cannot always understand **context** — may confuse words with similar spelling.
