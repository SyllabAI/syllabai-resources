## Question 9

**(a)**

 *Use of a hub.*

* A hub connects multiple devices together in a LAN.

* It **broadcasts data** to every device on the network.

* It can act as a **repeater**, amplifying signals to extend the network.

**(b)**

 *One disadvantage of using hub in a local area network.*

* Creates **unnecessary network traffic** because it sends data to all devices.

* This wastes bandwidth and reduces efficiency.
