## Question 13

**(a)**

*Describe what is meant by hacking.*

* Hacking is **gaining unauthorized access** to a computer system or data.

* Consequences can include **identity theft**, **misuse of personal data**, or data being deleted/corrupted.

**(b)**

*One method of preventing a system being hacked is the use of passwords.*

*Describe the password rules that should be put in place to reduce the threat of hacking.*

* Use **strong passwords** that are long and include a mix of characters.

* Do not use personal information (like names or birthdays).

* Change passwords regularly, or use **biometric passwords**.
