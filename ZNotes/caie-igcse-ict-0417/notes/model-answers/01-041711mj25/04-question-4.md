## Question 4

**(a)**

*Extreme data set.*

Data at the **boundary of the acceptable range**.

**(b)**

*State one example for each test data.*

* Normal test data: **7** (any value within the range of 2-9).

* Extreme test data: **1 or 10** (at the boundary).

* Abnormal test data: **–2 or 12** (outside the range) **OR letters/symbols.**
