## Question 10

**(a)**

*Characteristics of each research methods.*

*(i) Observation*

* Watching people use the current system to **see** how it works.

* Allows the analyst to view the **inputs, processes, and outputs**.

* But behaviour may change because people know they are being watched (Hawthorne effect).

*(ii) Interviews.*

* One-to-one conversations between analyst and user.

* Allows the analyst to ask detailed questions and clarify answers.

* Helps to understand specific parts of the system.

**(b)**

**Four items to produce in design:**

1. Data capture/input forms.

2. Data/file structures.

3. Output formats/layouts.

4. Validation rules/test strategy.

**(c)**

*Reason why new system should be tested.*

* To make sure it meets the **client’s needs**.

* To check it produces the **correct outputs**.

* To ensure there are **no errors** and that the system works properly under normal use.

**(d)**

*Three requirements in a test plan*

* Test data.

* Reason for test.

* Actual outcomes or remedial action.
