## Question 2

**(a)**

(i) *Word processing*

* A **word processor** is a piece of software that allows users to **create, edit, format, and print text documents**.

* It is used for writing essays, letters, and reports. It allows features such as **text formatting** (bold, italics), inserting images, spell check, and saving the document for future use.

(ii) *Control software*

* Control software is designed to **manage, regulate, or control hardware or processes automatically**.

* It interacts with external hardware (for example, a washing machine controller) and can respond to changes in the environment by making adjustments automatically.

(iii) *Apps*

* Apps are **small pieces of software** designed for a specific purpose or task (for example, a calculator app or a messaging app).

* They are easy to download, often run on smartphones or tablets, and help users carry out everyday activities.

**(b)**

*Analogue data to digital data.*

* A computer can only **understand, process, and store digital data** (binary 0s and 1s).

* Analogue data must therefore be converted into digital so that the computer system can work with it.

**(c)**

*Digital data to analogue data*

* Digital data must be converted to analogue so that **humans or analogue devices** can use or understand it (for example, sound output to a speaker).

* Without this conversion, devices like speakers or monitors could not present the information in a way people can perceive.
