# Storage Devices and Media

## Differentiating Storage Devices and Storage Media

**Storage Device:** This is the physical machine, drive, or electronic unit that reads data from and writes data to the storage medium. It contains the laser heads, motors, circuitry, or read/write arms.

* Examples: A DVD drive, a Hard Disk Drive (HDD) unit, a Magnetic Tape Drive slot, or a USB port/card reader slot.

**Storage Media:** This is the actual physical material or surface layer where the digital binary data ($0$s and $1$s) is locked and stored permanently.

* Examples*:* A plastic DVD disc, the magnetic platters inside a hard drive, a magnetic plastic tape, or the silicon flash memory chips.

### Backing-Up Data

* Backing up refers to copying files or data into a different medium in case of damage or loss of original copy.

### Why do we back up data?

* To safeguard against loss of data.
* To safeguard against damages caused by hackers.
* In case files need to be sent elsewhere, to protect the original format of files.
* Backups can be stored online for easy access.
