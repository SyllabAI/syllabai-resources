# The Accounting System

## The Double Entry Bookkeeping for Cash Items

* The accounting system is a process of identifying, analysing, and recording accounting events of a company to provide useful information to stakeholders to make decisions.
* Ex: Selling goods/purchasing raw materials
* Double-entry bookkeeping is a process that means that each transaction has two sides Golden rule: For every debit entry in a ledger account, there must be an equal credit entry in another ledger account.
* Ex: You pay 5 dollars for candy to a shopkeeper (one), and the shopkeeper receives 5 dollars (second)
* Both aspects are recorded
* Ex A cash sale:
* Debit Cash a/c (Increase in Assets)
* Credit Sales a/c (increase in income)
* Double Entry Book entries are displayed in a “T” shape


|Debit|Credit|
|:---|:---|
|Increase Assets|Decrease Assets|
|Increase Expenses|Decrease Expenses|
|Decrease Liabilities|Increase Liabilities|
|Decrease Income|Increase Income|
