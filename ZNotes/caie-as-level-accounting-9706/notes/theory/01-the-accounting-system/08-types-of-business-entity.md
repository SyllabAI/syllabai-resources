* Limited companies are further divided into two main types:

  *  Private limited company (Ltd)

    * Shares are privately held and cannot be offered to the general public.

    * Share transfers may be restricted.

    * Can raise capital from private shareholders.

    * Generally has fewer disclosure requirements than a public limited company.

  * Public limited company (plc)

    * Can offer shares to the public, subject to legal requirements.

    * Shares are generally more freely transferable.

    * Has greater potential to raise large amounts of capital.

    * Usually faces stricter reporting and regulatory requirements.
