### **Cost accounting**

* Enables managers to manage their
  business effectively and efficiently.

* Used by all types of businesses as all types of business organisations need to know the total costs involved in running the business.

### Cost Classification

* Costs may be classified in one of three ways:

1. By element

2. By function

3. By nature

### **Classification by element**

* In a manufacturing organisation, there are three elements to cost.

1. Material – cost of goods used

2. Labour – the wages and salaries cost

3. Expenses – all other expenses, including overheads.

* Each of these elements may be split into :

  * Direct costs – those costs that can be specifically associated with the manufacture of one unit of production (e.g., raw material, wages paid.)

  * Direct costs are the prime cost of manufacture.

  * Indirect costs are those costs that cannot be specifically associated with the manufacture of one unit of production. (e.g., Salaries, rent, light, and heat)

  * The indirect costs of a business are called overheads.

### **Classification by function**

* Production
  * The cost of production is calculated as:
    Opening inventory + Net purchases + Carriage inwards – Drawings of inventory – Closing inventory.
* Sales and distribution: Expenses related to the transportation/distribution and the sales made by the business.
* Administration: Expenses related to the administration of the business
* Finance: interest, taxes, etc. Those that are charged to business finances
* **NOTE**: Charges, in this case depreciation, can be divided among two of the classifications, these divisions will be clearly stated in the paper. At Least one classification will be addressed in the question to charge depreciation to.

### Classification by nature

* **Fixed costs**: Those costs that do not vary with the levels

of business activity. Eg- rent, salaries, insurance.

* **Variable cost**: those costs that change in direct

proportion to changes in the levels of activity. Eg- direct

material, piece rate labor cost.

* **Semi variable cost**: It has characteristics of both fixed

cost and variable cost. Eg – telephone bill.

* **Stepped cost**: remain fixed until a certain level of activity

is reached, at which they rise to a higher fixed level. Eg –

supervisor salaries – 20 employees, from 21st employee

further supervisor required.
