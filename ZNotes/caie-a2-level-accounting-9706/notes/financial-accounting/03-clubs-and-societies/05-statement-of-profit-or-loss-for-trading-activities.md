## Statement of profit or loss for trading activities

A club or society may carry out trading activities such as operating a shop.

A **separate statement of profit or loss** is prepared for each trading activity.

The purpose is to calculate the profit or loss made by that particular activity. The resulting profit or loss is then transferred to the income and expenditure account.

Only income and expenses relating to the trading activity should be included.

If an expense is shared between the trading activity and the club, it should be **apportioned**.

For example, if 40% of rent relates to the shop:

$$ \text{Shop rent} = \frac{40}{100} \times \text{Total rent} $$

The remaining 60% would relate to the club itself.

### Cost of sales

The trading activity uses the normal calculation:

$$ \text{Cost of sales} = \text{Opening inventory} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Purchases returns} - \text{Closing inventory} $$

Then:

$$ \text{Gross profit} = \text{Sales} - \text{Cost of sales} $$

Finally:

$$ \text{Profit} = \text{Gross profit} - \text{Expenses} $$

The profit from the trading activity is transferred to the income and expenditure account.
