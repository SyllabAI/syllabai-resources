## Donations and grants

### General donations

A general donation can be used for any purpose.

The **full amount** is included as income in the income and expenditure account.

### Restricted donations

A restricted donation is provided for a specific purpose.

The amount is only recognised as income when it is actually used for that purpose. Any unused amount remains as a liability.

The remaining amount is often called **deferred income**.

### Grants covering multiple years

If a grant is intended to cover several years, only the portion relating to the current year is recognised as income.

For example, if a $3,000 grant is intended to cover three years:

$$ \text{Grant Income for Current Year} = \frac{\text{Total Grant Received}}{\text{Number of Years Covered}} $$

The remaining $2,000 is treated as deferred income and remains a liability
