# Clubs and Societies

## Nature of clubs and societies

Clubs and societies are **non-trading organizations**. They exist primarily to provide services or facilities to their members rather than to make a profit.

Members usually pay a regular **subscription** to use the services provided by the organization.

Instead of profit or loss, the organization has a:

* **Surplus** when income is greater than expenditure

* **Deficit** when expenditure is greater than income

The main accounting records prepared are:

* Receipts and payments account

* Subscription account

* Income and expenditure account

* Statement of financial position

* Statement of profit or loss for any trading activities

The income and expenditure account is similar to the statement of profit or loss used by a profit-making business. However, clubs and societies use the term **surplus or deficit** instead of profit or loss.
