## Accumulated fund

The accumulated fund is the equivalent of **capital** for a club or society.

It consists of capital invested into the organisation and the accumulated surpluses from previous years.

At the end of the year:

$$ \text{Closing accumulated fund} = \text{Opening accumulated fund} + \text{Surplus} $$

If there is a deficit:

$$ \text{Closing accumulated fund} = \text{Opening accumulated fund} - \text{Deficit} $$

### Calculating the accumulated fund

Use the accounting equation:

$$ \text{Assets} = \text{Accumulated fund} + \text{Liabilities} $$

Therefore:

$$ \text{Accumulated fund} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities} $$

If the accumulated fund is not given, calculate it using the assets and liabilities provided in the question.

### Calculating surplus or deficit from accumulated funds

If the income and expenditure account is not available, the change in the accumulated fund can be used.

First calculate the opening accumulated fund.

Then calculate the closing accumulated fund.

$$ \text{Surplus or deficit} = \text{Closing accumulated fund} - \text{Opening accumulated fund} $$

An increase in the accumulated fund means there is a **surplus**.

A decrease means there is a **deficit**.
