## Manufacturing account

The manufacturing account calculates the **total cost of producing goods during the year**.

The main stages are:

1. Calculate materials consumed.

2. Calculate prime cost.

3. Calculate factory overheads.

4. Add prime cost and factory overheads.

5. Adjust for work in progress.

6. Add factory profit to calculate transfer value.

### Materials consumed

$$ \text{Materials Consumed} = \text{Opening Raw Materials} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Purchases Returns} + \text{Carriage Inwards} - \text{Closing Raw Materials} $$

The result is the amount of raw materials used during production.

### Cost of production

$$ \text{Cost of Production} = \text{Direct Materials} + \text{Direct Labour} + \text{Direct Expenses} + \text{Factory Overheads} + \text{Opening Work in Progress} - \text{Closing Work in Progress} $$

Opening work in progress is **added** because it was partly completed at the beginning of the year.

Closing work in progress is **subtracted** because it has not yet been completed.

### Important point

**Finished goods do not appear in the manufacturing account.**

Finished goods are dealt with in the statement of profit or loss.
