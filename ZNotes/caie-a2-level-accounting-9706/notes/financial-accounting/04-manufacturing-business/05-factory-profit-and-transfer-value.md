## **Factory Profit and transfer value**

### Factory profit

A manufacturing business may add a **factory profit** to the cost of production.

The factory profit is usually calculated as a percentage of the cost of production.

$$ \text{Factory Profit} = \text{Cost of Production} \times \text{Factory Profit Rate} $$

For example, if the cost of production is $200,000 and factory profit is 20%:

$$ \\$200,000 \\times 20\\% = \\$40,000 $$

### Transfer value

Transfer value is the value at which manufactured goods are transferred from the factory to the trading section.

$$ \text{Transfer Value} = \text{Cost of Production} + \text{Factory Profit} $$
