## Revaluation Account

### What is a Revaluation Account?

A **temporary account** used to record changes in the value of **assets and liabilities** when there is a change in the partnership.

Used when the partnership **continues to operate**, e.g.:

* Admission of a new partner

* Retirement of a partner

* Change in profit-sharing ratio

### Easy rule

**Assets:**

* Increase then **Credit**

* Decrease then **Debit**

**Liabilities:**

* Increase then **Debit**

* Decrease then **Credit**

### **Profit / Loss**

If the **credit side is greater** then **profit** on revaluation

If the **debit side is greater** then **loss** on revaluation

The profit/loss is transferred to the partners' **capital accounts using the old profit-sharing ratio**.
