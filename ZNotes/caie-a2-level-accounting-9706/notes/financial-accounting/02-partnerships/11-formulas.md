# Formula

$$\text{Average profit} = \frac{\text{Total profits}}{\text{Number of years}}$$
$$\text{Goodwill} = \text{Average profit} \times \text{Years' purchase}$$

$$\text{Revaluation change} = \text{Revalued value} - \text{Book value}$$

$$\text{Revaluation share} = \text{Profit/Loss} \times \text{Old ratio}$$

$$\text{Goodwill raised share} = \text{Goodwill} \times \text{Old ratio}$$

$$\text{Goodwill eliminated share} = \text{Goodwill} \times \text{New ratio}$$

$$\text{Time-apportioned amount} = \text{Full-year amount} \times \frac{\text{Months in period}}{12}$$

$$\text{Interest on capital} = \text{Capital} \times \text{Rate} \times \frac{\text{Months}}{12}$$

$$\text{Realisation share} = \text{Profit/Loss on realisation} \times \text{Profit-sharing ratio}$$
