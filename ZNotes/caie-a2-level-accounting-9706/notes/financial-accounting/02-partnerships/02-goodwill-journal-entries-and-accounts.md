## **Goodwill Journal Entries And Accounts**

### **1. Creating Goodwill (When goodwill is raised/Journal Entry)**

When goodwill is created in partnership accounts:

**Dr -  Goodwill Account**
**Cr  - Partners' Capital Accounts (in old profit-sharing ratio)**

**Reason:**

* Goodwill is an asset hence debit

* Partners gain from goodwill hence credit their capital accounts

Example:

Goodwill valued at $20 000, partners share profits 3:2:

### **2. Removing Goodwill (Writing off goodwill/Journal Entry)**

When goodwill is removed:

**Dr - Partners' Capital Accounts (in new profit-sharing ratio)**
**Cr - Goodwill Account**

**Reason:**

* Goodwill is no longer an asset hence credit

* Partners bear the loss hence debit

Example:

Goodwill = $20 000
