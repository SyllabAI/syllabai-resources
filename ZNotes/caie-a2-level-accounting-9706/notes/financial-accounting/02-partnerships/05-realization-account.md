## Realisation Account

### What is a Realisation Account?

A **temporary account** used when a partnership is **dissolved**.

It is used to:

* record the disposal of assets

* record the settlement of liabilities

* calculate the **profit or loss on realisation**

> **Revaluation = partnership continues**
>
> **Realisation = partnership closes**

### Main rules

**Assets** are on the **Debit** side

**Assets sold** are on the **Credit** side

**Liabilities** are on the **Credit** side

**Liabilities paid → Debit** side

**Expenses** are on the **Debit** side

**Income** are on the **Credit** side

### Bank

**Bank balance and bank overdraft are NOT entered in the Realisation Account.**

The bank account records the actual cash received and paid during the dissolution.

### Profit and Loss

if **Credit side is greater than the Debit side = Profit on realisation**

if **Debit side is greater than the Credit side = Loss on realisation**

The profit/loss is transferred to the partners' **capital accounts in their profit-sharing ratio**.
