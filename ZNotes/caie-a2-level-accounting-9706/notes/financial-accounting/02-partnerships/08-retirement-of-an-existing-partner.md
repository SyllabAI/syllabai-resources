## Retirement of an existing partner

### An existing partner leaves the partnership while the business continues.

### Revaluation

* Prepare a **Revaluation Account**

* Profit/loss → **OLD ratio**

* The retiring partner is included

### Goodwill

**Goodwill raised:**

* All existing partners, including the retiring partner

* **OLD ratio**

* Credit capital accounts

**Goodwill written off:**

* Remaining partners only

* **NEW ratio**

* Debit capital accounts

### Retiring Partner's Capital Account

Transfer their current account balance to the capital account.

* Credit current account balance goes to Credit capital

* Debit current account balance goes to Debit capital

**If the partner takes an asset** then we Debit capital account

**If the partner has a loan** then we Credit capital account

### Settlement

**Paid immediately:**

Debit - Retiring partner's capital
Credit - Bank

**Paid later:**

Debit - Retiring partner's capital
Credit - Partner's loan account

### Key points

**Revaluation → OLD ratio**

**Goodwill raised → OLD ratio**

**Goodwill written off → NEW ratio, remaining partners**

**Retiring partner → receives amount owed**
