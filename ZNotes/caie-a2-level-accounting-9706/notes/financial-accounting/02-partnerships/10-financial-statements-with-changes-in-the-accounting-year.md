## Financial Statements with changes in the accounting year

When the **partnership agreement changes during the accounting year**.

A change may affect:

* Profit-sharing ratio

* Partners' salaries

* Interest on capital

* Capital account balances

### Split the Year

The year is divided into **two periods** using the date of the change.

**Before change use Old terms**

**After change use New terms**

The Statement of Profit or Loss and **Appropriation Account** usually have two columns, one for each period.

### Apportioning profit

### If profit for each period is given

Use the figures given.

### If only the full-year profit is given

Apportion using **time**.

**Profit for period= Full-year profit ×  months in period / 12** ​

### Preparing for the financial statements

### Step 1

Identify:

* Date of change

* Old and new profit-sharing ratios

* Salaries

* Interest rates

### Step 2

Calculate the profit for each period.

### Step 3

Adjust the **capital accounts** at the date of change.

Include:

* Revaluation

* Goodwill

### Step 4

Prepare the **two-column Appropriation Account**.

Apportion:

* Partners' salaries

* Interest on capital

* Profit/loss

### Step 5

Update the **current accounts**.

Include amounts from **both periods**.

### Step 6

Prepare the **Statement of Financial Position** using the updated capital and current account balances.
