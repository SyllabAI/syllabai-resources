## Changes in partner's profit - sharing ratio

When existing partners change the ratio in which they share profits and losses.

### **Revaluation**

* Prepare a **Revaluation Account**

* Profit/loss on revaluation we use **OLD ratio**

* In case of Profit then Credit partners’ capital accounts

* In case of Loss then Debit partners’ capital accounts

### **Goodwill**

**Goodwill raised:**

* Use **OLD ratio**

* Credit partners’ capital accounts

**Goodwill written off:**

* Use **NEW ratio**

* Debit partners’ capital accounts

### **Capital Accounts**

* Record revaluation profit/loss

* Record goodwill adjustments

* Balance the capital accounts

### Key points to remember:  

**In Revaluation we use Old ratio**

**In Goodwill raised we use Old ratio**

**In Goodwill written off we use New ratio**
