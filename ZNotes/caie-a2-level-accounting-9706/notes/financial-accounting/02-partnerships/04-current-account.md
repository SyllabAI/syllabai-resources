## Current Account

The **current account** keeps track of the partner's **regular financial dealings with the business** during the year. It records things such as their share of profit, drawings, salary, commission, interest on capital and interest on drawings.

### Format

**Credit side — amounts the partner receives:**

* Share of **profit**

* **Interest on capital**

* Partner's **salary**

* Partner's **commission**

* Other amounts credited to the partner

**Debit side — amounts the partner gives/takes:**

* **Drawings**

* Interest on **drawings**

* Share of **loss**

* Other amounts charged to the partner

Rule:

> **Credit = increases what the business owes the partner**
>
> **Debit = decreases what the business owes the partner**

### Example

Ari and Briar are partners.

* Profit = **Rs.10,000**

* They share profits **equally**

* Ari takes **Rs.2,000 drawings**

So Ari gets:

**Profit share:** Rs.5,000 → **Credit**

**Drawings:** Rs.2,000 → **Debit**
