## Dissolution of a partnership

A partnership is dissolved when it **formally ceases to trade**.

### Realisation Account

* Used when the partnership is **closing**

* Used to calculate the **profit/loss on realisation**

* Use a **Realisation Account**, not a Revaluation Account

### Realisation

**Assets**

* Enter book value to the **Debit side**

* Sale proceeds to the **Credit side** 

* Asset taken by partner to the **Credit side**

**Liabilities**

* Enter liability to the **Credit side**

* Amount paid to the  **Debit side**

**Other items**

* Realisation expenses is **Debitted**

* Income/discount received is **Creditted**

**Bank balance / bank overdraft is NOT entered into the Realisation Account.**

### Profit / Loss

Profit/loss on realisation → partners' capital accounts

Use the **profit-sharing ratio**.

### Capital Accounts

1. ### Transfer profit/loss on realisation

2. Transfer current account balances

3. Record assets taken by partners

4. Balance the capital accounts

5. Pay the amount owed to partners from **Bank**

### Remember

**Partnership continues → Revaluation**

**Partnership closes → Realisation**

**Realisation profit/loss → Profit-sharing ratio**
