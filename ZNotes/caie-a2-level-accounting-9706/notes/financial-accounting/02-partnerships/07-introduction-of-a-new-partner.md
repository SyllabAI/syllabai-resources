
## Introduction of a new partner

A new partner joins an existing partnership.

### **Revaluation**

* Prepare a **Revaluation Account**

* Profit/loss then **OLD ratio**

* Only the **existing partners** share the revaluation profit/loss

### Goodwill

**Goodwill raised:**

* Existing partners then  **OLD ratio**

* Credit capital accounts

**Goodwill written off:**

* All partners, including the new partner then **NEW ratio**

* Debit capital accounts

### Capital

* New partner introduces **capital**

* Add the capital introduced to the new partner's capital account

* Balance the capital accounts
