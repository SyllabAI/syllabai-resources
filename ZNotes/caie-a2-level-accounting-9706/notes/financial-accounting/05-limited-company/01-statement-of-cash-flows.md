# **Statement of cash flows**

A statement of cash flows shows the movement of cash and cash equivalents during the year.

Cash flows are divided into:

* Operating activities

* Investing activities

* Financing activities

Use cash from when the net cash flow is positive and cash used in when it is negative.

## **Uses of a statement of cash flows**

It is used to:

* Show money entering and leaving the business.

* Explain why profit is different from the increase in cash.

* Show sources of finance.

* Help directors assess liquidity.

* Allow stakeholders to assess how cash has been used.

* Compare cash flows with previous years or other businesses.
