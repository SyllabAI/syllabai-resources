# Stewardship and Auditing of Limited Companies

## Stewardship and Directors

In a limited company, **ownership and control are separated**.

* Shareholders are the owners.

* Directors control and manage the company.

* Directors are accountable to shareholders.

* Directors act as **stewards** of the resources entrusted to them.

* They should manage the company's assets in the best interests of shareholders.

* They should act professionally, show integrity and avoid conflicts of interest.

## Responsibilities of Directors

Directors must:

* Sign the financial statements.

* Ensure the financial statements give a **true and fair view**.

* Take personal legal responsibility for their accuracy.

* Present financial results to shareholders at the AGM.

* Provide a directors' report alongside the financial statements.

The directors' report can include information such as directors' names and roles, proposed dividends, political donations and future business plans.

## Auditors

An **audit** is an examination of a company's financial statements.

An **auditor** is a financial professional who carries out the audit.

## Responsibilities of an Auditor

**Internal auditor**

* Examines accounts and systems.

* Identifies risks and suggests improvements.

* Evaluates the effectiveness of control systems.

* Checks compliance with legal requirements and internal policies.

**External auditor**

* Examines the company's books and accounts.

* Checks compliance with accounting standards and legal requirements.

* Verifies the existence and value of assets and liabilities.

* Produces an audit report.

* Gives an opinion on whether the financial statements give a true and fair view.

## Qualified vs Unqualified Audit Report

An **unqualified audit report** is issued when the auditor is satisfied that the financial statements give a true and fair view.

This generally means:

* They comply with international accounting standards.

* They are free from material errors and omissions.

* They comply with legal requirements.

A **qualified audit report** is issued when the auditor has reservations about the financial statements.

Possible reasons include:

* Limitation of scope

* Incorrect accounting treatment

* Going concern doubts

* Material misstatements

A qualified report may reduce investor and lender confidence, affect the share price and make it harder for the business to obtain finance.

## True and Fair View

A true and fair view means that the financial statements:

* Are free from material misstatements and errors.

* Faithfully represent the financial performance and financial position of the business.

* Follow applicable accounting standards and legal requirements.

It is important because it:

* Gives shareholders confidence.

* Helps lenders and creditors make informed decisions.

* Helps potential investors assess the business.

* Increases the credibility and transparency of the company.
