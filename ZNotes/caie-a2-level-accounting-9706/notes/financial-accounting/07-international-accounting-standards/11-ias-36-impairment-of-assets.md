
## IAS 36 Impairment of Assets

**Value in use** is the present value of the future cash flows expected from an asset.

**Recoverable amount** is the higher of:

Fair value less costs to sell

An impairment loss occurs when:

Carrying value\>Recoverable amount

Impairment loss=Carrying value−Recoverable amount

Causes of impairment include:

* Physical damage

* Obsolescence

* Decline in market value

The impairment loss is an **expense in the statement of profit or loss**, and the asset's carrying value is reduced.
