## IAS 2 Inventories

Inventories must be valued at the **lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV)**.

NRV=Estimated selling price−Estimated selling costs\\text\{NRV\} = \\text\{Estimated selling price\} - \\text\{Estimated selling costs\}

Cost includes the purchase price and costs incurred to bring inventory to its present location and condition.

IAS 2 permits:

* FIFO

* AVCO

**LIFO is not permitted.**

The rule follows the prudence concept so that assets and profits are not overstated.
