## IAS 10 Events After the Reporting Period

There is a period between the end of the reporting period and the date the financial statements are authorised.

There are two types of events:

**Adjusting events**

The condition existed at the end of the reporting period.

The financial statements **are adjusted**.

Examples:

* A customer becomes insolvent because of financial difficulties that existed at year end.

* Settlement of a court case that was already pending at year end.

* Discovery of errors in the financial statements.

**Non adjusting events**

The condition arose after the reporting period.

The financial statements **are not adjusted**.

Examples:

* A major fire after year end.

* A major restructuring announced after year end.

* Dividends declared after the reporting period.

* A fall in investment values after year end.
