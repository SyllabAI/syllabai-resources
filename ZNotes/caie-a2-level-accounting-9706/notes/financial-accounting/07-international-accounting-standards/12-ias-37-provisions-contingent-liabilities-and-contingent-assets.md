## IAS 37 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets

Likelihood terms:

* **Virtually certain:** very high chance

* **Probable:** more than 50% chance

* **Possible:** less than 50% chance

* **Remote:** very little chance

A **provision** is an amount recognised for a probable future expense where the exact amount or timing is uncertain.

A provision is recognised when:

* There is a present obligation from a past event.

* It is probable that the obligation will have to be settled.

* A reliable estimate can be made.

A **contingent liability** is a possible financial obligation that depends on an uncertain future event, such as a lawsuit.

A **contingent asset** is a potential financial gain depending on an uncertain future event.

Contingent liabilities are not included in the financial statements but are disclosed in the notes.

Contingent assets are only included when they are **virtually certain** and are disclosed when they are **probable**.
