## IAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements

IAS 1 sets out the basis for presenting financial statements and helps ensure **comparability** between different periods and businesses.

A complete set of financial statements includes:

* Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income

* Statement of changes in equity

* Statement of financial position

* Statement of cash flows

* Notes to the financial statements

* Schedule of non current assets

Key principles include:

* **Going concern:** assumes the business will continue operating for the foreseeable future.

* **Accrual basis:** transactions are recognised when they occur, not when cash is received or paid.

* **Consistency:** presentation and classification should remain consistent between periods.

* **Materiality:** omissions or misstatements are material if they could influence users' decisions.

* **Offsetting:** assets and liabilities, or income and expenses, should not be offset unless another standard permits it.
