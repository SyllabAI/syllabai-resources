## IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment

Non current assets are initially measured at **cost**.

Cost includes the purchase price and directly attributable costs needed to bring the asset into use, such as delivery costs.

Two models can be used:

**Cost model**

# Carrying value=Cost−Accumulated depreciation−Accumulated impairment losses

# **Revaluation model**

# Carrying value=Fair value at revaluation date−Subsequent depreciation−Impairment losses

Depreciation:

* Is allocated over the asset's useful life.

* Has its useful life and residual value reviewed at least annually.

* Does not apply to land.

* Begins when the asset is available for use.

On disposal, the carrying value is compared with the proceeds to calculate the **profit or loss on disposal**.
