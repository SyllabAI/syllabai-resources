# International Accounting Standards

International Accounting Standards are rules used for **financial reporting**.

The IAS required for the syllabus are:

* IAS 1

* IAS 2

* IAS 7

* IAS 8

* IAS 10

* IAS 16

* IAS 36

* IAS 37

* IAS 38

When referring to a standard in an exam, **name the relevant IAS**.
