
## IAS 8 Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors

IAS 8 covers three areas:

**Accounting policies**

Policies should be applied consistently. Changes are generally applied **retrospectively**.

**Changes in accounting estimates**

Changes are applied **prospectively**, meaning from the point of change onwards.

**Prior period errors**

Errors are corrected **retrospectively**, including adjusting opening retained earnings where necessary.
