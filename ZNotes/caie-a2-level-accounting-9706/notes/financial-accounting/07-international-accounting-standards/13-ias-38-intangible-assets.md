## IAS 38 Intangible Assets

An intangible asset is an **identifiable non monetary asset without physical substance**.

Examples include:

* Goodwill

* Research and development

* Patents

* Trademarks

* Copyright

An intangible asset is recognised when it is identifiable, the business controls it and future economic benefits are expected.

Intangible assets are:

* Purchased or internally generated.

* Only shown in the financial statements if they are purchased.

* Amortised if they have a finite useful life.

* Subject to an annual impairment review if they have an indefinite life.

Research costs are normally treated as **revenue expenditure**.

Development costs may be treated as **capital expenditure** and recognised as an intangible asset.
