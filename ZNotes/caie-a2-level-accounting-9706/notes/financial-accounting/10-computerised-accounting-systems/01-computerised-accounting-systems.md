
# Computerised accounting systems

## Transferring accounts to a computerised accounting system

The transfer process occurs when a business moves from a **manual accounting system to a computerised accounting system**. All existing account balances and records must be transferred **accurately and completely**.

### Stage 1: Planning

* Choose an appropriate accounting system for the needs of the business.

* Agree a **changeover date**.

* Transactions up to and including this date are recorded in the manual system.

* Transactions after this date are recorded in the computerised system.

* Appoint a skilled person to oversee the transfer, such as an IT specialist or accountant experienced with computerised systems.

### Stage 2: Setting up the chart of accounts

A **chart of accounts** is the list of all nominal ledger account codes in the new system.

* Recreate every account from the manual system with a corresponding code.

* No account should be omitted, otherwise balances may be lost.

* The structure should reflect the categories used in the financial statements, such as non current assets, current assets, liabilities, income and expenses.

### Stage 3: Entering opening balances

* Use the **trial balance from the last day of the manual system** as the source of the opening balances.

* Enter each balance into the corresponding account in the new system.

* Opening balances should only be entered by an authorised member of staff.

### Stage 4: Verifying opening balances

* Extract a trial balance from the new system.

* It should agree with the trial balance from the manual system on the same date.

* Investigate and correct any discrepancies before recording live transactions.

* An independent person should check the entries.

### Stage 5: Parallel running

Both systems are operated **simultaneously for a trial period**.

Transactions are recorded in both systems and the outputs are compared, such as financial statements and control accounts.

If the results agree, the business can be confident that the new system is working correctly. Once satisfied, the manual system can be discontinued.
