## Interview (साक्षात्कार लेखन)

* Usually for 16 marks

* You have the freedom to write any question that fits the topic they have asked

* Write atleast 3 questions

* The answers should be elaborate with good command over the language

* The interview should progress slowly and smoothly, intro --> experience --> future

* Checklist

  1. the tone should be formal

  2. the questions should be linked to each (needs to look like a conversation)

  3. the questions are a little open ended (leaves you with more space to form the answer)
