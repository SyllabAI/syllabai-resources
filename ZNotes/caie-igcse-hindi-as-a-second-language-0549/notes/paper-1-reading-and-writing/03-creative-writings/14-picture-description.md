## Picture Description (चित्र वर्णन)

* Could be for 8 or 16 marks

* A picture would be given, which you’ll have to describe

* First, start with what is shown in the image

* Then, implicitly explain the image

  for example, if a picture shows people celebrating a festival, you could explain the importance of the festival and how it brings people together

* No strict format as such, just start with what you think the topic of the picture is

* Title is not required

* Checklist

  1. the composition adds onto the picture, and does not go off-topic

  2. tone: informative (and projects your thoughts about the picture in a respectful way)

  3. the composition is properly concluded without any abrupt endings
