## Report Writing (वृतांत लेखन)

* Usually for 8 marks

* Topics are usually about an incident

  (examples: school events, accidents, etc.)

* Title required

* Indentation for the first paragraph is not necessary

* Write atleast 3 paragraphs

* Describe the incident according to the timelines of the events that took place in it

* Checklist

  1. the tone is informative

  2. the writing is in past tense

  3. the composition does not contain any opinions and strictly sticks to facts

  (the only exception here that can be made is if the opinion is collective, for example, the students enjoyed the Independence Day event)
