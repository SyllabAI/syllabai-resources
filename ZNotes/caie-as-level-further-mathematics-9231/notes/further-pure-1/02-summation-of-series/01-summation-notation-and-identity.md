# Summation of series

## Introduction

A summation is the addition of a sequence of numbers, usually denoted by the greek letter sigma $(\sum)$.

$$

\sum_{r ~ = ~ a}^{n} f(r) 
$$

Such that

$$

\sum_{r ~ = ~ a}^{n} f(r) = f(a) + f(a + 1) + f(a + 2) + \cdots + f(n)
$$

Where:

* $r = a$ denotes that the sum starts from $a$
* $n$ denotes that the sum shall go till the $n^{th}$ term

This sum would be read as the “summation of f(r) from r = a till n”




### **General properties and results**

$$

\text{[k denotes a constant here]}
$$

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} k = k_1 + k_2 + \cdots + k_n = kn 
$$

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} kf(r) = kf(1) + kf(2) + \cdots + kf(n) = k \sum_{r \ =  \ 1}^{n} f(r) 
$$

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ a}^{n} f(r) + g(r) = \sum_{r \ = \  a}^{n} f(r) \ + \ \sum_{r  \ = \ a}^{n} g(r)
$$

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ a}^{n} f(r) = \sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} f(r) \ - \ \sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{a \ - \ 1} f(r)
$$



### **Standard summations**

Sum of **natural numbers**:

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} r = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
$$

Sum of **squares of natural numbers**:

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} r^2 = \frac{n(n + 1)(2n + 1)}{6}
$$

Sum of **cubes** **of natural numbers**:

$$

\sum_{r \ = \ 1}^{n} r^3 = \frac{n^2(n + 1)^2}{4}
$$
