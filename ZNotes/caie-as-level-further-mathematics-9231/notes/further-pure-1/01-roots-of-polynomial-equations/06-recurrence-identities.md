## Recurrence Identities

Remembering these **identities** can help you solve questions swiftly:

* $S_1 = \sum \alpha$
* $S_2 = \alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\sum \alpha)^2 - 2(\sum \alpha\beta)$
  * If $S_2 < 0$, both roots are complex.
* $S_3 = \alpha^3 + \beta^3 = (\sum \alpha)^3 - 3(\sum \alpha)(\sum \alpha\beta)$
* $S_{-1} = \frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} = \frac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta} = \frac{\sum \alpha}{\sum \alpha\beta}$
