## Cubics

Let us now consider a **cubic** with roots, $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$, such that:

$$

ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d = a(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)(x - \gamma)
$$

**Dividing** both sides by **a** and then **expanding** the right hand side gives us:

$$

x^3 + \frac{bx^2}{a} + \frac{cx}{a} + \frac{d}{a} = x^3 - (\alpha + \beta + \gamma)x^2 + (\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha)x - (\alpha\beta\gamma)
$$

Upon **comparing coefficients** we can see that:

$$

\text{Sum of roots taken one at a time} = \sum \alpha = \alpha + \beta + \gamma = \frac{-b}{a}
$$

$$

\text{Sum of roots taken two at a time} = \sum \alpha\beta = \alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \frac{c}{a}
$$

$$

\text{Product of roots} = \sum \alpha\beta\gamma = \alpha\beta\gamma = \frac{-d}{a}
$$

Thus, a **cubic** can also be written as:

$$

x^3 - \left(\sum \alpha \right)x^2 + \left(\sum \alpha\beta \right)x - \left(\sum \alpha\beta\gamma \right)
$$
