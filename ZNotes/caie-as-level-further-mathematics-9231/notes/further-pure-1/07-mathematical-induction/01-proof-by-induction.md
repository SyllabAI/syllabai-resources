# Mathematical Induction

## Proof by Induction
Steps in proving any expression using induction:

$P_n$ represents the original expression.
- Prove $P_n$ is true for any variable, such as $P_1$.
- Let $P_k$ be the statement, for some positive value $k$, for which the expression is true.
- Show that the statement leads to the next value, $k + 1$.
- Complete the proof by saying "Since $P_n$ is true for $P_1$ and $P_k$ implies $P_{k + 1}$, by mathematical induction the statement is true for all $n \geq 0$"
