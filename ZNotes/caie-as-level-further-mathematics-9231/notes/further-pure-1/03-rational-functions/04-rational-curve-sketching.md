## Rational Curve Sketching

* While sketching a rational curve, make sure to follow these steps:
  * Find the $x$ and $y$ intercepts (You need to note these points on the sketch)
  * Find any stationary points (Local maxima, minima and turning points)
  * Find the Vertical, Horizontal and Oblique asymptotes (Drawn with a dotted line on the sketch)
  * Examine the behaviour near the asymptotes
  * Examine the behaviour as $x \rightarrow \pm \ \infty$
* It is important to find and use all these points on your graph to draw the most **accurate** sketch.
