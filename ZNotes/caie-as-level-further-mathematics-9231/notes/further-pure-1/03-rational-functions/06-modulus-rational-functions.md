## Modulus Rational Functions

The graph of $f(x)$ can help us sketch the graphs of |$f(x)$| and $f($|$x$|$)$.

$$

\phantom{invis text}
$$

Graph of |$f(x)$|

* Using the graph of $f(x)$, reflect the part where **$y < 0$** in the $x$ axis.
  * All the points on the curve, where $y < 0$, get reflected to $(x,$ |$y$|). (Including any stationary points)
  * The oblique asymptote gets reflect in the $x$ axis, resulting in 2 asymptotes $y = mx + c$ and $y = -mx - c$.
  * Nothing happens to the horizontal and vertical asymptote.

$$

\phantom{invis text}
$$

Let us use the sketch of the rational function

$$

f(x) = \frac{x^2}{x - 1}
$$

 ![](../../../assets/87cc3cd3b096-6688e5f65b0e2c153a3c320c716ed6112f964d2db18486da.43)

* The red curve represents our function, f(x).
  * The green line represents the vertical asymptote $x = 1$.
  * The purple line represents the oblique asymptote $y = x + 1$.

$$

\phantom{invis text}
$$

We first note that the oblique asymptote becomes $y =$ |$x + 1$|. Now we can reflect the part of the curve which is below $y < 0$ in the $x$ axis.

$$

|f(x)| = |\frac{x^2}{x - 1}|
$$

 ![](../../../assets/d50ce5a1d4cb-6688e5f65b0e2c153a3c320c95a4290e6dd62ed869789cdd.45)


---

Graph of $f($|$x$|$)$

* Using the graph of $f(x)$, reflect the part where $x < 0$ in the $y$ axis.
  * The vertical asymptote gets reflected in the $y$ axis resulting in 2 asymptotes, $x = \pm \ a$.
  * The oblique asymptote gets reflected in the $y$ axis resulting in 2 asymptotes, $y = +mx + c$ and $y = -mx + c$.
  * Nothing happens to the horizontal asymptote.
* The easiest way to sketch the graph of  $f($|$x$|$)$ is to sketch the graph of $f(x)$ where $x > 0$, and then use $x = 0$ as a line of symmetry to reflect it in the $y$ axis.

Let us use the sketch of the rational function

$$

f(x) = \frac{x^2 + 1}{x - 1}
$$

 ![](../../../assets/6394966d8963-6688e5f65b0e2c153a3c320c68ad192b115179cfc36f03a3.46)

* The red curve represents the function $f(x)$
  * The green line represents the vertical asymptote $x = 1$
  * The purple line represents the oblique asymptote $y = x + 1$

We first note that the oblique asymptote is now $y = x + 1$ and $y = -x + 1$, and there is another vertical asymptote $x = -1$. Now we can reflect the curve, where $x > 0$, in the y-axis.

$$

f(|x|) = \frac{|x|^2 + 1}{|x| - 1}
$$
