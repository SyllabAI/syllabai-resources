## Range

The range is the set of $y$ values or outputs; a function is defined to produce.

Assume a rational function, where $x \in \R$, exists such that

$$

y = \frac{ax^2 + bx + c}{dx^2 + ex + f}
$$

We can re-write this as

$$

(dy)x^2 + (ey)x + fy = ax^2 + bx + c
$$

$$

(dy - a)x^2 + (ey - b)x + (fy - c) = 0
$$

Now we have a quadratic equation.

* As $x \in \R$, the range of $y$ values will also have to be real.
* Solving for $b^2 - 4ac$ $\geq 0$ will give us the range of values $y$ **can** take.
* Solving for $b^2 - 4ac < 0$ will give us the range of values $y$ **cannot** take.
