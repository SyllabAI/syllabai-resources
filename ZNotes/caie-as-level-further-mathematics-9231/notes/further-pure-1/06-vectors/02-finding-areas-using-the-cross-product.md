## Finding areas using the cross product
### **Area of a parallelogram**
![Screenshot 2024-10-17 at 11.23.32 AM.png](../../../assets/12307002e869-66c2349ba2f5b93cd4300164f5593804c2e341fc63334141.23)

Referencing the image above, it is clear that
$$
h = \text{|}A\text{|}sin(\theta)
$$
Now we can use the formula for the area of a parallelogram to get
$$
\text{Area}~ = \text{|}A\text{|}\text{|}B\text{|}sin(\theta)
$$
But $a \times b = \text{|}A\text{|}\text{|}B\text{|}sin(\theta)$, so the area of a parallelogram is the magnitude of the cross product of the two vectors.
$$
\text{Area}~ = \text{|}A \times B \text{|} = \text{|}A\text{|}\text{|}B\text{|}sin(\theta)
$$

### **Area of a triangle**
Using the same logic, we can deduce the area of a triangle to be
$$
\text{Area}~ = \frac{\text{|}A \times B \text{|}}{2} = \frac{\text{|}A\text{|}\text{|}B\text{|}sin(\theta)}{2}
$$
