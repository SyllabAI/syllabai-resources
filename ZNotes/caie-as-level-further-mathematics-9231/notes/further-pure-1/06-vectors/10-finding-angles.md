## Finding angles

### **Angle between a line and a plane**
- We can use the dot product to first find the angle between the normal vector to the plane and the line
$$
a \cdot \hat{n} = \text{|}a\text{|}\text{|}\hat{n}\text{|}cos(\theta)
$$
$$
cos(\theta) = \frac{a \cdot \hat{n}}{\text{|}a\text{|}\text{|}\hat{n}\text{|}}
$$

Since this angle is created with the line and the normal vector, we must **subtract** it from 90 to get the angle the line makes with the plane.

$$
90^\circ - \theta 
$$

### **Angle between two planes**

- The angle between two planes is the angles made by their normal vectors
Using the dot product
$$
cos(\theta) = \frac{n_1 \cdot n_2}{\text{|}n_1\text{|}\text{|}n_2\text{|}}
$$
To find the acute angle between two planes:
$$
180^\circ - \theta
$$
