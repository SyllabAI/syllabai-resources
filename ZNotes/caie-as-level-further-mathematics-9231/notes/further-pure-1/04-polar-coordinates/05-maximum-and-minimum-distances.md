## Maximum and Minimum Distances

### **Greatest Distance from the Origin or Pole**

* Differentiate $r = f(\theta)$ and equate it to 0
  * $\frac{dr}{d\theta} = 0$




### **Furthest distance from the line $\theta = 0$**

* Find a parametric equation in terms of $y$
  * Differentiate $y = f(\theta)sin(\theta)$ and equate it to 0
  * $\frac{dy}{d\theta} = 0$
  * Substitute this value of $\theta$ into $y = f(\theta)sin(\theta)$ to get the minimum or maximum $y$ value.




### **Furthest distance from the line $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$**

* Find a parametric equation in terms of $x$
  * Differentiate $x = f(\theta)cos(\theta)$ and equate it to 0
  * $\frac{dx}{d\theta} = 0$
  * Substitute this value of $\theta$ into $x = f(\theta)cos(\theta)$ to get the minimum or maximum $x$ value.
