# Mensuration 

## 2D Shapes: Perimeters & Areas

**<span class="underline">Question 38:</span>**

The base of a triangle is 9 cm correct to the nearest cm. The area of this triangle is 40 cm<sup>2</sup> correct to the nearest 5 cm<sup>2</sup>. Calculate the upper bound for the perpendicular height of this triangle. (3 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*The area of a triangle is*

$$
A = \frac{1}{2} \times base \times height
$$

*Here we have*

$$
\sim 40 = \frac{1}{2} \times \sim 9 \times h
$$

*For the upper bound on height, we need the area to be as large as possible and the base to be as short as possible, i.e.,*

$$
A = 42.5
$$

$$
b = 8.5
$$

*Hence*

$$
42.5 = \frac{1}{2} \times 8.5 \times h
$$

$$
\mathbf{h = 10}
$$
