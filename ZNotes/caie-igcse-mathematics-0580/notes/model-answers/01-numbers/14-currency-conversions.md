## Currency Conversions

**<span class="underline">Question 14:</span>**

1.  In 2007, a tourist changed 4000 Chinese Yuan into pounds (£) when
    the exchange rate was £1 = 15.2978 Chinese Yuan. Calculate the
    amount he received, giving your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
    (2 marks)

2.  In 2006, the exchange rate was £1 = 15.9128 Chinese Yuan. Calculate
    the percentage decrease in the number of Chinese Yuan for each £1
    from 2006 to 2007. (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

**Part (1):**

*In order to change from Chinese Yuan into pounds, we can do this:*

$${1\ Chinese\ Yuan = £ 0.06537}$$

$${4000\ Chinese\ Yuan = £ 0.06537 \times 4000}$$

$${4000\ Chinese\ Yuan = £ 261.4755}$$

*But we need this to the nearest penny*

$$\mathbf{4000\ Chinese\ Yuan = £ 261.4}$$

**Part (b):**

*To calculate the percentage decrease we need to do the following:*

$$\frac{change\ in\ amount\ of\ chinese\ yuan\ for\ each\ £ 1}{amount\ of\ chinese\ yuan\ for\ each\ £ 1\ in\ 2006} \times 100$$

$$\frac{15.9128 - 15.2978}{15.9128} \times 100 = 3.8648$$

**Hence the percentage decrease is 3.865%**
