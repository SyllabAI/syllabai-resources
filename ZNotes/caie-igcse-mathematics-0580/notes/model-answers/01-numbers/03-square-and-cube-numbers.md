## Square and Cube Numbers

**<span class="underline">Question 2:</span>**

Simplify $\left( 32x^{10} \right)^{\frac{3}{5}}$ (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*Apply the power to everything inside the brackets¸ and use the general
rule that* ${{(a}^{x})}^{y} = \ a^{\text{xy}}$

$$32^{\frac{3}{5}} \times x^{(10 \times \frac{3}{5})}$$

*Note that* $32 = 2^{5}$ *hence* $32^{\frac{1}{5}} = 2$

$$= \left( 32^{\frac{1}{5}} \right)^{3} \times x^{6}$$

$$= 2^{3}x^{6}$$

$$= \mathbf{8}\mathbf{x}^{\mathbf{6}}$$
