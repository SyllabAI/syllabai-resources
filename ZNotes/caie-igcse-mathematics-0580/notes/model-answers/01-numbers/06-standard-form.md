## Standard Form

**<span class="underline">Question 6:</span>**

Write 2.8 × $10^{2}$ as an ordinary number. (1 mark)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*We can write 2.8 x 10<sup>2</sup> as an ordinary number like this: 2.8
x 10<sup>2</sup> simply means 2.8 x 100*

$$\mathbf{2.8 \times 100 = 280}$$
