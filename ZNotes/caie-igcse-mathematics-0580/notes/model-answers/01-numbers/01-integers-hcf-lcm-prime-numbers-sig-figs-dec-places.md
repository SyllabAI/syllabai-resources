# Numbers

## Integers, HCF/LCM, Prime numbers, Sig Figs, Dec Places

**Question 1:**

Find the lowest common multiple (LCM) of 36 and 48. (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*We can do this by writing out all of the multiples of the two numbers.
The multiples of 36 are:*

$$36,\ 72,\ 108,\ 144,\ 180,\ \ldots$$

*The multiples of 48 are*

$$48,\ 96,\ 144,\ 192,\ \ldots$$

*We can see that the lowest common multiple is:*

$$144$$
