## Conversion - Percentages, Fractions & Decimals

**<span class="underline">Question 4:</span>**

Write the recurring decimal 0.32 ̇ (0.3222...) as a fraction. (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*We need to get rid of the recurring decimal by doing the following*

$$100 \times 0.3\dot{2} = 32.\dot{2}$$

$$10 \times 0.3\dot{2} = 3.\dot{2}$$

$$100 \times 0.3\dot{2} - 10 \times 0.3\dot{2} = 90 \times 0.3\dot{2}$$

$$= 32.\dot{2} - 3.\dot{2}$$

$$\rightarrow 90 \times 0.3\dot{2} = 29$$

*Now divide by 90*

$$\mathbf{0.3}\dot{\mathbf{2}}\mathbf{=}\frac{\mathbf{29}}{\mathbf{90}}$$
