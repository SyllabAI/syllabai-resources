## Using a Calculator

**<span class="underline">Question 12:</span>**

Use your calculator to find the value of

$$
\frac{({\cos 30{^\circ})}^{2} - {\ \left( \sin{30{^\circ}} \right)}^{2}\ }{2(\sin 120{^\circ})(\cos{120{^\circ})}}
$$

<div style="text-align: right">(2 marks)</div>

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*By inputting the values into your calculator, you get:*

$$
\frac{({cos(30))}^{2} - \left( \sin\left( 30 \right) \right)^{2}\ }{2(sin(120)(\cos\left( 120 \right))}
$$

$$
= \frac{\frac{3}{4} - \ \frac{1}{4}}{2\  \times \ \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\  \times \frac{- 1}{2}} = \frac{0.5}{\frac{- \sqrt{3}}{2}} = \frac{- \sqrt{3}}{3}
$$

*So, the answer is:*

$$
\mathbf{=}\frac{\mathbf{-}\sqrt{\mathbf{3}}}{\mathbf{3}}
$$
