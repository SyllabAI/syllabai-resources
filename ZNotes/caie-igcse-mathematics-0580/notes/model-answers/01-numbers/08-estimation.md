## Estimation

**<span class="underline">Question 8:</span>**

By writing each number correct to 1 significant figure, estimate the
value of$\ $

$$\frac{\sqrt{3 \cdot 9}\  \times \ 29 \cdot 3}{8 \cdot 9\  - \ 2 \cdot 7}$$

Show all your working. (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*Write all numbers correct to one significant figure:*

$$\frac{\sqrt{4} \times 30}{9 - 3}$$

*Do the calculations.*

$$\frac{2 \times 30}{6} = \frac{60}{6}$$

*We get the final answer:*

$$\mathbf{10}$$
