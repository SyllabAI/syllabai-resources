## Percentages

**<span class="underline">Question 11:</span>**

In 1970 the population of China was 8.2 x $10^{8}$. In 2007 the
population of China was 1.322 x $10^{9}$. Calculate the population in
2007 as a percentage of the population in 1970. (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*The population in 2007 as a percentage of the population in 1970 can be
calculated by:*

$$\frac{Population\ in\ 2007}{Population\ in\ 1970}\  \times 100,$$

*Substituting in the values gives:*

$$\frac{1.322\  \times \ 10^{9}}{8.2\  \times \ 10^{8}}\  \times 100 = 161.\dot{2}195\dot{1}$$

*The answer after rounding is:*

$$\mathbf{161\%}$$
