## Linear equations

**<span class="underline">Question 21:</span>**

Solve the equation. (2 marks)

$$5\ –\ 2x\  = \ 3x\ –\ 19$$

**<center>Solution:</center>**

$$5 - 2x = 3x - 19$$

*Add 2x to both sides of the equality:*

$$5 = 5x - 19$$

*Add 19 to both sides:*

$$5x = 24$$

*Divide both sides by 5:*

$$x = \frac{24}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{x = 4.8}$$
