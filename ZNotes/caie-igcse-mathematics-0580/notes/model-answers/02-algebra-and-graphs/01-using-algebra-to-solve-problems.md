## Using Algebra to Solve Problems

**<span class="underline">Question 17:</span>**

Simplify $16 - 4\left( 3x - 2 \right)^{2}$. (3 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*Simplifying the equation gives:*

$$
16 - 4\left( 3x - 2 \right)^{2} = 16 - 4\left( 9x^{2} - 12x + 4 \right)
$$

$$
= 16 - 36x^{2} + 48x - 16
$$

$$
= - 36x^{2} + 48x
$$

$$
= 12x(4-3x)
$$
