## Kinematics, Distance-Time & Speed-Time Graphs

**<span class="underline">Question 28:</span>**

A car of length 4.3 m is travelling at 105 km/h. It passes over a bridge
of length 36m. Calculate the time, in seconds, it takes to pass over the
bridge **completely**. (3 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*The car is on the bridge when its front is on the bridge and exits when
its rear leaves. The total distance* *to travel is therefore*

$$36 + 4.3 = 40.3m$$

*Converting the speed into m/s, first we multiply by 1000 to get the
units in m/hr*

$$Speed = 105\ kmh^{- 1} \times 1000$$

$$Speed = 105000mh^{- 1}$$

*Next, we have to turn it into m/s*

$$Speed = 105000mh^{- 1} \div \left( 60^{2} \right)$$

$$Speed = \frac{105000}{3600}ms^{- 1}$$

$$Speed = \frac{175}{6}ms^{- 1}$$

*Using the speed distance time relation*

$$speed = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

$$\frac{175}{6}ms^{- 1} = \frac{40.3m}{\text{time}}$$

*Rearrange for time*

$$time = 40.3 \times \frac{6}{175}$$

$$\mathbf{= 1.38\ s\ (2}\mathbf{dp)}$$
