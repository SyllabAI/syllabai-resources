## Simultaneous Linear Equations

**<span class="underline">Question 22:</span>**

Solve the simultaneous equations. (3 marks)

$$
0.4x\ –\ 5y\  = \ 27
$$

$$
2x\  + \ 0.2y\  = \ 9
$$

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*Rearrange one of the equations to get just x or just y on one side:*

$$
0.4x = 27 + 5y
$$

*Substitute this into the second equation:*

$$
2\left( 27 + 5y \right) + 0.2y = 9
$$

*Simplify:*

$$
135 + 25.2y = 9
$$

*Solve:*

$$
25.2y = - 126\mathbf{y = - 5}
$$

*Substitute your answer into one of the equations:*

$$
2x - 1 = 9
$$

*Solve for x:*

$$
2x = 10\mathbf{x = 5}
$$

*So the answer is:*

$$
\mathbf{x = 5,\ y = - 5}
$$
