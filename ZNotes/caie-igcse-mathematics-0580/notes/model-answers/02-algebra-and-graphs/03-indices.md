**<span class="underline">Question 20:</span>**

Simplify
$\frac{5}{8}x^{\frac{3}{2}} \div \frac{1}{2}x^{- \frac{5}{2}}$ (2 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*To simplify the equation, we use the fact that*

$$x^{a} \div x^{b} = \ x^{a - b}$$

*Hence:*

$$\frac{5}{8}x^{\frac{3}{2}} \div \ \frac{1}{2}x^{\frac{- 5}{2}} = \left( \frac{5}{8} \div \frac{1}{2} \right)x^{\frac{3}{2} - \frac{- 5}{2}} = \ \frac{5}{4}x^{4}$$

*So, the answer is:*

$\mathbf{1.25}\mathbf{\text{ x}}^{\mathbf{4}}$
$\mathbf{= 1\ }\frac{\mathbf{1}}{\mathbf{4}}\mathbf{x}^{\mathbf{4}}$
