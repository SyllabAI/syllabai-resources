## Direct & Inverse proportionality

**<span class="underline">Question 27:</span>**

*t* varies inversely as the square root of *u*. *t* = 3 when *u* = 4.
Find *t* when *u* = 49. (3 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*We are told that* $t$ *varies inversely with the square root of*
$u$*. Written mathematically, this says:*

$$
t \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{u}}
$$

*By adding a constant of multiplication, we can make this a proper
equation:*

$$
t = \frac{k}{\sqrt{u}}
$$

*We are given values for t and u, so we can rearrange and solve for k.*

$$
t = \frac{k}{\sqrt{u}}
$$

*Multiply both sides by*$\ \sqrt{u}$

$$
t\sqrt{u} = k
$$

*Plugging in values*

$$
{3\sqrt{4} = k = 3\left( 2 \right) = 6
}{k = 6}
$$

*Hence, our equation becomes*

$$
t = \frac{6}{\sqrt{u}}
$$

*We are asked to find* $t$ *when* $u = 49.$

$$
\mathbf{t =}\frac{\mathbf{6}}{\sqrt{\mathbf{49}}}\mathbf{=}\frac{\mathbf{6}}{\mathbf{7}}
$$
