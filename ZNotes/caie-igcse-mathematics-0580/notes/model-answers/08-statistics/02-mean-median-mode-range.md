## Mean/Median/Mode/Range

**<span class="underline">Question 47:</span>**

Shahruk plays four games of golf. His four scores have a mean of 75, a
mode of 78 and a median of 77. Work out his four scores. (3 marks)

**<center>Solution:</center>**

*The mean is the sum of the four scores divided by 4*

$$\frac{s_1 + s_2 + s_3 + s_4}{4} = 75$$

*The mode is the number that occurs most frequently, i.e. 2 or more of
his scores must be 78. Let*

$$s_3 = s_4 = 78$$

*Where we have and even number of items, the median is the mean of the
middle 2 numbers when put in rank order. Let the middle two scores be
$s_2$ and $s_3$. Thus, we have*

$$\frac{s_2 + 78}{2} = 77$$

$$\rightarrow s_2 = 76$$

*We can now figure out* $s_1$ *from the mean*

$$s_1 + 76 + 78 + 78 = 4 \times 75$$

$$\rightarrow s_1 = 68$$

*Final answer is*

$$\mathbf{68,\ \ 76,\ \ 78,\ \ 78}$$
