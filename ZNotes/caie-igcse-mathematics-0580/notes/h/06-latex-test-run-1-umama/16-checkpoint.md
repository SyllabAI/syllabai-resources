2. ### **Myriapods**

   * Contains multiple segments

   * Each segment contains at least 1 pair of jointed legs

   * 1 pair of antennae

3. ### **Insects**

   * 3 part body (*Head, Thorax, Abdomen*)

   * 3 pairs of jointed legs

   * 2 pairs of wings

   * 1 pair of antennae

   * 1 pair of ***compound eyes*** - hundreds of separate lenses with light sensitive cells beneath

4. ### **Arachnids**

   * 2 part body (*Cephalothorax, Abdomen*)

   * 4 pairs of jointed legs

   * Several pairs of *simple eyes*

   * *Chelicerae* for biting and poisoning prey

5. ### **Crustaceans**

   * 2-part body (*Cephalothorax, Abdomen*)

   * More than 4 pairs of jointed legs

   * Exoskeleton formed from hardened calcium

   * Breathes through gills

   * 2 pairs of antennae

   * 1 pair of *compound eyes*

### **Mammals**

* Fur/hair on skin

* Have a placenta

* Young feed on milk from *mammary glands*

* Externals ears (*pinna*) visible

* *Endothermic* - absorbs heat

* Warm blooded

* Types of mammals - Insectivores, Carnivores, Rodents, Primates

* Example : Humans and monkeys are primates

### **Birds**

* Skin covered in feathers

* Have 2 legs \+ 2 wings instead of forelimbs

* Lay eggs with hard shells on land

* Have beaks

* Endothermic - absorbs heat

* Warm blooded

* Example : Kingfisher, Eagle

### **Reptiles**

* Dry scaly skin

* Fixed scales - they don’t move

* Lay eggs with rubbery shells on land

* Cold blooded

* Example : Lizard, Alligator

### **Amphibians**

* Smooth, moist, slimy skin

* Adults live on land - has lungs

* *Larvae* live on water - has gills

* Lay eggs without shells in water

* Cold blooded

* Example : Frog

### **Fish**

* Wet skin

* Loose scales - move easily

* Lay eggs without shells in water

* Cold blooded

* Breathes with gills

* Do not have roots, stems, or leaves

* Contains green pigment called *chlorophyll* - which all plants have.

* Most species of algae need a microscope to be seen.

* When they occur in large numbers, they can be clearly seen.

* They turn pond water green, form bright green patches on tree trunks, and create slime on rocks.

* Algae form a large part of the plankton that is found in seas and oceans.

### **Liverworts and Mosses**

* Liverworts are small plants that do not have true roots, stems, or leaves.

* They grow in damp places near streams or ponds.

* Mosses have stems and leaves, but no roots.

* They are found growing together in different habitats. Dry walls, soils, etc

* Both liverworts and mosses reproduce by producing *spores*.

* Spores are made in a capsule, which is carried by the air.

* The capsule opens to let out tiny spores - which are spread by air currents.

### **Ferns**

* Has true toots and stems.

* Reproduces by making spores.

* Spores are made in structures called *sporangia* on the underside of the fern’s leaves called *fronds*.

* When the *sporangia* opens, spores are released to the air.

### **Conifers**

* Has roots, woody stem, and needle-like leaves.

* They are evergreen. They lose and replace their leaves all year round.

* Almost all conifers reproduce by making seeds that develop in cones.

* When seeds are ready, the cone opens up and the seeds fall out.

* Each seeds has a ‘wing’ that prevents the seed from falling quickly, and allows the wind to blow it away.

### **Flowering plants**

* Has root, stem, and leaves.

* In some plants the stem is woody.

* All these plants reproduce by flowering and making seeds.
