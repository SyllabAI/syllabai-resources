## Solubility Product K<sub>sp</sub>

* When a sparingly soluble salt is shaken with distilled water and
  left to settle, the water contains aq. ions from the salt in very
  small conc. and a dynamic equilibrium is set up between ions and
  insoluble solid

* Solubility product (K$_\text{(sp)}$): product of conc. of each ion in a saturated solution of sparingly soluble salt at 298K raised to the power of moles in dissociation equation, e.g.

  $\text{Fe}_2 \text{S}_3 \text{(s)} \rightleftharpoons 2 \text{Fe}^{3+} \text{(aq)} + 3 \text{S}^{2-} \text{(aq)}$

  K$_\text{(sp)}$ = $[ \text{Fe}^{3+} ]^{2} [ \text{S}^{2-} ]^{3}$ units: $\text{mol}^{5} \text{dm}^{-15}$

* The conc. of ions is independent of the amount of solid

* K$_\text{(sp)}$ values change only with temperature

* Concentration of ions = solubility of salt

**Calculating solubility product from solubility:**

A saturated solution of magnesium fluoride, MgF$_2$, has a
solubility of $1.22 \times 10^{- 3} \text{ mol } \text{dm}^{-3}$. Calculate the
solubility product of magnesium fluoride.

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

*Write down the equilibrium equation*

$$
\text{Mg} \text{F}_{2} (s) \rightleftharpoons \text{Mg}^{2} \text{(aq)} + 2\text{F(aq)}
$$

*Write down the equilibrium expression*

$$
K_{sp} = \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack \lbrack F^{-} \rbrack^{2}
$$

*Solubility  = conc. of dissolved salt*

*Ion conc. = Solubility /times{coefficient of the ion}*

\therefore \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack = 1 \times{1.22 \times 10^{-3}}
\lbrack F^{-} \rbrack = 2 \times{1.22 \times 10^{-3}} = 2.44\times 10^{-3}

$$
\lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack=\lbrack F^-\rbrack=1.22 \times 10^{-3}
$$

Substitute into equilibrium expression

$$

K_{sp} = (1.22 \times 10^{-3}) \times (2 \times 1.22 \times 10^{-3} )

$$

$$
K_{sp}= 7.26 \times 10^{- 9}mol^{3} dm^{-9}
$$

* To calculate the solubility from the solubility product, let x represent the solubility of the salt (the concentration of one of the ions). Substitute x into the Ksp expression according to the stoichiometry of the dissociation, then solve for x.
