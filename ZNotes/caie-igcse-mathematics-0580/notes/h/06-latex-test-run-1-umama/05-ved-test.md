A saturated solution of magnesium fluoride, MgF$_2$, has a
solubility of $1.22 \times 10^{- 3} \text{ mol } \text{dm}^{-3}$. Calculate the
solubility product of magnesium fluoride.

$$
\text{Solution}
$$

*Write down the equilibrium equation*

$$
\text{Mg} \text{F}_{2} (s) \rightleftharpoons \text{Mg}^{2} \text{(aq)} + 2\text{F(aq)}
$$

*Write down the equilibrium expression*

$$
K_{sp} = \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack \lbrack F^{-} \rbrack^{2}
$$

*Solubility  = conc. of dissolved salt*

*Ion conc. = Solubility \times(coefficient of the ion)*

Molar ratio of \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack : \lbrack F^{-} \rbrack = 1:2

$$

\therefore \lbrack Mg^{2+}\rbrack = 1 \times{1.22 \times 10^{-3}}
$$

$$
\therefore \lbrack F^{-} \rbrack = 2 \times{1.22 \times 10^{-3}} = 2.44\times 10^

$$

$$

K_{sp} = (1.22 \times 10^{-3}) \times (2.44 \times 10^{-3} )

$$

$$
K_{sp}= 7.26 \times 10^{- 9}mol^{3} dm^{-9}
