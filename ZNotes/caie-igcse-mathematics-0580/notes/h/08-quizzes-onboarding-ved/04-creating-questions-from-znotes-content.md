## Creating Questions from ZNotes Content

ZNotes provides high-quality notes that can serve as a strong foundation for quiz questions. Here’s how to create effective questions:

### **Generating Questions via Inbuilt AI**

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/78e3122c211fc91c081dd736.webp =500x)

The "**Create Quiz**" interface features an integrated AI tool. With a single click, the AI analyzes the attached chapter notes to automatically generate a baseline set of **5 easy, 2 medium, and 2 hard questions.**

Contributors must carefully review these AI-generated questions, select the most effective ones, and ensure they adhere to the following quality standards:

* **Accuracy:** The correct answer option must be accurately selected, and all incorrect options (distractors) must be highly plausible.

* **Syllabus Alignment:** Questions must align directly with the official syllabus and the specific assigned chapter.

* **Balanced Coverage:** The quiz must offer a balanced representation of topics without over-emphasizing a single concept.

* **Clarity:** Language must be clear, grammatically correct, and easily understandable for students.

* **Appropriate Difficulty:** Questions must accurately match their designated difficulty tier (adjust if necessary).

* **Comprehensive Explanations:** Every question must include an accurate, clear, and comprehensive explanation.

* **Diverse Question Styles:** Quizzes should feature a mix of various question types, including conceptual questions, application-based scenarios, and graph interpretations where relevant.

> **Note:** Contributors are highly encouraged to draft original questions, embed relevant images or graphs, and mimic official exam paper patterns.

Repeat the generation and drafting process until the chapter quiz contains a minimum requirement of **10 Easy, 4 Medium, and 4 Hard questions** (additional questions are welcome)
