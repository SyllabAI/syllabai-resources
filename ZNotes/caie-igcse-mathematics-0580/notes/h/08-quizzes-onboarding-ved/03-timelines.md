## **Timelines**

* During the onboarding session, you will collaborate with your assigned Notes Developer to establish a Contributor Roadmap. This roadmap defines the Estimated Time for Completion (ETC) for both your quizzes and notes, serving as a shared reference to ensure alignment on progress and deadlines.

  * **Flexibility:** Quizzes may be developed progressively alongside individual chapters or compiled after all chapter notes are completed.

  * **Communication:** Regular communication is vital. The team is consistently available to address questions or concerns, and mandatory weekly check-ins are conducted to monitor progress.

  * **Inactivity Policy:** If you need to temporarily pause your contributions, please notify the team immediately. If a contributor is inactive with no response for 2 weeks, a formal warning email will be issued. Failure to respond within 7 days of the warning will result in the termination of the contribution agreement.
