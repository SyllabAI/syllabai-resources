## Geometrical Terms

### Congruent

* Two shapes that have the identical shape and size

### Scale Factor

* A number that indicates how much a shape has been enlarged or reduced in size

### Vertex

* An angular point where two or more lines or edges meet

### Perpendicular Bisector

*  A line that bisects a line segment in two equal parts and makes a angle of 90 degrees at the point of intersection

### Parallel Lines

*  Two or more lines that are always the same distance apart and never intersect

### **Similar**

* They have the **same shape** but **different sizes**.

* All corresponding **angles are equal**.

* All corresponding **side lengths are in the same ratio** (scale factor)

### **Acute Angle**

* An angle **less than 90°**

### **Obtuse Angle**

* An angle **greater than 90° but less than 180°**

### **Reflex Angle**

* An angle **greater than 180° but less than 360°**

### **Interior Angles**

* The angles **inside** a polygon or shape

### **Exterior Angles**

* The angles **outside** a polygon, formed by extending one side.

* For any polygon, **exterior angles add up to 360°**

### **Right Angle**

* An angle of **90°**.

* Shown with a **small square** in diagrams

### **Bearings**

* A **direction** measured:

  * **Clockwise**

  * **From North**

  * Written as a **three-digit number** (e.g., 045°, 120°, 270°)

### **Perpendicular**

* Two lines are **perpendicular** if they meet at a **right angle (90°)**

### **Parallel**

* Two lines are **parallel** if:

  * They are always the **same distance apart**.

  * They **never meet**, no matter how far extended

### **Line**

* A **straight path** that:

  * Extends **infinitely** in both directions.

  * Has **no thickness**

### **Point**

* A precise **location** in space.

* Has **no size**, no length, and no width
