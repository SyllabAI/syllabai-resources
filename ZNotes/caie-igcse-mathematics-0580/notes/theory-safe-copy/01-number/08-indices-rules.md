## Indices Rules

* When two powers with the same base are multiplied, the values are added

$$
2^3 \times 2^4 = 2^{3+4} = 2^7
$$

* When two powers with the same base are divided, the values are subtracted:

$$
2^5 \div 2^2 = 2^{5-2} = 2^3
$$

* Any number raised to the power of 0 equals 1:

$$
5^0 = 1
$$

* When a power is raised to another power, the values are multiplied:

$$
(3^2)^3 = 3^{2 \times 3} = 3^6
$$

* A negative power means the reciprocal of the base raised to the positive power:

$$
4^{-2} = \frac{1}{4^2} = \frac{1}{16}
$$

* A fractional power indicates a root; the denominator is the root, and the numerator is the power:

$$
8^{\frac{2}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{8^2} = \sqrt[3]{64} = 4
$$

* When two numbers that share the same exponent, they’re able to share their operation and be raised to the same power.

  $
  2^2 \times 3^2 = (2 \times 3)^2
  $

  $2^2 \div 3^2 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2$
