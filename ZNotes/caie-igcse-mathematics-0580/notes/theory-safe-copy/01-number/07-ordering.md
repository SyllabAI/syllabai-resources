## Ordering

* $=$ shows when 2 values are equal
* ≠ when 2 values are not equal
* $>$ when the value on the left-hand side is strictly greater than the right-hand side
* $<$ when the value on the left-hand side is strictly less than the right-hand side
* $⩾$ when the value on the left-hand side is greater than or equal to the right-hand side
* $⩽$ when the value on the left-hand side is less than or equal to the right-hand side
