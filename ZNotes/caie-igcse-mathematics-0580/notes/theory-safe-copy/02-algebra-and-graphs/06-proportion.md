## Proportion

### Direct Proportion

* Two variables, $x$ and $y$, are **directly proportional** if:
  $$
  y \propto x
  $$

* This means:

  $$
  y = kx
  $$

  * where $k$ is a non-zero constant, called the constant of proportionality.

* As $x$ increases, $y$ increases and vice versa.

* The ratio $\frac{y}{x} = k$ remains constant

### Inverse Proportion

* Two variables, $x$ and $y$, are **inversely proportional** if:
  $$
  y \propto \frac{1}{x}
  $$

* This means:

  $$
  y = \frac{k}{x}
  $$

  * where $k$ is a non-zero constant, called the constant of proportionality.

* As $x$ increases, $y$ decreases and vice versa

* The product $xy = k$ remains constant.

### Other Types of Proportion

* Linear Proportion: $y \propto x$ $\rightarrow$ $y=kx$

* Square Proportion: $y \propto x^2$ $\rightarrow$ $y=kx^{2}$

* Square Root Proportion: $y \propto \sqrt{x}$ $\rightarrow$ $y=k \sqrt{x}$

* Cube Proportion: $y \propto x^3$ $\rightarrow$ $y=kx^{3}$

* Cube Root Proportion: $y \propto \sqrt[3]{x}$ $\rightarrow$ $y=k \sqrt[3]{x}$
