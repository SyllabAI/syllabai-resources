## Algebraic Fractions

* An **algebraic fraction** is a fraction that contains **algebraic expressions** in its numerator, denominator, or both

* $\frac{2x+3}{x^2 -4}$

### Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

* Factorize the numerator and denominator completely

* Cancel out common factors

* $\frac{x^2 -9}{x^2 -3x} = \frac{ (x-3) (x+3) }{x(x-3)} = \frac{x+3}{x}$

### Multiplying Algebraic Fractions

* $\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c} {b \times d}$

### Dividing Algebraic Fractions

* $\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$

### Adding and Subtracting Algebraic Fractions

1. Find the **Lowest Common Denominator**

2. Make denominators the same

3. Add or subtract numerators

4. Simplify if possible

   $\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2}{x} = \frac{x^2 +6}{3x}$
