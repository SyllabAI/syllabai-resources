## Volume and Surface Area

### Cuboid

* $\text{Volume} = lwh$

* $\text{Surface Area} = 2(lw + wh + hl)$

  ![](../../../assets/d82acbeb7653-8bd2fd010c5b2b422ce65aea.webp)

### Cylinder

* $\text{Volume} = \pi r^{2}h$

* $\text{Curved Surface Area} = 2\pi rh$

* $\text{Total Surface Area} = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh$

  ![](../../../assets/f55c4c309978-d87988a530954b03b10a20e6.webp)

### Cone

* $\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3}(\pi r^2h)$

* $\text{Curved Surface Area} = \pi r l$

* $\text{Total surface Area} = \pi r^2 + \pi r l$

* ![Surface area of cones](../../../assets/6c8703ad8a35-sa-of-cone-1.gif)

### Sphere

* $\text{Volume} = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$

* $\text{Surface Area} = 4 \pi r^2$

* ![IXL ](https://www.ixl.com/\~media/1/wnAWAgoIYPO0Unzw4-XuKOrJVxOfMyMihAdFXAV-tpnW-gJvXuOC-RG_mSMS4Fo7MMatkNH4cyJG7O6BM5KpvM4WOc0EY_hjhBujbvwtmrg.svg)

### Hemisphere

* $\text{Volume} = \frac{2}{3} \pi r^3$

* $\text{Curved Surface Area} = 2\pi r^2$

* $\text{Surface Area} = 3 \pi r^2$

* ![What is the formula for calculating the volume of a hemisphere? - Formula &  Definition ](../../../assets/21bf508e523c-f-d-14887c007defb4e92961fdecd1e98e43c59d4f4c05a7.png)

### Pyramid

* $\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3} b^2 h$

* $\text{Surface Area} = b^2 + 2bs$

  * b = base length

  * s= slant height

    ![Pyramid Shape](../../../assets/6524bd620c2e-pyramid-01.png)

### Prism

$\text{Volume} = \tfrac{1}{2} b a h$

$\text{Curved Surface Area} = 2(b+h) \times a$

$\text{Total Surface Area} = \text{Curved Surface Area} + 2(b \times h)$
