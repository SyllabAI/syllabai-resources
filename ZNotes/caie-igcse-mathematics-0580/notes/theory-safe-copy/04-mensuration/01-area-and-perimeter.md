# Mensuration

## Area and Perimeter

### Parallelogram

* $\text{Area} = b \times h$ or $ab \sin(\theta)$

* $\text{Perimeter} = 2a + 2b$

![Show that the area of a parallelogram with two dimensions a and b where is  an angle between them is ab sin . ](../../../assets/80b556f966b8-screen-shot-2021-09-15-at-9-56-28-pm320945736687.png)

### Triangle

* $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}b \times h$

* $\text{Perimeter} = b + 2s$

  * b = base , s = slant height

    ![](../../../assets/c4cc8497fff4-7a378d11e6de5832ca0bd2ee.webp)

### Rectangle

* $\text{Area} = l \times b$

* $\text{Perimeter} = 2l + 2b$

  * l= length , b= breadth

    ![](../../../assets/cc2f825cb896-a8725065e4dc7e601805a102.webp)

### Trapezium

* $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\left( a + b \right)h$

* $\text{Perimeter} = a + b + 2s$

  * s = slant height

    ![](../../../assets/2a6dd7e26e6a-573c55975e8b4cbeec706490.webp)

### Circle

* $\text{Area} = \pi r^{2}$

* $\text{Circumference} = 2\pi r$

  ![](../../../assets/a5e968f798bd-73b4f9bd3197a6f612ede5e1.webp)

### Sector

* $\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$

* $\text{Arc Length} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$

  ![](../../../assets/8d94ec52b553-f5168bbb618e921dccab95d4.webp)

### Kite

* $\text{Area} = \frac{d1\times d2}{2}$

* $\text{Perimeter} = AB + BD + DC + CA$
