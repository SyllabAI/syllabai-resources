## Pythagoras Theorem

* To find the Hypotenuse
  * $c^2 = a^{2} + b^{2}$

![](../../../assets/abcf7773f361-eqtnn2k68dnhki3pximage.png)

* To find one of the shorter sides
  * $a^{2} = c^{2} - b^{2}$
  * $b^{2} = c^{2} - a^{2}$
