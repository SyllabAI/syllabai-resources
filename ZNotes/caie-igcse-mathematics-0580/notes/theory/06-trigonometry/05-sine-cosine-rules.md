## Sine & Cosine Rules

### Sine Rule

$$
\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}
$$

### Cosine Rule

* To find the angle given 3 sides:

$$
\cos A = \frac{b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2}}{2bc}
$$

* To find the side given angle and two sides

$$
a^{2} = b^{2} + c^{2} - 2bc\cos{A}
$$

### **Area of a Non-right-angled Triangle**

$$
\frac{1}{2} ab \sin{C}
$$
