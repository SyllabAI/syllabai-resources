## **Understanding Trigonometric Ratios**

## 1. Equilateral Triangle $\rightarrow$ 30° and 60° Ratios

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/05b868ad81a16375f23eca4b.webp =299x)

An equilateral triangle has all sides equal ($2\text{ cm}$). The perpendicular bisector of the base:

* Bisects the base into two equal parts ($1\text{ cm}$ each).

* Is perpendicular to the base.

* Bisects the top angle of 60° into two angles of 30°.

Using Pythagoras' Theorem in the right-angled triangle to find the perpendicular height:

$$(\sqrt{3})^2 = 2^2 - 1^2 \Rightarrow 3 = 4 - 1 \Rightarrow \sqrt{3}\text{ cm}$$

**For 30° (at the top):**

* $\sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$

* $\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

* $\tan 30^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

**For 60° (at the base):**

* $\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

* $\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$

* $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$

## 2. 45° Ratios (Isosceles Right-Angled Triangle)

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/423a445c0151ebfd7ccfaafa.webp =353x)

In this triangle:

* Opposite (to 45°) = $1$

* Adjacent (to 45°) = $1$

Using Pythagoras' Theorem:

$$(\sqrt{2})^2 = 1^2 + 1^2 \Rightarrow 2 = 1 + 1$$

* Hypotenuse = $\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$

**For 45°:**

* $\sin 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

* $\cos 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

* $\tan 45^\circ = 1$
