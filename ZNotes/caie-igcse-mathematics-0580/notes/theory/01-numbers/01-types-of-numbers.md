# Numbers

## Types of Numbers

* **Natural Numbers**

  * They start from 1

  * Used for counting purposes

  * All positive integers excluding zero

* **Integer**

  * All whole numbers: positive, negative and zero.
  * For eg: $0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 3$

* **Prime Numbers**

  * A number which has exactly **two distinct factors**: itself and 1

  * 1 is **not** a prime number

* **Rational Numbers**

  * Any number that can be written as a fraction p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

* **Irrational Numbers**

  * Numbers that **cannot** be written as simple fractions.

  * Their decimals are **non-terminating** and **non-recurring**, e.g.  $\pi$

* **Square Numbers**

  * The result of multiplying a number by itself twice

  * For eg, 1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81,100

* **Cube Numbers**

  * Made from multiplying a number by itself three times. For eg, 1,8,27,64,125

* **Reciprocals**

  * The reciprocal of a number is **1 divided by that number**

  * For eg, Reciprocal of 4 is ¼

  * Zero has **no reciprocal**

* **Common Multiples**

  * A number that is a multiple of two or more given numbers

  * For eg, Common Multiples of 6 and 8 are 24 and 48, as they appear common in both tables of 6 and 8

* **Common factors**

  * A number that divides two or more numbers exactly

  * For eg,

    Factors of 12 : 1, 2 , 3 , 4 , 6 and 12

    Factors of 18 : 1, 2 , 3 , 6 , 9 and 18

    Common factors of 12 and 18: 1, 2, 3 and 6
