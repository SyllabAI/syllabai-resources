## Time and Currency

### Time Conversions

* 1 Minute = 60 seconds

* 1 Hour = 60 minutes

* 1 Year = 365 days (or 366 in a leap year).

* 1 Day = 24 Hours

* 1 Week = 7 Days

* 1 Month = 30/31 Days

* If you are asked to convert 3 hours into minutes:

  $3  \text{ hours} \times 60   \text{ minutes} = 180 \text{ minutes}.$

* The **12-hour clock** uses "a.m." for morning and "p.m." for afternoon/evening.

* The **24-hour clock** uses a continuous format from 00:00 (midnight) to 23:59.

* Steps to convert 12-hour to 24-hour format:

  * For **a.m.**, keep the time as is but add leading zeros if needed (e.g., $3:15 \text{ a.m.} = 03:15$)
  * For **p.m.**, add 12 to the hour (e.g., $3:15 \text{ p.m.} = 15:15$)

### Time Zones

* If it is 10 a.m. in London (GMT) and New York is 5 hours behind, the time in New York is:\
  $10:00 - 5  \text{ hours} = 5:00  \text{ a.m.}$

### Currency Conversion

$$
\text{Amount} = \frac{\text{New Rate}}{\text{Old Rate}}
$$

$$
\text{Amount in Currency B} = \text{Amount in Currency A} \times \text{Exchange Rate}
$$
