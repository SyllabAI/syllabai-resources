## Estimation and Accuracy

### Accuracy

* Upper Bound - The largest possible value a measurement could be, given its degree of accuracy.

* Lower Bound - The smallest possible value a measurement could be, given its degree of accuracy.

* Worked Example

  Q. Find the upper and lower bound of 450 to the nearest 10

  Half the degree of accuracy = $\frac{10}{2}$ = 5

  Add to original value for upper bound = 450 + 5 = 455

  Subtract from original value for lower bound = 450 - 5 = 445

### Estimation

**Significant Figures**

* Significant figures is how precise a number is

**Rules for Significant Figures**

* All non-zero digits count

  * 348 $\rightarrow$ 3 s.f

* Zeros between digits count

  * 406 $\rightarrow$ 3 s.f

* Leading zeroes do not count

  * 0.54 $\rightarrow$ 2 s.f

* Trailing zeros count only if there is a decimal

  * 1500 (no decimal) $\rightarrow$ 2 s.f

  * 12.30 $\rightarrow$  4 s.f

**Examples**

* Round to 1 s.f

  * 3.76 $\rightarrow$ 4

  * 248 $\rightarrow$ 200

* Round to 2 s.f

  * 3.76 $\rightarrow$ 3.8

  * 248 $\rightarrow$ 250
