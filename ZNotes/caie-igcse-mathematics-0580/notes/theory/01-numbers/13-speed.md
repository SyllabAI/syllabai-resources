## Speed, Distance & Time

$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$

Example:

Convert 36 km/h into metres per second.

1\. Distance = 36 km = 36 000 m

2\. Time = 1 hour = 3600 s

3\. Apply formula:

Speed = 36 000 3600 = 10 m/s

Therefore, 36 km/h = 10 m/s.

Speed = Distance/Time

Step 1: Convert distance 10 m = 0.01 km

Step 2: Convert time 1 s = $\frac{1}{3600}$ hours

Step 3: Apply the formula Speed = $\frac{0.01}{\frac{1}{3600}}$

= 0.01 × 3600

= 36 km/h

$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance Travelled}}{\text{Total Time Taken}}$

- **Units of Speed:** $km/hr$ or $m/s$.

- **Units of Distance:** $\text{km}$ or $m$

- **Units of Time:** $\text{hr}$ or $\sec$

$$km/hr \times \frac{5}{18} = m/sec$$

$$m/sec \times \frac{18}{5} = km/hr$$
