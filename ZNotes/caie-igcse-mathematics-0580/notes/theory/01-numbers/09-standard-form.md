## Standard Form

* Used to express large or small numbers in factors of 10

### To Express Numbers Greater than $0$

* Let us take the example of the number 450,000
* Count the number of digits after the first digit, in this case, 5
* Take that as the power of 10
* Place a decimal point after the first number and remove any non-significant 0's
* $450,000 = 4.5 \times 10^5$

### To Express Numbers Less than $0$

* Let us take the example of the number 0.00031
* Count the number of digits before the first significant digit, in this case, 4
* Take that as the negative power of 10
* Place a decimal point after the first number and remove any non-significant 0's
* $0.00031 = 3.1 \times 10^{-4}$

### Ratio Simplification

* Find the HCF of the numbers in the ratio

* Divide both by the HCF

* $21{:}35$

* HCF = 7

* $3{:}5$

### **Surds**

**Rules of Surds**

* $\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a} \sqrt{b}$
  * $\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5 \sqrt{2}$
* $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$
  * $\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{12} = \sqrt{36} = 6$
* $\frac{ \sqrt{a} }{ \sqrt{b} } = \sqrt{ \frac{a}{b} }$
  * $\frac{ \sqrt{27} }{ \sqrt{3} } = \sqrt{ \frac{27}{3} } = \sqrt{9} = 3$
* You can only add or subtract **like** surds
  * $3 \sqrt{5} + 2 \sqrt{5} = 5 \sqrt{5}$

  * $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} \rightarrow$ do not combine
