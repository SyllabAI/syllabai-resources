## **Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages**

### Fractions

* Used to express a part of a whole. eg, $\frac{2}{5}$
* **Numerator**: The numerator is how many parts you have
* **Denominator**: The denominator is how many parts the whole is divided into
* **Proper Fraction**: When the numerator is less than the denominator. For example, $\frac{3}{8}$
* **Improper Fraction**: When the numerator is greater than the denominator. For example, $\frac{5}{3}$
* **Mixed Fractions**
  * This is only possible for improper fractions

  * A mixed fraction is a whole number with a proper fraction

  * Conversion of an Improper Fraction to a Mixed Fraction:

    The remainder becomes the numerator of the proper fraction, with the denominator remaining the same.

    $\frac{9}{4} = 2 \frac{1}{4}$

### Decimals

* Consists of a whole number part and a fractional part separated by a decimal point

* **Recurring Decimals:** A digit repeating infinitely after the decimal point (e.g $7.\overline{3}$ ,which indicates 7.33333….)

* **Recurring Decimals to Fraction :**

  * Let x = recurring decimal

  * Let n = number of recurring digits

  * Multiply the recurring decimal by $10^n$

  * Subtract the original recurring decimal from the product above.

  * Solve for x, expressing your answer in the simplest form

    Method

    1\. Let 𝑥 equal the recurring decimal.

    2\. Multiply by 10 or multiple of 10 so the recurring part shifts left of the decimal point.

    3\. Subtract to eliminate the recurring digits.

    4\. Solve for 𝑥 and simplify.

  * **Example 1: One-digit recurring decimal**

    Convert $0.\dot{3}$ to a fraction.

    Let $x = 0.\dot{3}$

    $10x = 3.\dot{3}$

    Subtract:

    $10x - x = 3.\dot{3} - 0.\dot{3}$

    $9x = 3$

    $x = \frac{3}{9} = \frac{1}{3}$

  * **Example 2: Two-digit recurring decimal**

    Convert $0.\dot{4}\dot{5}$ (i.e. $0.454545...$) into a fraction.

    Let $x = 0.\dot{4}\dot{5}$

    $100x = 45.\dot{4}\dot{5}$

    Subtract:

    $100x - x = 45.\dot{4}\dot{5} - 0.\dot{4}\dot{5}$

    $99x = 45$

    $x = \frac{45}{99} = \frac{5}{11}$

  * **Addition Example**

    Add $0.\dot{6}$ and $0.\dot{2}$.

    $0.\dot{6} = \frac{2}{3}$, $0.\dot{2} = \frac{2}{9}$

    $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{2}{9} = \frac{6}{9} + \frac{2}{9} = \frac{8}{9}$

    **Subtraction Example**

    $0.272727... - 0.1060606... = \frac{3}{11} - \frac{7}{66}$

    Find common denominator:

    $\frac{3}{11} = \frac{18}{66}$

    $\frac{18}{66} - \frac{7}{66} = \frac{11}{66}$

    Simplify:

    $\frac{11}{66} = \frac{1}{6}$

### Percentages

* Per cent means ‘out of a hundred’, referring to values out of 100

### Conversions of Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

* To convert a Percentage to a Decimal

  * Divide by 100

    $\text{45} % = \frac{\text{45}}{100} = 0.45$

* To convert a Decimal to a Percentage

  * Multiply by 100

    $\text{0.7} \times 100 = 70 %$

* To convert a Percentage to a Fraction

  * Write the fraction with a denominator of 100 and simplify

    $25% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}$

* To convert a Fraction to a Percentage

  * Divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100

    $\frac{13}{29} = 0.4482,\quad 0.4482 \times 100 = 44.82 %$
