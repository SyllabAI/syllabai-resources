## Relative & Expected Frequencies

### Expected Frequencies

* This is the number of times we would expect an event to occur over a given number of trials that take place during an experiment

  $ \text{Expected Frequency} = \text{Probability of Event} \times \text{Number of Trials} $

### **Relative Frequencies**

* Relative frequency is an estimate of the **probability** of an event based on **experimental (or observed) results** rather than theoretical probability.

* $\text{Relative Frequency} = \frac{ \text{Number of times event occurs} }{ \text{total number of trials} }$

* **Example**

  If you toss a coin 100 times and it lands on heads 55 times, the **relative frequency** of getting heads is:
  $\frac{55}{100} = 0.55$
