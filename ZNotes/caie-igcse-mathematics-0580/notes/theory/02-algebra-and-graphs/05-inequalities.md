## Inequalities

* Solve like equations. Perform operations on both sides (add, subtract, multiply, divide) to isolate the variable.

* When you multiply or divide by a negative number, reverse the inequality sign

* **Worked Examples**

  $\text{Q1. Solve }y-3 \geq -7$

  **Step 1:** Add 3 to both sides

  $y-3+3 \geq -7+3$

  $\boxed{y \geq 4}$

  $\text{Q2. Solve } \frac{y}{- 3} \ge - 7$

  **Step 1:** Multiply both sides by -3

  Because you are multiplying by a negative, reverse the sign

  $y \leq - 7 \times - 3$

  **Step 2:** Simplify

  $\boxed{y \leq 21}$

* When two inequalities are present, split them into two

* **Worked Example**

  $\text{Q1. Solve }x < 3x - 1 < 2x + 7$

  **Step 1:** Split into two inequalities

  1\. $x < 3x-1$

  $-2x<-1$

  Divide by -2, flip sign: $x > \frac{1}{2}$

  2\. $3x-1 < 2x+7$

  $x<8$

  **Step 2:** Combine

  $\boxed{\frac{1}{2} < x < 8}$
