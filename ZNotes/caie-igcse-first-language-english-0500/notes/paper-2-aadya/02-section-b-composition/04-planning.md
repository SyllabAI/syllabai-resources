## Planning

Before writing, spend **10–15 minutes** planning your story.

* Choose the prompt you can visualise most clearly.

* Decide on the setting, main character, central conflict or main event, climax and resolution

* Plan the **beginning and ending** before you start writing to ensure the story is coherent.

* Keep the plot **simple and focused**, limiting the story to one main event and no more than two main characters.

* Briefly note any **literary devices** (e.g. foreshadowing, repetition, personification) you plan to incorporate.

* Aim for **350–450 words**, leaving **5 minutes** at the end to proofread for spelling, punctuation, grammar, and consistency.
