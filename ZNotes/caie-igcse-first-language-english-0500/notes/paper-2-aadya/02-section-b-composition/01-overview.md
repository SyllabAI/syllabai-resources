# Section B: Composition

## Overview

In this section, you must choose **one composition task** from four options. You can choose either a **descriptive** or **narrative** writing task and develop it into a complete piece of writing.

* **Marks:** 40

* **Word count:** 350–450 words

* **Choice of questions:** **One** from Q2, Q3, Q4, or Q5

* **Types of writing:** Descriptive or Narrative

You are assessed on two areas:

* **Content and Structure (16 marks)** - the quality of your ideas, organisation, development, and overall effectiveness

* **Style and Accuracy (24 marks)** - vocabulary, sentence structure, **SPaG** (spelling, punctuation, and grammar)
