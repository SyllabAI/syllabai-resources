## Section A: Directed Writing

In this question, you are given **two short texts** that present **different or opposing viewpoints** on a topic. You must write a response using ideas from both texts while also **evaluating and developing them**.

* **Marks:** 40

* **Word count:** 250–350 words

* **Possible formats:** Speech, letter, or article

* **Style:** Discursive, argumentative, or persuasive

Although this question includes some reading marks, most marks are awarded for **writing and evaluation**.
