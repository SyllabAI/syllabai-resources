## **Step-by-Step Answering Method**

* Read both texts and the question carefully. Identify the task, format (speech, letter, article), and the viewpoint you should take. If not stated, infer it.

* Select **4–6 key ideas from each text**. Focus on points you can **develop and challenge**, not just repeat. Form **implicit ideas** by spotting assumptions, weaknesses, or consequences.

* Plan briefly in bullet points:

  * Point from the text

  * Your development

  * Counter-argument

**Guiding Questions**: Ask yourself: What is the writer saying? Why might this be flawed? How can I expand or challenge it?

* Do not just summarise—**evaluate and add depth**.

* Write in your **own words**. Maintain strong **SPaG (Spelling, Punctuation & Grammar)** with clear sentences, accurate punctuation, and varied vocabulary. Stay within **250–350 words** and leave time to check for errors.
