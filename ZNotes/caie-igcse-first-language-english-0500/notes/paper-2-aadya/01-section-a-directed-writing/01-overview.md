# Paper 2: Writing

## Overview

This paper tests your ability to understand two texts with different viewpoints, identify both explicit and implicit ideas, evaluate and compare the arguments presented, and develop a clear, well-supported response of your own.

The Paper 2 examination lasts **2 hours** and requires you to answer **two questions**. You must complete **Question 1** and choose **one question** from Section B (Questions 2–5). In total, the paper is worth **80 marks**. You will receive an insert containing one or two texts, which will be used for Question 1.

The paper is structured as follows:

* **Question 1: Directed Writing** → 40 marks

* **Section B: Composition (Q2–5)** → 40 marks
