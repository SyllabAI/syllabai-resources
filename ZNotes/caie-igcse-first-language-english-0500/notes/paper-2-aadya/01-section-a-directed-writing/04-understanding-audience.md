## **Understanding audience**

Audience refers to the group of people a piece of writing is intended for. Understanding the audience influences the writer’s tone, vocabulary, level of formality, point of view, and the way ideas are organised and presented. Effective writing adapts to the needs of its intended audience in order to communicate clearly and persuasively.
