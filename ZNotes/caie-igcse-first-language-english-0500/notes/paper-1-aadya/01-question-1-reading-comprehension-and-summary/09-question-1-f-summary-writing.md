## **Question** 1(f): Summary Writing

### **Key Requirements**

* **Number of points:** Typically **10–12 content points** to gain full marks.

* **Word count:** Aim for **around 120 words** maximum. Writing slightly more (\>=130) is usually fine, but excessive length can reduce conciseness. Avoid exceeding the word count further.

* **Conciseness:** Write in paragraph form, avoiding unnecessary words or repeated ideas — each point should carry meaning.

* **Own words:** Paraphrase wherever possible. Only copy exact words if they are **precise or technical**, otherwise do **not** take reference from the insert.

* **Logical grouping:** Combine similar ideas together so the summary flows naturally.

### **Step-by-Step: Answering Method**

1. **Highlight relevant points:** Read through the text carefully and underline key information that directly answers the question, focusing only on content needed for the marks.

2. **Group ideas:** Organise points by similarity or theme rather than the order they appear in the text.

3. **Paraphrase effectively:** Reword sentences so that they convey the same meaning **without** copying text.

4. **Use linking words sparingly:** Connect ideas clearly (e.g., additionally, moreover, in contrast) but don’t overcomplicate sentences.

5. **Keep it short and clear:** Each sentence should be short, direct and meaningful.

### **Formal Linking Words and Connectors**

* The use of connectors helps link ideas clearly, improving the writing flow. When used correctly, connectors improve clarity, organisation, and coherence in exam writing.
