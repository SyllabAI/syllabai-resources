## Question 1(c), 1(d)(i): Giving Reasons or Points

* Since the question is typically of 2 marks, you are required to give **two ideas**.

* Write in bullet points or short statements, where each point elaborates a **different idea**.

* You may refer to the insert, but it is not essential — points that correlate to each other heavily may be conjoined in the mark schemes. Avoid mentioning 2 pointers that are too similar to each other
