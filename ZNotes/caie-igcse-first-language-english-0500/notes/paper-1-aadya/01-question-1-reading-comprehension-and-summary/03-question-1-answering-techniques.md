## Question 1: Answering Techniques

### **General Guidelines for Q1**

* Answers may be taken **directly from the insert** unless the question specifically asks for **your own words**.

* Bullet-point questions **do not require full sentences**.

* Always match the **number of ideas** you give to the **number of marks** available.

* Do not exceed the space provided — extra writing does **not** earn you extra marks.

* Read the wording of each question **carefully** before answering.
