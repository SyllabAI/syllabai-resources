# Question 1: Reading Comprehension & Summary

## Overview

When working on reading comprehension, keep your answers **short and direct**. Focus on the main points quickly. Don't spend too much time on these questions, and avoid adding extra details that might confuse your answer.

The Paper 1 examination lasts 2 hours and requires you to answer three major questions. Questions 1 and 2 are broken down into several smaller parts. In total, the paper is worth 80 marks. You’ll be provided with an insert (along with the question paper and answer booklet) consisting of **three** texts you’ll use to answer the questions on the paper. The paper is broken into:

* **Question 1**: Comprehension and summary → 30 marks

* **Question 2**: Short-answer questions and language → 25 marks

* **Question 3**: Extended response to reading → 25 marks
