## Question 1(b): Explaining Words and Phrases

* Each word in the given phrase must be explained **separately**. If a phrase contains two key words, **both must be addressed**.

* Your answer should **replace the original words with synonyms**, not repeat them.

* When asked for the meaning of a phrase, explain **all parts of the phrase together** so the meaning remains accurate.

**Q:** *raise concerns*

**A:** express worries

*raise* → express,
*concerns* → worries
