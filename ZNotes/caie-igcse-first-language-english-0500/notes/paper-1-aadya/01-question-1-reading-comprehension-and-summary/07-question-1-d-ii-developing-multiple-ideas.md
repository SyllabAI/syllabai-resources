## Question 1(d)(ii): Developing Multiple Ideas

* Write **one bullet point per mark**.

* Ensure each point focuses on a **different aspect**.

* If comparing differences, be explicit about **what is being compared** — avoid vague statements.

  ***Q:*** *Identify the benefits of hosting a Formula 1 event for a country or organisation. \[3m\]*

  ***A:*** *Formula 1 benefits organisations in several ways. **It increases global brand exposure**, as races are watched by millions around the world. **It encourages technological innovation**, because teams compete to develop faster and more efficient cars. **It also boosts local economies**, since race events attract tourists and increase spending in host cities.*
