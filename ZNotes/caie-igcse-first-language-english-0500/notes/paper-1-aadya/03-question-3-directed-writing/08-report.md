## Report

### **Format & Structure**

* Begin with a **short, factual headline** that summarises the entire event.

* Do **not** use articles (a, an, the) in the headline.

* Give a **short title or focus** to each paragraph if required.

### **Content Requirements**

* The **introduction** should summarise the event clearly.

* The **second paragraph (first bullet point)** must answer:

  * **Who, What, Where, When, Why & How**

* For the **final bullet point**, refer to how the passage ends and include **neutral outcomes or predictions**.

* Include **facts only** **–** no opinions or emotions.

### **Voice & Language**

* Write in the **third person**.

* Maintain a **neutral, unbiased, and factual tone**.

* Use:

  * Formal vocabulary

  * Clear connectives

  * Precise phrasing

* Personal opinions, emotional language, and expressive comments must be avoided entirely.
