## **Step-by-Step Answering Method**

* Read the question carefully and identify **who you are writing as.**

* Identify the **text type** you are required to write (**tested formats include**: letter, speech, interview, newspaper/formal report, article, and journal)

* **Apply VARP:**

* You’ll be asked to address three bullet points throughout your writing. Make sure to:

  * Treat all bullet points as absolutely **compulsory**.

  * Reserve **equal weightage** for each point

  * Remember that the *last bullet point typically requires the most thinking* and realistic development beyond the text.

* Plan out your writing briefly:

  * Read the passage and highlight details linked to each bullet point

  * Convert highlighted details into:

    * **Point**: a condensed idea of the overall point

    * **Detail**: explicit information from the text

    * **Expansion**: any inferred thoughts/feelings that stem from the phrase

      <https://images.znotes.org/allimages/9a8ee226479e289653ffdf7b.webp>

* You should aim for 3-4 ideas per bullet point.

* Follow text-type conventions:

  * Use correct openings, sign-offs, headings, or layout depending on the format.

  * Maintain a **consistent tone** throughout

**While writing:**

* Do **not quote** from the passage.

* Do **not invent** your own ideologies

* Depending on format, use elevated vocabulary and varied sentence structures.
