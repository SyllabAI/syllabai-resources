## Developing Thoughts, Feelings & Imagination

When developing an idea, ask:

> **What happened?** → **How would this person react?** → **Why would they feel this way?** → **What might they think, imagine, remember or expect?**

### **Example:**

* ### **Passage detail:** ‘The character arrives at a new school and knows nobody.’

  #|
  ||
  
  ***Sentence Type***

  |
  
  ***Basic***

  |
  
  ***Developed***

  ||
  ||
  
  Sentence

  |
  
  ‘I arrived at my new school and did not know anyone.’

  |
  
  ‘Walking through the unfamiliar corridors, I felt completely out of place. Everyone seemed to know exactly where they were going, while I could barely bring myself to approach anyone. I wondered whether I would ever feel as though I belonged there.’

  ||
  ||
  
  Notes

  |
  
  This transfers the information but doesn't develop the character.

  |
  
  Through this write-up, the writer has developed:

  * **Thought:** “I wondered whether…”

  * **Feeling:** “I felt completely out of place.”

  * **Imagination/expectation:** imagining whether they will eventually belong

  * **Detail:** unfamiliar corridors / other student
