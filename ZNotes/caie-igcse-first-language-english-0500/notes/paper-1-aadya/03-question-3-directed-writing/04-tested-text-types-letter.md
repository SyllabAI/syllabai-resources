## Tested Text types: Letter

### **Format**

* **No address, date, or subject line** is required

* The letter should begin with an appropriate **greeting**, such as **“Dear” or “Hi” followed by a name**.

* Clear **paragraphing** is essential. Each paragraph should have a clear purpose and link logically to the next, ending with a suitable closing, chosen according to whether the letter is formal or informal.

### **Structure**

* ***Paragraph 1 – Introduction***

  * Clearly state the purpose of the letter

  * This is your **thesis**

* ***Paragraphs 2–4 – Body***

  * One paragraph for **each bullet point**

  * Develop ideas fully with explanation and detail

  * Aim for **13-15 points total**

* ***Paragraph 5 – Conclusion***

  * Summarise the main ideas

  * Make a final request/reflection
