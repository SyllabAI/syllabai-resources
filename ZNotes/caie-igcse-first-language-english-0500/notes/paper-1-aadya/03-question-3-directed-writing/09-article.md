## Article

### **Format & Structure**

* Must be engaging and relevant to the topic

* Can use techniques like, rhetorical questions, alliteration and wordplay

* **Introduction (Paragraph 1):**

  * Hook the reader (question, bold statement, or anecdote)

  * Introduce the topic clearly

  * State the focus or purpose of the article

* **Body (Paragraphs 2–4):**

  * One paragraph per bullet point

  * Develop ideas with: explanation, examples and anecdotes

  * Maintain clear paragraphing and logical progression

* **Conclusion (Paragraph 5):**

  * Summarise key ideas

  * End with: a final thought, a recommendation/solution or a question to engage the reader
