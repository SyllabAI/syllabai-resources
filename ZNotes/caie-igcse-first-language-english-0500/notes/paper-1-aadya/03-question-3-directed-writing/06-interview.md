## Interview

### **Format & Structure**

* Write in **script format**

* Do **not remove or reword questions**

* **Write out the full question exactly as given**

* Use the following layout:

*Interviewer: \[full question exactly as given\]*

*Interviewee: \[response\]*

**Note**: The question is part of the word count

### **Emphasis: Final Question**

* The final question should:

  * Respond to how the passage ends

  * Include opinions, reflections, or predictions

  * Act as a **conclusion** to the interview
