## Journal / Diary Entry

### **Format & Structure** 

* Write the **day and date** at the top before beginning the entry.

* Do **not** include a greeting, heading, or sign-off.

* Write in **first person**, as the entry is meant for yourself.

* The entry should be organised into **five paragraphs**:

  * **Paragraph 1:** Introduce the situation and your emotional state.

  * **Paragraphs 2–4:** One paragraph for **each bullet point**, reflecting on events and personal reactions.

  * **Paragraph 5:** Respond to how the passage ends, including reflections, opinions, or predictions.

### Correct Use of Tenses

* Use the **past tense** to describe events that have already occurred.

* The **present tense** may be used for current thoughts and feelings.

* Tense usage must be **accurate and consistent** throughout the entry.
