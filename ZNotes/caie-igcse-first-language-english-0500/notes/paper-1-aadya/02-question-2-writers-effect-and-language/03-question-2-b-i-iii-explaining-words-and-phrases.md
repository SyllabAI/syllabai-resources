## **Question 2(b)(i)–(iii): Explaining Words and Phrases**

* Consider how the word is used within the context and elaborate with a brief explanation.

* Avoid using the same meaning for multiple words; aim for accuracy in capturing the intended sense.

* ***Example Question:*** What does the word “staggered” suggest about the way the man walked?

  ***Context****: “Exhausted by the long journey, he staggered towards the doorway, barely able to remain upright.”*

  ***Answer***: “Staggered” means that he walked unsteadily, struggling to keep his balance because of his exhaustion.
