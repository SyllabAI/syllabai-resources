## **Question 2(c): Effect of a Short Quotation**

* This question is essentially a shorter Writer’s Effect.

  * Pick one quote, think on what it says literally. Keep focus targeted to what the quote tells you, **not** how the writer wrote it.

  * Look into possible implications (i.e., characterisation, tone, formality and impact)

* Elaborate selected quote into three separate points:

  * **Point 1**: Literal meaning

  * **Point 2**: What it suggests about the general subject

  * **Point 3**: Potential wider effect/impact on reader

* For example: *“The village seemed frozen in time.”*

  * **Point 1: Literal meaning**

    What does the quotation directly tell us?

    > The village appears unchanged and inactive.

  * **Point 2: Implication**

    What does this suggest about the subject?

    > The phrase **“frozen in time”** suggests that the village has remained unchanged for a long period, giving it an old-fashioned or isolated quality.

  * **Point 3: Wider effect**

    How might this effect the reader?

    > This creates an impression of a quiet, isolated place and encourages the reader to imagine a setting untouched by modern life
