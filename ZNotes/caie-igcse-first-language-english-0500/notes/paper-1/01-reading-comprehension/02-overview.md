## Task Overview

You'll answer several sub-questions about a text. These questions check how well you understand both clear (explicit) and hidden (implicit) meanings, and how you pick out information from the text. The questions total **15 marks**, divided like this:

- **Question 1(a):** Find two specific pieces of information from the text. *(1 mark)*
- **Question 1(b)(i):** Explain what a word or phrase means in your own words. *(2 marks)*
- **Question 1(b)(ii):** Explain a word or phrase with a deeper meaning. *(2 marks)*
- **Question 1(c):** Find two specific pieces of information from the text. *(2 marks)*
- **Question 1(d)(i):** Find two specific pieces of information from the text. *(2 marks)*
- **Question 1(d)(ii):** Make three points to explain something from the text. *(3 marks)*
- **Question 1(e):** Explain something from the text in your own words, making three points. *(3 marks)*
