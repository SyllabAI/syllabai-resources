# Interview Writing

## Steps

1. **Read the Question**

   - Underline key words.

2. **Select Relevant Points**

   - Find 5 points for each bullet point.

3. **Consider VARP**

## Format

- **Script Format**

  - Write as a dialogue.
    - **Interviewer:** Question
    - **Name:** Answer

- **Introduction**

  - Not necessary, but you can write a brief one to set the scene.

- **Body Paragraphs**

  - Use the provided questions without changing them.
  - One paragraph per question.

- **Closing**

  - No conclusion needed.

## Tips

- **Understand the Character**

  - Infer attitudes and reactions.
  - Reflect their way of speaking.

- **Use Conversational English**

  - Write as if it's spoken aloud.
  - Include varied punctuation like ellipses (...) and dashes (—).

- You *can* make some stuff up-but keep it believable and relavent

- **Avoid Slang**

  - Keep language appropriate for all readers.
