# Journal Entry

## Steps

1. **Read the Question**
   - Underline key words.

2. **Select Relevant Points**
   - Find 5 points from the text for each bullet point.

3. **Consider VARP**

## Format

- **Opening**
  - Start with "Dear Journal" or a similar greeting.

- **Body Paragraphs**
  - Write three paragraphs, one for each bullet point.

- **Closing**
  - Sign off with the character's name.

## Tips

- **Be Reflective and Thoughtful**
  - Express emotions and feelings.

- **Use a Chronological Approach**
  - Narrate events in order.

- **Avoid Copying the Text**
  - Use your own words and infer feelings.

- **Language Style**
  - Usually informal.
  - Include features like hyperbole, sarcasm, idioms, and rhetorical questions.
