# Speech Writing

## Steps

1. **Read the Question**
   - Underline key words.

2. **Select Relevant Points**
   - Find 15 points from the text, including inferences.

3. **Consider VARP**

## Format

- **Introduction**
  - Start with a hook to grab attention.
    - Use a "what if" or "imagine" scenario.
    - Ask a rhetorical question.
    - Share a statistic or powerful phrase.
  - Introduce yourself if necessary.

- **Body Paragraphs (3)**
  - Begin with a topic sentence connecting the main idea.
  - Analyze and expand on points from the text.

- **Conclusion**
  - Summarize your main points.
  - State your stance and justify it.
  - End with a **call to action**.
    - Use imperative verbs.
    - Ask rhetorical questions.

## Tips

- **Use Impactful Techniques**
  - Shock tactics, strong facts, and statistics.
  - Hyperbole and vivid imagery.
  - Repetition and lists of three.

- **Language Style**
  - Use forceful, imperative phrases.
  - Choose emotive and evocative words.
  - Simple language is effective if clear.

- **Connect with the Audience**
  - Use personal pronouns: I, you, we.
  - Make it personal to engage listeners.
