# News Report

## Steps

1. **Read the Question**
   - Underline key words.

2. **Select Relevant Points**
   - Find 5 points for each bullet point.

3. **Consider VARP**
   - **Voice:** Impersonal journalist.
   - **Audience:** General public.
   - **Register:** Extremely formal.
   - **Purpose:** To inform.

## Format

- **Title**
  - Make it short and catchy.
  - Use techniques like alliteration, powerful words, or rhetorical questions.

- **Paragraph 1: Introduction**
  - Summarize the main events in 1-2 sentences.
  - Cover what happened, where, when, and who was involved.

- **Paragraph 2: Background**
  - Explain why it happened.
  - Provide history and causes.

- **Paragraph 3: Account**
  - Detail how it happened.
  - Provide specifics of the event.

- **Paragraph 4: Responses**
  - Include reactions from participants or witnesses.
  - Use direct and reported speech.

- **Conclusion**
  - Evaluate the situation.
  - Mention future events or actions.

## Tips

- **Stay Impersonal and Objective**
  - Don't include personal opinions.
  - Remain neutral.

- **Language Use**
  - Use dramatic vocabulary without bias.
  - Avoid personal pronouns.

- **Incorporate Quotes**
  - Use direct speech: "It was strange," he said.
  - Use reported speech: He said it was strange.

- **Passive Reporting Verbs**
  - Phrases like "It is believed that..." or "It is expected that..."

- **Clear and Concise Sentences**
  - Be straightforward and avoid unnecessary details.
