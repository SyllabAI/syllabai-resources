# Magazine Article

## Steps

1. **Understand the Question**
   - Read carefully and underline key words.
   - Note what each bullet point is asking.

2. **Select Relevant Points**
   - Choose exactly 15 points from the passages.
   - Balance points from Text A and Text B equally.

3. **Consider VARP**

## Format

- **Headline**
  - Use what's given or create your own.
  - Make it catchy: use humor, alliteration, or a rhetorical question.

- **Introduction**
  - Grab the reader's attention.
  - Set the context of the topic.

- **Body Paragraphs**
  - Discuss ideas from both texts.
  - Include both direct and inferred points.
  - Analyze attitudes and ideas presented.

- **Conclusion**
  - Provide your final thoughts.
  - State your opinion and justify it.
  - Evaluate the overall stances of both texts.

## Tips

- **Evaluate and Analyze**
  - Don't just list points; delve into their meanings.

- **Pick Up on Undertones**
  - Notice subtle messages or biases in the texts.

- **Use Relevant Vocabulary**
  - Match your language to the topic (e.g., for social media, mention posts, likes, etc.).

- **Express Your Opinion**
  - Use adverbs like "strongly," "unfortunately," "personally."

- **Incorporate Figurative Language**
  - Use similes, metaphors, and alliteration to enhance your writing.
