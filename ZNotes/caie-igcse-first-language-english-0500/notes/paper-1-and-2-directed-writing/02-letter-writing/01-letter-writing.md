# Letter Writing

## Steps

1. **Understand the Question**

   - Highlight key words.
   - Identify the three main points you need to cover.

2. **Gather Relevant Points**

   - Find five points from the text for each bullet point in the question.

3. **Think About VARP**

   - Consider Voice, Audience, Register, and Purpose.

## General Format

- **Opening**

  - Begin with "Dear [Name or Title]".
  - No need to include an address or date.

- **Introduction**

  - A brief statement of purpose.
  - Example: "I am writing to..."

- **Body Paragraphs**

  - Write one paragraph for each bullet point.

- **Closing**

  - Depends on the context.
  - Examples: "Sincerely," "Yours truly," "Regards," "With love,"

## Tips

- **Use Ideas from the Text**

  - Include both direct information and inferences.
  - Avoid adding unrelated details.

- **Plan Ahead**

  - Organize your thoughts before writing.

- **Write in Your Own Words**

  - Don't copy directly from the text.

- **Stay on Topic**

  - Focus on the points asked in the question.
