# Formal Report 
## Steps

1. **Read the Question**
   - Underline key words.

2. **Select Relevant Points**
   - Find 5 points for each bullet point.

3. **Consider VARP**
   - **Voice:** Polite, objective, possibly as a student or employee.
   - **Audience:** An official or someone in authority.
   - **Register:** Very formal.
   - **Purpose:** To highlight issues and offer solutions.

## Format

- **Overview**
  - State the purpose of the report.
  - Include basic details: who, what, where, when, why, how.

- **Body Paragraphs**
  - Use subheadings to organize content.
  - Highlight problems and provide details.

- **Conclusion**
  - Offer evaluations.
  - Suggest sensible solutions to the issues.

## Tips

- **Language Style**
  - Be precise and concise.
  - Use formal and neutral language.
  - Avoid figurative language and slang.

- **Include Facts**
  - Use formal linking words like "therefore" and "hence."
  - Provide facts, statistics, and quotes if relevant.

- **Focus on Findings**
  - Don't just narrate events.
  - Present what was discovered and learned.
  - Analyze and evaluate the information.
