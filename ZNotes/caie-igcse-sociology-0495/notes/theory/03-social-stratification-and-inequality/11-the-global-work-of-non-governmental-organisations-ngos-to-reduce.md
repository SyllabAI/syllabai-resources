## The Global Work of Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs) to Reduce Inequalities

* There are many non-governmental organisations (NGOs).

* They are not associated with the government, and they do not aim to make a profit

* They are voluntary organisations of people who want to help others.

* The NGOs provide aid in the form of money, resources and expertise.

* Governments often distribute their aid through NGOs

* NGOs raise money through fundraising appeals

  * e.g., Red Cross, WWF, Amnesty International. Greenpeace, Médecins Sans Frontières

### **Reducing Poverty**

* They aim to reduce poverty

  * They provide opportunities for people to move themselves out of poverty to avoid a dependency culture

    * e.g., they may give a homeless person a food cart and cooking ingredients so that he may make a living

### **Providing Medical Aid**

* Health services are often limited and poor in LEDCs

* There are a few health centres and doctors per person

* GOs work to improve health services by:

* providing training to health workers

* providing equipment and medicine

* sending volunteer doctors and other medical staff  to support local health services

### **Promoting Business**

NGOs help people support themselves by enabling them to earn an income rather than rely on aid.

* In areas with few or low-paid jobs, NGOs support small businesses, such as selling food at markets.

* A small investment (e.g. equipment like a refrigerator) can significantly improve chances of success.

* NGOs often provide low-interest or interest-free loans (microcredit).

* Some NGOs use online platforms so people in developed countries can directly lend money to entrepreneurs in developing countries.

### **Improving Education**

* NGOs work to improve education in many ways:

  * They provide money, equipment and buildings for schools

  * Education is prioritised by NGOs because it can affect society more widely
