## Life Chances

* Life chances is how likely someone is to **do well in life** (education, job, income, health, security).

* Strongly shaped by **social class**,  the biggest factor in most societies.

* Also affected by **gender, age and ethnicity**, these can increase or limit opportunities.

* People with similar backgrounds usually end up with **similar outcomes**, not by accident.

* Life chances depend on how **stratified** a society is (how unequal it is).

* **Laws, norms and values**

  * Some laws protect groups and improve chances.

  * Some traditions and attitudes keep inequalities in place.
