## The Intersectionality of Age, Ethnicity, Gender, and Social Class in Understanding the Impact of Inequality on Individuals

* Age, gender, ethnicity and social class are **interconnected** and *cannot be viewed separately when explaining inequality.*

* **Everyone has all 4 characteristics**, so inequality results from different combinations, not just one factor on its own.

* Social class is often seen as the most important factor, but its effect depends on gender, ethnicity and age.

  * For example, a working-class person has fewer life chances than a middle-class person,

    * But if they belong to the majority ethnic group, they may experience ethnic advantages,

    * And if they are male, their advantages may increase even more.

* **Intersectionality** refers to *the way age, gender, ethnicity and social class combine to shape an individual’s life chances*
