## Differences in Life Chances due to Ethnicity

* **Racial prejudice** is the belief that another racial or ethnic group is inferior in some way.

* **Racial discrimination** is when an individual is treated unfairly or disadvantaged because of their perceived ethnic or racial group.

* **Racism** is a term that refers to both racist beliefs and racist behaviour.

* **Institutional racism** occurs when racism is built into the policies, practices, and structures of an organisation or system

  * For example, law enforcement can be racist

* **Cultural racism** refers to prejudice and discrimination based on perceived cultural differences, where one culture’s language, beliefs, or lifestyle is seen as superior or inferior to another.

* **Minority ethnic groups** often experience reduced life chances because they tend to be positioned lower on the socioeconomic scale.

* **Life chances of minority ethnic groups** can be negatively affected through discrimination in education, employment, health, housing, and life expectancy.

* **Anti-discrimination laws** exist in many societies, but discrimination is difficult to prove in practice, allowing it to continue

### Ethnicity and Education

* Teachers are likely to be from the majority ethnic group and may stereotype minority ethnic learners as lazy or disobedient.

  * This will affect learners’ mental health and self-perception, so they are less likely to succeed in education.

* The curriculum may be based on the history and culture of the majority group

* These reduce minority learners’ life chances by limiting the ability to get qualifications

### Ethnicity and Employment

* People from minorities are often low-paid, unskilled labour

* They may face discrimination in the workplace

  * They may not receive promotions and suffer from glass ceilings, as well as horizontal/vertical segregation

* They may not be considered for jobs and may be subjected to prejudice

### Ethnicity and Health

* Minority ethnic groups may face inequalities in health and suffer problems arising from poor housing or poverty

  * Some ethnicities are more genetically prone to certain diseases

    * The Ashkenazi-Jewish ethnic group are much more prone to Tay-Sachs disease

  * Lifestyle differences such as increased consumption of tobacco, alcohol, etc.

  * Possible hesitance to seek medical aid from external sources

  * Increased stress and mental health issues due to racism and discrimination

### Ethnicity and Housing

* The govt may sometimes not offer minority groups available housing for rent

* In private housing areas, minorities may be made to feel unwelcome

  * This leads to them living in different areas, a kind of segregation, with different areas occupied by different ethnicities.

    * Ethnic groups may settle in their own areas, such as Chinatown, Little Italy or the Bronx

* Due to low wages from discrimination in employment, they maylive in poor quality, overcrowded homes from which they may be evicted.

* Immigrants settle together and form communities such as Little Tokyo, Chinatown or Desitown.

### Ethnicity and Life Expectancy

* Minority ethnic groups may suffer from a lower life expectancy due to health problems described above
