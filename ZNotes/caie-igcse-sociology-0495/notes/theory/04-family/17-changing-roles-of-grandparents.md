## Changing Roles of Grandparents

* More people live into old age, so becoming grandparents (and even great-grandparents) is more common.

* Role of active, healthy grandparents is increasingly important.

* Family changes affect grandparents’ roles:

  * Children may divorce, remarry, or become lone parents

  * Divorce/separation can reduce grandparent-grandchild contact

  * Family feuds may separate grandparents from grandchildren

* Rise of step-grandparents from reconstituted families

* Grandparents now include those of children with:

  * Adopted children or children in care

  * Neglected or abused children

* Many children have grandparents from multiple ethnic groups

* Grandparents themselves are more likely to be divorced or remarried

### Childcare

* Look after grandchildren while parents work

* Often associated give grandchildren gifts and treats and form a strong emotional bond

* Grandmothers more likely to provide this support than grandfathers

* Maternal grandparents often play a larger role than paternal grandparents

### Economic Support

* Grandparents provide money through gifts or loans to their children and grandchildren

### Wisdom and Advice

* Offer emotional and moral support through listening and guidance

* Provide parenting advice to their own children

* Build strong bonds with grandchildren, mediating in conflicts between children and parents

### Dependent / a Burden

Grandparents may need support from their adult children in old age:

**++Accommodation++**

* Grandparents may live with their children and grandchildren in **vertically extended families**

**++Personal Care++**

* Assistance with daily needs (eating, washing)

* Adult children, especially daughters, provide most support

* Extra burden if grandparents have mobility or memory problems

**++Economic Support++**

* Financial help for grandparents with little or no income or small pensions
