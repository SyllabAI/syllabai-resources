## **Variations in Types of Marriage**

### Arranged Marriage

* In this marriage, the parents or other family of the children decide who they will be, usually agreed upon by the couple

* The couple are introduced to each other and presented with a choice

* This was the norm in most societies and still is in some parts of the world, such as Pakistan or India

* This marriage involves the traditional payment of a dowry or bride price

* Supporters argue that this marriage is longer lasting, as love marriages are based on feelings that can fade over time, unlike arranged marriages, where there is no expectation of love

### Love Marriage

* This marriage involves the couple marrying eachother with or without the consent of the family, based on love and attraction for eachother

* This is the norm in Western countries, and this term is not used, as all marriages are assumed to be love marriages

### Same-sex Marriage

* This is when both partners are of the same sex

* This is legal in some but not all parts of the world, so study according to the laws of your region

### Empty Shell Marriage

* This is when a married couple live together, and may appear normal, but have no feelings or an intimate relationship with each other

* This usually happens because they do not want to divorce, either:

  * for financial reasons

  * or because they have dependent children whose lives they don’t want to disrupt

### Monogamy

* This involves marriage to only one partner at a time

* This is the only legal form of marriage in many countries, especially in the West

### Serial Monogamy

* This is when someone is married to several people across their life, but only one at a time

  * This is becoming more common in industrial societies due to higher divorce rates, so many people remarry

### Polygamy

* This marriage involves at least 3 people

  * A man married to 2 or more women is called polygyny

  * A woman married to 2 or more men is called polyandry

* This is legal in most of the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia

* Study according to the laws of your region
