## **The Impact of Industrialisation and Urbanisation on Family Structures and Roles**

* Functionalists: the modern nuclear family evolved as society changed

  * The extended family suited pre-industrial society; the nuclear family suits modern industrial society

* Urbanisation and industrialisation changed family life

  * People moved from villages to cities for work → families became smaller and more nuclear

  * Example: UK 1800 → 80% in villages; 1830 → 50% in towns

* The nuclear family was never completely new

  * Mid-late 20th century, Willmott & Young: extended families still strong in working-class London

  * Mothers and married daughters often lived close; men worked locally

* Redevelopment weakened extended family bonds

  * Families moved further apart → women had less contact with their mothers

  * The nuclear family became dominant; the husband-wife bond replaced the mother-daughter bond

  * Less interaction with neighbours, community, and other relatives
