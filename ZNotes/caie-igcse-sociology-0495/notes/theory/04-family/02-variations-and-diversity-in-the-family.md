## **Variations and Diversity in the Family**

Family diversity (changes in the number and types of families) is influenced by culture, social class, and ethnicity

### Cross-Cultural

* Göran Therborn identified 7 broad types of family systems

* Christian-European family is assumed to be spreading worldwide

  * Some aspects of this family type are becoming more common, but other family types remain strong

### Social Class

* Family types can be linked to social class, with different classes tending to live in different family structures

* In the UK during the 20th century, extended families were strongly associated with the working class

* Older industrial areas (e.g. coal, steel, shipbuilding) had stable working-class communities

  * Secure local employment meant families stayed close to relatives

* Late 20th-century industrial decline led to job losses and automation

  * Migration in search of work weakened extended family ties

* Similar patterns occur in other countries where people migrate to cities for employment

* Globally, poorer regions are more likely to have extended families

  * Relatives provide mutual support

  * Larger numbers of children may contribute to family income

### Ethnicity

* In some societies, family types are linked to ethnicity

* Differences between ethnic groups may include:

  * Family size, with some groups having more children or more extended family members

  * Marriage patterns, such as arranged marriages versus greater individual choice

  * Division of labour, with different expectations of gender roles within the family
