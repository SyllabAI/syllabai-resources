### Positive Sanctions

Positive sanctions reward certain behaviour.

* Informal positive sanctions can be a praise from a teacher, a like on social media or a smile

* Formal positive sanctions can be an award, a good exam grade or a pay raise

### Negative Sanctions

Negative sanctions discourage or punish certain behaviour.

* Informal negative sanctions include an angry look, a parent taking away priveleges and a child being ostracised by their peer group.

* Formal negative sanctions include being dismissed from work, a fine, imprisonment or expulsion from school.#

### An Example of an Institution Exercising Both Forms of Social Control

**Education (schools)**

* Informal social control

  * Teachers praising or scolding students

  * Peer pressure to follow school norms

* Formal social control

  * Written school rules

  * Detentions, suspensions, expulsion
