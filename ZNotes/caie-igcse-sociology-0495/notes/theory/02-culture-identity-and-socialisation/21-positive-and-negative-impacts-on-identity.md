### Assimilation

* Occurs when minority groups gradually adopt the dominant culture.

**Impact on identity**

* Encourages a **shared national identity**.

* Can reduce visible cultural differences.

* However, it often leads to the **loss of original cultural identity**.

* Individuals may feel pressure to reject their background to be accepted.
