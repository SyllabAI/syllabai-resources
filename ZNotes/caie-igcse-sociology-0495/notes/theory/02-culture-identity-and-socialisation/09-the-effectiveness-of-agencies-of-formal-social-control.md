## The Effectiveness of Agencies of Formal Social Control

* Main formal agencies of social control include government, police, courts, prisons and armed forces

* They work together: police arrest, courts try, penal system punishes

* Their presence acts as a deterrent

* Effectiveness varies by country, but legal authority makes them powerful

* Some sociologists argue that informal control (consent, persuasion) is more effective

* Formal control should be a last resort, since people may reoffend, and it is costly financially and socially
