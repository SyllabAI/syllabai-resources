## Key Agencies of Socialisation

### Family

* The family is the first and foremost agency

  * It has the greatest impact of socialisation

* You are born into your family, and spend the most time with them, and will spend time thorughout your lives

* You were brought up with the language behaviour matching the social class of your family

* Most sociologists argue that it is the most important agency, as people raised without a family can develop into deviant individuals.

### Education

* Another vital agency where children spend 10 or more years

* First contact with a formal institution

* They learn not only the formal curriculum but a hidden curriculum as well, socialising them for the real world

* Many sociologists argue that education is the most important agency because the hidden curriculum is important in shaping future behaviour

### Peer Groups

* Peer groups are people of the same age and status

* They can be friends, but not necessarily

* They apply peer pressure, meaning they pressure you to act in ways approved by the majority

  * Peer pressure is the **direct or indirect social influence exerted by a peer group** to align an individual's attitudes, values, or behaviors with group norms

* During childhood and education, they are very effective at socialisation because people tend to interact mostly with peer groups

* Children also have a strong desire to fit in and feel like a part of a group

### Media

* An important agent of socialisation throughout life, influencing values, attitudes, and behaviour

* Provides most people with ideas and information about other parts of the world and their own culture

* Often portrays social groups in stereotypical ways

* This makes media socialisation very influential, but also divisive, as stereotypes can reinforce prejudice and social divisions

* For example, the media can show kids in commercials how to act or not act, and influence their consumer behaviour

* There is always a gap between media of different generations, such as Gen Z knows about the 21 meme, but Gen Alpha knows the 67 meme

### Religion

* Religious organisations are important agents of socialisation that teach important ideas and acceptable behaviour

* Socialisation occurs via attendance at places of worship, religious texts and sermons

* Formal membership and organisational rules strongly influence individuals’ values

* Can be a very effective form of socialisation because values are seen as coming from a higher authority and are less likely to be questioned

* In some societies, especially in the West, religion is declining in importance, a.k.a. secularisation

* Due to growing secularisation, the effectiveness of religion as an agent of socialisation declines, although it is still strong in many communities worldwide

### Workplace

* Learning a new job role means understanding expectations and adjusting accordingly

* Workplace behaviour and skills are learned through training and interaction with colleagues

* Workers are expected to conform to workplace norms to gain approval, promotion, or job security

* As adults spend most of their time at work, the workplace is a strong agent of socialisation

* A shared “canteen culture” develops, where workers influence each other and form common workplace norms
