
## **The Effectiveness of Agencies of Informal Social Control**

* Agencies of socialisation also act as informal social control by encouraging conformity to norms and values

* Main agencies: family, education, peer groups, media, religion, workplace

* Informal control is often more effective than formal control, as it avoids confrontation; people conform willingly and are less likely to break rules

* Examples:

  * **Family:** positive/negative sanctions (treats for good behaviour, scolding for misbehaviour), canalisation for gender roles

  * **Education:** hidden curriculum, informal rewards/punishments, peer pressure

* These agencies shape behaviour by teaching what is expected and reinforcing it through sanctions, helping individuals repeat or avoid actions in the future
