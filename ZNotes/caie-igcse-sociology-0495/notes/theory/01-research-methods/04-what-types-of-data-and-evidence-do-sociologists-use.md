## **What Types of Data and Evidence do Sociologists use?**

### Primary and Secondary Data

* **Primary data** is information collected by the researcher **firsthand.**

* **Secondary data** is information collected **earlier by other researchers** **and sources** and then used by sociologists. The 4 types of secondary data are:

  * **Official statistics** produced by governments and other official organisations.

  * **Research by other sociologists, journalists**

  * **Media sources** (TV, social media, newspapers, radio)

  * Other sources (mainly **qualitative**), such as diaries, letters, and photographs.
