## **Qualitative and Quantitative Data**

### Qualitative Data

* **Qualitative data is descriptive data, as words describing phenomena**

* It gives **more insight** and is **more valid**

* It is preferred by **interpretivists**.

* **Primary data** includes participant observation and unstructured interviews.

* **Secondary sources** include personal documents, historical records and media content.

### Quantitative Data

* **Numerical** data in the form of **statistics**

* **Primary sources** include coded questionnaires and structured interviews.

* **Secondary sources** include official statistics, govt. census

* Preferred by **Positivists**
