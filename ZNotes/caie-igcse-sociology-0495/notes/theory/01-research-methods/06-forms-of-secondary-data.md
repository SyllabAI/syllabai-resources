## **Forms of Secondary Data**

### Official Statistics

* **Numerical data** produced by **national and local governments,** and other **official bodies**

* There are 2 types:

  * **Hard Statistics:** Need to be free from any error or incompleteness; these need to be accurate (birth, marriage and death rates).

  * **Soft Statistics**: **It depends** on the decision-makers **what** to show and **how**. e.g., crime, corruption.

### Historical and Personal Documents

* Written records from the past are called historical documents

* First-person accounts such as letters, diaries and journals are called personal documents

  * They may be intended for others to read, such as journals of famous people. In this case, they may be biased and hence less valid and representative, as the person may want to portray themselves in a good light.

  * Some accounts may be private; it is difficult to gain ethical access to them. E.g. the Diary of Anne Frank

  * Autobiographies, but they are not valid, as human memory is imperfect, and the account may be biased.

* Sociologists also use many other documents for research, which include:

  * Social media posts and blogs

  * Declassified government documents from surveys

  * Household accounts, wills and even shopping lists

  * School reports and exam papers

  * Miscellaneous items like graffiti, notes passed in class

### Digital Sources

* Digital sources are the internet and other online sources

* They include:

  * Blogs and vlogs

  * Online forums and servers

  * Webpages

  * Social media such as Instagram, Reddit and X.

* They are useful as they can allow sociologists to gather data from around the globe.

### Media Content

* Media Content is the content of magazines, newspapers, television, films and other media sources

* Because many people across the world consume media content, it is of significant sociological interest in how different groups are portrayed in the media, because they can represent and affect the views of people

* Some media sources, such as documentaries, can be sources of information to sociologists and can be used in the initial stage of the literature review.
