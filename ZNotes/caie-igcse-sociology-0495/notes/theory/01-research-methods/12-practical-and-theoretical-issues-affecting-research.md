## **Practical and Theoretical Issues Affecting Research**

### Access to Sample

* Practical issue

* It may not always be possible to get access to a sample, for example:

  * A sample of schoolchildren would require consent from the school or parents

  * Criminal activity is difficult and even dangerous to study

### Response Rate

* Practical issue

* May affect the representativeness of research

* Questionnaires have low response rates

### Funding/Cost

* Practical issue

* Funding is needed from a body such as the government or a university

* Quantitative data is relatively easy to get funding for, as it produces trends, patterns and statistical data

* Qualitative data is seen as less important/valuable by funding bodies

* Researcher has to convince to get funding

* Researchers may have to control costs, as money is limited

### Time

* Some research requires more time than others

* Quantitative research, like surveys, is less time-consuming

* Longitudinal studies are extremely time-consuming and difficult to get funding for

* Qualitative research is generally more time-consuming

* Large-scale research, such as a census, is also very time-consuming

**++Theoretical Issue++**

### Researcher Perspective

* Each researcher has their own perspective that may influence research

* A positivist may be objective and produce quantitative data

* An interpretivist may be subjective and produce qualitative data

* What the researcher feels is worth studying is also important

* A Marxist, feminist, and Functionalist may each deem different issues more important
