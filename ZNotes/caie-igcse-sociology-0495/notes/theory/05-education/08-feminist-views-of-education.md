## Feminist Views of Education

### The Patriarchal Culture of Education

* Feminists argue that education is based on patriarchy, with male roles prioritised

* Boys are more likely to hold positions of authority in schools

* Boys’ activities, such as sport, are often given higher status than girls’.

### Male Power and Gender Hierarchy

* Feminists argue that education reinforces **male power** through the curriculum, school organisation, and teaching

* Although many teachers are women, men are more likely to be **head teachers**, holding authority

* This creates a **gender hierarchy**, sending messages about male dominance to learners

### Role Models

* Feminists argue that **role models influence subject and career choices**

* Male-dominated subjects (e.g. science) may discourage girls from choosing them

* Gendered role models in school and society reinforce ideas about **“appropriate” careers** for males and females

### Access to Education

Globally, girls are less likely to attend school. This is explored later on.

### Gendered Curriculum / Subject Choice

* The curriculum is **male-centred**, reflecting male perspectives and achievements

  * Literature often features male authors, history focuses on men, and textbooks show boys in active or prestigious roles

* This **reinforces gender stereotypes**

  * Girls are subtly shown as passive or limited in roles, and boys as leaders and achievers

* These stereotypes **influence subject choices and careers**

  * Girls may avoid male-dominated subjects like science or history

  * Leads to restricted career options and helps maintain gender inequality

### Teacher Expectations

* Teachers tend to have **lower expectations of girls**, discouraging them from working as hard or taking challenging subjects

* Behaviour is judged differently:

  * Girls may be **punished for calling out or talking**, while boys are often allowed similar behaviour

* This reflects and reinforces **wider societal gender roles**, teaching girls to be submissive and boys to be dominant

### Peer Groups and Social Control

Girls’ **peer groups influence attitudes** towards education

* Higher-level study may require leaving home/community

* Peers may discourage this, applying **pressure to conform** to societal gender expectations
