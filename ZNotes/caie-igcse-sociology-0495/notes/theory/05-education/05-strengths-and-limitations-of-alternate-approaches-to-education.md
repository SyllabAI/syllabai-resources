## Strengths and Limitations of Alternative Approaches to Education

### Strengths

* Alternative approaches give a second chance to students who have (been) rejected (by) mainstream schools

* Alternative schools aim to develop well-rounded learners with broader skills than those in mainstream schools

* Alternative schools provide access to education for learners who may struggle or be excluded from mainstream schools

* Alternative schools allow learners to follow a learning style that suits their needs.

### Limitations

* Alternative education may limit access to formal exams and qualifications, which can restrict entry into certain careers and education paths.

* Alternative education requires high self-discipline; some learners may struggle without a strict structure and fail to use time effectively.
