## Question 4

**a)** 

- The energy required to raise the temperature of unit mass by 1$\degree$C.

**b)** 

**i)**

C = 2000 J/(kg °C)

$\theta$ = 180-20

$\rho$= 910 kg/$m^3$

V = 0.0024 $m^3$

t = 7 minutes

$$\rho=\frac{mass}{volume}$$

$$\text{mass}= 0.0024 m^3 \times 910 kg/m^3$$

$$\text{mass}=2.184kg$$

$$\text{mass}=2.2kg,\text{(2 s.f)}$$

**ii)** 

$$\text{E}= mC\Delta T$$

$$\text{E}= 2.2kg \times 2000J/(kg°C) \times (180-20)°C$$

$$\text{E}= 704000J$$

$$\text{E}= 70000J,\text{(2 s.f)}$$

**iii)** Calculate the power required to supply the energy calculated in (b)(ii).

$$\text{Power}=\frac{Energy}{time}$$

$$\text{Power}=\frac{704000}{7 \times 60}$$

$$\text{Power}=\frac{704000}{420}$$

$$\text{Power}=1676W$$

$$\text{Power}=1700W,\text{(2 s.f)}$$
