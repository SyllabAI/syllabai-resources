## Pressure

* Pressure is force per unit area
* $p=\frac{F}{A}$ Unit: Pa (Pascals)
* Everyday examples of pressure:

1. Hammering nails on a wall

2. Needle

3. Skis

4. Wide tires for large vehicles

* Pressure in liquids is determined by the density of liquid and the depth of the object.

* The greater the depth and density, the more the pressure. (keep in mind that the shape of the container of a liquid doesn’t affect the pressure as long as the depth and density of the liquid is same in 2 containers)
