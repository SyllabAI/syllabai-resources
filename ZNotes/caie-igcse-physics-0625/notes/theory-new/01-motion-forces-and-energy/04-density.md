## Density

* Density is mass per unit volume
* ${\rho}=\frac{m}{V}$     Unit: g/$cm^3$ or kg/$m^3$
* An object less dense than water floats on water. Eg: Wood, rubber and ice

**Density of a liquid**

* Measure the volume of a liquid in a measuring cylinder

* Measure the mass of an empty measuring cylinder using a balance

* Pour the liquid into the measuring cylinder and read its volume

* Find the difference between the 2 masses

* Apply the formula. Density=mass/volume

**Density of regularly shaped solid**

* Measure the mass by placing the solid on a balance.

* Measure the volume by measuring the length, width and the height and multiply the values.

* Apply the formula. Density= mass/volume

**Density of an irregularly shaped solid which sinks in a liquid (Volume by displacement)**

* Measure the volume of water in a measuring cylinder

* Add the solid to the water and make sure it is fully submerged

* Measure the new volume of the water

* Find the difference between the 2 volumes

* Calculate the mass using a beam balance

* Apply the formula. Density=mass/volume
