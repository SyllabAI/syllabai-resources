# Picking a Better Insulator

  - **Apparatus:** Two large cans, two small cans, cotton wool,
    polystyrene beads, boiling water, thermometers, stopwatch

  - **Procedure:**
    
      - Put the small cans into the large cans and insulate the small
        can with (i) cotton wool and (ii) polystyrene beads
    
      - Pour boiling water into the small cans and place the
        thermometers in them.
    
      - Start the stopwatch and take readings of temperature at regular
        intervals.
    
      - Record readings in a table for each insulator.
    
      - The small can that has the higher temperature over the fixed
        period is better insulated.
    
      - Hence, object that provides a less temperature loss over the
        period is the better insulator.
