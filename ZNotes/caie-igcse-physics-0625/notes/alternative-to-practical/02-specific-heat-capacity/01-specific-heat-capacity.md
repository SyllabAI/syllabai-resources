# Specific Heat Capacity

* **Apparatus:** Solid block, Drill, Thermometer, Heater (of known power), Cotton wool.
* **Procedure:**
  * Drill two holes in the block.
  * Measure the mass of the block.
  * Place the heater in one of the holes, the thermometer in the other.
  * Use cotton wool to properly insulate/lag the block.
  * Note the initial temperature of block and turn on heater for
    $x$seconds
  * Calculate Heat Energy Supplied by heater using formula Q=Pt.
  * Note the final temperature of block.
  * $Specific\ heat\ capacity\  = \frac{Q}{m \times \mathrm{\Delta}T}.$
* **Apparatus:** Immersion heater, Power supply, Voltmeter, Ammeter, Insulated calorimeter, Thermometer, Stopwatch, Liquid of known mass ($m$).
* **Procedure**:
  * Measure the mass ($m$) of the liquid using a digital balance

  * Place the heater and thermometer into the insulated calorimeter; record the initial temperature ($\theta_1$)

  * Turn on the power supply and start the stopwatch ($t$); record the voltage ($V$) and current ($I$)

  * Stir continuously to ensure uniform heat distribution.

  * After a set time, turn off the heater and record the maximum temperature reached ($\theta_2$).

  * $$c = \frac{VIt}{m\Delta\theta}$$

  * Calculate $\Delta\theta = \theta_2 - \theta_1$.

  * Substitute $V, I, t, m,$ and $\Delta\theta$ into the formula to find $c$.
