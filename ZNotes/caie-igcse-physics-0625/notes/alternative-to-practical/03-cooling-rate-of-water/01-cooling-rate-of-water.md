# Cooling Rate of Water

  - **Apparatus:** Heater, Thermometer, Beaker, Stopwatch, Beaker
    containing Water.

  - **Procedure:**
    
      - Place heater into beaker and turn it on to raise the temperature
        of water to 60°C
    
      - Stir the contents of the water and place thermometer into the
        beaker.
    
      - Note the starting temperature and turn on the stopwatch.
    
      - Take readings of the thermometer and stopwatch at regular
        intervals (e.g. 60 sec).
    
      - Draw up a table and plot a graph to conclude your experiment.
