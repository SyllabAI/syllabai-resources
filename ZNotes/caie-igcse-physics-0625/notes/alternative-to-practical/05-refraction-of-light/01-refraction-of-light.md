# Refraction of Light

  - **Apparatus:** Ray Box, Rectangular piece of glass, Plain paper,
    Pencil.

  - **Procedure:**
    
      - Place the Plain paper below the rectangular piece of glass.
    
      - Project a ray towards the glass.
    
      - Make two points to mark the incident ray, two to mark the
        refracted ray and two to mark the emergent ray.
    
      - Join all the lines, measure the angles and calculate refractive
        index.
    
      - Repeat with different angles; Snell's law shown.
