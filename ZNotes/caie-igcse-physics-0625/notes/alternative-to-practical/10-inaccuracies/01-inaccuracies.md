# Inaccuracies

  - **Why angle i is NOT equal to angle r in ray experiment:**
    
      - Thickness of pins
    
      - Thickness of mirror
    
      - Protractor is not precise

  - **Inaccuracy of ray box method:** thickness of rays.

  - **Inaccuracy of pin method:** pins not straight, or too close, or
    thickness of lines drawn.

  - **Measuring 10 oscillations rather than 1:**
    
      - Reduce human errors
    
      - Give more accurate value of time taken (T)
    
      - Gives an average of T
