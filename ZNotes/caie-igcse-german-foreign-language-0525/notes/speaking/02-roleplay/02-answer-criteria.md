## Answer Criteria

**Please note again that you will not see those questions you’ll only hear them on the spot.**

* For this part you do not need to give a long answer just give a short answer that fulfills communication and answers the question simply.
* Your answer should be in the **__correct tense,__** if the question is in the past, reply in past tense. If the question is in the future tense, reply in future tense as well. This is very important to obtain the full mark.
* Minor errors e.g., ein instead of einer, are tolerated, meaning you will still be able to achieve a full mark.
