## Word Order of a Sentence

When there are more pronouns and/or nouns, word orders can change:

| **Sentence** | **Meaning** | **Rule** |
|----|----|----|
| Ich schenke meiner Schwester die Blume. | I send my sister the flower. | If both are nouns, Dative obj. before Acc obj. |
| Ich schenke ihr meiner Schwester./ Ich schenke ihr die Blume. | I send it to my sister./I send her the flower. | Pronoun goes first. |
| Ich gebe sie ihr | I give it to her. | If both are pronouns, Acc. Obj. before Dative Obj. |

TMP stands for time, manner, place. It’s a sentence that starts with a time e.g., manchmal, meistens, letzte Woche, … and ends with a place. Ex: Manchmal gehe ich in den Klub.
