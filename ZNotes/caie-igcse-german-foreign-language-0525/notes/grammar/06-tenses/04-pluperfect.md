## Pluperfect

Pluperfect or past perfect describes an event that took place **before the present perfect and simple past.** It’s basically Präteritum and Perfekt combined; the auxiliary verbs ‘Haben’ or ‘Sein’ are used in their simple past form so, ‘Hatte’ and ‘War’.

Ex:

* Er hatte gut Fußball gespielt. *(He had played football well.)*
* Ich war in Berlin nur einmal gewesen. *(I had only been in Berlin once.)*
* Ich hatte nichts davon gewusst. *(I knew nothing about it.)*
