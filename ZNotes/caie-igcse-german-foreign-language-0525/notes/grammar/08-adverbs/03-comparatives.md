## Comparatives

* Comparatives in German are made by adding **-er** to a word like: laut **→** laut**er**
* Sometimes an umlaut must be added, like: groß (big) **→** größer (bigger)
* The endings differ according to the case, gender, and whether a definite or an indefinite article is used:

Strong endings (Indefinite article):

| **Gender** | **Nominative** | **Accusative** |
|----|----|----|
| **Masculine** | **-r or -er**    älterer | **-n or -en**   älteren |
| **Feminine** | **-e**               ältere | **-e**               ältere |
| **Neutral** | **-s or -es**    älteres | **-s or -es**    älteres |
| **Plural** | **-e**               ältere | **-e**               ältere |

Weak endings (definite article):

| **Gender** | **Nominative** | **Accusative** |
|----|----|----|
| **Masculine** | **-e**              ältere | **-en**    älteren |
| **Feminine** | **-e**             ältere | **-e**       ältere |
| **Neutral** | **-e**            älteres | **-e**       älteres |
| **Plural** | **-en**         älteren | **-en**    älteren |

**Als** means (as) but when making a comparative sentence it is used like (than) in English.

Ex:

* Ich bin älter als er. (I’m older than him)
