## Superlative

**Am or an article** can be used when making a superlative sentence.

Superlatives are formed by adding **-sten or -esten** and sometimes ‘am’ is added before the word, like: laut **→** **am** laut**esten**.

\*Sometimes an umlaut must be added, like: groß (big) **→** am größten (the biggest).

Ex:

* Meine Prüfung war **die** einfach**ste.** *(Mine was the easiest exam.)*
* Meine Flasche ist **am** voll**sten**. *(My bottle is the fullest.)*

Common irregular comparatives and superlatives:

| **Word** | **Comparative** | **Superlative** |
|----|----|----|
| **Viel(a lot)** | Mehr(more) | Am meisten(most) |
| **Gut(good)** | Besser(better) | Am besten(best) |
| **Gern(like)** | Lieber(prefer) | Am liebsten(favourite) |
| **Hoch(high)** | Höher(higher) | Am höchsten(highest) |
| **Nah(near)** | Näher(nearer) | Am nächsten(nearest) |
