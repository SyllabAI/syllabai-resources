## Adjectival Endings (part 2)

Weak endings are used with **__definite articles__**:

| Weak Endings | Masculine | Neutral | Feminine | Plural |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| Nom. | **-e** | **-e** | **-e** | **-en** |
| Akk. | **-en** | **-e** | **-e** | **-en** |
| Dat. | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** |
| Gen. | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** |

Ex:

* Die laut**e** Musik. *(The loud music) (-e from Die Musik)*
* Ich kaufe den schön**en** Hut. (I buy the beautiful hat) (-e from Der Hut)

  \

Mixed endings are used with **indefinite articles:**

| Mixed endings | Masculine | Neutral | Feminine |
|----|----|----|----|
| Nom. | **-er** | **-es** | **-e** |
| Akk. | **-en** | **-es** | **-e** |
| Dat. | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** |
| Gen. | **-en** | **-en** | **-en** |

Ex:

* Ein gut**es** Buch. (A good book) (-es from Das Buch)
* Eine schwarz**e** Katze. *(A black cat)* *(-es from Die Katze)*




Demonstrative pronouns like: Dieser(this), jeder(every), jener(that), derjenige(the one), derselbe(same) take the same endings as the adjectives.




If there are 2 or more successive adjectives, they all take the same ending.
