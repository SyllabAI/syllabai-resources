## How Marks are Awarded

**__For the 2nd question (80-90 words):__**

* **All** tasks must be completed.
* Writing must be relevant to the question.
* Minor errors, e.g. einem instead of einen/einer are tolerated (you will still be able to achieve a full mark).
* No need for overly complicated sentences, vocabulary or connectors; just using a wide range of them is enough e.g., use weil and denn interchangeably, außerdem, dann, danach, bevor and dass etc.
* Meaning must be clear, and communication should be achieved, although some linguistic inaccuracies may exist.
* Uses straightforward vocabulary and structures.
