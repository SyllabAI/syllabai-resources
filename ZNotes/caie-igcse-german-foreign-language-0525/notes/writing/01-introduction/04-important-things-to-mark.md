## __Important Things to Mark__

These things should be included in the writing as these are things that you earn marks for:


1. Use of capital and lower-case letters.

   
   1. All nouns are written with a capital case.
   2. Some infinitives can be used as nouns, e.g. essen (to eat)  das Essen (the food).
2. Nouns must follow prepositions in their respective case (dat. Or akk.)
3. Nouns must be used in their respective case.
4. Word order.

   
   1. Inversion. (usually scores more marks): putting connectors at the beginning of the sentence, e.g. Wenn ich Hunger habe, esse ich Pizza. Or starting with a word for time, e.g., Am Wochenende spiele ich Volleyball. Manchmal spiele ich Tennis Lieber.
   2. Verb position (always second place in a normal sentence)
   3. Time, manner, place. e.g., Gestern(time) bin ich allein/mit meinem Bruder(manner) ins kino(place) gegangen. (You start with a time and end with a place)
   4. Conjugation of verbs.
5. Subordinate clauses like e.g., wenn, weil, dass, bevor, obwohl. Put the verb at the end.
