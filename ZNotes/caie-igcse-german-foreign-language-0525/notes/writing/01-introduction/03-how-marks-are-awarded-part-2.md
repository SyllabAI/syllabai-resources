## How marks are awarded (Part 2)

**__For the 3rd question (130-140 words):__**

It is worth 28 marks.

* **A** 10 marks for the completion of tasks.
* **B** 10 marks for range (of vocabulary and connectors)
* **__C__** 8 marks for accuracy (grammatical accuracy)

**__A (Tasks):__**

* All tasks should be completed.
* Give a detailed explanation, information, and opinion e.g., Ich lese gern, weil lesen mein Lieblingshobby ist. Ich finde es sehr friedlich und macht viel Spaß.
* Ideas should also be relevant.

**__B (Range):__**

* Use extended well linked sentences frequently.
* Use both simple and complex structures.
* Wide range of vocabulary.

**__C (Accuracy):__**

* Accurate spelling and grammar, so long as they do not disrupt communication.

Ex: Reis (rice) instead of Reise (trip) does disrupt communication because it alters the meaning, however “English” instead of “Englisch” does not change the meaning completely.
