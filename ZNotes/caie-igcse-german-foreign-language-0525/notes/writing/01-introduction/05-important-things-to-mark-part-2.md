 6. Linking words and conjunctions other than und/aber (use them as well), like: dann, danach, deshalb, außerdem, als.
 7. Negatives (nicht, kein, nie, noch nie,…)
 8. Wide range of adjectives e.g. ziemlich instead of sehr.
 9. Appropriate use of letter/email etiquette e.g., Liebe/er (Name), Deine/er (name)
10. Do not start sentences with ‘ich’ frequently, start with time words e.g. gestern, nächste, morgen, manchmal, meistens, fast jeden Tag, …
11. Correct usage of tenses. If the task is written in the past then you must answer it in the past, if it’s in the future it should be answered in the future tense. If it’s also a general task then it’s answered for in present.
12. Comma usage:

    
    1. Linking clauses e.g., Ich gehe in die Bibliothek, wo ich lesen kann.
    2. Series e.g., Gestern habe ich getanzt, gekocht, gegessen, und im Garten gearbeitet.
    3. Numerical expressions e.g., 3,78€
