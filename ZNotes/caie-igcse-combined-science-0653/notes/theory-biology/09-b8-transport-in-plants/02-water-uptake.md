## Water uptake

### Water is taken up the roots via the root hair cells by diffusion and take up minerals and ions by active transport. Their large surface area increases the uptake for water and ions.

### Water is taken up the root hair cells, then goes to the root cortex cells to the xylem then reaches the mesophyll cells where it reacts with carbon dioxide to produce oxygen and glucose (photosynthesis).

## Transpiration

### Is the loss of water vapor from the leaves via evaporation.

### Water is absorbed by the plant roots from the soil, moves up through the plant, and then evaporates from pores called stomata.

## Advantages of transpiration 

* ### Cooling the plant.

* ### Creating a continuous flow of water and mineral ions from the roots to the leaves.

* ### Maintain nutrient uptake.
