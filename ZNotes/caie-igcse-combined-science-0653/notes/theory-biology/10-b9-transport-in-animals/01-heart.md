# B9: Transport in animals

## Circulatory system

### Definition: a system of blood vessels with a heart and valves to ensure one way flow of blood in the right direction.

### Functions of the circulator system

* ### transport nutrients and oxygen to the cells.

* ### removes waste and carbon dioxide from the cells.

* ### provides an efficient gas exchange surface.

## The heart

The heart has four chambers which contract and relax to transport lood to the heart and the organs.

## Blood vessels

### Blood vessels transport blood into and away from the heart. Each type has different structures that help it carry out its function.
