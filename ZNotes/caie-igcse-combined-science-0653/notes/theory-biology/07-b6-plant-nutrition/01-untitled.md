# B6: Plant nutrition

## Photosynthesis

### Definition: the process by which plants synthesise carbohydrates from raw materials using energy from light.

### the word and symbol equations for photosynthesis is:

* ### word equation: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen

* ### symbol equation: **CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂**

### Photosynthesis must happen in the presence of (sun)light and chlorophyll. Chlorophyll is a green pigment contained in chloroplasts. It also converts the light energy from the sun into chemical energy, glucose. 

## Factors affecting photosynthesis

### 1) Light intensity: as light intensity increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases.

### 2) Carbon dioxide concentration: as the concentration of carbon dioxide increases,  the rate of photosynthesis increases.

### 3) Temperature: as temperature increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases, reaches a peak, then decreases, this is because high temperatures would harm the leaf tissues and result in more water evaporating from the leaf.
