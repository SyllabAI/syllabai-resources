* **Block Math**

  $\Rightarrow$ Wrap your equation in double dollar signs for a larger, centred display

  `$ $`
  `E = mc^2`
  `$ $`

  gives

  $$ E = mc^2 $$

  $\Rightarrow$**Note:** There should be no space between the double dollar signs. Spaces have been added here for visual purposes.

  $\Rightarrow$**You** have to `Shift` + `Enter` after starting `$ $` and before ending `$ $`
