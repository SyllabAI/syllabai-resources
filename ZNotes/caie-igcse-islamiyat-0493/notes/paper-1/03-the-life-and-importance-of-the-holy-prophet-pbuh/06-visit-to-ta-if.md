## Visit to Ta’if

### **The Journey to Ta'if**

**Background and Context**

* **Unsafe Conditions in Makkah:** Following the Year of Grief (YOG), the streets of Makkah became highly unsafe for the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Preaching Restrictions:** The Makkans actively prevented the Prophet (PBUH) from preaching Islam within the city.

* **Stagnant Dawah:** Due to fierce opposition, there were no new converts to Islam in Makkah during this difficult period.

* **Strategic Target:** Ta'if was chosen as a preaching destination, being home to two major rival tribes of the Quraish: Banu Thaqif and Banu Hawazin.

**Meetings with the Leaders of Ta'if**

* **Approach to Umayya bin Amr:** The Prophet (PBUH) first approached Umayya bin Amr, a leader widely known for his extreme generosity. Despite this reputation, the Prophet (PBUH) was flatly turned down.

* **Approach to the Three Chiefs:** He then went to present the message of Islam to the three chiefs of Ta'if, who were brothers:

  1. Habib

  2. Abdi Yalil

  3. Mas'ud

* **Rejection:** The three chiefs completely rejected the message and the Prophet (PBUH).

### **Persecution and Trial at Ta'if**

* **Pelted with Stones:** The Holy Prophet (PBUH) was chased and pelted with stones by street hooligans incited by the leadership.

* **Severe Injuries:** He was so badly injured during the assault that his boots filled completely with blood.

* **Hazrat Zaid (RA):** His companion, Hazrat Zaid (RA), was also injured along with him while trying to protect the Prophet (PBUH).

### **Events Following the Departure**

* **The Orchard:** The Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Zaid (RA) left Ta'if and took refuge in a nearby orchard.

* **Conversion of a Slave:** While resting in the orchard, a Christian slave accepted Islam.

* **Conversation with the Angel of Mountains:** The Angel of Mountains appeared and spoke with the Prophet (PBUH), offering to crush the residents of Ta'if between the hills, though the Prophet (PBUH) chose forgiveness instead.

* **Conversion of the Jinn:** A group of Jinn heard the recitation of the Quran during the return journey and converted to Islam.

### **Safe Return to Makkah**

* **Escort by Mut'im bin Adi:** The Prophet (PBUH) was safely escorted back to his home in Makkah by Mut'im bin Adi and his three sons.

* **Legacy of Mut'im bin Adi:** Mut'im bin Adi was notably one of the Makkan chiefs who had previously stepped forward to end the cruel social and economic Boycott of Banu Hashim.
