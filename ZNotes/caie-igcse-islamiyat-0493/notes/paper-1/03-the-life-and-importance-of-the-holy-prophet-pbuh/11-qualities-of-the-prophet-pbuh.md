## Qualities of the Prophet(PBUH)

### **1. Honesty & Truthfulness (Al-Sadiq & Al-Amin)**

* **Titles & Reputation:** Known as *Al-Sadiq* (The Truthful) and *Al-Amin* (The Trustworthy) even in a corrupt Arabian society.

* **Hadith:** *"The one who cheats is not amongst us"* and *"Truth leads to piety and piety leads to Jannah (Paradise)"*.

* **Marriage to Hazrat Khadija (RA):** Managed her trade trip to Syria with complete honesty. Her slave, Maisra, praised his integrity, leading Hazrat Khadija (RA) to send a marriage proposal.

* **Event of Mount Safa:** When he publicly invited Makkans to Islam, they all unanimously attested that he had never lied to them.

* **Trusts During Migration:** Even after declaring prophethood, Makkans entrusted their valuables to him. Upon migrating to Madinah, he left Hazrat Ali (RA) behind specifically to safely return these possessions to their owners.

### **2. Mercy & Forgiveness**

* **Divine Purpose & Hadith:** Quran states: *"We sent you not but as a mercy for mankind"*. Hadith: *"Allah does not show mercy to those, who do not show mercy to others"*.

* **Preaching at Al-Taif:** Stoned by the people until his shoes filled with blood. When Angel Jibrael offered to let the Angel of the Mountains crush the town, the Prophet (PBUH) refused, praying: *"Why should I pray for the destruction of these people, I’m sure their posterity will surely be among the followers in One Allah"*.

* **Conquest of Makkah:** Announced a general amnesty for the Quraish who had persecuted him for years. He told them: *"I will treat you as Yousaf treated his brothers. You are free from all fears today, may Allah forgive you"*.

* **Treatment of Sworn Enemies:** Offered funeral prayers for Abdullah bin Ubai (leader of the hypocrites) and forgave Wahshi (who martyred his uncle Hazrat Hamza).

### **3. Humility & Modesty**

* **Distinctive Quality:** Hadith: *"Every religion has a distinctive quality and distinctive quality of Islam is modesty"*.

* **Household Work:** Swept the house, stitched his clothes, repaired his own sandals, and shared meals with employees and slaves.

* **Treating Others as Equals:** Disliked when companions stood up to welcome him, stating he never wished to be treated like a ruler. Worked alongside common men in constructing Masjid-e-Nabawi and digging the Trench.

### **4. Fulfillment of Promises & Honoring Covenants**

* **Sign of Faith:** The Prophet (PBUH) strictly warned against breaking promises, calling betrayal a sign of hypocrisy.

* **Treaty of Hudaibiyah:** Adhered strictly to the terms of the treaty by returning Abu Jandal (a Muslim escapee from Makkah) back to the Quraish, advising him to remain patient as Muslims cannot break covenants.

### **5. Steadfastness, Courage & Bravery**

* **Firmness in Mission:** When offered immense wealth, leadership, or status by the Quraish to stop preaching, he refused. He famously told his uncle Abu Talib that even if they placed the sun in his right hand and the moon in his left, he would never abandon his mission.

* **Enduring Persecution:** Remained firm through physical abuse, public ridicule, and a painful 3-year socio-economic boycott in *Shib Abi Talib*.

* **Bravery in Battle:**

  * **Battle of Badr:** Remained steadfast with $313$ Muslims against $1,000$ Quraish warriors.

  * **Battle of Hunain:** When many scattered during a surprise attack, he stood his ground and advanced toward the enemy.

### **6. Justice & Generosity**

* **Impartial Justice:** Decided cases purely on merit regardless of status or relation. When a noble woman (Fatimah bint Al-Aswad) committed theft, he refused to show favoritism, stating: *"If Fatimah bint Muhammad had committed this theft, I would have cut off her hand"*.

* **Matchless Generosity:** Hadith: *"Generosity is near to Allah, near to Paradise..."*. He never stored wealth for the next day and freed thousands of captives (e.g., $6,000$ captives after the Battle of Hunain)
