## Isra wal Miraj (Event of Ascension)

* **Background and Purification:** The journey occurred in the $10th$ or $11th$ year of Prophethood following the Year of Sorrow and the harsh treatment at Ta'if. To console and honor him, Allah granted him this journey. While resting in Hateem near the Ka'aba, Jibrail and Mikail opened his chest (*Shaq-e-Sadar*), washed his heart with Zamzam water, removed all sorrow, and filled it with faith (*Imaan*), conviction, and wisdom.

* **The Journey to Jerusalem (Isra):** The Prophet (PBUH) mounted the **Buraq**—a white, winged creature smaller than a mule and larger than a donkey, moving at extraordinary speed.

  * **Stops along the way:** He stopped to offer two units of prayer at Madinah (signifying the future Islamic state), Mount Tur (where Musa spoke to Allah), and Bait-ul-Lahm (birthplace of Isa).

  * **Masjid al-Aqsa:** He led all preceding Prophets in congregational prayer, earning the title *Imam-ul-Anbiya*.

  * **Choice of Vessels:** Offered vessels of milk, water, and wine, he selected milk. Jibrail noted that he chose *Fitrah* (pure human nature), ensuring his Ummah would remain guided.

* **Ascension through the Heavens (Mi'raj):** Ascending via a heavenly ladder accompanied by Jibrail, he greeted previous Prophets at each level:

  * **$1st$ Heaven:** Hazrat Adam (AS)

  * **$2nd$ Heaven:** Hazrat Yahya (AS) and Hazrat Isa (AS)

  * **$3rd$ Heaven:** Hazrat Yusuf (AS)

  * **$4th$ Heaven:** Hazrat Idris (AS)

  * **$5th$ Heaven:** Hazrat Haroon (AS)

  * **$6th$ Heaven:** Hazrat Musa (AS)

  * **$7th$ Heaven:** Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) resting against *Bait-ul-Mamur*

* **Sights and Divine Meeting:**

  * **Bait-ul-Mamur:** The heavenly Ka'aba directly above the earthly Ka'aba, encompassed daily by $70,000$ angels.

  * **Sidrat-ul-Muntaha:** The boundary lote tree with immense leaves and fruit, where four rivers originate (Nile, Euphrates, and two heavenly rivers). He also observed the River of Kausar.

  * **Visions:** He witnessed the punishment for backbiting and saw Jibrail in his true six-hundred-winged form.

  * **Divine Injunctions:** Beyond the tree, he conversed directly with Allah. He received the last two verses of Surah al-Baqarah ($285$–$286$), the promise of forgiveness for non-polytheists, and the initial obligation of $50$ daily prayers. Following Hazrat Musa's advice, the Prophet (PBUH) returned to negotiate until prayers were reduced to $5$ daily, carrying the reward of $50$.

* **Return and Makkah’s Reaction:** Returning to Makkah before dawn, he stayed at the house of Umme Hani. The Quraish mocked his account, but he accurately described Jerusalem and predicted the arrival of an oncoming trade caravan. Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) validated the account immediately without hesitation, “If Muhammad says it, it must be true” earning the title *As-Siddiq*.
