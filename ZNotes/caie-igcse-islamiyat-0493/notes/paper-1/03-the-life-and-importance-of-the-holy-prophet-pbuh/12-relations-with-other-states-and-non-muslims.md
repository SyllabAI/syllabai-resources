## Relations with other states and Non-muslims

### **1. Religious Tolerance & Peaceful Coexistence**

* **Freedom of Belief:** Practiced the Quranic principle *"Let there be no compulsion in religion"*. Preached to Makkah pilgrims without forcing conversions.

* **Charter of Madinah:** Granted full religious freedom, civil rights, and equal treatment to Jewish tribes, requiring mutual defense of Madinah.

* **Christian Delegation of Najran:** Allowed a Christian delegation to perform their religious worship inside his mosque in Madinah.

### Inferences & Contemporary Application for Modern Communities

* **Protection of Religious Minorities:** Serves as a constitutional blueprint for modern Muslim-majority states to safeguard the civil rights, places of worship, and religious freedoms of non-Muslim citizens.

* **Interfaith Dialogue:** Encourages modern Muslim communities to engage in respectful dialogue, hospitality, and civic cooperation with other faith groups rather than fostering isolationism or hostility.

### **2. Conduct in Treaties & Honesty**

* **Treaty of Hudaibiyah ($6$ AH):** Agreed to $10$ years of peace with the Quraish despite harsh terms (e.g., returning Makkan converts while Makkan defectors were kept).

* **Case of Hazrat Abu Jandal (RA):** Sent Abu Jandal (RA) back to Makkah because the treaty terms were agreed upon, demonstrating strict honesty and discipline.

* **Trustworthiness (Al-Amin):** Returned Makkan belongings via Hazrat Ali (RA) during migration, protecting the property of those actively plotting his death.

### Inferences & Contemporary Application for Modern Communities

* **Sanctity of International Treaties:** Mandates that modern Muslim nations and individuals strictly honor international agreements, trade pacts, and diplomatic treaties, even when politically inconvenient.

* **Ethical Leadership & Moral Consistency:** Teaches that political and diplomatic strategy must never compromise core moral standards, honesty, and individual integrity.

### **3. Humane Attitude, Mercy, and Forgiveness**

* **Rules of Warfare:** Banned harming women, children, and praying worshippers during combat.

* **Prisoners of War:** Freed Badr captives who paid ransom or taught Muslim children. Pardoned Hunain captives out of respect for his foster tribe, Banu Saad.

* **Pardon of Ta'if & Makkah:** Forgave the people of Ta'if despite being pelted with stones. Declared a general amnesty at the Conquest of Makkah, forgiving lifelong enemies like Ikrima bin Abu Jahl, Wahshi, and Hind.

### Inferences & Contemporary Application for Modern Communities

* **International Humanitarian Law:** Provides a foundation for modern warfare ethics, setting clear precedents that parallel and predate Geneva Conventions regarding the protection of civilians and war captives.

* **Reconciliation over Retribution:** Demonstrates to modern leaders and nations that long-term stability and peace are achieved through magnanimity, conflict resolution, and restorative justice rather than systemic revenge.

### **4. Relations with Jewish Tribes of Madinah**

* **Initial Status:** Granted equal status and security under the Charter of Madinah. Relationships deteriorated as tribes mocked revelations, broke defensive pacts, and conspired with enemies.

* **Banu Qainuqa ($2$ AH):** Initiated fighting after Badr; exiled following a siege.

* **Banu Nadir ($4$ AH):** Plotted to assassinate the Prophet (PBUH) by dropping a boulder; exiled after a two-week siege.

* **Banu Qurayzah ($5$ AH):** Betrayed Madinah by siding with Quraish during the Battle of Trench. Their punishment (men executed, women/children enslaved) was determined by Saad bin Mu'adh according to the Old Testament.

* **Jews of Khaybar:** Allowed to live peacefully and cultivate their land in exchange for half of their agricultural output.

### Inferences & Contemporary Application for Modern Communities

* **Rule of Law & Proportionality:** Illustrates that state action or punitive measures must be based strictly on specific contractual violations, treason, or threats to national security—never on religious or racial prejudice.

* **Customary & Scriptural Arbitration:** Shows respect for local legal traditions and agreed-upon arbitration frameworks when resolving domestic or international disputes.
