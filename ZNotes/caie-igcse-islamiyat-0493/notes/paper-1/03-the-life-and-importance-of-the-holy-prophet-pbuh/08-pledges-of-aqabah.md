## Pledges of Aqabah

* The Prophet (PBUH), with Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) and Hazrat Ali (RA), sought a secure base by approaching annual pilgrims.

* **$11th$ Year of Prophethood ($620$ AD) – Initial Meeting:**

  * Met $6$ pilgrims from the Khazraj tribe at the valley of Aqabah near Mina.

  * Pilgrims knew of monotheism through local Jews and needed a neutral leader to resolve the Aws-Khazraj rivalry.

  * Key figures: As’ad bin Zaraarah, Auf bin Harith, Rafay bin Malik, Qatbah bin Amir, Uqbah bin Amir, and Jabir bin Abdullah.

  * All $6$ accepted Islam and promised to preach back home; no formal oath was taken yet.

* **Aftermath & Reaction:**

  * The Prophet (PBUH) appointed $12$ leaders (*Nuqaba*) and accepted their invitation to migrate.

  * Quraish gathered at *Dar-un-Nadwa* where Abu Jahl proposed assassination using multi-clan fighters.

  * Divinely warned of the plot, the Prophet (PBUH) instructed Muslims to migrate and prepared to depart.
