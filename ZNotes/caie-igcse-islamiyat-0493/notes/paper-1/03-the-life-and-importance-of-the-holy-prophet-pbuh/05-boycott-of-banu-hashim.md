## Boycott of Banu Hashim

* **Migrations to Abyssinia:** In the $5th$ year of Prophethood ($615$ AD), Allah revealed:

  > *"Good is for those who do good in this world and Allah's earth is spacious..."* (**Surah Az-Zumar $39$:$10$**)

  The Prophet (PBUH) permitted $12$ men and $4$ women to migrate to Abyssinia in Rajab. Due to escalated persecutions, a second batch ($83$ men and $19$ women, including Jafar bin Abi Talib, Asma, Umme Habiba, and Abdullah bin Masood) migrated in Shawwal. King Negus (Ashama) welcomed them, resulting in a major diplomatic defeat for the Quraish when their demands to return the refugees failed.

* **Strengthening of Muslims & Demands on Abu Talib:** The conversions of Hazrat Hamza (RA) and Hazrat Umar (RA) gave Muslims immense strength. Unable to halt the growth of Islam, the Quraish demanded that Abu Talib stop his nephew from preaching and withdraw tribal protection, or face a total social and financial boycott.

### Events & Life in Shib Abi Talib

* **The Pact ($616$ CE / $7$th Year of Prophethood):** The Quraish held a meeting at Banu Kinana to boycott the tribes of Banu Hashim and Banu Al-Muttalib (excluding Abu Lahab, Umme Jameel, and their sons). Abu Jahl convinced $40$ Quraish chiefs to sign a boycott document written by Mansoor bin Ikramah.

* **Confinement in the Valley:** The document was hung inside the Ka'abah to give it a sacred impression. Banu Hashim and Banu Al-Muttalib were expelled from their homes and forced into **Shib Abi Talib**, a narrow, rocky valley owned by Abu Talib on the outskirts of Makkah.

* **Conditions & Hardships:**

  * The siege was characterized by intense heat, hunger, and thirst. Food supplies were cut off, forcing Muslims to eat tree leaves and boiled leather.

  * Saad bin Abi Waqqas (RA) narrated:

    > *"During a night my foot touched something wet, I picked it up and ate without investigating what it was."*

  * Financial exploitation was rampant; Makkan traders were ordered to double their prices for the boycotted tribes.

  * Generous relatives, particularly those related to Hazrat Khadijah (RA), occasionally smuggled food into the valley. Hazrat Khadijah (RA) spent all her personal wealth to sustain the community.

  * The public growth and propagation of Islam came to a complete halt during these three years.

### Ending of the Boycott & Year of Grief (*Aam al-Huzn*)

* **Opposition to the Boycott:** After $3$ years of siege, sympathetic Quraishi leaders—Hisham bin Amr, Al-Mutim, Zuhayr bin Umaiya, Abu Bukhtari, and Zamah—protested the cruel boycott and demanded negotiations with Abu Talib.

* **Miracle of the Termites:** Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was divine informed that termites had eaten the entire boycott document inside the Ka'abah, except for the name of Allah. Abu Talib informed the Quraish of this miracle, and upon inspection inside the Ka'abah, they found only Allah's name intact.

* **Lifting of the Boycott ($619$ CE / $10$th Year of Prophethood):** Shocked by this divine sign, the Quraish officially ended the boycott. However, many stubborn Quraishi leaders still refused to accept Islam, fulfilling Allah's verse:

  > *"Yet, whenever they see a sign, they turn away, saying, 'Same old magic!'"* (**Surah Al-Qamar $54$:$2$**)

* **Deaths of Key Supporters:**

  * Six months after the end of the confinement in Rajab ($10th$ year of Prophethood), Abu Talib fell ill and passed away at $80$ years of age.

  * Two months later in Ramadan, Hazrat Khadijah (RA) passed away at $65$ years of age.

  * Having lost his two greatest earthly protectors and supporters, the Prophet (PBUH) designated this tragic period as the **Year of Grief** (*Aam al-Huzn*).
