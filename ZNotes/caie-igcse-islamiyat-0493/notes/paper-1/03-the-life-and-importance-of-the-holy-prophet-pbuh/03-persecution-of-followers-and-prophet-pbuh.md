## Persecution of Followers and Prophet(PBUH)

### Persecution of Followers

**Meeting at Dar-ul-Nadwa & Insecurities of Quraish**

* **Financial, Social, and Religious Insecurities:** Open preaching threatened the authority, political power, and financial gains of the Quraish derived from idol worship at the Ka'abah.

* **Resolution at Dar-ul-Nadwa:** The leaders of the Quraish convened at their assembly hall (**Dar-ul-Nadwa**) and resolved to systematically oppose Islam and brutally persecute converts to force them to renounce their faith.

* **Primary Targets:** Torture focused heavily on slaves and individuals without strong tribal protection.

### Systematic Torture of Specific Companions

To avoid inter-tribal warfare, chiefs tortured converts within their own clans, while masters brutalized slave converts:

* **Hazrat Uthman bin Affan (RA):** Bound in a mat of palm leaves by his uncle (chief of Banu Umayya), who set fire beneath it to suffocate him.

* **Mus’ab bin Umair (RA):** Deprived of food and water by his mother upon conversion, expelled from his luxurious home, and left with severely burnt and scarred skin.

* **Khabbab bin Aratt (RA):** Branded with red-hot iron rods by his female master, dragged by his hair, and forced to lie bare-backed on burning coals with heavy rocks on his chest.

* **Ammar bin Yasir (RA) & Family:** Beaten severely on burning sand by Banu Makhzoom. His mother, **Sumayyah (RA)**, was killed by Abu Jahl piercing her stomach, making her the **first female martyr of Islam**.

* **Hazrat Bilal bin Rabah (RA):** Tied with ropes and dragged through the streets by his master, Umayya bin Khalaf, forced onto burning sand/coals, and weighed down with boulders on his chest. He was bought and freed by Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA).

* **Hazrat Talha (RA) & Hazrat Zubair (RA):** Dragged through public streets and subjected to suffocation.

* **Female Converts:** **Hazrat Zunairah (RA)** was tortured until she lost her eyesight. **An-Nahdiyah (RA)** was subjected to continuous physical abuse by her master.

* **Other Methods:** Selected companions were thrown directly into animal enclosures.

### Escalation, Migrations, and Social Boycott

* **Concealment of Faith:** The Prophet (PBUH) initially advised Muslims to practice their faith in secret to avoid extreme danger.

* **$1st$ Migration to Abyssinia ($5th$ Year of Prophethood):** Due to unbearable persecution, $12$ men and $4$ women—including **Hazrat Uthman (RA)**, **Ruqayya (RA)**, and **Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA)**—were permitted to migrate to Abyssinia, where they were welcomed safely by the Negus.

* **$2nd$ Migration to Abyssinia:** Enraged by the safe refuge found by the Muslims, the Quraish intensified their torture, leading to a second migration of $82$–$83$ Muslims to Abyssinia.

* **Social and Economic Boycott ($7th$ Year of Prophethood):** The Quraish imposed a complete trade and social ban on Banu Hashim and Banu Muttalib. Confined to the narrow valley of **Shib Abi Talib** for three years, Muslims suffered extreme famine, surviving on tree leaves and boiled leather.

* **Steadfastness:** Despite severe hardship, the early Muslims displayed extraordinary patience (*Sabr*), courage, and unshakable faith (*Iman*).

### Persecution of Prophet(PBUH)

**Background**

* **Hostility of Abu Lahab:** When the Prophet (PBUH) warned the Makkans from Mount Safa, his uncle Abu Lahab rudely replied, *"Perish you all the day. You called us for such a thing."*

* **Reasons for Rejection:** Condemning idol worship outraged the Quraish, who refused to accept being called *"people who had gone astray."* They feared that adopting monotheism (*Tawheed*) would destroy their social authority, wealth, and status as custodians of the Ka'abah.

* **Delegation to Abu Talib:** Quraishi leaders approached Abu Talib complaining, *"Your nephew curses our gods and finds fault with them..."*, but Abu Talib refused to stop the Prophet (PBUH).

### **Systematic Persecution Campaign**

When the Quraish realized preaching could not be stopped, they formed a committee of $25$ influential leaders headed by Abu Lahab to forcibly oppose the Prophet (PBUH) and inflict physical, mental, emotional, and financial torture:

* **Emotional & Family Attacks:** Abu Lahab forced his two sons to divorce their wives, **Ruqayyah (RA)** and **Umm Kulthum (RA)**, the daughters of the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Mockery & Slander:**

  * Accused of being insane or possessed by jinns:

    > *"They say, O you to whom the Reminder is revealed! You must be insane..."* (**Surah Al-Hijr $15$:$6$**) and *"And those who disbelieve would almost strike you with their eyes when they hear the reminder, and they say: Most surely he is mad."* (**Surah Al-Qalam 68:51**)

  * Abu Jahl mocked and laughed at the Prophet (PBUH) upon the death of his infant son, calling him **'Abtar'** (cut off from male lineage).

* **Physical Harassment by Umme Jameel:** The wife of Abu Lahab threw bundles of sharp thorns tied with palm-leaf ropes onto the Prophet's (PBUH) path to cause physical injury.

  * **Divine Warning:**

    > *"He will burn in a Fire of flames and his wife the carrier of firewood, around her neck is a rope of twisted fibre."* (**Surah Al-Lahab $111$:$3$–$5$**)

* **Physical Abuse by Abu Jahl & Banu Makhzum:**

  * Abu Jahl followed the Prophet (PBUH) during pilgrimage season, shouting and ridiculing him in front of visiting tribes.

  * While the Prophet (PBUH) was praying near the Ka'abah, Abu Jahl and his companions placed the bloody entrails of a slaughtered camel on his back while prostrating, laughing mockingly. His daughter, **Hazrat Fatima (RA)**, came and removed the filth from his back.

* **Invocation of Divine Wrath:** The Prophet (PBUH) invoked Allah’s judgment against key persecutors including Abu Jahl, Umayya bin Khalaf, Utba bin Rabiah, and Shayba bin Rabiah.

* **Social & Economic Boycott:** Quraishi leaders imposed a complete $3$-year social and financial boycott on the Prophet (PBUH), his family, and his followers.

### Response & Steadfastness of the Prophet (PBUH)

* **Unshakable Patience (*Sabr*):** Despite facing continuous mental, physical, financial, and emotional attacks, the Prophet (PBUH) never retaliated or stopped preaching.

* **Encouragement to Followers:** He comforted suffering companions, telling them:

  > *"Be patient, O family of Yasir, you will surely find your abode in Paradise."*

* **Firm Belief:** Though small in number, the Prophet (PBUH) and his early followers faced all opposition with immense courage, patience, and absolute trust in Allah (*Tawakkul*).
