## Preaching in Makkah

### Secret Preaching

($1$st & $2$nd Years of Prophethood / $610$–$611$ AD)

* **Preaching to Close Contacts:** The Prophet (PBUH) began inviting those closest to him to Islam in secret.

* **Earliest Converts:**

  * **Hazrat Khadijah (RA):** First person to learn of his mission and the first adult female convert.

  * **Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA):** First adult male convert.

  * **Hazrat Ali (RA):** Raised in the Prophet’s household, became the first child convert.

  * **Hazrat Zaid bin Harithah (RA):** First freed slave convert.

  * **Other Early Converts:** Umm-e-Fazal (RA) and her sisters; Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) (brother of Hazrat Ali) and Zubair bin Awwam (RA).

  * **Converts via Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA):** Talha bin Ubaidullah (RA), Uthman bin Affan (RA), Sa'ad bin Abi Waqqas (RA), and Arqam bin Abi al-Arqam (RA). In total, around $40$ people accepted Islam during this phase.

* **Secret Worship & Dar-e-Arqam:**

  * Muslims prayed twice daily in secluded mountain valleys to avoid ridicule from the polytheists.

  * Following a small physical clash with the Quraish, the Muslims established their headquarters at the house of Hazrat Arqam (RA) (**Dar-e-Arqam**).

  * **Dar-e-Arqam** became known as the **first school and community center of Islam**, where the Prophet (PBUH) taught revelations and instructed new converts.

* **Private Invitation to Kindred (Banu Hashim):**

  * Allah commanded the Prophet (PBUH):

    > *"And warn your tribe of near kindred."* (**Surah Ash-Shu'ara $26$:$214$**)

  * With help from Hazrat Ali (RA), he hosted a feast for the elders of Banu Hashim.

  * Abu Lahab interrupted and attempted to silence him. Despite this, the Prophet (PBUH) shared his message and the details of Cave Hira:

    > *"I celebrate Allah’s praise, I seek His help, I believe in Him, I bear witness that there is no god to be worshipped but Allah, with no associate..."*

  * Hazrat Ali (RA) offered his unconditional support. Following the feast, Hazrat Safiah (RA) accepted Islam.

  * Abu Talib offered tribal protection, promising:

    > *"Do what you have been ordered. I shall protect and defend you."*

### Open Preaching

* **Divine Command for Public Preaching:**

  * Allah revealed:

    > *"Therefore, proclaim openly Allah’s message, that which you are commanded, and turn away from the polytheists."* (**Surah Al-Hijr $15$:$94$**)

* **Public Declaration at Mount Safa:**

  * The Prophet (PBUH) climbed Mount Safa and called out loudly to assemble the Makkan tribes.

  * **Character Approval:** He established his integrity first by asking:

    > *"Will you believe if I say that there were some horsemen in the valley planning to attack you?"* The crowd responded unanimously in the affirmative, confirming his honesty.

  * **The Warning:** He declared:

    > *"I am here to warn you before a severe torment reaches you."*

* **Hostile Reaction of Abu Lahab:**

  * Abu Lahab cursed and insulted him publicly.

  * In response, Allah revealed:

    > *"Perish the two hands of Abu Lahab..."* (**Surah Al-Lahab $111$:$1$**)

  * Despite the insult, a few members accepted the faith, recognizing it as the monotheistic religion of Ibrahim.

* **Reasons for Quraish Opposition:**

  * As custodians of the Ka'abah, the Quraish feared losing their social authority, political power, and financial gains tied to idol worship.

  * They felt threatened socially, religiously, and economically.

* **Delegation to Abu Talib:**

  * The Quraish approached Abu Talib, demanding he make his nephew stop preaching.

  * Abu Talib politely turned them away, allowing the Prophet (PBUH) to continue preaching Islam steadfastly.
