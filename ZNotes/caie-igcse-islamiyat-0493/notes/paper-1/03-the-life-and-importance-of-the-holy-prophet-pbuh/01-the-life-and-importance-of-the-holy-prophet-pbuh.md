# The Life and Importance of the Holy Prophet (PBUH)

## Early Life of Prophet

### Birth, Childhood, and Upbringing

* **Year of the Elephant** ($570$ AD)**:** Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born in $570$ AD as an orphan; his father, Abdullah, had died four months prior to his birth. His birth year is known as the *"Year of the Elephant"* because Abraha, the Yemenite ruler, marched to destroy the Ka'abah with an army of elephants. Allah protected the Ka'abah by sending swarms of small birds (*Ababil*) that dropped stones upon the army, forcing their retreat.

* **Foster Motherhood (Haleema Sadia):** Following Makkan custom, the infant Prophet (PBUH) was entrusted to Hazrat Haleema Sadia, a Bedouin wet nurse, so he could grow up in a healthy desert environment and acquire the purest Arabic dialect.

* **Splitting of the Chest:** Haleema cared for him for roughly five years like a mother. Following the miraculous event where angels opened and cleansed his chest, she grew concerned for his safety and returned him to his biological mother, Hazrat Amna (RA).

* **Loss of Mother (Abwa):** At age 6, his mother took him to Yathrib (Madinah) to visit his father’s grave. On their return journey, Hazrat Amna fell ill and passed away at Abwa. The maid, Umm-e-Aimen, brought the young orphan back to Makkah and placed him in the care of his grandfather, Abdul Muttalib.

* **Loss of Grandfather:** Abdul Muttalib showered him with deep affection for two years until his own death when the Prophet (PBUH) was $8$ years old. Guardianship then passed to his paternal uncle, Abu Talib, the newly appointed chief of Banu Hashim.

### Youth, Trade Journeys, and Social Compacts

* **Guardianship of Abu Talib:** Abu Talib and his wife, Fatima bint Asad, cared for the Prophet (PBUH) with extreme love and warmth, filling the emotional void of his childhood.

* **Monk Bahira at Bosra (Age $12$):** During a trade journey to Syria with his uncle, they met a Christian monk named Bahira in Bosra. Bahira observed miraculous signs: trees and stones bowing down, a cloud shading the boy, and the Mark of Prophethood between his shoulder blades. Bahira foretold:

  > *"God will send him with a message that will be mercy for all mankind."*

* **Occupation as a Shepherd:** In his youth, the Prophet (PBUH) tended sheep across the Makkan hills, a experience that cultivated patience and humility.

  * **Authentic Reference:**

    > *"Allah sent no messenger who was not a shepherd; Moses was a shepherd, Daud (A.S) was also a shepherd."* (Sahih Muslim)

* **Sacrilegious Wars (*Harb al-Fijar*):** Fought between 580–590 AD during the sacred months ($1$st Muharram, $7$th Rajab, $11$th Dhul-Q'adah, $12$th Dhul-Hijjah) at the Ukaz fair. In the war of *Fajar Barraz* between Banu Kinana and Banu Hawazain, the young Prophet (PBUH) refused to shed blood, assisting his uncle solely in a logistical role by collecting stray arrows. This exposed him early to battle tactics.

* **Alliance of Peace (*Hilf al-Fudul*):** Following the sacrilegious wars, representatives from Banu Hashim, Banu Zehra, and Banu Asad gathered at the house of Abdullah bin Jada’an to form an alliance aimed at protecting victims of injustice in Makkah. The Prophet (PBUH) attended and valued it immensely throughout his life:

  * **Authentic Reference:**

    > *"I witnessed a confederacy in the house of Abdullah bin Jadaan. It was more appealing to me than a herd of red camels. I would respond positively to attending such a meeting if I were invited."*

### Marriage, Dispute of Black Stone, and First Revelation

* **Titles of *Al-Sadiq* and *Al-Ameen*:** Renowned for his honesty, integrity, and avoidance of Makkan vices, the community honored him with the titles *Al-Sadiq* (The Truthful) and *Al-Ameen* (The Trustworthy).

* **Trade for Hazrat Khadijah (RA) and Marriage (Age $25$):** Impressed by his reputation, a wealthy Makkan widow, Hazrat Khadijah (RA), hired him to lead her trade caravan to Syria alongside her servant, Maisarah. Upon their return with exceptional profits, Maisarah reported his immaculate conduct and honesty. Deeply impressed, Khadijah (RA) sent a marriage proposal.

  * The Prophet (PBUH) accepted after consulting Abu Talib.

  * Ages at marriage: Prophet (PBUH) was $25$; Hazrat Khadijah (RA) was $40$.

  * They had 6 children; their two sons died in infancy, and only Hazrat Fatima (RA) outlived him.

* **Arbitration of the Black Stone (*Hajar al-Aswad*) (Age $35$):** Flash floods damaged the Ka'abah, requiring a complete rebuild. When rebuilding reached the placement of the Black Stone, tribal leaders nearly drew swords over who would enjoy the honor. They agreed to let the next person entering the sanctuary arbitrate.

  * Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) entered first.

  * He spread a cloak, placed the Black Stone in the center, instructed all tribal chiefs to lift the cloak together, and then placed the stone into the wall with his own hands, averting tribal bloodshed.

* **First Revelation ($610$ AD / Age $40$):** Seeking spiritual seclusion from Makkan idolatry, he meditated in the Cave of Hira on Mount Al-Noor. In Ramadan $610$ AD, Angel Jibra’eel appeared, initiating the divine message that marked his appointment as the Messenger of Allah
