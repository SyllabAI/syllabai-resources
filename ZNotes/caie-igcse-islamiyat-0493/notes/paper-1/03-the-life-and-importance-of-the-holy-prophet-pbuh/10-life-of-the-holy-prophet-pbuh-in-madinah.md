## Life of The Holy Prophet(PBUH) in Madinah

### 1st Year after Hijrah ($1$ AH)

* **Construction of Masjid-e-Nabawi:** Built on a plot belonging to two orphans, Sahl and Suhail; price paid by Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA). Built with palm trunks, leaf roofs, and unbaked bricks.

  *Quranic Reference:* *"There is a mosque whose foundation was laid on the first day on piety..."* \[Surah At-Tawbah $9$:$108$\].

* **Introduction of Azaan:** Dreamt by Abdullah bin Zayd and Hazrat Umar (RA). Hazrat Bilal (RA) appointed as the first Muezzin.

* **System of Brotherhood (Mwakhat):** Paired $45$ Muhajireen (Emigrants) with $45$ Ansar (Helpers) to share homes and fields (e.g., Saad bin Rabi & Abdul Rahman bin Awf).

  *Quranic Reference:* *"Those who believed and adopted exile and fought... as well as those who gave them asylum and aid, these are all friends and protectors, of one another."* \[Surah Al-Anfal $8$:$72$\].

* **Charter of Madinah (Treaty with Jews):** Agreement signed with local Jewish tribes (Banu Qainuqa, Banu Nadir, Banu Quraizah) ensuring religious freedom, equal civil rights, joint defense of Madinah, and declaring the Prophet (PBUH) as the Head of State.

### **$2$nd Year after Hijrah ($2$ AH)**

* **Obligations:** Fasting in Ramadan and Zakat made compulsory.

* **Change of Qibla:** Direction of prayer shifted from Masjid Al-Aqsa (Jerusalem) to the Ka'abah (Makkah).

* **Permission for Jihad:** Granted via divine revelation due to ongoing Quraish threats.

  *Quranic Reference:* *"Permission to fight is given to those who are fought against because they have been wronged..."* \[Surah Al-Hajj $22$:$39$\].

* **Battle of Badr ($17$th Ramadan $2$ AH):**

  * **Forces:** $313$ ill-equipped Muslims vs. $1,000$ well-armed Quraish led by Abu Jahl.

  * **Key Details:** Rain fell before the battle (smoothing the ground for Muslims). The Prophet (PBUH) prayed: *"O Allah if this band of Muslims perishes, You will not be worshipped on earth."*

  * **Help of Angels:** Allah sent $1,000$ angels.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"I will help you with a thousand of the angels, ranks on ranks."* \[Surah Al-Anfal $8$:$9$\].

  * **Outcome:** Abu Jahl killed by Ansari brothers (Muadh & Muawwidh); Umayyah bin Khalaf killed by Bilal (RA). $70$ Quraish killed, $70$ taken captive, 14 Muslims martyred. Captives freed for ransom, or by teaching $10$ Muslim children to read/write.

* **Exile of Banu Qainuqa:** First Jewish tribe exiled following a $15$-day siege after breaching the Charter by charging interest, humiliating a Muslim woman, and killing a Muslim man.

### **$3$rd Year after Hijrah ($3$ AH)**

* **Battle of Uhud:**

  * **Forces:** $3,000$ Quraish (financed by Abu Sufyan's trade caravan) vs. $1,000$ Muslims. Abdullah bin Ubayy (leader of hypocrites) deserted with $300$ men, leaving $700$ Muslims.

  * **Key Details:** Prophet (PBUH) stationed $50$ archers on Jabal-e-Rumat under Abdullah bin Jubair. Initial victory caused $40$ archers to abandon their post for war booty. Khalid bin Al-Waleed launched a surprise attack from the rear.

  * **Martyrdoms & Injuries:** Hazrat Hamza (RA) killed and mutilated by Wahshi/Hind. Mus'ab bin Umair (RA) martyred. Rumour spread that Prophet (PBUH) was killed. Prophet (PBUH) was injured (broken teeth, helmet rings pressed into face).

    *Quranic Reference:* *"Muhammad is no more than a messenger; many were the messengers that passed away before him..."* \[Surah Ali 'Imran $3$:$144$\].

  * **Outcome:** $70$ Muslims martyred. Muslims retreated to Mount Uhud; Quraish left without capturing Madinah.

### **$4$th Year after Hijrah ($4$ AH)**

* **Exile of Banu Nadir:** Second Jewish tribe expelled after plotting to assassinate the Prophet (PBUH) by dropping a boulder on him from a rooftop.

* Exiled after a $14$-day siege; settled in Khaybar.

### **$5$th Year after Hijrah ($5$ AH)**

* **Battle of the Trench (Ahzab/Tribes):**

  * **Forces:** $10,000$ combined allied forces (Quraish, Banu Nadir, Ghatfan) vs. $3,000$ Muslims.

  * **Strategy:** Salman Al-Farsi (RA) suggested digging a deep trench on the open side of Madinah. Took $20$ days to dig during extreme severe weather and famine.

  * **Key Events:** Amr bin Abdwud crossed the trench and was killed by Hazrat Ali (RA). Nu'aim bin Mas'ood secretly converted to Islam and broke the alliance between Ghatfan and Banu Quraizah.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"When they came upon you from above you and from below you, and when eyes grew wild..."* \[Surah Al-Ahzab $33$:$10$\].

  * **Outcome:** Allah sent a harsh sandstorm and severe cold, forcing the enemy forces to retreat after a $20$\+ day siege.

* **Punishment of Banu Quraizah:** The last Jewish tribe in Madinah betrayed Muslims during the siege. Besieged for $25$ days. Appointed Saad bin Mu'adh (RA) as arbitrator. Judgment passed according to the Torah: $600$–$700$ men executed, women/children taken captive.

### **$6$th Year after Hijrah ($6$ AH)**

* **Treaty of Hudaibiyyah:**

  * **Context:** Prophet (PBUH) set out with $1,400$ unarmed companions in Ihram to perform Umrah after seeing a dream. Blocked at Hudaibiyyah by Quraish.

  * **Bait-e-Rizwan:** Rumour spread that envoy Hazrat Uthman (RA) was murdered; companions pledged death-defying loyalty under a tree.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"Allah's good pleasure was on the believers when they swore fealty to you under the tree..."* \[Surah Al-Fath $48$:$18$\].

  * **Terms:** $10$-year truce; Muslims return without Umrah this year; Makkans fleeing to Madinah returned, but Muslims fleeing to Makkah kept; tribes free to ally with either side. Signed by Suhayl bin Amr. Abu Jandal (RA) returned to Makkah to uphold the treaty.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"Indeed We have granted you a manifest victory."* \[Surah Al-Fath $48$:$1$\].

### **$7$th Year after Hijrah ($7$ AH)**

* **Letters to Foreign Rulers:**

  * **Byzantine Emperor (Heraclius):** Polite, acknowledged prophecy, did not convert.

  * **Negus of Abyssinia, Rulers of Oman & Bahrain:** Accepted Islam.

  * **Muqawqis of Egypt:** Sent gifts (including Maria al-Qibtiyya).

  * **Chosroes II of Persia:** Tore the letter; Prophet (PBUH) predicted his empire would be torn apart.

* **Khyber Expedition:**

  * **Context:** March against $20$ fortified Jewish strongholds $70$ miles north of Madinah due to ongoing conspiracies.

  * **Events:** $1,700$ Muslims besieged forts. Fort Qamus resisted until Hazrat Ali (RA) was given leadership, killed Jewish leader Marhab, and captured it.

  * **Terms:** Jews allowed to stay on land in exchange for paying $50$% of agricultural yield.

### **$8$th Year after Hijrah ($8$ AH)**

* **Battle of Mu'tah:**

  * **Cause:** Muslim envoy Harith bin Umair murdered by Ghassanid chief Sharhabeel.

  * **Events:** $3,000$ Muslims fought $200,000$ Byzantine/allied troops. Commanders Zaid bin Haritha, Jafar bin Abi Talib, and Abdullah bin Rawaha were martyred. Khalid bin Waleed took command, broke $9$ swords, and skillfully retreated.

* **Conquest of Makkah:**

  * **Cause:** Banu Bakr (allied with Quraish) attacked Banu Khuza'a (allied with Muslims), violating the Hudaibiyyah Pact.

  * **Events:** $10,000$ Muslims marched. Abu Sufyan converted. Peaceful entry; $360$ idols inside Ka'abah destroyed.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"Truth has come and falsehood has vanished..."* \[Surah Al-Isra $17$:$81$\].

  * **General Amnesty:** Prophet (PBUH) pardoned Makkans: *"I speak to you as Joseph (Yousuf) spoke to his brothers: No reproach upon you this day. Go for you are free men."*

* **Battle of Hunain & Siege of Ta'if:**

  * **Battle:** $12,000$ Muslims ambushed in narrow pass by Hawazin and Thaqif archers under Malik bin Auf. Muslims regrouped after initial disarray and won.

    *Quranic Reference:* *"On the day of Hunain... your great numbers elated you, but they availed you not..."* \[Surah At-Tawbah $9$:$25$-$26$\].

  * **Siege:** $1$-month siege of Ta'if lifted peacefully; Thaqif later converted voluntarily.

### **$9$th Year after Hijrah ($9$ AH)**

* **Tabuk Expedition (Battle of Straitness):**

  * **Cause:** Reports of Roman build-up on the Syrian border.

  * **Events:** Scorching summer, severe drought/famine. Major donations: Abu Bakr gave all wealth, Umar gave half, Uthman gave $1,000$ dinars and $300$ camels.

  * **Outcome:** $30,000$ Muslims reached Tabuk; Byzantine forces withdrew. Prophet (PBUH) stayed $20$ days signing peace pacts. Hypocrites exposed and Masjid Dirar demolished.

* **Year of Deputations (Aam-ul-Wufood):** Tribes across Arabia sent delegations to Madinah to accept Islam.

### **$10$th Year after Hijrah ($10$ AH)**

* **Farewell Pilgrimage (Hajjat-ul-Wida):** Prophet (PBUH) performed his only Hajj with $114,000$–$124,000$ companions.

* **Farewell Sermon (Mount Arafat):**

  * Declared sanctity of life, property, and honor.

  * Abolished interest/usury (Riba) and blood feuds.

  * Ordered kind treatment and equal rights for women and slaves.

  * Declared universal racial equality: *"An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab..."*

  * Left behind two guides: **The Quran and Sunnah**.

    *Completion Verse:* *"This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you..."* \[Surah Al-Ma'idah $5$:$3$\].

### **$11$th Year after Hijrah ($11$ AH)**

* **Final Days:** Prophet (PBUH) fell ill in Safar. Stayed in Hazrat Ayesha’s (RA) apartment. Appointed Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) as Imam for prayers.

* **Demise:** Passed away on $12$th Rabi-ul-Awwal, $11$ AH.

* **Reaction:** Hazrat Umar (RA) was overwhelmed; Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) calmed the community reciting:

  *Quranic Reference:* *"Muhammad is no more than a messenger; many were the messengers that passed away before him..."* \[Surah Ali 'Imran $3$:$144$\].

  Abu Bakr (RA) stated: *"Whosoever worshipped Muhammad, let him know that Muhammad has died. But whosoever worshipped Allah, Allah is alive and never dies."*
