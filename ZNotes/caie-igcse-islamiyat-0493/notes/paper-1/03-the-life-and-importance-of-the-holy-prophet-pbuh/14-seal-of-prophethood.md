## Seal of Prophethood

### Significance of the Finality of Prophethood According to the Qur'an

* **Qur'anic Proof:** The Qur'an explicitly states that prophethood ended with Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) through a single clear verse:

> *"Muhammad is not the father of any man among you, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets."*

> — Surah Al-Ahzaab ($33$:$40$)

### Significance According to the Hadith (Sunnah)

* **The Parable of the Cornerstone:** The Prophet (pbuh) explained his position as the final link in the chain of Prophets using the analogy of a house:

  * He compared previous prophets to a person who built, furnished, and decorated a house, leaving a blank space for a single missing brick/cornerstone in one corner.

  * Visitors admired the completed house but noted it was incomplete without that final brick.

  * The Prophet (PBUH) stated: *"Here I am like that cornerstone, the place of which was left unoccupied. Thus, I am the last of the Prophets."*

* **Universal Mission:** Previous prophets were sent specifically to their own respective nations, whereas Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was sent as a universal Messenger for all of humanity:

*"Every Prophet who preceded me was sent especially to his own people, but I have been sent as a Prophet to all mankind."*

— Sahih Bukhari

### Importance as a Religious Obligation

* **End of Divine Institution:** Scholars of the Qur'an and Sunnah emphasize that the divine institution of Prophethood ended completely after Prophet Muhammad (pbuh).

* **Perpetual Religion:** The religion (*Islam*) given to Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) is finalized, perfect, and intended to remain perpetual until the end of time.

* **Fundamental Obligation:** Believing in the finality of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)’s Prophethood is a mandatory matter of religious obligation for every Muslim.

* **Increased Responsibility:** The finality of Prophethood places greater responsibilities on all Muslims to preserve, practice, and convey the divine message to mankind.
