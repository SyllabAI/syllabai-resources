## Migration to Madinah

* **Causes:** Severe persecution by the Quraish, the pledge of allegiance by Madinan tribes (Pledges of Aqabah), and divine permission from Allah to migrate.

  *Quranic Reference:* *"Permission to fight \[or migrate\] is given to those who are fought against because they have been wronged..."* \[Surah Al-Hajj $22$:$39$\]

* **Plot of the Quraish:** The Quraish met at *Dar-un-Nadwa* and decided to select one youth from each tribe to assassinate the Prophet (PBUH) together in his sleep.

* **The Night of Departure:**

  * Hazrat Ali (RA) slept in the Prophet’s (PBUH) bed to return the people's trusts/belongings.

  * The Prophet (PBUH) threw a handful of dust toward the assassins surrounding his house and recited Surah Yaseen ($36$:$9$) to walk past them unseen.

* **Cave of Thawr (Saur):**

  * The Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) hid in the Cave of Thawr for $3$ nights.

  * When the Quraish tracked them to the entrance of the cave, Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) grew anxious. The Prophet (PBUH) reassured him: *"Do not grieve, indeed Allah is with us."* \[Surah At-Tawbah $9$:$40$\]

  * A spider spun a web

  * A pigeon laid eggs in a tree near by

  * Acacia tree branches grew to cover the cave entrance

  * Due to these miracles, the Quraish didnt even thought of entering the cave and went back

* **Journey to Madinah:**

  * Guided by an expert non-Muslim guide, Abdullah bin Urayqit, taking an unconventional coastal route.

  * **Suraqah bin Malik:** Pursued them for the bounty of $100$ camels, but his horse’s legs sank into the hard sand three times. He asked for forgiveness and turned back.

* **Arrival at Quba:**

  * Stopped at Quba (outskirts of Madinah) for a few days.

  * Built **Masjid Quba**, the first mosque in Islamic history.

* **Entry into Madinah:**

  * Arrived in Madinah to a grand welcome; the people sang *Tala'al Badru 'Alayna*.

  * The Prophet's camel (*Qaswa*) stopped at the land belonging to two orphans (Sahl and Suhail), which became the site for Masjid-e-Nabawi.
