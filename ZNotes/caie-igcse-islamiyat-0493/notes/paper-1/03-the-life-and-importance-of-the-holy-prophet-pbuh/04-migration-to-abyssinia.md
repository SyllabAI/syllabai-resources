## Migration to Abyssinia

* **Escalation of Persecution:** As tortures claimed lives and escalated daily, the early followers turned to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) for a solution. The Prophet (PBUH) sought divine guidance to find a safe refuge for the suffering believers.

* **Divine Permission:** In the $5$th year of prophethood ($615$ AD), Allah revealed:

  > *"Good is for those who do good in this world and Allah's earth is spacious..."* (**Surah Az-Zumar $39$:$10$**)

### The First Migration to Abyssinia ($615$ AD / $5$th Year of Prophethood)

* **Departure:** In the month of Rajab, the Prophet (PBUH) permitted a group of $12$ men and $4$ women to migrate to Abyssinia (modern-day Ethiopia).

* **Selection of Abyssinia:**

  * Ruled by King Negus (Ashamah), a just Christian ruler knowledgeable in divine scriptures and angels.

  * Reached via a sea route (Red Sea) instead of a land route to evade Makkan pursuers.

* **Notables in the First Batch:** Hazrat Uthman bin Affan (RA), his wife Ruqayyah (RA) (daughter of the Prophet PBUH), Abdur Rehman bin Auf (RA), Zubair bin Awwam (RA), and Umm Salama (RA).

* **Journey & Arrival:** The group departed from the port of Shuibah across the Red Sea. Quraish pursuers failed to capture them, and King Negus welcomed them warmly, providing peace and religious freedom.

* **False Rumors & Temporary Return:** Outraged Makkans intensified local persecutions. Soon after, false rumors reached Abyssinia claiming mass conversions in Makkah, causing some refugees to return. Upon discovering the rumors were false, persecution hit unprecedented levels.

### The Second Migration & Diplomatic Battle at Negus's Court

* **Departure:** In the month of Shawwal, a larger group of $83$ men and $19$ women migrated to Abyssinia.

* **Notables in the Second Batch:** Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA), Asma bint Umais (RA), Umm Habiba (RA), and Abdullah bin Masood (RA).

* **Quraish Delegation:** The Quraish dispatched Amr bin Aas and Abdullah bin Rabiah—two skilled debaters with prior personal ties to Negus—carrying valuable gifts (especially leather goods) for the King and his court.

* **Arguments of the Quraish:** In court, Amr bin Aas demanded the extradition of the Muslims, claiming they were a mix of runaway slaves belonging to Makkan masters and rebellious free men who abandoned their ancestral faith.

* **Negus’s Stance:** Negus refused to extradite anyone without hearing their side, stating that free individuals move at their own will and slave status required proper verification.

### Defense by Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) & Divine Revelation

* **Refusal to Bow:** When summoned, the Muslims did not bow to the King. Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) declared:

  > *"We only bow down to Allah, Muhammad (PBUH) is the messenger of Allah and we do not worship him."*

* **The Miracle of the Qur'an:**

  * When Negus asked for the Prophet (PBUH)’s miracle, Jafar (RA) stated that his miracle was the Holy Qur'an.

  * Amr bin Aas mocked the idea of following a book written by an unlettered person.

  * Jafar (RA) delivered a passionate plea referencing Negus's justice, explaining that just as Allah spoke to Jesus and Moses through revelation, He now spoke to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) through the Qur'an.

  * Deeply moved, King Negus uttered his famous words:

    > *"These words and the words of the Bible are the rays of the same light."*

* **The Interrogation on Jesus and Mary:**

  * Attempting a second trap, Amr bin Aas urged Negus to ask the Muslims about their beliefs regarding Jesus and Mary, accusing them of blasphemy.

  * Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) recited verses from **Surah Maryam ($19$:$16$–$21$)**, detailing the miraculous virgin birth of Isa (AS) and the purity of Maryam (RA).

### The Verdict of King Negus

* **Emotional Impact:** The recitation moved the entire court; tears flowed down the face of King Negus.

* **Historical Declaration:** Negus drew a thin line on the ground and proclaimed that the difference between Islam and Christianity was no greater than that line.

* **Final Ruling:** Negus rejected the Quraishi delegation, refused to hand over the refugees, and granted the Muslims permanent sanctuary and freedom of worship in Abyssinia for as long as they wished.
