## The Muhajireen and The Ansar

### The Emigrants (*Muhajireen*)

* **Definition:** A *Muhajir* is a person who leaves their place of residence to live elsewhere. As a term, *Muhajireen* refers to the early Makkan Muslims who migrated to Abyssinia and later to Madina due to severe persecution and torture by the Makkans.

* **Early Migration to Abyssinia:** In the fifth year of Prophethood, when Makkan torture grew unbearable, the Prophet (pbuh) advised Muslims to move to Abyssinia (Ethiopia). A small band including Hazrat Uthman and his wife Ruqaiyyah (daughter of the Prophet) migrated first, followed later by a larger group.

* **Migration to Madina (*Hijrah*):**

  * When persecution worsened, Allah commanded migration to Madina. Muslims migrated quietly in small groups.

  * The Prophet (pbuh) left with Abu Bakr while Ali slept in the Prophet’s bed before joining them at Quba.

  * Migration was made compulsory (*Farz*) for capable converts between the initial command of Hijrah and the Conquest of Makkah (after which the obligation was removed).

* **Sacrifices and Hardships:**

  * Emigrants faced severe emotional and financial stress, leaving behind homes, families, and birthplace memories.

  * Many left spouses and children, could not collect debts owed to them, or had valuable goods confiscated by Makkan idolaters.

* **Prominent Figures:** Abu Bakr, Umar bin al-Khattab, Uthman bin Affan, Ali, Zaid bin Haritha, Abdur Rahman bin Awf, Sa'ad bin Abi Waqas, Talha bin Ubaidullah, Zubair bin al-Awwam, Abu Ubaida bin al-Jarrah, Abdullah Ibn Arqam, Abdullah bin Masood, Mus'ab bin Umair, Bilal bin Rabah, and Abu Zar Ghaffari.

* **Status in the Qur'an and Hadith:**

  * Qur'an describes them as higher in degree and successful:

    *"Those who believed and emigrated and strove hard and fought in Allah's Cause with their wealth and their lives are far higher in degree with Allah. They are the successful."* — Surah Al-Taubah **($9$:$20$)**

### The Helpers (*Ansaar*)

* **Definition:** *Ansaar* means "supporters/helpers". The Ansaar were the local people of Madina from the two primary tribes of **Aws** and **Khazraj**.

* **Conversion and Pledges at Al-Aqaba:**

  * Before Islam, Aws and Khazraj fought frequent civil wars.

  * In the $11$th year of Prophethood, $6$ people from Khazraj accepted Islam in Makkah.

  * **First Pledge of Al-Aqaba:** $12$ people ($10$ Khazraj, $2$ Aws) swore allegiance in the $12$th year of Prophethood.

  * **Second Pledge of Al-Aqaba:** $75$ people ($73$ men, $2$ women) took an oath in the $13$th year to protect the Prophet (pbuh) and Makkan Muslims like their own families, laying the foundation for the Islamic state.

* **Bond of Brotherhood (*Mawakhat*):**

  * The Prophet (pbuh) established a formal bond pairing each Muhajir with an Ansari as brothers.

  * The Ansaar showed unprecedented generosity—sharing accommodation, food, orchards, and property without jealousy.

  * From the Battle of Badr until a later Quranic revelation restricting inheritance to blood relatives, Ansaar and Muhajireen even inherited from one another.

* **Sacrifices in Battle & Loyalty:**

  * Protected the Prophet (pbuh) alongside Muhajireen against plots from hypocrites, local tribes, and polytheists.

  * In the Battle of Uhud, most who surrounded and shielded the Prophet (pbuh) during the chaotic attack were Ansaar.

  * After the Conquest of Makkah, when Ansaar feared the Prophet (pbuh) might remain in Makkah, he reassured them he would return and live with them in Madina.

  * After the Prophet's death, they remained loyal, voluntarily accepted Abu Bakr's caliphate, stayed clear of political rebellions, and dedicated themselves to agriculture, trade, religious learning, and conquests.

* **Prominent Figures:** Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, Anas ibn Malik, Ka'ab ibn Malik, Sa'ad ibn Muaz, and Asad ibn Zurarah.

* **Praise in the Qur'an:**

  *"And (it is also for) those who, before them, had homes (in Madina) and had adopted the Faith, love those who emigrate to them, and have no jealousy in their breasts... and give them preference over themselves even though they were in need of that..."* — Surah Al-Hashr ($59$:$9$)
