## Ten Blessed Companions

**Primary Hadith Reference:** Narrated by Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf and Saeed ibn Zayd (R.A), the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: *"Abu Bakr is in Paradise, 'Umar is in Paradise, 'Uthman is in Paradise, 'Ali is in Paradise, Talhah is in Paradise, Az-Zubair is in Paradise, 'Abdur-Rahman bin 'Awf is in Paradise, Sa'd is in Paradise, Sa'eed is in Paradise, and Abu 'Ubaidah bin Al-Jarrah is in Paradise."*

**Sources:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* (Hadith $3747$), *Sunan Abi Dawud* (Hadith $4649$), *Musnad Ahmad* (Hadith $1631$).

### **1. Abu Bakr As-Siddiq (R.A)**

* **Lineage & Background:** Born in Mecca in $573$ CE to Usman (Abu Quhafa) and Salma (Umm Al-Khair). His original name was Abdullah, and he belonged to the Banu Taym clan of Quraysh. He was a prosperous cloth merchant.He was also the closest friend of the Holy Prophet(PBUH) even before Prophethood.

* **Conversion & Services:** The first adult male to embrace Islam without hesitation. He used his wealth to buy and free persecuted slaves (including Bilal ibn Rabah) and brought key figures like Usman ibn Affan and Zubair ibn Al-Awwam into Islam.

* **Key Contributions:** Accompanied the Prophet (pbuh) during the Hijrah. Participated in all major battles and gave all his personal wealth for the Expedition of Tabuk. Led prayers during the Prophet's final illness.

* **Caliphate:** First Caliph of Islam ($632$–$634$ CE). He unified Arabia after the Prophet's death by defeating the apostates (Riddah Wars), launched campaigns against the Byzantine and Sasanian Empires, and commissioned the first compilation of the Quran.

* **Character & Death:** Known for his unwavering truthfulness (*As-Siddiq*) and gentle nature. Passed away in 634 CE at age 63 and was buried next to the Prophet (pbuh).

* **Quran:** Surah At-Tawbah ($9$:$40$) – Referencing him as the companion in the cave during the Hijrah (*"the second of two, when they were in the cave..."*).

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3654$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) stated: *"If I were to take a Khaleel (intimate friend) other than my Lord, I would have taken Abu Bakr."*

### **2. Umar ibn Al-Khattab (R.A)**

* **Lineage & Background:** Born in Mecca around $584$ CE to Khattab ibn Nufayl of the Banu Adi clan. Known for his intelligence, strength, and skills in martial arts and public speaking.Being a well known soldier he was also one of the most feared and strict men of the Quraish.

* **Conversion & Impact:** Converted in the $6$th year of Prophet-hood after hearing Surah Ta-Ha. His conversion gave Muslims the strength to pray openly at the Kaaba. The Prophet (pbuh) gave him the title *Al-Farooq* (the distinguisher between truth and falsehood).

* **Key Contributions:** Participated in all major battles. He was a close advisor to both the Prophet (pbuh) and Abu Bakr (R.A).

* **Caliphate:** Second Caliph of Islam ($634$–$644$ CE). Established a vast empire encompassing Persia, Egypt, and the Levant. He introduced administrative reforms including the Hijri calendar, regular salaries, judicial system, and public treasury (*Bayt al-Mal*).

* **Death:** Martyrdom by Abu Lu'lu'a (a Persian slave) while leading Fajr prayer in $644$ CE. Buried beside the Prophet (pbuh) and Abu Bakr (R.A).

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3689$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"By Him in Whose Hands my soul is, whenever Satan sees you taking a path, he follows a path other than yours."*

* **Hadith:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* ($3686$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) named him *Al-Farooq* and stated: *"Allah has placed truth upon Umar's tongue and in his heart."*

### **3. Usman ibn Affan (R.A)**

* **Lineage & Title:** Born in $576$ CE into the wealthy Umayyad clan of Quraysh. Earned the title *Dhun-Nurayn* ("Possessor of the Two Lights") for marrying two daughters of the Prophet (pbuh), Ruqayyah and later Umm Kulthum.

* **Persecution in Makkah:** His tribal chief, also his uncle, used to wrap him in palm leaves and suffocate him.

* **Generosity (*Dhun-Nafasat*):** Known for his immense wealth and charity. Purchased the well of Ruma for public use, funded the army for the Tabuk expedition, and expanded the Prophet's Mosque in Medina.

* **Caliphate:** Third Caliph of Islam ($644$–$656$ CE). Expanded the Islamic empire into North Africa, Cyprus, and parts of Central Asia.

* **Compilation of the Quran:** Standardized the Quranic text into a single dialect (Qurayshi) and distributed official copies across the empire.

* **Death:** Martyred in $656$ CE at age $82$ by rebels inside his home while reciting the Quran.

* **Hadith:** The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"Should I not feel shy before a man whom even the angels feel shy before?"* (*Sahih Muslim $2401$*).

* **Hadith:** When Usman equipped the army for the Tabuk expedition (*Jaysh al-Usrah*), the Prophet (ﷺ) stated: *"Nothing that Usman does after today can harm him."* (*Jami' at-Tirmidhi $3701$*).

### **4. Ali ibn Abi Talib (R.A)**

* **Lineage & Relation:** Cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet (PBUH), married to Fatima (R.A). Raised directly in the house of the Prophet (PBUH). First child to accept Islam.

* **Conversion:** Saw Hazrat Khadija and the Holy Prophet (PBUH) prostrating without any idol infront of them. When he asked and was told about Islam, he converted.

* **Scribe of The Prophet(PBUH):** He was a scribe of official documents for the Prophet (PBUH) and had written the Official Document of the Treaty of Hudaibiyyah from the Muslim’s side.

* **Bravery & Achievements:** Known as *Asadullah* ("Lion of Allah"). Slept in the Prophet's bed to distract the Quraysh during the Hijrah. Standard-bearer in major battles, renowned for his victory at the Fort of Khaybar.

* **Caliphate:** Fourth Caliph of Islam ($656$–$661$ CE). His rule was marked by internal division and civil strife (Battle of Camel, Battle of Siffin). He moved the capital from Medina to Kufa.

* **Wisdom & Death:** Famous for his deep knowledge of Islamic jurisprudence, eloquence, and piety. Assassinated by Ibn Muljam (a Kharijite) while entering the mosque for Fajr in Kufa in $661$ CE.

* **Hadith:** *Sahih Muslim* ($2404$) – At Khaybar, the Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"Tomorrow I will give the banner to a man who loves Allah and His Messenger, and Allah and His Messenger love him."*

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3706$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) said to him: *"You are to me in the position that Aaron was to Moses, except that there is no prophet after me."*

### **5. Talha ibn Ubaydullah (RA)**

* **Background & Early Conversion:** Born to the Banu Taym clan. Accepted Islam at a young age through Abu Bakr (R.A). Successful merchant known for his extreme generosity (*Talhat al-Khayr*).

* **Heroism at Uhud:** Shielded the Prophet (pbuh) with his own body during the Battle of Uhud, taking over $70$ wounds and losing the use of his fingers. The Prophet (pbuh) referred to him as a "living martyr".

* **Death:** Participated in the Battle of Camel and was struck by a stray arrow, leading to his martyrdom in $656$ CE.

* **Hadith:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* ($3739$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"Whoever would be pleased to look at a man walking upon the earth who has fulfilled his vow, let him look at Talhah ibn Ubaydullah."*

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3724$) – Details his heroism at Uhud, where he shielded the Prophet (ﷺ) with his hand.

### **6. Zubair ibn Al-Awwam (R.A)**

* **Relation & Title:** Nephew of Khadijah (R.A) and cousin of the Prophet (pbuh). Known as *Hawari Rasulullah* ("The Disciple of the Messenger of Allah").

* **Contributions:** Accepted Islam at age $15$. First person to draw a sword in defense of Islam in Mecca. Fought bravely in all major battles, including Badr, Uhud, and the conquest of Egypt.

* **Death:** Martyred shortly after the Battle of Camel in $656$ CE while engaged in prayer.

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3719$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"Every prophet has a disciple (Hawari), and my disciple is Al-Zubayr."*

* **Hadith:** *Sahih Muslim* ($2415$) – Praising his bravery and readiness to defend Islam from a young age.

### **7. Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf (R.A)**

* **Background & Conversion:** Early convert who migrated to both Abyssinia and Medina. Refused handouts upon reaching Medina and built a massive fortune through honest trading.

* **Service through Wealth:** Spent vast amounts of money funding campaigns, freeing slaves, and supporting the wives of the Prophet (pbuh) after his passing.

* **Role in Caliphate:** Appointed by Umar (R.A) to the $6$-member council to choose the next Caliph. He selflessly withdrew his own candidacy to arbitrate and select Usman (R.A). Passed away in $652$ CE.

* **Hadith:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* ($3750$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) prayed for him: *"O Allah! Give Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf drink from the streams of Paradise."*

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($2048$) – Documents his immense success in trade and generosity in Medina after leaving behind his wealth during the migration.

### **8. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (R.A)**

* **Early Life & Heroism:** Maternal uncle-in-relation to the Prophet (pbuh). Converted at age $17$. First person to shed blood in defense of Muslims and the first to shoot an arrow in the cause of Islam.

* **Military Genius:** Commander-in-Chief of the Muslim army at the historic Battle of Al-Qadisiyyah, which brought down the Sasanian Persian Empire. Founded the city of Kufa.

* **Death:** The last of the Ten Blessed Companions to pass away, dying peacefully at his estate in Aqiq around $674$ CE.

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3725$) – At Uhud, the Prophet (ﷺ) encouraged him during archery, saying: *"Shoot, Sa'd! May my father and mother be sacrificed for you!"*

* **Hadith:** *Jami' at-Tirmidhi* ($3752$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) supplicated for him: *"O Allah, answer Sa'd when he supplicates to You."*

### **9. Saeed ibn Zayd (R.A)**

* **Background:** Son of Zayd ibn Amr, one of the few Monotheists (*Hanifs*) in Mecca before Islam. Married to Fatimah bint al-Khattab (sister of Umar R.A).

* **Services:** He and his wife were instrumental in Umar’s conversion. Participated in all major battles except Badr (due to being sent on a scouting mission).

* **Conquests & Piety:** Fought heroically in the Battle of Yarmouk and the Siege of Damascus. Served briefly as governor of Damascus before stepping down to remain a frontline soldier. Passed away around $671$ CE.

* **Hadith:** *Sunan Abi Dawud* ($4649$) – Narrated by Saeed himself when asked about his standing, detailing his direct inclusion among the ten promised Paradise.

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3867$) – Highlights his early acceptance of Islam alongside his wife Fatimah (Umar's sister) before Umar's conversion.

### **10. Abu Ubaidah ibn Al-Jarrah (R.A)**

* **Title & Character:** Earned the title *Ameen al-Ummah* ("The Trustworthy of this Nation") from the Prophet (pbuh) due to his integrity and humility.

* **Key Role:** Early convert who migrated to Abyssinia. In the Battle of Uhud, he used his teeth to pull out two iron rings embedded in the Prophet's cheeks, losing two of his front teeth in the process.

* **Supreme Command:** Appointed by Umar (R.A) as the supreme commander of the Muslim forces in Syria. Successfully led the conquest of the Levant.

* **Death:** Passed away in $639$ CE due to the Plague of Amwas in Syria.

* **Hadith:** *Sahih al-Bukhari* ($3744$) – The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"Every nation has a trustworthy guardian (Ameen), and the trustworthy guardian of this nation is Abu Ubaidah ibn Al-Jarrah."*

* **Hadith:** *Sahih Muslim* ($2419$) – Reinforcing his title *Ameen al-Ummah* when sent to evaluate and lead affairs in Najran.
