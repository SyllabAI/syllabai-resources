## Other Important Companions

### **Salman al-Farsi (RA)**

* **Origins & Search for Truth:** Originally named Ruzbeh from Isfahan, Persia, he was raised as a Zoroastrian priest and fire temple custodian before leaving his wealthy family to pursue Christianity. He traveled across the Middle East, studying under several pious bishops in Syria, Mosul, and Ammuriyah.

* **Enslavement to Medina:** Sold into slavery by an Arab tribe, he was eventually taken to Yathrib (Medina).

* **Acceptance of Islam:** Recognized the signs of prophethood (refusing charity, accepting gifts, and having the seal of prophethood) and accepted Islam. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) helped buy his freedom by planting $300$ date palms and providing gold.

* **Key Contributions:** Suggested digging the defensive trench during the Battle of Khandaq. He was the first person to translate parts of the Qur'an into a foreign language (Persian). Passed away during the reign of Caliph 'Uthman.

### **Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA)**

* **Prior to Islam:** Member of a prominent Meccan family that opposed Islam; played a critical tactical role against the Muslims at the Battle of Uhud.

* **Conversion & Title:** Converted to Islam after the Treaty of Hudaybiyya. Earned the title *Saifullah* ("Sword of Allah") after successfully orchestrating a safe retreat at the Battle of Mu'ta.

* **Military Achievements:** Commanded Muslim armies in the Conquest of Makkah, Battle of Hunayn, and Battle of Tabuk. Undefeated in over a hundred battles during the expansion of Islam under Caliphs Abu Bakr and 'Umar.

### **Zayd ibn Haritha (RA)**

* **Early Life:** Captured as a child and purchased for Khadija (RA), who gifted him to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). The Prophet (PBUH) freed and adopted him, after which he was called Zayd ibn Muhammad until revelation restricted adoption naming conventions.

* **Loyalty & Conversion:** When given the choice to return to his biological family, he chose to remain with the Prophet (PBUH). He was the third person (and first freed slave) to accept Islam.

* **Service & Martyrdom:** Accompanied the Prophet (PBUH) during the trip to Ta'if. He is the only companion mentioned by name in the Qur'an (Surah Al-Ahzab). Commanded multiple military expeditions and was martyred as standard bearer at the Battle of Mu'ta.

### **Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib (RA)**

* **Relationship:** Paternal uncle and foster brother of the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Acceptance of Islam:** Publicly declared his allegiance after confronting Abu Jahl for harassing the Prophet (PBUH), greatly empowering early Muslims in Makkah.

* **Key Contributions & Martyrdom:** Commanded early expeditions (e.g., Saif Al-Bahr) and defeated key leaders in pre-battle duels at Badr. Martyred during the Battle of Uhud by Wahshi, earning the titles *The Master of Martyrs* and *The Lion of God*.
