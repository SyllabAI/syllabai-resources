## Children of the Holy Prophet(PBUH)

### **1. Hazrat Qasim (RA)**

* Firstborn child and eldest son of the Holy Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Khadija (RA).

* Born when the Prophet (PBUH) was $28$ years old.

* The Prophet (PBUH) took the kunya *Abul Qasim* (Father of Qasim) after him and requested that others not adopt this title.

* Passed away in Makkah before reaching his second birthday (around $600$/$601$ AD), prior to the start of divine revelation.

### **2. Hazrat Zainab (RA)**

* Eldest daughter, born ten years before Prophethood when the Prophet (PBUH) was $30$ years old.

* Married her maternal cousin, Abu al-'As ibn Rabi', prior to Prophethood. When she embraced Islam, her husband initially remained a non-believer but treated her with immense respect.

* **Ransom of Abu al-'As:** Fought on the side of Makkans at Badr and was captured. Zainab sent her mother Khadija's necklace as ransom. Seeing the necklace brought tears to the Prophet (PBUH); the Companions agreed to release Abu al-'As and return the necklace on the condition that Zainab be allowed to migrate to Madinah.

* **Injury & Death:** While departing Makkah, her cousin Habar-bin-Aswad struck her with a spear, knocking her off her camel. She suffered lingering health issues from this fall and eventually died in $8$ A.H. The Prophet (PBUH) remarked: *"She was my best daughter, for she has suffered much on my account."*

### **3. Hazrat Ruqayya (RA)**

* Second daughter, born three years after Zainab when the Prophet (PBUH) was $33$.

* Initially married to Utba, son of Abu Lahab. Abu Lahab and his wife Umme Jameel forced Utba to divorce her after the declaration of Islam.

* Married Hazrat Usman (RA). Earned the title *Zaat ul Hijratain* ("Owner of Two Migrations") after migrating with Usman to Abyssinia twice (in $613$ AD and $615$ AD) before migrating to Madinah.

* Her son, Abd-Allah, died at age six in Madinah.

* Fell gravely ill during the Battle of Badr in $624$ AD. Hazrat Usman (RA) stayed behind to nurse her under the Prophet's instructions. She passed away before the victorious Muslim army returned.

### **4. Hazrat Umm-e-Kulthum (RA)**

* Third daughter, born three years before Prophethood when the Prophet (PBUH) was $34$.

* Initially married to Utaiba, another son of Abu Lahab, who was forced by his parents to divorce her upon the advent of Islam.

* Migrated to Madinah alongside her stepmother, Hazrat Sawdah (RA).

* After Ruqayya's death, she married Hazrat Usman (RA) in $624$ AD.

* Passed away at the age of $25$ in $9$ A.H. while her husband and father were away on the Tabuk expedition. Left no surviving children.

### **5. Hazrat Abdullah / Tahir (RA)**

* Second son of the Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Khadija (RA), also known by the titles *Tahir* and *Tayib*. Named after the Prophet’s father.

* Passed away in infancy (around $18$ months of age) in $615$ CE.

* **The Revelation of Surah Al-Kauthar:** Upon his passing, the Makkan pagans taunted the Prophet (PBUH) as *Abtar* (cut off from male lineage). Allah comforted the Prophet (PBUH) by revealing Surah Al-Kauthar: *"Verily, We have granted you Al-Kauthar... For he who makes you angry, he will be cut off."*

* The Quran clarifies that Muhammad (PBUH) is the "Seal of the Prophets" and "not the father of any of your men", explaining why his sons were taken in infancy.

### **6. Hazrat Fatima (RA)**

* **Early Life & Character:** Youngest daughter, born in Makkah during the first year of Prophethood (*Zahra* as title). Endured the severe hardships of Makkah, including the brutal economic blockade at Shi'b Abi Talib.

* **Prophet's Affection:** The Prophet (PBUH) held deep love for her, standing up to receive her and offering her his seat. He stated: *"Fatima is part of me. Whatever upsets her upsets me, and whatever harms her harms me."* He also named her among the four sacred women of the world (alongside Mary, Khadija, and Asiya).

* **Marriage & Simple Life:** Married Hazrat Ali (RA) in Madinah at age $15$. Lived a life of voluntary poverty and hard labor—drawing water and grinding flour with hand mills until her hands blistered. When she sought a servant, the Prophet (PBUH) instead taught them *Tasbih Fatima* ($33$x Subhan Allah, $33$x Alhamdulillah, $34$x Allahu Akbar) before sleep.

* **Service:** Nursed the Prophet's wounds during the Battle of Uhud and tended to injured soldiers.

* **Passing:** Nursed her father during his final illness. He whispered to her that his death was near (causing her to weep), and then whispered that she would be the first of his family to join him in Paradise (making her smile). Passed away six months after him at age $28$ and was buried at night in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

### 7.Hazrat Ibrahim (RA)

* **Parentage & Birth:** Hazrat Ibrahim (RA) was the third and youngest son of the Holy Prophet (PBUH). He was born in Madinah to Hazrat Maria Qibtia (RA) towards the later years of the Prophet's life.

* **Distinction:** He was the only child of the Holy Prophet (PBUH) born to a mother other than Hazrat Khadija (RA).

* **Elevated Status for His Mother:** Upon giving birth to Ibrahim, Hazrat Maria Qibtia (RA) rose significantly in esteem and position among the household of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* **Infancy & Death:** Like his elder brothers Qasim (RA) and Abdullah (RA), Ibrahim passed away in infancy, causing the Holy Prophet (PBUH) immense sorrow and grief towards the end of his life.

* **Burial:** He was buried in the *Jannat-ul-Baqi* cemetery in Madinah.
