## Imamat (The Twelve Imams)

### Imamat (The Twelve Imams)

### Concept of Imamat

* According to the Shi'a school of thought, Imamat is the concept of choosing a leader from the family of the Holy Prophet (PBUH) who, before passing away, transfers leadership to another person from the same family.

* Leadership is restricted specifically to the descendants of Hazrat Ali (RA) and Hazrat Fatima (RA).

* In the Isna Ashari (Twelver) Shi'a school of thought, Twelve chosen Imams are considered to be political and spiritual leaders from the family of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

### 1. Ali bin Abi Talib (RA) ($600$ C.E. – $661$ C.E.)

**Background & Early Life**

* Born roughly thirty years after the birth of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* Son of Abu Talib, the paternal uncle of the Prophet (PBUH) who raised the Prophet (PBUH).

* Married Hazrat Fatima (RA); together they had three sons and two daughters.

* Second person to accept Islam.

**Bravery & Services**

* Slept in the Prophet’s (PBUH) bed during the migration to Madinah to return the belongings entrusted to the Prophet (PBUH) by the people of Makkah.

* Participated in almost all battles against non-believers during the Prophet's (PBUH) lifetime and was considered one of the greatest warriors of Arabia.

**Scholarship & Contributions**

* The Prophet (PBUH) said of him: *"I am the city of knowledge... and Ali is its gate"* (Tirmizi).

* Master of the Arabic language; narrated $540$traditions (Ahadith) of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* Served as one of the scribes of the Holy Prophet (PBUH), wrote treaties for him, and was appointed as the Qazi (Judge) of Yemen during the Prophet's (PBUH) lifetime.

* Great Mufti (jurist) of Madinah; important member of Shura (Advisory Council) during the era of the first three Caliphs.

* Nominated by Hazrat Umar (RA) in the panel of six persons to choose a Caliph from among themselves.

* Fourth Rightly Guided Caliph of Islam.

### 2. Hassan Ibn Ali (RA) ($3$ A.H. – $50$ A.H.)

* Son of Hazrat Ali (RA) and Hazrat Fatima (RA), and the elder of the three grandsons of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* Taught solely by his grandfather and parents.

* Took part in all battles during Hazrat Ali's (RA) caliphate and managed the Bait-ul-Maal.

* Elected as caliph by the people of Kufah after Hazrat Ali’s (RA) martyrdom.

* Abdicated the office of caliphate after about $6$ months in favor of Muawiyah to preserve unity, stating: *"People, I have abdicated the position of caliph for the improvement of the community"* (*Usdul-Ghaba*).

* Buried in the graveyard of Jannat-ul-Baqi in Madinah.

* Regarded by Shi'a belief as the Second Imam, sinless, and perfect in his actions.

### 3. Hussain Ibn Ali (RA) ($4$ A.H. – $61$ A.H.)

* Born to Hazrat Ali (RA) and Hazrat Fatima (RA) on $3$rd Sha'ban, $4$ A.H.

* Handed the charge of Imamat by Hassan (RA) on his deathbed, making him the Third Imam.

* Retired into seclusion in Madinah. After Muawiyah’s death, when Yazid became caliph, Hussain (RA) along with certain prominent Companions refused to accept Yazid's caliphate.

* Invited by supporters in Kufah to lead an uprising against Yazid; left Madinah towards Kufah with $72$ family members and supporters.

* Reached Karbala on $2$nd Muharram $60$ A.H.

* Battle took place on $10$th Muharram against Yazid's army under governor Ubaidullah bin Ziyad after peaceful negotiations failed.

* Hussain (RA) and his $72$ supporters were martyred. Umm-e-Salmah (RA) was deeply saddened upon hearing of his martyrdom.

* Lineage continued through his son Zain-ul-Abideen.

### 4. Zain-ul-Abideen (RA) ($36$ A.H. – $94$ A.H.)

* Real name was Ali; sole surviving son of Hussain (RA) from the tragedy of Karbala.

* Taken in chains to Yazid in Damascus, but Yazid later freed him and sent him back to Madinah due to public anger.

* Led a life of seclusion, deep devotion, learning, and prayer.

* Given the title *"Sajjad"* (one who performed excessive prostration).

* Authored/attributed work: *"Sahifah-Sajjadiyah"*.

* Known for helping the poor and oppressed.

### 5. Muhammad Al-Baqir (RA) ($57$A.H. – $114$ A.H.)

* Son of Ali Zain-ul-Abideen; present at Karbala at four years old.

* Mother was the daughter of Hassan (RA).

* Lived through four Umayyad Caliphs (Walid bin Abdul Malik, Sulaiman bin Abdul Malik, Umar bin Abdul Aziz, and Hasham bin Abdul Malik).

* Known for vast knowledge of the Qur'an, Sunnah, and piety; considered among the *Tabi'een* (followers of the Companions).

### 6. Jafar as-Sadiq (RA) ($83$ A.H. – $148$ A.H.)

* Son of Muhammad Al-Baqir.

* Era coincided with the end of the Umayyad dynasty and the rise of the Abbasid dynasty.

* Dedicated his time to teaching the sciences of the Qur'an, Sunnah, and Shariah.

* Prominent scholars like Imam Abu Hanifah and Malik bin Anas were his students.

* Imam Abu Hanifah remarked: *"I have not seen a greater Faqih than him"*.

* Exceptionally generous in charity.

### 7. Musa Al-Kazim (RA) ($128$ A.H. – $188$ A.H.)

* Son of Jafar as-Sadiq.

* Lived during the era of three Abbasid Caliphs: Mansoor, Mehdi, and Haroon.

* Spent extensive periods in hiding and was repeatedly imprisoned/transferred by the Caliph's forces.

* Given the title *"Al-Kazim"* ("the controller of anger") for exercising extreme self-control, patience, and devotion under intense hardship.

### 8. Ali ar-Raza (RA) ($148$ A.H. – $203$ A.H.)

* Son of Musa Al-Kazim; born in Madinah.

* Admired by Abbasid Caliph Mamoon, who offered him the post of caliphate. Upon refusal, Mamoon declared him his successor.

* Passed away before he could assume the caliphate.

* Highly knowledgeable in Qur'anic affairs and Sunnah; known to complete the recitation of the entire Qur'an in three days.

### 9. Muhammad at-Taqi (RA) ($195$ A.H. – $220$ A.H.)

* Son of Ali ar-Raza; born in Madinah.

* Known for being pious, generous, soft-spoken, and helpful to the poor and oppressed.

* Spent his lifetime teaching Qur'an and Sunnah in Madinah.

### 10. Ali Al-Hadi (RA) ($212$ A.H. – $254$ A.H.)

* Son of Muhammad at-Taqi; born in Madinah.

* Contemporary of seven Abbasid caliphs.

* Spent his life preaching Islam, exhibiting refined manners, and guiding people towards the path of Qur'an and Sunnah.

### 11. Hassan Al-Askari (RA) ($232$ A.H. – $260$ A.H.)

* Son of Ali Al-Hadi.

* Lived mostly in hiding due to fear of persecution by enemies of the *Ahl al-Bait*.

* Noted for humbleness, piety, and deep mastery over Shariah sciences; widely respected by contemporary scholars.

### 12. Muhammad Al-Mahdi (RA) ($256$ A.H. – Present)

* Born in Samarrah in $256$ A.H.; son of Hassan Al-Askari.

* Mother was the granddaughter of the Roman king.

* According to Shi'a belief, following his father's death, he went into occultation (*Ghaybat*).

* Initially appeared rarely to deputies (Short Occultation), and from $329$ A.H., entered a longer period of occultation.

* Believed to reappear during a time of immense chaos and disruption in the world to restore justice.
