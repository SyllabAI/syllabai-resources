## Grandchildren of the Holy Prophet(PBUH)

### **1. Hazrat Hassan ibn 'Ali (RA)**

* **Birth & Early Life:** Born in Madinah on the $1$st of Ramadan, $3$ AH. The Holy Prophet (PBUH) performed the *Aqiqa* (slaughtered a ram for the poor) and named him Hassan. He was also known by the title *Shabbar* ("Handsome"). He spent seven years in the company of the Holy Prophet (PBUH), closely resembling him in physical appearance, mannerisms, and nobility.

* **Role in Caliphates:**

  * **Hazrat Usman's (RA) Caliphate:** Participated in defense missions against non-believers. During the siege of Hazrat Usman's house, Hassan actively guarded the home and brought food and water to him.

  * **Hazrat Ali's (RA) Caliphate:** Stood firmly by his father and participated in the Battles of Jamal (Camel), Siffin, and Nahrawan. He was also entrusted with the charge of the *Bait-ul-Maal* (Public Treasury).

* **Caliphate & Abdication:** On the $21$st of Ramadan, following Hazrat Ali’s (RA) assassination, Hassan was elected Caliph by $40,000$ people in Kufa. Facing intense opposition and potential civil war from Mu'awiya, Hassan sent a vanguard under 'Ubayd Allah ibn al-Abbas but instructed them not to initiate fighting. To avoid further bloodshed among Muslims, he abdicated after several months, concluding a peace treaty that left the choice of future caliphs to the Ummah.

* **Martyrdom:** In $670$ AD (at age $48$), Imam Hassan was poisoned. Historical reports suspect his wife, Joda bint Ash, was induced with promises of financial reward and marriage to Yazid to commit the act. He was buried in *Jannat-ul-Baqi*.

### **2. Hazrat Hussain ibn 'Ali (RA)**

* **Birth & Early Life:** Born in Madinah in $4$ AH, one year after his elder brother, and was similarly named by the Holy Prophet (PBUH). Shared the same close upbringing alongside Hassan, frequently climbing on the Prophet (PBUH) during his prayers and sitting beside him on the pulpit during sermons.

* **Opposition to Yazid:** After Mu'awiya’s death in $680$ AD, his son Yazid was appointed successor, violating the earlier peace agreement. Refusing to pledge allegiance to Yazid due to his corruption, Hussain departed Madinah for Makkah to prevent bloodshed in the holy city.

* **The Kufan Invitation & Treachery:** The people of Kufa invited Hussain to lead them against Yazid’s tyranny. Hussain dispatched his cousin, Muslim bin Aqeel, to assess the situation. Upon receiving initial widespread pledges of support, Muslim written to Hussain to proceed. However, Yazid’s governor violently suppressed the uprising, coerced/bribed the town leaders, and executed Muslim bin Aqeel.

* **The Tragedy of Karbala:** Unaware of the sudden betrayal, Hussain set out with a small group of family and devoted companions. Intercepted by Yazid’s forces, they were forced to camp at Karbala near the Euphrates River.

  * On the $7$th of Muharram, food and water were cut off from Hussain's camp.

  * On the $10$th of Muharram (Ashura), a brutal battle ensued between Hussain’s $72$ supporters and an army of thousands.

  * After his companions and family members were killed one by one, Hussain was martyred. His head was severed and taken to Yazid in Damascus, and his camp was looted and burned.

* **Survivors & Burial:** His ill son, Zain-ul-Abedin, survived through the intervention of his aunt Zaynab and succeeded as head of the family. Hazrat Hussain's body was buried at the site of his martyrdom in Karbala.

### 3.Hazrat Umama

* **Lineage & Childhood:** She was the granddaughter of the Holy Prophet (PBUH), born to his eldest daughter, Hazrat Zainab (RA), and her husband, Abul Aas bin Rabi.

* **Upbringing & Prophet's Affection:** The Holy Prophet (PBUH) loved her deeply, especially after her mother passed away and left her in the care of her cousin, Zubair bin Awam (RA). She was brought up with immense affection by the Prophet (PBUH) alongside her aunts and uncles.

* **Presence in Masjid-e-Nabwi:** As a young child, she was often seen playing in Masjid-e-Nabwi, even during the congregational prayers.

* **Marriages:**

  * **First Marriage:** Following the passing of Hazrat Fatima (RA), she was married to Hazrat Ali bin Abi Talib (RA).

  * **Second Marriage:** After Hazrat Ali’s (RA) assassination, she married another Sahabi, Mughaira bin Nofal (RA).

* **Offspring & Later Life:** She had a son named Yahya with Mughaira bin Nofal (RA), starting another line of the Prophet's progeny. She eventually relocated to Syria with her husband.

* **Passing:** She passed away in Syria in the year $66$ AH.
