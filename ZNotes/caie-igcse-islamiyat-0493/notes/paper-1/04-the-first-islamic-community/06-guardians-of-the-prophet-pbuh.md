## Guardians of the Prophet(PBUH)

### **Amina bint Wahb**

* **Marriage & Pregnancy:** She was the mother of the Prophet (pbuh) and had been married to 'Abdullah for only a few months when he passed away, leaving her widowed and pregnant. She noted that she did not feel any hardship during her pregnancy.

* **Miraculous Birth:** At his birth, Amina stated that a light came out with him illuminating what was between the East and West, revealing the palaces and markets of Syria down to the necks of camels in Basra. She also saw three flags erected: one in the East, one in the West, and one over the Ka'ba.

* **Entrusting to Foster Mother:** Recognizing her son was special, she sent him to a wet-nurse in the desert following Arab custom. When he was $2$ years old, Halima requested to keep him longer, to which Amina agreed. He was later returned after the incident where angels cleaned his heart.

* **Final Journey & Death:** When the Prophet (pbuh) was six, Amina took him to Yathrib to visit family and the grave of his father, 'Abdullah. On the return trip to Makka, she fell ill and passed away at the village of Abwa.

### **Halima Sa'adia**

* **Taking the Prophet (pbuh):** She served as the Prophet’s wet-nurse, taking him when no one else wanted to because she did not want to return home to the desert empty-handed. On the journey back from Makkah, she immediately began noticing miracles.

* **Blessings & Fortune:** Her family's fortunes changed while the Prophet (pbuh) lived with them; their animals became healthier and gave more milk.

* **Extended Care:** Due to these blessings and her close bond with him, she requested to keep the Prophet (pbuh) past the initial two-year period.

* **Splitting of the Chest Incident:** While living with her family, angels visited the Prophet (pbuh) to clean his heart. Shaken by this event and worried for his safety under her care, she returned him to his mother.

### **Abu Talib**

* **Guardianship:** He was a leader of one of the Quraysh clans and became the Prophet's guardian following the death of the Prophet's grandfather. He loved Muhammad (pbuh) like his own son, often preferring him over his own children.

* **Trade Journey & Bahira:** Abu Talib took the Prophet (pbuh) on trade trips. On one journey, the monk Bahira informed Abu Talib that his nephew would be the final prophet. Abu Talib quickly sold his goods and returned to Makka to protect Muhammad (pbuh) from potential danger if discovered.

* **Protection:** Although Abu Talib did not convert to Islam when the Prophet (pbuh) began preaching openly, he promised to protect him. His protection directly prevented the Prophet (pbuh) from being more severely persecuted by the Quraysh.

### **Umm Ayman**

* **Identity:** An Abyssinian slave girl originally named Barakah, owned by the Prophet’s (PBUH) father, Abdullah bin Abdul Muttalib.

* **Inheritance:** Passed down to the Holy Prophet (PBUH) after his father's passing.

* **Guardianship:** She accompanied him and his mother to the journey to Yathrib. When his mother died she accompanied him back from Abwa and handed him over to his granfather Abdul Muttalib.

* **Life as a Muslim:** she was freed by the Prophet (PBUH) upon his marriage to Hazrat Khadija (RA).One of the earliest converts to Islam in Makkah. she first married Obaid bin Zaid (RA) and had a son named Ayman (who was later martyred at the Battle of Hunayn).Later she married Zaid bin Harithah (RA), the Prophet's adopted son and gave birth to Usamah bin Zaid (RA), whom the Prophet (PBUH) loved dearly.
