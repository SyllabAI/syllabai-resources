## Scribes of Holy Prophet(PBUH)

### **Prominent Scribes**

### **1. Zayd bin Thabit (RA)**

**Background & Early Life**

* **Identity:** An *Ansari* who accepted Islam at the age of $11$ at the hands of Mus'ab bin Umair (RA), one year before the Hijra.

* **Ahl-i-Suffa:** Member of the *Ahl-i-Suffa* (People of the Porch/Bench), spending his time in the constant company of the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Military Participation:** Prevented from fighting at Badr due to his young age, but actively participated in Uhud, Trench (Allies), and other battles. He was later injured at the Battle of Yamama against Musailima the Liar.

**Role as a Scribe & Scholar**

* **Senior Scribe:** Chosen by the Prophet (PBUH) for writing down the Holy Quran due to his sharp memory and extraordinary intelligence. He possessed most of the Quran in written form.

* **Personal Reciter & Secretary:** Memorized the Quran with such grace that the Prophet (PBUH) would ask him to recite it. Served as the Prophet's secretary until his passing.

* **Linguistic Mastery:** Instructed by the Prophet (PBUH) to learn Hebrew, Persian, and Coptic languages, becoming a scholar of the Torah and Injil.

* **Hadith:** Related $92$ Ahadith from the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

**Services Under the Caliphs**

* **Abu Bakr (RA):** Retained as a scribe, included in the *Shoora* (Advisory Council), and appointed head of the team that compiled the official Quranic manuscript.

* **Umar (RA):** Served as a scribe, member of the Shoora, and *Qadi* (Judge) of Madinah. Appointed acting Caliph whenever Umar (RA) left Madinah.

* **Uthman (RA):** Designated as part of the team (and head of the preparation) to make seven standardized copies based on 'Mushaf-e-Hafsa'. Appointed head of the *Bait-al-Maal* (Treasury).

* **Legacy:** Title given: *"Rahber-e-Umma"* (Guide of the Ummah). Passed away at age $56$ in approximately $45$ AH.

### **2. Abdullah Ibn Mas'ud (RA)**

**Background & Conversion**

* **Early Profession:** Tended the flocks of a Quraishite chieftain; known by people as *"Ibn Umm Abd"* (son of the mother of a slave).

* **Acceptance of Islam:** Met the Prophet (PBUH) and Abu Bakr (RA) while they were thirsty; refused to give them milk without permission as the flocks were not his, demonstrating high honesty. Impressed by the Prophet (PBUH), he converted and immediately offered to enter his service.

**Service to the Prophet (PBUH) & Sacred Knowledge**

* **Personal Attendant:** Devoted himself to looking after the Prophet's needs inside and outside his house—shielded him during washing, carried his staff and *miswak* (toothbrush), and woke him up.

* **Scribe & Scholar:** Served as a scribe of the Divine Revelation. Attained deep knowledge of the Quran and Shariah directly under the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Praise by the Prophet (PBUH):**

  *"Whoever wants to read the Quran as fresh as when it was revealed then let him read according to the recitation of Ibn Umm Abd."*

* **Knowledge of Revelation Context:** Remarked that no verse was revealed without him knowing where and under what circumstances it was revealed.

**Contributions & Services**

* Narrated $800$ Ahadith.

* Played a vital role in the initial compilation of the Quran.

* Appointed administrator of the public treasury (*Bait-al-Maal*) of Kufa during the Caliphate of Umar (RA).

### **3. Ubayy bin Kaab (RA)**

**Background & Early Life**

* **Conversion:** Young man from Yathrib (Madinah) who accepted Islam at the **Second Pledge of Aqaba**.

* **Brotherhood:** Paired in brotherhood with Saeed bin Zaid (RA) (one of the Ten Blessed Companions) upon the Hijra to Madinah.

* **Titles:** Known as the *"Chief of Ansar"* and awarded numerous honorable titles by the Prophet (PBUH) and companions.

**Services as a Scribe & Reciter**

* **First Ansar Scribe:** The very first companion among the Ansar appointed as a scribe to the Holy Prophet (PBUH). Wrote down both the Holy Quran and Ahadith.

* **Master Reciter (*Muqree*):** Ranked among the primary trainers/teachers of Quranic reciters (*Qurra*). Reverted to throughout his life as the ultimate authority on the Holy Quran.

* **Zakah Collector:** Appointed as one of the first collectors of Zakah by the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Military Record:** Participated alongside the Prophet (PBUH) in all battles.

**Services Under the Caliphs & Legacy**

* **Quran Compilation:** Top choice to assist Zayd bin Thabit (RA) in compiling the official Quranic text.

* **Leader of Taraweeh:** Led the very first congregational *Taraweeh* prayers during the Caliphate of Umar (RA).

* **Advisory Role:** Served in senior advisory roles to the first three Caliphs (Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman).

* **Hadith & Death:** Narrated over $100$ traditions. Died in Madinah in $39$ AH; his funeral prayer was led by the $3$rd Caliph, Uthman bin Affan (RA).
