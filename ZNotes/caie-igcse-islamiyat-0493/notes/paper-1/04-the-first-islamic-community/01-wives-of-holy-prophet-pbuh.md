# The First Islamic Community

## Wives of The Holy Prophet(PBUH)

### **1. Hazrat Khadija (RA)**

* **Lineage & Early Life:** Daughter of Khuwalid bin Asad and Fatima bint Za'idah, coming from a noble, wealthy Quraish family. Her successful businessman father left her a large fortune. Known as *Tahira* (the Pure) and *Umm-e-Hind*.

* **Previous Marriages:** Married twice previously—first to Abu Hala bin Zurarah (had two sons: Hala and Hind) and then to Ateeq bin Aidh Makhzumi (had a daughter named Hindah).

* **Business & Marriage Proposal:** After her father died in the Fijar battle, she needed an honest manager for her business. Recommended the $22$-year-old Prophet (PBUH), known as *Al-Amin*. His first trade trip to Syria with her slave Maisra yielded immense profits. Her deep admiration turned to affection, leading her to send a marriage proposal. They married when he was $25$ and she was $40$.

* **Sacrifices & Support:** First convert to Islam; abandoned ancestral idol worship and placed all her wealth at the Prophet's disposal. She was his true counselor (*Ibn-e-Hashim*) and the only wife to bear the full persecution and hardships of Makkah alongside him.

* **Family:** Bore him two sons (who died in infancy) and four daughters (only Fatima survived to see his major life events). The Prophet (PBUH) did not marry anyone else during her lifetime.

* **Death & Legacy:** Died on $11$th Ramazan, $10$th year of Prophethood ($3$ years before Hijrat) at age $65$; buried in Makkah. Her death was a devastating loss. When Aisha expressed jealousy over her memory, the Prophet (PBUH) replied: *"She hailed my mission at a time when everyone was against it... How can I forget her?"* Bukhari and Muslim record: *"The noblest women of the world are Hazrat Mariam and Hazrat Khadija."*

### **2. Hazrat Sawdah bint Zama'ah (RA)**

* **Background:** Early convert to Islam married to Sakran bin Amr. Migrated to Abyssinia to escape persecution; returned to Makkah around the time of Khadija’s death, where her husband died, leaving her a widowed mother with one son.

* **Marriage Rationale:** Facing hostility as the only Muslim in her family, Hazrat Khawla bint Hakeem suggested her to the Prophet (PBUH). He married her to offer protection and honor an early convert.

* **Virtues & Household Role:** Managed household duties and looked after his children. Welcomed Hazrat Aisha affectionately and voluntarily gave up her turn of the Prophet's time in favor of Aisha. Her dwelling and Aisha’s were the first built adjacent to Masjid-e-Nabawi.

* **Prophet's (PBUH) Praise:** The Prophet (PBUH) referred to her as *"the most charitable and generous of his wives."*

* **Pious Lifestyle & Death:** Extremely devout, punctual in prayer, and praised by the Prophet (PBUH) as the most charitable and generous of his wives. On the Farewell Pilgrimage, when ordered to remain in retirement after his death, she strictly obeyed and never left her home even for Hajj or Umrah. Died in $22$ A.H. (end of Umar's Caliphate) and buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi; reported $5$ traditions.

### **3. Hazrat Aisha (RA)**

* **Lineage & Status:** Daughter of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA); titled *Humaira* and *Umm-e-Abdullah*. Married $10$ years after the first revelation; it was her first marriage.

* **Scholarly Leadership:** Revered as a top jurist, theologian, and interpreter of Sunnah and Shariat. Prominent Companions consulted her on complex issues. Umar bin Zubair noted she was unmatched in Quranic knowledge, Fiqh, poetry, medicine, history, and law. Imam Zuhri stated her collective knowledge exceeded that of all men and other wives combined.

* **Contributions to Hadith:** Pioneered a systematic coordination between the Quran and Sunnah, refusing any Hadith that contradicted the Quran. Key jurists like Imam Abu Hanifa and Imam Bukhari followed her legal principles. Narrated $2,210$ Ahadith.

* **Life as a Widow & Death:** Aged $25$ when the Prophet (PBUH) passed away in her apartment; spent her remaining $48$ years preaching. Khalifa Umar granted her an elevated stipend of $12,000$ dirhams due to her closeness to the Prophet (PBUH). Died on $17$th Ramazan $58$ A.H. (age $73$) and buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

* **Imam Zuhri:** *"Hazrat Aisha was the most learned person among the people. Senior and learned companions used to consult her"* and *"If the knowledge of all men and the wives of the Prophet were put together, the knowledge of Aisha would exceed it all."*

### **4. Hazrat Hafsa (RA)**

* **Background:** Daughter of Khalifa Umar bin Khattab; born $5$ years before Prophethood. Embraced Islam with her parents and first husband, Khunais bin Huzaifa. Migrated to Abyssinia, then Madinah. After Khunais was martyred at Badr, the Prophet (PBUH) married her to strengthen ties with Umar and his tribe.

* **Knowledge & Role:** Ranks second to Aisha in religious knowledge; proficient in reading and writing. Acted as a teacher, guide, and legal authority, quoting $60$ Ahadith.

* **Custodian of the Quran:** Selected as the official custodian of the original compiled script of the Holy Quran (*Mus'haf*), created under Abu Bakr. It passed to Umar, who bequeathed it to Hafsa for safe custody before it became the standard text under Usman.

* **Death:** Died in Shaban $45$ A.H. (Muawiya’s reign). Instructed her brother Abdullah bin Umar to distribute her property to the poor.

### **5. Hazrat Zainab bint Khuzaimah (RA)**

* **Background & Title:** Daughter of Khuzaimah bin Abdullah. Widowed after her husband Abdullah bin Jahsh was martyred at Uhud. Lived in poverty until the Prophet (PBUH) proposed in Ramazan $3$ A.H. to support her.

* **Benevolence:** Renowned for extreme charity and care for the needy; known universally as *Umm-ul-Masakin* ("Mother of the Poor").

* **Death:** Died just three months after her marriage at age $30$. The Prophet (PBUH) personally offered her funeral prayers in accordance with newly revealed Quranic guidance; buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

### **6. Hazrat Umm-e-Salamah (RA)**

* **Background:** Real name Hind bint Ali Umayya (Makhzoom tribe). Raised in luxury as the daughter of a wealthy leader. Embraced Islam early with husband Abdullah bin Asad (Abu Salamah). Distinguished as the very first Muslim woman to migrate to Madinah. Widowed after Abu Salamah died from wounds sustained at Uhud. Married the Prophet (PBUH) in $4$ A.H.

* **Intellectual Stature:** Narrated $378$ Ahadith; second only to Aisha in legal learning. Possessed an exceptional recitation style identical to the Prophet's.

* **Death:** Outlived all other wives; died in $63$ A.H. at age $84$. Abu Hurairah led her funeral prayer; buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

### **7. Hazrat Zainab bint Jahsh (RA)**

* **Background & First Marriage:** Prophet's first cousin (daughter of Umaima bint Abdul Muttalib). In $3$ A.H., the Prophet (PBUH) arranged her marriage to his freed slave and adopted son, Zaid bin Haris, to break social class barriers. Due to personal incompatibilities, Zaid divorced her after a year.

* **Divine Injunction:** Married directly by Divine Command, as revealed in Surah Al-Ahzab ($33$:$37$): *"Then when Zaid had dissolved (his marriage) with her, We joined her in marriage to thee..."*

* **Charity & Prophecy:** Earned a living through manual work and donated all profits to the poor. Aisha praised her as unmatched in religious piety, truthfulness, and charity. The Prophet (PBUH) foretold that the wife with the "longest hand" (meaning benevolence) would join him first in heaven; she was indeed the first wife to pass away after him ($20$ A.H., age $53$).

### **8. Hazrat Jawairiyah bint Haris (RA)**

* **Background:** Real name Barra; daughter of Haris (chief of Banu Mustaliq). Captured in $5$ A.H. after her tribe was defeated. Assigned to Sabit bin Qais, she sought ransom money to buy her freedom.

* **Liberation of Her Tribe:** Approached the Prophet (PBUH), who offered to pay her ransom and marry her. Upon marriage, Muslim soldiers immediately released $600$ prisoners of war, refusing to keep relatives of the Prophet's wife as captives.

* **Life & Death:** Spent long hours in continuous prayer and fasting. Transmitted numerous traditions; died at age $65$ under Muawiya's rule and buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

### **9. Hazrat Umm-e-Habibah (RA)**

* **Background:** Real name Ramlah; daughter of Quraish chief Abu Sufyan. Migrated to Abyssinia with husband Ubaidullah bin Jahsh. When her husband apostatized to Christianity, she bravely left him and remained steadfast in Islam.

* **Marriage:** Married in $6$/$7$ A.H. upon a proposal sent by the Prophet (PBUH) to resettle her and protect her from her hostile father.

* **Loyalty & Piety:** Looked after orphans and widows and narrated 65 Ahadith. During the siege of Khalifa Usman’s house, she personally attempted to deliver food and water despite rebels blocking her. Died in $44$ A.H. (age $73$) during her brother Muawiya's reign.

### **10. Hazrat Safiyah (RA)**

* **Background:** Real name Zainab; belonged to the Jewish tribe Banu Nazir. Daughter of Huyaiy bin Akhtab and widow of Kinana bin Rabi. Captured after the fall of Khyber. The Prophet (PBUH) threw his mantle over her, indicating his decision to free and marry her.

* **Intellectual Renown:** Historians like Allama Abdul Birr and Ibn-e-Aseer lauded her immense wisdom, intelligence, and patience.

* **Service:** During the siege of Usman's house, she attempted to carry supplies to him and later sent Imam Hassan (RA) to deliver food and water. Died at age $60$; buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi.

### **11. Hazrat Maimunah bint Haris (RA)**

* **Background:** Original name Barra; sister-in-law of Hazrat Abbas. Widowed after previous marriages to Masud bin Amr and Abu Rahim bin Abdullah.

* **Marriage:** Married in $7$ A.H. upon the initiative of Hazrat Abbas when Muslims peacefully entered Makkah. Renamed *Maimunah* ("the blessed one").

* **Traits:** Called a "Symbol of Goodness" by the Prophet (PBUH). Known for freeing slaves, maintaining family ties, and transmitting $46$ Ahadith. Died in $51$ A.H. (or $61$ A.H.) at the exact site of her marriage; funeral led by Ibn Abbas.

### **12. Hazrat Rehana bint Sham'oon (RA)**

* **Background:** Belonged to the Jewish tribe Banu Quraiza. Taken captive in $6$ A.H.

* **Status:** Accepted Islam, whereupon the Prophet (PBUH) freed and married her. Passed away ten years after the Prophet's death.

### **13. Hazrat Maria Qibtia (RA)**

* **Background:** Sent as a gift to the Prophet (PBUH) by the ruler of Egypt following the Treaty of Hudaibiya. Accepted Islam before arriving in Madinah and subsequently married the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Motherhood & Status:** Honored as the only wife besides Khadija to bear the Prophet (PBUH) a son, named Ibrahim. Deeply mourned Ibrahim's death in infancy alongside the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Later Years:** Passed away $5$ years after her son; buried in Jannat-ul-Baqi. Held in high esteem by Khalifas Abu Bakr and Umar, who granted her a state pension that continued to her family after her death.
