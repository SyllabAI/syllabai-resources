## Allah's relation with the Created World

### **6. Surah Al-Fatihah ($1$:$1$–$7$)**

**Translation:**

In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds,Most Gracious, Most Merciful,Master of the Day of Judgment.You alone we worship, and Your aid we seek.Show us the straight way,The way of those on whom You have bestowed Your Grace,

Those whose portion is not wrath, and who go not astray.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Summary of Guidance:** The opening chapter of the Quran, establishing God's relationship with creation as the Sustainer of all worlds, the ultimate source of mercy, justice, and guidance.

* **Free Will & Prayer:** God does not compel humans to obey Him; rather, He commands them to ask Him directly for guidance on the straight path.

* **Accountability:** Highlights that following or straying from His guidance carries consequences on the Day of Judgment.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"And your Lord says: Call on Me, I shall answer your supplication."* (Surah Ghafir $40$:$60$)

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Core of Prayer:** Recited in every unit (*Rakat*) of daily prayers, keeping the believer in constant connection with God.

* **Seeking Guidance:** Reminds Muslims to seek help and moral direction solely from God and follow the path outlined in the Quran and Sunnah.

* **Balancing Hope & Fear:** Encourages believers to rely on God's divine mercy while remaining conscious of His absolute justice on the Day of Reckoning.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"He has inscribed for Himself the rule of mercy."* (Surah Al-An'am $6$:$12$, $54$)

  > *"...and only on the Day of Judgment shall you be paid your full recompense."* (Surah Ali 'Imran $3$:$185$)

### **7. Surah Al-Baqarah ($2$:$21$–$22$)**

**Translation:**

21\. O people! Adore your Guardian-Lord, who created you and those who came before you, so that you may have the chance to learn righteousness; 22. Who has made the earth your couch, and the heavens your canopy; and sent down rain from the heavens; and by it brought forth fruits for your sustenance; then do not set up rivals to Allah, when you know.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Creator & Sustainer:** A Madani passage highlighting God's creative power and His provision of sustenance (earth as a couch, sky as a canopy, rain producing fruits) for mankind across generations.

* **Command to Worship:** Directs all human beings to identify, serve, and worship their true Lord exclusively.

* **Rejection of Rivals:** Forbids setting up equals or partners alongside God in any form.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"There is no god but I; therefore worship and serve Me."* (Surah Al-Anbiya $21$:$25$)

  > *"And your God is one God."* (Surah Al-Baqarah $2$:$163$)

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Developing Taqwa:** Encourages believers to cultivate *Taqwa* (god-consciousness/piety) and express gratitude for God's endless provisions.

* **Avoiding Ingratitude:** Reminds Muslims that using God’s bounties while committing *Shirk* is supreme ingratitude.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"Then which of the bounties of your Lord will you deny?"* (Surah Ar-Rahman $55$)

  > *"O you who believe! Fear God as He should be feared and die not except in a state of Islam."* (Surah Ali 'Imran $3$:$102$)

### **8. Surah Al-Alaq ($96$:$1$–$5$)**

**Translation:**

Read! In the name of your Lord, who created,Created man out of a clot of congealed blood:Proclaim! And your Lord is Most Bountiful,He who taught by the pen,Taught man what he did not know.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **First Divine Revelation:** Highlights God's role as the Creator who brought man into being from a humble origin (a clot of congealed blood).

* **Intellectual Development:** Emphasizes God's benevolence in granting humans the ability to read, write, and acquire both worldly and spiritual knowledge.

* **Total Dependence:** Demonstrates that human beings are entirely dependent on their Creator for their physical, intellectual, and spiritual growth.

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Value of Seeking Knowledge:** Obligates Muslims to pursue both religious and beneficial secular education.

* **Humility:** Reminds humans of their humble origins to eliminate pride and arrogance.

* **Gratitude for Divine Books:** Teaches believers to appreciate divine revelations (like the Quran) as the ultimate guidance for human elevation.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"We have indeed created man in the best of moulds."* (Surah At-Tin $95$:$4$)

  > *"It is He Who has taught the Quran."* (Surah Ar-Rahman $55$:$2$)

### **9. Surah Al-Zilzal ($99$)**

**Translation:**

When the earth is shaken to her utmost convulsion,And the earth throws up her burdens,

And man cries out: 'What is the matter with her?',On that day will she declare her tidings:

For that your Lord will have given her inspiration.On that day will men proceed in companies sorted out, to be shown their deeds.Then shall anyone who has done an atom's weight of good see it!And anyone who has done an atom's weight of evil shall see it.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **The Day of Reckoning:** Describes the apocalyptic events at the end of time, where cataclysmic/severe earth-shaking convulsions will reverse normal physical laws.

* **Unearthing Secret Deeds:** The earth will discharge its contents, and human beings will be sorted to witness the absolute outcome of their actions.

* **Absolute Accountability:** Every single deed, no matter how small (atom's weight), will be brought into full view for justice.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"And when the earth is flattened and casts forth what is within it and becomes empty."* (Surah Al-Inshiqaq $84$:$3$-$4$)

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Faith in the Hereafter (*Akhirah*):** Anchors a Muslim's belief in the Day of Judgment and divine accountability.

* **Ethical Conduct:** Motivates believers to avoid even minor sins and perform small acts of goodness, knowing nothing is overlooked.

* **Detachment from Materialism:** Realizing the temporary nature of this world reduces greed and material attachment.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"Nay, you prefer the life of this world, but the Hereafter is better and more enduring."* (Surah Al-A'la $87$:$16$-$17$)

### **10. Surah Al-Nas ($114$)**

**Translation:**

In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful. 1.Say: I seek refuge with the Lord of Mankind,

2\. The King of Mankind, 3.The Allah of Mankind, 4.From the evil of the whisperer who withdraws,

5\.Who whispers in the hearts of Mankind, 6.From among Jinn and Mankind.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Threefold Relationship:** Defines God's authority over man as his Lord (*Rabb*), King (*Malik*), and God (*Ilah*).

* **Protection against Stealthy Evil:** Instructs humans to seek shelter in God against invisible internal evils—specifically Satan (*Iblis*), his whispers, and evil companions among Jinns and humans.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"...for Satan is an avowed enemy to man."* (Surah Yusuf $12$:$5$)

  > *"And on God let the believers put their trust."* (Surah Al-Mujadila $58$:$10$)

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Spiritual Safeguard:** Encourages Muslims to constantly recite the *Mu'awwidhatayn* (Surahs Al-Falaq and Al-Nas) to guard against internal temptations, jealousy, and evil influences.

* **Constant Remembrance (*Dhikr*):** Teaches believers that the strongest weapon against Satanic whispers is the active remembrance of God and regular prayer.

* **Prophetic Example:** Follows the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), who regularly recited these verses for spiritual protection.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"And establish regular prayer in order to remember Me."* (Surah Taha $20$:$14$)

  > *"When you recite the Quran, seek God's protection against the evil ones."* (Surah An-Nahl $16$:$98$)
