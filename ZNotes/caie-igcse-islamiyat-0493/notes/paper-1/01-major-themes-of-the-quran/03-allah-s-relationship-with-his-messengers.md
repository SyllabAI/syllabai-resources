## Allah's Relationship with His  Messengers

### **11. Surah Al-Baqarah ($2$:$30$–$37$)**

**Translation:**

30\. Behold, your Lord said to the angels: 'I will create a vicegerent on earth.' They said: 'Will You place there one who will make mischief there and shed blood?—whilst we celebrate Your praises and glorify Your holy (name)?' He said: 'I know what you do not know.' 31. And He taught Adam the names of all things; then He placed them before the angels, and said: 'Tell Me the names of these if you are right.' 32. They said: 'Glory to You, of knowledge we have none, save what You have taught us: In truth it is You who are perfect in knowledge and wisdom.' 33. He said: 'Adam! Tell them their names.' When he had told them, Allah said: 'Did I not tell you that I know the secrets of heavens and earth, and I know what you reveal and what you conceal?' 34. And behold, We said to the angels: 'Bow down to Adam.' And they bowed down. Not so Iblis: he refused and was haughty: he was of those who reject faith. 35. We said: 'Adam! You and your wife dwell in the Garden; and eat of the bountiful things in it as you wish. But do not approach this tree, or you will run into harm and transgression.' 36. Then Satan made them slip from there, and got them out of what they had been in. We said: 'Go down, with enmity between yourselves. On earth will be your dwelling-place and your means of livelihood, for a time.' 37. Then Adam learnt from his Lord words of inspiration, and his Lord turned towards him; for He is Often-returning, Most Merciful.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Creation of Adam:** Details the creation of Prophet Adam (AS), his elevated status over angels due to superior divine knowledge, and his appointment as God's vicegerent (*Khalifah*) on earth.

* **Arrogance of Iblis:** Describes Iblis's refusal to bow due to pride and his subsequent plot to trick Adam and Eve into eating from the forbidden tree.

* **Repentance & Forgiveness:** Adam and Eve were exiled to Earth, but upon sincere repentance, God mercifully pardoned them.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"It is We Who have placed you with authority on earth..."* (Surah Al-A'raf $7$:$10$)

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Human Responsibility:** Reminds Muslims of their role as stewards/vicegerents of God on Earth, blessed with intellect and free will.

* **Vigilance Against Satan:** Warns believers that Satan is an active enemy continuously trying to mislead mankind through temptation.

* **Hope through Repentance:** Demonstrates that making mistakes is human, but God's door of forgiveness (*Tawbah*) is always open to those who genuinely repent.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"...for Satan is an avowed enemy to man."* (Surah Yusuf $12$:$5$)

  > *"If anyone does evil or wrongs his own soul, but afterwards seeks God's forgiveness, he will find God Oft-Forgiving and Most Merciful."* (Surah An-Nisa $4$:$110$)

### **12. Surah Al-An'am ($6$:$75$–$79$)**

**Translation:**

75\. So also did We show Abraham the power and the laws of the heavens and the earth, so that he might have certainty. 76. When the night covered him over, he saw a star: He said: 'This is my Lord.' But when it set, he said: 'I do not love things that set.' 77. When he saw the moon rising in splendour, he said: 'This is my Lord.' But when the moon set, he said: 'Unless my Lord guides me, I will surely be among those who go astray.' 78. When he saw the sun rising in splendour, he said: 'This is my Lord; this is the greatest.' But when the sun set, he said: 'O my people! I am indeed free from your giving partners to Allah.' 79. 'For me, I have set my face firmly and truly towards Him who created the heavens and the earth, and never shall I give partners to Allah.'

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Search for Truth:** Depicts Prophet Ibrahim's (AS) intellectual journey to deduce the existence of the true God by observing celestial bodies (stars, moon, sun).

* **Temporary Nature of Creation:** Ibrahim realized that created objects setting/fading cannot be divine, leading him to reject polytheism (*Shirk*).

* **Declaration of Faith:** Concludes with Ibrahim's firm declaration of *Shahadat*—turning exclusively to the Originator of the heavens and earth.

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Rejection of Shirk:** Teaches believers that physical, celestial, or material objects should never be worshipped or elevated to divine status.

* **Unity of Prophetic Message:** Demonstrates that all Prophets—from Ibrahim (AS) to Muhammad (PBUH)—preached the exact same message of pure monotheism (*Tawhid*).

* **Using Reason in Faith:** Encourages Muslims to use observation, logic, and deep thought to strengthen their personal faith.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"He has made subject to you the night and the day; the sun and the moon; and the stars are in subjection by His command."* (Surah An-Nahl $16$:$12$)

### **13. Surah Al-Ma'idah ($5$:$110$)**

**Translation:**

Then will Allah say: 'Jesus son of Mary! Recount My favour to you and to your mother. Behold! I strengthened you with the Holy Spirit, so that you spoke to the people in childhood and in maturity. Behold! I taught you the Book and Wisdom, the Law and the Gospel. And behold! You make out of clay, as it were, the figure of a bird, by My leave, and you breathe into it and it becomes a bird by My leave, and you heal those born blind, and the lepers, by My leave. And behold! You bring forth the dead by My leave. And behold! I restrained the Children of Israel from you when you showed them the clear signs, and the unbelievers among them said: 'This is nothing but evident magic.'

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Address to Jesus (*Isa AS*):** God’s address to Prophet Isa (AS) on the Day of Judgment, recalling the special favors and miracles granted to him (speaking from the cradle, healing the blind and leper, bringing clay birds to life, raising the dead).

* **Divine Scriptures:** Highlights that Isa (AS) was taught the Torah, Gospel, and divine wisdom directly by God.

* **Rejection by Disbelievers:** Shows that despite clear, undeniable miracles, the spiritually stubborn among the Israelites rejected him and accused him of sorcery.

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Status of Isa (AS):** Teaches Muslims that Isa (AS) was a revered human prophet of God, endowed with miracles by divine permission, not a deity or son of God.

* **Warning Against Spiritual Blindness:** Warns believers not to become hard-hearted or blind to truth like past communities who rejected clear signs.

* **Accountability:** Reminds humans that all individuals—including Prophets and their nations—will account for their deeds before God.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"Thus does God make clear to you His signs in order that you consider."* (Surah Al-Baqarah $2$:$219$)

  > *"Those who reject Our signs are deaf and dumb and in the midst of darkness profound."* (Surah Al-An'am $6$:$39$)

### **14. Surah Al-Duha ($93$)**

**Translation:** In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

By the Glorious Morning Light,And by the Night when it is still,Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor is He displeased.And indeed, the Hereafter will be better for you than the present,And soon your Lord will give to you, and you will be well pleased.Did He not find you an orphan and gave you shelter?

And He found you wandering, and He gave you guidance.And He found you in need, and made you independent.Therefore do not treat the orphan with harshness,

Nor repulse the petitioner (unheard);But proclaim the bounty of your Lord.

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Divine Comfort:** Revealed after a pause in revelation (*Fatrat al-Wahy*) to reassure Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) that God had neither abandoned nor despised him during times of mockery by Makkan enemies.

* **Past Favors:** Reminds the Prophet of past care—being an orphan and provided shelter, being in search and guided, being in need and enriched.

* **Moral Directives:** Commands kindness to orphans and beggars as an expression of gratitude for divine blessings.

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Hope in Difficult Times:** Assures Muslims that periods of hardship or feeling distant from help are temporary, urging them to remain steadfast during trials.

* **Care for the Vulnerable:** Teaches that true gratitude to God is demonstrated through active charity, compassion, and protecting orphans, beggars, and the weak.

* **Cross-References:**

  > *"So, verily with every difficulty there is a relief."* (Surah Al-Inshirah $94$:$5$)

  > *"It is righteous to spend of your substance out of love for Him, for your kin, for orphans..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah $2$:$177$)

### **15. Surah Al-Kauthar ($108$)**

**Translation:** In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

We have given you the Abundance;Therefore pray to your Lord, and sacrifice.For the one who hates you, he will be cut off (from future hope)

### **(a) Main Theme**

* **Context of Revelation:** Revealed to console Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) when Makkan enemies (Abu Lahab) mocked him over the death of his male children, calling him *Abtar* (cut off).

* **Divine Reassurance:** Promises the Prophet *Al-Kauthar* (abundance of blessings, ultimate success, a river in Paradise, and an unending spiritual lineage).

* **Destruction of Enemies:** Declares that those who insult or oppose the Prophet's mission will themselves be cut off from legacy and mercy.

### **(b) Importance in a Muslim's Life Today**

* **Patience under Ridicule:** Encourages Muslims facing hardship or insults for their faith to remain firm, knowing that truth eventually triumphs over falsehood.

* **Triumph of Truth:** It reassures believers that a noble mission will always survive, even if opposed by the misguided. The "abundance" promised to the Prophet (PBUH) materialized through his millions of followers and his descendants through Hazrat Fatima (RA), whereas his enemies perished without a legacy.

* **Patience and Perseverance:** Muslims learn that righteousness requires patience (*Sabr*), as supported elsewhere in the Quran:

  > *"Rejected were the Messengers before you; with patience and constancy they bore their rejection."* (Surah Al-An'am $6$:$34$)
