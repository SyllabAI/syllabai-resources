## Main Themes of the Quran

### Theme $1$: Allah in Himself

**Passage $1$: Ayat al-Kursi ($2$:$255$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Highlights Tauhid (Oneness of Allah), His majesty, infinite power, eternal existence, and absolute dominion. Unlike humans, He does not need sleep or rest.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Hadid ($57$:$3$):** *"He is the First, the Last, the Most High and the Most Near..."*

  * **Surah Al-Baqarah ($2$:$284$):** Allah's complete knowledge of everything hidden and open.

  * **Hadith:** *"Ayat al-Kursi is the chief of the verses of the Quran."* (Musnad Ahmad / Sahih Bukhari)

**Passage $2$: Surah Al-An'am ($6$:$101$–$103$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Emphasizes that Allah is the Originator of the heavens and earth, having no partner, spouse, or child. He is Omniscient and beyond human physical sight, yet He sees everything.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Hashr ($59$:$23$–$24$):** *"He is Allah, besides whom none has the right to be worshipped... Creator, Inventor, Bestower of forms..."*

  * **Surah Al-A'raf ($7$:$185$):** Encourages looking into the Kingdom of the heavens and the earth.

**Passage $3$: Surah Fussilat ($41$:$37$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Points to signs in nature—the sun, moon, day, and night—as evidence of Allah's power. Instructs humans to worship the Creator of these objects, not the objects themselves.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Ash-Shura ($42$:$11$):** *"There is nothing whatever like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing."*

**Passage $4$: Surah Ash-Shura ($42$:$4$–$5$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Speaks of Allah’s supreme authority, grandeur, and forgiveness. Angels glorify Him and pray for the forgiveness of those on Earth.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-A'raf ($7$:$156$):** *"And My Mercy embraces all things."*

  * **Surah Az-Zumar ($39$:$53$):** *"Despair not of the Mercy of Allah: Verily, Allah forgives all sins."*

**Passage $5$: Surah Al-Ikhlas ($112$:$1$–$4$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Defines pure Tawhid. Declares Allah is Eternal and Absolute (*Samad*), has no family, offspring, or ancestral ties, and has no equivalent.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Sad ($38$:$5$):** Refuting polytheistic claims (*"What makes He the god a single Allah?"*).

  * **Surah At-Tawbah ($9$:$30$):** Rejecting beliefs of attributing sons to God (*"The Jews say Uzair is the son of Allah; and the Christians say the Messiah..."*).

### Theme $2$: Allah’s Relationship with the Created World

**Passage $6$: Surah Al-Fatiha ($1$:$1$–$7$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Establishes Allah as Creator (*Rab-bul-Alameen*), Beneficent, Merciful, and Master of the Day of Judgment. Teaches Muslims to seek His guidance alone to stay on the Straight Path (*Sirat al-Mustaqim*).

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-A'raf ($7$:$54$):** Sustainer of the entire universe.

  * **Surah Al-An'am ($6$:$153$):** Seeking and following the Straight Path.

**Passage $7$: Surah Al-Baqarah ($2$:$21$–$22$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Commands mankind to worship Allah alone who created Earth as a bed, the sky as a canopy, and sent down rain to yield sustenance. Warns against setting up rivals to Him.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Ibrahim ($14$:$7$):** *"If you give gratitude... I will give you more of My blessings..."*

  * **Surah An-Nahl ($16$:$78$):** Gratitude for faculties given for human survival.

**Passage $8$: Surah Al-Alaq ($96$:$1$–$5$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Highlights human origin from a humble clot of blood (*Alaq*) and Allah's grace in elevating man by teaching knowledge through the pen.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Baqarah ($2$:$31$–$33$):** Granting Adam knowledge of things beyond the reach of angels.

**Passage $9$: Surah Az-Zilzal ($99$:$1$–$8$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Describes the convulsion of the Earth on Judgment Day and full accountability where even an atom's weight of good or evil will be weighed.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Infitar ($82$:$14$):** Punishment for the wicked in blazing fire.

  * **Surah Al-Qari'ah ($101$:$9$):** Hawiyah for those whose good deeds are light.

**Passage $10$: Surah An-Naas ($114$:$1$–$6$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Asks believers to seek refuge in Allah (Lord, King, and God of mankind) against the secret whispers of Satan and evil forces.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Falaq ($113$:$1$–$5$):** Seeking refuge from outer harm

### Theme $3$: Allah’s Relationship with His Messengers

**Passage $11$: Hazrat Adam (A.S.) ($2$:$30$–$37$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Creation of Adam as vicegerent (*Khalifah*), superiority in knowledge over angels, testing through the forbidden tree, transgression due to Satan, and divine forgiveness upon repentance.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Kahf ($18$:$29$):** Freedom of choice given to humans.

  * **Hadith Qudsi:** *"My mercy overcomes my wrath."*

**Passage 12: Hazrat Ibrahim (A.S.) (6:75–79)**

* **Main Teaching:** Ibrahim’s intellectual reflection observing the stars, moon, and sun, rejecting them as gods because they set, arriving at pure monotheism (*Khalilullah*).

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah An-Najm ($53$:$39$):** *"Man only gets what he strives for."*

  * **Surah Al-Anbiya ($21$:$51$–$70$):** Ibrahim's rejection and destruction of idols.

**Passage $13$: Hazrat Isa (A.S.) ($5$:$110$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Favors granted to Isa and Maryam; miracles given to Isa (speaking in cradle, making clay birds come to life, healing lepers/blind) strictly by Allah's leave (*Injeel*).

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Ali 'Imran ($3$:$45$–$49$):** Announcement of Isa's miraculous birth and powers by Allah's permission.

**Passage $14$: Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) - Surah Ad-Duha ($93$:$1$–$11$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Comforts the Prophet (PBUH) during a pause in revelation , reminding him of past favors (orphanhood sheltered, guided, enriched) and commanding kindness to orphans and beggars.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Inshirah ($94$:$1$–$8$):** Consolation, lifting burden, and expanding his chest.

**Passage $15$: Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) - Surah Al-Kauthar ($108$:$1$–$3$)**

* **Main Teaching:** Reassures the Prophet (PBUH) during persecution following the death of his sons; promises abundant goodness (*Al-Kauthar*) and declares his enemies to be cut off from future hope.Abundance has been given in the form that millions remember him with love even today whilst his enemies are not remembered with love.

* **Cross-References:**

  * **Surah Al-Ahzab ($33$:$40$):** Establishing Muhammad (PBUH) as the Seal of Prophets
