## Significance of the Holy Quran

### 1. Primary & Supreme Source of Authority

* **Ultimate Foundation:** The Qur'an is the supreme, primary source of Islamic law and belief. All other sources (Sunnah, Ijma, Qiyas) are dependent upon and subordinate to its guidance.

* **Universal & Unalterable:** It is an eternal miracle and a complete guide meant for all humanity regardless of time, race, or status. Its divine nature ensures it remains unchangeable and preserved.

* **Command of Obedience:** The Qur'an explicitly commands complete obedience to Allah's divine laws and the guidance of the Prophet (pbuh).

> *"Obey Allah and obey the Messenger, and those charged with authority among you..."*
>
> — Surah Al-Nisa ($4$:$59$)

### 2. Complete Guidance for Human Destiny & Autonomy

* **Human Vicegerency (*Khalifah*):** Allah has endowed human beings with free will, intellect, and moral autonomy, appointing them as His representatives on earth.

* **The Right Path vs. Misguidance:**

  * Following Quranic guidance brings peace, contentment in this life, and eternal bliss in Paradise (*Jannat*).

  * Rejecting divine principles leads to corruption, disorder, and severe punishment in the Hereafter.

* **All-Encompassing System:** The Qur'an establishes a comprehensive framework covering spiritual, moral, social, economic, and political dimensions of human life.

> *"Indeed, this Qur'an guides to that which is most suitable..."*
>
> — Surah Al-Isra ($17$:$9$)

### 3. Moral & Social Order

* **Promotion of Virtue:** Commands Muslims to enjoin good (*Amr bil-Ma'ruf*) and forbid evil (*Nahi 'an al-Munkar*).

* **Justice & Equality:** Establishes absolute justice, fraternity, and protection of human rights within society.

> *"Verily, Allah commands justice, the doing of good, and giving to kinsfolk, and forbids all shameful deeds, injustice, and rebellion..."*
>
> — Surah An-Nahl ($16$:$90$)

### 4. Directives on the Sources of Law

The Qur'an outlines the hierarchy of authority in Islamic jurisprudence:

1. **The Qur'an:** The ultimate, unalterable word of Allah.

2. **The Sunnah:** The practical implementation and explanation of Quranic principles by the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh).

3. **Ijma (Consensus):** Agreement among Islamic scholars on a legal issue derived from Quranic principles.

4. **Qiyas (Analogical Reasoning):** Legal deduction applied to new circumstances based on existing Quranic laws.

> *"We have revealed to you the Book in truth, so that you may judge between people by that which Allah has shown you."*
>
> — Surah An-Nisa ($4$:$105$)
