# History and Importance of the Holy Quran

## First Revelation

* **Initial Event:** The first revelation was brought down to the Prophet (PBUH) in $610$ CE in the Cave of Hira on Mount Al-Noor (Jabal al-Nour) by the Angel Jibra'eel.

* **Background & Preparation:**

  * **Retreat from Society:** Between the ages of $35$ and $40$, the Prophet (PBUH) distanced himself from the corrupt practices of Makkan society to meditate in the Cave of Hira ($4$m long, $1.75$m wide), where his grandfather had also gone.

  * **Support:** His wife, Hazrat Khadija (RA), supported him by providing food such as barley porridge  and water.

  * **Early Signs:** Prior to the event, stones greeted him, and his dreams consistently came true.

* **The Encounter in Cave Hira:**

  * During *Laylat al-Qadr* (Night of Power), Angel Jibra'eel appeared and ordered the Prophet (PBUH) to read.

  * The unlettered (*Ummi*) Prophet (PBUH) replied that he could not read.

  * The Angel embraced and squeezed him tightly three times—until the Prophet (PBUH) felt his ribs would be crushed and that he might suffocate—before releasing him.

* **The First Verses:** Upon the third command, the Prophet (PBUH) asked what to read, and the revelation began with the first $5$ verses of Surah Al-Alaq ($96$:$1$–$5$):

  > *"Read in the name of your Lord who created—created man from a clinging clot. Read! And your Lord is the Most Generous—who taught by the pen—taught man that which he knew not."*

* **The Vision & Reaction:**

  * On his return, the Prophet (PBUH) saw Angel Jibra'eel as a gigantic figure covering the entire horizon, declaring:

    > *"O Muhammad, you are indeed the messenger of Allah and I am the archangel Jibril."*

  * Scared, shivering, and sweating, he returned home and asked Hazrat Khadija (RA) to cover him in a cloak, fearing something might happen to him.

  * Hazrat Khadija (RA) comforted him, stating Allah would never disgrace him because he unites blood relations, helps the poor/needy, and bears the burden of the weak.

* **Validation by Waraka bin Nauful:**

  * Hazrat Khadija (RA) took him to her cousin, Waraka bin Nauful, a blind Christian scholar of the Torah and Bible.

  * Waraka confirmed that the visitor was Angel Jibra'eel, who had visited Musa and Isa, confirming Muhammad (PBUH) was chosen as a Prophet.

  * Waraka predicted that the Prophet (PBUH) would be treated with hostility and turned out by his people—a prediction fulfilled $13$ years later during the exile from Makkah.

* **Pause and Resumption:** Following a temporary gap in revelations, divine communication resumed with Surah Al-Muddaththir (*"O you wrapped in your cloak! Arise and warn..."*), initiating the public preaching of Islam.

### **The Different Modes of Reception**

The Prophet (PBUH) received divine revelations through several distinct forms:

* **Angel Jibra'eel in His Real Form:** The Prophet (PBUH) saw the angel in his actual majestic appearance.

* **Angel Jibra'eel in Human Form:** The angel would appear in the form of a man, often resembling the companion Dihyah al-Kalbi. This was considered the easiest way for the Prophet (PBUH) to receive the message.

* **Ringing of Bells:** The revelation would arrive with a sound resembling the ringing of bells.

  * **The Hardest Form:** This was the most physically demanding and hardest form of revelation for the Prophet (PBUH).

  * **Physical Weight:** Hazrat Zaid bin Thabit narrated that the Prophet's (PBUH) leg was once on top of his leg when a revelation came down. The weight became so intense that Zaid felt as though his leg would crush.

  * **Surah Al-An'am:** This chapter was revealed using this intense mode while the Prophet (PBUH) was on top of a camel.

* **True Dreams:** Revelations were also communicated clearly to the Prophet (PBUH) through dreams, a fact supported by testimonies from Hazrat Ayesha.

### **Contextual Revelations**

**Occasion-Specific Surahs (Asbab al-Nuzul)**

Revelations were frequently sent down on different occasions to address specific events, crises, or questions:

* **Surah Al-Kauthar:** Revealed directly to console the Prophet (PBUH) when his detractors called him *Abtar* (cut off from lineage).

* **Surah Ad-Duha:** Revealed to comfort and console the Prophet (PBUH) during a temporary pause in revelations.

* **Surah Abasa:** Sent down by Allah to guide the Prophet (PBUH) regarding a specific interaction with a blind companion.

* **Surah An-Nur:** Revealed specifically to declare and definitively prove Hazrat Ayesha's innocence to the community.
