## Compilation of Quran

The Holy Qur’an was revealed over a period of $23$ years ($610$–$632$ AD) through various modes of revelation (e.g., direct speech, dreams, ringing of bells, and Angel Jibra’eel).

* **Imprinted on the Heart:** Whenever a revelation occurred, it was immediately imprinted upon the heart of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

* **System of Preservation:** The Prophet (PBUH) maintained memorizers (*Huffaz*) who committed verses to memory, and a team of 34 learned scribes (*Kattab-e-Wahy*)—including Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA), Hazrat Umar (RA), Hazrat Ali (RA), Hazrat Ubbay bin Ka'ab (RA), Hazrat Zubair bin Awwam (RA), and Hazrat Zaid bin Thabit (RA)—who recorded them upon dictation.

* **Writing Materials:** Verses were recorded on available scattered materials, such as flat stones, leather pieces, camel scapula (shoulder bones), paper, and palm leaves.

* **Verification & Arrangement:** After dictation, the Prophet (PBUH) asked scribes to read it back to confirm accuracy. Under divine instruction, he specified the exact Surah and position for every verse, arranging them into $114$ chapters (not in order of revelation).

* **Completion:** Upon the demise of the Holy Prophet (PBUH) in $632$ AD, divine revelation ended with the text fully preserved in memory and on scattered written fragments, but not yet bound into a single book.

### **Stage 1: Compilation under Caliph Abu Bakr (RA)**

**Battle of Yamama**

* During the Caliphate of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA), false prophets arose, most prominently Musailmah the Liar, who raised an army of $40,000$ men.

* In the fierce **Battle of Yamama** (also called the Battle of the Garden), led by Hazrat Khalid bin Waleed (RA), Musailmah was killed.

* **Impact:** Nearly **70 senior memorizers (*Huffaz*)** were martyred in this single battle.

**Initiative and Hesitation**

* Hazrat Umar (RA) realized that if memorizers continued to die, authentic verification of the Qur’anic text and sequence would become endangered.

* He advised Caliph Abu Bakr (RA) to collect and compile the Qur'an into a unified volume (*Mushaf*).

* Abu Bakr (RA) initially hesitated, stating he could not perform a task the Prophet (PBUH) had not done himself. Umar (RA) convinced him that the Prophet (PBUH) always intended for the text to be preserved together once revelation ceased at his demise.

**Methodology of Zaid bin Thabit (RA)**

* Abu Bakr (RA) appointed **Hazrat Zaid bin Thabit (RA)**—the chief scribe and personal secretary to the Prophet (PBUH)—to lead the task. Reflecting on the weight of the task, Zaid remarked that shifting a mountain would have been easier.

* Assisted by a committee of 75 companions, Zaid (RA) collected all written fragments from across Arabia, cross-checked them against his own memory and living *Huffaz*, required **two reliable witnesses** for every written piece, and verified the text with senior companions.

**Outcome: Mushaf-e-Siddiqi**

* Within approximately one year, the Qur'an was compiled into a master volume arranged from Surah Al-Fatihah to Surah Al-Nas, achieving absolute consensus (*Ijma*) among all companions.

  **Custody Lineage:**

* Because it was held by Hazrat Hafsah (RA) (daughter of Umar RA and wife of the Prophet PBUH), this official copy became known as **Mushaf-e-Siddiqi** or **Mushaf-e-Hafsa**.

### **Stage 2: Standardization under Caliph Uthman (RA)**

**Conflict of Dialects**

* As the Islamic empire expanded into non-Arab territories, converts began reciting the Qur’an in regional Arabic accents and dialects (*Ahruf*).

* Reports of heated disputes over pronunciation were brought to Caliph Uthman (RA) by **Hazrat Huzaifah (RA)** (from border campaigns) and **Hazrat Anas (RA)**.

* **The Danger:** Pronunciation differences risked altering the core meanings of the divine text and causing deep division within the *Ummah*.

**Methodology of Standardization**

* Hazrat Uthman (RA) formed a committee comprising **Hazrat Zaid bin Thabit (RA), Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair (RA), Hazrat Sa'eed bin Aas (RA), and Hazrat Abdur Rehman bin Harith (RA)**.

* **The Quraishi Dialect:** Uthman (RA) instructed that if any disagreement arose on dialect, it must be resolved in the **dialect of the Quraish**, as it was the dialect in which the Qur'an was originally revealed to the Prophet (PBUH).

* The original master copy (**Mushaf-e-Hafsa**) was borrowed from Hazrat Hafsah (RA) to ensure total textual accuracy, and standard copies were transcribed from Surah Al-Fatihah to Surah Al-Nas.

**Outcome: Universal Standardization & Jami-ul-Qur'an**

* Official copies were dispatched to every provincial capital (e.g., Kufa, Basra, Damascus) to be kept in the main mosques as standard reference texts.

* Caliph Uthman (RA) ordered all unofficial, private, or regional dialect copies to be burned to prevent future confusion or disagreement.

* Due to this monumental service in uniting the Muslims under one standard text, Hazrat Uthman (RA) earned the title **Jami-ul-Qur'an** (The Collector/Unifier of the Qur'an).
