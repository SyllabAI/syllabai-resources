## Communal and Individual Hadith

**Individual Ahadith**

**1. "Religion is sincerity." We said "To whom?" Prophet said "To Allah, His Book, His Messenger, the leaders of the Muslim world and the common people."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Actions are judged by intentions."

* Prophet (PBUH) tells us to be sincere.

* Read Quran and follow Allah's order.

* Accept good advice from elders.

* Follow our leader, peacefully protest or decline wrong decisions.

* Do not fake our deeds to show off but have pure intentions.

**2. Once a man asked the messenger of Allah: "Do you think if I offer my obligatory prayers and fast in Ramadan, treat as lawful which is lawful and treat as unlawful which is unlawful, and do nothing more, shall I enter paradise?" He said, "Yes."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Between a man and disbelief is disregarding prayer."

* Offer our regular prayer.

* Fast in Ramadan.

* Differentiate between lawful and unlawful.

**3. "No one eats better food than that which he eats out of the work of his hand."**

* **Sub-reference:** "The one who earns through the sweat of his brows is the friend of Allah."

* To work hard.

* Maintain difference between haram and halal.

* Barkat in Halal.

**4. "The world is the believer's prison and unbeliever's paradise."**

* **Sub-reference:** "The world is the cultivation ground for Hereafter."

* We should spend life preparing for Hereafter.

* Submit to Allah and strive for His cause.

* Do good deeds.

**5. "Allah does not regard your appearance and possessions, but He regards your hearts and your actions."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Allah knows it all..."

* Intentions matter most.

* Allah knows everything and our hearts, so we should have good thoughts and intentions.

* We can lie to people, but not Allah.

**6."Modesty produces nothing but good."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Islam is a religion of modesty."

* Modesty (*Haya*) is an essential branch of faith (*Iman*) that protects a believer from committing sins.

* Reflects inner purity and self-respect, influencing both behavior and appearance.

* True modesty prevents a person from engaging in immoral acts both in public and in private.

* Dress modestly and adhere to Islamic guidelines for attire.

* Lower our gaze and refrain from using vulgar language or engaging in inappropriate conversations.

* Avoid shameless behavior both in real life and on social media platforms.

**7."He who has in his heart as much faith as a grain of mustard seed will not enter Hell, and he who has in his heart as much pride as a grain of mustard seed will not enter Paradise."**

* **Sub-reference:** "And do not walk upon the earth exultantly. Indeed, Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful."

* Pride (*Kibr*) is a severe spiritual disease that distances a believer from Allah and Paradise.

* All blessings, wealth, and achievements belong to Allah, so arrogance has no place in a believer's heart.

* Humility is a key attribute required to enter Paradise.

* Treat everyone with respect regardless of their financial status, lineage, or social standing.

* Attribute all personal successes to Allah's grace rather than our own abilities.

* Avoid boasting about wealth, status, intelligence, or deeds.

**8."He who studies the Quran is like the owner of tethered camels. If he attends to them he will keep them, but if he lets them loose they will go away."**

* **Sub-reference:** "The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it."

* Memorization and understanding of the Quran require constant practice, revision, and effort.

* Neglecting sacred knowledge leads to forgetting it, just as untethered animals wander off.

* Maintaining a strong relationship with the Quran is a lifelong individual responsibility.

* Recite and revise the Quran daily to keep memory and understanding fresh.

* Act upon the teachings of the Quran so that the knowledge remains firmly rooted in our daily lives.

* Set aside dedicated time every day for religious study and reflection.

**9."It was said: 'O Messenger of Allah, which of men is greatest?' The Messenger of Allah said: 'A believer who strives in the cause of Allah with his person and his property.'"**

* **Sub-reference:** "Strive with your wealth and your lives in the cause of Allah."

* The highest status of a believer is achieved by dedicating one's life, physical efforts, and wealth to Allah's cause.

* Striving (*Jihad*) includes fighting temptation, doing good deeds, and using resources to uplift Islam.

* Material wealth and physical capacity should be utilized for divine reward rather than worldly vanity.

* Spend money in charity (*Sadaqah*) and support religious and humanitarian causes.

* Dedicate physical effort and time to volunteering, seeking knowledge, and serving the community.

* Actively resist personal desires and bad habits for the sake of Allah (*Jihad an-Nafs*).

**10."The Messenger of Allah said: 'Whom do you consider to be a martyr among you?' They said: 'O Messenger of Allah, the one who is killed in the cause of Allah is a martyr.' He said: 'Then the martyrs of my nation would be few! The one who is killed in the cause of Allah is a martyr, the one who dies in the cause of Allah is a martyr, the one who dies of plague is a martyr, the one who dies of a stomach disease is a martyr, and the one who drowns is a martyr.'"**

* **Sub-reference:** "And do not say about those who are killed in the way of Allah, 'They are dead.' Rather, they are alive..."

* Allah’s mercy is vast, and He grants the status of martyrdom (*Shahadah*) to believers who die under difficult or painful circumstances.

* True intention (*Niyyah*) to live a righteous life in the way of Allah earns divine rewards.

* Trials and illnesses in life serve as an expiation of sins for a patient believer.

* Remain patient (*Sabr*) during illness, hard times, and tragic circumstances, trusting Allah's decree.

* Live every day with the intention of pleasing Allah so that death comes while in a state of righteousness.

* Pray for a noble death and seek Allah's protection and mercy in times of affliction.

**Communal Ahadith**

**1. "None of you believe until you want for yourself what you want for your brother."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Believers are a single Brotherhood."

* Should be kind towards others.

* Help his Muslim brother in need.

* Don't be jealous, but happy for them.

**2. "I and the man who brings up an orphan would be like this in paradise," then he pointed with his two fingers, the middle and the index finger.**

* **Sub-reference:** "Treat not the orphan with harshness."

* Muslims should help the needy.

* Give orphans shelter or financial support.

* Highlighting that treating the one who is alone in the world with kindness will bring us closer to the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

**3. "May God show mercy to the one who is kind when he sells, when he buys, or when he demands his money back."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Allah is gentle and loves gentleness in all things."

* Teaches Muslims basic principles of honesty.

* Should be kind when they buy or sell.

* Should not be rude or arrogant.

* When demanding money back, should be gentle.

**4. "Allah does not show mercy to those who don't show mercy to others."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Allah does not show mercy to those who don't show mercy to others."

* Elaborates that mercy of Allah is to be earned by being merciful to others.

* We should be forgiving to others.

* We should control our anger.

* Should not beat or punish our subordinates for making mistakes.

**5. "The believers are like a single man; if the eye is affected, he is affected; if the head is affected, he is affected."**

* Victory of a Muslim brother is the victory of all Muslims.

* Loss of a Muslim brother is the loss of all Muslims.

* Supports our Muslim brother.

* Console our Muslim brother.

**6."Let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day speak good or keep silent, and let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day be generous to his neighbor, and let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day be generous to his guest."**

* **Sub-reference:** "Worship Allah... and do good to neighbors who are near, neighbors who are strangers, the companion by your side..."

* True faith (*Iman*) is directly linked to social conduct, controlling one's speech, and honoring guests and neighbors.

* Refraining from gossip, slander, and hurtful talk preserves communal peace.

* Neighborly rights and hospitality are sacred duties in Islam that foster a caring society.

* Think carefully before speaking; choose beneficial words or remain silent.

* Share food, check on the well-being of neighbors, and avoid causing them annoyance.

* Welcome guests warmly and extend hospitality regardless of their background.

**7."Every day upon which the sun rises, charity is due from every joint of a person: to judge justly between two people is charity; to help a man with his mount, lifting him onto it or hoisting his goods onto it, is charity; a good word is charity; every step you take towards prayer is charity; and removing a harmful thing from the road is charity."**

* **Sub-reference:** "And do good; indeed, Allah loves the doers of good."

* Charity (*Sadaqah*) is not limited to monetary wealth; every physical gift and capability can be used as charity.

* Simple, daily positive actions create a harmonious, civic-minded society.

* Reconciliation, helpfulness, kind speech, and public hygiene are all acts of worship.

* Remove trash, stones, or dangerous obstacles from public pathways.

* Help reconcile differences between friends or family members fairly.

**8."Whosoever of you sees an evil, let him change it with his hand; and if he is not able to do so, then with his tongue; and if he is not able to do so, then with his heart—and that is the weakest of faith."**

* **Sub-reference:** "You are the best nation produced for mankind. You enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong..."

* Muslims have a collective responsibility to eradicate injustice, moral corruption, and evil from society.

* Action against wrongdoings must be proportionate to one's authority and ability.

* Disliking evil in one's heart is the absolute minimum level of faith required.

* Use legitimate authority to stop wrongdoing directly.

* Speak out against injustice, bullying, and oppression using gentle yet firm words.

* Never condone, applaud, or silently accept sinful or harmful behavior in society.

**9."The Prophet sent Mu'adh and Abu Musa to Yemen and said: 'Make things easy and do not make them difficult, spread glad tidings and do not cause people to turn away, and cooperate with each other and do not divide.'"**

* **Sub-reference:** "Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship."

* Religious leadership, teaching, and administration should focus on kindness, accessibility, and unity.

* Over-complicating religious matters or being overly harsh drives people away from Islam.

* Unity and cooperation among leaders and community members are essential for societal progress.

* Teach and preach Islam with patience, wisdom, and encouragement rather than harsh judgment.

* Work collaboratively in group tasks and community projects instead of creating factions.

**10."Surely every one of you is a shepherd and every one of you is responsible for his flock. The ruler is a shepherd and is responsible for his subjects..."** 

* **Sub-reference:** "O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire..."

* Every individual holds a position of trust (*Amanah*) and authority in some capacity within society.

* Leadership is an accountability to be questioned on the Day of Judgment, not a privilege for self-aggrandizement.

* Social harmony depends on each person fulfilling their specific role dutifully.

* Fulfill leadership duties in school, home, or work with fairness, honesty, and care.

* Take responsibility for younger siblings, family members, or subordinates placed under our care.

* Guard and manage shared property, public resources, and workplace duties with integrity.
