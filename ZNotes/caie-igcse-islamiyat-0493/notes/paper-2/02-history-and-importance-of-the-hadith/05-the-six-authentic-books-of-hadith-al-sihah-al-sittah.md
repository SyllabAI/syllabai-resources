## **The Six Authentic Books of Hadith (Al-Sihah al-Sittah)**

### **Overview of the Sihah al-Sittah**

**Core Terminology**

* **Imam ul Muhadditheen:** The title given to the two most authentic leaders of Hadith collection, Imam al-Bukhari and Imam Muslim.

* **Al-Sahihain:** The collective term used to refer to the two most authentic books of Hadith: *Sahih al-Bukhari* and *Sahih Muslim*.

* **Mutafaqun 'Alayhi:** A term meaning "agreed upon," used when a specific Hadith is reported by both Imam al-Bukhari and Imam Muslim.

### **Pioneering Early Compilers (Pre-Sihah al-Sittah Era)**

* **Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri (d. 124 AH):**

  * **Activities & Travel:**

    * Based in Madinah; traveled across Hijaz, Syria, and Egypt under Umayyad patronage.

    * Studied directly under *Tabi'un* and surviving Companions like Anas bin Malik (RA).

  * **Key Contributions:**

    * Appointed by Caliph Umar bin Abdul Aziz to lead the first official state-sponsored Hadith collection.

    * Pioneered recording the full chain (*Isnad*) alongside the text (*Matn*) to verify authenticity.

* **Hammam bin Munabbih (d. 131 AH):**

  * **Activities & Travel:**

    * Yemenite *Tabi'i* who traveled to Madinah specifically to study under Abu Hurairah (RA).

  * **Key Contributions:**

    * Authored *Al-Sahifa al-Hammamiyya*, recording 138 Ahadith directly from Abu Hurairah.

    * Provided historical proof that Hadith texts were written down in the 1st century AH without alteration.

* **Imam Malik bin Anas (93 AH – 179 AH):**

  * **Activities & Travel:**

    * Known as *Imam Dar al-Hijrah* (Imam of Madinah); remained primarily in Madinah.

    * Held that the lived traditions (*'Amal*) of Madinah represented the purest transmission of the Sunnah.

  * **Key Contributions:**

    * Authored *Al-Muwatta*, selecting \~1,700 highly authentic Hadiths over 40 years.

    * Pioneered the thematic (*Musannaf*) legal structure used as a blueprint for later collections.

**Detailed Profiles of the Six Books**

**1. Sahih al-Bukhari**

* **Compiler:** Imam Muhammad bin Ismail al-Bukhari, who was originally from Bukhara.

* **Status:** Universally considered by scholars to be the most authentic book of Hadith after the Holy Quran.

* **Early Background of the Compiler:**

  * His father was also a dedicated scholar of Ahadith.

  * He began his formal study of Ahadith before reaching 10 years of age.

  * By the age of 16, he had memorized numerous books written by early Islamic scholars.

  * He dedicated significant effort to studying the biographies of individual narrators.

* **Structure and Composition:**

  * Divided systematically into **97 books**, which are further subdivided into **3,450 chapters**.

  * Contains a total number of **4,082 Ahadith**.

**2. Sahih Muslim**

* **Compiler:** Imam Muslim bin Hajjaj.

* **Status:** Ranked second in authenticity, directly following *Sahih al-Bukhari*.

* **Early Background of the Compiler:**

  * He initiated his specialized study of Ahadith at the age of 15.

  * He traveled extensively across various regions to collect narrations firsthand.

* **Structure and Selection:**

  * From a massive pool of 300,000 collected Ahadith, he strictly selected and included only the extremely authentic ones.

**3. Sunan Al-Tirmidhi**

* **Compiler:** Imam Muhammad bin Isa.

* **Scholarly Credentials:**

  * Traveled widely across the world to acquire knowledge.

  * Studied directly under the mentorship of Imam al-Bukhari.

  * Gained widespread fame for his exceptional memorization skills, trustworthiness, and deep knowledge.

* **Significance:** It is recognized as the most comprehensive book of the *Al-Sunan* genre, proving exceptionally useful to both the *Muhaddith* (Hadith scholar) and the *Faqih* (jurist).

* **Structure and Composition:**

  * The work is divided into **50 distinct chapters**.

  * Contains a total of **3,956 Ahadith**.

**4. Sunan Abu Dawud**

* **Compiler:** Imam Abu Dawud Sulaiman ibn al-Ash'ath al-Sijistani (Imam Abu Dawud).

* **Scholarly Profile:** He was a highly trusted narrator, a careful collector, a precise compiler, and a well-known *Faqih* (jurist).

* **Structure and Classification:**

  * He collected a total of half a million (500,000) Ahadith.

  * Out of those, this book preserves **4,800 absolutely authentic Ahadith**.

  * It features *Musannaf* Ahadith (arranged systematically by legal topics).

**5. Sunan Ibn Majah**

* **Compiler:** Imam Ibn Majah (Abu Abdullah Muhammad ibn Yazid al-Qazwini)

* **Geographical Journey:** Traveled extensively to collect narrations. His educational travels included Iraq, Egypt, Makkah, and Baghdad.

* **Distinct Features:**

  * The text is organized neatly into chapters and contains very little repetition.

  * Contains a total of **4,341 Ahadith**.

**6. Sunan Nasa'i**

* **Compiler:** Imam an-Nasa'i (Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb an-Nasa'i).

* **Background:**

  * Began his formal study of prophetic traditions at the age of 15.

  * Took long, arduous journeys to learn and verify various Ahadith.

* **Structure and Composition:**

  * Well-divided into logical chapters.

  * Contains a total of **5,662 Ahadith**.
