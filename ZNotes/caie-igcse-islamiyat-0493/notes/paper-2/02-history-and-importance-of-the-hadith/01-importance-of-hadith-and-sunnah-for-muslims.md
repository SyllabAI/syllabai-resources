# History and Importance of the Hadith

## **Importance of Hadith and Sunnah for Muslims**

* **Essential for Muslims:** Ahadith provide the practical explanation of Quranic teachings.

* **Clarification of Commandment Details:**

  * The Quran commands obligations like prayer (*Salah*), fasting (*Sawm*), and charity (*Zakat*).

  * The Ahadith teach Muslims exactly how to pray, fast, and how much *Zakat* to distribute.

  * **Prophet's Guidance:** The Prophet (PBUH) instructed, *"Pray as you see me pray."* Without the Sunnah, it is impossible to understand how to fulfill Allah's commands.

* **Divine Authority:** Allah states in the Quran:

"He who obeys the Messenger has obeyed Allah." (Surah an-Nisa, 4:80)

* **Preservation of Moral Conduct:** Hadith preserves the Prophet’s (PBUH) moral example for all of humanity for all time. The Quran refers to him as *"the best example"* (Surah al-Ahzab, 33:21).

* **Comprehensive Life Guide:** Hadith guides Muslims in every aspect of life, including eating, speaking, treating others, and worship.

* **Dual Foundations of Islam:** The Prophet (PBUH) stated:

"I have left among you two things, the Book of Allah and my Sunnah."

* * This emphasizes that both sources are absolutely necessary.

* **Deepening Devotion:** Studying Hadith deepens a Muslim's love for the Prophet (PBUH).

* **Tafsir al-Quran:** The Sunnah serves to explain unclear verses of the Quran.

* **Legal and Social Coverage:** Sunnah extends to areas not explicitly detailed in the Quran, such as marriage, business, and social conduct.

  *Example:* The rules concerning the inheritance of a grandmother are derived directly from the Sunnah, not the Quran.
