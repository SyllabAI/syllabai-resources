**Who Introduced Them First?**

* **Musnad First Compilers:** Individual *Sahifah* notes traced back to specific companions originated during the lifetime of the Prophet (PBUH) and the Tabi'een era (such as the compilations traced directly to Abu Hurairah or Hazrat Ayesha). On a formal scale, early compilers like **Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal** authored major dedicated Musnad collections. The collector of a Musnad collection is referred to as a "Musnid".

* **Musannaf First Compilers:** Work on Musannaf collections began around **176 AH**. The first well-known thematic compilation was **Al-Muwatta by Imam Malik bin Anas**, which contained 1,700 well-arranged Ahadith and was accepted as a standard thematic text. The full thematic compilation model was completed during the era of the Taba Tabi'een.
