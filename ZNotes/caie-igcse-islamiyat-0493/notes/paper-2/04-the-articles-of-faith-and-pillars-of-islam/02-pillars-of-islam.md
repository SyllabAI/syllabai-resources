## Pillars of Islam

### 1. Shahadah (Declaration of Faith)

### Words of the Shahadah

"I bear witness that there is no god except Allah, Who is One, Who has no partners, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and apostle."

### Significance of the First Part (Tawhid)

* **Essence & Foundation of Islam:** It is the primary expression of belief that differentiates a Muslim from a non-believer.

* **Rejection of Shirk:** Declares Allah’s unique existence and oneness, avoiding *Shirk* (associating partners with Him), which is the only unforgivable sin in Islam.

  *Qur'an:* "Allah forgives not that partners should be made with Him."

  *Qur'an:* "Say: He is Allah, the One and Only... And there is none like unto Him." (Surah Ikhlas, 112)

* **Monotheism & Attributes:** Acknowledges Allah as the sole Creator, Sustainer, Supreme Authority, and Judge of the universe who possesses unique names and attributes (e.g., *Ar-Rahman*, *Al-Rahim*).

  *Qur'an:* "To Him belongs all the best names."

  *Qur'an:* "He does not beget nor is He begotten."

* **Exclusive Worship:** Establishes that all worship and prayers are due solely to Allah.

  *Qur'an:* "I created Jinn and man to worship Me alone."

### Significance of the Second Part (Risalah)

* **Seal of Prophethood:** Affirms Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as the final Messenger, after whom no other prophet will come.

  *Hadith:* "I am the last messenger and there shall be no messengers after me."

* **Obligation to Follow the Sunnah:** Binds Muslims to obey the Holy Prophet’s teachings, actions, and silent approvals (Sunnah and Hadith) as a guide to achieve success in this life and the Hereafter.

  *Qur'an:* "Obey Allah and obey the Messenger."

  *Qur'an:* "You have an excellent model in the Messenger of Allah."

* **Inseparable Connection:** Linking faith in Allah directly to His Messenger ensures that believers follow the divine path as modeled by the Prophet (PBUH).

  *Qur'an:* "He who obeys the Prophet, obeys Allah."

### Key Occasions of Recitation

* Recited daily in every formal prayer (*Salat*) during *Tashahhud*.

* Whispered into a newborn baby's ear.

* Recited by a person converting to Islam.

* Recited on a believer's deathbed.

### 2. Salat (Prayer)

### Preconditions for Prayer (Conditions of Validity)

1. **Ritual Purity (Taharah):** Cleansing from minor impurities via *Wudu* or major impurities via *Ghusl*.

   *Qur'an:* "O you who have believed, when you rise for prayer, wash your faces and your forearms to the elbows..."

2. **Cleanliness:** Clothes, body, and the site of prayer must be free from physical impurities.

   *Qur'an:* "Purify your garments."

3. **Covering the Awrah:** Modest dress—covering from navel to knees for men, and the entire body except face and hands for women.

   *Qur'an:* "O children of Adam, take your adornment at every place of prayer."

4. **Facing the Qiblah:** Directing the prayer toward the Ka'bah in Makkah.

   *Qur'an:* "And turn your face to the direction of Ka'bah."

5. **Correct Timing:** Performing each of the five daily prayers at its designated time.

   *Qur'an:* "Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times."

6. **Intention (Niyyah):** Making a sincere intention in the heart before praying.

   *Hadith:* "Actions are judged by intentions."

### Methods of Purification

* **Wudu (Minor Impurity - Obligatory Steps):**

  1. Washing the face.

  2. Washing both hands up to the elbows.

  3. Wiping (Masa'h) over a quarter of the head with wet hands.

  4. Washing both feet up to the ankles.

     *Sunnah steps include:* Making intention, using Miswak, washing hands to wrists, gargling water 3 times, rinsing nose 3 times with left hand, and passing wet fingers over ears/neck.

* **Ghusl (Major Impurity - Janabah):**

  * *Obligatory (Farz) Steps:* Gargling water, rinsing water into the nostrils, and washing the entire body thoroughly without leaving any part dry.

  * *Sunnah Method:* Making *Niyyah*, washing hands, cleaning impurities, performing complete *Wudu*, and pouring water over the head and body three times.

* **Tayammum (Dry Ablution):** Permitted when water is unavailable, or a person is too ill to use water.

  * *Qur'an:* "But if you are ill or on a journey... and you find no water, then perform Tayammum..."

  * *Method:* Make intention (*Niyyah*), strike hands on clean dust/earth, wipe over the face, strike dust again, and wipe over both arms up to the elbows.

### Essential Elements and Wajib Acts of Salat

* **Takbir-e-Tahreema:** Opening declaration (*Allahu Akbar*).

* **Qiyam:** Standing upright during prayer.

* **Qira'at:** Recitation of Surah Al-Fatihah and an additional passage of the Qur'an.

* **Ruku:** Bowing at waist level.

* **Sajdah:** Prostrating twice per raka'at.

* **Qa'da:** Sitting for *Tashahhud*.

* **Salam:** Concluding the prayer by turning the face right and left.

* **Sajdah-e-Suhv:** Two corrective prostrations performed in the final *raka'at* (after *Attahiyat*) if an essential or *Wajib* act is unintentionally missed or misarranged.

### Congregational & Friday (Jum'a) Prayers

* **Congregational Prayer (Jama'at):**

  * Strongly emphasized for men in the mosque.

  * Led by an Imam who is most knowledgeable in the Qur'an.

  * *Hadith:* "Praying in congregation is 27 times more beneficial for a believer than praying individually."

* **Friday Prayer (Jum'a):**

  * Replaces *Zuhr* prayer on Fridays and is obligatory for sane, free adult men.

  * Women, children, slaves, and the sick are exempt.

  * *Preparations (Sunnah):* Taking a bath (*Ghusl*), clipping nails, wearing clean/best clothes, applying fragrance, and reciting Surah Al-Kahf.

  * *Features:* Involves two calls to prayer (*Adhan*), a two-part sermon (*Khutbah*) delivered by the Imam explaining Islamic principles, and 2 obligatory (*Farz*) raka'at prayed in congregation.

  * *Qur'an:* "O you who have believed, when \[the adhan\] is called for the prayer on the day of Jum'ah, then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade."

### Importance and Benefits of Salat

* **Foundation of Faith:** The primary deed checked on the Day of Judgment.

  *Hadith:* "What stands between a man and disbelief is the abandonment of Salat."

  *Hadith:* "The first thing which a person shall be called to account for on the Day of Judgment is Salat."

* **Deterrent against Sins:** Keeps believers mindful of Allah and steers them away from evil.

  *Qur'an:* "Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing."

* **Direct Connection & Peace:** Serves as a direct link between the servant and Allah, providing mental comfort and spiritual relief.

  Hadith: "O Bilal, stand and comfort us with prayer."

### Private Prayer (Du'a)

* Concept and Benefits

  * **Personal Connection:** Voluntary, intimate communication with Allah to seek forgiveness, express gratitude, and ask for personal needs in this life and the Hereafter.

  * **Prevents Shirk:** Reinforces reliance on Allah alone for aid.

  * **Alters Destiny:**

    *Hadith:* "Taqwa/Destiny cannot be changed except through Du'a."

  Optimal Times for Acceptance of Du'a

  * After completing every obligatory (*Farz*) prayer.

  * While in a state of *Wudu* or prostration (*Sajdah*).

  * Between the *Adhan* and *Iqamah*.

  * Between the two Friday *Khutbahs* (sermons).

  * During times of illness, adversity, or when visiting a sick person.

### 3. Sawm (Fasting)

### Definition and Obligation

* **Meaning:** Literally means "to abstain". Religiously, it means abstaining from food, drink, smoking, and marital relations from predawn (*Fajr*) until sunset (*Maghrib*) with the intention of pleasing Allah.

* **Obligation:** Prescribed in the 2nd year of Hijrah (*2 AH*) during the month of Ramadan.

* **Scriptural Evidence:**

  * *Qur'an:* "Fasting is prescribed to you as it was prescribed to those before you, that you may learn self-restraint (Taqwa)."

  * *Hadith Qudsi:* "Fasting is for Me, and I will surely compensate for it."

### Observance and Daily Routine

1. **Suhoor (Sehr):** Pre-dawn meal. It is a blessed *Sunnah* act.

   *Hadith:* "Take Sehr as there is a blessing in it."

2. **Niyyah:** Intention made before commencing the fast ("I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow/today in Ramadan").

3. **Restraint During Fasting:** Refraining from bad language, quarrels, lies, and worldly luxuries.

   *Hadith:* "If anybody speaks ill of you or tries to pick a quarrel with you, do not respond... simply tell him that you are fasting."

4. **Iftar:** Breaking the fast promptly at *Maghrib*, preferably with dates or water.

   *Supplication at Iftar:* "O Allah, I rested/fasted for You, believed in You, and break my fast with Your sustenance."

### Special Ramadan Observances

* **Tarawih:** Night prayers offered after *Isha*. Consists of 8 or 20 *raka'at* (prayed 2 by 2), in which the entire Qur'an is recited over the month.

* **Laylat al-Qadr (Night of Power):** Found in the odd nights of the last 10 days of Ramadan. Worship on this night is better than 1,000 months.

  *Qur'an:* "The Night of Power is better than a thousand months."

* **Itikaf:** Seclusion in the mosque during the last 10 days of Ramadan to focus purely on worship. (Men observe it in mosques; women at home).

* **Sadaqat-ul-Fitr:** Mandatory charity paid before Eid-ul-Fitr (equivalent to 1.63 kg of wheat per family member) to ensure the poor can join Eid celebrations.

### Exemptions, Qada, Fidya, and Kaffarah

* **Exempted Groups:** Non-Muslims, children, and the insane are permanently exempt.

* **Temporary Exemption (Qada Required):** Travelers, the temporarily sick, pregnant/nursing women, and soldiers in battle may miss fasts but must make them up (*Qada*) fast-for-fast after Ramadan.

* **Permanent Exemption (Fidya Required):** Elderly people or the chronically/permanently ill pay *Fidya* (feeding one needy person twice a day or giving 1.63 kg wheat per missed fast).

* **Intentional Violation (Kaffarah Required):** Intentionally breaking a fast without a valid reason incurs a severe penalty (*Kaffarah*): fasting for 60 consecutive days OR feeding 60 needy people two meals each.

### 4. Zakat (Almsgiving)

### Definition and Status

* **Meaning:** Literally means "to purify" or "to grow".

* **Obligation:** Made compulsory in 2 AH. Mentioned alongside Salat over 32 times in the Qur'an.

* **Scope:** Payable annually at a rate of 2.5% on accumulated wealth and savings held for a full lunar year (not on income or personal items of daily use).

* **Scriptural Evidence:**

  * *Qur'an:* "And establish prayer and pay Zakat."

  * *Qur'an:* "Take charity from their wealth to purify them and sanctify them."

### Rates of Nisab (Minimum Exemption Limit)

* **Gold:** 7.5 Tolas (87.48 grams).

* **Silver / Cash / Merchandise:** 52.5 Tolas (612.32 grams) of silver equivalent (Rate: 2.5%).

* **Agricultural Produce (Ushr):** 10% on naturally irrigated land; 5% on artificially irrigated land.

* **Mines/Extracts (Khums):** 20% (1/5th).

* **Livestock:**

  * *Goats/Sheep:* 1 sheep per 40–120 head; 2 sheep per 121–200 head.

  * *Cows/Buffaloes:* 1 one-year-old calf per 30–40 head; 1 two-year-old calf per 40–59 head.

  * *Camels:* 1 goat/sheep per 5 camels; 1 female camel per 25 camels.

### Eligible Recipients (Surah At-Tawbah 9:60)

1. **Faqeer:** The poor who possess less than Nisab.

2. **Miskeen:** The destitute/needy who cannot fulfill basic survival needs.

3. **Amilina Alayha:** Official collectors of Zakat.

4. **Mu'allafatu Qulubuhum:** Reconciling hearts / new converts to Islam.

5. **Riqab:** Emancipating slaves or freeing captives/prisoners.

6. **Gharimin:** People overburdened with legitimate debt.

7. **Fi Sabilillah:** In the cause of Allah (e.g., Islamic work, education, defense).

8. **Ibn-us-Sabil:** Stranded travelers in need of assistance.

### Ineligible Recipients

* Non-Muslims.

* Direct ascendants (parents, grandparents) and direct descendants (children, grandchildren).

* Spouse (husband to wife or vice-versa).

* The lineage/family of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) (*Banū Hashim / Syeds*).

* Public infrastructure, mosques, or school construction projects (since the recipient must receive direct ownership of the wealth).

### 5. Hajj (pilgrimage)

* **5th Pillar of Islam:** Hajj is an obligatory act of worship for every free Muslim who is physically healthy, sane, and financially capable (able to afford the journey while ensuring family maintenance in their absence) without fear of danger.

* **Timing:** Performed in the 12th Islamic month, Dhul-Hajj, between the $8\text{th}$ and $13\text{th}$ Dhul-Hajj.

* **Key Textual References:**

  * Quran: *"Hajj thereto is a duty men owe to Allah - those who can afford the journey."*

  * Hadith: *"Hajj which is free from sins & defects is rewarded in paradise."*

  * Hadith: *"Hajj is halting at Arafat."* (Tirmidhi)

**Key Rituals & Terminology**

**Ihram (Sacred State & Garment)**

* **Definition & Station:** The sacred state and dress assumed before crossing the designated boundary (*Miqat*).

* **Attire:** Men wear two unstitched white sheets; women wear their normal modest garments.

* **Prohibitions:** Hunting animals, cutting trees, wearing stitched clothing (for men), covering the face, shaving/cutting hair, trimming nails, and using foul language. Violations require a sacrificial penalty (*Dam*).

* **Talbiyah:** Recited upon entering Ihram: *"Labbaika Allahuma labbaik..."*

**Tawaf (Circumambulation)**

* Walking 7 rounds anti-clockwise around the Holy Kaabah, starting and ending at the Black Stone (*Hajr-al-Aswad*).

* **Rites:** Pilgrims kiss, touch, or point at Hajr-al-Aswad. For the first 3 rounds, men walk at a brisk pace (*Raml*), and the remaining 4 at a normal pace. Near the Yamani gate, pilgrims recite: *"Rabbana Aatina Siddunya..."*

* **5 Types of Tawaf:**

  * *Tawaf-e-Qudoom:* Arrival Tawaf.

  * *Tawaf-e-Ziarah:* Obligatory (Farz) Tawaf performed on $10\text{th}$ Dhul-Hajj.

  * *Tawaf-ul-Wida:* Farewell Tawaf before leaving Makkah.

  * *Tawaf-e-Umrah:* Obligatory Tawaf for Umrah.

  * *Tawaf-e-Nafl:* Optional Tawaf performed anytime.

**Sa'i (Striving between Safa & Marwa)**

* Walking/running 7 times between the hills of Safa and Marwa in memory of Hazrat Hajra's search for water for her son, Hazrat Ismael (which led to the miracle of Zamzam water).

* Men walk fast between the two green pillars. Supplications are made on reaching each mount.

* Quranic Reference: *"Behold! Safa and Marwa are among the Symbols of Allah..."* (2:158)

**Significance of Key Elements**

**Day of Arafah**

* It is the climax of Hajj. The Prophet (PBUH) said, *"Hajj is standing at Arafah."*

* Demonstrates universal equality—millions gather regardless of race, wealth, or status in identical Ihrams.

* Fasting on this day for non-pilgrims expiates the sins of the previous and coming year.

**Eid-ul-Adha & Qurbani (Sacrifice)**

* Commemorates the absolute submission of Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) and Hazrat Ismael (AS) to Allah's command.

* Meat from sacrificed animals (sheep, goat, cow, or camel) is divided into 3 portions: 1/3rd for family, 1/3rd for relatives/friends, and 1/3rd for the poor.

* Quranic Principle: *"Their meat will not reach Allah, nor their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you."* (22:37)
