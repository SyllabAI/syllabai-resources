## Jihad

**Definition & Concept**

* **Literal Meaning:** Derived from Arabic, it means **"to strive"** or **"to exert effort"**.

* **Islamic Terminology:** Every action involving a struggle, effort, or resistance carried out purely in the name of Allah.

* **Quranic Command:** *"And strive for Allah with the striving due to Him..."*

**The 5 Types of Jihad**

**1. Jihad of the Self (*Jihad bil-Nafs*)**

* **The Greater Jihad:** Considered the highest form of struggle in Islam.

* **Internal Struggle:** Fighting against one's own evil temptations, whims, and un-Islamic desires to maintain spiritual purity.

* **Prophetic Tradition:** Upon returning from a military campaign, the Prophet (PBUH) told his companions: *"We have returned from the lesser jihad to the greater jihad."* When asked what the greater jihad was, he replied: *"The struggle of the heart \[against one's own desires\]."*

**2. Jihad of the Tongue (*Jihad bil-Lisan*)**

* **Upholding Truth:** Publicly speaking out against corruption, falsehood, and societal injustice.

* **Prophetic Tradition:** A man once asked which Jihad is best; the Prophet (PBUH) replied: *"A word of truth spoken in front of a tyrannical ruler."*

**3. Jihad of Knowledge (*Jihad bil-Qalam*)**

* **Intellectual Striving:** Dedicating effort to deeply study, research, write about, and correctly preach the message of Islam.

* **Prophetic Tradition:** Strive against the polytheists with your wealth, your lives, and your tongues." (Sunan Abi Dawud 2504).

* **Scholarly Quote:**  *"The ink of a scholar is holier than the blood of a martyr."*

**4. Jihad of Action (*Jihad bil-Yad*)**

* **Physical & Financial Effort:** Using one's hands or wealth to perform righteous deeds, give charity, or assist the poor and oppressed.

* **Prophetic Tradition:** The Prophet (PBUH) stated: *"A person whose feet become dust-ridden because of striving in the way of Allah will never be touched by the flames of hell."*

**5. Jihad of the Sword (*Jihad bis-Saif*)**

* **Physical Warfare:** Armed fighting in the way of Allah (*Qital*).

* **Quranic Authority:** *"Fight in the way of Allah those who fight you..."*

* **Conditions of Enforcement:** Becomes strictly mandatory only when the lives, religious freedom, and core ideology of Muslims are under direct threat.

* **Islamic Rules of Engagement:** The Prophet (PBUH) strictly prohibited total warfare. Under Islamic law, soldiers are forbidden from:

  * Killing civilians, the elderly, women, children, and infants.

  * Harming domestic animals or destroying vegetation and fruit-bearing trees.

  * Demolishing civilian property or neutral places of worship.

  * Mistreating or abusing prisoners of war.

### **Significance in the Faith & Action of Individual Muslims**

* **Elevates Daily Intentions into Worship:** Jihad transforms an individual's routine efforts—whether studying (*Jihad bil-Qalam*), working to earn an honest living, or helping others (*Jihad bil-Yad*)—into continuous, rewarded acts of devotion when performed purely for Allah.

* **Fosters Self-Discipline and Moral Character:** Through *Jihad bil-Nafs*, a Muslim engages in constant inner reflection (*Muhasabah*), suppressing personal ego, greed, anger, and harmful desires to achieve spiritual purity (*Tazkiyah*).

* **Instills Moral Courage:** Practicing *Jihad bil-Lisan* trains individuals to stand firmly for truth and justice without fear of social pressure, rejection, or authority, reinforcing reliance on Allah alone (*Tawakkul*).

* **Guarantees Immense Spiritual Reward:** Actively striving for Allah gives individuals a profound sense of purpose in this life and promises the highest rewards, divine mercy, and forgiveness in the Afterlife.

### **Significance in the Faith & Action of Muslim Communities**

* **Safeguards Social Justice & Human Rights:** When a community practices Jihad through speech, action, and law, it actively opposes corruption, child labor, bribery, and tyranny, ensuring that the rights of the weak and marginalized are protected.

* **Promotes Collective Welfare & Development:** Through *Jihad bil-Yad* and *Jihad bil-Qalam*, communities prioritize building educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and relief systems, driving socioeconomic progress and unity (*Ummah*).

* **Defends Religious Freedom & Peaceful Coexistence:** *Jihad bis-Saif* provides a collective framework for self-defense against aggression, protecting not only Muslim life and ideology but also the broader freedom of conscience and places of worship.

* **Establishes Ethical Standards in Times of Crisis:** The strict Islamic rules of engagement force the community to uphold moral restraint even during armed conflict, preventing total destruction, civilian harm, and revenge-driven violence.
