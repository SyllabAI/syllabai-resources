# Articles of Faith and Pillars of Islam

## Articles of  Faith

**1. Belief in One God (Tawhid)**

**Core Principles**

* **Most Fundamental Article:** *Tawhid* is the foundational pillar of faith in Islam.

* **Universal Evidence:** The universe itself serves as absolute evidence of its Creator. Allah states in the Quran:

"Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of the night and day are signs for those of understanding."

* **Divine Roles:** Allah is acknowledged as the Supreme Lord, Creator, Sustainer, and ultimate Judge of all things.

* **Submission:** Muslims must believe in Him, recognize Him as the ultimate lawgiver, and submit to His will alone.

* **Exclusive Worship:** Allah alone is worthy of worship. The universal message of His absolute Lordship over all mankind has been preached by every prophet:

"Worship Allah, you have no god but Him." "He is Allah the one and only."

**Attributes and Names**

* **Divine Attributes:** Muslims believe in the specific attributes and names of Allah, such as *Al-Rehman* (The Most Merciful) and *Al-Ghafoor* (The All-Forgiving), which describe His divine nature.

* **Absolute Monotheism:** Muslims uniquely believe that Allah is entirely alone and unmatched in His Lordship, His Worship, and His Attributes.

**2. Belief in Angels**

**Nature of Angels**

* **Second Article of Faith:** Belief in angels is the second core requirement of Islamic faith.

* **Creation and Realm:** They are created from light (*Noor*) and form part of the unseen world.

* **Absolute Obedience:** They possess no free will to disobey Allah and submit fully to His commands.

"They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded."

* **Physical Characteristics:** Angels do not eat, drink, sleep, or bear offspring. They remain perpetually busy celebrating His praises and bowing down before Him.

* **Human Appearance:** Angels have the capacity to manifest in human form when required.

**3. Belief in the Divine Books**

**Mandatory Belief in All Scripture**

* **Article of Faith:** Belief in the original Divine Books sent by Allah is a mandatory article of faith.

* **Requirement for True Faith:** Muslims cannot be considered true believers unless they believe in the Holy Quran as well as all previously revealed scriptures.

**The Core Revealed Scriptures**

The Quran explicitly references several books sent down to earlier prophets:

* **The Book of Hazrat Musa (AS) (Torah):** *"And before this was the Book of Musa (AS)."*

* **The Book of Hazrat Dawud (AS) (Zabur):** *"And we bestowed Zabur on Dawud."*

* **The Book of Hazrat Isa (AS) (Injeel):** *"And we bestowed Injil on Isa."*

* **The Suhuf of Hazrat Ibrahim (AS):** Small scrolls referred to as *"The Book of Ibrahim and Musa."*

**Distinctive Status of the Holy Quran**

* **Final and Complete Message:** The Holy Quran stands as the complete, final, and ultimate message of Allah.

* **Immunity from Alteration:** It is perfectly preserved and fully immune to any human changes or corruption:

"Indeed We have sent down the Quran and Indeed We are its guardian."

* **Universal Validity:** It remains fully valid for all of mankind until the Day of Judgement, explaining the practical life of a Muslim in exhaustive detail.

"We have explained in detail in this Quran, for the benefit of mankind..."

**4. Belief in the Prophets**

**Characteristics of Prophethood**

* **Core Article:** Belief in all prophets sent by Allah—spanning from Hazrat Adam (AS) to the Holy Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)—is mandatory.

* **Mortal Nature:** All prophets were normal, mortal human beings chosen specifically by Allah to preach His divine message.

"These were the men to whom We gave the Book, authority, and Prophethood..."

* **Unity of Faith:** All prophets were Muslims who submitted to Allah and preached the exact same message of *Tawheed* (Monotheism) to their respective nations.

* **Moral Guidelines:** They instructed their communities to lead pious lives, practice modesty, care for one another, and remain diligent in worship, such as prayer and fasting (*Sawm*).

* **Finality of Prophethood:** Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is the final Prophet of Allah, and the line of prophethood is closed.

**Key Terminological Distinction**

* **Nabi:** A prophet who received divine revelation from Allah.

* **Rasul:** A prophet who was specifically granted a new Divine Book/Scripture.

**Miracles as Signs of Prophethood**

All prophets were granted explicit signs and miracles to prove their divine authority to their people:

* **Hazrat Ibrahim (AS):** Miraculously survived being thrown into a massive execution fire. Allah commanded: *"O fire! Be cool and safe for Ibrahim."*

* **Hazrat Musa (AS):** Granted the unique privilege where Allah spoke directly to him.

* **Hazrat Isa (AS):** His miraculous birth without a father was a major sign; he could speak articulately as an infant in the cradle and possessed the miracle to cure the blind.

**Prophets Explicitly Named in the Text:** Hazrat Adam, Hazrat Nuh, Hazrat Zakariya, Hazrat Yusuf, Hazrat Dawud, Hazrat Yaqub, Hazrat Younus, Hazrat Hud, Hazrat Lut, Hazrat Shoaib, Hazrat Saleh, and Hazrat Harun.

**5. Belief in the Day of Judgement**

**Concept and Meaning**

* **6th Article of Faith:** Belief in the Last Day is the 6th primary article of faith in Islam.

* **Core Elements:** It includes absolute belief in life after death, physical bodily resurrection, final accountability, and eternal life in either Paradise or Hellfire.

* **Life-Shaping Impact:** This belief shapes a Muslim's practical daily life according to the explicit wishes of Allah and His prescribed path.

"How can you reject faith in God... seeing that you were without life and He gave you life; then He will cause you to die, and will again bring you to life, and again to Him will you return."

**Timeline of the Last Day**

1. **The Phase of Death:** Death acts as the vital transition phase in the journey toward entering eternal life after the final verdict.

2. **The First Trumpet:** The Entire universe, along with all existing forms of life, will perish. This global cataclysm begins with the first blow of the trumpet by Angel Israfil.

3. **The Second Trumpet:** Angel Israfil will blow the trumpet a second time, triggering the immediate resurrection of all the dead from their graves.

4. **The Gathering:** All human beings will stand together before Allah for final accountability.

**The Process of Ultimate Justice**

* **Perfect Scales:** Allah will set up the scale of ultimate justice.

* **Bodily Witnesses:** Human speech is suspended, and individual body organs will bear witness to what the person committed.

"Then anyone who has done an atom's weight of good, shall see it. And anyone who has done an atom's weight of bad shall see it."

* **Final Destination:** Those who performed good deeds will enter Paradise (*Jannah*), while evildoers and unrepentant sinners will receive horrible punishment in Hell (*Jahannam*).

* **Psychological Impact:** This core belief instills a deep, true fear of Allah in the hearts of Muslims, driving true believers to avoid sins and actively increase their good deeds.

**6. Belief in Predestination (*Qadr*)**

**Core Principles and Meaning**

* **Sixth Article of Faith:** Belief in divine decree and predestination, known as *Qadr*, represents the sixth primary article of faith in Islam.

* **Divine Origin:** Everything, whether deemed good or bad, originates entirely from Allah. Nothing can occur in the universe except by His explicit knowledge and divine will.

* **Fixing a Limit:** Predestination essentially means fixing a defined limit for all things. The Quran highlights this perfect balance:

"We have created everything in measure and proportion."

* **All-Encompassing Knowledge:** Everything is systematically decided by Allah, spanning across the past, present, and future. The Quran states:

"He knows what is behind before and after Him."

* **Fixed vs. Free Elements:** A person's gender, date of birth, and exact time of death are entirely predestined, whereas human moral actions are not rigidly fixed.

* **Control and Trust:** This article of faith helps build a Muslim’s internal trust, providing reassurance that life remains completely under Allah’s control.

**The Four Aspects of Qadr**

To understand the relationship between divine decree and human responsibility, *Qadr* is divided into four central aspects:

1. **Knowledge (*'Ilm*):** Allah possesses complete, perfect knowledge of everything before it manifests.

2. **Writing (*Kitabah*):** Absolutely everything that will happen is already recorded in the Preserved Tablet (*Lauh-e-Mahfuz*).

3. **Will (*Mashiyyah*):** No event or action can ever occur unless Allah wills it to happen.

4. **Creation (*Khalq*):** Allah stands as the primary Creator of all actions and all beings.

**Human Free Will and Accountability**

* **Limited Free Will:** Human beings are granted a limited degree of free will, allowing them to choose consciously between good and evil.

* **Personal Responsibility:** Because human moral choices are made freely within the broader framework of Allah's ultimate will, personal accountability remains fully intact.

* **Justice:** Predestination does not eliminate individual responsibility for actions. Consequently, divine reward or punishment remains completely just.

**Moral Lesson: The Story of Hazrat Ali (RA) and the Saddle**

* **The Incident:** Hazrat Ali (RA) once asked a man to watch over his camel and its saddle. Instead, the man stole the saddle and ran away.

* **The Intended Reward:** Hazrat Ali (RA) had intended to honestly give the man 50 dirhams as a reward for his help.

* **The Discovery:** Later, Hazrat Ali (RA) discovered his stolen saddle being sold in the public market. The thief had sold it for exactly 50 dirhams.

* **Moral Lesson:** Allah had already destined a provision of 50 dirhams for the man; however, what truly mattered was the man's personal choice in how he earned it, showing that humans are responsible for choosing lawful (*halal*) or unlawful (*haram*) means.

**Practical Impact on a Muslim's Life**

Belief in *Qadr* actively shapes a Muslim's mindset and approach to life's challenges:

* **Trust (*Tawakkul*):** Instills deep trust in Allah through every life situation.

* **Patience (*Sabr*):** Fosters patience during periods of intense trial, reminding the believer that hardship is an intentional part of Allah’s plan.

* **Gratitude (*Shukr*):** Encourages active gratitude during periods of ease and worldly success.

* **Humility:** Teaches Muslims humility by reminding them that they do not possess absolute control over their own lives.

* **Effort vs. Outcome:** Reminds believers that while personal effort is strictly required, the ultimate results rest solely with Allah.

* **Emotional Balance:** Protects believers from falling into deep despair during failures, or becoming arrogant during successes.

* **Hope:** Strengthens a believer's continuous hope in Allah’s boundless mercy.
