## Caliphate of Hazrat Umar (RA)

### **Election of Hazrat Umar**

* **Nomination:** Unlike the election of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) through open consultation at Saqifah, Hazrat Abu Bakr nominated Hazrat Umar (RA) as his successor during his final illness after consulting prominent companions like Hazrat Usman, Hazrat Ali, and Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf (RA).

* **Public Pledge (*Bay'ah*):** Hazrat Abu Bakr dictated his will, which was read to the public. The companions and the general community unanimously accepted the nomination and pledged allegiance (*Bay'ah*) to Hazrat Umar upon Hazrat Abu Bakr's passing in $634$ AD.

### **Key Achievements & Reforms**

* **Democratic Governance (*Majlis-e-Shura*):** Established a consultative council (*Majlis-e-Shura*) comprising prominent Companions; Hazrat Umar remarked, *"There is no Caliphate without consultation."*

* **Division of Empire:** Divided the expanding empire into distinct provinces (e.g., Makkah, Madinah, Syria, Kufa, Basra, Egypt) governed by appointed officers (*Wali* and *Amil*).

* **Selection & Accountability of Officers:** Issued explicit instructions and held strict public accountability during the annual Hajj; Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated: *"All of you are guardians and responsible for your charges."*

* **Independent Judicial System (*Qada*):** Separated the judiciary from the executive branch by appointing independent judges (*Qadis*); Hazrat Umar advised Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, *"Justice is a firm obligation."*

* **Public Treasury (*Bait-ul-Maal*):** Organized the public treasury system to manage state revenue collected from *Zakat*, *Jizya*, *Kharaj*, *Ushr*, and *Fay'*.

* **Establishment of the *Diwan*:** Introduced a state pension register (*Diwan*) to distribute stipends to citizens, family members of the Prophet (PBUH), and soldiers; the Prophet (PBUH) noted: *"The leader of a people is their servant."*

* **Police Department (*Shurta*) & Vigilance:** Created a dedicated police department (*Shurta*) and night patrols to maintain law and order, protect property, and monitor markets.

* **Land & Tax Reforms:** Conducted land surveys, fixed fair agricultural taxes (*Kharaj*), and prohibited army commanders from seizing cultivated lands to protect the local economy.

* **Public Works & Infrastructure:** Built canals (e.g., *Nahr Umar*), expanded the Grand Mosque in Makkah, constructed roads, guest houses (*Ribat*), and new military cities like Kufa, Basra, and Fustat.

* **Social Welfare & Relief:** Established pensions for poor non-Muslim citizens (*Dhimmi*), orphans, and widows; Hazrat Umar stated, *"If a dog dies of hunger on the banks of the Euphrates, Umar will be responsible for it."*

* **Standardization of Calendar:** Introduced the official Islamic (Hijri) Calendar starting from the Year of Migration (*Hijrah*).

### **Assassination**

* In $644$ AD, Hazrat Umar was fatally attacked during morning prayers by Abu Lulu (Firuz), a Persian slave dissatisfied over a tax dispute.

* Before passing away three days later, he appointed a six-member council to elect the next Caliph and was buried alongside the Holy Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Abu Bakr.

### Modern Application & Contemporary Legacy of Hazrat Umar (RA)

* **Model for Governance Today:** Created institutional structures still used in modern governance, including independent judiciaries, public pensions, census-taking, police forces (*Shurta*), and provincial audit systems. His accountability model shows that leaders are directly answerable to the public.

* **Relations with Other States & Non-Muslims:** His treaty with Jerusalem (*Amān*) serves as a timeless blueprint for international relations, minority rights, and religious freedom, guaranteeing protection of life, property, and places of worship for non-Muslim citizens (*Dhimmi*).

* **Example for Modern Muslim Communities:** Highlights the necessity of institutional welfare (*Bait-ul-Maal*) and proactive public service, showing that modern Islamic societies must prioritize social security and poverty alleviation regardless of religious affiliation.
