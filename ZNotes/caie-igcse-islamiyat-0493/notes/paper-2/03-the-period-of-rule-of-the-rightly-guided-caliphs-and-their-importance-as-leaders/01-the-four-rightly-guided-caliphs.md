# The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs

## Caliphate of Hazrat Abu Bakr(RA)

### **The Election of the First Caliph**

**Background and Context**

* **No Successor Nominated:** The Holy Prophet (PBUH) had not explicitly nominated a successor or appointed any official leader for the Muslim community during his lifetime.

* **Sudden Leadership Vacuum:** Following his death, the *Muhajirin* (Emigrants) were heavily occupied with conducting the burial ceremonies for the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* **Gathering at Saqifah:** In the meantime, the *Ansar* (Helpers) gathered independently in a meeting hall known as *Saqifah Bani Sa'idah* to urgently discuss who should assume the office of leadership.

**The Arguments of the Ansar**

* **Speech of Sa'd bin Ubadah:** Sa'd bin Ubadah delivered a speech asserting that the leader must come from the *Ansar*, as they were the ones who provided vital help to Islam.

* **Selection of Sa'd bin Ubadah:** Many among the *Ansar* agreed that Sa'd bin Ubadah should take the position based on three primary reasons:

  1. He was the first individual to pledge his oath to the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

  2. The Holy Prophet (PBUH) had personally handed him the flag to lead in multiple battles.

  3. The Holy Prophet (PBUH) had specifically prayed to Allah to grant him blessings.

* **Proposal for Dual Leadership:** Habab bin Mandhar countered later arguments by reaffirming the *Ansar's* right to lead due to their sacrifices. He suggested a compromise of appointing two separate leaders for the community, a proposal which Hazrat Umar (RA) firmly refused.

**Intercession and Speeches by the Muhajirin**

* **Arrival of the Companions:** Upon discovering the situation, Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA), Hazrat Umar (RA), and Abu Ubaidah bin al-Jarrah reached the assembly hall.

* **The Quraish Precedent:** Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) explained that the Arabs in general, and the tribe of Quraish in particular, would not accept any leader who did not belong to the Quraish lineage.

* **Praise for the Ansar:** Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) spoke again, highly praising the *Ansar* by quoting the Holy Prophet (PBUH):

"If the world goes one way, and the Ansars the other, I would go with the Ansars."

* **Reminding Sa'd bin Ubadah:** Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) reminded Sa'd bin Ubadah of the specific day the Holy Prophet (PBUH) told his companions that the leader must be from the Quraish, while the *Ansar* should act as helpers.

* **Warning against Disunity:** Abu Ubaidah bin al-Jarrah added a direct appeal, stating:

"O Ansars! You were the helpers, why would you be the first cause of Islamic disintegration?"

* **Consensus Reached:** The *Ansar* finally agreed that since the Holy Prophet (PBUH) belonged to the Quraish, the overarching leader should also be from them.

**Pledging the Oath of Allegiance**

* **Nomination by Abu Bakr:** Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) stood up and suggested that the people select either Hazrat Umar (RA) or Hazrat Abu Ubaidah bin al-Jarrah as their Caliph.

* **Rejection of Nomination:** Both companions immediately refused his offer, declaring that Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) was the absolute best choice due to three main factors:

  * Allah explicitly mentioned him in the Quran as the companion in the cave (*Thani ath-Nayn*).

  * He had been chosen to lead the congregational prayers during the final illness of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

  * He had been officially appointed by the Prophet (PBUH) as the *Amir al-Hajj*.

* **The Initial Pledge:** They asked him to hold out his hand to receive their pledge. Although Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) resisted initially, Hazrat Umar (RA) and Abu Ubaidah bin al-Jarrah successfully took the pledge of loyalty on his hands.

* **The General Pledge:** On the following day, a formal, public pledge ceremony took place at *Masjid-e-Nabawi*.

**Establishment of the Caliphate:** Through this collective process, he officially became the first Caliph of Muslims in $632$ AD via the application of *Ijma-e-Ummah* (consensus of the community).

### **Background to the False Prophet**

**Prophetic Propshecies and Context**

* **The Vision of the Gold Bangles:** Abu Hurairah reported that the Prophet (PBUH) said that while sleeping, the treasures of the earth were presented to him and he was made to wear two gold bangles. Feeling their heavy burden, it was suggested he blow over them, causing them to disappear. He interpreted them as two great liars who would appear, namely Aswad and Musailimah.

* **Exposure of Musailimah:** Ibn Abbas reported the same narration, noting an event where the Prophet (PBUH) directly exposed Musailimah when he came to Medina demanding a leadership position.

* **The Thirty Liars:** The Prophet (PBUH) warned his community:

"Verily there will be thirty liars among my nation, all of them claiming to be a prophet. But I am the seal of Prophets. There is no Prophet after me." (Sunan Tirmidhi)

* **Root Cause of the Rise:** The false prophets primarily emerged from newly converted tribes who had not yet understood Islam in its true essence and depth

### **Detailed Profiles of the Main False Prophets**

**1. Aswad Ansi**

* **Origins:** Belonged to the Al-Ans tribe, but lived and rose to prominence in Yemen. His real name was Abhala bin Kab.

* **The Veil:** Known as *"Zhu al-Khimar"* (the veiled one) because he covered his face due to his dark complexion and ugliness, and to create an air of mystery to support his claims.

* **False Miracles:** Trained a donkey to perform tricks, such as bowing down on command. He claimed this was a prophetic miracle, earning him the title *"Zhu al-Himar"* (master of the donkey).

* **Religious Concessions:** Claimed he received divine revelations and attracted followers by granting them relaxations in core Islamic duties like fasting, prayers, and *Zakat*.

* **Military Campaigns:** Gathered an army to conquer Najran and Sana'a. He attacked Shahr (son of Badhan), the ruler of Yemen who had accepted Islam. Aswad killed him, married his widow Azad, and removed his ministers Feroz and Qais.

* **Fall and Death:** Because he broke his alliance with Medina, the Prophet (PBUH) sent Hazrat Muaz bin Jabal to fight him. Ultimately, Aswad was killed through an internal conspiracy hatched by Azad, Feroz, and Qais just a few days before the Prophet's (PBUH) death. His remaining followers gathered under Qais bin Abd Yaghus.

**2. Tulaiha Ibn Khuwailad**

* **Origins:** Belonged to the Banu Asad tribe. He declared himself a prophet among deserter tribes, including the Banu Ghafan and Banu Khuzaima.

* **False Miracles:** Claimed he could perform miracles, stating he once miraculously produced water when he and his tribesmen were completely lost in the desert.

* **Opposition to Islam:** Challenged Muslim practices of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving. When the Prophet (PBUH) sent a Muslim to eliminate him, Tulaiha escaped and claimed that no sword could harm him, calling himself the "living prophet".

* **The Battle of Zhu Qissa:** Following the Prophet's (PBUH) death, Tulaiha planned an attack on Medina. In $632$ AD ($11$ AH) Hazrat Abu Bakr raised an army containing prominent companions like Hazrat Ali, Hazrat Talha, and Hazrat Zubair. Tulaiha was defeated at the Battle of Zhu Qissa and forced to reside in Zhu Hissa.

* **The Battle of Buzakha** ($632$ AD)**:** Tulaiha rebelled again. Caliph Abu Bakr appointed Hazrat Khalid bin Waleed to crush him. During the battle, Tulaiha refused to fight, claiming he was waiting for a divine revelation.

* **Exposed as a Liar:** When his commander-in-chief, Uyaynah, saw the battle turning against them, he asked if any revelation had arrived. Tulaiha failed to answer, prompting Uyaynah to desert the field, declaring, *"Tulaiha is a liar, I am leaving."* This exposure led to a Muslim victory.

* **Repentance:** Tulaiha fled to Syria with his wife. During the Caliphate of Hazrat Umar (RA), he sincerely accepted Islam again and fought bravely for Muslims in the battles of Jalula, Qadisiya, and Nihawand.

**3. Musailimah Ibn Kazab (The Liar)**

* **Origins:** Hailed from the Banu Hanifa tribe residing in the Yamama region of Central Arabia.

* **Claim to Partnership:** After visiting the Prophet (PBUH) in Medina, he claimed prophethood on his return to Yamama. He wrote a letter to the Prophet (PBUH) demanding a division of Arabian lands, claiming they were partners in prophethood.

* **The Prophet's Response:** The Holy Prophet (PBUH) firmly replied that all land belongs to Allah, who grants it to whom He wishes, and labeled Musailimah a liar. The Prophet sent Nahrur Rijal to preach the truth to the people, but Nahrur Rijal betrayed Islam, joined Musailimah, and falsely claimed to the locals that the Prophet had accepted Musailimah's partnership. Consequently, many accepted Musailimah after the Prophet's (PBUH) death.

* **Alliance with Sajjah:** Musailimah married the false prophetess Sajjah, combining their forces. Together, they offered severe religious concessions to local populations.

**4. Sajjah bint al-Harith**

* **Origins:** She was a Christian Arab who belonged to the Banu Taghlib tribe, a powerful sub-tribe of the larger Banu Tamim federation located in northeastern Arabia and Iraq.

* **Claim to Prophethood:** Following the death of the Holy Prophet (PBUH), she took advantage of the widespread tribal rebellions and claimed to receive divine revelations, declaring herself a prophetess.

* **Rise to Power:** She possessed strong leadership skills and successfully united several clans of the Banu Tamim tribe under her banner. She quickly raised a formidable army of thousands of loyal fighters to march toward Medina.

* **The Strategic Shift:** On her march, she realized that the forces of Caliph Abu Bakr (RA) were highly organized. Recognizing the immense strength of Musailimah's army in Yamama, she decided to change her route to form a tactical alliance instead of fighting alone.

* **Alliance and Marriage:** She met Musailimah Ibn Kazab in Yamama. To solidify their political and military strength against the growing Muslim army, Musailimah proposed marriage, which she accepted.

* **Religious Concessions:** As a wedding gift, Musailimah granted her and her followers severe religious exemptions. Together, they officially dropped the morning and evening prayers, reduced the five daily prayers to three, and declared that alcohol consumption and adultery were fully permissible.

* **Fall and Dispersal:** After Musailimah's complete defeat and death at the Battle of Yamama, her military alliance collapsed entirely. Stripped of her army and power, she abandoned her false claims and fled back to her tribal lands in Iraq.

* **Sincere Repentance:** Years later, during the Caliphate of Hazrat Muawiyah, she moved to Basra, where she formally rejected her past, sincerely accepted Islam, and lived out the remainder of her life as a devout Muslim.

### **The Battle of Yamama (The Battle of the Garden of Death)**

* **The Armies:** Caliph Abu Bakr sent Hazrat Khalid bin Waleed with $15,000$ Muslims to confront Musailimah, who commanded an army of $40,000$ men along with thousands of Sajjah's forces.

* **The Turning Point:** After an initial setback on the first day, Khalid bin Waleed launched a tactical assault directly on Musailimah’s camp. Musailimah fled into a nearby enclosed garden to save himself.

* **Death of Musailimah:** He was spotted and killed by Wahshi ibn Harb using the exact same weapon he had used to kill the Prophet's uncle, Hazrat Hamza (RA), at the Battle of Uhud. Wahshi famously proclaimed:

  "Today I have repented truly for my sins."

* **The Cost of Victory:** The battle resulted in a decisive Muslim victory, but came at a high cost. A vast number of Muslims were martyred, including $70$ *Huffaz* (memorisers of the Quran). According to historical sources, more than $1,000$ companions of the Prophet (PBUH) lost their lives.

### **Achievements of Hazrat Abu Bakr(RA)**

**1. Administrative Achievements**

**The Ridda Wars and Political Consolidation**

* **Crushing Rebellions:** Faced immediate widespread political and religious turmoil, which he successfully resolved by crushing the rebellions of the Arab tribes across the Arabian Peninsula in the **Ridda Wars**.

* **Preserving Unity:** Continued the political consolidation originally initiated by the Holy Prophet (PBUH) with minimal interruption, establishing complete Islamic unity over Arabia by the end of the wars.

**Provincial and Democratic Governance**

* **Provincial Division:** Divided the Arabian Peninsula into **ten distinct provinces**.

* **The Capital:** Maintained **Madinah** as the centralized capital of the Islamic state.

* **Democratic Shura:** Laid the foundational pillars of a truly democratic state by handling all caliphate affairs through consultation with the **Majlis-e-Shura**.

* **Legal Standards:** Ensured all decisions were made strictly in accordance with the Quran and Sunnah.

**Duties of a Provincial Governor**

Each individual province was assigned a governor who was directly responsible for his designated territory. A governor's core duties included:

* Leading the congregational prayers.

* Managing the collection of *Zakat*.

* Maintaining general law and order.

* Working alongside an assigned revenue collector and a judge (*Qazi*).

**Financial and Military Administration**

* **Strict Public Finance:** Set an enduring precedent by maintaining incredibly strict accounting standards for all public finances, closely mirroring the practice of the Holy Prophet (PBUH).

* **Zakat Distribution:** Collected *Zakat* centrally in Madinah and ensured it was quickly distributed to the needy.

* **Military Budgeting:** Reserved a specific portion of state revenue exclusively to purchase the weapons and horses needed by the army.

* **Military Organization:** Divided the army systematically into distinct divisions under different commanders.

* **Reserve Forces:** Always maintained a designated reserve force ready in case of Jihad.

**Islamic Rules of Engagement**

Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) was highly particular about upholding Islamic ethical teachings and strictly instructed his military commanders to follow these rules of war:

* Do **not** kill women, children, old people, or worshippers.

* Do **not** practice cruelty.

* Do **not** destroy agricultural crops or fruit-bearing trees.

* Do **not** destroy non-Muslim places of worship.

* Fully protect all citizens who pay the protection tax (*Jizya*).

**2. Expansion of the Islamic Empire**

**Facing the Superpowers**

* **Strategic Challenges:** After successfully uniting Arabia under a single, highly centralized state with a potent military, Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) boldly faced challenges from the two global superpowers of the era: the **Persian Empire** and the **Roman Empire**.

* **Territorial Gains:** Through his personal determination and courage, he expanded the territories of the Islamic state as far out as **Syria, Palestine, and Iraq**.

**3. Preservation of the Quran**

**4. Critical Services for Islam**

**Consoling the Ummah at the Prophet's Death**

At the critical moment of the Holy Prophet’s (PBUH) passing, when the unity of the faith was at stake, Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) firmly counselled the panicked community. He famously stated:

"O people! Listen! As for those who worshipped Muhammad (PBUH), let me tell you that Muhammad (PBUH) is dead. But for those who worship Allah, He is alive and does not die." (Sahih Bukhari)

He stabilized the companions by reciting verse $144$ of Surah Aal-e-Imran:

"Muhammad (PBUH) is no more than a messenger, and indeed many messengers have passed away before him..."

**Safeguarding Islamic Principles**

* **No Compromise on Pillars:** Took an unyielding stand and refused to compromise with tribes that attempted to defect from paying the mandatory *Zakat*.

* **Eliminating False Prophets:** Curbed all rising false prophets to secure the finality of prophethood and fully safeguarded the fundamental principles of Islam.

**Personal Asceticism:** Despite his high status as Caliph, he lived a life of poverty and sought absolutely no personal financial gains. He left strict instructions that after his death, his personal property should be sold to refund the treasury for the salary he had drawn during his rule.

### Modern Application & Contemporary Legacy of Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA)

* **Model for Governance Today:** Demonstrates crisis management, constitutional continuity, and financial accountability. Modern states learn the importance of protecting state sovereignty and maintaining public order during political transitions or internal unrest.

* **Relations with Other States & Non-Muslims:** Established strict Islamic rules of engagement during military expeditions—forbidding the killing of non-combatants, destruction of property, or harming places of worship—setting an early precedent for international humanitarian law.

* **Example for Modern Muslim Communities:** Teaches modern leaders to uphold foundational principles without compromise during times of confusion or societal division, prioritizing communal unity over popularity.
