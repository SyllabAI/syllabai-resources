## Hadith 16

**Translation:**

*"The believers in their mutual kindness, compassion, and sympathy are like one body. When one of the limbs suffers, the whole body responds to it with sleeplessness and fever."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * The global Muslim community (*Ummah*) functions like an organic body—pain in one part affects the whole.

  * Faith requires solidarity, empathy, and collective responsibility.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Raise relief funds and awareness for global and local humanitarian crises.

  * Stand up against bullying and defend fellow believers facing injustice.
