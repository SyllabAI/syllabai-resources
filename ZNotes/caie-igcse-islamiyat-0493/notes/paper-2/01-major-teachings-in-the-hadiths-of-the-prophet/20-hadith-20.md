## Hadith 20

**Translation:**

*"Allah does not look at your forms or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Internal sincerity (*Ikhlas*), *Taqwa*, and clean intentions determine spiritual value over wealth or physical appearance.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Continuously check intentions before performing good deeds.

Avoid evaluating individuals based on outer looks, clothing, or material status.
