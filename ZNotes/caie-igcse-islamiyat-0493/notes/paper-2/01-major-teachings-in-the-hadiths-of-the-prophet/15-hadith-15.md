## Hadith 15

**Translation:**

*"Allah will not show mercy to him who does not show mercy to others."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Universal mercy to all creatures (Muslims, non-Muslims, and animals) is a core Islamic requirement.

  * God’s forgiveness toward a person depends on their capacity to show mercy to others.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Forgive personal offenses instead of harboring grudges or seeking revenge.

  * Care for animals by providing food, water, and humane treatment.
