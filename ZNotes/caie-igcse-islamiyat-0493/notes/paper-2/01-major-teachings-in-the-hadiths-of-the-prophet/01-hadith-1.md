# Major Teaching of Hadith of the Prophet

## Hadith 1

**Translation:**

"Religion is sincerity." We said: "To whom?" The Prophet said: "To Allah, His Book, His Messenger, and to the leaders of the Muslims and their common folk."

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Faith requires sincerity (*Nasihah*) in word, belief, and practice.

  * Demands complete sincerity toward Allah (Tawhid and obedience), the Quran (reading with understanding), the Prophet (following the Sunnah), community leaders, and the common public.

  * Actions are only meaningful when grounded in sincere faith.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Live according to Sharia and fulfill the Pillars of Islam (e.g., offering the 5 daily prayers).

  * Read and teach the Quran daily, and follow the Prophet's Sunnah.

  * Elect honest leaders, support them in good deeds, and hold them accountable if they contradict Islamic teachings.

  * Promote social harmony by being kind, polite, and helping those in need.
