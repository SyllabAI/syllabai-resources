## Hadith 11

**Translation:**

*"I and the person who looks after an orphan will be in Paradise like this," putting his index and middle fingers together and separating them slightly.*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Caring for orphans guarantees close proximity to the Prophet in Paradise.

  * The best home is one where an orphan is treated with love and dignity.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Provide nourishment, basic needs, and emotional mentorship to orphans.

  * Manage an orphan’s inheritance or business with absolute honesty until they come of age.

  * Support orphanages or financial relief funds to settle debts left by deceased parents.
