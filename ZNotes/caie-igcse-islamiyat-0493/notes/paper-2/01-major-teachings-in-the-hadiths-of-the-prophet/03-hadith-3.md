## Hadith 3

**Translation:**

"Let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day speak good or keep silent; and let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day be generous to his neighbor; and let him who believes in Allah and the Last Day be generous to his guest."

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Outward social conduct directly reflects inward belief in Allah and the Day of Judgment.

  * Emphasizes speech control (*Jihad bil Lisaan*), neighborly generosity, and hospitality to guests.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Avoid backbiting, slandering, cursing, or gossiping; speak constructively or remain silent.

  * Fulfill neighbors' needs (e.g., sharing food during special events).

  * Welcome guests warmly with food and lodging, following the Prophetic example regardless of their background.
