## Hadith 4

**Translation:**

*A man asked the Prophet: "Do you think that if I perform the obligatory prayers, fast in Ramadan, treat as lawful that which is halal, and treat as forbidden that which is haram, and do nothing which is more than that, I shall enter Paradise?" He replied: "Yes."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Fulfilling basic obligations (five daily prayers, Ramadan fasting, abiding by Halal/Haram) forms the foundation to enter Paradise.

  * Demonstrates the simplicity of Islam while encouraging additional voluntary worship to gain Allah’s pleasure.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Observe the obligatory daily prayers on time and fast during Ramadan.

  * Earn a Halal livelihood and strictly shun Haram earnings (e.g., interest/Riba, bribes).

  * Engage in consistent, small voluntary deeds (supplications, charity, or volunteering).
