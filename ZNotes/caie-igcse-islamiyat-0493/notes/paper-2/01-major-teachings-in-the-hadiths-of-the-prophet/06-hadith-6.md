## Hadith 6

**Translation:**

*"Whosoever of you sees an evil action, let him change it with his hand; and if he is not able to do so, then with his tongue; and if he is not able to do so, then with his heart—and that is the weakest of faith."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Islam rejects complacency and demands active resistance against social evils.

  * Defines three levels of intervention: hand (physical action/authority), tongue (verbal rejection/advocacy), and heart (internal disapproval, representing the weakest faith).

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Intervene or report illegal and corrupt practices (e.g., theft, cheating, bribery).

  * Use speech, writing, or social media to advocate against public hazards, injustice, and child labor.

  * Shun evil in one's heart if physical or verbal intervention is impossible.
