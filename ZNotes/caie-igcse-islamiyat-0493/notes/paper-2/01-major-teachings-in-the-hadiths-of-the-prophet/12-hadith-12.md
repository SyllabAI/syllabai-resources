## Hadith 12

**Translation:**

*"Make things easy and do not make them difficult; give glad tidings and do not repel people."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Leaders, teachers, and governors must exercise gentleness, justice, and compassion.

  * Religion should inspire joy and hope rather than alienation or harshness.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Leaders and teachers should maintain an approachable, supportive environment.

  * Conduct Dawah by highlighting mercy and ease rather than rigid severity.
