## Hadith 13

**Translation:**

*"He who has memorized the Quran is like the owner of a tethered camel. If he pays attention to it, he will keep it, but if he releases it, it will go away."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Quranic knowledge and memorization require continuous revision and practice to prevent them from slipping away.

  * Studying the Quran is a lifelong obligation.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Establish a fixed daily routine for reciting and reflecting upon the Quran.

  * Revise memorized portions (*Manzil*) regularly and apply Quranic principles to daily life.
