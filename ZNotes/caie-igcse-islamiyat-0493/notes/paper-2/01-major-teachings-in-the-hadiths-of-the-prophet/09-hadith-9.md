## Hadith 9

**Translation:**

*"No one eats better food than that which he eats from the work of his own hands."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Highlights the dignity of labor, self-sufficiency, and earning a Halal livelihood.

  * Discourages begging, undue dependency, and unlawful (*Haram*) wealth.

  * Earning integrity matters more than the net volume of income.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Emulate Prophets who worked as shepherds or manual laborers.

  * Practice manual self-reliance and reject corrupt financial practices (usury/Riba, bribery).

  * Work diligently in societal roles (education, healthcare, trade) with honesty.
