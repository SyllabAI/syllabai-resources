## Hadith 10

**Translation:**

*"One who manages the affairs of the widow and the poor man is like the one who exerts himself in the way of Allah, or the one who stands for prayer all night and fasts all day."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Assisting vulnerable community members (widows, the poor) is equated in reward to physical Jihad, night prayers (*Qiyam*), and continuous fasting.

  * Connects *Huqooq Allah* (rights of Allah) with *Huqooq-ul-Ibaad* (rights of people).

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Donate funds, Zakat, or time to food banks, orphanages, and medical facilities.

  * Assist widows and the underprivileged directly with chores or financial support.
