## Hadith 7

**Translation:**

*It was said: "O Messenger of Allah, who is the best of men?" He said: "A believer who strives in the cause of Allah with his life and his wealth."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Highest status belongs to those who sacrifice their life, physical self, and property for Allah's cause.

  * Broadens *Jihad* (striving) to include personal spiritual growth, financial contribution, and community service.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Perform voluntary worship (e.g., Tahajjud) and dedicate personal skills to serve the Ummah.

  * Donate wealth to build mosques, schools, and hospitals, or sponsor humanitarian relief for refugees.
