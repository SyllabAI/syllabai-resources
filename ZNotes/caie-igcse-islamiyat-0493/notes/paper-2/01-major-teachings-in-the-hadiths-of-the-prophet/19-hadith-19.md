## Hadith 19

**Translation:**

*"The world is a prison for the believer and a paradise for the disbeliever."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Worldly life is a temporary testing ground bounded by divine rules for believers.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Endure trials patiently and prioritize deeds that benefit the Afterlife.

  * Restrain desires from forbidden temptations (e.g., alcohol, illicit income).
