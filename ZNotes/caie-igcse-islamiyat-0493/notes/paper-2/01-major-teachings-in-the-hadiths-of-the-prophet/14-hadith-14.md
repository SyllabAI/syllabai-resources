## Hadith 14

**Translation:**

*"May Allah show mercy to a man who is lenient when he sells, lenient when he buys, and lenient when he demands his due."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Promotes ethical commerce, honesty, and leniency in trade and debt recovery.

  * Divine mercy is granted to those who show leniency in worldly affairs.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Charge fair prices, avoid overcharging, and deliver high-quality goods.

  * Grant extra time or ease payment terms for debtors struggling financially.
