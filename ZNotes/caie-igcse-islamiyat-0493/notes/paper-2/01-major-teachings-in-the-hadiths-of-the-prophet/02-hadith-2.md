## Hadith 2

**Translation:**

"None of you believes until he wants for his brother what he wants for himself."

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * True faith is intrinsically tied to caring for others; a heart lacking love for fellow Muslims possesses weak faith.

  * Establishes spiritual brotherhood and requires reciprocity (treating others as one wishes to be treated).

  * Equality and caring for mankind eradicate social evils.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Provide humanitarian aid, set up refugee centers, and support global relief efforts (e.g., Zakat to conflict areas).

  * Open orphanages or vocational centers for widows.

  * Eliminate personal jealousy and avoid offensive nicknames or taunting.
