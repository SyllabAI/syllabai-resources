## Hadith 17

**Translation:**

*"Modesty (Haya) produces nothing but good."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Modesty (*Haya*) safeguards character, prevents sinful actions, and fosters humility.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Practice modesty in speech, dress, and deportment.

  * Lower the gaze and avoid arrogant or vulgar behavior.
