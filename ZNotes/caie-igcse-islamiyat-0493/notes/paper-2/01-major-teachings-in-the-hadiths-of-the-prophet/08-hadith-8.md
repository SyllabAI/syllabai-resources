## Hadith 8

**Translation:**

*"The martyrs are five: the one who dies of plague, the one who dies of an intestinal ailment, the one who drowns, the one who dies under a collapsed building, and the martyr in the cause of Allah."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Expands martyrdom beyond battlefields to include righteous believers who die of natural causes, sudden trials, or severe illnesses (e.g., plague, cholera).

  * Emphasizes that Allah rewards sincere intention and righteous living.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Maintain righteous consistency in daily worship, prayers, and fasting.

  * Practice patience during severe illness or afflictions, trusting in Allah's decree.

  * Speak out against oppression and stand up for truth as a daily form of striving.
