## Hadith 18

**Translation:**

*"He who has in his heart the weight of a mustard seed of pride shall not enter Paradise, and he who has in his heart the weight of a mustard seed of faith shall not enter Hell."*

* **Teachings (Part A):**

  * Even a tiny grain of sincere faith (Tawhid) protects against eternal Hell, whereas a tiny grain of pride (Kibr) prevents entry to Paradise.

* **Action & Implementation (Part B):**

  * Practice self-reflection to clear the heart of vanity or feelings of superiority.

  * Treat all individuals with respect regardless of wealth, knowledge, or status.
