## Nature of Liability in Negligence

### **Personal Liability**

* **Definition**: Personal liability in negligence arises when an individual is held directly responsible for causing harm or damage to another party by their actions or omissions.
* **Elements**: For personal liability to be established, three key elements must be present:

  
  1. Duty of Care: The individual must owe a duty of care to the affected party. The relationship between the parties and the foreseeability of harm often determines this duty.
  2. Breach of Duty: The duty of care must be breached, meaning that the individual failed to meet the standard of care expected in the given circumstances.
  3. Causation: The breach of duty must be the direct cause of the harm suffered by the affected party.

### **Vicarious Liability (Outline Only)**

* **Definition**: Vicarious liability is a legal doctrine that holds one party responsible for the negligent actions of another, typically an employer, for the actions of an employee.
* **Conditions**: To establish vicarious liability, two main conditions must be met:

  
  1. Occurrence in the Course of Employment: The negligent act must occur within the scope of the employee's job responsibilities.
  2. Employer-Employee Relationship: There must be a recognized employer-employee relationship between the parties.

### **Joint Liability**

* **Definition**: Joint liability occurs when two or more parties share liability for the same damage or harm caused by negligence.
* **Conditions**: In cases of joint liability, each party is individually responsible for the entire harm caused, and the injured party has the option to sue one or more of the liable parties.
* **Example**: In a car accident involving two negligent drivers, both drivers may be held jointly liable for the injuries sustained by a third party.

### **Summary**

* Personal Liability:
  * Arises from an individual's direct actions or omissions.
  * Elements: Duty of care, breach of duty, causation.
* Vicarious Liability (In Outline Only):
  * Holds one party responsible for the negligence of another.
  * Conditions: Occurrence in the course of employment, employer-employee relationship.
* Joint Liability:
  * Multiple parties share liability for the same damage.
  * Each party is individually responsible, and the injured party can choose whom to sue.
