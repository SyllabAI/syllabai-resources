## Breach of Duty

### **The Standard of Care and the Objective Test**

* **Definition**: Breach of duty is a crucial element in a negligence claim, signifying the failure of the defendant to meet the required standard of care owed to the claimant.
* **Objective Test**: The determination of breach of duty is generally approached through an objective test, meaning that the court assesses the defendant's conduct against what a reasonable person in the defendant's position would have done in similar circumstances.
* **Dynamic Standard:** The standard of care is not a fixed benchmark but adapts based on the particular circumstances of each case. What is reasonable can vary, and the law acknowledges this flexibility.

### **The Standard of Care and Different Classes of Defendant**


1. **Children:**
   * **Special Standard**: Recognizing the developmental differences among individuals, children are held to a standard commensurate with their age, experience, and understanding.
   * **Exceptional Cases:** When a child engages in activities typically associated with adults, the court may apply a higher standard to reflect the elevated risk.
2. **Experts:**
   * **Higher Standard**: Professionals and experts, owing to their specialized knowledge and training, are held to a higher standard of care.
   * **Comparison to Peers**: The actions of a professional are often measured against what a competent professional in the same field would have done under similar circumstances.
   * **Specialised Knowledge**: Courts recognize that professionals possess expertise beyond an average person's, and their standard of care reflects this higher level of competence.
3. **Professionals:**
   * **Specialised Standard**: Similar to experts, professionals such as doctors or lawyers are held to a standard reflecting the skill and knowledge expected of a reasonably competent professional.
   * **Informed Consent**: In certain professions, like medicine, professionals also must provide information to their clients or patients to ensure informed consent. This emphasizes the importance of transparency and autonomy in professional relationships.

### **Summary**

* The Standard of Care and the Objective Test:
  * Breach of duty signifies a failure to meet the required standard of care.
  * An objective test assesses the defendant's conduct against the actions of a reasonable person in similar circumstances.
  * The standard of care is adaptable and varies based on case-specific factors.
* The Standard of Care and Different Classes of Defendant:
  * Children:
    * Held to a standard aligned with their developmental stage.
    * Higher standards may apply in adult-like activities.
  * Experts:
    * Subject to a higher standard due to their specialized knowledge.
    * Benchmark is often the conduct of competent professionals in the same field.
  * Professionals:
    * Held to a standard reflecting their professional competence.
    * Informed consent is a duty in professions like medicine, emphasizing transparency and autonomy.
