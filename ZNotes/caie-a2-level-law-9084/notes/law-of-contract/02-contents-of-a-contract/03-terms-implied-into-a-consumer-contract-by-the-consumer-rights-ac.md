## Terms Implied into a Consumer Contract by the Consumer Rights Act 2015

### **To Supply Goods**


1. **Satisfactory Quality (s9):**
   * Requirement: Goods must meet the standard that a reasonable person would consider satisfactory.
   * Consumer Rights for Breach (s20):
     * Right to Reject (s20): If the product is unsatisfactory, the consumer can reject it within 30 days.
     * Time Limit for Short-term Right to Reject (s22): Short-term right applies within 30 days.
     * Right to Repair or Replacement (s23): If within 30 days, the right is to repair or replace.
     * Right to Price Reduction or Final Right to Reject (s24): If not met after one repair or replacement, the right to a price reduction or final rejection.
2. **Fit for a Particular Purpose (s10):**
   * Requirement: Goods must fit the specific purpose made known to the trader.
   * Consumer Rights for Breach (s20): Similar to the rights for satisfactory quality.
3. **As Described (s11):**
   * Requirement: Goods must match any description given, whether written or oral.
   * Consumer Rights for Breach (s20): Similar to the rights for satisfactory quality.

### **To Supply a Service**


1. **Performed with Reasonable Care and Skill (s49):**
   * Requirement: Service must be completed with reasonable care and skill.
   * Consumer Rights for Breach (s55):
     * Right to Repeat Performance (s55): The consumer has the right to request the service to be performed again.
     * Right to a Price Reduction (s56): If repeat performance is impossible or not done within a reasonable time.
2. **Performed Within a Reasonable Time (s52):**
   * Requirement: Service must be performed within a reasonable time.
   * Consumer Rights for Breach (s56): Right to a price reduction if not performed within a reasonable time.

### **Summary**

* **To Supply Goods:**
  * Satisfactory Quality (s9):
    * Requirement for goods to meet a reasonable standard.
    * Consumer rights include rejecting, repairing, replacing, or reducing prices.
  * Fit for a Particular Purpose (s10):
    * Requirement for goods to be fit for a specified purpose.
    * Consumer rights are similar to those for satisfactory quality.
  * As Described (s11):
    * Requirement for goods to match any given description.
    * Consumer rights are similar to those for satisfactory quality.
* **To Supply a Service:**
  * Performed with Reasonable Care and Skill (s49):
    * Requirement for services to be performed with care and skill.
    * Consumer rights include the right to repeat performance or a price reduction.
  * Performed Within a Reasonable Time (s52):
    * Requirement for services to be performed within a reasonable time.
    * Consumers have the right to a price reduction if not performed within a reasonable time.
