## Express Terms

### **Distinction Between Representations, Terms and Importance**

* **Representations vs. Terms:**
  * Representations: Statements that induce a party to enter a contract but are not intended to be part of the contract.
  * Terms: Specific promises or undertakings intended to be part of the contract.
* **Importance:**
  * Legal Significance: Terms are legally binding, and a breach can lead to remedies.
  * Remedies: Misrepresentation may lead to remedies, but terms provide clearer grounds for legal action.

### **Written Terms**


1. **Incorporation by Signature:**
   * Definition: Incorporating terms by having the parties sign a document containing the terms.
   * Effect: Signature indicates acceptance and awareness of the terms.
2. **Parol Evidence Rule:**
   * Definition: Rule limiting the admissibility of oral or extrinsic evidence to vary, add, or contradict the terms of a written contract.
   * Purpose: Preserves the integrity and certainty of written contracts.
   * Exceptions: Ambiguity, fraud, mistake, or incomplete terms may allow the admission of parol evidence.

### **Summary**

* Distinction Between Representations and Terms:
  * Representations:
    * It is not intended to be part of the contract.
  * Terms:
    * It is intended to be part of the contract.
* Importance:
  * Terms:
    * Legally binding; breach leads to remedies.
  * Representations:
    * It may lead to remedies, but terms provide clearer legal grounds.
* Incorporation by Signature:
  * Method: Signatures indicate acceptance and awareness of the terms.
* Parol Evidence Rule:
  * Definition: Limits admissibility of oral evidence to vary written terms.
  * Purpose: Preserves the integrity and certainty of written contracts.
