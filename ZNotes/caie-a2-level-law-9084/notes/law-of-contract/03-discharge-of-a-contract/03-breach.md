## Breach

### **Actual Breach and Anticipatory Breach**

**Actual Breach:**

* **Definition**: Occurs when one party fails to perform its contractual obligations at the agreed-upon time or delivers defective performance.
* **Effects**: The innocent party is entitled to remedies, including damages and, in some cases, contract termination.

**Anticipatory Breach:**

* **Definition**: Occurs when one party, by words or actions, indicates an intention not to perform its contractual obligations in the future.
* **Effects:** The innocent party can treat the anticipatory breach as an immediate breach, enabling them to seek remedies without waiting for the actual performance date.

### **Summary**

* Actual Breach:
  * It occurs when a party fails to perform as agreed.
  * Entitles the innocent party to remedies, including damages and possible termination.
* Anticipatory Breach:
  * Occurs when a party indicates it will not perform in the future.
  * Allows the innocent party to treat it as an immediate breach and seek remedies without waiting.
