## Performance

### **The Entire or Strict Performance Rule**

* **Definition**: The general principle that parties must fully and precisely perform their contractual obligations as agreed.
* **Expectations**: Each party expects the other to fulfil the contract's terms precisely.

### **Exceptions to the Entire or Strict Performance Rule**


1. **Substantial Performance:**
   * Definition: Performance that, while not precisely meeting the contract's terms, is substantial enough to achieve the contract's purpose.
   * Effect: The innocent party may be required to pay, but they can claim damages for any deficiencies.
2. **Voluntary Acceptance of Partial Performance:**
   * Definition: When the innocent party willingly accepts partial performance.
   * Effect: The performing party is entitled to payment for the partial performance, and the innocent party can claim damages for defects.
3. **Divisible Contracts:**
   * Definition: Contracts with separable parts, each capable of being considered a separate agreement.
   * Effect: Each part is treated as a separate contract, and the performance of one part does not necessarily affect the performance of the others.
4. **Prevention of Performance:**
   * Definition: When one party prevents the other from performing their obligations.
   * Effect: The prevented party may be excused from strict performance.
5. **Tender of Performance:**
   * Definition: The offer to perform made by one party to the other.
   * Effect: If the other party rejects a valid tender, the tendering party is not in breach.
6. **Time of Performance:**
   * Definition: Contracts may specify a time for performance.
   * Effect: Failure to perform on time may lead to a breach, but time may be of the essence or flexible depending on the contract terms.
7. **Vicarious Performance:**
   * Definition: Performance by a third party on behalf of one of the contracting parties.
   * Effect: If allowed by the contract, vicarious performance is a valid way of fulfilling contractual obligations.

### **Summary**

* The Entire or Strict Performance Rule:
  * Parties are generally expected to fulfil their contractual obligations precisely.
* Exceptions:
  * Substantial Performance:
    * Performance does not precisely meet terms but is substantial.
  * Voluntary Acceptance of Partial Performance:
    * Willing acceptance of partial performance.
  * Divisible Contracts:
    * Contracts with separable, independent parts.
  * Prevention of Performance:
    * One party prevents the other from performing.
  * Tender of Performance:
    * Offer to perform; rejection does not constitute a breach.
  * Time of Performance:
    * Time may be of the essence or flexible.
  * Vicarious Performance:
    * The third party performs on behalf of one of the parties.
