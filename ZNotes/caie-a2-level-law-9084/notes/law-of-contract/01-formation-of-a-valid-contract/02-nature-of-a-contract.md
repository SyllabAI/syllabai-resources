## Nature of a Contract

### **Agreement**

* **Definition**: An essential element of a contract where two or more parties reach a mutual understanding.
* **Characteristics:**
  * Offer and Acceptance: One party makes an offer, and the other accepts it, creating a meeting of minds.
  * Intention to Create Legal Relations: Parties must intend their agreement to be legally binding.
  * Certainty and Possibility of Performance: Terms must be clear, and performance must be possible.

### **Unilateral Contract**

* **Definition**: A contract where one party makes a promise in exchange for the other's performance.
* **Characteristics:**
  * Offer: One party makes a promise.
  * Acceptance: Performance of the specified act by the other party constitutes acceptance.
  * Revocation: The Offeror can usually revoke the offer before acceptance.

### **Bilateral Contract**

* **Definition**: A contract where both parties exchange promises, forming a mutual agreement.
* **Characteristics:**
  * Offer: One party makes a promise.
  * Acceptance: The other party responds with a promise to perform.
  * Mutuality: Both parties are bound by their promises once accepted.

### **Collateral Contract**

* **Definition**: A secondary contract related to the main contract, often involving a third party.
* **Characteristics:**
  * Existence: A separate contract formed alongside the main contract.
  * Independent Obligations: The performance of the collateral contract is not contingent on the main contract.
  * Liability: Breach of the collateral contract may result in legal consequences.

### **Summary**

* Agreement:
  * Involves mutual understanding.
  * Requires offer and acceptance, intention to create legal relations and certainty of terms.
* Unilateral Contract:
  * One party makes a promise.
  * Acceptance through performance.
  * The offeror can revoke before acceptance.
* Bilateral Contract:
  * Both parties exchange promises.
  * Offer, acceptance, and mutuality.
* Collateral Contract:
  * Secondary contract related to the main contract.
  * Independent obligations.
  * The breach may result in legal consequences.
