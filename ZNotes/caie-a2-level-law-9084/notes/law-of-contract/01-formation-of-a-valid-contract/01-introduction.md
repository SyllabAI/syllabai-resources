# Formation of a Valid Contract

* This topic is assessed in **Paper 3, Law of Contract.**
* This topic links with the **English Legal System**, which is assumed knowledge.
* NOTE: Students studying A2 Level Law must study from Topic 1.1-4.4.

## Introduction

This topic considers the nature of a contract and the rules that a court of law applies to determine whether a contract is valid. This topic is an opportunity to reflect on the key concepts of liability: rights, duties and responsibilities, freedoms, and effectiveness and certainty.
