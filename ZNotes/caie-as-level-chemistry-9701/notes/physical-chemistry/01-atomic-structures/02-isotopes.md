## Isotopes

Isotopes : Atoms of the **same element** with the **same number of protons**, but a **different number of neutrons**

Isotopes of the same element have **identical chemical properties** due to having the **same electronic configuration**

Isotopes of the same element have **different physical properties such as mass and density:**

* Isotopes have different number of neutrons thus different mass

* $\text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$

* Isotopes have identical electronic configuration, thus same volume

  * Since mass is different Density is also varied
