# Atoms, Molecules, and Stoichiometry

## Relative masses of atoms and molecules

* Unified atomic mass unit: One twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom

* Relative Isotopic Mass: The mass of a particular isotope of an element relative to one unified atomic mass unit

* **Relative Atomic Mass ($A_{\text{r}}$):** The weighted mean mass of an atom of an element, compared to $\frac{1}{12}\text{th}$ of the mass of an atom of carbon-12

* **Relative Molecular Mass ($M_{\text{r}}$):** The weighted mean mass of a molecule of a substance, compared to $\frac{1}{12}\text{th}$ of the mass of an atom of carbon-12

* **Relative Formula Mass:** The weighted mean mass of a formula unit of an ionic compound, compared to $\frac{1}{12}\text{th}$ of the mass of an atom of carbon-12
