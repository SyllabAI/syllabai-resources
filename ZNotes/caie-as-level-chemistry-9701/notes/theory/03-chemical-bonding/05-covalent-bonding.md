## Covalent Bonding

* **Covalent Bond**: the bond formed by the sharing of pairs of electrons between the nuclei of two atoms.
* **Bonding Electrons**: e<sup>-</sup>s involved in bond formation
* **Non-bonding electrons** or **lone pair**: pair of valence
  e<sup>-</sup>s that are not involved in bond formation
* Covalent compounds are made of molecules which are held together by
  weak intermolecular forces
* Elements in period 3 can expand their octet, including the compounds sulfur dioxide, SO<sub>2</sub>, phosphorus pentachloride, PCl<sub>5</sub>, and sulfur hexafluoride, SF<sub>6</sub>.
* They have low melting and boiling points.
