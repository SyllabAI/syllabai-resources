## Shapes of Molecules

* The shape and bond angles of molecules depend on:
  * The number of pairs of electrons around the central atom
  * Whether these pairs are lone pairs or bonded pairs
* Valence shell electrons are arranged in pairs to minimize repulsion
  between themselves
* **Order of Repulsion Strength (VSEPR Theory):**

![](../../../assets/bdc9c55fb314-image019.png)

* **2 pairs of e’s**
  * 2 bonded, 0 lone pair
    * Linear
    * 180<sup>O</sup>
    * E.g. CO<sub>2</sub>

![](../../../assets/31f884cabab6-image020.png)

* **3 pairs of e’s**
  * 3 bonded, 0 lone pair
    * Trigonal planar
    * 120<sup>O</sup>
    * E.g. BF<sub>3</sub>

![](../../../assets/e826b468f92a-image021.png)

* **4 pairs of e’s**
  * 4 bonded, 0 lone pair
    * Tetrahedral
    * 109.5<sup>O</sup>
    * E.g. CH<sub>4</sub>

![](../../../assets/04e43dd6149e-image022.png)

* 3 bonded, 1 lone pair
  * Pyramidal
  * 107<sup>O</sup>
  * E.g. NH<sub>3</sub>

![](../../../assets/4d0e9165893b-image023.png)

* 2 bonded, 2 lone pair
  * Angular
  * 104.5<sup>O</sup>
  * E.g. H<sub>2</sub>O

![](../../../assets/69efa838001f-image024.png)

* **5 pairs of e’s**
  * 5 bonded, 0 lone pair
    * Trigonal Bipyramid
    * 90<sup>O</sup> and 120<sup>O</sup>
    * E.g. PF<sub>5</sub>

![](../../../assets/a41e775b19ac-image025.png)

* **6 pairs of e’s**
  * 6 bonded, 0 lone pair
    * Octahedral
    * 90<sup>O</sup>
    * E.g. SF<sub>6</sub>
