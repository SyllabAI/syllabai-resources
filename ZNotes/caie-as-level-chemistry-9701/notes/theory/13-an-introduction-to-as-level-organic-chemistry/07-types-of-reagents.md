## Types of Reagents

**Nucleophilic reagent (nucleophile):** donator of pair of e<sup>-</sup>

  - Must have lone pair of e<sup>-</sup>s

  - Attack centre of +ve charge (positive pole)

  - Reaction with nucleophile called nucleophilic reactions

  - Examples: CH<sup>-</sup>, Cl<sup>-</sup>, NH<sub>3</sub>,
    H<sub>2</sub>O, CN<sup>-</sup>

&nbsp;

**Electrophilic reagent (electrophile):** acceptor of pair of
e<sup>-</sup>

  - \+ve ions or e<sup>-</sup> deficient molecules

  - Attack regions of high e<sup>-</sup> density

  - Examples: Br<sup>+</sup>, CH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup>,
    AlCl<sub>3</sub>
