## Breaking of Covalent Bonds

**Homolytic Fission:**

* Two atoms sharing e<sup>-</sup> pair of similar electronegativity
* When bond breaks, each atom takes one e<sup>-</sup> from pair of
  electrons forming free radicals
* **Free radicals:** electrically neutral atoms or groups of atoms with unpaired electrons are very reactive
* Free radical reaction catalysed by heat or light

**Heterolytic Fission:**

* Two atoms sharing e<sup>-</sup> pair are of different electronegativity
* When the bond breaks, one of the bonded atoms takes both bonding e<sup>-</sup>s
* Results in the formation of +ve and –ve ions
* If +ve charged on C, it is called carbocation or carbonium
* If –ve charged on C, it is called carbanion

<em>**Note:** homolytic fission requires less energy than heterolytic</em>
