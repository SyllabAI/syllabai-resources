## Food Preservation

  - SO<sub>2</sub> is used by itself or as a sulphite to preserve food

SO<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O $\rightarrow$ H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>3(aq)</sub>

  - SO<sub>2</sub> & sulphites inhibit growth of bacteria, yeasts, etc.
    & are reducing agents, so reduce rate of oxidation of food.

  - Used to prevent spoilage of dried fruit, dehydrated vegetables and
    fruit juices.
