## Ammonium

  - Lone pair of e<sup>-</sup>s of nitrogen forms a coordinate bond with
    the H<sup>+</sup> ion

  - **Formation:** NH<sub>3(g)</sub> + H<sup>+</sup>
    NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>

  - Shape: tetrahedral

  - Bond angle: 109.5<sup>o</sup>

  - Bond length: equal lengths

 ![](../../../assets/a91f0b03a8f1-image077.png)

**Displacement of ammonia from its salts:**

$$Any\ Ammonium\ Salt\ +\ Any\ Base\ \xrightarrow{warm}\ Ammonia\ Gas\ +\ salt\ +\ water$$
