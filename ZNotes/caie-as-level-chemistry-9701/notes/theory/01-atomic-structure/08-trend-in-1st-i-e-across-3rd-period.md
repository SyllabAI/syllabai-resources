## Trend in 1<sup>st</sup> I.E across 3<sup>rd</sup> Period 

![](../../../assets/eb814c94ba31-image009.png)

  - **I.E of Al lower than Mg:** e<sup>-</sup> removed in Al is from
    higher energy 3p orbital which is further away from nucleus than 3s
    e<sup>-</sup> being removed from Mg. Nuclear attraction is less for
    3p than 3s $\therefore$ I.E of Al is lower than Mg

  - **I.E of S lower than P:** e<sup>-</sup> being removed in P is in a
    half filled, more stable 3p orbital whereas in S, the pairing of
    electrons in 3p results in increased repulsion $\therefore$ less
    energy need to remove an e<sup>-</sup>
