## Oxidation of Alkenes

* Both oxidation and addition to double bond involved
* KMnO<sub>4</sub> changes from purple/pink to colourless

**With Cold Dilute Acidified KMnO<sub>4</sub>/H<sup>+</sup>**

* Diol is formed

 ![](../../../assets/8da27078cc36-image118.png)

**With Hot Concentrated Acidified KMnO<sub>4</sub>/H<sup>+</sup>**

* This leads to the rupture of the double bond
* Two compounds are formed
* Products formed depend on alkene
