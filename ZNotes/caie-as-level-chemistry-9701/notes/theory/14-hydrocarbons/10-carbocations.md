## Carbocations

![](../../../assets/cedb45f0419b-image117.png)

- **Markovnikov’s principle:** an electrophile adds to an
  unsymmetrical alkene so that the most stable carbocation is formed
  as an intermediate

- Hydrogen binds to carbon that is least stable

  - Producing a positive inductive effect
