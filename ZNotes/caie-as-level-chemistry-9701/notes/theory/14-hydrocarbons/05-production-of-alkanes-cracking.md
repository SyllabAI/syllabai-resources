## Production of Alkanes & Cracking

* **Methods to Produce Alkanes:**

  1. **Hydrogenation of Alkenes:** Reacting alkenes with hydrogen gas ($\text{H}_2$) using a nickel catalyst at $100^\circ\text{C}$ and $2\text{ atm}$ directly produces alkanes.

  2. **Cracking:** Breaking down larger, less useful crude oil fractions.

* **Cracking Details:**

  * **Thermal cracking:** Uses high temperature and high pressure to produce shorter alkanes and alkenes.

  * **Catalytic cracking:** Uses high temperature and a catalyst (e.g., zeolite) to produce branched alkanes, cycloalkanes, and aromatic hydrocarbons.
