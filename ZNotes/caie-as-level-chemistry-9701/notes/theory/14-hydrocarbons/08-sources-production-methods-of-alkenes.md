## Sources & Production Methods of Alkenes

Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons containing at least one $\text{C}=\text{C}$ double bond ($\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n}$). They can be produced via:

1. **Elimination of $\text{HX}$ from a halogenoalkane:**

   * **Reagent:** Ethanolic sodium hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$).

   * **Condition:** Heat / reflux.

2. **Dehydration of an alcohol:**

   * **Reagents & Conditions:** Passing alcohol vapour over a heated catalyst (e.g., aluminium oxide, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$) *or* heating an alcohol with a concentrated acid catalyst (e.g., concentrated sulfuric acid, $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$).

3. **Cracking of longer-chain alkanes:**

   * Cracking heavy petroleum fractions yields alkenes alongside alkanes.
