## Alkenes

* Unsaturated hydrocarbons

* Contain at least one C=C double bond

* General formula: $\text{C}*n\text{H}*$ (like cycloalkanes)

## Reactivity & Electrophilic Addition of Alkenes

* Alkenes are far more reactive than alkanes because the $\pi$ (pi) bond in the $\text{C}=\text{C}$ double bond contains loosely held electrons that are easily attacked by electrophiles.

* **Markovnikov’s Principle:** When adding an electrophile to an unsymmetrical alkene, the hydrogen attaches to the carbon with more hydrogens to form the most stable carbocation intermediate.
