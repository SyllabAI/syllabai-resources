## Substitution

* Alkanes react with halogens ($\text{Cl}_2$ or $\text{Br}_2$) via **free radical substitution** in the presence of UV light or heat.

* **Steps:**

  1. **Initiation:** Homolytic fission of halogen molecules to form radicals ($\text{Cl}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{Cl}^\bullet$).

  2. **Propagation:** Chain reactions maintaining radical balance ($\text{CH}_4 + \text{Cl}^\bullet \rightarrow \text{CH}_3^\bullet + \text{HCl}$, followed by $\text{CH}_3^\bullet + \text{Cl}_2 \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{Cl} + \text{Cl}^\bullet$).

  3. **Termination:** Combination of two radicals to form a stable molecule ($\text{CH}_3^\bullet + \text{Cl}^\bullet \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$).

**Example: Chlorination of Methane**
