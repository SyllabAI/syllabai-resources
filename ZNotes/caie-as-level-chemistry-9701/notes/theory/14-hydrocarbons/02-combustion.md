## Combustion

* Used as fuels because they burn in oxygen to give out large amounts of energy.

* Alkanes are kinetically stable in the presence of $\text{O}_2$; combustion occurs only when the necessary activation energy ($\text{E}_\text{a}$) is supplied in the gas phase.

* **Complete combustion:** $\text{carbon dioxide} + \text{water}$

* **Incomplete combustion:** $\text{carbon monoxide} + \text{carbon (soot)} + \text{water}$

* **General Equation:**

  $$\text{C}_x\text{H}_y + \left\(x + \frac{y}{4}\right\)\text{O}_2 \rightarrow x\text{CO}_2 + \frac{y}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
