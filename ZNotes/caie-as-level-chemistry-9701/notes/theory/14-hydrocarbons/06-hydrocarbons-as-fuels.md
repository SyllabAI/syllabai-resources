## Hydrocarbons as Fuels

* Source of alkanes: crude oil
* Steady change in b.p. of alkanes allows crude oil to be separated by
  fractional distillation
* Catalytic conversion of CO and NO<sub>x</sub>:
  * 2NO<sub>2</sub> + 4CO → N<sub>2</sub> + 4CO<sub>2</sub>
  * 2NO + 2CO → N<sub>2</sub> + 2CO<sub>2</sub>
