## Production of Carbonyl Compounds

### **Oxidation of Primary Alcohols (Aldehydes)**

* **Reactants:** acidified $K_2Cr_2O_7$/$KMnO_3$.
* Distillation to separate Aldehyde from other compounds.
* Aldehyde can easily be oxidized into Carboxylic acid, so it needs to be distilled as soon as it forms.
* $CH_3CH_2OH + [O] => CH_3CHO + H_2O$.

### **Oxidation of Secondary Alcohols (Ketones)**

* **Reactants:** acidified $K_2Cr_2O_7$/$KMnO_4$.
* Distillation to separate Ketone from other compounds.
* Ketones cannot be further oxidized.
* $CH_3CHOHCH_3 + [O] => CH_3COCH_3 + H_2O$.
