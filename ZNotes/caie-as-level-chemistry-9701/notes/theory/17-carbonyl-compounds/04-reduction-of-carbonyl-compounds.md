## Reduction of Carbonyl Compounds

- **Type of Reaction:** nucleophilic addition (H<sup>+</sup> ions)

- **Reducing agents:**

  - NaBH<sub>4</sub> – sodium tetrahydrioborate

  - LiAlH<sub>4</sub> – lithium aluminium hydride

  - H<sub>2</sub>/Pt or Ni

- **Aldehydes** $\mathbf{\Longrightarrow}$ **1<sup>o</sup>
  Alcohols**

  - R-CHO + 2`[H]` RCH<sub>2</sub>OH

- **Ketones** $\mathbf{\Longrightarrow}$ **2<sup>o</sup> Alcohols**

  - R-CO-R + 2`[H]` R-CH(OH)-R
