## General Gas Equations

$$
PV = nRT
$$

$$
M_{r} = \frac{Mass\  \times \ RT}{\text{PV}}
$$

$$
\frac{P_1V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2V_2}{T_2}
$$

| **Quantity** | **Unit** | **Conversion** |
|----|----|----|
| Pressure | Pascal | 1KPa = 1000Pa |
| Volume | m<sup>3</sup> | 1m<sup>3</sup> = 1000dm<sup>3</sup> = 1x10<sup>6</sup>cm<sup>3</sup> |
| Temperature | K | <sup>O</sup>C + 273 |

* **Standard Conditions:** 101KPa and 298 K

Mole Fraction
$= \frac{\text{Mols of One Gas}}{\text{Total Mols of Gases}}$

Partial Pressure of a Gas $=$
$Mole\ Fraction \times Total\ Pressure$
