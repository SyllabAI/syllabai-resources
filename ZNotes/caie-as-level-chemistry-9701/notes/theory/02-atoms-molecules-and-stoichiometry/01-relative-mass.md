# Atoms, Molecules and Stoichiometry

## Relative Mass

* **Atomic mass (Ar):** weighted average mass of all the naturally occurring isotopes of that element
* **Molecular mass (Mr):** mass of a molecule
* **Formula mass:** mass of one formula unit of a compound
* **Isotopic mass:** mass of a particular isotope of an element
* Compared with <sup>12</sup>C where one atom of <sup>12</sup>C has
  mass of exactly 12 units
* Unified atomic mass unit: u = 1.66 x 10<sup>-27</sup>kg
