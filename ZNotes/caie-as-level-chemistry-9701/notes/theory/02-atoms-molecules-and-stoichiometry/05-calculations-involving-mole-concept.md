## Calculations Involving Mole Concept

$$
Moles = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Molar Mass}}
$$

$$
Volume\ of\ a\ Gas = Moles\  \times \ 24
$$

* The formula applies to gases at r.t.p.
* Unit of volume is $dm^{3}$and $1000cm^{3} = 1dm^{3}$

$$
Concentration = \frac{\text{Moles}}{\text{Volume}}
$$

* Concentration unit $= mol\ dm^{- 3}$


* **Anhydrous**: a compound in which all water molecules are removed
* **Hydrated**: a compound which has a number of water molecules associated with its crystalline structure
* **Water of Crystallisation**: these water molecules in a hydrated compound are called water of crystallisation
