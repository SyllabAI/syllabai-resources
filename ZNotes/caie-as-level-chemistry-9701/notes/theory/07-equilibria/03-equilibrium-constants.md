## Equilibrium Constants 

  - Equilibrium constant expressed in terms of **concentration**
    
      - $K_C = \frac{\left\lbrack \text{Product} \right\rbrack^{\text{mols}}}{\left\lbrack \text{Reactant} \right\rbrack^{\text{mols}}}$
    
      - Only liquids and gases

  - Equilibrium constant expressed in terms of **partial pressure**
    
      - $K_P = \frac{{p(Product)}^{\text{mols}}}{{p(Reactant)}^{\text{mols}}}$
    
      - Only gases

<!-- end list -->

  - Large value of $K_C$/$K_P\  \Rightarrow$ equi. towards
    products side

<!-- end list -->

  - Smaller value of $K_C$/$K_P\  \Rightarrow$ equi. towards
    reactants side

  - $K_C$/$K_P$ changes only with changes in temperature

  - The amount of reactants that disappear will always appear in the
    products in the same ratio as present in a balanced equation
