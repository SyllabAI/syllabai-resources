## Properties

**Physical Properties:**

* Colourless liquids at r.t.p
* b.p. and density increases with increasing C atoms and also with
  increasing OH groups

**Solubility of Alcohols in Water:**

* Smaller alcohols mix completely with water since strong hydrogen bonds occur between alcohol and water
* As hydrocarbon nature increases (i.e. more C-C… bonds), the non-polar character outweighs the ability of the OH to form hydrogen bonds and $\therefore$ solubility decreases
* Small alcohols (e.g. ethanol) are good solvents for both polar and
  non-polar compounds as they have polar and non-polar components
