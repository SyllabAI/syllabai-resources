## Dehydration of Alcohols 

  - **Alcohol<sub>(l)</sub>** **Alkene + H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>(l)</sub>**
    
      - **Condition:**
        
          - Conc. H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> OR
        
          - H<sub>3</sub>PO<sub>4</sub> at 180<sup>o</sup>C OR
        
          - Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> at 300<sup>o</sup>C
    
      - **Type of reaction:** Elimination

**Mechanism:**

![](../../../assets/b0a001b0add5-image137.png)

  - Adjacent carbon to carbon with OH must have at least one hydrogen
    (tertiary cannot undergo dehydration)
