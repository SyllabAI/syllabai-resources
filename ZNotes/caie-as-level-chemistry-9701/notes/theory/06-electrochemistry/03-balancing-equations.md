## Balancing Equations

  - **Equation:**
    $HI + HNO_3 \rightarrow I_2 + N_2O_3 + H_2O$

  - **Half ionic:** ${2I}^{-} - 2e^{-} \rightarrow I_2$
    $N^{+ 5} + 2e^{-} \rightarrow N^{+ 3}$

  - For every 2 iodines, there will be 1 nitrogen

  - Thus first put in correct ratio for iodine and nitrogen then balance
    hydrogens and oxygens

  - **Balanced:**
    $4HI + 2HNO_3 \rightarrow 2I_2 + N_2O_3 + {3H}_2O$
