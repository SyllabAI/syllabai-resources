## Important Uses of Halogens and Halogen Compounds

* **Fluorine:**
  * To make chlorofluorocarbon (CFCs)
  * As fluoride in toothpaste
  * To make polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) – non-sticking coating in pots and pans
* **Bromine and Iodine:** manufacture of photographic films
* **Chlorine:**
  * In bleaches
  * To make PVC and chlorofluorocarbon (CFCs)
  * As solvents
* **Use of chlorine in water purification:**
  * The oxidising power of chlorine is used in the treatment of water to kill bacteria
