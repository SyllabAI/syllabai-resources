## The Reactions of Chlorine with Aqueous Sodium Hydroxide

* **Disproportionation:** a reaction in which the same substance is oxidized and reduced simultaneously, producing two different products
* When chlorine reacts with a solution of cold aqueous sodium
  hydroxide, the disproportionation goes to lower oxidation states

$$
Cl_2 + 2NaOH \rightarrow NaCl + NaClO + H_2O
$$

* With a hot solution, the oxidation state of chlorine goes up to +V

$$
3Cl_2 + 6NaOH \rightarrow NaClO_3 + 5NaCl + 3H_2O
$$

* This happens as the chlorate is formed by disproportionation of
  hypochlorite and hypochlorous acid

$$
ClO^- + 2HClO\  \rightarrow ClO_3^- + 2HCl
$$
