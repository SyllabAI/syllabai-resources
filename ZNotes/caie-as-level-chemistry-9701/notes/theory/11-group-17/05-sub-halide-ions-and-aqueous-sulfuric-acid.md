## (Sub) Halide Ions and Aqueous Sulfuric Acid

**Metal Halide + Conc. H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4(aq)</sub>** **Hydrogen
Halide**

* Conc. H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4(aq)</sub> is an oxidising agent (except for chloride and fluorides as it is not strong enough)
* This reaction is used for the preparation of hydrogen halides

<table>
<tr>
<td><strong>Chlorine</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr>
<td rowspan=2><strong>Bromine</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr>

</tr>
<tr>

</tr>

</tr>
