## Hydrolysis of Nitriles

### **Reactants and Conditions**

* Nitriles
* Heat Under Reflux
* Acid/Base

### **Acid Hydrolysis**

**Products:**

* Salt
* Carboxylic Acid

**Reaction:**

$CH_3CH_2CN + HCl => CH_3CH_2CO_2H + NH_4Cl$

### **Base Hydrolysis**

**Products:**

* Base
* Salt of Carboxylic Acid

**Reaction:**

$CH_3CH_2CN + NaOH => CH_3CH_2CO_2 Na + NH_3$
