## Reactions with Water

**Metals:** $ \mathrm{M} \text{(s)} + 2 \mathrm{H_{2}O} \text{(l)} \xrightarrow{} \mathrm{M(OH)2} \text{(aq)} + 2 \mathrm{H_{2}} \text{(l)} $

**Metal Oxides:** $MO + H_2O \rightarrow M(OH)_2$

* Solubility of MO (Metal Group 2 Oxide) and M(OH)<sub>2</sub>(Metal Group 2 Hydroxides) increases down the group
* Alkalinity of solution increases down the group
* Solubility of MSO<sub>4</sub> decreases down the group
* M(CO<sub>3</sub>) don’t react with water
