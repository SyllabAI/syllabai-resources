* **m.p./b.p. decreases down group:** atoms/ions get larger, the distance between nuclei & e<sup>-</sup>s increases $\therefore$ bonds weaker
* **m.p./b.p. higher in gp. 2 than 1:** 2e<sup>-</sup>s per atom donated into delocalized system $\therefore$ metallic bonding stronger
* **density increases down group:** mass of atoms increases faster than their size (volume) as atomic no. increases
