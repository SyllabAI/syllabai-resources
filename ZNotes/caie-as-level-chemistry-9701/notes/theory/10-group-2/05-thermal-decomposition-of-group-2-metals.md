## Thermal Decomposition of Group 2 Metals

$\text{MCO}_{3(s)} \rightarrow \text{MO}_{(s)} + \text{CO}_{2(g)}$

$2 \text{M(NO}_3)_{2(s)} \rightarrow 2\text{MO}_{(s)} + 4 \text{NO}_{2(g)} + O_{2(g)}$

* **$NO_2$:** thick brown, acidic and soluble gas
* Thermal stability increases down the group $\therefore$ decomposition becomes more difficult.
