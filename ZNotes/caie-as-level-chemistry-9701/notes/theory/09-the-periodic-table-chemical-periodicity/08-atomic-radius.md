## Atomic Radius

![](../../../assets/c9c935ae62a4-image070.png)

  - P<sup>+</sup> in nucleus increases so nuclear charge increases

  - There are more e<sup>-</sup>, but increase in shielding is
    negligible because each extra e<sup>-</sup> enters same principal
    energy level

  - $\therefore$ force of attraction between nucleus & e<sup>-</sup>
    increases ...

  - So atomic radius decreases.
