## Reactions of Chloride with Water

![](../../../assets/8a1f88f1a806-image068.png)

  - Sodium chloride simply dissolves in water. Water is polar
    $\therefore$ positive Na<sup>+</sup> attracted to OH<sup>-</sup>
    while Cl<sup>-</sup> attracted to H<sup>+</sup>

  - MgCl<sub>2</sub> slightly acidic because Mg ion has smaller radius &
    higher charge $\therefore$ attraction to water is so strong that
    H<sub>2</sub>O loses a proton and solution becomes slightly acidic
