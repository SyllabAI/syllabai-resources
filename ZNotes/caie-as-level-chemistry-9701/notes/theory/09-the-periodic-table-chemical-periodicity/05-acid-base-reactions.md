## Acid-Base Reactions

* $\text{Na}_2\text{O(s)} + 2\text{HCl(aq)} \rightarrow 2\text{NaCl(aq)} + \text{H}_2\text{O(l)}$

* $\text{MgO(s)} + 2\text{HCl(aq)} \rightarrow \text{MgCl}_2\text{(aq)} + \text{H}_2\text{O(l)}$

* $\text{Mg(OH)}_2\text{(s)} + 2\text{HCl(aq)} \rightarrow \text{MgCl}_2\text{(aq)} + 2\text{H}_2\text{O(l)}$

* **Aluminum oxide is amphoteric** $\therefore$ reacts with the acid and base

$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\text{(s)} + 6\text{HCl(aq)} \rightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3\text{(aq)} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O(l)}$

$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3\text{(s)} + 2\text{NaOH(aq)} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O(l)} \rightarrow 2\text{NaAl(OH)}_4\text{(aq)}$

* **Silicon dioxide is acidic:** SiO\<sub\>2(s)\</sub\> \+ 2NaOH (hot & conc.)
  Na\<sub\>2\</sub\>SiO\<sub\>3(aq)\</sub\> \+ H\<sub\>2\</sub\>O
* $\text{P}*4\text{O}*\text{(s)} + 12\text{NaOH(aq)} \rightarrow 4\text{Na}_3\text{PO}_4\text{(aq)} + 6\text{H}_2\text{O(l)}$
* **Sulphur dioxide and trioxide are strongly acidic**
