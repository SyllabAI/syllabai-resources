# Graphs and Tables

* When finding gradient, always use a triangle with hypotenuse greater than 75% of the line.
* Label axis with quantity and unit.
* Plot graph with a fine cross or encircle dots.
* For each heading in a table, write the quantity measured with the
  unit separated with a slash.
* Keep significant figures consistent in values in a table.
* Make **only one** table of result for each question.
* Circle anomalous results and exclude them from calculations.
* The line of best fit drawn should ignore anomalous results.
* Ensure your graph covers greater than 3/4th of the page.
* Points must be within half a small square of the correct position.
