b)i)State two of the assumptions of the kinetic theory as applied to an ideal gas.

No intermolecular forces of attraction between ideal gas particles.

Particles have negligible volume.

Collisions between ideal gas particles are perfectly elastic.

Ideal gas particles behave as rigid spheres.
