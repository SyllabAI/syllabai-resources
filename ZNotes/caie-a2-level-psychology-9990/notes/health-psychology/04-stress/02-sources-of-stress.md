## Sources of Stress

* Many factors can trigger stress in a person
* One way to investigate the causes of stress is through investigating
  environmental changes which can cause stress
* For many people, stress is so common that that it has become part of their life
* Positive effects of stress can include motivation, as well as
  assisting in performing well under pressure
* The top five sources of stress, according to the American
  Psychological Association include:

<!-- end list -->


1. Money (76%)
2. Work (70%)
3. The economy (66%)
4. Family responsibilities (59%)
5. Relationships (55%)
