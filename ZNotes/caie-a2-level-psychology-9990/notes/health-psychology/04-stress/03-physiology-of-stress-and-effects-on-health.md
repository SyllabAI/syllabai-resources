## Physiology of Stress and Effects on Health

* Concerns about the responses of the person to the stressor
* Based on arousal

<!-- end list -->

* Arousal refers to a general physiological state in which the sympathetic division of the autonomic nervous system is activated.
  * **Sympathetic Nervous System**: the autonomic nervous system that controls the internal organs and plays a role in keeping the body’s internal environment stable

<!-- end list -->

* The sympathetic division rouses the body to action through
  activating physiological mechanisms which produce and maintain
  alertness and energy

### **GAS Model (Selye, 1936)**

* Immediate responses to stressful events generate a high arousal
  level
* Selye observed that long-term adaptation to stress followed a
  three-stage pattern, known as the **General Adaptation Syndrome
  (GAS)**
* GAS is a term describing the body’s short and long-term reactions
  and adaptations to stress to restore homeostasis
  * **Homeostasis**: the control of internal conditions, e.g.
    temperature and blood conditions

<!-- end list -->

* The three stages of Selye’s GAS are:
  * **Alarm**: prepares the body’s resources for the stressful
    response
  * **Resistance**: the body adapts to the stressor and
    physiological arousal declines but is still above normal
  * **Exhaustion**: the body’s energy reserves are depleted and the
    ability to resist can decline
