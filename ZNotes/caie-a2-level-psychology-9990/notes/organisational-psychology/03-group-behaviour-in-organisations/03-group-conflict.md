## Group conflict

### Levels and causes of group conflict: organisational to interpersonal

* **Levels:**
  * Intra-group conflict
  * Inter-group conflict
  * Inter-individual conflict (within a group)
* **Causes:**
  * **Organisational/situational factors:** conflict over status, salary, disagreements, resources, space and such.
  * **Interpersonal factors:** personality clash and such.

### Positive and negative effects of conflict

* **Positive effects:**
  * Produces change, especially in small organisation where change can be more easily implemented.
  * Resolution of conflict may strengthen unity/commitment
  * Decisions are fully explored, thus preventing risky decision making and group think
  * Produce more creative/innovative suggestions
  * Forms healthy competition for eg highest sales which can have a positive impact on revenue
* **Negative effects:**
  * Distracts workers, reduces overall productivity and wastes time
  * Goals become distorted as focus is towards the conflict
  * Toll on psychological and physical health, increasing absenteeism and turnover and reducing satisfaction
  * If made public, it could reduce the reputation of the organisation

### Managing group conflict (Thomas, 1976)

* **Competition:** individuals may persist in conflict until there is a winner.
* **Accommodation:** when one individual makes a sacrifice to end the conflict.
* **Compromise:** compromises are effective in ending conflicts when both sides give up something.
* **Collaboration:** working together to finish the conflict
* **Avoidance:** suppressing/withdrawing from the conflict entirely. This doesn’t end the conflict but creates a cool off period.
* **Subordinate goal:** groups/individuals are working towards a goal rather than engaging in conflict
