# **Health and Education**

**Case Study 10.2**

* Name of the programme: Female Secondary Stipend and Assistance Program.

* Why it is needed: To increase girls’ enrolment and completion at secondary level and reduce dropouts caused by poverty and early marriage.

* Who it helps: School-age girls, especially in rural and disadvantaged areas where access to secondary education is limited.

* Positive impacts and challenges:

  * Impacts include higher female enrolment and completion rates, improved opportunities for girls, and social benefits such as delayed early marriage.

  * Challenges include ensuring consistent funding, reaching the most disadvantaged students, and maintaining education quality as enrolment increases.
