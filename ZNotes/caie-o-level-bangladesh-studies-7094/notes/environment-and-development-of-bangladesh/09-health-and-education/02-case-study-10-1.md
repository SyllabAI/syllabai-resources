# **Health and Education**

## **Case Study 10.1**

* Name of the programme: Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI).

* Why it is needed: To prevent vaccine-preventable diseases and reduce child illness and death.

* Who it helps: Infants and children, and also mothers through immunisation-related health services provided in communities.

* Positive impacts and challenges:

  * Impacts include fewer cases of vaccine-preventable diseases, healthier children, and improved overall public health.

  * Challenges include reaching remote and hard-to-access areas, maintaining vaccine storage and delivery systems, and reducing missed doses.
