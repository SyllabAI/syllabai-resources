## Skills

### Pie Charts

* Show the proportion of each sector (primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary) in Bangladesh’s GDP at a given year.

* Calculate the percentage contribution of each sector.

* Convert each percentage into degrees for the pie chart (percentage × 360 ÷ 100).

* Draw the circle accurately and mark sectors using a protractor.

* Label each sector clearly with its name and percentage.

* Interpretation: Identify the dominant sector and compare the relative size of sectors at a specific point in time.

### Multiple Line Graphs

* Show changes in sector contributions or GDP over time.

* Use the x-axis for time (years) and y-axis for percentage contribution or GDP value.

* Draw separate lines for each sector using different colours or symbols.

* Plot data points accurately and connect them with straight lines.

* Include a clear key/legend and title.

* Interpretation: Identify trends such as growth of tertiary sector or decline of primary sector, compare changes between sectors over time, highlight key years with significant changes.

### Line Graphs for GDP

* Show GDP growth of Bangladesh over time and compare with other countries.

* x-axis: Years; y-axis: GDP in current or constant prices.

* Plot Bangladesh and comparator country (e.g., India) using different lines.

* Include a legend to distinguish countries.

* Interpretation: Identify periods of rapid growth or stagnation, compare relative growth rates with other countries, relate growth trends to historical events, policies, or global economic changes.

  ### Skills for Trade

* Interpret flow line maps showing the direction and volume of imports and exports.

* Use arrows or lines of different thickness to indicate trade volume (thicker lines for higher volume).

* Compare the patterns of imports vs exports for raw materials, food, and manufactured goods.

* Identify changes over time and relate them to economic development, industrialisation, or global demand.
