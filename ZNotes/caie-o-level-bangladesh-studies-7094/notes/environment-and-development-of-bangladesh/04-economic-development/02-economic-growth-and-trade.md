# **Economic Development**

## **Economic Growth and Trade**

## Changes in International Trade Patterns

### Raw Materials

* Bangladesh imports raw materials that are needed for industry and manufacturing.

* Historically, exports included jute, jute products, and some rice.

* Over time, the volume of raw material exports has declined as global demand for jute fell and domestic consumption of rice increased.

* Imports of raw materials like petroleum, chemicals, and machinery have increased steadily to support industrial growth.

### Food

* Bangladesh exports food products such as rice, fish, and seafood.

* The volume of rice exports has increased gradually due to improvements in agricultural production.

* Bangladesh imports food items like wheat, sugar, and edible oils to meet domestic demand.

* The import volume of food has risen with population growth and changing consumption patterns.

### Manufactured Goods

* Bangladesh exports a large volume of manufactured goods, especially textiles and garments.

* Export volume has grown significantly since the 1980s, making garments the main source of foreign income.

* Bangladesh imports manufactured goods such as machinery, electronics, and vehicles to support industrial and infrastructure development.

* The import volume of manufactured goods has also increased with industrial expansion.
