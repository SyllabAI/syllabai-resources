# **Agriculture and its Importance to Bangladesh**

Food Production and the Environment

* Mordern Technology

  * HYV seeds increase crop yields and allow farmers to produce more food from the same land.

  * Mechanisation (tractors, harvesters, irrigation pumps) reduces labour needs and speeds up farming activities, increasing efficiency.

  * Fertilisers and irrigation improve soil nutrients and ensure water supply, leading to higher and more reliable agricultural production.

* Aquaculture Development:

  * Impacts on the environment, including water pollution and loss of biodiversity.

* Soil Erosion:

  * Overuse of land leading to soil degradation, affecting agricultural yields.
  * Overuse of fertilisers and pesticides, water pollution, and biodiversity loss linked to agriculture.

* Salinisation:

  * Causes and effects of salt accumulation in irrigated areas, affecting crop production.

* Natural Hazards

  * Floods and cyclones damage crops, wash away fertile soil, and reduce total agricultural output.

  * Droughts lower crop yields by reducing water availability, especially in rain-fed farming areas.

  * Natural hazards cause loss of income for farmers, food insecurity, and increased poverty in rural areas.

* Sustainable Agriculture:

  * Development and practices to ensure long-term agricultural health, such as organic farming.
