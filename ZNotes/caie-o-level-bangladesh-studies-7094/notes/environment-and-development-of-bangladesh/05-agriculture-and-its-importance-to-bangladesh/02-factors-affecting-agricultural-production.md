# **Agriculture and its Importance to Bangladesh**

## **Factors Affecting Agricultural Production**

* Different crops are grown in different parts of Bangladesh because relief, soil type, and climate vary from region to region.

* Examples:

  * Rice is widely grown in the low-lying floodplains due to fertile alluvial soil and high rainfall, while jute grows well in river basin areas with fertile soil and standing water.

  * Tea is grown in the hilly areas of Sylhet and Chattogram because of higher relief, heavy rainfall, and well-drained soils.

* Physical Factors:

  * Relief, Climate, and Soil: These influence the location and development of crops. The Ganges Delta is ideal for rice.

* Economic Factors:

  * Demand, Labour, Technology, Capital, Land Fragmentation, Chemicals, Seeds, Irrigation, Economic Aid: These impact crop production.
  * Explicit human factors such as education, farming methods, government support, and market access.
