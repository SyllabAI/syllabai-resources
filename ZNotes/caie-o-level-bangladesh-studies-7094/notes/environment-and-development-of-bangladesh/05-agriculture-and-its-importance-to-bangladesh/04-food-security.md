# **Agriculture and its Importance to Bangladesh**

### **Food Security**

* ### Definition of Food Security:

  * Access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food at all times.
  * Domestic agricultural production ensures a stable supply of food, reducing dependence on food imports.
  * Increased local production helps control food prices 
  *  Improves access to food for the population.
  * Strong agricultural output supports national food security, especially during natural hazards or global supply disruptions.

* Factors Influencing Food Security:

  * Population growth, land fragmentation, natural disasters, political unrest, access to food, income, and food prices.

* Contribution of Different Foods:

  * Cereal crops, pulses, and animals (including fish) contribute to food security.

* Strategies to End Hunger:

  * Improving food security through initiatives like better irrigation, agricultural training, and food distribution.
