# **Population Structure, Growth, and Impact on Quality of Life**

## Population Structure

* Population Pyramid:Shows age distribution. Bangladesh's pyramid has shifted, with a larger youth population and fewer elderly due to falling birth and death rates.

* Population pyramids show the age and sex structure of a population.

* Wide base: Indicates a high number of children (0–14 years), suggesting high birth rates and a large dependent population.

* Narrowing top: Fewer older people (65+ years), reflecting lower life expectancy and smaller elderly population.

* Interpretation for dependency:

  * Wide base → high youth dependency ratio.

  * Narrow top → relatively low elderly dependency ratio.

* Future population trends:

  * A pyramid with a wide base predicts continued population growth.

  * If the base begins to narrow over time, it suggests falling birth rates and slowing population growth.

* Can be used to plan education, healthcare, and employment policies for future population needs.
