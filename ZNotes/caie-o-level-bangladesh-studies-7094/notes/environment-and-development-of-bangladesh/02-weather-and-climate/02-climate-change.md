# **Weather and Climate**

## Climate Change

* Global temperatures are rising due to greenhouse gases (e.g. CO₂, methane).

* Main causes: burning fossil fuels, deforestation, industrial activity.

* Bangladesh is at high risk due to:

  * Rising sea levels threatening coastal regions.

  * Increased cyclones, floods, and changing rainfall.

  * Impact on farming, water supply, and public health.

* Two approaches to respond:

  * **Mitigation**: Reduce emissions, plant trees, use renewable energy.

  * **Adaptation**: Build flood defences, improve early warnings, use salt-tolerant crops.
