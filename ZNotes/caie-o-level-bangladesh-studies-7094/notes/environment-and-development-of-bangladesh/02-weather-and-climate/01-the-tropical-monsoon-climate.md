# **Weather and Climate**

## The Tropical Monsoon Climate

* Bangladesh has a tropical monsoon climate with distinct wet and dry seasons.

* In summer land heats faster than sea, air rises over land, low pressure pulls in moist air from Bay of Bengal which rises, cools and gives heavy rain.

* In winter land is cooler than sea, high pressure over land sends dry north east winds
  toward the Bay of Bengal.

* Summer (June–October):

  * Low pressure over land, high pressure over ocean.

  * South-west monsoon winds bring heavy rain.

* Winter (November–March):

  * High pressure over land, low pressure over ocean.

  * North-east winds are dry and bring little rain.

* Located near the Tropic of Cancer → high temperatures year-round.

* Temperatures:

  * Hottest around April–May (\~35°C).

  * Coolest in January (\~15–20°C).

* Rainfall:

  * Most rain falls June–September.

  * Dry months are from November to February.

Climate graph pattern:

* Single rainfall peak in June to September and a small annual temperature range of about
  10°C, with April as the hottest month just before the rains.
