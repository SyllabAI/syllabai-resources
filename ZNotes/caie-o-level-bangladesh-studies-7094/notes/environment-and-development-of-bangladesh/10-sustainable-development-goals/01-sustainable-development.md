# **Sustainable Development Goals**

## Sustainable development

* Sustainable development means meeting present needs without harming the ability of future generations to meet theirs.

* Sustainable development balances economic development, social development, and environmental protection so progress can continue in the long term.

* Importance of sustainable development:

  * Balances economic growth, social development, and environmental protection.

  * Helps reduce poverty, improve health and education, and protect the planet.

* United Nations SDGs:

* The SDGs are a global framework with goals and targets. Countries use targets and indicators to measure progress by 2030.

  * 17 global goals set in 2015, to be achieved by 2030.

  * Aim to end poverty, protect the environment, and ensure peace and prosperity.

  * All UN member states, including Bangladesh, committed to them.

  * The SDGs are universal. All countries are expected to take action, while priorities may differ depending on development level.

  *  Sustainable Cities and Communities 

  *  Responsible Consumption and Production 

  *  Climate Action 

  *  Life Below Water 

  *  Life on Land 

  *  Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions 

  *  Partnerships for the Goals

* Progress in Bangladesh:

  *  Improved access to primary education

  * Better maternal and child health services

  *  More community level health programmes. 

  * Ongoing challenges can include urban overcrowding, informal housing growth, pollution, climate related hazards, and unequal access to quality services between rural and urban areas.

* Action in one area affects others:

  * Improving clean water and sanitation reduces disease, which improves school attendance and productivity at work. This supports health, education, and economic growth goals at the same time.

  *  improving education helps reduce poverty and promote gender equality.

  * Sustainable development requires a joined-up approach.

* United Nations’ role in Bangladesh:

  * Supports projects in health, education, climate resilience, and women's rights.

  * Partnerships mean governments, NGOs, communities, and international organisations share funding, skills, technology, and planning to meet SDG targets, especially for health, education, water, and climate resilience.
