# **Landcape**

## Plate Tectonics

* Tectonic plate movement causes earthquakes and formed the Chattogram Hills and Himalayas.
* Chattogram Hills are fold mountains formed as sediments in the Bay of Bengal were
  compressed by plate collision.

### **Structure of the Earth**

* Crust: Thin outer layer, divided into tectonic plates.

* Mantle: Semi-molten rock, responsible for plate movement.

* Outer Core: Liquid metal, generates Earth's magnetic field.

* Inner Core: Solid metal, extremely hot.

### **Tectonic Plates in Southeast Asia**

* Indian Plate: Moves northward, colliding with the Eurasian Plate.

* Eurasian Plate: Collision with the Indian Plate forms the Himalayas.

* Philippines Plate: Subduction zone, can cause earthquakes in the region.

* Australian Plate: Influences tectonic activity near the Indian Ocean.

### **Causes of Earthquakes in Bangladesh**

* Bangladesh is near the boundary of the Indian and Eurasian plates.

* Types of plate boundaries affecting Bangladesh:

  * Collision Boundary: Indian Plate colliding with Eurasian Plate → Forms Himalayas.

  * Subduction Zone: Indian Plate sinking under another → Causes earthquakes.

* Several active fault lines run near Sylhet, Chattogram and Dhaka, increasing earthquake
  risk.
