# **Landscape**

## Rivers of Bangladesh

Rivers play a crucial role in shaping Bangladesh’s landscape and causing hazards.

Changes in River Flow

* River discharge changes due to rainfall, glacier melt, and human activities.

* Hydrographs illustrate changes in river flow after precipitation.

### **Seasonal Changes in River Flow**

* Monsoon (June–October) → High discharge, flooding risk.

* Dry Season (November–April) → Low discharge, droughts possible.

### **River Processes**

* Erosion – The wearing away of land by moving water. Types include hydraulic action, abrasion, attrition, solution.

* Transport – The movement of eroded material.Types include traction, saltation, suspension, solution.

* Deposition – The dropping of sediment when the river loses energy.

### **Formation of Landforms**

* Floodplains: Formed by repeated flooding, rich in alluvial soil.

* Deltas: Formed by river deposition at the mouth (e.g., Ganges Delta).

### **Important terms:**

* Peak discharge : highest flow on the hydrograph after a storm.

* Lag time : time between peak rainfall and peak discharge.

* Rising limb / falling limb : how quickly discharge increases then decreases.
