# **The Service Sector**

## Importance of Transport

* Water, road, and rail transport are essential for moving goods (freight) and passengers across Bangladesh.

* Freight transport: Water transport (rivers and ports) is most important because it is cheaper and efficient for heavy and bulk goods.

* Passenger transport: Road transport is most important because it is flexible, reaches rural areas, and connects towns and cities quickly.

* Rail transport is important for both freight and passengers over longer distances but less flexible than road transport.

### Transport Challenges

* Natural hazards: Cyclones and floods disrupt transport routes.

* Traffic congestion and poor rural connectivity make transport inefficient.

* High numbers of road accidents are a major safety concern.

* Presence of unlicensed drivers contributes to accidents and reduces road safety.

### Strategies to Reduce Atmospheric Pollution from Transport

* Promoting the use of public transport to reduce the number of private vehicles.

* Encouraging non-motorised transport such as cycling and walking.

* Enforcing emission standards for vehicles and regular vehicle inspections.

* Introducing cleaner fuels and electric vehicles to reduce air pollution.

* Implementing traffic management policies to reduce congestion and vehicle idling.
