# **The Service Sector**

## The Informal Sector

* ### Formal Sector

  * Workers have legal contracts and receive regular wages.

  * They pay taxes and often have access to social benefits like healthcare and pensions.

  * Examples: Employees in garment factories (with contracts), office workers, government employees, bank staff.

  ### Informal Sector

  * Workers are self-employed or work for others without contracts or taxes.

  * Work is generally less secure and lacks social benefits.

  * Examples: Domestic servants, rickshaw drivers, street food sellers, informal manufacturing workers

    Advantages: Flexibility in work hours.

  * Disadvantages: Insecure work conditions and no social benefits.

    ### Challenges to Moving to the Formal Sector:

    * ### Barriers include lack of education, resources, and infrastructure.
