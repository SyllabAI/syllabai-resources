# **Manufacturing Industry**

## Types of Manufacturing Industry

* Conditions for Growth:

  * Access to raw materials, education of the workforce, capital, including investment from transnational companies, development of infrastructure (ICT, transport systems), and globalization.

* **Types of Industries:**

  * Cottage Industry: Small-scale, local production, often by hand.

  * Garment and Textile Industry: Major export sector, influenced by globalization and low labor costs.

  * Capital-Intensive Industries: Requires significant investment, such as chemicals and pharmaceuticals.

### Case Study: Ready-Made Garment Manufacturer in Bangladesh

* Name and Location: A ready-made garment factory located in Dhaka or other industrial areas.

* Source of Materials: Uses local fabrics and imported textiles as raw materials.

* Production Processes: Designing, cutting, sewing, quality control, and packaging of garments.

* Workforce: Employs a large number of workers, mainly women from rural areas.

* Products and Markets: Produces shirts, trousers, t-shirts, and knitwear for export to countries such as Europe, the USA, and Canada.

* Changes in Production Volume: Production has increased significantly since the 1980s due to global demand for garments.

* Impact on Environment and Employment: Provides employment and income, particularly for women; environmental issues include water use and chemical waste from production processes.
