# **Manufacturing Industry**

## Impact of the Growth of Manufacturing Industry on People and the Environment

* **Raw Materials**

  * Cottage industry: Because of easy access to local raw materials like jute and clay, small-scale production can grow.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because of imported fabrics and local yarn, factories can produce large volumes for export.

  * Capital-intensive industry: Because of steady supply of raw materials like steel, cement, or chemicals, large factories can operate efficiently.

* **Education and Skills**

  * Cottage industry: Because workers have basic skills passed through family tradition, small industries can develop locally.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because workers receive training in machine operation and quality control, production volume and quality improve.

  * Capital-intensive industry: Because engineers and skilled technicians are available, complex machinery can be used effectively.

* **Capital**

  * Cottage industry: Because small investments are needed, local entrepreneurs can start production.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because factories have access to larger investments, they can scale up and export internationally.

  * Capital-intensive industry: Because significant machinery and infrastructure investment is available, heavy industries like steel or chemical plants can grow.

* **ICT (Information and Communication Technology)**

  * Cottage industry: Because basic record-keeping and online marketing are possible, artisans can reach wider markets.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because ICT supports design, production planning, and global orders, exports increase.

  * Capital-intensive industry: Because ICT monitors production and supply chains, efficiency and productivity improve.

* **Transport**

  * Cottage industry: Because local roads allow goods to reach nearby markets, production is sustained.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because good transport links connect factories to ports, exports are possible.

  * Capital-intensive industry: Because rail, roads, and ports allow raw material import and product export, industries expand.

* **Globalisation**

  * Cottage industry: Because of access to niche international markets, traditional products can find customers abroad.

  * Garment and textile industry: Because global demand for ready-made garments is high, exports and growth are encouraged.

* Capital-intensive industry: Because foreign investment and technology transfer are available, large-scale industries develop.

  ## Working Conditions

  * In some factories, poor safety standards, long hours, and low wages can negatively affect the workforce, especially in garments and textiles.

  ### Factory Regulation

  * Ensures decent work through health and safety laws and fair wages.

  * Helps prevent exploitation and accidents in all industry types.

  ### Pollution

  * Industrial growth can produce air, water, noise, light, and visual pollution.

  * Garment factories may produce chemical waste; capital-intensive factories may emit gases or solid waste.

  Sustainability in Manufacturing

  * Reducing pollution, using eco-friendly technologies, and improving resource efficiency support sustainable growth of the manufacturing industry in Bangladesh.
