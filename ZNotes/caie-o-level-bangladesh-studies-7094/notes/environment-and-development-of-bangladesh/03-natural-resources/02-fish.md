# **Natural Resources**

## Fish

* Types of Fishing:

  * Inland fishing: Fishing in freshwater bodies like rivers, lakes, and ponds. Includes aquaculture.

  * Marine fishing: Takes place in saltwater, primarily in the Bay of Bengal. Includes inshore and deep-sea trawling.

* Economic Contribution:

  * Fishing plays an important role in Bangladesh's economy, providing both food and employment, particularly in coastal and rural areas.
  * Fishing contributes to GDP, export earnings from shrimp and supports related industries such as ice plants and boat building.

* Overfishing:

  * Causes include illegal fine mesh nets, too many boats, weak enforcement and high export demand.
  * Consequences include depletion of fish stocks, affecting both the environment and the economy.
  * Overfishing reduces the population of key species like hilsa.
  * Lower income for fishers, loss of biodiversity and collapse of key species such as hilsa.

* Sustainable Management:

  * Sustainable practices are necessary to protect fish populations and ensure the long-term success of the fishing industry.
  * Sustainable methods like closed seasons, mesh size regulations, fish sanctuaries and promoting aquaculture.
