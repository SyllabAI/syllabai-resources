# **Natural Resources**

## Forests

* Types of Forests:

  * Hill forests: Hill forests are found in the Chattogram Hill Tracts and Sylhet hills., important for biodiversity.

  * Mangrove forests (e.g., the Sundarbans): Coastal forests providing critical ecosystem services like protecting biodiversity.

  * The Sundarbans are located in Khulna, Bagerhat and Satkhira districts.

  * Mangroves reduce the impact of cyclones and storm surges by absorbing wave energy.

* Environmental and Economic Value:

  * Protecting soil and coastlines: Prevents erosion and protects the coastline from storms.

  * Maintaining biodiversity: Home to various species, some endangered like the Bengal tiger.

  * Economic exploitation: Timber, pulp, and paper are important exports.

* Impact of Deforestation:

  * Deforestation negatively affects ecosystems and rivers: Loss of biodiversity, soil erosion, and disruption of water cycles.
  * Hill deforestation increases soil erosion which leads to higher river siltation and increased flooding.

* Sustainable Management:

  * Sustainable forest management is critical for long-term resource availability and protecting the environment.
