# **Natural Resources**

## Water

* Sources of Water:

  * Rainfall: The primary source during the monsoon season.

  * Rivers: Major rivers like the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Meghna provide freshwater.

  * Groundwater: Used extensively for irrigation and drinking, though it faces issues like arsenic contamination.

  * Advantages and disadvantages of each source: Rainfall is abundant but seasonal. Rivers provide large supplies but face pollution. Groundwater is reliable but overuse lowers water tables and increases arsenic risk.

* Sustainable Management:

  * Managing water resources is crucial as demand increases with population and industrial growth.

* Risks:

  * Arsenic poisoning from excessive use of groundwater: A significant health issue in some regions of Bangladesh.
  * Arsenic contamination occurs when excessive pumping draws arsenic rich water from deep sediments.

* Water Supply:

  * Methods include dams for dry season storage, surface water treatment plants, irrigation efficiency improvements and sewage treatment.
