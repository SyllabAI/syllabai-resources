# **Migration and Urban Growth**

## Population Migration

* Migration: The movement of people from one place to another to live, either temporarily or permanently.

* Types of migration:

  * Internal: Within the same country (e.g. village to city).

  * International: Across country borders (e.g. Bangladesh to Middle East).

  * Voluntary: People choose to move (for jobs, education).

  * Forced: People are forced to move (due to conflict, disasters).

* Push factors are negative conditions at the place of origin that make people want to leave

  * Poverty and unemployment.

  * Poor education and healthcare.

  * Natural disasters (e.g. floods, river erosion).

  * Climate change can increase flooding, cyclones, salinity intrusion, drought, and riverbank erosion, which damage homes, farmland and income causing forced internal migration from rural areas to towns and cities and may also increase overseas migration if local opportunities fall.

* Pull factors are positive attractions at the destination

  * More jobs and higher wages.

  * Better schools and hospitals.

  * Improved transport and housing.

* International migration:

  * People leave Bangladesh mainly for work (Middle East, Malaysia).

  * Remittances sent home support families and the economy.

  * Some migrate due to climate change or lack of opportunities.

  * Refugee movements also occur (e.g. Rohingya entering Bangladesh from Myanmar).

  * Reasons for migration Into Bangladesh:

    * refugee movements for safety from conflict or persecution

    * return migration of Bangladeshi workers coming back after overseas employment

    * cross border movement in border regions for family connections and livelihoods

  * Reasons for migration out of Bangladesh:

    * overseas employment

    * education

    * improved income with remittances sent back home as an important reason.
