# **Migration and Urban Growth**

## **Impact of Population Migration**

* On rural areas:

  * Fewer working-age people remain hence labour shortages.

  * Services like schools and clinics may close or reduce.

  * Older population left behind, increasing dependency.

* On urban areas (especially Dhaka):

  * Rapid population growth and overcrowding.

  * Pressure on housing, transport, water, and sanitation.

  * Increase in informal settlements (slums).

  * Economic benefits from cheap labour and expanding workforce.

* Refugee migration:

  * Challenges: pressure on land, housing, clean water, sanitation, healthcare, education, jobs and local environments in host area

  *  Benefits: international humanitarian aid and NGO support; some employment and local economic activity created by aid spending; refugees may contribute labour in some contexts.
