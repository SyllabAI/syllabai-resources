# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Hussain Muhammad Ershad’s Rule (1982–1990)**

* 1982 Military Coup

  * Ershad took power after a coup, establishing military rule.

* Reforms Under Ershad

  * Administrative Reforms aimed to modernize the government.

  * Constitutional Reforms focused on stabilizing political structures.

* Economic Policies

  * Privatization promoted private sector growth.

  * Land Reform aimed to improve agricultural output and rural development.

  * rural development included large irigation process and expansion of schools.

  * Foreign Investment attracted international capital to fuel economic growth.

* Decentralization

  * Rural Development programs empowered local governments and promoted rural growth.

* End of Ershad’s Rule

  * Military rule ended in 1990 amid increasing public discontent.
  * Mass protests led by opposition parties and student groups forced Ershad to resign in 1990.

* 1983 Development Programme

  * focused on rural infrastructure, irrigation and education.
