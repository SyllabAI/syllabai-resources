# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Political, Social, and Economic Changes (1971–2001)**

* Political Shifts

  * Cycles of military rule and return to democracy.

  * Increased political stability after the 1990s.

* Social Transformations

  * Educational reforms focused on literacy and female education.

  * Improved gender equality through expanded access to education and employment.

* Economic Reforms

  * Shift from agriculture to industrial and digital economies.

  * Increased foreign investment and privatization.

  * Growth of telecommunications and remittances as key economic drivers.

* Cultural Preservation

  * Strengthened national identity through art, literature, and cultural symbols.

* Political continuity

  * Power often remained with a small elite; corruption and political rivalry continued.

* Economic continuity

  * Agriculture remained the main livelihood for many; rural poverty and landlessness persisted.

* Social continuity

  * Patriarchy and social inequality continued, especially in rural areas.
