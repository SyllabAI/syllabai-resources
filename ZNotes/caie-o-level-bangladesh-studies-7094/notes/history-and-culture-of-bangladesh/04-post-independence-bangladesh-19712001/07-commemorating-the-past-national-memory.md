# Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001

---

### **Commemorating the Past: National Memory**

* Cultural & Religious Festivals

  * Celebrate heritage and national unity.

* Literary Contributions

  * Works by Rabindranath Tagore, Kazi Nazrul Islam, and Begum Rokeya shaped the cultural and social landscape.

* Art & Monuments

  * Zainul Abedin’s art represented social and political struggles.

  * National Monument of Bangladesh symbolized independence.

  * Shaheed Minar honored those who fought for the Bangla language.

* International Mother Language Day (February 21)

  * Commemorates the Language Movement.

  * Village fairs and rural markets keep traditional crafts and trade alive. 

  * Traditional dances and folk performances preserve rural heritage and stories of struggle.

  *  Shaheed Minar and International Mother Language Day remember the Language Movement and defence of Bangla.

  * Honors the Bangla language and its cultural significance.

* Victory Day (16 December) commemorates success in the War of Liberation.
