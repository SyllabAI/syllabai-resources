# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Post-War Struggles and Triumphs (1971–1975)**

* Challenges after the War of Liberation & Bhola Cyclone

  * Devastated infrastructure and widespread poverty.

  * Large population displacement due to war and cyclone.

  * Urgent needs included hunger, lack of housing, law and order, and medical shortages.

* Political Stability

  * 1971 Parliamentary System established after independence.

  * Awami League’s Victory (1973) solidified Sheikh Mujibur Rahman’s leadership and political control.

  * 1974 Constitutional Amendments introduced reforms to strengthen the nation.

  * Sheikh Mujibur Rahman’s assassination in 1975 triggered political instability.

* Recovery from War & Cyclone

  * Government focused on recovery efforts including food, housing, and healthcare.

  * First Five-Year Plan emphasized agriculture and industrial recovery.

  * Literature and arts played a key role in rebuilding national identity.

* Refugee

  * millions of refugees returned from India and had to be resettled.

* Achievements, 1971–75

  * New constitution adopted (1972) with nationalism, socialism, democracy, secularism.

  * Fundamental rights guaranteed and parliamentary democracy introduced.

  * Rebuilt infrastructure and restored key industries.

  * Food production and agriculture supported; industrial recovery began.

  * Bangladesh gained international recognition and joined the UN (1974).
