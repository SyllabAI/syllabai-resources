# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Ziaur Rahman’s Military Rule (1975–1982)**

* Ziaur Rahman’s Leadership

  * Martial Law established, instituting military rule in the late 1970s.

  * Formed the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP) under his leadership.

  * 1977 Martial Law Decree strengthened Zia’s control over governance.

* Policies

  * Mass Irrigation programs aimed to ensure food security and improve agricultural productivity.

  * Promotion of free markets encouraged trade and economic activity.

  * Greater freedom of press and speech compared with previous period, though criticism of the regime still faced limits.

  * Expanded education and encouraged literacy programmes.

* Achievements

  * Achievements included increased food production, some economic stabilisation, and the revival of multi-party politics.
