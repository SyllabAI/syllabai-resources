# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Economic and Technological Growth (1996–2001)**

* Return of Sheikh Hasina’s Leadership

  * Opposition movement over election issues lead to caretaker government 

  * 1996 election brought Awami League to power

  * Focus on Food Self-Sufficiency ensured the country’s food security.

* Economic Development

  * Telecommunications & Digital Industries fostered growth of the tech sector.

  * Privatization of Electricity strengthened energy infrastructure.

  * Role of Remittances: Money sent by Bangladeshis abroad became a key economic contributor.

* International Relations

  * Agreements with India focused on water-sharing and trade.

  * Strengthened Diplomatic Ties worked to improve regional relations with neighboring countries.
