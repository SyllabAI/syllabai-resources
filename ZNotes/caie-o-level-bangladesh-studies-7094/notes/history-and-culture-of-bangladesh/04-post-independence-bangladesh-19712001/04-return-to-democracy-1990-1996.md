# **Post-independence Bangladesh, 1971–2001**

### **Return to Democracy (1990–1996)**

* Shift Back to Parliamentary Democracy

  * Return of Democracy marked the end of military rule and a return to parliamentary governance.
  * Mass movement against President Ershad forced him to resign.
  * A caretaker government was set up to oversee fair elections.
  * 1991 election brought BNP to power, restoring parliamentary democracy.

* Khaleda Zia first female prime minister of Bangladesh

  * Became Prime Minister after 1991 election.
  * First female Prime Minister of Bangladesh.
  * Significant for promoting women’s political representation.
  * Girls’ education up to Grade 10 made compulsory for girls, increasing enrolment.
  * Improved female empowerment and access to education.

* Key Social Reforms

  * Compulsory & Free Primary Education improved literacy, especially for girls.

  * Educational Reforms expanded access to education.

* Political Reforms

  * Direct Election of Mayors increased local governance participation.

  * Local Government Reforms empowered towns and cities through decentralization.

* Economic Achievements

  * Banking & Trade Reforms strengthened financial and trade systems.

  * Foreign Investment encouraged international capital inflows.

* International Relations

  * Visits to China & India strengthened diplomatic relations.

  * United Nations Peacekeeping highlighted Bangladesh's involvement in international peace efforts.
