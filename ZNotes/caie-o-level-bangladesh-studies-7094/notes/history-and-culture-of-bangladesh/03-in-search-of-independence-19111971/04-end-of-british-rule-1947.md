# **In search of independence, 1911–1971**

## **End of British Rule (1947)**

* Attempts at Compromise

  * 1942 Cripps Mission

    * British proposed Dominion status for India after World War II.

    * The offer was rejected by both Congress and the Muslim League, as it did not meet demands for full self-rule or separate Muslim homeland.

  * 1946 Cabinet Mission Plan

    * Proposed a loose federation with limited autonomy for provinces.

    * Congress rejected the plan, fearing it weakened central authority.

    * Muslim League demanded Pakistan, seeing it as insufficient for Muslim interests.

* Final Steps to Independence

  * Failure of United Bengal Idea

    * H.S. Suhrawardy proposed an independent Bengal to avoid partition.

    * Lacked support from both Congress and the Muslim League, so it did not materialize.

  * 3rd June Plan (1947)

    * Announced the Partition of India into India and Pakistan.

    * Set the framework for independence, but did not solve communal tensions.

  * Radcliffe Award (1947)

    * Divided Bengal and Punjab along religious lines, finalizing borders.

    * Triggered mass migrations, communal violence, and widespread chaos.

    * Millions of people were displaced, and thousands lost their lives in the resulting upheaval.
