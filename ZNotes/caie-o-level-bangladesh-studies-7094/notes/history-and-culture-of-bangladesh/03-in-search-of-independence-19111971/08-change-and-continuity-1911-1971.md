# In search of independence, 1911–1971

## Change and continuity, 1911–1971

* Political change

  * British colonial rule continued until 1947, followed by Partition and the creation of Pakistan.

  * East Bengal became East Pakistan and later achieved independence as Bangladesh in 1971.

  * Mass politics expanded through political parties, protests and elections.

  * Elected representatives and popular movements became more important over time.

* Political continuity

  * Political power often remained in the hands of a small elite.

  * Ordinary villagers had limited direct influence for much of the period.

  * Decision-making was usually controlled from outside Bengal, first by the British and later by West Pakistan.

* Economic change

  * Transport and infrastructure expanded, including railways and ports.

  * Industry grew, especially jute production and export.

  * Urban areas developed and employment opportunities increased in towns and cities.

* Economic continuity

  * Agriculture remained the main source of livelihood for most people.

  * Rural poverty continued despite economic growth in urban areas.

  * Land inequality and dependence on landlords remained common.

* Social change

  * Education spread, with more schools and colleges.

  * Political awareness increased among students and workers.

  * Women began to take part in education and public life, though slowly.

* Social continuity

  * Patriarchal attitudes remained strong in both rural and urban society.

  * Class and social inequalities continued to shape daily life.

  * Village-based lifestyles remained dominant for most of the population.

* Cultural change

  * New forms of literature, music, art and cinema developed.

  * Nationalism and language identity were increasingly expressed through culture.

  * Artists and writers used culture to protest injustice and promote unity.

* Cultural continuity

  * The Bengali language remained central to identity throughout the period.

  * Folk traditions, music and rural culture continued to influence daily life.
