# **In search of independence, 1911–1971**

### **Path to Independence (1949–1971)**

* Major Events

  * Language Movement (1948–52)

    * Protesters demanded Bengali be recognized as a state language.

    * On 21 February 1952, police opened fire on demonstrators in Dhaka, killing several students.

    * Bengali was later recognized as an official language in 1956.

  * Awami League & Six-Point Plan (1966)

    * Sheikh Mujibur Rahman’s plan demanded economic and political autonomy for East Pakistan.

    * West Pakistan rejected these demands, increasing tensions between the two wings.

  * Agartala Conspiracy Case (1968)

    * Pakistani government accused Mujibur Rahman of plotting to break away, arresting him and other leaders.

    * Mass protests forced his release, further strengthening the independence movement.

  * 1969 Mass Uprising

    * Students and workers protested against military rule.

    * President Ayub Khan was forced to step down.

  * 1970 Cyclone & Elections

    * The Bhola Cyclone killed over 300,000 people.

    * Destroyed homes, crops and fishing boats; huge loss of livelihoods in coastal areas; increased poverty in East Pakistan.

    * Many countries and aid agencies sent relief; the world saw West Pakistan’s poor response, damaging its image.

    * Bhola Cyclone convinced many Bengalis that only their own government would protect them which helped Awami League win 160/162 East seats in 1970.

    * West Pakistan’s slow response angered Bengalis, fueling calls for independence.

    * Awami League won 160 of 162 East Pakistani seats in the national assembly but was denied power.

  * Operation Searchlight & War (1971)

    * On 25 March 1971, the Pakistani army launched Operation Searchlight, killing thousands in Dhaka.

    * Sheikh Mujibur Rahman was arrested, beginning the War of Liberation.

    * India joined the war in December 1971, leading to Pakistan’s defeat.

    * On 16 December 1971, Pakistan surrendered, and Bangladesh gained independence.

  * Reasons why the independence movement succeeded

    * Overwhelming popular support for Awami League in East Pakistan (landslide 1970 election).

    * Pakistani army was overstretched and lost local support because of atrocities.

    * Indian military support in 1971 shifted the balance decisively.

    * Strong leadership and clear demands from Sheikh Mujibur Rahman and the Awami League.
