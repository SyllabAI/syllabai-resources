# **In search of independence, 1911–1971**

## East Pakistan’s Struggle (1947–1971)

### **Growing Discontent**

* Language Issue

  * Government imposed Urdu as the only national language, ignoring Bengali, which was spoken by more than 50% of Pakistan’s population.

  * This led to widespread protests, strikes, and student movements in East Pakistan.

* Economic Exploitation

  * East Pakistan produced the majority of Pakistan’s exports, such as jute, but received little economic development in return.

  * Resources and revenues were disproportionately allocated to West Pakistan, fueling resentment.

  * Large rural population in East Pakistan stayed poor while urban centres (mainly in West Pakistan) developed faster, deepening resentment.

* Political Discrimination

  * West Pakistan dominated the central government and denied leadership opportunities to Bengalis.

  * Demands for increased political representation and autonomy were suppressed.

* Military Marginalization

  * East Pakistanis were underrepresented in the military and administrative services.

  * This limited their influence in matters of national security and defense policymaking.
