# **The Struggle for Control of the Indian Subcontinent, 1204–1784**

## Establishment of Muslim Rule – Timeline of Events

### **Establishment of Muslim Rule in Bengal (1204–1538)**

* **1204:** Bakhtiyar Khilji defeats Lakshman Sen, establishing Muslim rule.

* **1206:** Khilji assassinated, political instability follows.

* **1342:** Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds the Ilyas Shahi dynasty, unifying Bengal.

* **1494:** Alauddin Hussain Shah starts the Hussain Shahi dynasty, expanding trade and culture.

* **1519–1532:** Nusrat Shah strengthens diplomacy with the Mughals and the Portuguese.

* **1538:** Sher Shah Suri conquers Bengal, ending independent Sultanate rule.

---

### **The Rise and Decline of the Mughal Empire (1526–1757)**

* **1526:** First Battle of Panipat; Babur defeats Ibrahim Lodi, establishing the Mughal Empire.

* **1530–1556:** Humayun’s reign; temporary loss of empire to Sher Shah Suri.

* **1576**: Bengal annexed by Akbar; Subadars appointed to govern on behalf of the Mughal emperor.

* **1556–1605:** Akbar consolidates rule, annexing Bengal in 1576.

  * Appointed Subahdars of Bengal under Akbar:

    * Sulaiman Kararni – Extended authority over Bengal and maintained relations with Akbar until his death in 1572.

    * Daud Khan – Son of Sulaiman; rebelled against Mughal authority and was defeated at the Battle of Rajmahal (1576), after which Bengal came firmly under Mughal control.

    *  Raja Mansingh Led Mughal forces to suppress resistance in Bengal, effectively acting as governor after Daud Khan’s defeat.

* **1605–1627:** Jahangir reigns; promotes trade with the English East India Company.

* **1628–1658:** Shah Jahan strengthens architecture (Taj Mahal).

* **1658–1707:** Aurangzeb reign; empire expansion and rising revolts.

* **1707:** Death of Aurangzeb; start of Mughal decline.

* **1717:** English East India Company gains trade rights from Mughal Emperor Farrukhsiyar, laying the foundation for British expansion.

---

### **The Rise of the East India Company and British Control (1600–1784)**

* **1600:** British East India Company founded.

* **1757:** Battle of Plassey; Robert Clive defeats Nawab Siraj-ud-Daulah.

* **1764:** Battle of Buxar; British defeat Mughal Emperor Shah Alam II, gaining revenue rights.

* **1765:** Treaty of Allahabad establishes Dual Administration; British control of revenue, Nawabs retain nominal authority.

* **1770:** Bengal famine due to British mismanagement.

* **1772:** Warren Hastings becomes Governor-General, ending Dual Administration.

* **1784:** India Act passed, bringing Bengal under direct British rule.
