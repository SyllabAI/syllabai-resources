## Establishment and Impact of Muslim Rule in Bengal (1204–1538)

### **Bakhtiyar Khilji’s Achievements**

* 1204: Defeated Lakshman Sen and captured Nadia, establishing Muslim rule in Bengal.

* Led Ghurid invasions into Bengal and Bihar using fast cavalry and surprise tactics.

* Key battles include conquest of Lakhnauti and suppression of local resistance.

* Exploited weaknesses in Sena rule and lack of unity among local rulers.

* Used small, fast cavalry units and surprise raids to capture Nadia and other key centres.

* Died in 1206, creating political instability in Bengal.

### **Role of Sufis and Sufism**

* Sufis like Shah Jalal and Khan Jahan Ali spread Islam peacefully.

* Promoted social and political awareness among Muslims; worked closely with lower castes.

* Established khanqahs and madrasas, providing education and spiritual guidance.

* Strengthened tolerance and unity between Hindus and Muslims.

* Shah Jalal emphasized the concept of Jihad as spiritual and social reform.

### **Development of Muslim Rule**

### **Ilyas Shahi Dynasty (1342–1487)**

* Founded by Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah; first to unify Bengal as one political unit.

* Expanded territory into Assam, Bihar, and Orissa through military conquest.

* Resisted Delhi Sultanate domination, asserting Bengal’s independence.

* Promoted Islamic religion: built mosques, madrasas, encouraged conversions.

* Advanced Persian art, architecture, and culture (e.g., Adina Mosque in Pandua).

* Strengthened Bengal as a centre of trade, especially textiles.

### **Hussain Shahi Dynasty (1494–1538)**

* Founded by Alauddin Hussain Shah; strengthened Bengal’s economy and administration.

* Promoted Bengali language and literature, supporting poets and scholars.

* Expanded trade, with Dhaka and Sonargaon becoming key commercial hubs.

* Continued cultural and religious patronage; maintained diplomatic relations with regional powers.

### **Nusrat Shah (1519–1532)**

* Maintained ties with the Mughals and Portuguese traders.

* Continued expansion of culture, religion, and trade.

* Played a key role in stabilizing Bengal politically, granting privileges to Afghan leaders.
