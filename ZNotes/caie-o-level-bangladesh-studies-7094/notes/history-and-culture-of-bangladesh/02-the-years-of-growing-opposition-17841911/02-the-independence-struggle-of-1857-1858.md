## The Independence Struggle of 1857–1858 (War of Independence)

## Why Did the Struggle Happen

### Political & Economic Causes:

* Doctrine of Lapse: Annexation of states without direct male heirs; major kingdoms like Jhansi, Satara, and Nagpur were annexed.

* Dethronement of Nawab Wajid Ali Shah of Awadh and humiliation of Bahadur Shah II caused resentment among nobles and soldiers loyal to them.

* Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, and other land revenue policies overburdened peasants.

* Indian artisans and weavers lost their livelihoods due to cheap British goods.

* Traditional elites and landlords lost power to the British.

Military Discontent:

* Indian sepoys faced racism, low pay, and poor treatment.

* Use of Enfield rifles allegedly greased with cow/pig fat offended Hindu and Muslim beliefs.

Religious Concerns:

* Fear of forced religious conversions through missionary activity.

* Sati ban and widow remarriage threatened traditional customs.

* Western education threatened traditional customs.

---

### Key Events:

* Mangal Pandey attacked British officers in Barrackpore, leading to his execution in March 1857.

* Meerut garrison revolted in May 1857.

* Kanpur revolt led by Nana Sahib.

* Lucknow revolt led by Begum Hazrat Mahal.

* Rebels declared Bahadur Shah II as symbolic Mughal emperor during the Siege of Delhi.

* British recaptured Delhi in September 1857.

* The war ended in mid-1858 after all rebels were defeated.

---

### Why Did It Fail:

* Lack of common objective: Motives ranged from religion to local territory disputes.

* Limited geographical spread: Revolts were mainly confined to northern and central India; southern and eastern regions remained largely quiet.

* Many Indian princes and elites (like the Nizam of Hyderabad) sided with the British.

* British military strength and superior weapons contributed to the defeat.

---

### Impact on Bengal and India:

* British executed thousands of rebels and punished civilians suspected of aiding them. Widespread massacres occurred in Delhi, Kanpur, and Lucknow.

* Hindus: Some were favored by the British after the revolt.

* Muslims: Blamed for the revolt, faced exclusion from government jobs and education.

### Sir Syed Ahmad Khan’s Role:

* Wrote *“The Causes of the Indian Revolt”*, explaining misunderstandings between British and Indians.

* Wrote *“The Loyal Muhammadans of India”* to highlight Muslims’ loyalty during the revolt and counter British accusations.

* Founded the Aligarh Movement, encouraging scientific learning and cooperation with the British.

* Proclamation of 1858 / Government of India Act 1858

  * Rule transferred from the East India Company to the British Crown.

  * A Viceroy was appointed to govern India in the name of the British monarch.

  * Indian princes were guaranteed their states if they stayed loyal.

  * British promised non-interference in religion and customs.

  * Indian army reorganised to reduce the number of Indian soldiers and increase
    European control.
