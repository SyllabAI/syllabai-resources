# **The years of growing opposition, 1784-1911**

### Changes in Bengal’s History (1784–1911)

### Political Changes:

* Shift from East India Company control (1784) to British Crown control (1857).

* Partition of Bengal and rise of nationalism.

* Foreign rule, elite influence, and limited Indian participation largely remained the same.

### Social Changes:

* Social reform movements against sati, child marriage, and purdah.

* Promotion of women’s education.

* Caste system, gender roles, and rural hardships largely remained unchanged.

### Economic Changes:

* Permanent Settlement Act and shift to cash crops.

* Decline of local industries and urbanization of Calcutta.

* Wealth flowed out of Bengal to Britain; agriculture remained the backbone of the economy.

### Cultural Changes:

*  Bengal Renaissance, rise of literature, music, and architecture; emergence of key figures like Raja Rammohun Roy, Bankim Chandra Chatterjee, and Rabindranath Tagore.
