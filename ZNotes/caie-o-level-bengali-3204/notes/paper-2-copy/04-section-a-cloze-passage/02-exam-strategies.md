## Exam Strategies

* Read the full passage first to get a feel for the topic. Could be calligraphy, a cultural tradition, a place, a social habit. You need that general sense before you start filling anything in. Then read through the word list carefully. Do not just skim the words. Think about what each one means and where it might plausibly belong.

* There are 15 words in the list but only 10 blanks. Five words will not be used. They are there to mislead you. This is important for elimination. If you are down to one or two remaining words and neither feels right, one of them is probably a distractor. Do not force a word into a blank just because it seems to be the only one left. If it does not fit the context, leave it and come back.

* **F**ill in the blanks you are confident about first. Skip anything uncertain and come back to it. For the harder blanks, use elimination. Cross out words already placed and anything that clearly does not fit the topic or tone. The remaining options narrow quickly. When a blank is giving you trouble, read the surrounding sentences slowly. There is always a contextual clue nearby, either in the sentence before or the one after. Look for a logical connection between the blank and the rest of the paragraph. The right word fits the grammar and makes the sentence feel like it was always meant to say that.

Ultimately, the key to acing this section is regular practice. The more passages you solve, the more intuitive your understanding of sentence context, vocabulary nuances, and structural flow will become.
