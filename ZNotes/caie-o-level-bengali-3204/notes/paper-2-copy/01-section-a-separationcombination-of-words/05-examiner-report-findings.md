
## **Examiner Report Findings**

### **Mistakes That Come Up Every Year**

* **Spelling the answer incorrectly after applying the right rule**. This is the dominant failure mode across every year in the archive. Candidates who understand the sandhi rule often still lose marks because the resulting word is spelled wrong. A correctly reasoned answer with one spelling error scores zero. Examiners have flagged this explicitly: a potentially correct answer is invalidated by a mistake of this kind. Understanding the process is not enough. The final written form must be exact.

* **Missing diacritical marks.** রেফ and দীর্ঘ ই-কার are the most consistently omitted marks across all years. A word written correctly in every other respect loses its mark if a diacritical sign is absent or in the wrong position. These marks are part of the answer, not optional additions.

* **Confusing the three sibilants.** Using শ or ষ where স is required, and vice versa, is a recurring error across multiple sessions. This is not a sandhi error; it is a straightforward spelling error that costs the mark regardless of whether the candidate understood the rule.

* **Applying the wrong sandhi rule entirely.** Several candidates each year produce a word that looks vaguely similar to the correct one but follows the wrong grammatical process. These are rule failures, not spelling failures, and they score zero.

* **Writing completely unrelated words.** In several sessions, some candidates produced answers with no grammatical or semantic connection to the correct word at all. These suggest the candidate did not attempt the sandhi process and instead guessed.

* **Omitting a conjunct consonant.** When two consonants combine to form a conjunct, candidates sometimes write the individual consonants separately rather than in their merged form. The conjunct form is the required form. Writing it any other way scores nothing.

### **What Candidates Who Score Well Actually Do**

* **Practise writing the output, not just identifying the rule.** The mark is lost at the spelling stage far more often than at the rule-identification stage. Drilling the written form of combined and separated words is where preparation should be focused.

* **Check diacritical marks before finalizing any answer.** Confirm that every required mark is present and correctly placed. রেফ and দীর্ঘ ই-কার are the two most frequently absent. This takes seconds and prevents an avoidable mark loss.

* **Check the three sibilants deliberately.** For any answer containing a sibilant, pause and confirm which of স, শ, and ষ the correct form requires. It is a one-second check that repeatedly separates correct answers from incorrect ones.

* **Write conjunct consonants in their combined form.** Where two consonants merge in sandhi, the conjunct form must appear in the written answer. Writing the individual consonants separately, even in the correct order, will not receive the mark.
