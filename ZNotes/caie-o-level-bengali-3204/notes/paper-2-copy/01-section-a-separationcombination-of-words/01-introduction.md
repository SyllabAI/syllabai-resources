# Section A: Separation/Combination of Words

## Introduction

This section consists of Questions 1 to 5. Each question is worth 2 marks, making a total of 10 marks.

The task alternates by year: 

* In odd years (2019, 2021, 2023, 2025), the task is **সন্ধি কর** - joining separated parts into one word.

* In even years (2020, 2022, 2024, 2026), the task is **সন্ধি বিচ্ছেদ কর** - splitting a compound word into its parts.

Despite having a number of rules to learn, this is the most scoring-friendly section on the paper. With solid preparation, full marks of 10 out of 10 are achievable in less time than any other section.

## Understanding the Marks

Each question carries 2 marks. For separation questions, 1 mark is awarded for each correctly written part on either side of the \+ sign. For combination questions, the fully combined word must be spelled correctly to receive both marks. In both cases, a correct sandhi rule applied with an incorrect spelling scores zero.

Diacritical marks are part of the answer. রেফ (র্), দীর্ঘ ই-কার (ী), ব-ফলা (্ব), য-ফলা (্য), and conjunct consonants must be written in their correct form. A word that is otherwise correct but missing one of these marks will not receive the mark for that part.
