## Examiner Report Findings

### **Mistakes That Come Up Every Year**

* **Copying Without Transforming:** Writing out the original sentence unchanged, or deleting speech marks without altering any grammatical structure. Both score zero.

* **Superficial Word Reshuffling:** Shifting word order without changing the underlying grammatical structure. This also fails to score.

* **Writing Only Part of the Answer:** Providing just the altered clause rather than the complete transformed sentence.

* **The One-Mark Trap:** Changing one required element but missing the other. In positive-to-negative switches, candidates might change সর্বদা (always) to কখনও (ever) but leave the verb positive, dropping the second mark.

* **Pronoun mismatches:** Keeping আমি instead of converting it to তুমি or আপনি, or choosing the wrong level of formality.

* **Unchanged time references:** Leaving time phrases as written in the original, for instance failing to change গতকাল to আগেরদিন.

* **Tense and punctuation errors:** Dropping quotation marks without adjusting the verb tense of the reported clause, or omitting required final punctuation.

* **Flawed Exclamatory Structures:** Producing a negative or interrogative sentence when the prompt required an exclamatory one scores zero. Using কী alone without the necessary marker (হে or ধিক্কার) scores 1 mark rather than 2.

* **Reversing the Meaning:** Accidentally flipping the core meaning when attempting rhetorical transformations, usually by misapplying the question tag না.

* **Spelling Errors in Key Words:** Each question is worth 2 marks. A spelling mistake in the transformed word is a disproportionately expensive error.

### **What Candidates Who Score Well Actually Do**

* **Identify the transformation type before writing anything.** The cue word or structure at the start or end of each question tells you exactly what is being asked. Read it first, decide what category of transformation it is, then write.

* **In negative/positive transformations, the adverb and the verb must both change.** সর্বদা becomes কখনও and the verb takes না. Changing one without the other is exactly how candidates end up with 1 mark instead of 2.

* **Test rhetorical questions by reading them back.** If the transformed sentence means the opposite of the original, না has been misapplied. The rhetorical form should preserve the original meaning, not flip it.

* **For exclamatory sentences, কী is never enough on its own.** The sentence needs the full structural pair. Read the finished sentence aloud. If it does not sound like an exclamation, it is probably still a question or a statement.

* **Write the whole sentence, even if only one word changed.** The rubric requires the complete sentence. Writing only the transformed part will not receive full marks regardless of how accurate the change is.
