## Exam Strategies

### The 5-Question Breakdown

Sentence transformation questions always total five.

* **Two structural questions:** These involve switching between Simple, Complex, and Compound structures. So expect at least one of each major structural type to appear.

* **One meaning-based question:** This will ask you to convert between Interrogative and Exclamatory forms.

* **One polarity question:** This will ask you to move between Affirmative and Negative.

* **The final question:** This is always a narration change, typically from Direct Speech to Indirect Speech.

### How to Transform

* **Step 1**: Before writing anything, identify what type the given sentence already is.

  * **For structural sentences:** Look for the clearest hint: a single finite verb (সমাপিকা ক্রিয়া) means Simple; a conjunction like তাই, কিন্তু, or এবং joining two independent clauses means Compound; a pair word like যে–সে or যখন–তখন means Complex.

  * **For meaning-based sentences:** Look for the presence or absence of a question mark, an exclamation, or a negative word.

* **Step 2**: Once you know what you have, use the answer space to figure out what you need. Sometimes the question gives you the first word of the answer; sometimes it gives the last word or the closing punctuation mark. Either way, that hint tells you exactly which format to target, so read it before you start writing.

* **Step 3**: When transforming, change as few words as possible. Your only job is to alter the grammatical structure while leaving the meaning and most of the original vocabulary untouched. Do not rephrase or rewrite from scratch. Do not leave out any of the main words from the original sentence by mistake.
