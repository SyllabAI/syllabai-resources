# Section A: Sentence Transformation

## Introduction

This section covers questions 11 to 15. This segment tests your ability to convert sentences from one grammatical form to another. Worth a total of 10 marks, it has 5 questions worth 2 marks each. Earning full marks requires you to have a solid grasp of sentence mechanics and most importantly routine practice.

A **sentence (বাক্য)** is a word or a combination of words that expresses a complete thought or idea from the speaker. To master sentence transformation, you must understand the two primary ways sentences are classified:

* **Classification by Meaning (অর্থগত শ্রেণিবিভাগ):** Categorizing sentences based on the intent of the message (e.g., statements, questions, commands, or exclamations).

* **Classification by Structure (গঠনগত শ্রেণিবিভাগ):** Categorizing sentences based on how their clauses are built and connected (e.g., simple, complex, or compound).
