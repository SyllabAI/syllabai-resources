## How to Solve

* **Understanding the Content:** To correctly fill in the blanks, students must understand and learn the meanings of the idioms, proverbs, and words in pairs provided.

* **Elimination Method:** If the meaning of an idiom, proverb, or word pair is unknown, you can use the elimination method. Try inserting the options with known meanings into the blanks and check whether the sentence makes sense with each option.

## Key Points Before Practice

1. **Review the Options:** Whether you know the meaning or not, read the provided options carefully once or twice.

2. **Scan the Passage:** Read the entire passage with the blanks attentively and quickly to grasp the overall context.

3. **Prioritize by Confidence:** Fill in the blanks that you are most confident about first, followed by the moderately certain ones. Finally, attempt the completely unfamiliar ones.

4. **Iterate and Analyze:** Remember, repeated attempts at analyzing the remaining blanks against the leftover options will often reveal the correct answers easily.
