# Section B: Multiple Choice Comprehension

## Introduction

This section covers questions 26 to 32. Seven multiple-choice questions worth 2 marks each, for a total of 14 marks. You tick the correct answer from the options given, based on the comprehension passage in the insert. There are no partial marks in this section. Every question is either 2 or 0. Never put two answers for the same question. If you make two attempts and one is wrong, the wrong answer cancels the correct one and you score 0.

## **Passage Structure**

The passage usually has 5-6 paragraphs, all on one topic, with each paragraph shifting focus slightly. As a general rule: one paragraph, one answer. Two exceptions to know: the final paragraph sometimes holds answers to 2 questions, and occasionally a middle paragraph (often the third) does too. So stay alert as you approach the end of the passage.
