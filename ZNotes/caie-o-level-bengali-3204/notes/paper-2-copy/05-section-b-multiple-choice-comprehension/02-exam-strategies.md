## Exam Strategies

* **Read the questions first:** Before touching the passage, read through all the questions. Right before you begin reading the passage itself, re-read the first question so you can keep it in mind.

* **Skim for keywords:** You do not need to deeply understand the entire passage. Instead, focus on finding clues or keywords that match the questions and answer choices.

* **Follow the paragraph order:** Questions generally follow the same chronological order as the paragraphs. You will typically find one answer per paragraph, though the final paragraph may sometimes contain two.

* **Answer as you read:** The moment you spot an answer, stop, mark it, and move on. Do not save the questions for the end, as hunting through the entire passage later wastes valuable time.

* **Use the process of elimination:** Cross out the clearly wrong options first. Read the remaining choices carefully, as a small difference in wording is often the deciding factor.

* **Evaluate all options:** Read all four answer choices before committing to one. Do not jump at the first answer that simply looks correct.

* **Stick strictly to the text:** Base your answers solely on the passage. Your general knowledge does not count here, even if you are highly familiar with the topic.

* **Re-read when stuck:** If a question is not clicking, go back and re-read that specific paragraph before making a guess.
