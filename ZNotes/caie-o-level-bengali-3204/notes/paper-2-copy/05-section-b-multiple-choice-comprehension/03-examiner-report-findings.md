## Examiner Report Findings

### Mistakes That Come Up Every Year

* **Superficial and Fast Reading (The Main Mistake):** The single biggest cause of lost marks across almost all reports is reading too quickly. The multiple-choice options are intentionally designed with "almost look-alike" sentences that easily trick candidates who read loosely.

* **Relying on Memory or Plausibility:** Selecting an option simply because it "sounds plausible" from memory or general impression, rather than verifying the choice directly against the passage.

* **Falling for Distractors:** Large numbers of candidates regularly fall for distractors (a usually plausible but incorrect answer). For example, choosing option A or B when the correct answer is actually D.

### What Candidates Who Score Well Actually Do

* **Read the Entire Passage First:** They read the full text thoroughly before looking at the questions, rather than trying to skim the passage in fragments while answering.

* **Read All Options with Absolute Precision:** They examine every single question and all available choices with extreme care, actively analyzing the subtle differences in wording or meaning to spot the traps.

* **Verify Directly Against the Text:** They physically return to the relevant section of the passage to cross-reference and verify the option they are considering before committing to an answer.

* **Execute a Process of Elimination:** When unsure, they systematically rule out the options they know are wrong one by one, rather than guessing blindly among the remaining choices.
