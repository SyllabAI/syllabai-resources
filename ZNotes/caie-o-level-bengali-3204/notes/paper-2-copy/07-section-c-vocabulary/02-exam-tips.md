## Exam Tips

Do not answer vocabulary questions first. By the time you have read the passage several times for comprehension, the underlined words make much more sense. Save these questions for last and you will find many of them have already answered themselves. If you are stuck on a word, work through this in order:

* First, read the full sentence several times and try to understand what the sentence is saying as a whole.

* Second, figure out what all the other words mean and use that to narrow down what the remaining word could be.

* Third, if you still cannot get it, translate the whole sentence into English in your head, find the English word, and reconstruct the Bengali from that. And if none of that works, write something anyway. A close guess can earn partial credit. A blank earns nothing.
