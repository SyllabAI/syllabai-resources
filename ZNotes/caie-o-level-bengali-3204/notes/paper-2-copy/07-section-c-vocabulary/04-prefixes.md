## Prefixes

In Bengali, **উপসর্গ** (prefixes) are essential particles that precede root words to create new meanings. They act as modifiers that transform a word's nuance, intensity, or logical state. Understanding these allows you to derive and understand the meanings of diverse vocabulary from single roots.

### 1. Negating Prefixes (অভাব, নিষেধ বা বিয়োগ)

These prefixes indicate absence, lack, or the opposite of the root word.

অবাধ (unrestricted), অজান (unknown), অনাবৃত (uncovered)

নির- / নি-

|

Without / Free from

|

নিরাপদ (safe), নিরলস (tireless), নিঃশেষ (exhausted)

||
||

বি-

|

Separation / Opposite

|

বিচ্ছিন্ন (separated), বিরল (rare), বিকল (broken)

|#

### 2. Intensifying Prefixes (প্রকাশ বা উৎকর্ষ)

These prefixes amplify, emphasize, or complete the meaning of the root word.

#|
||

**Prefix**

|

**Meaning**

|

**Examples**

||
||

প্র-

|

Forward / Fully / Intense

|

প্রচণ্ড (fierce), প্রসার (expansion), প্রখর (sharp/intense)

||
||

সু-

|

Good / Very / Excellent

|

সুদৃশ্য (beautiful), সুশীল (well-behaved), সুনিপুণ (very skilled)

||
||

সম-

|

Equal / Together / Complete

|

সমতা (equality), সমগ্র (entire), সমকক্ষ (equal-level)

||
||

আ-

|

Up to / Entirely

|

আশৈশব (from childhood), আপাদমস্তক (head to toe), আকণ্ঠ (up to the throat)

||
|#
