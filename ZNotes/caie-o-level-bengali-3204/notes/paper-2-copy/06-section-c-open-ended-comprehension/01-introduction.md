# Section C: Open Ended Comprehension

## Introduction

This section covers questions 33 to 38. Six questions, each worth 6 marks, totaling 36. The marks split as 4 for content and 2 for language per question. The passage is provided in a separate insert. Write your answers on the question paper in a clean handwriting.

## Passage Overview

The passage typically has 6 paragraphs, and each question usually corresponds to one paragraph in order. If the passage has only 5 paragraphs, two questions may draw from the same paragraph. The passages tend to focus on a single theme throughout, such as a travel experience, a place, a cultural tradition, or a notable figure, with each paragraph covering a slightly different angle of that same topic.

## **Understanding the marks**

Content and language are scored independently.

Content (4 marks): 1 mark per correct point, up to 4. No partial credit within a single point.

Language (2 marks):

* 2/2: your own words, clear meaning, minor spelling errors tolerated

* 1/2: meaning comes through but with spelling errors or occasional lifting

* 0/2: confused meaning, limited vocabulary, or heavy copying from the passage

These two scores do not affect each other. A partially complete answer that is genuinely rephrased can still get 2/2 on language. Copying all four points word-for-word gets 4/4 on the content but gets 0/2 on language, no matter how correct the content is.
