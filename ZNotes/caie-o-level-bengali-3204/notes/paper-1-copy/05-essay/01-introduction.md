# Essay
## Introduction

The essay section is the second part of Paper 1. In this section, three different essay topics are provided, and students must choose one and write an essay within 200 words. The three topics will cover different types of essays:

-  **Personal Essay:** These essays are based on personal experiences or memories, such as "A Boat Trip" (নৌকা ভ্রমণ) or "The First Day of School" (স্কুলের প্রথম দিন).

- **Topic-Based Essay:** These essays focus on specific topics that require factual information or descriptions, such as "Mobile Phones" (মোবাইল ফোন) or "Social Media" (সামাজিক যোগাযোগ মাধ্যম).

- **Opinion-Based Essay:** These essays require students to present their views on a topic, discussing its positive or negative aspects, such as the advantages or disadvantages of a certain issue (ইতিবাচক বা নেতিবাচক দিক)
 
 
Students should carefully select the essay type that best suits their knowledge and writing style.
