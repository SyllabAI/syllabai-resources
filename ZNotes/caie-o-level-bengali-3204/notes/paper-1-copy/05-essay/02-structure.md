## Structure

An essay generally follows a three-part structure:
- ### Introduction:
  Start with a brief introduction that introduces the topic to the reader. The introduction should be engaging and provide a clear idea of what the essay will cover.

- ### Body:
  This is the main part of the essay, where you describe three key points related to the topic. Use clear, precise, and beautiful language to express your ideas. Each point should be developed in a separate paragraph, providing details, examples, or arguments to support the point.

- ### Conclusion:
  End the essay with a concise summary of the main points discussed in the body. Include a closing statement that reflects on the overall topic, indicating whether it is beneficial or harmful, or outlining its advantages and disadvantages. This helps to provide a thoughtful ending to the essay.
