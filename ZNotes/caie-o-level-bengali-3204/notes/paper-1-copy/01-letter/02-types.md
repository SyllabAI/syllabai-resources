## Types of Letters:

* Personal Letter– A personal letter is an informal message sent to family, friends, or acquaintances. Examples include birthday wishes, expressions of gratitude, etc.
* Official Letter– An official letter is used for formal communication within institutions or organizations. Examples include job applications, circulars, etc.
* Business Letter– A business letter is used for various business transactions. Examples include orders, invoices, customer correspondence, etc.
* Formal Letter– A formal letter is used for social or cultural occasions. Examples include invitation letters, congratulatory letters, etc.
