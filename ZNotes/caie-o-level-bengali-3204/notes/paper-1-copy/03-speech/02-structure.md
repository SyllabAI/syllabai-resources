## Structure

- ### Introduction:
Clearly state the main topic of the speech in one or two sentences. This sets the context and prepares the audience for what is to come.

- ### Body:
Address the 4 bullet points given in the question, which will cover the key aspects of the speech. Each point should be clearly written in simple and precise language. Ensure that each point logically flows into the next.

- ### Conclusion:
Summarize the main points of your speech in a concise manner. Highlight any positive takeaways or calls to action.
