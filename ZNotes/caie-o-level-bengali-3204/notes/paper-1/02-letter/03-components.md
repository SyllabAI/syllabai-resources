## Components of a Letter:

* **Letterhead:** The heading of a letter includes the sender's details and is often called the letterhead. It is important to note that often, you are instructed not to write the address. Be sure to check the instructions carefully.
* **Salutation:** The beginning of the letter greets the recipient. In a personal letter, this is "Dear(প্রিয়)" , while in an official letter, it is "Respected(শ্রদ্ধেয়)"।
* **Main Body:** The main body of the letter contains the message or content you wish to convey. It should be clear and to the point, with careful attention to both clarity and conciseness.
  * The four bullet points provided in the question should be written in four different paragraphs.
* **Closing:** The closing section of the letter generally ends with a polite expression, such as "With love," "Sincerely," or "Thank you." Common closings include "Yours faithfully(আপনার বিশ্বস্ত")" or "Best regards(শুভেচ্ছান্তে)."
* **Signature**: At the end of the letter, sign your name to complete the letter.
