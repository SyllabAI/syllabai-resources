# Introduction

Paper 1 is divided into two sections, with a total of 90 marks:

## Section One

It contains 30 marks and offers a choice between two tasks from the following options:

* Letter writing
* Dialogue
* Speech
* Report

  You need to select and complete only one of the two given tasks within 120 words. Although previous years have frequently featured letter writing paired with another option, it is advisable to be familiar with the formats of all four types. Among the 30 marks, 12 marks is for addressing the bullet points, other 18 marks is for neat and clean handwriting, proper use of words and phrases, little or no spelling mistakes.
