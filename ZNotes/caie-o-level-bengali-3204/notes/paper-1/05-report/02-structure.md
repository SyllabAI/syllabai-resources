## Structure

- ### Heading:
  Begin the report with a heading. It should be related to the topic, an incomplete sentence, and preferably within 6 words.

- ### Bullet Points:
  Generally, 4 bullet points will be provided in the question. The report should address these 4 bullet points in 4 separate paragraphs. Each bullet point will have 3 sub-points, each carrying 1 mark. Therefore, properly addressing these points will earn a total of 12 marks (4*3).
- ### End with:
1. প্রতিবেদকের নাম ও ঠিকানাঃ(Reporter's Name and Address: )
2. প্রতিবেদনের সময় ও তারিখঃ( eg:সকাল ১০ টা, ১৯ আগস্ট, ২০২৪।)(Report Time and Date: eg.10 AM, 19th August 2024)

- ### Language and Presentation:
  The remaining 18 marks are based on the proper use of language, neat 
  handwriting, and the use of beautiful and in-depth phrases.
1. Avoid unrelated information and address the points directly.
2. Keep the content as concise as possible. 

- ### Word Limit:
  The report should be within 100 to 170 words. Aim to complete the report within 120 words and make sure not to exceed the word limit.
