## Difficulty & Approach

There is no specific rule or trick to answering these questions. The key is to select the correct word that best completes the sentence, ensuring that the entire passage makes sense.

The passage usually revolves around one central topic, such as a forest, music, tradition, or place. To solve the passage, it is most suitable to use the **elimination method**—filling in the blanks with potential answers and checking if the sentence, and ultimately the passage, makes sense.
