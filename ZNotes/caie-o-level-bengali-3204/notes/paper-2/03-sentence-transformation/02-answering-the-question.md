## Answering the Question:
### Sentence Structure: 
A sentence will be provided, followed by a large blank space where you need to write your transformed sentence.
### Keywords:
One or two words or symbols (like a question mark or exclamation mark) will be given underneath the blank space. These indicate the required transformation.

### Meaning Consistency: 
Ensure that the meaning of the sentence remains unchanged after transformation.
### Multiple Answers: 
There may be more than one correct answer as long as the transformation reflects the required structure and the meaning is preserved.
