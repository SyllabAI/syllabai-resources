## Approach When Unsure:

If you do not know the meaning of a word, follow these steps:


1. **Re-read the Sentence**: Read the sentence repeatedly to grasp the overall meaning of the sentence.
2. **Infer the Meaning**: Try to understand the implicit meaning of the sentence, which can give you a clue about the meaning of the underlined word.
3. **Use Elimination**: Identify the meanings of the other words in the sentence, eliminate those meanings, and focus on what the remaining word could mean.
4. **Write a Close Guess**: Write down the meaning as closely as possible to the word's context. Although this method does not guarantee full marks, if your guess is close to the actual meaning, you might receive partial credit.
