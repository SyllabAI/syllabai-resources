## Answering Strategy:


1. **Address the question first**: Begin by stating the writer's overall experience.
2. **Provide the four points**: Paraphrase the key points from the passage, ensuring not to copy them directly.
3. **End with a conclusion**: Summarize or conclude with a beautiful, concise sentence reflecting the writer's feelings or overall experience.

For example, answering the question, *"What experience did the writer get from his travels abroad?"* might look like this: *"The writer had an amazing experience while traveling abroad. He got to know different cultures... (provide the four points). From his tone, it is clear that he truly enjoyed the trip and wished he could stay a few more days."*
