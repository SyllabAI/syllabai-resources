## Format

The comprehension provided typically consists of 6 paragraphs. Often, each of the 6 questions corresponds to a different paragraph. However, in cases where the passage has 5 paragraphs, two questions may be derived from the same paragraph.

The passages generally revolve around a singular theme such as someone's travel experience, music, food, or a famous poet. Each paragraph, while focused on the same topic, varies slightly in content.

Questions typically require four points based on the passage. For example: "What experience did the writer get from his travels abroad? Provide four points." 




Marks are awarded as follows:

4 marks for providing four relevant points.
2 marks for effective word usage and paraphrasing.
