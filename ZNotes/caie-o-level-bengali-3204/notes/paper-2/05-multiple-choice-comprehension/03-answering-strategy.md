## **Answering Strategy:**


1. **Understand the Structure:** The questions generally follow the order of the paragraphs. This makes it easier to locate the answers within the passage.
2. **Read the Questions First:** Carefully read each question to identify which paragraph it refers to. This will help you focus on the relevant section of the passage.
3. **Eliminate Wrong Answers:** Start by eliminating the obviously incorrect options. This narrows down your choices and helps you focus on the potential correct answers.
4. **Identify Keywords:** Look for keywords in the question and in the passage. These will help you match the correct answer to the corresponding part of the text.
5. **Be Aware of Distractions:** Some options may seem similar or equally correct, but subtle differences in the passage can reveal the right answer. Pay close attention to context clues, specific phrases, and hints.
6. **Read All Options Before Deciding:** Never choose an answer without reading all the options. This ensures that you don’t miss the best possible answer.
7. **Re-read When Necessary:** If in doubt, re-read the relevant paragraph to clarify your understanding before selecting an answer.
8. **Stay Calm and Focused:** Maintain your focus and take your time. Answer based on the passage, not on your general knowledge, to ensure accuracy.
