## Differing Views and Tensions within Socialism

### **Revolutionary Socialism (Marxism / Communism)**

* Believe that a revolution is inevitable, where the working class will rise against the middle class
* This is because industrialisation and capitalism produced poverty and suffering for them
* The state is responsible for this and should be overthrown
* Only via a revolution can current structures be overthrown, resulting in a classless and stateless society
* Leninism: faithful to the idea of a revolution, but argues in order for this to happen, we need a  ‘vanguard party’ to help raise ‘revolutionary consciousness’ amongst the working class - make them aware of the need for a revolution
  * Marx thinks this is unnecessary- the working class will naturally know
* Marxism today = Neo-Marxism
  * the inevitable collapse of capitalism turns out to be false
  * Marxism today believes that capitalism can be improved through democracy

### **Social Democracy**

* Believes in using the state to reform capitalism by:
  * nationalisation
  * regulation and legal protection
  * welfare
  * redistribution of wealth from the rich to the poor to achieve social justice
  * democratically
  * mixed economy- state and private ownership
  * economic management
* MANAGE instead of replace capitalism
* Aim to narrow inequality instead of achieving absolute equality

### **The Third Way (Neo-revisionism)**

* Globalisation of the market economy
* communitarianism- citizens have duties (to help those in need) as well as rights
* Equality of opportunity
* Welfare focusing on the most marginalised groups only (instead of universal redistribution)
* The state should invest in education and prepare people for competition in the globalised world
