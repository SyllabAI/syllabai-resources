# Core Political Ideas - 3 Socialism

## Socialism: Core Ideas and Principles

1\. **Collectivism**

* Society > individuals
* Corporation instead of competition
* Human nature is good and rational
* Humans are inseparable from their community




2\. **Common humanity**

* Humans work best together to achieve common goals
* Humans only become selfish due to capitalism, not due to our nature
* ‘no man is an island entire of itself, every man is a piece of the continent’- John Donne




3\. **Equality**
* Inequality is often due to unequal treatment in society, not due to innate differences in ability
* Inequality leads to conflict
* Different views about equality:
  * **Marx**: equal rights / equal opportunities are not enough; we need absolute equality & equality of outcome- i.e. equal resources and wealth- by abolishing private property
  * **Social democrats**: equality of outcome & relative equality through redistribution and welfare
  * Third way: moved away from equality of outcome, relative equality should still be achieved by welfare




4\. **Social class**

* People in the same class share similar economic and social positions- class is the principal agent of change
* Socialism = focus on the working class
  * Marx argues the bourgeoisie (middle class) & proletariat (working class) will always be in conflict- we need to create a classless society through revolution
  * Social democrats reject the idea of an inevitable revolution- they believe in creating a fairer society through redistribution and welfare
  * Third-way supporters do not use the term ‘class’ anymore due to class dealignment in recent decades - instead, they use the term ‘communities.’’


5\. **Workers’ control**

* Private property is unjust and morally corrupts people
* We need common ownership
  * Marx: private property to be fully abolished
  * Social democrats: the state to intervene in the economy to make it fairer- a mixed economy of publicly and privately owned industries
  * Third way: does not use the language of ‘workers’ control’, accepts private ownership
