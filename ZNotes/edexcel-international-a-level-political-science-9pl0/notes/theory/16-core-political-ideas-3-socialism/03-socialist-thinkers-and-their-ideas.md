## Socialist Thinkers and their Ideas




### **Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels**

* Class is the main driver for historical change
  * the interaction between competing forces (working class vs middle class)
  * Capitalism leads to alienation and oppression of the working class
  * The working class will rise up to overthrow capitalism spontaneously through a revolution
  * Ultimate goal = classless and stateless society (i.e. communism)




### **Rosa Luxemburg**

* Agrees that there is a need for revolution
* The struggle faced by the working class will automatically create the class consciousness necessary for the revolution to take place (criticised Lenin’s idea of the need for a vanguard party)




### **Beatrice Webb**

* Rejects the idea of a revolution
  * In the late 19th century, wages and living standards improved, working-class men were allowed to vote
* Believes that changes can take place through democratic means gradually (‘the inevitability of gradualness’)
* State intervention to reduce inequality and promote education




### **Anthony Crossland**
* Manage capitalism instead of overthrowing it
* Redistribution of wealth to achieve social justice
* Government spending on welfare
* Managed and mixed economy (public + private) to support the poorest




### **Anthony Giddens**

* Equality of opportunity instead of equality of outcome
* Welfare to focus on the marginalised only instead of universal redistribution
* Emphasis on the importance of education
* Individual responsibility to help others
