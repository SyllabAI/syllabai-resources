## The aims, role And impact of the European Union (EU) on the UK government




### The aims of the EU, including the ‘four freedoms’ of the single market, political and economic union, and the extent to which these have been achieved




### Aims

* Promote peace
* Reduce borders
* Provide employment opportunities
* Reduce discrimination
* Environmental protection
* Promote diversity
* Monetary union- EURO

### The four freedoms

* Free movement of workers- right to live and work in any EU member state
* Free movement of goods- removal of custom duties and trade tariffs
* Free movement of capital- payments to move freely between member states
* Free movement of services- allows EU companies to set up in other EU countries

### Impact of the EU on the UK

* Sovereignty - UK parliament vs EU laws
* Public opinion - 2016 referendum
* International trade
* Immigration policies
* Parties - different views on leaving the EU
