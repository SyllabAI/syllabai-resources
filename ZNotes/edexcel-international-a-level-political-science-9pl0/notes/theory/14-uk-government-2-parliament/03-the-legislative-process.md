## The legislative process




### Stages


1. Commons first reading- introduce the bill
2. Commons second reading- debate and vote
3. Commons committee stage- scrutinise, amend, and vote
4. Commons report stage and third reading- amend, debate and vote on final bill
5. Lords first reading- introduce the bill
6. Lords second reading- debate and vote
7. Lords committee stage- scrutinise, amend, and vote
8. Lords report stage and third reading- amend, debate and vote on final bill
   * if no changes are made, the bill will be signed by the monarch and becomes law (royal assent)
   * if changes are made, the bill will go back to the commons for further debate (accept / reject changes)
     * a bill can go back and forth between the two houses for one year (ping-pong) before it becomes law
