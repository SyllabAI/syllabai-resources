4\. Opposition

Their success in scrutinising the government depends on:

* How united the government (and the opposition) is
* Public support
* Media support
* Events (e.g. covid)
* Government majority?

But opposition parties can also be pre-occupied with election campaigns, instead of effectively scrutinising the government
