## The influence of the media

### Broadcasting

* Required by the law to remain neutral and balance different views in the UK
* Parties use TV and radio as a way to spread messages across

### The press

* Highly politicised and biased
* Fairly close correlation between who a newspaper supports and the voting preference of its readers
* This leads to the debate as to whether the newspaper affects its readers’ opinions, or simply reflects their opinions

### Social media

* Especially useful for small parties
* More commonly used amongst young people
* Unregulated- so there is risk of fake news
* There is also the problem of ‘seeing only what you want to see’, rather than the full picture
