### The role and significance of the global civil society and non-state actors, including non-governmental organisations (NGOs) in addressing and resolving the issues above

* There are over 3,380 registered NGOs, working with the UN
* They have become the key channel to tackling humanitarian crises, and making sure marginalised voices are heard
* However, they are funded and therefore still dominated by powerful and rich countries in the north
