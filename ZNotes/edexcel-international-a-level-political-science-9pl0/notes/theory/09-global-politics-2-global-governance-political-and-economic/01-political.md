## Political

### The United Nations (UN)

**Origins and development of the UN**

* Established by 51 countries in 1945
* Now expanded to 193 countries
* 1945 charter- outlines the key purposes and principles of the UN:
* Purposes: maintain peace and security, maintain friendly relations between nations, international cooperation in solving global problems, act as a centre for coordinating actions of nations in the attainment of achieving those common ends
* Principles: sovereign equality between member states, the UN should not intervene in domestic matters of any states

**The UN Security Council (UNSC)**

* 15 members- 5 permanent (France, Russia, China, USA, UK), 10 non-permanent members (elected for a two-year term)
* Role: to maintain peace
* When there is a threat to peace, the council considers ways to settle the dispute
* Resolutions must be passed by a majority of nine, and must include the 5 permanent members
* Each member has one vote
* Decisions are binding on all UN member states

**The UN General Assembly**

* Made up of all UN member states
* Acts as a ‘parliament of all nations’
* Each member state has a vote- two-third majority required to reach a decision
* Decisions are NOT binding

**The UN Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)**

* Responsible for coordinating the economic, social and environmental work of the UN
* The World Bank, IMF, WTO, WHO are all under the ECOSOC
* Also includes the UN Development Programme- emphasise on the less developed countries
* However, it lacks enforcement powers

**The International Court of Justice (ICJ)**

* The judicial body of the UN
* Deals with disputes between states

### North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO)

**Origins and role of NATO**

* Formed in 1949
* 12 member states
* An alliance of countries from Europe and North America
* Role = guarantee freedom and security, peacekeeping and humanitarian intervention
* Decisions need to be agreed by ALL member states
