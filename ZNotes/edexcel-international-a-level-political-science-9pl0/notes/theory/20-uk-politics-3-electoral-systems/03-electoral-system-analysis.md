## Electoral System Analysis

### Benefits of using a proportional system:

* Makes it fairer for minor parties
* Makes it more likely that the number of seats won reflects the % of votes won
* Makes coalitions more common, and majority governments rarer

### Weaknesses of using a proportional system:

* Coalition governments make it more difficult for the government to act and take actions
* Using a proportional system does not necessarily increase voter turnout- in fact, the complexity of PR systems increases the chances of spoiled ballots as it becomes easier for voters to make mistakes
