## Differing views and tensions within conservatism

### Traditional Conservatism

* Origin: 18th century- reaction against the French revolution and the Enlightenment
* Key beliefs:
  * Monarchy to preserve order
  * Revolution would lead to chaos
  * Preserve tradition- keep society stable
  * Hierarchal society and paternalism
  * Pragmatic change is welcomed, but not ideological change
* Key thinkers: Hobbes, Burke

### One Nation Conservatism

* Origin: a pragmatic updating of traditional conservatism in response to fears during the industrial revolution (the dangers of inequality)
* Key beliefs:
  * Reject liberal ideas that promote individualism- risk of forming ‘two nations: the rich and the poor’
  * Emphasises ONE nation- where the rich has an obligation to take care of the poor (also known as ‘noblesse oblige’)
  * Preserve tradition to prevent division
  * ‘Middle way’ between unrestrained liberalism (free market) and extensive state control
* Key thinker: Oakeshott

### The New Right

* Origin: 1970s- interventionist government policies failed to solve problems
* Divided into 2 strands: neoconservatism and neoliberalism
* Key thinkers: Rand, Nozick

### Neoliberalism

* Key beliefs:
  * ‘rolling back of the state’
  * Free market
  * Privatisation
  * Atomistic individualism
  * Individual freedom- humans are self-interested but rational and should be allowed to make their own decisions
  * Thatcher: ‘there is no such thing as society’

### Neoconservatism

* Key beliefs:
  * ‘limited but strong state’- limit state intervention in economic areas, but tough law and order to maintain security and social order
  * Strengthen national identity and common culture
