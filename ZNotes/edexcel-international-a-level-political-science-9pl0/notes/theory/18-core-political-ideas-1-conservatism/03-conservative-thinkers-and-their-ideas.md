## Conservative thinkers and their ideas




### Thomas Hobbes

* Human nature = selfish and irrational
* ‘State of nature’ = ‘all against all’- life would be ‘nasty, brutish and short’
* As a result, a strong authoritarian government is needed to maintain law and order
* Social contract: citizens consent to obey the government, in exchange they get to live a peaceful life




### Edmund Burke

* Gradual change: changes are allowed, but has to be done gradually (‘change to conserve’)
* Rejects radical / ideological changes
* Noblesse oblige- paternalism
* Tradition promotes social continuity and stability




### Michael Oakeshott

* Like Burke, believes in human imperfection and pragmatism- use experience (what works) to maintain stability, rather than abstract ideas




### Ayn Rand

* Atomistic society, individualism- ‘man exists for his own sake’
* Rejects human imperfection- men are able and should be allowed to make their own decisions
* Rejects welfare, state intervention
* Supports ‘rolling back of the state’, free market, minimal state intervention




### Robert Nozick

* Inspired by Kant: humans should be treated as an end and never as a means
* Humans have their own goals- state should not intervene with our freedom
* The only role of the state is just to protect human rights
* Rejects welfare and taxation
