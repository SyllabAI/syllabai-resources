## Main ideas of liberalism




### The significance of morality and optimism on human nature

* Humans are guided by reason
* Humans can live in peace, harmony and mutual respect- as a result- UNIVERSAL HUMAN RIGHTS
* War only as last resort




### Possibility of harmony and balance

* States are important, but so are non-state actors
* They help to deal with disputes and come up with negotiations 
* Democratic peace thesis- democratic states do not go to war with each other, because: 
  * government is constrained by public opinion
  * democracies are based on civil liberties 




### Complex interdependence

* Military power is not the only thing that matters, other issues such as human rights and the environment require and encourage cooperation
* Soft power > hard power




### Likelihood of global governance

* Rule of law, collective security through international organisations 
* States cooperate as it is in their interests to do so




### Impact and growth of international organisations

* Growth of international organisations as there are now more areas for states to work together
* They help to build bond and trust between states- making sure they all contribute and do not freeride 
* Avoid conflicts as cost is too high and gains too small
