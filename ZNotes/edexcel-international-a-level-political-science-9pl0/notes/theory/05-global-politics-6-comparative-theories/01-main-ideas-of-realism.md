# Global Politics - 6 Comparative Theories

## Main Ideas of Realism

### **States as Key Actors in Global Politics and the Balance of Power**

* States as key actors in global politics
* Rational for states to compete for power in order to survive
* State sovereignty- they always prioritise their own self-interests before the common interests
* Human nature - ‘restless desire of power after power’
* Leads to a war of all against all


### **International Anarchy and its Implications**

* The idea that there is no higher power to prevent the use of force, so states can only ensure their survival through self-help
* States must increase its power to provide for its own security 
* States aim to be stronger than others, so cooperation is unlikely
* States cannot rely on international organisations, since their decisions cannot be enforced


### **The Inevitability of War**

* Offensive realists: Great powers are motivated to acquire more power 
* Defensive realists: States are motivated to maximise security, not power - states are reluctant to go to war but will if necessary


### **The Security Dilemma**

* One state increases its military power to ensure its own security 
* This is seen as aggressive by others- so they increase their own
* Then the cycle keeps repeating… 
* Result = endless fear and tension


However, realists believe a balance of power can prevent conflict.

* Classical realists believe negotiations between state leaders can achieve this 
* Neo-realists believe balance is the result of international anarchy and the distribution of power globally
