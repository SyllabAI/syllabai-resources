Q1a

Examine the differences that exist between hyperglobalisers and globalisation sceptics.

* Hyperglobalisers are positive about globalisation- it leads to a global economy with the prospect of prosperity for all

* Hyperglobalisers believe that state sovereignty is becoming less relevant

* Globalisation sceptics believe that we are seeing regionalism, not globalisation

* Globalisation sceptics believe that states are still important- the power of international organisations depend on states

Q1b

Examine the different ways in which political and economic factors have led to regionalism.

* Economic regionalism brings great opportunities to states in that region- e.g. EU

* Development of NAFTA / AU- single market and currency

* Economic regionalism brings smaller states together to defend against larger states

* Regionalism as a way to protect common political values / interests
