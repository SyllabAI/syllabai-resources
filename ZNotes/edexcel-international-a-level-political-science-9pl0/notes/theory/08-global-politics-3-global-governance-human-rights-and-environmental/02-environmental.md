### The role and significance of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)

* The conference took place in 1992, attended by 172 countries, 108 heads of state, over 2,400 representatives of NGOs
* Established a framework convention- came into force in 1994
* Goal: reduce greenhouse gas
* States agreed to act in the common interest of protecting the environment
* HOWEVER- not legally binding

**Kyoto Protocol**

* The UNFCCC was negotiated and came into force in 2005, with scientific evidence and binding goals on developed countries
* Each developed state was set different targets based on their current emission levels, and an emission trading system was set up
* However, the targets set were not enough, and developing countries were not set binding targets
* The USA also failed to reach its target!

**The creation of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) and its role and significance**

* Set up in 1998
* Provides regular reports on:
  * the science of climate change
  * its impacts and future risks
