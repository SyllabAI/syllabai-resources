## Regionalism

### The different forms of regionalism

**Economic regionalism**

* States of a region coming together and cooperating to increase economic opportunities
* e.g. free trade, free movement of goods
* Protect against globalisation / integrate themselves into the global market

**Security regionalism**

* Protecting member states against enemies
* E.g. ASEAN- The Association of Southeast Asian Nations- created in 1967 to protect themselves against communism

**Political regionalism**

* To protect and enhance shared values / culture
* Aim to gain political influence in the global stage
