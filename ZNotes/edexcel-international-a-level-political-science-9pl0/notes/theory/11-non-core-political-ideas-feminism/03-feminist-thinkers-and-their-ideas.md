## Feminist thinkers and their ideas

### Charlotte Perkins Gilman

* Gender stereotyping from childhood should be avoided- boys and girls should play the same toys / wear the same clothes
* Financial independence is crucial for women - so they do not depend on their husbands

### Simone de Beauvoir

* ‘one is not born, but rather becomes, a woman’
* Otherness: men are the norm and women are the ‘other’ - this idea should be rejected

### Kate Millett

* The traditional family model should be rejected
* Art and literature that degrade women shows the impact of patriarchal culture- this needs to be changed

### Sheila Rowbotham

* The family becomes a place where men take refuge from alienation under a capitalist economy
* Capitalism is responsible for patriarchy

### Bell Hooks

* Intersectionality - mainstream feminism focuses too much on white women - ethnic minorities are largely ignored
