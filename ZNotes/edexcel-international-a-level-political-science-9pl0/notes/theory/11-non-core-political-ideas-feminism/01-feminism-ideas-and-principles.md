# Non-Core Political Ideas - Feminism

## Feminism: ideas and principles

1\. Sex and gender

  * Sex = biological (male / female)
  * Gender =  social and cultural
  * Feminists believe there is no justification for gender stereotypes / gender roles

2\. Patriarchy

  * In the state: women are underrepresented
  * In the household: traditional domestic role
  * In the workplace: often under male leaders
  * Violence: domestic abuse

3\. The personal is political

  * Feminists believe that ‘private matters’ such as distribution of labour in the household- are what keep women under men in society
  * These ‘private matters’ make women powerless and weak- therefore should not be private
  * They are in fact political

4\. Equality feminism and difference feminism

  * Equality feminism believes men and women are the same and deserve equal treatment- differences between them are due to culture / society, not due to nature
  * Difference feminism (aka ESSENTIALISM) believes men and women are fundamentally different - women should not try to act like men - but should be encouraged to be powerful in their own way

5\. Intersectionality

  * Feminism should not only consider white women
  * Women of ethnic minorities should also be considered!
