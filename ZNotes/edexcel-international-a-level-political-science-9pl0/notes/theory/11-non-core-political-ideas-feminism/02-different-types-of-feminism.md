## Different types of feminism

### Liberal Feminism

* Individualism: women should be independent and free
* Equality of opportunity: for men and women
* Legal and political equality: equality before the law, equal rights (including the right to vote)
* Make changes happen by changing the law
* Key thinker: Betty Friedan

### Socialist Feminism

* Believes that capitalism is responsible for patriarchy and subordinating women under men
* Women are oppressed in four ways: reproduction, sexuality, socialisation of children, production
* A revolution is required to remove capitalism and make women free
* Women should be financially independent
* Key thinker: Juliet Mitchell

### Radical Feminism

* The biggest problem facing society is gender equality and patriarchy
* Split into: equality feminism (see above), difference feminism (see above), and separatist feminism (believes that women should separate themselves from men entirely)
* Key thinker: Dworkin - studies the effect of pornography on gender relationships

### Post-modern Feminism

* Intersectionality
* It is impossible to generalise the experience of all women, because everyone’s experience is different
* Women should define what feminism means for themselves
* E.g. nudity - degrading or empowering?
