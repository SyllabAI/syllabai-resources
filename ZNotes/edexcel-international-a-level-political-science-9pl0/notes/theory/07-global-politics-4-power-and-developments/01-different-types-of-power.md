# Global Politics - 4 Power and Developments

## Different types of power 




### Power 

* ‘the ability to influence the behaviour of others to get the outcome one wants’
  * strength and capability - economic, military 
  * relationship with others
  * control to access / development 
* Hard power = coercive, military intervention, economic sanctions 
  * Could damage the credibility and standing of states which employ it
  * War too costly - economic sanctions are more effective
  * But only work on weaker and smaller states
* Soft power = ability to persuade others without force , but instead by culture, political values, moral authority, etc
  * Due to globalisation and interdependence, soft power is becoming increasingly common
  * Spread of democracy, education standards lead to more cooperation 
* Smart power = combination of hard and soft
