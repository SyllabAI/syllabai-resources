## Polarity

* Describes the number of significant states in the international system
* Unipolarity - only one (USA)
  * Can lead to peace because only one state has so much power, no one dare to attack it?
  * Or potential hegemons are constantly striving for more power- lead to instability?
* Bipolarity - two (USA and USSR in the past, USA and China now?)
  * Only one conflict relationship- others are nowhere near?
  * The two are so close that they don’t dare to fight each other- leads to balance and equality?
* Multipolarity - three or more
  * Many states with similar powers = increase chance of war?

### Changing nature of world power

* During Cold War - bipolar (USA vs USSR)
* After Cold War - unipolar (USA)
* In modern days, it could be argued that we are living in a multipolar world, due to the decline of the USA since the start of the 21st century, the rise of emerging powers, and globalisation and rising interdependence
  * Pessimistic view: this will lead to more conflict as every state wants to become stronger
  * Optimistic view: this will encourage peace and cooperation, as each state has similar power, no one dares to challenge others
