## Differing significance of states in global affairs and how and why state power is classified




* Great power = top, enough military power for own survival, significant economic power, ability to engage globally using both hard and soft power, foreign policies that have a global impact
  * USA, UK, France, China, India, Russia 
* Superpower = ability to achieve global objectives through soft power, pre-eminent military capability and economic powers within their own region 
  * USA and the West, USSR and the East
* Emerging powers = states emerging as powerful economies
  * Brazil, South Africa
