## Different systems of government




### Democratic states 

E.g. UK, USA 

* Civil liberties and rights 
* Capitalist economy 
* Free, fair, regular elections
* Interdependence and global governance




### Semi-democratic states 

E.g. Russia under Putin, states across Latin America 

* Elections may take place but unfair / involve fraud 
* Single-party dominance
* May or may not behave like democracies regarding global issues such as human rights 




### Non-democratic states 

* E.g. China
* Tend to be authoritarian and repress opposition 
* Usually reduce global stability and order  




### Autocratic states 

* E.g. North Korea 
* Single person with unlimited power
* Likely to violate international law and human rights 
* Weaken global order and stability 




### Failed states

E.g. South Sudan  

* Unable to make binding decisions 
* No real system of law and order 
* Often divisions and civil war
* Unstable - may trigger refugee crisis and humanitarian intervention 




### Rogue states 

* E.g. Iran, Iraq, North Korea


* Seen as a threat to others due to one of the following reasons: 
  * Aggressive foreign policies 
  * Weapons 
  * Supporting terrorism
