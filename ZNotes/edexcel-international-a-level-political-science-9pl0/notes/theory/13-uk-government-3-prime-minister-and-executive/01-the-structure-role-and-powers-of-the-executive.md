# UK Government - 3 Prime Minister and Executive

## The structure, role, and powers of the executive




### Structure

The executive is made up of:

* The Prime Minister
* Cabinet ministers - each heads a government department
* Junior ministers - work under cabinet ministers in specific departments




### Role

* Propose legislation to parliament
* Propose budget
* Run and rule the country
* Make formal decisions
* Coordinate government policies




### Power

* The executive has royal prerogative powers
* These are powers traditionally belonged to the monarch, but in modern days the executive can claim to have these powers without parliament’s approval, since they are elected by the people
* These powers include:
  * to call an election
  * to deploy armed forces
  * to grant pardons and honours
  * to appoint judges
  * to conduct foreign policies and make treaties
