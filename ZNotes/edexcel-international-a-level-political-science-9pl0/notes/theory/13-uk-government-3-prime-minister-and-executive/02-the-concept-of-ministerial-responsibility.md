## The concept of ministerial responsibility




### Individual ministerial responsibility

* Ministers are responsible for all that goes on within their department
* Ministers are expected to give accurate information to parliament
* Ministers are responsible for their own personal conduct and image




### Collective ministerial responsibility

* Government ministers are expected to agree on a policy and do not question it in public
* This ensures discussions inside the cabinet remain private and confidential
* This means when things go wrong, all ministers have collective responsibility for it
* It ensures an united front
