## Emerging and minor UK political parties


1. Scottish National Party (SNP)

* Scotland’s largest political party
* Centre left
* Aims for equality where no one gets left behind
* Stands for Scottish independence
  * 2014 referendum: 55% voted to remain in the UK, 45% voted for independence
* The party holds 63 out of 129 seats in the Scottish Parliament, and 43 out of 59 Scottish seats in the House of Commons currently


2. UK Independence Party (UKIP)

* The party most responsible for Brexit
* Had a huge impact on UK Politics, despite being a very small party
* Claims itself to be the ‘only truly patriotic’ party in the UK
* Stands for British culture, national democracy and liberalism


3. Democratic Unionist Party (DUP)

* A Northern Ireland party
* Signed a ‘confidence-and-supply’ agreement with the conservatives in 2017, when May failed to win a majority


4. Green Party

* Stands for building a greener, healthier, fairer country
* Holds one seat in Westminster Parliament currently


5. Plaid Cymru

* A center left Welsh nationalist party
* Stands for fairness and ambition
* Supports Welsh independence

### The importance of other parties in the UK

* Provides an alternative choice other than the ‘Big three’ - which forces the big parties to respond to the people and hold them accountable
* Despite being minor parties, some of them had huge impacts and consequences on the UK (e.g. SNP on Scottish independence, UKIP on Brexit)
