## How the constitution has changed since 1997




### Human Rights Act 1998

* Incorporates the rights set out in the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR) into UK law




### House of Lords Reform 1999

* reduces the number of hereditary peers in the House of Lords from several hundred to 92




### Freedom of Information Act 2000

* provides public access to documents held by the public authorities




### Fixed-term Parliaments Act 2011

* General elections to take place at least once every five years




### Other changes include:

* Devolution in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
* Introduction of proportional voting systems in devolved assemblies
* Police and crime commissioners: elected representatives responsible for the policing of an area
* English votes for English laws (EVEL): only MPs representing English constituencies have an input on laws that only affects England
