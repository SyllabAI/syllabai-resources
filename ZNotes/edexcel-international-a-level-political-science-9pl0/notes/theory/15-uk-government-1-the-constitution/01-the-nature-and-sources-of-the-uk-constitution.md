* The UK has an uncodified constitution
* Sources of the UK’s constitution include:
  * statute laws- laws passed by parliament (take precedence over all other sources)
  * long established conventions and practices (common law- equal status as statute laws but cannot contradict)
  * authoritative works- books written to help us understand the UK’s constitution (not binding but are seen as strong guidance)
  * EU treaties
* Some important constitutional documents include:
  * Magna Carta 1215- equality before the law
  * Bill of Rights 1689- free elections, freedom of speech
  * Act of Settlement 1701- guarantees a parliamentary system of government
  * Acts of Union 1707- Union of England and Scotland under the name of Great Britain
  * Parliament Act 1911- removes the right of the House of Lords to reject money bills, and for other public bills, it has the power to delay them for a maximum of two years
  * Parliament Act 1949- reduces the duration of delay to a maximum of one year
