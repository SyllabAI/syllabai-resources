## The role and powers of devolved bodies in the UK, and the impact of this devolution on the UK

The Scottish Parliament, The Welsh Assembly and the Northern Ireland Assembly have the following powers:

* Education
* Economic development
* The environment
* Housing
* Sports and culture
* Agriculture
* Policing and fire services
* Health and social services
* Employment
* Transport

Elected mayors in England have the following powers:

* Economic development
* Housing
* Employment
* Transport

Mayors have responsibilities and powers across the city regions
