# Global Politics - 1 The State and Globalisation

## The State: Nation-State and of National Sovereignty

### **Characteristics of a Nation-State and National Sovereignty**

* A state is a territorially based unit which has:
  * a stable population
  * a government
  * the ability to enter into relations with other states (in other words, recognised by other states)
  * a legally defined territory
* A nation is a group of people united together by common values/traditions
* National sovereignty is the idea that a state has absolute power within its territory
  * Power to make decisions (both internal and external)
  * Other states do not have the right to interfere
  * There is no power above the state
