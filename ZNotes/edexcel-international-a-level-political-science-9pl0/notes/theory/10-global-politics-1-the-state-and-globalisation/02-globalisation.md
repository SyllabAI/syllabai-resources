## Globalisation

* Web of interconnectedness between states
* Geographical distance, borders are becoming more and more irrelevant




### **The Process of Globalisation**




**Factors that lead to globalisation**

* technology: people can now communicate with each other, no matter which part of the world they are in
* culture: global brands, celebrities, media
* economics: the constant thirst for profit and people’s rational desire for a better life
* politics: democratic ideals
* migration
* role of international institutions- e.g. WTO, EU, etc




**Evidence of globalisation**

* Economical: growth in international trade and transnational production, globalised financial system
* Cultural: growth of Westernised global culture- consumerism, human rights, liberal democracy
* Political: growth of global governance and solving global issues- e.g. global warming + the emergence of international treaties/laws
* Humanitarian and forcible interventions- e.g. Libya 2011




### **Impact on the State System **

**Different Views on Globalisation**

* Hyperglobalisers  / liberals- positive view:
  * a single, global market will bring wealth to all
  * growing economic interdependence reduces the possibility of war
  * it helps people to understand each other better
* Sceptics/realists- argue the impact of globalisation has been exaggerated:
  * globalisation mainly benefits the global north, whereas the south is increasingly marginalised
  * states are still highly relevant- international organisations is a way for states to grow their power
* Transformationalists- globalisation neither strengthens or weakens the state- it simply transforms the state
