## Liberal thinkers and their ideas




### John Locke

* Classical liberal
* *Two Treatises of Government (1690)*
* Social contract: citizens obey the state, in return they are guaranteed certain rights
  * If the state breaks the contract, it should be opposed
  * Humans are rational and will not voluntary agree to an unjust state
* Limited government- by separating powers




### Mary Wollstonecraft

* *A Vindication of the Rights of Woman (1792)*
* Women, not just men, are rational and capable of reason
* Women should also enjoy full civil liberties, which was not the case in the 18th century, when they were not allowed to vote
* Formal education = vital




### John Stuart Mill

* *On Liberty (1859)*
* Harm principle: individuals should be free to do anything, unless the action harms others
* Tolerance: be prepared to accept ideas and values from others that you disagree with




### John Rawls

* Modern liberal
* *A Theory of Justice (1971)*
* Each citizen should be guaranteed a life worth living
* This requires the state to redistribute wealth and increase public spending to reduce inequality and improve the conditions for the poor
* Veil of ignorance: when people do not know their relative positions in society, they would prefer a fairer and more equal society




### Betty Friedan

* *The Feminine Mystique (1963)*
* Women and men should have equal opportunities
* This requires the state to criminalise discrimination
