# Core Political Ideas - 2 Liberalism

## Liberalism: core ideas and principles




1\. Individualism

* Individuals > society
* Egoistical individualism: the idea that humans are self-seeking and self-reliant
* Developmental individualism: the idea that every human can grow and achieve their potential
* Links to tolerance- be prepared to accept ideas from others that you may not agree with




2\. Freedom and liberty

* Individuals are capable of and should be allowed to make their own decisions freely
* This freedom, however, is UNDER THE LAW (in other words: not absolute)




3\. The state

* May be threatening to individual freedom
* Therefore- should be limited
* Its only role should be to prevent social disorder and exploitation of the disadvantaged
* Intervention in the economy should be minimum




4\. Rationalism

* Positive view of human nature- humans are guided by reason rather than emotion
* Individuals benefit from taking responsibility for  themselves, rather than relying on the state




5\. Equality and social justice

* The idea that humans are born equal and should be treated fairly
* Foundational equality: equal human rights
* Formal equality: equal political and legal rights- equality before the law
* Equality of opportunity: given the same chance to succeed / fail- differing outcomes are acceptable, as humans have different abilities and potential
* Modern liberals believe some state intervention is fine in order to allow individuals to fulfil their full potential




6\. Liberal democracy

* Free and fair elections
* Neutral state with limited powers
* Respect and tolerance
* Civil rights
