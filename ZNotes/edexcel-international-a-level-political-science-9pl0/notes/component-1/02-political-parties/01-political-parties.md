## **Political parties**

### **The functions of political parties**

* **Representation**: parties represent people’s views and act in their interests

* **Policy-making**: parties initiate and formulate policy proposals

* **Elections**: parties offer candidates for the public to choose from during election time

* **Govern**: the winning party forms a government

* **Education**: parties educate and inform the public about different policy areas

### **The features of political parties**

* Organisation: parties are organised with a formal membership structure

* Ideologies: parties have their own ideologies; members share the same preferences and formulate policies based on that

* Goal: all parties share a common goal- to win an election and form a government
