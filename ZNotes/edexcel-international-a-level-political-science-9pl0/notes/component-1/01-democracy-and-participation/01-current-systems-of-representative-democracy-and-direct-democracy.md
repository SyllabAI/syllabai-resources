# **Democracy and Participation**

## **Current systems of representative democracy and direct democracy**

### **What is democracy?**

* ‘Government of the people, by the people, for the people’ - Abraham Lincoln

* People choose who rules over them

* Those in power must rule in the interests of the people

### **2 types of democracy**

**Is the UK a direct democracy, or representative democracy?**

* The UK is a representative democracy

* Elections take place at least once every five years

### **How healthy is UK’s democracy?**

#|

Not very healthy

### **Consideration of the case for reform**

* Some have argued that the UK is facing a participation crisis- not a large enough proportion of the population is participating in politics

* Some have also said that the UK is facing a democratic deficit- representatives are elected with insufficient democratic input and insufficient accountability

Several ways have been suggested to improve UK’s democracy
