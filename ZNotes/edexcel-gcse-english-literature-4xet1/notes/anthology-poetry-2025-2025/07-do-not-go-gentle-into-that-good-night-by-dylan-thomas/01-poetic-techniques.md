# "Do Not Go Gentle Into That Good Night" by Dylan Thomas

## **Poetic Techniques**

* Villanelle form: 19-line poem with two refrains repeated throughout — gives the poem power and rhythm

* Refrain: “Do not go gentle into that good night” & “Rage, rage against the dying of the light” — emotional insistence builds urgency

* Symbolism:

  * “Good night” = death

  * “Light” = life, consciousness, vitality

* Contrast: different types of men all respond to death — wise, good, wild, grave

* Tone: urgent, passionate, defiant, grieving

## **Themes**

* Mortality and resistance

* The fight for life and meaning

* Grief and familial love

* Human struggle in the face of death

## **Context**

* Written for the poet’s dying father — a personal and emotional plea

* Dylan Thomas was a Welsh poet known for his lyrical and emotional poetry

* Written during the WWII era, reflecting themes of death and resistance

## **Structure & Form**

* Villanelle: 5 tercets + 1 quatrain, tightly structured

* Regular ABA rhyme scheme and repeated lines create a chant-like quality

* Controlled form mirrors the desire to control fate or resist its inevitability

* Final stanza: directed at the poet’s father, deeply emotional and raw
