# "Search For My Tongue" by Sujata Bhatt

## Poetic Techniques

* Extended metaphor: compares language loss to a “tongue” dying and regrowing

* Bilingual structure: switches between English and Gujarati, creating cultural immersion

* Symbolism: "tongue" = language, identity, roots

* Imagery: natural growth (e.g., “a stump of a shoot”) to show recovery of mother tongue

* Tone: personal, reflective, conflicted, eventually empowering

## Themes

* Language and identity

* Cultural duality and displacement

* Loss and rediscovery of heritage

* Power of native language and memory

## Context

* Sujata Bhatt is an Indian-born poet raised in the USA and educated in the UK

* Her poetry often deals with bilingualism, cultural confusion, and belonging

* The poem reflects real emotional tension between the mother tongue (Gujarati) and the adopted language (English)

## Structure & Form

* Free verse mirrors natural, personal expression

* Three sections:

  1. English narrative

  2. Middle Gujarati section (emotional heart of the poem)

  3. Return to English with a tone of reclamation

* Lack of punctuation: represents fluidity and blending of thoughts/languages
