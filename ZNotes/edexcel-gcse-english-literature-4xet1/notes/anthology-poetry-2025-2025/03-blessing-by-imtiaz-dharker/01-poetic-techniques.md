# **"Blessing" by Imtiaz Dharker**

## **Poetic Techniques**

* Imagery: sensory details ("silver crashes to the ground", "roar of tongues") evoke sound and scarcity

* Contrast: dry, suffering land vs sudden abundance of water

* Symbolism: water as both life-giver and divine blessing

* Onomatopoeia: "splash", "crash" emphasize the chaotic relief of rain

* Juxtaposition of deprivation and joy in diction and structure

## Themes

* Poverty and survival

* Religious symbolism and gratitude

* Hope, community, and shared humanity

* Natural elements are sacred

## Context

* Likely set in an urban slum in India or Pakistan

* Inspired by real water shortages and the desperation they cause

* Dharker’s work often explores religion, gender, and culture in South Asia

## Structure & Form

* Free verse with short stanzas

* Starts with a single-line stanza (emphasizing dryness and tension)

* Sudden shift in pace and tone when water arrives

* Final stanza = energetic, chaotic celebration
