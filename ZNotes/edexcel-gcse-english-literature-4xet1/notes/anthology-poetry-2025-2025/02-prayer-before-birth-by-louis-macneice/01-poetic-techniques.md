# "Prayer Before Birth" by Louis MacNeice

## Poetic Techniques

* ## Dramatic monologue from an unborn child (creates an urgent, helpless voice)

* Anaphora: repetition of "I am not yet born..." creates rhythm and builds intensity

* Imagery: disturbing, surreal images (e.g. "bloodsucking bat", "white light", "walls")

* Juxtaposition: innocence of the speaker vs horrors of the world

* Alliteration and assonance: enhance musicality and emotional tension

  ## Themes

* Innocence vs corruption

* Fear of the future and the human condition

* Plea for guidance, protection, and identity

* Criticism of war, dehumanisation, and societal cruelty

  ## Context

* ## Written during World War II (1944) amid anxiety and global violence

* MacNeice was concerned with political, moral, and spiritual decay

* Reflects existential fear and need for moral integrity in chaotic times

## Structure & Form

* ## Free verse: irregular stanzas and no set rhyme scheme

* Each stanza = escalating plea or warning

* Last stanza ends with loss of self: "me into a lethal automaton"

* No set form reflects the chaotic world being feared
