# "Piano" by D.H. Lawrence

## **Poetic Techniques**

* Imagery: vivid sensory details (“a woman is singing to me,” “tinkling piano”) evoke warmth and intimacy

* Enjambment: lines flow without punctuation, mirroring the flood of emotion

* Alliteration: “vista of years,” “the boom of the tingling strings” — adds musicality and softness

* Symbolism: the piano = childhood, emotional safety, maternal love

* Tone shift: from calm recollection to emotional breakdown

## **Themes**

* Childhood and nostalgia

* Loss and longing

* Emotional vulnerability

* Music as a memory trigger

## **Context**

* D.H. Lawrence was an early 20th-century writer from a working-class background

* Much of his work explores emotional repression and personal relationships

* In this poem, he reflects on the deep emotional ties between mother and child, sparked by music

## **Structure & Form**

* 3 quatrains (4-line stanzas)

* Rhyme scheme: Regular AABB in each stanza — gives the poem a song-like, gentle rhythm

* Steady form contrasts with the rising emotional intensity

* Use of the first-person voice emphasizes personal reflection
