
# "Remember" by Christina Rossetti

## Poetic Techniques

* Sonnet form: 14-line Petrarchan sonnet, divided into octet and sestet

* Volta (turn) at line 9: shifts from asking to be remembered → asking to be happy even if forgotten

* Euphemism: “gone away” and “silent land” = soft, gentle ways of saying “death”

* Tone: calm, thoughtful, and ultimately selfless

* Repetition of “remember” emphasizes longing but also acceptance

## Themes

* Love and remembrance

* Grief vs acceptance

* Death and the afterlife

* Selflessness in relationships

## Context

* Christina Rossetti was a 19th-century poet from the Victorian era

* Her work often dealt with faith, death, love, and sacrifice

* Reflects religious beliefs about death, memory, and the soul

* May have been written about her own declining health or lost loved ones

## Structure & Form

* Petrarchan sonnet:

  * Octet (first 8 lines) = request to remember

  * Sestet (last 6 lines) = shift to acceptance

* Rhyme scheme: ABBAABBA CDDECE

* Gentle rhythm reflects the peaceful and reflective nature of the poem

* Final message = release loved one from guilt of forgetting
