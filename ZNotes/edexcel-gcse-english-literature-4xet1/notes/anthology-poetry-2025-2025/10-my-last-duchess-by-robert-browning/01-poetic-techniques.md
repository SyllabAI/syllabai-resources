"My Last Duchess" by Robert Browning

#### Poetic Techniques

* Dramatic monologue: entire poem is the Duke’s speech to a silent listener

* Irony: the Duke unintentionally reveals his cruelty while trying to show off

* Enjambment: mimics natural speech and creates a sense of power and control

* Euphemism: avoids directly saying he killed her — adds cold, disturbing tone

* Symbolism: the painting represents control, possession, and objectification

#### H3: Themes

* Power and control

* Gender and dominance

* Jealousy and pride

* Appearance vs reality

* Art as control

#### H3: Context

* Robert Browning was a Victorian poet known for psychological realism

* Based loosely on the real Duke of Ferrara, who may have had his wife killed

* Reflects Victorian attitudes toward women, status, and ownership

* A critique of aristocratic pride and emotional coldness

#### H3: Structure & Form

* Rhymed couplets in iambic pentameter (heroic couplets)

* Enjambment prevents it from sounding sing-songy — adds realism and manipulation

* One continuous verse = the Duke never lets the listener (or reader) interrupt

* Carefully controlled speech that reveals unintended truths
