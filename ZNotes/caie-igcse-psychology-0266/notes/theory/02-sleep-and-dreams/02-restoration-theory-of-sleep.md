## Restoration Theory of Sleep

### Restoration Theory

The restoration theory suggests that sleep restores the body and brain after the demands of the day.

* nREM sleep restores physiological (physical) functions.

* REM sleep restores psychological (brain) functions.

* Both types of sleep are essential for healthy functioning.

### The Amygdala and Sleep

The amygdala is part of the limbic system involved in processing emotions.

**Role of Sleep**

* Sleep helps regulate activity in the amygdala.

* Adequate sleep allows better emotional control.

* Sleep deprivation causes the amygdala to become more reactive.

* This can lead to stronger emotional responses and difficulty regulating emotions.

**Example**

* A person who has not slept well may become angry or upset more easily.

### Robinson et al. (2022) – Sleep Restriction

* Robinson et al. investigated how restricting sleep affects emotional functioning. Participants had their sleep reduced over several nights before completing emotional tasks.

* The study found that sleep restriction increased emotional reactivity, supporting the idea that sleep, particularly REM sleep, helps restore psychological functions.

### Aim

* To investigate the effects of sleep restriction on emotional functioning.

### Method

* Experiment.

* Participants experienced restricted sleep over several nights.

* Emotional responses were measured using psychological tasks.

### Results

* Sleep restriction increased emotional reactivity.

* Participants showed greater amygdala activity.

* Emotional regulation became less effective.

### Conclusion

* Supports the restoration theory.

* Sleep is important for restoring psychological functioning and emotional control.

### Evaluation of the Restoration Theory

**Strengths**

* Supported by Robinson et al., who found sleep restriction reduced emotional functioning.

* Explains why people feel mentally and physically refreshed after sleeping.

* Supported by research showing sleep improves learning, memory and emotional regulation.

**Weaknesses**

* Muscle repair also occurs during waking rest, not only during sleep.

* Does not explain why different species require very different amounts of sleep.

* Some brain activity remains high during REM sleep despite the body resting.

### Application of the Restoration Theory

**Scenario:** A student stays awake revising for two nights before an important exam and becomes forgetful and emotionally sensitive.

**Explanation:**

* Lack of REM sleep reduces psychological restoration.

* The brain has less opportunity to process memories and regulate emotions.

* This leads to poorer concentration, memory and emotional control.

**Scenario:** An athlete sleeps for eight hours every night during a competition.

**Explanation:**

* nREM sleep helps restore physical functions by repairing muscles and replenishing energy.

* REM sleep restores psychological functions, improving focus and emotional regulation.

* Adequate sleep supports both physical and mental performance.

**Scenario:** A new parent sleeps only a few hours each night for several weeks and becomes irritable.

**Explanation:**

* Repeated sleep loss prevents normal restoration of brain functions.

* The amygdala becomes more reactive.

* This makes emotional responses stronger and more difficult to control.
