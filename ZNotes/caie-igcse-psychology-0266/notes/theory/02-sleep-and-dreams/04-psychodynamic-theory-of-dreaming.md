## Psychodynamic Theory of Dreaming

### Parts of the Mind

Freud believed the mind consists of three levels that influence thoughts, behaviour and dreams.

#|
||

**Part of the Mind**

|

**Description**

||
||

Conscious

|

Thoughts and feelings currently in awareness

||
||

Pre-conscious

|

Thoughts and memories not currently in awareness but can be recalled

||
||

Unconscious

|

Thoughts, memories and desires outside awareness that influence behaviour

||
|#

### Dream Content

Freud believed dreams have two levels of meaning.

#|
||

**Type of Content**

|

**Description**

||
||

Manifest content

|

The actual events, images and story remembered after waking

||
||

Latent content

|

The hidden symbolic meaning of the dream

||
|#

**Example**

* Manifest content: A person dreams they are climbing a mountain.

* Latent content: The mountain may symbolise a difficult challenge or goal in the person's life.

### Wish Fulfilment

Freud believed dreams allow unconscious wishes and desires to be expressed safely.

* Dreams satisfy wishes that cannot be fulfilled in everyday life.

* These wishes originate from the unconscious mind.

* The true meaning is disguised through dreamwork.

### Dreamwork

Dreamwork is the process that disguises unconscious wishes so they appear in a less threatening form.

### Mechanisms of Dreamwork

#|
||

**Mechanism**

|

**Description**

|

**Example**

||

Condensation

|

Several ideas, people or emotions are combined into one dream object

|

One person in a dream represents several people

||
||

Displacement

|

### Dream Analysis

Dream analysis is used to uncover the hidden meaning of dreams by bringing unconscious thoughts into conscious awareness.

### Techniques Used in Therapy

**Free Association**

* The person says whatever comes to mind without censoring their thoughts.

* These thoughts help reveal the dream's latent content.

**Symbol Analysis**

* The therapist interprets objects or events in the dream.

* Symbols are believed to represent unconscious thoughts or wishes.

### Iorio et al. – Dreams and Trauma

* Iorio et al. investigated the relationship between dreams and traumatic experiences. Participants who had experienced trauma reported and analysed their dreams.

* The researchers found that dreams often reflected emotional experiences linked to trauma, supporting Freud's idea that dreams can reveal unconscious thoughts and emotions.

### Aim

* To investigate the relationship between dreams and traumatic experiences.

### Method

* Participants who had experienced trauma reported their dreams.

* Researchers analysed recurring themes and emotional content.

### Results

* Dreams frequently reflected emotional experiences related to previous trauma.

* Similar themes appeared across multiple dreams.

### Conclusion

* Supports the psychodynamic theory.

* Dreams may express unconscious thoughts and emotions linked to past experiences.

### Evaluation of the Psychodynamic Theory

**Strengths**

* Supported by Iorio et al., who found dreams often reflected emotional experiences and trauma.

* Explains why dreams can feel personally meaningful.

* Highlights the influence of unconscious thoughts on behaviour.

**Weaknesses**

* Symbol interpretation is subjective and may differ between psychologists.

* Difficult to test scientifically because unconscious thoughts cannot be directly measured.

* Many dreams appear random rather than symbolic.

### Application of the Psychodynamic Theory

**Scenario**: A student repeatedly dreams about being unprepared for an important exam, even though they have studied well.

**Explanation:**

* The exam is the manifest content.

* It may symbolise deeper unconscious anxiety about failure or high expectations.

* The dream acts as wish fulfilment or reflects unresolved emotional concerns.

**Scenario:** After experiencing a frightening accident, a person has recurring dreams involving different dangerous situations.

**Explanation:**

* The manifest content changes, but the latent content remains linked to the unresolved trauma.

* Dreamwork disguises the original experience using different symbols and events.

* Dream analysis could help bring these unconscious emotions into conscious awareness.

**Scenario:** A person dreams that a close friend suddenly becomes their teacher and criticises their work.

**Explanation:**

* Condensation combines two important people into one dream figure.

* Displacement transfers feelings towards one person onto another.

* The dream may symbolise unconscious concerns about judgement or approval rather than the friend or teacher themselves.
