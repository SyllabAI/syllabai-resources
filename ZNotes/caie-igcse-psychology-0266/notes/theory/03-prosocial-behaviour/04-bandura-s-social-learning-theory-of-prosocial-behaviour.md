## Bandura's Social Learning Theory of Prosocial Behaviour

### Bandura's Social Learning Theory

* Bandura proposed that prosocial behaviour is learned by observing and imitating the behaviour of others.

* People do not need to experience rewards or punishments themselves. Instead, they can learn by watching what happens to others.

* Learning is influenced by observation, imitation and vicarious reinforcement.

### Vicarious Reinforcement

* Vicarious reinforcement occurs when a person observes someone else being rewarded for a behaviour and becomes more likely to copy that behaviour.

* The observer learns that the behaviour leads to positive consequences without experiencing the reward directly.

**Example**

* A child sees their sibling being praised for helping with household chores and begins helping as well.

### Social Learning Theory and Prosocial Behaviour

* Bandura suggested that prosocial behaviours develop through observation and imitation.

* Children and adults are more likely to copy helping behaviours when they observe respected or admired models.

* Prosocial behaviour becomes more frequent when models are rewarded for helping others.

**Key Processes**

* Observation of prosocial behaviour.

* Imitation of the model's behaviour.

* Vicarious reinforcement increases motivation to repeat the behaviour.

### Social Learning Theory and Prosocial Media Use

* Media such as television, films, video games, music and social media can influence prosocial behaviour by providing models for observational learning.

### Modelling

* People may imitate prosocial behaviours displayed by characters, influencers or other media figures.

* Positive media models can encourage helping, cooperation and kindness.

### Parental Mediation

* Parental mediation refers to strategies parents use to monitor, discuss and guide children's media use.

* **Examples include:**

  * Discussing positive messages shown in media.

  * Limiting inappropriate content.

  * Encouraging children to think critically about behaviours shown online or on television.

* Effective parental mediation can strengthen the positive effects of prosocial media.

### Empathy

* Empathy is the ability to understand another person's feelings and see situations from their point of view.

* Prosocial media can encourage empathy by helping viewers understand the experiences and emotions of others.

* Greater empathy increases the likelihood of helping behaviour.

### Prot et al. (Study 1) - Prosocial Media

* Prot et al. investigated whether exposure to prosocial media influences helping behaviour. Participants' media use and prosocial behaviours were measured to examine whether observing positive behaviours through media encourages people to act more prosocially.

* The study found that greater exposure to prosocial media was associated with increased helping behaviour, supporting Bandura's Social Learning Theory.

**Aim**

* To investigate whether exposure to prosocial media influences prosocial behaviour.

**Method**

* Participants completed measures of their media use and prosocial behaviour.

* Researchers examined the relationship between exposure to prosocial media and helping behaviour.

**Results**

* Greater exposure to prosocial media was associated with higher levels of helping behaviour.

* Participants who regularly viewed prosocial content were more likely to behave prosocially.

**Conclusion**

* Prosocial behaviour can be learned through observing positive models in the media.

* The findings support Bandura's Social Learning Theory.

### Argument for Social Learning Theory

**Evidence**

* Prot et al. found that greater exposure to prosocial media was associated with increased helping behaviour.

* Observing models being rewarded encourages imitation through vicarious reinforcement.

**Conclusion**

* Prosocial behaviour can be learned by observing positive role models in everyday life and the media.

### Argument Against Social Learning Theory

* Environmental influences do not fully explain prosocial behaviour.

* Biological factors, including serotonin, oxytocin, the prefrontal cortex, the amygdala and genetics, also influence empathy and helping behaviour.

* Prosocial behaviour is best explained by an interaction between biological and environmental influences.

### Application of Social Learning Theory

* **Scenario:** A primary school student regularly sees their teacher praise classmates who help tidy the classroom and soon begins helping without being asked.

* **Explanation:**

  * The teacher acts as a live model.

  * The student observes classmates being rewarded through praise.

  * Vicarious reinforcement increases motivation to imitate the helping behaviour.

* **Scenario:** A child frequently watches television programmes where characters cooperate, comfort others and solve problems through kindness.

* **Explanation:**

  * The television characters are symbolic models.

  * The child observes and remembers these prosocial behaviours.

  * Repeated exposure increases the likelihood of imitating similar behaviours in everyday life.

* **Scenario:** Parents regularly watch educational videos with their child and discuss why the characters chose to help others.

* **Explanation:**

  * Parental mediation encourages the child to think about positive behaviours shown in the media.

  * Discussing the characters' actions strengthens understanding and empathy.

  * The child becomes more likely to imitate the prosocial behaviours they observed.
