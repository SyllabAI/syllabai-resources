## Sampling

### Population

A **population** is a group of people with one or more characteristics in common, from which a sample is selected.

**Example:**

* A researcher wants to investigate exam stress among secondary school students.

  * Population = secondary school students

### Sample

A **sample** is a group of people selected to represent the population in research.

**Example:**

* A researcher selects 100 secondary school students to take part in the study.

  * Population = secondary school students

  * Sample = 100 students selected to participate

### Sampling technique

A **sampling technique** is the method used to select research participants from the population.

The two sampling techniques required are:

* Opportunity sampling

* Volunteer sampling

### Opportunity sampling

**Opportunity sampling** involves selecting participants because they are available at the time of the research.

The researcher selects people who are accessible when the research is being conducted.

**Example:**

* A researcher wants to investigate students’ attitudes towards homework.

  * They go to a school cafeteria and ask students who are there at the time to participate.

  * The students are selected because they are available.

### Volunteer sampling

**Volunteer sampling** involves participants choosing to take part in research by responding to an invitation to participate.

The researcher invites people to participate and those who are interested volunteer.

**Example:**

* A researcher advertises a study about sleep and asks students who are interested to participate.

* Students who respond to the invitation become the sample.

### Application to novel scenarios

**Example 1:**

A researcher approaches students who are sitting in a university café and asks them to participate.

→ **Opportunity sampling**

They were selected because they were available at the time.

**Example 2:**

A researcher advertises a study online and students who are interested contact the researcher.

→ **Volunteer sampling**

They choose to take part by responding to an invitation.

**Application tip:**

Ask:

“Why were these participants selected?”

Available at the time → Opportunity sampling

Responded to an invitation → Volunteer sampling
