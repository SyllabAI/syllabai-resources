# Coordinate Geometry
## Straight Line Equation

* A straight line equation can come in the form:
  * $y = mx + c$
  * $ax + by + c = 0$
* The formula $(y - y_{1}) = m(x - x_{1})$ , where $(x_{1},y_{1})$ are any coordinates on the line
* The distance between any two points on a line can be found using the formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_{2} - x_{1})^2 + (y_{2} - y_{1})^2}$
