## JAN-C12 q6

The straight line $l_{1}$ has the equation $2y=3x+5$, the line $l_{1}$ cuts the x-axis at point A as shown in figure 2.

a) 	  (i) State the gradient of $l_{1}$
(ii) Write down the coordinates of point A

________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

a(i)

Rearrange the equation of $l_{1}$ so that it’s in the form $y=mx+c$,

$2y=3x+5$

$y=3/2 x+5/2$

$m=3/2$,  Rearrange; hence the gradient is 3/2

_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

a(ii)

Since the line $l_{1}$  cuts the x-axis at point A., $y=0$ can be equated into the equation of $l_{1}$.
$2y=3x+5$

$2(0)=3x+5$

$0=3x+5$

$-5=3x$

$-5/3=x$

hence the coordinates of point A are (-5/3,0)
