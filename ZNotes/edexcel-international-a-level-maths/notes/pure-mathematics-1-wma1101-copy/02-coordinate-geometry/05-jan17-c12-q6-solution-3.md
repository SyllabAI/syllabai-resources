## Solution c


c) find the exact area of the triangle ABC 

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Solution:

To find the area of the triangle ABC, the length/distances of sides AB and BC need to be found.

Firstly to find the coordinates of the point C (the point at which  $l_{2}$ intersects the x-axis) substitute $y=0$ into $l_{2}$.

$2x+3(0)-14=0$

$2x-14=0$

$2x=14$

$x=14/2$

$x=7$

Secondly organize all the information required to find the distances (coordinates of points)

A(-5/3,0)   B(1,4)   C(7,0)
  
The distances required to find the area of the triangle are AB and BC.

Distance AB:
$d=\sqrt{(1+5/3)^2+(0+4)^2}$

$d=\sqrt{(8/3)^2+(4)^2}$

$d=\sqrt{64/9+16}$

$d=\sqrt{209/9}$   

$d=\frac{4\sqrt{13}}{3}$



Distance BC:

$d=\sqrt{(7-1)^2+(0-4)^2}$

$d= \sqrt{(6)^2+(-4)^2}$

$d=\sqrt{36+16}$

$d=\sqrt{52}$

$d=2\sqrt{13}$

Area:

$A=1/2(AB ×BC)$

$A=1/2 (\frac{4\sqrt{13}}{3}  \times 2\sqrt{13})$

$A=1/2 (104/3)$

$A=\frac{52}{3}$
