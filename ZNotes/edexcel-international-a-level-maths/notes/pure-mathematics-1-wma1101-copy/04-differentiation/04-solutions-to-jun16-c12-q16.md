## Solutions to JUN16-C12 q16 

b) Use part (a) to find an equation for l in $ax+by+c=0$, where a, b and c
are integers.


________________________________________________________________________________________________

Solution:

To find the y-coordinate of point P substitute $x=\frac{1}{2}$ into C

$y=1/2(1/2-1)(1/2-2)=3/8$

To find the gradient of the line substitute $x=\frac{1}{2}$ into $\frac{dy}{dx}$

$\frac{dy}{dx} =3(1/2)^2-6(1/2)+2= -1/4$

To find the equation of l the formula: $(y-y_{1} )=m(x-x_{1} )$ can be used.

$(y-3/8)=-1/4 (x-1/2)$

$y=-1/4 x+1/8+3/8=-1/4 x+2/4$

$-1/4 x-y+2/4=0$

Multiply the equation by -4

$x+4y-2=0$
