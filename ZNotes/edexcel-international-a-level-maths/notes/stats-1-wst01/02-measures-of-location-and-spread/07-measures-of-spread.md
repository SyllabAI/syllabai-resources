## Measures of Spread

* **Range**: The absolute difference between the largest value and the smallest value.
  Range=$x_{max}-x_{min}$

* **Interquartile Range (IQR)**: The difference between the upper quartile $Q_3$ and lower quartile $Q_1$. It measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data and ignores extreme outliers.
  IQR=$Q_3$-$Q_1$

* **Interpercentile range**: The difference between two given values

**Exam tips: Some exam questions require you to calculate $Q_3$ which is adding the interquartile range to $Q_1$**
