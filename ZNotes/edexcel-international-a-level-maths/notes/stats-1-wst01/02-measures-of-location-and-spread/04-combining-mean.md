## Combining mean

Combined mean (two or more groups): if group A has $n_1$ frequency with mean $\overline{x_1}$, and group B has $n_2$ frequency with mean $\overline{x_2}$,  combined mean is:

$\overline{x_c}= \frac{n_1 \overline{x_1}+n_2\overline{x_2}}{ n_1 + n_2}$

### Example question:

A math class is split into two groups. Group A has 10 students with a mean score of 50. Group B has 30 students with a mean score of 70. What is the combined mean score for the entire class?

### Answer:

$n_1=10$

$\overline{x_1}=50$

$n_2=30$

$\overline{x_2}=70$

$\overline{x_c}= \frac{10 \times 50 +30 \times 70}{ 10 + 30} = 65$
