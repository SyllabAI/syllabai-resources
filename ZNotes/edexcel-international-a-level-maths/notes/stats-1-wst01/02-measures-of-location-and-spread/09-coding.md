## Coding

* When raw data values are large or complex, they can be coded into simpler values using the transformation linear formula:

  * Eg. data are coded using $y= \frac{x-a}{b}$
  * Then, mean of the coded data would also be $\overline{y}= \frac{\overline{x}-a}{b}$

* The primary reason we use data coding is to simplify complex, large, or awkward datasets to make manual calculations faster and less prone to errors.

* However, in standard deviation, adding and subtracting doesn’t affect the coding, only the multiplication and division

  * Because adding or subtracting shifts every single number up or down by the exact same amount so the gaps (spread) between them do not change. Multiplying or dividing stretches or shrinks those gaps

### Example question:

A complex dataset $x$ is coded using $y = \frac{x - 100}{5}$. The coded summary statistics are calculated as $\overline{y} = 8.2 $ and $\sigma_y = 3.1$. Find the original mean and standard deviation.

### Answer:

* Step 1: Decoding mean:

  * $\overline{x}=5\overline {y}+100=5(8.2)+100=41+100=141$

* Step 2: Decoding the Standard Deviation

  * $\sigma _{x}=5\times \sigma _{y}=5\times 3.1=15.5$
