## Designing a model

There are seven stages involved in designing a model

1. Recognising the real-world problem

2. A statistical model is devised

3. The model is used to make predictions and outcomes about the behaviour of the real-world problem

4. Experimental data is collected

5. Comparisons are made against observed data and predicted outcomes

6. Statistical concepts are used to test how accurate the model is

7. The model is refined and adjusted
