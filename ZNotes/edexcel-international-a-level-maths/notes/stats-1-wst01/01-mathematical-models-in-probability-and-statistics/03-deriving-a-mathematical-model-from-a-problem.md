## Deriving a mathematical model from a problem

### Problem 1:

A taxi company charges a fixed booking fee plus a charge based on distance travelled.
The booking fee is £10, and the distance charge is £3 per kilometre.

We want to build a model for the total cost of a journey.

### Answer 1:

First, identify the variables

* $C $= total cost of the taxi fare

* $d$ = distance travelled, in kilometres

Here, C depends on d.

Second, write the word equation

$\boxed{\text{total cost=fixed fee+(rate per km)×(distance)}}$

Third, substitute

* $C=10+3d$

It is important to note that this is a **linear model** because the cost changes at a constant rate

### Problem 2:

A bacteria culture starts with 200 bacteria and doubles every hour.

Find a model for the number of bacteria after t hours.

### Answer 2:

Since the population doubles every hour, the model is exponential:

$N=A(b^t)$

We know that at $t=0, N=200.$ Therefore,

* $A= 200$

growth factor is 2 because the population doubles every hour. Therefore,

* $b= 2$

The equation:

$N=200(2^t)$
