## Standard Normal Distribution Model (Z-distribution model)

* We Model standardized human traits like IQ scores, adult heights across a large population, or natural variations in physical measurements

* **Key Assumptions Made:**

  * The data is perfectly symmetrical around its mean

  * The data is continuous and can theoretically span from negative infinity to positive infinity.

* **Why It May Be Inaccurate:**

  * Real-world data rarely extends to infinity and often exhibits "heavy tails" (where extreme outliers occur more frequently than the model predicts) or slight skewness

  * For example, in a normal distribution of human weight, the distribution model is skewed to the right
