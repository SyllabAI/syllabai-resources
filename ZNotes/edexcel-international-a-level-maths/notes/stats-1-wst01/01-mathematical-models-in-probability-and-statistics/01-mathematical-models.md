# Mathematical Modelling

## Mathematical models

### What is a mathematical model?

* it is a simplified representation of a real-world situation

* It uses mathematical language to describe how a system behaves

* It can also identify patterns, and make predictions about future outcomes

*Common examples:*

* *Using a straight-line model y= mx \+ c to relate revision hours to exam score*
