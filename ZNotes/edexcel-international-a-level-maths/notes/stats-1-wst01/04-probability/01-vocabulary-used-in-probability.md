# Probability

## Vocabulary used in Probability

* Sample Space: The set of all possible outcomes. The total probability of a sample space is always 1.

* Equally likely events: Events that have the exact same probability of occurring. For a finite sample space $S$ with equally likely outcomes: $P(A) =  \frac{\text {Number of outcomes in A}}{\text{Total number of outcomes in S }} $

* Mutually Exclusive: Events that **cannot happen** at the same time. They have no overlap: P($A \cap B$)= 0

* Independent Events: The occurrence of one event has **no effect** on the probability of the other occurring
