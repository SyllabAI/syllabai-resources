## Position Vectors

* To find a vector passing through two points:
  * $\vec{AB}=b-a=\vec{OB}-\vec{OA}$
  * The vector $\vec{AB}$ is equal to the position vector B - the position vector of A
* To find the distance between two points $\left(x_1, y_1\right)$ and $\left(x_2, y_2\right)$:
  * $d = \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$
* To find the position of a particle at time $t$:
  * $r = r_0 + vt$
  * $r$ = position at time $t$ | $r_0$ = initial position | $v$ = velocity | $t$ = time
