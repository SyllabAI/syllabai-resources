# Vectors

## Introduction

* Vectors in 2 directions will be assessed in this unit of Mechanics 1 (WME01)
* 2-dimensional vectors can be represented in two ways:
  * Column Vectors → $\binom{\ p\ }{\ q\ }$
  * $i$ and $j$ vectors → $(p\textbf{i}+ q\textbf{j})$
  * In this example the vector represents $p$ units in the positive horizontal direction (to the right) and $q$ units in the positive vertical direction (upwards).
* The magnitude of a vector $\textbf{v}$ is written as **$\left\|\textbf{v}\right\|$.**
* Convert $i$ and $j$ vectors to column vectors for simplicity in manipulations, then convert to $i$ and $j$ vectors in the final answer.
* Vectors may be added to each other.
  * Ex. $\binom{\ a\ }{\ b\ } + \binom{\ c\ }{\ d\ } = \binom{\ a + b\ }{\ c + d\ }$
* Vectors may be multiplied by scalars.
  * Ex.  $\binom{\ p\ }{\ q\ }\times\ y=\binom{\ py\ }{\ qy\ }$
* When two vectors are parallel cross multiply.
  * $\binom{\ a\ }{\ b\ }$ is parallel to $\binom{\ p\ }{\ q\ }$ → $aq = bp$
