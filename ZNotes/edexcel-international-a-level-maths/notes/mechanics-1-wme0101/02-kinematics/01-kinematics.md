# Kinematics

## Introduction

* A vector quantity has both a magnitude and a direction.
* A scalar quantity only has a magnitude.
* A vector can be positive or negative to indicate the direction.
* A scalar is always positive.
* Vectors: (Velocity, Displacement, and Acceleration)
* Scalars: (Speed, Distance, and Time)
* Motion upwards and motion towards the right will be considered positive in these notes.
* Always write your final answer as a scalar quantity.
* Deceleration is negative acceleration.
* Displacement is the distance between the starting point and the ending point.
* $suvat$ Formulae (Not included in the formula sheet):

| Formula | Used when |
|----|----|
| $v = u + at$ | $s$ is not given and not required |
| $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ | $t$ is not given and not required |
| $s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$ | $v$ is not given and not required |
| $s=vt-\frac{1}{2}at^2$ | $u$ is not given and not required |
| $s=\left(\frac{u+v}{2}\right)t$ | $a$ is not given and not required |

* $u $ = initial velocity | $v$ = final velocity | $s$ = displacement | $a$ = acceleration | $t$ = time
