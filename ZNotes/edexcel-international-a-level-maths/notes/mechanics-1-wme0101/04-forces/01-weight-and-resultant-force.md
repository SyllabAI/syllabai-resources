# Forces

## Weight and Resultant Force

* Weight is a downward force that pulls objects towards Earth.
* Weight is calculated using the formula $w=mg$ with $m$ being the mass and $g$ being the force of gravity (9.8 $N kg^{-1}$).
* A reaction force always acts upwards on the object if it rests on a surface.
* A force acting at an angle can be split into vertical and horizontal components.
* Newton’s Second Law → $F=ma$ with $F$ being the resultant force, $m$ being the mass, and $g$ being the acceleration.
* Always draw a diagram and label the forces to understand the problem.
* Tension is a force that acts away from the particle; Thrust is a force that acts towards a particle.
* If the object is in equilibrium, the resultant force is zero, meaning that the forces to the right are equal to the forces on the left and that the forces upwards are equal to the forces downwards.
