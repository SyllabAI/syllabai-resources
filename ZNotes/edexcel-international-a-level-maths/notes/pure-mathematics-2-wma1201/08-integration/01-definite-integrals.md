# Integration

## Definite Integrals

* Definite integrals are when you calculate the value of an integral between two given limits.

  → Definite integrals produce a value

  → Indefinite integrals produce a function
* Steps to solve a definite integral:


1. Write the definite integral within its limits
2. Integrate the function and write in square bracket notation “$[F(x)]^a_b$”
3. Evaluate the definite integral by working out f(b) - f(a).

---

→ Example:

Evaluate $\int^2_0 x^3 dx$


---

→ Answer:


1. After integrating, we get $[\frac{x^4}{4}]$

2\. This expands to $\frac{2^4}{4}$ - $\frac{0^4}{4}$


3. Thus giving a final answer of 4.
