## Solutions of Simple Trigonometric Equations

* To understand how to solve trigonometric equations, it is first essential to understand the CAST diagram:

   ![](../../../assets/fc6a4bef5fec-654a86ca429d7d3a48a5907ca1d0745e0705242afc5107a7.54)

→ The CAST/ASTC diagram states that:


1. All angles between $0^\degree$ and $90^\degree$ have positive solutions for sin, cos and tan.
2. All angles between $90^\degree$ and $180^\degree$ only have positive solutions for sin ratios.
3. All angles between $180^\degree$ and $270^\degree$ degrees only have positive solutions for tan ratios.
4. All angles between $270^\degree$ and $360^\degree$ degrees only have a positive solution for cos ratios.

→ **Note 1:** Always go in an anticlockwise direction for positive and clockwise direction for negative angles. (i.e for $-90^\degree$, the placement would be at $270^\degree$)

→**Note 2:** Always only add or subtract using $180^\degree$ or $360^\degree$ (NOT $90^\degree$ or $270^\degree$)


---

For example:

Q) Find $\theta$ when $\cos(\theta) = \frac{\sqrt3}{2}$ in the interval of $0^\degree<\theta<360^\degree$


---

A)

* $cos^{-1}(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}) = 30^\degree$
* $30^\degree$ is a positive solution for the cos ratio, so we know it would be in quadrants 1 and 4.
* Therefore, the first solution would be $30^\degree$ itself
* The second solution would be 360-30 = $330^\degree$


---

Example 2:

Q) Find x when $\cos(x+30) = \frac{1}{2}$ in the interval of $-180^\degree < x < 180^\degree$


---

A)

* Since our trigonometric ratio requires a solution of x+30, we must update our interval to reflect this and therefore be $-150^\degree < x +30 < 210^\degree$
* We can now solve for $x+30= \cos^{-1}\frac{1}{2}$
* This gives a solution of x+30 = $60^\degree$
* Since $60^\degree$ is positive, the solution will be in quadrant 1 and 4
* Therefore, the  positive solutions would be x+30=$30^\degree$ ,  x+30 = $330^\degree$ and the negative solutions would be x+30 = $-60^\degree$ , x+30 = $-420^\degree$
* We will now ignore the solutions of $330^\degree$ and $-420^\degree$ as they fall outside our interval
* **Therefore; our solutions for x will now be x = $30^\degree$ and x = $-90^\degree$**
