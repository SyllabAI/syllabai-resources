# Trigonometry

## Trigonometric Identities

* Key trigonometric identities to know are:

  $$
  \tan(\theta) = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}
  $$

$$
\sin^2(\theta) + \cos^2(\theta) = 1
$$

* There are also other standard trigonometric identities:


1. $\sin(180 - \theta) = \sin(\theta)$
2. $\cos(360-(\theta) =\cos(\theta)$
3. $\tan(180 + \theta) = \tan(\theta)$

**For example:**

* The value of cos(90) is equal to 0
* Therefore, the value of cos(360-90) → cos(270) is also equal to 0
