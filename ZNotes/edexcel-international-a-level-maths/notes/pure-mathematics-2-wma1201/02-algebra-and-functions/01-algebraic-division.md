## Algebraic Division

* In division:

$\frac{5}{3} $ can be written as $1 + \frac{2}{3}$

In this fraction, 5 is the dividend while 3 is the divisor

Therefore, we can use this relationship to show that

$\frac{A}{B} = Q + \frac{R}{B}$

In this case, Q is the quotient, and R is the remainder

* Polynomials are denoted by f(x), g(x) etc.
