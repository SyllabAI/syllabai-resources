## The Factor and Remainder Theorem

* The Factor Theorem Is used to find all factors of a polynomial

  →If P(x) is divided by (x-a) and the remainder is 0, then (x-a) is a factor of P(x)

  →If P(x) is divided by (ax+b), and P ($\frac{-b}{a}$) = 0; then (ax+b) is a factor of P(x)
* The Remainder theorem Is used to find the remainder when dividing a polynomial by an expression

  → If P(x) is divided by (x-a); then the remainder is P(a)

  → If P(x) is divided by (ax+b); then the remainder is P ($\frac{-b}{a}$)
