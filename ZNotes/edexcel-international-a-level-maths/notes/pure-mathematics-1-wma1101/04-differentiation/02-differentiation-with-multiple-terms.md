## Differentiation with Multiple Terms

Example 1:

Given that $f(x) = 4x^2-8x+3$ find the gradient of the curve at the point ($\frac{1}{2}$, 0)

$f'(x)$ = $4(2 * x^(2-1))$ - $8(x^(1-1))$, Constants get cancelled out,

$f'(x)$ = 8x-8

Insert $x = \frac{1}{2}$ into $f'(x)$ ,
$f'(x)$ = 8($\frac{1}{2}$) - 8 = -4
