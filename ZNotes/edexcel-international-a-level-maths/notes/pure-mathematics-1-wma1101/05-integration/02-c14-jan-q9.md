## C14-Jan q9

A curve with equation $y=f(x)$ passes through the point (3,6)

$f'(x) = (x-2)(3x+4)$

Use integration to find $f(x)$. Give your answer as a polynomial in its simplest form


______________________________________________________________________________________________________

Solution: First, expand and simplify the given differential

$f'(x)=3x^2-2x-8$

Integrate
$f(x)= \frac{3x^{2+1}}{2+1}-\frac{2x^{1+1}}{1+1}-\frac{8x^{0+1}}{0+1}+c$

$f(x)=x^3-x^2-8x+c$     ,                             Substitute (3,6) into f(x)

$6=3^3-3^2-8(3)+c= -6+c$

$6+6=c$

$c=12$

$f(x)=x^3-x^2-8x+12$
