# Trigonometry

## Sine, Cosine Rule and Area

* Sine rule:
  * $\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b} = \frac{\sin C}{c}$ or
  * $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$. either of the two formulas can be used however, the first formula is preferable for finding missing angles and the second for missing sides.
* Cosine rule:
  * $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 -2(bc\cos A)$
  * The formula can be rearranged as such to find missing angles: $\cos A = \frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2bc}$
* Area of a triangle:
  * $A = \frac{1}{2}(ab\sin C)$
