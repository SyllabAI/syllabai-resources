## Reciprocal Graphs 

* Sketching a reciprocal graph:
   * Reciprocal Functions come in the form $y = \frac{k}{x}$ or $y = \frac{k}{x^2}$ , where $k$ is a real constant
   * the graphs have asymptotes at $x = 0$ and $y = 0$
   * An asymptote is a line which the graph approaches but never reaches/meets
