## Surds

* Surds can be manipulated using the following rules:
  * $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$
  * $\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt\frac{a}{b}$
* Rationalisation:
  * for surds in the form $\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}}$ multiply numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{a}$
  * for surds in the form $\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}\pm b}$ multiply numerator and denominator by its conjugate ($\sqrt{a} \pm b$)
