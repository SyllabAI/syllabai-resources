## Graph Transformations 

* Given a line/curve where $y = f$($x + a$) is a translation where all points on the graph are shifted $a$ units to the left
* Given a line/curve where $y = f$($x$) $+  a$ is a translation where all points on the graph are shifted $a$ units up
* Given a line/curve where $y = af$($x$) is a vertical stretch of the graph by the factor $a$
* Given a line/curve where $y = f$($ax$) is a horizontal stretch of the graph by the factor $\frac{1}{a}$
