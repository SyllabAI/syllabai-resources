# Economic Assumptions

In Economics, assumptions can be made to isolate two variables, and negate external variables to look at a clear relationship.

**Consumers** aim to maximise their benefit

* When making a decision, consumers will assess and choose the option that maximises their benefit

**Businesses** aim to maximise their profit

* Similarly in business, businesses will make choices that benefits them by maximising profit

**Consumers** will not always be able to maximise their benefit
