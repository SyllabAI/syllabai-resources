## Aims of Sentencing – What Sentences Are Trying to Achieve

### **Adult Offenders**


1. **Punishment:**
   * Purpose: Impose a penalty proportionate to the gravity of the offence.
   * Objective: Deterrence, both specific (discouraging the offender) and general (sending a message to society).
2. **Rehabilitation:**
   * Purpose: Address underlying causes of criminal behaviour and support the offender's reform.
   * Objective: Reduce the likelihood of reoffending and facilitate reintegration into society.
3. **Public Protection:**
   * Purpose: Ensure the safety of the public by restricting the offender's liberty.
   * Objective: Prevent harm and minimize the risk posed by dangerous offenders.
4. **Restitution and Compensation:**
   * Purpose: Compensate victims for losses suffered due to the offence.
   * Objective: Provide financial and symbolic restitution to victims.
5. **Deterrence:**
   * Purpose: Discourage others from committing similar offences.
   * Objective: Influence potential offenders through the fear of consequences.
6. **Denunciation:**
   * Purpose: Express societal condemnation for the offender's actions.
   * Objective: Reinforce societal norms and values.

### **Young Offenders**


1. **Rehabilitation and Education:**
   * Purpose: Address developmental needs and support educational growth.
   * Objective: Equip young offenders with skills for positive social integration.
2. **Prevention of Reoffending:**
   * Purpose: Intervene early to prevent the establishment of criminal behaviour patterns.
   * Objective: Break the cycle of offending and reduce long-term criminality.
3. **Restorative Justice:**
   * Purpose: Repair harm caused to victims and the community.
   * Objective: Encourage accountability, empathy, and the rebuilding of relationships.
4. **Education and Skill Development:**
   * Purpose: Provide opportunities for education, training, and skill development.
   * Objective: Enhance prospects for future employment and stability.
5. **Family and Community Integration:**
   * Purpose: Strengthen family and community connections.
   * Objective: Foster a supportive environment for the young offender's rehabilitation.
6. **Accountability and Responsibility:**
   * Purpose: Instill a sense of accountability for one's actions.
   * Objective: Encourage personal responsibility and understanding of the consequences of criminal behaviour.
