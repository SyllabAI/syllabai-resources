## Adult Offenders

**Types of Sentence:**


1. **Custodial Sentence:**
   * Description: Involves imprisonment or detention in a secure facility.
   * Purpose: Punishment, protection of the public, and rehabilitation.
   * Duration: Varies based on the severity of the offence and legal provisions.
2. **Community Sentence:**
   * Description: Served in the community under specified conditions.
   * Purpose: Rehabilitation and reintegration.
   * Components: Probation, community service, electronic monitoring, or attendance centres.
3. **Fines:**
   * Description: Monetary penalty imposed by the court.
   * Purpose: Deterrence and financial accountability.
   * Amount: Varies based on the offence and financial circumstances of the offender.
4. **Discharges:**
   * Description: Conditional or absolute discharge, indicating guilt but no further punishment.
   * Purpose: Recognition of mitigating factors and rehabilitation.

### **Factors in Sentencing**


1. **Aggravating Factors:**
   * Definition: Circumstances that increase the severity of the offence.
   * Examples: Use of violence, premeditation, or targeting vulnerable victims.
2. **Mitigating Factors:**
   * Definition: Circumstances that reduce the severity of the offence.
   * Examples: Genuine remorse, cooperation with authorities, or mental health issues.
3. **Sentencing Guidelines:**
   * Definition: Frameworks provided by legislation or precedent.
   * Purpose: Ensure consistency and fairness in sentencing.
   * Considerations: Severity of the offence, culpability of the offender, and harm caused.
4. **Previous Convictions:**
   * Impact: Prior criminal history can influence the severity of the sentence.
   * Considerations: Repeat offences may lead to harsher penalties.
5. **Victim Impact Statements:**
   * Definition: Statements by victims describing the impact of the crime.
   * Purpose: Inform the court about the personal consequences of the offence.
6. **Agreed Upon Pleas:**
   * Definition: Plea bargains or agreements between the prosecution and defence.
   * Impact: This may result in reduced charges or more lenient sentencing.
7. **Rehabilitation Opportunities:**
   * Consideration: Availability of programs to address the underlying causes of criminal behaviour.
   * Purpose: Support the offender's rehabilitation and reduce reoffending.
