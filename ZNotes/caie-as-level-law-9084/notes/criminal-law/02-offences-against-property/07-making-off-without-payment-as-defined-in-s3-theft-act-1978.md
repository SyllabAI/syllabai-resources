## Making Off Without Payment as Defined in s3 Theft Act 1978

**Definition (s3 Theft Act 1978):**

Making off without payment, as outlined in Section 3 of the Theft Act 1978, refers to the offence committed by an individual who, having knowingly used a vehicle or other conveyance for the carriage of goods or a passenger, intentionally makes off without paying and with the intent to avoid payment.

**Actus reus:**

The actus reus of making off without payment involves the following elements:


1. Knowingly Using a Vehicle or Conveyance:
   * The defendant must knowingly use a vehicle or conveyance to transport goods or passengers.
   * This could include situations involving taxis, buses, or any other form of transport.
2. Intentional Departure Without Payment:
   * The individual intentionally makes off without paying.
   * This departure may occur after receiving goods or services for which payment is expected.

**Mens rea:**

The Mens rea for this offence includes:


1. **Knowledge of Using Conveyance:**
   * The defendant must know they are using a vehicle or conveyance designed to transport goods or passengers.
2. **Intent to Avoid Payment:**
   * The intentional act of making off without payment must be accompanied by the intent to avoid payment for the goods or services received.

### **s4 - Sentencing**

Section 4 of the Theft Act 1978 outlines the sentencing provisions for the offence of making off without payment:


1. **Summary Conviction:**
   * The offence is triable only summarily, meaning it is dealt with in the Magistrates' Court.
   * The maximum sentence for summary conviction is imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both.
2. **Exceptions for Summary Conviction:**
   * The court may consider exceptional circumstances justifying a sentence exceeding the normal maximum for summary offences.
3. **No Separate Offense for Aggravated Circumstances:**
   * Unlike other offences, there is no separate offence for making off without payment with aggravating circumstances.

### **Summary**

* Actus reus:
  * Knowingly using a vehicle or conveyance designed for transporting goods or passengers.
  * Intentionally making off without payment for goods or services.
* Mens rea:
  * Knowledge of using a conveyance.
  * Intent to avoid payment for goods or services received.
* s4 – Sentencing:
  * Summary conviction in the Magistrates' Court.
  * Maximum sentence: Three months' imprisonment, a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both.
  * Consider exceptional circumstances for a sentence exceeding the normal maximum for summary offences.
  * No separate offence for making off without payment with aggravating circumstances.
