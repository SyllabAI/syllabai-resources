## Fraud as Defined in the Fraud Act 2006

### **Definition (Fraud Act 2006)**

Fraud, under the Fraud Act 2006, encompasses various offences related to dishonestly deceiving others for personal gain.

**s2 – Fraud by False Representation – Actus Reus and Mens Rea:**


1. **Actus Reus:**
   * It involves making a false representation.
   * Representation can be made by words or conduct.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires dishonest intent when making false representations.
   * Knowledge of the representation being false or being reckless as to its truth.

**s3 – Fraud by Failing to Disclose Information – Actus Reus and Mens Rea:**


1. **Actus Reus:**
   * It involves failing to disclose information when there is a legal duty to do so.
   * Legal duty may arise from a contractual, fiduciary, or statutory obligation.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires dishonesty in the failure to disclose.
   * Knowledge of the duty to disclose and a dishonest intent not to fulfil that duty.

**s4 – Fraud by Abuse of Position – Actus Reus and Mens Rea:**


1. **Actus Reus:**
   * It involves abusing a position of trust or responsibility.
   * The abuse can be in the form of an action or failure to act.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires dishonesty in the abuse of the position.
   * Knowledge of the abuse or being reckless about whether the action constitutes abuse.

**s11 – Obtaining Services Dishonestly – Actus reus and Mens rea:**


1. **Actus reus:**
   * It involves obtaining services dishonestly.
   * Services can include any valuable service, not just services for payment.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires dishonest intent when obtaining the services.
   * Knowledge of the dishonesty or being reckless about the dishonest nature of obtaining the services.

### **Summary**

* s2 – Fraud by False Representation:
  * Actus Reus: Making a false representation.
  * Mens Rea: Dishonest intent, knowledge of falsity, or recklessness.
* s3 – Fraud by Failing to Disclose Information:
  * Actus Reus: Failing to disclose information with a legal duty to do so.
  * Mens Rea: Dishonest intent, knowledge of the duty, and dishonesty in the failure to disclose.
* s4 – Fraud by Abuse of Position:
  * Actus Reus: Abusing a position of trust or responsibility.
  * Mens Rea: Dishonest intent, knowledge of the abuse, or recklessness about the abuse.
* s11 – Obtaining Services Dishonestly:
  * Actus Reus: Obtaining services dishonestly.
  * Mens Rea: Dishonest intent, knowledge of dishonesty, or recklessness about the dishonest nature of obtaining services.
