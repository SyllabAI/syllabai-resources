## Criminal Damage as Defined in Criminal Damage Act 1971

### **Definition (Criminal Damage Act 1971)**

Per the **Criminal Damage Act 1971**, criminal damage covers various offences related to intentionally damaging or destroying property.

**s1 – Destroying or Damaging Property – Actus Reus and Mens Rea:**


1. **Actus reus:**
   * It involves destroying or damaging property belonging to another.
   * It can include complete destruction, partial damage, or rendering the property useless.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires intention or recklessness in causing the damage.
   * The defendant must foresee the risk of damage due to their actions.

**s2 – Threats to Destroy or Damage Property – Actus reus and Mens rea:**


1. **Actus reus:**
   * It involves making threats to destroy or damage property.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires an intent to cause the recipient to fear that the threat will be carried out.

**s3 – Possessing Anything with Intent to Destroy or Damage Property – Actus reus and Mens rea:**


1. **Actus reus:**
   * It involves possessing any item with the intent to destroy or damage property.
2. **Mens rea:**
   * Requires the defendant to know or be reckless as to whether the possession of the item is for causing damage.

**s4 – Sentencing:**


1. **Sentencing Options:**
   * The offence is indictable, and sentencing options include imprisonment, a fine, or both.
2. **Aggravating Factors:**
   * Aggravating factors, such as targeting religious or cultural property, may lead to a more severe sentence.

**s5 – ‘Without Lawful Excuse’:**


1. **Defining ‘Without Lawful Excuse’**:
   * The defendant may defend having a lawful excuse for their actions.
   * The burden of proof is on the defendant to establish a lawful excuse.

### **Summary**

* s1 – Destroying or Damaging Property:
  * Actus reus: Destroying or damaging another's property.
  * Mens rea: Intention or recklessness in causing the damage.
* s2 – Threats to Destroy or Damage Property:
  * Actus reus: Making threats to destroy or damage property.
  * Mens rea: Intent to cause fear that the threat will be carried out.
* s3 – Possessing Anything with Intent to Destroy or Damage Property:
  * Actus reus: Possessing an item intending to destroy or damage property.
  * Mens rea: Knowledge or recklessness regarding the item's possession for causing damage.
* s4 – Sentencing:
  * Sentencing Options: Indictable offence with imprisonment, fine, or both.
  * Aggravating Factors: Factors such as targeting specific properties may lead to a more severe sentence.
* s5 – ‘Without Lawful Excuse’:
  * Defining ‘Without Lawful Excuse’: The defendant may raise a defence bearing the burden of proving a lawful excuse.
