## Legal Professionals

### **Barristers, Solicitors and Legal Executives**

**Qualifications:**

* Barristers:
  * Typically, a qualifying law degree is followed by the Bar Professional Training Course (BPTC).
  * Non-law graduates may take a conversion course before the BPTC.
  * Pupillage, a period of practical training, follows successful completion.
* Solicitors:
  * A qualifying law degree or Graduate Diploma in Law (GDL) for non-law graduates.
  * The Legal Practice Course (LPC) follows the academic stage.
  * Training Contract: Practical training period supervised by a qualified solicitor.
* Legal Executives:
  * Academic qualifications vary; some enter through law degrees and others via vocational routes.
  * Legal Executive courses, offered by professional bodies like CILEx, provide specialized training.
  * A qualifying period of practical experience completes the qualification process.

**Training:**

* Barristers:
  * Bar Professional Training Course (BPTC) provides practical skills and knowledge.
  * Pupillage involves shadowing experienced barristers before becoming a fully-fledged practitioner.
* Solicitors:
  * Legal Practice Course (LPC) focuses on practical legal skills.
  * Training Contract involves supervised practice in various legal areas.
* Legal Executives:
  * Legal Executive courses (e.g., CILEx) offer training alongside practical experience.
  * Practical experience is gained in legal practice under the supervision of qualified professionals.

**Role:**

* Barristers:
  * Provide specialist legal advice.
  * Represent clients in court, presenting arguments and examining witnesses.
  * Typically instructed by solicitors, but can also take on direct public access work.
* Solicitors:
  * Offer general legal advice and services.
  * Handle client communication, prepare legal documents, and negotiate settlements.
  * Some solicitors specialize in specific areas such as family, corporate, or criminal law.
* Legal Executives:
  * Focus on a specific legal area.
  * Handle legal research, document preparation, and client liaison.
  * Roles often involve specialization, similar to solicitors.

**Regulation:**

* Barristers:
  * Regulated by the Bar Standards Board.
  * The Bar Council is a professional body representing barristers' interests.
* Solicitors:
  * Regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA).
  * The Law Society is a professional association that represents solicitors.
* Legal Executives:
  * Regulated by the Chartered Institute of Legal Executives (CILEx).
  * CILEx maintains professional standards and oversees qualifications.

**Regulation Bodies:**

* Bar Standards Board (BSB):
  * Sets educational and ethical standards for barristers.
  * Monitors and enforces compliance.
* Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA):
  * Regulates solicitors and law firms.
  * Ensures adherence to professional standards and ethics.
* Chartered Institute of Legal Executives (CILEx):
  * Regulates legal executives.
  * Oversees qualifications and maintains professional standards.

**The Law Society:**

* Represents solicitors in England and Wales.
* Provides support, guidance, and training for solicitors.

**Legal Ombudsman:**

* Role:
  * Independent body handling complaints about legal services.
  * Provides redress for clients dissatisfied with the service received.
* Responsibilities:
  * Investigate complaints and facilitate resolutions.
  * Ensures legal professionals adhere to standards.

### **Summary**

* Qualifications:
  * Barristers: Law degree, BPTC, pupillage.
  * Solicitors: Law degree or GDL, LPC, training contract.
  * Legal Executives: Varied qualifications, CILEx courses, practical experience.
* Training:
  * Barristers: BPTC, pupillage.
  * Solicitors: LPC, training contract.
  * Legal Executives: CILEx courses, practical experience.
* Role:
  * Barristers: Specialist legal advice, court representation.
  * Solicitors: General legal services, client liaison.
  * Legal Executives: Specialized legal focus, similar to solicitors.
* Regulation:
  * Barristers: Bar Standards Board, Bar Council.
  * Solicitors: SRA, Law Society.
  * Legal Executives: CILEx.
* Regulation Bodies:
  * BSB, SRA, CILEx, Law Society.
* Legal Ombudsman:
  * Independent body handling complaints.
  * Ensures adherence to professional standards.
