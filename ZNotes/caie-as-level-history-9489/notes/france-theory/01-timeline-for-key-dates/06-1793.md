## 1793:

* 21 January 1793, Louis XVI is executed in the Place de la Revolution
* 1 February, War declared on Great Britain and the Dutch Republic
* 10 March Revolutionary Tribunal established
* 11 March, Revolt in the Vendee
* 6 April Committee of Public Safety created
* 2 June, Girondin deputies purged
* 24 June, New constitution approved
* 17 July, Abolition of feudalism in France
* 27 July, Robespierre joined the CPS
* 23 August, Decree of levee en mass issued
* 17 September, Law of Suspects
* 22 September, Year II began
* 29 September, General Maximum introduced
* 5 October, New revolutionary calendar
* 16 October, Battle of Wattignies
* 4 December, Law of 14 Frimaire established revolutionary government
