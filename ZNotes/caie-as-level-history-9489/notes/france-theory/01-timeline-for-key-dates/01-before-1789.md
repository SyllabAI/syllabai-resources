# Timeline for Key Dates

## Before 1789:

* 1614, last summoning of the Estates-General before 1789 (175 years ago and only met in times of national emergency)
* 1756 - 1763, The Seven Years’ War
* 1770, Louis XVI marries Marie Antoinette
* June 1774, Louis XVI becomes king at age 20
* 1778, France enters the American War of Independence
* 1781 - 1787, Economic crisis
