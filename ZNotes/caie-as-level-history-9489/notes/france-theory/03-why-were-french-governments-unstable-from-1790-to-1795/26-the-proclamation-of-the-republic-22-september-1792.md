## **The Proclamation Of The Republic (22 September 1792)**

* Following Louis’ overthrow, the constitutional monarchists went into hiding 
* This left the Girondins in charge of the Assembly 
* Before the National Convention replaced the Assembly, the Assembly did everything the Commune wanted 
* It passed several radical measures: 
  * Refractory priests who did not leave would be deported 
  * Abolition of all feudal dues without compensation 
* This effectively ended the feudal system, which peasants had unsuccessfully been trying to do since the August Decrees of 1789 
* This was to win over the peasantry 
* After the attack on the Tuileries, Louis XVI was suspended from exercising his powers 
* Royal documents were found in the Tuileries after 10 August which showed how the King betrayed the nation by maintaining links with France’s enemies 
* On 21-22 September 1792, the monarchy was abolished and the National Convention was proclaimed 
* This was in effect a second Revolution
