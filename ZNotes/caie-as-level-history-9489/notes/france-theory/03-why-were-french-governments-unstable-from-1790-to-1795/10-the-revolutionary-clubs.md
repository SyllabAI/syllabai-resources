## **The Revolutionary Clubs**

* As there were no political parties, clubs played an important role
* Clubs informed the public on major issues, supported election candidates and pressured deputies in the Assembly
* The Jacobin Club:
  * The Jacobins had a high entrance fee
  * They were against a monarchy
  * Maximilian Robespierre was the leader of a minor group of radical Jacobin deputies
* The Cordeliers Club:
  * This club was more radical than the Jacobin Club and had no entrance fee
  * It supported direct democracy and the right to insurrection
  * Its main support was from the working class, though its leaders were bourgeois
  * George Danton and Camille Desmoulins were a part of this club
  * Marat was a radical journalist that wrote the *L’Ami du Peuple*, a newspaper
