## **Declaration Of Pillnitz (27 August 1791)**

* After the flight to Varennes, the Austrians and Prussians felt that they had to support Louis 
* They made it in the form of a public declaration 
* It appeared to threaten French internal affairs 
* It did not have much of an impact on the French 
* When the constitution was passed in September 1791, the Austrian monarch Leopold welcomed it, so Austrian intervention seemed remote
