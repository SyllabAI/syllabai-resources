## **The Coup Of Thermidor**

* During these divisions, Robespierre took a month away from public life, possibly due to exhaustion 
* He returned on 26 July and made a speech to the Convention about colleagues who were plotting against the government 
* He did not mention any names which was a huge mistake 
* Everyone felt threatened as they could be outed by Robespierre 
* A number of Robespierre’s colleagues decided to plot against him before he ordered their arrest 
* When Robespierre attempted to speak at the Convention on 27 July he was shouted down 
* The Convention then voted for the arrest of Robespierre
* However, since Robespierre was still popular in the Commune, Robespierre was released from prison and an insurrection occurred 
* Robespierre tried to order the National Guard to mobilise against the Convention 
* Since the two committees were dictatorships, they refused 
* Meanwhile, the Convention also called on the National Guard to support the Convention 
* Only a few Sections sent troops 
* Robespierre had little faith in the uprising so he did nothing 
* Meanwhile, Robespierre was declared as an outlaw so he could be executed by trial 
* This persuaded most of the Sections to side with the Convention 
* On 28 July 1794, Robespierre and his supporters were executed, known as the Coup of Thermidor 
* Over 100 members of the Commune were also executed 
* The Terror was finished
