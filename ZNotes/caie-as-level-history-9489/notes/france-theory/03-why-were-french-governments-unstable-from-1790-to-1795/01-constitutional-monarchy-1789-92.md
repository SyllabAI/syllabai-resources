# **Constitutional Monarchy 1789-92**

## **Reforming Programmes**

* By the end of 1789, many people wanted a constitutional monarchy
* Deputies in the National Assembly sought to reform France to create a constitutional monarchy
* They tried to apply the principles of the Declaration of Rights which were the ideas of equality, liberty and fraternity
* France was divided into departments, districts and communes
* Elected councils would run these divisions, thus decentralising power
* The principle of democracy within these divisions was also implemented
