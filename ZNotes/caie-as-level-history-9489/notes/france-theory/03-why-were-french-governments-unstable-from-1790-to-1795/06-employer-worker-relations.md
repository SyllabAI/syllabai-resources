## **Employer-Worker Relations**

* The deputies were determined to get rid of any organisations that had special privileges and restrictions regarding employment
* This would open up a range of crafts and occupations to more people
* As a result, guilds were abolished in 1791
* The Le Chapelier Law (14 June 1791) was passed, forbidding trade unions and employers’ organisations
* Collective bargaining, picketing and strikes were declared illegal
* No one in the Assembly objected to these measures
