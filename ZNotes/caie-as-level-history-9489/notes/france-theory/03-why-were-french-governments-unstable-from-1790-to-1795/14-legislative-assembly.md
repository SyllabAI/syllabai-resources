## **Legislative Assembly**

* The King’s acceptance of the constitution on September 1791 marked the end of the National Assembly
* It was replaced by the Legislative Assembly
* Those in it were mainly bourgeois
* The makeup of the assembly was:
  * The Left: 136 deputies, most of them part of the Jacobins, a small group from the Girondins
  * The Right: 264 deputies who were members of the Feuillant Club (supported the constitutional monarchy)
  * The Centre: 345 deputies who were on neither side
