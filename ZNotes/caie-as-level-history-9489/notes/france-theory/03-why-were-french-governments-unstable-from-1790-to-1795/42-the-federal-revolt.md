## **The Federal Revolt**

* In many departments, rebels resented the influence of Paris over the Convention and the power of the Jacobins 
* The first significant city to rebel was Marseille where they turned against the local Jacobin club 
* The Montagnards called these revolts ‘federalism’ 
* The most serious revolt occurred in Lyon (31 May) 
* Bordeaux started to revolt after the purge of the Girondin deputies 
* Significant resistance occurred in 8 departments 
* Another serious revolt occurred in the naval base of Toulon 
* The government cut off supplies to the city, so the inhabitants negotiated with the British 
* British troops entered the town on 28 August 
* Half of the French fleet was in Toulon, so this was a major setback 
* Many of the federalists were supporters of the Republic 
* Their armies were also pitifully small and they did not want to move too far from home 
* The government was able to pick them off one by one
