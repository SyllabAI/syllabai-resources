## **The Enragés**

* The economic situation continued to deteriorate in 1793 
* In mid-August, the assignat was below a third of its face value and drought reduced the grain supplies in Paris by three-quarters 
* One group, the Enragés, and their leader Jacques Roux demanded action from the government 
* Roux wanted the Convention to immediately deal with starvation and poverty 
* He demanded a programme of economic terror where hoarders would be executed and ex-nobles to be purged from the army 
* Roux continued to denounce the Convention and supported a journée on 5 September 1793 
* During the journée, Roux was arrested and he took his own life in February 1794
