## **Economy**

* All deputies in the National Assembly believed in laissez-faire
* Therefore, they introduced free trade in grain in August 1789 and removed price controls
* They wanted the price and distribution of essential goods to be controlled by the market itself to avoid scarcity, high prices and starvation
* On October 1790, internal tariffs were abolished so a national market was created (all goods could move freely within France)
* The metric system was also created to help with this national market
