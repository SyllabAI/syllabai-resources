## **The End Of The Terror**

* The Coup of Thermidor brought to an end the most dramatic period of the Revolution 
* The Jacobin dictatorship ensured the defeat of the Republic’s internal and external enemies 
* The Republic created a highly motivated citizen army 
* However, there was massive loss of life and devastation in the Vendée and the areas of the federalist revolt 
* Many of the extremist policies of the CGS and CPS alienated the Catholics and bourgeoisie
