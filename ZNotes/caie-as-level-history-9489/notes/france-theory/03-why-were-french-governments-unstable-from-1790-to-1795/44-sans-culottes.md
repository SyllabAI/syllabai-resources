## **Sans-Culottes**

* The growth in power of the sans-culottes was largely due to the war 
* They played an important role in the Revolution in 1789 by storming the Bastille and bringing the King to Paris during the October Days 
* The National Guard was able to keep them under control, until in July 1792, when the National Guard was opened to ‘passive citizens’ 
* No one could control Paris without their support 
* The sans-culottes hated the aristocracy 
* They were against priests because they had joined the aristocrats 
* Most importantly, they wanted direct democracy 
* They believed the people had the right to control and change their elected representative at any time 
* A series of concessions were made to the sans-culottes by the CPS: 
  * A new constitution was presented on 24 June 1793 
  * A new Declaration of Rights which went further than that of 1789 was included which stated the right of insurrection and all adult males to have the vote 
  * Conscription was demanded as part of the levée en masse 
  * Economic concessions like maximum prices and anti-hoarding laws were introduced
