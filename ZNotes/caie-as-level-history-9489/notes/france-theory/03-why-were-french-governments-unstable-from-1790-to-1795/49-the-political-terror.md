## **The Political Terror**

* In October 1793, the recently approved constitution was suspended until France was at peace 
* This allowed for extreme politics 
* The Political Terror took three forms: 
  * The official Terror controlled by the CPS and CGS 
  * The Terror in the areas of federal revolt like the Vendée and Lyon 
  * The Terror in other parts of France, under the control of watch committees, representatives-on-mission and the revolutionary armies 
* The CGS was largely responsible for bringing cases to the Revolutionary Tribunal
