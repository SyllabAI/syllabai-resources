## **Support For War**

* Marie Antoinette hoped for war in hopes of France being defeated and Louis regaining his old powers 
* Army commanders like Lafayette and Dumouriez wanted the war to increase their prestige and strengthen the Revolution 
* Brissot, a politician in support of the Revolution, wanted the war to expose traitors and the King’s true colours 
* He hoped it would allow France to extend its revolutionary ideals (fraternity)
