## **The Armées Révolutionnaires (September 1793)**

* On 4 September a crowd gathered before the Hotel de Ville to demand bread and higher wages 
* The following day, Roux urged them to march on the Convention 
* The Convention authorised the formation of a Parisian armées révolutionnaires which consisted of mainly sans-culottes 
* Their purpose was to:
  * Ensure the food supplies of Paris and large cities 
  * Round up hoarders, refractory priests etc 
  * Mobilise the nation's resources for the war effort 
* The Parisian army was successful in supplying Paris with bread until the spring of 1794 
* Their success was unlikely to last as their numbers were small and they met enormous hostility from the rural population 
* The CPS did not like the revolutionary armies as they were outside the control of the authorities 
* The armies also created opposition to the Revolution through their vicious methods of dealing with the peasants
