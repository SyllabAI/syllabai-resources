## **New CPS**

* Now most deputies feared the Montagnards for their treatment of the Girondins 
* However, they needed to be unified to defeat foreign enemies so they reluctantly followed the Montagnards 
* A new CPS was formed between July and September 1793 
* The 12 members were either Montagnards or deputies of the Plain who supported them 
* Robespierre joined the CPS on 27 July 1793 
* He was very influential and was linked with the Terror 
* He saw the need to garner the support of the sans-culottes 
* He consistently put the good of the country over everything else
