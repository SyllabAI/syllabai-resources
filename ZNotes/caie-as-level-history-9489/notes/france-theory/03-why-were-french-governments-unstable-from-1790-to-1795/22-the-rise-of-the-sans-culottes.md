## **The Rise Of The Sans-Culottes**

* Leaders of the Paris Sections held an armed demonstration on 20 June 1792 in response 
* Their leaders came from the Cordeliers Club 
* They invaded the Tuileries, many of them part of the National Guard 
* Louis stayed calm and did not withdraw his veto 
* However, it showed the weakness of the King and the Assembly and the growing power of the Sections 
* On 11 July, the Assembly declared a state of emergency in which every Frenchman was called on to fight 
* In return, the sans-culottes would get the vote (previously only ‘active citizens’ could vote)
