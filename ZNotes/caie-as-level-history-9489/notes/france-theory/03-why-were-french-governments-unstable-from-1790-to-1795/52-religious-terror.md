## **Religious Terror**

* Dechristianisation was not an official government policy 
* The driving force behind this campaign came from the sans-culottes in the Paris Commune, the revolutionary armies and the representatives-on-mission 
* They hated Catholicism because they felt that it had betrayed the Revolution 
* The Convention was merely drawn along with it 
* The destruction of churches was a symbol of the revolutionaries’ determination to destroy everything connected with the old monarchy 
* Churches were closed, church bells were removed and crosses were destroyed 
* The Paris Commune stopped paying clerical salaries in May 1793 
* In November 1793, they also ordered that all churches in Paris should be closed 
* Other areas in France followed and by the spring of 1794, most of the country’s churches had been closed 
* These actions were deeply resented in villages 
* For many people outside the civil war areas, dechristianisation was the most impactful
