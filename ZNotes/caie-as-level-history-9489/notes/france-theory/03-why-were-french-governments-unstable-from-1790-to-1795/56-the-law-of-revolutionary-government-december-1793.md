## **The Law Of Revolutionary Government (December 1793)**

* The Law of Frimaire on 4 December 1793 established a revolutionary government 
* This law gave the CGS and CPS full executive power 
* The CPS was given more extensive powers like controlling foreign policy and directing local governments 
* Representative-on-missions were now under the control of the CPS 
* All revolutionary armies would now be disbanded 
* This ended the anarchy and broke the power of the sans-culottes 
* However, it rejected many of the principles of 1789 
* Instead of decentralisation, it became more of a dictatorship
