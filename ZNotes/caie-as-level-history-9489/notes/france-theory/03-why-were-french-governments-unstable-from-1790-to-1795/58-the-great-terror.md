## **The Great Terror**

* The Great Terror was centred on Paris and lasted from 10 June until 27 July, 1794 
* In order to control all repression, the government abolished the provincial Revolutionary Tribunals in May 1794 
* Any suspects would now have to be brought to Paris to be tried 
* The supporters of Danton and Hebert still lived, so the Terror had to continue 
* The Law of Prairial was passed on 10 June 1794 
* This law removed any semblance of a fair trial and was designed to speed up the process of revolutionary justice 
* More people were sentenced to death in Paris by the Revolutionary Tribunal during June/July 1794 than in the previous 14 months of its existence 
* Many of the victims were nobles and clergyman
