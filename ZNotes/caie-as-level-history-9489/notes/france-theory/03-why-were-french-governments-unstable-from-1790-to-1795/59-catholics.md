## **Catholics**

* Robespierre believed in God
* He hated the dechristianisation campaign as it made enemies of the Revolution
* He organised a new religion, the Cult of the Supreme Being which was passed on 7 May 1794 
* On 8 June 1794 Robespierre organised a large ‘Festival of the Supreme Being’ in Paris 
* This new religion pleased no one 
* Catholics disliked it because it ignored Catholic doctrine and the Pope 
* Anti-clericals disliked it because it was the first step toward the reintroduction of Catholicism 
* Many thought Robespierre was setting himself up as the high priest of the new religion
