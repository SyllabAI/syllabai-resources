## **The Growth Of The Counter-Revolution**

* The deputies were worried by the nonjuring clergy and the *émigrés*, whose numbers had increased greatly since the flight to Varennes.
* What alarmed the Assembly most was the desertion of army officers.
* By the end of 1791, 6,000 army officers had emigrated (60% of all officers)
* The Assembly passed two laws in November 1791
* One declared that all non-jurors were suspects
* The other said *émigrés* who did not return to France would forfeit their property and be regarded as traitors
* The King vetoed these laws, which decrease his popularity
* He appeared to be undermining the Revolution
