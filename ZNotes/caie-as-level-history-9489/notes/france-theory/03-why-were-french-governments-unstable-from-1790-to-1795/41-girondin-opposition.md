## **Girondin Opposition**

* To win the support of the people, on 4 May 1793, a maximum price was fixed for grain 
* The Girondins were opposed to all price controls and they continued to be against the sans-culottes 
* On 26 May, Robespierre sided with the sans-culottes and called the sans-culottes to revolt against the Girondins 
* On 31 May, a rising began in Lyon against the Jacobins 
* On 2 June, 80,000 National Guardsman demanded the expulsion of the Girondins and a maximum price to be imposed on all essential goods 
* The Convention was forced to arrest 29 Girondin deputies and two ministers 
* Following the purge, a young royalist, Charlotte Corday, assassinated Marat in the vain belief that it would end the Revolution
