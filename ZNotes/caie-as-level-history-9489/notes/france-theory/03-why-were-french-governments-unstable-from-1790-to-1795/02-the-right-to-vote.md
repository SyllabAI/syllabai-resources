## **The Right To Vote**

* A concept of ‘active citizens’ was introduced
* This only allowed men who paid a certain amount of tax to vote (three days’ labour in taxes)
* Citizens who did not pay this amount were known as ‘passive citizens’
* However, ‘active citizens’ could only vote for electors
* Those who paid even more tax (ten days’ labour in taxes) could become an elector, who chose members of councils and elected deputies to the National Assembly
* To become a deputy in the Assembly, an ‘active citizen’ had to pay 54 days of manual labour in taxes.
* Most Frenchmen could not meet this qualification
* Therefore, this system was heavily in favour of the wealthy and was a form of indirect democracy
