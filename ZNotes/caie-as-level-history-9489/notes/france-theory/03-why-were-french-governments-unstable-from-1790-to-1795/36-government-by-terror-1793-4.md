# **Government By Terror 1793-4**

## **The Terror**

* The Terror was the most dramatic phase of the Revolution
* The extremist sans-culottes forced France’s leaders to adopt policies that were contrary to the liberal reforms of the Constituent Assembly
* France’s leaders needed their support to preserve the Revolution
* Government by Terror came because of internal and external threats
* There were two periods of terror:
  * The first began with the attack on the Tuileries on 10 August 1792 and ended with the Battle of Valmy, September 1792, where the allied invasion was pushed back
  * The second began with the journée of 31 May to 2 June 1793, where Girondin deputies were arrested and it ended with the execution of Robespierre in July 1794
