## **Taxation And Finance**

* The old taxation system was despised
* The *gabelle* (salt tax) was abolished in March 1790
* Within a year, nearly all unpopular indirect taxes were also abolished
* The new tax system would be based on equality, where citizens would pay according to their ability to do so
* Taxes would be collected by a municipal council
* The abolishment of indirect taxes meant taxation fell on producers rather than consumers
* This system was fairer as there would be no more exemptions
