## **Provincial Repression**

* By the end of 1793, the federal revolt had been suppressed by the regular army 
* Marseille, Lyon and Toulon were recaptured and the Vendée rebels were crushed 
* Repression followed 
* From January to May 1794, troops moved through the Vendée, killing nearly every peasant they met and burning down their farms 
* The Vendée became a depopulated wasteland 
* Thousands were crammed into prisons and were shot later 
* Most were ordinary peasants 
* Representative-on-mission and the revolutionary armies were often responsible for the worst atrocities 
* Yet the government encouraged them 
* At Nantes, 1800 people were drowned In Toulon, 800 were shot without trial 
* On 12 October 1793, CPS ordered the destruction of Lyon 
* 1900 people were killed by cannon fire or guillotined 
* Around 72% of the executions during the Terror took place in these rebel areas
