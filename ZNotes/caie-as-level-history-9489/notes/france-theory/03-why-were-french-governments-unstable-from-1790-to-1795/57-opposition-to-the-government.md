## **Opposition To The Government**

* Left-wing opposition came from the published Jacques Hebert and his followers 
* His newspaper demanded that more hoarders should be executed and property redistributed 
* This was very popular with the sans-culottes 
* Hebert called for an insurrection at the beginning of March 1794 and was subsequently arrested 
* Paris remained calm after he and his supporters were guillotined on 24 March 
* The CPS took advantage of the situation by: 
  * Closing the Cordeliers Club 
  * Disbanding all popular societies 
  * The Paris Commune was purged and replaced by supporters of Robespierre 
  * Representatives-on-mission were recalled to Paris 
* The opposition of the right was centred around Danton 
* Danton was a leading Montagnard/Jacobin 
* Danton wanted to end the war to end the Terror as well 
* Danton had become very wealthy so he was accused of bribery and corruption 
* Danton’s friend, Camille Desmoulins supported him and wanted the release of citizens who were called suspects 
* Danton had a large following in the Convention so he was regarded as a much more serious threat by the CPS 
* The CPS felt the end of the Terror left the door open for the return of the monarchy 
* Therefore, Danton and his followers were executed on 5 April 1794 
* These purges created an atmosphere of hatred and suspicion
