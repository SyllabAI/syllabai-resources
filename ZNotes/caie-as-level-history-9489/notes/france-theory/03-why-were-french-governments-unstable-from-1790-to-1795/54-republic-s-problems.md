## **Republic’s Problems**

* By the end of 1793, the government had begun to overcome the problems it had faced 
* The federal revolts had been crushed 
* Food supplies were moving into towns and cities 
* The value of the assignat was rising 
* The civil war in the Vendée ended 
* French armies in the war were doing well 
* The Convention could now start taking back the power given to the sans-culottes
