## **The Sans-Culottes**

* The sans-culottes were the normal urban workers in Paris
* They were not a class
* Many of them were ‘passive’ citizens
* They suffered greatly from inflation and led riots as grain prices rose by 50% in 1791 due to poor harvests
* Clubs were able to use their discontent to demand the government for change
* The sans-culottes made the Revolution more radical in many ways
