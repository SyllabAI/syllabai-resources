## **Committee Of Public Safety**

* On 6 April 1793, the Convention set up the CPS 
* The CPS depended on the support of the Convention who approved the CPS’s power each month 
* The CPS’s purpose was to supervise the activities of ministers 
* Danton and the Plains wanted a committee without extremists 
* Thus, of the nine members selected in April, seven were from the Plain and two were Montagnards 
* There were no Girondins
