## **The Declaration Of War (20 April 1792)**

* On 7 February 1792, Austria and Prussia became allies 
* In response, the King was forced to dismiss his Feuillant minister in March 1792 
* He replaced them with a more radical government, which included some Girondins 
* The new ministers now obeyed the Assembly and not the King 
* Now the government and Assembly wanted war 

  When Austria’s monarch died, France decided to declare war on 20 April 1792 
* They hoped to fight Austria solely, but Prussia joined Austria 
* This resulting conflict is known as the Revolutionary War 
* This would last 10 years, resulting in the death of 1.4 million French people and would alter the course of the Revolution
