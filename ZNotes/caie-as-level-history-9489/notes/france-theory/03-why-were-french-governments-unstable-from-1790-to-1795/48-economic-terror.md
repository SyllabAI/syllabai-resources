## **Economic Terror**

* The Convention had bowed to popular pressure from Roux in July by passing a law that imposed the death penalty for hoarding food 
* On 29 September 1793, the law of the General Maximum was passed to control prices 
* It fixed the price of bread and many essential goods and services 
* Wages were also fixed 
* The peasants hated it because the prices were often below the cost of production 
* The sans-culottes needed it so that they could afford to buy bread 
* The sans-culottes eventually went into the countryside to enforce the Maximum 
* The government was in a difficult position as the peasants could simply stop farming 
* Therefore, the government revised prices in February 1794 
* The sans-culottes were disgusted 
* However, it was successful short term as towns and the army was fed 
* The assignat also rose in value from 22% of its face value in August to 48% in December 1793
