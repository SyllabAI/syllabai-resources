## **The Fédérés**

* Many of the fédérés were militant revolutionaries and republicans 
* They became a powerful pressure group, calling for the removal of the King 
* New insurrections were being prepared by radicals and the fédérés 
* The Girondins changed their stance and tried to prevent the uprisings 
* The Girondins warned Louis that he should recall the ministers dismissed on 13 June 
* The King rejected their offer 
* The Jacobin leader, Robespierre, was cooperating with the central committee of the fédérés and on 29 July he put forward his proposals: 
  * Abandonment of the constitution of 1791 
  * The overthrow of the monarchy 
  * The establishment of a National Convention, elected by universal male suffrage
