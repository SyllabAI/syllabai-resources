## **Military Crisis**

* The French army was not prepared 
* Over half of its 12,000 officers had emigrated 
* When France advanced into the Austrian Netherlands on 20 April 1792, they met firm resistance 
* By the end of May, all three commanders advised that peace should be made 
* Treason and traitors were blamed for France’s failures, which were justified 
* Marie Antoinette had sent details of French military plans to the Austrians
