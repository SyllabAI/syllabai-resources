## **Royal Vetoes**

* The government had other problems to deal with 
* There was opposition from refractory priests and counter-revolutionaries 
* The Girondins had to act 
* On 27 May 1792, the Assembly passed a law for the deportation of refractory priests 
* Another law disbanded the King’s guard and a third set up a camp of 20,000 National Guards (known as the fédérés) in the provinces 
* They were to protect Paris from invasion and a coup by generals 
* Louis refused to approve these laws 
* The Girondins protested, but Louis dismissed them on 13 June 
* Dumouriez resigned 
* There was now an expectation of a military coup in support of the King
