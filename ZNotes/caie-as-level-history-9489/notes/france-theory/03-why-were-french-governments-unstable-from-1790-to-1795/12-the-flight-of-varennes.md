## **The Flight Of Varennes**

* The Flight occurred on June 1791
* Louis XVI regretted accepting the Civil Constitution of the Clergy as he was deeply religious
* He wanted to flee to Lorraine and use their army to negotiate
* He left on 20 June 1791
* However, he was caught the day after and was brought back to Paris
* Before he left, he had left a letter which denounced the Revolution
* The King lost all his popularity and the people’s trust
* On 16 July, the Assembly suspended the King until the new constitution was made
