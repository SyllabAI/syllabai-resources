## **The Sale Of Church Land**

* To deal with the financial crisis, Church land was nationalised on 2 November 1789 and *assignats* were introduced
* This provided money for the state
* Those who bought the land would be more invested in the revolution
* It was hoped the Clergy would support the new regime as the state now paid their salaries
* *Assignats* were bonds that the public could use to buy Church lands
* It would be used just like banknotes
* The bourgeoisie benefited from this as they had a surplus of cash
