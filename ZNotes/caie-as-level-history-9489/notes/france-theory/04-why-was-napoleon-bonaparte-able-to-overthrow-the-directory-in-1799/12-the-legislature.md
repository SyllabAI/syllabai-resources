## **The Legislature**

* The legislature was divided into two chambers:
  * The Council of Five Hundred, all of whom had to be over the age of 30
  * This Council would initiate legislation and then pass it onto the Council of Ancients
  * The Council of Ancients was 250 men over the age of 40 who would approve or object bills, but could not introduce or change them
* Elections were to be held every year where a third of the members would retire
