## **Increasing Revenue**

* In addition to cutting expenditure, Ramel wanted to increase revenue
* He put in place a number of policies:
  * A tax on trading licences
  * A land tax
  * A tax on movable property
  * A tax on doors and windows
* These measures were among the lasting achievements of the Directory
* Ramel also changed the method of collecting direct taxes
* Commissioners appointed by the Directors were to collect taxes
* The continuing war caused the government to revive the indirect taxes from the monarchy
* The *octrois* was reintroduced and was again very unpopular as it raised the price of goods
* Another source of revenue was the plunder from foreign states like Germany and Italy
* Peace with Austria also reduced government expenditure
* This allowed Ramel to balance the budget for the first time since the Revolution began
