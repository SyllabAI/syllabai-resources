## **Ending The Terror**

* The Convention set about dismantling the machinery of Terror
* Between the end of July 1794 and 31 May 1795 the Convention:
  * Abolished the Revolutionary Tribunal
  * Released all suspects from prison
  * Repealed the Law of Prairial
  * Closed the Jacobin Club
* In August, the Convention decreed that 25% of the members of the CGS and CPS would be changed each month
* The CPS was now confined to running the war and diplomacy
* The Paris Commune was abolished and power passed to the moderates and property owners
* The Thermidorians renounced the Constitutional Church
* In September 1794, the Convention stopped paying clerical salaries, effectively separating the Church and State
* On 21 February 1795, the government restored freedom of worship
* State recognition of the Supreme Being was ended
* The state was now neutral in all matters of religion
