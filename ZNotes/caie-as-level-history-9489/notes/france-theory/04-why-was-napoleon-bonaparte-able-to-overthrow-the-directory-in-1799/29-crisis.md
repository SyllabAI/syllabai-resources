## **Crisis**

* With its armies being forced back into France, the Republic could no longer pay for war by seizing foreign assets
* A forced loan on the rich was decreed
* This was intended to raise 100 million livres
* This meant that the wealthy had to give up as much as three-fourths of their income
* The Law of Hostages of 12 July allowed for the imprisonment for anyone resisting the new laws
* These measures appeared to be a return to the military arrests and harassment of the Terror of Year II
* However, only 10 million livres of the forced loans had been collected
* Conscription planned to raise 402,000 troops but only 248,000 actually joined because of widespread resistance
* Many became brigands or royalist rebels to avoid conscription
* In 1799 there was a virtual collapse of government administration in the provinces
* The National Guard was not large enough to keep order, so substantial areas of the countryside were not policed at all
* Government commissioners were killed as they were replaced
* By November 1799 there was civil war in southern France
