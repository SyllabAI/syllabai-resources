## **The Executive**

* The executive was to be a Directory of five chosen by the Ancients from a list drawn up by the Five Hundred
* The five directors would hold office for five years, but one had to be replaced each year
* The Directors could not be members of either Council and their powers were limited
* They could not initiate or veto laws
* They could not declare war and had no control over the treasury
* They were in charge of diplomacy, military affairs and law enforcement
* Government ministers and commissioners were under the executive and would make sure that government policy was implemented in the provinces
