## **The Second Coalition**

* Following his successes in Italy, Napoleon departed for Egypt in May 1798
* However, his fleet was destroyed
* This defeat encouraged other countries to take up arms against France again
* The Second Coalition was formed and Russia also joined by declaring war in December
* France declared war on Austria in March 1799 and fighting resumed
* France’s victories were now followed by a series of defeats
* However, arguments within the allies meant the immediate danger to France was over
