## **Financial Reform**

* Many of the financial problems of the Directory were the legacy of previous regimes
* By February 1796, the *assignats* were virtually worthless
* Therefore, the Directory issued a new paper currency known as the *mandates territoriaux*
* These also lost value and by July were worth less than 5% of their nominal value
* In February 1797 they ceased to be legal tender
* Metal coins became the only legal currency but these were in short supply
* This resulted in deflation as producers and retailers lowered prices
* The inflation of 1795-7 had made the Directory unpopular with workers
* Now the Directory became unpopular with businessmen since lower prices meant lower profits
