## **The Uprisings Of Germinal And Prairial**

* The Thermidorians removed price controls in December 1794 because they supported a free market and they believed it was unenforceable
* This led to massive inflation and a fall in the value of the *assignat*
* The government now had to buy its war materials at market price
* To do this, they printed even more *assignats*
* Before the Maximum was abolished, the *assignat* was 34% of its 1790 value
* Now in April 1795 it was worth 8% and 4% in May
* Poor harvests in 1794 worsened the situation
* The winter of 1794-5 froze rivers and factories had to be closed down
* There was an enormous increase in grain shortages and general misery
