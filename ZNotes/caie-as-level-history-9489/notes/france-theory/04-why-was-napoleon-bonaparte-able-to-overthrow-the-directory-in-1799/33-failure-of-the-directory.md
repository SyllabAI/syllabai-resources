## **Failure Of The Directory**

* The Directory failed to great stability partly due to the constitution of 1795 with its annual elections and no provision for settling disputes
* The directors also interfered with election results, destroying any respect they had
* Increasing reliance on the army paved the way for a military coup
* Many of the Directory’s supporters left due to its economic policies
* Prolonged war created opposition, but the war was needed to enable the regime to survive
* The reappearance of the Terror convinced many that the Directory should not continue
