## **The Vendemiaire Uprising (5 October 1795)**

* The Verona Declaration failed to attract mass support for the royalist cause
* News of the two-thirds law came as a shock to many Parisians who had hoped that the Convention would be replaced
* The Convention’s inability to deal with food shortages and inflation turned many people against the Convention, yet many of its deputies would return to the new assembly
* Royalists felt that restoration of the monarchy was unlikely given the known hostility of the Convention
* Frustration and anger turned into rebellion
* On 5 October 1795, a large royalist crowd of over 25,000 gathered to march on the Convention and seize power
* They greatly outnumbered the 8,000 government troops, but they had cannons
* Under General Bonaparte, the government forces crushed the rebellion
* Over 300 were killed or wounded
* Now the people of Paris would not attempt to intimidate an elected assembly again
* Repression was light as only two people were executed
* However, the Sectional Assemblies were abolished and the National Guard was put under the control of the new General of the Army of the Interior, Napoleon Bonaparte
* For the second time in six months, the army had saved the Thermidorians
