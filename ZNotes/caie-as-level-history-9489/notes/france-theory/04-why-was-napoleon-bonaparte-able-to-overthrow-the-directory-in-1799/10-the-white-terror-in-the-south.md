## **The White Terror In The South**

* In the south, the murder gangs of the White Terror were not considered to be a serious threat, so little effort was spent to eradicate them
* However, this allowed the rebels to quickly establish themselves
* Prison massacres like the September Massacres took place
* Gangs of youths killed as many as 2,000 in the south-east in 1795
* The killing continued throughout 1796 and for much of the following year
