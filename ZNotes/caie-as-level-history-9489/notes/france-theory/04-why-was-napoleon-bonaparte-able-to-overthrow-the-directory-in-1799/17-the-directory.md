## **The Directory**

* The directors wanted to provide a stable and liberal government, which would maintain the gains of the Revolution
* Yet they faced many problems
* There was no end in sight of the war and it had to be paid for
* The treasury was empty, taxes were unpaid and the *assignat* had plummeted in value
* Many people did not expect the Directory to last more then a few months
* Yet the Directory did survive and survived longer than any of the other revolutionary regimes
* There were a number of factors that contributed to this:
  * The Directory was committed to restoring the rule of law
  * Many key opponents had been discredited
  * The royalists were deeply divided between extremists and constitutional monarchists
  * The public was tired of revolution as revolutionary enthusiasm had disappeared
  * Importantly, the army supported the Directory as a royalist restoration would end the war
  * It was the army, above all, that enabled the Directory to overcome all challenges to its authority
