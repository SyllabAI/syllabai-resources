## **The Coup Of Fructidor 1797**

* The elections of 1797 revealed more support for the monarchists
* People were tired of war and religious conflict and believed a constitutional monarchy offered peace and stability
* Royalists won 180 of the 260 seats being contested
* This meant the Directory no longer had majority support and could only rely on a third of the deputies
* It seemed that in the next elections, the monarchists would gain a majority and thus could restore the monarchy legally
* The royalist strength was shown as two of the directors were sympathetic to the monarchists
* Of the remaining directors, two were Republicans
* They were determined to prevent the restoration of the monarchy
* On 3-4 September, troops seized all the strong points in Paris and surrounded the council chambers
* The two monarchist directors and 53 deputies were arrested
* The remaining deputies still felt intimidated so two decrees demanded by the remaining directors were approved
* One decree cancelled the elections in 49 departments, removing 177 deputies without providing for their replacement
* A second decree allowed for the deportation of the arrested deputies, directors and some leading royalists
* The directors then cancelled the local government elections and made appointments themselves
* This marked the end of parliamentary government and the constitution of 1795
* The monarchists had been dealt a severe blow
* The Directory could now govern without a hostile council
