## **Failures Of The Sans-Culottes**

* Prairial marked the end of the sans-culottes as a political and military force
* 1795-6 was equally as bad as 1794-5 yet there were no more uprisings
* The sans-culottes failed because:
  * The National Guard units were divided
  * There was no central organisation like the Paris Commune to coordinate their actions
  * They were politically inexperienced
  * They had lost the support of the radical bourgeoisie
  * The regular army was used to intervene
