## **The Coup Of Brumaire (November 1799)**

* The military situation improved in the late summer of 1799
* The Russians were being driven out in September
* Sieyès, who had become a director, saw this as an opportunity to stage a coup
* He wanted to strengthen the executive, but knew that the Five Hundred would not agree
* Therefore a coup was required for which the support of the army would be needed
* Moreau was approached, but he recommended Bonaparte, who had returned from Egypt on 10 October
* On his way to Paris, Bonaparte was greeted enthusiastically by the population
* He agreed to join Sieyès’ coup but only on the condition that a provisional government of three consuls, who would draft a new constitution, would be set up
