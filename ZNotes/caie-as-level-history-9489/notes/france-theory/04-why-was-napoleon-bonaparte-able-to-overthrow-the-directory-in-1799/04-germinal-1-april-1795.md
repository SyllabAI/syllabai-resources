## **Germinal (1 April 1795)**

* The hungry turned their fury towards the Convention
* The uprising of Germinal was more of a demonstration
* 10,000 unarmed people marched on the Convention
* They demanded bread, the constitution of 1793 and the release of former members of the CPS
* No one in the Convention supported them
* The National Guards appeared and the insurgents withdrew peacefully
* In retaliation, the Convention exiled the former members of the CPS but repression towards the people was light
