## **War 1794-9**

* The battle in June 1794 was the first of a series of successes for France
* In 1796, France wanted to defeat Austria
* Led by General Napoleon Bonaparte, the French army defeated the Austrians and an armistice was agreed upon at Leoben in April 1797
* The Peace of Campo Formio (18 October) 1797 confirmed what had been agreed in Leoben and confirmed Napoleon as a great international statesman
