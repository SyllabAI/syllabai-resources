## **Constitution Of 1795**

* The Thermidorians wanted a new constitution that would guarantee the main features of the Revolution of 1789 (the abolition of privilege, freedom of the individual, and the control of local and national affairs by an elected assembly)
* They also did not want a dictatorship like the CPS to ever occur again
* A new constitution was agreed on 22 August 1795, whose main features included:
  * All men over 21 who paid direct taxation were allowed to vote in primary assemblies to choose electors
  * The real power laid with the electors who actually chose the deputies
  * Electors had to pay taxes equivalent to 150-200 days’ labour, so electors were very rich and had subsequently suffered from the Revolution in 1793-4
  * To prevent a dictatorship, the legislature was rigidly separated from the executive
