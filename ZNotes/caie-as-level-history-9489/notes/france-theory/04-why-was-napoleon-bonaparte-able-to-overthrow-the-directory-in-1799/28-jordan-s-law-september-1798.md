## **Jordan’s Law (September 1798)**

* By 1798, there were concerns about the size of the French army which was only 270,000 strong
* Jordan’s law proposed that military conscription be reintroduced for the first time since 1793
* The councils approved this in September 1798
* However, this provoked widespread resistance
* The 1799 elections once again showed the unpopularity of the Directory
* The moderates had become disillusioned with the government as news of military defeats reached Paris
