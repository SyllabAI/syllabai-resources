## **Weaknesses In The New Constitution**

* Despite the complex system to balance power, the new Constitution had many weaknesses:
  * The yearly elections created instability as majorities in the councils could be quickly overturned
  * There was no means of resolving conflicts between the legislature and the executive
  * The Directory could be paralysed if the council refused to pass laws as the directors could not dissolve or veto laws passed by the councils
  * Meanwhile, the legislature could only alter the Directory by replacing one director a year
* The new constitution rigidly enforced the separation of powers
* The Directory would have to cancel election results or call in the army to resolve disputes
* While drawing up the new constitution, the Convention feared that free elections might produce a royalist majority
* In order to avoid this it decreed that two-thirds of the new deputies would be chosen from among the existing deputies of the Convention
* After the constitution was agreed upon on 22 August, a plebiscite was held
* One million were in favour, 50,000 was against, but four million did not vote
* The two-thirds decree was only accepted 205,000 to 108,000
