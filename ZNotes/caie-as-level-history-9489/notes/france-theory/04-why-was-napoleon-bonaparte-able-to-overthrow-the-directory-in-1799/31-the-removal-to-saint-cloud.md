## **The Removal To Saint-Cloud**

* Sieyès wanted to move the councils to Saint-Cloud as the Jacobins in the Five Hundred had numerous support in Paris
* The Ancients persuaded the councils to move to the safer location
* On 10 November it was clear that a plot was organised by Sieyès
* The Council of Five Hundred was furious, so Bonaparte reluctantly agreed to address both councils
* The appearance of Napoleon was not taken positively
* He was attacked by Jacobin deputies and had to be rescued by fellow officers
* Napoleon’s brother and president of the Five Hundred, Lucien, came to the rescue when he told the troops that some deputies were trying to assassinate their general
* The troops came in and cleared the hall where the Five Hundred were meeting
* Some hours later, a small group of councillors met and approved the decree which abolished the Directory
* It was replaced with a provisional executive committee of three members: Sieyès, Roger Docus and Napoleon
