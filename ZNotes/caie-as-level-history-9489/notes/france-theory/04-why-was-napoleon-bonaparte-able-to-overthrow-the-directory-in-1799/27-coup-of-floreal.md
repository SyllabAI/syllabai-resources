## **Coup Of Floreal**

* The persecution of royalists after Fructidor was so severe that they tended to keep away from assemblies in 1798
* Although the Jacobins did well in elections, they captured less than a third of the seats
* The Directory could be sure of majority support among the deputies
* However, the directors persuaded the councils to pass the Law of 22 Floreal (11 May 1798) which annulled elections of 127 deputies, 86 of whom were suspected Jacobins
* There was little justification of the coup of Floreal as the Republic was not in danger
