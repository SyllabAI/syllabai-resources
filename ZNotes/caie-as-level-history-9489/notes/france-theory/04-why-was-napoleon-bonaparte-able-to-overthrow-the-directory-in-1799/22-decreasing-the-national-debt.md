## **Decreasing The National Debt**

* After the coup of Fructidor, the Directory had little trouble with the purged councils
* Therefore the minister of finance, Ramel, introduced some far-reaching reforms
* In September 1797 two-thirds of the national debt was renounced by a one-off payment to debt holders
* Their loans to the government were converted into non-interest-bearing bonds which could be used to buy national property
* This reduced the government’s annual interested on national debt from 240 million livres to 80 million
* The bond holders had no benefit
* Within a year the value of the bonds had fallen by 60% and soon after they became worthless
* The bond holders were unhappy with the measures, but it did help stabilise French finances for a time
