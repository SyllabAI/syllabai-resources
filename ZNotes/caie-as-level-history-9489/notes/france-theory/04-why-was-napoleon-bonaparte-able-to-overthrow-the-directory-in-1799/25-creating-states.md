## **Creating States**

* French foreign policy became increasingly aggressive as the directors sought to keep French conquest and even extend them
* France reorganised a number of foreign territories, effectively redrawing the map of Europe
* These new territories were in effect satellite republics under French influence or control
* The spring of 1798 marked the high point of the Republic’s power
* In parts of Europe, France had gained a degree of dominance unparalleled in modern European History
