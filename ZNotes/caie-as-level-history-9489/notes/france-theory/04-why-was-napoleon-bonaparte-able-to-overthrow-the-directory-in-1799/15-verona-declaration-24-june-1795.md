## **Verona Declaration (24 June 1795)**

* The royalists wanted to promote their cause
* Constitutional monarchists, wanting a return to a limited monarchy, felt they were gaining public support as they appeared to offer a prospect of stability
* They had hoped to put Louis XVI’s son on the throne, but he died in June 1795
* The Comte de Provence, Louis XVI’s brother immediately proclaimed himself Louis XVII and on 24 June issued the Verona Declaration
* Louis promised to return to the ‘ancient constitution’ of France, which meant restoring the old three orders
* He also promised to return the properties of the Church and *émigrés*
* This antagonised all those who had bought biens nationaux and those who had benefited from the abolition of the tithe and feudal dues
* Although it was not intended as such, the Verona Declaration turned out to be a great boost to those who favoured a Republic
