## **The White Terror In The Vendée**

* The ‘White Terror’ was much more violent in the north-west and south-east of France
* Guerrilla warfare was revived in the Vendée in 1794
* In the spring, a movement known as the Chouan began in Brittany
* They were men who were against conscription
* They attacked grain convoys and destabilised local government by murdering officials
* The Chouans controlled most of Brittany and sought English support
* In June 1795, 3,000 emigres troops landed and joined the rebel forces
* The total rebel forces numbered around 22,000
* General Hoche led 10,000 troops to seal them off
* The rebel forces surrendered and 6,000 prisoners were taken while 640 were shot
* This was a huge disaster for the *émigrés*
* The government wanted to fully wipe out the Chouan, so Hoche was sent with an army of 140,000 to wipe out the Chouan and the Vendée rebels
* By summer of 1796, government authority was restored in those regions
