# **The Directory 1795-99**

## **The Thermidorians**

* Those who overthrew Robespierre were known as Thermidorians
* The Thermidorians were a mixed group of members from the CGS, CPS and deputies of the Plain
* The Plain deputies emerged to take control
* It was made up of men who gained from the Revolutions by buying *biens nationaux* or obtaining government contracts
* Therefore, they did not want to return to a monarchy
* They disliked the Jacobins, popular democracy and the Terror
