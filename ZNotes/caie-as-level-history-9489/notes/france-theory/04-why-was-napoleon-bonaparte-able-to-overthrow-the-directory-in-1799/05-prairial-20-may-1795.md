## **Prairial (20 May 1795)**

* This was a much more serious affair
* It was an armed rising
* On 20 May, a large crowd of housewives, workers and some National Guard units marched on the Convention to demand bread
* The Convention’s troops sided with the rebels
* The rebel troops was not prepared to fire on the Convention
* The crisis was resolved when the Convention agreed to accept the petition and set up a food commission
* National Guards loyal to the Convention showed up later and cleared the Assembly
* On 22 May, the Convention took action
* Rebel suburbs were surrounded by 20,000 troops and were forced to surrender
* The repression resulted in:
  * 40 Montagnards were arrested and 6 were executed
  * Another 36 where executed
  * About 6,000 militants were disarmed and arrested
