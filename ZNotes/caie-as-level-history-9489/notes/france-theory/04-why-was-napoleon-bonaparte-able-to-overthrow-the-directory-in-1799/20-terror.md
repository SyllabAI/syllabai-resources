## **Terror**

* After Fructidor, the new Directory took action against emigres and refractory priests
* During the next few months, many were hunted down and sentenced to death
* Clergy were now required to take an oath, rejecting any support for the monarchy
* Those who refused would be deported
* In the short term, this succeeded in destroying the royalist movement
* However, this alienated the Catholics, creating more opponents for the Directory
