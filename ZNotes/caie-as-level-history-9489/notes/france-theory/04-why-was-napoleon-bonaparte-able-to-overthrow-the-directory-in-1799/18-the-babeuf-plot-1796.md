## **The Babeuf Plot 1796**

* The first real challenge to the Directory came from Gracchus Babeuf
* He disliked the Constitution of 1795 as it gave power to the wealthy
* From 1796, Babeuf organised a coup to overthrow the Directory
* However, he received no support from the sans-culottes or former Jacobins
* He was arrested in May 1796 after being betrayed by a fellow conspirator
