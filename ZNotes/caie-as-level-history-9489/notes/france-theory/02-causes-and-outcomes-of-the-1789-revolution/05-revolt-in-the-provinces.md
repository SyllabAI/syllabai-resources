## **Revolt In The Provinces**

* These were known as the Municipal Revolution of July 1789
* Many rural towns supported the National Assembly
* In response to the King’s actions, old councils were removed and a National Guard was formed in every town
* The King’s old intendants fled so the King lost control over provincial towns
* Peasants also revolted by attacking nobles and landlords in fear of plots against them
* This was known as the Great Fear
