# **Causes and Outcomes of the 1789 Revolution**

## **Long Term Causes**

* Louis XVI’s weaknesses
  * He was indecisive and easily influenced by those around him
* Taxation system
  * There was a lot of corruption due to the system of venality (the ability to buy/sell positions, so the King could not dismiss ministers)
  * The Third Estate had to pay many taxes like *taille* (land tax), *gabelle* (salt tax), *tithes* (paid to the Church) and feudal dues (peasants paid to landowners)
* Structure of French society
  * Made up of First, Second and Third Estate
  * First Estate contained any one part of the Church
  * The Second Estate contained the nobility
  * The Third Estate contained everyone else (made up of around 95% of the total population)
  * The First (clergy) and Second (nobility) Estate had many privileges
  * The First was
    * Exempt from taxes (only paid an annual *don gratuit* to the King which was about 5% of the Church’s income)
    * Had censorship powers
    * Controlled many educational institutions and hospitals
  * The Second was
    * Exempt from taxes
    * Had the right to operate mills, ovens and wine presses
    * Had rights to hunting and fishing
    * Were tried in their own courts
  * The Third had none of these privileges which increased financial burden and thus resentment towards the State
* The Enlightenment
  * It was a movement of writers and thinkers in the 18th century
  * They challenged a range of views and ideas
  * They used rational thought instead of tradition which undermined the idea of an absolute monarchy and the Church’s position
  * However, they were not against the regime and were not revolutionary
