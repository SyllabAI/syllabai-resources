## **Meeting Of The Estates-General**

* 5 May 1789, the Estates-General met in Versailles
* The Estates-General was made up of deputies from all three Estates
* There was much excitement surrounding the meeting as it had not been called for the past 175 years
* However, the King did not make a decision or set an agenda at the meetings
* The Third Estate frustratedly broke away and formed the National Assembly on 17 June
* They also took the Tennis Court Oath on 23 June
* In the oath, they agreed to not dissolve until they created a constitution for France
* This was an act of defiance against the King
* The King responded by ignoring their actions and tried to set up reforms
* However, the reforms were not enough and popular protest rose
* Therefore, the King was forced to recognise the National Assembly and allowed the First and Second Estate to join the National Assembly
