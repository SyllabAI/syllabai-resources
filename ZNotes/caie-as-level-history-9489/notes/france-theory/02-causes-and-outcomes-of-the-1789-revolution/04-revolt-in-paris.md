## **Revolt In Paris**

* Many Parisians were unhappy with the King as they supported the Assembly
* The King, however, brought in 25,000 troops to the Paris-Versailles area
* The King became more confident as he now had a considerable military force
* This resulted in the King dismissing Necker (Controller-General) who was widely popular
* Revolutionary speakers told the Parisians to take up arms in response to the mobilisation and the sacking
* This resulted in the Storming of the Bastille
  * After Parisians seized muskets and cannons, they needed gunpowder
  * The Bastille was a royal prison and had gunpowder
  * On July 1789, the Bastille was under siege and captured
  * The commander of the Bastille resisted and was beheaded
* This disturbance resulted in the need for a governing body
* The Commune of Paris was set up
* They set up the National Guard to protect people’s property from royal forces
