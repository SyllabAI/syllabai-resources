## **Short Term Causes**

* War
  * The Seven Years’ War (1756-63)
    * France lost against Britain in India and North America
    * As a result, France lost most of its oversea colonies
    * This included profitable sugar-producing colonies
  * The American War of Independence (1776 - 1783)
    * France joined the rebels and provided military and financial support
    * They spent over one billion *livres* on the war effort
    * They did regain some lost territories
    * Soldiers were also exposed to ideas of liberty and democracy, which they would spread when they returned to France
* Financial crisis
  * Caused by the wars, corruption in the tax system and bad harvests
  * During the 1780s, there was disastrous weather causing very poor harvests
  * Food prices rose, demand for manufactured goods dropped and unemployment rose
  * France was close to bankruptcy by 1786
  * Calonne, the Controller-General, created a reform in 1786
  * This reform would tax the First and Second Estate
  * The King and Calonne tried to get approval from the Assembly of Notables in February 1787
  * The Assembly rejected it as they believed the approval of the nation was needed
  * Louis exiled the Assembly, but a noble uprising forced Louis to call the Assembly back
  * France was bankrupt by 1788, resulting in the calling of the Estates-General in order to get the approval of the nation
