## **The August Decrees**

* In order to quell the chaos, the National Assembly created new reforms
* From 4-11 August, the reforms included:
  * Abolishment of *tithes* and venality
  * All financial and tax privileges to be abolished
  * Special privileges for areas to be abolished
  * All citizens to be eligible for all offices
* This was a huge success, but the King did not recognise it officially
