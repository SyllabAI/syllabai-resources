## **October Days, 5 - 6**

* During October, the King reinforced his guards
* The soldiers held anti-revolutionary demonstrations in support of the King
* Many Parisians resented the King for this
* Meanwhile, there was also a food shortage in Paris
* Thousands of women marched from Paris onto Versailles to demand bread
* The National Guard under Lafayette joined them
* The women got into the King’s palace
* The King was forced to move to Paris and accept the reforms made by the National Assembly
* The National Assembly was also forced to come to Paris
* Many thought this marked the end of the Revolution
