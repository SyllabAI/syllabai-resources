## **Chouans**

* While Napoleon was seizing power in Paris, royalist unrest had broken out in Brittany
* The rebels there were known as Chouans and the unrest as the Chouannerie
* Napoleon offered them amnesty if the rebels laid down their arms, concessions on religion and an end to laws that attacked emigres
* When they refused, in January 1800, Napoleon ordered the army to crush the Chouannerie
* By April, they were wiped out
* There were no more widespread royalist unrest during the Napoleonic period
* The royalist threat was still there, but they were limited to assassination attempts and relied on foreign support
