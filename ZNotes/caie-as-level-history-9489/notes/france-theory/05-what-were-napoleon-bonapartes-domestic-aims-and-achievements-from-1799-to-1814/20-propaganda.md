## **Propaganda**

* Key to Napoleon’s propaganda was attaching falsehoods to truth
* Napoleon had David paint him riding on a white stallion during his campaign, when in reality he rode on a mule
* The campaign in Egypt was muddled and murderous, yet it was presented as a triumph
* His other military actions abroad undoubtedly assisted in cementing his popularity at home
* The *Arc De Triomphe*, begun in 1809, was a celebration of Napoleon’s victories
* Festivals were organised in Napoleon’s honour
* Napoleon also aimed to reconcile people through a policy called *ralliement*
* Napoleon would forgive and forget what people had done in the past if they were loyal to the regime
* For example, he allowed *émigrés* to return if they accepted his authority
