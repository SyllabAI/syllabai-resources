## **Education**

* Education for ordinary people was neglected by Napoleon
* Napoleon often declared his beliefs in equal opportunities for all, something he called ‘careers open to talents’, but he generally failed to ensure that this was carried out in practice
* There was no provision for the education of girls
* Napoleon recognised the need for well-trained and loyal servants
* In 1802, he instituted a system of state-run *lycées* or schools, for the sons of officers and nobles
* The curriculum was closely supervised and free thinking was discouraged
* In 1808, Napoleon also set up the Imperial University to oversee the curriculum and inspect schools
