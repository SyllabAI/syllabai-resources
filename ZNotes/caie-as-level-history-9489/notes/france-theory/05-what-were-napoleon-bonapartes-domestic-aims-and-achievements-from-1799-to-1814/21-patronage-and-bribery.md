## **Patronage And Bribery**

* To increase support for Napoleon, he issued a considerable amount of gifts, money, land, titles, honours and government appointments
* After peace with Britain in 1802, Napoleon instituted the *legion d’honneur*, to reward those who sacrificed themselves and to encourages other to emulate them
* Between 1804 and 1808, new titles were created for the officials of the new imperial court
* On 1 March 1808, Napoleon went further and created a new imperial nobility
* These new titles were awarded directly by the Emperor for state service
* Members of Senate would receive large country estates and a large annual income
