## **The End Of The Consulate**

* In 1804, Napoleon abandoned the Consulate and crowned himself emperor
* Pope Pius VII was in attendance to represent the approval of God and the Church
* Napoleon claimed that the change would unify the country
* The establishment of the empire was confirmed by popular approval though plebiscites, giving the impression of a democracy
* However these votes were doctored
* This move allowed him to have hereditary succession
* This also could have reconciled royalists to a certain extent
