## **Repression System**

* The repression of opposition depended on the effectiveness of local government, who were directed by the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Police
* Officials like the prefects were crucial as they were the link between the government and the provinces
* The Ministry of Police’s main purpose was to keep a close watch on all forms of subversion
* This was done through spies and informants
* Joseph Fouche, the first minister, reported daily to Napoleon
* Napoleon also had a network of spies to check on Fouche’s reports
* Department prefects sent reports to Napoleon on public opinion
* The Ministry of Police also had responsibility over censorship, prison surveillance and monitoring food prices
* This system prevent opposition from becoming open and denied critics publicity
