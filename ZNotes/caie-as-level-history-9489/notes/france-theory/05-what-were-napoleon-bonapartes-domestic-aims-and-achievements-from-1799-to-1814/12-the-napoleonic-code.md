## **The Napoleonic Code**

* Since the start of the Revolution, all governments had tried to bring order to the system of law in France, but few had made any significant progress
* To address this problem, Napoleon decided to issue a unified code of law to replace the complex system that existed at the time.
* Many of the old laws were based on ancient traditions, others were dictated by the Church
* This generated confusion for governments and the population
* Therefore, Napoleon asked the Council of State to design one set of laws for all parts of France
* Napoleon took personal interest in the process, attending many meetings
* While developing these reforms, Napoleon used men of talent, not caring about their background
* The outcome of this was the Civil Code of 1804 or Napoleonic Code
* This gave the country a common set of laws that were imposed by Napoleon and could not be challenged by traditions and local rights
