## **Reforms Under The Consulate**

* All aspects of life were reformed by Napoleon
* Napoleon had no ideology, so he would adopt any ideas that would ensure order and efficiency
* However, this meant his regime would be full of contradictions
* For example, in theory Napoleon’s laws guaranteed equality, but in practice they favoured employers over workers, men over women
* Also, all laws were proclaimed by Napoleon using his powers
* Napoleon claimed to rule by the will of the people and the will of God, a claim made by the monarchs before him
