## **Revisions Of The Constitution**

* In 1802, the Constitution was revised
* The Tribunate would be purged of troublesome elements
* Napoleon was made first consul for life
* Napoleon could also name his successor
* This essentially made him a sort of hereditary monarch
* In 1804, a further revision was made which recognised Napoleon as Emperor
* Plebiscites were held for both revisions and the results were similar to the one in 1800 - overwhelming support for Napoleon
