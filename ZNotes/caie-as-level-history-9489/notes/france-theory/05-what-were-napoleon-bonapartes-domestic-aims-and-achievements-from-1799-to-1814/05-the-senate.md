## **The Senate**

* Sieyes intended the Senate to act as a brake on the executive
* However, Napoleon was able to turn it into an instrument of his personal power
* Senators were appointed for life, given a substantial salary and were rewarded with gifts of land and money
* Therefore, when Napoleon appointed extra members, they were all loyal to Napoleon
* When the Senate increased from 80 to 140 by 1814, most of the extra members were appointed by Napoleon
* This meant the Senate would essentially accept everything Napoleon suggested
