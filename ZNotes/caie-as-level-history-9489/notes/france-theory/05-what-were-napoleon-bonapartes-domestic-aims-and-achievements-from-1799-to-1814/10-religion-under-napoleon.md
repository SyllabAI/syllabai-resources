## **Religion Under Napoleon**

* The Concordat with Pius VII in July 1801 was a major achievement by Napoleon
* After the election of the new pope, Pius VII, Napoleon sought reconciliation between France and Rome
* Napoleon’s decision was purely from a political standpoint
* He thought of religion as a way to bond people
* This would also break up the opposition of the Catholics and the vast majority of French people would have less reasons to oppose him
* However, Napoleon’s price for reconciliation was high
* He gained effective control of the church, appointments of clerics, a clerical oath of loyalty to the state and acceptance of the loss of church lands during the Revolution
* In return, he recognised Catholicism as the ‘religion of the majority of Frenchmen’, the restoration of Sunday worship and state responsibility for the payment of the clergy
* After this agreement, Napoleon added the ‘Organic Articles’ to the Concordat in 1802, without the Pope’s agreement
* These articles introduced religious toleration and made the Protestant and Jewish churches subject to state authority
