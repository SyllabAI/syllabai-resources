## **Legislature**

* Neither Sieyès nor Napoleon wanted the legislature to have a dominant place in the Constitution
* The legislative process was divided between four bodies:
  * The Council of State
  * The Tribunate
  * A legislative body
  * The Senate
* The Council of State drew up proposals
* The Tribunate could discuss legislations, but could not vote on it
* The legislative body would vote on legislation but could not discuss it
* The Senate considered whether the proposed legislation conformed to the Constitution
