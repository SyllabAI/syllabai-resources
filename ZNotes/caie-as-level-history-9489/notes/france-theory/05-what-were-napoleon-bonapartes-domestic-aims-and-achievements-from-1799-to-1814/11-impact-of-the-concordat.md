## **Impact Of The Concordat**

* In the shorter term, the Concordat reconciled the Catholic church to the regime
* It helped pacify unrest in the Vendée
* Priests were now a vehicle for Napoleon’s propaganda as they had to say prayers for Napoleon
* Meanwhile, most of the population was content as they had their churches, priests and kept the land the Church had forfeited
* Napoleon also insisted on Pius VII to attend his coronation ceremony to suggest that his reign was authorised by God himself
