## **Censorship**

* Napoleon did not believe in freedom of the press
* In January 1800, the number of newspapers in Paris was reduced from 73 to 13, and in 1810 to 4
* The press bureau of the Police kept a close watch on all publications
* Napoleon silenced actors, exiled writers, sacked journalists and imprisoned club leaders
* A lot of articles could only be viewed in the government newspaper *Le Moniteur*
* Plays, books and art were censored as well
