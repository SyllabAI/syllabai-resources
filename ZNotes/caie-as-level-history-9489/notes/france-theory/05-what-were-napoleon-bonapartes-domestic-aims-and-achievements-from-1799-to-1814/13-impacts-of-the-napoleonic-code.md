## **Impacts Of The Napoleonic Code**

* The Code kept many of the achievements of the Revolution, but also included a number of illiberal measures
* On one hand it confirmed the abolition of feudalism, equality before the law and freedom of conscience
* On the other, it reintroduced slavery to the French colonies, gave employers the upper hand in wage disputes and workers were forced to carry a passbook or *livret*, effectively limiting their freedom of movement
* Many of the rights that women had been granted since 1789 were reversed
* The authority of a husband over his wife and children were restored
