## **New Constitution**

* Sieyes and Napoleon recognised the need for the Constitution to acknowledge the principle of popular sovereignty
* However, they wanted to ensure that the common people were excluded from effective political power
* As a compromise, they agreed on the principle of universal manhood suffrage, but ensured the electing system would be indirect and have limited influence on the legislature and executive
* This Constitution essentially gave Napoleon effective political power in France
* To lend legitimacy to the new Constitution, a plebiscite was held in 1800
* The Constitution was supported by over 3 million votes to around 1,500
