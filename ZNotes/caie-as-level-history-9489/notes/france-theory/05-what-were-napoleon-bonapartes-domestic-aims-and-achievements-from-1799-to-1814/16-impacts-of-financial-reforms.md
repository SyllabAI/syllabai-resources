## **Impacts Of Financial Reforms**

* By 1802, Napoleon was able to balance the budget
* When compared with the financial chaos of the previous regimes, both the Consulate and the Empire were much more successful
* The currency was stabilised, public debts were honoured and the wages of public officials and the army were paid
* However, State military expenditure increase from around 700 million francs in 1806 to over 1 billion in 1813
* The military defeats of 1813-14 also removed this source of income and marked a renewed period of financial instability
