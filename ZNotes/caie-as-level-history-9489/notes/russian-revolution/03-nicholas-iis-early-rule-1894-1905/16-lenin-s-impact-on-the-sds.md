## **Lenin’s Impact On The SDs**

* When Lenin returned to western Russia in 1900, he set about changing the SD to comply with his ideas
* With a colleague, Julius Martov, he founded a party newspaper, *Iskra* (The Spark), which he used to put his case to the party members
* Lenin often criticised Plekhanov for being to broad
* Lenin insisted that Russia would need to be led by a party of dedicated, professional revolutionaries
