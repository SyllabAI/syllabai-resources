## **Moscow Uprising Suppressed**

* On 7 December, a group of Bolsheviks, Mensheviks and SRs came together in the recently formed Moscow Soviet to organise a general strike
* They seized a number of key installations in an attempt to take over the whole city
* However, the army was rushed to Moscow and heavy artillery was used
* The soviet members surrendered on 18 December
* The 12 day uprising resulted in the deaths of over 1000 people
