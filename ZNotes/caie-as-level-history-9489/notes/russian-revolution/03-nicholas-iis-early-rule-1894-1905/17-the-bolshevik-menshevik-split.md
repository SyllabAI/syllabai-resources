## **The Bolshevik-Menshevik Split**

* The disputes between Lenin and Plekhanov came to a boiling point during the meeting of the SD Party in 1903
* Plekhanov wanted a broad-based party, while Lenin wanted a small, tightly knit party
* Julius Martov, Lenin's co-editor of the *Iskra*, supported Plekhanov’s ideas, which created a deep divide between him and Lenin
* There was a vote for members to see who had the majority, but they were split evenly
* However, Lenin was able to claim that his supporters were the majority, so they were called the Bolsheviks (majority), while Martov’s group was called the Mensheviks (minority)
* By 1912, the Bolsheviks and Mensheviks had become two distinct, conflicting Marxist parties.
* A Bolshevik daily paper, *Pravda* (The Truth), was first published in 1912.
