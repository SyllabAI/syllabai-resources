## **The Populists**

* The Populists regarded the future of Russia as being in the hands of the peasants
* They would go to the countryside to ‘educate’ the peasants on political matters
* The peasants tended to regard them as prattlers who had no knowledge of real life
* Some Populists turned to terrorism and created a group called ‘The People’s Will’ in 1879.
* The group successfully killed Alexander II
* This act weakened the Populists instead of strengthening it as it gave rise to a even more repressive tsar
* However, the Populists did establish a violent anti-tsarist tradition
