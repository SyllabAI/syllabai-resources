## Anti-Semitism

* Over 600 new measures were introduced to impose heavy social, political and economic restrictions on the Jewish population.
* A group of ultra-conservative Russian nationalists, known as the ‘Black Hundreds’, were notorious for their attacks on Jews.
* During the reign of Nicholas II, the number of pogroms (attacks on Jews) increased sharply, which was proof of the tsarist regime’s support of the terrorising of the Jews.
