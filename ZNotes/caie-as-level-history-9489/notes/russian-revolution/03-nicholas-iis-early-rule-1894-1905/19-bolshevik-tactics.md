## **Bolshevik Tactics**

* Lenin and his followers set up training schools for revolutionaries to infiltrate workers organisations
* They also spent their time raising money for the party, usually through the use of violence
* Despite these activities, the police did not see them as a major challenge to the tsar
* The Bolsheviks were no more than 25,000 strong in February 1917
* Yet, they were able to take over the government due to their readiness
