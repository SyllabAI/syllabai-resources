## The Importance Of The Railways

* Much of the foreign capital that Witte had raised was invested in railways
* Witte’s special project was the trans-Siberian railway, which was constructed between 1891 and 1902.
* The line stretched 6000 km from Moscow to Vladivostok and was intended to connect the remoter regions of the central and eastern empire with the industrial west.
  * This encouraged the migration of workers to areas where they were most needed.
* The railway was also a symbol of Russian enterprise, and major improvements in transport would boost exports and foreign trade.
