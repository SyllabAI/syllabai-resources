## **Witte’s Problems**

* Witte’s policies had a major impact on the expansion of the Russian economy, but critics have pointed out drawbacks in his reforms
  * Witte made Russia too dependent on foreign loans and investments
  * Witte neglected vital light engineering areas, such as machine tool production, which would have helped to modernise manufacturing
  * Witte paid no attention to Russia’s agricultural needs
* However, these criticisms need to be balanced by the problems he faced
* The army often took first priority which interfered with his scheme for railway construction and the building of new industrial plants
* Witte was largely limited by the court and government as they resisted change and disliked Witte
* In 1903, the tsar forced him to resign despite his great talent
