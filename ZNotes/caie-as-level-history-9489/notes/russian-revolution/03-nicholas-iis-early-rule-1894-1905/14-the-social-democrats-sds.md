## **The Social Democrats (SDs)**

* The SDs came into being in 1898
* Their aim was to achieve revolution through the ideas of Karl Marx
* Marx was a German revolutionary who advanced the idea that human society followed scientific principles
* Marx believed that the behaviour of people were governed by social laws, similar to the laws of physics and chemistry
* Marx believed that the critical determinant of behaviour was class struggle
* When a country enters the industrial age, Marx predicts that the proletariats (workers) would rise against their bosses who would be exploiting them
* Then afterwards, the workers would remove all opposition and the perfect society would emerge
* This was particularly pertinent in Russia because of the great spurt in the 1890s which created the industrial conditions Russia would need for the worker’s revolution
* The first Marxist revolutionary was George Plekhanov, who founded the SD party and translated Marx’s writings into Russian
* Despite this, a number of members were impatient with his theoretical approach
* They wanted a more active revolutionary programme, which Lenin would provide
