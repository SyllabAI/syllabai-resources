## **Bloody Sunday**

* The 1905 Revolution began with Blood Sunday
* On the 22 January, Father Georgy Gapon, an Orthodox priest, led a peaceful march of workers and their families to the Winter Palace in St Petersburg
* Their intention was to present a petition to the tsar to relieve the desperate conditions
* However, the march induced panic in the police forces and the marchers were fired on
* Around 200 were killed and many more injured
* Although Nicholas II was absent from St Petersburg, this event gravely damaged the traditional image of the tsar as the ‘Little Father’ (promoting the tsar’s paternal care for the people)
