## **The Constitutional Democrats (Kadets)**

* This was the largest liberal party
* They wanted Russia to develop into a constitutional monarchy
* They were made up of progressive landlords, smaller industrial entrepreneurs and members of the professional class
* They were the most outspoken critics in the Dumas
* They played a significant role in the February Revolution in 1917
* The Kadet’s program included:
  * An all Russian constituent assembly
  * Full equality and civil rights for all citizens
  * The ending of censorship
  * The abolition of the mortgage repayments on land
  * The recognition of trade unions and the right to strike
  * The introduction of universal and free education
