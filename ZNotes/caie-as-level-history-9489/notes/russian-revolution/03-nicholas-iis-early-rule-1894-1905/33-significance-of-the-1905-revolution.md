## **Significance Of The 1905 Revolution**

* It must be noted that none of the revolutionaries played a major role, with the exception of Trotsky
* Furthermore, the peasants and liberals readily accepted the reforms, perhaps suggesting that they were not ready for revolution
* The concessions the tsar granted were not very impactful
* The introduction of dumas did not limit the tsar’s power because of the Fundamental Laws of April 1906 which gave the Tsar the power to dissolve the duma whenever he wanted
