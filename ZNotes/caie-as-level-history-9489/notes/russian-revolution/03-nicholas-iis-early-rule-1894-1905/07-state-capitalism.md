## State Capitalism

* Witte was impressed by the results of the Industrial Revolution in Western Europe and the USA.
* He argued that Russia could successfully modernise by planning along the same lines.
* Witte judged that Russia’s greatest need was to acquire capital to invest in industry.
* He negotiated large loans and investments from abroad and imposed heavy taxes and high interest rates at home
* At the same time, he encouraged the inflow of foreign capital and limited the import of foreign goods.
* In 1897, the Russian currency was put on the gold standard
* The hope was that this would create financial stability and so would encourage international investment
* The aim was largely successful, but consumers at home had to pay higher prices due to tariffs and the increased value of the rouble
