## **End Of The Great Spurt By 1900**

* The improvement of the Russian economy in the 1890s was not simply the work of Witte
* It was part of a worldwide industrial boom, which by 1900 had ended and serious international trade recession had set in
* This was a major problem for Russia as industrial expansion had led to a ballooning of population in towns and cities
* The facilities for accommodating the influx of workers were inadequate, so there was severe overcrowding
* When the recession set in, there was widespread unemployment, which increased unrest in cities and urban areas.
