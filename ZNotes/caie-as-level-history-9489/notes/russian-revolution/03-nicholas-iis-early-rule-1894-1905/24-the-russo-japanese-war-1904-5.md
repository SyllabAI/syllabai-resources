## **The Russo-Japanese War, 1904-5**

* Russia had three main motives for going to war with Japan
  * To pursue an expansionist policy in the Far East, to make up for what it saw as its relative decline in Europe
  * To obtain an ice-free port, as all its own major ports would freeze in the winter
  * To distract the people from Russia’s domestic problems by rallying  the nation in a patriotic struggle
* The Russians believed Japan was an inferior nation and would be no match for them
* The war revealed that Russia had severely underestimated the strength of Japan
* The Japanese were far better equipped and prepared
* After a long siege, Port Arthur fell to Japan in January 1905
* Japan then seized the key Manchurian town of Mukden
* The final humiliation for Russia was the Russian Baltic fleet which took eight months to reach its destination, only to be blown up by a Japanese fleet
* The tsarist government had to make peace and created the Treaty of Portsmouth signed in September 1905
* Within the treaty, Russia would withdraw its forces from Manchuria and accepted Japanese control of Korea and Port Arthur
* Russia lost the war because of its military leaders
* The incompetence of the government was exposed, stirring social unrest
* This defeat was a huge factor in the 1905 Revolution
