## Nicholas II’s Upbringing

* As a young man, Nicholas had been tutored at court by Konstantin Pobedonostsev.
* He was known as the ‘Grand Inquisitor’ because of his repressive attitude.
* He was also against all forms of democracy and condemned the growth of parliamentary democracy in Europe.
* In his mind, autocracy was the only possible government for Imperial Russia.
* He played a major part in shaping the reactionary attitude of Alexander III and Nicholas II.
* ‘The Reaction’ coincided with a period of remarkable economic expansion.
* However, the tsarist government, with its resistance to change, denied itself the chance to adapt to a changing world.
