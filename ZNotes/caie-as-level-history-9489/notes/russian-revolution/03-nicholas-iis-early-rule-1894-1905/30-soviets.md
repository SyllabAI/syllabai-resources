## **Soviets**

* By October 1905, the industrial unrest had grown into a general strike
* In a number of cities, most notably in St Petersburg and Moscow, workers formed into elected soviets (councils made up of elected representatives)
* The soviets began as organisations to represent the workers’ demands for better conditions
* However, their potential for political agitation was immediately recognised by revolutionaries
* Leon Trotsky, a Menshevik, became chairman of the St Petersburg soviet and organised several strikes
