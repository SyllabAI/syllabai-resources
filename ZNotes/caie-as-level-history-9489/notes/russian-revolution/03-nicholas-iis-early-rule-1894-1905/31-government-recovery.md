## **Government Recovery**

* The government had to give concessions to the range of oppositions
* The Liberals were appeased by Witte’s October Manifesto in which he accepted the creation of a legislative duma.
* The manifesto also promised introducing a range of civil rights, including freedom of speech, assembly and worship, and the legalising of trade unions.
* This largely satisfied the liberals
* The peasants were appeased in November as mortgage repayments would be progressively reduced and abolished all together
* This resulted in a drop in the number of land seizures by the peasants and a decline in lawlessness in the countryside
* Now, the government was only seriously opposed by the industrial workers
* The government felt strong enough to use the army that just returned from the Far East to suppress the workers as they were still loyal to the tsar
* The army stormed the headquarters of the St Petersburg soviet where the leaders, like Trotsky, were arrested
