## **The Social Revolutionaries (SRs)**

* This party grew directly from the Populist movement
* Since the urban workforce was rapidly growing due to the economic spurt of the 1890s, the SRs began to agitate the workers
* They wanted to to include all people in society who wanted to see the end of tsardom
* However, they were weakened by internal disagreements
* The SRs could be split into the anarchists (Left Social Revolutionaries) and revolutionaries (Right Social Revolutionaries)
* The anarchists wanted to continue the policy of terrorism
* The revolutionaries were more moderate and were prepared to cooperate with other parties
* Between 1901 and 1905, the terrorist faction dominated the party
* The SRs were responsible for over 2000 political assassinations
* These killings did little to encourage the workers to cooperate with them
* Their land policy was the main reason they were the most popular party with the peasants
* From 1906, the party was a collection of radical groups, rather than a united party
* Regardless, before the Bolsheviks outlawed them after the 1917 Revolution, the SRs remained the party with the largest popular following in Russia
