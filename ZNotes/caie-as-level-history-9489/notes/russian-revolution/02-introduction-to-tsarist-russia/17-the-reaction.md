## The Reaction

* This was the Statute of State Security, 1881
* Special government controlled courts were set up
* Officials sympathetic towards liberal ideas were removed
* The power of the *Okharana*, the tsarist secret police, were extended
* Censorship was tightened
* This statute remained until 1917
* The University statute in 1887 brought universities under strict government control
* The Zemstva Act in 1890 decreased the independence of local councils and empowered government officials
