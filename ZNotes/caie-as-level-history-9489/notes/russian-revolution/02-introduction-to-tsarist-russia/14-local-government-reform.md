## Local Government Reform

* Alexander II’s reign (1855-81) coincided with the loss of the Crimean War.
* Therefore, Alexander II set up reforms like the Emancipation of the Serfs in 1861, followed three years later by the introduction of elected rural councils known as *zemstvos*.
* Along with this, they implemented the *Mir*, which was a local organisation that would keep order and collect payments (tax, mortgage, etc.)
