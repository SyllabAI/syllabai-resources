## Weaknesses Within The Army

* The higher ranks of the army were reserved for the aristocracy.
* Commissions were bought and sold so there was little room for promotion on merit.
* This weakened the fighting force, but Russia was not engaged in any major conflict (other than the Crimean War 1854-6).
* The army’s active service was essentially putting down national risings or serious disturbances within or along the borders.
