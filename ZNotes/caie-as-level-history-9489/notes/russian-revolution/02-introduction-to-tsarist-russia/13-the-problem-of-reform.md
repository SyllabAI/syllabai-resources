## The Problem Of Reform

* Many in the ruling class accepted that major reforms were needed.
* However, there were major disagreements between the ‘Westerners’ and ‘Slavophiles’.
* ‘Westerners’ believed that Russia would need to adopt the best features of the political and economic systems of the countries of Western Europe.
* ‘Slavophiles’ were those who regarded Western values as corrupting and wanted to preserve their historical Slav traditions.
* The dispute between these groups made it difficult to achieve reforms.
* Another barrier was Russia's autocratic structure.
* There were no representative institutions, so only the tsar could implement change.
* However, reforms were usually implemented by individual tsars in response to some form of national crisis or humiliation.
