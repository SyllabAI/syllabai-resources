## The Bureaucracy (Civil Service)

* Another pillar of tsarism
* Ironically, this was the area with the most attempted reform but also the most corruption.
* Peter I (1683-1725) tried to modernise Russia by establishing a full-scale civil service with the aim of maintaining central government control throughout the empire.
* However, everything was in the hands of a set of men who first thought of their convenience and advantage.
* Ordinary civilians had no say since any challenges to the system were lost in bureaucratic procedures.
* This was a major source of discontent.
