## The Russian Army

* A key pillar of tsarism
* One common method to keep the ‘dark masses’ in check was to recruit them into the armed services.
* The lower ranks of the army and navy were largely filled by conscription, which was also used as a form of punishment.
* The Russian army was notorious for the severity of discipline and the grimness of the living conditions.
* It was a widespread belief that Russia needed a large army, so around 1.5 million men were in service.
* The cost to maintain this accounted, on average, for 45% of the government’s annual expenditure.
* Comparatively, only 4% of the budget was spent on education.
