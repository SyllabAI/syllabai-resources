## The Limited Nature Of The Reforms

* Alexander II was not a supporter of reform, he used it to lessen opposition to the tsarist system.
* He hoped that these reforms would attract the support of the intelligentsia, which it did.
* However, he did not want to compromise his powers so he returned to the tsarist tradition of oppression.
* He was assassinated by a group of Social Revolutionaries known as the People’s Will, which led to even more severe measures being imposed by Alexander III (1881-94).
* These were so oppressive they were known as ‘the Reaction’.
