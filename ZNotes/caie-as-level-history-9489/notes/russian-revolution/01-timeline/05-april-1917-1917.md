## April 1917 - 1917

* 3 April 1917, Lenin returned to Petrograd
* 4 April 1917, Lenin issued his ‘April Theses’
* 3-6 July 1917, Failed Bolshevik ‘July Days’ uprising
* 8 July 1917, Kerensky becomes prime minister
* 1 September 1917, Kornilov’s abortive march on Petrograd
* 9 October 1917, the Petrograd Soviet set up the Military Revolutionary Committee
* 23 October 1917, Bolsheviks rose against Kerensky’s government
* 24-25 October 1917, Bolsheviks take control of Petrograd
* 25-26 October 1917, Kerensky flees Petrograd
* 26 October 1917, Bolsheviks established *Sovnarkom*, with Lenin as chairman
* 27 October 1917, Lenin informs the Congress of Soviets that the Bolsheviks had taken power in their name
* November 1917, November Decrees on Land and Workers’ Control
