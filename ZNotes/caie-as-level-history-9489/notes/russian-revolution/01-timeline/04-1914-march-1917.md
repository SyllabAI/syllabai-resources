## 1914 - March 1917

* 1914, General strike in St Petersburg, Germany declares war on Russia
* 28 June 1914, the Assassination of Franz Ferdinand
* 28 July 1914, Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia
* 30 July 1914, Russia's full mobilisation orders given
* 1 August 1914, Germany declared war on Russia, fourth duma suspended
* June-July 1915, the Fourth Duma reconvened
* 25 June 1915, the Progressive Bloc formed in the Duma
* 22 August 1915, Nicholas II put himself in charge of the military
* 1 December 1916, Rasputin murdered
* 18 February 1917, Strike began at Putilov factories in Petrograd
* 23 February 1917, International Women’s Day, Widespread workers’ demonstrations
* 25 February 1917, a General strike began
* 27 February 1917, Unofficial meeting of the duma, the First meeting of the Petrograd Soviet
* 28 February 1917, Nicholas II was prevented from returning to Petrograd, Provisional Government claimed authority
* 1 March 1917, Petrograd Soviet issued Soviet Order Number 1
* 2 March 1917, a Provisional government formed from the Duma committee
* 4 March 1917, Tsar’s abdication was publicly announced
* 12 March 1917, Bolsheviks begin to return to Petrograd
