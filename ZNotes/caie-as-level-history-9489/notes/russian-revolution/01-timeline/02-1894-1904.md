## 1894 - 1904

* 1894, the Start of Nicholas II’s reign
* 1894-1906, Sergei Witte’s economic reforms
* 1897, the Jewish Bund was formed
* 1898, Social Democrats (SD) Party formed
* 1901, Social Revolutionaries (SR) Party formed
* 1903, the SD party split into Mensheviks and Bolsheviks
* 1904-5, Russo-Japanese War
* 1904, the Union of Liberation formed
