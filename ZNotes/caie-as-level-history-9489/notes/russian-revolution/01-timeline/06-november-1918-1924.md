## 1918 - 1924

* 1918 - 20, Russian Civil War and foreign intervention
* 1918-21, War Communism
* January 1918, Bolsheviks forcibly dissolved the Constituent Assembly, Red Army established
* March 1918, Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
* June 1918, Decree on Nationalisation
* July 1918, Forced grain requisitions began, and the murder of the Tsar and his family.
* September 1918, Red Terror
* In March 1919, the Comintern was established, and the Bolshevik Party was renamed the Communist Party.
* April 1920, the Invading Red Army driven from Poland
* March 1921, Kronstadt Rising, Introduction of New Economic Policy
* 1921-4, NEP leads to economic recovery
* January 1924, Death of Lenin
