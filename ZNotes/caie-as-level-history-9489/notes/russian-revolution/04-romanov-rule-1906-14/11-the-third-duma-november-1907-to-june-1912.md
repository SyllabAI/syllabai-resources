## **The Third Duma, November 1907 To June 1912**

* Despite the failure of the first two dumas, the tsar continued with them because of two main reasons:
  * The tsar wanted to protect Russia’s image as a democratic nation
  * The duma became more dominated by right-wings (due to changes in the voting system), the opposite of the first two dumas meaning there was a lot less criticisms about tsardom
* The new make up of the dumas meant Stolypin could continue with his land reforms
* The duma was able to pass social reforms like schools for the poor and national insurance for industrial workers
