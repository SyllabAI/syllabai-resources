## **The Second Duma, February To June 1907**

* The new assembly was radical and anti-government due to the Vyborg fiasco
* The number of right-wing parties also increased, creating considerable disagreement in the duma
* Stolypin was willing to work with the duma, but his land programme was rejected
* The duma also criticised the imperial army, resulting in the duma being dissolved again by Nicholas II
