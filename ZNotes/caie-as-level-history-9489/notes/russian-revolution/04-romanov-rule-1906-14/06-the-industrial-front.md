## **The Industrial Front**

* From 1908 to 1914 there was an overall increase in industrial output of 8.5%
* However, many workers were not paid well and working conditions were tough
* The trade unions were very weak and there was minimal legal protection for the workers
* The large number of strikes in the pre-1914 years and the general strike in 1914 show the worker’s dissatisfaction with the conditions
