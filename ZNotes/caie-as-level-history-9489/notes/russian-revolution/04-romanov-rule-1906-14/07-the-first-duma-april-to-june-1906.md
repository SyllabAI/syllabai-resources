## **The First Duma, April To June 1906**

* Early in 1906, France gave a substantial loan to Russia
* This lessened the dumas ability to exercise financial control over the government
* The opening of the first duma also coincided with the Fundamental Laws
* The Fundamental Laws included:
  * Every law needed the tsar’s approval
  * He could dissolve the dumas at any time
* In addition, the dumas would have two chambers
* One would be an elected lower house, the other would be a state council where members would be appointed by the tsar
* The state council had the right to veto, depriving the first chamber of any real power
