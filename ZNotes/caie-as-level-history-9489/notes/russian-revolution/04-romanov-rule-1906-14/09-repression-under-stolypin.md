## **Repression Under Stolypin**

* Martial law (placing the population under military authority) was proclaimed
* Disturbances were put down by military courts
* Between 1906 and 1911 there were over 2500 executions in Russia
* The Kadet failure meant it could not recover from its humiliating defeat
* The liberal cause was now discredited, allowing the left and right to argue for more extreme actions
