## **Duma Radicalism, The Vyborg Appeal**

* Many in the duma were bitter, so they took a radical stance
* They demanded the rights and powers of the duma to be increased
* Nicholas II reacted by dissolving the duma
* In frustration, 200 Kadet and Labourist deputies assembled in Vyborg, Finland, where they drew up an appeal, urging the people of Russia to defy their government
* They wanted the people to stop paying taxes and to disobey conscription orders
* The Duma Manifesto was met with nearly-universal indifference from the people. That allowed the government to use repressive methods to silence its most outspoken liberal critics.
* The Vyborg group was arrested and disallowed from re-elections in the duma
