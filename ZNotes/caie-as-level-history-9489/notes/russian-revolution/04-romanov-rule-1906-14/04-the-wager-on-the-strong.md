## The ‘Wager On The Strong’

* In 1906-7, Stolypin introduced measures to restore the peasants’ sense of security.
  * Farmers were urged to use the more efficient fenced field pattern, which existed in Western Europe.
  * Peasant grouping in *obshchinas* was discouraged, and incentives were given to peasants to return to individual farming.
  * A Land Bank was set up to provide funds for independent peasants
  * Schemes for voluntary resettlement of the peasants were implemented, which aimed to populate the empire’s remoter areas like Siberia
* Stolypin defined his policy as a ‘wager on the strong.’
* He hoped to create a prosperous and productive peasantry who would support the tsarist system because of their newfound wealth
