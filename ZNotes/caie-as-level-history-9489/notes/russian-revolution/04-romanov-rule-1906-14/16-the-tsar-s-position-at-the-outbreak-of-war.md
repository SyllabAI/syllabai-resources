## **The Tsar’s Position At The Outbreak Of War**

* Tsardom was in a relatively strong position and had emerged stronger from the 1905 Revolution
* The army was still loyal and the three groups who rose up were appeased
* The October Manifesto had not decreased the tsar’s power
* The Dumas had no threat to the tsar’s powers
* The liberals could not openly challenge the tsar
* Revolutionary parties were not capable of taking over the government as many of the leading figures were in exile
* Russia was still feared by European countries and regarded as a great military power
* The tsar believed that only a minority of industrial workers were opposed to his rule
* There were still economic and social problems, but those had been here for a long time
* Russia’s economy was overall behind, but their railways allowed for migration and the agricultural and industrial sectors had grown by 6% by 1914
* There were no clear signs of the revolution to come
