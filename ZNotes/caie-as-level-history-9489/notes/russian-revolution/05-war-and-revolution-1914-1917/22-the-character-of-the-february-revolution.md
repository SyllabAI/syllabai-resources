## **The Character Of The February Revolution**

* Between 1500 and 2000 people were killed or wounded in the February disturbances
* The lack of direction and leadership at the top stood out as a factor
* The tsardom had collapsed from within
* Revolutionary pressures had no direct effects
* It was the tsar’s biggest supporters who were the first to turn their backs on the tsar
* The defection of the tsar’s supporters and Nicholas II’s failure to resist caused the fall of the Romanov dynasty
