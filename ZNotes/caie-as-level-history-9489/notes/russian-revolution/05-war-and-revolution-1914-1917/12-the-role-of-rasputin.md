## **The Role Of Rasputin**

* Gregory Rasputin contracted much of the hatred centered on the tsarist system
* Rasputin was known as the ‘mad monk’ who was rumoured to have healing powers
* The Empress Alexandra wanted to cure her son, Alexei, the heir to the throne, of his haemophilia
* She invited Rasputin, who apparently eased Alexei’s conditions when Rasputin was with him
* However, scandals followed as Alexandra was German and Rasputin was hated
* When Nichola was absent at military headquarters, it was effectively Alexandra and Rasputin running the country, a German women and a debauched monk
