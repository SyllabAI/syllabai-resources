## **The Breakdown Of Order**

* The tsar was 600 km away from Petrograd in his military headquarters
* The tsarina sent news of the disturbances
* The tsar told the commander of the Petrograd garrison, General Khabalov, to restore order
* However the police and the garrison troops were disobeying orders
* By 26 February, all but a few thousand of the original 150,000 Petrograd garrison troops had deserted
* Reinforcements for the garrison under General Ivanov were also depleted by desertions
