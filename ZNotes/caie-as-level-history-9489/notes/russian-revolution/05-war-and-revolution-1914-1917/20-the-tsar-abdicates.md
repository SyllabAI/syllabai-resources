## **The Tsar Abdicates**

* On 28 February, Nicholas decided to return to Petrograd
* However, his train was redirected by mutinous soldiers to Pskov, 300 km from Petrograd
* They advised the tsar to abdicate as the situation in Petrograd was desperate
* Nicholas agreed and signed on 2 March to nominate his brother, the Grand Duke Michael as the new tsar
* Michael was not willing to accept the offer and refused on the pretext that it had not been accepted by a Russian constituent assembly (a gathering of elected representatives of the Russian people)
* This spelt the end for tsardom
