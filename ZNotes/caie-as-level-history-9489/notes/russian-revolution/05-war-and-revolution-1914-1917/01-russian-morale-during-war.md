# War and Revolution 1914 - 1917

## Russian Morale During War

* Nicholas II became the symbol of the nation’s resistance
* All deputies in the duma, except for 5 Bolsheviks, pledged themselves to the national struggle
* Many in the socialist parties abandoned their policies and joined the national war effort
* Lenin called on all true revolutionaries to transform the war into a civil war
* However, everyone was against him, so the remaining Bolsheviks had to flee or go into hiding
