## **Provisional Committee Formed, 27 February**

* Rodzianko, on behalf of the duma, informed the tsar that only a major concession would offer any hope of preserving the imperial power
* Nicholas instead ordered the duma to dissolve
* A group of 12 members refused and remained in session as a ‘Provisional Committee’
* This was the first unconstitutional defiance of the tsar
* A leading SR member, Alexander Kerensky, also called for the tsar to stand down
