## **Key steps In The February Revolution**

* 18 February, strikes begin at the Putilov factories in Petrograd
* 23 February, International Women’s Day, socialist groups demand female equality
* 25 February, a general strike begins
* 26 February, desertion of Petrograd garrison
* 27 February, members from the duma form a Provisional Committee, Petrograd Soviet formed
* 28 February, Nicholas II prevented from returning to Petrograd
* 2 March Provisional Committee declared itself a Provisional Government, tsar abdication decree
* 3 March Provisional Government declares that a revolution has taken place
* 4 March, tsar’s abdication is publicly proclaimed
