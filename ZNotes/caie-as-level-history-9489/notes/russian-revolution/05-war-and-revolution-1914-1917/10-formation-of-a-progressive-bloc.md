## **Formation Of A ‘Progressive Bloc’**

* The duma forced Nicholas II to allow the duma to reassemble in July 1915
* The duma wanted to replace the tsar’s cabinet with members from the duma
* Nicholas II rejected this proposal, which destroyed any support from the progressive parties
* 236 of the 422 duma deputies formed into a ‘Progressive Bloc’
* The group included Kadets, Octobrists, Progressive Nationalists and the Party of Progressive Industrialists
* The SRs did not formally join, but did vote with them
* The bloc tried to persuade Nicholas II to introduce reforms, but he refused
* The government continued to shuffle its ministers to try find a competent team
* From 1915-16, there were 4 prime ministers, 3 foreign secretaries, 3 ministers of defence and 6 interior ministers
* None of them succeeded
