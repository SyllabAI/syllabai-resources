# From Provisional Government to the October Revolution

## The Dual Authority

* The Provisional Government, led by Prince Lvov, was really the old Duma in a new form
* The new government suffered from two crippling weaknesses:
  * It was not an elected body thus it did not have legitimate authority and had no constitutional claim on the loyalty of the Russian people
  * Its authority was limited by its unofficial partnership with the Petrograd Soviet
