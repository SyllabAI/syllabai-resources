## **Early Achievements Of The Provisional Government**

* A set of progressive measures were adopted by the Provisional Government:
  * Amnesty for all political prisoners
  * Trade unions were legally recognised
  * 8 hour days for industrial workers
  * Replacement of the tsarist police with a people’s militia
  * Full civil and religious freedoms being granted
  * Preparations made for the election of a constituent assembly
* These changes did not touch on the critical issue of the war and land
* It would be these two factors that would destroy the Dual Authority
