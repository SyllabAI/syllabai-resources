## **The Bolsheviks Take Power**

* The Bolsheviks took power extraordinarily quickly
* On the 27 October the All-Russian Congress of Soviets began their first session
* The Chairman Lev Kamenev then informed them that they were now the supreme authority in Russia since the Petrograd soviet had seized power
* Kamenev then read out a list of 14 names in the government, all of whom were Bolsheviks or left SRs
* Lenin was made head of the Russian ministers
* The SRs and Mensheviks walked out saying that it was a Bolshevik coup
