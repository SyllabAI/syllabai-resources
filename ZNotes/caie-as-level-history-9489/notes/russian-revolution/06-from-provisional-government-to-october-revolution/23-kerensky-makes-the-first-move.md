## **Kerensky Makes The First Move**

* An article by two members of the Bolshevik Central Committee, Gregor Zinoviev and Lev Kamenev, said that it would be a mistake to attack the government in current circumstances
* Kerensky interpreted this as meaning a date was already set
* Rather than waiting, he ordered a pre-emptive attack on the Bolsheviks
* On 23 October, the Bolshevik newspapers, *Pravda* and *Izvestia* were closed down by government troops
* An attempted round-up of leading Bolsheviks started
* The Bolsheviks no longer had a choice
* Lenin ordered the planned insurrection to begin
