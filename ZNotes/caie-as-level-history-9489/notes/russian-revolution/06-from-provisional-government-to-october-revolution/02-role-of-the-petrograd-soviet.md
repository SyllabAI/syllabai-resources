## **Role Of The Petrograd Soviet**

* The soviet did not intend to be an alternative government
* It regarded it self as a supervisor to make sure the interests of soldiers and workers were met
* However, the Provisional Government often seemed unsure of its own authority
* This uncertainty gave the soviet greater prominence
* The soviets were not opposed to the Provisional Government
* However, the soviets issued ‘Soviet Order Number 1’, which declared that in military matters, the orders of the Provisional Government were to be obeyed if it did not go against the soviet’s values
