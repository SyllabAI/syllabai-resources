## **Failure Of The Uprising**

* The uprising was confusing and disorderly
* Members of soviets were reluctant to make a real bid for power
* This meant loyal troops from the front quickly restored order
* It is not clear who started the rising
* A month before, at the first All-Russian Congress of Soviets, Lenin had declared the Bolsheviks were ready to take power
* However, many regarded that as a general intention
* A number of other revolutionaries had also demanded the Petrograd soviet take over the Provisional Government
* Yet none of this came to fruition during the July Days
