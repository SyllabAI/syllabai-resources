## **The Weakness Of The Non-Bolshevik Parties**

* Many parties had actually accepted the February Revolution as a genuine revolution
* Consequently, they cooperated with the Provisional Government
* For example, the Mensheviks believed the February Revolution was when the bourgeoisie had overthrown the old feudal forces, so they were content
* This allowed the Bolsheviks a powerful propaganda weapon
* Furthermore, the other parties did not oppose the continuation of the war, unlike Lenin
