## **Trotsky’s Role**

* The Bolshevik plan was made by Trotsky
* Lenin undoubtedly influenced the October revolution, but it was Trotsky who planned it
* The key to Trotsky’s success was the fact that he was chairman of the Petrograd soviet, which he was elected to in September
* On 9 October the soviet had set up the Military Revolutionary Committee (MRC) to organise the defence of Petrograd in case of attack
* Trotsky used his influence to have him made one of the three men appointed to run the MRC
* This meant he had an effective military force in Petrograd at his disposal
* It was also a legitimate force since it theoretically acted on the authority of the soviet
* When Lenin gave orders for the uprising, Trotsky directed the Red Guards to seize key vantage points in Petrograd
