## **The Bolshevik Position On The Land Question**

* The Bolsheviks did not have a land policy
* When Lenin returned in April, he declared that it would be pointless to work with the peasants
* However, Lenin asserted that because of the unique circumstances of post-tsarist Russia, the peasants were acting as a truly revolutionary force
* He therefore adapted Marxist theory to add Russian peasants in the proletarian cause
* Lenin simply stole the land policy from the SR programme, producing a slogan ‘Land to the Peasants’
* What this meant was the Bolsheviks accept the land seizures
* This increased support for the Bolsheviks in the countryside
* It also split the SRs as some of them started to align with the Bolsheviks
* They became to be known as the Left SRs
