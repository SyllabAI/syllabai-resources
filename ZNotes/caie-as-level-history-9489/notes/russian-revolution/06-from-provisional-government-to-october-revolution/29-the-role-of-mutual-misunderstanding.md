## **The Role Of Mutual Misunderstanding**

* Before October, the Provisional Government and the Bolsheviks both overestimated each other’s powers
* They delayed their moves in fear of their opposition
* Kerensky was also more afraid of the right, so Kornilov was regarded as a bigger threat then Lenin
* Meanwhile, Lenin thought if government agents ever found him, he would be shot
* This explained his constant absence
