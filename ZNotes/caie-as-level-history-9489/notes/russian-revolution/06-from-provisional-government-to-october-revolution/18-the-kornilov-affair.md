## **The Kornilov Affair**

* In August, Kerensky’s government became involved in the Kornilov Affair
* Kornilov declared that Russia and the government stood in grave danger of a socialist-inspired insurrection
* He intended to bring his loyal troops to Petrograd to save the Provisional Government
* Kerensky publicly condemned Kornilov’s advance
* He ordered Kornilov to surrender and placed Petrograd under martial law
* In fear of an attack, Kerensky called on all loyal citizens to fight
* The Bolsheviks were freed from prisons and received weapons from the government
* However, railway workers refused to let Kornilov through and a worker’s militia was being formed to oppose him
* Kornilov gave up and allowed himself to be arrested
