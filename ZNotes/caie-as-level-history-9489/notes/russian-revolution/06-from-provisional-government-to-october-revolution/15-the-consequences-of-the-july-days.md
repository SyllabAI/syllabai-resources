## **The Consequences Of The July Days**

* While the July Days may have been uncertain, the failed uprising revealed a number of important facts:
  * The opposition movement was disunited
  * The Bolsheviks was still far from being the dominant revolutionary group
  * The Provisional Government still had sufficient strength to put down an armed insurrection
* This boosted the Provisional Government’s credibility
* Two days after the rising, Kerensky became prime minister
* He put the Bolsheviks under pressure and closed down *Pravda*
* Many of the Bolshevik leaders were arrested, while Lenin fled to Finland
* Kerensky also initiated a propaganda campaign, branding the Bolsheviks as traitors
* The Bolsheviks seemed to be broken, but the Provisional Government’s misjudgement over the land question and the Kornilov Affair saved the Bolsheviks
