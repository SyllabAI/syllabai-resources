## **The April Theses**

* On the following day, Lenin issued his April Theses which outlined the future of Bolshevik policy:
  * Abandon cooperation with all other parties
  * Work for a true revolution by themselves
  * Overthrow the Provisional Government
  * Transfer power to the workers
  * Total authority should be given to the soviets
* Lenin’s arguments were summed up in two provocative Bolshevik slogans he coined
* ‘Peace, Bread and Land’ and ‘All Power to the Soviets’
* ‘Peace’ related to the war
* ‘Bread’ related to the chronic food shortages
* ‘Land’ related to the disruption in the countryside
* Lenin believed that the Provisional Government could not solve these problems
* He was right as these problems led to the Provisional Government’s downfall
