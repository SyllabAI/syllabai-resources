## **Lenin Urges A Rising (October Revolution)**

* Despite Lenin’s conviction, his colleagues doubted his idea of striking against the Provisional Government
* In an effort to enforce his will, Lenin slipped back into Petrograd on 7 October
* His personal presence did not produce total unity, so he spent hours convincing the Central Committee members
* On 10 October, they pledged to an armed insurrection, but failed to agree on a specific date
* It was Kerensky and the government that actually initiated the actual rising
