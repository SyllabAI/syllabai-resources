## **The Land Question**

* The peasants assumed the Provisional Government would redistribute the land
* However, when the government did nothing, the peasants decided to do it themselves
* These disturbances occurred daily in 1917
* The Provisional Government had no real answer
* The Land Commission body could not handle this massive task
* Also, the Provisional Government was made up of propertied classes, so it was unlikely for them to be enthusiastic for a policy that threatened their own interests
