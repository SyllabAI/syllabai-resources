## **Russia And The Western Allies**

* The Provisional Government remained committed to the war against Germany in return for Allied war-credits and supplies
* However, the October Revolution led to the Bolsheviks seizing power
* Within weeks, an armistice was agreed with Germany and fighting ended in December 1917
* Britain and France initially hoped the Bolsheviks would continue fighting in return for supplies
* However, the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk in March 1918 ended all hope of Lenin fighting Germany
* Any Allied help would now go to anti-Bolshevik groups
* After the signing of the Treaty, British, French and US troops occupied key areas in European, central and far-eastern Russia
* The Allies did not coordinate their attacks, they all wanted to protect their own interests
