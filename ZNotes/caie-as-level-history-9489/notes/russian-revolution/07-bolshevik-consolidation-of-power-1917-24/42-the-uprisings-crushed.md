## **The Uprisings Crushed**

* Trotsky ordered the Red Army to crush the workers on Kronstadt
* 60,000 Red troops stormed the base, while the sailors and workers there fought fiercely
* The uprising was suppressed and the ringleaders were announced as White reactionaries
* The Cheka was tasked with hunting down rebels who had escaped
* Lenin justified this as the work of the bourgeois enemies of the October Revolution
* Lenin learnt from his mistake and softened the severity of War Communism
* He announced his introduction of the New Economic Policy
