## **Immediate Difficulties**

* The shortage of raw materials and investment capital reduced industrial production to two-thirds of its 1914 level
* Inflation had rocketed
* The transport system was broken
* Hunger was prevalent, grain supplies were over 13 million tonnes short of the nation’s needs
* The food crisis had been further deepened by the ceding of Ukraine to Germany, Russia’s richest grain producing region
* All of Lenin’s economic policies from 1917 onwards can be seen as attempts to deal with these problems
* He needed to first persuade the peasantry to produce enough food for the nation
* They introduced two new measures:
  * ‘Decree on Land’
  * ‘Decree on Workers’ Control’
* These were both issued in November 1917
