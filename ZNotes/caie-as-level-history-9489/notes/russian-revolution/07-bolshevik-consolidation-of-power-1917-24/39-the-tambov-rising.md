## **The Tambov Rising**

* There were outbreaks of resistance throughout 1920
* The most serious was the one in Tambov
* Peasants had formed their own armies
* The principal leader was Alexander Antonov, a SR member
* The peasants attacked requisition squads and took over whole regions in the countryside
* By the end of 1920, Antonov had a guerrilla army of around 20,000
* The Red Army was successful in re-establishing government control
