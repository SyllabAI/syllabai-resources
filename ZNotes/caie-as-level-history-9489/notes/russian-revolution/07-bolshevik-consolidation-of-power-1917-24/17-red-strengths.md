## **Red Strengths**

* They remained in control of the central area of western Russia by maintaining their inner communications and supply lines
* Petrograd and Moscow remained in their hands along with most of the railway network
* They had control over the industrial centres of Russia
* The Red accused the Whites of being in the same league of foreign interventionalists since they received foreign supplies
* The Red Army was brilliantly organised and led by Trotsky
