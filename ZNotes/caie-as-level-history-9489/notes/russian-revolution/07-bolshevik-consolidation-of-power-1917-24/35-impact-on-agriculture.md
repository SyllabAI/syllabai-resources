## **Impact On Agriculture**

* For Lenin, War Communism was used to tighten government control over agriculture and force the peasants to provide more food
* However, the peasants were a conservative class and were resistant to a centralised government
* The government blamed this on the kulaks (class of the rich who were exploiting peasants) as they were hoarding grain
* However, the kulaks were actually just more efficient farmers who were marginally more prosperous
* Furthermore, the resistance to change was because the peasants simply saw no point in producing more food if the government, their main grain purchaser, did not pay a fair price
