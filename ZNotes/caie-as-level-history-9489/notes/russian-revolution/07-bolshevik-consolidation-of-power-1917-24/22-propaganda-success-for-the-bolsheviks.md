## **Propaganda Success For The Bolsheviks**

* The foreign withdrawals were not a military victory
* However, Soviet propaganda made it seem like it
* Lenin’s government was portrayed as the nation’s saviour from foreign powers who were trying to overthrow the revolution
* This helped to mellow the effects of the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
* It also lent credibility to the Bolshevik depiction of the Whites as agents of foreign powers, intent on restoring reactionary tsardom
