## **The Decree On Workers’ Control**

* This measure also authorised what had already been happening
* During 1917, a large number of factories had been taken over by workers
* However, the workers’ committees that were formed ran the factories inefficiently
* The result was a serious fall in industrial output
* The decree instructed the workers to maintain a strict order and discipline in the workplace
* In December, *Vesenkha* (Supreme Council of the National Economy) was set up to enforce all economic matters
* It achieved:
  * The nationalisation of banks and railways
  * Cancelling of foreign debts
  * The repair of the transport system
* These achievements suggested the potential of the Bolshevik regime
