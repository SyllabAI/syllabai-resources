## **Trotsky’s Role**

* Trotsky’s strategy was simple and direct:
  * Defend the Red Army’s internal lines of communication
  * Deny the Whites the opportunity to concentrate large forces in one area
  * Prevent the Whites from maintaining regular supplies
* The basis of this strategy was the control of Russia’s railways
* The railways could transport troops swiftly to any critical locations
* Trotsky also plotted the destruction of Russian trade unions and in 1921, ordered the suppression of rebellious Kronstadt workers
* The Reds also pointed out that all the land the peasants seized in 1917 would be returned if the Whites won
* This was the underlying reason that caused peasants to continue supporting the Bolsheviks
