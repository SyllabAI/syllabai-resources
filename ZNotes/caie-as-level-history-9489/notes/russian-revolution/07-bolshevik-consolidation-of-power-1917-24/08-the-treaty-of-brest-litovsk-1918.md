## **The Treaty Of Brest-Litovsk 1918**

* Lenin felt that Russia’s military exhaustion made it impossible for it to fight on successfully
* Also between 1914 and 1917, the German foreign office had given substantial amounts of money to the Bolsheviks in the hope they would pull Russia out of the war after their takeover
* Having a settlement with Germany was in Lenin’s best interests since it would guarantee the German source of Bolshevik revenue
* Trotsky also believed Russia could not continue their military campaign
* He wanted to buy time to allow Bolshevik agitators to exploit mutinies in Austrian-German armies
* Trotsky and Lenin were prepared to sacrifice Russia to spread their proletarian revolution
* This explains why they were prepared to sign the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk on 3 March 1918
