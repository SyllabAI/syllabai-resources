## **Consequences Of The Treaty**

* Many Bolsheviks wanted to continue the struggle against Germany
* However, Lenin stressed that his policy was the only realistic one
* Lenin believed Russia would be able to reclaim its lost territories after Germany lost the war
* Lenin gained his way by a majority of only one vote
* A number of Bolsheviks, known as Left Communists, condemned the signing of the Treaty
* It was only after Lenin’s insistence on the absolute need for party loyalty that they reluctantly accepted the Treaty
* The collapse of Germany’s western front in August 1918 and the withdrawal of German forces from Russia cleared all doubts on Lenin’s decision
* Lenin’s gamble had paid off and his position over the party was strengthened
* He was also able to expel the Left SRs from the government and to outlaw them politically
