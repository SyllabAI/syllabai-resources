## **White Weaknesses**

* The various White armies fought as separate detachments
* They were not bounded by a single aim, other then overthrowing the Bolsheviks
* They were unwilling to sacrifice their individual interests in order to form a united anti-Bolshevik front
* They were also too geographically scattered to combine
* The Whites were reliant on foreign supplies which seldom arrived in sufficient quantities, in the right places or at the right time
* The Whites lacked good leaders
