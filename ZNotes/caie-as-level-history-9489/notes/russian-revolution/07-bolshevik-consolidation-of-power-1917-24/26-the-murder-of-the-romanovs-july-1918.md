## **The Murder Of The Romanovs, July 1918**

* In July 1918, a group of SRs assassinated the German ambassador as a protest against the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
* A month later, an attempt was made on Lenin’s life and the Petrograd chairman of the Cheka was murdered
* This allowed the Bolsheviks to enforce their terror
* Lenin also personally ordered the execution of the ex-tsar and his family in July 1918
