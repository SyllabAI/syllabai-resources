## **Challenge From The SRs**

* The desperate circumstances encouraged others to openly challenge the Bolsheviks
* The SRs, who had been outlawed because of their refusal to accept the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk attempted a coup in Moscow (in 1918, Moscow replaced Petrograd as the capital of Russia due to security reasons)
* This failed
* Therefore, the Civil War began as an effort by one set of revolutionaries to take power from another
* The SRs joined the Whites in their struggles against Lenin
