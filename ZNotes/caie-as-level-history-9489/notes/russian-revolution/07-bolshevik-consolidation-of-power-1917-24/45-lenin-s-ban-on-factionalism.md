## **Lenin’s Ban On Factionalism**

* NEP became such a large issue within the party, Lenin had to take steps to prevent the party from splitting
* He introduced the ‘One Party Unity’
* Lenin’s ban on factionalism prevented criticism of government or Central Committee decisions
* He also declared all parties other then the Bolsheviks were banned
* A further boost to Bolshevik unity was the decision by Bukharin
* He was an outstanding party economist and he enthusiastically supported NEP
