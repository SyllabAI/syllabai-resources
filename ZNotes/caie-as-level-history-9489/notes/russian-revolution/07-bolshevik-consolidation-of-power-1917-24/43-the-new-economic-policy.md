## **The New Economic Policy**

* The New Economic Policy (NEP) was intended to meet Russia’s urgent need for food
* Unanimous support for NEP was given because of the grim economic situation
* The NEP included:
  * Central economic control to be relaxed
  * The requisitioning of grain to be abandoned and replaced by a tax (peasants gave a certain amount of their produce away)
  * The peasants can keep their surplus and sell them for a profit
  * Public markets to be restored
  * Money to be reintroduced as a means of trading
* Lenin knew this was a step back from state control, therefore he stressed to his fellow Bolsheviks that it was a temporary concession
