## **Grain Requisitioning**

* The government resorted to using the Cheka to take grain by force
* The requisitions squads systematically terrorised the countryside between 1918 and 1921
* Yet the amount of food decreased
* Peasants knew that any surplus would be confiscated, so they only produced the bare minimum to feed their own families
* Nevertheless, the Bolsheviks persisted in the belief that grain hoarding was the problem
