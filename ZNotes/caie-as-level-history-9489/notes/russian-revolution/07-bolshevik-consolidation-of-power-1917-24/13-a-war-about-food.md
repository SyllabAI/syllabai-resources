## **A War About Food**

* Sometimes, the fighting was simply a struggle for food
* Famine was prevalent during the Civil War
* The breakdown of food supply during the war against Germany continued
* The failure of the Bolsheviks in solving the food crisis formed the initial military opposition to the Bolsheviks in 1918
* Additionally, the broken transport system and the treaty of Brest-Litovsk meant many went hungry
* By June 1918, the workforce in Petrograd had shrunk by 60% and the overall population went from 3 million to 2 million
