## **Reasons For The Russian Civil War 1918-20**

* The Bolshevikʻs crushing of the Constituent Assembly and their outlawing of other parties showed that they were not prepared to share power
* This made civil war highly likely
* Lenin may have wanted a destructive civil war as it would be better to completely wipe out any opposition then to have them remain for years
* Lenin did not want to join the coalition of all the revolutionary parties in 1918 because the Bolsheviks were very much a minority party
