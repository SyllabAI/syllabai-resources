## **Famine**

* By 1921, the combination of requisitioning, drought and the consequences of war had created a national famine
* The grain harvests in 1920 and 1921 were half off that in 1913
* Even the government’s propaganda newspaper, Pravda, said that one in five of the population were starving in 1921
* The Bolsheviks, while continuing to blame the kulaks and Whites, accepted foreign assistance
* Despite these efforts, of the 10 million fatalities in the Civil War period, half of them were because of starvation
