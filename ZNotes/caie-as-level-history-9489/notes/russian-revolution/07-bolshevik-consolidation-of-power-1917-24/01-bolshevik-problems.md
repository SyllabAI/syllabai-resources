# Bolshevik Consolidation of Power 1917-24

## Bolshevik Problems

* The Bolsheviks under Lenin faced huge difficulties in trying to consolidate their hold over Russia
* These can be summarised in four basic questions:
  * Could the Bolsheviks survive at all?
  * If so, could they extend their control over the whole of Russia?
  * Could they negotiate an end to the war?
  * Could they bring economic stability to Russia?
* From the beginning, the Bolshevik regime was struggling
* The Bolsheviks spent much of their time preparing for revolution, so they did not know how to govern after they had taken power
* The Central Committee of the Bolshevik Party was under Lenin and provided the members of the government
* This meant power was exercised top-down and not bottom-up
