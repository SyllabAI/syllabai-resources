## **The Kronstadt Manifesto**

* Early in March, the sailors and workers of Kronstadt elected Petrechenko as chairman of a 15 man revolutionary committee, responsible for representing their grievances to the government
* This committee produced a manifesto that demanded:
  * New elections to the soviets, to be held by secret ballot
  * Freedom of speech and of the press
  * Freedom of assembly
* The Bolsheviks were afraid of the people who drafted it as they were great supporters of the Bolsheviks in 1917
