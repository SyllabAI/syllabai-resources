## **Economic Results Of NEP**

* The most powerful reason for the party to accept NEP was its statistical results
* The production figures suggested that the policy worked
* By 1924, the Soviet economy began to make a recovery
* NEP had produced an economic balance between private ownership and state domination
* However, NEP was not a total success
* Opponents of the policy criticised it as the industry failed to expand as rapidly as the agriculture sector
