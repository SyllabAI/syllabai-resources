## **Impact On Industry**

* The first step of War Communism was a formal policy in June 1918
* Lenin knew he could enforce his policy of centralisation with the Red Army and Cheka
* Therefore, he issued the Decree on Nationalisation in June 1918
* This was a programme that would allow the state to takeover large industrial concerns
* In two years, practically all major industrial enterprises were under central government control
* This policy did nothing to increase production
* Military needs were given priority
* There was a shortage of workers due to conscription and the large number of urban inhabitants who fled in search of food or to escape the Civil War
* The populations of Petrograd and Moscow dropped by a half between 1918 and 1921
* Hyperinflation also occurred as the government started printing more money and goods became scarce
* In 1920, the value of the rouble had fell to 1% of its original value in 1917
* War communism was a failure in economic terms
