## **The Dissolution Of The Constituent Assembly**

* Lenin did not want to win mass support, he wanted to seize power when the moment came
* Therefore, he did not want the elections to undermine the Bolshevik’s position
* However, the October events had come too late to prevent the elections of the All-Russian Constituent Assembly in November
* The Bolsheviks had been outvoted by nearly two to one by the Social Revolutionaries
* They had only won 24% of the total votes and had barely gained a quarter of the seats in the assembly
* Lenin had originally supported the idea of a Constituent Assembly as it could weaken the authority of the Provisional Government
* However, with his party now in power, he had no need for the assembly
* Furthermore, since it was overwhelmingly non-Bolshevik, it would make life difficult for his government
* When the Constituent Assembly gathered in January 1918, it was dissolved at gunpoint by the Red Guards
* Lenin justified the Bolshevik action by arguing that the all-Russian body was useless as its purpose was achieved by the creation of the Soviet government in October 1917
* Lenin’s ruthlessness made some of his own supporters uneasy
