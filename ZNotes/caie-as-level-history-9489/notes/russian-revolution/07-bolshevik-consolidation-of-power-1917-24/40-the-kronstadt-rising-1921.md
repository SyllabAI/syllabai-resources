## **The Kronstadt Rising 1921**

* This was the most serious challenge to Bolshevik control since the October Revolution
* Lenin was deeply worried by the development of opposition within the party
* Two prominent Bolsheviks, Alexander Shlyapnikov and Alexandra Kollontai, led a ‘Workers’ Opposition’ movement against War Communism
* Kollontai produced a pamphlet which accused the party leaders of losing touch with the proletariat
* Groups of workers in Petrograd went on strike in early 1921 in response
* By February 1921, thousands of Petrograd workers went to the naval base on Kronstadt
* There, they joined with the workers in the naval base and demonstrated for greater freedom
* Lenin sent a team of political commissars who were treated with derision
