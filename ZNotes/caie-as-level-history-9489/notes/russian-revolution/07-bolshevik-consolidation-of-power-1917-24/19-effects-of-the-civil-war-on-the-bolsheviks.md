## **Effects Of The Civil War On The Bolsheviks**

* Of all the members in the Bolsheviks (renamed the Communist party in 1919), a third had joined in the years 1917-20 and had fought in the Red Army
* This meant the Bolsheviks of this generation were hard men
* This also created a tradition of military obedience and loyalty
* This military victory further developed the use of authoritarian measures
* The Civil War resulted in the creation of two subcommittees, the Politburo (the Political Bureau, responsible for major policy decisions) and the Orgburo (Organisation Bureau, which turned policies into practice)
* These were set up in 1919 and could act with the necessary speed required
