# **Growth Of Markets And Free Trade**

### **National Market**

* The rapid population growth and rising wages ensured that the domestic demand grew
* Britain, being the first to industrialise, had no competitors so had a huge overseas market as well
* By 1850, exports generated over 10% of Britain’s national income
* The infrastructure needed to serve a growing overseas market was already in place
* There were well-established ports and a large merchant navy
* In 1760, Britain had about 600,000 tons of merchant shipping
* This rose to 1.5 million tons by 1792
* The canals and railways delivered goods to the dockside to load directly onto ships
* A sophisticated banking and insurance system supported manufacturers and merchants
* The government also encouraged free trade without tariffs
* By the end of the period, Britain had the most amount of exports in the world
