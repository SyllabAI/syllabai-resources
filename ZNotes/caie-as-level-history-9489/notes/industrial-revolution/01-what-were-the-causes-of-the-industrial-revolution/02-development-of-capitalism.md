# **Development Of Capitalism**

### **Investment**

* Britain had an established system of country banks and respected local money lenders
* They were able to lend money at low-interest rates to people who wanted to start a business
* There was a general belief that it was sensible to invest surplus money into businesses
* The thriving insurance market meant the risks of investment could be spread
* This created a positive environment for innovations

### **Overseas Trade**

* Foreign trade was an important feature of the British economy
* Those who wished to sell goods were able to do so easily
* The Royal Navy was the most powerful in the western world at the time
* The government and influential parliament at the time were determined to protect and develop British trade
* Britain had also developed a large merchant navy, rivalling the Dutch
* Some of the country’s rivers lead to major ports such as Bristol and Liverpool
* These made it easy to import and export goods
* However, a lot of this trade came from slavery
* The overseas trade was one of the factors that allowed Britain to increase their manufacturing output

### **Commerce**

* In 1750, Britain already had a flourishing trade in woollen goods
* These were sold all over the world
* Other industries included nail making, boot and shoe making and cutlery manufacturing
* There was already a complex production and distribution network for textiles before the ‘revolution’
* The major difference between this type of manufacturing and the one in the revolution was that early manufacturing processes took place in homes
* This was known as a cottage or domestic industry
