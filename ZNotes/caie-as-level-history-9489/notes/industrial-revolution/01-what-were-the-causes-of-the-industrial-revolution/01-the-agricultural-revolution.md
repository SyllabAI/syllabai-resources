# What were the causes of the Industrial Revolution?

## The Agricultural Revolution

### **Agricultural Output**

* New techniques for improving output have been developed
* The factors that played a major part in increasing the quality and quantity of agricultural output were:
  * The enclosure movement and the growth of larger farming units
  * Improved soil fertility
  * Crop rotation
  * Selective livestock breeding
  * The spread of scientific knowledge about farming

### **The Enclosure Movement**

* The enclosure movement broke up the traditional farming units that had belonged to a small community and merged them into a larger unit owned by a single individual.
* This process had started before 1750 but grew in popularity between 1750 and 1850
* This technique entailed joining farming land to form units of 100 acres or more.
* These larger units could be farmed more productively, increasing the amount of food being produced, and better-quality animals could be kept.
* Between 1750 and 1800, more than 7 million acres of farmland were enclosed

### **Improved Soil Fertility**

* Farmers made greater use of fertilisers, such as lime
* They also used crops which increase nitrogen levels in the soil
* More animals being bred and more crop yield meant animals did not need to be slaughtered in the winter, increasing manure production and reducing soil exhaustion
* This also allowed farmers to bring unproductive land into use through fertilisation.

### **Crop Rotation**

* One technique that spread widely after 1750 was crop rotation
* The land would be planted with wheat, barley, clover and turnips in a four-year cycle
* Clover puts nutrients back into the soil
* Turnips provided good animal feed
* This meant that significantly more land could be used productively each year.

### **Selective Livestock Breeding**

* Enclosing land meant farmers could ensure that animals were bred selectively.
* The breeds of cattle which were best for meat or milk could be encouraged
* Isolating animals in separate fields also reduced the risk of spreading animal diseases

### **Links Between Agriculture And Industry**

* Many factors in the process of industrialisation are interconnected
* More food enabled a growing population to be fed
* Growing food was also more profitable for farmers
* These profits created a demand for manufactured goods and produced capital for investment.
* These investments allowed new forms of transport and manufacturing to be discovered.
* More efficient transport enabled fresh food like vegetables to be transported quickly to cities, improving diets.
* This led to more babies living and, therefore, more demand and greater profits for farmers.
