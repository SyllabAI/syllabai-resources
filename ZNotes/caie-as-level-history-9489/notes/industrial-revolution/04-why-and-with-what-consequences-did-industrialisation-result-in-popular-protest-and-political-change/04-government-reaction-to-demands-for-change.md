# **Government Reaction To Demands For Change**

### **Government Reaction**

* The reactions by the government to protest and demands for reform were varied
* Sometimes the government simply ignored them
* Sometimes they opposed them and sometimes they agreed with them
* The government rarely took positive initiatives to help the working classes
* Its approach tended to reflect the interests of the upper and middle classes
* The reactions to Luddism and Chartism was always to maintain law and order
* This was sometimes backed by force and law
* However, when it was faced with the real threat of revolution like 1832, it made concessions
* But these concessions were limited to the middle class
* In nearly every other scenario they did things in the aristocracy’s interests
