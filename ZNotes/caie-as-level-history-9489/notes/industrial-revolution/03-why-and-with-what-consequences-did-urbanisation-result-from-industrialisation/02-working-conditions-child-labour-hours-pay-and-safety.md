# **Working Conditions: Child Labour, Hours, Pay And Safety**

### **Working Conditions**

* The average work day ranged from 12 to 14 hours for 6 days a week
* The workplace was noisy, polluted and lacked adequate sanitation
* Over 65% of the workforce in the textile mills were women and children
* Men who were skilled workers would earn more than enough for a family
* Unskilled men earnt much less, meaning their wives and children had to work as well
* For many, the income earned by children was vital
* In many factories, children made up 30% of the total workforce
