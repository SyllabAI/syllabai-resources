# **Impacts On Different Social Classes**

### **The Aristocracy**

* Throughout this period, the aristocracy remained the dominant economic and social influence in British society
* Its members held most of the land and wealth
* In many cases, aristocrats made large amounts of money out of both agricultural improvements and industrial investment
* Parliament was largely made up of aristocratic investors who would profit from the Revolution
* Their constitutional monarchy also usually accepted the demands of parliament and ministers

### **The Growth Of A Middle Class**

* Industrialisation led to the rapid expansion of this group
* Entrepreneurs, innovators, factory owners, landlords and other skilled workers emerged in large numbers
* The new middle class, with a large income, demanded servants and luxury goods which stimulated an enormous retail system

### **The Working Class**

* The working class enjoyed no real benefits
* The condition of some groups worsened from mechanisation like agricultural workers
* Unskilled men relied on their families for additional income
* Adults in the working class had no educational opportunities, so rising out of the working class was unlikely
* The conditions in coal mines were terrible
* Serious injury or death was common
* The only people who benefitted were skilled workers
* They were guaranteed work and could earn three times as much as the unskilled workers
