## Ionic half-equations

* Show what happens at each electrode separately

* Include electrons to balance the charge

* **Cathode = reduction**→ electrons on the left (gained)

* **Anode = oxidation**→ electrons on the right (lost)

### Remember: **OIL RIG**

* **O**xidation **I**s **L**oss of electrons

* **R**eduction **I**s **G**ain of electrons

### **Example on how to write one:**

Cathode: Al³⁺ ion gains 3 electrons to become Al

* **$Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al$** (reduction)

Anode: 2 Cl⁻ ions lose electrons to form Cl₂

* **$2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻$** (oxidation)
