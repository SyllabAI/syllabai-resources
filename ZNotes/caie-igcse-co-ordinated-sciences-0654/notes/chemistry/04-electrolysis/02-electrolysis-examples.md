## Electrolysis examples

#|
||

Electrolyte

|

Cathode (−)  reduction

|

Anode (\+)  oxidation

||
||

Molten PbBr₂: lead(II) bromide

|

Lead (Pb)→ silvery-grey

$Pb²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Pb$

|

Bromine (Br₂)→ red-brown vapour

$2Br⁻ → Br₂ + 2e⁻$

||
||

**Concentrated aq** NaCl (brine)

|

Hydrogen (H₂)→ bubbles, squeaky pop

$2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂$

*Na is more reactive than H, so H₂ forms*

|

Chlorine (Cl₂)→ pale green, bleaches damp litmus

$2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻$

||
||

Dilute H₂SO₄ (sulfuric acid)

|

Hydrogen (H₂)→ bubbles

$2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂$

|

Oxygen (O₂)→ bubbles, relights glowing splint

$4OH⁻ → O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻$
