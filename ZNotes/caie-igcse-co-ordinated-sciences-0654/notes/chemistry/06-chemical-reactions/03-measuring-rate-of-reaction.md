## Measuring rate of reaction

#|
||

Method

|

How it works

||
||

Change in mass

|

Place reaction flask on a balance; if a gas is produced and escapes, mass decreases over time. 

Graph: Plot mass lost against time.

||
||

Volume of gas collected

|

Collect gas produced in a gas syringe or over water. Graph: Plot volume against time.

||
||

Time for a change

|

Time how long it takes for a colour to disappear or a precipitate to form (e.g. disappearing cross experiment).
