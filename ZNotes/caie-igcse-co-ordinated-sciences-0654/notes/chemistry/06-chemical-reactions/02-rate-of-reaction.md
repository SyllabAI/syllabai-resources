## **Rate of reaction**

* how fast reactants turn into products; measured by how quickly a reactant is used up or a product is formed.

### Factors affecting the rate:

#|
||

Factor

|

Effect on rate

Concentration (of solution) ↑

Increases

|

More reactant particles per unit volume → more frequent collisions per second → more successful collisions

||

Pressure (of gases) **↑**

|

Increases

|

Particles pushed closer together → more particles per unit volume → more frequent collisions

||
||

Surface area (of solids) ↑

|

Increases

|

More particles of solid exposed to reactant → more frequent collisions on the surface

||
||

Temperature ↑

|

Increases

|

Particles gain kinetic energy → move faster → collide more frequently, a greater proportion of collisions have energy ≥ Eₐ (successful)

||
||

Catalyst added

|

Increases

|

Provides an alternative pathway with a **lower activation energy (Eₐ)** → more collisions succeed per second

### Catalyst:

* increases the rate of a reaction and is unchanged at the end of a reaction

* Lower the activation energy Eₐ by providing an alternative reaction pathway
