# Chemical Energetics

## **Exothermic & Endothermic Reactions**

1. **Exothermic reaction:** transfers thermal energy **to** the surroundings → temperature of surroundings **increases**

2. **Endothermic reaction:** takes in thermal energy **from** the surroundings → temperature of surroundings **decreases**

### Key terms:

* **Enthalpy change (∆H):** the transfer of thermal energy during a reaction.
  Negative for exothermic (energy released), positive for endothermic (energy absorbed).

* **Activation energy (Eₐ):** the minimum energy that colliding particles must have to react.
