## The mole and the Avogadro constant

* **The mole (mol):** the unit of amount of substance.

* One mole contains **6.02 × 10²³ particles** (atoms, ions or molecules). This number is the **Avogadro constant**.

* 1 mole of any substance has a mass equal to its Aᵣ or Mᵣ **in grams,**

  Eg: 1 mole of Sodium is 23 g.
