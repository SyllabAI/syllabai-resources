
## Relative masses

1. Relative atomic mass (Aᵣ): the average mass of the isotopes of an element compared to 1/12 of the mass of an atom of ¹²C.

2. Relative molecular mass (Mᵣ): sum of the relative atomic masses of all atoms in one molecule. 

   For **ionic compounds** it is called **relative formula mass.**

**Example:** Mᵣ of H₂SO₄ = (2 × 1) \+ 32 \+ (4 × 16) = **98**

### **Reacting masses by simple proportion**

* Use the balanced equation to find the mass ratio 

* Scale up or down using proportion

**Example:** 2Mg \+ O₂ → 2MgO


Aᵣ values: Mg = 24, MgO = 40


48 g of Mg → 80 g of MgO


So 12 g of Mg → **20 g of MgO** (÷ 4)
