## Key formulas

$\text{Moles} = \frac{\text{Mass (g)}}{\text{Mᵣ (g/mol)}}$

**Eg: How many moles are in 8 g of NaOH?**

Mᵣ = 23 \+ 16 \+ 1 = 40

Moles = 8 ÷ 40 = **0.2 mol**

### Concentration:

* how much solute is dissolved per dm³ of solution

* units: **g/dm³**

* Unit conversion: **1 dm³ = 1000 cm³**

* $\text{Concentration (g/dm³)} = \frac{\text{Mass of solute (g)}}{\text{Volume (dm³)}}$

  Eg: 10 g of NaCl is dissolved in 500 cm³ of water. Find the concentration in g/dm³.

  Convert volume: 500 ÷ 1000 = 0.5 dm³
  Concentration = 10 ÷ 0.5 = **20 g/dm³**

### Gas volumes at r.t.p

* 1 mole of any gas occupies **24 dm³** at room temperature and pressure (r.t.p.)

  $\boxed{volume of gas (dm³) = moles × 24}$

  Eg: **What volume does 0.5 mol of CO₂ occupy at r.t.p.?**

  V = 0.5 × 24 = **12 dm³** (= 12 000 cm³)
