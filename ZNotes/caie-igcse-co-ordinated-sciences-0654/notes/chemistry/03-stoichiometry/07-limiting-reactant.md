## Limiting reactant

* A chemical reaction stops when one reactant is completely used up -\>this is the **limiting reactant**

* The other reactant is in **excess**; some is left over unused

* The amount of product depends on the limiting reactant

### How to find it:

* Work out moles of each reactant

* Divide each by its coefficient in the balanced equation

* The one with the smallest value is the limiting reactant

**Example:** 2H₂ \+ O₂ → 2H₂O


Given 4 g H₂ and 16 g O₂. Which is limiting?


Moles H₂ = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 → ÷ 2 = **1.0**


Moles O₂ = 16 ÷ 32 = 0.5 → ÷ 1 = **0.5**


**O₂ is limiting**; H₂ is in excess.
