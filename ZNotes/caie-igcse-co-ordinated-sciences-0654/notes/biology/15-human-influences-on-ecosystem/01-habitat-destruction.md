# Human Influences on Ecosystem

## Habitat Destruction

* **Ecosystem:** a unit containing the community of organisms and their environment, interacting together
* **Biodiversity:** the number of different species that live in an area
* **Reasons for habitat destruction:**
  * Increased area for housing, crop plant production and livestock production
  * Extraction of natural resources
  * Freshwater and marine pollution
* **Undesirable effects of deforestation:**
  * Extinction of species
    * Due to the loss of natural habitat → **reduced biodiversity**
  * Loss of soil
    * Soil becomes exposed to the rain and washed away → **soil erosion**
    * Causes **flooding**
  * Increase of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere
    * Fewer plants to remove carbon dioxide
    * Trees decay/burnt → releases carbon dioxide
