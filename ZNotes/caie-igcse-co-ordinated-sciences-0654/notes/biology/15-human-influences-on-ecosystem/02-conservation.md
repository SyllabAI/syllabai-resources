## Conservation

* Organisms become endangered or extinct due to: 
  * Climate change
  * Habitat destruction
  * Hunting
  * Overharvesting
  * Pollution
  * Introduced species
    * Non-native species could increase competition (for food, water,...) and introduce diseases to native species 
* Endangered species can be conserved by:
  * Monitoring and protecting species and habitats
  * Education - to raise awareness 
  * Captive breeding programs
  * Seed banks:
    * A place that conserves seeds from different plant species to preserve their genetic diversity for the future
