## Transpiration

* **Transpiration:** the loss of water vapour from leaves
  * Water **evaporates** from the surfaces of the mesophyll cells into the air spaces
  * Then, it **diffuses out of the leaves** through the stomata as water vapour

### **Factors Affecting Transpiration Rate**


1. **Temperature**
   * Transpiration increases as temperature increases
   * Water evaporates more quickly as they gain more kinetic energy
2. **Wind speed**
   * Transpiration increases as wind speed increases
   * Since water vapor is carried away faster
3. **Humidity**
   * Transpiration decreases as humidity increases
   * Humidity means a high concentration of water vapor in the atmosphere
     * Leading to a small concentration gradient of water vapor between the inside and outside of the plant 
     * Resulting in lower rate of diffusion of water vapour out of the plant

### **Wilting**
* Occurs when plant cells lose their **turgor pressure** as a result of water loss
