Iodine solution

|

Starch

|

Add a few drops of iodine solution (yellow-brown) directly to the sample

|

Blue-black colour

|

Remains yellow-brown

||
||

Benedict's solution

|

Reducing sugars (e.g. glucose, maltose, fructose)

|

Add Benedict's reagent (blue) to sample; heat in a water bath at \~80 °C for a few minutes

|

Brick-red precipitate (highest concentration of reducing sugar)

Color intensity indicates concentration; changes from blue to green to yellow to orange to brick-red (least to most)

|

Remains blue

||
||

Biuret Test

|

Proteins

|

Add sodium hydroxide (NaOH) solution, then a few drops of dilute copper sulfate (CuSO₄) solution;

mix gently

|

Purple/violet color

|

Remains blue

||
||

Ethanol emulsion test

|

Fats and oils

|

Dissolve sample in ethanol; pour the ethanol solution into distilled water and shake

|

**Cloudy white emulsion** forms as fat droplets precipitate out

|

Remains clear
