Starch

|

Glucose

|

Energy storage in plant cells; insoluble so does not affect osmosis

(can be broken down to form glucose again when plant needs it)

||
||

Glycogen

|

Glucose

|

Energy storage in animal cells (liver and muscle); highly branched for rapid glucose release

||
||

Cellulose

|

Glucose

|

Structural, forms plant cell walls; indigestible to most animals

||
||

Proteins 

|

Amino acids

|

Structural (eg. collagen, keratin), functional (eg. enzymes, antibodies, haemoglobin);

sequence of any of the 20 different amino acids determines protein shape and function

||
||

Fats and oils

|

Fatty acids \+ glycerol (1 glycerol : 3 fatty acids)

|

Energy storage, insulation, cell membrane structure;

oils are unsaturated (liquid at room temperature), fats are saturated (solid)
