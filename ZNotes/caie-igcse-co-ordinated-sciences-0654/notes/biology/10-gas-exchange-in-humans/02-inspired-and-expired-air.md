## Inspired and Expired Air 

### **Composition**

|    | Inspired Air | Expired air | Reason for difference |
|----|----|----|----|
| **Oxygen** | 21% | 16% | Oxygen is used in respiration   |
| **Carbon dioxide** | 0.04% | 4% | Carbon dioxide is produced during respiration  |
| **Water vapour** | Variable | High |  Gas exchange surfaces are moist, some of this moisture also evaporates  |
