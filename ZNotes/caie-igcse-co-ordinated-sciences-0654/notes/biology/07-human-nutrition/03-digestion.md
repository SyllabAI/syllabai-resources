## Digestion

* **Physical digestion:** breakdown of food into smaller pieces without chemical change to the food molecules
  * **Increases the surface area** of food for the action of enzymes in chemical digestion
* **Chemical digestion:** breakdown of large insoluble molecules into small soluble molecules
  * Producing small soluble molecules that can be absorbed
* Enzymes present:
  * **Amylase:** starch breaks down to simple reducing sugars
  * **Proteases:** protein breaks down to amino acids
  * **Lipase:** fats and oils breaks down to fatty acids and glycerol
* Hydrochloric acid in gastric juice (stomach):
  * Kill harmful microorganisms in food
  * Provide an acidic pH for optimum enzyme activity of proteases in the stomach
* Bile is an alkaline mixture:
  * Contains sodium hydrogen-carbonate → neutralizes chyme entering the duodenum from the stomach
  * Provide a suitable pH for enzyme action in the small intestine
  * **Emulsifies fats and oils** by bile salts:
    * Increase the surface area for chemical digestion of the enzyme lipase
