# Human Nutrition

## Diet

* **Balanced diet:**
  * A diet containing all the **seven types** of nutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water, fibre)
  * In appropriate **amounts and proportions**

| **Nutrients** | **Sources** | **Importance** |
|----|----|----|
| Carbohydrates | Potatoes, rice, corn, spaghetti,etc | Energy |
| Fats and oils | Dairy products | Energy storage Insulation |
| Proteins | Fish, chicken, egg, etc | Making new cells                           Enzymes, haemoglobin,etc |
| Calcium | Dairy products | For bones and teeth                      For blood clotting |
| Iron | Red meat, egg yolkDark green vegetables | Making haemoglobin |
| Vitamin C | Citrus fruits (oranges, lime, etc) 
Raw vegetables | Make collagen                    Repair tissues |
| Vitamin D | Butter, egg yolk,etc                                 Made by the skin when sunlight falls on it | Absorb calcium |
| Fibre | Fruits and vegetables include oats, wheat, barley, brown rice, and more. | Helps peristalsis              Prevent constipation |
| Water |    | Solvent for metabolic reactions |

* **Scurvy -** deficiency of vitamin C
  * Pain in the joints and muscles
  * Bleeding gums
* **Rickets -** deficiency of vitamin D
  * Brittle bones and teeth
  * Poor blood clotting
