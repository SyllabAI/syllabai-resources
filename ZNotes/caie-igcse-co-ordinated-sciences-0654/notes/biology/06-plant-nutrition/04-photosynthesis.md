# Plant Nutrition

## Photosynthesis

### **The process:**

* Plants synthesise **carbohydrates** from raw materials using **light energy**

* **Chlorophyll**: green pigment in chloroplasts;
  transfers light energy into chemical energy for carbohydrate synthesis

### **Equations:**

1. **Word:**

   carbon dioxide \+ water **→** glucose \+ oxygen (in the presence of light & chlorophyll)

2. **Symbol:**

   6CO₂ \+ 6H₂O **→** C₆H₁₂O₆ \+ 6O₂
