## Investigations
* We have to make sure all the starch is removed before an investigation to produce **fair results**
* This can be done by placing the plant in a dark cupboard for 2-3 days
### **Test for Starch**
1. Place a leaf in boiling water 
* To break down the cell membranes
2. Remove the leaf, using forceps, and drop it into a tube of **alcohol** in a water bath
* To remove the chlorophyll so that it won't mask the color of the iodine solution
3. Leaf is dipped in boiling water to soften 
4. Spread out the leaf on a white tile, and cover it with **iodine solution** 
* (+) result - blue-black
* (-) result - remains orange-brown 

### **The Need for Chlorophyll**
* **Materials/Apparatus:**
  * Potted plant with **variegated leaves**
* **Method:**
  * Destarch the plant and then place it in sunlight for several hours
  * Test ones of the leaves for starch 
* **Results:** 
  * The white areas of the leaf (no chlorophyll) -> remain orange-brown indicating that there's no starch and thus no photosynthesis
  * The green areas of the leaf (contain chlorophyll) -> stain blue-black indicating the presence of starch and thus photosynthesis occurred 
  
### **The Need for Light**
* **Materials/Apparatus:**
  * Potted plant 
  * Black paper/ Aluminium foil 
  * Paper clips
* **Method:**
  * Destarch the plant
  * Use foil/black paper and cut out a shape. Secure the foil/black paper using paper clips 
  * Leave the plant near a warm, sunny window for a few days 
  * Test the leaf for starch    
* **Results:**
  * The areas that were exposed to the light stain blue-black and thus photosynthesis occurred
  * The areas that were covered by the silver foil remain orange-brown and thus photosynthesis did not take place

### **The Need for Carbon Dioxide**
* **Materials/Apparatus:**
  * Potted plant 
  * Bottle 
  * Split cork  
  * Potassium hydroxide solution (absorbs carbon dioxide) 
* **Method:**
  * Destarch the plant
  * Insert one half of a selected leaf through the split cork 
  * Leave the plant near a warm, sunny window for a few days
  * Detach the leaf from the plant and take it out from the bottle
  * Test the leaf for starch 
* **Results:**
  * Part of the leaf inside of the bottle remains orange-brown as no starch was made thus no photosynthesis 
  * The leaf outside of the bottle stained blue-black as starch was made thus photosynthesis occurred
 
### **Gas Exchange in Aquatic Plants**
* We can use **hydrogencarbonate indicator solution** to  describe the effect of light and dark conditions on gas exchange
* **Observations**:
  * **In bright light:**
    * Colour of indicator: Purple - low concentration of CO2
    * Rate of photosynthesis > respiration 
  * **In the dark:**
    * Colour of indicator: Yellow - high concentration of CO2
    * Rate of respiration > photosynthesis
   * **Normal conditions:**
     * Colour of indicator: Red - normal concentration of CO2
     * Rate of respiration = photosynthesis
