# Plant Nutrition

## Photosynthesis

* **Photosynthesis:** The process by which plants synthesise carbohydrates from raw materials **using energy from light**
  * Carbon dioxide \+ water → glucose \+ oxygen - in the presence of light and chlorophyll
  * 6CO₂ \+ 6H₂O **→** C₆H₁₂O₆ \+ 6O₂
* **Chlorophyll** is a green pigment that is found in chloroplasts
  * It transfers light energy → chemical energy, for the synthesis of carbohydrates
* Uses of glucose:
  * **Starch** as an energy store
  * **Cellulose** to build cell walls
  * Used in **respiration** to provide energy
  * Sucrose for **transport** in the phloem
    * It is **not as reactive** as glucose but still **small and soluble** to dissolve in the phloem sap
  * **Nectar** to attract insects for pollination


|Mineral ions|Importance|Deficiency|
|:---|:---|:---|
|Nitrate ions|Making amino acids - making proteins for **growth**|Stunted growth, yellow leaves|
|Magnesium ions|Making chlorophyll - for **photosynthesis**|Yellowing between the veins of leaves|

### **Factors Affecting the Rate of Photosynthesis**

* **Light intensity:**
  * As light intensity increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases
* **Temperature:**
  * As temperature increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases
  * However, as photosynthesis is an enzyme-controlled reaction, the rate of photosynthesis will decrease if temperature gets too high
  * As a result of enzymes denaturing -\> a change in active site -\> no reaction occurs
* **Carbon dioxide concentration:**
  * As carbon dioxide concentration increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases
