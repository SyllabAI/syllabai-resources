## Enzymes

1. ++What are enzymes?++

   * Proteins that act as **biological catalysts** -\> speed up metabolic reactions

   * Involved in **all** metabolic reactions

   * Not used up or changed by the reaction; can be used repeatedly

   * **Substrate:** substance present at the beginning of the reaction

   * **Active site:** the part of the enzyme where substrate molecules bind 

2. ++Enzyme action:++

   * Each enzyme has an **active site**: a region with a specific shape

   * The **substrate** has a **complementary** shape → fits into the active site

   * An **enzyme–substrate complex** forms temporarily

   * Reaction occurs → **products** released → enzyme unchanged and free again

3. ++Specificity:++

   * Each enzyme only catalyses **one specific reaction**

   * Because only one substrate has the **complementary shape** to fit the active site

Naming enzymes:

\-\> most enzymes are named after their substrate \+ *-ase* suffix

e.g. lip**ase**, amyl**ase**, prot**ease**.
