## Effects of pH on Enzyme Activity

* **Optimum pH →** active site has complementary shape to substrate → perfect fit with substrate → max activity

* **pH changes →** alters charges on active site → shape changes → poor fit → ↓ activity

* **Extreme pH →** bonds break permanently → **denaturation** → activity = 0

Note: pH affects shape only, never kinetic energy or collision frequency.
