## Effects of Temperature on Enzyme Activity

* **Low temp →** (molecules have) low kinetic energy → fewer effective collisions → low activity

* **Increasing temp →** ↑ kinetic energy → ↑ frequency of effective collisions → ↑ activity

* **Above optimum →** bonds break → active site shape changes → substrate no longer fits (no longer complementary) → activity ↓

* **Extreme temp → active site** shape change permanent → **denaturation** → activity = 0

Note: At low temperatures the enzyme is **inactive**, not denatured.
