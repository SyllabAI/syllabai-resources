# Inheritance

## Chromosomes and genes

**Inheritance**: The passing on of genetic information down generations.

* **Chromosome**: A thread-like structure within the cell's nucleus that contains genetic information in the form of genes. Humans contain 46 chromosomes, 23 pairs, in each cell.
* **Gene**: A sequence of DNA containing coded instructions for producing a protein.
* **DNA**: An inherited molecule that contains genetic code.

* **Allele**: Variations of genes that determine characteristics.
* **Haploid nucleus**: A nucleus that contains a single set of unpaired chromosomes.
* **Diploid nucleus**: A nucleus containing two complete sets of chromosomes arranged in pairs.

### **Inheritance of Sex**

* Each parent has two sex chromosomes; the mother has 2 X chromosomes, and the father has 1 X and 1 Y chromosome.
* During meiosis, gametes containing a haploid nucleus are formed. The sex chromosome, X or Y, the sperm that penetrates the ovum, will determine the sex of the child.
* Fertilisation combines the alleles from both parents, restoring the diploid number of chromosomes.
