## Cell division

### Meiosis

* **Reduction division**: The cell has half the number of chromosomes as the parent cell.
* Results in producing haploid gametes that are genetically different from their parent cell.

### Mitosis:

* **Nuclear division**: The parent cell divides into two identical copies.
* Results in the cell being genetically identical to its parent cell.

* It should be noted that chromosome duplication is not a part of mitosis or
  meiosis, only precedes it.

|    | Meiosis | Mitosis |
|----|----|----|
| Why it occurs | Sexual reproduction | Cell repair, growth, asexual reproduction |
| Advantages | High genetic variation, adaptation, and mutation are more likely better chance to fight off disease | Rapid division, energy efficiency, large number of offspring |
| Disadvantages | Courtship casts time and resources, fewer offspring, energy costly | Low genetic variation, low chance for adaptation, competition between offspring and parents |
