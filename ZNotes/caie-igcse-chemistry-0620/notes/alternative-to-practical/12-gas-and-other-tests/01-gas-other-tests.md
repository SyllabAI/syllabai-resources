# Gas & Other Tests

## Gas & Other Tests

In the 2023-2025 syllabus, the Gas Test Reference Sheet is **ONLY** given in Papers 5 & 6.

| Gas | Test and test result |
|----|----|
| Ammonia (NH<sub>3</sub>) | Damp red litmus paper turns blue |
| Carbon dioxide (CO­<sub>2</sub>) | Bubble gas through limewater - from colourless to cloudy |
| Chlorine (Cl<sub>2</sub>) | Bleaches red/blue litmus paper |
| Hydrogen (H<sub>2</sub>) | Place lighted splint, squeaky pop |
| Oxygen (O<sub>2</sub>) | Place glowing splint, splint relights |
| Sulfur Dioxide (SO<sub>2</sub>) | Turns Acidified Aqueous Potassium Manganate (VII) from purple to colourless |
