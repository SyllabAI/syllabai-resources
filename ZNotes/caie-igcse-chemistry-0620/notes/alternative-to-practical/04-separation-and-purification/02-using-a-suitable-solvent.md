## Using a suitable solvent

### What It Does

A **suitable solvent** dissolves **one substance** but **not the other**, allowing them to be separated.

### Method

||

|

### Example

**Mixture:** Salt \+ Sand

* **Solvent:** Water

* **Why:** Salt dissolves in water; sand does not

* **Method:** Add water → stir → filter (sand = residue) → crystallise filtrate (salt)
