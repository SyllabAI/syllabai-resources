## Crystallisation

### What It Does

**Crystallisation** recovers a **soluble solid** from a **solution**.

### Example

**Preparation of copper(II) sulfate crystals:**

* Heat dilute sulfuric acid gently

* Add excess copper(II) oxide with stirring until no more dissolves

* Filter to remove excess CuO

* Collect filtrate (CuSO₄ solution)

* Evaporate to 1/3 volume

* Cool and then blue crystals will start to form

* Filter crystals

* Dry between filter papers
