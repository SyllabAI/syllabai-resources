## Filtration

### What It Does

**Filtration** separates an **insoluble solid** from a **liquid** (or solution).

### Key Terms

* **Residue:** Solid left on filter paper

* **Filtrate:** Liquid that passes through filter

### Example

**Mixture:** Sand \+ Salt Solution

* **Residue:** Sand

* **Filtrate:** Salt solution
