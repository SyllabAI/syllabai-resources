# Test for Anions and Cations

In the 2023-2025 syllabus, the Cation and Anion Test are ONLY given in Paper 5 & 6!

## Cations Test

| Cation | Sodium Hydroxide | Ammonia |
|----|----|----|
| Aluminum (Al<sup>3+</sup>) | Soluble white ppt, gives colourless solution | White ppt, insoluble in excess |
| Ammonium (NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>) | Ammonia gas - damp red litmus turns blue | N/A |
| Calcium (Ca<sup>2+</sup>) | White ppt, Insoluble in excess | No ppt. |
| Chromium (Cr<sup>3+</sup>) | Green ppt, soluble in excess | Green ppt, insoluble in excess |
| Copper (Cu<sup>2+</sup>) | Light blue ppt, Insoluble in excess | Light blue, soluble ppt, giving dark blue |
| Iron(II) (Fe<sup>2+</sup>) | Green ppt, Insoluble in excess, turns brown near surface | Green ppt, Insoluble in excess, turns brown near surface |
| Iron(III) (Fe<sup>3+</sup>) | Red-brown ppt, insoluble | Red-brown ppt, insoluble |
| Zinc (Zn<sup>2+</sup>) | White soluble ppt, giving colourless solution | White soluble ppt, giving colourless solution |
