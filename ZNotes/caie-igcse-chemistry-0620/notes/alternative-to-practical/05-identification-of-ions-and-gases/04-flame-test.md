## Flame Test

### Method

#|
||

Step

|

Action

||
||

1

|

Clean flame test wire with **concentrated HCl**

||
||

2

|

Heat wire in **Bunsen flame** until no colour

||
||

3

|

Dip wire in **solid sample**

||
||

4

|

Hold in **Bunsen flame**

||
||

5

|

Observe **flame colour**

||
|#
