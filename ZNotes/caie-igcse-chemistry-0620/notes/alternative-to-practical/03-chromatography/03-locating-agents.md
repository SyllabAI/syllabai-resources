## Locating agents

### What is a locating agent?

* A **locating agent** is a chemical that **reacts with colourless substances** to produce a **coloured spot** on the chromatogram.

**Why needed?**

* Many substances (e.g., amino acids, sugars) are **colourless** and cannot be seen on the chromatogram.
