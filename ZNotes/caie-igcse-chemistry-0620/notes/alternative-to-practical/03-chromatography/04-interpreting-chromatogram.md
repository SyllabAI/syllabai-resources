## Interpreting chromatogram

### Identifying Unknown Substances

Method:

* Run a chromatogram of the **unknown** mixture

* Run a chromatogram of **known** substances under the **same conditions**

* Compare the **positions** of the spots

**Key Point:** If the spot from the unknown travels the **same distance** as a known substance, they are the **same substance**.
