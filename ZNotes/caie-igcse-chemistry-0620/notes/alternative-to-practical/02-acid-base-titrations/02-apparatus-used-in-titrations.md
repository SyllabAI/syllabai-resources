## Apparatus used in titrations

### Burette

#|
||

Feature

|

Detail

||
||

Volume

|

$50$ cm³

||
||

Precision

|

$0.05$ cm³

||
||

Use

|

Delivers variable, accurate volumes of liquid

||
|#

### **Key Points:**

* Rinse with the solution it will contain

* Fill using a small funnel (remove funnel before titrating)

* Read **bottom of meniscus** at eye level

* Record **initial** and **final** readings

### Volumetric Pipette

#|
||

Feature

|

Detail

||
||

Volume

|

$25$ cm³ (fixed)

||
||

Precision

|

$0.05$ cm³

||
||

Use

|

Delivers a fixed, accurate volume of liquid

||
|#

### **Key Points:**

* Rinse with the solution it will contain

* Use a **pipette filler** never mouth pipette

* Touch tip to side of flask after delivery

* Do **not** blow out the last drop
