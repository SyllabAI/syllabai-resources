## Calculations

### Titration Formula

$\frac{C_aV_a}{n_a} = \frac{C_bV_b}{n_b}$

### Where:

* $C_a$ = concentration of acid ($mol.dm^{-3}$)
* $V_a$ = volume of acid (cm³ or dm³)
* $n_a$ = mole ratio of acid
* $C_b$ = concentration of base ($mol.dm^{-3}$)
* $V_b$ = volume of base (cm³ or dm³)
* $n_b$ = mole ratio of base

### Worked Example

Question: 25.0 cm³ of NaOH is titrated with 0.100 $mol/dm^{3}$ HCl. The mean titre is 24.50 cm³. Calculate the concentration of NaOH.

**Step 1:** Write balanced equation

$HCl+NaOH→NaCl+H_2​O$

**Step 2:** Identify mole ratio

$n_a : n_b = 1 : 1$

**Step 3:** Calculate moles of HCl used

$Moles=Concentration×Volume(dm^3)$

$Moles HCl=0.100×\frac{24.50}{1000}​=0.00245 mol$

**Step 4:** Use mole ratio to find moles of NaOH

$Moles NaOH=0.00245 mol$

**Step 5:** Calculate concentration of NaOH

$\text{Concentration}= \frac{Moles}{Volume(dm^3)}$

$\text{Concentration NaOH}= \frac{0.00245}{25/1000}$

Answer: 0.0980 mol/dm³
