# **Acid-Base Titrations**

## What is titration?

* A **titration** is a technique used to find the **exact volume** of one solution needed to **neutralise** a known volume of another solution.

### **Key Idea:**

$\text{Acid + Base → Salt + Water}$

### **Purpose:**

* Find **unknown concentration** of an acid or alkali

* Find **unknown volume** of a solution
