### Forming Conclusions

**Steps:**

* Describe the **trend** (increase/decrease)

* Quote **data** to support

* **Explain** using scientific knowledge

* **Compare** with prediction (if given)

**Example:**

As temperature increases from 20°C to 60°C, the rate of reaction increases. At 20°C, 12 cm³ of gas was produced in 10 s; at 60°C, 30 cm³ was produced in 10 s. This is because higher temperature gives particles more kinetic energy, so more frequent successful collisions occur.
