### Example Method: Rate of Reaction

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/1c576f5770ce427e742e10a4.webp =999x)

**Question:** Investigate how temperature affects the rate of reaction between magnesium and hydrochloric acid.

**Method:**

* Measure 25 cm³ of 1.0 mol/dm³ HCl into conical flask

* Heat to required temperature in water bath

* Record temperature with thermometer

* Add 0.1 g Mg ribbon

* Start stop-watch immediately

* Record volume of gas every 10 s

* Repeat at 20, 30, 40, 50, 60°C

* Repeat each temperature for reliability
