## Question 4

- Use a measuring balance to weigh a known mass of the polish

- Put the polish into a beaker and add dilute nitric acid into the beaker and heat it

- Filter the mixture

- Wash the residue with distilled water and let it dry between two filter papers

- Weigh the residue

- Calculate percentage by mass of silicon (iv) oxide

- Percentage = $\frac{\text{mass of } \text{SiO}\\_2}{\text{mass of polish}} \times 100$
