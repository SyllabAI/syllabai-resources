## Addition and Condensation Polymerisation

### **Addition Polymerisation**

* This only occurs in monomers that contain double carbon (**C=C)** bonds.
* Polymers produced using alkene monomers
* Forms only a polymer molecule
* Poly(ethene): is a polymer produced from ethene by addition polymerisation

![](../../../assets/ee27cc712fe0-image057.png)

Double bond splits, and the polymer is formed. (Polymers have no double bonds)

### **Condensation Polymerisation**

* When two different monomers are linked together with the removal of a smaller molecule, usually **water** (forms one H<sub>2</sub>0 molecules per linkage).

  1. **Nylon** (polyamide) is made from a **dicarboxylic acid monomer and a diamine monomer** (a compound with an NH<sub>2</sub> functional group). Forms **amide linkage.**

     ![](../../../assets/c39d04462f97-b2fbxsjw9nydz7rtn178114284fd1694f21e0eb25.png)

  2. **PET** (polyester) is made from a dicarboxylic acid monomer and diols (alcohol with an -OH functional group). Forms **ester linkage.**

     ![](../../../assets/ab431f02019c-b2fbxsjw9nydz7rtn8dbe30e2d48e7e2521c48fb3.png)
