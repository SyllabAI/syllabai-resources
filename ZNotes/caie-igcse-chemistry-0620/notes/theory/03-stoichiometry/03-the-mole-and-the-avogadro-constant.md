## The Mole and the Avogadro Constant

A **mole** of a substance is the amount that contains the same number of
units as the number of carbon atoms in 12 grams of carbon-12

* A mole is the A<sub>r</sub> or M<sub>r</sub> expressed in grams e.g.
  1 mole of Carbon-12 is equal to 12 grams.
* It is equal to 6.02 × 10<sup>23</sup> particles; this number is called **Avogadro’s constant**.
* 1 mole also occupies a volume of 24dm<sup>3</sup> at room temperature and pressure
