## Percentage Purity & Yield

* $\text{Percentage}\ \text{Purity} = \ \frac{\text{mass}\ \text{of}\ \text{product}\ \left( \text{pure} \right)}{\text{mass}\ \text{of}\ \text{compound}\ \left( \text{impure} \right)} \times 100$
* $\text{Percentage}\ \text{Yield} = \ \frac{\text{actual}\ \text{mass}\ \text{obtained}}{\text{calculated/theoretical}\ \text{mass}} \times 100$
