## Types of Oxides

* Metal oxides are **basic,** e.g. Copper oxide and Calcium oxide
* Non-metal oxides are **acidic,** e.g. sulfur dioxide and carbon dioxide
* Aluminium and zinc form **amphoteric oxides,** e.g. zinc oxide
* Reaction with Acid:
  * $ZnO + 2HCl$ → $ZnCl_{2}$ + $H_{2}O$
  * $Al_{2}O_{3}$ + $6HCl$ → $2AlCl_{3}$ + $3H_{2}O$
* Reaction with Base:
  * $ZnO + 2NaOH$ → $Na_{2}ZnO_{2}$ + $H_{2}O$

  * $Al_{2}O_{3}$ + $2NaOH$ → $2NaAlO_{2}$ + $H_{2}O$
* Oxides that react with neither acids nor bases are **neutral,** e.g. nitrous monoxide and carbon monoxide
