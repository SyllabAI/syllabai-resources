# Acids, Bases and Salts

## Properties of Acids

**Acid**: a substance that produces hydrogen ions (H<sup>+</sup>) when dissolved in water.

Definition of Acids: **Proton donors**.

**Indicators**

* Have a pH between 1 (strong) and 6 (weak)
* Turns blue **litmus paper** red
* Turns **methyl orange** indicator red
* Colourless in **Thymolphthalein**

### **Weak and Strong Acids**

1. **Strong acids:** **completely dissociated in aqueous solution** producing lots of H<sup>+</sup> ions

e.g: HCl (aq) → H<sup>+</sup> (aq) + Cl<sup>-</sup> (aq)

2. **Weak acids:** **partially dissociated in an aqueous solution** producing fewer H<sup>+</sup> ions

e.g: CH<sub>3</sub>COOH (aq) ⇌ H<sup>+</sup> (aq) +  CH<sub>3</sub>COO<sup>-</sup> (aq)

**Chemical properties**

* Acid + metal → salt + hydrogen gas
* Acid + base → salt + water
* Acid + metal carbonate → salt + carbon dioxide + water
