## Mixture of Substances

* Mixture: A mixture is a substance made from two or more other substances not chemically bonded together
* Solution: A mixture of one or more solutes dissolved in a solvent
* Solute: A substance that dissolved in a solvent
* Solvent: A substance that dissolves a solute
* Saturated Solution: A solution containing the maximum amount of solute that can be dissolved in a solvent at a given temperature and pressure
