## Simple Distillation

**Used to separate a solvent from a solution**

* The impure liquid is heated in a round bottom flask
* When it boils, the steam rises into the attached condenser
* Condenser cools the steam to a pure liquid, and it drops into the beaker
* A thermometer is placed to ensure the **highest boiling point is not exceeded.**
