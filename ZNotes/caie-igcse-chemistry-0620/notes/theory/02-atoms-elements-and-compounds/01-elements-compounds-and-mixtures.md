# Atoms, Elements and Compounds

## Elements, Compounds and Mixtures

* **Elements**: A substance made of atoms that share the **SAME number of protons** and cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical methods.
  * There are 118 elements in the periodic table, such as sodium.
* **Compounds**: Two or more elements **chemically bonded together (in a fixed proportion).**
  * e.g. Carbon Dioxide, Sodium Chloride
* **Mixtures**: Two or more substances **not chemically bonded together.**
  * e.g. Sand and Water, Oil and Water
