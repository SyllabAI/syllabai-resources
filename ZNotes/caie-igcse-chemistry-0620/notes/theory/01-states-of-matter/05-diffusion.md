## Diffusion

**Diffusion:** the net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration as a result of their random movement (Brownian motion) until equilibrium is reached.

* The rate of diffusion is most rapid in gases \> liquids \> solids.

### **Effect of Relative Molecular Mass in Diffusion**

* The rate at which gases diffuse depends on the molecular mass of the gas. At the same temperature, gases with a lower molecular mass diffuse faster because their particles have a higher average speed than those of gases with a higher molecular mass.
* At the same temperature, molecules with a lower mass move faster on average than those with a higher mass.
