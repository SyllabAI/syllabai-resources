## Uses of Metals

1. **Aluminium**
   * Manufacture of Aircraft/Cars (Low density)
   * Food Containers (Resistant to corrosion)
   * Overhead electrical cable (Good conductor of electricity/ductile)
2. **Zinc**
   * Galvanizes Iron = coats it to stop it from rusting (protective coating)
   * Alloys – brass/bronze
   * Batteries
   * Sacrificial Protection
3. **Copper**
   * Electrical Wiring (Good conductor of electricity/Ductile)
