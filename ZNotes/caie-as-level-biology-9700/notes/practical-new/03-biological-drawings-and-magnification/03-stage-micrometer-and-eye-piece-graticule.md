
## Stage Micrometer and Eyepiece Graticule

* Sometimes, when we need to measure the actual size of a specimen, using measurement tools like rulers or measuring tapes is impossible.

* To combat this issue we use:

  * Stage micrometer: A small glass slide with a scale of known length. It is used to calibrate the eyepiece graticule.

  * Eye piece graticule: A small scale placed inside the microscope eyepiece. It has arbitrary divisions and is used to measure the size of specimens.

* We use these two tools together and follow these steps to find the actual size of an image:

   1. Place your eye piece graticule into the microscope eyepiece.

   2. Place the stage micrometer onto the stage of the microscope

   3. Find the 0 point on the stage micrometer and line it up to the 0 on the eyepiece graticule

   4. Find a point where one division on the stage micrometer and the eyepiece graticule line up.

   5. Count the eyepiece graticule divisions between the two matching points

   6. Count the stage micrometer divisions between the two matching points

   7. Since the stage micrometer is a known length of 1 mm and there are 100 divisions, each single division is equal to 0.01mm (10 μm) and 1 of the 10 divisions is 0.1 mm (100 μm).

      ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/e3a4db62a508ff9c5f7c00e7.webp =480x)

   8. You can then use the formula to find the length of one eyepiece graticule division: Value of 1 EPG division = number of EPG divisions / distance on stage micrometer​ 

   9. Remove the stage micrometer and place the specimen on the stage.

  10.  Count how many eyepiece graticule divisions span the structure you want to measure.

  11.  Multiply this number by the calibrated value of 1 eyepiece graticule division to calculate the actual size.

* Remember: you must recalibrate the value of the eyepiece graticule division length after changing the lens powers.
