## Explaining trends

* Some questions will ask you to identify the trend and the reason behind it. 

* When you have identified the trend, you need to be able to give reason and explain the scientific reason as to why that trend happened. 

* To have a thorough explanation, your answer must include:

  * Scientific / factual information 

  * How this effects the DV

  * Evidence from the graph. 

* For example, take the example of rate of change of reaction based on temperature. 

  * The trend would be rapidly changing. 

  * First, add scientific information. 

    * As temperature increases, so does the rate of the reaction. This is because molecules gain more kinetic energy and move faster allowing for more collisions of the molecules.

  * Then, how does this effect the DV

    * So as the temperature increases, so does the rate of reaction

  * Lastly, add the evidence from the graph 

    * The rate of reaction increases as temperature increases, rising from 2 cm³ min⁻¹ at 20°C to 8 cm³ min⁻¹ at 50°C.
