# Using a Microscope

## Parts of a Microscope

- **Eyepiece(ocular) lens**  – lens you look through; magnifies the image.
- **Objective (lenses)** – provide different magnifications; closest to the specimen.
- **Stage** – platform where the slide is placed.
- **Stage clips** – hold the slide in place.
- **Coarse adjustment knob** – moves the lens/stage quickly to roughly focus the image.
- **Fine focus knob** – makes small adjustments to produce a sharp image.
- **Diaphragm** – controls the amount of light passing through the specimen.
- **Light source** – provides light to illuminate the specimen.
- **Revolving nosepiece** – rotates to change objective lenses/magnification.
