## How to focus a Microscope

* Once you have prepared your slide, you now need to view it under a microscope

* You can do this by:

  1. Placing your prepared slide on the stage and securing it with the clips

  2. Start by placing the microscope on the lowest setting (usually the yellow banded objective lenses 10x)

  3. Use the Course adjustment knob to bring the specimen into a rough focus (until you can see a rough outline of the specimen) 

  4. Then use the fine focus knob to sharpen the image until you can see the clearest possible image of the specimen. 

  5. If asked, move to a higher setting (usually blue banded objective lens 40x)

  6. Use only the fine focus adjustment knob until a sharp image of specimen is formed. 

* Now your micrograph is ready!

  * Micrograph: The magnified image of a specimen formed under a microscope.
