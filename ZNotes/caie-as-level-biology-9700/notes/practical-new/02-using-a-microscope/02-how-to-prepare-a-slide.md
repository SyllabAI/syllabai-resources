## How to prepare a slide

* Preparing a slide means setting up the specimen to be as clear and visible under the microscope as possible. 
* You can prepare the slide by:
  1. Selecting the most suitable specimen (*this means either the thinnest, largest or clearest depending on what the experiment asks for*).

  2. Placing the specimen onto your slide using the forceps and flattening it to remove any folds.

  3. If the specimen is clear or very lightly colored, use the staining agent. 

     * place the specimen onto the slide as flat as possible

     * use a dropper to place staining agent onto the specimen until the whole specimen is covered. 

     * once the specimen is appropriately stained, remove the excess

  4. To prevent the specimen from drying out, cover the specimen with distilled water, the original solution it came in (or the staining agent). 

  5. once the specimen is as flat as possible, place the coverslip onto the specimen. 

  6. Take a second to flatten the coverslip as much as possible and remove as many air bubbles as possible. 

  7. remove any excess hydration that isn’t under the coverslip.
* Now your slide is ready to be viewed under a microscope!
