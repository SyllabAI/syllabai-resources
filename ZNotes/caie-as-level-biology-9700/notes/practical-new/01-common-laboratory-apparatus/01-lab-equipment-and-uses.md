## Know the difference

* Some apparatus can be very similar but they all serve different purposes

  * **Beaker vs measuring cylinder:**

    * **Beaker**: Mainly to hold and mix larger volumes of liquids

    * **Measuring cylinder**: Mainly used to measure smaller volumes of liquids more accurately

  * **Measuring cylinder vs Pipette:**

    * **Measuring cylinder**: Measures volumes of liquid up to 20 cm³

    * **Pipette**: measures much smaller volumes of liquid e.g 1-5 cm³

  * **Forceps vs Scalpels:**

    * **Forceps**: Used to pick up and handles the specimens

    * **Scalpel**: Used to cut specimens

  * **Coverslips vs slide:**

    * **Coverslips**: Much smaller than the slide, used to hold down and flatten specimens

    * **Slide**: Where the specimen is placed along with the stain
