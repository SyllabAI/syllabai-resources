## Safety Precautions

* Safety precautions are rules that must be followed in a laboratory to prevent accidents and keep the experiment running smoothly.

* These rules include:

  * **Personal safety tips**:

    * Wear gloves, lab coat and safety goggles when handling irritants or chemicals.

    * Tie back long hair to prevent it from getting in the way

    * Wear close-toed shoes

  * **Chemical safety tips**:

    * Read labels of chemicals before use

    * Do not touch or have any contact with the chemicals to eyes, open wounds or skin

    * Wash hands before and after the experiment

  * **Apparatus safety tips**: 

    * Handle glassware carefully

    * When using sharp instruments, cut away from the skin

    * Turn off electrical equipment after use
