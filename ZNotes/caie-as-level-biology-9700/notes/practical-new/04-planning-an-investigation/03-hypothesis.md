
## Hypothesis

* **Definition:** A hypothesis is a testable prediction about the expected outcome of an investigation.

* **Purpose:** States the expected relationship between the independent and dependent variables.

* **Example:** Increasing light intensity will increase the rate of photosynthesis in pondweed

* **Types of hypothesis:**

  * **Null hypothesis:** Predicts that there will be no significant relationship or difference between the variables.

  * **Alternative hypothesis**: Predicts that there will be a significant relationship or difference between the variables.
