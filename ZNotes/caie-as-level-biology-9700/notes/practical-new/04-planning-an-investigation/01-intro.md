# Planning an Investigation

* Before conducting an experiment, it’s important to have a rough idea of the procedures, aim and materials needed.

* To be able to make a thorough plan, you would need to consider:

  * **Variables**: factors that will change in the experiment

  * **Hypothesis**: a prediction that will be tested 

  * **Controls and standardisation**: factors that remain the same through out the experiment

  * **Method and apparatus**: the order of procedures and the tools used to acquire the data

  * **Data collection and analysis**: Reading and interpreting the data

  * **Safety and ethical considerations**: ways to prevent harm and follow ethical guidelines.
