 

## Standardization

* Procedures must be kept the same through out the experiment to ensure that the experiment is only testing the effect of the independent variable. 

* The process of ensuring method of procedures are kept same through out the experiment is called standardization

* Example, you are testing the effect of enzyme concentration on a reaction’s rate. 

  * Your method of testing, approach and data collection must be the same

## **Control variable**

* Controls are variables that are kept same through out the experiment

* They show that the IV has an effect on the specimen and not the other factors in the experiment

* In the same example of “you are testing the effect of enzyme concentration on a reaction’s rate”

  * the control variables would be ensuring that all the solutions have the same temperature, buffer solution, volume, etc..
