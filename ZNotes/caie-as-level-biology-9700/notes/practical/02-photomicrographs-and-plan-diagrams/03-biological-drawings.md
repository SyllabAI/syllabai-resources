## Biological drawings

  - Draw in clear, unbroken lines. Do not sketch.
 
  - No shading, show the layers and proportion of tissues, not individual cells. This can be identified by looking at the change in staining of the cells

  - Label using ruled lines.

  - Low power diagrams:
    
      - Identify the different tissues, using high power to help if
        necessary.
    
      - Draw all tissues and completely enclose each tissue by lines.
    
      - Do not draw individual cells.
    
      - Accuracy is important. Do not draw from imagination. Draw only what you see.
      
      - Make sure to keep the proportions of tissues the same as seen in the microscope, this is the part being tested i.e. the ability to recognize different tissues in a specimen and their proportions\shape.

  - High power diagrams:
    
      - Make large drawings of the group of cells indicated in the question.
    
      - Draw the nuclei but do not shade it
