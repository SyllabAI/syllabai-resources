## Experiment-wise Errors and its Improvements

### Qualitative food test (relating to color change only)

**Errors**

* Difficulty in judging colours especially if the concentrations are low resulting in light colours.

* If heating, then temperature is not constant, as there is heat loss to the surroundings.

* Different molecules in the same solution, may interfere with the colours

**Improvements**

* In case of heating, thermostatic water bath should be used to control temperature and avoid the heat loss to the surroundings.

* Colour standard (colorimeter) to help in better judgement of colours.

* Use a white tile/card to help with judging/comparing colours

### Quantitative food test (relating to the colour change with concentrations)

**Errors**

* In case of determining unknown concentrations: it can be between 2 known concentrations

* Difficulty in comparing colours.

* Temperature varies due to heat loss to the surrounding - DCPIP test for vitamin C: drops fall on the sides of test tubes.

**Improvements**

* Use more concentrations with a narrower range (smaller gaps between concentrations) or wider range (wider gaps between the concentrations), then plot a graph with the obtained results and find the unknown concentration by reading off the graph, this is a more accurate way to estimate concentration for the unknown.

* Use colorimeter or a colour standard to judge the colour better.

* Use a white tile/ card to compare colour better.

* Use a thermostatic water bath to avoid heat loss.

* Use wider test tube when using dropper for adding reagent so it doesn’t fall on to sides of test tube.

* Repeat and take average for more accuracy.

### Osmosis

**Errors**

* Time tissues left in solutions is not enough to observe complete plasmolysis.

* Solution may evaporate and concentration might change due to this.

* Difficulty in judging degree of plasmolysis.

* Difficulty in cutting the samples in same/ correct dimensions.

* When reading lengths parallax error may occur.

**Improvements**

* Leave for longer time in the solution.

* Prepare more concentrations with a narrower or wider range (give the example of conc. from dilution).

* Cover petri dish to prevent any evaporation of the solution.

* Prepare multiple samples of each solution to take average later for accurate results.

### Enzyme reactions

**Errors**

* Difficulty in judging the endpoint (e.g. in an amylase experiment, the colour with iodine is not clear.

* Temperature or pH is not controlled (when these variables aren't being tested).

* Bubbles produced aren't of the same size, or bubbles are being produced at a faster rate so difficult to count and may be missed.

* Paper may stick to the wall or the bottom of the test tube (when using litmus paper to check pH of the solution)

**Improvements**

* Use a colorimeter instead of judging colours or use a white tile/ card against the test tube for better colour comparison.

* Measure the volume of gas using a gas syringe instead of counting bubbles.

* Use wider test tubes (in case of using litmus paper).

* Control temperature using a thermostatic water bath.

* Control pH using buffer solution.

### Agar

**Errors**

* Difficulty in cutting the agar squares into equal dimensions.

* Difficulty in judging the colour range.

* Agar is not of equal depth.

* Pigmentation of the agar is not even.

* Agar may be damaged during the cutting

**Improvements**

* Use moulds for preparing agar squares with equal dimensions.

* Use different indicators with clearer endpoint.

* Use a wider or narrower range of concentrations (when you want to find unknown).

* Use a white tile/ card below the beakers for better judgment of colours.

### Immobilization of enzyme (formation of beads)

**Errors**

* Beads are not of equal size.

* Beads stick to the sides of the test tube and with each other.

* Forceps may damage beads.

* Difficulty to introduce drops using a syringe.

* The test tube isn't vertical and of the same size.

* Temperature and/or pH is not controlled (only use this if these variables are not being tested).

**Improvements**

* Use a sieve with equal size holes to produce equal size beads with equal diameter.

* Use wider test tubes.

* Stain the beads for clearer movement.

* Use a retort stand to keep the test tube vertical, use test tubes with equal sizes.

* Use a spatula or spoon instead of forceps to put beads in the test tube.

* Control temperature using a thermostatic water bath and for pH control use a buffer solution.

### Color diffusion

**Errors**

* Difficulty in judging the intensity of diffused colour.

* Some pigments remain on the surface of the tissue despite washing.

* Temperature is not controlled.

* Time is not enough for diffusion to take place.

* There is a narrow range of concentration

**Improvements**

* Use colour standards with degree of colour and its name for better colour description, or numbered colour scale with images showing the colour.

* Use white tile/ card against test tubes to compare colours properly.

* Control the temperature using thermostatic water bath.

* Dry the samples with paper towel before placing them in solutions.

* Leave the samples for longer time in solution.

* Use wider range of concentrations so the colours produced show difference

### Density of solution

**Errors**

* Difficulty in introducing drops by syringe.

* The pigmentation is too dark or light.

* The drop ruptures (it releases a flow that is not clear).

* Too wide or narrow range of concentrations.

* Test tube may not be vertical.

**Improvements**

* Use dropper instead of syringe.

* Increase or decrease the drops of the stain (to make the pigment lighter or darker) or use different stains.

* Prepare a wider/ narrow range of concentrations.

* Use a retort stand to make the test tube vertical.

### Visking tubes

**Errors**

* Difficulty in opening and tying the visking tubes.

* Visking tube is too short or too long.

* Difficulty in rinsing the visking tube properly (water may enter inside it).

* Mixing is not constant.

* Drops aren't of equal volume.

* Difficulty in judging colour.

**Improvements**

* Make the visking tube longer or shorter (depending on what size it was before).

* Dry the visking tube with paper towel before placing in the beaker.

* Mix using an electronic mixer.

* Use a syringe instead of a dropper to introduce an equal volume of solution and indicator.

* Use color standard/ colorimeter to get a better judgment of color.
