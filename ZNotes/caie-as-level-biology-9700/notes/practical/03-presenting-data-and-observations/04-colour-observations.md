## Colour observations

- When writing results for the tests for biological molecules;

  - mention color with terms like 'dark' or 'pale'

  - or with whatever color changes you have learned during preparation

  - e.g. for a positive test for proteins you observe a subtle lilac or purple, term it as pale lilac.

  - For reducing sugars, it is best to stick with green, yellow, orange, brown, and brick-red.
