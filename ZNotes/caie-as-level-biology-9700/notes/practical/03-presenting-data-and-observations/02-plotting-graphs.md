## Plotting Graphs 

  - Decide type of graph:
    
      - Line graph (Both variables are continuous)
    
      - Histogram (Independent variable is continuous)
    
      - Bar chart (Dependent variable is continuous)
    
      - Bars touch in histograms only, not in bar charts.

  - Independent variable at x-axis and dependent at y-axis.

<!-- end list -->

  - Use linear scale with sensible (1s, 2s, 5s, 10s, …) intervals.

  - Use more than half of the graph paper provided.

  - Label each axis fully, according to the variable’s column heading.

<!-- end list -->

  - For line graphs:
    
      - Plot points with ✕ or ⊙ marks.
    
      - Join successive points with straight lines.
    
      - If there is a clear relation, draw a smooth wave, or line of best fit.
      - Do not extrapolate the line.

<!-- end list -->

- For histogram and bar chart:

  - Do not shade your bars

  - You could be asked to create a key for the graph. Create different patterns like criss-cross, and diagonal lines on the bars with their corresponding key at the side (very rare to be asked in your exam)
