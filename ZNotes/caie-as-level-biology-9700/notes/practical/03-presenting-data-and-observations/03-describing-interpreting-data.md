## Describing & Interpreting Data 

  - Describe overall trend.

  - Comment on changes in gradient.

  - Quote figures with units to support claim.

  - Avoid phrases that suggest something is happening over time unless
    it is the independent variable.

  - Draw a conclusion by connecting it to description using theoretical
    reasoning.

  - Conclusion should be simple, clean, focused and scientifically
    explainable statement describing deduction regarding the hypothesis
    from results.
