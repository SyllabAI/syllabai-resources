## Water

1. Water is an excellent solvent: due to its polarity, dissolves things such as ions and any polar compounds its a **universal** solvent.

2. Excellent transport medium: due to its dipole nature.

3. Low density as ice: prevent water in oceans from freezing all the way and killing marine life.

4. High specific heat capacity: (due to many hydrogen bonds) water is slow when releasing and absorbing heat. for example when you’re at the beach the sand is scorching hot yet the water is cool.

5. High latent heat of vaporization: meaning water does not turn from liquid to gas easily by evaporation so can cause a cooling effect, thats why you sweat to cool down.

6. Water molecules are cohesive: forces between molecules meaning they stick together, thats how water gets transported in xylem by forming a continuous column.

   Water molecules are adhesive: are attracted to different molecules, such as cellulose part in xylem.

7. High surface tension: as the attraction between other water molecules is greater than the attraction between water molecules and air.
