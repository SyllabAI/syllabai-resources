# Biological molecules

## Test for biological molecules

### Benedict’s test for reducing sugars:

* To standardize the test, we control certain variables by keeping them constant  such as :
  * * Same volume of sample.

    * Same volume of reagent.

    * Same heating temperature.

    * Same duration of heating.
* Add equal volumes of sample and benedicts solution.
* Heat in a water bath till boiling or 80°C.
  * If negative stays blue.

  * If positive it turns a range of colors over time it turns from blue to green to yellow to orange to red.

  * Changes colour as blue $Cu^{2+}$ ions to  insoluble red $Cu_2O$
* To estimate concentration of glucose :
  * Compare the final colour to a standardized colour chart or concentration of known glucose concentration. The intensity of the precipitate correlates to the amount of sugar present.

  * Record the time taken for the first visible colour change to occur, a higher concentration results in a faster colour change.
* Reducing sugars are all sugars except sucrose, so glucose, galactose, fructose, lactose, maltose.

### Benedicts test for non-reducing sugars:

* Non-reducing sugar is sucrose.

* Add similar volume of hydrochloric acid to hydrolyze sugar.

* Heat in water bath for  2 minutes sucrose breaks down to glucose and fructose.

* Add sodium bicarbonate till no more bubbles (to neutralize the acid) add a volume of benedict to cover both the volumes of solution and hydrochloric acid.

* If negative stays blue.

* If positive it turns a range of colors over time it turns from blue to green to yellow to orange to red.

### Iodine test for starch:

* Add a volume of sample and 2-10 drops of iodine.

* Add equal number of drops.

  * Positive result from yellow/brown to blue-black.

  * Negative remains yellow/brown.

* Turns blue-black as it forms a poly-iodine complex.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/b5bb1875b43301a0243cfe07.webp =755x)

### Biuret test for proteins:

* Add a volume of sample and equal volume of sodium hydroxide and few drops of Copper sulphate.

  * Positive result turn from blue to purple.

  * If negative remains blue.

* Copper sulphate forms a purple complex with protein under alkaline medium.

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/a93510ff74b00266690a2f7a.webp =771x)

### Emulsion test for lipids:

* Add volume of sample ($2cm^{3}$) add few drops of ethanol, shake and then add ($2cm^{3}$) of cold water.

  * Positive results add turn cloudy.

  * Negative result remain clear.

* Lipids are insoluble in water so it reflects light rays.

### Key terms to remember :

* **Macromolecule:** large molecule formed by linking monomers.

* **Polymer**: large molecule formed by identical monomers.

* **Condensation reaction**: removing water by a chemical reaction to join two molecules together.

* **Hydrolysis:** adding water by a chemical reaction to break down a large molecule into smaller molecules. A covalent

  bond forms between 2 monomers joined together via a condensation reaction.

* to provide a stable, permanent link between subunits
