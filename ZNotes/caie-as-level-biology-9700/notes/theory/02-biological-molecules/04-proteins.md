## Proteins

* Formed from **C**arbon, **H**ydrogen, **O**xygen, **N**itrogen, **S**ulfur.

* Polymers formed from amino acids linked by **peptide bonds** in condensation reaction.

* All amino acids have the same basic structure but different variable groups/R groups.

* R groups affect how amino acids interact with others within the protein molecule but are not involved in condensation reaction.

* There are 20 different amino acids.

* Peptide bonds are broken down by hydrolysis in which they return back.
