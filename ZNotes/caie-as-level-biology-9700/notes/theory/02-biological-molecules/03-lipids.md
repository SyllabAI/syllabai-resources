## Lipids

* Formed from ++C++arbon, ++H++ydrogen, and ++O++xygen but has less oxygen than carbohydrates.

* Types of lipids in this syllabus are triglycerides and phospholipids.

### Triglycerides

* Act as energy source (more than carbohydrates).

* Thermal insulator.

* Electrical insulator.

* Supports organs/mechanical protection.

* Hydrophobic: does not dissolve in water.

* Converted into hydrophilic lipoproteins.

* Dissolve in organic compounds e.g. ethanol.

* Made with 1 glycerol molecule and 3 fatty acid molecule joined by **ester bond**.

### Phospholipids

* Made with 2 fatty acids 1 glycerol molecule and a phosphate head.

* The phosphate head is hydrophilic so can form hydrogen bonds with water but fatty acid tails are hydrophobic

* Makes up the phospholipid bilayer in the cell surface membrane.
