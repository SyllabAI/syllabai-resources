## Cells

* Basic structural and functional unit of life.

* For a structure to be called a cell, it must have:

  * Cell surface membrane

  * Cytoplasm

  * Genetic material

  * Ribosome

* Divided into **2 major types**

1. Eukaryotes

2. Prokaryotes

### Major Eukaryotic cells

1. **Animal cell**

   * $10 - 30$ $\mu \text{m}$

   * Cell wall is absent

   * Certain organelles are found in animal cells but never in plants, such as :

     * Centriole

     * Lysosome

     * Microvilli

     * Cilia

     * Flagella

2. **Plant cell**

   * $10 - 30$ $\mu \text{m}$

   * Cellulose cell wall - present
