## Organelles found only in plant cells

### Chloroplast

* Double membrane-bound.

  * Both membranes are smooth.

* Contain thylakoid which are packed with chlorophyll.

* Groups of granum (plural: grana) are interconnected by intergranal thylakoids/lamella.

* They contain **stroma**, which consists of:

  * Circular DNA

  * Enzymes

  * 70S ribosomes

  * Starch granules

* **How to differentiate between thylakoid, granum, and grana**

  * Imagine a coin that’s the thylakoid

  * A stack of coins is the granum

  * 2 or more stacks are grana

* **Function of chloroplast:** Site of photosynthesis

### Cell wall

* Rigid as it contains cellulose fibers.

* Prevents cell wall from bursting.

* Freely permeable.

* Allow the plant to remain turgid.

### Plasmodesmata

* Cytoplasmic connections between cells that allow for a symplastic pathway and link the cell to the neighboring cell.

![plasmodesmata](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/40d5b079478c2395ac365ef2.webp =544x)

### Vacuole

* Single membrane-bound, called the tonoplast.

* Contains sap solution.

* **Function of the vacuole:** Maintain turgidity.
