## Organelles found only in animal cells

### Lysosome

* Single membrane bound $0.5$ $\mu\text{m}$

* Contains hydrolytic enzymes.

**Function:**

* Breakdown of old and dead organelles.

* Autolysis ( breakdown of dead cells).

* Breakdown of bacteria inside phagocyte.

### Centriole

* Non-membrane bound.

* Consists of a pair of centrioles.

* Found at 90° angles.

* 9 triplets made of 27 microtubules.

  * Microtubules are hollow cylinders made of alpha and beta tubulin dimers arranged into 13 protofilaments.

**Function:**

* Duplicate during cell division and move to opposite poles then form spindle fibers.

* Microtubule organizing center; microtubules extend from centrioles and attach themselves to chromosomes forming spindle fibers.

### Cilia

* Whip like extensions in many eukaryotic cells.

* Hair like structure.

* The basal body has a microtubule arrangement of 9 triplets like centrioles.

  * However, the cilium has a 9\+2 arrangement 9 doublets and 2 central singlets.

  * Each doublet has 2 microtubules, one smaller (B) and one larger (A).

  * B has 10 protofilaments while A has 13 protofilaments.

  * A had arms called dynein arms, which holds B of the neighboring microtubule during beating of the cilia.

### Microvilli

* Finger-like extensions.

* Used to increase surface area to volume ratio.
