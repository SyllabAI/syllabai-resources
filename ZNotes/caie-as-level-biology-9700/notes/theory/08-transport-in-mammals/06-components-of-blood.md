## Components of blood

### Red blood cells

* Carry and transport oxygen from lungs to body cells.
* Has haemoglobin which binds with 4 oxygen molecules
  * * The first oxygen molecules, the whole haem molecule becomes slightly distorted.

    * Making the 2nd and 3rd oxygen molecules easier to bind to the haem due to the distortion

    * The last oxygen molecule binds with difficulty as the haemoglobin is almost fully saturated

  * Carbon dioxide and haemoglobin form carbamino haemoglobin.

    * When binding, haemoglobin binds to the amino group, not the haem group.

  * When reacting with carbon monoxide, it forms carboxy haemoglobin

    * Also binds to the haem group like oxygen

    * Has higher affinity to haemoglobin than oxygen

    * Acts as a competitive inhibitor

  * When reacting with hydrogen ions, it forms hemoglobinic acid

**Adaptations**

* Biconcave to increase surface area

* No nucleus to make space for haemoglobin

* Haemoglobin to combine with oxygen to form oxyhaemoglobin

* 7 micrometers to fit into capillaries

### White blood cells

* Immune cells that protect the body against pathogens and include **monocytes, neutrophils and lymphocytes**.

* They are divided into different types based on their structure and functions, which are :

  1. Monocytes

  2. Neutrophils

  3. Lymphocytes

* **Neutrophils**

  * Make up the largest proportion of WBCs in the blood.

  * They are **phagocytes** that engulf and digest pathogens, especially bacteria.

  * They are involved in the **non-specific immune response**.

  * They have a **multi-lobed nucleus**.

* **Monocytes**

  * Are large WBCs with a **large, kidney-shaped nucleus**.

  * They are also **phagocytes**.

  * They can leave the blood and develop into **macrophages** in tissues.

  * Macrophages engulf pathogens and dead cells by **phagocytosis**.

  * They also help activate the specific immune response by **presenting antigens**.

* **Lymphocytes**

  * Have a **large nucleus** that occupies most of the cell.

  * They are involved in the **specific immune response**.

  * **B lymphocytes** produce antibodies.

  * **T lymphocytes** are involved in cell-mediated immunity, including destroying infected cells and activating other immune cells.

### Plasma

* It’s the liquid component of blood.

* It is mainly composed of water.

* It transports blood cells, nutrients, hormones, carbon dioxide, urea and other dissolved substances.

* It contains plasma proteins, including antibodies, fibrinogen and albumin.

* Fibrinogen is involved in blood clotting.

### Platelets

* Platelets are cell fragments formed from large cells in the bone marrow.

* They have no nucleus.

* They are involved in blood clotting.

* When a blood vessel is damaged, platelets become activated and help form a platelet plug.

* They release substances that help trigger the clotting cascade, leading to the formation of fibrin.
