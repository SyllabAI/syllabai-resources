## Cardiac cycle

#|
||

Steps in cardiac cycle

|

Events that happen

||
||

Atrial systole

|

* Atrial muscle contracts

* Increasing pressure in the atria and pushing blood into the ventricles

* Atrioventricular valve opens

* Semilunar valve closes

* Volume of atria decreases

||
||

Ventricular systole

|

* Ventricle contracts

* Causing ventricular pressure to increase. When ventricular pressure becomes greater than atrial pressure

* Atrioventricular valve closes causing the sound

* Semilunar valve opens

||
||

Atrial and ventricular diastole

|

* Ventricle muscles relax

* When arterial pressure becomes greater than ventricular pressure, the semilunar valves close. causing another sound
