##  Heart beat

1. The sinoatrial node is placed on the  muscular wall of the right atrium

2. It sends waves of excitation which spread throughout the atria

3. Non-conductive tissue bands prevent the waves of excitation from reaching the ventricles.

4. Waves of excitation reach the atrioventricular node, which delays the impulse by 0.1s so blood fills up the ventricle

5. Impulses travel down the Bundle of His to the apex (the bottom tip) of the heart, then spread upwards through Purkyne tissue

   * ventricular contraction squeezes blood upward toward the arterial exits.

* heart beat makes a lub-dub sound, which are the sounds when valves open and close

* If the heart beats are not controlled, it could be fatal and may be due to electric shock or damage to a large area of muscle in the heart wall.
