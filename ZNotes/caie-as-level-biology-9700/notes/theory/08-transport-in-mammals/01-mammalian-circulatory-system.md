# Transport in mammals

## Mammalian circulatory system

The mammalian circulatory system is a closed double circulatory system.

* Double meaning that blood passes twice through the heart in one complete circuit around the body.
* Closed meaning that blood passes through blood vessels.

The mammalian circulatory system consists of:

* Heart

* Blood vessels (arteries, arterioles, capillaries, venules, veins)

* Blood
