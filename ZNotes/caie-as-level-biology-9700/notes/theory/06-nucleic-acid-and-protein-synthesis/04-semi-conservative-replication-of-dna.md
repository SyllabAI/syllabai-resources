## Semi-conservative replication of DNA

* Occurs in S phase in mitosis (check chapter 5)

* From 2 strands to 4 strands

* The 2 strands are antiparallel; they are known as the leading strand and the lagging strand.

  * two strands run in opposite directions
  * Allows complementary bases to align correctly through **hydrogen bonding**.
  * Produces a stable, uniform **double-helical structure**.
  * Determines the direction in which DNA is **replicated** and nucleic acids are synthesised.
  * Means that DNA polymerase synthesises a new strand only in the **5′ → 3′ direction**, using the template strand in the 3 ′ → 5 ′ **direction**.

* Process where a DNA molecule is copied to give 2 DNA molecules; each DNA molecule has an old parental strand that acts as a template and forms a new strand

### Process of semi-conservative replication of DNA

* DNA unzips and unwinds as hydrogen bonds are broken by **DNA** helicase.

* 2 strands act as templates.

* The 2 strands:

  * **Leading strand**: formed by **DNA** polymerase, which arranges nucleotide in a sequence $5’$ to $3’$.

    * Arranges free nucleotides and ensures there is no mismatch.

    * Forms the phosphodiester bond.

    * Hydrogen bonds are **formed on their own** without an enzyme according to the base pairing rule.

  * **Lagging strand**: forms at a slower rate

    * As DNA polymerase only works from $5'$ to $3'$so forms okazaki fragments.

    * **Okazaki fragments** are short sections of newly synthesised DNA formed on the **lagging strand** during DNA replication.

      * Because DNA polymerase can only synthesise DNA in the **$5’ \rightarrow 3’$** direction

      * The strands are antiparallel; the lagging strand is made **discontinuously** in fragments. Imagine a person who can only go from the 5 to 3 and not the opposite, so they go to the start of end 5 and go to end 3 and repeat this will cause the fragments.

    * The fragments are later joined together by **DNA ligase** to form a continuous strand.
