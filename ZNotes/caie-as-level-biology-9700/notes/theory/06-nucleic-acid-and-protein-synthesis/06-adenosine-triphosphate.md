## Adenosine Triphosphate

Adenosine triphosphate (ATP) is a **phosphorylated nucleotide** that consists of three components:

* A nitrogenous base: adenine

* A pentose sugar: ribose

* Three phosphate groups: arranged in a chain

The adenine \+ ribose form adenosine, which is a nucleoside. When three phosphate groups are attached to the ribose, the molecule becomes ATP, a phosphorylated nucleotide.
