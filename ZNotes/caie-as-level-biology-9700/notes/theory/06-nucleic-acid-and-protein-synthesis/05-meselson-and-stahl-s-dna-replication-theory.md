## Meselson and Stahl's DNA Replication Theory

Provided strong experimental evidence that DNA replication is **semi-conservative**.

The experiment demonstrated that each newly formed DNA molecule contains one original (parental) strand and one newly synthesised strand.

**Experimental setup**

* grew *E. coli* bacteria in a medium containing **heavy nitrogen, 15N\^\{15\}N**. Over time, the bacteria incorporated $^{15}N$ into their DNA, making the DNA **denser** than DNA containing normal $^{14}N$.

* The bacteria were then transferred to a medium containing **$^{14}N$** and allowed to replicate.

* DNA samples were extracted after successive rounds of replication and separated using **density-gradient centrifugation**.

**Results**

* **Before transfer to $^{14}N$:**

  * All DNA contained $^{15}N$, producing a **heavy DNA band**.

  * $^{15}N/^N$

* **After one round of replication:**

  * All DNA formed an **intermediate-density band**.

  * $^{15}N/^N$

  * This showed that each DNA molecule contained one heavy parental strand and one newly synthesised light strand.

* **After two rounds of replication:**

  * Two bands appeared:

  * **Intermediate-density DNA:** $^{15}N$/$^N$

  * **Light DNA:** $^{14}N$/$^N$

The proportion of light DNA increased with further replication.
