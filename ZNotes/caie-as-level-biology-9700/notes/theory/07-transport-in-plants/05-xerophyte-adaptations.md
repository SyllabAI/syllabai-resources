## Xerophyte Adaptations

* Roots

  * Thick and branched to reach deep under ground to reach water and mineral ions.

  * Short and broad so absorb rainfall.

* Stem

  * Thick and fleshy to store water.

  * Short to avoid it being from knocked out.

  * Green as it has chlorophyll to absorb energy from sunlight.

  * Spikes to avoid being eaten by animals.

* Have stomata located exclusively or predominantly on the lower (abaxial) surface which are often sheltered inside sunken pits or rolled leaf folds to shield them from direct solar radiation

* Trichomes are hair-like structures which trap water vapor.

* Thick waxy cuticle reflects heat energy and is impermeable to water (such as vaseline so prevent water from getting in)

* Rolled leaves trap humid air and reduce water potential.

* Sunken stomata create humid air around it and reduce water potential gradient
