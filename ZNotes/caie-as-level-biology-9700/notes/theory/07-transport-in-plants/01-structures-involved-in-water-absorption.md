# Transport in plants

## Structures involved in water absorption

### Root hair cells

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/3a95fdec2cf90cb775f13725.webp =609x)

* Finger like projections to increase the surface area to volume ratio for absorption

* No chloroplasts as there is no light underground

* Has large number of mitochondria to release energy for active transport for uptake of ions

* Sap vacuole of low water potential so water goes in by osmosis from high water potential to lower water potential

### Cell wall

* Primary

  * Cellulose microfibrils arrange in criss cross patterns given high tensile embedded in the matrix.

  * Formed from pectin and hemicellulose.

  * Hemicellulose provides flexibility

* Secondary

  * Cellulose microfibrils arranged parallel
