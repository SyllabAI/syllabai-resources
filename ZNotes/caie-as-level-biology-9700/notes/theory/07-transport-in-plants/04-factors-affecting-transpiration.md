## Factors Affecting Transpiration

#|
||

### Factor

|

### Explanation

||
||

Humidity

|

Humidity is a measure of moisture. When it increases it reduces steepness of water potential gradient so decrease rate of transpiration

||
||

Light intensity

|

When light intensity increases guard cells respond so stomata opens so water vapor is lost increasing rate of transpiration

||
||

Temperature

|

As temperature increases, kinetic energy increases so transpiration rate increases as more energy supplied for water vapor to evaporate

||
||

Air movement

|

As air movement increases it removes water vapor so creates a steep concentration gradient so more water vapor exits the plant increasing rate of transpiration

||
|#

* In light, guard cells take up water by osmosis and become turgid, causing stomata to open

* In darkness, guard cells lose water by osmosis and move closer together, so stomata close
