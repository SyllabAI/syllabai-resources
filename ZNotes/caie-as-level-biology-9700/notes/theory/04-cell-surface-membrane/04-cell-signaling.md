## Cell Signaling

* Signaling cells produce specific chemicals called **ligands** and release them.

* The cell signaling molecule/**ligand** will bind to receptor on a specific binding site on the target cell

* This receptor binding site is complementary in shape

* Receptor change shape

* Activating G-protein

* Causes intracellular response

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/8af3edf8dd3f304dee97d197.webp =744x)

### The ligand can be:

* Hydrophilic

  * Can’t pass the hydrophobic core

  * Bind to receptors specific to their shape

  * Allows contact of a G protein, which triggers a series of enzyme-controlled reactions

* Hydrophobic

  * Pass through the core

  * Bind with receptor

  * Triggering the appropriate reaction
