# Enzymes

## Overview of enzymes

* Globular proteins (3D shape).

* An enzyme **reduces the activation energy** and forms the products.

* A biological catalyst.

* A catalyst speeds up the rate of a chemical reaction without being used up.

* An enzyme can be intracellular(inside the cell) or extracellular(outside the cell), **do not** confuse with **intercellular** which means in between cells.

* Enzymes are hydrophilic as they are globular proteins, and must at least reach tertiary structure.

### Activation Energy

* Minimum amount of energy needed to start the reaction.
