## Types of inhibitors and their effect on enzyme action

1. ### **Competitive inhibitor**

   * *Similar* (not same) shape to the substrate so can fit in the active site instead.

   * Increasing concentration reduces the effect of this inhibitor as it increases the chances of enzyme-substrate complexes being formed.

   ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/a853408ac7eb19ea8dd5e83a.webp =848x)

2. ### **Non- competitive**

   * A molecule which binds to a site other than the active site and is called the allosteric site.

   * Inhibitor disrupts the weak bonds and changes the shape of the active site.

   * Reversible, as it’s not affected by an increase in substrate concentration.

     ![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/90cf05c342cebfa7aaa1ce08.webp =806x)
