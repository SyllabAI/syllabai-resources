## Immobilization of enzyme

* enzymes are mixed with sodium alginate and dripped into a solution of calcium chloride

* sodium is displaced resulting in calcium alginate where the enzyme molecules are trapped

* beads are washed to remove sodium chloride

* beads are then placed into a suitable container forming column of beads

* Smaller beads are better to increase the surface area to volume ratio

* milk containing lactose is then poured onto the beads the lactase breaks down lactose into glucose and galactose and lactose-free milk is formed.
