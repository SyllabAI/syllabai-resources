## Stem Cells

* Undifferentiated cells that can undergo **mitosis** to produce new cells for **growth, cell replacement, and tissue repair**.

* They can also **differentiate** into specialised cell types.

* Their ability to differentiate into different types of cells is described by their **potency**:

  * **Totipotent:** can differentiate into **all cell types**, including extra-embryonic tissues. (Embryonic stem cells)

  * **Pluripotent:** can differentiate into **almost all body cell types**, but not extra-embryonic tissues.

  * **Multipotent:** can differentiate into a **limited range of related cell types**, such as different types of blood cells.(Adult stem cells)

* Stem cells differentiate, undergo mitosis, and the cytokinesis process
