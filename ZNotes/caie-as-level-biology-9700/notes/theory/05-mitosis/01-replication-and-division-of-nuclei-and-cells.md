# The Mitotic Cell Cycle

## Replication and division of nuclei and cells

### Structure of chromosome:

* Made up of 2 chromatids

  * A chromatid is made up of a DNA molecule wrapped around histone proteins to prevent tangling
  * Has 1 centromere
  * Sister chromatids - two genetically identical copies of a chromosome joined together at the centromere after DNA replication.

* Made of telomeres at each end

  * Protect  genetic code from being lost

### Homologous chromosomes

* Paternal and maternal

* Similar not identical

* Same

  * Position of centromere

  * Same length

  * Same type and sequence of genes

### Telomere

* Found on each end of **eukaryotic chromosomes**

* Repeating sequence

* Prevent chromosomes from **deteriorating** 

*  each time a cell divides, a **small section of telomere DNA is lost** from the chromosome ends.

  * $\therefore$ after repeated cell divisions, **telomeres gradually become shorter**.

  * When telomeres become critically short, the cell may **stop dividing** and undergo cell death.

* Determines the life span of a cell

* Telomerase enzyme keeps the length of telomeres constant in dividing cells, which are:

  * Cancer cells

    * Cancer cells divide at an uncontrollable rate, and that is because telomeres finish; telomerase enzyme helps it to regenerate so cancer can keep on dividing.

  * Stem cells

  * Memory B and T lymphocytes
