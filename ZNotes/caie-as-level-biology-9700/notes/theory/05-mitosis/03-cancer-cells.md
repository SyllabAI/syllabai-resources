## Cancer Cells

* Uncontrollable division in which a mass of cells forms, called a tumor.

* Exposed to carcinogens

  * Such as skin cancer due to exposure to UV light.

  * Smoking.

* A proto-oncogene is a normal gene that helps control cell growth and division. If it is altered to become permanently or excessively active, it becomes an oncogene, which can contribute to uncontrolled cell division.

* Cells divide continuously at an uncontrollable rate and ignore signals from other cells.

* Not removed by the immune system.

* Tumor gets bigger.

* Tumor supplied with blood and lymph vessels; if it's a malignant tumor, it spreads into the blood and lymph systems.

* Metastasis is the spread of cancer cells from a primary tumour to other parts of the body, where they form secondary tumours.
