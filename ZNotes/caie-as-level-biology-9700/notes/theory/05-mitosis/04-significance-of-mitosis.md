## Significance Of Mitosis

* Cell replacement /Tissue repair

  * Mitosis allows tissues to regenerate by producing exact replacements for dead or damaged cells.

  * Tissues can be repaired by replacing damaged or dead cells with new cells produced through cell division.

* Asexual reproduction

  * It allows them to multiply quickly, producing offspring that are exact genetic clones of the parent.

* Growth: by increasing the number of cells

  * Starting as a single-celled zygote, organisms grow into mature adults through repeated cycles of mitotic division.
