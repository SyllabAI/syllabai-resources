## Cholera

* Caused by ***Vibrio cholerae* (beware of spelling)**

* Can be killed by $\text{HCL}$ in stomach

* Takes action in the walls of the small intestine

**Process**

* ***Vibrio cholerae*** releases toxin choleragen.

* Which disrupts the function of the epithelial cells.

* Causing chloride ions to leave by facilitated diffusion, and water follows by osmosis.

* This results in **diarrhea**

**Symptoms**

* Vomiting

* Dehydration

* Diarrhea

**Methods to reduce cholera:**

* Oral Rehydration Therapy for patients

* Isolate patients

* Good sewage treatment

* Education

* Antibiotic (as it's a bacteria)

* Healthy cooking

* Vaccination
