# Infectious disease

## Disease 

An abnormal condition affecting an organism, which reduces the effectiveness of the function of the organism 

Infectious diseases are caused by pathogens and are transmissible (transferred from one organism to another) 

* **Carrier of disease:** can spread the disease even if they don't show symptoms.

* **Prevalence:** the number of people with the disease.

* **Mortality:** the number of people who died from the disease
