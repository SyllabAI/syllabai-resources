## Malaria

Transmitted by a vector, which is a female Anopheles mosquito

Malaria spreads in tropical regions as mosquitos thrive in heat and fresh water.

* Anopheles mosquitoes require warm temperatures and stagnant water to breed,

* *Plasmodium* parasites require temperatures above 20C inside the vector to complete their maturation cycle.

Caused by 4 different species of ***Plasmodium :***

* *Plasmodium malariae*

* *Plasmodium ovale*

* *Plasmodium vivax*

* *Plasmodium falciparum*

### Life cycle of Malaria

* Female *Anopheles* mosquito injects **sporozoites** during a blood meal.

* Sporozoites enter **liver cells (hepatocytes)** and multiply into **merozoites**.

* Merozoites infect **red blood cells** and multiply by **schizogony**.

* RBCs rupture, releasing merozoites and causing **periodic fever and chills**.

* Some parasites develop into **gametocytes**.

* Another mosquito takes up the gametocytes during a blood meal, completing the cycle.

### **Symptoms**

* Anemia and headache

* Nausea

### **Prevention**

* Breeding fish to feed on larvae

* Using insecticides

* Cover water collection with oil to suffocate larvae

* Protective clothes

* Using prophylactic drugs

### **Why malaria is a concern**

* Increase in number of drug resistance

* Hard to develop vaccine due to different genes
