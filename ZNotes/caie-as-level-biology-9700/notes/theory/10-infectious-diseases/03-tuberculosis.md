## Tuberculosis

Caused by :

* *Mycobacterium bovis*

* *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*

### **Mycobacterium bovis**

* Food borne

* Uncooked meat and unpasteurised milk

### Mycobacterium tuberculosis

* Air borne

* Air droplets from infected person

Infect lungs and can be dormant for years it is an opportunistic disease

### Prevention

* Use 4 Antibiotics as it's a resistant bacteria

* Isolation

* Cattle tested for TB

* Pasteurization for milk

* Education

* Vaccination

  * **BCG**  is a vaccine used to protect against tuberculosis (TB).

  * It contains a **weakened strain of *Mycobacterium bovis***.

  * It stimulates the immune system to develop immunity against TB.

  * It is particularly effective at protecting children against severe forms of TB, such as TB meningitis.

  * It is usually given as a single injection, commonly during infancy in countries where TB is common.

  * The vaccine does not provide complete protection against all forms of TB, and its effectiveness against pulmonary TB varies.

  * Widespread BCG vaccination helps reduce the spread and severity of TB.

### Reason for increase of pathogen

* Poor nutrition

* Refugees

* Low education

* Overcrowding
