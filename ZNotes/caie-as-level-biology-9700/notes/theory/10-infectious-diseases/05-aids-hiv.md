## AIDS/HIV

AIDS: Acquired immune deficiency syndrome

HIV: Human immunodeficiency virus

### HIV

### HIV as a retrovirus

* **HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus) is a retrovirus** because its genetic material is **RNA**, but it uses **reverse transcriptase** to make a DNA copy of this RNA.

* HIV enters **host cells**, particularly **T-helper cells**.

* Takes action in the T helper lymphocytes

  * Multiply inside T helper cells

**Method of transmission**

* Sexual contact with infected person

* Sharing needles with infected people

* Blood transfusion or organ transplant with an infected person

* From infected mother through the placenta

### AIDS

Has no specific symptoms but allows opportunistic diseases to enter the host’s body e.g. tuberculosis

Testing if you have HIV uses antibodies (look at chapter 11)

**Prevention**

* Use condoms during sexual activity to reduce transmission.

* PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis) can be used by people at increased risk of HIV infection.

* Screen donated blood to prevent transmission through blood transfusions.

* ART (antiretroviral therapy) reduces the amount of HIV in the body and greatly lowers the risk of transmission.

* Regular HIV testing helps identify infections early so treatment and prevention measures can begin.

* Public Health education

**Reason for spread of virus**

* Symptomless carriers

* No effective vaccines

  * As virus change in surface protein

* Low education

**Why people with HIV are Susceptible to any disease**

* Reduction In number of T-Helper Lymphocyte → Which are needed for  increasing macrophage action.

* So lowered immune system
