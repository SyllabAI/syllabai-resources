## Antibodies

Globular Y-shaped proteins produced by plasma cells. Their structure dictates how they bind to and neutralize pathogens.

* Four Polypeptide Chains: Composed of two identical heavy (H) chains and two identical light (L) chains held together by disulfide bonds. This forms a stable Y-shaped quaternary structure.

* Variable Regions: Located at the tips of the “Y” arms. The amino acid sequence varies highly between different antibodies, forming two identical antigen-binding sites complementary in shape to a specific antigen, allowing the formation of antibody-antigen complexes.

* Constant Region: Forms the stem of the “Y”. It is identical in all antibodies of the same class and binds to receptors on phagocytes, allowing the pathogen to be recognised and engulfed.

* Hinge Region: A flexible region containing disulfide bonds that allows the arms of the antibody to flex and move, helping the antigen-binding sites attach to antigens at different positions.
