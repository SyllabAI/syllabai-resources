## Vaccines

Vaccines work by mimicking a natural infection to train your adaptive immune system without causing the actual illness.

Components of a Vaccine: Vaccines contain antigens derived from pathogens. These can take several forms:

* Live weakened pathogens

* Killed/inactivated pathogens

* Isolated antigenic fragments (e.g., cell surface proteins)

* Toxoids (harmless, inactivated toxins)

### The Mechanism of Action

* Antigen Presentation: When the vaccine is administered, antigen-presenting cells (APCs) macrophages engulf the antigens and present them on their cell surfaces via MHC proteins.

* Clonal Selection & Expansion: Helper T-cells release cytokines which activate specific B-lymphocytes whose surface receptors match the vaccine antigen. These B-cells undergo rapid mitosis (clonal expansion).

* Primary Response: The B-cells differentiate into:

  * Plasma cells: Synthesize and secrete specific antibodies to target the antigen.

  * Memory B-cells & Memory T-cells: Remain dormant in the lymphoid tissue for long periods.

Secondary Response (Long-Term Immunity): If the individual is exposed to the pathogen in the future:

* Memory cells recognize the antigen immediately.

* They undergo rapid clonal expansion into plasma cells.

* Antibodies are produced much faster and at significantly higher concentrations than during the primary response.

* The pathogen is destroyed before it can reproduce enough to cause disease or symptoms.

  **Natural Immunity**

* Natural Active: Gained by catching a pathogen naturally. The body undergoes an immune response, experiences symptoms, and builds memory cells. Giving long term immunity

* Natural Passive: Gained when a fetus receives maternal antibodies across the placenta, or a newborn receives them via colostrum/breast milk. Giving short term immunity

**Artificial Immunity**

* Artificial Active: Gained through vaccination. An antigen is introduced intentionally to trigger an immune response without causing severe disease. Giving long term immunity

* Artificial Passive: Gained by directly injecting pre-formed antibodies (e.g., antitoxins for rabies, tetanus, or snake venom) for immediate protection. Giving short term immunity
