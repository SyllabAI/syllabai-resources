# Immunity

## Types of immune cells and antigens

### Stem cells

Stem cells produce 2 types of cells, which are :

* Myeloid

  * Red blood cells and platelets

  * Neutrophils

  * Monocytes which mature into macrophages

* Lymphoid

  * B lymphocytes

  * T lymphocytes

    * T killer cells engulf:

      * Infected cells

      * Cancer cells

      * Transplanted organs

    * T helper (most important)

      * Releases cytokines which activate

        * B cells

        * T killer cells

        * Macrophage

### Antigens: Self vs. Non-Self

* Antigen: A macromolecule (usually a glycoprotein or protein) located on cell surfaces that can trigger an immune response.

* Self Antigens: Markers produced by the organism's own body cells that do not stimulate an immune response (recognized as "self" by the immune system).

* Non-Self Antigens: Foreign molecules not recognized as belonging to the host, which stimulate an immune response (e.g., antigens on bacteria, viruses, or transplanted tissue).
