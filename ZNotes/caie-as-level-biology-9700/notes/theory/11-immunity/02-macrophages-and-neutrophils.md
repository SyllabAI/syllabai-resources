### Mode of action of phagocytes ( phagocytosis )

* Chemotaxis: Phagocytes are attracted to the site of infection by chemicals released by pathogens and damaged cells.

* Recognition & attachment: Receptors on the phagocyte recognise and bind to molecules on the pathogen.

* Endocytosis: The phagocyte engulfs the pathogen, forming a phagosome.

* Phagolysosome formation: Lysosomes fuse with the phagosome to form a phagolysosome.

* Digestion: Hydrolytic enzymes digest and destroy the pathogen.

* Antigen presentation: Macrophages can display pathogen antigens on their cell surface and act as antigen-presenting cells (APCs), helping activate lymphocytes.
