# Gas exchange

## Overview of the structures involved in gas exchange

![](https://images.znotes.org/allimages/db73f750d4a34163a88942ac.webp =845x)

#|
||

Structure

|

Function

||
||

Nose/ nasal cavity

|

Warms, filters, and moistens inhaled air.

||
||

Trachea

|

main airway (windpipe) leading from the throat.

||
||

Bronchi (Singular: Bronchus)

|

The trachea divides into two primary bronchi, one entering each lung.

||
||

Bronchioles

|

The bronchi branch repeatedly into smaller, narrower tubes called bronchioles.

||
||

Lungs

|

2 main organs located in the thoracic cavity that facilitate breathing and gas exchange.

||
||

Alveoli

|

Millions of tiny, grape-like air sacs located at the ends of the smallest bronchioles. They provide a massive surface area for gas exchange

||
||

Capillary Network

|

A dense, web-like network of microscopic blood vessels (capillaries) that completely surrounds and shares a membrane with the alveoli.
