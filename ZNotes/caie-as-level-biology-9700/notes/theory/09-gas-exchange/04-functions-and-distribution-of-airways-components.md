## Functions and Distribution of Airways Components

#|
||

Component

|

Distribution

|

Function

||
||

Cartilage

|

Trachea and Bronchi (decreases as airways get smaller).

|

Provides structural support; keeps airways open during inhalation.

||
||

Smooth Muscle

|

Trachea, Bronchi, and Bronchioles.

|

Regulates airway diameter by contracting or relaxing to control airflow.

||
||

Goblet Cells

|

Lining of trachea, bronchi, and larger bronchioles.

|

Secrete mucus to trap dust and pathogens.

||
||

Mucous Glands

|

In trachea and bronchi.

|

Produce additional mucus to maintain airway moisture and trapping capacity.

||
||

Cilia

|

Lining of trachea, bronchi, and bronchioles.

|

Sweep trapped particles (in mucus) upwards towards throat to be swallowed or coughed out.

||
||

Elastic fibers

|

widely distributed throughout the walls of the alveoli, forming a network around the air sacs.

|

allow the alveoli to stretch during inhalation and recoil during exhalation.
