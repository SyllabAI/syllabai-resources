## Identifying structures involved in  gas exchange from photomicrographs

* Trachea: C-shaped cartilage rings, pseudostratified ciliated epithelium, abundant goblet cells, and submucosal glands.

* Bronchus: Irregular cartilage blocks/plates, circular smooth muscle band, ciliated epithelium.

* Bronchiole: No cartilage, folded inner lining, prominent smooth muscle wall, simple ciliated/cuboidal epithelium.

* Alveolus: Ultra-thin squamous cells, delicate network of capillaries, elastic fibers in walls.
