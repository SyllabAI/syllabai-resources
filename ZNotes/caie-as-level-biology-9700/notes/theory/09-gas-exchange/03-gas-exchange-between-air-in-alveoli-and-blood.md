## Gas exchange between air in alveoli and blood

* Oxygen in the air inside the alveoli has a higher concentration than in the blood arriving at the alveolar capillaries.

* Oxygen therefore diffuses down its concentration gradient from the alveoli, across the squamous epithelium, through the thin alveolar wall and capillary endothelium, and into the blood.

* Carbon dioxide has a higher concentration in the blood than in the alveolar air.

* Carbon dioxide therefore diffuses down its concentration gradient from the blood into the alveoli and is then exhaled.

* The alveolar and capillary walls are very thin, giving a short diffusion distance and increasing the rate of gas exchange.

* A large surface area of alveoli and a good blood supply help maintain concentration gradients, allowing gas exchange to occur rapidly.
