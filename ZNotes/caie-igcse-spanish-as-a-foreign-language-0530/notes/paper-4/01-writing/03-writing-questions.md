## Writing Questions

### What the paper includes

* There will be two writing questions. The first will be a short writing and the other a long one
* The short one will give you the topic and 2-3 accompanying questions, with the word limit being approx. 80-90 words
* The long one will give you two choices, from which you will select and do **one**. Again, accompanying questions may be provided, and the word limit will be longer, approx. 120-130 words

### Helpful Tips

* Practice, practice, practice! - You will not be able to write if you haven't practiced writing long answers before
* Know what you will say if a generalized prompt comes up
  * For instance, you may have a set vacation in mind, so that if anything related to travelling/ideal place/dream vacation comes up, you can use those details!
* Know your tenses - this is super important
* Make the correct choice for the long answer - pick the prompt you think you will fare better at considering the tense and what is being asked
