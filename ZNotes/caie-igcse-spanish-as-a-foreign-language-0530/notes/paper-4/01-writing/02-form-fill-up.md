## Form Fill-up

### What the paper includes

* There will be a form that you will need to fill-up (somewhat like an appointment form)
* There will be blank spaces to write your answer which will be a word/phrase

### Helpful Tips

* It will be helpful if you  know basic vocabulary like food, colors, hobbies, names, languages, nationalities, etc.
* Write **only** the answer - do not try and frame sentences in the blank provided!
