## Email format

* Although not marked on heavily, writing the long answer in the correct format for an email (only when asked to write an email though!) can be beneficial:

*Querido/Hola \[Recipient Name\] (Dear/Hello…)*

*Espero que estás bien (I hope you are well)…\[continue the body paragraph\]…*

*Un cordial saludo/gracias/saludos \[Regards/Thanks\]*

*\[Your name\]*
