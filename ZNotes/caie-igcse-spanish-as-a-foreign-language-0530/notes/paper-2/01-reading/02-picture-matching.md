## Picture Matching

### **What the paper includes**

* In these types of questions, you will be asked to match the picture to the question
* The pictures will mostly be signs and notices in public places, such as streets, restaurants, railway stations and airports

### **Helpful Tips**

* Look at the pictures carefully, as sometimes two images may be intentionally similar to confuse you
* Make sure you know vocabulary related to directions and numbers, as they often show up
