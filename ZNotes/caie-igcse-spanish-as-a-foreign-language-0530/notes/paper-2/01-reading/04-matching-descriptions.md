## Matching Descriptions

### **What the paper includes**

* There are 5 short texts and 8 descriptions on a certain topic
* You have to read the texts and match the best description to each

### **Helpful Tips**

* This portion is considered to be one of the most daunting parts of the exam. If you feel the same, perhaps plan so you can spend some more time on this section
* Read the texts very carefully. Identify and underline (with a pencil) keywords
  * Be wary of negatives
  * The sentences are often long and winding to hide the message; try and locate it
  * Do not skip over words, as they may be important. If you are unable to understand something, try using context clues
* Read the descriptions and try to extract the crux of the message
  * You may find it helpful to jot down some key things each description mentions briefly
