# Paper 2: Reading

## General Tips

* Memorize the question words in Spanish - this is imperative to understand the question!
* Watch out for negations (negative words) such as *ni* (neither), *no* (not), *nada* (nothing), *ningún* (none)
* Know certain ‘phrases’ in Spanish, for example, *Me llevo bien con* (I get along with..)
  * This is especially useful when a certain word does not translate exactly to Spanish
* Answer all the questions, even if you are unsure, because a random answer is better than no answer!
* Know verbs in different tenses. This comes in handy as a question may ask you to write the answer in a different tense
* Practice as many past papers as possible to familiarize yourself with the pattern of questions
