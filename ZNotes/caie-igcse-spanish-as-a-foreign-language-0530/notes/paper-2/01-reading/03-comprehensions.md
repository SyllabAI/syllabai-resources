## Comprehensions

### **What the paper includes**

* There are 3 comprehension passages in the paper
  * Question #3 - Short comprehension with multiple-choice style questions
  * Questions #4  and #6 - Comprehension with long-answer style questions

### **Helpful Tips**

* You can lift phrases directly from the passage but only lift **relevant** information, or this may show the examiner you don't understand what is written
* Full sentences are not required
* Write only on the lines - small letters, if required!
* If you get stuck on finding an answer, try the next question. Usually, you will find the answer this way because the questions are asked chronologically
