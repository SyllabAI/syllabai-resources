## Relative Pronouns

* Used to link two parts of a sentence (or clauses)
* Que → who, what, which
  * El chico que es en mi clase tiene ojos verdes → The boy who is in my class has green eyes
* Lo que → what (refers to an idea/action)
  * Es importante hacer lo que te gusta → It is important to do what you like
* Quien → who, whom
  * La chica con quien trabajo vive en la ciudad → The girl with whom I work lives in the city
