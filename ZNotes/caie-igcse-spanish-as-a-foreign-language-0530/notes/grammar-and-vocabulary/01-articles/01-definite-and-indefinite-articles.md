# Articles

## Definite and indefinite articles

* Definite Masculine → el/los
* Definite Feminine → la/las
* The articles may also combine if it is preceded by ‘a’ or ‘de’
  * a el → al
  * de el → del
* Indefinite masculine → un/unos
* Indefinite feminine → una/unas
* Note: ‘lo’ can act as an article (or a noun) if followed by an adjective
  * Lo bueno (the good), lo importante (the important thing)
