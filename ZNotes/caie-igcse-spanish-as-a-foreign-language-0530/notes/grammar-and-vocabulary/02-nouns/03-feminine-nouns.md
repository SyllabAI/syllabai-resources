## Feminine Nouns

* Feminine nouns generally end with ‘a’ in their singular form
  * Eg: planta (plant)
* Feminine nouns may also end in ‘d’, ‘tud’
  * Eg: amistad (friendship), dificultad (difficulty)
* These nouns will always be preceded by ‘la’, so for example, la planta, la amistad, etc.
* A simple mnemonic about the endings which generally indicate feminine nouns:

  **D -** eg: ciudad (city)

  **IÓN -** eg: canción (song)

  **Z** - eg: actriz (actress)

  **A** - eg: computadora (computer)

  These nouns are always feminine:
  * Time
  * Letters of the alphabet
  * Islands
  * Roads
* Some important exceptions to masculine nouns are:
  * el día - day
  * el mapa - map
  * el poema - poem
  * el problema - problem
  * el programa - program
  * el sistema - system
  * el tema - topic
