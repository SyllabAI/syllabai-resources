## Masculine Nouns

* Masculine nouns generally end with ‘o’ in their singular form

  * Eg: carros (cars), amigo (friend)

* Masculine nouns may also end in ‘l’, or ‘aje’

  * Eg: mantel (tablecloth), garaje (garage)

* These nouns will always be preceded by ‘él’, so for example, él amigo, él mantel, etc.

* A simple mnemonic about the endings which generally indicate masculine nouns:

  **L -** eg: mantel

  **O -** eg: abuelo (grandfather)

  **N** - eg: sillón (armchair)

  **E** - eg: presidente (president)

  **R** - eg: amor (love)

  **S** - eg: tenis (tennis)

* These nouns are always masculine:

  * Days of the week
  * Months of the year
  * Numbers
  * Rivers/seas
  * Colors

* Some important exceptions to masculine nouns are:

  * la mano - hand
  * la modelo - the model
  * la moto - motorcycle
  * la radio - radio
