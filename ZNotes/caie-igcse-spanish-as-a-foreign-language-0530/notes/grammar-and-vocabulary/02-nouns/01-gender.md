# Nouns (Los sustantivos)

## Gender in Spanish language

* All spanish nouns have a gender (male or female)
  * This causes a different article (él or la) to be added before the noun
* There are some general rules that may be followed, but exceptions are always present, so you are encouraged to learn them on your own
* Note: Nouns ending in ‘ista’ have no separate feminine/masculine form
  * Eg: el/la artista (artist)
