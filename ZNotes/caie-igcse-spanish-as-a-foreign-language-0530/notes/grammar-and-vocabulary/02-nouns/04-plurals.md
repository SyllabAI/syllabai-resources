## Plural Nouns

* Note - for every change from singular to plural, the article also takes the plural form
* If a noun ends in a vowel, add ‘s’  
  * la mano → las manos (hand)
* If a noun ends in a consonant, add ‘es’
  * la red → las redes (social network)
* If a noun ends in ‘z’ change the end to ‘ces’
  * la voz → las voces (voice)
* Days of the week (except for sábado and domingo) have the same singular and plural form
  * el lunes → los lunes (Monday)
  * el sábado → los sábados (Saturday)
