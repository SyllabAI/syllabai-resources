# Degrees of Comparison

## What are degrees of comparison?

* Comparatives are forms of adjectives used to compare a person or thing possessing the same quality with another
* There are specific words that you need to know for comparative degrees:
  * más → more (eg: Estoy más inteligente → I am more intelligent)
  * más que → more than (eg: Tengo más libros que Juan → I have more books that Juan)
  * menos → less
  * menos que → less than
  * mejor → better (eg: Tu casa es mejor que un hotel → Your house is better than a hotel)
    * Note: *mejor* has many other uses in different contexts
  * peor → worse
* There are specific words that you need to know for superlative degrees:
  * es el mejor → it is the best (masculine, considering use of ‘el’)
  * es la mejor → it is the best (feminine, considering use of ‘la’)
  * es lo mejor → it is the best (does not refer to any noun, like ‘it is the best that…')
  * es lo maximo → it is the most (achievement)
