# Adjectives

## Basics

* Adjectives are words that describe nouns
* Adjectives ending in ‘o’ (masculine) or ‘a’ (feminine) → add ‘s’ for plural
  * limpio → limpios
* Adjectives ending in vowel other than o/a → add ‘s’ for plural
  * triste → tristes
* Adjectives ending in consontant → add ‘es’ for plural
  * azul → azules
* Adjectives ending in ‘or’ (masculine) or ‘a’ (feminine) → add ‘es’ or ‘as’ respectively for plural
  * encantador → encantadores
  * encantadora → encantadoras
