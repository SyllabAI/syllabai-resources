## Past Continuous Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that were happening in the past
* To conjugate in past continuous, refer to the *estar* conjugation in the chart, and then add the *gerundio*

| Subject pronoun | Present conj. haber | Examples of participles |
|----|----|----|
| yo | estaba | estud**iando** (estudi**ar**) |
| tú | estabas | com**iendo** (com**er**) |
| él/ella/usted | estaba | viv**iendo** (viv**ir**) |
| nosotros/nosotras | estabamos | cant**ando** (cant**ar**) |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | estaban | bali**ando** (bail**ar**) |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked

* Example: According to the chart, I was studying would be → *Yo **estaba estudiando***
