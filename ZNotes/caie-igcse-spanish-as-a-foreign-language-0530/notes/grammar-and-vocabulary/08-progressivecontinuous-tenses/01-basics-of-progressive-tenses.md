# Progressive/Continuous Tenses

## What are Progressive/Continuous Tenses?

* All continuous tenses show actions that **are going on at the moment**
* They follow a general structure:
  * \[*Estar* verb conjugated + gerundio + complement (rest of sentence)\]
* *Estar* means to be and translates to am/was (based on conjugation)
* The gerund, called the *gerundio*  is like the English -ing form of the verb
  * I am study-ing, sleep-ing
* Note that the *__gerundio__* is **not conjugated**. It is formed by adding *-ando* (-ar ending) or *-iendo* (-ir ending) to the root of the verb 
  * In continuous tenses, it is the *estar* verb that is conjugated according to the chart
