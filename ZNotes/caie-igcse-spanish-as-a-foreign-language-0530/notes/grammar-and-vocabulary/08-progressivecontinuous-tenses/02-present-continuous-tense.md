## Present Continuous Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that are happening right now
* To conjugate in present continuous, refer to the *estar* conjugation in the chart, and then add the *gerundio*


|Subject pronoun|Present conj. estar|Examples of participles|
|:---|:---|:---|
|yo|estoy|estud**iando** (estudi**ar**)|
|tú|estás|com**iendo** (com**er**)|
|él/ella/usted|está|viv**iendo** (viv**ir**)|
|nosotros/nosotras|estamos|cant**ando** (cant**ar**)|
|ellos/ellas/ustedes|están|bali**ando** (bail**ar**)|

*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked

* Example: According to the chart, I am studying would be → *Yo **estoy estudiando***
