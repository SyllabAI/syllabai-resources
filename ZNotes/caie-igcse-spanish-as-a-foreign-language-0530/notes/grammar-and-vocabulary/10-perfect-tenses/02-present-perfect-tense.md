## Present Perfect Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that started and ended in the past
  * But the ending was very close to the current moment
* This tense corresponds to *have/has* ___ in English
  * I have studied or She has eaten
* To conjugate in present perfect, refer to the *haber* conjugation in the chart, and then add the participle
  * To form the participle, check if the verb ends in *-ar* or *-ir/-er* then add *-ado* or *-ido* respectively

| Subject pronoun | Present conj. haber | Examples of participles |
|----|----|----|
| yo | he | estudi**ado** (estudi**ar**) |
| tú | has | comi**do** (com**er**) |
| él/ella/usted | ha | viv**ido** (viv**ir**) |
| nosotros/nosotras | hemos | cant**ado** (cant**ar**) |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | han | bali**ado** (bail**ar**) |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked

* Example: According to the chart, I have studied would be → *Yo **he estudiado***
