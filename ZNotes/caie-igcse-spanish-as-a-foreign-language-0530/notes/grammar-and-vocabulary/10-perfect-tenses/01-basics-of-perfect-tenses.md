# Perfect Tenses

## What are perfect tenses?

* All perfect tenses show actions that **conclude close to the present moment**
* They follow a general structure:
  * \[Subject Pronoun + *Haber* verb conjugated + verb participle + complement (rest of sentence)\]
* *Haber* translates to has/have/had (based on conjugation)
* The verb participle called *participio* is like the English past participle, which indicates a sense of completion
  * I have studied, seen, asked, etc.
* Note that the participle is **not conjugated**. It is formed by adding *-ado* or *-ido* to the root of the verb
  * In perfect tenses, it is the *haber* verb that is conjugated according to the chart
