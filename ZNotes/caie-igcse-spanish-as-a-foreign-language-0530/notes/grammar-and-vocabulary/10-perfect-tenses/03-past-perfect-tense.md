## Past Perfect Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that were completed before another
* This tense corresponds to *had already* ___ in English
  * Eg: I had already studied
* To conjugate in past perfect, refer to the *haber* conjugation (imperfect conjugation of *haber*) in the chart, and then add the participle
  * To form the participle, check if the verb ends in *-ar* or *-ir/-er* then add *-ado* or *-ido* respectively
  * You can use *ya* in front of the *haber* conjugation - the *ya* means ‘already’

| Subject pronoun | Present conj. haber | Examples of participles |
|----|----|----|
| yo | había | estudi**ado** (estudi**ar**) |
| tú | habías | comi**do** (com**er**) |
| él/ella/usted | había | viv**ido** (viv**ir**) |
| nosotros/nosotras | habíamos | cant**ado** (cant**ar**) |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | habían | bali**ado** (bail**ar**) |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked

* Example: According to the chart, I had already studied would be → *Yo **ya habia estudiado***
