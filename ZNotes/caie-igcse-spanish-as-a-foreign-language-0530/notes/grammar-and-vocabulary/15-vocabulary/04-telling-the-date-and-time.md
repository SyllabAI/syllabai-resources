## Telling the Date and Time

* To tell the date in spanish, follow this format:
  * ***el* + *\[date\] + de + \[month\] + de + \[year\]***
* Eg: 16 July 2024 → el 16 de julio de 2024
* To ask what time it is → *¿Qué hora es?* (What time is it?)
* To tell the time, the general format is \[***es/son + la/las + {hour} + y + {minutes}\]***
  * The verb used is *ser*
  * For all times between 1 o’clock and 2 o’clock, use “*es la…”* 
  * For all other times, use “*son las…"*
* For the hours → es la una, son las dos, son las tres… 
* Therefore, the correct way to say the time (eg: It is 1:40) → Es la una y cuarenta
* Some common words that can be used instead of specifying the minutes:
  * media → half past i.e. 30 minutes past (1:30, 3:30, 4:30…)
  * cuarto → quarter past i.e. 15 minutes past (1:15, 2:15, 5:15…)
  * menos cuarto → quarter to i.e. 45 minutes past (2:45, 7:45, 11:45…)
  * menos \[any number\] → \[number\] to (eg: menos diez → 10 minutes to \[hour\])
  * en punto → exactly (eg: 3:00)
