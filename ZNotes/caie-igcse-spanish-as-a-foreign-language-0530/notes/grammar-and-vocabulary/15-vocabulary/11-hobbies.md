## Hobbies

* Some common hobbies/pass times in spanish are (make sure to know these in detail!):
  * andar en patineta → to skateboard 
  * aprender idiomas → to learn languages
  * baliar → to dance
  * caminar → to walk 
  * cantar → to sing 
  * correr → to run 
  * dibujar → to draw 
  * escribir → to write 
  * escuchar a la música → to listen to music 
  * hacer deportes → to play sports
  * hacer ejercicio → to do exercise 
  * hacer senderismo → to go hiking 
  * hacer yoga → to do yoga 
  * ir al cine → to go to the movies 
  * jugar al básquetbol → to play basketball 
  * jugar al béisbol → to play baseball 
  * jugar al fútbol → to play soccer 
  * jugar videojuegos → to play video games 
  * leer → to read 
  * montar en bicicleta → to bike 
  * nadar → to swim 
  * pintar → to paint 
  * sacar fotos → to take pictures 
  * viajar → to travel
  * tocar la guitarra \[or any instrument\] → to play the guitar
  * ir de compras → to go shopping
