## Family Members

* Common vocabulary for members of the family are as follows:
  * abuela →  grandmother
  * abuelo → grandfather
  * esposa → wife 
  * esposo → husband
  * familia → family
  * hermana → sister 
  * hermano → brother 
  * hija → daughter 
  * hijo → son
  * madre → mom 
  * padre → dad
  * primo → cousin
  * sobrino/sobrina → nephew/niece
