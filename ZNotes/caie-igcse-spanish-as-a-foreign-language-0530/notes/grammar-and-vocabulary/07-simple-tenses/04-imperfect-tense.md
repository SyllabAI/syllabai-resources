## Imperfect Tense

* This tense is used to talk about:
  * things that were **habitual in the past**
  * actions that **were not completed** in the past
  * **emotional states** in the past
* It roughly translates to *used to...* in English
  * Eg: I used to ride a bike in my neighborhood
* To conjugate in imperfect tense, remove the ending (-ar, -ir, -er) of the verb, and use the endings as indicated in the chart below (with example verbs **hablar**, **comer**, **vivir**):

| Subject pronoun | -ar | -er | **-ir** |
|----|----|----|----|
| yo | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| tú | hablabas | comías | vivías |
| él/ella/usted | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| nosotros/nosotras | hablabamos | comíamos | vivíamos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hablaban | comían | vivían |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked
