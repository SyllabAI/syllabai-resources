# Simple Tenses

## Present Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that happen **now**
* To conjugate in present tense, remove the ending (-ar, -ir, -er) of the verb, and use the endings as indicated in the chart below (with example verbs **hablar**, **comer**, **vivir**):

| Subject pronoun | -ar | -er | **-ir** |
|----|----|----|----|
| yo | hablo | como | vivo |
| tú | hablas | comes | vives |
| él/ella/usted | habla | come | vive |
| nosotros/nosotras | hablamos | comemos | vivimos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hablan | comen | viven |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked
