## Future Tense

* This tense is used to talk about things that will **happen**
* To conjugate in future tense, use the endings as indicated in the chart below (with example verbs **hablar**, **comer**, **vivir**) **IN FRONT OF** the imperative form
  * This means you do not remove any endings like the present/past tense
* **Alternatively**, to use future tense in Spanish, you can simply add the present tense conjugation of the verb *ir* (to go) with the imperative (to be) form of the spanish verb
  * Eg: I will read → *Voy* (I am going to/I will) *a leer* (to read in imperative form)

| Subject pronoun | -ar | -er | **-ir** |
|----|----|----|----|
| yo | hablaré | comeré | viviré |
| tú | hablarás | comerás | vivirás |
| él/ella/usted | hablará | comerá | vivírá |
| nosotros/nosotras | hablaremos | comeremos | viviremos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hablarán | comerán | vivirán |

\*Use of vosotros/vosotras is not generally asked
