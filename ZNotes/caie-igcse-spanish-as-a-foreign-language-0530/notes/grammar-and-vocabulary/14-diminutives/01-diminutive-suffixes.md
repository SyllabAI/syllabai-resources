# Diminutives

## Diminutive Suffixes

* These suffixes are used to allude to small size
* They may also be used to change emotions - like the feeling of love/warmth/closeness towards a person
* Most common suffixes used are *-ito/-ita* or *-illo/-illa* - they are added after the noun with some changes in spelling:
  * abuela → abuelita (grandmother)
  * chica → chiquita (little girl)
