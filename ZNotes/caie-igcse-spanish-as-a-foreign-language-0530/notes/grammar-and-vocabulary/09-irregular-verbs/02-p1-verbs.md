## P1 Verbs

* P1 verbs have a conjugation rule that only the *yo* form of the verb follows irregular conjugation rules, rest are regular
* Common P1 verbs you will need are:
  * Dar - to give
  * Estar - to be
  * Hacer - to do
  * Saber/Conocer - to know
  * Poner - to put
  * Salir - to leave
  * Ver - to see
  * Ofrecer - to give/offer
* For instance, the conjugation of one of these verbs (eg: hacer) would be as follows:

| **Subject Pronoun** | Hacer conjugation |
|----|----|
| yo | hago |
| tú | haces |
| él/ella/usted | hace |
| nosotros/nosotras | hacemos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | hacen |

* Note that in this chart, it is the conjugation of the *yo* form that does not follow the stem (hac-) of the verb. All of the other conjugations are not stem-changing, and hence regular
