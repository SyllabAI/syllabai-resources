## P3 Verbs

* P3 verbs have a conjugation rule that all forms of the verb follow **irregular conjugation** rules
* The P3 verbs you will need are:
  * Ir - to go
    * One of the most commonly used verbs in Spanish, it can also be used to represent future tense
  * Ser - to be
    * To describe am, is, are, permanent conditions (anything which is not estar)
  * Haber - used to represent existence of something in the past perfect/present perfect tense
* For instance, the conjugation of one of these verbs (eg: ir) would be as follows:

| **Subject Pronoun** | Querer conjugation |
|----|----|
| yo | voy |
| tú | vas |
| él/ella/usted | va |
| nosotros/nosotras | vamos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | van |

* Note that in this chart, all conjugations are stem-changing, hence irregular
