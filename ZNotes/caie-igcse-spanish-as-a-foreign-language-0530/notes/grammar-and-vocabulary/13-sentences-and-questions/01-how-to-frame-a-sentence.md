# Sentences and Questions

## How to frame a sentence

* To frame a sentence in Spanish, you need to connect grammar with vocabulary, following these 5 steps
  * This also applies to judging a sentence that has already been written
* Step 1 - Make sense of the tense
  * Identify if the tense is simple or compound
  * Then identify which tense it is under these two categories
* Step 2 - Identify the verb
  * Look at the endings of the words to understand which one is the verb
* Step 3 - Conjugation of the verb
  * Using the tense from Step 1 and the verb from Step 2, try and make sense of the conjugation the verb should have
    * When doing this step, keep in mind regular and irregular verbs
* Step 4 - Vocabulary (nouns)
  * Identify the noun(s) in the sentence
* Step 5 - Adjectives, prepositions, articles
  * Identify the adjectives, prepositions and articles used
    * Keep in mind the gender of the noun
    * Also keep in mind the order - most adjectives will come before the noun

  \
* For example, you are asked to write this sentence in spanish: **“I am playing football”**
  * Step 1 - the sentence is in present continuous tense
  * Step 2 - the verb is ‘to play’, in spanish, this is *jugar*
  * Step 3 - the verb should be conjugated using *yo* form in present continuous tense, this would be *estoy jugando* (referring to the chart)
  * Step 4 - the noun is ‘I’ and ‘football’, in spanish these are *yo* and *fútbol*
  * Step 5 -  \*no prepositions/adjectives/articles are used
* Therefore, the sentence would be: **Yo estoy jugando el fútbol**
