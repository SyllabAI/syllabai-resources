## How to conjugate

* For reflexive verb conjugations, follow these steps:
  * Identify which reflexive pronoun the subject agrees with (select from chart below)
  * Remove the *-se* ending from the verb
  * Conjugate the verb, mostly this will require present tense conjugation (but be careful of regular or irregular verbs!)
  * Add the reflexive pronoun **before** the conjugated verb

| **Subject Pronoun** | Querer conjugation |
|----|----|
| yo | me |
| tú | te |
| él/ella/usted | se |
| nosotros/nosotras | nos |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | les |

* Example: I wake up (levantarse → to wake up)
  * I → yo → me
  * levantarse → levant__o__
  * Therefore the final sentence will be: Yo me levanto 
* Although regular reflexive verbs will not be that difficult to grasp, these common irregular verbs may be kept in mind:
  * irse - to leave
  * dormirse - to fall asleep
  * despertarse - to wake up
  * ponerse - “to put on" or "to wear" when referring to clothing
  * sentirse - to feel
