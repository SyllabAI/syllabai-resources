## Subject Pronouns

* **Who** is performing the action is given by the **8 subject pronouns** in spanish

| Type | Singular | Plural |
|----|----|----|
| First Person | *yo →* me | *nosotros/nosotras →* us/we |
| Second person (informal) | *tú* → you | *vosotros/vosotras\** → you all |
| Second person (formal) | *usted* → you | *ustedes* → you all |
| Third person | *él/ella* → he/she/it | *ellos/ellas* - they |

\*Use of *vosotros/vosotras* is not generally required for IGCSE Spanish
