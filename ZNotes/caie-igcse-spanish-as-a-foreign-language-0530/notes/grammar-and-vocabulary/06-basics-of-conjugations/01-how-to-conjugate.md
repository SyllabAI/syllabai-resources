# Verb Conjugations

## How to conjugate

* In Spanish, the ending of the verb helps indicate two things: 
  * **Who** is performing the action
  * **When** the action occurs
* Verb endings can change based on the tense (present, past, future), person (pronouns), and number (singular, plural)
* The *infinitivo* (base form/infinitive) of the verb in spanish usually ends with *-ar, -ir, -er*
  * The infinitive form translates to the ‘to \[verb\]’ form in English: 
    * *leer* (to read) 
    * *tener* (to have)
* To conjugate the infinitive, simply remove the *-ar, -ir, -er* endings and replace them with the correct ending based on the tense/mood
