## Tenses

* The basic tenses in Spanish (like in English) are **past, present** and **future**
* However, compound tenses (again, like in English) are also present - **progressive/continous** and **perfect**
* Basic tenses required for you to know are:
  * Simple present - *El presente*
  * Simple past - *El Preteritó*
  * Simple Future - *El futuro*
  * Imperfect - *El Imperfecto*
* Compound tenses required for you to know are:
  * Present perfect
  * Past perfect
  * Present progressive/continuous
  * Past progressive/continuous
* Note that you may not be asked to write in all these tenses, but they can appear in reading/listening, so it is important to be able to understand them!
