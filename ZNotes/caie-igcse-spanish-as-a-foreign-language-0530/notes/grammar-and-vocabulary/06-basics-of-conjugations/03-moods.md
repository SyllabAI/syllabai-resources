## Moods

* **When** the action is being performed is essentially the time
* This corresponds to the **tense** of the verb that you need to conjugate in
* Tenses fall under categories known as **moods**. In spanish, there are 4 moods:
  * Indicative - to talk about reality
  * Subjunctive - to express desire or hypotheses
  * Imperative - to give orders and commands
  * Conditional - to express probability, desire, request (use of would, could, should, etc)

\*The tenses IGCSE Spanish students usually require fall under the indicative mood only
