## Questions to Prepare

* This is a list of possible questions you may prepare for under the topics mentioned previously
* My family
  * Basics about you and the members of your family
  * What do you like to do with your family?
  * Who do you like/dislike in your family?
  * Problems in your family
* My home
  * Description of your home (in detail!)
  * What aspects of your home do you like/dislike?
  * Location of your home
* My school
  * Your favourite subjects
  * What do you like/dislike about your school
  * What extra-curriculars does your school offer?
  * Description of the campus
* Going shopping
  * Basic vocabulary for **different aspects of shopping** - groceries, clothes, accessories, etc.
  * Who do you shop with?
  * Do you like to shop on the street or in malls?
* Transport
  * Public vs private transport - and which one do you prefer?
  * Advantages and disadvantages of each type of transport
* Education and career
  * Your preferred profession **and vocabulary related to it**
  * What do your parents work as?
  * How will you prepare yourself to get into this profession
  * The degree you will need/subjects you need to study
  * Studying close/far from home
  * A profession in your interests vs. a profession to earn money
* Technology
  * Social media and its advantages and disadvantages
* Health and well-being
  * Your meals - breakfast, lunch, dinner (and snacks)
  * What do you do to have a healthy life?
  * Common illnesses - headaches, stomach-aches, fever, etc. and what measures you can take to get cured
* Vacations
  * Beach vs. Mountain vacations
  * Where do you usually travel during vacations?
  * Spending the vacations with friends vs. with family
* Neighbourhood
  * What do you like/dislike about your neighbourhood (in detail!)
  * Where is your neighbourhood located?
  * How convenient is it to access basic services in your neighbourhood?
* Special occasions
  * Your ideal birthday
  * What type of festivals do you prefer/take part in?
