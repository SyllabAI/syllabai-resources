# Tips for Preparation

## Topics

* NOTE: Please do not consider any of the content/trends observed below to be exhaustive; these are things I noted. Please refer to your teacher for the content you have to study
* Some common topics in *juego de rol* and *conversaciónes* are:
  * My family
  * My home
  * My school/class
  * Health and well-being
  * Activities at home (free time, chores, etc.)
  * Food and drinks (in a restaurant, at home, etc.)
  * My vacations
  * Special occasions (birthdays, anniversaries, festivals)
  * My city and neighbourhood (differences and similarities)
  * Going shopping
  * The environment
  * Transport (public and private, advantages and disadvantages)
  * Education (subjects, classmates, teachers)
  * Career (future, university, etc.)
  * Technology (its impact, usage, advantages, disadvantages)
