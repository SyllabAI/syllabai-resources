# Paper 3: Speaking

## General Tips

* Do not panic, and make sure to exhibit confidence in whatever you are speaking
* If you do not understand something, you may ask for the question to be repeated. **Do not** do this more than two times.
* Even if, after repetition, something is unclear, take your best shot at it. **Do not** stay silent for any question (this will earn you no marks!)
* Stay in control of what you speak - try not to repeat yourself
* Get your tenses right as far as possible
* Learn some commonly used phrases/synonyms to enhance your vocabulary
* Practice as many past papers as possible to familiarize yourself with the pattern of questions
  * It may help to record audio clips of yourself speaking and get them corrected by a teacher/friend/parent
