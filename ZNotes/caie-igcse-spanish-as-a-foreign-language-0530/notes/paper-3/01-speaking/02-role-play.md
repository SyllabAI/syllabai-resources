## Role Play

### What the paper includes

* Role play is called *Juego de Rol*
* In this section, you have to assume a role and interact with the teacher in a specific situation.
  * For instance, you may be a sick patient who has gone to the pharmacist (your teacher) and is having a discussion about buying a medicine (situation)

### **Helpful Tips**

* Use your preparation time before the exam **very well**
* Pay attention to the role you have been given
* Try to think of possible questions that may be asked of you and formulate answers to those in your mind.
  * For example, if the situation is a discussion with the pharmacist, then have a few pointers prepared about your illness, precautions you have been taking, etc. (it can be hypothetical!)
* Keep calm and **answer the question.**
* The last two questions are two-part (they will usually have *Why?* as the second question, but it may be something else, too)
  * Be aware that you do not elaborate **too much** in the first part itself.
