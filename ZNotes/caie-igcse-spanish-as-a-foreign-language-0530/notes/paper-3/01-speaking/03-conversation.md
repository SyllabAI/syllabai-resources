## Conversation

### **What the paper includes**

* The conversation question is referred to as *conversaciones*
* Each conversation has to be 4 minutes long, for a total of 8 minutes
* The questions/topics for the conversations are **not pre-provided**

### **Helpful Tips**

* Study all important over-arching topics/themes - and may appear in the conversations!
* Under every topic/theme, study possible questions.
  * For example, under environment (*medio ambiente*), study your environment/neighbourhood, improvements you may make, actions you have been doing, etc.
* Try to elaborate on the 4th and 5th questions asked in the conversation
* Just **answer the question**. Do not divert from the topic!
* Add all details by using adjectives/phrases/verbs.
* Timing is very important - pace yourself when you are answering so you don't go significantly over or under time.
* Try to only stick to the questions IGCSE has pre-prepared
  * If you finish all the questions given in under 10 minutes, the teacher will ask you another question - try to avoid this
  * Technically, you should be able to come to 10 total minutes with the pre-provided questions
  * Personally, it is better not to leave room for the extra questions as sometimes; it shows IGCSE you didn't have enough content to talk about
