## Variables

- Independent variable (IV): the factor under investigation in an
  experiment which is manipulated to create two or more conditions and
  is expected to be responsible for changes in the DV.

- Dependent variable (DV): the factor in an experiment which is
  measured and is expected to change under the influence of the IV.

- Example: IV = amount of caffeine consumed (0mg vs 100mg)
  DV = number of words recalled from a list

- Operationalization is the definition of variables that can
  be accurately manipulated, measured or quantified and replicated. Operationalization means translating  abstract theoretical concepts into measurable observations.
