## Controlling of Variables

* It is essential to control variables to be certain about the findings of a study. During the investigation of a psychological question, the researches want to know whether the IV actually causes the observed effect. If other variables are allowed to vary, it becomes impossible to determine the true cause of the results. Keeping variables constant allows fair comparison between groups, increases internal validity, and improves accuracy of conclusions.
* An extraneous variable is any variable that is not the subject of the investigation and can potentially affect the outcomes of the research study. It can either act randomly, affecting the DV in all levels of the IV, or systematically, affecting only one level of the IV (called a confounding variable), so can obscure the effect of the IV, making the results difficult to interpret. Extraneous and uncontrolled variables are the same. 
  * Example of an extraneous variable: A loud ambulance passing by while participants are completing a memory test.
* Confounding variable is a type of extraneous variable that is associated with both the independent and dependent variables. While an extraneous variable is anything that could influence the dependent variable, a confounding variable influences the dependent variable and also correlates with or causally affects the independent variable.
  * Example of a confounding variable:

    * Group A drinks coffee in the morning.

    * Group B drinks no coffee in the evening.

    * If Group A scores higher on an alertness test, you cannot tell whether it was due to the coffee or the time of day.
* A situational variable is a confounding variable caused by an aspect
  of the environment. Example: One group completes a test in a quiet room, while another completes it in a noisy room.
* Participant variables are another confounding variable caused by the individual differences of participants. Example: Students in group A naturally have better memories than others.
* Control is a way to keep any potential extraneous variables
  constant. These are required to improve internal validity and reduce the influence of confounding variables' as validity is the primary purpose.
