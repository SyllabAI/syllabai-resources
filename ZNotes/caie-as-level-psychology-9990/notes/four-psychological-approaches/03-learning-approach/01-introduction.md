# Learning Approach

## Introduction

* **Main assumptions of the learning approach:**
  * Conditioning helps to explain changes in behaviour- According to the learning approach, behaviour is shaped by experience and interaction with the environment. Behaviour can be learned through classical conditioning, operant conditioning, and social learning. The approach often explains behaviour using a stimulus-response model and focuses on observable changes in behaviour.
  * Social learning helps to explain changes in behaviour- The learning approach assumes that people learn not only through direct experience but also by observing others. Social learning, or observational learning, occurs when individuals watch the behaviour of models such as parents, peers, or media figures and then imitate those behaviours. This can affect attitudes, beliefs, and behaviours, especially when the model is rewarded or appears successful. Vicarious reinforcement, where the observer sees the model being rewarded, increases the likelihood of the behaviour being learned.
