9b. One difference is that Holzel used an MRI scan to see brain density changes after the mindfulness stress reduction course. On the other hand, Dement and Kleitman used an EEG that could only monitor brain wave activity. Therefore, Holzel’s method looked at structure whereas Dement and Kleitman looked at (real-time) function.

Another difference is that Dement and Kleitman used 9 participants in their sample : seven adult males and two adult females. While Holzel used 33 participants with 16 adult females and 17 adult males. This shows that Holzel’s study is more generalisable than Dement and Kleitman as is it has a wider sample.

10\. The study by Fagen et al. has many strengths and weaknesses. 

Firstly, one strength of this study is that quantitative data was collected in terms of the number of sessions it took for the elephant to pass the trunk wash test, as well as the number of offers it took for them to learn each task. This leads to an objectivity by the researchers as little researcher bias is involved when the data has to be analyzed, making the study more internally validity.

To add on, another strength of this study is that a behavioral checklist was used which had detailed operational descriptions of all the behaviors that were taught. This help increase the reliability of the study because when it is replicated the researchers will be able to exactly teach the elephants the same behaviors.

On the other hand, a weakness of this study is that the study did not have any adult or male elephants. There was only one adult female elephant along with 4 other female children. This makes it difficult to generalize the data as we cannot be sure that males and adults will respond to secondary positive reinforcements the way these elephants did.

Lastly, the measurement of the elephants’ performance was subjective. The researcher had to decide whether the elephant would be successful in performing a real trunk wash, not just following the checklist. The researchers might have gotten close to the animals and bias can be involved in this decision, reducing the internal validity of the study.
