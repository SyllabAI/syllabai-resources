9b. One similarity between Hassett et al. and Hölzel et al. was that both studies conformed to the ethical guidelines relevant to their studies. In the study by Hassett, this was in accordance with NIH Guidance to include enrichment and appropriate environmental management. In the study by Hölzel, this was via guidelines from a hospital and a medical school to include obtaining informed consent. Therefore, both studies followed set ethical guidelines relevant to the setting the study took place in.

One difference between Hassett et al. and Hölzel et al. was that Hassett was an oberservational study with a behavioral checklist whereas Hölzel et al. had correlation. This shows that Hölzel was looking for a relationship between variables while Hassett was looking for patterns in real world settings.

10\.The study by Bandura et al. had many strengths and weaknesses.

Firstly, one strength of this study is that the participants were rated on aggression prior to the study, so Bandura could be more confident it was the IV affecting the DV, increasing the internal validity of the study.

Additionally, another strength is that the study was highly standardized. Each participant had the same layout of toys and all children in the aggression conditions saw the same exact routine being performed. The length of time observed was also kept the same at 20 minutes. This ensures that the study is easily replicable and reliable.

However, one weakness of this study is that it used young children aged 37 to 69 months, who are at a sensitive age in their development. This raises ethical issues for those being exposed to an aggressive model, as there may be a greater risk of psychological harm due to long-term after-effects. 

Lastly, another weakness is that the study was conducted on children coming from a prestigious nursery, the data may not be generalizable to children from lower socioeconomic backgrounds. Their home lives may not be representative of the general population due to their affluent background, reducing generalizability.
