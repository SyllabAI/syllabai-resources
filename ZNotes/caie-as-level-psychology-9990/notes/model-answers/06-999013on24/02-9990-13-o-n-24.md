9a. The sample consisted of 5 elephants from the same elephant stable in Nepal, four were juveniles (aged 5-7) while one was an adult in her 50s. They were all females.

9b. One similarity is that both the Fagen study and the Bandura study used observation as part of their data collection techniques. This means that specific behaviors could be operationalized to improve validity. For example, in Fagen there was trunk down which meant the trunk was hanging loose and in Bandura gun play was pretending to shoot at an object in the room.

One difference is that the Fagen et al. studied elephants in their normal environment, while Bandura et al. used a laboratory setting. Since the elephants were studied in their own stable the data collected is more ecologically valid as they are less likely to act out. The children in Bandura may not imitate behavior as much as they did in the study at home, so the data may be less ecologically valid.

10\. The study by Piliavin et al. had many strengths and weaknesses.

Firstly, one weakness of this study is that it was unethical. One ethical guideline they broke was informed consent as no passenger had any information about the study or agreed to take part. Also, the event of watching someone stagger and fall to ground could have been psychologically stressful for the passengers.

To add on, another weakness is that the study lacked population validity, as participants were mainly commuters on a specific New York subway route. Urban residents may experience more deindividuation due to the American individualistic culture. Therefore, this limits the extent to which the findings on helping behavior can be applied to people living in rural areas, smaller cities or collectivist cultures.

On the other hand, one strength of this study is that the procedure was highly controlled. The victims always wore the same clothing and the collapse always took place when the train passed the first station. This ensure that the procedure can be easily replicated to test for reliability.

Lastly, a strength is that this study mainly collected quantitative data, for example the time taken for the victim to be helped in seconds. This helps to keep the data collection objective as well as the data analysis, increasing internal validity of the study.
