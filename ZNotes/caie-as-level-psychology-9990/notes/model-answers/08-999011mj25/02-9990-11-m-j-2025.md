9a. The sampling technique was **opportunity sampling** and the participants had just **come after finishing another study.** They were **aged 18-55** with **35 females, 5 males.** (Topic : Doodling)

9b. One difference between the studies by Andrade and Pozzulo is that they used **different experimental designs within them.** Andrade was only **independent groups** with participants being placed in either the experimental doodling group or control group. Part of the Pozzulo study was **repeated measures** as all participants saw target absent and target present line-ups. Therefore, in Andrade participants only participated in one level of the independent variable whilst in Pozzulo participants participated in multiple levels of the independent variable.

Another difference is that **Andrade used an auditory memory test while Pozzulo used a visual test.** Pozzulo showed multiple short films to test memory and Andrade had participants listen to a monotonous tape recording. This shows that they were testing how cognitive processes work through different senses. (Topics : Doodling, Line-Ups)

10\. The study by Miligram et al. had many strengths and weaknesses.

Firstly, a strength of this study is that **voltage levels were used as a dependent variable**, this allows for quantitative data to be collected free from researcher bias. Therefore, **valid results** could be obtained from participants in terms of their obedience or defiance.

To add on, another strength of this study is the **use of a false study aim and covert observations**, this decreased demand characteristics and participants' obedient or defiant behaviour is likely to have been genuine for the majority of participants. They believed that 'learning and memory' was the focus of the research and were not aware that their reactions were being observed from behind a one-way mirror. This **increases the validity** of the study.

One weakness is that it **lacks mundane realism**, reading out word pairs is not an everyday task. Participants had to judge accuracy and give what they thought was an electric shock. However, this does not happen in reality, we do not think we are giving people an electric shock as punishment to a stranger in the real world.

Lastly, another weakness is that the **sampling technique used was volunteer sampling.** This can lead to participants with certain characteristics being attracted and taking part in the study. This can reduce generalizability to people that do not share these features, such as introverted people.
