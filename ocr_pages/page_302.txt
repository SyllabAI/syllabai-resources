The most familiar one is ethanoic acid (old name acetic acid). Vinegar is an
aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid.

Carboxylic acids are formed by oxidation of the corresponding alcohols, for
example ethanoic acid is formed when ethanol is left in the air or oxidised
using potassium dichromate(VI) in dilute sulfuric acid. If you need a reminder,
have at look at Chapter 26, pages 289-290.
