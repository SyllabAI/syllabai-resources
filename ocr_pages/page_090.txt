GIANT IONIC STRUCTURES

All ionic compounds form crystals that consist of lattices of positive and
negative ions packed together in a regular way. A lattice is a regular array of
particles. The lattice is held together by the strong electrostatic attractions
between the positively and negatively charged ions.
