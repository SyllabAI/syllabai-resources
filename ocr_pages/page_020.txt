Part iii: Solubility measures the maximum mass of potassium chloride
which will dissolve in 100g of water at a particular temperature. Crystals
will start to appear as soon as the solution becomes saturated. From the
graph, it can be seen that more than 51.0g of solid will be soluble at
90°C, but as the solution is cooled the solubility decreases. Drawing a
line across at 51.0g shows that this is the maximum mass that will
dissolve at 80°C and therefore crystals will first appear at temperatures
below this.
