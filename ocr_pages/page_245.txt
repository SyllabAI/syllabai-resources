Using a weighing bottle like the one

mixing the chemicals together without
losing any oxygen before you can get

start the reaction, shake the flask so
that the weighing bottle falls over and
the manganese(IV) oxide comes into
contact with the hydrogen peroxide.
You need to keep shaking so that an
