MAKING MAGNESIUM SULFATE CRYSTALS

We can add excess magnesium to sulfuric acid. This time the acid does
not have to be heated.

When we add the magnesium the reaction mixture will fizz (hydrogen
is given off). We keep adding magnesium until the fizzing stops and
there is magnesium left in the beaker. This means that all the acid has
reacted. The equation for the formation of the solution of magnesium
sulfate is:

Mg(s) + H,SO,(aq) + MgSO,({aq) + H2(9)

Again the solution is concentrated by heating it and allowed to crystallise.
The crystallisation reaction is:
