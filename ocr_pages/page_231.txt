More energy is released when bonds are formed than is required to break
them; the reaction is exothermic.

Energy is given out when the reactants are converted to the products. Therefore
we know that the products must have less energy than the reactants; the products
are more stable. We can show this on an energy level diagram (Figure 19.17).
