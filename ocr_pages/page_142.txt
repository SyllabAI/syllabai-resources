P REMINDER

Some reminders about oxidation and
reduction:

oxidation is loss of electrons
reduction is gain of electrons

An oxidising agent is something that
oxidises something else by taking
electrons away from it. Oxidising
agents accept electrons and therefore
are the things that are reduced in a
reaction.

A reducing agent is something that
reduces something else by giving
electrons to it. Reducing agents give
away electrons and therefore are the
things that are oxidised in a reaction.

The reactions above that do show a colour change are often discussed in
terms of oxidation and reduction, they are redox reactions. If you have not
already covered redox reactions, you should look at Chapter 14 before reading
the next section.
