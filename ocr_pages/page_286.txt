24 ALKANES

The hydrocarbons separated from the fractional distillation of crude oil are mainly alkanes. This is the simplest family of
hydrocarbons, containing only carbon—carbon and carbon-hydrogen single bonds. Here we look into the structures and
chemical reactions of these compounds in more detail. It is assumed that you have already read Chapter 22 (Introduction
to Organic Chemistry).
