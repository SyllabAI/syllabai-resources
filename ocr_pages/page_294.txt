could produce two different products,
propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol. In
reality, much more propan-2-ol is
formed than propan-1-ol. In the major
reaction, a hydrogen atom from water
is first added onto the end carbon
and then the -OH group is attached
to the middle carbon. The addition

of compounds to an unsymmetrical
alkene like propene will be explored in
detail when you study chemistry at a
