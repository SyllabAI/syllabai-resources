THE POLYMERISATION OF PROPENE

High-density poly(ethene) is used where greater strength and rigidity are
needed, for example to make plastic bottles such as milk bottles. If you can
find a recycling symbol with the letters HDPE next to it, then the bottle is made
of high-density poly(ethene). If it has some other letters there, then it is made
of a different polymer.
