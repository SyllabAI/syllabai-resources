COVALENT BONDING IN AN OXYGEN
MOLECULE: DOUBLE BONDING

An oxygen atom has 6 electrons in its outer shell and so if two oxygen atoms
combine, they will both share 2 electrons each; this means that each atom
will have 8 electrons in its outer shell. There are therefore two shared pairs of
electrons between the oxygen atoms; this is called a double covalent bond or,
usually, just a double bond.
