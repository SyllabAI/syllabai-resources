What mass of potassium chloride would crystallise from the solution in
biii if the temperature fell to 10°C?

You can use the data in the table to find the solubility at 10°C. This
value is 31.2g per 100g of water. That means that 31.2g of potassium
chloride will stay in solution at 10°C. Since you started with 51.0g, the
rest of it must have formed crystals:

mass of crystals = 51.0 -31.2g = 19.8g
19.8g of potassium chloride will crystallise out.
