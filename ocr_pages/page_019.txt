You can use solubility curves to work out what mass of crystals you would get
if you cooled a saturated solution.

Consider the solubility curve for potassium nitrate (KNO;) in Figure 1.18. At 90°C
200g of potassium nitrate dissolves in 100g water. At 30°C only 50g will dissolve.
Therefore, if we have a solution containing 200g of potassium nitrate dissolved

in 100g of water and let it cool down from 90°C to 30°C, 150g of potassium
nitrate must be released from the solution, which it does as crystals. We say that
potassium nitrate crystallises out of the solution or precipitates out of the solution.
