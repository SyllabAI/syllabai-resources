## Electric fields

### Extended tier only

- An electric field is defined as: **A region of space in which an electric charge experiences a force**
- The direction of an electric field at a point is defined as: **The direction of the force on a positive charge at that point**
- Charged objects **create** electric fields around themselves

  - This is similar to how magnets create magnetic fields

- An electric field is a **vector **quantity as it has both **magnitude **(strength) and **direction**
