## Uses of electromagnetic waves

#### Typical uses of the different regions of the electromagnetic spectrum

| **Wave** | **Use** |
|---|---|
| Radio | Radio and television transmissions<br><br>Radar |
| Microwave | Satellite television<br><br>Mobile (cell) phones<br><br>Microwave ovens |
| Infrared | Television remote controllers<br><br>Thermal imaging |
| Visible light | Vision<br><br>Photography |
| Ultraviolet | Detecting fake banknotes |
| X-rays | Medical scanning<br><br>Security scanners |
| Gamma rays | Detection of cancer<br><br>Treatment of cancer |
