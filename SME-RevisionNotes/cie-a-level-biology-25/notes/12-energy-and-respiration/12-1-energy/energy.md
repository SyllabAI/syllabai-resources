# Energy

## The Need for Energy

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_kwdNJWVFYPdzFWT4` · outline the need for energy in living organisms, as illustrated by active transport, movement and anabolic reactions, such as those occurring in DNA replication and protein synthesis

## The need for energy

- Living organisms are composed of cells, and within each cell, many **activities** and **processes** are constantly being carried out to maintain life

  - Work in a living organism requires **energy**

| **Type of work** | **Examples** |
|---|---|
| Transporting substances across membranes | - [Active transport](https://www.savemyexams.com/a-level/biology/cie/22/revision-notes/4-cell-membranes--transport/4-2-movement-into--out-of-cells/4-2-3-active-transport/) using the sodium-potassium pump in cell membranes<br>- Exocytosis of digested bacteria from white blood cells |
| *Anabolic* reactions | - Synthesis of DNA from nucleotides<br>- Synthesis of proteins from amino acids |
| Movement | - Cellular movement of chromosomes via the spindle<br>- Mechanical contraction of muscles |
| Maintaining body temperature | - Occurs only in mammals and birds |
