## Chemical Digestion

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_Ks73GtZTt68tJ3Gp`

## Chemical Digestion

- Food taken into the body goes through 5 different stages during its passage through the alimentary canal

  - One of these is chemical digestion
- **Chemical digestion** is the breakdown of large, insoluble molecules into small, soluble molecules
- The role of chemical digestion is to produce small soluble molecules that can be absorbed
