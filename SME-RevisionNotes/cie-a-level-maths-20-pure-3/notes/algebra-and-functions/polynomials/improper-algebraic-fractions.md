## Improper algebraic fractions

### What are improper algebraic fractions?

- The **degree** of the numerator is **greater than** or **equal** to the degree of the denominator

![Top Heavy Rational Expressions Notes Diagram 1, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/8690abd6ade2-2-6-2-top-heavy-rational-expressions-notes-diagr.png)

### How do I simplify improper algebraic fractions?

![Top Heavy Rational Expressions Notes Diagram 2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/3dcd7e68b654-2-6-2-top-heavy-rational-expressions-notes-diagr.png)

- Write as a **quotient** and a **remainder**
- The algebraic equivalent of changing a top-heavy fraction to a mixed number
