## Integrating other functions

### Is integration the reverse of differentiation?

- Yes, but remember “+c”, the constant of integration …

  - … unless finding a definite integral
- Recognising common results helps to make integration easier (See Differentiating Other Functions)

- You are given all the standard integrals in the formula booklet

![Notes diff_results, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/f26685f6e90e-8-2-2-notes-diff-results.png)

### How do I integrate e<sup>x</sup> and e<sup>kx</sup>?

![Notes ex, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/e8d4186b7d5f-8-2-2-notes-ex.png)

- The gradient of **e**<sup>**kx**</sup> is **ke**<sup>**kx**</sup>

  - ie **y = e**<sup>**kx**</sup>, **dy/dx = ke**<sup>**kx**</sup>
- The reverse applies when integrating
- This is an example of **reverse chain rule**

![Notes ekx, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/76d8359e7117-8-2-2-notes-ekx.png)

### How do I integrating 1/x?

![Notes ln, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/d107c2c666ca-8-2-2-notes-ln.png)

- Remember **1/x = x**<sup>**-1**</sup>

  - The method for integrating powers does **not** apply if the power is -1

### How do I integrating sin x and cos x?

![Notes sin_cos, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/8245752fc05a-8-2-2-notes-sin-cos.png)

- Note the minus in the integral of **sin x**

### How do I integrate tan x?

- The integral of **tan x** is **ln|sec x| + c**

### How do I integrate $\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}$ and $\frac{1}{a^{2}+x^{2}}$?

- Recall that $\frac{d}{dx}(\mathrm{tan}^{-1}x)=\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}$
- Then `integral fraction numerator 1 over denominator 1 space plus space x squared end fraction space straight d x space equals space tan to the power of negative 1 end exponent x space plus space c` and `integral fraction numerator 1 over denominator a squared space plus space x squared end fraction space straight d x space equals space 1 over a tan to the power of negative 1 end exponent open parentheses x over a close parentheses space plus space c`
