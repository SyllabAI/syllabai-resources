## Logarithmic functions

### What are logarithmic functions?

![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig1, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/450306875f31-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig1.png)

- $a^{x}=b$ and $\mathrm{log}_{a}b=x$ are equivalent statements
- **a > 0**
- **a** is called the base
- Every time you write a logarithm statement say to yourself what it means

  - **log**<sub>**3**</sub>** 81 = 4** “the power you raise 3 to, to get 81, is 4”
  - **log**<sub>**p**</sub>** q = r** “the power you raise **p** to, to get **q**, is **r**”

### How are logarithms and powers inverses of each other?

- A logarithm is the inverse of raising to a power so we can use rules to simplify logarithmic functions ![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig2, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](assets/93f89aee4b30-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig2.png)

### How do I use the inverse property of logs and powers?

![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/20bbf549e637-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig3.png)

- Recognising the rules of logarithms allows expressions to be simplified

![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig4, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/d1376f92bede-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig4.png)

- Recognition of common powers helps in simple cases For more awkward cases a calculator is needed** ** ![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig5, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](assets/afff1260dcdf-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig5.png)

  - Powers of 2: 2<sup>0</sup> = 1, 2<sup>1</sup> = 2, 2<sup>2</sup> = 4, 2<sup>3</sup> = 8, 2<sup>4</sup> = 16, …
  - Powers of 3: 3<sup>0</sup> = 1, 3<sup>1</sup> = 3, 3<sup>2</sup> = 9, 3<sup>3</sup> = 27, 3<sup>4</sup> = 81, …
  - The first few powers of 4, 5 and 10 should also be familiar

- Calculators can have, possibly, three different logarithm buttons

- This button allows you to type in any number for the base

### What notation is used with logarithms?

![Logarithmic Functions Notes fig9, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/573fee6e371d-6-1-2-logarithmic-functions-notes-fig9.png)

- 10 is a common base

  - **log**<sub>**10 **</sub>**x** is abbreviated to **log x** or **lg x**
- The value **e** is another common base

  - **log**<sub>**e**</sub>** x** is abbreviated to **ln x**
- **(log x)**<sup>**2**</sup>** ≠ log x**<sup>**2**</sup>
