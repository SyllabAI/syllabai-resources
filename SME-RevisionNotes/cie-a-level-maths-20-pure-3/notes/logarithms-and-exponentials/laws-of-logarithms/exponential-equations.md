## Exponential equations

### What are exponential equations?

![Exponential Equations Notes fig1, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/a3818a1898f7-6-2-2-exponential-equations-notes-fig1.png)

- An equation where the unknown is a power
- In simple cases the solutions can be “spotted” ![Exponential Equations Notes fig2, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](assets/ac77cc343ae4-6-2-2-exponential-equations-notes-fig2.png)

### How do I solve exponential equations?

- Laws of Indices are often needed to rewrite equations
- Laws of Logarithms are used to solve exponential equations
- **ln** (**log**<sub>**e**</sub>) is often used
- Answers are often written in terms of **ln**

### How do I solve hidden quadratic exponential equations?

- Look out for “hidden” squared terms, these are hidden quadratics which will need to be solved

  - **4**<sup>**x**</sup>** = (2**<sup>**2**</sup>**)**<sup>**x**</sup>** = 2**<sup>**2x**</sup>** = (2**<sup>**x**</sup>**)**<sup>**2**</sup>
  - **e**<sup>**2x**</sup>** = (e**<sup>**2**</sup>**)**<sup>**x**</sup>** = (e**<sup>**x**</sup>**)**<sup>**2**</sup>
