## Limitations of accounting statements

### What are the limitations of using accounting statements?

| Limitation | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Historic cost | - The *historic cost* accounting principle means that the actual cost is recorded on the statements for each transaction<br>- It can be difficult to compare transactions if they did not happen at the same time |
| Time factor | - There is a gap between the end of a financial year and the preparation of the financial statements<br>- The current information might be different from the information stated on the financial statements |
| Application of accounting policies | - Businesses might have different layouts of their financial statements<br><br>  - Some businesses might subtract the goods taken for the owner's use from purchases before stating it on the statement of profit or loss<br>  - Some businesses might subtract sales returns from the sales before stating it on the statement of profit or loss<br>  - Some businesses might not explicitly state the profit from operations whereas others might<br>- It can be difficult to make comparisons if the items do not represent exactly the same thing |
| Non-financial aspects | - The *money measurement* accounting principle means that only information involving money is recorded<br>- There are a lot of other important aspects which are not included in the statements<br><br>  - Quality of the goods<br>  - Employee satisfaction<br>  - Reputation |
