## Electromotive force

### Extended Tier Only

- Electromotive force (e.m.f.) is the name given to the potential difference of the power source in a circuit
- It is defined as **The electrical work done by a source in moving a unit charge around a complete circuit**
- Electromotive force (e.m.f.) is measured in volts (V)

![Electromotive force, IGCSE & GCSE Physics revision notes](../../../assets/41b572f09f29-electromotive-force.png)

***The e.m.f. is the voltage supplied by a power supply: 12 V in the above case***
