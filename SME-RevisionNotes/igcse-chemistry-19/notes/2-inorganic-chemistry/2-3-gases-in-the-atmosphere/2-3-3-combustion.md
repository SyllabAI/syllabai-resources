# Combustion

## Combustion

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_pDNB2HzMGjXjz9Dn` · describe the combustion of elements in oxygen, including magnesium, hydrogen and sulfur

## Combustion

### What is combustion?

- **Combustion** is the scientific word for burning
- All combustion reactions involve a chemical change in which oxygen reacts with elements or compounds to produce **oxides**
- Combustion reactions give out heat, so they will always be **exothermic** reactions
- You need to be able to describe the combustion reactions of magnesium, hydrogen and sulfur

#### The combustion reactions of Mg, H<sub>2 </sub>& S

| **Element** | **Observation during combustion** | **Symbol equation** |
|---|---|---|
| Magnesium | Intense white flame<br><br>White powder produced (magnesium oxide) | 2Mg (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2MgO (s) |
| Hydrogen | Exothermic<br><br>Water is produced | 2H<sub>2</sub> (g) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2H<sub>2</sub>O (g) |
| Sulfur | Blue flame<br><br>Colourless, poisonous gas produced | S (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → SO<sub>2</sub> (g) |

> **Exam Hint**
> Combustion reactions can also be classified as oxidation reactions.
