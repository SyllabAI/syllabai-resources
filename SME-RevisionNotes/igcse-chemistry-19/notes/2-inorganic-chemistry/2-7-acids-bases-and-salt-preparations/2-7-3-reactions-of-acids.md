# Reactions of Acids

## Reactions of Acids

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_7xwyvWNrjMXWQYC5` · describe the reactions of hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid and nitric acid with metals, bases and metal carbonates (excluding the reactions between nitric acid and metals) to form salts

## Reactions of Acids

### Reactions of acids with metals

- Only metals **above hydrogen** in the reactivity series will react with dilute acids
- The more reactive the metal then the more vigorous the reaction will be
- Metals that are placed high on the reactivity series such as potassium and sodium are very dangerous and react **explosively** with acids
- When acids react with metals they form a** salt** and **[hydrogen gas](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/chemistry/edexcel/19/revision-notes/2-inorganic-chemistry/2-8-chemical-tests/2-8-1-tests-for-gases/)**
- The general equation is:

**metal + acid ⟶ salt + hydrogen**

- Some examples of metal-acid reactions and their equations are given below:

#### Table of acid-metal reactions

| **Metal** | **Sulfuric acid** | **Hydrochloric acid** |
|---|---|---|
| Magnesium | Mg  + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>  → MgSO<sub>4</sub>  + H<sub>2</sub> | Mg + 2HCl  → MgCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> |
| Zinc | Zn + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> → ZnSO<sub>4</sub>+ H<sub>2</sub> | Zn + 2HCl  → ZnCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> |
| Iron | Fe + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>  → FeSO<sub>4</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> | Fe + 2HCl → FeCl<sub>2</sub>  + H<sub>2</sub> |

- In general, we can summarise the reaction of a metal that forms a +2 ion as follows:

#### Acids-Metals Summary Table

| **Acid** | **Name of products** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrochloric acid | Metal chloride and hydrogen | Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl<sub>2</sub>  + H<sub>2 </sub> |
| Sulfuric acid | Metal sulfate and hydrogen | Zn + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> → ZnSO<sub>4</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> |

### Reaction of acids with bases

- When an acid reacts with a [base](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/chemistry/edexcel/19/revision-notes/2-inorganic-chemistry/2-7-acids-bases--salt-preparations/2-7-4-bases/), a **neutralisation** reaction occurs
- In all acid-base neutralisation reactions, a **salt** and **water** are produced:

**acid + base ⟶ salt + water**

- The identity of the salt produced depends on the** acid** used and the **positive ions** in the base
- Hydrochloric acid produces** chlorides**, sulfuric acid produces** sulfate** salts and nitric acid produces **nitrates**
- Metal **oxide**s and metal **hydroxides** act as** bases**

#### Acids and Metals Oxides or Hydroxides Summary Table

| **Acid** | **Name of products** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrochloric acid | Metal chloride and water | NaOH + HCl ⟶ NaCl + H<sub>2</sub>O |
| Sulfuric acid | Metal sulfate and water | CuO + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> ⟶ CuSO<sub>4</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O |
| Nitric acid | Metal nitrate and water | ZnO + 2HNO<sub>3</sub> ⟶ Zn(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O |

### Reactions of Acids with Metal Carbonates

- Acids will react with metal carbonates to form the corresponding metal** salt**, **[carbon](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/chemistry/edexcel/19/revision-notes/2-inorganic-chemistry/2-8-chemical-tests/2-8-1-tests-for-gases/)**[ ](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/chemistry/edexcel/19/revision-notes/2-inorganic-chemistry/2-8-chemical-tests/2-8-1-tests-for-gases/)**[dioxide](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/chemistry/edexcel/19/revision-notes/2-inorganic-chemistry/2-8-chemical-tests/2-8-1-tests-for-gases/)** and **water**
- These reactions are easily distinguishable from acid – metal oxide/hydroxide reactions due to the presence of **effervescence** caused by the carbon dioxide gas

#### Acids & Metal Carbonates Reactions Table

| **Acid** | **Name of products** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrochloric acid | Metal chloride, carbon dioxide and water | CuCO<sub>3</sub> + 2HCl ⟶ CuCl<sub>2</sub> + CO<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O |
| Sulfuric acid | Metal sulfate, carbon dioxide and water | ZnCO<sub>3</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> ⟶ ZnSO<sub>4</sub> + CO<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O |
| Nitric acid | Metal nitrate, carbon dioxide and water | Na<sub>2</sub>CO<sub>3</sub> + 2HNO<sub>3</sub> ⟶ 2NaNO<sub>3</sub> + CO<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O |

> **Exam Hint**
> If in an acid-base reaction there is effervescence produced then the base must be a metal carbonate which produces carbon dioxide gas.
