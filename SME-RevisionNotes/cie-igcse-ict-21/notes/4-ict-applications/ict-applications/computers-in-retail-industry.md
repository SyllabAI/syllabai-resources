## POS & EFTPOS

### What is POS & EFTPOS?

- Point of Sale (**POS**) is a computer terminal** used to read product barcodes**
- Barcodes are used in an **automatic stock control system** in shops
- A barcode is part of a process that involves:

  - **Every barcode being assigned to a product in stock**
  - **Each product record contains its price, stock level and a description**
  - **When a barcode is matched with a record the information is transferred to the POS terminal and the price is displayed on the screen**
  - **The stock level is reduced by one**
  - **If the current stock level falls below a  minimum reorder stock level it triggers a restocking process automatically**
- Electronic Funds Transfer at the Point of Sale (**EFTPOS**) is** how a payment is made** to  purchase good or services **using a card or electronic devices** such as smartphones
- Examples of EFTPOS include:

  - **Chip and Pin**
  - **Contactless**
  - Near field communication devices (**NFC**)

| Chip and Pin | Contactless | NFC |
|---|---|---|
| - Enhance security with two-factor authentication<br>- Require customers to enter a personal identification number (PIN) | - Allow for faster transactions<br>- Enable customers to tap their card on the terminal | - Facilitate payments through smartphones and other devices<br>- Increase convenience for customers |

## Internet Shopping

### What are the advantages and disadvantages of internet shopping?

- Internet shopping is the **act of purchasing good or services online **through a website or mobile application
- Characteristics of Internet Shopping

  - **Online stores accessible through web browsers**
  - **Wide variety of products and services are available**
  - **Convenient and often open 24/7**

#### Advantages and disadvantages to the customer

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| - Time-saving and convenient<br><br>  - Shop from home or on the go<br>  - Avoid queues and busy stores<br>- Greater product variety<br><br>  - Access to the global market<br>  - Compare products and prices easily<br>- Customisation options<br><br>  - Personalise items or services<br>  - Tailor purchases to individual preferences<br>- Potential cost savings<br><br>  - Competitive pricing due to lower overheads<br>  - Take advantage of online sales and promotions | - Security concerns<br><br>  - Risk of fraud or identity theft<br>  - Need to provide personal and financial information<br>- Limited physical interaction<br><br>  - Can't touch or try products before purchasing<br>  - This may lead to dissatisfaction or returns<br>- Delivery delays and fees<br><br>  - Wait for items to be shipped and delivered<br>  - Additional costs for shipping and handling<br>- Impersonal customer service<br><br>  - Difficulty resolving issues or returning items<br>  - Lack of face-to-face interaction with staff |

#### Advantages and disadvantages to the business

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| - Can target prices, products and services at specific groups based on buying data<br>- Can update stock availability and prices more quickly than a physical store through their website<br>- Cheaper to publicise special offers rather than mail shots<br>- International customer base<br>- Increased profits due to lower overheads (e.g. fewer staff) | - Increased Competition<br>- Digital Fraud and Security Concerns<br>- Technical Issues and Downtime<br>- Costs of Delivery and Returns<br>- Customer Trust<br>- Inventory Management<br>- Depersonalisation<br>- Online Reviews<br>- Dependency on Internet Infrastructure<br>- Logistical Challenges<br>- Legal and Regulatory Compliance<br>- Increased Customer Expectations |
