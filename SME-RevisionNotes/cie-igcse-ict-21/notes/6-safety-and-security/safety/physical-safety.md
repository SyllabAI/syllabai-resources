## Physical Safety

### What is physical safety?

- Physical safety is** creating a safe environment when using technology**
- It includes **considering dangers** that could lead to **serious injury or loss of life **
- Identifying **strategies to mitigate **dangers
- Safety risks include:

  - **Electrocution from spilling drinks near electrical equipment**
  - **Fire hazard from overloading plug sockets**
  - **Equipment overheating**
  - **Trailing cables (trip hazard)**
  - **Heavy equipment falling and causing injury**

| Risk | Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| **Electrocution** | - Spilling drinks near electrical equipment | - Keep liquids away from electrical equipment |
| - Touching live cables | - Ensure that cables are properly insulated and protected<br>- Use ***non-conductive*** materials where possible<br>- Ensure that electrical equipment is **turned off **and **unplugged **before cleaning or maintenance<br>- Use ***circuit breakers*** or **fuses **to prevent electrical overload |   |
| **Fire hazards** | - Sockets being overloaded | - Use surge protectors to prevent electrical overload<br>- Ensure enough plug sockets in the room<br>- Don’t plug too many devices into the same plug socket<br>- Don’t leave devices plugged in and unattended |
| - Equipment overheating | - Ensure that equipment is properly ventilated and not obstructed<br>- Keep flammable materials away from heat sources<br>- Regularly check equipment for signs of wear or damage<br>- Use fire extinguishers in case of emergencies<br>- Turn off or unplug devices when away from the location<br>- Do not cover any air vents on devices |   |
| **Trip hazard** | - Cables not properly secured or organised | - Use cable ties or clips to secure cables<br>- Keep cables away from areas where people are walking<br>- Secure cables where you can, like under desks to stop protruding into open areas<br>- Use cable covers to protect cables and prevent tripping hazards<br>- Regularly inspect cables for signs of wear or damage<br>- Where possible use wireless devices to reduce cables |
| **Personal safety** | Improperly secured equipment | - Ensure that equipment is properly secured and stable<br>- Regularly check the stability of locations containing devices |
| Equipment not placed on stable surfaces | - Keep equipment away from edges and other potential hazards<br>- Regularly inspect equipment and locations containing devices for signs of wear or damage |   |
