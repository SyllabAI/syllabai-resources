## Chlorophyll

- Chlorophyll:

  - is a **green pigment** that is found in **chloroplasts** within plant cells
  - reflects green light, giving plants their characteristic green colour
  - absorbs light energy; its role is to transfer energy from **light** into energy in **chemicals**, for the synthesis of carbohydrates, such as glucose

- Photosynthesis will not occur in the absence of chlorophyll

> **Exam Hint**
> Recall that the word equation for photosynthesis is:
> 
> $carbondioxide+water\rightarrowglucose+oxygen_{}$
> 
> The reaction between carbon dioxide and water to form glucose and oxygen **only occurs** **in the presence of light, which is absorbed by chlorophyll **in chloroplasts. No chlorophyll, no photosynthesis!
