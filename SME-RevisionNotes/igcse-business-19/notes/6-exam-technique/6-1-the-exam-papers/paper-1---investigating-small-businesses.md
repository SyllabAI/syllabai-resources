# Paper 1: Investigating Small Businesses

## What is examined in the Edexcel IGCSE Business Paper 1?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_qgHph2PrSgzsBBb4`

## What is examined in the Edexcel IGCSE Business Paper 1?

- Paper 1 draws on knowledge and understanding from across the entire specification content
- It focuses on small businesses of up to 49 employees that have a local or national market.
- **The paper's duration** is 90 minutes
- The total number of marks available is **80,** representing** 50%** **of the qualification**
- The paper combines **multiple-choice, short-answer, calculation, analysis** and **evaluation **questions, supported by short case studies
- You must **answer all questions**

### Structure of the paper

- The exam is divided into **four multipart questions**, each focused on a short business case study
- Each question **builds in demand**, testing knowledge, application, analysis, and evaluation skills

#### Question 1: MCQ, short-answer, calculation and analysis questions

- This question totals **20 marks**
- Questions are based on a real-world style case study
- The main skills tested are **recall, basic application, simple calculations, brief explanations **and **analysis**
- Tasks include

  - Six **multiple-choice questions** worth 1 mark each, that require the selection of  a correct terms, formula or definition
  - One-mark **'state' questions** that require a brief response
  - **Simple calculations**, worth 2 marks, requiring clear workings and correct application of formulae
  - **Short explanations**, worth 3 marks, that usually require a valid knowledge point, followed by a suitable development
  - An **analysis question, **worth 6 marks, that  requires a chains of reasoning in context

#### Questions 2 and 3: Short-answer, calculation, analysis and justify questions

- These questions each total **20 marks**
- Questions are based on real-world style case studies
- The main skills tested are **recall, basic application, simple calculations, brief explanations, analysis **and **making justified decisions**
- Tasks include

  - One-mark **'state' questions** that require a brief response
  - **Simple calculations**, worth 2 marks, requiring clear workings and correct application of formulae
  - Brief **outline questions** that require a point to be developed in context
  - **Short explanations**, worth 3 marks, that usually require a valid knowledge point, followed by a suitable development
  - An **analysis question, **worth 6 marks, that  requires a chains of reasoning in context
  - A** justify question **that requires two or more options to be compared and an appropriate contextual decision to be made, with reasoning

#### Question 4: Short-answer or calculation, analysis and evaluate questions

- This question totals **20 marks**
- Questions are based on a real-world style case study
- The main skills tested are **simple calculations **or **brief explanations, analysis **and **evaluation**
- Tasks include

  - **Simple calculations**, worth 2 marks, requiring clear workings and correct application of formulae
  - **Short explanations**, worth 3 marks, that usually require a valid knowledge point, followed by a suitable development
  - An **analysis question, **worth 6 marks, that  requires a chains of reasoning in context
  - An** evaluation question **that requires a justified decision in relation to a business concept to be made, within the context of the business
