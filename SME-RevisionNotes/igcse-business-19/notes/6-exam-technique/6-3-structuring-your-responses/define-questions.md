# Define Questions

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business 'Define' question?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_fm9ncztTjYQCkqHc`

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business 'Define' question?

- In these questions you need to provide a** clear meaning** of a given business term

  - You will **not** receive any marks for giving examples
