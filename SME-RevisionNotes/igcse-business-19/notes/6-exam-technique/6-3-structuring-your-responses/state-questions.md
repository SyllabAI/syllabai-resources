# State Questions

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business 'State' question?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_74BB6GmD8gJQWks6`

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business 'State' question?

- In these questions you need to consider the case study and **name**,** select, recognise **or **express, in clear terms**, one relevant point
- Your answer does not have to be a full sentence
