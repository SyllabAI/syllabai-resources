# Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business multiple choice question?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_QBRxT9DQkP6tZG5G`

## How do I answer an Edexcel IGCSE Business multiple-choice question?

- These questions require you to **select a correct answer** from a range
- They test how effectively you can **recall knowledge** from the specification content
- Multiple-choice questions are** worth one mark** and appear in **Question 1 only**

### Top tips

- Look for at least one obvious incorrect option that is unrelated to the focus of the question

- In this example, the obvious incorrect option is excluded
