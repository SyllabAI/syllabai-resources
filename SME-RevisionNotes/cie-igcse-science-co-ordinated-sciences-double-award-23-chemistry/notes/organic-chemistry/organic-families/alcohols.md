## Alcohols

- All alcohols contain the hydroxyl (**-OH**) functional group which is the part of alcohol molecules that is responsible for their characteristic reactions
- Alcohols are a homologous series of compounds that have the general formula **C**<sub>**n**</sub>**H**<sub>**2n+1**</sub>**OH**
- They differ by one -CH<sub>2</sub> in the molecular formulae from one member to the next

### Uses and reactions of alcohol

- Ethanol (C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub>OH) is one of the most important alcohols

  - Ethanol can also be represented by its structural formula CH<sub>3</sub>CH<sub>2</sub>OH
- It is the type of alcohol found in **alcoholic drinks** such as wine and beer
- It is also used as **fuel **for cars and as a **solvent**
- Ethanol will undergo complete combustion and burn in excess oxygen to produce carbon dioxide and water

C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub>OH (l) + 3O<sub>2 </sub>(g) → 2CO<sub>2</sub> (g) + 3H<sub>2</sub>0 (l)
