# Saturated & Unsaturated Compounds

## Saturated & unsaturated Compounds

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_zcjHMGnpDkQvZpXP` · State that a saturated compound has molecules in which all carbon–carbon bonds are single bonds
State that an unsaturated compound has molecules in which one or more carbon–carbon bonds are not single bonds

## Saturated & unsaturated Compounds

### What are saturated compounds?

- **Saturated compounds** are molecules with only **single carbon-carbon bonds **(C-C)

  - Each carbon atom has 4 single bonds to other atoms
- **Alkanes** are examples of saturated compounds ** **
- Alkanes have the general formula **C**<sub>**n**</sub>**H**<sub>**2n+2**</sub>
- The first three alkanes are:

1. Methane, CH<sub>4</sub>

![methane](../../../assets/2a3003e806a7-methane.png)

2. Ethane, C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub>

![ethane](../../../assets/21ec4d0a4ee6-ethane.png)

3. Propane, C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub>

![propane](../../../assets/625c09c3e966-propane.png)

### What are unsaturated compounds?

- **Unsaturated compounds** are molecules with one or more carbon-carbon **double bonds** (C=C)

  - Each carbon atom has 4 bonds to other atoms
  - But, one bond is a double bond
- **Alkenes** are examples of unsaturated compounds ** **
- Alkenes have the general formula **C**<sub>**n**</sub>**H**<sub>**2n**</sub>
- The first three alkenes are:

1. Ethene, C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>4</sub>

![ethene](../../../assets/c905b447320d-ethene.png)

2. Propene, C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>6</sub>

![propene](../../../assets/b9fdeaaf358a-propene.png)

3. Butene, C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>

![but-1-ene](../../../assets/3bd75c6173ba-but-1-ene.png)

> **Exam Hint**
> **Remember:**
> 
> **S**aturated compounds have **S**ingle bonds only.
> 
> **U**nsaturated compounds have do**U**ble bonds
