# Paper 1: Short Answer & Data Response

## What is examined in the CIE IGCSE Business Paper 1?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_FdknbzwCYTJqTPMY`

## What is examined in the CIE IGCSE Business Paper 1?

- This paper is made up of** four questions** requiring a mixture of** short** **answers **and **structured data responses**
- Each question starts with a **short case study**, to which you should refer when answer the question parts that follow
- The paper's duration is 90 minutes
- The total number of marks available is **80,** representing** 50%** **of the qualification**
- You must answer all questions

### Structure of the Paper

In **each **of the **four questions:**

- **Parts a **and **b** are worth 2 marks

  - You are usually required to either
  
    - **Define **a key term
    - Carry out a simple **calculation**
    - **State **or **identify **two factors, features, ways or impacts
  - Advice on answering 2-mark questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-2-mark-questions/)
- **Part c **is worth 4 marks

  - You are usually required to either
  
    - **Outline** two factors
    - Carry out a multi-stage **calculation**
    - **State **or **identify **four factors, factors, features, ways or impacts
  - Advice on answering 4-mark questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-4-mark-questions/)
- **Part d** is an 'explain' question worth 6 marks

  - You are required to explain either
  
    - Two advantages and two disadvantages of a given issue
    - Two reasons, ways or impacts of a given issue
  - Advice on answering 6-mark explain questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-6-mark-questions/)
- **Part e** is a 'justify' question worth 6 marks

  - You are required to either
  
    - Make a judgement about the significance of two or more factors
    - Make a recommendation about a suitable course of action
  - Advice on answering 6-mark justify questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-6-mark-questions/)
