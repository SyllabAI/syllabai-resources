# Paper 2: Case Study

## What is examined in the CIE IGCSE Business Paper 2?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_fMRyXFvWxJkDv7bj`

## What is examined in the CIE IGCSE Business Paper 2?

- This paper is made up of** four questions **based on a **case study, **provided as an** insert**
- The** case study** provides information about a business as well as a selection of data, such as market research, economic indicators or financial performance
- You should refer to this case study material in all of your answers
- The paper's duration is 90 minutes
- The total number of marks available is **80,** representing** 50%** **of the qualification**
- You must answer all questions

### Structure of the Paper

In **each **of the **four questions:**

- **Part a** is worth 8 marks

  - You are usually required to either
  
    - **Explain **two advantages and two disadvantages of a given issue
    - **Explain** four points related to a particular issue
    - Carry out a multi-part **calculation **and then **outline **two factors
  - Advice on answering 8-mark questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-8-mark-questions/)
- **Part b** is worth 12 marks

  - You are usually required to
  
    - Consider a range of factors and make a** justified recommendation**
    - Weigh up advantages and disadvantages and make a **justified choice**
    - Consider a business's position (financial, competitive etc) and make an **overall judgement**
    - Consider a range of impacts and make a** judgement about the most significant**
  - Advice on answering 12-mark questions can be found [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/business/cie/23/revision-notes/7-exam-technique/7-3-structuring-your-responses/answering-12-mark-questions/)
