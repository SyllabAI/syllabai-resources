# Part-Time Versus Full-Time

## Part-time or Full-time Contracts

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_6yP4PtWyg4kJKc93` · "• Benefits and limitations of part-time employees
and full-time employees"

## Part-time or full-time contracts

- Businesses can employ workers on a variety of employment contracts, including** part-time and full-time**

  - **Part-time** employment is often considered to be **between 1 and 30 hours a week**
  - **Full-time employees **will usually work** 35+ hours a week**
- **Flexible working **is the development of** **a culture where workers are able to work in a range of employment patterns (full-time, part-time, ***zero hours contracts***, work from home, etc.)

### Evaluating full-time contracts

- Hiring full-time workers is expensive but it generates significant benefits

#### The impact of full-time contracts on a business

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
|---|---|
| - Full-time contracts **encourage loyalty** to the business from employees<br>- Having a **stable and consistent workforce** reduces the need for frequent hiring<br>- Full-time employees are often more motivated, leading to higher levels of ***productivity*** | - Providing benefits and a steady salary to full-time employees is **more expensive than hiring part-time** or contract workers<br>- Full-time employees may be** less flexible with working hours** which can be a challenge in industries with fluctuating workloads<br>- If business demands fluctuate, having a full-time workforce **may lead to overstaffing **during slow periods |

### Evaluating part-time contracts

#### The impact of part-time contracts on a business

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
|---|---|
| - **Part-time contracts** can help businesses attract and retain staff who value work-life balance<br>- **Productivity may improve** as staff feel valued, having been given the option of working part-time<br>- Can **enhance workforce flexibility** as it may be easier to schedule employees to work at busy times<br>- **Low business costs** if the worker is only needed for part-time hours | - **Part-time contracts** can create challenges in terms of communication and collaboration<br>- **Monitoring and managing part-time workers **can be challenging<br><br>  - E.g. an employee may be absent from a weekly meeting regularly as it is their day off<br>- It may be **harder for part-time employees to be promoted** as they have less opportunity to gain the skills needed<br>- Part-time employees may be **less committed to the business** and may be more likely to leave to get another job |
