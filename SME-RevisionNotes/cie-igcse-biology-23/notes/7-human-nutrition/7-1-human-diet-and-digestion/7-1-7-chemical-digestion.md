# Chemical Digestion

## Chemical digestion

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_mngsmcDjzNGDv4zp` · Describe chemical digestion as the break down of large insoluble molecules into small soluble molecules
State the role of chemical digestion in producing small soluble molecules that can be absorbed

## Chemical digestion

- Chemical digestion is the **breakdown of large insoluble molecules** into **small soluble molecules**

  - E.g. starch (a large insoluble molecule) is broken down into simple sugars (small, soluble molecules) by the [enzyme amylase](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/7-human-nutrition/7-1-human-diet-and-digestion/7-1-8-enzymes-in-digestion/?_ai=internal-linking)
- Chemical digestion is required because large insoluble molecules are **unable to be absorbed** through the wall of the small intestine
- Small soluble molecules produced by chemical digestion are **easily absorbed** into the surrounding capillaries in the wall of the small intestine
- **Enzymes** are required for chemical digestion to take place
