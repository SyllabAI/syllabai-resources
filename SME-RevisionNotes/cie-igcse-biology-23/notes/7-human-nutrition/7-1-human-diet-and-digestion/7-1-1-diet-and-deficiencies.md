## Balanced diet

- A balanced diet consists of all of the food groups in the correct proportions
- The necessary food groups are:

  - **carbohydrates**
  - **proteins**
  - **lipids**
  - **vitamins**
  - **minerals**
  - **dietary fibre**
  - **water**

### Food groups table

| Food type | Function | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrate | Source of energy | Bread, cereals, pasta, rice, potatoes |
| Protein | Growth and repair | Meat, fish, eggs, pulses, nuts |
| Lipid | Insulation and energy storage | Butter, oils, nuts |
| Dietary fibre | Provides bulk (roughage) for the intestine to push food through it | Vegetables, whole grains |
| Vitamins | Needed in small quantities to maintain health | Fruits and vegetables |
| Minerals | Needed in small quantities to maintain health | Fruits and vegetables, meats, dairy products |
| Water | Needed for chemical reactions to take place in cells | Water, juice, milk, fruits and vegetables |

### Vitamin and mineral requirements table

| Vitamin / mineral | Function | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin C | Forms an essential part of **collagen** protein, which is found in **skin, hair, gums and bones** | Citrus fruit, strawberries, green vegetables |
| Vitamin D | Helps the body to absorb calcium (and so required for **strong bones and teeth**, see below) | Oily fish, eggs, liver, dairy products, also made naturally by the body in sunlight |
| Calcium | Needed for **strong bones  and teeth** and involved in **blood clotting**.<br><br>Deficiency can lead to **osteoporosis** later in life. | Milk, cheese, eggs |
| Iron | Needed to make **haemoglobin**, the pigment in red blood cells that binds oxygen to transport it around the body | Red meat, liver, leafy green vegetables (e.g. spinach) |

### Varying dietary needs of individuals table

| Factor | Dietary needs |
|---|---|
| Age | The amount of energy that young people need **increases towards adulthood as this energy is needed for growth**. Children need a higher proportion of protein in their diet compared to adults as this nutrient is required for **growth**.<br><br>Energy needs of adults **decrease** as they age. |
| Activity levels | The more active, the more energy required for movement as **muscles are respiring more to contracting more**. |
| Pregnancy | During pregnancy, energy requirements increase as energy is **needed to support the growth of the developing foetus**, as well as the increased mass that the mother needs to carry around. <br>Extra **calcium** and **iron** are also needed in the diet to help build the bones, teeth and blood of the foetus. |
| Breastfeeding | **Energy** requirements **increase** and extra **calcium** still needed to make high quality breast milk |

## Scurvy & rickets

### Scurvy

- Scurvy is the name for a severe **vitamin C deficiency**
- Scurvy is caused by a lack of vitamin C in the diet for over 3 months
- Symptoms of scurvy include:

  - anaemia
  - exhaustion
  - spontaneous bleeding
  - pain in the limbs
  - swelling
  - gum ulcerations
  - tooth loss
- Scurvy is a condition that was commonly seen in sailors between the 15th to 18th centuries

  - Long sea voyages made it very hard to access a ready supply of fresh produce rich in Vitamin C
- Scurvy can be treated with **oral or intravenous vitamin C supplements**

### Rickets

- Rickets is a condition in children characterised by **poor bone development**
- Symptoms of rickets include:

  - bone pain
  - lack of bone growth
  - soft, weak bones (sometimes causing deformities)
- Rickets can be caused by a severe **lack of vitamin D**

  - Vitamin D is required for the absorption of calcium into the body; calcium is a key component of bones and teeth

- The body can naturally produce a form of Vitamin D when a substance in the skin (derived from cholesterol) is exposed to UV radiation in sunlight
- Vitamin D can also be found in some foods e.g. fish, eggs and butter
- The treatment for rickets is to increase consumption of **foods containing calcium and vitamin D**
- Alternatively, vitamin D **supplements** can be prescribed
