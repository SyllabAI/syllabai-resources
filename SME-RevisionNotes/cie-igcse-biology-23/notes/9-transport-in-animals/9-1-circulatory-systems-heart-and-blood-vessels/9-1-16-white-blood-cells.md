## Lymphocytes & phagocytes: Extended

### Extended Tier Only

- White blood cells are part of the body’s **immune system**, defending against infection by pathogens
- There are two main types: **phagocytes and lymphocytes**

#### Phagocytes

- Phagocytes carry out **phagocytosis** by **engulfing and digesting pathogens**

![Diagram of a phagocyte engulfing and digesting bacterial pathogens, showing stages of phagocytosis, phagosome formation and enzyme-mediated destruction.](../../../assets/aab1b609ccf1-phagocytosis.png)

*Phagocytosis*

- Phagocytes have a sensitive cell surface membrane that can detect chemicals produced by pathogens
- Once phagocytes encounter a pathogen, they will engulf it and **release digestive enzymes** to digest it
- Phagocytes can be easily recognised under the microscope as cells with **multi-lobed nucleus** and **granular cytoplasm**

#### Lymphocytes

- Lymphocytes produce **antibodies** to destroy pathogens and **antitoxins** to neutralise toxins released by pathogens
- Lymphocytes can easily be recognised under the microscope as cells with a **large round nucleus** which takes up nearly the whole cell and **clear, non-granular cytoplasm**
