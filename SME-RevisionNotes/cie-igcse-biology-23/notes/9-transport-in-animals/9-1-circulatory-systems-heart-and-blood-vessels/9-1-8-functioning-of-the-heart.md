# Functioning of the Heart

## Functioning of the heart: Extended

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_cpfD34dXn3rcgdzC` · Describe the functioning of the heart in terms of the contraction of muscles of the atria and ventricles and the action of the valves

## Functioning of the heart: Extended

### Extended Tier Only

- **Deoxygenated blood** coming from the body flows into the **right atrium** via the **vena cava**
- Once the right atrium has filled with blood, it contracts, pushing the blood through the **tricuspid (atrioventricular) valve** into the **right ventricle**
- The walls of the ventricle **contract**, forcing the blood past the **semilunar valve** into the **pulmonary artery**; the valve then closes to prevent blood flowing backwards into the heart
- The blood travels to the lungs and moves through the capillaries past the alveoli where gas exchange takes place

  - The right side of the heart pumps blood at a lower pressure than the left, since it's going directly to capillaries in the lungs, which would burst under higher pressure
- **Oxygen-rich blood** returns to the **left atrium** via the **pulmonary vein**
- Once the left atrium is full, it contracts, pushing blood through the **bicuspid (atrioventricular) valve** into the **left ventricle**
- The thicker muscle walls of the ventricle contract strongly to push the blood forcefully into the **aorta** and all the way around the body
- The **semilunar valve** in the aorta prevents the blood flowing back down into the heart
