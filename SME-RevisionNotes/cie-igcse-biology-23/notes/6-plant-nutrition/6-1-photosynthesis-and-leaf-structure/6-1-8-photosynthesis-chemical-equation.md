# Photosynthesis Chemical Equation

## Balanced photosynthesis chemical equation: Extended

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_CQbQQ9Ysjbvfrbxg` · State the balanced chemical equation for photosynthesis as: CO + HO → CHO + O

## Balanced photosynthesis chemical equation: Extended

### Extended Tier Only

- The balanced chemical equation for photosynthesis is:

$6CO_{2}+6H_{2}O\overset{light}{\underset{chlorophyll}{\rightarrow}}C_{6}H_{12}O_{6}+6O_{2}$

- The **light energy** is converted into **chemical energy** in the **bonds** holding the atoms in the glucose molecules together

> **Exam Hint**
> The photosynthesis equation is the exact reverse of the aerobic respiration equation so if you have learned one you also know the other one! You will usually get more marks for providing the **balanced chemical equation** than the word equation.
