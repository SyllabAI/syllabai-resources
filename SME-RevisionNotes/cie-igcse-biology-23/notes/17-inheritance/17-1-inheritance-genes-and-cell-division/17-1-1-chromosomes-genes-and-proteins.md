## The structure of genes

### Inheritance of chromosomes, genes and DNA

- **Inheritance** is the **transmission of genetic information from generation to generation**
- **Chromosomes** are located in the **nucleus** of cells
- They are **thread-like structures of DNA, carrying genetic information in the form of genes**
- A **gene** is a short length of **DNA** found on a chromosome that **codes for a specific protein**
- This could be a structural protein such as collagen found in skin cells, an enzyme or a hormone
- Genes control our characteristics as they [code for proteins](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/17-inheritance/17-1-inheritance-genes-and-cell-division/17-1-4-protein-synthesis/?_ai=internal-linking) that play important roles in what our cells do

![Diagram showing DNA double helix forming a chromosome, labelled as a long DNA strand wrapped round proteins, inside a cell nucleus that contains chromosomes](../../../assets/01dcc180ad74-dna-genes-and-chromosomes.png)

* Genes are short lengths of DNA that code for a protein; genes are found on chromosomes*

- Alleles are **variations** of the same gene
- Humans have two copies of each chromosome (one from each parent), so they have **two copies of each gene** - and therefore **two alleles for each gene**
- One of the alleles for each gene is inherited from the mother and the other from the father
- The two alleles code for the same characteristic, but may code for different versions of it
- For example, an individual has two copies of a gene for eye colour, but **one allele could code for brown eyes and one allele could code for blue eyes**
