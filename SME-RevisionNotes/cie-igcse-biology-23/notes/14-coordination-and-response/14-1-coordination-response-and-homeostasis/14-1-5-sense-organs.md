# Sense Organs

## Sense organs as receptors

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_jf6F9Zd2YqQXzpZK` · Describe sense organs as groups of receptor cells responding to specific stimuli: light, sound, touch, temperature and chemicals

## Sense organs as receptors

- Receptors are groups of specialised cells
- They **detect a change in the environment and stimulate electrical impulses in response**
- Sense organs contain **groups of receptors** that respond to specific stimuli

| Sense organ | What it is sensitive to | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | Sensitive to pressure, heat and cold (temperature) and pain | Touch and temperature |
| Tongue | Sensitive to chemicals in food and drink | Taste |
| Nose | Sensitive to chemicals in the air | Smell |
| Ear | Sensitive to sound and movement | - Hearing<br>- Balance |
| Eye | Sensitive to light | Sight |

- Once the receptor cell in the sense organ has been stimulated, it generates an **electrical impulse**
- This is passed on to a [sensory neurone ](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/14-coordination-and-response/14-1-coordination-response-and-homeostasis/14-1-2-types-of-neurones/?_ai=internal-linking)which carries the impulse to the [central nervous system](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/14-coordination-and-response/14-1-coordination-response-and-homeostasis/14-1-1-mammalian-nervous-system/?_ai=internal-linking)
- Here a response will be decided on and the impulse will be passed to a **motor neurone** (via a[ relay neurone](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/14-coordination-and-response/14-1-coordination-response-and-homeostasis/14-1-3-the-reflex-arc/?_ai=internal-linking))
- The motor neurone carries the impulse to the **effector** (muscle or gland)
- The effector **carries out the response**
