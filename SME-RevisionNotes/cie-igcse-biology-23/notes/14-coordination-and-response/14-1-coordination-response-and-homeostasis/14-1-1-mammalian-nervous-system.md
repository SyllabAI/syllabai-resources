(b) the peripheral nervous system (PNS) consisting of the nerves outside of the brain and spinal cord
Describe the role of the nervous system as coordination and regulation of body functions

## Structure & role of the mammalian nervous system

- The **human nervous system** consists of the:

  - **central nervous system** (CNS) - the brain and the spinal cord
  - **peripheral nervous system** (PNS) - all of the nerves in the body
- The nervous system allows the body to:

  - make sense of our surroundings and respond to them
  - **coordinate** and **regulate body functions**
- Information is sent through the nervous system as **nerve impulses** - electrical signals that pass along nerve cells known as [neurones](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/biology/cie/23/revision-notes/14-coordination-and-response/14-1-coordination-response-and-homeostasis/14-1-2-types-of-neurones/?_ai=internal-linking)
- A bundle of neurones is known as a **nerve**
