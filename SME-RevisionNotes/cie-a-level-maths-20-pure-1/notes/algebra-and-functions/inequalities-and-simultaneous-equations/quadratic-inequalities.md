## Quadratic inequalities

### What is a quadratic inequality?

- Similar to quadratic equations
- Sketching a quadratic graph is essential
- Can involve the **discriminant** or **applications** in **mechanics** and **statistics** ![2.4.2 Quadratic Inequalities Notes Diagram 1, Edexcel A Level Maths: Pure revision notes](assets/c6826b8399d3-2-4-2-quadratic-inequalities-notes-diagram-1.png)

### How do I solve quadratic inequalities?

- **STEP 1**: **Rearrange** the inequality into quadratic form with a **positive squared term**

  - ***ax***<sup>**2**</sup>** + *****bx***** + *****c***** > 0** (>, <, ≤ or ≥)
- **STEP 2**: Find the **roots** of the quadratic equation

  - **Solve** *ax*<sup>2</sup> + *bx* + *c *= 0 to get ***x***<sub>**1**</sub>* *and* ****x***<sub>**2 **</sub>where** *****x***<sub>**1**</sub>** < *****x***<sub>**2**</sub>
- **STEP 3**: **Sketch** a graph of the quadratic and **label the roots**

  - As the squared term is positive it will be **"U" shaped**
- **STEP 4**: **Identify** the **region** that satisfies the inequality

  - For ***ax***<sup>**2**</sup>** + *****bx***** + *****c***** > 0 **you want the region **above** the *x*-axis
  
    - The solution is ***x *****< *****x***<sub>**1**</sub> **or** ***x *****> *****x***<sub>**2**</sub>** **
  - For ***ax***<sup>**2**</sup>** + *****bx***** + *****c***** < 0 **you want the region below the *x*-axis
  
    - The solution is ***x >***** *****x***<sub>**1**</sub> **and** ***x *****< *****x***<sub>**2**</sub>**  **
    - This is more commonly written as ***x***<sub>**1**</sub>** < *****x***** < *****x***<sub>**2**</sub>
- Be careful:

  - **avoid** multiplying or dividing by a negative number if unavoidable, “**flip**” the inequality sign so **<** → **>**, **≥** → **≤**, etc
  - **avoid** multiplying or dividing by a **variable** (**x**) that **could** be negative (multiplying or dividing by **x**<sup>**2**</sup> guarantees positivity (unless **x** could be 0) but this can create extra, invalid solutions)
  - **do** rearrange to make the x<sup>2</sup> term positive
