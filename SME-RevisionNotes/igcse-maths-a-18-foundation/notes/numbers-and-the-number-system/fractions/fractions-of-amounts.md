## Fractions of amounts

### What is a fraction?

- A fraction is written as $\frac{a}{b}$ where *a* and *b* are** *****integers***

  - The number on the top, *a*, is called the **numerator**
  - The number on the bottom, *b*, is called the **denominator**
- The **values** of the numerator and denominator tell us about the** size** of the fraction** **

  - The denominator is how many **parts** each whole number is split into
  - The numerator is how many** parts** you **have**
  
    - For example, $\frac{2}{3}$ means that each whole number  is divided into 3 parts and you have 2 of these parts
- A fraction is **less **than 1 if the numerator is **smaller **than the denominator
- A fraction is **more **than 1 if the number is **bigger **than the denominator

### How do I shade a fraction on a diagram?

- **Divide** by the **denominator** and **multiply** by the **numerator**

  - To shade $\frac{3}{5}$ of a grid of 20 squares
  
    - **Divide** 20 squares by 5 to get 4 squares
    - **Multiply** this amount by 3 to get 12 squares
    - **Shade** 12 squares
  - To shade $\frac{1}{4}$ of a circle
  
    - **Divide** the circle into four quarters by drawing a horizontal and a vertical line through the centre
    - **Shade** one of these quarters

### How do I find a fraction of an amount?

- **Method 1**
**Divide** by the **denominator** and **multiply** by the **numerator**

  - To find $\frac{1}{3}$ of $90$, divide it by 3
  
    - $90\div3=30$
  - To find $\frac{2}{5}$ of $60$, divide it by 5 then multiply it by 2
  
    - $60\div5=12$, then $12\times2=24$
- **Method 2**
**Multiply **the  amount by the fraction

  - To find $\frac{9}{10}$ of $15$, multiply them together
  
    - $\frac{9}{10}\times15=13.5$
