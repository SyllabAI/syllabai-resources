## Tally charts & frequency tables

### What is a tally chart?

- **Tally charts** are a quick way to** count **data
- Each time you count an item, draw a vertical line

  - Do this the **first four** times
  - The **fifth time**, draw a **diagonal line** through the four vertical lines $\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert}$
  - Continue counting in **sets of five**
- For example

  - Counting different coloured flowers in the garden

| Colour | Tally |
|---|---|
| Blue | $\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert}$ |
| Red | $\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \vert \vert$ |
| Yellow | $\vert \vert \vert$ |

### What is a frequency table?

- A **frequency** table counts **how many times** an item appears
- **Tally charts **can be converted** **into frequency tables

  - Find the** totals**
  
    - Count up in 5's then add any extras
    - $\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \vert \vert$means 5 + 5 + 2 = 12
- The tally chart above gives the following frequency table:

| Colour | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Blue | 5 |
| Red | 12 |
| Yellow | 3 |

> **Worked Example**
> Susie asks pupils in her class to select their favourite type of pet.
> 
> Write the results in a frequency table.
> 
> | Dog | Cat | Dog | Dog | Dog |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Rabbit | Dog | Rabbit | Cat | Cat |
> | Cat | Dog | Dog | Rabbit | Dog |
> | Cat | Cat | Dog | Dog | Dog |
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> > *It helps to use a tally chart initially*
> 
> | ***Type of pet*** | ***Tally*** |
> |---|---|
> | *Dog* | * *$\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \vert \cancel{\vert \vert \vert} \vert$ |
> | *Cat* | * *$\cancel{\vert \vert \vert \vert} \vert$ |
> | *Rabbit* | * *$\vert \vert \vert$ |
> 
> *Then write the tally chart as a frequency table*
> 
> | **Final answer:** **Type of pet** | **Final answer:** **Frequency** |
> |---|---|
> | **Final answer:** **Dog** | **Final answer:** **11** |
> | **Final answer:** **Cat** | **Final answer:** **6** |
> | **Final answer:** **Rabbit** | **Final answer:** **3** |
