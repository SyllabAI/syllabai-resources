## Homologous series

**Extended tier only**

- A homologous series is a family of organic compounds with **similar chemical properties**
- This is because all members of a homologous series contain the **same functional group**

  - The functional group is a group of atoms bonded in a specific arrangement that is responsible for the characteristic reactions of the series

#### Table of compounds & their functional groups

| **Family** | **Functional Group** | **Name ends in...** |
|---|---|---|
| Alkane | C-C | -ane |
| Alkene | C=C | -ene |
| Alcohol | -OH | -ol |

> **Exam Hint**
> Make sure you can identify the functional group for each homologous series.

### General characteristics of a homologous series

- All members of a homologous series have the **same general formula**

  - This means that each member differs from the next by a CH<sub>2</sub> unit
- They also show a **trend in physical properties**

  - For example, as the molecules get larger:
  
    - Boiling point increases
    - Melting point increases
    - Viscosity increases

> **Exam Hint**
> Be careful with your language for homologous series:
> 
> - **SAME**
> 
>   - The same functional group
>   - The same general formula
> - **SIMILAR**
> 
>   - Similar chemical properties, e.g. similar types of reactions
> - **TREND**
> 
>   - A trend in physical properties, e.g. melting point
