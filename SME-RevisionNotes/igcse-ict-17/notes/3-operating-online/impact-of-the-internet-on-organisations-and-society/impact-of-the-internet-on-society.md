> **Spec point** — `spcpt_WCq7SvFQNpwgFHDy`

## Less-restricted networks

### What is the impact of less-restricted networks?

- The impact of less-restricted networks is evident in mass communication (**media**) such as:

  - **Broadcasting**
  - **Publishing**
  - **The internet**

| Impact | Impacts |
|---|---|
| **Broadcasting a message** | - Before the internet, an individual would need to persuade a media outlet to broadcast their message<br>- It was difficult to get a message to the masses<br>- Social media and video streaming have given society a platform to share their own messages to a global audience |
| **Online content** | - Controlled by moderators<br>- Service owners control content can be published<br>- Algorithms determine what content watchers see based upon browsing history & online activity<br>- Paid promotion will be seen by more users than individuals that don't pay |
| **User generated reference sites** | - Sites such as Wikipedia have removed the element of fact checking by experts or editors<br>- Users can contribute content that may or may not be true |
| **Law enforcement** | **Negative**<br><br>- Use of social media and video messaging services to organise illegal activities whilst avoiding police<br><br>**Positive**<br><br>- Use of a network of CCTV cameras to record illegal actions<br>- Footage captured on social media to help identify suspects<br>- Social media used to organise events to support victims |

## Information rich & information poor

### What is the information gap?

- The information gap refers to the **divide created between people** who have access to online information, and those that do not

| Information rich | Information poor |
|---|---|
| Good access to information, this improves education and helps enable them to make decisions | Reduced access to information, this decreases education and reduces their ability to make decisions |

- The gap has been there since television and radio were introduced
- The internet has further widened the gap
