## Transactional data

### What is transactional data?

- Transactional data is **data that is sent between two connected digital devices** on the internet
- This data is **stored in cookies**
- Cookies are** information left on a users device** so that a website can **recognise the user **when they visit again
- **Encryption **is used when transmitting cookies
- Data stored in a cookie includes:

  - **Time & date of the transaction**
  - **IP address**
  - **Data on products or services that a user has previously viewed/purchased**
  - **Items added to a shopping cart**
  - **Buttons pressed**
  - **Data entered in to web forms **
- There are** three types** of cookies

| Type | Features | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Session cookies** | - Temporary - lost when you close the browser<br>- Considered less of a privacy concern | - Keeping track of shopping carts<br>- Remembering login status<br>- Form inputs |
| **Persistent cookies** | - Stored on a local storage device<br>- Have an expiration date set by website (days to years) | - Remembering login details for future visits<br>- Tracking browsing history<br>- Delivery of targeted advertising |
| **Third-party cookies** | - Do not belong to the website visited<br>- Owned by third-parties that provide services to the website<br>- Examples include:<br>- Social networking sites<br>- Advertising networks<br>- Considered a bigger privacy concern as create a much large profile of a user | - Allowing external companies to track activity<br>- Used to target user with ads or personalise website experience |

#### Targeted marketing

- Targeted marketing is when** third-party cookie data is used to match people** based on **browsing history** or** personal attributes**
- When a user visits a website that uses an *ad server*, the adverts will be **specific to the users third-party cookie data**
