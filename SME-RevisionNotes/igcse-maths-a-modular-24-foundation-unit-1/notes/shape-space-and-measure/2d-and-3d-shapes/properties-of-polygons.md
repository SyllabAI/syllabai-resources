> **Spec point** — `spcpt_CBHJZtkzvkpJJPwX`

## Properties of polygons

### What are the names of common 2D shapes?

- You should know the general names of all the 2D **polygons**

  - A **triangle** has 3 sides
  - A **quadrilateral** has 4 sides
  - A **pentagon** has 5 sides
  - A **hexagon** has 6 sides
  - A **heptagon** has 7 sides
  - An **octagon **has 8 sides
  - A **nonagon** has 9 sides
  - A **decagon **has 10 sides
  - A **polygon **is a flat (plane) shape with *n* straight sides
  
    - A **regular **polygon has all sides the same length and all angles the same size**  **

![Names of shapes with 3 to 10 sides](../../../assets/1b97bafbceb9-54801-properties-of-polygons-1.png)

*Polygons*

### What are the names of the different types of triangles?

- You should know the names and properties of the different types of **triangles**

  - An **equilateral** triangle has **3 equal sides** and **3 equal angles**
  - An **isosceles **triangle has **2 equal sides** and **2 equal angles**
  - A **right-angled **triangle has **one 90° angle**
  - A **scalene **triangle has **3 sides all of different lengths**

![Types of triangles: Equilateral with equal sides and angles; Isosceles with two equal sides and angles; Scalene with no equal sides; Right-angled with 90° angle.](../../../assets/4c212626474d-3505-angles-in-triangles.png)

### What are the names of the different types of quadrilaterals?

- You should know the names and properties of the different types of **quadrilaterals**

  - These are **squares**, **rectangles**, **parallelograms**, **rhombuses**, **trapeziums** and **kites**

![types of quadrilaterals](../../../assets/2e9f8b9e4ee0-18897-properties-of-polygons-3.png)

### What are the properties of rectangles and squares?

- A **square **is a **rectangle **where all the **sides **have the **same length**
- The table below shows the **properties **of a rectangle and square

![Properties of a rectangle](../../../assets/d0984b27f0b4-53426-properties-of-polygons-4.png)

*Rectangle*

|   | Rectangle | Square |
|---|---|---|
| Sides | - Two pairs of equal, opposite sides<br>- Two pairs of parallel sides | - All sides have the same length<br>- Two pairs of parallel sides |
| Angles | - All angles are 90° | - All angles are 90° |
| Diagonals | - Both diagonals bisect each other<br>- Diagonals have the same length | - Both diagonals bisect each other<br>- Diagonals have the same length<br>- Diagonals are perpendicular |
| Symmetries | - Two lines of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is two | - Four lines of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is four |

### What are the properties of parallelograms and rhombuses?

- A **rhombus **is a **parallelogram **where all the **sides **have the **same length**
- The table below shows the **properties **of a parallelogram and rhombus

![Properties of a parallelogram.](../../../assets/01508e5efcdb-60048-properties-of-polygons-5.png)

*Parallelogram*

|   | Parallelogram | Rhombus |
|---|---|---|
| Sides | - Two pairs of equal, opposite sides<br>- Two pairs of parallel sides | - All sides have the same length<br>- Two pairs of parallel sides |
| Angles | - Two pairs of equal, opposite angles<br>- Each pair of adjacent angles add up 180° | - Two pairs of equal, opposite angles<br>- Each pair of adjacent angles add up 180° |
| Diagonals | - Both diagonals bisect each other | - Both diagonals bisect each other<br>- Diagonals are perpendicular |
| Symmetries | - No lines of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is two | - Two lines of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is two |

### What are the properties of trapeziums?

- The table below shows the **properties **of a trapezium and an isosceles trapezium

![Diagram comparing a trapezium and isosceles trapezium, highlighting equal angles with colours and marked parallel sides, both with dashed diagonals.](../../../assets/dcd23ac8b161-2788-properties-of-polygons-6.png)

|   | Trapezium | Isosceles trapezium |
|---|---|---|
| Sides | - One pair of parallel sides<br>- The parallel sides have different lengths | - One pair of parallel sides<br>- The parallel sides have different lengths<br>- The non-parallel sides have the same length |
| Angles | - Two pairs of adjacent angles that add up to  180° | - Two pairs of adjacent angles that add up to  180°<br>- Two pairs of adjacent angles that are equal |
| Diagonals | - Do not bisect each other | - Do not bisect each other<br>- Diagonals have the same length |
| Symmetries | - No lines of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is one | - One line of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is one |

### What are the properties of kites?

- The table below shows the **properties **of a kite

![Geometric diagram of a kite shape with vertices labelled A, B, C, D and intersecting diagonals at point O. All sides are marked equal in pairs.](../../../assets/949e78e5d795-61702-properties-of-polygons-7.png)

*Kite*

|   | Kite |
|---|---|
| Sides | - Two pairs of equal, adjacent sides<br>- No parallel sides |
| Angles | - One pair of equal, opposite angles |
| Diagonals | - One diagonal bisects the other<br>- Diagonals are perpendicular |
| Symmetries | - One line of symmetry<br>- Order of rotational symmetry is one |
