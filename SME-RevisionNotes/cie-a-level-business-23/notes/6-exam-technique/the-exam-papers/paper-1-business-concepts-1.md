# Paper 1: Business Concepts 1

## What is examined in the CIE A level Business Paper 1?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_D3xT2C5t3BwcxZBd`

## What is examined in the CIE A-level business Paper 1?

- This paper is focused on AS Level syllabus content and is called **Business Concepts 1**
- The paper's duration is **one hour and fifteen minutes**
- The total number of marks available is **40**
- Paper 1 represents **20%** **of the qualification total**
- It is made up of** two sections**

### Section A

- Section A contains **four compulsory questions** of five marks each
- You must answer all questions
- The first three questions have two parts that can include

  - Define questions, worth 2 marks
  - Explain questions, worth 3 marks
- The fourth is an analyse question, worth 5 marks
- Section A **does not include a business context**, but the question stem may refer to a particular type of business, industry or business circumstance

### Section B

- Section B contains two questions of 20 marks - you will **answer** **one **question
- These are essay questions and have two parts

  - An analysis question, worth 8 marks
  - An evaluation question, worth 12 marks
- Section A **does not include a business context either**, but the question stem may refer to a particular type of business, industry or business circumstance, or may provide a quotation to which you must respond
