## What is examined in the CIE A level business Paper 2?

- This paper is focused on AS Level syllabus content and is called **Business Concepts 2**

  - The paper's duration is **one hour and thirty minutes**
  - The total number of marks available is **60**
  - It represents **30%** **of the qualification total**
- Paper 1 is made up of** two six-part data response questions**

  - You must answer both questions
- Questions can include

  - Identify questions, worth 1 mark
  - Explain questions, worth 3 marks
  - Calculate questions, worth 3 marks
  - An analysis question, worth 8 marks
  - An evaluation question, worth 12 marks
- All questions are **based on unseen case studies**, which provide data and information on small- to medium-sized businesses
