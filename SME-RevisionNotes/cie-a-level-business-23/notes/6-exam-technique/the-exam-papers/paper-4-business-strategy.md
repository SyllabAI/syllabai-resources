# Paper 4: Business Strategy

## What is examined in the CIE A level Business Paper 4?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_PphZx3R5HDjdqc3s`

## What is examined in the CIE A level Business Paper 4?

- This paper is focused on **A-Level syllabus content** and is called **'Business Strategy'**

  - Knowledge of material from the AS Level subject content is assumed
  
    - The paper’s duration is **one hour 15 minutes**
    - The total number of marks available is **100**
    - It represents **50% of the A Level** (and **25% of the overall qualification**)
- Paper 4 is based on **one strategic case study**
- The paper is divided into **two 20-mark compulsory essay questions**, based on an** unseen case study**

  - In both of the questions you must assess strategic options and recommend the **most appropriate strategy**
- The questions focus on **strategic decision-making** in **medium- to large-sized businesses**
- You are expected to apply **strategic models** (such as SWOT, Ansoff, Porter, investment appraisal, or organisational strategy) directly to the case study context

### Content examined in this paper

| Section | AS-level topics | A-level topics |
|---|---|---|
| **Business and its environment** | - Enterprise<br>- Business structure<br>- Size of business<br>- Business objectives<br>- Stakeholders in a business | - External influences on business activity<br>- Business strategy |
| **Human resource management** | - Human resource management<br>- Motivation<br>- Management | - Organisational structure<br>- Business communication<br>- Leadership<br>- Human resource management strategy |
| **Marketing** | - The nature of marketing<br>- Market research<br>- The marketing mix | - Marketing analysis<br>- Marketing strategy |
| **Operations management** | - The nature of operations<br>- Inventory management<br>- Capacity utilisation and outsourcing | - Location and scale<br>- Quality management<br>- Operations strategy |
| **Finance and accounting** | - Business finance<br>- Sources of finance<br>- Forecasting and managing cash flows<br>- Costs<br>- Budgets | - Financial statements<br>- Analysis of published accounts<br>- Investment appraisal<br>- Finance and accounting strategy |
