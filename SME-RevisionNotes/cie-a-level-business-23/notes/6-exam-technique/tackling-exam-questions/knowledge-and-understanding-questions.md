# Knowledge & Understanding Questions

## What is knowledge and understanding?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_wgdrKp4QzfhqcyZn`

## What are knowledge and understanding?

- Knowledge and understanding involves recognising and effectively using key:

  - **business terms**
  - **business concepts**
  - **formulas**
  - **business theories**, **methods **and **models**
- Knowledge can also be demonstrated by **making relevant points**, such as stating a valid feature, benefit, drawback or implication

### Marks for knowledge in exam papers

| Paper 1 | Paper 2 | Paper 3 | Paper 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35% | 30% | 20% | 15% |

- Overall, **25% of the total marks available **are awarded for knowledge and understanding across all four exam papers

## Knowledge and understanding questions

### Define questions

- **Define **requires you to give a precise meaning of a business term

  - Define questions are awarded 2 marks
- In this example, two marks are awarded for **clear understanding** and** valid examples **of secondary research
