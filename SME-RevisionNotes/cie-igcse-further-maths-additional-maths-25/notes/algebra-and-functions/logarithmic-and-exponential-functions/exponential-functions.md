## Exponential functions

### What is an exponential function?

- An exponential functions in a function where the variable is the power
- They are of the form **y = a**<sup>**x**</sup> with **a > 0**

### What is an exponential graph?

- All graphs of the form **y = a**<sup>**x**</sup> will pass through (0, 1) because **a**<sup>**0**</sup>** = 1**
- The **x**-axis is an **asymptote** ![Exponential curves](assets/f3129cff5153-6-1-1-exponential-functions-fig1.png)

### Exponential graphs when a > 1

- Where **x < 0** the higher value of **a** is the “lower” graph
- Where **x > 0** the higher value of **a** is the “higher” graph
- **a > 1** is **exponential** **growth **

![The graph y=3^x increases faster then the graph y=2^x](../../../assets/6a9b897d6ece-6-1-1-exponential-functions-fig3.png)

### What about when a ≤ 1?

- You may like to think about why** a = 1** is **not** considered... If a = 1, y = 1<sup>x</sup> = 1 for all values of x
- **0 < a < 1** represents **exponential decay** ![The graphs y=a^x decrease if a is between 0 and 1](assets/117edd01025f-6-1-1-notes-fig4.png)

  - Where **x < 0** the higher value of **a** is the “higher” graph
  - Where **x > 0** the higher value of **a** is the “lower” graph

## "e"

### What is e, the exponential function?

- The exponential function is **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup>

  - **e** is an *irrational* number
  - **e ≈ 2.718**
- As with other exponential graphs **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> ![The graph of y=e^x in comparison to y=3^x and y=2^x](assets/31a3bbcb7f0f-6-1-3-e-notes-fig1.png)

  - passes through (0, 1)
  - has the x-axis as an asymptote

### What is the big deal with e?

- **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> has the particular property

$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{x}$

- i.e. for *every* real number **x**, the gradient of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> is also equal to **e**<sup>**x **</sup>(see Differentiating e^x and lnx)

![The graph of y=e^x and some of its tangents](../../../assets/83e9ac29519c-6-1-3-e-notes-fig2.png)

![Some values of y=e^x and the gradients at the corresponding points](../../../assets/3df93ed751ea-6-1-3-e-notes-fig3.png)

### The negative exponential graph

- **y = e**<sup>**-x**</sup> is a reflection in the **y**-axis of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> ![y=^x and y=e^-x are reflections in the y-axis](assets/484361008669-6-1-3-e-notes-fig4.png)

** **

### What is exponential growth and decay?

![Exponential growth and decay](../../../assets/54c3253d089f-6-1-3-e-notes-fig5.png)

** **

- **y = Ae**<sup>**kx**</sup> (**k > 0**) is exponential growth
- **y = Ae**<sup>**-kx**</sup> (**k > 0**) is exponential decay
- **A** is the initial value
- **k** is a (usually positive) constant
- A** negative sign** is used in the equation making clear whether it is growth or decay
