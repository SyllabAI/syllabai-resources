## Tangents to circles

### What is a tangent to a circle?

- A **tangent** (to a circle) is a line that **touches** the circle at a **single point** but does *not* cut across the circle ![Lines which are and are not tangents to a circle](assets/3d9f816b3be1-3-2-5-tangent.png)
- A **tangent** to a circle is **perpendicular** to the **radius** of the circle at the point of intersection

![The radius from the centre to a point is perpendicular to the tangent at that point](../../../assets/8cbb54792b72-3-2-5-tangent-radius.png)

### How can I find the equation of the tangent line to a circle at a given point?

![Finding the equation of a tangent to a circle](../../../assets/cfbbd7406120-3-2-5-tangent-radius-illustr-1.png)

- **STEP 1** Find the **gradient** of the **radius *****OP***

![Formula for the gradient of the radius](../../../assets/d8752d6e1442-3-2-5-tangent-radius-illustr-2.png)

- **STEP 2** Find the **gradient** of the **tangent **As they are perpendicular, if the gradient of the radius is $m_{r}$, then, the gradient of the tangent is $m_{t}=-\frac{1}{m_{r}}$** **(Negative reciprocal) Alternatively,

![Formula for the gradient of a tangent](../../../assets/b385f5f84036-3-2-5-tangent-radius-illustr-3.png)

- **STEP 3** The **equation** of the **tangent** is the equation of the line with that **gradient** that goes through point ***P (x***<sub>***2***</sub>***, y***<sub>***2***</sub>***) ***Write down the equation using `y−y_ 2=m_ t (x−x_ 2)`where $m_{t}$is the gradient

> **Exam Hint**
> - If you understand the formula in Step 2 above
> 
> Gradient of tangent $=-\frac{x_{2}-x_{1}}{y_{2}-y_{1}}$
> 
> you can find the gradient of the tangent directly without having to find the gradient of the radius first.
