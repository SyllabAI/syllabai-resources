## Squared & cubic units

### How do I convert between squared units (areas)?

- You need to **square** the **unit conversion** rates

  - E.g., 1 cm<sup>2</sup> = 10<sup>2</sup> mm<sup>2</sup> = 100 mm<sup>2</sup>
  - This is because area is 2D
  - The fact the units have a 'squared' on them will help you remember
- It can help to **imagine a square**

  - E.g. 1 m<sup>2</sup> is a square measuring 1 m × 1 m
  - In cm this would be 100 cm × 100 cm
  - So 1 m<sup>2</sup> is equivalent to 10 000 cm<sup>2</sup>
- The **basic conversions** for **area **are

  - 1 cm<sup>2</sup> = 100 mm<sup>2</sup>
  - 1 m<sup>2</sup> = 10 000 cm<sup>2</sup>
  - 1 km<sup>2</sup> = 1 000 000 m<sup>2</sup>
- You may be told conversions for other units in a question, such as

  - 1 hectare (ha) = 10 000 m<sup>2</sup>

### How do I convert between cubed units (volume)?

- You need to** cube** the **unit conversion** rates

  - E.g. 1 cm<sup>3</sup><sub> </sub>= 10<sup>3 </sup>mm<sup>3</sup> = 1000 mm<sup>3</sup>
  - This is because volume is 3D
  - The fact the units have a “cubed” on them will help you remember
- It can help to **imagine a cube**

  - E.g. 1 m<sup>3</sup> is a cube measuring 1 m × 1 m × 1 m
  - In cm this would be 100 cm × 100 cm × 100 cm
  - So 1 m<sup>3</sup> is equivalent to 1 000 000 cm<sup>3</sup>
- The **basic conversions **for volume are

  - 1 cm<sup>3</sup><sub> </sub>= 1000 mm<sup>3</sup>
  - 1 m<sup>3</sup><sub> </sub>= 1 000 000 cm<sup>3</sup>
  - 1 km<sup>3</sup><sub> </sub>= 1 000 000 000 m<sup>3</sup>

> **Worked Example**
> Convert
> 
> (a) 8254 mm<sup>2 </sup>to cm<sup>2</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> > *1 cm = 10 mm*
> 
> > *1 cm*<sup>*2*</sup>* = 10*<sup>*2*</sup>* mm*<sup>*2*</sup>* = 100 mm*<sup>*2*</sup>
> 
> *8254 mm**<sup>2</sup>** = (8254 ÷ 100) cm**<sup>2</sup>** *
> 
> **Final answer:** **82.54 cm**<sup>**2**</sup>**  **
> 
> (b) 2.54 m<sup>3</sup> to cm<sup>3</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> > *1 m = 100 cm*
> *1 m*<sup>*3*</sup>* = 100*<sup>*3*</sup>* cm*<sup>*3*</sup>* = 1 000 000 cm*<sup>*3*</sup>
> 
> *2.54 m**<sup>3</sup>** = (2.54 × 1 000 000) cm**<sup>3</sup>** *
> 
> **Final answer:** **2 540 000 cm**<sup>**3**</sup>
