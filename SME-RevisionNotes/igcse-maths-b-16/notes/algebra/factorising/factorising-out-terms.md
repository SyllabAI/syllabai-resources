# Factorising Out Terms

## Basic Factorising

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_qX5rMkysQfN7SK2M`

## Basic factorising

### What is factorisation?

- A **factorised **expression is one written as the **product** (**multiplication**) of two, or more, terms (**factors**)

  - 3(*x*  + 2) is factorised
  
    - It is 3 × (*x*  + 2)
  - 3*x*  + 6* * is **not** factorised
  - 3*xy * is factorised
  
    - It is 3 × *x*  × *y*
  - Numbers can also be factorised
  
    - 12 = 2 x 2 x 3
- In algebra, factorisation is the **reverse** of **expanding brackets**

  - It's putting it into brackets, rather than removing brackets

### How do I factorise two terms?

- To factorise 12*x*<sup>2</sup> + 18*x *

  - Find the highest common factor of the **number **parts
  
    - 6
  - Find the highest common factor of the **algebra** parts
  
    - *x*
  - Multiply both to get the overall **highest common factor**
  
    - 6*x*
  - 12*x*<sup>2</sup> + 18*x * is the same as 6*x * × 2*x*  + 6*x*  × 3
  
    - Using the highest common factor
  - **Take out** the highest common factor
  
    - Write it **outside** a set of **brackets**
    - Put the remaining terms, 2*x * + 3,  inside the brackets
  - This gives the answer
  
    - 6*x *(2*x*  + 3)
- To factorise an expression containing multiple variables, e.g. 2*a*<sup>3</sup>*b* - 4*a*<sup>*2*</sup>*b*<sup>2</sup>

  - Use the same approach as above
  - Find the highest common factor of the **number **parts
  
    - 2
  - Find the highest common factor of the **algebra** parts
  
    - *a* and *b* appear in both terms
    - The highest common factor of *a*<sup>3</sup> and *a*<sup>2</sup> is *a*<sup>2</sup>
    - The highest common factor of *b* and *b*<sup>2</sup> is *b*
    - *a*<sup>*2*</sup>*b*
  - Multiply both to get the overall **highest common factor**
  
    - 2*a*<sup>2</sup>*b*
  - 2*a*<sup>3</sup>*b* - 4*a*<sup>*2*</sup>*b*<sup>2</sup>* * is the same as 2*a*<sup>2</sup>*b * × a  -  2*a*<sup>2</sup>*b*  × 2*b*
  
    - Using the highest common factor
  - **Take out** the highest common factor
  
    - Write it **outside** a set of **brackets**
    - Put the remaining terms, *a - 2b*,  inside the brackets
  - This gives the answer
  
    - 2*a*<sup>2</sup>*b *(*a* - 2*b*)

> **Exam Hint**
> In the exam, check that your factorisation is correct by **expanding **the brackets.
> 
> **Factorise** means factorise **fully.**
> 
> *x *(6*x  *+ 10) is not fully factorised but 2*x *(3*x *+ 5) is.

> **Worked Example**
> (a) Factorise 5*x* + 15
> **Answer:**
> 
> *Find the highest common factor of 5 and 15*
> 
> *5*
> 
> > *There is no **x** in the second term, so no highest common factor in **x**  is needed*
> *Think of each term as 5 × something*
> 
> *5 × ***x*** + 5 × 3*
> 
> > *Take out the 5 and put **x ** + 3 in brackets*
> 
> **Final answer:** **5(****x **** + 3)**
> 
> (b) Factorise fully 30*x*<sup>2</sup> - 24*x*
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> *Find the highest common factor of 30 and 24*
> 
> *6*
> 
> > *Find the highest common factor of **x*<sup>*2*</sup>*  and **x*
> 
> **x**
> 
> > *Find the overall highest common factor by multiplying these together*
> 
> *6***x**
> 
> > *Think of each term as 6**x**  × something*
> 
> *6***x***  × 5***x***  - 6***x***  × 4*
> 
> > *Take out the 6**x ** and put 5**x**  - 4 in brackets*
> 
> **Final answer:** **6****x ****(5****x **** - 4)**
> 
> (c) Factorise fully 5*x*<sup>4</sup>y + 25*x*<sup>*3*</sup>*y*<sup>*3*</sup>
> 
> **Answer**:
> 
> *Find the highest common factor of 5 and 25*
> 
> *5*
> 
> > *Find the highest common factor of **x*<sup>*4*</sup>*  and **x*<sup>*3*</sup>
> 
> **x***<sup>3</sup>*
> 
> > *Find the highest common factor of **y** and **y*<sup>*3*</sup>
> 
> **y**
> 
> > *Find the overall highest common factor by multiplying these together*
> 
> *5***x***<sup>*3*</sup>***y**
> 
> > *Think of each term as 5**x*<sup>*3*</sup>*y**  × something*
> 
> *5***x***<sup>*3*</sup>***y***  × ***x***  + 5***x***<sup>*3*</sup>***y***  × 5***y***<sup>2</sup>*<sup> </sup>
> 
> > *Take out the 5**x*<sup>*3*</sup>*y ** and put **x**  + 5**y*<sup>*2*</sup>* in brackets*
> 
> **Final answer:** **5****x**<sup>**3**</sup>**y**** ****(****x****  + 5****y**<sup>**2**</sup>**)**
