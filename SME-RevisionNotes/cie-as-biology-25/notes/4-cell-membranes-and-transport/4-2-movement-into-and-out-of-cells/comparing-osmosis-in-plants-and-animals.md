# Comparing Osmosis in Plants & Animals

## Osmosis in Cells: Comparison

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_wsZ8Kh8t7MGbQjQk`

## Osmosis in cells: comparison

|   | **Plant cells** | **Animal cells** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cell membrane** | Present<br><br>Composed of a phospholipid bilayer<br><br>Selectively permeable |   |
| **Cell wall** | Present<br><br>Composed of cellulose<br><br>Fully permeable | Absent |
| **Osmosis of water into and out of cell** | Can occur |   |
| **Cell placed in solution with a lower water potential** | Water leaves through the selectively permeable cell surface membrane by osmosis<br><br>Volume of the cell decreases<br><br>Protoplast shrinks / pulls away from the cell wall<br><br>Cell is plasmolysed | Water leaves the cell through the selectively permeable cell surface membrane by osmosis<br><br>Volume of cell decreases<br><br>Cell shrinks / shrivels up |
| **Cell placed in solution with a higher water potential** | Water enters the cell through the selectively permeable cell surface membrane by osmosis<br><br>Volume of cell increases<br><br>Protoplast expands / pushes against the cell wall<br><br>The cell wall withstands the increased pressure created<br><br>The pressure increases until the cell is rigid and firm (turgid)<br><br>The cell is fully inflated with water and no more can enter | Water enters the cell through the selectively permeable cell surface membrane by osmosis<br><br>Volume of cell increases<br><br>There is no cell wall to resist the increased pressure that is created<br><br>The cell membrane is eventually overstretched and the cell bursts |
