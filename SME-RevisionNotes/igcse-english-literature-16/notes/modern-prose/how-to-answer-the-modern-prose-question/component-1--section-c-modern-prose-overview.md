## Component 1: Section C: Edexcel IGCSE Modern Prose Overview

If you are studying Edexcel International GCSE in English Literature (4ET1/01), Component 1 is a compulsory, examined component.

You will also either take Component 2, another examined component *or* Component 3, a coursework option (Component 3). All of your exam papers must be taken at the end of your course of study.

In your Component 1 exam, the exam question in **Section C** requires you to write one 40-mark essay question (from a choice of two) on the prose text you have studied.

You **must **complete Component 1. There are three sections in this exam paper:

| **Component 1: Poetry and Modern Prose  (4ET1/01)** |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| Externally assessed | 60% of the total International GCSE |   |
| Section A | Unseen Poetry | - explore how writers create meaning and effects in a range of seen and unseen poetry |
| Section B | Anthology Poetry | - develop skills to compare a range of poetry from Part 3 of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Anthology |
| **Section C** | **Modern Prose** | - **explore how writers create meaning and effects in a range of modern prose texts** |

In** Section C**, you will be expected to show your knowledge and understanding of your chosen prose text and its relationship to the context in which it was written:

You must:

- demonstrate a close understanding of your prose text
- maintain a critical style
- present an informed personal engagement
- identify and use relevant examples from your prose text
- understand the influence of contextual factors on the interpretation of your text

Find out more about how to answer the Modern Prose question [here](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/english-literature/edexcel/16/revision-notes/3-modern-prose/how-to-answer-the-modern-prose-question/how-to-approach-the-modern-prose-question/).
