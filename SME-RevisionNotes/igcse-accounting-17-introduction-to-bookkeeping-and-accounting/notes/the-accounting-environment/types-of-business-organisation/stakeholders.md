# Stakeholders

### What are the stakeholders of a business?

- **Stakeholders **are **people **or **groups **of people who have an **interest in the business**
- Stakeholders can be ***internal ***or ***external ***to the business
- Stakeholders have **different interests** in the **financial statements** of the business

## Internal Stakeholders

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_93fh4WgyqbpVCBxM`

## Internal stakeholders

### Why might internal stakeholders be interested in the accounting information?

| **Stakeholder** | **Reason for their interest in the financial statements** |
|---|---|
| **Owners** | - To compare the performance with previous years<br>- To evaluate the profitability of the business<br>- To plan for the future<br>- To make decisions about which areas need action |
| **Managers** | - To check on the efficiency of the business<br>- To check on the progress of the business compared to previous years |
| **Employees** | - To check that the business is profitable and likely to continue to trade so that they have job security<br>- To check the profitability to see whether a pay rise is reasonable |

## External stakeholders

### Why might external stakeholders be interested in the accounting information?

| **Stakeholder** | **Reason for their interest in the financial statements** |
|---|---|
| **Customers** | - To check on the probability that their goods will continue to be supplied by the business |
| **Suppliers** | - To check whether the business can pay for its supplied goods as agreed<br>- To set the credit terms for goods and services |
| **Banks** | - To assess whether the business is suitable for an overdraft or a bank loan<br>- To assess the value of the assets owned by the business so that they can be used as security against loans |
| **Investors** | - To assess the profitability of the business<br>- To assess the likelihood of the investors making money if they invested in the business |
| **Competitors** | - To compare the profitability of the business against their own business<br>- To identify gaps in the market |
| **Government and tax authorities** | - To calculate how much tax is owed<br>- To gather data for the government statistics |
