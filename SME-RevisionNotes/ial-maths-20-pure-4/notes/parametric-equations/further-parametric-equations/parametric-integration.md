## Parametric integration

### How does integration work with parametric equations?

- Integration is used to find the area under a curve where the curve has been defined by parametric equations ![Notes para_int, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](assets/c798c302c8c5-9-2-2-notes-para-int.png)

  - **x = f(t)**
  - **y = g(t)**

- The key point is to ensure the limits of the integral are changed to the parameter
