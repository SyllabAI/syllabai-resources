# Introducing Paper 1 & Paper 2

## Paper 1 Guidance

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_TZ5dd7zqPdY8VBvj`

## What is Examined in the Edexcel IGCSE Economics Paper 1?

- This paper is focused on **Microeconomics and Business Economics**
- The paper's duration is **1 hour 30 minutes**
- The total number of marks available is **80**
- Paper 1 represents **50%** of the qualification total
- The paper has **four compulsory questions**

  - Each question is a mixture of **multiple choice,** **short-answer, data response** and **open ended questions**

- This paper is focused on **Macroeconomics and the Global Economy**
- The paper's duration is **1 hour 30 minutes**

- The paper has **four compulsory questions**
