## Solving equations graphically

### What does solving simultaneous equations graphically mean?

![Solving Equations Graphically Notes Diagram 1, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/21072b111f5f-2-7-3-solving-equations-graphically-notes-diagra.png)

- This is a way to solve **simultaneous** **equations** (see Simultaneous Equations)
- Coordinates of the *intersections* are the **solutions**

### How do I solve simultaneous equations using a graph?

![Solving Equations Graphically Notes Diagram 2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/27baa7188551-2-7-3-solving-equations-graphically-notes-diagra.png)

- Use **graphs** and **algebra** together
- **Sketch** a graph if it has not been given
- **Read/interpret** a graph if it has been given

![Solving Equations Graphically Notes Diagram 3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/04cceb90be8d-2-7-3-solving-equations-graphically-notes-diagra.png)

- It can be difficult to tell from a sketch if graphs intersect once, more, or not at all

![Solving Equations Graphically - Notes Diagram 4, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/d373ae6a8364-2-7-3-solving-equations-graphically-notes-diagra.png)

> **Exam Hint**
> When writing out your solutions to simultaneous equations, always pair the correct *x* solution with the correct *y* solution
