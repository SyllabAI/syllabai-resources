## Equation of a straight line

### What is the equation of a straight line?

- **y = mx + c **is the equation for any straight line
- **m** is **gradient** given by “difference in **y**” ÷ “difference in **x**” or **dy**/**dx**
- **c** is the **y**-axis **intercept**
- Alternative form is **ax + by + c = 0** where **a**, **b** and **c** are integers

### How do I find the equation of a straight line?

![Equation of a Straight Line Notes Diagram 2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/b3bbb2fa155b-3-1-3-equation-of-a-straight-line-notes-diagram-.png)

- **Two** features of a straight line are needed

  - gradient, **m**
  - a point the line passes through, (**x**<sub>**1**</sub>, **y**<sub>**1**</sub>)

- The equation can then be found using **y – y**<sub>**1**</sub>** = m**(**x - x**<sub>**1**</sub>)
- This can be rearranged into either **y = mx + c** or **ax + by + c = 0**

![Equation of a Straight Line Notes Diagram 6, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/d029dca62fd0-3-1-3-equation-of-a-straight-line-notes-diagram-.png)

### How do I find the gradient of a straight line?

- There are lots of ways to find the gradient of a line

- Using t**wo** **points** on a line to find the change in y divided by change in x

$m=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}$

![Equation of a Straight Line Notes Diagram 3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/d5297a3a089b-3-1-3-equation-of-a-straight-line-notes-diagram-.png)

- Using the fact that lines are **parallel** or **perpendicular** to another line (see Parallel and Perpendicular Gradients)

![Equation of a Straight Line Notes Diagram 4, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/7256702fb10e-3-1-3-equation-of-a-straight-line-notes-diagram-.png)

- Using **Tangents **and **Normals** - **Differentiation **(see Gradients, Tangents & Normals)

![Equation of a Straight Line Notes Diagram 5, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/8c3992d32f8d-3-1-3-equation-of-a-straight-line-notes-diagram-.png)

- Other ways

  - **Collinear** lines are the same straight line so gradients are equal
  - Angle facts and **circle** **theorems** eg. a **radius** and **tangent** are **perpendicular**
