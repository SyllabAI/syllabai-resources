# Answering 2-Mark Questions

## How do I answer a 2-mark Economics question?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_wT5scH8XTtrtGKSV`

## How do I answer a 2-mark economics question?

- To answer IGCSE Economics 2-mark questions, consider the following:

  - These questions test **AO1 knowledge**.
  You must either:
  
    - **Define** a term precisely (two key elements)
    - **Identify** two correct points
    - **State** two features, causes, or effects
    - **Calculate** a numerical answer (showing working)
    - **Explain  **a single point
- **You gain marks for:**

  - **Two accurate points**
  OR
  - **A concise definition containing two essential elements**
  OR
  - **A correct calculation with working**

> **Worked Example**
> **Define **opportunity cost
> 
> **Model Answer**
> 
> Opportunity cost is the **next best alternative foregone** (1) when a choice is made between different options (1).
> 
> **Mark Scheme Guidance**
> 
> - Definitions must contain **two clear sub-parts**
> - Avoid circular definitions

> **Worked Example**
> **Identify** two causes of inflation
> 
> **Model Answer**
> 
> - Higher consumer demand (1)
> - Rising production costs (1)

> **Worked Example**
> **State **two functions of money
> 
> **Model Answer**
> 
> - Medium of exchange (1)
> - Store of value (1)
> 
> **Mark Scheme Guidance**
> 
> - Provide **two separate, clear functions**
> - No explanation required
> - Answers must be **distinct** (e.g. “medium of exchange” and “means of payment” are *not* distinct)

> **Worked Example**
> **Calculate **the unemployment rate
> 
> Population = 50 million
> Labour force = 30 million
> Unemployed = 3 million
> 
> **Model Answer**
> 
> Unemployment rate = (Unemployed ÷ Labour force) × 100
> 
> = (3 ÷ 30) × 100 = **10%** (2)
> 
> **Examiner Tip**
> Always include **units**: %, \$, index number, etc

> **Worked Example**
> **Explain **one reason why productivity increased in Algeria’s tertiary sector.
> 
> **Model Answer**
> 
> Productivity may have increased because firms invested in new technology (1).
> which allowed workers to produce more output in the same amount of time (1)
> 
> **Mark Scheme Guidance**
> 
> - 1 mark for the reason
> - 1 mark for the development
> - Only **one** reason needed
> - No chains or evaluation required
> 
> **Examiner Tip**
> Use the pattern **reason → effect**
