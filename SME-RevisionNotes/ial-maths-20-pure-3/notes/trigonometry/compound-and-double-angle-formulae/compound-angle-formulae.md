# Compound Angle Formulae

## Compound Angle Formulae

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_5bGK2QnqzT8s8d2z`

## Compound angle formulae

### What are the compound angle formulae?

- There are six **compound angle formulae** (also known as **addition formulae**), two each for **sin**, **cos** and **tan**:
- For **sin** the +/- sign on the left-hand side **matches** the one on the right-hand side

$\mathrm{sin}(A+B)\equiv\mathrm{sin}A\mathrm{cos}B+\mathrm{cos}A\mathrm{sin}B$ $\mathrm{sin}(A-B)\equiv\mathrm{sin}A\mathrm{cos}B-\mathrm{cos}A\mathrm{sin}B$

- For **cos** the +/- sign on the left-hand side is **opposite to** the one on the right-hand side

$\mathrm{cos}(A+B)\equiv\mathrm{cos}A\mathrm{cos}B-\mathrm{sin}A\mathrm{sin}B$ $\mathrm{cos}(A-B)\equiv\mathrm{cos}A\mathrm{cos}B+\mathrm{sin}A\mathrm{sin}B$

- For **tan** the +/- sign on the left-hand side **matches** the one in the **numerator** on the right-hand side, and is **opposite to** the one in the **denominator**

$\mathrm{tan}(A+B)\equiv\frac{\mathrm{tan}A+\mathrm{tan}B}{1-\mathrm{tan}A\mathrm{tan}B}$ $\mathrm{tan}(A-B)\equiv\frac{\mathrm{tan}A-\mathrm{tan}B}{1+\mathrm{tan}A\mathrm{tan}B}$

- You can **derive** the tan identity by:

  - Writing $\mathrm{tan}(A+B)\equiv\frac{\mathrm{sin}(A+B)}{\mathrm{cos}(A+B)}$
  - Dividing the numerator and denominator by $\mathrm{cos}A\mathrm{cos}B$

> **Exam Hint**
> - All these formulae are in the formulae booklet – you don't have to memorise them.
