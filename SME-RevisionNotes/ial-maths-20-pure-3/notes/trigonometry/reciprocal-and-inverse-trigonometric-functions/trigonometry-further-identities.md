## Identities with reciprocal trigonometric functions

### What are the reciprocal trigonometric identities?

- There are two identities with **sec**,** cosec** and **cot **that you need to know and be able to use:

  - **tan**<sup>**2**</sup>***x *****+ 1 ≡ sec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***
  - **1 + cot**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***** ≡ cosec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***
- These are not really 'new' identities – they can both be derived from **sin**<sup>**2**</sup>***x *****+ cos**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***** ≡ 1**

  - To derive the identity for **sec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***  divide  **sin**<sup>**2**</sup>***x *****+ cos**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***** ≡ 1  **by **cos**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***
  - To derive the identity for **cosec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x *** divide  **sin**<sup>**2**</sup>***x *****+ cos**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***** ≡ 1  **by **sin**<sup>**2**</sup>***x***
