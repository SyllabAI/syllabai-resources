## Exponential growth & decay

### What is exponential growth?

- **y = Ae**<sup>**kt**</sup> is exponential **growth**

![Exponential Growth & Decay Notes fig1, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/2a7109a66bcb-6-3-1-exponential-growth-decay-notes-fig1.png)

### What is exponential decay?

- **y = Ae**<sup>**-kt**</sup> is exponential **decay**

![Exponential Growth & Decay Notes fig2, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/7e7b25879120-6-3-1-exponential-growth-decay-notes-fig2.png)

- **k > 0** so plus or minus sign in equation immediately indicates growth or decay
- **A** is the **starting** value or **initial** value, when **t = 0**
- **t** is often used, rather than **x**, as many models are time-dependent

### How do I write a^x in terms of e^x?

- Using **e** makes the maths easier
- With the proper choice of **k**, all exponential functions of the form **a**<sup>**x**</sup> can be written in the form **e**<sup>**kx**</sup>

  - **a**<sup>**x**</sup>** = e**<sup>**kx**</sup>

![Exponential Growth & Decay Notes fig3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/363fdcf3d5ff-6-3-1-exponential-growth-decay-notes-fig3.png)

### How are e<sup>x</sup> and ln x inverses of each other?

![Exponential Growth & Decay Notes fig4, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/00b408d2fbdd-6-3-1-exponential-growth-decay-notes-fig4.png)

- **ln** is the inverse of **e**

![Exponential Growth & Decay Notes fig5, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/6009596da5c2-6-3-1-exponential-growth-decay-notes-fig5.png)

- The graph of **y = ln** **x** is the reflection of the graph of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> in the line** y = x**
