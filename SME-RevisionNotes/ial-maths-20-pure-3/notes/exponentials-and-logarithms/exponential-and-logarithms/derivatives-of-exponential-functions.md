## Derivatives of exponential functions

### What is the derivative of e<sup>x</sup>?

- **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> has the particular property that

$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{x}$

- i.e. for *every* real number **x**, the gradient of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> is also equal to **e**<sup>**x **</sup>(see Derivatives of Exponential Functions)

- **e ≈ 2.718 **(see "e")
- Recall that the derivative is the gradient function for a curve (see First Principles Differentiation)

### What is the derivative of y = e<sup>kx</sup> and y=e-<sup>kx</sup>?

![Derivatives of Exponential Functions Notes fig3, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/24d2cad1c915-6-1-4-derivatives-of-exponential-functions-notes.png)

- ** **The derivative of **y = e**<sup>**kx**</sup> is

  - $\frac{dy}{dx}=ke^{kx}$
- The derivative of **y = e**<sup>**-kx**</sup> is

  - $\frac{dy}{dx}=-ke^{-kx}$

> - Remember that (like **π**) **e** is a **number**.
> - Exam questions can ask for answers to be given as exact values in terms of **e** (see the Worked Example below).
