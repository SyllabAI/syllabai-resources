### How do I know which trigonometric identities to use?

- There is no set method
- This is a matter of experience, familiarity and recognition

  - Practice as many questions as possible
  - Be familiar with trigonometric functions that can be integrated easily
  - Be familiar with common identities – especially **squared** terms
  - **sin**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x**, **cos**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x**, **tan**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x**, **cosec**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x**, **sec**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x**, **cot**<sup>**2 **</sup>**x** all appear in identities

![Notes squared_trig_id, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/469ad14fc2d5-8-2-6-notes-squared-trig-id.png)

### How do I integrate tan<sup>2</sup>x, cot<sup>2</sup>x, sec<sup>2</sup>x and cosec<sup>2</sup>x?

- The **integral** of **sec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x*** is **tan *****x***** (+c)**

  - This is because the **derivative** of tan x is sec<sup>2</sup>x
- The **integral** of **cosec**<sup>**2**</sup>***x*** is **-cot *****x***** (+c)**

  - This is because the **derivative** of cot x is -cosec<sup>2</sup>x
- The **integral** of **tan**<sup>**2**</sup>***x*** can be found by using the identity to rewrite tan<sup>2</sup>*x *before integrating:

  - 1 + tan<sup>2</sup>*x* = sec<sup>2</sup>*x*
- The **integral** of **cot**<sup>**2**</sup>***x*** can be found by using the identity to rewrite cot<sup>2</sup>*x *before integrating:

  - 1 + cot<sup>2</sup>*x* = cosec<sup>2</sup>*x*

### How do I integrate expressions involving sin x and cos x?

- For functions of the form **sin kx**, **cos kx** … see Integrating Other Functions
- **sin kx × cos kx** can be integrated using the identity for **sin 2A**

  - **sin 2A = 2sinAcosA**

![Notes sin_cos, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/bf1aab0b6aa0-8-2-6-notes-sin-cos.png)

- **sin**<sup>**n **</sup>**kx cos kx** or **sin kx cos**<sup>**n **</sup>**kx** can be integrated using **reverse chain rule** or **substitution**
- Notice no identity is used here but it looks as though there should be!

![Notes sin_cos_powers, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/5877f62f66c2-8-2-6-notes-sin-cos-powers.png)

- sin<sup>2 </sup>kx and cos<sup>2 </sup>kx can be integrated by using the identity for cos 2A

  - For sin<sup>2 </sup>A, cos 2A = 1 - 2sin<sup>2 </sup>A
  - For cos<sup>2 </sup>A, cos 2A = 2cos<sup>2 </sup>A – 1

![Notes sin_cos_squared, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/8ff681fc6086-8-2-6-notes-sin-cos-squared.png)

### How do I integrate other expressions involving trig functions?

- The formulae booklet lists many standard trigonometric derivatives and integrals

  - Check both the “Differentiation” and “Integration” sections
  - For integration using the "Differentiation" formulae, remember that the integral of **f'(x)** is **f(x)** !
