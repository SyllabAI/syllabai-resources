## Geometric sequences

### What do I need to know about geometric sequences?

- In a **geometric sequence,** there is a **common ratio** between consecutive terms in the sequence

![Geom Seq Illustr, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/9fb74f1ac39c-4-5-1-geom-seq-illustr.png)

- You need to know the ***n***<sup>**th**</sup>** term formula** for a geometric sequence $u_{n}=ar^{n-1}$

  - a is the first term
  - *r *is the common ratio

> **Exam Hint**
> If you know two terms in a geometric sequence you can find ***a*** and ***r*** using simultaneous equations.
