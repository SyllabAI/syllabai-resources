## Binomial expansion

### What is the binomial expansion?

![Binomial Expansion Notes Diagram 1, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/498f786e0499-4-1-1-binomial-expansion-notes-diagram-1.png)

- Look at the pattern

  - Start at <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**0**</sub>, then <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**1**</sub>, <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**2**</sub>, etc
  - Powers of **a** start at **n** and decrease by 1
  - Powers of **b** start at 0 and increase by 1
- There are shortcuts but these hide the pattern

  - <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**0**</sub>** = **<sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**n**</sub>** = 1**
  - <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**1**</sub>** = **<sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**n-1 **</sub>**= n**
  - <sup>**n**</sup>**Cr = **<sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**n-r**</sub>
  - **(b)**<sup>**0**</sup>** = (a)**<sup>**0**</sup>** = 1**
- Use the shortcuts once familiar with the pattern
- ! means *factorial*

![Binomial Expansion Notes Diagram 2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/1f55ea1c806d-4-1-1-binomial-expansion-notes-diagram-2.png)

- This is given in the formula booklet

### What is Pascal’s triangle?

![Binomial Expansion Notes Diagram 3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/35348aeff32a-4-1-1-binomial-expansion-notes-diagram-3.png)

- Pascal’s triangle is an alternative to <sup>**n**</sup>**C**<sub>**r**</sub>
- It is useful for lower values of **n**
- For larger **n** it is slow and prone to arithmetic errors
