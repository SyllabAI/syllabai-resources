## Equation of a circle

### What is the equation of a circle?

- A circle with centre **(*****a, b*****)** and radius ***r*** has the equation

$(x-a)^{2}+(y-b)^{2}=r^{2}$** **

![Eqn of Circle graph illustr, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/071248dbd9ab-3-2-1-eqn-of-circle-graph-illustr.png)

- ** **You need to be able to find the centre and radius of a circle from its equation

![Eqn of Circle Illustr 1, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/aa7906358b78-3-2-1-eqn-of-circle-illustr-1.png)

- You need to be able to find the equation of a circle given its centre and radius

![Eqn of Circle Illustr 2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/4d05b65a7c96-3-2-1-eqn-of-circle-illustr-2.png)

> **Exam Hint**
> - Remember that the numbers in the brackets have the opposite signs to the coordinates of the centre. ![Eqn of Circle sign flip, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](assets/f49cb500941a-3-2-1-eqn-of-circle-sign-flip.png) ** **
> - Don't forget to take the **square root **of the right-hand side of the equation when finding the radius.
