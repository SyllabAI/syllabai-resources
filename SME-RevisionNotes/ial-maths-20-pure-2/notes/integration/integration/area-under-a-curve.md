## Area under a curve

### What is the area under a curve?

- The phrase “area under a curve” refers to the area bounded by …

  - ... the **x**-axis
  - … the graph of **y = f(x)**
  - … the vertical line **x = a**
  - … the vertical line **x = b**

![Notes fig1, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/9cd6b197d4f8-8-1-4-notes-fig1.png)

### How do I find the area under a curve using definite integration?

![Notes fig2, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/aa66133f4a6a-8-1-4-notes-fig2.png)

- The value from **definite** **integration** is equal to the “area under a curve” ![Notes fig3, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](assets/334f1688ae7c-8-1-4-notes-fig3.png)

### How do I find the limits of integration?

![Notes fig4, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/b885340c0a1b-8-1-4-notes-fig4.png)

- If limits are not provided they will be the **x**-axis intercepts

  - Set **y = 0** and solve the equation to find the **x**-axis intercepts

![Notes fig5, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/c9127c933bda-8-1-4-notes-fig5.png)

### How do I find areas that are below the x-axis (negative areas)?

![Notes fig6, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/d6a88b835609-8-1-4-notes-fig6.png)

- If the area lies underneath the **x**-axis the value of the integral will be negative
- An **area** cannot be negative, so take the **modulus** of the integral

![Notes fig7, AS & A Level Maths revision notes](../../../assets/11c5b00f0c15-8-1-4-notes-fig7.png)

### How do I find total areas without areas cancelling each other out?

![Notes fig8, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/3bd1667158d3-8-1-4-notes-fig8.png)

- Be careful when one section is above and one section is below the **x**-axis ![Notes fig9, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](assets/18e9c3a0bb9a-8-1-4-notes-fig9.png)

  - You will need a separate integral for each section, BUT...
  - ...One section's integral will be **negative**
  - ...One section's integral will be **positive**
  - So you’ll need to take the **modulus** before adding to find the total area
