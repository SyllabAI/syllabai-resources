## Quadratic & Cubic Sequences

### What is a quadratic sequence?

- A **quadratic **sequence has an ***n *****th term formula** that involves ***n***<sup>**2**</sup>
- The **second differences** are **constant **(the same)

  - These are the differences between the first differences
  - For example,   3, 9, 19, 33, 51, …
  1st Differences: 6, 10, 14, 18, ... 2nd Differences:  4,   4,   4, ...

### How do I find the n<sup>th</sup> term formula for a simple quadratic sequence?

- The sequence with *n* th term formula** *****n***<sup>**2**</sup> are the **square numbers **

  - 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, ...
  
    - From 1<sup>2</sup>, 2<sup>2</sup>, 3<sup>2</sup>, 4<sup>2</sup>, ...
- Finding the ***n *****th term formula** comes from **comparing** sequences to the square numbers

  - 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50, ... has the formula *n*<sup>2 </sup>+ 1
  
    - Each term is 1 more than the square numbers
  - 2, 8, 18, 32, 50, 72, 98, ... has the formula 2*n*<sup>2</sup>
  
    - Each term is double a square number
- It may be a simple **combination** of both

  - For example, doubling then adding 1
  
    - 3, 9, 19, 33, 51, 73, 99, ... has the formula 2*n*<sup>2</sup> + 1
- Some sequences are just the square numbers but starting later

  - 16, 25, 36, 49, ... has the formula (*n* + 3)<sup>2</sup>
  
    - Substitute in *n* = 1, *n* = 2, *n* = 3 to see why

### What is a cubic sequence?

- A **cubic **sequence has an ***n *****th term formula** that involves ***n***<sup>**3**</sup>
- The **third differences** are **constant **(the same)

  - These are the differences between the second differences
  - For example,   4, 25, 82, 193, 376, 649, ...
  1st Differences:  21, 57, 111, 183, 273, ... 2nd Differences:   36,  54,  72,   90, ...
  3rd Differences:      18,    18,   18, ...

### How do I find the n<sup>th</sup> term formula for a simple cubic sequence?

- The sequence with *n* th term formula** *****n***<sup>**3**</sup> are the **cube numbers **

  - 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, ...
  
    - From 1<sup>3</sup>, 2<sup>3</sup>, 3<sup>3</sup>, 4<sup>3</sup>, ...
- Finding the ***n *****th term formula** comes from **comparing** sequences to the cube numbers

  - 2, 9, 28, 65, 126, ... has the formula *n*<sup>3 </sup>+ 1
  
    - Each term is 1 more than the cube numbers
  - 2, 16, 54, 128, 250, ...  has the formula 2*n*<sup>3</sup>
  
    - Each term is double a cube number

### Can I use the differences to help find the n<sup>th</sup> term formula?

- Yes, for the simple **quadratic** sequence *an*<sup>2</sup> + *b*

  - *a*  is $\frac{1}{2}$ of the** second **difference
- For the simple **cubic** sequence *an*<sup>3</sup> + *b*

  - *a*  is $\frac{1}{6}$ of the **third** difference

> **Exam Hint**
> - You must learn the square numbers from 1<sup>2</sup> to 15<sup>2</sup> and the cube numbers from 1<sup>3</sup> to 5<sup>3</sup> (and know 10<sup>3</sup>)

> **Worked Example**
> For the sequence 6, 9, 14, 21, 30, ....
> 
> (a) Find a formula for the *n*<sup>th </sup>term.
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> > *Find the first and second differences*
> 
> *Sequence:   6,   9,   14,   21,   30*
> *     First differences:   3,    5,     7,     9, ...           *
> *    Second differences:   2,    2,     2, ...   *
> 
> > *The second differences are constant so this must be a quadratic sequence*
> *Compare each term to terms in the sequence **n*<sup>*2* </sup>*(the square numbers)*
> 
> **           n***<sup>2 </sup>**:  1,   4,   9,   16,   25,  ... *
> * Sequence:  6,   9,   14,   21,   30, ..*.
> 
> > *Each term is 5 more than the terms in **n*<sup>*2*</sup>*, so add 5 to **n*<sup>*2*</sup>
> 
> **Final answer:** **n**<sup>**th**</sup>** term =**** n**<sup>**2**</sup>** + 5**
> 
> **You could also have used ****an**<sup>**2**</sup>** + ****b**** where ****a **** is half the second difference**
> 
> (b) Hence, find the 20<sup>th</sup> term of the sequence.
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> > *Substitute **n**  = 20 into **n*<sup>*2*</sup> *+ 5*
> 
> *(20)**<sup>2</sup>** + 5 = 400 + 5*
> 
> **Final answer:** **The 20**<sup>**th**</sup>** term is 405**
