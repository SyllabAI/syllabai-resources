## Fractions of amounts

### How do I find a fraction of an amount?

- **Method 1**
**Divide** by the **denominator** and **multiply** by the **numerator**

  - To find $\frac{1}{3}$ of $90$, divide it by 3
  
    - $90\div3=30$
  - To find $\frac{2}{5}$ of $60$, divide it by 5 then multiply it by 2
  
    - $60\div5=12$, then $12\times2=24$
- **Method 2**
Change the fraction into a **decimal**, then **multiply**

  - To find $\frac{1}{4}$ of $16$, turn $\frac{1}{4}$ into $0.25$ then multiply
  
    - $0.25\times16=4$
  - To find $\frac{9}{10}$ of $15$, turn $\frac{9}{10}$ into $0.9$ then multiply
  
    - $0.9\times15=13.5$
- **Method 3**
Write both numbers as fractions and **multiply two fractions** together

  - To find $\frac{2}{3}$ of $60$, write $60$ as $\frac{60}{1}$
  
    - work out $\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{60}{1}=\frac{120}{3}=40$
  - To find $\frac{5}{6}$ of $\frac{8}{3}$
  
    - work out $\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{8}{3}=\frac{40}{18}=\frac{20}{9}$
