# Real Images

## Converging Lens - Real Image

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_8S3gZM44CxHkwbP6` · Draw and use ray diagrams for the formation of a real image by a converging lens

## Converging lens - real image

### Constructing converging ray diagrams

- The three main rules for constructing [ray diagrams](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/cie/23/revision-notes/3-waves/3-2-light/3-2-6-thin-lenses/) are as follows:

1. **Rays passing through the principal axis will pass through the optical centre of the lens undeviated** ![Ray diagram of a converging lens showing a slanted incident ray passing through the optical centre of the lens and passing through undeviated.](assets/eb730ebf5937-9-1-1-ray-diagram-construction-rule-1.png)
2. **Rays that are parallel to the principal axis will be refracted and pass through the focal point *****f*** ![Diagram of a converging lens showing an incident ray parallel to the horizontal principal axis refracted through the focal point on the far side.
](assets/edb9b150bc52-9-1-1-ray-diagram-construction-rule-2.png)
3. **Rays passing through the focal point *****f***** will emerge parallel to the principal axis** ![Diagram of a converging lens showing an incident ray through the focal point emerging parallel to the principal axis, with focal points marked f on both sides.](assets/c45ced6d0d7e-9-1-1-ray-diagram-construction-rule-3.png)

### Drawing converging ray diagrams of real images

#### Object placed beyond 2f

![Ray diagram of a converging lens forming a smaller, inverted real image when the object is placed beyond twice the focal length on the principal axis.](../../../assets/b5c530b265b0-pej3djox-9-1-1-lens-image-distance-greater-than-.png)

- When an **object **is placed **beyond 2*****f*** (to the left of the lens), the **image **that forms (to the right of the lens) will have the following properties:

| **The image forms...** | between *f* and 2*f* |
|---|---|
| **The nature of the image is...** | real |
| **The orientation of the image is...** | inverted |
| **The size of the image is...** | diminished |

#### Object placed at 2f

![Ray diagram of a converging lens showing an object at 2f forming an inverted real image at 2f on the opposite side, with principal focus points marked.](../../../assets/66979a1b7e38-9-1-1-lens-image-distance-equal-to-2f.png)

- When an **object **is placed at **2*****f*** (to the left of the lens), the **image **that forms (to the right of the lens) will have the following properties:

| **The image forms...** | at 2*f* |
|---|---|
| **The nature of the image is...** | real |
| **The orientation of the image is...** | inverted |
| **The size of the image is...** | the same |

#### Object placed between f and 2f

![Ray diagram of a converging lens with object between f and 2f, showing rays forming a real, inverted, magnified image beyond 2f on the other side.](../../../assets/423dd87e8529-9-1-1-lens-image-distance-greater-than-2f.png)

- When an **object **is placed **between *****f***** and 2*****f*** (to the left of the lens), the **image **that forms (to the right of the lens) will have the following properties:

| **The image forms...** | beyond 2*f* |
|---|---|
| **The nature of the image is...** | real |
| **The orientation of the image is...** | inverted |
| **The size of the image is...** | magnified |

> **Worked Example**
> Draw a ray diagram to show how a converging lens can be used to form a diminished image of a real object.
> 
> Label the object, image and principal foci of the lens on your diagram.
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> **Step 1: Start by drawing and labelling a principal axis and the lens as a line or a very thin ellipse**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis.](assets/e1422574cc99-2n6wi3i3-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 2: Mark and label the focal points on each side of the lens**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens.](assets/68b2027e8df7-htvliwle-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 3: Draw and label the object at a distance greater than the focal length on the left side of the lens**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens.](assets/d72b094877be-8khmusig-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> - Tip: For a diminished image, the object should be placed a distance of at least 2*f* away from the lens
> 
> **Step 4: Draw a ray through the optical centre of the lens **
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens. A ray is drawn from the top of the object towards the optical centre of the lens.](assets/decec05254c3-tid0zx2q-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 5: Draw a second ray from the object to the lens which is parallel to the principal axis**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens. A ray is drawn from the top of the object towards the optical centre of the lens. A ray parallel to the principal axis is drawn towards the lens.](assets/66b8a6232e97-f2ta3-ii-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 6: Draw the continuation of the ray passing through the focal point on the right side of the lens**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens. A ray is drawn from the top of the object towards the optical centre of the lens. A ray parallel to the principal axis is drawn towards the lens. The parallel ray is refracted and passes through the focal point on the far side.](assets/33eb8ce66657-ar17-81v-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 7: Draw and label the image at the point where the rays meet**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens. A ray is drawn from the top of the object towards the optical centre of the lens. A ray parallel to the principal axis is drawn towards the lens. The parallel ray is refracted and passes through the focal point on the far side. The image is drawn at the point where the two rays meet.](assets/a5e584ba7ded-1btamsh5-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> **Step 8: Check your final image and make sure everything is included to gain the marks**
> 
> ![Diagram of a converging lens shown by a vertical double arrow crossing a horizontal line labelled principal axis and focal points F marked symmetrically on either side of the lens. An upright object is placed at a distance at least 2F away from the centre of the lens. A ray is drawn from the top of the object towards the optical centre of the lens. A ray parallel to the principal axis is drawn towards the lens. The parallel ray is refracted and passes through the focal point on the far side. The image is drawn at the point where the two rays meet.](assets/7d968de0eacf-ltx4j6bi-9-1-1-we-converging-lens-ray-diagram-st.png)
> 
> - For a three-mark question, examiners will be looking for:
> 
>   - One ray drawn through the optical centre of the lens
>   - A second ray drawn which produces a diminished (smaller) image (which must pass through a labelled focal point)
>   - Both the object and the image must be drawn and labelled correctly
