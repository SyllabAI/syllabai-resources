# Geothermal Energy

## Geothermal energy

 * geothermal resources

## Geothermal energy

- **Tectonic activity** causes areas where the rocks below ground become **heated**
- Energy in the thermal store of the rocks can be obtained by:

  - Drilling **shafts **into the Earth's surface to expose the heated rocks
  - Injecting **water **into the shaft which is heated by the rocks
  - The water is returned via another shaft as **steam **or hot water
  - The steam turns a **turbine**, powering a **generator** that produces electricity.
  - The hot water can be used to heat homes and provide a **hot water** supply to households

![A geothermal power station, with cold water pumped down one shaft into hot rocks and returning as steam up a second shaft to turn a turbine and a generator.](../../../assets/959549014813-1-8-4-geothermal-energy-cie-igcse-23-rn.png)

*Cold water is heated by the rocks and returned as hot water or steam, which can be used to generate electricity*

### Advantages of geothermal energy

- Geothermal energy is **renewable **— it comes from the long-term continuous decay of radioisotopes in the Earth's core that will outlive the Earth
- Geothermal power stations do not produce greenhouse gases (such as **carbon dioxide**) during operation and do not contribute to global warming
- Generating electricity from geothermal energy does not release acidic gases (such as **sulfur dioxide**) into the atmosphere and does not contribute to acid rain
- Geothermal power stations are **reliable **because the rocks remain hot for thousands of years so they can consistently produce electricity

### Disadvantages of geothermal energy

- Geothermal energy depends on tectonic activity, so there are **few suitable locations** on Earth
- Drilling can release gases trapped underground, including methane, which is a powerful **greenhouse gas**
- The initial cost to build the geothermal power station and drill deep into the Earth is extremely **expensive **
- Most geothermal power stations are **smaller** than fossil-fuel or nuclear stations

> **Exam Hint**
> Note that geothermal energy is one of three energy resources whose energy does **not** come from the Sun. The other two energy resources are nuclear energy (nuclear fission) and tidal energy.
