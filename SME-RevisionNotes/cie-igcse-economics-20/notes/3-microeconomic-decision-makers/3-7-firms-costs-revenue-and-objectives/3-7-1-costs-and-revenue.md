# Costs & Revenue

## Different Types of Costs

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_4hTdfQ6kq2q3Y7y2` · Total cost (TC), average total cost (ATC), fixed cost (FC), variable cost (VC), average fixed cost (AFC), average variable cost (AVC). Note: marginal cost is not required. Definition, drawing and interpretation of diagrams that show how changes in output affect costs of production.

## Different Types of Costs

- In preparing goods/services for sale, **firms incur a range of costs**. These costs can be be broken into different categories

1. **Fixed costs (FC) **are costs that do not change as the level of output changes

  - These have to be paid whether output is zero or 5000
  
    - E.g. Building rent, management salaries, insurance, bank loan repayments etc.
2. **Variable costs (VC) **are costs that vary directly with output

  - These increase as output increases and vice versa
  
    - E.g. raw material costs, wages of workers directly involved in production
3. **Total costs (TC) **are the sum of the fixed + total variable costs

## Cost Calculations

## Cost Calculations

- Based on the above definitions, we can calculate several different types of costs

1. $\mathrm{Total} \mathrm{costs}(\mathrm{TC})=\mathrm{total} \mathrm{fixed} \mathrm{costs}(\mathrm{TFC})+\mathrm{total} \mathrm{variable} \mathrm{costs}(\mathrm{TVC})$
2. $\mathrm{Total} \mathrm{variable} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{TVC})=\mathrm{variable} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{VC})\times\mathrm{quantity}(Q)$
3. $\mathrm{Average} \mathrm{total} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{AC})=\frac{\mathrm{total} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{TC})}{\mathrm{quantity}(Q)}$
4. $\mathrm{Average} \mathrm{fixed} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{AFC})=\frac{\mathrm{Total} \mathrm{fixed} \mathrm{costs}(\mathrm{TFC})}{\mathrm{quantity}(Q)}$
5. $\mathrm{Average} \mathrm{variable} \mathrm{cost}(\mathrm{AVC})=\frac{\mathrm{Total} \mathrm{variable} \mathrm{costs}(\mathrm{TVC})}{\mathrm{quantity}(Q)}$ ** **

## Different Types of Revenue

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_nJN2FnfSNMHwKGyB` · Total revenue (TR) and average revenue (AR). Note: marginal revenue is not required. Calculation of TR and AR. The influence of sales on revenue.

## Different Types of Revenue

- **Total revenue** is the total value of all sales a firm incurs

$\mathrm{Total} \mathrm{revenue}(\mathrm{TR})=\mathrm{selling} \mathrm{price}(P)\times\mathrm{quantity} \mathrm{sold}(Q)$

- **Average revenue** is the overall revenue per unit

$\mathrm{Average} \mathrm{revenue}(\mathrm{AR})=\frac{\mathrm{TR}}{Q}$

**An Example of Revenue Calculations**

| **P (\$)** | **Q** | **TR **$(P\timesQ)$ | **AR **$\frac{\mathrm{TR}}{Q}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 1 | 8 | 8 |
| 7 | 2 | 14 | 7 |
| 6 | 3 | 18 | 6 |
| 5 | 4 | 20 | 5 |
| 4 | 5 | 20 | 4 |
| 3 | 6 | 18 | 3 |
| 2 | 7 | 14 | 2 |
| 1 | 8 | 8 | 1 |

- **Average revenue** information is especially useful to a firm **selling multiple products** (e.g. supermarkets) or a firm that sells the **same item at different prices** (e.g. rail tickets are usually priced differently for different types of commuters e.g. pensioners)
