# pH Scale

## The pH Scale

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_Jb2WrHsjhdsHyqv6`

## The pH Scale

- The pH scale is a numerical scale that shows how **acidic **or **alkaline** a solution is
- The values on the pH scale go from 1-14 (extremely acidic substances have values of below 1)

  - All acids have pH values **below **7
  - All alkalis have pH values **above **7
- The **lower **the pH, the **more acidic **the solution is
- The **higher **the pH, the **more alkaline **the solution is

### pH of water

- An equilibrium exists in water where few water molecules dissociate into proton and hydroxide ions:

**H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O (l) ⇌ H**<sup>**+ **</sup>**(aq) + OH**<sup>**– **</sup>**(aq)**

- The equilibrium constant expression for this reaction is:

***K***<sub>**c**</sub>** = **$\frac{[ H^{+} ] [ OH^{-} ]}{[ H_{2} O ]}$

- The equilibrium constant expression can be rearranged to:

***K***<sub>***c***</sub>*** *****x [H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O] = [H**<sup>**+**</sup>**] [OH**<sup>**–**</sup>**]**

- Since the concentration of the H<sup>+</sup> and OH<sup>-</sup> ions is very small, the concentration of water is considered to be a constant

  - So, the expression can be rewritten as:

***K***<sub>***w***</sub>** = [H**<sup>**+**</sup>**] [OH**<sup>**–**</sup>**]**

- Where ***K***<sub>***w***</sub>*** *****(ionic product of water) = *****K***<sub>***c***</sub>*** *****x [H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O] = 10**<sup>**-14 **</sup>**mol**<sup>**2 **</sup>**dm**<sup>**-3**</sup>** at 298K**

- Water at 298K has **equal amounts** of **OH**<sup>**-**</sup> and **H**<sup>**+**</sup>** ions** with concentrations of **10**<sup>**-7**</sup>** mol dm**<sup>**-3**</sup>
- To calculate the pH of water, the following formula should be used:

**pH = –log [H**<sup>**+**</sup>** (aq)]**

- Where [H<sup>+</sup> (aq)] is the concentration of H<sup>+</sup> / H<sub>3</sub>O<sup>+</sup> ions
- So, the calculation is:

  - **pH = -log (10**<sup>**-7**</sup>**) = 7**

- Thus, water has a pH of 7

### pH of acids

- **Acidic** solutions (strong or weak) **always **have more H<sup>+</sup> than OH<sup>-</sup> ions
- Since the concentration of **H**<sup>**+**</sup> is always **greater** than the concentration of **OH**<sup>**-**</sup> ions, [H<sup>+</sup>] is always **greater** than 10<sup>-7</sup> mol dm<sup>-3</sup>
- Using the pH formula, this means that the **pH of acidic solutions** is always **below** 7
- The higher the [H<sup>+</sup>] of the acid, the lower the pH

### pH of bases

- **Basic** solutions (strong or weak) **always **have more OH<sup>-</sup> than H<sup>+</sup> ions
- Since the concentration of **OH**<sup>**-**</sup> is always **greater** than the concentration of **H**<sup>**+**</sup> ions, [H<sup>+</sup>] is always **smaller** than 10<sup>-7</sup> mol dm<sup>-3</sup>
- Using the pH formula, this means that the **pH of basic solutions** is always **above** 7
- The higher the [OH<sup>-</sup>] of the base, the higher the pH
