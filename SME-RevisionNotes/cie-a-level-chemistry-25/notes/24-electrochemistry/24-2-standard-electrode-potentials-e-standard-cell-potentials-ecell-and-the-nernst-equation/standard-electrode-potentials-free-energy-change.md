# Standard Electrode Potentials: Free Energy Change

## Free Energy Change Calculations Using Standard Electrode Potentials

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_9h2zdD3sCnvBMsK8`

## Calculating Free Energy Change Using Standard Electrode Potentials

- The **standard free energy change **can be calculated using the standard cell potential of an electrochemical cell

**Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** = - n x *****E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** x *****F***

- **Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup> = standard Gibbs free energy

  - **n** = number of electrons transferred in the reaction
  - ***E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup> = standard cell potential (V)
  - ***F*** = Faraday constant (96 500 C mol<sup>-1</sup>)

> **Worked Example**
> **Calculating the standard Gibbs free energy change**
> 
> Calculate the standard Gibbs free energy change for the following electrochemical cell:
> 
> 2Fe<sup>3+</sup> (aq) + Cu (s) ⇌ 2Fe<sup>2+</sup> (aq) + Cu<sup>2+</sup> (aq)
> 
> **Answer**
> 
> - **Step 1: **Determine the two half-equations and their *E*<sup>ꝋ</sup> using the Data booklet:
> 
> **Fe**<sup>**3+**</sup>** (aq) + e**<sup>**- **</sup>**⇌ Fe**<sup>**2+**</sup>** (aq)        *****E***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** = +0.77 V**
> 
> **Cu**<sup>**2+**</sup>** (aq) + 2e**<sup>**- **</sup>**⇌ Cu (s)       *****E***<sup>**ꝋ **</sup>**= +0.34 V **
> 
> - **Step 2: **Calculate the *E*<sub>*cell*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup>
> 
>   - ***E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** = *****E***<sub>***red***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** - *****E***<sub>***ox***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>
>   - ***E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** ** = (+0.77) - (+0.34)
>   - ***E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** ** = +0.43 V
> 
> - **Step 3: **Determine the number of electrons transferred in the reaction
> 
>   - The Cu<sup>2+</sup>/Cu has a smaller *E*<sup>ꝋ </sup>value which means that it gets **oxidised**
>   - It **transfers **two electrons to  **two **Fe<sup>3+</sup> ions
>   - Each Fe<sup>3+</sup> ion accepts one electron so the total number of electrons transferred is **two**
> 
> - **Step 4: **Substitute the values in for the standard Gibbs free energy equation
> 
>   - **Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** = - n x *****E***<sub>***cell***</sub><sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** x *****F***
>   - **Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup>** ** = -2 x (+0.43) x 96 500
>   - **Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ **</sup>= -82 990 J mol<sup>-1</sup> = -83 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
