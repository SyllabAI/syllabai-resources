# Energy Cycle Calculations

## Energy Cycle Calculations

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_SnnZTcRwCg2frBFn`

## Energy Cycle Calculations

- The energy cycle involving the enthalpy change of solution (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup>), lattice energy (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>), and enthalpy change of hydration (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>) can be used to calculate the different enthalpy values
- According to Hess’s law, the enthalpy change of the direct and of the indirect route will be the same, such that:

**Δ*****H***<sub>***hyd***</sub><sup>**θ **</sup>**= Δ*****H***<sub>***latt***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>** + Δ*****H***<sub>***sol***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>

- This equation can be rearranged depending on which enthalpy value needs to be calculated
- For example, Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup> can be calculated using:

** Δ*****H***<sub>***latt***</sub><sup>**θ **</sup>**= Δ*****H***<sub>***hyd***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>** - Δ*****H***<sub>***sol***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>

- **Remember**: the total Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> is found by adding the Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> values of both anions and cations together
- **Remember:** take into account the number of each ion when completing calculations

  - For example, MgCl<sub>2</sub> has **two chloride ions**, so when completing calculations this will need to be accounted for
  - In this case, you would need to double the value of the hydration enthalpy, since you are hydrating **2 moles** of chloride ions instead of 1

> **Worked Example**
> Calculate the Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> of the chloride ion in potassium chloride using the following data:
> 
> - Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [KCl] = -711 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [KCl] = +26 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [K<sup>+</sup>] = -322 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> - **Step 1: **Draw the energy cycle of KCl
> 
> ![Diagram illustrating the hydration process of KCl with direct and indirect pathways. Includes equations, arrow annotations, and explanation on ion hydration.](assets/c8820c8a7980-23391-httpscdn-savemyexams-comuploads2021015-1-c.png)
> 
> - **Step 2: **Apply Hess’s law to find Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [Cl<sup>-</sup>]
> 
>   - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> = (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl])
>   - (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[K<sup>+</sup>]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Cl<sup>-</sup>])   =  (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl])
>   - (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Cl<sup>-</sup>])   = (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[KCl]) - (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[K<sup>+</sup>])
> - **Step 3: **Substitute the values to find Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> [Cl<sup>-</sup>]
> 
>   - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [Cl<sup>-</sup>]   = (-711) + (+26) - (-322) = -363 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>

> **Worked Example**
> Calculate the Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup><sub>*hyd*</sub>* *of the magnesium ion in the magnesium chloride using the following data:
> 
> - Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [MgCl<sub>2</sub>] = -2592 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [MgCl<sub>2</sub>] = -55 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [Cl<sup>–</sup>] = -363 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> - **Step 1: **Draw the energy cycle of MgCl<sub>2</sub>
> 
> ![Flowchart of enthalpy changes for MgCl2(s), showing direct and indirect hydration, with equations and explanation of Cl⁻ doubling enthalpy.](assets/037912818768-14161-httpscdn-savemyexams-comuploads2021015-1-c.png)
> 
> - **Step 2: **Apply Hess’s law  to find Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> [Mg<sup>2+</sup>]
> 
>   - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> = (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[MgCl<sub>2</sub>]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ </sup>[MgCl<sub>2</sub>])
>   - (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Mg<sup>2+</sup>]) + (2Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [Cl<sup>-</sup>])   = (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup> [MgCl<sub>2</sub>]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ </sup>[MgCl<sub>2</sub>])
>   - (Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Mg<sup>2+</sup>])   = (Δ*H*<sub>*latt*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[MgCl<sub>2</sub>]) + (Δ*H*<sub>*sol*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[MgCl<sub>2</sub>]) - (2Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Cl<sup>-</sup>])
> - **Step 3: **Substitute the values to find Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Mg<sup>2+</sup>]
> 
>   - Δ*H*<sub>*hyd*</sub><sup>θ</sup>[Mg<sup>2+</sup>]  = (-2592) + (-55) - (2 x -363) = -1921 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
