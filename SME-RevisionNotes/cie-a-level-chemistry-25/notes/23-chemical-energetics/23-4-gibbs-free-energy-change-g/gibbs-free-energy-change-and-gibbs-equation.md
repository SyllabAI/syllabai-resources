# Gibbs Free Energy Change & Gibbs Equation

## The Gibbs Equation

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_jwHMMFYwBVHjq8Bd`

## The Gibbs Equation

### Gibbs free energy

- The feasibility of a reaction does not only depend on the entropy change of the reaction but can also be affected by the enthalpy change
- Therefore, using the entropy change of a reaction **only **to determine the feasibility of a reaction is inaccurate
- The **Gibbs free energy (*****G*****) **is the energy change that takes into account **both** the **entropy** change of a reaction and the **enthalpy** change
- The Gibbs equation is:

**Δ*****G***<sup>**θ**</sup>** = Δ*****H***<sub>***reaction***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>** - *****T*****Δ*****S***<sub>***system***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>

- The units of Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> are in **kJ mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>

  - The units of Δ*H*<sub>*reaction*</sub><sup>θ</sup> are in **kJ mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>
  - The units of T are in K
  - The units of Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ</sup> are in **J K**<sup>**-1 **</sup>**mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>

> **Worked Example**
> Calculate the free energy change for the following reaction at a temperature of 298 K:
> 
> **2NaHCO**<sub>**3 **</sub>**(s) → Na**<sub>**2**</sub>**CO**<sub>**3**</sub>** (s) + H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O (l) + CO**<sub>**2**</sub>** (g)**
> 
> - Δ*H*<sup>θ </sup>= +135 kJ mol<sup>-1       </sup>
> - Δ*S*<sup>θ </sup>= +344 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> - **Step 1:** Convert the entropy value in kilojoules
> 
> Δ*S*<sup>θ </sup>= +344 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1  </sup>÷ 1000 = +0.344 kJ K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1 </sup>
> 
> - **Step 2:** Substitute the terms into the Gibbs Equation
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sub>*reaction*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> – *T*Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup>
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ </sup>= +135 – (298 x 0.344)
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ </sup>= **+32.49 kJ mol**<sup>**-1 **</sup>

> **Exam Hint**
> **Careful: **When calculating **Δ*****G***<sup>**θ **</sup>the value for Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ</sup> must be divided by 1000
> 
> J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1 </sup>$\overset{\div1000}{\rightarrow}$ kJ K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>

## The Gibbs Equation: Calculations

- The Gibbs equation can be used to calculate the Gibbs free energy change of a reaction

**Δ*****G***<sup>**θ**</sup>** = Δ*****H***<sub>***reaction***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>** - *****T*****Δ*****S***<sub>***system***</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>

- The equation can also be rearranged to find values of Δ*H*<sub>*reaction*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup>, Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> or the temperature, *T*
- For example, if for a given reaction, the values of Δ*G*<sup>ꝋ</sup>*,* Δ*H*<sub>*reaction*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> and Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>ꝋ</sup> are given, the temperature can be found by rearranging the Gibbs equation as follows:

***T***** = **$\frac{\DeltaH_{reaction}^{θ}-\DeltaG^{θ}}{\DeltaS_{system}^{θ}}$

> **Worked Example**
> Calculate the Gibbs free energy for the reaction of methanol, CH<sub>3</sub>OH, with hydrogen bromide, HBr, at 298 K.
> 
> **CH**<sub>**3**</sub>**OH (l) + HBr (g) → CH**<sub>**3**</sub>**Br (g) + H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O (l)     Δ*****H***<sub>**r**</sub><sup>**θ**</sup>** = -47 kJ mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>
> 
> - *S*<sup>θ </sup>[CH<sub>3</sub>OH (l)] = +240 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - *S*<sup>θ </sup>[HBr (g)] = +99.0 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - *S*<sup>θ </sup>[H<sub>2</sub>O (l)] = +70.0 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - *S*<sup>θ </sup>[CH<sub>3</sub>Br (g)] = +246 J K<sup>-1 </sup>mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> **Answer:**
> 
> - **Step 1: Calculate Δ*****S***<sub>**system**</sub><sup>**θ **</sup>
> 
> Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ </sup>= Σ*S*<sub>*products*</sub><sup>θ</sup> - Σ*S*<sub>*reactants*</sub><sup>θ</sup>
> 
> Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ </sup>= (*S*<sup>ꝋ</sup> [CH<sub>3</sub>Br (g)] + *S*<sup>θ</sup> [H<sub>2</sub>O (l)]) -  (*S*<sup>θ</sup> [CH<sub>3</sub>OH (l)] + *S*<sup>*θ*</sup><sup> </sup>[HBr (g)])
> 
> Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ </sup>= (246 + 70.0) - (240 + 99.0)
> 
> Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ </sup>= -23.0 J K<sup>-1</sup> mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> - **Step 2: Convert Δ*****S***<sup>**θ **</sup>**into kJ K**<sup>**-1**</sup>** mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>
> 
> Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ </sup>=** **$\frac{-23.0}{1000}$= -0.023 kJ K<sup>-1</sup> mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> - **Step 3: Calculate Δ*****G***<sup>**ꝋ**</sup>
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sub>*reaction*</sub><sup>θ</sup> - *T*Δ*S*<sub>*system*</sub><sup>θ</sup>
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ </sup>= -47 - (298 x -0.023)
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ </sup>= -40.146 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> Δ*G*<sup>θ </sup>= -40.1 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
