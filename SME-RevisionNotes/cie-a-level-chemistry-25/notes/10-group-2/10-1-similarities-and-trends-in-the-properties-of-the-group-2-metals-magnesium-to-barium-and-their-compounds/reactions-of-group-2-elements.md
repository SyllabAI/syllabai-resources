# Reactions of Group 2 Elements

## Reactions of the Group 2 Elements

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_VxkBdpWt7QbVj8qs`

## Reactions of the Group 2 Elements

- The Group 2 elements react with oxygen, water and dilute acids

#### Reaction of Group 2 elements table

| **Group 2 element** | **Reaction with oxygen** | **Reaction with water** | **Reaction with dilute HCl** | **Reaction with dilute H**<sub>**2**</sub>**SO**<sub>**4**</sub> |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mg** | Burns easily Bright white flame | Vigorous reaction with steam Very slow reaction with water | Reacts vigorously | Reacts vigorously |
| **Ca** | Difficult to ignite Red flame | Reacts moderately Hydroxide formed | Reacts vigorously | Reaction is slowed by the formation of a sparingly soluble sulfate layer which coats the metal surface stopping hydrogen bubbles from rising |
| **Sr** | Difficult to ignite Red flame | Reacts rapidly Hydroxide formed | Reacts vigorously | Reaction is quickly stopped by the formation of an insoluble sulfate layer on the metal surface |
| **Ba** | Difficult to ignite Green flame | Reacts vigorously Hydroxide formed | Reacts vigorously | Reaction is quickly stopped by the formation of an insoluble sulfate layer on the metal surface |

#### Group 2 reactions with oxygen & water chemical equations

| **Group 2 element** | **Reaction with oxygen** | **Reaction with water** |
|---|---|---|
| **Mg** | 2Mg (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2MgO (s) | Mg (s) + H<sub>2</sub>O (l) → Mg(OH)<sub>2</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) * |
| **Ca** | 2Ca (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2CaO (s) | Ca (s) + 2H<sub>2</sub>O (l) → Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |
| **Sr** | 2Sr (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2SrO (s) Sr (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → SrO<sub>2</sub> (s) | Sr (s) + 2H<sub>2</sub>O (l) → Sr(OH)<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |
| **Ba** | 2Ba (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → 2BaO (s) Ba (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → BaO<sub>2</sub> (s) | Ba (s) + 2H<sub>2</sub>O (l) → Ba(OH)<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |

*Mg reacts with steam: Mg (s) + H<sub>2</sub>O (g) → MgO (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g)

#### Group 2 reactions with dilute hydrochloric acid & dilute sulfuric acid chemical equations

| **Group 2 element** | **Reaction with dilute HCl** | **Reaction with dilute H**<sub>**2**</sub>**SO**<sub>**4**</sub> |
|---|---|---|
| **Mg** | Mg (s) + 2HCl (aq) → MgCl<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) | Mg (s) + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (aq) → MgSO<sub>4</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |
| **Ca** | Ca (s) + 2HCl (aq) → CaCl<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) | Ca (s) + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (aq) → CaSO<sub>4</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |
| **Sr** | Sr (s) + 2HCl (aq) → SrCl<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) | Sr (s) + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (aq) → SrSO<sub>4</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |
| **Ba** | Ba (s) + 2HCl (aq) → BaCl<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) | Ba (s) + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (aq) → BaSO<sub>4</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub> (g) |

- The reaction of all metals with oxygen follows the following general equation:

**2M (s) + O**<sub>**2**</sub>** (g) → 2MO (s)**

- Where M is any metal in Group 2

  - Remember that Sr and Ba **also **form MO<sub>2</sub>
- The reaction of all metals with water follows the following general equation:

**M (s) + 2H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O (l) → M(OH)**<sub>**2**</sub>** (s) + H**<sub>**2**</sub>** (g)**

- The exceptions to this general equation are:

  - Be which does not react with water
  - Mg which forms MgO (s) and H<sub>2</sub> (g)

- The reaction of all metals with dilute HCl follows the following general equation:

**M (s) + 2HCl (aq) → MCl**<sub>**2 **</sub>**(aq) + H**<sub>**2 **</sub>**(g)**

- The reaction of all metals with dilute H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> follows the following general equation:

**M (s) + H**<sub>**2**</sub>**SO**<sub>**4 **</sub>**(aq) → MSO**<sub>**4 **</sub>**(aq) + H**<sub>**2 **</sub>**(g)**

- Remember that CaSO<sub>4</sub>, SrSO<sub>4 </sub>and BaSO<sub>4</sub> are insoluble

> **Exam Hint**
> Learn the general equation for the reaction with oxygen, water and dilute HCl / H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> and the exceptions instead of memorizing the entire table!
