# Solubility Product

## The Solubility Product, Ksp

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_phqcyxJvJchDcTMN`

## The Solubility Product, Ksp

- **Solubility **is defined as the **number of grams **or **moles **of compound needed to **saturate **100 g of **water**, or it can also be defined in terms of 1 kg of water, at a given temperature

  - For example, sodium chloride (NaCl) is considered to be a **soluble **salt as a saturated solution contains 36 g of NaCl per 100 g of water
  - Lead chloride (PbCl<sub>2</sub>) on the other hand is an **insoluble **salt as a saturated solution only contains 0.99 g of PbCl<sub>2</sub> per 100 g of water

### Solubility product

- The **solubility product **(***K***<sub>***sp***</sub>) is:

  - The product of the concentrations of each ion in a saturated solution of a relatively soluble salt
  - At 298 K
  - Raised to the power of their relative concentrations

**C (s) ⇌ aA**<sup>**x+**</sup>** (aq) + bB**<sup>**y-**</sup>** (aq)**

***K***<sub>***sp***</sub>*** *****= [A**<sup>**x+**</sup>** (aq)]**<sup>**a**</sup>** [B**<sup>**y-**</sup>** (aq)]**<sup>**b**</sup>

- When an **undissolved ionic compound** is in contact with a **saturated solution of its ions**, an equilibrium is established
- The ions move from the solid to the saturated solution at the same rate as they move from the solution to the solid

  - For example, the undissolved magnesium chloride (MgCl<sub>2</sub>) is in equilibrium with a saturated solution of its ions

**MgCl**<sub>**2**</sub>** (s) ⇌ Mg**<sup>**2+**</sup>** (aq) + 2Cl**<sup>**-**</sup>** (aq)**

#### Ions in a saturated solution

![Diagram showing dynamic equilibrium of MgCl2 solution. Arrows indicate ion exchange between undissolved salt layer and saturated solution.](../../../assets/33fa1e07f6b7-15771-httpscdn-savemyexams-comuploads20250614209.png)

*When the undissolved magnesium chloride salt comes in contact with its ions in a saturated solution, an equilibrium between the salt and ions is established*

- The **solubility product **for this equilibrium is:

***K***<sub>***sp***</sub>*** =***** [Mg**<sup>**2+**</sup>** (aq)] [Cl**<sup>**-**</sup>** (aq)]**<sup>**2**</sup>

- The *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> is only useful for **sparingly soluble salts**
- The smaller the value of *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>, the lower the solubility of the salt

## Ksp Expressions

- The general equilibrium expression for the **solubility product** (*K*<sub>*sp*</sub>) is:

**C (s) ⇌ aA**<sup>**x+**</sup>** (aq) + bB**<sup>**y-**</sup>** (aq)**

***K***<sub>***sp***</sub>*** *****= [A**<sup>**x+**</sup>** (aq)]**<sup>**a**</sup>** [B**<sup>**y-**</sup>** (aq)]**<sup>**b**</sup>

> **Worked Example**
> **Expressing *****K***<sub>***sp***</sub>** of ionic compounds**
> 
> - Give the equilibrium expressions, including units, for the solubility products of the following ionic compounds:
> 
>   1. Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub>
>   2. Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
>   3. SnCO<sub>3</sub>
> 
> **Answer 1 - Ca(OH)**<sub>**2**</sub>**:**
> 
> - **Step 1** - Write the balanced chemical equation:
> 
> Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub> (s) $\rightleftharpoons$ Ca<sup>2+</sup> (aq) + 2OH<sup>–</sup> (aq)
> 
> - **Step 2** - Write the *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *expression:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [Ca<sup>2+</sup> (aq)] [OH<sup>–</sup> (aq)]<sup>2</sup>
> 
> - **Step 3 **- Deduce the units:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>] x [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>2</sup>
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>3</sup>
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *= mol<sup>3</sup> dm<sup>-9</sup>
> 
> **Answer 2 - Fe**<sub>**2**</sub>**O**<sub>**3**</sub>**:**
> 
> - **Step 1** - Write the balanced chemical equation:
> 
> Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> (s) $\rightleftharpoons$ 2Fe<sup>3+</sup> (aq) + 3O<sup>2–</sup> (aq)
> 
> - **Step 2** - Write the *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *expression:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [Fe<sup>3+</sup> (aq)]<sup>2</sup> [O<sup>2–</sup> (aq)]<sup>3</sup>
> 
> 1. **Step 3 **- Deduce the units:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>2</sup> x [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>3</sup>
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>5</sup>
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *= mol<sup>5</sup> dm<sup>-15</sup>
> 
> **Answer 3 - SnCO**<sub>**3**</sub>**:**
> 
> - **Step 1** - Write the balanced chemical equation:
> 
> SnCO<sub>3</sub> (s) $\rightleftharpoons$ Sn<sup>2+</sup> (aq) + CO<sub>3</sub><sup>2–</sup> (aq)
> 
> - **Step 2** - Write the *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *expression:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [Sn<sup>2+</sup> (aq)] [CO<sub>3</sub><sup>2–</sup> (aq)]
> 
> - **Step 3 **- Deduce the units:
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>] x [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub> = [mol dm<sup>-3</sup>]<sup>2</sup>
> 
> *K*<sub>*sp*</sub>* *= mol<sup>2</sup> dm<sup>-6</sup>
