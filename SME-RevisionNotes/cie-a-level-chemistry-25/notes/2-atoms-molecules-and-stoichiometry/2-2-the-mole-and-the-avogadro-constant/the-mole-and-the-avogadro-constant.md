# The Mole & the Avogadro Constant

## The Mole & the Avogadro Constant

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_VnC68jpxv3TKmwXX`

## The Mole & the Avogadro Constant

- The **Avogadro constant **(*N*<sub>A</sub> or *L*) is the number of particles equivalent to the relative **atomic** **mass** or **molecular mass **of a substance

  - The Avogadro constant applies to atoms, molecules, ions and electrons
- The value of *N*<sub>A</sub> is **6.02 x 10**<sup>**23**</sup>** g mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>
- The mass of a substance with this number of particles is called a **mole **(mol)

  - The **mass** of a substance containing the same number of fundamental units as there are atoms in exactly 12.00 g of <sup>12</sup>C
- One mole of any element is equal to the relative atomic mass of that element in grams

  - One mole of carbon, that is if you had 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> atoms of carbon in your hand, would have a mass of 12 g
  - One mole of water would have a mass of (2 x 1 + 16) = 18 g

> **Worked Example**
> Determine the number of atoms, molecules and the relative mass of 1 mole of:
> 
> 1. Na
> 2. H<sub>2</sub>
> 3. NaCl
> 
> **Answer 1**
> 
> - The relative atomic mass of Na is 23.0
> - Therefore, 1 mol of Na has a mass of 23.0 g mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - 1 mol of Na will contain 6.02 x 10<sup>23 </sup>atoms of Na (Avogadro’s constant)
> 
> **Answer 2**
> 
> - The relative atomic mass of H is 1.0
> - Since there are 2 H atoms in H<sub>2</sub>, the mass of 1 mol of H<sub>2</sub> is (2 x 1.0) = 2.0 g mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - 1 mol of H<sub>2</sub> will contain 6.02 x 10<sup>23 </sup>molecules of H<sub>2</sub>
> - Since there are 2 H atoms in H<sub>2</sub>, 1 mol of H<sub>2</sub> will contain 2 x 6.02 x 10<sup>23 </sup> = 1.204 x 10<sup>24 </sup>H atoms
> 
> **Answer 3**
> 
> - The relative atomic mass of Na and Cl is 23.0 and 35.5 respectively
> - Therefore, 1 mol of NaCl has a mass of (23.0 + 35.5) = 58.5 g mol<sup>-1</sup>
> - 1 mol of NaCl will contain 6.02 x 10<sup>23 </sup>molecules of NaCl
> - Since there is one Na and one Cl atom in NaCl, 1 mol of NaCl will contain 2 x 6.02 x 10<sup>23 </sup> = 1.204 x 10<sup>24 </sup>atoms in total
> 
> | **1 mole of** | **Number of atoms** | **Number of molecules** | **Relative mass (g mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>**)** |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **Na** | 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> | - | 23.0 |
> | **H**<sub>**2**</sub> | 1.204 x 10<sup>24 </sup> | 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> | 2.0 |
> | **NaCl** | 1.204 x 10<sup>24 </sup> | 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> | 58.5 |
