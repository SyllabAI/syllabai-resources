# Changes Affecting the Equilibrium Constant

## Changes that Affect the Equilibrium Constant

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_74qGNPtqhh3SNbcd`

## Changes that Affect the Equilibrium Constant

### Changes in concentration

- If all other conditions stay the same, the equilibrium constant* K*<sub>*c*</sub> is **not affected** by any changes in concentration of the reactants or products
- For example, the decomposition of hydrogen iodide:

**2HI ⇌ H**<sub>**2**</sub>** + I**<sub>**2**</sub>

- The equilibrium expression is:

***K***<sub>**c**</sub>** = **`(stretchy left square bracket H _ 2 stretchy right square bracket stretchy left square bracket I _ 2 stretchy right square bracket)/(stretchy left square bracket HI stretchy right square bracket ^ 2)`**= 6.25 x 10**<sup>**–3**</sup>

- Adding more HI makes the ratio of [ products ] to [ reactants ] smaller
- To restore equilibrium, [H<sub>2</sub>] and [I<sub>2</sub>] increase and [HI] decreases
- Equilibrium is restored when the ratio is 6.25 x 10<sup>-3</sup> again

### Changes in pressure

- A change in pressure **only **changes the **position** **of the equilibrium **(see [Le Chatelier’s principle](https://www.savemyexams.com/a-level/chemistry/cie/25/revision-notes/7-equilibria/7-1-chemical-equilibria-reversible-reactions-and-dynamic-equilibrium/le-chateliers-principle/))
- If all other conditions stay the same, the equilibrium constant* K*<sub>*c*</sub> is **not affected **by any changes in the pressure of the reactants and products

### Changes in temperature

- Changes in temperature **change **the equilibrium constant *K*<sub>*c*</sub>
- For an endothermic reaction such as:

2HI (g) $\rightleftharpoons$ H<sub>2</sub> (g) + I<sub>2</sub> (g)   *K*<sub>c</sub> = $\frac{[ \mathrm{H}_{2} ] [ \mathrm{I}_{2} ]}{[ \text{HI} ]^{2}}$

- With an increase in temperature:

  - [H<sub>2</sub>] and [I<sub>2</sub>] **increases**
  - [HI] **decreases**
  - Because [H<sub>2</sub>] and [I<sub>2</sub>] **increase** and [HI] **decreases**, the equilibrium constant *K*<sub>*c*</sub> **increases**

- For an exothermic reaction such as:

2SO<sub>2</sub> (g) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) $\rightleftharpoons$ 2SO<sub>3</sub> (g)   *K*<sub>c</sub> = $\frac{[ SO_{3} ]^{2}}{[ SO_{2} ]^{2} [ \mathrm{O}_{2} ]}$

- With an increase in temperature:

  - [SO<sub>3</sub>] **decreases**
  - [SO<sub>2</sub>] and [O<sub>2</sub>] **increases**
  - Because [SO<sub>3</sub>] **decreases** and [SO<sub>2</sub>] and [O<sub>2</sub>] **increase**, the equilibrium constant *K*<sub>*c*</sub> **decreases**

### Presence of a catalyst

- If all other conditions stay the same, the equilibrium constant* K*<sub>*c*</sub> is **not affected **by the presence of a catalyst
- A catalyst speeds up both the forward and reverse reactions at the same rate so the ratio of [ products ] to [ reactants ] remains unchanged

> **Worked Example**
> **Factors affecting *****K***<sub>***c***</sub>** **
> 
> An equilibrium is established in the following reaction:
> 
> AB (aq) + CD (aq) $\rightleftharpoons$ AC (aq) + BD (aq)   Δ*H* = +180 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> Which factors would affect the value of *K*<sub>c</sub> in this equilibrium?
> 
> **Answer**
> 
> - Only a change in temperature will affect the value of *K*<sub>*c*</sub>
> - Any other changes in conditions would result in the position of the equilibrium moving to oppose this change
> - Adding a catalyst increases the rate of reaction meaning the state of equilibrium will be reached faster but has no effect on the position of the equilibrium and, therefore, *K*<sub>*c*</sub> is unchanged

> **Worked Example**
> **Factors which increase *****K***<sub>***p***</sub>*** *****value**
> 
> What will increase the value of *K*<sub>p</sub> for the following equilibrium?
> 
> 2A (g) + B (g) $\rightleftharpoons$ 2C (g)   Δ*H* = +6.5 kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>
> 
> **Answer**
> 
> - Only temperature changes permanently affect the value of *K*<sub>p</sub>
> - An increase in temperature shifts the reaction in favour of the products
> - The [ products ] increases and [ reactants ] decreases, therefore, the *K*<sub>*p*</sub>* *value increases
