## Multi-step Synthetic Routes

- Many organic molecules are made in multi-step synthetic routes
- You should be able to recall the different reactions each functional group undergoes and apply this knowledge when devising multi-step synthetic routes for preparing organic molecules

> **Worked Example**
> Devise multi-step synthetic routes for preparing the following organic molecules from propan-1-ol
> 
> 1. 2-hydroxybutanoic acid
> 2. Ethyl propanoate
> 3. Sodium propanoate salt
> 
> **Answers:**
> 
> 1. **2-hydroxybutanoic acid** ![Three-step conversion of propan-1-ol to 2-hydroxybutanoic acid: oxidation forms propanal, then nucleophilic addition and hydrolysis yield final product.](assets/514fe6bf2729-3-9-organic-synthesis-answer-1-worked-example-de.png)
> 2. **Ethyl propanoate** ![Step 1: Oxidation of propan-1-ol to propanoic acid. Step 2: Condensation of propanoic acid with ethanol to form ethyl propanoate and water.](assets/42f3d7dfc363-3-9-organic-synthesis-answer-2-worked-example-de.png)
> 3. **Sodium propanoate salt** ![Step-by-step chemical reactions: oxidation of propan-1-ol, esterification forming ethyl propanoate, and hydrolysis resulting in sodium propanoate and ethanol.](assets/1f8c51a10ad8-3-9-organic-synthesis-answer-3-worked-example-de.png)
