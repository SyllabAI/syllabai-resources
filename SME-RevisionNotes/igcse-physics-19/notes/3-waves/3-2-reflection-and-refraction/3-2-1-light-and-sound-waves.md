# Light & Sound Waves

## Light

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_gvcwsrCzCT37wtbH` · know that light waves are transverse waves and that they can be reflected and refracted  

## Light

- Visible light is a part of the [Electromagnetic spectrum](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-1-waves-and-the-electromagnetic-spectrum/3-1-5-electromagnetic-em-waves/) which means it is a ***transverse wave***

  - This is explained in [Transverse & longitudinal waves](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-1-waves-and-the-electromagnetic-spectrum/3-1-1-transverse-and-longitudinal-waves/)

#### Representing a transverse wave

![Transverse wave diagram, downloadable AS & A Level Physics revision notes](../../../assets/8f8f4c2bd999-7-1-2-1-transverse-wave-diagram-e1631727269728.png)

***Light waves are transverse: the particles vibrate in a perpendicular direction to the energy transfer***

- Light can undergo:

  - [Reflection](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-2-reflection-and-refraction/3-2-2-reflection-and-refraction/)
  - [Refraction](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-2-reflection-and-refraction/3-2-2-reflection-and-refraction/)

## Sound

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_vzbHjvmTf9vGgShj` · know that sound waves are longitudinal waves which can be reflected and refracted 

## Sound

- Sound waves are ***longitudinal waves***

  - This is explained in [Transverse & longitudinal waves](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-1-waves-and-the-electromagnetic-spectrum/3-1-1-transverse-and-longitudinal-waves/)

- Longitudinal waves are usually drawn as several lines to show that the wave is moving **parallel** to the direction of energy transfer

  - Drawing the lines closer together represents the **compressions**
  - Drawing the lines further apart represents the **rarefactions**

#### Representing a longitudinal wave

![Longitudinal wave diagram, downloadable AS & A Level Physics revision notes](../../../assets/bda0eb324083-7-1-2-1-longitudinal-wave-diagram.png)

***Longitudinal waves are represented as sets of lines with rarefactions and compressions***

- Sound can also undergo:

  - [Reflection](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-2-reflection-and-refraction/3-2-2-reflection-and-refraction/)
  - [Refraction](https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/physics/edexcel/19/revision-notes/3-waves/3-2-reflection-and-refraction/3-2-2-reflection-and-refraction/)
- The reflection of a sound wave is called an **echo**
