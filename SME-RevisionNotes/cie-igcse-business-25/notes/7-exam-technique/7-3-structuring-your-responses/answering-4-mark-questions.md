# Answering 4-Mark Questions

## How do I answer a CIE IGCSE Business 4-mark question?

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_sNXjvhgQMKnbRFDN`

## How do I answer a CIE IGCSE Business 4-mark question?

### State or Identify questions

- In these questions you need to either

  - **Name**,** select **or** recognise** four distinct points
  - **Express**,  **in clear terms**, four distinct points

### Example

| **State the four elements of the marketing mix.** |

## Outline questions

> **Spec point** — `spcpt_9Ydpb9SnYqX5WQ7b`

## Outline questions

- In these questions you need to either

  - Set out **two main points**, with **reference to the case study**
  - Up to **two knowledge**** [K] ****marks** are available for stating key points
  - Up to **two application**** [Ap]**** marks** are available for relevant references to the case study

### Example

- The response below would achieve 4 marks as it

  - Clearly identifies two distinct reasons, each with specific **references to the business**
