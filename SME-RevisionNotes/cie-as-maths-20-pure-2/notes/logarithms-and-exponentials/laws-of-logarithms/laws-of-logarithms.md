## Laws of logarithms

### What are the laws of logarithms?

![Laws of Logarithms Notes fig1, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/f463b06c8290-6-2-1-laws-of-logarithms-notes-fig1.png)

- There are many laws or rules of indices, for example

  - **a**<sup>**m**</sup>** x a**<sup>**n**</sup>** = a**<sup>**m+n**</sup>
  - **(a**<sup>**m**</sup>**)**<sup>**n**</sup>** = a**<sup>**mn**</sup>
- There are equivalent laws of logarithms (for a > 0)

  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}xy=\mathrm{log}_{a}x+\mathrm{log}_{a}y$
  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}(\frac{x}{y})=\mathrm{log}_{a}x-\mathrm{log}_{a}y$
  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}x^{k}=k\mathrm{log}_{a}x$

![Laws of Logarithms Notes fig2, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/b3870da6fd3f-6-2-1-laws-of-logarithms-notes-fig2.png)

- There are also some particular results these lead to

  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}a=1$
  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}a^{x}=x$
  - $a^{\mathrm{log}_{a}x}=x$
  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}1=0$
  - $\mathrm{log}_{a}(\frac{1}{x})=-\mathrm{log}_{a}x$

![Laws of Logarithms Notes fig3, A Level & AS Level Pure Maths Revision Notes](../../../assets/c9b61f5fe62a-6-2-1-laws-of-logarithms-notes-fig3.png)

- Two of these were seen in the notes Logarithmic Functions
- Beware …

  - … **log (x + y) ≠ log x + log y**
- Results apply to** ln **too

  - $\mathrm{ln}x\equiv\mathrm{log}_{e}x$
  - In particular $e^{\mathrm{ln}x}=x$ and $\mathrm{ln}(e^{x})=x$
