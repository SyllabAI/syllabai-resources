## "e"

### What is the number "e" in maths?

- **e** is an *irrational* number, sometimes called Euler's number

  - **e ≈ 2.718**

### How do I sketch y = e<sup>x</sup>?

- The exponential function is **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup>

  - As with other exponential graphs **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> ![e Notes fig1, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](assets/31a3bbcb7f0f-6-1-3-e-notes-fig1.png)
  
    - passes through (0, 1)
    - has the x-axis as an asymptote

### What is the special property of "e"?

- **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> has the particular property that

$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{x}$

- ie for *every* real number **x**, the gradient of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup> is also equal to **e**<sup>**x **</sup>(see Derivatives of Exponential Functions)

![e Notes fig3, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/3df93ed751ea-6-1-3-e-notes-fig3.png)

### How do I sketch y = e<sup>-x</sup>?

- **y = e**<sup>**-x**</sup> is a reflection in the **y**-axis of **y = e**<sup>**x**</sup>
- They are of the form **y = f(x)** and **y = f(-x) **(see Transformations of Functions - Reflections) ![e Notes fig4, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](assets/484361008669-6-1-3-e-notes-fig4.png)

### What is exponential growth and decay?

![e Notes fig5, A Level & AS Maths: Pure revision notes](../../../assets/54c3253d089f-6-1-3-e-notes-fig5.png)

** **

- **y = Ae**<sup>**kx**</sup> (**k > 0**) is exponential growth
- **y = Ae**<sup>**-kx**</sup> (**k > 0**) is exponential decay
- **A** is the initial value
- **k** is a (usually positive) constant
- “-“ is used in the equation making clear whether it is growth or decay
