## What the box is

An ADDITIVE, authored restatement of the section prose in plain student
register. It never replaces, contradicts or re-cites the SB-derived prose —
it answers "say that again, but human". Placement: FIRST box after the
section prose (before :::key-point).

## The seven rules

1. **2–5 sentences.** One idea per sentence; short sentences; no lists, no
   headings, no "In this section..." fluff (START-HERE §4 bar applies).
2. **Student register, authoritative voice.** Direct second person. Everyday
   anchors are encouraged (brick wall, puddle, number line) but every anchor
   must be chemistry-true.
3. **Chemistry guardrail (hard):** spaces between particles change, particles
   never expand; melting/freezing meet at one temperature; evaporation is
   surface-only below the boiling point; diffusion runs high -> low
   concentration until even. Never import a misconception to be friendly.
4. **Voice law inheritance (lesson-prose@2.0.0):** teach, never attribute; no
   QA leakage, no chunk ids, no page deflection, no platform self-naming.
5. **Chips in PLAIN form only** (`[[slug]]` — the corpus lint grammar; the
   pipe form fails lessonctl V6). Only slugs already in the lesson glossary.
6. **No mark-scheme quotes here** — MS lines belong in :::exam-hint boxes
   (verbatim-true + paper/question ref, per f-0041).
7. **Banned-phrase list applies** (lint_rules/chem.yaml): "free electrons",
   unqualified "sea of electrons", "particles expand", "the equilibrium
   shifts to use up", platform self-naming, print-page deflection regexes.
