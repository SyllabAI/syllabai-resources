# PRO-06 — Worked-Reasoning Patterns

## Purpose

This artifact catalogues the worked-reasoning patterns of record in 4CH1: the
official exemplar booklets that pair real student responses with examiner
commentary and mark tallies, the full published reasoning chains inside the
mark schemes (linked point-pairs with cause-effect structure), the one-line
decision reasonings with their alternates, and the credit-note conventions that
show how a reasoning step is allowed to be expressed — for sources SRC-TL-4CH1
and SRC-QPMS-4CH1. Claims ride only on this artifact's own bounded pack excerpts.
Canonical planes are untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** exemplar response/commentary/mark-tally apparatus; two-pair linked
cause-effect chains; decision reasonings with alternate credit; identity credit
notes (name/formula pairs); examiner verdicts on reasoning quality.

**Out of scope:** the frameworks layer (PRO-01) and multi-step chaining
(PRO-03).

## Executive Summary

The worked-reasoning corpus is a published apparatus. The exemplars booklets
reproduce real student responses — including a June 2019 series booklet — and
walk the examining team's marking over them, so a reasoning pattern is visible
as response, verdict, and mark tally together. Inside the mark schemes, complete
reasoning chains are printed: two linked point-pairs (baseline in ink, therefore
it interferes; water above the spots, therefore the inks mix) show cause-effect
reasoning as the markable unit. One-line decision reasonings carry their
alternates (helium inert versus hydrogen flammable), and identity answers show
their credit conventions (either name or formula, with mismatched pairs
ignored). The examiner's verdicts then grade reasoning at the chemistry-language
level — ions, not molecules, in dilute solution.

## Main Analysis

### PRO-030 — The exemplar apparatus: response, commentary, tally

**Claim:** worked reasoning is trained through a published apparatus that pairs
real student responses with examiner commentaries and mark tallies.
**Evidence:** the chemistry exemplars booklet explains that it shows "real
student responses to these questions" and how the examining team follow the
mark scheme (SRC-TL-4CH1, international-gcse-chemistry-exemplars-commentary.pdf
p 3); the double-award booklet is titled as "Exemplars with examiner commentaries
June 2019 examinations" and structures each item as exemplar response A and B
with commentary (SRC-TL-4CH1, ig-chemistry-science-double-award-paper-1c-exemplar-responses.pdf p 2, p 3); the first chemistry exemplar prints the
malachite method and works the marking over it (SRC-TL-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-exemplars-commentary.pdf p 4). Temporal scope:
CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-031 — Examiner verdicts grade reasoning language

**Claim:** the commentary grades reasoning at the level of chemical language,
with the mark tally shown.
**Evidence:** the part (c) verdict — printed as "3/5 Examiner Comments" — states
that "In dilute solution HCl exists as ions, not molecules" and requires the
answer to state what the ions are (SRC-TL-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-exemplars-commentary.pdf p 8); the second response
follows for comparison (SRC-TL-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-exemplars-commentary.pdf p 9). Temporal scope:
CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-032 — Two-pair linked reasoning chains published in full

**Claim:** the mark schemes publish complete cause-effect reasoning chains as
linked point-pairs — the reasoning pattern itself is the markable object.
**Evidence:** the chromatography-mistakes scheme prints "Explanations that link
together the following two pairs of points:" — baseline in ink and therefore it
will interfere with the results; water level above the ink spots and therefore
the inks will mix with the water — each pair carrying its own marks
(SRC-QPMS-4CH1, 2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 256-287). Temporal scope:
CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-033 — One-line decision reasonings with alternates

**Claim:** single-step reasonings are published with their tolerated alternate
wordings attached.
**Evidence:** the airship scheme credits "helium is inert / helium does not
react (with air/oxygen)" with the unreactive and not-flammable alternates
allowed, against "hydrogen is flammable/explosive" for the hydrogen side
(SRC-QPMS-4CH1, 2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 358-380); the catalyst
purpose accepts "to increase the rate of reaction / to speed up the" reaction
with the activation-energy lowering alternate (SRC-QPMS-4CH1,
2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 391-405). Temporal scope: CURRENT —
AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-034 — Identity credit notes

**Claim:** identity answers carry their own credit conventions — either the
name or the formula, with mismatched pairs handled by note.
**Evidence:** the precipitate identity is credited as "silver bromide / AgBr If
correct name given ignore incorrect formula" (SRC-QPMS-4CH1,
2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 174-188); the ion identity is credited as
"Li+ ALLOW Sr2+" where the evidence supports either (SRC-QPMS-4CH1,
2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 172-186). Temporal scope: CURRENT —
AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-035 — Reasoning preserved against the question's own scenario

**Claim:** worked reasonings anchor to the question's scenario rather than
generic statements — the Hindenburg framing makes the reasoning situational.
**Evidence:** the airship item sets the 1937 hydrogen airship scenario and the
scheme's credited reasoning reads in those terms — helium inert against air or
oxygen, hydrogen flammable or explosive (SRC-QPMS-4CH1,
2019-06/4CH1-1C/questions.md lines 358-380). Temporal scope: CURRENT —
AUTHORITATIVE.
### PRO-060 — The withheld mark and its printed reason

**Claim:** the commentary withholds a mark with its reason printed in full: the candidate's answer contained the accepted ion-language claim but not the successful-collision statement, so the second mark could not be awarded. The apparatus therefore publishes not only verdicts that award but the exact missing element behind a non-award — the negative space of the mark scheme made explicit.
**Evidence:** the printed verdict reads "3/5 Examiner Comments" and states "In dilute solution HCl exists as ions, not molecules. It is also necessary to state that there are more particles/ions in the same volume." with the withhold "The second mark could not be awarded since the candidate did not state that there are more successful collisions per unit time." (SRC-TL-4CH1, igcse-chemistry/teaching-and-learning-materials/international-gcse-chemistry-exemplars-commentary.pdf p8).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-061 — The improvement-guidance strand in the exemplar apparatus

**Claim:** the exemplar booklets publish an improvement strand alongside the grading decision: each example carries the marker's decision with commentary that explains the decision and guides how the answer can be improved to earn more marks, and the responses are selected to span achievement levels. The apparatus is therefore forward-looking — it grades the response as given and simultaneously models the better answer.
**Evidence:** the booklet structure prints "Example of the marker grading decision based on the mark scheme, accompanied by examiner commentary including the explanation for the decision and guidance on how the answer can be improved to earn more marks" and "The examples highlight the achievement of the assessment objectives at lower to higher levels of candidate responses." (SRC-TL-4CH1, igcse-chemistry/teaching-and-learning-materials/ig-chemistry-science-double-award-paper-1c-exemplar-responses.pdf p3).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PRO-062 — The non-creditworthy ruling bounding creditable content

**Claim:** the commentary can rule a whole paragraph non-creditworthy and, in the same sentence, bound the creditable answer set: the obvious diagram mistakes are named as the only two answers the marking recognised. The worked apparatus thus publishes a boundary on acceptable content for the item — a ruling that tells the reader what the space of creditable responses contains, not only which responses earned what.
**Evidence:** the verdict prints "This response was given 2 marks." with the ruling "The first paragraph in this response is not creditworthy, as the obvious mistakes in the diagram are the baseline being drawn in ink and the water level being above the inks" (SRC-TL-4CH1, igcse-chemistry/teaching-and-learning-materials/ig-chemistry-science-double-award-paper-1c-exemplar-responses.pdf p6).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
