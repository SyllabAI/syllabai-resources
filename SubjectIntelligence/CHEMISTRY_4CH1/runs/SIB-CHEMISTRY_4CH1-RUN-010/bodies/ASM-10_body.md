# ASM-10 — Assessment Coverage Analysis

## Purpose

This artifact cross-reads the specification's topic frame against the question topics
observed in the official exam corpus of record (SRC-QPMS-4CH1), producing a grounded
coverage view: which areas of the specification the held papers demonstrably probe,
and where the excerpt evidence stops. It closes the ASM family by tying its
structural findings (ASM-01..ASM-09) back to the specification of record. Every claim
is carried on a bounded, locator-addressed excerpt from the run's evidence pack.
Research artifact; canonical planes untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** the specification's topic/sub-topic frame; the observed topic coverage of
the held papers; the assessment-objective envelope; and the disclosed boundaries of
this coverage view.

**Grounding:** SRC-SPEC-4CH1 (Issue 3 — Topic 1 sub-topic list page and the
assessment-objectives page) and SRC-QPMS-4CH1 (topic-marker excerpts across the
2019-2023 papers of the pack).

**Out of scope:** statement-by-statement coverage mapping (canonical SpecPoints
planes are read-only and this run does not rewrite them); legacy 4CH0 coverage (HIS
family).

## Executive Summary

The specification frames the content as four numbered topics, with Topic 1
(Principles of chemistry) itself split into nine named sub-topics, (a) States of
matter through (i) Electrolysis. The held papers demonstrably probe that frame: their
question announcements name principles topics (bonding, atomic structure), organic
chemistry (alkanes, alkenes, crude oil fractions) and the physical/inorganic strands,
with topic-marker excerpts appearing across 2019-2023 sessions. The
assessment-objective page fixes the coverage envelope in band form (AO1/AO2 at
38-42% each, AO3 at 19-21%). The honest boundary: excerpt windows prove presence,
not absence — areas not visible in the excerpt net are recorded as not-proven rather
than uncovered.
