
# PED-04 — Explanation Strategies

## Purpose

This artifact catalogues the explanation strategies the 4CH1 evidence of record
actually deploys: imagination- and model-based explanation in the student book
(SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1), research-informed strategy guides for the hard topics
(SRC-TL-4CH1), examiner-verified explanation routes (SRC-ER-4CH1), and
spec-point-bound strategy notes (SRC-SME-NOTES-4CH1). Claims ride only on this
artifact's own bounded pack excerpts. Canonical planes are untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** imagine-style cognitive anchoring; models with stated limits;
worked-example ladders; alternative-route hints; diagram-first explanations;
examiner-observed multiple valid routes; note-level explanation scaffolds.

**Out of scope:** when to teach what (PED-01) and error diagnosis (PED-09).

## Executive Summary

The dominant explanation strategy in the book is cognitive anchoring: abstract
amounts and probabilities are explained by inviting the reader to imagine
countable, concrete scenarios (a mole of carbon-12, sixty-four blue squares
changing colour, a world without fossil fuels). Models are explained with their
limits attached — the probability model is followed by a technical caution about
smooth curves — and calculations are explained through worked-example ladders
that interpret equations in moles. Hints offer a second route to the same result
rather than a different result. The examiner report confirms the reward
structure behind this: full credit is given for different valid approaches to
the same calculation, and named difficulties (drawing branched polymer
structures) confirm where explanation effort pays. Topic guides supply the
strategy layer for the two hardest explanatory jumps in the course — bonding and
equilibrium — from published education research.
The deepened records extend the strategy claim into mechanism and inference: the book's own equilibrium ladder (pressure and temperature rules summarised, then walked through the nitrogen dioxide colour demonstration with the visible outcome stated), the mechanism-first explanations that ground rules in theory (the activation-energy gate behind catalysts, the Bronsted-Lowry generalisation behind the hydroxonium ion), and the inference-from-observation pattern in which measured properties suggest the bonding model and energy accounting fixes the diagram.

## Main Analysis

### PED-021 — Imagination-based cognitive anchoring

**Claim:** the book's core explanation strategy is to anchor abstractions in
imagine-style concrete scenarios the reader can count or picture.
**Evidence:** the mole is introduced as "Imagine you had 1 mole of" carbon-12
with its 12 g mass and enormous atom count worked through
(SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 165); the
probability treatment of rate asks the reader to "Imagine you started with 64
blue squares" with a one-in-four chance of changing colour each second
(SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 831); a
crude-oil counterfactual opens with "Imagine a world in which fossil fuels" had
never formed (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line
938). Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PED-022 — Models taught with their limits attached

**Claim:** explanatory models are taught together with their correctness limits,
so the model is not mistaken for the mechanism.
**Evidence:** immediately after the blue-squares probability picture, the book
cautions that smooth plots can be "technically wrong" because a smooth curve
should only be used for a continuous indicator (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1,
edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 739); the enthalpy hint attaches
its own convention caveat, that energy given out is shown with a negative sign
(SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 765).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PED-025 — Diagram-first explanations for structure and process

**Claim:** structural and process topics lead with diagrams — energy level

1036). Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### PED-072 — The book's equilibrium ladder: rules summarised, then a visible demonstration

**Claim:** the book explains equilibrium by first summarising the condition rules, then walking a single colour-change system so the abstract shift becomes visible.
**Evidence:** the rules land as a summary — "Increasing pressure: the position of equilibrium shifts to the side which has fewer gas molecules." with the temperature logic worked through the enthalpy sign (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 840); the demonstration system is then named — "AN EXAMPLE TO ILLUSTRATE HOW CHANGING REACTION CONDITIONS CAN AFFECT THE POSITION OF EQUILIBRIUM IN A REVERSIBLE REACTION" on the nitrogen dioxide/dinitrogen tetroxide pair — and each condition's outcome is stated with its observable: pressure shifts are walked molecule-count first (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 843), and heating closes the loop — "If you increase the temperature, the position of equilibrium will shift to lower it again – the position of equilibrium shifts in the reverse, endothermic direction." so "more nitrogen dioxide will be formed and the colour of the gas will darken." (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 844).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
### PED-073 — Mechanism-first explanations ground the rules in theory

**Claim:** where a rule could stay memorised, the book instead grounds it in mechanism: the activation-energy gate behind catalysis, and the proton-donor theory behind the hydroxonium-ion writing convention.
**Evidence:** the catalyst explanation starts from the collision gate — "Not all collisions result in a reaction happening. Collisions have to involve at least a certain minimum amount of energy, called the activation energy" — so a slow reaction means very few collisions clear it (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 815); the acid-base rules are then re-derived from theory — "To understand the reaction between ammonia and hydrochloric acid in relation to the Arrhenius theory we have to realise that ammonia reacts with water to form OH- ions" before the generalisation — "An acid is a proton (hydrogen ion) donor." / "A base is a proton (hydrogen ion) acceptor." — named as the Bronsted-Lowry theory, with the hydroxonium ion disclosed as what "we usually write simply as H⁺(aq)" (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 638).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
### PED-074 — Explanations infer the model from the observation

**Claim:** the book's explanatory pattern runs observation-first: measured properties suggest the bonding model, and the energy accounting then fixes the diagram's signs.
**Evidence:** the metallic model is inferred from the properties — "Most metals are hard and have high melting points. This suggests that the electrostatic forces of attraction between the positive ions and the delocalised electrons are strong." — and the model is then interrogated across the series (sodium's single delocalised electron versus magnesium's two, with the melting-point consequence) (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 349); the endothermic diagram follows the same pattern from energy accounting — "the products have more energy than the reactants so we say that the products are less stable than the reactants" — down to the sign convention: "Because the reactants are gaining energy, the enthalpy change of the reaction ∆H is given a positive sign." (SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1, edexcel_igcse_chemistry_chunks_v3.jsonl line 724).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
