# PED-08 — Scaffolding Strategies

## Purpose

This artifact catalogues the scaffolding strategies of record for 4CH1: the
official practical-terminology guidance that fixes the vocabulary scaffolds
(SRC-TL-4CH1), the core-practicals guide's spec-coded practical index pattern,
the book's stepwise construction scaffolds for drawing, naming and calculating
(SRC-BOOK-4CH1-SB1), and the examiner report's calibration of which scaffolds
earn credit (SRC-ER-4CH1). Claims ride only on this artifact's own bounded pack
excerpts. Canonical planes are untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** official terminology guidance and its planning scaffold; the
core-practicals guide's spec-coded index pattern; stepwise bond-drawing and
name-decoding scaffolds; arithmetic laid out step by step; extend-by-pattern

**Out of scope:** practical procedure detail (PRA family) and retrieval devices

## Executive Summary

Scaffolding in the evidence of record is explicit and load-bearing. The board
publishes a dedicated practical-terminology guide because definitions vary
between sources, and its planning scaffold starts from variables. The
core-practicals guide indexes every practical against its specification code —
a scaffold pattern that makes each investigation addressable. The book builds
its hardest constructions stepwise: draw every bond first so counts stay
correct, decode a name starting from the longest chain, construct esters the
easier way; arithmetic is laid out subtraction by subtraction; predictions are
scaffolded by extending a known group trend to an unseen element; answer
construction gets ready-made starters and a glossary. The examiner report then
calibrates the scaffolds that matter: terminology precision earns or loses
marks, and diagram neatness is explicitly warned about.
