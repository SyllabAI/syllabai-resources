# HIS-01 — Specification Evolution

## Purpose

This artifact reconstructs the documentary lineage of Edexcel International GCSE
Chemistry as the lineage is actually legible in the evidence of record: the legacy
4CH0 specification documents held in SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0 (the Issue 3 specification
of October 2009 and the Issue 5 specification-and-sample-materials combination) and
the current 4CH1 specification held in SRC-SPEC-4CH1. Every claim is carried on a
bounded, locator-addressed excerpt from this artifact's own evidence pack; nothing is
asserted beyond the packs. Legacy documents are quoted only for explicitly labeled
LEGACY — HISTORICAL claims; all statements about the present qualification ground on
the current specification. Canonical planes are untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** the identity pages and issue dates of the three documents; the legacy
five-section architecture and the current four-topic architecture; the legacy and
current paper models as each specification states them; and the current
specification's own issue-evolution log.

**Out of scope:** statement-level content migration (HIS-02), assessment-objective
detail (HIS-03), naming and terminology (HIS-04), and question-style change (HIS-05).

## Executive Summary

The pack holds three generations of the same qualification's defining documents. The
oldest is the Issue 3 specification, printed for an Edexcel IGCSE in Chemistry (4CH0)
with first examination 2011. The middle document is the Issue 5 specification and
sample assessment material combination, issued for first examination June 2013. The
current document is the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE in Chemistry (4CH1)
specification, carried at Issue 3, September 2024. Across that lineage the document
set keeps a two-paper untiered model while the papers' mark allocations and the
content architecture change: the legacy documents carry five sections, the current
specification carries four topics. The current specification also carries its own
change log, recording amendments between issues — a self-documenting evolution the
legacy pack documents do not show.

## Main Analysis

### HIS-001 — The three-document lineage of the qualification's defining documents

**Claim:** the qualification's specification history is legible as three dated
documents in the pack: an Edexcel IGCSE Chemistry (4CH0) specification at Issue 3
with first examination 2011, an International GCSE Chemistry (4CH0) specification at
Issue 5 with first examination June 2013, and the current Pearson Edexcel
International GCSE in Chemistry (4CH1) specification at Issue 3, September 2024.
**Evidence:** the legacy cover reads: "Specification Edexcel IGCSE in Chemistry
(4CH0) First examination 2011 Issue 3" (SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0, Issue 3.pdf p1); the
Issue 5 document's own title page reads: "Specification and Sample Assessment
Material Edexcel International GCSE in Chemistry (4CH0) First examination June
2013" (SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0, INT_GCSE_CHEM_SPECSAMS_ISS5_comb.PDF p1); the current
specification's running header reads: "Specification – Issue 3 – September 2024 ©
Pearson Education Limited 2024" (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-2017-specification.pdf p10).
Temporal scope: LEGACY — HISTORICAL. The current-document identity rests on
SRC-SPEC-4CH1 (CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE); both legacy document identities are quoted
from the legacy documents themselves.

### HIS-002 — The legacy specification carried a five-section content architecture

**Claim:** the legacy 4CH0 specification organized its content into five named
sections — Principles of chemistry; Chemistry of the elements; Organic chemistry;
Physical chemistry; Chemistry in society.
**Evidence:** the Issue 3 contents page lists: "Section 1: Principles of chemistry
3 Section 2: Chemistry of the elements 7 Section 3: Organic chemistry 10 Section 4:
Physical chemistry 11 Section 5: Chemistry in society 13" (SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0,
Issue 3.pdf p5); the paper overview page repeats the same five section names under
both papers (SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0, Issue 3.pdf p7).
Temporal scope: LEGACY — HISTORICAL.

### HIS-003 — The current specification carries a four-topic content architecture

**Claim:** the current 4CH1 specification organizes its chemistry content into four
numbered topics — Principles of chemistry; Inorganic chemistry; Physical chemistry;
Organic chemistry.
**Evidence:** the current specification's chemistry content page lists: "1
Principles of chemistry 11 2 Inorganic chemistry 16 3 Physical chemistry 21 4
Organic chemistry 23" (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-2017-specification.pdf p15).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### HIS-004 — The legacy assessment model: two papers, 120 and 60 marks

**Claim:** the legacy Issue 3 specification defined a two-paper model — Paper 1
(code 4CH0/1C) at 120 marks over two hours worth 66.6% of the total IGCSE marks,
and Paper 2 (code 4CH0/2C) at 60 marks over one hour worth 33.3% — with first
assessment June 2011 and availability in January and June series.
**Evidence:** the Issue 3 specification-at-a-glance page states: "Chemistry Paper 1
Paper code: 4CH0/1C ... Availability: January and June series ... First assessment:
June 2011 66.6% of the total IGCSE marks ... The total number of marks is 120" and
for Paper 2: "Paper code: 4CH0/2C ... The total number of marks is 60"
(SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0, Issue 3.pdf p7).
Temporal scope: LEGACY — HISTORICAL.

### HIS-005 — The current assessment model: two papers, 110 and 70 marks

**Claim:** the current 4CH1 specification defines a two-paper model at level 1/2 —
Paper 1C at 110 marks over two hours weighted 61.1%, and Paper 2C at 70 marks over
one hour and fifteen minutes weighted 38.9%.
**Evidence:** the current specification's assessment table states: "Paper 1C ...
Assessed through a 2-hour written examination set and marked by Pearson. The paper
is weighted at 61.1% of the qualification. A mixture of different question styles,
including multiple-choice questions, short-answer questions, calculations and
extended open-response questions. ... 110" and "Paper 2C ... Assessed through a
1-hour and 15-minute written examination ... The paper is weighted at 38.9% of the
qualification" (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-2017-specification.pdf p33).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### HIS-006 — The current specification self-documents its issue-to-issue changes

**Claim:** the current specification opens with a summary of changes made between
the previous issue and the current issue, recording that the availability of series
was amended to November and June and that "Which" was added to the command word
taxonomy — an explicit self-documenting evolution mechanism.
**Evidence:** the current specification's change-summary page states: "Summary of
changes made between previous issue and this current issue ... Availability of
series amended to November and June ... Appendix 5: Command word taxonomy 'Which'
added to the command word taxonomy" (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-2017-specification.pdf p3).
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### HIS-043 — The examiner-report cover series mirrors the qualification's identity evolution

**Claim:** the legacy examiner-report covers document the same identity evolution the

International GCSE in Chemistry (4CH0) Paper 1C" (SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY,
2014-06/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p1); the January 2019 cover reads "Examiners' Report Principal
Examiner Feedback January 2019 Pearson Edexcel International GCSE In Chemistry
(4CH0) Paper 1C" (SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2019-01/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p1). Temporal scope:
LEGACY — HISTORICAL. The cover series is thus a per-session documentary witness to
the same Edexcel-to-Pearson imprint migration that HIS-019 and HIS-020 established
from the specification documents themselves, and it keeps that witness current to

### HIS-045 — The Certificate naming takes two forms across the covers

**Claim:** even inside the co-branding window the Certificate's printed name varies:
some covers carry the full "Level 1/Level 2 Certificate" form while the January 2017

(SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2017-01/4CH0-2C/er.pdf p1). Temporal scope: LEGACY —
HISTORICAL. For lineage work this means the Certificate's documentary trail must be
sought under both name forms; a search keyed only on the Level 1/Level 2 form would
miss the 2017 witness.
### HIS-046 — The report series documents the paper model's continuity and the 2013-2017 CR-variant window

International GCSE In Chemistry (4CH0) Paper 2C" (SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY,
2019-01/4CH0-2C/er.pdf p1). Temporal scope: LEGACY — HISTORICAL. The paper-model
