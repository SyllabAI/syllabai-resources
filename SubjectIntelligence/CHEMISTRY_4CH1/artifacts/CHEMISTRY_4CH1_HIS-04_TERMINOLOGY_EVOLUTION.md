# HIS-04 — Terminology Evolution

## Purpose

This artifact documents how the qualification's own vocabulary shifted across the
lineage, as the documents themselves show it: the qualification's name, the issuing
body's name, the content-architecture names, the statement stem that introduces
every assessable objective, the arrival of a formal command-word taxonomy, and the
terminology discipline the legacy examiner reports enforced against the
specification's own wording. Legacy wording is quoted only for LEGACY — HISTORICAL
claims; current wording grounds on SRC-SPEC-4CH1. Canonical planes are untouched.

## Scope

**Covers:** "IGCSE" versus "International GCSE" versus the Pearson-prefixed current
title; Edexcel versus Pearson Education Limited as the named issuer; the section
and topic naming of the chemistry territory; "Students will be assessed on their
ability to" versus "Students should:"; the command-word taxonomy appendix; and the
legacy reports' terminology rulings.

**Out of scope:** assessment-model numbers (HIS-03) and question-presentation
style (HIS-05).

## Executive Summary

The vocabulary record shows two kinds of change. Cosmetic-institutional change
renames the wrapper: "Edexcel IGCSE" becomes "International GCSE" and finally the
Pearson-prefixed title, while the issuer's imprint moves from Edexcel Limited to
Pearson Education Limited. Substantive-pedagogical change renames the machinery:
the legacy statement stem "Students will be assessed on their ability to:" gives
way to the current "Students should:", the content sections are renamed and
reorganized, and the current specification adds a formal command-word taxonomy
appendix that the legacy documents' contents pages do not list — a taxonomy the
current issue still actively amends. Beneath both, the legacy examiner reports
enforce a continuity of discipline: terms must be used as the specification uses
them, a rule the reports apply to the specification of their own era.

### HIS-022 — The statement stem shifts from assessed-ability phrasing to direct command

**Claim:** the legacy specification introduces each objective block with the
assessed-ability stem 'Students will be assessed on their ability to:'; the
current specification introduces blocks with the direct-command stem 'Students
should:'.
**Evidence:** the legacy block stem reads: "Students will be assessed on their

(SRC-LEGACY-SPEC-4CH0, Issue 3.pdf p9); the current block stem reads: "Students
should: 1.1 understand the three states of matter in terms of the arrangement,
movement and energy of the particles" (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,
international-gcse-chemistry-2017-specification.pdf p17). The legacy stem is
LEGACY — HISTORICAL; the current stem is CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
Temporal scope: CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### HIS-024 — The legacy reports enforce specification-anchored terminology discipline

**Claim:** the legacy examiner reports police terminology against the specification

(SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2012-01/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p3). Both are LEGACY — HISTORICAL
witnesses.
