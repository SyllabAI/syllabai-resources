### HIS-031 — Bonding difficulty is documented in the legacy era and the sub-topics persist in the current spine

**Claim:** the legacy reports named bonding a standing difficulty — candidates
describing covalent-bond formation where electrostatic attraction was required —
and bonding remains a named current sub-topic trio (ionic, covalent, metallic) with
electrostatic-attraction wording inside the current statements.
**Evidence:** the January 2012 report states: "Questions on bonding continue to
prove a minefield for many candidates, but it was pleasing to see several parts of
this question well answered. In (a)(i), the drawing of dot and cross diagrams was

candidates described the formation of covalent bonds instead of the electrostatic
attraction between the bonding pair of electrons and the nuclei of the two atoms."
(SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2012-01/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p4); the current topic list carries

### HIS-032 — Dot-and-cross diagram craft is advised in the legacy era and named in the current statements

**Claim:** the legacy reports gave execution advice on dot-and-cross diagrams —
clarity, crossing out and restarting — and the current specification names
dot-and-cross diagrams as an assessed representation across diatomic, inorganic

**Evidence:** the June 2018 report states: "Candidates should be encouraged to draw

### HIS-033 — Balancing and state-symbol errors recur across both eras' reports

**Claim:** the legacy reports document recurring balancing errors — omitted
coefficients leaving equations unbalanced — and unnecessary-coefficient ambiguity,
while equations and calculations remain a named current sub-topic.
**Evidence:** the June 2018 report states: "The main error here was to omit the '2'
in front of the HCl therefore leaving the equation unbalanced."
(SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2018-06/4CH0-2C/er.pdf p6); the June 2013 report adds an

doubt is not given." (SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2013-06/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p3); the current
topic list carries "(e) Chemical formulae, equations and calculations"

reports are LEGACY — HISTORICAL; the sub-topic list is CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.

### HIS-034 — Precision in readings is marked strictly in the legacy era and experimental skills remain written-assessed

**Claim:** the legacy reports enforced instrument precision across instruments —
candidates who gave burette-reading precision but dropped the trailing zero on

**Evidence:** the June 2016 report states: "Although candidates are used to

(SRC-ER-4CH0-LEGACY, 2016-06/4CH0-1C/er.pdf p4); the current specification states:
"The skills developed through these and other practicals will be assessed through
written examinations." (SRC-SPEC-4CH1,

LEGACY — HISTORICAL; the current statement is CURRENT — AUTHORITATIVE.
