# 4CH1-2.33C — describe how to carry out an acid-alkali titration

## **How**   **to**   **carry**   **out**   **a**   **titration:** {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2f-acids-alkalis-and-titrations-s3}
1. Wash burette using acid and then water 

2. Fill burette to 100cm<sup>3</sup> <sup></sup> with acid with the meniscus’ base on the 100cm<sup>3</sup> <sup></sup> line 

3. Use 25cm<sup>3</sup> pipette to add 25cm<sup>3</sup> of alkali into a conical flask, drawing alkali into the pipette using a pipette filler 

4. Add a few drops of a suitable indicator to the conical flask (eg: phenolphthalein which is pink when alkaline and colourless when acidic) 

5. Add acid from burette to alkali until end-point is reached (as shown by indicator) 

6. The titre (volume of acid needed to exactly neutralise the acid) is the difference between the first (100cm<sup>3</sup> )<sup></sup>  and second readings on the burette) 

7. Repeat the experiment until you get concordant results
