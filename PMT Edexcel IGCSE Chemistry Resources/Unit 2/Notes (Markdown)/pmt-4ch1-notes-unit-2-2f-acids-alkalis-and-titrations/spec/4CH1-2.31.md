# 4CH1-2.31 — know that acids in aqueous solution are a source of hydrogen ions and alkalis in aqueous solution are a source of hydroxide ions

- Acids produce H<sup>+</sup> <sup></sup> ions in aqueous solutions 

- Alkalis produce OH<sup>-</sup> <sup></sup> ions in aqueous solutions
