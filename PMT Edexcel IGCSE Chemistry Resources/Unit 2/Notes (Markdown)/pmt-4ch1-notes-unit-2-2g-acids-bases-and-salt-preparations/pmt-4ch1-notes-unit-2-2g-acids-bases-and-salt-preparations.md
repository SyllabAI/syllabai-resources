# _<mark>2.35</mark>_   _<mark>understand</mark>_   _<mark>acids</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>bases</mark>_   _<mark>in</mark>_   _<mark>terms</mark>_   _<mark>of</mark>_   _<mark>proton</mark>_   _<mark>transfer</mark>_ {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s2}
- Acids donate protons (H<sup>+</sup> )<sup></sup> 

- Bases accept protons 

# _<mark>2.36</mark>_   _<mark>understand</mark>_   _<mark>that</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>acid</mark>_   _<mark>is</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>proton</mark>_   _<mark>donor</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>base</mark>_   _<mark>is</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>proton acceptor</mark>_ {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s3}
- acids are proton donors 

- bases are proton acceptors 

_<mark>2.37</mark>_   _<mark>describe</mark>_   _<mark>the</mark>_   _<mark>reactions</mark>_   _<mark>of</mark>_   _<mark>hydrochloric</mark>_   _<mark>acid,</mark>_   _<mark>sulfuric</mark>_   _<mark>acid</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>nitric</mark>_   _<mark>acid with</mark>_   _<mark>metals,</mark>_   _<mark>bases</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>metal</mark>_   _<mark>carbonates</mark>_   _<mark>(excluding</mark>_   _<mark>the</mark>_   _<mark>reactions</mark>_   _<mark>between nitric</mark>_   _<mark>acid</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>metals)</mark>_   _<mark>to</mark>_   _<mark>form</mark>_   _<mark>salts</mark>_ 

acid + base → salt + water 

acid + metal → salt + hydrogen 

acid + metal carbonate → salt + water + carbon dioxide 

![2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations figure](images/1a7afc41ff0d8b1a.png)

## to form the formula of the salt formed: {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s4}
- take the metal ion in the base/metal/carbonate e.g. Na<sup>+</sup>  

- take the ion part of the acid which isn’t hydrogen for HCl= Cl<sup>-</sup> for HNO3 =  NO3 - for H2SO 4=  SO42- 

- balance the charges 

## Examples include: {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s5}
   - H2SO 4 + MgO -> MgSO4 + H2O  

   - HNO3  + NaOH -> NaNO3 + H2O  

   - 2HCl + 2Na -> 2NaCl + H2 

   - H2SO 4 + Na2 CO 3 -> ****  Na2 SO 4 + H2O  + CO2 

- _<mark>2.38</mark>_   _<mark>know</mark>_   _<mark>that</mark>_   _<mark>metal</mark>_   _<mark>oxides,</mark>_   _<mark>metal</mark>_   _<mark>hydroxides</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>ammonia</mark>_   _<mark>can</mark>_   _<mark>act</mark>_   _<mark>as bases,</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>that</mark>_   _<mark>alkalis</mark>_   _<mark>are</mark>_   _<mark>bases</mark>_   _<mark>that</mark>_   _<mark>are</mark>_   _<mark>soluble</mark>_   _<mark>in</mark>_   _<mark>water</mark>_ 

   - HCl + NH3  -> NH4 Cl  (example of the use of ammonia as a base) 

   - alkalis: bases which are soluble in water 

_<mark>2.39</mark>_   _<mark>describe</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>experiment</mark>_   _<mark>to</mark>_   _<mark>prepare</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>pure,</mark>_   _<mark>dry</mark>_   _<mark>sample</mark>_   _<mark>of</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>soluble</mark>_   _<mark>salt, starting</mark>_   _<mark>from</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>insoluble</mark>_   _<mark>reactant</mark>_ 

   - add excess insoluble base to the acid 

   - filter to remove unreacted base 

   - heat the solution so that water evaporates and crystals of the salt remain 

- _<mark>2.40</mark>_   _<mark>(chemistry</mark>_   _<mark>only)</mark>_   _<mark>describe</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>experiment</mark>_   _<mark>to</mark>_   _<mark>prepare</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>pure,</mark>_   _<mark>dry</mark>_   _<mark>sample of</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>soluble</mark>_   _<mark>salt,</mark>_   _<mark>starting</mark>_   _<mark>from</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>acid</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>alkali</mark>_ 

   - use a titration to find the exact volume of the alkali that reacts with the acid 

   - mix the exact volumes of the acid and base 

   - warm solution so that water evaporates and crystals of the salt remain 

_<mark>2.41</mark>_   _<mark>(chemistry</mark>_   _<mark>only)</mark>_   _<mark>describe</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>experiment</mark>_   _<mark>to</mark>_   _<mark>prepare</mark>_   _<mark>a</mark>_   _<mark>pure,</mark>_   _<mark>dry</mark>_   _<mark>sample of</mark>_   _<mark>an</mark>_   _<mark>insoluble</mark>_   _<mark>salt,</mark>_   _<mark>starting</mark>_   _<mark>from</mark>_   _<mark>two</mark>_   _<mark>soluble</mark>_   _<mark>reactants</mark>_ 

- mix solutions of 2 soluble reactants 

- filter mixture (insoluble salt will remain on filter paper) 

- wash salt with distilled water 

- leave salt to dry
