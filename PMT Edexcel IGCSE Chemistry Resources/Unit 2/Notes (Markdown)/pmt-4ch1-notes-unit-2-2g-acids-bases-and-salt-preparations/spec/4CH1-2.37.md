# 4CH1-2.37 — describe the reactions of hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid and nitric acid with metals, bases and metal carbonates(excluding the reactions between nitric acid and metals) to form salts

acid + base → salt + water 

acid + metal → salt + hydrogen 

acid + metal carbonate → salt + water + carbon dioxide 

![2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations figure](images/1a7afc41ff0d8b1a.png)

## to form the formula of the salt formed: {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s4}
- take the metal ion in the base/metal/carbonate e.g. Na<sup>+</sup>  

- take the ion part of the acid which isn’t hydrogen for HCl= Cl<sup>-</sup> for HNO3 =  NO3 - for H2SO 4=  SO42- 

- balance the charges 

## Examples include: {#pmt-4ch1-notes-unit-2-2g-acids-bases-and-salt-preparations-s5}
   - H2SO 4 + MgO -> MgSO4 + H2O  

   - HNO3  + NaOH -> NaNO3 + H2O  

   - 2HCl + 2Na -> 2NaCl + H2 

   - H2SO 4 + Na2 CO 3 -> ****  Na2 SO 4 + H2O  + CO2
