# 4CH1-3.20C — know that the characteristics of a reaction at dynamic equilibrium are: • the forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate • the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant.

- A reaction at dynamic equilibrium: one in which the forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate and the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant
