_<mark>4.36</mark>_   _<mark>(chemistry</mark>_   _<mark>only)</mark>_   _<mark>describe</mark>_   _<mark>the</mark>_   _<mark>reactions</mark>_   _<mark>of</mark>_   _<mark>aqueous</mark>_   _<mark>solutions</mark>_   _<mark>of carboxylic</mark>_   _<mark>acids</mark>_   _<mark>with</mark>_   _<mark>metals</mark>_   _<mark>and</mark>_   _<mark>metal</mark>_   _<mark>carbonates</mark>_ 

- Dissolve in water to produce acidic solutions 

- React with metal carbonates to produce a salt + carbon dioxide(g) (bubbles are formed / effervesces) 

- React with more reactive metals to produce a salt + hydrogen
