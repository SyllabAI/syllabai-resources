# 4CH1-4.9 — know the names and uses of the main fractions obtained from crude oil: refinery gases, gasoline, kerosene, diesel, fuel oil and bitumen

- Refinery gases o Domestic heating and cooking 

- Gasoline / Petrol 

   - Fuel for cars 

- Kerosene 

   - Fuel for aircraft 

- Diesel 

   - Fuel for some cars and trains 

- Fuel oil 

   - Fuel for large ships and in some power stations 

- Bitumen o Surface roads and roofs
