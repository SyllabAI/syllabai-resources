# 4CH1-4.16 — understand how sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen contribute to acid rain

- When sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen are emitted into the atmosphere they react with rain water to create H<sup>+</sup> <sup></sup> ions 

- When the rain falls the acid can corrode rocks and buildings 

- The acid can also alter the pH in soil or rivers which can affect an ecosystem 

- Acid rain corrodes limestone, which damages buildings and statues etc
