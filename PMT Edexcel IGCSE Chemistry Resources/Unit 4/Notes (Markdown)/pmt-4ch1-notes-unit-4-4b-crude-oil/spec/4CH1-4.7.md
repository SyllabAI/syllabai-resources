# 4CH1-4.7 — know that crude oil is a mixture of hydrocarbons

- Crude oil is a complex mixture of hydrocarbons 

- It contains molecules in which carbon atoms are in chains or rings (names, formulae and structures of specific ring molecules not required) 

- An important source of useful substances (fuels and feedstock for the petrochemical industry) 

- A finite resource
