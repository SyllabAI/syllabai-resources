# 4CH1-4.12 — know the possible products of complete and incomplete combustion of hydrocarbons with oxygen in the air

- Complete combustion 

   - CO2 and H2O  are produced 

- Incomplete combustion 

   - If there’s not enough oxygen, some of the fuel doesn’t burn – this is partial combustion. Here, solid particles of soot (carbons) and unburnt fuel are released. Carbon monoxide is also released.
