### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

The more vigorous the reaction (faster bubbling and heat produced), the more reactive the metal:

- **R** is the most reactive because it shows rapid bubbling and the reaction is exothermic (test tube becomes warm)

### Q4

- Zinc is a more reactive metal so is more likely to **lose** **electrons** (and become oxidised) than iron and it readily donates its electrons to any iron ions that might have formed, stopping them from corroding.
