### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- To identify the pigment, you must calculate the *R*<sub>f</sub> value of the unknown spot using the formula:

$R_{f}=\frac{\mathrm{distance} \mathrm{travelled} \mathrm{by} \mathrm{substance}}{\mathrm{distance} \mathrm{travelled} \mathrm{by} \mathrm{solvent}}$

- Using the diagram:

![Chromatogram. Horizontal baseline at 0.0 cm. Horizontal solvent front line at 7.0 cm. One spot at 3.0 cm above baseline. Vertical arrows show 3.0 cm (spot) and 7.0 cm (solvent front).](assets/002-chromatogram-horizontal-baseline-at-0-0-cm-horiz.png)

$R_{f}=\frac{3.0}{7.0}=0.428..=0.43$

> **[exam-tip]**
> Always measure the distance from the **baseline to the centre of the spot**, not the top or bottom. Using the centre of the spot ensures accurate **Rf values** and avoids losing marks for measurement errors.
