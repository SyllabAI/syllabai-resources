## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 2((b)) — 1 marks
**Correct answer: A** — Consumers may not accurately calculate benefits

**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

Consumers often cannot work out accurately how much satisfaction a product will actually give them, so they may buy something that does not maximise their benefit

**B** is incorrect as habits are typically hard to give up, and that difficulty is itself a reason consumers fail to maximise their benefit
