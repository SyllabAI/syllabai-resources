## Exam Questions (26 questions)


### Q1 — hard — 4 marks

**Q1** [4 marks]

The masses, in kg, of the feed in sacks produced by a manufacturer have the distribution `\text{N}\left(20, \; \left(\dfrac{1}{60}\right)^{2}\right)`.

Find the probability that 12 randomly chosen sacks all contain feed with a mass within 35 grams of the mean.

### Q2 — very_hard — 8 marks

**Q2** [5 marks]

The heights, in metres, of the trees on a plantation are normally distributed with mean $μ$ and standard deviation $σ$. The shortest 10% of the trees and the tallest 2.5% of the trees are not sold. All the other trees are sold.

Find, in terms of $μ$ and $σ$, the least height and the greatest height of the trees that are sold.

**Q2** [3 marks]

It is given that 20% of the trees are shorter than $( μ - 8 )$ m.

Find the probability that a randomly chosen tree is taller than $( μ + 8 )$ m, given that it is taller than $( μ - 8 )$ m.

### Q3 — hard — 9 marks

### Q4 — very_hard — 8 marks

**Q4** [4 marks]

**Q4** [4 marks]

### Q5 — hard — 8 marks
