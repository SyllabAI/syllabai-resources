### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****C**

- **Starch**, a large biomolecule, is made from many molecules of **glucose** molecules bonded together
- **Salivary** **amylase**, an enzyme produced by the salivary glands, acts on starch and breaks it down into **maltose** (a disaccharide)
- **Amylase** is also produced by the **pancreas**, to help digest any remaining starch in the small intestine
- Maltose is broken down by the enzyme **maltase** (which is found in the lining of the small intestine), producing **glucose** molecules

**D** is incorrect because starch was the starting molecule in this process (starch is a polymer of glucose molecules).
