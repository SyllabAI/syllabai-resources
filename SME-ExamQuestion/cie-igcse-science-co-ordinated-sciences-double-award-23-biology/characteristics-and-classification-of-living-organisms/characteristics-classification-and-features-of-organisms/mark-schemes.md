### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****B**** (respiration) because:**

Yeast is a living organism that can respire both aerobically and anaerobically. The gas carbon dioxide (CO<sub>2</sub>) is produced as a waste product, forming bubbles in the test tube.

**A** is incorrect because reproduction is the process by which more of the same kind of organism is made and doesn’t directly result in the release of carbon dioxide.

**C** is incorrect because growth is a permanent increase in size and dry mass caused by an increase in cell number or cell size (or both), and doesn’t directly result in the release of carbon dioxide.

**D** is incorrect because sensitivity is the ability to detect or sense stimuli in the internal or external environment and to make appropriate responses, and doesn’t directly result in the release of carbon dioxide.

### Q5
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****D**** (sensitivity and growth) because:**

The seed is developing roots and shoots – this demonstrates growth.
