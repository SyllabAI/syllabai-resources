### Q5
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- Inventory is valued at the lower of cost and net realisable value

  - Therefore, the goods should have been valued at \$400
  - The goods were overvalued by \$100
- Consider the effect on the gross profit

  - Cost of sales = opening inventory + net purchases - closing inventory
  
    - If the closing inventory is overstated then the cost of sales will be understated
    - Because you are taking more away from opening inventory + net purchases
  - Gross profit = net revenue - cost of sales
  
    - If the cost of sales is understated then the gross profit will be overstated
    - Because you are taking less away from net revenue
  - Therefore overstating the closing inventory causes the gross profit to be overstated
- Consider the effect on the capital

  - Overstating the profit for the year will overstate the capital
  - Therefore overstating the closing inventory causes the capital to be overstated
- Therefore, both the gross profit and the capital are overstated

**A** and **B** are incorrect; overvaluing the closing inventory causes the gross profit to be overstated not understated

**C** is incorrect; the capital is overstated due to the profit and also the assets being overstated
