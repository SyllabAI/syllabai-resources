### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- Consider the "Capital =" version of the accounting equation

  - capital = assets - liabilities
- Capital will increase if

  - assets increase
  - liabilities decrease
  
    - because you are taking less away from assets
- Capital will decrease (be reduced) if

  - assets decrease
  - liabilities increase
  
    - because you are taking more away from assets
- Consider the 4 options

  - A: withdrawing cash from the business bank account for personal use causes capital to decrease
  
    - Because assets (the bank account) decrease without affecting liabilities
  - B: paying business expenses using personal cash causes capital to increase
  
    - Because liabilities (the money owed for the expenses) decrease without affecting assets
  - C: taking goods from the business inventory for personal use causes capital to decrease
  
    - Because assets (goods for sale) decrease without affecting liabilities
  - D: paying personal expenses with a business cheque causes capital to decrease
  
    - Because assets (the bank account) decrease without affecting liabilities
- So option B is the only one that does not reduce the owner's capital

**A**, **C** and **D** are incorrect; each of these **does** reduce the owner's capital

### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- The formula for the accounting equation is: Assets = Liabilities + Capital

  - You can rearrange the equation in order to find the capital:
  
    - Capital = Assets - Liabilities
- Here the assets are 'non-current assets' and 'trade receivables'

  - Assets = \$32 000 + \$14 500 = \$46 500
- The liabilities are 'bank overdraft' and 'other payables'

  - Liabilities = \$2 000 + \$700 = \$2 700
- Capital = \$46 500 - \$2 700 = \$43 800

**A** is incorrect; this is what you would get if you did not include 'non-current assets' in the assets (i.e. if you subtracted 'bank overdraft' + 'other payables' from 'trade receivables')

**B** is incorrect; this is what you would get if you included 'trade receivables' in the liabilities (i.e. if you subtracted 'trade receivables' + 'bank overdraft' + 'other payables' from 'non-current assets')

**D** is incorrect; this is what you would get if you included 'bank overdraft' in the assets (i.e. if you subtracted 'other payables' from 'trade receivables' + 'bank overdraft' + 'non-current assets')

### Q10
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**
