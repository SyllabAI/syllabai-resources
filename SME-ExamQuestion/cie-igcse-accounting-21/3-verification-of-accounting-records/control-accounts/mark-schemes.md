### Q1

- The 'logic' of a sales ledger control account is the same as for a trade receivables ledger account

  - Trade receivables is an asset
  
    - So debits increase the value of the asset
    - And credits decrease the value of the asset
- Cash is refunded to credit customers because, for example, they have returned goods that they paid for

  - So money is owed to the customer
  
    - This essentially makes them a liability, not an asset
  - Refunding money to the customer reduces the amount that is owed to the customer
  
    - A reduction to a liability account is entered on the debit side
- You can also think of a refund as undoing a cash payment from a customer

  - Originally the trade receivables account is credited and the cash/bank is debited
  - So for a refund the trade receivables account is debited and the cash/bank is credited
- So refunds are debit entries in a trade receivables ledger account

### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Consider the effect of the mistake on the balance
- Irrecoverable debts belong on the credit side of a sales ledger control account

  - Putting \$1500 on the debit side by mistake will mean that the total debits were overstated by \$1500
  - But *not* putting it on the credit side also means that total credits were understated by \$1500
- A debit balance is equal to total debits - total credits

  - Total debits being overstated by \$1500 would cause the debit balance to be overstated by \$1500
  
    - Because \$1500 more was added on than should have been
  - Total credits being understated by \$1500 would *also* cause the debit balance to be overstated by \$1500
  
    - Because \$1500 less was taken away than should have been
- So the mistake caused the debit balance to be overstated by \$1500 + \$1500 = \$3000

  - Subtract that \$3000 to find the correct debit balance
  - \$13 500 - \$3000 = \$10 500

**B** is incorrect; \$1500 has only been subtracted from \$13 500 one time, instead of twice

**C** is incorrect; \$1500 has been added to \$13 500, instead of being subtracted twice

**D** is incorrect; \$1500 has been added to \$13 500 twice, instead of being subtracted twice
