### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- The sales journal is the book of prime entry for credit sales
- Therefore it is the first place that invoices for sales on credit are recorded

**A** is incorrect; the general journal is not used to record credit sales

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Credit notes **received** will be with reference to purchases made (and then returned) by the business

  - They are recorded in the purchases returns journal
- When they are transferred to the ledger

  - The total is credited to the purchases returns account
  - (The sub-totals for each merchant are debited from the individual trade payables accounts)

**B** is incorrect; totals of credit notes *issued* (not received) are debited (not credited) to the sales returns account

**C** is incorrect; the purchases returns account is credited (not debited)

**D** is incorrect; totals of credit notes *issued* (not received) are debited to the sales returns account

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Invoices for sales are issued *to* a credit customer *by* the business
- Credit notes for sales are issued *to* a credit customer *by* the business
- So 'A' is the correct option

**B** is incorrect; credit notes received would be with reference to *purchases* by the business which have, for example, been returned to a supplier

**C** is incorrect; invoices received would be with references to *purchases* by the business, and would be issued by the supplier

**D** is incorrect; invoices and credit notes received would both be with reference to *purchases* made by the business

### Q4

  - The **total** is credited to the sales account
  - The sub-totals pertaining to each customer are debited to the customers' individual trade receivables accounts

**A** is incorrect; the total is credited to the sales account

**D** is incorrect; the total is credited to the sales account

### Q5
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- A trade discount is a reduction in the selling price of goods or services, and is applied before a transaction takes place

  - Therefore the amount recorded in the sales journal will be the full price minus the trade discount
  - 12%  ✕ \$3500 = \$420
  - \$3500 - \$420 = \$3080
- If if the customer pays by the cash discount deadline, then the discount will be recorded at that time in the cash book as a separate entry

  - A cash discount is  not recorded in the sales journal at the time of the sale

**A** is incorrect; this is what you would get if you combined the trade and cash discounts (12% + 5% = 17%), and then reduced the full price by 17%

**B** is incorrect; this is what you would get if you first reduced the sale price by 12% to \$3080, and then reduced \$3080 by 5%

**D** is incorrect; this is the full price without any discounts applied

### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- When processing accounting data

  - Information is taken from the business documents and entered into the books of prime entry
  
    - Journals are books of prime entry
  - The details are then transferred from the books of prime entry to the ledger accounts
  - A trial balance is then a way to check for arithmetic errors in the ledger accounts
- So the correct order is business documents, journals, ledgers, trial balance

**B**, **C** and **D** are incorrect; none of these options have all 4 items in the correct order
