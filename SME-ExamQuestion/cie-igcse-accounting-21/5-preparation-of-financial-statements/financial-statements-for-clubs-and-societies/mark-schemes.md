### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- The accumulated fund has decreased over the year by \$21 500 - \$17 800 = \$3 700

  - Therefore, there has been a deficit for the year of \$3 700
- If there is a deficit then the expenditure is higher than the income
- The expenditure was \$8 750 and the income must be \$3 700 less than this

  - \$8 750 - \$3 700 = \$5 050
- The purchase of the new equipment does not affect the deficit for the year

  - Therefore, it does not affect the accumulated fund

**B** is incorrect; you get this amount if you include the purchase of the new equipment in the amount for expenses

**C** is incorrect; you get this amount if you add the difference between the accumulated funds to the value of the expenses (this would be correct if the accumulated funds were the other way around)

**D** is incorrect; you get this amount if you add together the difference between the accumulated funds, the new equipment, and the expenses

### Q3

- A receipts and payments account is prepared in a similar way to a cash book

  - Amounts received are debited
  - Amounts paid are credited

### Q5
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- The capital of a club or society is referred to as the accumulated fund

  - This is made up of
  
    - any **capital **which is invested into the organisation
    - the accumulation of **surpluses **of income over expenditure over time

**B** is incorrect; capital refers to how much a business owes the owner(s), which does not apply to a club as it is not-for-profit

**C** is incorrect; retained earnings is a term used for limited companies and is the accumulation of the profits over the years

**D** is incorrect; subscriptions are a form of income for a club

### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Calculate the amount that was due to the suppliers for the current year
- Start with the amount paid for that year

  - subtract any amounts that were due in the previous year but paid in the current year
  - add any amounts for the current year that have not been paid by the end of the year
- \$5 400 - \$150 + \$500 = \$5 750
- Adjust for the opening and closing inventory

  - There is more inventory at the end of the year than at the start so the difference should be subtracted as it has not been used in the current year

- If you are unsure about whether to add or subtract the \$200 then make up a value for the opening inventory and add \$200 to get the closing inventory

  - e.g. Cost of sales = opening inventory (\$1 000) + purchases (\$5 750) - closing inventory (\$1 200) = \$5 550

**A** is incorrect; the amount owed at the start of the year has been added and the amount owed at the end of the year has been subtracted
