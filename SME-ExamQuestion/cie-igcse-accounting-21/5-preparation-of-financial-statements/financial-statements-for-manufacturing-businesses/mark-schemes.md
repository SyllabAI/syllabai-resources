### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- The cost of raw materials consumed

  - = opening inventory of raw materials
  - + purchases of raw materials
  - - closing inventory of raw materials
- As the opening inventory is bigger than the closing inventory the overall effect is an increase in the cost of raw materials
- Cost of production

  - = prime cost
  
    - this includes the cost of raw materials consumed
  - + factory overheads
  - + work in progress at the start of the year
  - - work in progress at the end of the year
- An increase in the cost of raw materials consumed causes an increase in the cost of production
- Cost of sales

  - = opening inventory of finished goods
  - + cost of production
  - - closing inventory of finished goods
- An increase in the cost of production causes an increase in the cost of sales
- Therefore both the cost of production and the cost of sales increase

**A** is incorrect; this would have been the answer if the closing inventory was greater than the opening inventory

**C** is incorrect; if the cost of production increases then the cost of sales increases

**D** is incorrect; this would have been the answer if the inventories were referring to finished goods rather than raw materials
