### Q16
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****A ****because:**

- Blood group alleles show **codominance **- alleles I<sup>A</sup> and I<sup>B</sup> are **both **expressed if present in the genotype (they cannot mask each other), but both are dominant over the i (O) allele. There are therefore 4 possible phenotypes for blood type (A, B, AB and O)

### Q22

- Variation in the population means that there are a wide variety of genotypes and therefore phenotypes; making the population as a whole better able to adapt to changes in the environment
