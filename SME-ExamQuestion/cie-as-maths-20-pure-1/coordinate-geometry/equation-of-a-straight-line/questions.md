## Exam Questions (40 questions)


### Q1 — easy — 6 marks

**Q1** [6 marks]

A straight line passes through the points $P ( x_{1} ,  y_{1} )$ and $Q ( x_{2} ,  y_{2} )$. Work out the gradient of each of the following lines:

(i) $P ( 2 ,  6 ) ,  Q ( 4 ,  12 )$

(ii) $P ( - 3 ,  4 ) ,  Q ( - 8 ,  24 )$

(iii) $P ( 1 ,  - 3 ) ,  Q ( - 3 ,  6 )$

### Q2 — easy — 5 marks

**Q2** [5 marks]

(i) $y = 3 x - 5$

(ii) $y = \frac{1}{2} x + 7$

(iii) $\frac{1}{3} y = \frac{1}{6} x - \frac{1}{9}$
