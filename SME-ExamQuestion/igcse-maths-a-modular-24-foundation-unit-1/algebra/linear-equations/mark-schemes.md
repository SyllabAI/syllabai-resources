## Q1 — medium — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 18((d)) — 3 marks
> **[exam-tip]**
> Solve is an algebraic command word that means to find the value of the unknown variable, in this case the value of $x$.

> *Start to solve the equation, using the balance method  and doing the same thing to both sides of the equation at each step*

> *First multiply both sides by 2 to get rid of the fraction*

$\begin{gathered} \frac{6 x - 5}{2} & = x + 1 \\ 6 x - 5 & = 2 ( x + 1 ) \end{gathered}$

**[M1]**

> *Expand the single bracket on the right hand side of the equation*

$6x-5=2x+2$

> *Continue to solve the equation by collecting 'x terms' on one side of the equation and constant terms on the other side of the equation*

$\begin{gathered} 6 x - 2 x & = 2 + 5 \\ 4 x & = 7 \end{gathered}$

**[M1]**

> *Solve for x*

$x=7\div4$

$x=1.75$

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: For a correct first step in solving the equation. E.g. $6 x - 5 = 2 ( x + 1 )$ or $6x-5=2x+2$.
> 
> **M1**: For a correct method of isolating the $x$ terms.
> 
> **A1**: Is awarded for any equivalent answer to 1.75, e.g. $\frac{7}{4}$, but is dependent on at least one of the M1 marks being gained.

## Q3 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 13((a)) — 2 marks
> *Solve for *$x$* by using inverse operations and doing the same to both sides*

> *The first step is to add three to both sides*

$\begin{gathered} 2 x - 3 & = 18 \\ 2 x - 3 + 3 & = 18 + 3 \\ 2 x & = 21 \end{gathered}$

**[M1]**

> *Divide both sides by 2*

$\begin{gathered} \frac{2 x}{2} & = \frac{21}{2} \\ x = 10.5 \end{gathered}$

$x=10.5$

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> Any equivalent answer to 10.5 will gain the final mark (e.g. $\frac{21}{2}$)
