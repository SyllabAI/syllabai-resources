### Q1 — very_hard — 40 marks

**Q1** [40 marks]

**Question 1**

**Read Text A and Text B in the insert (as found in the **<u>**[November 2023 (0500/21](https://pastpapers.co/cie/IGCSE/English-First-Language-0500/2023-Oct-Nov/0500_w23_in_21.pdf)**</u>**) exam paper) and then answer the question.**

Imagine that you are a student of a school which is considering whether or not to demolish an old building on its site.

**Write the speech** you give to a meeting of local people, expressing your views.

In your speech you should:

- evaluate the ideas, attitudes and opinions about buildings given in both texts
- based on what you have read, justify your views on whether the school’s old building should be replaced or reused.

Base your speech on what you have read in **both **texts, but be careful to use your own words. Address both of the bullet points.

**Write about 250 to 350 words. Up to 15 marks are available for the content of your answer, and up to 25 marks for the quality of your writing.**
