### Q2
### Model answer

**To the Manager**
**A report on the Meet and Greet team’s work at Golden Tours**

The Meet and Greet team works at the heart of the Departures concourse, often before passengers arrive. This report outlines the current role of the team, highlights the challenges faced by both staff and passengers, and offers suggestions for how the service could be developed further.

**Overview**

The role centres on welcoming passengers in a courteous and professional way, checking identity documents swiftly to avoid errors, and guiding them through the system of roped queues towards the check-in desks. Staff must also confirm that luggage has been packed and kept in sight, before directing travellers to the correct point for bag drop. Alongside this, they answer questions and offer reassurance so that passengers feel confident about their journey. This work is crucial in maintaining both safety and the positive reputation of Golden Tours.

**Challenges**

Despite this, a number of issues affect the efficiency of the team. The concourse layout is confusing for first-time travellers, who often wander uncertainly and need repeated directions. Families with young children struggle with trolleys, which are frequently unstable, leading to inconvenience and even safety concerns. Staff face pressures too: the queues can become overwhelming, security disputes occasionally arise, and short lunch breaks leave little time for rest. Senior employees must also supervise new colleagues, who may be slower or less confident when handling difficult situations. These combined factors risk lowering morale and service quality.

**Improvements**

There are several ways the experience could be improved for both staff and passengers. A fast-track or extra seating could support elderly or unsteady travellers, while families would benefit from designated assistance at busy times. Clearer signage would make the route through the concourse less confusing. Replacing or repairing trolleys and investing in more reliable document scanners would also improve efficiency. Recruiting additional staff or assigning more specific roles would ease pressure and ensure fairer breaks. Finally, better training for new employees, together with pre-travel information for passengers via apps or leaflets, would help to raise standards further.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, the Meet and Greet team already plays an essential role in ensuring passenger safety and comfort, as well as upholding the reputation of Golden Tours. However, with targeted improvements to equipment, staffing, and passenger guidance, the system could become smoother and more supportive for everyone involved.

The register is consistently formal and appropriate for management, avoiding slang or contractions. Sentences are fluent and controlled, and technical accuracy is secure throughout.
