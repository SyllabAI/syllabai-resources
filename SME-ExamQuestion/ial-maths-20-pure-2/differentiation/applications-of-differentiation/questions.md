## Q17 — hard — 10 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 mark — structured

![7-1-sq-5a-hard-edexelmodelling-with-sequences-and-series-](assets/021-7-1-sq-5a-hard-edexelmodelling-with-sequences-an.png)

## Q23 — very_hard — 9 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 2 marks — structured

![7-1-sq-5a-very-hard-edexelmodelling-with-sequences-and-series-](assets/016-7-1-sq-5a-very-hard-edexelmodelling-with-sequenc.png)

$P=2r+\frac{2A}{r}$

### 3() — 5 marks — structured
