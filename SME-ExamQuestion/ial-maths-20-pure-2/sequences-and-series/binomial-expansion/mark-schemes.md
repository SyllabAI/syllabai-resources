## Q8 — easy — 5 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 3 marks
> *Use the binomial expansion formula*

$( a + b )^{n = a^{n} + ( \begin{gathered} n \\ 1 \end{gathered} ) a^{n} - 1} b + ( \begin{gathered} n \\ 2 \end{gathered} ) a^{n - 2} b^{2}... + ( \begin{gathered} n \\ r \end{gathered} ) a^{n - r} b^{r} +... + b^{n}$

> *Substitute *$a=5$*, *$b = ( 2 x )$* and *$n=5$* into the first three terms of the formula*

$( 5 + 2 x )^{5 = 5^{5} + ( \begin{gathered} 5 \\ 1 \end{gathered} ) 5^{5} - 1} ( 2 x ) + ( \begin{gathered} 5 \\ 2 \end{gathered} ) 5^{5 - 2} ( 2 x )^{2} +...$

> *Find the binomial coefficients e.g. using the *`C _ r _ _ ^ n`* button on your calculator or Pascal's triangle*

$( \begin{gathered} 5 \\ 1 \end{gathered} ) = 5
( \begin{gathered} 5 \\ 2 \end{gathered} ) = 10$

**[M1]**

> *Substitute these into the formula and simplify*

> *Remember to use index laws, e.g. *$( 2 x )^{2} = 2^{2} x^{2} = 4 x^{2}$

$\begin{gathered} ( 5 + 2 x )^{5} & = 5^{5} + 5 \times 5^{4} ( 2 x ) + 10 \times 5^{3} ( 2 x )^{2} +... \\ = 5^{5} + 5 \times 5^{4} \times 2 x + 10 \times 5^{3} \times 4 x^{2} +... \end{gathered}$

$3125+6250x+5000x^{2}$

**[M1 A1]**

### 2() — 2 marks
> *Use the expansion from part (a) of *$(5+2x)^{5}$

> *Think of *$( 5.04 )^{5}$* as *$( 5 + 0.04 )^{5}$* i.e.  *$2x=0.04$* so *$x=0.02$

> *Substitute *$x=0.04$* into *$3125+6250x+5000x^{2}$* and simplify*

$3125+6250\times0.02+5000\times0.02^{2}$

**Final answer:** **3252**

**[M1 A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> "3250 to 3 s.f." is also accepted.

> **[exam-tip]**
> You can check $(5.04)^{5}$ directly on your calculator, $3252.016...$ to see if your estimate is along the right lines!
