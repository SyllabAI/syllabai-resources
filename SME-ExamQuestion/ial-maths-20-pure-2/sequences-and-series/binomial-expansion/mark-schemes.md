## Q8 — easy — 5 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 3 marks
> *Use the binomial expansion formula*

`open parentheses a plus b close parentheses to the power of n equals a to the power of n plus open parentheses table row n row 1 end table close parentheses a to the power of n minus 1 end exponent b plus open parentheses table row n row 2 end table close parentheses a to the power of n minus 2 end exponent b squared... plus open parentheses table row n row r end table close parentheses a to the power of n minus r end exponent b to the power of r plus... plus b to the power of n`

> *Substitute *$a=5$*, *`b equals open parentheses 2 x close parentheses`* and *$n=5$* into the first three terms of the formula*

`open parentheses 5 plus 2 x close parentheses to the power of 5 equals 5 to the power of 5 plus open parentheses table row 5 row 1 end table close parentheses 5 to the power of 5 minus 1 end exponent open parentheses 2 x close parentheses plus open parentheses table row 5 row 2 end table close parentheses 5 to the power of 5 minus 2 end exponent open parentheses 2 x close parentheses squared plus...`

> *Find the binomial coefficients e.g. using the *`scriptbase straight C subscript r end scriptbase presubscript blank presuperscript n`* button on your calculator or Pascal's triangle*

`open parentheses table row 5 row 1 end table close parentheses equals 5
open parentheses table row 5 row 2 end table close parentheses equals 10`

**[M1]**

> *Substitute these into the formula and simplify*

> *Remember to use index laws, e.g. *`open parentheses 2 x close parentheses squared equals 2 squared x squared equals 4 x squared`

`table row cell open parentheses 5 plus 2 x close parentheses to the power of 5 end cell equals cell 5 to the power of 5 plus 5 cross times 5 to the power of 4 open parentheses 2 x close parentheses plus 10 cross times 5 cubed open parentheses 2 x close parentheses squared plus... end cell row blank equals cell 5 to the power of 5 plus 5 cross times 5 to the power of 4 cross times 2 x plus 10 cross times 5 cubed cross times 4 x squared plus... end cell end table`

$3125+6250x+5000x^{2}$

**[M1 A1]**

### 2() — 2 marks
> *Use the expansion from part (a) of *$(5+2x)^{5}$

> *Think of *`open parentheses 5.04 close parentheses to the power of 5`* as *`open parentheses 5 plus 0.04 close parentheses to the power of 5`* i.e.  *$2x=0.04$* so *$x=0.02$

> *Substitute *$x=0.04$* into *$3125+6250x+5000x^{2}$* and simplify*

$3125+6250\times0.02+5000\times0.02^{2}$

**Final answer:** **3252**

**[M1 A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> "3250 to 3 s.f." is also accepted.

> **[exam-tip]**
> You can check $(5.04)^{5}$ directly on your calculator, $3252.016...$ to see if your estimate is along the right lines!
