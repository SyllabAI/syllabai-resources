> **Indicative content** — *AO1 Knowledge and understanding*
> 
> *Knowledge of JIT:*
> 
> - Orders made from suppliers as needed
> - Products produced as required by customers
> - Limited or zero inventory held
> 
> *Disadvantages of JIT:*
> 
> - Risk of running out of stock
> - Reliance on (new) suppliers — frequency of ordering / deliveries
> - Requires planning / time
> - Limited scope for mass / batch production (little scope for economies of scale)
> - Finished product inventory cannot be used for promotional purposes (e.g. shop display)
> - Difficult to forecast / budget
> - Cost of infrastructure / capital
> - Might be difficult to respond to changing demand
> 
> **AO2 Application** — *context applied to CS* (max 2 marks)
> 
> - Currently selling to mass market (professionals for work / smart dress)
> - Currently using batch production
> - Abir has suggested a move towards niche, unique (job-produced) shoes
> - New local supplier suggested by Abir (no existing relationship)
> - Shoes on display in the shop
> - Leather must be in climate-controlled storage
> - Range of sizes and colours
> 
> **AO3 Analysis** (4 marks total: 2 per disadvantage)
> 
> *Disadvantage 1 example* — 'reliance on a new untested supplier'
> 
> - *Developed (2): Abir's proposed local supplier has no track record with CS; if deliveries are late, production halts, customers walk out empty-handed, and reputation suffers in the long term*
> 
> *Disadvantage 2 example* — 'no inventory on shop display'
> 
> - *Developed (2): customers entering CS's shop expect to try shoes on; with JIT there are no finished shoes to display, so walk-in customers leave without buying — reducing revenue from the shop channel*
> 
> Other chains:
> 
> - Risk of running out of stock — dissatisfied customers — bad publicity — lower sales / profit; also Yazeem may be idle (paid but unproductive) — lower profitability
> - Batch production with range of sizes / colours — batches need raw material in advance — JIT clashes with the current production model
> - Climate-controlled leather storage already exists — the cost is already sunk; JIT may not eliminate it
> 
> Accept all valid responses

> **[exam-tip]**
> The 4 analysis marks split 2 + 2 across two disadvantages. Pick CONTRASTING disadvantages — e.g. one supply-chain risk (untested new supplier) and one customer-facing issue (no shop-floor display for walk-ins). For CS specifically, the shop-display problem is a strong angle because customers want to try on shoes before buying. Don't just analyse JIT in general — anchor in CS's batch production and physical shop.

> - SCM includes all activities to get the right product into the right consumer's hands in the right quantity at the right time

> 
> - If suppliers don't supply on time — customers may not get goods on time — bad reputation for reliability — shop elsewhere
> - Leads to increased efficiency — order to despatch time reduced — customer loyalty

> - Outsourcing deliveries can lead to increased customer complaints — customers switch to alternative retailers

> - It might be concluded that SCM can bring cost savings — and that ultimately survival is about being profitable

> - A conclusion that nowadays most businesses have some form of SCM — but it only really benefits larger businesses

> - A judgement about SCM having a cost to the business — affects bottom line in the short term
