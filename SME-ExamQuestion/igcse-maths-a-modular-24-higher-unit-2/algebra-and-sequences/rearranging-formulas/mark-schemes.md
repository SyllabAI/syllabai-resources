> **[exam-tip]**
> 'Make t the subject' means to rearrange the given formula so that it begins with  $t=$

> *Rearrange the formula, using the balance method and doing the same thing to both sides of the equation at each step*

> *The inverse of *$\frac{1}{2}$* is to multiply both sides by 2 giving:*

$\begin{gathered} s = 1 \frac{1}{2} a t^{2} \\ 2 s & = a t^{2} \end{gathered}$

> *Divide both sides by *$a$

$\begin{gathered} \frac{2 s}{a} & = t^{2} \end{gathered}$

> *Square root both sides*

$\begin{gathered} \pm \sqrt{ \frac{2 s}{a} } & = t \end{gathered}$

$t=\pm\sqrt{\frac{2s}{a}}$

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: Awarded for a correct first step in rearranging the formula. E.g. $2s=at^{2}$ or $\frac{s}{a}=\frac{1}{2}t^{2}$ or $\frac{2s}{a}=t^{2}$

> **A1**: This mark is still awarded with $\pm$ omitted in final answer.
