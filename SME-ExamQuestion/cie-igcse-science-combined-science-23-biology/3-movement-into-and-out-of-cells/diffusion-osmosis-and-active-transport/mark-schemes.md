# Mark Schemes — Diffusion, Osmosis & Active Transport
**3. Movement into & out of Cells** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (3 questions)


### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Active transport moves substances against their concentration gradient, from a low to a high concentration
- This requires energy released from respiration

**B** is incorrect because diffusion moves substances down, not against, a concentration gradient and does not require energy

**C** is incorrect because osmosis is the movement of water down a concentration gradient and does not require energy

**D** is incorrect because xylem transport describes movement through xylem tissue, not the type of transport across the cell membrane

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- A small concentration gradient means there is little difference in concentration across the membrane, reducing the driving force for diffusion

**A** is incorrect because a high temperature increases, not decreases, the rate of diffusion

**B** is incorrect because a large surface area increases, not decreases, the rate of diffusion

**D** is incorrect because a small diffusion distance increases, not decreases, the rate of diffusion

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Osmosis causes water to move from the dish (lower solute concentration), through the semi-permeable potato tissue, into the concentrated sugar solution (higher solute concentration)
- This means the level in the well rises and the water level in the dish falls
