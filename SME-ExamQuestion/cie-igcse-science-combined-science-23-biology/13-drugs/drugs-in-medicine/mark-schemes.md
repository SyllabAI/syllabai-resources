## Exam Questions (1 questions)


### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Antibiotics work by targeting bacterial cell structures, which viruses do not have
- This means antibiotics have no effect on viruses

**B** is incorrect because being a drug does not explain why antibiotics have no effect specifically on viruses

**C** is incorrect because reduced effectiveness from long-term use applies to antibiotic resistance in bacteria, not to viral infections

**D** is incorrect because bacterial resistance to antibiotics does not explain why antibiotics have no effect on a viral infection
