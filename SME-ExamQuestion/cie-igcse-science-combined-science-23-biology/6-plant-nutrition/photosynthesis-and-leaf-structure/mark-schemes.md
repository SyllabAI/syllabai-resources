# Mark Schemes — Photosynthesis & Leaf Structure
**6. Plant Nutrition** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (4 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> One mark from each section, plus any other two marks:
> 
> **Apparatus / chemicals**
> 
> - Lamp / suitable named light source **[1 mark]**
> - Gas syringe / measuring cylinder **[1 mark]**
> - Named timing device / named apparatus for varying light intensity / ruler **[1 mark]**
> 
> **Method and safety precautions**
> 
> - Method described clearly enough that another student could obtain suitable results **[1 mark]**
> - Safety precaution specific to water and electricity / hot lamps **[1 mark]**
> 
> **Measurements**
> 
> - Suitable way of varying and recording light intensity, e.g. distance of lamp from plant, brightness, number of lamps **[1 mark]**
> - Suitable way of recording volume of gas, e.g. displacement of water in a measuring cylinder **[1 mark]**
> 
> **Control variables**
> 
> - Concentration of CO<sub>2</sub>; temperature of water; another valid variable e.g. wavelength of light **[1 mark]**
> 
> **Processing results and conclusion**
> 
> - Comparing volume per unit time for each intensity **[1 mark]**
> - Using repeated measurements at each intensity to identify/exclude anomalies **[1 mark]**
> - Plotting a graph of volume of gas against light intensity **[1 mark]**
> 
> Apparatus must be relevant to the method described; marks may be awarded from a labelled diagram/results table/suggested graph axes; safety precaution must link to a specific hazard relevant to the method; control variables must be appropriate to the method

> **[exam-tip]**
> For any practical-planning question, structure your answer under the same five headings examiners mark against — apparatus, method/safety, measurements, control variables, and processing/conclusion — so you don't miss a whole category of marks.

### Q2
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - 6CO<sub>2</sub> + 6H<sub>2</sub>O → C<sub>6</sub>H<sub>12</sub>O<sub>6</sub> + 6O<sub>2</sub> **[2 marks]**
> 
> Accept reactants in any order; accept products in any order; ignore anything written over the arrow

> **[exam-tip]**
> Learn the balanced symbol equation exactly, including all six coefficients — a common mistake is remembering the word equation but forgetting to balance the symbol version.

> **[exam-tip]**
> A blue-black result with iodine confirms starch is present, which in turn confirms photosynthesis has occurred — so a blank iodine test (yellow-brown) tells you a factor needed for photosynthesis (here, CO2) was missing.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) 30 °C **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Enzymes work best at lower temperatures / the enzyme stops working above 25 °C **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Photosynthesis stops, so no glucose/sugar/carbohydrates are produced **[1 mark]**

### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- In the dark, the plant cannot photosynthesise, so it only respires and releases carbon dioxide
- Carbon dioxide dissolves in water to form carbonic acid, making the solution more acidic and turning the hydrogencarbonate indicator yellow
