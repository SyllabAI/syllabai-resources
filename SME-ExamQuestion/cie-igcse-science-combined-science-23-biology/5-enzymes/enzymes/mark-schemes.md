# Mark Schemes — Enzymes
**5. Enzymes** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (1 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) The stomach contents are acidic / have a lower pH **[1 mark]**
> - (i) The active site changes shape **[1 mark]**
> - (i) The substrate no longer fits into (or is no longer complementary to) the active site, so the enzyme-substrate complex no longer forms **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Bell-shaped curve drawn with an optimum pH of 1–3 **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Always explain enzyme pH effects through the active site's shape changing, not just "the enzyme doesn't work anymore" — examiners want the mechanism (shape change → substrate no longer fits), not just the outcome.
