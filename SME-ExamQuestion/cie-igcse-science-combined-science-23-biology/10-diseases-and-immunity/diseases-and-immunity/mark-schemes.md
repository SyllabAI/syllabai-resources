# Mark Schemes — Diseases & Immunity
**10. Diseases & Immunity** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (4 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Any two from: pathogens spread diseases / good personal hygiene stops the spread of pathogens **[2 marks]**
> 
> Accept named pathogen; accept removes pathogens from hands; accept stops pathogens getting onto food

> **[exam-tip]**
> Handwashing is a hygiene measure that physically removes pathogens before they can be transferred to food or other people — link any hygiene control back to reducing pathogen transmission.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) Any two from: the initial vaccination produces fewer antibodies / antibodies increase more slowly / antibodies decrease to a lower number after the peak / antibodies decrease more rapidly after the peak **[2 marks]**
> - (ii) Active immunity ticked **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> A booster vaccination triggers a faster, larger antibody response because memory cells from the first vaccination are already present — this is the key idea behind every initial-vs-booster comparison question.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Platelets help form a clot **[1 mark]**
> - The blood clot seals the wound / prevents entry of pathogens **[1 mark]**
> 
> Ignore produces antibodies

> **[exam-tip]**
> Don't confuse platelets (clotting) with white blood cells (antibody production and phagocytosis) — they are different blood components with different defensive roles.

### Q2
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Skin / hairs in the nose / mucus / stomach acid **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> White blood cells are the body's internal defence, but skin, mucus and stomach acid act as physical/chemical barriers that stop pathogens entering in the first place — make sure you can name at least one of these barrier defences too.

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Active immunity is produced when the immune system is stimulated to make its own antibodies
- Both infection by a pathogen and vaccination expose the body to antigens, triggering antibody production

### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- A pathogen is any organism or agent that causes disease

**A** is incorrect because bacteria are a type of organism that can be a pathogen, not the general term for a disease-causing organism

**C** is incorrect because a pollutant is a harmful substance, not an organism

**D** is incorrect because a virus is a type of pathogen, not the general term for a disease-causing organism
