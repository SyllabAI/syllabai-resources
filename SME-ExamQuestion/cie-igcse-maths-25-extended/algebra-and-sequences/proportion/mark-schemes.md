### Q3

> *As *$y$* is inversely proportional to the square of *$( x + 1 )$*, this relationship can be written as*

$y = \frac{k}{( x + 1 )^{2}}$* where *$k$* is a constant*

**[1]**

> *Substituting in *$y=0.875$* and *$x=1$* and simplifying*

$k=3.5$

> *So the relationship can be written as*

$y = \frac{3.5}{( x + 1 )^{2}}$

> *Substitute in *$x=4$* to find *$y$

$y = \frac{3.5}{( 4 + 1 )^{2}}$

**[1]**

### Q4
> *As *$p$* and *$( q + 2 )^{2}$*are directly proportional, a formula for *$p$* can be found by multiplying a constant (of proportionality) by  *$( q + 2 )^{2}.$

$p = k ( q + 2 )^{2}$

**[1]**

> *Substitute *$p=1$* and *$q=1$* into the formula to form an equation.*

$\begin{gathered} 1 = k \times ( 1 + 2 )^{2} \\ 1 = 9 k \end{gathered}$

> *Solve the equation to find the value of *$k$*.*

$\begin{gathered} 9 k & = 1 \\ k = \frac{1}{9} \end{gathered}$

> *Substitute the value of *$k$* back into the formula for *$p$*.*

$p = \frac{1}{9} ( q + 2 )^{2}$

> *Substitute *$q=10$* into the formula to find the value of *$p.$

$p = \frac{1}{9} \times ( 10 + 2 )^{2}$

**[1]**

$p=16$ **[1]**

### Q6

> *Substitute the value of *$k$* back into the formula for *$y$*.*

$y = 1 \frac{1}{3} \sqrt[3]{x + 3}$

> *Substitute *$x=24$* into the formula to find the value of *$y.$

$y = 1 \frac{1}{3} \times \sqrt[3]{24 + 3}$

**[1]**
