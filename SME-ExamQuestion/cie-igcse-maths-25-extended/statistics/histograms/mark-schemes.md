$\mathrm{Frequency}=\mathrm{Frequency} \mathrm{density}\times\mathrm{Class} \mathrm{width}$

*15 - 5 = 10*

*Frequency = 10 × 1.5 = 15*

*50 - 30 = 20*

> *From the bar in the histogram we see that the frequency density is 1.1, so*
