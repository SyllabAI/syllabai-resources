### Q3 — hard — 9 marks

**Q3** [5 marks]

$p(x)=ax^{3}+3x^{2}+bx-12$ has a factor of $2x+1$. When $p(x)$ is divided by $x-3$ the remainder is 105.

Find the value of $a$ and of $b$.

*Source: March 2020 0606/12 Q7A*


**Q3** [2 marks]

**Extract:**

p(x) = ax³ + 3x² + bx - 12 has a factor of 2x + 1.

a = 6, b = -24

Using your values of $a$ and $b$, write $p(x)$ as a product of $2x+1$ and a quadratic factor.

*Source: March 2020 0606/12 Q7B*


**Q3** [2 marks]

**Extract:**

Using your values of a and b, write p(x) as a product of 2x + 1 and a quadratic factor.

a = 6, b = -24 so p(x) = 6x³ + 3x² - 24x - 12

Rearrange p(x) so that the first 2 terms have a common factor of 2x and the second 2 terms have a common factor of 1

p(x) = 6x³ - 24x + 3x² - 12

Take out the common factor of the first 2 terms and the second 2 terms

p(x) = 2x(3x² - 12) + 1(3x² - 12)

Factorise

p(x) = (2x + 1)(3x² - 12)

Hence solve$p(x)$= 0.

*Source: March 2020 0606/12 Q7C*

### Q7 — very_hard — 8 marks

**Q7** [6 marks]

The polynomial $p(x)=ax^{3}+bx^{2}-19x+4$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants, has a factor $x+4$ and is such that $2p(1)=5p(0)$.

Show that $p(x)=(x+4)(Ax^{2}+Bx+C)$, where $A$, $B$ and $C$ are integers to be found.

*Source: Nov 2020 0606/13 Q7A*


**Q7** [1 mark]

**Extract:**

The polynomial p(x) = ax³ + bx² - 19x + 4, where a and b are constants, has a factor x + 4 and is such that 2p(1) = 5p(0).

From part a, we found that a = 6 and b = 19, giving:
p(x) = 6x³ + 19x² - 19x + 4

And we showed that:
p(x) = (x + 4)(Ax² + Bx + C)

where A = 6, B = -5, and C = 1.

Therefore:
p(x) = (x + 4)(6x² - 5x + 1)

Hence factorise $p(x)$.

*Source: Nov 2020 0606/13 Q7B*

### Q8 — hard — 8 marks

**Q8** [6 marks]

$p(x)=6x^{3}+ax^{2}+12x+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers.

$p(x)$ has a remainder of 11 when divided by $x-3$ and a remainder of $-21$ when divided by $x+1$. 
Given that $p(x)=(x-2)Q(x)$, find $Q(x)$, a quadratic factor with numerical coefficients.

*Source: june 2020 0606/13 Q5A*


**Q8** [2 marks]

**Extract:**

p(x) = 6x³ + ax² + 12x + b, where a and b are integers.

p(x) has a remainder of 11 when divided by x - 3 and a remainder of -21 when divided by x + 1.

Given that p(x) = (x - 2)Q(x), find Q(x), a quadratic factor with numerical coefficients.

Using remainder theorem and solving simultaneous equations: a = -23 and b = 20.

Therefore p(x) = 6x³ - 23x² + 12x + 20

Using algebraic long division:

p(x) = (x - 2)(6x² - 11x - 10)

Hence solve $p(x)=0$.

*Source: june 2020 0606/13 Q5B*
