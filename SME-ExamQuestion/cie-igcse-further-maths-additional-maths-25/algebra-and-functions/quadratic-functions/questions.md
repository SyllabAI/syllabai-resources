### Q15 — medium — 3 marks

**Q15** [2 marks]

Write the expression $x^{2}-6x+1$ in the form $(x+a)^{2}+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants.

*Source: June 2021 Paper 21 Q1A*


**Q15** [1 mark]

**Extract:**

Write the expression x² - 6x + 1 in the form (x + a)² + b, where a and b are constants.

x² - 6x + 1 = (x - 3)² - (3)² + 1
= (x - 3)² - 9 + 1
= (x - 3)² - 8

Hence write down the coordinates of the minimum point on the curve $y=x^{2}-6x+1$.

*Source: June 2021 Paper 21 Q1B*
