**Q3** [2 marks]

**Extract:**

The points A and B have coordinates (-2, 4) and (6, 10) respectively.

Midpoint of AB = (2, 7)

The point C has coordinates (5, p) and lies on the perpendicular bisector of AB.

From the equation 4x + 3y - 29 = 0, when x = 5:
(4 × 5) + 3y - 29 = 0
20 + 3y - 29 = 0
3y = 9
y = 3

Therefore C = (5, 3)

### Q5 — very_hard — 8 marks

**Q5** [3 marks]

The line $y=5x+6$ meets the curve $xy=8$ at the points $A$ and $B$.

Find the coordinates of $A$ and of $B$.

*Source: june 2020 0606/11 Q6A*


**Q5** [5 marks]

**Extract:**

The line y = 5x + 6 meets the curve xy = 8 at the points A and B.

Substituting the equation of the line into the equation of the curve and solving these equations simultaneously by substitution
x(5x + 6) = 8

Expanding the brackets and setting the equation equal to 0
5x² + 6x - 8 = 0

Factorising to find the solutions
(5x - 4)(x + 2) = 0
x = 4/5, x = -2

Substituting to find the y-coordinates
y = 5(4/5) + 6 = 10
y = 5(-2) + 6 = -4

(4/5, 10)
(-2, -4)

Find the coordinates of the point where the perpendicular bisector of the line $AB$ meets the line $y=x$.

*Source: june 2020 0606/11 Q6B*

### Q7 — very_hard — 8 marks

**Q7** [4 marks]

**Solutions to this question by accurate drawing will not be accepted.** The points $A$ and $B$ are (4, 3) and (12, −7) respectively.

Find the equation of the line $L$, the perpendicular bisector of the line $AB$.

*Source: june 2020 0606/22 Q5A*


**Q7** [4 marks]

**Extract:**

The points A and B are (4, 3) and (12, −7) respectively.

Find the equation of the line L, the perpendicular bisector of the line AB.

From the mark scheme:
Calculate the gradient between points A and B:
gradient_AB = (-7 - 3)/(12 - 4)
gradient_AB = -5/4

Find the midpoint, M, of AB:
M = ((4 + 12)/2, (3 + (-7))/2)
M = (8, -2)

Find the gradient of the perpendicular line, L:
gradient_L = 4/5

Find the equation of line L through the midpoint M = (8, -2) with gradient 4/5:
y - (-2) = 4/5(x - 8)
or
y + 2 = 4/5(x - 8)

The line parallel to $AB$ which passes through the point (5, 12) intersects $L$ at the point $C$. Find the coordinates of $C$.

*Source: june 2020 0606/22 Q5B*
