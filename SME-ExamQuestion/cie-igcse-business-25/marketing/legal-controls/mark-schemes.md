### Q2
Compliance checks would allow SSE to systematically review their marketing materials and practices against specific legal requirements in each country **[Ap]**. For example, they could verify that helmet advertisements comply with safety claim regulations or consumer protection laws **[Ap]**. This would help identify potential legal violations before they occur, reducing the risk of fines or legal action **[An]**. However, these checks can be expensive to conduct regularly, especially across multiple countries with different legal frameworks **[Ap]**. This could increase operational costs and require specialist legal expertise **[An]**.

Staff training programmes would be more cost-effective for SSE in the long term. They could educate marketing teams about advertising standards and consumer protection laws in different countries **[Ap]**. This would help prevent legal violations from occurring in the first place **[Ap]** and create a culture of compliance throughout the organisation **[Ap]**. Training materials like online courses could provide accessible information about legal requirements, but staff may forget key details over time or new laws may be introduced. Regular updates and refresher training may therefore be needed to maintain effectiveness **[An]**.

Overall, SSE should prioritise staff training programmes, supported by periodic legal compliance audits **[E]**. While compliance checks are important for verification, training programmes will embed compliance knowledge directly into daily marketing decisions **[E]**. This is crucial as SSE expands internationally and marketing laws vary significantly between countries **[Ap]**. The proactive approach of training will help SSE avoid costly legal violations and maintain their reputation in new markets **[E]**.

>   - Analysis (4) – Chain effects clearly, using business terms
>   - Evaluation (4) – Balanced judgement, rejects others with reasoning

> **Mark scheme**
> 
> - **Compliance checks**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - Systematic review of marketing materials → identifies specific legal violations → prevents fines and legal action
>     - Expert assessment of advertising claims about helmet safety → ensures compliance with consumer protection laws → maintains credibility
>     - Regular monitoring of changing legal requirements → helps adapt marketing strategies → reduces compliance risks
>     - Independent verification provides assurance → builds confidence with regulators → protects company reputation
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - Specialist legal expertise required → higher consultancy costs → increased operational expenses

>     - Online delivery enables quick updates → keeps staff informed of law changes → maintains current knowledge

>     - Generic training may not cover specific country requirements → gaps in legal knowledge → potential non-compliance
>     - Requires regular updates as laws change → ongoing training costs → administrative burden

>   - Compliance checks: best for thorough verification and expert assessment of complex legal requirements
>   - Staff training programmes: best for embedding compliance knowledge and preventing violations proactively
>   - Practical approach: implement comprehensive staff training as foundation, supplemented by targeted audits for high-risk marketing materials or new market entries
