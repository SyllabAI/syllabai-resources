### Q2
Shareholders want TGR to make more money. TGR made £5 million profit last year **[Ap]**. Shareholders might push for international expansion to increase profits further to maximise dividends and increase the share price **[An]**.

Employees are crucial for TGR's success. The company has 250 workers, including skilled technicians **[Ap]**. Customers are vital for TGR's sales. Customers are worried about the environmental impact of electronic devices **[Ap]**, so TGR might need to develop more eco-friendly products to keep customers happy and maintain sales **[An]**

I think customers will have the most influence on TGR's future strategy **[E]**. The smart home device market is getting more competitive **[Ap]**, so keeping customers happy is crucial for TGR's success. Customers' growing concerns about the environmental impact of electronic devices **[Ap]** could shape what products TGR develops in the future. Most importantly, without satisfied customers buying their products, TGR will neither be able to make money for shareholders nor provide security or higher pay for their 250 skilled employees **[Ap]** so, by focusing on meeting customer needs, TGR can stay competitive and grow in the long term. >   - Concerned about supply-chain risks (exchange-rate fluctuations) → push for local sourcing or hedging to protect margins

### Q3
Online sales have been growing, reaching 22% in 2022 **[Ap]**, so RMT's customers are increasingly shopping online. Expanding e-commerce could help RMT compete better with online rivals, increasing market share **[An]**. It would also reduce costs from running physical stores, like rent and utilities, which could contribute to improving RMT's profit **[An]**. However, it would mean making 100 store staff redundant **[Ap]**, which could damage employee morale and RMT's reputation **[An]**

Keeping all stores open but reducing costs avoids job losses, which would please employees **[An]**. It maintains RMT's physical presence, which some customers prefer **[Ap]**. Costs could be reduced by negotiating better deals with suppliers, reducing inventory or cutting marketing expenses **[An]**. However, it might not improve profits enough, as RMT's profit has been declining despite increasing revenue **[An]** from \$80m to \$85m between 2020-2022 **[Ap]**

I recommend closing 10 stores and expanding e-commerce **[E]**. This recognises changing customer behaviour, with more and more buying RMT's products online **[Ap]**. While 100 jobs would be lost, 50 new jobs would be created in e-commerce and distribution **[Ap]** and there is potential to enter overseas markets by selling online **[E]**. While shareholder concerns about falling profits would be addressed by reducing costs, simply cutting costs and carrying on operating stores doesn't address the fundamental market changes and limits potential to gain new customers, especially in other countries **[E]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Structure**
> 
> - Write one PEA paragraph for each point identified in the question:
> 
>   - Point: Identify the problem
>   - Evidence (Ap): Apply case study detail
>   - Analysis (An): Explain business impact
> - Add a final Evaluation (E):
> 
>   - Decide which problem is easiest/hardest to overcome
>   - Justify your choice
>   - Compare and reject alternatives
> 
> **Marking approach**
> 
> - This is a levelled response answer in which the examiner determines how well you have demonstrated skills of application, analysis and evaluation
> 
>   - Application (4) – Use case study facts
>   - Analysis (4) – Chain effects clearly, using business terms
>   - Evaluation (4) – Balanced judgement, rejects others with reasoning
> 
> **Mark scheme**
> 
> - **Close 10 stores and expand e-commerce operations**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - Reduces fixed costs by closing 10 underperforming stores → cuts rent and staffing expenses
>     - Frees up funds to improve the online platform and distribution centre → meets customer demand for easier web shopping
>     - Online sales have risen from 15 % in 2020 to 22 % in 2022 → aligns investment with a growing channel
>     - Focuses the store network on profitable locations → increases average revenue per remaining store
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - 100 store-staff redundancies vs only 50 new online roles → net loss of jobs may harm staff morale and public image
>     - Loss of physical presence in 10 communities → risks alienating loyal local shoppers
>     - Upfront investment needed for website upgrades and warehouse expansion → may strain cash flow in the short term
>     - Reduced ability for customers to try on products in-store → could weaken omnichannel experience
> - **Keep all stores open but reduce costs**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - Protects all 800 employees’ jobs → maintains morale and local goodwill
>     - Retains full network of 50 stores → preserves brand visibility and customer convenience
>     - Cost cuts (e.g. negotiate rent reductions, streamline staffing rotas, bulk-buy supplies) → improves margins without closing sites
>     - Less disruptive for customers and staff → avoids the risks of large-scale closures
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - Cost savings may be too small to reverse falling profits → limited impact on overall financial performance
>     - Risk of cutting too deeply → service levels or product quality could suffer, driving customers away
>     - Underperforming stores continue to lose money → ongoing drain on resources that could fund growth
>     - Delay in e-commerce investment → competitors may pull further ahead online
> - **Recommendation**
> 
>   - Close 10 stores and expand e-commerce is the stronger choice to improve financial performance because it delivers larger, more immediate cost savings and aligns with clear growth in online sales
>   - To balance the impact on staff and customers, RMT should offer generous redundancy packages, retrain some store staff for online roles, and maintain an omnichannel focus by enhancing click-and-collect services
>   - This strategy uses savings from store closures to fund a robust e-commerce upgrade, positioning RMT for long-term success in a digital retail environment
