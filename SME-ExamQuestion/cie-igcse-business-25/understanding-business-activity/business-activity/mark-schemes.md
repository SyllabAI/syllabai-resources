### Q3
Land provides the physical space and natural resources needed for production **[K]**, such as raw materials, location for facilities, and access to natural resources. Without suitable land, businesses may struggle to operate efficiently or access essential inputs for their production processes **[An]**

Labour is also crucial for business success **[K]**. Skilled workers are needed to transform inputs into outputs, and without adequate human resources, even the best land and equipment cannot generate products or services that meet customer needs **[An]**

Land is not always the most important factor of production, particularly for service-based businesses or those operating in the digital economy **[E]**. For many modern businesses, skilled labour and technology may be more critical than physical location. A software company, for example, can operate successfully with minimal land requirements but needs highly skilled programmers and advanced technology. However, for manufacturing or agricultural businesses, land remains essential for providing space and raw materials needed for production **[E]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - You are writing a mini-argument with a decision
> - Give one relevant reason to support your view → 1 K mark
> - Explain why this reason helps the business (cause-effect chain) → 1 An mark
> - Give a different possible reason or viewpoint → 1 K mark
> - Explain why this could also help the business → 1 An mark
> - Make a decision — which argument is stronger or more important overall? → up to 2 E marks
> 
> **Mark scheme**
> 
> - Points might include:
> 
>   - **Land**: Provides physical space and natural resources for production [k]; essential for manufacturing, agriculture, and retail businesses that need premises [an]; poor location can limit customer access and increase costs [an]
>   - **Labour**: Skilled workers transform inputs into outputs [k]; without adequate human resources, production cannot take place effectively [an]; skilled labour can increase productivity and quality [an]
>   - **Capital**: Machinery and equipment needed for efficient production [k]; modern technology can increase output and reduce costs [an]; lack of capital investment may make businesses uncompetitive [an]
>   - **Enterprise**: Entrepreneurial skills needed to combine other factors effectively [k]; without good management and decision-making, other factors may be wasted [an]
> - Justification might include:
> 
>   - Land is crucial for businesses requiring physical premises or natural resources [k]; however, service businesses may need minimal land but require skilled labour instead [eval]
>   - Modern technology-based businesses may operate with small physical footprints but need significant capital investment [eval]
>   - The importance of each factor depends on the type of business and industry sector [eval]
>   - Other appropriate responses should also be credited

### Q9
Adding value is important if a business wants to make a profit **[K]**, as it involves increasing the difference between the costs of making a product and the price it can be sold. If a business does not make a profit, it is unlikely to survive in the long term** [An]**

Meeting customer needs **[K]** is also important. If customers are not satisfied with a business's products, they may choose to buy from competitors, reducing business sales and making it difficult to break even **[An]**

Adding value is not always the most important purpose of business activity, especially for less established business start-ups **[E]**. It is more important to meet customer needs so they buy products repeatedly. This may involve lowering prices or increasing quality, which could actually reduce added value in the short run. Once brand-loyal, adding value to make a profit may be more important to ensure the long-term survival of a business **[E]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - You are writing a mini-argument with a decision
> 
>   - Give one relevant reason to support your view → 1 K mark
>   - Explain why this reason helps the business (cause-effect chain) →  1 An mark
>   - Give a different possible reason or viewpoint → 1 K mark
>   - Explain why this could also help the business → 1 An mark
>   - Make a decision — which argument is stronger or more important overall? → up to 2 E marks
> 
> **Mark scheme**
> 
> - Points might include:
> 
>   - Adding value: Increases the difference between cost of production and selling price [k]; helps the business generate profit [an]; without profit, a business may not survive in the long term [an]
>   - Meeting customer needs: Providing products or services that satisfy demand [k]; if customers are unhappy, they may switch to competitors [an]; loss of customers can reduce sales/revenue and threaten survival [an]
>   - Making a profit: Businesses need profit to reinvest, grow or reward investors [k]; no profit means the business may not be able to operate long-term [an]
>   - Survival: Especially important for new or small businesses [k]; focus might be on covering costs rather than adding value at first [an]
>   - Social/ethical aims: Some businesses prioritise sustainability or social impact [k]; these aims might reduce short-term profit or added value [an]
> - Justification might include:
> 
>   - Adding value is important for generating profit [k]; however, for start-ups, meeting customer needs may be more important to ensure repeat sales and survival [eval]
>   - A business may need to reduce prices or increase quality to attract buyers, even if this lowers added value [eval]
>   - Once brand loyalty is built, the business can then focus more on adding value to improve profit and ensure long-term success [eval]
> - Other appropriate responses should also be credited
