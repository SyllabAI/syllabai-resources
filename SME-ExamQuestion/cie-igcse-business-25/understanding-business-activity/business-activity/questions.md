### Q6 — hard — 12 marks

**Q6** [12 marks]

**Extract:**

**EcoTech Solutions (ETS)**

ETS is a medium-sized technology company based in Country X that specialises in developing eco-friendly smart home devices. Founded in 2010, ETS has grown steadily and now employs 250 people. The company's main products include energy-efficient thermostats, water-saving shower systems, and solar-powered garden lights.

ETS currently sells its products primarily through its website and a network of independent retailers in Country X. The company has been profitable for the past five years, with revenues reaching £15 million and profits of £2 million in the last financial year. However, rising costs of raw materials and increasing competition in the domestic market have put pressure on profit margins.

The Board of Directors is considering expanding into neighbouring countries to increase sales and spread risk. They believe there is growing demand for eco-friendly home technology in these markets. However, this would require significant investment in marketing, distribution, and possibly new manufacturing facilities.

ETS prides itself on its eco-friendly ethos and has won several awards for its innovative products. The company uses locally sourced materials where possible and has implemented a recycling programme for its products. However, the proposed expansion could potentially increase the company's carbon footprint.

The Human Resources Director has suggested that if the expansion goes ahead, they should use a mix of internal promotions and external recruitment to staff the new operations. She believes this approach would provide opportunities for existing staff while also bringing in new expertise.

As ETS considers this strategic decision, it must weigh the potential for growth against the risks and challenges of entering new markets. The company also needs to consider how to maintain its commitment to sustainability while expanding its operations.

Consider the advantages and disadvantages of the following two options for ETS to add value to its products.

- Improve product quality by using more expensive, highly durable materials.
- Enhance brand image through an extensive marketing campaign.

Which option should ETS choose? **Justify **your answer.

**GHI Cosmetics**

GHI is a medium-sized manufacturer of beauty products based in Country Y. The business was established 20 years ago and has grown steadily. It now employs 200 people in its factory and head office. GHI specialises in mid-priced organic skincare and makeup products. All items in the product range are packaged in brightly-coloured recycled plastic containers and are targeted at environmentally conscious young consumers.

GHI uses a range of social media channels, which include popular podcasts that provide guidance on the application of makeup products. These are shared widely, and GHI makes use of feedback and viewer comments to improve existing products and develop new ones. It also shares new product launches with online followers.

The beauty industry has become increasingly competitive in recent years, with many new entrants focusing on natural and sustainable products. GHI's sales have grown slowly over the past two years (see Appendix 1). The CEO is considering expanding the product range to include a line of vegan hair care products to boost sales and attract new customers. She is also looking for a suitable celebrity to act as the face of its brand.

GHI currently sells its products through department stores and specialist beauty retailers. In some larger outlets, brand representatives are employed to offer advice and demonstrations to consumers. It is investigating the possibility of selling directly to consumers through its own website and social media channels to increase profit margins.

The government of Country Y has recently introduced new regulations requiring all cosmetics companies to list full ingredients on packaging and reduce waste. GHI is evaluating the potential impact on its production costs and manufacturing processes.
