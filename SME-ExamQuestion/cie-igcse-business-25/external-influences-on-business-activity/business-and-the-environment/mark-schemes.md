>   - Explain why this could also help the business → 1 An mark
>   - Make a decision — which argument is stronger or more important overall? → up to 2 E marks
>

>

>

>   - Regulatory compliance costs are expensive [k] reducing funds available for growth [an]

>   - Yes, businesses need to prioritise profit to survive and satisfy shareholders [k] as environmental investments increase costs [an].

### Q8
An increase in the minimum wage would likely raise CC's costs, as they currently pay their 10 skilled production workers just above the minimum wage **[Ap]**. With plans to hire two additional workers for their new personalised covers **[Ap]**, this could further increase labour costs. CC might need to raise prices or find ways to increase productivity to maintain profits **[An]**

More controls over claims made in advertising could impact CC's marketing strategy. Their current advertisement states, "Your phone will never be damaged if you use one of our high quality covers!!"**[Ap]** If this claim is found to be misleading, CC may need to change their advertising approach, which could incur costs for market research **[An]**

The ban on sending waste plastic to landfill sites would require CC to find alternative methods of disposing of the large amount of plastic waste **[Ap]** it generates. However, recycling this waste could significantly increase their costs because it may involve investing in new equipment or outsourcing to specialist recycling companies **[An]**

The ban on sending waste plastic to landfill sites is likely to have the greatest effect on profit **[E]**. It would require a complete overhaul of CC's waste management processes, affecting both their standardised covers and new personalised range **[Ap]**. While the other changes are significant, they may be easier to adapt to through gradual wage increases or changing marketing strategies **[E]**. The waste management issue, however, affects all of CC's production process and would require immediate and costly solutions **[E]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Structure**
> 
> - Write one PEA paragraph for each point identified in the question:
> 
>   - Point: Identify the problem
>   - Evidence (Ap): Apply case study detail
>   - Analysis (An): Explain business impact
> - Add a final Evaluation (E):
> 
>   - Decide which problem is easiest/hardest to overcome
>   - Justify your choice
>   - Compare and reject alternatives
> 
> **Marking approach**
> 
> - This is a levelled response answer in which the examiner determines how well you have demonstrated skills of application, analysis and evaluation
> 
>   - Application (4) – Use case study facts
>   - Analysis (4) – Chain effects clearly, using business terms
>   - Evaluation (4) – Balanced judgement, rejects others with reasoning
> 
> **Mark scheme**
> 
> - **Increase in the minimum wage**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - May boost worker motivation → higher productivity and quality
>     - If CC already pays just above minimum wage → costs may remain unchanged
>     - Higher wages increase disposable income for employees → potential rise in cover sales
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - Wage bill rises as 10 workers earn more → higher operating costs
>     - Competing employers may lure staff if CC holds wages flat → recruitment and training costs increase
> - **More controls over advertisement claims**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - If CC’s current adverts are truthful → no revision needed → zero extra cost
>     - Competitors must also comply → CC’s superior product claims stand out → possible sales gain
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - Misleading adverts must be reworked → may become less appealing → potential drop in sales
> - **Ban on sending plastic waste to landfill**
> 
>   - **Advantages**
>   
>     - Recycling focus can cut long-term raw-material costs → boost margin
>     - Competitors with higher disposal costs become less competitive → CC may capture market share
>   - **Disadvantages**
>   
>     - Proper plastic recycling incurs higher disposal fees → increases short-term costs
>     - Training 10 workers and changing processes → upfront expense and possible production delays
> - **Conclusion**
> 
>   - Minimum wage rise has the greatest profit impact due to significantly higher wage costs
>   - Plastic-waste controls impose moderate costs but offer long-term savings and competitive advantage
>   - Advertisement rules carry minimal cost if CC’s claims are already accurate, making this the least impactful change
