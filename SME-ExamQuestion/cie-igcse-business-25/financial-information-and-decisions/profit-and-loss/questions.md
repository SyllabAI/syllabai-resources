**Educational Toys Limited (ETL)**

ETL is a private limited company that manufactures educational toys in Country Y. It has been in business for 15 years and has a reputation for producing high-quality, innovative toys that support children's learning. ETL's main products include interactive science kits, mathematical puzzles, and language learning games for children aged 3-12.

ETL currently sells its products primarily through toy stores and educational supply shops in Country Y. The company has one factory employing 150 workers and uses batch production methods. ETL's sales revenue last year was \$8 million, with a gross profit of \$3.2 million and a net profit of \$800,000.

The CEO of ETL is considering expanding the business by entering the online market and selling directly to consumers through an e-commerce website. This would require an investment of \$500,000 in website development and digital marketing. The finance director has prepared a break-even analysis for this new sales channel, projecting that ETL would need to sell 25,000 units online per year to break even.

ETL is facing increasing competition from imported toys, which are often cheaper but of lower quality. The government of Country Y has recently announced it is considering introducing import tariffs on toys to protect domestic manufacturers.

The company is also aware of growing consumer interest in environmentally friendly and ethically produced toys. ETL's marketing director believes there is an opportunity to rebrand the company as a sustainable toy manufacturer, but this would require changes to production processes and materials.
