## Exam Questions (28 questions)


### Q1 — hard — 4 marks

**Q1** [4 marks]

A large organisation is deciding whether to install an Artificial Intelligence (AI) system that will monitor and control the heating, the cooling and the lighting in all of its buildings.

The organisation wants to know whether installing the system will be good for the environment.

Explain why the environmental impact of this system is difficult to predict.

### Q2 — medium — 3 marks

**Q2** [3 marks]

Describe **one** economic impact of this system on the bank.

### Q3 — hard — 4 marks

**Q3** [4 marks]

A small company has written a photo-editing program for personal computers.

The company has these needs:

- the program must earn money to pay the company's programmers
- the company's research shows that many customers will not pay for a program they have not used
- nobody outside the company should be able to change the program code

Identify the most appropriate type of software licence for the program and justify your choice.

### Q6 — hard — 4 marks

**Q6** [4 marks]

Explain how AI is used to suggest a page, and how the suggestions become more accurate.

### Q10 — hard — 4 marks
