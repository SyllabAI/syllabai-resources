## Exam Questions (8 questions)


### Q1 — hard — 4 marks

**Q1** [4 marks]

Each night a company copies a very large archive file from one of its sites to another. The file is not viewed or used while it is being copied.

The connection between the two sites is shared with other traffic at the same time, and one of the routes between the sites fails occasionally.

Explain why packet switching is a better choice than circuit switching for this transfer.

### Q2 — medium — 3 marks

**Q2** [3 marks]

Give **three** differences between circuit switching and packet switching.

### Q3 — easy — 2 marks

**Q3** [2 marks]

A company sets up a link between its two offices for live video meetings. The link must carry continuous picture and sound in both directions for as long as a meeting lasts.

State which switching method is more suitable for this link, and give **one** reason for your answer.

### Q4 — hard — 4 marks

**Q4** [4 marks]

The video file still arrives complete and in the correct order.

Explain how this is possible.
