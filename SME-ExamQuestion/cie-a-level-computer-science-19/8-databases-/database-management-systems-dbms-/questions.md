## Exam Questions (7 questions)


### Q1 — hard — 4 marks

**Q1** [4 marks]

An online bookshop uses a Database Management System (DBMS).

Two members of staff work with the database on the same day.

- Anika is a database developer. She needs to add a new table to the database and build a screen that other staff will use to type in new stock.
- Ben works in the warehouse. He needs a list of every order that was placed in March.

For **each** member of staff, identify the DBMS software tool they use and explain why that tool is the correct one.

### Q2 — medium — 3 marks

**Q2** [3 marks]

A travel company uses a Database Management System (DBMS) to hold data about its bookings.

A member of staff at the company uses the developer interface of the DBMS.

Describe **three** tasks that the member of staff can carry out using the developer interface.

### Q3 — easy — 2 marks

**Q3** [2 marks]

### Q4 — hard — 4 marks

**Q4** [4 marks]

A chain of language schools operates in six countries. The chain asks a designer to produce one new database that all six countries will use.

Before any tables are created, the designer produces a logical schema for the database.

Explain why the designer produces a logical schema before the database is built.
