`\text{Gain in KE} = \frac{1}{2}(80)\left(2v\right)^2 - \frac{1}{2}(80)v^2 = 120v^2`

**[M1]**

Air resistance is ignored, so mechanical energy is conserved and the two are equal

$120 v^{2} = 80000$

**[M1]**

**Final answer:** $v = 25 . 8$** (3 s.f.)**
