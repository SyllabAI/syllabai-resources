> - longitudinal waves: the direction of vibration is parallel to the direction of propagation **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> A quick way to remember the difference: in a transverse wave the vibrations are perpendicular ("across") the direction of travel, like a wave on a rope; in a longitudinal wave the vibrations are parallel ("along") the direction of travel, like sound waves.
