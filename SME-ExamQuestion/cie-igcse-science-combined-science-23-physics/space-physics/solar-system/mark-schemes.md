### Q1
To calculate the orbital speed of the Earth:

- List the known quantities:

- Radius of orbit, r = 1.51 × 10<sup>8</sup> km
- Time for one orbit, T = 365.25 days

- Convert the time to hours:

$T = 365 . 25 \times 24$ **[1 mark]**

$T = 8766 \text{h}$

- Write down the equation:

$v = \frac{2πr}{T}$

- Substitute the known values:

$v = \frac{2π\times1.51\times10^{8}}{8766}$ **[1 mark]**

- Calculate the answer:

v = **1.08 × 10**<sup>**5**</sup>** km/h** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> 1 mark for correctly converting 365.25 days to 8766 hours
> 
> 1 mark for correct substitution into $v = \frac{2πr}{T}$ (accept formula in any form)
> 
> 1 mark for a correct final answer of 1.08 × 10<sup>5</sup> km/h

> **[exam-tip]**
> Always check the units required by the question — here the answer needs to be in km/h, so make sure you convert the orbital period from days into hours before substituting into the equation.
