To show that the efficiency of the motor is 89%:

- List the known quantities:

- Power input, P = 3.6 W
- Time, t = 10 s
- Useful energy output, = 32 J

- Calculate the useful power output:

$\text{useful power output} = \frac{\text{energy}}{\text{time}} = \frac{32}{10}$ **[1 mark]**

$= 3 . 2 \text{W}$

- Calculate the efficiency:

`\text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful power output}}{\text{total power input}} \times 100 \% = \frac{3.2}{3.6} \times 100` **[1 mark]**

= **88.9 \%** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**

### Q2
To calculate the increase in gravitational potential energy:

- List the known quantities:

- Mass, m = 55 kg
- Height, h = 0.15 m
- Gravitational field strength, g = 9.8 N/kg

- Write down the equation:

$\Delta E_{p} = m g h$

- Substitute the known values:

$\Delta E_{p} = 55 \times 9 . 8 \times 0 . 15$ **[1 mark]**

- Calculate the answer:

∆ E<sub>p</sub> = 80.85 \approx **81** **[1 mark]**

Unit: **J (joules)** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**

> 
> 1 mark for a correct answer of 81 J
> 
> - Accept 80.9 or 80.85
> 
> 1 mark for the correct unit, J (joules)

> **[exam-tip]**

### Q3
To calculate the energy transferred:

- List the known quantities:

- Power, P = 2200 W
- Time, t = 15 s

- Write down the equation:

$\text{power} = \frac{\text{energy}}{\text{time}}$

- Rearrange for energy:

$E = P \times t$

- Substitute the known values:

$E = 2200 \times 15$ **[1 mark]**

- Calculate the answer:

E = **33 000 J** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> 1 mark for correct substitution into $E = P \times t$ (accept formula in any form)
> 
> 1 mark for a correct final answer of 33 000 J

> **[exam-tip]**
> Watch out for extra information you don't need in the question — the voltage of 240 V isn't required here, since energy transferred depends only on power and time.

**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Work done = force × distance moved in the direction of the force

$W = F \times d = 200 \times 5 . 0$

$= 1000 \text{J}$

- The distance used is the distance along the slope (5.0 m), not the vertical or horizontal component
