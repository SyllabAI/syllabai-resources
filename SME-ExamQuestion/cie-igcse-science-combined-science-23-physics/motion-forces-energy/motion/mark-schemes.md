To calculate the distance walked at constant speed:

- List the known quantities:

- Speed, v = 1.5 m/s (read from the graph between 40 s and 100 s)
- Time, t = 100 - 40 = 60 s

- Write down the equation:

$\text{distance} = \text{speed} \times \text{time}$

> **[mark-scheme]**

>

> 
> 1 mark for a correct final answer of 90 m
> 
> - Marks must be awarded in this order

> **[exam-tip]**
> On a speed–time graph, deceleration is shown by a downward-sloping section — mark your point anywhere along that section, not just at the start or end. For distance at constant speed, remember you can either multiply speed by time or find the area under the flat section of the graph — both give the same answer.
