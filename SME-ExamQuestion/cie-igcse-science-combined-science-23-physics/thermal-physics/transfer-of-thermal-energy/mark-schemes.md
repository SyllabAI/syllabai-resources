> **[exam-tip]**
> The Earth's temperature rises when it absorbs more radiation from the Sun than it emits back into space — this imbalance between absorbed and emitted radiation is the key idea behind the greenhouse effect.

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Cooling increases the density of the liquid
- Placing the cooling unit at position P (top) allows the cooled, denser liquid to sink while warmer, less dense liquid rises, creating a convection current that circulates and cools all the liquid

**A** is incorrect because although position P is correct, cooling decreases the density is wrong — cooling increases the density of the liquid

**B** is incorrect because both parts are wrong — cooling increases density (not decreases), and position P (not Q) is best since the denser cooled liquid needs to sink from the top

**D** is incorrect because although cooling increasing density is correct, position Q is wrong — the cooling unit should be at P so cooled, denser liquid sinks and creates a convection current

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Thermal radiation (infrared) can travel through a vacuum, for example from the Sun to the Earth

**B** is incorrect because thermal radiation is absorbed more quickly by dull, dark surfaces than by shiny surfaces

**C** is incorrect because thermal radiation is emitted more quickly by dull, dark surfaces than by shiny surfaces

**D** is incorrect because thermal radiation is mainly infrared radiation, not ultraviolet radiation
