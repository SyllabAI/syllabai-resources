# Mark Schemes — Practical Skills II: Implementation & Measurements
**6. Practical Skills in Physics II** · Edexcel International A Level (IAL) Physics (YPH11)


## Q1 — medium — 7 marks · structured-questions

### 1((a)) — 5 marks
i) Two other control variables for this investigation are:

- Mass **OR** volume (of water); **[1 mark]**
- Time **OR **distance between resistor and thermometer;** ****[1 mark]**

ii) Criticise the recording of this data

- Significant figures are inconsistent**; [1 mark]**
- Not enough sets of readings taken **OR** no repeats shown**; [1 mark]**
- No units given for ∆*θ ***OR** initial and final values of *θ* not recorded**; [1 mark]**

**[Total: 5 marks]**

- *For part i), answers that would not be accepted are *

  - *Room temperature*
  - *Temperature of the surroundings*
  - *Size of the bucket or insulation*
  - *Potential difference*

### 1((b)) — 2 marks
One improvement the student could make to reduce the uncertainty in the measurement of ∆*θ* for each value of *I *is:

**EITHER**

- Insulate the beaker **OR** cover the beaker; **[1 mark]**
- To reduce the amount of energy transferred to the environment; **[1 mark]**

**OR**

- Stir the water **OR** ensure the thermometer is close to the resistor; **[1 mark]**
- To ensure the water is at the same temperature as the resistor; **[1 mark]**

**[Total: 2 marks]**

- *Your second point **must** relate back to the first in order to achieve both marks*

  - *Therefore, a good answer is either of these **pairs*
