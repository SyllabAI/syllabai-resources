# Mark Schemes — Robotics
**6. Automated & Emerging Technologies** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE computer-science (0478 & 0984)


## Exam Questions (6 questions)


### Q1
Characteristics of a robot include:

Any **three **of the following:

- It has a mechanical structure/framework** ****[1 mark]**
- It has electrical components **[1 mark]**
- It is programmable **[1 mark]**
- It can move **[1 mark]**

### Q2
The **two **characteristics of a robot are:

- It has a mechanical structure/framework **[1 mark]**
- It has electrical components **OR** by example **[1 mark]**
Examples of advantages of using robots include:

Any **two **of the following:

- Employees don’t need to lift heavy furniture **[1 mark]**
- Employees can be protected from dangerous tasks **[1 mark]**
- Employees can utilise their skills in other tasks (accept: focus on / do other tasks) **[1 mark]**
- Employees don’t need to perform repetitive/mundane tasks **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Read the question carefully: it asks for advantages to employees, not to the company, so avoid points like speed, efficiency or no pay
> 
> Make each point specific to a benefit for the worker rather than vague statements like being more relaxed

### Q3
Examples of disadvantages to the company include:

Any **one **of the following:

- Expensive to install/purchase/setup **[1 mark]**
- High ongoing costs/maintenance costs **[1 mark]**
- May deskill the workforce **[1 mark]**
- If they malfunction, production may stop **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Any **other **valid response can still be awarded a mark

### Q4
The **two **characteristics of a robot are:

- It has electrical components **[1 mark]**
- It is programmable **[1 mark]**
Examples of advantages of using robots include:

Any** two** of the following:

- more efficient than a human **[1 mark]**
- more accurate than a human **[1 mark]**
- can lift larger and heavier equipment than a human **[1 mark]**
- can replace humans working in a dangerous environment **[1 mark]**
- reduces running costs, such as wages and overheads **[1 mark]**
- can produce consistent results/output **[1 mark]**
- can work 24 hours a day **OR **do not need to take breaks **[1 mark]**
