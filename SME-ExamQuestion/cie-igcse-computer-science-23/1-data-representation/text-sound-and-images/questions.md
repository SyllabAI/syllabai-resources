### Q1 — easy — 2 marks

**Q1** [2 marks]

A music producer is working on a digital recording project. They need to understand the technical specifications of their audio files to ensure optimal quality for different distribution platforms.

The producer notices that their recording software allows them to adjust two key parameters when capturing audio: the **sample rate** and the **sample resolution**.

**State** what is meant by the **sample rate** and **sample resolution**.
