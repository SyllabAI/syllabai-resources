## Q1 — easy — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 3((e)) — 2 marks
> *Write 155 as a fraction of 205*

$\frac{155}{205}$

> *Convert the fraction to a percentage by multiplying by 100*

> *This can be done on a calculator*

$\frac{155}{205}\times100=75.609...$

**[M1]**

> *Round to 1 decimal place by looking at the value of the second decimal place*

> *If it is 5 or more then round the first decimal place up, if it is less than 5 then the first decimal place stays the same*

**Final answer:** **75.6**

### 20((b)) — 3 marks
> **[exam-tip]**
> The question asks for the 'normal' price of the camera.
> 
> This means that the final answer you are looking for represents 100% of the original value of the camera. The 'sale price' of the camera therefore represents less than 100% of the original value.
> 
> These questions are typically called 'reverse percentage' questions.

> *Calculate the percentage that the 'sale price' represents*

*100% - 15% = 85%*

> *Equate the percentage the sale price represents to the price of the camera in the sale*

*85%=6630*

**[M1]**

> *Find 1% of the price of the camera first (by dividing by 85) and then use this to calculate 100% (by multiplying by 100)*

`table row cell 85 percent sign end cell equals 6630 row cell 1 percent sign end cell equals cell 6630 divided by 85 equals 78 end cell row cell 100 percent sign end cell equals 7800 end table`

**[M1]**

> *Alternatively, this could be shown as workings below*

$6630\div0.85=7800$

**Final answer:** **7800 (HK\$)**

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: This mark is awarded for showing either 85%=6630 *or *$6630\div85$, *or* for value of 78 seen.
> 
> **M1**: Multiplying the value '78' (or its equivalent calculation) by 100 will gain this mark and is only awarded  if the first M1 mark is gained.
> 
> *Note:* the working $6630\div0.85$ will automatically receive both M1 marks.
> 
> **A1**: Correct answer of 7800 given

## Q4 — hard — 4 marks · exam-questions

### 18() — 4 marks
> *Change 70% to the decimal multiplier 0.7*

> *Calculate the number of notebooks sold for 22 dirhams each*

*0.7 x 60 = 42*

> *Calculate the income from these 42 notebooks*

*42 x 22 = 924 *

**[M1]**

> *Calculate the number of remaining notebooks*

*60 - 42 = 18*

> *Calculate the income for selling the remaining 18*

*18 x 19 = 342*

**[M1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> Calculating 924 and 342 can be done in either order but you need both for the second method mark.

> *Calculate the profit by subtracting the cost from the income*

*924 + 342 - 780 = 486*

> *Divide the profit by the income*

$\frac{486}{780}=0.6230768....$

> *Multiply this answer by 100 to change it to a percentage*

$\frac{486}{780}\times100=62.30768...$

**[M1]**

> *Give your answer to 3 significant figures*

**Final answer:** **62.3%**
