## Q1 — medium — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 18() — 3 marks
> *Add the interest rate to 100% and convert to an equivalent decimal*

`table row cell 100 percent sign plus 4 percent sign end cell equals cell 104 percent sign end cell row cell 104 divided by 100 end cell equals cell 1.04 end cell end table`

> *Calculate the total amount by multiplying the original amount by the decimal multiplier 3 times to represent each year of investment*

$18000\times1.04\times1.04\times1.04$

> *This can be simplified to give*

$18000\times1.04^{3}=20247.552$

**[M1 M1]**

> *Calculate the amount of interest by subtracting the original amount from the total amount calculated after 3 years*

$20247.552-18000=2247.552$

> *Round the answer to the nearest rupee as stated in the question*

**Final answer:** **2248 rupees**

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: For finding 4% of 18000.
> 
> **M1**: For a complete method for finding the value after 3 years.
> 
> **A1**: Correct answer in the range 2247 - 2248.

## Q2 — medium — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 24() — 3 marks
> *To calculate an increase of 4% we use the multiplier 1.04*

$680\times1.04$

**[M1]**

> *To do this efficiently for 3 increases in a row, we put the multiplier to the power of the number of years*

$680\times1.04^{3}=764.90752$

**[M1]**

> *The question asks for the answer to the nearest \$, so round to the nearest whole number*

**Final answer:** **765**
