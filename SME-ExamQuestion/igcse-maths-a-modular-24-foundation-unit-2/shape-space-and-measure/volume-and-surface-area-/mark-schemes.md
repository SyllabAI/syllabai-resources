## Q2 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 13((b)) — 2 marks
> *Volume of a cuboid = length × width × height*

> *Here *$7\timesw\times6=231$

> *Multiply the two lengths given together*

$7\times6=42$

> *Divide the volume by these two lengths to find *$w$

$231\div42=5.5$

**[M1]**

**Final answer:** **5.5**

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **A1**: Equivalent answers of $\frac{11}{2}$ or $5\frac{1}{2}$are equally acceptable.

## Q3 — medium — 4 marks · exam-questions

### 16() — 4 marks
> *Work out how many boxes could fit on each layer of the crate*

$120\div20=6\mathrm{boxes} \mathrm{across}$

$40\div20=2\mathrm{boxes} \mathrm{deep}$

**[M1]**

$6\times2=12$

> *Each layer would have 12 boxes*

> *Work out how many layers would be needed*

$48\div12=4\mathrm{layers}$

**[M1]**

> *Each layer is 20cm tall*

$4\times20\mathrm{cm}=80\mathrm{cm}$

**[M1]**

**Final answer:** **80cm**
