## Q1 — medium — 4 marks · exam-questions

### 13((e)) — 2 marks
> *Solve for *$q$*, using the balance method for example and doing the same thing to each side of the inequality*

`table row cell 2 q end cell greater or equal than cell 31 minus 3 q end cell row cell 2 q plus 3 q end cell greater or equal than 31 row cell 5 q end cell greater or equal than 31 row q greater or equal than cell 31 over 5 end cell end table`

**[M1]**

$q\geq\frac{31}{5}$

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: This mark is awarded for a correct first step for solving the inequality or for giving a solution of $\frac{31}{5}$or 6.2 (e.g. $q=6.2$).
> 
> **A1**: $q\geq6.2$ also accepted. This mark is only gained for a correct solution using the correct inequality (for example $q>6.2$ will only gain the first mark).
