### Q1

- The relative molecular mass of **X**<sub>2</sub> is **2***A*<sub>r</sub>.

### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- The **relative formula mass** of ionic and giant covalent compounds is calculated from all the atoms in the empirical formula.

- *M*<sub>r</sub> = 2*A*<sub>r</sub>(N) + 8*A*<sub>r</sub>(H) + *A*<sub>r</sub>(S) + 4*A*<sub>r</sub>(O)

*M*<sub>r</sub> = 2(14.0) + 8(1.0) + 32.1 + 4(16.0)

*M*<sub>r</sub> = 28.0 + 8.0 + 32.1 + 64.0

*M*<sub>r</sub> = **132.1**

**A** is incorrect as this has used the IGCSE atomic mass for sulfur (32.0 instead of 32.1) **AND **forgot to multiply the ammonium ion by 2, giving NH<sub>4</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> = 114.0.

**B** is incorrect as this has used the correct A Level atomic mass for sulfur (32.1) but forgot to multiply the ammonium ion by 2, giving NH<sub>4</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> = 114.1.

> **[exam-tip]**
> The most common error on this question type is forgetting to multiply all the atoms inside a bracket by the number outside. For (NH<sub>4</sub>)<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>, the subscript 2 outside the bracket applies to both N and H, giving 2N and 8H in total.
> 
> At A Level, always use the atomic masses from the CIE Data Booklet rather than the integer values you may have used at GCSE. For sulfur, the Data Booklet value is 32.1, not 32 and this is the key discriminator between options C and D.

### Q5
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Find the total percentage of the two unknown isotopes:

100 - (82.58 + 0.56) = 16.86%

- Let <sup>86</sup>Sr abundance = *y*, so <sup>87</sup>Sr abundance = (16.86 - *y*).

- Set up the relative atomic mass equation:

$\frac{(84\times0.56)+(86y)+(87(16.86-y))+(88\times82.58)}{100} = 87 . 71$

- Expand and simplify:

$47 . 04 + 86 y + 1466 . 82 - 87 y + 7267 . 04 = 8771$

$8780 . 90 - y = 8771$

*y* = **9.90%** (<sup>86</sup>Sr)

<sup>87</sup>Sr = 16.86 - 9.90 = **6.96%**

**A** is incorrect as these are the correct values but assigned to the wrong isotopes.

**B** is incorrect as 8.43% is obtained by dividing 16.86% equally between the two isotopes without performing the algebraic calculation.

**D** is incorrect as 11.58% and 5.84% are obtained when the <sup>84</sup>Sr abundance (0.56%) is ignored, incorrectly treating the unknown total as 17.42% rather than 16.86%.

### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- To calculate the **relative atomic mass**, multiply each isotopic mass by its percentage abundance, sum the results, and divide by 100.

*A*<sub>r</sub> = `\frac{\Sigma (\% \text{ abundance} \times \text{isotopic mass})}{100}`

*A*<sub>r</sub>(Fe) = $\frac{(5.845\times53.9396)+(91.754\times55.9349)+(2.119\times56.9354)+(0.282\times57.9333)}{100}$

*A*<sub>r</sub>(Fe) = **55.845**

**A** is incorrect as 55.800 is the value given in the CIE Data Booklet for iron. This traps people who read the answer from the periodic table instead of performing the calculation on the data provided.

**C** is incorrect as 56.186 is the simple unweighted average of the four isotopic masses, ignoring the percentage abundances.

**D** is incorrect as 56.250 is the simple unweighted average of the four mass numbers (54, 56, 57, 58), ignoring both the isotopic masses and the percentage abundances.

### Q8
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- The **unified atomic mass unit (u)** is defined as exactly $\frac{1}{12}$ of the mass of an atom of carbon-12.

- This is distinct from **relative atomic mass (*****A***<sub>**r**</sub>**)**, which is the weighted average mass of naturally occurring atoms of an element on a scale where an atom of carbon-12 has a mass of exactly 12 units.

**A** is incorrect as the mass of an electron is approximately $\frac{1}{1836}$ u, which is negligible compared to 1 u.

**C** is incorrect as an atom of hydrogen-1 has a mass of approximately 1.0078 u, not exactly 1 u. Hydrogen was the original standard before being replaced by carbon-12 in 1961.

**D** is incorrect as the mass of a proton is approximately 1.0073 u. The formal definition uses the entire carbon-12 atom, which accounts for protons, neutrons, electrons, and nuclear binding energy.

> **[exam-tip]**
> Do not confuse the unified atomic mass unit with relative atomic mass.
> 
> - The atomic mass unit is a fixed quantity, defined as exactly 1/12 the mass of one carbon-12 atom.
> - Relative atomic mass is a dimensionless ratio that uses this unit as its reference scale and takes into account the natural isotopic abundances of an element.

### Q9
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- Since the sample contains **only** these two isotopes, their percentage abundances must sum to 100%.

- Let the percentage of <sup>21</sup>Ne = *x*, so the percentage of <sup>20</sup>Ne = (100 - *x*).

- Substituting into the relative atomic mass expression:

$\frac{21x+20(100-x)}{100} = 20 . 2$

$21 x + 2000 - 20 x = 2020$

*x* = **20%**

**A** and **D** are incorrect as these values do not satisfy the equation above.

**C** is incorrect as 80% is the percentage of <sup>20</sup>Ne when the algebra is set up correctly but *x* is assigned to the wrong isotope.
