### Q12
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Starting with 1 Mg<sub>3</sub>N<sub>2</sub>:

  - 3 Mg and 2 N on the left
  - So, 3 Mg(OH)<sub>2</sub> and 2 NH<sub>3</sub> are needed on the right
