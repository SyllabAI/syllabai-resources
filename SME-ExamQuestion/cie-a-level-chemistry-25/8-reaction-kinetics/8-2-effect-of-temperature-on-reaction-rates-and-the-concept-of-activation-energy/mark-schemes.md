**B** is incorrect as the activation energy for the reverse reaction is larger than for the forward reaction, not smaller. The products of the exothermic forward reaction lie at lower energy, so more energy is needed to reach the transition state from the products.

**C** is incorrect as the Δ*H* for the reverse reaction is positive (endothermic), not negative.

### Q12
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- A catalyst lowers the activation energy by providing an alternative reaction pathway.

  - *E*<sub>a1</sub> (the lower value) is therefore the activation energy for the catalysed reaction.
  - *E*<sub>a2</sub> (the higher value) is for the uncatalysed reaction.

- The area under the Maxwell-Boltzmann curve to the right of a given *E*<sub>a</sub> represents the proportion of particles with energy ≥ *E*<sub>a</sub>.

![Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution curve showing two activation energy values Ea1 and Ea2 marked on the x-axis, with the shaded area to the right of Ea1 being greater than the area to the right of Ea2, showing more particles have sufficient energy for the catalysed reaction](assets/021-maxwell-boltzmann-distribution-curve-showing-two.jpg)

- As *E*<sub>a1</sub> < *E*<sub>a2</sub>, a greater proportion of particles have energy ≥ *E*<sub>a1</sub>, so the catalysed reaction has more effective collisions.

  - Fewer particles have energy ≥ *E*<sub>a2</sub>, so the uncatalysed reaction has fewer effective collisions.

**A** is incorrect as it assigns *E*<sub>a1</sub> to the uncatalysed reaction. A catalyst lowers activation energy, so *E*<sub>a1</sub> (the lower value) must correspond to the catalysed reaction.

**B** is incorrect as it states the catalysed reaction leads to fewer effective collisions. A lower *E*<sub>a</sub> increases the proportion of particles with sufficient energy, so effective collisions increase.

**C** is incorrect as it assigns the wrong collision frequency to each pathway — the uncatalysed reaction (higher *E*<sub>a2</sub>) has fewer effective collisions, not more.

**C** and **D** are incorrect as both show endothermic reactions. Endothermic reactions have products at higher energy than reactants, making the reverse reaction thermodynamically favoured and reducing the equilibrium yield of products.
