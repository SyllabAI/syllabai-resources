### Q10

### Q11
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- The Brønsted-Lowry theory defines acids as proton donors and bases as proton acceptors.

- Ammonia accepts a proton (H<sup>+</sup>) from water to form an ammonium ion (NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>), acting as a Brønsted-Lowry base:

NH<sub>3</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O ⇌ NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup> + OH<sup>-</sup>

- The nitrogen atom donates its lone pair to a hydrogen ion to form a dative covalent bond, producing NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>.

**A **is incorrect as ammonia cannot donate a proton under these conditions and so does not act as a Brønsted-Lowry acid.

**B **is incorrect as ammonia acts only as a base. It accepts protons but does not donate them, so it is not amphoteric.

**D **is incorrect as ammonia undergoes acid-base reactions due to its lone pair, so it is not neutral.

### Q12

- Write the unbalanced equation:

(NH<sub>4</sub>)<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (s) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) → H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (aq) + HNO<sub>3</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub>O (l)

  - 2 N atoms in (NH<sub>4</sub>)<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> → 2HNO<sub>3</sub> on the right
