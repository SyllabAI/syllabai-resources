> [H<sup>+</sup>]<sup>2</sup> = *K*<sub>a</sub> × [HA]
> 
> This assumes [H<sup>+</sup>] equals [A<sup>–</sup>] (both produced in equal amounts) and that the equilibrium concentration of the weak acid equals the initial concentration, since the degree of dissociation is small.
> 
> Here, [Fe(H<sub>2</sub>O)<sub>6</sub>]<sup>3+</sup> acts as the weak acid, so [HA]<sub>initial</sub> = 0.25 mol dm<sup>–3</sup>. Take the square root to find [H<sup>+</sup>], then use pH = –log[H<sup>+</sup>].
