### Q9
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- **Standard enthalpy of formation** (Δ*H*<sub>f</sub><sup>θ</sup>):

  - The enthalpy change when one mole of a compound is formed from its elements in their standard states under standard conditions
  - Enthalpy changes of formation can be either exothermic (negative) or endothermic (positive), so the sign is + / −
  - For example, Δ*H*<sub>f</sub><sup>θ</sup>[CO<sub>2</sub>] = −393 kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> (exothermic), whereas Δ*H*<sub>f</sub><sup>θ</sup>[NO] = +90 kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> (endothermic)
- **Standard enthalpy of combustion** (Δ*H*<sub>c</sub><sup>θ</sup>):

  - The enthalpy change when one mole of a substance burns completely in excess oxygen under standard conditions
  - Combustion reactions always release energy to the surroundings, so Δ*H*<sub>c</sub><sup>θ</sup> is always negative (−)
- **Standard enthalpy of neutralisation** (Δ*H*<sub>neut</sub><sup>θ</sup>):

  - The enthalpy change when one mole of water is formed from the reaction of an acid with an alkali under standard conditions
  - Neutralisation reactions are always exothermic, so Δ*H*<sub>neut</sub><sup>θ</sup> is always negative (−)

### Q19
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- According to Le Chatelier's Principle, increasing the temperature shifts the position of equilibrium in the endothermic direction.

  - As the yield of products increases when temperature rises, the forward reaction is **endothermic**

- In PCl<sub>3</sub>, the phosphorus atom forms 3 bonding pairs (one to each chlorine) and has 1 lone pair.
