> **[exam-tip]**
> In this reaction, there are two conjugate acid-base pairs:
> 
> - H<sub>3</sub>PO<sub>4</sub> / H<sub>2</sub>PO<sub>4</sub><sup>-</sup>:
> 
>   - H<sub>3</sub>PO<sub>4</sub> is the acid
>   - H<sub>2</sub>PO<sub>4</sub><sup>-</sup> is the conjugate base
> - H<sub>2</sub>O / OH<sup>-</sup>:
> 
>   - H<sub>2</sub>O is the acid
>   - OH<sup>-</sup> is the conjugate base
> 
> A conjugate acid-base pair differs by exactly one proton.
