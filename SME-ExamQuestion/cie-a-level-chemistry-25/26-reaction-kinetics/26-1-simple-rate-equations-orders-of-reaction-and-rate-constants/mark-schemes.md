> Here, rate = *k*[NO]<sup>2</sup>[H<sub>2</sub>]: both NO (coefficient 2) and H<sub>2</sub> (coefficient 1) appear. Step 1 is 2NO + H<sub>2</sub> → N<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub>, which matches exactly.
> 
> Step 2 involves H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> and H<sub>2</sub>, which do not match the rate equation, so Step 2 cannot be the rate-determining step.
