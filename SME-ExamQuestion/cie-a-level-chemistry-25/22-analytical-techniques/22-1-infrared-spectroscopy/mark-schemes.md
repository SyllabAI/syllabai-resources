### Q3

**D** is incorrect as ketones contain a C=O bond, which produces the characteristic absorption between 1670 and 1740 cm<sup>-1</sup>.

> **[exam-tip]**
> The three key infrared absorptions to learn for CIE IAL Chemistry are:
> 
> - O-H (alcohol): 3230–3550 cm<sup>-1</sup>.
> - C=O (aldehyde, ketone, carboxylic acid): 1670–1740 cm<sup>-1</sup>.
> - O-H (carboxylic acid, broad): 2500–3000 cm<sup>-1</sup>.
