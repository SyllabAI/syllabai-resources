### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> The meaning of the symbol <sup>⊖</sup> in Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> is:
> 
> - Standard conditions **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> The <sup>⊖</sup> symbol indicates that the value was measured under standard conditions: a pressure of 101 kPa and a temperature of 298 K (25 °C).
> **[mark-scheme]**
> The standard entropy change is positive because:
> 
> - A liquid is formed from a solid / ice melts to form liquid water **[1 mark]**
> - Liquid water is more disordered than solid ice / particles have greater freedom of movement in a liquid than in a solid **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Entropy is a measure of disorder in a system. The greater the disorder, the higher the entropy.
> 
> In this process, ice (a solid) changes to liquid water. Particles in a liquid have greater freedom of movement than those in a solid, so the system becomes more disordered and entropy increases, making Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> positive.
To calculate the temperature at which Δ*G*<sup>⊖</sup> = 0:

- List the known values:

  - Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> = +6.03 kJ mol<sup>−1</sup>
  - Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> = +22.1 J K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>
- Rearrange Δ*G*<sup>⊖</sup> = Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> − *T*Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> = 0 to find *T*:

*T* = `\frac{\Delta H^{\ominus}}{\Delta S^{\ominus}}` **[1 mark]**

- Convert Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> from kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> to J mol<sup>−1</sup> and substitute:

*T* = $\frac{6.03\times1000}{22.1}$ **[1 mark]**

*T* = **273 K** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> 1 mark for the correct rearrangement: *T* = Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> ÷ Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup>
> 
> 1 mark for correct substitution with consistent units (Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> converted to J mol<sup>−1</sup>, or Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> converted to kJ K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>)
> 
> 1 mark for the correct answer: 273 K

> **[exam-tip]**
> When using the Gibbs equation, Δ*H* and Δ*S* must be in the same units. Here, Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> is in kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> and Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> is in J K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>, so you must convert one to match the other before dividing.
> 
> The simplest approach is to multiply Δ*H*<sup>⊖</sup> by 1000 to convert to J mol<sup>−1</sup>: 6.03 x 1000 = 6030 J mol<sup>−1</sup>.
> 
> Alternatively, divide Δ*S*<sup>⊖</sup> by 1000 to convert to kJ K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>: 22.1 ÷ 1000 = 0.0221 kJ K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>. Both approaches give *T* = 273 K, which is 0 °C, the melting point of ice.

> Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> = ΣΔ*S*<sup>θ</sup>(products) − ΣΔ*S*<sup>θ</sup>(reactants)
> 
> Don't forget to multiply each standard entropy value by the stoichiometric coefficient of that species in the balanced equation. This balancing step must be carried through the entire calculation.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> The standard entropy change is positive because:
> 
> - There are 10 moles of gas in the products
> **AND**
> 9 moles of gas in the reactants

### Q3
> **[mark-scheme]**
> The equation for potassium nitrate dissolving in water is:
> 
> - KNO<sub>3</sub> (s) → K<sup>+</sup> (aq) + NO<sub>3</sub><sup>−</sup> (aq)
> **OR**
> KNO<sub>3</sub> (s) + aq → K<sup>+</sup> (aq) + NO<sub>3</sub><sup>−</sup> (aq) **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> The standard enthalpy change of solution (Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup><sub>sol</sub>) is the enthalpy change when 1 mole of an ionic substance dissolves in sufficient water to form a very dilute solution.
> 
> The symbol (aq) indicates that the ions are dissolved in sufficient water to form a dilute aqueous solution.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> is positive because:
> 
> - Disorder increases as the solid dissolves / solid changes to aqueous ions / particles have greater freedom of movement in solution **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> When KNO<sub>3</sub> dissolves, the ionic lattice breaks down:
> 
> KNO<sub>3</sub> (s) → K<sup>+</sup> (aq) + NO<sub>3</sub><sup>−</sup> (aq)
> 
> The ions are now free to move in solution, so disorder increases and entropy increases, making Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> positive.
To calculate the temperature above which the dissolving of potassium nitrate becomes feasible:

- List the known values:

  - Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> = +34.9 kJ mol<sup>−1</sup>
  - Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> = +117 J K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup>
- At the feasibility threshold, Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = 0. Rearrange Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> − *T*Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> = 0 to find *T*:

*T* = $\frac{ΔH^{θ}}{ΔS^{θ}}$ **[1 mark]**

- Convert Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> from kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> to J mol<sup>−1</sup> and substitute:

*T* = $\frac{34.9\times1000}{117}$ **[1 mark]**

*T* = **298 K** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> 1 mark for the correct rearrangement: *T* = Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> ÷ Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup>
> 
> 1 mark for correct substitution with consistent units
> 
> 1 mark for the correct answer: 298 K

> **[exam-tip]**
> The reaction becomes feasible when Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> ≤ 0. Setting Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = 0 gives the threshold temperature above which the reaction becomes feasible.
> 
> Remember to ensure Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> and Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> are in the same units before dividing. Here, multiply Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> by 1000 to convert from kJ mol<sup>−1</sup> to J mol<sup>−1</sup>: 34.9 x 1000 = 34 900 J mol<sup>−1</sup>. Then divide by Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> = 117 J K<sup>−1</sup> mol<sup>−1</sup> to get *T* = 298 K.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> i) The sign of Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> at a temperature lower than 298 K:
> 
> - Positive / Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> > 0 **[1 mark]**
> 
> ii) A positive Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> indicates that:
> 
> - The dissolving of potassium nitrate is not feasible / not spontaneous / does not occur at this temperature **[1 mark]**
> 
> Allow error carried forward from (d)(i): if negative, allow "feasible / spontaneous"

> **[exam-tip]**
> At the threshold temperature from part (c), Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = 0. Below this temperature, the *T*Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> term decreases in magnitude, so Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> − *T*Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> becomes more positive.
> 
> A positive Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> means the process is not feasible: potassium nitrate is less likely to dissolve at temperatures below 298 K.
