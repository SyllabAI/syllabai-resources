$\mathrm{lcd} \mathrm{of}x,2x\mathrm{and}4=4x$

$\begin{gathered} \frac{3}{x} & = \frac{3 \times 4}{4 x} = \frac{12}{4 x} \\ \frac{x + 2}{2 x} & = \frac{( x + 2 ) \times 2}{4 x} = \frac{2 x + 4}{4 x} \\ 1 \frac{1}{4} & = \frac{1 \times x}{4 x} = \frac{x}{4 x} \end{gathered}$

**[1]**

> *Now add the fractions by adding the numerators over a the single (lcd) denominator.*

$\frac{12}{4x}+\frac{2x+4}{4x}+\frac{x}{4x}=\frac{12+2x+4+x}{4x}$

**[1]**
