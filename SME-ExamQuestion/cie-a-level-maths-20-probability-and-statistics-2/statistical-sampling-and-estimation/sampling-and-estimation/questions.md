**Q1** [8 marks]

$n=30\sumt=827\sumt^{2}=25374$

Kenta takes a different random sample of 50 birthday candles and calculates unbiased estimates for the mean and variance of $T$, shown below.

$̅t=28.1\mathrm{and}s_{t}^{2}=105.7$
