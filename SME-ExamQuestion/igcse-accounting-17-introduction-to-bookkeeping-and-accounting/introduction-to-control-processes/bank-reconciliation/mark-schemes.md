### 13((b)) — 3 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Award marks as indicated.**
> 
> | Term | Definition |
> |---|---|
> | Unpresented cheques | Cheques entered in the cash book, not yet presented for payment to the bank. **(1)** |
> | Outstanding lodgements | Receipts entered in the cash book and banked that have not yet been processed by the bank **(1)** |
> | Dishonoured cheque | A cheque received or paid that the bank has refused to pay **(1)** |
> 
> **Accept any other appropriate responses.**
