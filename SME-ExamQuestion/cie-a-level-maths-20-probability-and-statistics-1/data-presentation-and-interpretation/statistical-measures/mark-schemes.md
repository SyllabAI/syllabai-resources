$Σ h = 276 + 219 + 198 + 154 + 213 + 243 + 192 + 161 + 218 = 1874$

$\overline{h} = \frac{1874}{9}$

$\overline{h} = 208 . 222 \dots$

**Final answer:** **Mean = 208 cm (3 s.f.)**

**[B1]**

To find the variance, first add the squares of the heights

$Σ h^{2} = 276^{2} + 219^{2} + 198^{2} + 154^{2} + 213^{2} + 243^{2} + 192^{2} + 161^{2} + 218^{2} = 401784$

Use the formula for the variance, `\dfrac{\Sigma h^{2}}{n} - \left(\bar{h}\right)^{2}`

`\text{Variance} = \dfrac{401784}{9} - \left(\dfrac{1874}{9}\right)^{2}`

**[M1]**

$\text{Variance} = 1286 . 17 \dots$

The standard deviation is the square root of the variance

$\text{Standard deviation} = \sqrt{1286.17\dots} = 35 . 863 \dots$

**Final answer:** **Standard deviation = 35.9 cm (3 s.f.)**
