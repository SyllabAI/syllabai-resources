### Q1 — hard — 7 marks

**Q1** [2 marks]

In a certain region, the probability that any given female mosquito carries the malaria parasite is 0.8.

State, in context, two assumptions needed for the number of female mosquitoes carrying the parasite, in a sample of $n$ female mosquitoes, to be modelled by a binomial distribution.

**Q1** [5 marks]

Male mosquitoes cannot transmit the malaria parasite to a human. A female mosquito can transmit the parasite to a human only if she carries it. In this region, 50% of the mosquitoes are male.

Find the probability that, in a random sample of six mosquitoes, none of them is able to transmit the parasite to a human. Give your answer as an exact value.

### Q4 — very_hard — 8 marks
