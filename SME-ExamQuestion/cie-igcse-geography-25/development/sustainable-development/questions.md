### Q4 — hard — 7 marks

**Q4** [7 marks]

| “Some governments rely on foreign aid to fund schools and hospitals. Others use debt relief to free money for investment. However, critics argue that aid can create dependency, and fair trade benefits only a small number of producers.” |
|---|

### Q5 — hard — 7 marks

**Q5** [7 marks]

Study the extract below, which describes ecotourism in Costa Rica.

| “Costa Rica has become a leading destination for ecotourism. Over a quarter of its land is protected in national parks and reserves. Tourists pay to visit rainforests and see wildlife such as sloths and toucans. Income supports local jobs, while park fees fund conservation. However, visitor numbers can damage fragile habitats and increase waste.” |
|---|

Evaluate the extent to which ecotourism has achieved sustainable development in Costa Rica?
