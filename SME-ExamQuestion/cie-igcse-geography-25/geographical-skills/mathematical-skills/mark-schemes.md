### Q4
The highest number of tourists to arrive in Singapore was  1.73 million people in July, dropping  to  1.68 million in August. **[1] **This number declined by an additional 18.5%, resulting in the lowest number of arrivals in November, which was 1.41 million tourists. **[1]**

Tourism rates fluctuate throughout the year, with a tendency towards rising in the early part of the year, peaking in July and August, and then declining during the later months before rising again. **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - 2 marks for identifying the months with the highest and lowest visitor numbers.
> - 1 mark for comparative statistics.
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Additional information that could be used in the answer includes:
> 
> - February, May and October are anomalous because they either fall or rise against the trend.
> - From January to February, there is a decline in numbers, followed by an increase in March, which is then followed by a two-month decline.
> - From May to July, tourism rates increased to a peak of 1.73 million, which declined to 1.68 million in August. This rapidly falls to 1.41 million in Sept., rising again to 1.47 million  in Oct.,  it then falls to 1.4 million  in Nov., where it begins to rise again to 1.6 million in Dec.

### Q5
- *20-5* **[1 mark]**
- *=15* **[1 mark]**
- *15 ***per*** 1000* **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - The birth rate in 2010 was 20 per 1000 people
> - The death rate in 2010 was 5 per 1000 people
> - Subtract them: 20-5 = 15
> - Remember to use the unit of 'per 1000'
> - The natural population growth rate of Mexico in 2010 was 15 per 1000 people.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **1 mark** per correctly calculated step.
