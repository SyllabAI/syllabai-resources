### Q6
Two advantages are:

1. The airport is on flat land, which is necessary for the runway and buildings. **[1]**
2. It is easy to get to because there is a main road access. **[1]**

Two disadvantages are:

1. There is a limited amount of land to build on. **[1]**
2. It is liable to damage from storms and flooding. **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> The command word is ‘**suggest**’; this means you need to consider both the advantages of building the airport in its current location and the potential disadvantages.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - 1 mark **[1]** per valid point.
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> Other ideas that could be used are:
> 
> - Planes approach over sea, which protects buildings and people in the event of an accident.
> - Planes do not have to approach over the built-up area, which lessens pollution.
> - It is a long journey from Kingston along a sandspit.
> - The exposed coastal area could be susceptible to high tides and tropical storm damage.
