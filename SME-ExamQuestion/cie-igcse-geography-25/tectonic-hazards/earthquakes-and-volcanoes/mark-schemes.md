### Q5

The Canary Islands are formed by a mantle plume **[KU]**, where hot magma rises from deep within the Earth's mantle towards the surface **[KU]**. On 19th September 2021, the pressure from the rising magma finally overcame the strength of the overlying rock **[KU]**. The eruption began with explosive activity followed by effusive lava flows that continued for 85 days, destroying over 1,600 buildings **[KU]**.
