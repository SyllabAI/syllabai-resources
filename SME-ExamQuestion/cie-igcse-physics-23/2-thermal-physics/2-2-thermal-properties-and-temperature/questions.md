**Extract:**

At t = 0, θ = 84°C
At t = 60 s, θ = 75°C
Between t = 0 and t = 60 s, Δθ₁ = (84 − 75) = 9°C
R₁ = 0.15 °C / s

At t = 120, θ = 70°C
At t = 180 s, θ = 67°C
Between t = 120 and t = 180 s, Δθ₂ = (70 − 67) = 3°C
R₂ = 0.05 °C / s
