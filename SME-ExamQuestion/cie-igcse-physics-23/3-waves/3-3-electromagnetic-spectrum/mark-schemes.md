### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****D**** because:**

- The frequency of a wave can be found using:

$\mathrm{wave} \mathrm{speed}=\mathrm{frequency}\times\mathrm{wavelength} \mathrm{or}v=\mathrm{fλ}$

- This equation can be rearranged to give:

$f=\frac{v}{λ}$

- The speed of light is 3.00 × 10<sup>8</sup> m/s so the frequencies can be found like this:

$f_{max}=\frac{3.00\times10^{8}m/s}{4.0\times10^{-7}m}=7.5\times10^{14}\mathrm{Hz}\,\,f_{\mathrm{min}}=\frac{3.00\times10^{8}m/s}{7.0\times10^{-7}m}=4.3\times10^{14}\mathrm{Hz}$

- This gives a range of  to $4.3\times10^{14}\mathrm{Hz} \mathrm{to}7.5\times10^{14}\mathrm{Hz}$

**A** and **B** incorrect as the powers (×10<sup>8</sup> etc.) have not been used correctly to reach this answer.

**C** is incorrect because the wrong value for the speed of light has been used in the calculation.

Calculations involving exponents and standard form can be tricky, especially negative powers of ten such as ×10<sup>-7</sup>. You should always check calculations like this twice and remember that dividing by something with a negative exponent will make the answer larger, not smaller.
