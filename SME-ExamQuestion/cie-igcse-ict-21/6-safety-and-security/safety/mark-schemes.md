### Q7
To ensure their data remains safe while gaming online, players should take several precautions. One important measure is to **use anti-spyware and keep antivirus software up to date**, as this helps prevent malware from stealing personal information. Additionally, gamers should always keep their **firewall enabled**, as it blocks unauthorised access to their system while playing online.

Another key safety measure is to **download games and updates only from reputable sources**. This ensures that players do not install pirated or modified versions that could contain malware. Furthermore, **choosing a username that does not reveal personal information** helps protect users from identity theft or unwanted contact from strangers.

To further enhance security, gamers should** delete their account details if they stop playing a game**, preventing hackers from accessing their old profiles. Finally, when disposing of a gaming device, **all personal information should be erased**, ensuring that no sensitive data remains stored on the system.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Use anti-spyware/up-to-date antivirus software **[1 mark]**
> - Play with the firewall operational **[1 mark]**
> - Download/buy files and new software from reputable sources **[1 mark]**
> - Choose a username that does not reveal any personal information **[1 mark]**
> - Delete account details when no longer playing **[1 mark]**
> - Ensure personal information is deleted when disposing of a gaming device **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> **Five **top tips for answering this 6-mark "describe" question effectively:
> 
> 1. **List six distinct safety measures**
> 
>   1. Avoid repeating similar points (e.g., don’t list both "use antivirus" and "use anti-spyware" as separate points).
> 2. **Explain why each measure improves security**
> 
>   1. Instead of just saying "use a firewall," write "keep the firewall enabled to block unauthorised access while gaming online."
> 3. **Consider both software and personal habits**
> 
>   1. Mention software-based protection (antivirus, firewalls, updates) and user behaviour (choosing safe usernames, deleting account details).
> 4. **Avoid vague statements**
> 
>   1. Instead of saying "stay safe online," specify how, e.g., "choose a username that does not reveal real names or personal details to protect identity."
> 5. **Think beyond gameplay**
> 
>   1. Include precautions before, during, and after gaming, such as deleting personal information when disposing of a device.
