Some services require users to pay a monthly or annual fee based on the amount of storage used.

> **Differences **

> - wi-fi has a greater range of transmission **[1 mark]**
> - wi-fi connects more devices **[1 mark]**
> 
> **[Total marks: 4/4]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Here are **five **examiner tips for answering this type of comparison question effectively:
> 
> 1. **Read the question carefully**
> 
>   1. Identify whether the question asks for similarities, differences, or both.
>   2. Note any restrictions (e.g., "max three from each category").
> 2. **Structure your answer clearly**
> 
>   1. Use separate sentences or bullet points for similarities and differences.
>   2. Start with similarities, then move on to differences to make the comparison clear.
> 3. **Be precise with terminology**
> 
>   1. Use correct ICT terms such as radio frequencies, encryption, transmission range, and data transfer rate.
>   2. Avoid vague descriptions like "Bluetooth is slower"—instead, say "Wi-Fi has a faster data transfer rate than Bluetooth."
