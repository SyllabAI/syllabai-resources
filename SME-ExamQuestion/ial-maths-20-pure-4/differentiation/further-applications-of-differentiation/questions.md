## Q1 — easy — 6 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 3 marks — structured
In a computer animation, the side length, s mm, of a square is increasing at a constant rate of 2 millimetres per second.

(i) Write down the value of $\frac{ds}{dt}$, where t is time and measured in seconds.

(ii) Write down a formula for the area, A mm<sup>2</sup>, of the square and hence find$\frac{dA}{ds}$.

### 2() — 3 marks — structured
