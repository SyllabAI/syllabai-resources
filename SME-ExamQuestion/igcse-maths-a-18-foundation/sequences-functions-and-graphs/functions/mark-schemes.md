### 7((a)) — 1 marks
> *The operations in a function machine are always performed in order from left to right*

`table row cell 15 cross times 5 end cell equals 75 row cell 75 minus 7 end cell equals 68 row cell 68 divided by 2 end cell equals 34 end table`

**Final answer:** **34**

**[B1]**

### 7((b)) — 2 marks
> *Working in reverse order through the function machine and using the inverse operations will calculate the input starting from the given output*

`table row cell 124 cross times 2 end cell equals 248 row cell 248 plus 7 end cell equals 255 row cell 255 divided by 5 end cell equals 51 end table`

**[M1]**

**Final answer:** **51**

**[A1]**

### 7((c)) — 2 marks
> **[exam-tip]**
> A formula must have an equals sign and at least two variables with one of the variables being isolated and therefore the subject of the formula.
> 
> In this case, the formula must be in the form $T=$with the variable $p$ appearing on the right hand side of the equals sign.

> *Following the order of the operations in the function machine, *$p$* is first multiplied by 5*

$5\timesp=5p$

> *Next, 7 is subtracted from the expression *$5p$* to give:*

$5p-7$

> *Finally, the expression is divided by 2 and this is best expressed as a fraction with 2 as the denominator*

$\frac{5p-7}{2}$

**[M1]**

> $T$* is the output  and so the expression can be written as a formula with *$T$* as the subject*

$T=\frac{5p-7}{2}$

**[A1]**

> *Work backwards starting with the output and do the opposite operation (subtract 9 instead of adding 9)*

$64-9=55$

**[M1]**

> *The input 11 must by multiplied by something to get 55, so divide 55 by 11 to find out what is missing*

$55\div11=5$
