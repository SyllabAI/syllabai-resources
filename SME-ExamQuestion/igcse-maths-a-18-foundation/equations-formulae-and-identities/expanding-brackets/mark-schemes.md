# Mark Schemes — Expanding Brackets
**2. Equations, Formulae & Identities** · Edexcel IGCSE Maths A (4MA1) — 18/Foundation


## Q1 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 18((b)) — 2 marks
> **[exam-tip]**
> Expand is an algebraic command word that means to multiply out the terms in the brackets so that they expression no linger contains brackets.

> *Expand the double brackets, using FOIL or an equivalent method*

$\begin{gathered} m \times m & = m^{2} \\ m \times - 5 & = - 5 m \\ 9 \times m & = 9 m \\ 9 \times - 5 & = - 45 \end{gathered}$

**[M1]**

> *Write the expanded terms out as a list*

$\begin{gathered} m^{2} + 9 m - 5 m - 45 \end{gathered}$

> *Collect the like terms to simplify the expression*

$m^{2}+4m-45$
