### 13((d)) — 2 marks
> *Rewrite in expanded form, placing similar terms next to each other*

$3\times4\timese^{7}\timese^{2}\timesf\timesf$

> *Simplify the terms with powers using the multiplication index law and simply multiply 3 and 4 together*

$12\timese^{7+2}\timesf^{1+1}=12\timese^{9}\timesf^{2}$

$12e^{9}f^{2}$

**[B1 B1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **B1**: For two correct 'parts' of the expression (e.g. two correct from 12 *or *$e^{9}$ *or* $f^{2}$).
> 
> **B1**: For correct final expression (e.g. $12f^{2}e^{9}$ will also secure the mark).
>

$y^{5}\timesy^{n}=y^{12}\timesy^{7}$

> *Simplify using laws of indices that state that to multiply indices with the same base, add their powers*
