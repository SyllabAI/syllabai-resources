### Q1 — medium — 5 marks

**Q1** [5 marks]

The line with equation $y = x - 1$ intersects the circle with equation $( x - 5 )^{2} + ( y - 4 )^{2} = 18$ at two distinct points.

Find the coordinates of the two points of intersection.

### Q2 — easy — 2 marks

### Q3 — easy — 6 marks
