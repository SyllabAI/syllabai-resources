## Exam Questions (24 questions)


### Q1 — easy — 3 marks

**Q1** [3 marks]

Show that

$\text{cot}  x \text{cosec}  x \text{sec}  x \equiv 1 + \text{cot}^{2} x$

### Q2 — easy — 4 marks

**Q2** [4 marks]

Solve the equation

$\text{sec}  θ \text{tan}  θ - \text{sec}  θ = 0$

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$, giving your answers in an exact form.

### Q3 — easy — 2 marks

**Q3** [2 marks]

### Q4 — easy — 3 marks

**Q4** [3 marks]

Solve the equation

$\text{cosec}^{2} x - 2 \text{cosec}  x - 8 = 0$

for $0 ^{\circ} \leq x \leq 360 ^{\circ}$, giving your answers correct to 1 decimal place where appropriate.

### Q5 — easy — 4 marks

**Q5** [4 marks]

Starting with the identity

$\text{sin}^{2} x + \text{cos}^{2} x \equiv 1$

show that

(i) $1 + \text{cot}^{2} x \equiv \text{cosec}^{2} x$

(ii) $\text{tan}^{2} x + 1 \equiv \text{sec}^{2} x$

### Q6 — easy — 3 marks

**Q6** [3 marks]

### Q7 — easy — 5 marks

**Q7** [2 marks]

### Q8 — easy — 4 marks

**Q8** [4 marks]

### Q9 — easy — 3 marks

**Q9** [3 marks]

### Q10 — hard — 6 marks

**Q10** [6 marks]

Solve the equation

`3 \text{cot}^{2} x - 4 \sqrt{3} = \left(6 - 2 \sqrt{3}\right) \text{cosec} \; x - 3`

for $0 \leq x \leq 2 π$, giving your answers in an exact form.

### Q11 — very_hard — 6 marks

**Q11** [6 marks]

Using the double angle formulae $\text{sin}  2 A \equiv 2 \text{sin}  A \text{cos}  A$ and $\text{cos}  2 A \equiv \text{cos}^{2} A - \text{sin}^{2} A$, solve the equation

`\left(\text{cosec} \; x - \text{sec} \; x\right) \left(\frac{1}{\text{sec} \; x} + \frac{1}{\text{cosec} \; x}\right) = \text{cot} \; 2 x + 3`

for $- π < x \leq π$, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

### Q12 — very_hard — 7 marks

**Q12** [3 marks]

Sketch, in the interval $- 2 π \leq θ \leq π$, the graph of `y = 2 + 3 \text{sec} \; \left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)`, including asymptotes and the coordinates of all maximum and minimum points.

**Q12** [4 marks]

Deduce the maximum and minimum values of `\frac{1}{2 + 3 \text{sec} \; \left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}`.

### Q13 — hard — 5 marks

**Q13** [5 marks]

for $- π < θ \leq π$.

### Q14 — hard — 6 marks

**Q14** [6 marks]

### Q15 — hard — 6 marks

**Q15** [6 marks]

### Q16 — hard — 6 marks

### Q17 — hard — 5 marks

### Q19 — medium — 6 marks

**Q19** [3 marks]

$2 \text{cot}^{2} x = 1 - 5 \text{cosec}  x$

**Q19** [3 marks]

### Q20 — medium — 5 marks

### Q21 — medium — 6 marks
