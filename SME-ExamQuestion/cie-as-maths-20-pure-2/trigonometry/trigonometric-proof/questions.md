## Exam Questions (27 questions)


### Q1 — easy — 5 marks

**Q1** [5 marks]

Show that

$2  \text{cosec}  2 x  \text{cot}  x \equiv \text{cosec}^{2}  x$

where $x \neq \frac{kπ}{2}$ and $k$ is an integer.

### Q2 — easy — 4 marks

**Q2** [4 marks]

Show that

$\text{sin}^{2}  θ  ( \text{sec}^{2}  θ + \text{cosec}^{2}  θ ) \equiv \text{sec}^{2}  θ$

### Q3 — easy — 3 marks

**Q3** [3 marks]

Show that

$3  \text{sin}  2 θ - 2  \text{sin}  θ \equiv 2  \text{sin}  θ  ( 3  \text{cos}  θ - 1 )$

### Q4 — easy — 3 marks

**Q4** [3 marks]

Use the identity

$R  \text{sin}  ( θ + α ) \equiv R  \text{cos}  α  \text{sin}  θ + R  \text{sin}  α  \text{cos}  θ$

to show that

`4 \; \text{sin} \left( \theta + \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) \equiv 2\sqrt{2} \; (\text{sin} \; \theta + \text{cos} \; \theta)`

### Q5 — easy — 5 marks

**Q5** [5 marks]

(i) Use the quotient rule to show that

`\dfrac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x} \left[ \text{cosec} \; x \right] = \dfrac{- \text{cos} \; x}{\text{sin}^2 \; x}`

(ii) Hence show that

`\dfrac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x} \left[ \text{cosec} \; x \right] = - \text{cot} \; x \; \text{cosec} \; x`

### Q6 — easy — 2 marks

**Q6** [2 marks]

Show that

$\frac{\text{sin}2θ}{2\text{sin}θ} \equiv \text{cos}  θ$

where $θ \neq k π$ and $k$ is an integer.

### Q7 — hard — 4 marks

**Q7** [4 marks]

Show that

$\text{cosec}  x \equiv \frac{\frac{1}{2}\text{sec}^{2}\frac{x}{2}}{\text{tan}\frac{x}{2}}$

### Q8 — hard — 5 marks

**Q8** [5 marks]

### Q9 — medium — 4 marks

**Q9** [4 marks]

### Q10 — hard — 5 marks

**Q10** [5 marks]

Show that

$\text{tan}  \frac{x}{2} \equiv \frac{1}{\text{cosec}x+\text{cot}x}$

where $x \neq 2 k π$ and $k$ is an integer.

### Q11 — hard — 5 marks

**Q11** [5 marks]

Show that

$\frac{1-\text{tan}^{2}x}{\text{cos}2x} \equiv \text{sec}^{2}  x$

where $x \neq \frac{(2k+1)π}{4}$ and $k$ is an integer.

### Q12 — easy — 4 marks

**Q12** [4 marks]

Given the identity

$\text{cos}  ( A + B ) \equiv \text{cos}  A  \text{cos}  B - \text{sin}  A  \text{sin}  B$

show that

(i) $\text{cos}  2 θ \equiv \text{cos}^{2}  θ - \text{sin}^{2}  θ$

(ii) $\text{cos}  2 θ \equiv 1 - 2  \text{sin}^{2}  θ$

(iii) $\text{cos}  2 θ \equiv 2  \text{cos}^{2}  θ - 1$

### Q13 — medium — 5 marks

**Q13** [5 marks]

### Q14 — medium — 4 marks

**Q14** [4 marks]

$\text{tan}  4 θ \equiv \frac{4\text{tan}θ(1-\text{tan}^{2}θ)}{1-6\text{tan}^{2}θ+\text{tan}^{4}θ}$

### Q15 — very_hard — 9 marks

**Q15** [4 marks]

Show that

$\text{sin}  3 θ \equiv 3  \text{sin}  θ  \text{cos}^{2}  θ - \text{sin}^{3}  θ$

**Q15** [5 marks]

Hence, or otherwise, show that

$\frac{\text{cos}3θ-\text{cos}θ}{\text{sin}3θ\text{sin}θ} \equiv \frac{4\text{cos}θ}{1-4\text{cos}^{2}θ}$

where $θ \neq k π$ and $k$ is an integer.

### Q16 — medium — 4 marks

**Q16** [4 marks]

### Q17 — hard — 5 marks

**Q17** [5 marks]

### Q18 — hard — 6 marks

### Q19 — hard — 5 marks

### Q20 — very_hard — 9 marks
