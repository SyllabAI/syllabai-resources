### Q1
> *Determine an equivalent fraction for *$\frac{7}{9}$* and *$\frac{8}{9}$* to determine a value between these two numbers*

$\frac{7\times2}{9\times2}=\frac{14}{18}$

$\frac{8\times2}{9\times2}=\frac{16}{18}$

> *A fraction between *$\frac{14}{18}$* and *$\frac{16}{18}$* is therefore *$\frac{15}{18}$

$\frac{7}{9}<\frac{15}{18}<\frac{8}{9}$ **[1]**

### Q2
> *The total amount that Saskia has spent can be found by adding the fractions together*

$\frac{1}{5}+\frac{1}{3}$

**[1]**

> *To add fractions together, they must have a common denominator. The lowest common multiple of 5 and 3 is 15 so multiply everything in the first fraction by 3, and everything in the second fraction by 5. *

$\frac{3}{15}+\frac{5}{15}$

**[1]**

> *Add the fractions together to work out how much Saskia has spent*

$\frac{3}{15}+\frac{5}{15}=\frac{8}{15}$

> *To work out how much Saskia has left, subtract this fraction from 1.*

$1-\frac{8}{15}=\frac{7}{15}$

$\frac{7}{15}$** [1]**
