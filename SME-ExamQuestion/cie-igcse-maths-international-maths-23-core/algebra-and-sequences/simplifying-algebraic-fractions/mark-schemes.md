### Q1
(i)

> *Write out the factors in the numerator and denominator *

$\frac{2\times3}{3\times5\timesx}$

> *Cancel the common factor of 3 from top and bottom*

`fraction numerator 2 cross times up diagonal strike 3 over denominator up diagonal strike 3 cross times 5 cross times x end fraction`

> *Write out the remaining fraction*

$\frac{2}{5x}$

**[B1]**

(ii)

> *Write out the factors in the numerator*

$\frac{t\timest}{t}$

> *Cancel the common factor of *$t$* from top and bottom*

`fraction numerator t cross times up diagonal strike t over denominator up diagonal strike t end fraction`

> *This makes the denominator 1 (not zero)*

$\frac{t}{1}$

> *Something divided by 1 is always itself*

$t$

**[B1]**

(iii)

> *All the terms on the top cancel with all the terms on the bottom*

`fraction numerator up diagonal strike 10 cross times up diagonal strike a over denominator up diagonal strike 10 cross times up diagonal strike a end fraction`

> *This leaves 1 over 1 (not 0)*

$\frac{1}{1}$

> *1 divided by 1 is 1*

$1$

**[B1]**
