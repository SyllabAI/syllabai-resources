### Q1
(i)

> *Write out the factors in the numerator and denominator *

$\frac{2\times3}{3\times5\timesx}$

> *Cancel the common factor of 3 from top and bottom*

$\frac{2 \times \cancel{3} }{ \cancel{3} \times 5 \times x}$

> *Write out the remaining fraction*

$\frac{2}{5x}$

**[B1]**

(ii)

> *Write out the factors in the numerator*

$\frac{t\timest}{t}$

> *Cancel the common factor of *$t$* from top and bottom*

$\frac{t \times \cancel{t} }{ \cancel{t} }$

> *This makes the denominator 1 (not zero)*

$\frac{t}{1}$

> *Something divided by 1 is always itself*

$t$

**[B1]**

(iii)

> *All the terms on the top cancel with all the terms on the bottom*

$\frac{ \cancel{10} \times \cancel{a} }{ \cancel{10} \times \cancel{a} }$

> *This leaves 1 over 1 (not 0)*

$\frac{1}{1}$

> *1 divided by 1 is 1*

$1$

**[B1]**

### Q2
> *Cancel out the common factor of *$8$* from the numerator and denominator *

$\frac{ \cancel{8} \times 2 p q^{2}}{ \cancel{8} \times 3 p^{3} q} = \frac{2 p q^{2}}{3 p^{3} q}$

> *Cancel out the common factor of *$p$* from the numerator and denominator *

$\frac{2 \cancel{p} q^{2}}{3 \cancel{p} \times p^{2} q}$

**Either part simplified correctly [1]**

$\frac{2q}{3p^{2}}$* ***[1]**
