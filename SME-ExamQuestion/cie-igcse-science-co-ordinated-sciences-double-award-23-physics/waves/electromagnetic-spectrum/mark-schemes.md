### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****C**** because:**

- The speed of all electromagnetic waves is usually given as 3.0 × 10<sup>8</sup> **m/s**

  - But in the solutions above the units are either **cm/s** or **km/s**
- There are 1 000, or 10<sup>3</sup> metres in one kilometre, therefore the speed in km / s = (3.0 × 10<sup>8</sup> m/s) ÷ 10<sup>3</sup> = 3.0 × 10<sup>5</sup> km/s

  - Option **D** is not correct
- There are 100, or 10<sup>2</sup> centimetres in one metre therefore the speed in cm / s = (3.0 × 10<sup>8</sup> m / s) × 10<sup>2</sup> = 3.0 × 10<sup>10</sup> cm/s

  - Option **C** is correct

Converting large and small numbers and units is a key skill for the Physics or Engineering student. This question is getting you to practice working with indices so that you can easily convert between cm, m and km.

### Q13
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****B**** because:**
