### Q6
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****B**** because:**

- Diameter of the Milky Way:

  - It's correctly stated that the Milky Way's diameter is approximately 100,000 light years. This represents the distance light would travel in a straight line across the entire galaxy
- From the centre to the edge: $\mathrm{distance} \mathrm{from} \mathrm{centre}=\frac{100000}{2}$

  - The distance from the centre to the outer edge is half the diameter:

  - Light years = 50,000 light years
- Time taken by light:

  - A light year measures the distance light travels in one year.
  - Therefore, to traverse 50,000 light years, light would take 50,000 years

Remember that a light year is a measure of <u>distance</u>, not time!

Light takes one year to travel the distance of one light year, hence the name.
