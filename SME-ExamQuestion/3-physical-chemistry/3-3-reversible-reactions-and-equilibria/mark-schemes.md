> 
> - The forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate

> - The concentrations of reactants and products remain constant **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Two key words to remember for this definition are **"rate"** and **"constant"**.
> 
> - The **rates** of the forward and reverse reactions are **equal**.
> - The **concentrations** of all substances are **constant**.
> 
> A common mistake is to say the concentrations are *equal*, which is incorrect. They are just no longer changing.
