Calculate the molar mass of oxygen gas, O<sub>2</sub> and give the associated unit.

**Q1** [2 marks]

**Extract:**

Calculate the molar mass of oxygen gas, O~2~ and give the associated unit.
To calculate the molar mass of oxygen gas: The atomic mass of oxygen is 16. Therefore, one mole of oxygen atoms = 16 g. Therefore, one mole of oxygen gas, O~2~ = 2 × 16 = 32 g. So the molar mass, Mr = 32. Unit = g mol^−1.

Calculate the number of moles in 0.5 kg of oxygen gas.
