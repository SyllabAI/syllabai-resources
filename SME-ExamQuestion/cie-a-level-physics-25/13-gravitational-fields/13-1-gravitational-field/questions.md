**Extract:**

The spacecraft Centurion leaves Earth for Venus to identify potential mining locations based on fluctuations in the gravitational field strength on the surface of Venus. The Centurion sends a landing party to the surface in a shuttle. The shuttle has a mass of 3.24×10^7 kg when launched from the Centurion, but loses 5% of its total mass in fuel combustion due to landing. The shuttle has a weight of 270 MN when it comes to rest on the surface of Venus.

The landing crew collect rock samples from the landing site. When the landing crew has finished its mission, the shuttle launches to return to the Centurion. At an altitude of 250 km above the surface of Venus, the mass lost through fuel combustion is equal to the total mass of the rocks collected at the surface.

Gravitational force is inversely proportional to the square of the distance between masses.

$F\propto\frac{1}{r^{2}}$

Calculate the gravitational field strength of Venus at 250 km above the surface.

The radius of Venus is 6.3 × 10<sup>6</sup> m.
