### Q2 — hard — 12 marks

**Q2** [12 marks]

**Extract:**

FBT Travel Agency 

FBT Travel Agency is a large private limited company based in Country X. Founded in 1998, it has grown from a small family-run business to a major player in the travel industry, specialising in both domestic and international travel packages. The company employs 500 people across its head office and 50 high-street branches, which are located in major cities and towns throughout Country X. FBT's core business involves creating and selling holiday packages that include flights, accommodation, and activities. It caters for various market segments, from budget travellers to luxury seekers. In recent years, FBT has also started offering cruise packages and adventure tourism options to diversify its product range. The company is considering expanding its online presence to compete with internet-based travel companies. Currently, 32% of FBT's bookings are made online, but management believes this could be significantly increased with better digital platforms and marketing. They are debating whether to close some of their physical branches and invest more in their website and mobile app. FBT is also considering diversifying into the business travel market. This would involve creating a new division to cater specifically to corporate clients, offering services such as conference bookings, group travel arrangements, and loyalty programs for frequent business travellers. The travel industry has faced significant challenges in recent years due to global events and changing consumer preferences. The COVID-19 pandemic had a particularly severe impact on FBT's operations, as shown in their financial performance over the past three years:

Growing demand for sustainable and eco-friendly travel options 2. Increasing interest in adventure tourism 3.
