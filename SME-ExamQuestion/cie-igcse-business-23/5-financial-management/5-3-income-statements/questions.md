### Q1 — hard — 12 marks

**Q1** [12 marks]

**Extract:**

**MapQuest Ltd**

MapQuest Ltd is a producer of printed maps and travel guides based in Country Z. The company has been operating for 25 years and is known for its high-quality, detailed maps of popular tourist destinations. MapQuest Ltd sells its products through bookstores and online retailers in several countries.

The company has seen a decline in sales over the past five years due to the increasing use of digital navigation apps. To address this challenge, MapQuest Ltd is considering developing a subscription-based digital app that would offer interactive maps, travel guides, and real-time updates.

MapQuest Ltd's financial performance for the last two years is shown in Appendix 1. The company currently employs 50 people, including cartographers, writers, and sales staff. The Managing Director believes that launching the app could help reverse the declining sales trend, but it would require significant investment in technology and new staff with digital skills.

The company is also facing increasing costs due to rising paper prices and shipping expenses. The Finance Director has suggested that if MapQuest Ltd proceeds with developing the app, they should consider discontinuing some of their less profitable printed products to reduce costs.

MapQuest Ltd's main competitor recently launched a similar app, which has been well-received by customers. The Marketing Director believes that MapQuest Ltd needs to act quickly to remain competitive in the changing market.

**Appendix 1: Extract from MapQuest Ltd's Income Statement (\$)**

| **Item** | **2022** | **2023** |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Revenue | 5,000,000 | 4,500,000 |
| Cost of sales | 2,000,000 | 1,950,000 |
| Gross profit | 3,000,000 | 2,550,000 |
| Expenses | 2,400,000 | 2,300,000 |
| Net profit | 600,000 | 250,000 |

Consider the advantages and disadvantages of the following two options for MapQuest Ltd to improve its financial performance.

- Develop the subscription-based digital app
- Focus on reducing costs in the existing printed map business

Which option do you recommend? Justify your answer.

### Q2 — hard — 12 marks

**Q2** [12 marks]

**Extract:**

**Educational Toys Limited (ETL)**

ETL is a private limited company that manufactures educational toys in Country Y. It has been in business for 15 years and has a reputation for producing high-quality, innovative toys that support children's learning. ETL's main products include interactive science kits, mathematical puzzles, and language learning games for children aged 3-12.

ETL currently sells its products primarily through toy stores and educational supply shops in Country Y. The company has one factory employing 150 workers and uses batch production methods. ETL's sales revenue last year was \$8 million, with a gross profit of \$3.2 million and a net profit of \$800,000.

The CEO of ETL is considering expanding the business by entering the online market and selling directly to consumers through an e-commerce website. This would require an investment of \$500,000 in website development and digital marketing. The finance director has prepared a break-even analysis for this new sales channel, projecting that ETL would need to sell 25,000 units online per year to break even.

ETL is facing increasing competition from imported toys, which are often cheaper but of lower quality. The government of Country Y has recently announced it is considering introducing import tariffs on toys to protect domestic manufacturers.

The company is also aware of growing consumer interest in environmentally friendly and ethically produced toys. ETL's marketing director believes there is an opportunity to rebrand the company as a sustainable toy manufacturer, but this would require changes to production processes and materials.

**Appendix 1: Extract from ETL's income statement for last year (\$000)**

| Sales revenue | 8,000 |
| --- | ---: |
| Cost of sales | 4,800 |
| Gross profit | 3,200 |
| Expenses | 2,400 |
| Profit | 800 |

Consider how each of the following three changes may affect ETL's profit.

- The introduction of import tariffs on toys in Country Y
- An increase in the cost of raw materials
- A decrease in corporate tax rates in Country Y

Which change is likely to have the greatest impact on ETL's profit? Justify your answer.
