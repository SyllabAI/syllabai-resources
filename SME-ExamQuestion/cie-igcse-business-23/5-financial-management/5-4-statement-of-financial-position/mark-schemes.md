### Q1
Tasty Tapas has non-current assets of \$800,000, such as restaurant buildings and kitchen equipment **[Ap]**, which shows it has a solid foundation. However, expanding into Country Y would require significant investment in new assets, as they may not be enough to support expansion while maintaining current operations **[An]**

The current ratio of 1.33 indicates Tasty Tapas' short-term financial health. It shows the company has \$1.33 in current assets for every \$1 of current liabilities **[Ap]**, which is not very high. Expanding into a new country is risky and could strain Tasty Tapas' ability to pay short-term debts if unexpected costs arise **[An]**

The \$500,000 long-term loan is a significant liability. Taking on more debt to fund expansion could be risky, especially with existing loan commitments. However, it shows Tasty Tapas has been able to secure substantial financing in the past **[Ap]**, so may be able to persuade lenders that it is capable of paying back debts **[An]**

The current ratio will have the greatest impact on the expansion decision **[E]**. Non-current assets, while substantial, are less crucial as they don't directly affect the company's ability to fund the expansion **[E]**. The long-term loan, is less immediate of a concern than the current ratio, as it relates to long-term debts rather than the short-term liquidity needed for expansion **[E]**. The relatively low current ratio suggests Tasty Tapas may not have enough flexibility to safely expand into such a highly competitive market **[Ap] **at this time **[E]**

### Q7
