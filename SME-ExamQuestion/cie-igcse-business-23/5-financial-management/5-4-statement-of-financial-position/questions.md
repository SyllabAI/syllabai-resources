### Q1 — hard — 12 marks

**Q1** [12 marks]

**Extract:**

**Tasty Tapas Restaurant Chain**

Tasty Tapas is a chain of 10 tapas restaurants in Country Z, established in 2010. The business is owned by Maria and Carlos Rodriguez, who started with one restaurant and gradually expanded. Tasty Tapas is known for its authentic Spanish cuisine and lively atmosphere. The restaurants are popular with both locals and tourists, especially in the evenings and on weekends.

In 2023, Tasty Tapas reported the following financial information:

- Revenue: \$5,000,000
- Gross profit: \$2,000,000
- Profit: \$500,000

Maria and Carlos are considering expanding the business by opening five new restaurants in neighbouring Country Y. This expansion would require an investment of \$2,000,000. They are exploring various financing options, including taking out a new loan, issuing shares to become a private limited company, or using a combination of retained earnings and a bank loan.

However, the restaurant industry in Country Y is highly competitive, with several established chains already operating there. Additionally, there are concerns about potential changes in import tariffs between Country Z and Country Y, which could affect the cost of importing authentic Spanish ingredients.

The owners must also consider the impact of the expansion on their current operations, including the need for additional management staff and the potential strain on their supply chain. They are aware that maintaining consistent quality across all locations will be crucial for the success of the expanded business.

**Appendix 1: Tasty Tapas Statement of Financial Position as of December 31, 2023 (\$000)**

| --- | ---: |
| Current assets | 400 |
| - Inventory | 200 |
| - Receivables | 50 |
| - Cash | 150 |
| Total assets | 1200 |
| Current liabilities | 300 |
| - Payables | 200 |
| - Short-term loans | 100 |
| Non-current liabilities | 500 |
| Owners capital | 400 |

Consider how each of the following three factors from Tasty Tapas' Statement of Financial Position could affect the decision to expand into Country Y.

- Non-current assets of \$800,000
- Current ratio of 1.33
- Long-term loan of \$500,000

Which factor is likely to have the greatest impact on the expansion decision? Justify your answer.
