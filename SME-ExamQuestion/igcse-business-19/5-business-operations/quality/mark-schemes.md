# Mark Schemes — Quality
**5. Business Operations** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1((c)) — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - A product or service that meets the standard set by the business **[1 mark]**
> 
> "Define" requires a clear statement of the meaning of the term.

> **[exam-tip]**
> Make sure you do not confuse **quality control** with **quality assurance**. Quality control focuses on checking that the product or service meets the standards set, whereas quality assurance is about preventing faults from occurring in the first place. A common mistake is to describe the process of inspecting products without linking it to meeting a set standard.

**[1 mark]**

## Q2 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
**Correct answer: D** — Quality control

**The correct answer is D**

Quality control involves inspecting finished products at the end of the production process to identify and remove defects before they reach customers.

**A** is incorrect because Total Quality Management involves every worker in the business being responsible for quality at all stages, not just a team of inspectors checking finished goods

**B** is incorrect because Kaizen is a philosophy of continuous improvement where workers suggest small changes to processes, not a method of inspecting finished products

**C** is incorrect because quality assurance involves checking quality at every stage of the production process, not only at the end when products are finished
