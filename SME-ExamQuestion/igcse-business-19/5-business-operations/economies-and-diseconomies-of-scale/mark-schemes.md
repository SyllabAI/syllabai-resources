# Mark Schemes — Economies & Diseconomies of Scale
**5. Business Operations** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1((c)) — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Award 1 mark for a correct definition of diseconomies of scale.
> 
> - When the average unit cost increases due to the growth of a business **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Make sure you focus on the idea that average (unit) costs *increase* as the business grows. A common mistake is confusing diseconomies of scale with economies of scale — remember, diseconomies of scale are the opposite: they occur when a business becomes too large and efficiency falls, causing average costs to rise. Avoid simply stating that "costs increase" without specifying that it is the average or unit cost.

**[1 mark]**
