# Mark Schemes — Internal & External Communication
**2. People in Business** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - The exchange of information between people within the same business **[1 mark]**
> 
> OR
> 
> - The sharing of messages/information between employees or departments inside a business **[1 mark]**
> 
> "Define" requires a clear, precise statement of the meaning of the term

> **[exam-tip]**
> Make sure you emphasise that the communication takes place *within* the business. A common mistake is to give a general definition of communication without specifying that it is internal — i.e. between people in the same organisation. Avoid confusing internal communication with external communication, which involves parties outside the business.

**[1 mark]**
