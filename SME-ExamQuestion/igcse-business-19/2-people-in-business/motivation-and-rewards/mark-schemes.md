> - Herzberg argued that achievement is a key motivator **[1 mark]**, as employees who feel a sense of accomplishment when completing a challenging task are more likely to remain engaged and committed to their work **[1 mark]**, which can lead to higher productivity and a reduced likelihood of staff leaving the business **[1 mark]**

However, according to Herzberg, financial rewards are hygiene factors rather than true motivators** [K]**, meaning that once Huawei's skilled employees **[Ap] **become accustomed to high pay they may no longer feel motivated to perform at their best, limiting the long-term effectiveness of financial methods** [An]**

One benefit of non-financial motivation is that methods such as recognition and promotion opportunities address higher-level needs in Maslow's hierarchy **[K]**, giving Huawei's employees a sense of achievement and career progression that sustains long-term motivation and reduces the likelihood of staff leaving for competitors such as Samsung** [Ap] [An]**

However, teachers who already have a full timetable of lessons may be unable to recruit new pupils and would therefore receive no benefit from commission **[K]**, meaning this option does not directly address TADS's goal of retaining all of its current teachers** [Ap] [An]**

One benefit of increasing the hourly rate is that it immediately improves the earnings of all of TADS's teachers **[K]**, making them feel more valued and financially rewarded for the lessons they teach, increasing the likelihood they will remain with the school **[Ap] [An]**

However, TADS does not have reliable profits and Ollie invested all of his savings into the business **[Ap]**, meaning a permanent increase in the hourly rate for all teachers would raise fixed costs **[K]*** *and could cause further financial difficulty **[An]**

TADS should choose an increase in the hourly rate **[E]**. Since TADS's goal is specifically to retain its current teachers** [Ap]**, a higher hourly rate directly rewards all staff for their existing contribution to the school** [An]**. Commission only benefits teachers who recruit new pupils and does not address the needs of those with a full timetable, making an hourly rate increase the more effective and fair solution for improving teacher retention **[E]**
