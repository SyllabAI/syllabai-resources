**2. People in Business** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Training given to new employees when they first join a business to familiarise them with its procedures/policies and colleagues **[1 mark]**
> 
> Award 1 mark for a correct definition of induction training
> 
> Acceptable alternative: training a new employee receives when starting a job, designed to help them settle into the business and understand how it operates

> **[exam-tip]**
> Make sure your definition focuses on two key aspects: that the training is for **new** employees and that its purpose is to help them become familiar with the business. A common mistake is to describe training in general terms without specifying that induction training is specifically for new starters. Including a reference to what the training covers (e.g. procedures, policies, meeting colleagues) will strengthen your answer.
