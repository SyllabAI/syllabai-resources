# Mark Schemes — Classification of Businesses
**1. Business Activity & Influences on Business** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - The provision/selling of services/goods to consumers/businesses **[1 mark]**
> 
> Award 1 mark for a correct definition of tertiary sector.
> 
> Alternative acceptable answers:
> 
> - The stage of production that provides services to customers
> - The sector involved in retail, banking, transport or other service activities

> **[exam-tip]**
> Make sure you focus on the idea of providing **services** rather than manufacturing or extracting raw materials, as these relate to the secondary and primary sectors respectively. A common mistake is confusing the tertiary sector with the secondary sector — remember, tertiary is about services (e.g. banking, retail, transport), not making products.

**[1 mark]**
