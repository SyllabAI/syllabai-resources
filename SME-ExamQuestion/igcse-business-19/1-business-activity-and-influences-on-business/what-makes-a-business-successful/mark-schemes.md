## Q12 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 2 marks
$\text{Market growth} = \frac{\text{New market size}-\text{Old market size}}{\text{Old market size}} \times 100$

**[1 mark]**

`\text{Market growth} = \frac{180\,000\,000 - 150\,000\,000}{150\,000\,000} \times 100 = \frac{30\,000\,000}{150\,000\,000} \times 100 = \text{20}\%`

**20%** **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - 1 mark for the correct formula or method
> - 1 mark for the correct final answer
> - If the final answer is incorrect, award marks for correct working shown

> **[exam-tip]**
> Remember that market growth measures the percentage change in the total size of a market over a period of time. Make sure you use the old (previous year) market size as the denominator — a common mistake is to divide by the new market size instead. Always show your working clearly so that even if your final answer is wrong, you can still pick up a method mark.
