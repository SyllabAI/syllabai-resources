# Mark Schemes — External Factors
**1. Business Activity & Influences on Business** · Edexcel IGCSE Business (4BS1)


## Q1 — easy — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Something outside of a business's control that can affect its decisions and performance **[1 mark]**
> 
> Award 1 mark for a correct definition of **external factor**.
> 
> Alternative acceptable answers:
> 
> - A force in the business environment that a company cannot directly influence but must respond to

> **[exam-tip]**
> When defining a term, make sure your answer captures two key elements: what the thing *is* and what it *does*. For "external factor", you need to show that it is outside the business's control and that it has an impact on the business. A common mistake is only stating one of these elements, e.g. just saying "something that affects a business" without mentioning that it is beyond the business's control.
