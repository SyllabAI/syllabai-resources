### Q1 — easy — 8 marks

**Q1** [2 marks]

The complex number $z$ is given by

`z = 9 \left( \text{cos} \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} + i \, \text{sin} \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} \right)`

Show that `3 \left( \text{cos} \dfrac{\pi}{3} + i \, \text{sin} \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)` is a square root of $z$.

**Q1** [3 marks]

Show that `3 \left( \text{cos} \left( - \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} \right) + i \, \text{sin} \left( - \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} \right) \right)` is also a square root of $z$.

**Q1** [3 marks]

Given that

`- 1 = \text{cos} \left( - \pi \right) + i \, \text{sin} \left( - \pi \right)`

show that

`3 \left( \text{cos} \left( - \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} \right) + i \, \text{sin} \left( - \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} \right) \right) = - 3 \left( \text{cos} \dfrac{\pi}{3} + i \, \text{sin} \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right)`

State how this compares with the relationship between the two square roots of a positive real number.
