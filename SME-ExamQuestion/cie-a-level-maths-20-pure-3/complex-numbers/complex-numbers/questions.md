## Exam Questions (36 questions)


### Q1 — easy — 5 marks

**Q1** [5 marks]

$f ( z ) = z^{3} - 9 z^{2} + c z + d$, where $c$ and $d$ are real numbers.

The roots of the equation $f ( z ) = 0$ are $z_{1}$, $z_{2}$ and $z_{3}$.

Given that $z_{1} = 3$ and $z_{2} = 3 - i$,

(i) state $z_{3}$ in the form $a + b i$, where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers

(ii) by first writing $f ( z ) = ( z - z_{1} ) ( z - z_{2} ) ( z - z_{3} )$, find the values of $c$ and $d$.

### Q3 — easy — 7 marks

(i) State $γ$ in the form $a + b i$, where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers.

(ii) Show that $( z - β ) ( z - γ ) = z^{2} - 2 z + 5$.

(iii) Hence fully factorise $z^{3} + z^{2} - z + 15$ and determine the value of $δ$.

### Q17 — very_hard — 12 marks

**Q17** [5 marks]

The principal square root of a complex number $z$ is defined as

$\sqrt{z} = x + i y$

where $x$ and $y$ are real numbers and $x \geq 0$. If $x = 0$ then the value for $y$ is chosen such that $y \geq 0$. Note that the other square root of $z$ will then be given by $- \sqrt{z} = - x - i y$.

Show that

`x = \sqrt{\dfrac{\text{Re}(z) + \sqrt{\left(\text{Re}(z)\right)^{2} + \left(\text{Im}(z)\right)^{2}}}{2}}`

**Q17** [2 marks]

Given that $x > 0$, derive a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$ and $\text{Im} ( z )$, and explain why $y$ in this case will always have the same sign (positive, negative, or zero) as $\text{Im} ( z )$.

**Q17** [2 marks]

Hence show that in general

`y = \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{-\text{Re}(z) + \sqrt{\left(\text{Re}(z)\right)^{2} + \left(\text{Im}(z)\right)^{2}}}{2}}`

with the choice of the positive or negative value being dependent on the properties of $z$.

**Q17** [3 marks]

Explain what must be true of $z$ for each of the following to be true:

(i) $x = 0 , y \neq 0$

(ii) $x \neq 0 , y = 0$

(iii) $x = 0 , y = 0$
