### Q4 — easy — 4 marks

**Q4** [4 marks]

For two complex numbers $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$,

`|z_{1}z_{2}| = |z_{1}||z_{2}|`

$\mathrm{arg} ( z_{1} z_{2} ) = \mathrm{arg} ( z_{1} ) + \mathrm{arg} ( z_{2} )$

`\left|\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}\right| = \frac{|z_{1}|}{|z_{2}|}`

`\arg\left(\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}\right) = \arg(z_{1}) - \arg(z_{2})`

(i) `5\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \times 4\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{2}\right)`

(ii) `12\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \div 3\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)`

(iii) `\sqrt{3}\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{6} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{6}\right) \times \sqrt{6}\left(\cos\left(-\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) + i\sin\left(-\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\right)`

(iv) $\frac{6.25(\mathrm{cos}3.05+i\mathrm{sin}3.05)}{0.5(\mathrm{cos}1.42+i\mathrm{sin}1.42)}$
