## Exam Questions (24 questions)


### Q1 — easy — 3 marks

**Q1** [3 marks]

Show that

$\text{cot}  x  \text{cosec}  x  \text{sec}  x \equiv 1 + \text{cot}^{2}  x$

### Q2 — easy — 4 marks

**Q2** [4 marks]

Starting with the identity

$\text{sin}^{2}  x + \text{cos}^{2}  x \equiv 1$

show that

(i) $1 + \text{cot}^{2}  x \equiv \text{cosec}^{2}  x$

(ii) $\text{tan}^{2}  x + 1 \equiv \text{sec}^{2}  x$

### Q3 — easy — 2 marks

**Q3** [2 marks]

Sketch the graph of $y = \text{cosec}  x$, for $- 180 ^{\circ} \leq x \leq 180 ^{\circ}$.

### Q4 — easy — 5 marks

**Q4** [3 marks]

Sketch the graph of $y = 2  \text{sec}  2 x$ for $- π \leq x \leq π$.

**Q4** [2 marks]

By sketching a suitable straight line on your diagram, show that the equation $2  \text{sec}  2 x = 4$ has four solutions in this interval.

### Q5 — easy — 4 marks

**Q5** [4 marks]

Label any points of intersection with the coordinate axes and state the equations of any asymptotes.

### Q6 — easy — 3 marks

**Q6** [3 marks]

### Q7 — easy — 3 marks

**Q7** [3 marks]

$\text{cosec}^{2}  x - 2  \text{cosec}  x - 8 = 0$

for $0 ^{\circ} \leq x \leq 360 ^{\circ}$, giving your answers correct to 1 decimal place where appropriate.

### Q8 — easy — 3 marks

**Q8** [3 marks]

### Q9 — easy — 4 marks

**Q9** [4 marks]

$\text{sec}  θ  \text{tan}  θ - \text{sec}  θ = 0$

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$, giving your answers in an exact form.

### Q10 — hard — 5 marks

**Q10** [5 marks]

Solve the equation

$\frac{\text{sec}θ\text{cot}θ}{\text{cosec}θ\text{tan}θ} = - \sqrt{3}$

for $- π < θ \leq π$.

### Q11 — hard — 6 marks

**Q11** [6 marks]

Solve the equation

$6  \text{sec}  θ + \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{\text{sec}θ} = - 3 - 4 \sqrt{3}$

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$, giving your answers in an exact form.

### Q12 — very_hard — 6 marks

**Q12** [6 marks]

Using the double angle formulae $\text{sin}  2 A \equiv 2  \text{sin}  A  \text{cos}  A$ and $\text{cos}  2 A \equiv \text{cos}^{2}  A - \text{sin}^{2}  A$, solve the equation

`\left( \text{cosec} \; x - \text{sec} \; x \right) \left( \dfrac{1}{\text{sec} \; x} + \dfrac{1}{\text{cosec} \; x} \right) = \text{cot} \; 2x + 3`

for $- π < x \leq π$, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

### Q13 — very_hard — 7 marks

**Q13** [3 marks]

Sketch, in the interval $- 2 π \leq θ \leq π$, the graph of `y = 2 + 3 \; \text{sec} \left( \theta + \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)`, including asymptotes and the coordinates of all maximum and minimum points.

**Q13** [4 marks]

Deduce the maximum and minimum values of `\dfrac{1}{2 + 3 \; \text{sec} \left( \theta + \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)}`.

### Q14 — hard — 6 marks

**Q14** [6 marks]

for $0 \leq x \leq 2 π$, giving your answers in an exact form.

### Q15 — hard — 6 marks

**Q15** [6 marks]

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$.

### Q16 — hard — 5 marks

**Q16** [5 marks]

(ii) Hence state the set of values of $k$ for which the equation $- 5 + \frac{1}{2}  \text{sec}  θ = k$ has no solutions.

### Q17 — medium — 6 marks

**Q17** [3 marks]

Show that the equation

$2  \text{cot}^{2}  x = 1 - 5  \text{cosec}  x$

can be expressed in the form

`\left( 2 \; \text{cosec} \; x - 1 \right) \left( \text{cosec} \; x + 3 \right) = 0`

**Q17** [3 marks]

Hence solve the equation

$2  \text{cot}^{2}  x = 1 - 5  \text{cosec}  x$

for $0 \leq x \leq 2 π$, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

### Q18 — hard — 6 marks

**Q18** [6 marks]

for $- π < x \leq π$, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

### Q20 — medium — 5 marks

**Q20** [5 marks]

### Q21 — medium — 6 marks

**Q21** [3 marks]

Show that the equation

$\text{tan}^{2}  x = 6  \text{sec}  x - 10$

can be expressed in the form

`\left( \text{sec} \; x - 3 \right)^2 = 0`

**Q21** [3 marks]

$\text{tan}^{2}  x = 6  \text{sec}  x - 10$

for $0 \leq x \leq 2 π$, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

### Q22 — medium — 5 marks

**Q22** [2 marks]

$\text{sec}  θ  \text{cot}  θ \equiv \text{cosec}  θ$

**Q22** [3 marks]

$\text{sec}  θ  \text{cot}  θ = - 2$

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$.

### Q23 — medium — 6 marks

**Q23** [2 marks]

for $0 \leq θ \leq 2 π$.
