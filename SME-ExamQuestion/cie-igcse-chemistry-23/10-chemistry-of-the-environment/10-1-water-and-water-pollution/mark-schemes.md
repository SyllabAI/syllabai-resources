### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Water from reservoir can contain big insoluble particles, big insoluble particles and microbes
- Filtration of big insoluble particles is done first to remove large solids such as leaves or rocks

  - This will prevent damaging or blockage in later stages of the treatment
- Sedimentation allows smaller suspended solids to settle under gravity because they are denser than water

  - This is done before the filtration of small particles to make the filtration of small particles more effective
  - If the fine filtration were done first, the filter can block quickly, reducing the efficiency
- Once the particles settle down by the action of gravity, filtration of small insoluble particles is performed

  - This removes remaining fine particles that did not settle in the sedimentation
- Chlorination is carried out last to kill harmful microorganisms

  - Doing this earlier would be ineffective because particles can protect microbes from chlorine
- Therefore, the correct order is **filtration of big insoluble particles → sedimentation → filtration of small insoluble particles →  chlorination**

**A **is incorrect because sedimentation is placed after filtration of small particles, but particles must settle before fine filtration is effective

**B **is incorrect because chlorination is carried out before final filtration, which can remove chlorine and reduce disinfection effectiveness

**D** is incorrect because chlorination is done too early and insoluble solids would reduce its ability to kill microbes
