# Mark Schemes — Reversible Reactions & Equilibria
**3. Physical Chemistry** · Edexcel IGCSE Science (Double Award) (4SD0) — 17/Chemistry


## Q1 — medium — 1 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 1 marks
**Correct answer: A** — Ammonia and hydrogen chloride

**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- When the ammonium chloride is heated it decomposes to form ammonia and hydrogen chloride:

NH<sub>4</sub>Cl (s) → NH<sub>3 </sub>(g) + HCl (g)

- As the gases cool down they recombine to form ammonium chloride
- A reversible reaction occurs:

NH<sub>4</sub>Cl (s) ⇌ NH<sub>3 </sub>(g) + HCl (g)
