### 1() — 8 marks
Rural Kenya faces serious environmental problems, including deforestation, soil erosion, and overgrazing. **[K]** Sustainable management tries to protect the environment now so future generations can still use it.** [K]**

One successful example is the Maasai Mara conservation scheme. **[K]** Local Maasai communities lease their land to wildlife conservation groups and receive regular income in return. **[K]** This works because communities earn money from protecting wildlife rather than overgrazing the land, so they have a real reason to look after it.** [U]** Wildlife numbers have increased as a result, which shows this approach can work. [**Ap]**

Agroforestry is another strategy used in rural Kenya, where trees are planted alongside crops to reduce soil erosion and retain water. **[K] **This suits smallholder farmers because it is cheap and does not need outside funding. **[U] **In the** **Upper Tana Catchment, micro grants are available for community-led activities such as irrigation, fisheries, and agriculture to improve incomes and living standards. **[Ap]**

However, sustainable management does not always succeed. **[U]** In Turkana County in northern Kenya, poverty means communities have to focus on surviving right now rather than protecting the environment long term. **[Ap]** Overgrazing continues because families cannot afford to reduce their herds even when the land is clearly suffering. **[U]**

Overall, sustainable management works best in Kenya when local communities are involved and earn real benefits from it. **[Ap] **But in the poorest areas it struggles, which means it needs to be combined with efforts to reduce poverty to truly succeed. **[Ap]**

>

> 
> - **AO1 ****[K]**: show knowledge of sustainable management strategies and relevant examples from a developing or emerging country
> - **AO2** **[U]**: show understanding of how and why sustainable management strategies do or do not succeed in rural environments, and the factors that affect their effectiveness
> - **AO3 ****[Ap]**: apply knowledge and understanding to reach a reasoned judgement about the extent to which sustainable management can successfully protect rural environments
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question, as it will depend on the examples used. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
>
