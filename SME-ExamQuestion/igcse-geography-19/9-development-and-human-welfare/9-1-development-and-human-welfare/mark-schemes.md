> **[exam-tip]**
> **This **question is asking you to think carefully about cause and effect. Does money automatically make lives better?
> 
> **Figure 10a** is deliberately designed to provide you evidence on both sides. Use it early and return to it at least once more in your answer.
> 
> **The **strongest conclusions do not just say 'yes' or 'no' to the statement. They explain the conditions under which economic development does and does not improve welfare.
> 
> **Phrases **such as 'economic growth is necessary but not sufficient', 'the evidence suggests that how wealth is used matters more than how much is produced', and 'welfare improves most when growth is combined with targeted investment' will push your answer into the top level.
