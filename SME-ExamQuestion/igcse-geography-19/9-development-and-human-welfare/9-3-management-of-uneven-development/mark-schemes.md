International strategies (like aid programs and global agreements) can help, but development is also shaped by national and local decisions, so international action is only one part of the solution. **[AO2]**

International strategies matter because some causes of uneven development cross borders, so cooperation is needed. **[AO3]**. For example, international aid can support health, education, and basic services, helping countries improve welfare and development outcomes. **[AO2]**
