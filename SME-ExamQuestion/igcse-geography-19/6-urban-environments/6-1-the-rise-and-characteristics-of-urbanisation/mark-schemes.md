> - Writing a conclusion that simply repeats earlier points rather than making a judgement

> **[exam-tip]**
> In an 8-mark 'analyse' question, your conclusion is worth marks. Do not treat it as optional. The examiner is looking for a moment where you make a clear decision: which reason is most important, and why?
