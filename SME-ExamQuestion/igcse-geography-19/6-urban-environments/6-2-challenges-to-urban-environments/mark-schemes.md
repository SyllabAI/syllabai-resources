## Q13 — easy — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 3 marks
Informal settlements tend to develop in emerging and developing cities because large numbers of people migrate from rural areas to cities in search of work and a better quality of life. **[1]** When they arrive, they cannot afford formal housing, so they build their own shelters on unused land. **[1]** This land is often on dangerous or unwanted sites, such as floodplains or steep slopes, because it is usually unoccupied and unguarded. **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> The command word ‘**suggest**’ means that the answer needs to apply knowledge and understanding to consider the reasons why informal settlements form, not describe what squatter settlements look like.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - **One mark** for each valid point [up to 2 marks]
> - **One mark** for a valid development of a point
> 
> **[3 marks]**
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
> 
> - Rural-to-urban migration driven by push factors such as drought, poverty, or lack of employment in rural areas
> - Rapid population growth meaning housing supply cannot keep pace with demand
> - Lack of affordable formal housing in the city
> - Weak planning regulations or government inability to manage urban growth
> - New arrivals settling on peripheral or hazardous land because it is unoccupied
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Describing the conditions inside a squatter settlement rather than explaining why they form
> - Stating that people are 'poor' without explaining why they cannot access formal housing
> - Confusing informal settlements with social housing or council estates

## Q14 — easy — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 3 marks
The data shows that even though London has a large public transport network, over a quarter of all journeys are still made by private car. **[1]** This contributes to severe congestion, with average speeds in central London falling to just 7.4 mph at peak times, which is barely faster than walking. **[1] **This level of congestion causes air pollution, increases journey times, and raises carbon emissions across the city. **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> The command word '**describe**' means the answer should set out the nature and extent of the transport challenges. Analysis or explanation of causes is not required, but direct use of the stimulus data is expected.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - **One mark** for each valid point drawn from the stimulus or own knowledge [max 2 marks]
> - **One mark** for direct reference to the data provided
> 
> **[3 marks]**
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
> 
> - Reference to the 3.9 million daily underground journeys
> - Reference to the 27% private car use figure
> - Overcrowding on public transport, cost of travel, air quality issues, greenhouse gas emissions, unreliable journey times
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Ignoring the stimulus entirely and only writing from own knowledge
> - Explaining why congestion happens rather than describing the challenge itself
> - Listing problems without any connection to the data provided

> **[exam-tip]**
> **In** a data stimulus question, the examiner wants to see you use the numbers.
> 
> **Do not** just say, 'London has traffic problems.'
> 
> **Quote **the figure, then explain what it tells you. The stimulus is there to help you: treat it as free marks.
> 
> **Even** one clear data reference can be the difference between 2 and 3 marks.

## Q19 — medium — 4 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 4 marks
One method used to improve living conditions in shanty towns is self-help schemes. This is where residents are given materials and support to improve their own homes, allowing them to take ownership of improvements and build stronger communities. For example, in Rocinha, Brazil, self-help schemes have improved homes from basic shacks to low-quality housing with electricity and other services.

A second method is site-and-service schemes, where the local authority provides plots of land with basic infrastructure such as running water, electricity and sanitation pipes already installed. Residents can then build or rent homes on these serviced plots, which improves health and hygiene compared to informal settlements without services.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> - The question asks for two methods of improvement
> - The command word '**explain**' requires students to outline the method and how it improves conditions
> - Can be answered generically or with reference to a named city
> - Each method should be identified and explained for full marks
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - 1 mark for identifying each valid method
> - 1 mark for explaining how each method improves conditions
> - Better responses will clearly link the method to specific improvements in living conditions
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
> 
> - Slum clearance and relocation to government-built housing
> - Provision of legal land ownership/tenure to residents
> - Rural development programmes to reduce migration to cities
> - Building of new towns or housing developments
