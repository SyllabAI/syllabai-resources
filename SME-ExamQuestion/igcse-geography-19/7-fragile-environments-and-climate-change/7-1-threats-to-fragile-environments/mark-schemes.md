## Q18 — easy — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 7((c)) — 2 marks
Volcanic eruptions are a natural cause of climate change **[1]** because large eruptions eject vast quantities of ash into the atmosphere, which blocks incoming solar radiation and leads to a decrease in global temperatures **[1]**.

**[2 marks]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> The command word is 'explain'. This means you need to give a reason, showing how or why the natural factor causes the climate to change. A simple statement of the cause on its own is not enough, so make sure you link it clearly to its effect on temperature.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - One mark for identifying a valid natural cause of climate change
> - One mark for developing the point to explain how it changes the climate
> - Maximum 2 marks
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
> 
> - Milankovitch cycles: long-term changes to the Earth's orbit, tilt and wobble alter how much solar radiation the Earth receives, leading to warmer or cooler periods
> - A more circular orbit leads to cooler periods, while a more elliptical orbit leads to warmer periods
> - Sunspot activity: increased sunspot activity is linked to higher average global temperatures
> - Atmospheric dust: asteroids and meteors entering the atmosphere can increase dust, which decreases temperatures
> - Any other suitable response
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Writing about human causes such as burning fossil fuels or deforestation, which are not natural causes

> - Being vague, for example just writing 'the sun' without linking it to solar radiation or sunspot activity

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Read the command word carefully: 'explain' needs a developed reason, so aim for a 'because' or 'which means' in your answer to earn the second mark
