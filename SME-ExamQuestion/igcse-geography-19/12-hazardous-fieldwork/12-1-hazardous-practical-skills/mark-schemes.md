> **[2 marks]**

> **Alternative content**

## Q11 — easy — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 1() — 2 marks
One risk is heat exhaustion caused by working outdoors during a heatwave. **[1] **If a student became severely overheated while collecting data, they could have collapsed or lost consciousness, requiring emergency medical assistance. **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> The command words '**identify**' and '**explain**' together mean you must name the risk and then state what harm it could cause. Do not describe how the risk was managed.
> 
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a** point-marked** approach.
> 
> - **One mark** for identifying a relevant health and safety risk
> - **One mark** for explaining the impact of that risk, not the management of it
> 
> **[2 marks]**
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Alternative information that could be provided in the answer includes:
> 
> - Dehydration caused by working in extreme heat
> - Traffic hazard when crossing roads or recording data on busy urban streets
> - Sunburn from prolonged exposure to direct sunlight
> - Tripping on uneven pavements or kerbs while moving between sites
> - Any other appropriate response

> **[exam-tip]**
> **A risk** is something that could go wrong. **Management **is how you tried to prevent it.
> 
> **This** question only wants the risk and its impact. Imagine the worst realistic outcome and describe it clearly.
> 
> **Do not **describe how you stayed safe.

### 8() — 6 marks
Accept for up to max marks answers defining each ofthe three methods.

Systematic sampling = sample taken in regular way (1)e.g. every tenth person; every 100 metres ** (1)**...

Random sampling = sample taken based on equalchance of inclusion for all (1) e.g. using randomnumbers table; drawn from a hat** (1)**….

Stratified sampling = samples are selected according to a known characteristic ** (1)** e.g. age-distribution ofpopulation in an opinions survey; sites reflecting rocktype in vegetation survey ** (1)**….

For responses focussing directly on differences mark on a similar points basis.
