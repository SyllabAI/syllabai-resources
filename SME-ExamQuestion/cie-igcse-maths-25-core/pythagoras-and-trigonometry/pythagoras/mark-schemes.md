### Q1
The flagpole, the ground, and the guy rope form a right-angled triangle

- so you can use Pythagoras' theorem

Use the Pythagoras formula $a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$

- with $a=3.2$ and $c=6.9$

  - Remember that $c$ must be the hypotenuse (longest side) of the triangle
- Then $b$ will be the height of the flagpole

$3.2^{2}+b^{2}=6.9^{2}$

**[M1]**

Subtract $3.2^{2}$ from both sides, to get $b^{2}$ on its own

$b^{2}=6.9^{2}-3.2^{2}$

**[M1]**

Take the square root of both sides

- This will get rid of the 'squared' on the left-hand side

$b=\sqrt{6.9^{2}-3.2^{2}}$

- Use your calculator to work out that value

$b=6.113100...$

Round to a suitable degree of accuracy

- In general you should round to 3 significant figures, unless the question specifies otherwise
- The fourth significant figure is a '3'

  - which tells you not to change the third significant figure '1'

**Final answer:** **6.11 m (3 s.f.)**
