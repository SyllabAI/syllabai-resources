### Q1
> **[exam-tip]**
> The formula for the area, $A$, of a circle is given on the list of formulas in the exam paper:
> 
> $A=πr^{2}$
> 
> - where $r$ is the radius

Here you know the area, and you want to know the radius

Start by putting the given area into the formula in place of $A$

$76.4=πr^{2}$

Now you need to solve that for $r$

- First divide both sides by $π$, to get rid of the $π$ on the right-hand side

$\frac{76.4}{π}=r^{2}$

**[M1]**

- You can use your calculator to work out the value of that

$r^{2}=24.318875...$

- To find the value of $r$, take the square root of that

  - Use your calculator's memory, or the Ans key, to make sure you are taking the square root of that exact value

$r=\sqrt{24.318875...}=4.931417...$

Round to a suitable degree of accuracy

- In general you should round to 3 significant figures, unless the question specifies otherwise
- The fourth significant figure is a '1'

  - which tells you not to change the third significant figure '3'

**Final answer:** **4.93 cm (3 s.f.)**

**[A1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **M1**: For $r^{2}=\frac{76.4}{π}$ or a correct subsequent step in the solution process.
> 
> **A1**: For 4.93 or 4.931 to 4.932.
