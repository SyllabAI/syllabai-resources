# Mark Schemes — Properties, Uses & Alloys of Metals
**9. Metals** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (1 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) Both aluminium and copper have high electrical conductivity **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Aluminium has low density / is lightweight **[1 mark]**
> - (iii) Some copper compounds are toxic **[1 mark]**
> 
> Accept aluminium is preferred because it does not corrode

> **[exam-tip]**
> Use the data given in the table directly — examiners want you to link the property (density, conductivity, toxicity) explicitly to the use, not just restate the use.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) A mixture of a metal with one or more other elements **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Steel contains particles of different sizes **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Which stop layers of atoms from sliding over each other **[1 mark]**
> - (iii) Stainless steel is hard / resistant to rusting **[1 mark]**
> 
> Accept stainless steel does not corrode

> **[exam-tip]**
> The "different-sized particles disrupt the layers" explanation applies to any alloy being harder than the pure metal — learn this reasoning once and reuse it for any alloy question.
