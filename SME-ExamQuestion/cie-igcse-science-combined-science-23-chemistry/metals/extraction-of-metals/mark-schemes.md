> **[exam-tip]**
> Aluminium is the odd one out here — it's more reactive than carbon so must be extracted by electrolysis, while copper and iron (less reactive than carbon) are extracted by reduction with carbon.

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Aluminium is more reactive than carbon, so it cannot be extracted by reduction with carbon
- Instead, aluminium is extracted from its ore, bauxite, by electrolysis

**B** is incorrect because iron is extracted by reduction with carbon, not by electrolysis

**C** is incorrect because iron is extracted by reduction, not oxidation, of iron(III) oxide

**D** is incorrect because the higher a metal is in the reactivity series, the more difficult, not easier, it is to extract
