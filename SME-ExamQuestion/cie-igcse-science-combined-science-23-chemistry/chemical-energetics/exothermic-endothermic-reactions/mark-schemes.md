> **[exam-tip]**
> On energy profile diagrams, activation energy is always measured from the reactants' energy level up to the peak — not from zero or from the products' level.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Temperature decreases, because the reaction is endothermic (takes in thermal energy; the products have more energy than the reactants) **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Link the direction of temperature change directly to the energy profile shape — if products are higher than reactants, energy is taken in from the surroundings, so temperature falls.
