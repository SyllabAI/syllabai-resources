### Q1
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- During condensation, a gas turns into a liquid
- Particles lose energy and move closer together as intermolecular forces draw them into the liquid state

**A** is incorrect because particles lose energy, not gain it, during condensation

**B** is incorrect because particles lose energy, not gain it, and move closer together, not further apart

**D** is incorrect because particles move closer together, not further apart, during condensation

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Charles's Law states that, at constant pressure, volume is directly proportional to temperature measured in kelvin
- If the temperature decreases, the volume must also decrease

**B** is incorrect because a temperature that stays the same would not cause the volume to change

**C** is incorrect because an increasing temperature causes the volume to increase, not stay the same

**D** is incorrect because an increasing temperature causes the volume to increase, not decrease
