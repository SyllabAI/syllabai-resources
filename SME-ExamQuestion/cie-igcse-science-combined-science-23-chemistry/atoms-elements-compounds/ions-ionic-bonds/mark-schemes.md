### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Ionic compounds contain ions that are free to move when dissolved in water, which allows them to conduct electricity
- In the solid state, the ions are held in fixed positions within the lattice and cannot move, so solid ionic compounds do not conduct electricity

**B** is incorrect because ionic compounds only conduct electricity when molten or dissolved, not when solid

**C** is incorrect because ionic compounds are formed when electrons are transferred, not shared

**D** is incorrect because ionic compounds have high melting points due to the strong electrostatic forces of attraction between ions
