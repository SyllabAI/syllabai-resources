### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- The calcium ion is Ca<sup>2+</sup> and the phosphate ion is PO<sub>4</sub><sup>3-</sup>
- Balancing the charges needs 3 × Ca<sup>2+</sup> (+6) and 2 × PO<sub>4</sub><sup>3-</sup> (−6), giving the formula Ca<sub>3</sub>(PO<sub>4</sub>)<sub>2</sub>

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- Magnesium reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid to produce magnesium chloride and hydrogen gas
- Dilute hydrochloric acid is aqueous, so the acid and the soluble salt product both take the aqueous state symbol, (aq)

Mg (s) + 2HCl (aq) → MgCl<sub>2</sub> (aq) + H<sub>2</sub> (g)

**A** is incorrect because it uses the state symbol (l) instead of (aq) for the acid and the salt

**C** is incorrect because it uses (l) instead of (aq) and unnecessarily doubles every coefficient in an already balanced equation

**D** is incorrect because it unnecessarily doubles every coefficient in an already balanced equation
