# Mark Schemes — Alkanes
**11. Organic Chemistry** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (1 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - CH<sub>4</sub>(g) + 2O<sub>2</sub>(g) → CO<sub>2</sub>(g) + 2H<sub>2</sub>O(l) **[1 mark]**
> - Correct balancing **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Always check the state symbols match room-temperature states: gases (g) for CH<sub>4</sub>/O<sub>2</sub>/CO<sub>2</sub>, and liquid (l) for water, since combustion products cool back to room temperature in these questions.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Any one from: low melting point / low boiling point / low electrical conductivity **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> Simple molecular substances like methane have weak intermolecular forces (not weak covalent bonds) — this is why they have low melting/boiling points, not because the covalent bonds themselves are weak.
