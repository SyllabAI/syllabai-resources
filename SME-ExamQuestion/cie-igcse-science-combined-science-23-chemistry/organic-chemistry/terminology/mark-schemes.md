# Mark Schemes — Terminology
**11. Organic Chemistry** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (1 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) Molecular formula of hydrocarbon A: C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub> **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Hydrocarbon A is saturated because all carbon–carbon bonds are single bonds **[1 mark]**
> - (iii) Test: add aqueous bromine; hydrocarbon A stays orange, hydrocarbon B decolourises/turns colourless **[2 marks]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> The bromine water test is the key way to distinguish saturated from unsaturated hydrocarbons — decolourisation only happens with a C=C double bond, so alkanes never decolourise bromine water.
