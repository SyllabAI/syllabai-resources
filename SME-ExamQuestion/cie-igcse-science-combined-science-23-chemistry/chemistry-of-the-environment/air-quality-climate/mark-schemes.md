# Mark Schemes — Air Quality & Climate
**10. Chemistry of the Environment** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE science (0653)


## Exam Questions (2 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Carbon monoxide and carbon particulates form by incomplete combustion **[1 mark]**
> - Carbon dioxide forms by complete combustion **[1 mark]**
> 
> Accept max 1 mark for identification of combustion alone

> **[exam-tip]**
> To earn both marks, link each named pollutant to the specific TYPE of combustion (complete vs incomplete) — just naming "combustion" without specifying which type only gets partial credit.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) The double bonds in CO<sub>2</sub> are not between two carbon atoms **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Any two from: reduce deforestation / decrease the use of fossil fuels / increase the use of renewable energy (wind, solar) **[2 marks]**
> 
> Accept reduce livestock farming; accept other suitable energy sources e.g. hydrogen

> **[exam-tip]**
> "Unsaturated" specifically refers to carbon-carbon double bonds — CO<sub>2</sub>'s C=O double bonds don't count, which is a common misconception worth double-checking.
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - (i) 2CO + 2NO → 2CO<sub>2</sub> + N<sub>2</sub> **[1 mark]**
> - (ii) Acid rain, because oxides of nitrogen are removed **[1 mark]**
> 
> Accept NO for oxides of nitrogen

> **[exam-tip]**
> Catalytic converters tackle two pollutants at once — carbon monoxide is oxidised while nitrogen oxides are reduced, cutting both toxic gas and acid rain risks.

### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is B**

- Particulates are fine solid particles produced by the incomplete combustion of fuels
- When inhaled, particulates can cause cancer and respiratory problems

**A** is incorrect because acid rain and global warming are caused by sulfur dioxide/nitrogen oxides and carbon dioxide/methane, not particulates

**C** is incorrect because global warming is caused by greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide, not particulates

**D** is incorrect because acid rain is caused by sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides, not particulates
