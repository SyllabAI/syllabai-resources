### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- Acidic oxides are formed when a non-metal element combines with oxygen; NO<sub>2</sub> is an example of an acidic oxide
- Basic oxides are formed when a metal element combines with oxygen; CaO is an example of a basic oxide

**B** is incorrect because CO<sub>2</sub> is also an acidic oxide, not a basic oxide

**C** is incorrect because both CuO and Na<sub>2</sub>O are basic oxides

**D** is incorrect because Li<sub>2</sub>O is a basic oxide and SiO<sub>2</sub> is an acidic oxide, so the columns are reversed

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- The reaction between a metal carbonate and an acid produces a salt, water and carbon dioxide
- Calcium carbonate reacts with hydrochloric acid to produce calcium chloride, water and carbon dioxide

CaCO<sub>3</sub> + 2HCl → CaCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O + CO<sub>2</sub>

**A** is incorrect because calcium reacting with an acid produces a salt and hydrogen only, not water or carbon dioxide

**B** is incorrect because calcium hydroxide reacting with an acid produces a salt and water only, not carbon dioxide

**D** is incorrect because calcium oxide reacting with an acid produces a salt and water only, not carbon dioxide
