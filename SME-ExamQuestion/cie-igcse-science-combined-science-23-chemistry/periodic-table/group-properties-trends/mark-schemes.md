### Q2
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- Helium and neon are noble gases, which exist as single atoms (monatomic), not as diatomic molecules
- Noble gases have no free electrons or ions, so they have very low electrical conductivities

**A** is incorrect because noble gases are monatomic, not diatomic, and have low, not high, thermal conductivities

**B** is incorrect because noble gases are monatomic, not diatomic

### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- Transition elements form coloured compounds, so statement 2 is correct
- Transition elements often act as catalysts, so statement 3 is correct
- Transition elements have high densities and high melting points, so statements 1 and 4 are incorrect

### Q4
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is D**

- Going down Group I, the atoms become larger and have a greater relative atomic mass, so the density of the elements increases
- Going down Group I, the outer electron becomes further from the nucleus and is more easily lost, so the reactivity of the elements increases

**A** is incorrect because both density and reactivity increase down the group, not decrease

**B** is incorrect because density increases down the group, not decreases

**C** is incorrect because reactivity increases down the group, not decreases
