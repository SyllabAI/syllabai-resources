### 2((e)) — 6 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> - Partners must share the profits/losses, so they must prepare the appropriation accounts to show the share of profit/loss.
> - Being partners, they may be entitled to interest on capital, drawings and interest on drawings, salaries etc so they must prepare the current account to show what business owes to them and what they owe to business.
> - Being partners the drawings and profit share is not adjusted to the capital account so they must show the capital and current account separately in the statement of financial position.
>
