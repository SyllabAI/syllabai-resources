### Q3
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is A**

- The overall dipole moment of a molecule is the vector sum of its individual polar bond dipoles.
- Carbon-chlorine (C-Cl) bonds are highly polar because chlorine is significantly more electronegative than carbon.
- In 1,1-dichlorobut-1-ene, both C-Cl bonds are attached to the same sp<sup>2</sup> carbon atom on the double bond.
- The two C-Cl bond dipole vectors point in broadly the same direction, so they reinforce each other, producing the largest combined vector sum and the greatest overall dipole moment.

**B**, **C**, and **D** are incorrect as in these structural isomers the chlorine atoms are positioned at different points on the carbon skeleton. When the C-Cl bond dipole vectors point in opposing or diverging directions, they partially or fully cancel, resulting in a smaller overall dipole moment than in **A**.

### Q8

### Q9
**Final answer:** **The correct answer is C**

- CHCl<sub>3</sub>:

  - Chlorine is significantly more electronegative than carbon, creating polar C-Cl bonds.
  - The CHCl<sub>3</sub> molecule is asymmetrical.
  - Unlike CCl<sub>4</sub>, where four identical C-Cl bonds point symmetrically in all directions and cancel, CHCl<sub>3</sub> has three C-Cl bonds and one C-H bond.
  - The bond dipoles do not cancel, giving the molecule an overall permanent dipole.
  - CHCl<sub>3</sub> therefore experiences both permanent dipole-permanent dipole forces and instantaneous dipole-induced dipole forces.
- Br<sub>2</sub>:

  - Both bromine atoms are identical, so the Br-Br bond is non-polar.
  - The molecule has no permanent dipole.
  - Instantaneous dipole-induced dipole forces arise from the continuous random movement of electrons within the Br<sub>2</sub> electron cloud, creating temporary uneven charge distributions that induce dipoles in neighbouring molecules.

**A** is incorrect as it reverses the properties of the two molecules, suggesting CHCl<sub>3</sub> has no permanent dipole and Br<sub>2</sub> does.

**B** is incorrect as it states that neither molecule has a permanent dipole. CHCl<sub>3</sub> does have a permanent dipole due to its polar, asymmetric structure.

**D** is incorrect as it states that Br<sub>2</sub> has permanent dipole-permanent dipole forces. Br<sub>2</sub> is a homonuclear diatomic molecule with no permanent dipole.

> Ethoxyethane does not dissolve in water because:
> 
> - Hydrogen bonds exist between H<sub>2</sub>O molecules **[1 mark]**
> - Ethoxyethane has no O-H bonds and cannot form hydrogen bonds with water / the interactions between ethoxyethane and water are insufficient to compensate for breaking water-water hydrogen bonds **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> To dissolve in water, hydrogen bonds between water molecules must be broken and new intermolecular interactions must form between the solute and water. If these new interactions are not strong enough to compensate, the substance is immiscible.
> 
> Ethoxyethane has a lone pair on oxygen, so it can accept a hydrogen bond from water. However, it has no O-H bond, so it cannot donate hydrogen bonds. The large non-polar C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub> groups also disrupt the water hydrogen bonding network. The net interaction between ethoxyethane and water is insufficient to overcome the extensive water-water hydrogen bonding.
