**4** You are Dael, the experienced sailor who accompanied Andy and Abi on their voyage. Write a letter to a friend describing the journey.

In your letter, you should:

- describe the highlights of the voyage
- explain how well suited Andy, Abi and the boat Josephine were to the journey
- explain what they hoped to achieve and what they will have learned from the experience

Write the words of the letter.

Base your letter on what you have read in Text C, but be careful to **use your own words**. Address each of the three bullet points.

Write about 250 to 300 words.

**Up to 10 marks are available for reading and up to 10 marks for writing.**
