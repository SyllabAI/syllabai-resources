### Q2
Overall, the pyramid has a similar shape for males and females  **[1 mark]**. There are more males than females in every age group from 0-4 to 45-49, for example 4.4% males compared with 4.1% females aged 5-9  **[1 mark]**. Over the age of 55, there are fewer males than females, for example 0.8% males compared with 1.0% females aged 75-79  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - **One mark** for an overall comparison
> - **One mark** each for any two further comparisons, to a maximum of 3 marks
> - Every point must compare males with females
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - The 50-54 age group has the same percentage of males and females (2.8%)
> - More males are born than females, shown by the wider male bar for 0-4
> - Any other suitable response
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Quoting figures from the pyramid without saying which is bigger or smaller

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Use comparative words such as 'more than', 'fewer than' or 'the same as' in every sentence, and back each one with a pair of figures.
