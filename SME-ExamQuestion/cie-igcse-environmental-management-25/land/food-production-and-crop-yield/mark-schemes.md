# Mark Schemes — Food Production & Crop Yield
**Land** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (2 questions)


### Q1
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Any **four** from:
> 
> - It reduces the growth of weeds, so there is less competition with the crop
> - It reduces evaporation, so the soil keeps more moisture
> - It reduces surface run-off
> - It reduces soil erosion
> - It adds organic nutrients to the soil as it breaks down
> - It improves the soil, for example its drainage
> - It reduces damage from pests
> - It protects roots from extreme heat or cold
> 
> **[4 marks]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Each way must be a different idea. Link each one to the crop, for example 'reduces evaporation, so the soil keeps more water for the crops'.
