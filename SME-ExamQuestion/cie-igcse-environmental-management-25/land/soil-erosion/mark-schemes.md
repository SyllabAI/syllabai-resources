# Mark Schemes — Soil Erosion
**Land** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (2 questions)


### Q1
Bunds slow down surface run-off and allow more water to infiltrate, so less soil is washed away  **[1 mark]**. They also reduce the wind speed across the field, so less soil is blown away  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for each way bunds reduce soil erosion, with the reason, to a maximum of 2 marks
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> - Bunds trap soil that is washed downslope
> - Any other suitable response

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Naming the bund is not enough. Explain what it does to the water or the wind and how this keeps the soil in place.

### Q2
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Answers about crop yield do not count, because the question has already given that impact.
> 
> Any **three** from:
> 
> - Silting of rivers
> - Desertification
> - Mass movement such as landslides or mudslides
> - Loss of habitats
> - Loss of biodiversity
> - Displacement of people
> - Malnutrition or famine
> 
> **[3 marks]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Read the stem carefully. Anything about lower crop yield repeats the question and scores nothing.
