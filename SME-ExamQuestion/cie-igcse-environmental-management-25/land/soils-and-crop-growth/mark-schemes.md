### Q1
The gases in soil include oxygen  **[1 mark]**. Plant roots need oxygen for aerobic respiration  **[1 mark]**, which releases the energy the roots use to grow and take up mineral ions  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - One mark for each valid point
> - Maximum 3 marks
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Writing 'anaerobic respiration', which does not need oxygen

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Build a chain: oxygen, then respiration, then energy, then what the energy is used for. Each link can earn a mark.

### Q6
Highest mean monthly temperature = 17 °C and lowest = -7 °C  **[1 mark]**

Range = 17 - (-7) = **24 °C**  **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Subtracting a negative number is the same as adding it, so the range is 17 + 7. A common wrong answer is 10 °C (17 - 7).
