# Mark Schemes — Water Sources & Supply
**Water** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (6 questions)


### Q1
Desalination increases the amount of fresh water available  **[1 mark]** because it uses sea water, which is not drinkable  **[1 mark]**. It can supply water for irrigating crops in dry areas  **[1 mark]**.

However, desalination plants are expensive to build, so the water costs more per litre than water from rivers or aquifers  **[1 mark]**. They also use large amounts of energy; if this comes from fossil fuels, it adds to carbon dioxide emissions and climate change  **[1 mark]**. They produce salty brine, which can harm marine ecosystems when it is released back into the sea  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - **One mark** for each valid benefit or limitation, to a maximum of 6 marks
> - A maximum of **5 marks** for benefits and a maximum of **5 marks** for limitations, so you must cover both sides to gain full marks
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - Desalination produces very pure water
> - It removes bacteria and other pathogens
> - It can reduce the risk of conflict over water supplies
> - Landlocked countries cannot use it
> - Some people are reluctant to drink desalinated water because of its taste
> - Any other suitable response
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Writing 'it is expensive' without saying why, for example the cost of building the plant or the cost per litre
> - Mentioning climate change without linking it to the energy the plant uses
> - Writing only benefits or only limitations

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Plan for at least two points on each side before you start writing.
> - Make every limitation specific. Say what is expensive and why, and link any climate change point to the energy the plant uses.

### Q5
Planting trees increases interception  **[1 mark]** because there are more leaves to catch the rain before it reaches the ground  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for the effect: interception increases
> - **One mark** for a reason linked to the effect, for example more leaves and branches to catch rain

> **[exam-tip]**
> - The question asks for an effect and a reason, so give both. Start with the direction of change (increases), then explain it.
