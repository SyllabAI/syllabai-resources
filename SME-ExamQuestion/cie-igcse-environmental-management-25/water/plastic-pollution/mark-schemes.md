# Mark Schemes — Plastic Pollution
**Water** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (6 questions)


### Q1
Animals such as turtles and seabirds become entangled in plastic  **[1 mark]**, so they cannot swim or feed properly  **[1 mark]**. Animals mistake floating plastic for food  **[1 mark]**, which blocks their digestive system and can cause starvation  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - One mark for each valid point
> - Maximum 4 marks
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - Floating plastic and litter on beaches cause visual pollution
> - People may stop using polluted beaches and coastal waters
> - Any other suitable response
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Repeating bioaccumulation or biomagnification, which the question has already given
> - Writing 'it causes pollution' without saying what the plastic does

> **[exam-tip]**
> - For a four-mark 'describe', give an impact and then say what it does to the animal. Each impact plus its effect can earn two marks.

### Q6
Draw a stacked bar for 2025. The biodegradable section goes from 0 to 2400 thousand tonnes, and the non-biodegradable section sits on top of it, from 2400 to 4900 thousand tonnes  **[1 mark]**.

Shade each section to match the key and draw the bar the same width as the 2021 and 2023 bars  **[1 mark]**.

> **[exam-tip]**
> - This is a stacked bar chart, so the top of the bar shows the total: 2400 + 2500 = 4900. Plotting the non-biodegradable section from 0 to 2500 is a common mistake.
