### Q2
About 10% of the energy passes from one trophic level to the next.

Water hyacinth plant: 100 × 10 = **1000 units**  **[1 mark]**

Egret: 100 ÷ 10 = **10 units** and eagle: 10 ÷ 10 = **1 unit**  **[1 mark]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Work backwards to the producer by multiplying by 10, and forwards to the consumers by dividing by 10.
