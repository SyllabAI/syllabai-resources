# Mark Schemes — Formation of Rocks
**Natural Resources** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (5 questions)


### Q1
Permeable: **sandstone**  **[1 mark]**

Impermeable: **shale**  **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for a named permeable sedimentary rock (sandstone or limestone)
> - **One mark** for a named impermeable sedimentary rock (shale or clay)

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Both answers must be sedimentary rocks. Granite is igneous and slate is metamorphic, so neither scores here even though they are impermeable.

### Q2
Permeability is the ability of water to pass through a rock or soil  **[1 mark]**, by moving through the pore spaces between its particles  **[1 mark]**.

> **[exam-tip]**
> - A two-mark definition needs two parts: what moves (water) and how it moves (through the pore spaces). Saying only 'lets water through' earns one mark at most.

### Q3
- Cementation to 'sediments are joined together by minerals' **[1 mark]**
- Weathering to 'break down of rocks into smaller pieces on the surface of the Earth'  **[1 mark]**
- Erosion to 'rock particles are transported by gravity, water, wind and ice'  **[1 mark]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for each correct line
> - Draw only one line from each term: an extra line from a term loses the mark for that term

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Two descriptions are left over on purpose. 'Pressed together and water is removed' describes compaction, and 'deposited in layers' describes deposition, so do not join them to any term here.
