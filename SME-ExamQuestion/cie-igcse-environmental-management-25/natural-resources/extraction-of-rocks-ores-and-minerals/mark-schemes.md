# Mark Schemes — Extraction of Rocks, Ores & Minerals
**Natural Resources** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (4 questions)


### Q1
| statement | true | false |
|---|---|---|
| Calcium carbonate is the raw material for manufacturing cement. | ✓ |   |
| Calcium carbonate is also known as lime. |   | ✓ |
| Calcium oxide is also a product of manufacturing cement. | ✓ |   |
| Calcium oxide is also known as limestone. |   | ✓ |

**[2 marks]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **2 marks** for all four rows correct
> - **1 mark** for three rows correct

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Limestone is calcium carbonate. Heating it makes calcium oxide, which is called lime.

### Q2
Environmental impact: a large area of land has been cleared, so habitats have been lost  **[1 mark]**.

Economic impact: the factory provides jobs for local people  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for an environmental impact other than carbon dioxide
> - **One mark** for an economic impact
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - Environmental: loss of biodiversity, as the ground around the site has little vegetation
> - Environmental: air pollution from dust and gases released by the chimneys
> - Environmental: noise pollution from machinery and from lorries travelling to and from the site
> - Environmental: visual pollution, as the large buildings and silos spoil the landscape
> - Economic: jobs in transport, such as driving the tanker lorries
> - Economic: more money spent in the local economy
> - Economic: improved infrastructure, such as roads to the factory
> - Any other suitable response
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Repeating carbon dioxide, which the question has already given
> - Writing 'pollution' without saying what type of pollution

> **[exam-tip]**
> - The question says 'use information in Figure 5.1', so link each impact to something you can see in the photograph, such as the chimneys, the lorries or the cleared ground.
> - Give one impact of each type and label them clearly, so the examiner can see which is environmental and which is economic.

### Q3

> - The plants take a long time to grow
> - Technology is needed to separate the minerals from the ash

### Q4
This process is called phytomining  **[1 mark]**. The plants grow in soil that contains the minerals and absorb the mineral ions through their roots  **[1 mark]**. The plants are then harvested and burned, and the minerals are extracted from the ash  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - One mark for each valid point
> - Maximum 3 marks
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - The minerals become concentrated in the ash, which makes them easier to extract
> - Any other suitable response

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Describe the steps in order: grow, absorb, harvest, burn, extract from the ash. A clear sequence makes it easy for the examiner to tick each step.
