# Mark Schemes — Energy Resources
**Natural Resources** · Cambridge (CIE) IGCSE environmental-management (0680)


## Exam Questions (4 questions)


### Q1
Demand for energy will increase in hot places because more electricity is needed to run air conditioning and cooling systems  **[1 mark]**.

Demand for energy will decrease in cooler regions because less energy is needed to heat homes and workplaces  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> - **One mark** for each way, which must give both the direction of the change in demand and the reason
> 
> **Alternative content**
> 
> The answer above is just one example of how to respond to this question. Other information that could be used includes:
> 
> - Demand will increase because more energy is needed to refrigerate food
> - Any other suitable response

> **[exam-tip]**
> - For each way, say whether demand goes up or down and then give the reason. A reason with no direction, or a direction with no reason, does not score.

### Q2
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Any **three** from:
> 
> - Large areas of land are needed to grow the fuel crops, so electricity production depends on crop yield
> - Less land is available for growing food crops
> - Forests may be cleared to grow crops for bioethanol
> - Bioethanol has a low energy content
> - Burning bioethanol releases carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas
> - It needs specialised infrastructure
> - Bioethanol is highly flammable and difficult to store safely
> 
> **[3 marks]**

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Make each limitation a different idea. Two points about land use only count once unless one is about land area and the other is about food crops.

### Q3
The bioethanol is burned  **[1 mark]**. The heat boils water to make steam  **[1 mark]**. The steam turns a turbine, which turns a generator to produce electricity  **[1 mark]**.

> **[mark-scheme]**
> **Mark allocation**
> 
> This answer is assessed using a point-marked approach.
> 
> - One mark for each valid point
> - Maximum 3 marks
> - The turbine turning and the generator turning can each earn a separate mark
> 
> **Common errors**
> 
> - Stopping at the turbine and not saying that the turbine turns a generator

> **[exam-tip]**
> - Finish the chain: fuel, heat, steam, turbine, generator. The generator is the part that produces the electricity.
