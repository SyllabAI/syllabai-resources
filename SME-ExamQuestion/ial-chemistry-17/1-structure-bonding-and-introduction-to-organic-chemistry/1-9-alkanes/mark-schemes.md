  - *Write the overall reaction equation twice (in pencil) 1) C*<sub>*7*</sub>*H*<sub>*16*</sub>* + Cl*<sub>*2*</sub>* → C*<sub>*7*</sub>*H*<sub>*15*</sub>*Cl + HCl 2) C*<sub>*7*</sub>*H*<sub>*16*</sub>* + Cl*<sub>*2*</sub>* → C*<sub>*7*</sub>*H*<sub>*15*</sub>*Cl + HCl*
