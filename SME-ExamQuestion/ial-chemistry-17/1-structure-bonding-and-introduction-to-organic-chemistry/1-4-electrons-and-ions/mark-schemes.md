### 6() — 1 marks
**Correct answer: C** — d

**Final answer:** **The correct answer is ****C**** because:**

- If an ion of **M** has a 3+ charge and 18 electrons, this means it has lost 3 electrons, so an atom of **M** must have 3 + 18 = 21 electrons
- Elements can form stable 3+ ions if they are either in Group 3 (i.e. the p block) or they are a transition element (i.e. in block d)
- No p block elements have 21 electrons, so it must be from the d block

**A** is incorrect as the s block elements don’t form stable 3+ ions
