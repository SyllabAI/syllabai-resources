The molecular formula of the alkene with a molar mass of 112 g mol<sup>−1</sup> is:

**[Total: 1 mark]**

- *The general formula of an alkene is C*<sub>*n*</sub>*H*<sub>*2n*</sub>* *
- *There are twice as many hydrogen atoms as there are carbon atoms, giving the empirical formula of an alkene as CH*<sub>*2*</sub>
- *The molecular formula can be found by dividing the relative formula mass of the molecular formula by the relative formula mass of the empirical formula*
- *The** M*<sub>*r*</sub>* of the empirical formula = 12.0 + (2 x 1.0) = 14.0 g mol*<sup>*-1*</sup>

  - *You can use this piece of information to determine the molecular formula *$\frac{112}{14}$*= 8*
  - *So, there are 8 CH*<sub>*2*</sub>* units in the alkene with a molar mass of 112 g mol*<sup>*−1*</sup>* *
  - *This means that it contains 8 carbon atoms and 16 hydrogen atoms, i.e. C*<sub>*8*</sub>*H*<sub>*16*</sub>* *
