### Q16
Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how sensitive the quantity demanded of a product is to a change in its price **[1]**. > 
> - Valid AO1 is awarded 1 mark

> **Indicative content**
> 
> AO1
> 
> - Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how much the quantity demanded of a product changes in response to a change in price
> - If demand is price elastic (PED > 1), a given percentage change in price leads to a larger percentage change in quantity demanded; if demand is price inelastic (PED < 1), the change in quantity demanded is smaller than the change in price
> 
> AO2
> 
> - If a business knows its product has price inelastic demand - for example, because it is unique or has few close substitutes - it can raise the price knowing that the percentage fall in demand will be smaller than the percentage increase in price.
