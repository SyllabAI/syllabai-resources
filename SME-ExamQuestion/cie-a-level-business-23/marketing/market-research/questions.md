### Q1 — medium — 8 marks

**Q1** [8 marks]

> **[case-study]**
> **Selkirk**
> 
> Selkirk hires cars in country G and is deciding whether to open a branch at a new airport terminal. Government tourism statistics give passenger numbers at the airport, forecast passenger growth, and average visitor length of stay. Industry reports list the car hire firms already operating there.
> 
> Selkirk has three months to decide before the retail units at the terminal are allocated.

Analyse **two** benefits to Selkirk of using secondary research when deciding whether to open a branch at the new airport terminal.

### Q6 — hard — 12 marks

**Q6** [12 marks]

> **[case-study]**
> **Stonebridge Fitness**
> 
> Stonebridge Fitness operates fitness studios in country B and is considering entering a new region. Research shows the fitness market in that region grew by 14% last year and is forecast to grow by 9% next year.
> 
> Three national chains already operate in the region and hold 71% of the market between them; two have opened new sites in the past year. ### Q8 — hard — 12 marks

**Q8** [12 marks]

> **[case-study]**
> **Redwing**
> 
> Redwing manufactures sports footwear in country F. It commissioned an agency to research a new running shoe. The agency surveyed 1 200 people at three city sports events. 74% rated the shoe 'good' or 'excellent' and 61% said they would pay \$85 for it. Redwing is considering investing \$600 000 in production.
>

Evaluate the extent to which Redwing should rely on its market research findings when deciding whether to invest \$600 000 in the new running shoe.

### Q10 — hard — 12 marks

**Q10** [12 marks]

>
