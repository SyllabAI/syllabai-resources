### Q1

This gives an employer a clear, consistent set of criteria to compare every candidate against, which makes it easier to spot the most suitable person for the role **[1]**. It also means the employer is less likely to rely on personal opinion or guesswork when choosing between applicants, reducing the risk of hiring the wrong person **[1]**

> **[mark-scheme]**
> Explain requires a clear statement of meaning (AO1) supported by contextual development (AO2)
> 
> - Valid AO1 is awarded up to 1 mark
> - Valid AO2 is awarded up to 2 marks
> 
> **Indicative content**
> 
> AO1
> 
> - A person specification sets out the skills, qualifications, experience, and personal qualities required for a role, distinguishing between essential and desirable attributes
> - It informs potential applicants about the type of person the business is looking for, helping them to self-select before applying.
> 
> AO2
> 
> - By making the requirements of the role clear, unsuitable candidates are less likely to apply, reducing the volume of applications the employer must review and shortening the recruitment process — saving both time and money
> - Having a clear list of essential and desirable criteria allows the employer to assess all candidates consistently and objectively, making it easier to identify the most suitable person for the role and reducing the risk of a poor hiring decision
> - A well-written person specification can also attract higher-quality applicants, as candidates who closely match the criteria are more likely to apply, improving the overall standard of the candidate pool
