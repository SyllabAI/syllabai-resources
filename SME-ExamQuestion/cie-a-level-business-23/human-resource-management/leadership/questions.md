### Q1 — medium — 8 marks

**Q1** [8 marks]

> **[case-study]**
> **Kestrel Rail (KR)**
> 
> Kestrel Rail (KR) operates regional train services in country B, employing 2,100 people including drivers, conductors and engineers.
> 
> KR's new operations director, Tomas, was appointed from outside the rail industry. He has the authority to change working practices and has issued an instruction changing how shifts are allocated.
> 
> Drivers are represented by a trade union that has recommended its members do not cooperate. Tomas has met the union twice but has not changed his instruction.
> 
> **Table 1.1: KR selected data**
> 
> | Employees | 2,100 |
> |---|---|
> | Drivers | 540 |
> | Drivers accepting the new shift arrangements (%) | 22 |
> | Trains cancelled last month because of staffing gaps | 148 |

Analyse **two** limitations of Tomas's power to influence KR's drivers.

### Q6 — easy — 3 marks
