### Q5
Depreciation spreads the cost of a non-current asset across the years in which the business uses it **[1]**. Without it, the full cost of a machine expected to last eight years would be charged to the year it was bought, making that year's profit look artificially low and the following seven look artificially high **[1]**. ### Q16
