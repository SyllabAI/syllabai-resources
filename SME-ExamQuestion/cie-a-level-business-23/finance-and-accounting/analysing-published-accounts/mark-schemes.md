### Q4
Financial efficiency measures how well a business manages the working capital tied up in its inventory, its customers and its suppliers **[1]**. It matters because a business can be profitable and still run out of cash: money sitting in unsold stock, or in invoices customers have not yet paid, cannot be used to pay wages or suppliers **[1]**. Improving these ratios releases cash the business already owns, which is cheaper than borrowing and avoids the interest costs that would otherwise reduce profit **[1]**
