# Mark Schemes — Impact of the Internet on Organisations & Society
**3. Operating Online** · Edexcel IGCSE ICT (4IT1)


## Q1 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 5((a)) — 2 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Any two from:
> 
> - Poverty / cost
> - Social restrictions e.g. lack of education
> - Political restrictions e.g. government regulation
> - Lack of infrastructure e.g. network/power (on a national/regional scale)
> - Cultural restrictions e.g. religion

## Q3 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 3((g)) — 2 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> An explanation to include two from:
> 
> - Affordability (1) because of economic position of certain countries (1)
> - Lack of infrastructure (1) because of geographical restrictions (1)
> - No legal/ethical right to access (1) because of social/political/religious restrictions (1)

## Q4 — medium — 4 marks · exam-questions

### 5((a)) — 4 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Two explanations, each to include **two** linked points such as:
> 
> - Improved communication (1) because people can talk to each other using a range of tools / quicker to send messages / messaging can be grouped to allow teams to work more effectively (1)
> - Access to global markets and workforce (1) because people can access/work in their own language / culture / time zones (1)
> - Information can be better managed and used (1) because people can collaborate more easily / data can be organised and searched/filtered (1)
