## Q5 — medium — 8 marks · exam-questions

### 4((d)) — 8 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Legal implications for venue. Privacy & Data Protection.
> 
> - Provides legal grounds if needing to prove damage/misuse etc.
> - Places legal responsibility on venue.
> - Policies may be required for staff and customers
> - Training and/or specialist staff required
> - Data management may need to be evidenced
> - Possible fines / legal action for breaches
> 
> Ethical implications for venue and visitors:
> 
> - Trust vs protection.
> - Civil liberties vs individual freedom.
> - Nothing to hide argument.

## Q10 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 5((e)) — 2 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> A linked explanation such as:
> 
> - Limit how much information you put online / use privacy settings (1) because then other users cannot see your location / use information maliciously (1)
> - Block malicious users (1) because then they cannot access your/your friends’ information (1)
> - Report malicious users (1) because this protects others from risk (1)
> - Install firewall / anti-malware / use secure (https) sites / VPN (1) because it protects access to personal information (1)
> - Do not make friends with strangers (1) because people can misrepresent who they are online (1)
> 
> **Do not accept**: responses related to safety of the devices
