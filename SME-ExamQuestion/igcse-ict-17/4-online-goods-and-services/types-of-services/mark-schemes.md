# Mark Schemes — Types of Services
**4. Online Goods & Services** · Edexcel IGCSE ICT (4IT1)


## Q1 — medium — 2 marks · exam-questions

### 1((c)) — 2 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> A description such as:
> 
> - Downloading/uploading only a part of the audio (1) so that it can be played without having to wait for the download/upload to finish (1)
> - Video/sound/media that is downloaded/shared/buffered (1) while being played/watched (1)
> 
> Example of a common response that gains two marks:
> “The ability to play a file without it being fully downloaded”

## Q2 — medium — 3 marks · exam-questions

### 1((b)) — 2 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> An explanation such as:
> 
> - Saves time / quicker (1) because there is no need to travel to the venue (1) 
> or
> - More convenient (1) because an online booking system is always available (1)

### 1((h)) — 1 marks
> **[mark-scheme]**
> Any one from:
> 
> - Placing bids
> - Search
> - Timing auctions
> - Listing types
