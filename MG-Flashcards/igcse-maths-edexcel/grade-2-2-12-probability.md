# Probability Flashcards

_Lane: `igcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 2 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What are the numerical values for impossible and certain events on the probability scale?

**Back:** Impossible: 0Certain: 1

**Choices:**

- Impossible: 0Certain: 1 ✔
  - _why:_ Probabilities range from 0 to 1. An impossible event has no chance of occurring, so its probability is 0, while a certain event has probability 1.
- Impossible: 1Certain: 0
  - _why:_ This reverses the two endpoints. Probability 0 represents no chance of an event occurring, while probability 1 means the event is guaranteed to occur.
- Impossible: -1Certain: 1
  - _why:_ This incorrectly extends the probability scale to negative values. The scale runs from 0 to 1, with impossible at 0 and certain at 1.
- Impossible: 0Certain: 100
  - _why:_ This confuses probabilities with percentages. Certainty can be written as 100%, but on the numerical probability scale it is 1; impossibility is 0.
