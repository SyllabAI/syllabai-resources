# Angles in Polygons Flashcards

_Lane: `igcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 4 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for the sum of interior angles in a polygon with $n$ sides.

**Back:** $$(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$

**Choices:**

- $$(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$ ✔
  - _why:_ A polygon can be divided into $n - 2$ triangles from one vertex. Each triangle has angle sum $180^\circ$, so multiplying gives the total interior angle sum.
- $$(n - 1) \times 180^\circ$
  - _why:_ This counts one triangle too many when dividing the polygon from a single vertex. The correct relationship uses $n - 2$ triangles, each contributing $180^\circ$.
- $$n \times 180^\circ$
  - _why:_ Multiplying the number of sides directly by $180^\circ$ ignores the two fewer triangles formed inside the polygon. The correct formula is based on $n - 2$ triangles.
- $$(n - 2) \times 360^\circ$
  - _why:_ The triangle angle sum has been incorrectly replaced by a full turn. Each of the $n - 2$ triangles contributes $180^\circ$, not $360^\circ$.
