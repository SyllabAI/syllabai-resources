## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** Which axis on a coordinate grid represents the horizontal value?

**Back:** The x-axis.

**Choices:**

- The x-axis. ✔
  - _why:_ The x-axis runs horizontally across a coordinate grid, so a point’s horizontal coordinate is its x-value. The y-axis represents the vertical value.
- The y-axis.
  - _why:_ The y-axis runs vertically and represents a point’s vertical coordinate. The horizontal value is represented by the x-axis.
- The origin.
  - _why:_ The origin is the point where the two axes intersect, usually at $(0,0)$; it is not an axis. The x-axis represents the horizontal value.
- The z-axis.
  - _why:_ The z-axis is used for depth in a three-dimensional coordinate system, not the horizontal value on a two-dimensional grid. The horizontal axis is the x-axis.
