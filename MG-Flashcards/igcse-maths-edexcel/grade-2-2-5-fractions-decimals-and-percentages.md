## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** In the fraction $\frac{3}{5}$, which number is the denominator?

**Back:** 5

**Choices:**

- 5 ✔
  - _why:_ The denominator is the bottom number of a fraction and shows how many equal parts make one whole. In this fraction, the bottom number is 5.
- 3
  - _why:_ The number 3 is the numerator, which counts the selected parts. The denominator is the bottom number, so the correct answer is 5.
- 2
  - _why:_ Subtracting the numerator from the denominator gives 2, but subtraction is irrelevant here. The denominator is read directly from the bottom of the fraction, giving 5.
- 15
  - _why:_ Multiplying the numerator and denominator gives 15, but that does not identify either part of the fraction. The denominator is the bottom number, 5.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** What does the word percent literally mean?

**Back:** Out of 100

**Choices:**

- Out of 100 ✔
  - _why:_ The word “percent” comes from the Latin meaning “per hundred”. A percentage therefore describes a quantity as a number of parts out of 100.
- Out of 10
  - _why:_ This confuses percentages with tenths. “Percent” means per hundred, so a percentage expresses a number of parts out of 100.
- Out of 1
  - _why:_ A proportion may be expressed out of one, but that is not the literal meaning of “percent”. The word means out of 100.
- Out of 1000
  - _why:_ This would describe parts per thousand rather than percent. The prefix and root in “percent” indicate that the quantity is measured out of 100.

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** How is a percentage converted into a decimal?

**Back:** Divide by 100

**Choices:**

- Divide by 100 ✔
  - _why:_ A percentage is a quantity out of 100, so dividing its numerical value by 100 converts it to an equivalent decimal.
- Multiply by 100
  - _why:_ Multiplying by 100 converts a decimal into a percentage, so this reverses the required conversion. To change a percentage into a decimal, divide by 100.
- Divide by 10
  - _why:_ Dividing by 10 moves the decimal point only one place left. Percentage-to-decimal conversion requires two places, which is equivalent to dividing by 100.
- Add 100
  - _why:_ Adding 100 changes the value rather than expressing the same proportion in decimal form. The percentage must instead be divided by 100.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is 0.06 smaller than 0.6?

**Back:** 0.06 is 6 hundredths, while 0.6 is 6 tenths.

**Choices:**

- 0.06 is 6 hundredths, while 0.6 is 6 tenths. ✔
  - _why:_ Hundredths are ten times smaller than tenths. Therefore 6 hundredths equals 0.06, whereas 6 tenths equals 0.6, making 0.06 smaller.
- 0.06 is 6 tenths, while 0.6 is 6 hundredths.
  - _why:_ This reverses the place values: 0.06 represents 6 hundredths and 0.6 represents 6 tenths. Since hundredths are smaller than tenths, 0.06 is smaller.
- 0.06 is 6 thousandths, while 0.6 is 6 hundredths.
  - _why:_ The decimal places have been shifted incorrectly. The 6 in 0.06 occupies the hundredths column, while the 6 in 0.6 occupies the tenths column.
- 0.06 is 6 hundredths, while 0.6 is 6 ones.
  - _why:_ The 6 in 0.6 is not in the ones column; it is in the tenths column. Meanwhile, 0.06 contains 6 hundredths, which is smaller.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Back:** 0.4

- 0.4 ✔
  - _why:_ Dividing the numerator by the denominator gives the decimal value: $2 \div 5 = 0.4$. Equivalently, multiplying both parts by 2 gives $\frac{4}{10}$.
- 0.25
  - _why:_ This results from reversing the decimal digits associated with the calculation. Dividing the numerator by the denominator correctly gives $2 \div 5 = 0.4$.
- 0.04
  - _why:_ This is ten times too small, as though the denominator were 50 rather than 5. The correct calculation is $2 \div 5 = 0.4$.
- 0.6
  - _why:_ This is the decimal equivalent of $\frac{3}{5}$, not $\frac{2}{5}$. Dividing 2 by 5 gives the correct value, 0.4.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** When is a fraction considered to be in its simplest form?

**Back:** When the numerator and denominator have no common factors except 1.

**Choices:**

- When the numerator and denominator have no common factors except 1. ✔
  - _why:_ With no common factor greater than 1, there is no number by which both parts can be divided, so the fraction cannot be simplified further.
- When the numerator is smaller than the denominator.
  - _why:_ A numerator smaller than the denominator makes a fraction proper, not necessarily simplified. A fraction is simplest when its numerator and denominator have no common factors except 1.
- When the numerator and denominator are both even numbers.
  - _why:_ If both numbers are even, they share a factor of 2 and the fraction can still be reduced. Simplest form requires no common factors except 1.
- When the numerator and denominator have no common multiples except 1.
  - _why:_ This confuses factors with multiples; numbers generally have infinitely many common multiples. Simplest form means the numerator and denominator have no common factors greater than 1.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** What is usually the easiest form to use when ordering mixed fractions, decimals, and percentages?

**Back:** Decimals

**Choices:**

- Decimals ✔
  - _why:_ Converting every value to a decimal gives them a common form. Their place values can then be aligned, making direct comparison and ordering straightforward.
- Fractions
  - _why:_ Fractions can be compared, but unlike denominators often require extra conversion or common denominators. Converting all the values to decimals usually makes their sizes easiest to compare.
- Percentages
  - _why:_ Percentages can be used for ordering, but mixed forms are usually quickest to compare after converting them to decimals, which align naturally by place value.
- Mixed numbers
  - _why:_ Mixed numbers are unsuitable for values originally given as percentages and decimals and often require extra conversion. Decimals usually provide the clearest common form for ordering.
