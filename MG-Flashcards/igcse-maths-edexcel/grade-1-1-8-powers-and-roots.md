## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** In the expression $a^n$, what are the names of $a$ and $n$?

**Back:** $a$ is the base$n$ is the index

**Choices:**

- $a$ is the base$n$ is the index ✔
  - _why:_ The base is the number or algebraic term being repeatedly multiplied, while the index tells you how many times the base is used as a factor.
- $a$ is the index$n$ is the base
  - _why:_ This reverses the two names. In $a^n$, $a$ is the base being raised to a power, and $n$ is the index showing the power.
- $a$ is the coefficient$n$ is the index
  - _why:_ A coefficient multiplies a variable, so $a$ is not a coefficient here. In $a^n$, $a$ is the base and $n$ is the index.
- $a$ is the base$n$ is the root
  - _why:_ A root is associated with operations such as square roots and cube roots, not the raised number here. In $a^n$, $a$ is the base and $n$ is the index.
