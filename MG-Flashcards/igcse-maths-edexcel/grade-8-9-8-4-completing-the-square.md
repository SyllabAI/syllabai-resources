# Completing the Square Flashcards

_Lane: `igcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 8 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you calculate the constant inside the square bracket when completing the square for $x^2 + bx$?

**Back:** Halve the coefficient $b$.

**Choices:**

- Halve the coefficient $b$. ✔
  - _why:_ Since $(x+a)^2=x^2+2ax+a^2$, matching the middle term gives $2a=b$, so the constant inside the bracket is $a=\frac{b}{2}$.
- Square the coefficient $b$.
  - _why:_ Squaring $b$ confuses the bracket constant with the constant generated when the bracket is expanded. The bracket contains $\frac{b}{2}$, found by halving $b$.
- Double the coefficient $b$.
  - _why:_ Doubling reverses the required operation. Expanding $(x+a)^2$ produces a middle coefficient of $2a$, so $a$ must be half of $b$.
- Negate the coefficient $b$.
  - _why:_ Changing the sign is used when solving an equation after factorisation, not when forming the completed square. The constant inside the bracket is found by halving $b$.
