# Area and Circumference of Circles Flashcards

_Lane: `igcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 3 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the mathematical name for a straight line from the centre to the edge of a circle?

**Back:** Radius

**Choices:**

- Radius ✔
  - _why:_ The radius is a straight line segment from the centre of a circle to any point on its circumference.
- Diameter
  - _why:_ The diameter passes through the centre and joins two points on the circumference. A line from the centre to one point on the circumference is the radius.
- Chord
  - _why:_ A chord joins two points on the circumference and does not have to pass through the centre. The required line from the centre to the edge is the radius.
- Tangent
  - _why:_ A tangent is a line that touches a circle at exactly one point. The straight line from the centre to the edge is called the radius.
