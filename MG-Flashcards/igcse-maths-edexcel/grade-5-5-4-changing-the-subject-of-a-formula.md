# Changing the Subject of a Formula Flashcards

_Lane: `igcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 5 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What defines the subject of a formula?

**Back:** The variable written on its own, usually on the left-hand side.

**Choices:**

- The variable written on its own, usually on the left-hand side. ✔
  - _why:_ The subject is the variable isolated on one side of the formula. By convention, it is usually written alone on the left-hand side.
- The variable written most often, usually on the right-hand side.
  - _why:_ Frequency does not determine the subject, and variables on the right usually form the expression. The subject is the variable written on its own, usually on the left.
- The variable with the highest power, usually on the left-hand side.
  - _why:_ The highest power matters when identifying a polynomial’s degree, not its subject. The subject is whichever variable has been isolated on its own, usually on the left.
- The variable being multiplied, usually on the right-hand side.
  - _why:_ Being part of a multiplication does not make a variable the subject. The subject must be isolated on its own and is usually placed on the left-hand side.
