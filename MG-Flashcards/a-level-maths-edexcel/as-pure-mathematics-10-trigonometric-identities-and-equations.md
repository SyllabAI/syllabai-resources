# Trigonometric Identities and Equations Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 22 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the period of the functions $y=\sin x$ and $y=\cos x$?

**Back:** $$360^\circ$

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the period of the function $y=\tan x$?

**Back:** $$180^\circ$

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** In which quadrants is cosine positive according to the CAST rule?

**Back:** Quadrant I (All)Quadrant IV (Cosine)

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** Define a reference angle in trigonometry.

**Back:** The acute angle made with the x-axis.

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** The identity linking sine and cosine is $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** The identity linking sine and cosine is $\boxed{\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x \equiv 1}$.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** How should you rearrange $\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x \equiv 1$ to substitute into an equation containing sine terms?

**Back:** $$\cos^2 x \equiv 1 - \sin^2 x$

## Card 7 — `cloze`

**Front:** The identity for tangent is $\text{[...]}$, provided that $\cos x \neq 0$.

**Back:** The identity for tangent is $\boxed{\tan x \equiv \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}}$, provided that $\cos x \neq 0$.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** The identity for tangent is $\tan x \equiv \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, provided that $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** The identity for tangent is $\tan x \equiv \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, provided that $\boxed{\cos x \neq 0}$.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** When solving $5\cos(x-35)=2$ for $0^\circ \leq x < 360^\circ$, how is the interval transformed for $u=x-35$?

**Back:** $$-35^\circ \leq u < 325^\circ$

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** Why must you reject a solution like $\sin x = 1.4$?

**Back:** Sine and cosine values must lie between -1 and 1.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the danger of dividing both sides of $3\tan x = 5\sin x$ by $\sin x$?

**Back:** You lose solutions where $\sin x = 0$.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** The calculator's inverse trig functions (e.g. $\sin^{-1}$) only provide the [ ___ ].

**Back:** The calculator's inverse trig functions (e.g. $\sin^{-1}$) only provide the principal value (one angle).

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** When solving $\tan^2(2x) = 3$, what two equations must be solved for the argument $2x$?

**Back:** $\tan(2x) = \sqrt{3}$$\tan(2x) = -\sqrt{3}$

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** How is the graph of $y=\sin(x-30)$ related to $y=\sin x$?

**Back:** It is a translation (shift) of $30^\circ$ to the right.

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the first step to prove the identity $\frac{3\sin x - \cos^2 x + 1}{3 + \sin x} \equiv \sin x$?

**Back:** Replace $\cos^2 x$ with $1 - \sin^2 x$.

## Card 16 — `cloze`

**Front:** The [ ___ ] of a trigonometric function is the expression inside it, such as $(3\theta - 20)$ in $\sin(3\theta - 20)$.

**Back:** The argument of a trigonometric function is the expression inside it, such as $(3\theta - 20)$ in $\sin(3\theta - 20)$.

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** If $0^\circ \leq \theta < 180^\circ$, find the transformed interval for $u = 3\theta - 20$.

**Back:** $$-20^\circ \leq u < 520^\circ$

## Card 18 — `cloze`

**Front:** According to the CAST rule, only [ ___ ] is positive in Quadrant II, and only Tangent is positive in Quadrant III.

**Back:** According to the CAST rule, only Sine is positive in Quadrant II, and only Tangent is positive in Quadrant III.

## Card 19 — `cloze`

**Front:** According to the CAST rule, only Sine is positive in Quadrant II, and only [ ___ ] is positive in Quadrant III.

**Back:** According to the CAST rule, only Sine is positive in Quadrant II, and only Tangent is positive in Quadrant III.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the second solution for $\sin u = k$ within $0^\circ \leq u < 360^\circ$ using the reference angle $\alpha$?

**Back:** $$180^\circ - \alpha$

## Card 21 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the second solution for $\cos u = k$ within $0^\circ \leq u < 360^\circ$ using the reference angle $\alpha$?

**Back:** $$360^\circ - \alpha$

## Card 22 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is $\tan x$ undefined when $\cos x = 0$?

**Back:** The identity $\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$ involves division by zero.
