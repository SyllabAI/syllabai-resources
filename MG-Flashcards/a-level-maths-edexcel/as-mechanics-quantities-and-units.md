# Quantities and Units Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 22 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** A value is given in kg in a Mechanics problem. Which quantity is it measuring?

**Back:** Mass.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** What SI unit is used for force in Mechanics?

**Back:** The newton, N.

## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Front:** Distance is a [ ___ ], while displacement is a [ ___ ].

**Back:** Distance is a scalar, while displacement is a vector.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** An object moves forwards then backwards. For displacement, why should you not just add the path lengths?

**Back:** Displacement uses net change in position with direction.

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** Speed is the rate of change of [ ___ ]; velocity is the rate of change of [ ___ ].

**Back:** Speed is the rate of change of distance; velocity is the rate of change of displacement.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** If east is chosen as positive, what sign should westward velocity have?

**Back:** Negative.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** A cyclist travels 80 m east then 30 m west. What is the distance travelled?

**Back:** 110 m.

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** A cyclist travels 80 m east then 30 m west. What is the displacement?

**Back:** 50 m east.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** The 80 m east, 30 m west journey takes 25 s. What is the average speed?

**Back:** 4.4 m s-1.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** The 80 m east, 30 m west journey takes 25 s. What is the average velocity?

**Back:** 2 m s-1 east.

## Card 11 — `cloze`

**Front:** Velocity has SI unit [ ___ ]; acceleration has SI unit [ ___ ].

**Back:** Velocity has SI unit m s-1; acceleration has SI unit m s-2.

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is acceleration measured in m s-2 rather than m s-1?

**Back:** It is change in velocity per second.

## Card 13 — `cloze`

**Front:** One newton is equivalent to [ ___ ].

**Back:** One newton is equivalent to 1 kg m s-2.

## Card 14 — `cloze`

**Front:** A distance of 1 km is [ ___ ]; a time of 1 minute is [ ___ ].

**Back:** A distance of 1 km is 1000 m; a time of 1 minute is 60 seconds.

## Card 15 — `cloze`

**Front:** A length of 1 cm is [ ___ ]; a time of 1 hour is [ ___ ].

**Back:** A length of 1 cm is 0.01 m; a time of 1 hour is 3600 seconds.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you convert a speed from km h-1 to m s-1?

**Back:** Multiply by 5/18.

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** A runner covers 1.5 km in 5 minutes. What is the average speed in m s-1?

**Back:** 5 m s-1.

## Card 18 — `cloze`

**Front:** In constant acceleration, acceleration stays [ ___ ] throughout the motion.

**Back:** In constant acceleration, acceleration stays the same throughout the motion.

## Card 19 — `cloze`

**Front:** The symbol s often means [ ___ ], but the unit symbol s means [ ___ ].

**Back:** The symbol s often means displacement, but the unit symbol s means seconds.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Why can at be added to u in v = u + at?

**Back:** at has units m s-1, same as velocity.

## Card 21 — `basic`

**Front:** Initial velocity is 4 m s-1, acceleration is 1.5 m s-2, time is 8 s. What is final velocity?

**Back:** 16 m s-1.

## Card 22 — `basic`

**Front:** In s = ut + ½at2, what units must both right-hand terms have?

**Back:** Metres.
