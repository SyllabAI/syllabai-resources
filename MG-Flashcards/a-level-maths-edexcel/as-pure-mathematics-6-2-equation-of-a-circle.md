# 6.2 Equation of a Circle Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 21 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the geometric definition of a circle?

**Back:** The set of all points a fixed distance from a fixed point.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** State the centre-radius form for a circle with centre $(a,b)$ and radius $r$.

**Back:** $$(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** Identify the centre of the circle with equation $(x+5)^2+(y-2)^2=49$.

**Back:** $(-5,2)$

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** Identify the radius of the circle with equation $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=25$.

**Back:** 5

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find the radius squared directly between centre $(x_1,y_1)$ and point $(x_2,y_2)$, use $r^2 = \text{[...]}.

**Back:** To find the radius squared directly between centre $(x_1,y_1)$ and point $(x_2,y_2)$, use $r^2 = \boxed{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is it often unnecessary to calculate the square root of the distance when finding a circle's equation from coordinates?

**Back:** The standard equation uses $r^2$, not $r$.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** Which algebraic method converts an expanded equation (e.g., $x^2+y^2+6x-8y=11$) into centre-radius form?

**Back:** Completing the square for $x$ and $y$ separately.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** After completing the square, if the right-hand side is [ ___ ], the equation does not represent a real circle.

**Back:** After completing the square, if the right-hand side is negative, the equation does not represent a real circle.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find an unknown constant $k$ in a circle equation if given a point $(x,y)$ on the circumference?

**Back:** Substitute the point's coordinates into the equation and solve for $k$.

## Card 10 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find where a circle intersects the y-axis, you must set $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** To find where a circle intersects the y-axis, you must set $\boxed{x=0}$.

## Card 11 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find where a circle intersects the x-axis, you must set $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** To find where a circle intersects the x-axis, you must set $\boxed{y=0}$.

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** Outline the steps to find the coordinates where a line $y=mx+c$ meets a circle.

**Back:** Substitute the line equation into the circle equationSolve the resulting quadratic for $x$Substitute $x$ values back into the line equation to find $y$

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** At what angle does a radius meet a tangent at the point of contact?

**Back:** 90° (perpendicular).

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** If the gradient of a radius is $m$, what is the gradient of the tangent at that point?

**Back:** The negative reciprocal ($-\frac{1}{m}$).

## Card 15 — `cloze`

**Front:** If a circle's radius is vertical, its tangent is [ ___ ]; if the radius is horizontal, its tangent is vertical.

**Back:** If a circle's radius is vertical, its tangent is horizontal; if the radius is horizontal, its tangent is vertical.

## Card 16 — `cloze`

**Front:** If a circle's radius is vertical, its tangent is horizontal; if the radius is horizontal, its tangent is [ ___ ].

**Back:** If a circle's radius is vertical, its tangent is horizontal; if the radius is horizontal, its tangent is vertical.

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** A tangent from point $A$ touches a circle at $B$. Given centre $C$, how is the exact length $AB$ calculated?

**Back:** Use Pythagoras' Theorem on triangle $ABC$: $AB = \sqrt{AC^2 - r^2}$.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the centre of a circle given the endpoints of a diameter?

**Back:** Calculate the midpoint of the endpoints.

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** How can you prove three points on a circle form a right angle in a semicircle?

**Back:** Show the gradients of the two lines meeting at the vertex multiply to -1.

## Card 20 — `cloze`

**Front:** Completing the square for $x^2 + 6x$ results in the expression $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** Completing the square for $x^2 + 6x$ results in the expression $\boxed{(x+3)^2 - 9}$.

## Card 21 — `basic`

**Front:** When finding the equation of a tangent at point $P$, which gradient must be calculated first?

**Back:** The gradient of the radius from the centre to $P$.
