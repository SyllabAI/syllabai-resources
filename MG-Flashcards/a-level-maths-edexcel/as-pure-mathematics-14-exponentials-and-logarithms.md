# Exponentials and Logarithms Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 21 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you solve $2^{x+1}=64$ without using logarithms?

**Back:** Rewrite 64 as $2^6$Equate indices: $x+1=6$$x=5$

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** Convert the logarithmic form $\log_a b = c$ into exponential form.

**Back:** $$a^c = b$

## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Front:** The natural logarithm, written as $\ln x$, is a logarithm with a base of $\text{[...]}$ (approximately 2.718).

**Back:** The natural logarithm, written as $\ln x$, is a logarithm with a base of $\boxed{e}$ (approximately 2.718).

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** How are the graphs of $y=e^x$ and $y=\ln x$ geometrically related?

**Back:** They are inverses, meaning they reflect in the line $y=x$.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** Express $\log_a M + \log_a N$ as a single logarithm.

**Back:** $$\log_a(MN)$

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** Express $\log_a M - \log_a N$ as a single logarithm.

**Back:** $$\log_a\left(\frac{M}{N}\right)$

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** Express $p \log_a M$ as a single logarithm.

**Back:** $$\log_a(M^p)$

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** The argument $x$ of any logarithm $\log_a x$ must satisfy the condition $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** The argument $x$ of any logarithm $\log_a x$ must satisfy the condition $\boxed{x > 0}$.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** Solve $5^x = 18$ for $x$ using natural logarithms.

**Back:** $$x = \frac{\ln 18}{\ln 5}$

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the standard substitution used to solve $3e^y + 10e^{-y} = 11$?

**Back:** Let $u = e^y$ (where $u > 0$).

## Card 11 — `cloze`

**Front:** In the growth model $P = Ae^{kt}$, the population grows if $\text{[...]}$ and decays if $k < 0$.

**Back:** In the growth model $P = Ae^{kt}$, the population grows if $\boxed{k > 0}$ and decays if $k < 0$.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** In the growth model $P = Ae^{kt}$, the population grows if $k > 0$ and decays if $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** In the growth model $P = Ae^{kt}$, the population grows if $k > 0$ and decays if $\boxed{k < 0}$.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** Find the derivative $\frac{dP}{dt}$ for the exponential model $P = Ae^{kt}$.

**Back:** $$\frac{dP}{dt} = kAe^{kt}$

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** In an exponential model $P = Ae^{kt}$, how is the initial value found?

**Back:** Set $t = 0$.

## Card 15 — `cloze`

**Front:** The range of the basic exponential function $f(x) = e^x$ is $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** The range of the basic exponential function $f(x) = e^x$ is $\boxed{f(x) > 0}$.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** State the horizontal asymptote of $y = e^{x+2} - 4$.

**Back:** $$y = -4$

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** Outline the steps to find the x-intercept of $f(x) = e^{x+2} - 4$.

**Back:** Set $f(x) = 0$$e^{x+2} = 4$$x = \ln 4 - 2$

## Card 18 — `cloze`

**Front:** If $y = \log_2 x$, then $\log_4 x$ can be written in terms of $y$ as $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** If $y = \log_2 x$, then $\log_4 x$ can be written in terms of $y$ as $\boxed{\frac{y}{2}}$.

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is the statement $\log_a(M+N) = \log_a M + \log_a N$ incorrect?

**Back:** Log laws apply to multiplication, not addition, inside the logarithm.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Find the gradient function $f'(x)$ for $f(x) = e^{x+2} - 4$.

**Back:** $$f'(x) = e^{x+2}$

## Card 21 — `cloze`

**Front:** To solve $\ln(3x-2) = 1$, first remove the natural log by writing $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** To solve $\ln(3x-2) = 1$, first remove the natural log by writing $\boxed{3x - 2 = e^1}$.
