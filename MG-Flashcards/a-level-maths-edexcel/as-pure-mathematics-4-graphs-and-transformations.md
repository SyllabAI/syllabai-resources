# Graphs and Transformations Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 25 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** How are the x-intercepts and y-intercept of a graph $y=f(x)$ found?

**Back:** x-intercepts: set $y=0$ (or $f(x)=0$)y-intercept: set $x=0$

## Card 2 — `cloze`

**Front:** An [ ___ ] is a straight line that a curve gets closer and closer to but does not meet.

**Back:** An asymptote is a straight line that a curve gets closer and closer to but does not meet.

## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Front:** An asymptote is a straight line that a curve gets closer and closer to but [ ___ ].

**Back:** An asymptote is a straight line that a curve gets closer and closer to but does not meet.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the end behaviour of a cubic graph with a positive $x^3$ coefficient.

**Back:** Down on the left, up on the right.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the end behaviour of a cubic graph with a negative $x^3$ coefficient.

**Back:** Up on the left, down on the right.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** How does a repeated root, such as $(x-2)^2$, affect the appearance of a graph at the x-axis?

**Back:** The graph touches the x-axis at that point rather than crossing it.

## Card 7 — `cloze`

**Front:** Changes outside the function bracket affect [ ___ ] in the obvious way, while changes inside the bracket affect x-values in the opposite way.

**Back:** Changes outside the function bracket affect y-values in the obvious way, while changes inside the bracket affect x-values in the opposite way.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** Changes outside the function bracket affect y-values in the obvious way, while changes inside the bracket affect [ ___ ] in the opposite way.

**Back:** Changes outside the function bracket affect y-values in the obvious way, while changes inside the bracket affect x-values in the opposite way.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the direction and magnitude of the translation $y=f(x-a)$.

**Back:** Moves the graph right by $a$ units.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the direction and magnitude of the translation $y=f(x+a)$.

**Back:** Moves the graph left by $a$ units.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** Contrast the reflections $y=-f(x)$ and $y=f(-x)$.

**Back:** $-f(x)$: Reflection in the x-axis$f(-x)$: Reflection in the y-axis

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the stretch transformation $y=af(x)$.

**Back:** Stretch parallel to the y-axis with scale factor $a$.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the stretch transformation $y=f(ax)$.

**Back:** Stretch parallel to the x-axis with scale factor $\frac{1}{a}$.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** If a quadratic is in the form $y=(x+a)^2+b$, what are the coordinates of the turning point?

**Back:** $$(-a, b)$

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** A curve $y=f(x)$ has a minimum at $(-3, 2)$. Find the new minimum point for $y=f(2x)$.

**Back:** $$\left(-\frac{3}{2}, 2\right)$ (x-coordinate is halved).

## Card 16 — `cloze`

**Front:** For the transformation $y=2f(x)$, only the [ ___ ] of the original points are changed; for $y=f(2x)$, only the x-values are changed.

**Back:** For the transformation $y=2f(x)$, only the y-values of the original points are changed; for $y=f(2x)$, only the x-values are changed.

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** For the transformation $y=2f(x)$, only the y-values of the original points are changed; for $y=f(2x)$, only the [ ___ ] are changed.

**Back:** For the transformation $y=2f(x)$, only the y-values of the original points are changed; for $y=f(2x)$, only the x-values are changed.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** State the asymptotes of the reciprocal graph $y=\frac{1}{x}$.

**Back:** Vertical: $x=0$Horizontal: $y=0$

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the vertical asymptote of $y=\frac{k}{x-a}+b$?

**Back:** Set the denominator to zero: $x-a=0 \Rightarrow x=a$.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** State the horizontal asymptote of the transformed reciprocal graph $y=\frac{k}{x-a}+b$.

**Back:** $$y=b$

## Card 21 — `cloze`

**Front:** Solving the inequality $f(x) > 0$ using a sketch means finding the values of $x$ where the graph is [ ___ ] the x-axis.

**Back:** Solving the inequality $f(x) > 0$ using a sketch means finding the values of $x$ where the graph is above the x-axis.

## Card 22 — `cloze`

**Front:** Solving the inequality $f(x) > 0$ using a sketch means finding the values of $x$ where the graph is above the [ ___ ].

**Back:** Solving the inequality $f(x) > 0$ using a sketch means finding the values of $x$ where the graph is above the x-axis.

## Card 23 — `basic`

**Front:** How is the number of real solutions to $f(x)=g(x)$ determined from a sketch?

**Back:** It is the number of intersections between the graphs of $y=f(x)$ and $y=g(x)$.

## Card 24 — `cloze`

**Front:** At an even repeated root, the sign of $f(x)$ usually [ ___ ] because the graph touches and turns around at the x-axis.

**Back:** At an even repeated root, the sign of $f(x)$ usually does not change because the graph touches and turns around at the x-axis.

## Card 25 — `cloze`

**Front:** At an even repeated root, the sign of $f(x)$ usually does not change because the graph [ ___ ] and turns around at the x-axis.

**Back:** At an even repeated root, the sign of $f(x)$ usually does not change because the graph touches and turns around at the x-axis.
