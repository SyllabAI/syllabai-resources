## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the radian equivalent of $180^\circ$?

**Back:** $$\pi$

**Choices:**

- $$\pi$ ✔
  - _why:_ A full turn is $2\pi$ radians, so a half-turn of $180^\circ$ is exactly $\pi$ radians.
- $$\frac{\pi}{2}$
  - _why:_ This is the radian measure of $90^\circ$, not $180^\circ$. Multiplying $180^\circ$ by $\frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$ gives the correct answer, $\pi$.
- $$2\pi$
  - _why:_ This represents a full turn of $360^\circ$, rather than the half-turn requested. The radian equivalent of $180^\circ$ is $\pi$.
- $$\frac{\pi}{180}$
  - _why:_ This confuses the conversion factor with the converted angle. Multiplying $180^\circ$ by $\frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$ gives $\pi$ radians.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** Define $\sec x$ in terms of the primary trigonometric functions.

**Back:** $$\frac{1}{\cos x}$

**Choices:**

- $$\frac{1}{\cos x}$ ✔
  - _why:_ Secant is defined as the reciprocal of cosine. Therefore, wherever $\cos x\ne 0$, $\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}$.
- $$\frac{1}{\sin x}$
  - _why:_ This is the definition of cosecant, not secant. Secant is the reciprocal of cosine, so $\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}$.
- $$\frac{1}{\tan x}$
  - _why:_ This is the definition of cotangent, not secant. Secant uses only the reciprocal of cosine: $\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}$.
- $$\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$
  - _why:_ This is equivalent to $\tan x$, not $\sec x$. Secant is obtained by taking the reciprocal of cosine, giving $\frac{1}{\cos x}$.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** State the two ways to express $\cot x$ in terms of $\sin, \cos,$ or $\tan$.

**Back:** $\frac{1}{\tan x}$$\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$

**Choices:**

- $\frac{1}{\tan x}$$\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$ ✔
  - _why:_ Cotangent is the reciprocal of tangent. Since $\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, taking its reciprocal also gives $\cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$.
- $\tan x$$\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$
  - _why:_ Both expressions shown are forms of tangent, not cotangent. Cotangent reverses these ratios, giving $\frac{1}{\tan x}$ and $\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$.
- $\frac{1}{\tan x}$$\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$
  - _why:_ The first expression is cotangent, but the second is tangent. The equivalent ratio must also be reciprocal, so it is $\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$.
- $\frac{1}{\cos x}$$\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$
  - _why:_ The second expression is cotangent, but the first is secant rather than cotangent. The required reciprocal expression is $\frac{1}{\tan x}$.
