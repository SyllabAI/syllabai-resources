# The Binomial Expansion Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 20 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** In binomial expansion, what order do terms follow when written in ascending powers of $x$?

**Back:** Constant term ($x^0$), followed by $x^1$, $x^2$, $x^3$, etc.

## Card 2 — `cloze`

**Front:** A [ ___ ] is an expression containing two terms, such as $a+b$ or $2-5x$.

**Back:** A binomial is an expression containing two terms, such as $a+b$ or $2-5x$.

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** In the expansion of $(a+b)^n$, what happens to the powers of $a$ and $b$ as you move through the terms?

**Back:** Powers of $a$ decrease from $n$ to 0Powers of $b$ increase from 0 to $n$

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** What does Pascal's triangle provide in a binomial expansion?

**Back:** The coefficients for each term of the expansion.

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** The notation $\binom{n}{r}$ represents a binomial coefficient and is read as [ ___ ].

**Back:** The notation $\binom{n}{r}$ represents a binomial coefficient and is read as n choose r.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** Which values of $r$ are needed to find the first three terms in ascending powers of $x$?

**Back:** $$r=0$, $r=1$, and $r=2$.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** Why must you include the constant part (e.g., $2^{n-r}$) when calculating the coefficient of an $x$ term?

**Back:** The term is the product of the binomial coefficient, the power of the constant, and the power of the $x$ part.

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** Why do the signs of terms alternate in the expansion of $(2-5x)^7$?

**Back:** Odd powers of the negative term ($-5x$) result in negative terms, while even powers result in positive terms.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** When using $(1+4x)^8$ to approximate $1.04^8$, what is the first step to find the value of $x$?

**Back:** Solve $1+4x=1.04$ (giving $x=0.01$).

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** In the expansion $(ax+b)(C + Dx + \dots)$, which calculation determines the constant term?

**Back:** The product of the two constants: $b \cdot C$.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** In the expansion $(ax+b)(C + Dx + \dots)$, which two products are summed to find the coefficient of $x$?

**Back:** $ax \cdot C$$b \cdot Dx$

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** What does comparing coefficients mean in an exam context?

**Back:** Equating the coefficient of a specific power of $x$ (like $x^3$) from the expansion to a given value or another coefficient.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** How are binomial expansions used in probability for repeated independent events?

**Back:** The coefficients count the number of arrangements for a specific number of successes and failures.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** If $p$ is the probability of success and $q$ of failure, which terms from $(p+q)^5$ calculate the probability of no more than once?

**Back:** The terms for 0 successes ($q^5$) and 1 success ($5pq^4$).

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** Why do odd powers of $x$ disappear when adding $(3+x)^4$ and $(3-x)^4$?

**Back:** The terms with odd powers in $(3-x)^4$ are negative and have the same magnitude as the positive odd-power terms in $(3+x)^4$.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** How does the expansion $162 + 108x^2 + 2x^4$ prove that $(3+x)^4 + (3-x)^4 \ge 162$?

**Back:** Because $x^2$ and $x^4$ are always non-negative, the sum is 162 plus a value $\ge 0$.

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** The finite binomial theorem for a positive integer $n$ is: $(a+b)^n = \text{[...]}.

**Back:** The finite binomial theorem for a positive integer $n$ is: $(a+b)^n = \boxed{\sum_{r=0}^{n}\binom{n}{r}a^{n-r}b^r}.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** In the expansion of $(1+kx)^{10}$, which value of $r$ identifies the term containing $x^3$?

**Back:** $$r=3$.

## Card 19 — `cloze`

**Front:** The [ ___ ] of a term is the numerical multiplier of the variable part (e.g., in $12x^2$, it is 12).

**Back:** The coefficient of a term is the numerical multiplier of the variable part (e.g., in $12x^2$, it is 12).

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Identify $a$, $b$, and $n$ to expand $(2-5x)^7$ using the binomial theorem.

**Back:** $a = 2$$b = -5x$$n = 7$
