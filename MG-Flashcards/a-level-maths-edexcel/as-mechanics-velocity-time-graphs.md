# Velocity-Time Graphs Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 22 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** On a velocity-time graph, what is on the horizontal axis?

**Back:** Time.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** On a velocity-time graph, what is on the vertical axis?

**Back:** Velocity.

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** If a journey starts from rest, what is the initial velocity?

**Back:** $0\text{ m s}^{-1}$.

## Card 4 — `cloze`

**Front:** When velocity never goes below zero, its size is the [ ___ ].

**Back:** When velocity never goes below zero, its size is the speed.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** What shape shows constant acceleration on a velocity-time graph?

**Back:** A straight line.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** What does a horizontal section mean for acceleration?

**Back:** Zero acceleration.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** On a velocity-time graph, what does a falling straight line represent if forward is positive?

**Back:** Negative acceleration.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** On a velocity-time graph, acceleration is found from the [ ___ ].

**Back:** On a velocity-time graph, acceleration is found from the gradient.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the formula for acceleration from a velocity-time graph section?

**Back:** $a=\frac{\text{change in velocity}}{\text{change in time}}$.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** What are the units of acceleration?

**Back:** $\text{m s}^{-2}$.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** The height of a velocity-time graph gives which quantity?

**Back:** Velocity, not acceleration.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** The [ ___ ] under a velocity-time graph gives displacement.

**Back:** The area under a velocity-time graph gives displacement.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** When does distance equal displacement on a velocity-time graph?

**Back:** When there is no area below the time axis.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** For displacement, what does area below the time axis represent?

**Back:** Negative displacement.

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find total distance if part of a velocity-time graph is below the time axis?

**Back:** Add the absolute areas.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** What units result from area under a velocity-time graph, using seconds and $\text{m s}^{-1}$?

**Back:** Metres.

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** The area of a triangular velocity-time section is [ ___ ].

**Back:** The area of a triangular velocity-time section is $\frac{1}{2}\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.

## Card 18 — `cloze`

**Front:** A trapezium area is [ ___ ], where $a$ and $b$ are parallel sides.

**Back:** A trapezium area is $\frac{1}{2}(a+b)h$, where $a$ and $b$ are parallel sides.

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** A tram goes from rest to $18\text{ m s}^{-1}$ in $30\text{ s}$. What is its acceleration?

**Back:** $0.6\text{ m s}^{-2}$.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** A car accelerates for $8\text{ s}$, then drives at constant speed for $12\text{ s}$. When does deceleration begin?

**Back:** $20\text{ s}$.

## Card 21 — `cloze`

**Front:** For the same change in velocity, a larger acceleration magnitude means a [ ___ ] time.

**Back:** For the same change in velocity, a larger acceleration magnitude means a shorter time.

## Card 22 — `basic`

**Front:** A car accelerates from rest to $V$ in $8\text{ s}$. Its deceleration magnitude is twice as large. How long does braking from $V$ to rest take?

**Back:** $4\text{ s}$.
