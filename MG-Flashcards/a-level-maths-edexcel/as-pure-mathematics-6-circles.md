# Circles Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 24 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for the distance between points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$.

**Back:** $$$\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$$

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** State the standard equation of a circle with centre $(a, b)$ and radius $r$.

**Back:** $$$(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$$

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** If the equation of a circle is $(x+3)^2+(y-4)^2=25$, state its centre and radius.

**Back:** Centre: $(-3, 4)$Radius: 5

## Card 4 — `cloze`

**Front:** A [ ___ ] is defined as the set of all points that are the same distance from a fixed point called the centre.

**Back:** A circle is defined as the set of all points that are the same distance from a fixed point called the centre.

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** A circle is defined as the set of all points that are the same distance from a fixed point called the [ ___ ].

**Back:** A circle is defined as the set of all points that are the same distance from a fixed point called the centre.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** Given the endpoints of a circle's diameter, how is the centre calculated?

**Back:** Calculate the midpoint of the diameter's endpoints: $$\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$$

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** How is the radius calculated if only the endpoints of a diameter are known?

**Back:** Calculate half the distance between the two endpoints.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** In a circle, if three points on the circumference form a right angle, the side opposite the right angle is a [ ___ ].

**Back:** In a circle, if three points on the circumference form a right angle, the side opposite the right angle is a diameter.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** What technique is used to find the centre and radius from the expanded form $x^2+y^2+4x-10y=7$?

**Back:** Complete the square separately for the $x$ terms and the $y$ terms.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the value of a missing constant $k$ in a circle equation if a point on the circumference is known?

**Back:** Substitute the coordinates of the point into the circle equation and solve for $k$.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** Explain how to find the coordinates where a straight line intersects a circle.

**Back:** Substitute the line equation (e.g., $y=mx+c$) into the circle equation.Solve the resulting quadratic equation for $x$.Substitute $x$ back into the line equation to find $y$.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** When a line and circle equation are combined into a quadratic, the line is a tangent if the quadratic has [ ___ ] (or the discriminant is zero).

**Back:** When a line and circle equation are combined into a quadratic, the line is a tangent if the quadratic has one repeated root (or the discriminant is zero).

## Card 13 — `cloze`

**Front:** When a line and circle equation are combined into a quadratic, the line is a tangent if the quadratic has one repeated root (or the discriminant is [ ___ ]).

**Back:** When a line and circle equation are combined into a quadratic, the line is a tangent if the quadratic has one repeated root (or the discriminant is zero).

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** How are the $y$-axis intersections of a circle determined from its equation?

**Back:** Set $x=0$ in the circle equation and solve for $y$.

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** Describe the geometric relationship between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact.

**Back:** They are perpendicular ($90^{\circ}$ to each other).

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** If the gradient of a circle's radius is $m$, what is the gradient of the tangent at that point?

**Back:** The negative reciprocal: $-\frac{1}{m}$

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** For a line $ax+by+c=0$, the radius of a circle tangent to this line is the [ ___ ] from the centre to the line.

**Back:** For a line $ax+by+c=0$, the radius of a circle tangent to this line is the perpendicular distance from the centre to the line.

## Card 18 — `cloze`

**Front:** For a line $ax+by+c=0$, the radius of a circle tangent to this line is the perpendicular distance from the [ ___ ] to the line.

**Back:** For a line $ax+by+c=0$, the radius of a circle tangent to this line is the perpendicular distance from the centre to the line.

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for the perpendicular distance from a point $(x_1, y_1)$ to the line $ax+by+c=0$.

**Back:** $$$\frac{|ax_1+by_1+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$$

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Outline the steps to find the equation of a tangent at point $P$ on a circle with centre $C$.

**Back:** Find the gradient of the radius $CP$.Find the perpendicular gradient for the tangent.Use $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ with the coordinates of $P$.

## Card 21 — `cloze`

**Front:** If a quadratic resulting from a line-circle intersection has no real roots, the line [ ___ ].

**Back:** If a quadratic resulting from a line-circle intersection has no real roots, the line misses the circle.

## Card 22 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find the centre and radius from $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-13=0$, you must [ ___ ] to reach the form $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=26$.

**Back:** To find the centre and radius from $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-13=0$, you must complete the square to reach the form $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=26$.

## Card 23 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find the centre and radius from $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-13=0$, you must complete the square to reach the form $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** To find the centre and radius from $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-13=0$, you must complete the square to reach the form $\boxed{(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=26}$.

## Card 24 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the common sign error when reading the centre from $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$?

**Back:** Forgetting that the centre coordinate is the opposite sign of the value in the bracket.
