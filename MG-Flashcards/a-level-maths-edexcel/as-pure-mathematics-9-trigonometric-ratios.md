# Trigonometric Ratios Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 3 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** In a right-angled triangle, what is the ratio for $\sin \theta$?

**Back:** $$\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** In a right-angled triangle, what is the ratio for $\cos \theta$?

**Back:** $$\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** In a right-angled triangle, what is the ratio for $\tan \theta$?

**Back:** $$\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$
