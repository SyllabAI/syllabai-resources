# 2.4 The Discriminant Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 20 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `cloze`

**Front:** For a quadratic equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the discriminant is given by the expression $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** For a quadratic equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the discriminant is given by the expression $\boxed{b^2-4ac}$.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** What condition must the discriminant $\Delta$ satisfy for a quadratic to have two distinct real roots?

**Back:** $$\Delta > 0$

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** What condition must the discriminant $\Delta$ satisfy for a quadratic to have one repeated root (equal roots)?

**Back:** $$\Delta = 0$

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** What condition must the discriminant $\Delta$ satisfy for a quadratic to have no real roots?

**Back:** $$\Delta < 0$

## Card 5 — `cloze`

**Front:** If a quadratic graph touches the $x$-axis at exactly one point, its discriminant is $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** If a quadratic graph touches the $x$-axis at exactly one point, its discriminant is $\boxed{\Delta = 0}$.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** In the equation $5x^2+(k-3)x-2=0$, identify the coefficients $a$, $b$, and $c$.

**Back:** $a = 5$$b = k-3$$c = -2$

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** Identify the common mistake when identifying coefficients from an equation like $x^2 - (k+1)x + 7 = 0$.

**Back:** Forgetting that the minus sign is part of the coefficient ($b = -(k+1)$).

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** Why must you check if the coefficient $a$ can equal zero when solving for a parameter?

**Back:** If $a=0$, the equation becomes linear and the discriminant test is invalid.

## Card 9 — `cloze`

**Front:** The discriminant comes from the part of the quadratic formula $\text{[...]}$; if this value is negative, the roots are not real.

**Back:** The discriminant comes from the part of the quadratic formula $\boxed{\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}$; if this value is negative, the roots are not real.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find the range of $k$ if $k^2 < 24$?

**Back:** $$-2\sqrt{6} < k < 2\sqrt{6}$

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the first step to determine if a line and a curve intersect using the discriminant?

**Back:** Set the expressions for $y$ equal to each other and rearrange into the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$.

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** What discriminant condition describes a line that is a tangent to a curve?

**Back:** $$\Delta = 0$ (the line touches the curve at one point).

## Card 13 — `cloze`

**Front:** If the discriminant of the equation formed by a curve and a line is negative, the curve and line [ ___ ].

**Back:** If the discriminant of the equation formed by a curve and a line is negative, the curve and line do not intersect.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** A circle and a line have one point of intersection. After substituting the line into the circle equation, what is the value of the discriminant?

**Back:** $$0$

## Card 15 — `cloze`

**Front:** In the equation $(r+2)x^2+5x+r=0$, the value $\text{[...]}$ must be excluded from the range of possible solutions for two distinct roots.

**Back:** In the equation $(r+2)x^2+5x+r=0$, the value $\boxed{r = -2}$ must be excluded from the range of possible solutions for two distinct roots.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you solve a quadratic inequality in a parameter (e.g., $4n^2-8n-7 < 0$) after finding the boundary roots?

**Back:** Identify the interval between the roots (since the $n^2$ coefficient is positive and the expression is $<0$).

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** When a curve and a line intersect at two distinct points, the discriminant of their combined equation is $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** When a curve and a line intersect at two distinct points, the discriminant of their combined equation is $\boxed{\Delta > 0}$.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** What does the term "distinct" imply about the roots of a quadratic?

**Back:** The roots are different (not equal).

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** If $kx^2+7x+k=0$ has equal roots, why might you reject the solution $k = -7/2$?

**Back:** Only if the question specifies $k$ must be a positive constant.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you determine the coefficients $a, b, c$ when they contain brackets, such as $x^2 + (2n-1)x + (n+2) = 0$?

**Back:** $a = 1$$b = 2n-1$$c = n+2$
