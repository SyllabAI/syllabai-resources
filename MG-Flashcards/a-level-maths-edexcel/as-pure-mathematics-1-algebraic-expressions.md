# Algebraic Expressions Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 20 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the coefficient in the term $3x^2$?

**Back:** 3

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** State the index law for negative powers, $a^{-n}$.

**Back:** $$a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$

## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Front:** To simplify a fractional power like $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$, you take the [ ___ ] and the $m$th power.

**Back:** To simplify a fractional power like $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$, you take the $n$th root and the $m$th power.

## Card 4 — `cloze`

**Front:** To simplify a fractional power like $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$, you take the $n$th root and the [ ___ ].

**Back:** To simplify a fractional power like $a^{\frac{m}{n}}$, you take the $n$th root and the $m$th power.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you rewrite $27^{2x-1}$ as a power of 3?

**Back:** $$(3^3)^{2x-1} = 3^{3(2x-1)}$

## Card 6 — `cloze`

**Front:** When simplifying $(a^m)^n$, you must [ ___ ] the indices together.

**Back:** When simplifying $(a^m)^n$, you must multiply the indices together.

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the common base for an equation containing $8^x$ and $4^x$?

**Back:** Base 2 ($2^3$ and $2^2$)

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** What substitution converts $4^x - 10(2^x) + 16 = 0$ into a quadratic equation?

**Back:** Let $y = 2^x$ (giving $y^2 - 10y + 16 = 0$)

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** If solving an exponential equation via substitution $y = a^x$, why must a result of $y = -2$ be rejected?

**Back:** An exponential term $a^x$ (where $a > 0$) is always positive.

## Card 10 — `cloze`

**Front:** The expression $2^{2x}$ is equivalent to the square of $\text{[...]}$.

**Back:** The expression $2^{2x}$ is equivalent to the square of $\boxed{2^x}$.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** What are the two main steps to factorise $6x - 24x^3$ completely?

**Back:** Factorise out the highest common factor ($6x$)Apply difference of two squares to $(1 - 4x^2)$

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** A negative fractional index like $\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ indicates you should first take the [ ___ ] of the fraction.

**Back:** A negative fractional index like $\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$ indicates you should first take the reciprocal of the fraction.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** Expand and simplify $(x + 1)^2$.

**Back:** $$x^2 + 2x + 1$

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the conjugate of $a + \sqrt{b}$ used in rationalising denominators?

**Back:** $$a - \sqrt{b}$

## Card 15 — `cloze`

**Front:** Multiplying a denominator by its conjugate rationalises it by creating a [ ___ ].

**Back:** Multiplying a denominator by its conjugate rationalises it by creating a difference of two squares.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you solve for $x$ in an equation like $\frac{2 + x}{x} = \sqrt{5}$?

**Back:** Multiply by $x$ to clear the fractionCollect $x$ terms on one side and factorise out $x$Divide by the coefficient bracket

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** Strictly speaking, $\sqrt{x^2} = \text{[...]}$.

**Back:** Strictly speaking, $\sqrt{x^2} = \boxed{|x|}$.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you rewrite $a^m \times a^n$ as a single power?

**Back:** $$a^{m+n}$

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the result of $\frac{a^m}{a^n}$?

**Back:** $$a^{m-n}$

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Define like terms in an algebraic expression.

**Back:** Terms that share exactly the same variable part (e.g., $5xy$ and $-2xy$)
