# Variance and Standard Deviation Flashcards

_Lane: `a-level-maths-edexcel` · grade — · 20 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What does a larger standard deviation indicate?

**Back:** The data are more spread out.

## Card 2 — `basic`

**Front:** For a value $x$, what expression gives its deviation from the mean?

**Back:** $x-\bar{x}$.

## Card 3 — `basic`

**Front:** Why are deviations squared when calculating variance?

**Back:** To stop positive and negative deviations cancelling.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** If heights are measured in cm, what units does variance have?

**Back:** cm².

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is standard deviation easier to interpret than variance?

**Back:** It is in the original units.

## Card 6 — `cloze`

**Front:** The raw-data mean formula is [ ___ ].

**Back:** The raw-data mean formula is $\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{n}$.

## Card 7 — `cloze`

**Front:** Variance is the mean of squared deviations: [ ___ ].

**Back:** Variance is the mean of squared deviations: $s^2=\frac{\sum (x-\bar{x})^2}{n}$.

## Card 8 — `cloze`

**Front:** The efficient raw-data variance formula is [ ___ ].

**Back:** The efficient raw-data variance formula is $s^2=\frac{\sum x^2}{n}-\bar{x}^2$.

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** After calculating $\frac{\sum x^2}{n}-\bar{x}^2$, what must you do to get $s$?

**Back:** Take the square root.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** For grouped data, which representative value is used for each class?

**Back:** The class midpoint.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** For the class $140<h\le150$, what midpoint is used?

**Back:** 145.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** For grouped data, the mean formula is [ ___ ].

**Back:** For grouped data, the mean formula is $\bar{x}=\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}$.

## Card 13 — `cloze`

**Front:** For grouped data, the variance formula is [ ___ ].

**Back:** For grouped data, the variance formula is $s^2=\frac{\sum fx^2}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^2$.

## Card 14 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is standard deviation from grouped data only an estimate?

**Back:** The original values are unknown, so midpoints are used.

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** If distances are recorded as 10-19 miles to the nearest unit, what midpoint is used?

**Back:** 14.5.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** Rearrange $x=\frac{t-12}{3}$ to make $t$ the subject.

**Back:** $t=3x+12$.

## Card 17 — `cloze`

**Front:** If $T=a+bX$, the mean and median are transformed using the [ ___ ].

**Back:** If $T=a+bX$, the mean and median are transformed using the same formula.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** If $T=a+bX$, by what factor is the standard deviation multiplied?

**Back:** $|b|$.

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Front:** If $T=a+bX$, by what factor is the variance multiplied?

**Back:** $b^2$.

## Card 20 — `basic`

**Front:** Why does the $+12$ in $t=3x+12$ not affect standard deviation?

**Back:** It shifts every value equally.
