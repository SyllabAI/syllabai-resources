## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What are the two properties that distinguish a vector from a scalar?

**Back:** Size (magnitude)Direction

## Card 2 — `cloze`

**Front:** In 2D vector notation, $\text{[...]}$ represents one unit in the positive horizontal direction and $\mathbf{j}$ represents one unit in the positive vertical direction.

**Back:** In 2D vector notation, $\boxed{\mathbf{i}}$ represents one unit in the positive horizontal direction and $\mathbf{j}$ represents one unit in the positive vertical direction.

## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Front:** In 2D vector notation, $\mathbf{i}$ represents one unit in the positive horizontal direction and $\text{[...]}$ represents one unit in the positive vertical direction.

**Back:** In 2D vector notation, $\mathbf{i}$ represents one unit in the positive horizontal direction and $\boxed{\mathbf{j}}$ represents one unit in the positive vertical direction.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** What is the rule for adding two vectors written in component form?

**Back:** Add like components separately: $\mathbf{i}$ with $\mathbf{i}$, and $\mathbf{j}$ with $\mathbf{j}$.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** How is a position vector of a point $A$ defined?

**Back:** The vector $\vec{OA}$, which is the vector from the origin $O$ to point $A$.

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for finding the vector $\vec{AB}$ using the position vectors of $A$ and $B$.

**Back:** $$$\vec{AB} = \vec{OB} - \vec{OA}$$

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** What does reversing the order of a vector (e.g. $\vec{BA}$ instead of $\vec{AB}$) do to its components?

**Back:** It reverses the signs of both the $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$ components.

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** How is the magnitude of vector $\mathbf{v} = a\mathbf{i} + b\mathbf{j}$ calculated?

**Back:** Use Pythagoras: $$|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$

## Card 9 — `basic`

**Front:** If $|5\mathbf{i} + k\mathbf{j}| = 13$, why are there two possible values for $k$?

**Back:** Squaring $k$ in the magnitude formula means both positive and negative values ($\pm 12$) result in the same length.

## Card 10 — `basic`

**Front:** Define a resultant vector.

**Back:** A single vector that has the same overall effect as multiple vectors acting together.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you calculate the resultant force when multiple force vectors are acting on a particle?

**Back:** Calculate the sum of all individual force vectors.

## Card 12 — `cloze`

**Front:** To find the vector between two endpoints in a triangle, $\vec{BC} = \vec{AC} - \text{[...]}.

**Back:** To find the vector between two endpoints in a triangle, $\vec{BC} = \vec{AC} - \boxed{\vec{AB}}.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** In triangle $ABC$, explain the vector path taken to find $\vec{BC}$ if starting from $B$.

**Back:** Travel from $B$ back to $A$ ($-\vec{AB}$), then from $A$ to $C$ ($+\vec{AC}$).

## Card 14 — `cloze`

**Front:** When working with velocity vectors, the speed is defined as the [ ___ ] of the velocity vector.

**Back:** When working with velocity vectors, the speed is defined as the magnitude of the velocity vector.

## Card 15 — `basic`

**Front:** What are the two defining characteristics of a bearing?

**Back:** Measured clockwise from NorthWritten as a three-figure angle

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Front:** If $\mathbf{i}$ is East and $\mathbf{j}$ is North, what direction is a vector with positive $\mathbf{i}$ and negative $\mathbf{j}$ components?

**Back:** South-East

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** A boat has velocity $6\mathbf{i} - 8\mathbf{j}$. How would you calculate the angle $\theta$ below the East line to find the bearing?

**Back:** $$$\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{6}\right)$$ (using the absolute values of the components).

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** If an object starts at position $\mathbf{r}_0$ and moves through vector $\mathbf{m}$, what is its new position vector?

**Back:** $$$\mathbf{r}_{new} = \mathbf{r}_0 + \mathbf{m}$$

## Card 19 — `cloze`

**Front:** A bearing between $180^{\circ}$ and $270^{\circ}$ corresponds to a velocity vector with a [ ___ ] $\mathbf{i}$ component and a negative $\mathbf{j}$ component.

**Back:** A bearing between $180^{\circ}$ and $270^{\circ}$ corresponds to a velocity vector with a negative $\mathbf{i}$ component and a negative $\mathbf{j}$ component.

## Card 21 — `basic`

**Front:** How do you find unknown components $x$ and $y$ of vector $\mathbf{b}$ if the resultant $\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}$ is known?

**Back:** Set up and solve separate equations for the $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$ components.
