## Card 3 — `cloze`

**Back:** To find the lower quartile ($Q_1$) of an ordered data set, calculate the median of the lower half of the data.

## Card 4 — `basic`

**Front:** When finding quartiles for an odd number of data values, how is the overall median treated?

**Back:** It is excluded from both the lower and upper halves of the data.

## Card 5 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for calculating the Interquartile Range (IQR).

**Back:** $$$IQR = Q_3 - Q_1$$

## Card 6 — `basic`

**Front:** What does a smaller IQR indicate about the data distribution?

**Back:** The middle 50% of the data are more tightly clustered (greater consistency).

## Card 7 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for the lower fence used to identify outliers.

**Back:** $$$Q_1 - 1.5 \times IQR$$

## Card 8 — `basic`

**Front:** State the formula for the upper fence used to identify outliers.

**Back:** $$$Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR$$

## Card 9 — `cloze`

**Front:** A value is considered an outlier if it is [ ___ ] the lower fence or greater than the upper fence.

**Back:** A value is considered an outlier if it is smaller than the lower fence or greater than the upper fence.

## Card 10 — `cloze`

**Front:** A value is considered an outlier if it is smaller than the lower fence or [ ___ ] the upper fence.

**Back:** A value is considered an outlier if it is smaller than the lower fence or greater than the upper fence.

## Card 11 — `basic`

**Front:** If a data value is exactly equal to the upper fence, is it classified as an outlier?

**Back:** No; it must be strictly greater than the fence.

## Card 12 — `basic`

**Front:** Where must the whiskers of a box plot end if outliers are present?

**Back:** At the smallest and largest non-outlier values.

## Card 13 — `basic`

**Front:** How are outliers represented on a box plot?

**Back:** Separately using crosses.

## Card 16 — `basic`

**Back:** The Upper Quartile ($Q_3$).

## Card 17 — `basic`

**Front:** When comparing two distributions, which statistic should be used to compare average location?

**Back:** The median.

## Card 18 — `basic`

**Front:** When comparing two distributions, which statistic should be used to compare consistency (spread of the middle 50%)?

**Back:** The Interquartile Range (IQR).

## Card 19 — `basic`

**Back:** The second set, because it has a lower IQR.

## Card 23 — `basic`

**Front:** Why is the IQR often preferred over the range when discussing spread?

**Back:** The range is significantly affected by extreme values (outliers), whereas the IQR focuses on the central values.
