# Solving Simultaneous Equations Graphically Flashcards

_Lane: `gcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 5 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** On a coordinate graph, which axis is the vertical one?

**Back:** The y-axis.

**Choices:**

- The y-axis. ✔
  - _why:_ The y-axis runs vertically, with y-values increasing upwards and decreasing downwards from the origin.
- The x-axis.
  - _why:_ The x-axis is the horizontal axis, not the vertical one. The vertical axis is the y-axis, which displays y-coordinates.
- The origin.
  - _why:_ The origin is the point where the two axes intersect, rather than an axis itself. The vertical axis is the y-axis.
- The coordinate.
  - _why:_ A coordinate is a value or ordered pair used to locate a point, not the name of an axis. The vertical axis is the y-axis.
