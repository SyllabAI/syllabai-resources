# Writing and Simplifying Ratio Flashcards

_Lane: `gcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 3 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What does a ratio compare?

**Back:** Amounts in a fixed order.

**Choices:**

- Amounts in a fixed order. ✔
  - _why:_ A ratio compares the relative sizes of two or more amounts. Their order must remain fixed because reversing the order produces a different ratio.
- Amounts in any order.
  - _why:_ Treating the order as interchangeable is a common mistake. A ratio compares amounts in a fixed order, since changing the order changes the meaning of the ratio.
- Amounts by their numerical difference.
  - _why:_ Subtracting amounts finds their difference, not their ratio. A ratio compares the relative sizes of amounts in a fixed order.
- Amounts as equal percentages.
  - _why:_ Ratios can sometimes be converted into percentages, but the amounts do not need to represent equal percentages. A ratio directly compares amounts in a fixed order.
