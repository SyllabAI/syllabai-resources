# Perpendicular Lines and the equation of a tangent Flashcards

_Lane: `gcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 8 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** Identify the gradient and y-intercept in the equation $y=mx+c$.

**Back:** Gradient: $m$y-intercept: $c$

**Choices:**

- Gradient: $m$y-intercept: $c$ ✔
  - _why:_ In slope-intercept form, $m$ gives the change in $y$ for each unit increase in $x$, while $c$ is the value of $y$ when $x=0$.
- Gradient: $c$y-intercept: $m$
  - _why:_ This reverses the roles of the two constants. In $y=mx+c$, $m$ is the gradient because it multiplies $x$, while $c$ is the y-intercept.
- Gradient: $-\frac{1}{m}$y-intercept: $c$
  - _why:_ The value $-\frac{1}{m}$ would be the gradient of a line perpendicular to this one, not the original line. Its gradient is $m$ and its y-intercept is $c$.
- Gradient: $m$y-intercept: $-c$
  - _why:_ The sign of the intercept must be read directly from the equation. Since the constant term is $+c$, the y-intercept is $c$, while the gradient is $m$.
