# Changing the Subject of a Formula Flashcards

_Lane: `gcse-maths-edexcel` · grade 5 · 1 cards (source: MathsGenie flashcards)_

## Card 1 — `basic`

**Front:** What defines the subject of a formula?

**Back:** The variable that is on its own on one side of the equals sign.

**Choices:**

- The variable that is on its own on one side of the equals sign. ✔
  - _why:_ The subject is the isolated variable on one side of the equals sign, with the rest of the formula expressing how its value is calculated.
- The variable that appears first on the left side of the equals sign.
  - _why:_ A variable’s position does not determine whether it is the subject. The subject is the variable that is on its own on one side of the equals sign.
- The variable that is used most often on both sides of the equals sign.
  - _why:_ The number of times a variable appears does not define the subject. The subject is the variable isolated on one side of the equals sign.
- The variable that has the largest coefficient on one side of the equals sign.
  - _why:_ A variable’s coefficient does not determine whether it is the subject. The subject is the variable that is on its own on one side of the equals sign.
