# Direct and Inverse Proportion

Master proportional relationships by translating words, graphs, and tables into equations with a constant multiplier.

**Practice questions**

1. $p$ is directly proportional to $q$. When $p=15$ and $q=5$, find $p$ when $q=12$.

   - 22

   - 30

   - 36 ✔

   - 60

   _Explanation:_ Since $p=kq$, $k=15\div5=3$. Therefore, when $q=12$, $p=3\times12=36$.

2. $r$ is directly proportional to $\sqrt{s}$. When $r=12$ and $s=16$, find $s$ when $r=18$.

   - 6

   - 36 ✔

   - 24

   - 54

   _Explanation:_ Write $r=k\sqrt{s}$. Using the first pair gives $12=k\sqrt{16}=4k$, so $k=3$. Then $18=3\sqrt{s}$, giving $\sqrt{s}=6$ and hence $s=36$.

3. The pairs $(x,y)$ are $(2,32)$, $(3,108)$ and $(4,256)$. Which formula fits all three pairs?

   - $y=4x^3$ ✔

   - $y=8x^2$

   - $y=16x$

   - $y=\frac{64}{x}$

   _Explanation:_ For each pair, $y\div x^3=4$: for example, $32\div2^3=4$. Therefore $y$ is directly proportional to $x^3$ and the formula is $y=4x^3$.

4. $m$ is inversely proportional to $n$. When $m=24$ and $n=3$, find $m$ when $n=9$.

   - 72

   - 216

   - 18

   - 8 ✔

   _Explanation:_ For inverse proportion, $mn$ is constant. Here, $mn=24\times3=72$, so when $n=9$, $m=72\div9=8$.

5. A graph of $y$ against $x$ has two curved branches above the $x$-axis and is symmetric about the $y$-axis. Which formula could describe it, where $k$ is positive?

   - $y=kx^2$

   - $y=\frac{k}{x^2}$ ✔

   - $y=\frac{k}{x}$

   - $y=kx$

   _Explanation:_ In $y=\frac{k}{x^2}$, squaring $x$ makes the denominator positive for both positive and negative $x$. This produces two positive branches symmetric about the $y$-axis.
