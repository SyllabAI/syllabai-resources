# Surface Area

Get confident finding the outside area of 3D shapes and choosing enough paint.

**Practice questions**

1. A cuboid measures $5$ cm by $4$ cm by $3$ cm. What is its total surface area?

   - $47\text{ cm}^2$

   - $60\text{ cm}^2$

   - $94\text{ cm}^2$ ✔

   - $118\text{ cm}^2$

   _Explanation:_ The three different face areas are $5\times4=20$, $5\times3=15$ and $4\times3=12$. There are two of each face, so the surface area is $2(20+15+12)=94\text{ cm}^2$.

2. A trapezium-based prism is $6$ cm long. The trapezium has parallel sides of $11$ cm and $3$ cm, a perpendicular height of $3$ cm, and two other sides of $5$ cm each. What is the total surface area?

   - $186\text{ cm}^2$ ✔

   - $165\text{ cm}^2$

   - $228\text{ cm}^2$

   - $144\text{ cm}^2$

   _Explanation:_ Each trapezium has area $\frac12(11+3)\times3=21\text{ cm}^2$. The trapezium's perimeter is $11+3+5+5=24$ cm, so the rectangular faces total $24\times6=144\text{ cm}^2$. Therefore, the total is $2\times21+144=186\text{ cm}^2$.

3. A closed cylinder has diameter $8$ cm and height $9$ cm. What is its total surface area in terms of $\pi$?

   - $88\pi\text{ cm}^2$

   - $72\pi\text{ cm}^2$

   - $272\pi\text{ cm}^2$

   - $104\pi\text{ cm}^2$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The radius is $4$ cm. The two circular ends have area $2\pi r^2=32\pi$, and the curved surface has area $2\pi rh=72\pi$. The total is $104\pi\text{ cm}^2$.

4. Six closed boxes each measure $80$ cm by $50$ cm by $30$ cm. One pot of paint covers $4\text{ m}^2$. How many whole pots are needed to paint all six boxes?

   - $2$ pots

   - $3$ pots ✔

   - $4$ pots

   - $6$ pots

   _Explanation:_ In metres, one box has surface area $2(0.8\times0.5+0.8\times0.3+0.5\times0.3)=1.58\text{ m}^2$. Six boxes have area $9.48\text{ m}^2$, and $9.48\div4=2.37$, so this must be rounded up to $3$ pots.

5. An L-shaped prism is $4$ cm long. Its end is formed from a $6$ cm by $5$ cm rectangle with a $2$ cm by $3$ cm corner removed. The perimeter of the L-shaped end is $22$ cm. What is the prism's total surface area?

   - $112\text{ cm}^2$

   - $116\text{ cm}^2$

   - $136\text{ cm}^2$ ✔

   - $148\text{ cm}^2$

   _Explanation:_ The area of one L-shaped end is $6\times5-2\times3=24\text{ cm}^2$. The side faces total $22\times4=88\text{ cm}^2$, so the total surface area is $2\times24+88=136\text{ cm}^2$.
