# Solving Quadratics

Learn how to split quadratics into brackets so you can find the values that make them equal zero.

**Practice questions**

1. Which factorisation is correct for $x^2+9x+20$?

   - $(x+2)(x+10)$

   - $(x-4)(x-5)$

   - $(x+4)(x+5)$ ✔

   - $(x-2)(x-10)$

   _Explanation:_ The two numbers must multiply to $20$ and add to $9$. These numbers are $4$ and $5$, so $x^2+9x+20=(x+4)(x+5)$.

2. Solve $(y-7)(y+3)=0$.

   - $y=7$ or $y=-3$ ✔

   - $y=-7$ or $y=3$

   - $y=7$ or $y=3$

   - $y=-7$ or $y=-3$

   _Explanation:_ For the product to equal zero, at least one bracket must equal zero. Therefore $y-7=0$ or $y+3=0$, giving $y=7$ or $y=-3$.

3. Solve $t^2-2t-15=0$.

   - $t=-5$ or $t=3$

   - $t=-3$ or $t=-5$

   - $t=3$ or $t=5$

   - $t=5$ or $t=-3$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The quadratic factorises as $(t-5)(t+3)=0$ because $-5\times3=-15$ and $-5+3=-2$. Setting each bracket to zero gives $t=5$ or $t=-3$.

4. Solve $x^2+5x=24$.

   - $x=8$ or $x=-3$

   - $x=-8$ or $x=3$ ✔

   - $x=-6$ or $x=4$

   - $x=6$ or $x=-4$

   _Explanation:_ First rearrange to get $x^2+5x-24=0$. This factorises as $(x+8)(x-3)=0$, so $x=-8$ or $x=3$.

5. Solve $2x^2+x-6=0$.

   - $x=-\frac{3}{2}$ or $x=2$

   - $x=3$ or $x=-2$

   - $x=\frac{3}{2}$ or $x=-2$ ✔

   - $x=-3$ or $x=2$

   _Explanation:_ The quadratic factorises as $(2x-3)(x+2)=0$. Therefore $2x-3=0$ gives $x=\frac{3}{2}$, while $x+2=0$ gives $x=-2$.
