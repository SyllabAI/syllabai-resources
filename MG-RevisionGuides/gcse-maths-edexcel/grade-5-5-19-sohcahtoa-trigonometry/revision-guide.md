# SOHCAHTOA (Trigonometry)

Master the rules of SOHCAHTOA to calculate missing sides and unknown angles in right-angled triangles.

**Practice questions**

1. In right-angled triangle $ABC$, angle $B$ is $90\text{°}$ and you are working from angle $A$. Which side is adjacent to angle $A$?

   - $AB$ ✔

   - $AC$

   - $BC$

   - Both $AB$ and $AC$

   _Explanation:_ The adjacent side touches angle $A$ but is not the hypotenuse. Therefore, $AB$ is adjacent, $AC$ is the hypotenuse and $BC$ is opposite.

2. A right-angled triangle has an angle of $28\text{°}$. The adjacent side is $9$ cm and the hypotenuse is $x$ cm. Which equation should be used?

   - $\sin(28\text{°})=\frac{9}{x}$

   - $\tan(28\text{°})=\frac{9}{x}$

   - $\cos(28\text{°})=\frac{9}{x}$ ✔

   - $\cos(28\text{°})=\frac{x}{9}$

   _Explanation:_ Cosine connects the adjacent side and hypotenuse: $\cos(\theta)=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$, so $\cos(28\text{°})=\frac{9}{x}$.

3. A right-angled triangle has an angle of $36\text{°}$ and an adjacent side of $18$ cm. What is the length of the opposite side, to 1 decimal place?

   - $24.8$ cm

   - $13.1$ cm ✔

   - $14.6$ cm

   - $10.6$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Tangent connects opposite and adjacent. Using $\tan(36\text{°})=\frac{x}{18}$ gives $x=18\tan(36\text{°})\approx13.1$ cm.

4. From angle $x$ in a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is $7$ cm and the adjacent side is $12$ cm. Find $x$ to 1 decimal place.

   - $59.7\text{°}$

   - $35.7\text{°}$

   - $54.3\text{°}$

   - $30.3\text{°}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Since opposite and adjacent are known, use inverse tangent: $x=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{7}{12}\right)\approx30.3\text{°}$.

5. Triangle $ABC$ has base $AC=20$ cm. A perpendicular height $BD=8$ cm meets $AC$ at $D$, and angle $BAD=35\text{°}$. What is angle $BCD$ to 1 decimal place?

   - $21.8\text{°}$

   - $35.0\text{°}$

   - $43.0\text{°}$ ✔

   - $29.1\text{°}$

   _Explanation:_ First, $AD=\frac{8}{\tan(35\text{°})}\approx11.4$ cm, so $DC\approx20-11.4=8.6$ cm. Then $\angle BCD=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{8.6}\right)\approx43.0\text{°}$.
