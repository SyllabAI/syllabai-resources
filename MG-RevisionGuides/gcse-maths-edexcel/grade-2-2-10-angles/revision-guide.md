# Angles

Learn the key angle facts for straight lines, right angles, triangles and simple shapes.

## Angles on a straight line add to 180°

**Example 1: Find the size of angle a**

Angles on a straight line add to 180°  
a + 130° = 180°  
a = 50°


**Example 2: Find the size of angle b**

Angles on a straight line add to 180°  
b + 90° + 65° = 180°  
b + 155° = 180°  
b = 25°


**Practice questions**

1. Two angles are on a straight line. One angle is $118^\circ$. What is the size of the other angle?

   - $62^\circ$ ✔

   - $72^\circ$

   - $118^\circ$

   - $298^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$, so the missing angle is $180-118=62^\circ$.

2. A straight line is split into three angles measuring $42^\circ$, $x^\circ$ and $68^\circ$. Find $x$.

   - $70^\circ$ ✔

   - $110^\circ$

   - $250^\circ$

   - $112^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The three angles add to $180^\circ$, so $x=180-42-68=70^\circ$. Therefore the correct option is $70^\circ$.

3. Two adjacent angles on a straight line are $3x^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find $x$.

   - $20$

   - $40$ ✔

   - $60$

   - $120$

   _Explanation:_ The angles add to $180^\circ$, so $3x+60=180$. Thus $3x=120$ and $x=40$.

## Angles around a point add to 360°

**Example 3: Find the size of angle e**

Angles around a point add to 360°  
e + 295° = 360°  
e = 65°


**Example 4: Find the size of angle f**

Angles around a point add to 360°  
f + 131° + 108° + 52° = 360°  
f + 291° = 360°  
f = 69°


**Practice questions**

1. Four angles meet at a point. Three of them are $85^\circ$, $110^\circ$ and $95^\circ$. Find the fourth angle.

   - $70^\circ$ ✔

   - $110^\circ$

   - $250^\circ$

   - $290^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Angles around a point add to $360^\circ$. The fourth angle is $360-85-110-95=70^\circ$.

2. Angles of $120^\circ$, $x^\circ$, $75^\circ$ and $95^\circ$ meet at a point. Find $x$.

   - $60^\circ$

   - $70^\circ$ ✔

   - $170^\circ$

   - $430^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The known angles total $120+75+95=290^\circ$. Therefore $x=360-290=70^\circ$.

3. A right angle, an angle of $145^\circ$ and an angle of $x^\circ$ meet at a point. Find $x$.

   - $35^\circ$

   - $125^\circ$ ✔

   - $215^\circ$

   - $305^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ A right angle is $90^\circ$. Using $360^\circ$ around a point, $x=360-90-145=125^\circ$. Thus the correct option is $125^\circ$.

## Angles in a triangle add to 180°

**Example 5: Find the size of angle j**

Angles in a triangle add to 180°  
j + 71° + 53° = 180°  
j + 124° = 180°  
j = 56°


**Example 6: Find the size of angle k**

Here we have an isosceles triangle. In an isosceles triangle two angles (and two sides) are equal.  
There are lines on two of the sides to show that they are equal. The other side of an isosceles triangle is called the base.  
The angles at the base of an isosceles triangle are equal. This means we have two 58° angles.

k + 58° + 58° = 180°  
k + 116° = 180°  
k = 64°


**Example 7: Find the size of angle m**

We have another isosceles triangle. This time the missing angle is at the base.

m + m + 32° = 180°  
2m = 148°  
m = 74°


**Practice questions**

1. A triangle has angles of $47^\circ$ and $68^\circ$. Find the third angle.

   - $65^\circ$ ✔

   - $75^\circ$

   - $115^\circ$

   - $245^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$, so the third angle is $180-47-68=65^\circ$. Therefore the correct option is $65^\circ$.

2. An isosceles triangle has two equal base angles and a top angle of $44^\circ$. Find each base angle.

   - $44^\circ$

   - $68^\circ$ ✔

   - $136^\circ$

   - $88^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The two base angles share the remaining $180-44=136^\circ$. Since they are equal, each is $136\div2=68^\circ$.

3. An isosceles triangle has a top angle of $70^\circ$ and two equal base angles. Find the size of each base angle.

   - $35^\circ$

   - $55^\circ$ ✔

   - $70^\circ$

   - $110^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The equal base angles add to $180-70=110^\circ$. Dividing equally gives $110\div2=55^\circ$.
