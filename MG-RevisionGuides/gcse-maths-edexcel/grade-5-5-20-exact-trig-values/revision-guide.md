# Exact trig values

Learn the special sine, cosine and tangent values that let you answer trig questions without a calculator.

**Practice questions**

1. What is the exact value of $\cos(90^\circ)+\tan(45^\circ)$?

   - $0$

   - Undefined

   - $2$

   - $1$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ $\cos(90^\circ)=0$ and $\tan(45^\circ)=1$, so the exact value is $0+1=1$.

2. What is the exact value of $\sin(60^\circ)$?

   - $\frac{1}{2}$

   - $\sqrt{3}$

   - $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ✔

   - $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ In a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle, the side opposite $60^\circ$ is $\sqrt{3}$ and the hypotenuse is $2$. Therefore, $\sin(60^\circ)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

3. A right-angled triangle has a $60^\circ$ angle. The side opposite this angle is $12$ cm. What is the exact length of the adjacent side?

   - $4\sqrt{3}$ cm ✔

   - $6\sqrt{3}$ cm

   - $12\sqrt{3}$ cm

   - $4$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Using $\tan(60^\circ)=\sqrt{3}$ gives $\sqrt{3}=\frac{12}{\text{adjacent}}$. Hence the adjacent side is $\frac{12}{\sqrt{3}}=4\sqrt{3}$ cm.

4. A right-angled triangle has a $30^\circ$ angle and a hypotenuse of $14$ cm. What is the exact length of the side opposite the $30^\circ$ angle?

   - $7\sqrt{3}$ cm

   - $7$ cm ✔

   - $14\sqrt{3}$ cm

   - $28$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Since $\sin(30^\circ)=\frac{1}{2}$, the opposite side is $14\times\frac{1}{2}=7$ cm.

5. What is the exact value of $2\sin(45^\circ)+\cos(60^\circ)$?

   - $\sqrt{2}+1$

   - $\frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{2}$

   - $\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}$ ✔

   - $2\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ $\sin(45^\circ)=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $\cos(60^\circ)=\frac{1}{2}$. Therefore, $2\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)+\frac{1}{2}=\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}$.
