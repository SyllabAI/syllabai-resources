# The Sine Rule

Master the sine rule for linking opposite angles and sides when right-angle trigonometry is not enough.

**Practice questions**

1. In triangle $ABC$, $AB=8$ cm, angle $A=50^\circ$ and angle $B=70^\circ$. Which complete opposite pair can be used in the sine rule?

   - $AB=8$ cm and angle $C=60^\circ$ ✔

   - $AB=8$ cm and angle $A=50^\circ$

   - $AB=8$ cm and angle $B=70^\circ$

   - $AC=8$ cm and angle $C=60^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The angles total $180^\circ$, so $C=180^\circ-50^\circ-70^\circ=60^\circ$. Side $AB$ is opposite angle $C$, giving the pair $AB=8$ cm and $C=60^\circ$.

2. In triangle $PQR$, $PR=10$ cm, angle $Q=80^\circ$ and angle $R=40^\circ$. Work out $PQ$ to 1 decimal place.

   - $15.3$ cm

   - $7.4$ cm

   - $6.5$ cm ✔

   - $8.8$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Side $PQ$ is opposite $40^\circ$ and $PR$ is opposite $80^\circ$. Therefore $\frac{PQ}{\sin40^\circ}=\frac{10}{\sin80^\circ}$, so $PQ=10\frac{\sin40^\circ}{\sin80^\circ}=6.5$ cm.

3. In triangle $JKL$, $JK=9$ cm, $JL=8$ cm and angle $L=50^\circ$. Work out angle $K$ to 1 decimal place.

   - $59.5^\circ$

   - $42.9^\circ$ ✔

   - $137.1^\circ$

   - $50.0^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Side $JL=8$ is opposite $K$, while $JK=9$ is opposite $L$. Hence $\frac{\sin K}{8}=\frac{\sin50^\circ}{9}$, giving $K=\sin^{-1}\!\left(\frac{8\sin50^\circ}{9}\right)=42.9^\circ$.

4. Triangle $ABC$ has $AC=20$ cm, $AB=14$ cm and angle $C=35^\circ$. Angle $B$ is obtuse. Find angle $B$ to 3 significant figures.

   - $35.0^\circ$

   - $145^\circ$

   - $125^\circ$ ✔

   - $55.0^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The sine rule gives $\sin B=\frac{20\sin35^\circ}{14}$, producing an acute value of about $55.0^\circ$. Since $B$ is stated to be obtuse, use the supplementary angle: $B=180^\circ-55.0^\circ=125^\circ$.

5. Triangle $LMN$ has $LM=10$ cm, angle $L=64^\circ$ and angle $N=46^\circ$. Work out its area to 1 decimal place.

   - $44.9$ cm$^2$

   - $47.0$ cm$^2$

   - $61.4$ cm$^2$

   - $58.7$ cm$^2$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ First, $M=180^\circ-64^\circ-46^\circ=70^\circ$. The sine rule gives $LN=\frac{10\sin70^\circ}{\sin46^\circ}\approx13.06$ cm. Then area $=\frac12\times10\times13.06\times\sin64^\circ=58.7$ cm$^2$.
