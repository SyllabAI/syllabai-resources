# Ratio

Learn how ratios help you compare and split amounts in everyday GCSE questions.

**Example 1: Abbie and Ben share £120 in the ratio 2:1 Work out how much each of them get.**

The first step is to work out how many equal parts there are.  
The ratio is 2:1 so there are 3 (2 + 1) equal parts

We now need to work out how much each of the parts is worth  
We divide the £120 between the 3 parts  
£120 ÷ 3 = £40  
Each part is worth £40

Now we can work out how much Abbie and Ben get  
Abbie has 2 parts so she gets £80 (2 × £40)  
Ben has one part which is worth £40


**Practice questions**

1. A school club has £81 to share between two teams in the ratio $5:4$. How much does the first team receive?

   - £36

   - £40

   - £45 ✔

   - £54

   _Explanation:_ There are $5+4=9$ parts, so each part is worth £9. The first team gets $5\times£9=£45$.

2. Mia and Noah have money in the ratio Mia:Noah = $7:4$. Mia has £21 more than Noah. How much does Noah have?

   - £21

   - £28 ✔

   - £49

   - £77

   _Explanation:_ The difference is $7-4=3$ parts, so 3 parts are worth £21 and 1 part is worth £7. Noah has 4 parts, so he has $4\times£7=£28$.

**Example 2: A, B and C share £240 in the ratio 4:3:1 Work out how much each of them get.**

4 + 3 + 1 = 8  
There are 8 equal parts

£240 ÷ 8 = £30  
Each part is worth £30



A gets 4 × £30 = £120  
B gets 3 × £30 = £90  
C gets 1 × £30 = £30


**Practice questions**

1. Three friends share £150 in the ratio $2:5:3$. How much does the second friend receive?

   - £30

   - £45

   - £75 ✔

   - £90

   _Explanation:_ There are $2+5+3=10$ equal parts, so each part is worth £15. The second friend receives 5 parts: $5\times£15=£75$.

2. The amounts shared by A, B and C are in the ratio $3:4:5$. C receives £35. How much is shared altogether?

   - £70

   - £84 ✔

   - £91

   - £105

   _Explanation:_ C's 5 parts are worth £35, so 1 part is worth £7. There are $3+4+5=12$ parts altogether, giving $12\times£7=£84$.
