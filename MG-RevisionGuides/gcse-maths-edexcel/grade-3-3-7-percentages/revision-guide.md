# Percentages

Build percentage skills from simple mental methods to exam-style money, offers, comparisons and interest problems.

## Finding a Percentage of an Amount without a calculator

Percent means out of 100

When we work out percentages without a calculator we usually start by working out 50%, 10% or 1%.

50% is the same as 50⁄100  
We can simplify 50⁄100 by dividing the top and bottom by 50  
50⁄100 = 1⁄2

## We work out 50% of a number by halving it.

10% is the same as 10⁄100  
We can simplify 10⁄100 by dividing the top and bottom by 10  
10⁄100 = 1⁄10

## We work out 10% of a number by dividing it by 10.

1% is the same as 1⁄100

## We work out 1% of a number by dividing it by 100.

**Example 1: Work out 50% of £320**

To work out 50% of an amount we divide it by 2

£320 ÷ 2 = £160  
50% of 320 = £160


**Example 2: Work out 10% of £320**

To work out 10% of an amount we divide it by 10

£320 ÷ 10 = £32  
10% of 320 = £32


**Example 3: Work out 1% of £320**

To work out 1% of an amount we divide it by 100

£320 ÷ 100 = £3.20  
1% of 320 = £3.20


**Practice questions**

1. Find $10\%$ of $470$.

   - $47$ ✔

   - $4.7$

   - $460$

   - $4700$

   _Explanation:_ To find $10\%$, divide by $10$. Therefore $470 \div 10 = 47$.

2. Find $50\%$ of £86.

   - £17.20

   - £172

   - £43 ✔

   - £36

   _Explanation:_ To find $50\%$, halve the amount. Half of £86 is £43.

3. Find $1\%$ of $650$.

   - $65$

   - $65000$

   - $0.65$

   - $6.5$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ To find $1\%$, divide by $100$. Therefore $650 \div 100 = 6.5$.

### Converting to a decimal or a fraction

We can convert percentages to decimals or fractions.

To convert a percentage to a decimal we divide by 100

54% = 0.54  
7% = 0.07  
84% = 0.84  
59% = 0.59

**Practice questions**

1. Write $7\%$ as a decimal.

   - $0.7$

   - $0.07$ ✔

   - $0.007$

   - $7$

   _Explanation:_ Divide the percentage by $100$: $7 \div 100 = 0.07$.

2. Write $17.5\%$ as a decimal.

   - $1.75$

   - $0.0175$

   - $17.5$

   - $0.175$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Divide by $100$, moving the decimal point two places left: $17.5\% = 0.175$.
