# Compound Interest and Depreciation

See how repeated percentage changes affect savings, house prices and cars, and learn reliable calculator methods.

**Practice questions**

1. A laptop loses $7\%$ of its value each year. Which multiplier is used to calculate its value after each year?

   - $0.07$

   - $0.93$ ✔

   - $1.07$

   - $1.93$

   _Explanation:_ After losing $7\%$, the laptop keeps $100\%-7\%=93\%$ of its value. As a decimal, $93\%=0.93$, so the multiplier is $0.93$.

2. Ava invests £800 at $5\%$ compound interest per year for 3 years. What is the final amount?

   - £840.00

   - £920.00

   - £926.00

   - £926.10 ✔

   _Explanation:_ The compound-interest multiplier is $1.05$, so the final amount is $800\times1.05^3=£926.10$.

3. £2500 is invested at $4\%$ compound interest per year for 2 years. How much interest is earned altogether?

   - £204 ✔

   - £200

   - £2704

   - £104

   _Explanation:_ The final amount is $2500\times1.04^2=£2704$. Subtracting the original £2500 gives total interest of $£2704-£2500=£204$.

4. A car worth £12,000 loses $20\%$ of its value in the first year and $10\%$ in the second year. What is it worth after 2 years?

   - £8400

   - £9600

   - £8640 ✔

   - £9720

   _Explanation:_ Use a multiplier of $0.80$ for the first year and $0.90$ for the second: $12000\times0.80\times0.90=£8640$. The percentage losses should not simply be added.

5. A saver invests £4000 for 4 years. Bank A pays $3\%$ compound interest per year, while Bank B pays $3.2\%$ simple interest per year. Which bank gives more money, and by how much to the nearest penny?

   - Bank A by £9.96

   - Bank B by £9.96 ✔

   - Bank B by £512.00

   - Bank A by £502.04

   _Explanation:_ Bank A gives $4000\times1.03^4=£4502.04$ to the nearest penny. Bank B gives $4000+(4000\times0.032\times4)=£4512.00$, so Bank B gives £9.96 more.
