# Substitution

Learn how to replace letters with numbers carefully, including negatives, brackets, powers and formulae.

Substitution means putting a number into a formula or expression in place of a letter

**Example 1: Find the value of 4a + b when a = 5 and b = 3**

In this question we are going to change a into 5 and b into 3

We cannot write 45 + 3 because 4b means 4 times b  
We can instead use brackets to substitute the numbers into the expression:  
4(5) + (3)

4(5) means 4 × 5  
4 × 5 = 20

We now have: 20 + 3  
20 + 3 = 23


**Example 2: Find the value of 4a - 3b when a = 7 and b = 2**

This time we are going to change a into 7 and b into 2

We can use brackets to substitute the numbers into the expression:  
4(7) - 3(2)

4(7) means 4 × 7  
4 × 7 = 28

3(2) means 3 × 2  
3 × 2 = 6

We now have: 28 - 6  
28 - 6 = 22


**Practice questions**

1. Given $a=4$ and $b=7$, work out $3a+2b$.

   - $19$

   - $21$

   - $26$ ✔

   - $33$

   _Explanation:_ Substitute the values and multiply first: $3(4)+2(7)=12+14=26$.

2. Given $m=6$ and $n=2$, work out $5m-4n$.

   - $22$ ✔

   - $38$

   - $18$

   - $10$

   _Explanation:_ Substitution gives $5(6)-4(2)=30-8=22$. The terms are subtracted, not added.


**Example 3: Find the value of 4a² - 3b when a = 3 and b = 7**

4(3)² - 3(7)

The order of operations tells us that the first step is to square the 3

3² = 9

We now have: 4(9) - 3(7)

4 × 9 = 36  
3 × 7 = 21

36 - 21 = 15


**Example 4: Find the value of 5a² - ab when a = -3 and b = -2**

5(-3)² - (-3)(-2)

The order of operations tells us that the first step is to square the negative 3

A negative times a negative is a positive  
(-3)² = (-3) × (-3) = 9

We now have: 5(9) - (-3)(-2)

5 × 9 = 45  
\-3 × -2 = 6

45 - 6 = 39


**Practice questions**

1. Given $a=3$, $b=-4$ and $c=2$, work out $b^2-2ac$.

   - $-28$

   - $4$ ✔

   - $-4$

   - $28$

   _Explanation:_ Use brackets around the negative value: $(-4)^2-2(3)(2)=16-12=4$. Squaring a negative number gives a positive result.

2. Given $x=-5$ and $y=3$, work out $2x^2-y$.

   - $-53$

   - $19$

   - $53$

   - $47$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Square before multiplying: $2(-5)^2-3=2(25)-3=50-3=47$.


**Example 5: Find the value of 5ab - bc when a = 3, b = -2 and c = 4**

5(3)(-2) - (-2)(4)

We can do the multiplication of 5 × 3 × -2 in any order  
5 × 3 = 15  
15 × -2 = -30 A positive times a negative is a negative

\-2 × 4 = -8

We now have: -30 - -8

When we take away a negative we go up the number line -30 - - 8 = -30 + 8 = -22


**Practice questions**

1. Given $p=-3$ and $r=-5$, work out $4p-2r$.

   - $-22$

   - $-2$ ✔

   - $2$

   - $-10$

   _Explanation:_ Substitute using brackets: $4(-3)-2(-5)=-12-(-10)=-12+10=-2$. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding.

2. A formula is $R=\frac{p^2-q^2}{2r}$. Find $R$ when $p=7$, $q=3$ and $r=5$.

   - $8$

   - $20$

   - $5$

   - $4$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Substitute into the numerator and denominator: $R=\frac{7^2-3^2}{2(5)}=\frac{49-9}{10}=\frac{40}{10}=4$.

3. Given $a=3$, $b=-2$ and $c=4$, work out $2ab+bc$.

   - $-4$

   - $20$

   - $-20$ ✔

   - $-14$

   _Explanation:_ Substitute all three values: $2(3)(-2)+(-2)(4)=-12-8=-20$. Both products are negative.

4. Given $k=5$, work out $3(k+2)-4$.

   - $17$ ✔

   - $13$

   - $21$

   - $25$

   _Explanation:_ Substitute $k=5$ and evaluate the bracket first: $3(5+2)-4=3(7)-4=21-4=17$.
