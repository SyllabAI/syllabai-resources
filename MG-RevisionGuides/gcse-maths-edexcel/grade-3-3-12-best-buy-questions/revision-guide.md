# Best Buy Questions

Learn how to compare packs and offers by finding the cost for one item, gram, kilogram or millilitre.

To work out which offer/product offers the best value for money we need to either compare:  
• the price for the same amount of the product  
• or how much of the product you get for the same amount of money

We need to either make the amount the same or the price the same.

## Example: Tea bags are available in the supermarket in small packs or large packs.

**Small packs: 160 tea bags for £2.50

Large packs: 240 tea bags for £4

****Which pack offers better value for money?**

We can either make the number of tea bags the same or the price the same

To make the number of tea bags the same we need to find a common factor, or a common multiple, of 160 and 240

480 is a common multiple of 160 and 240

We can compare the price of 480 tea bags by buying 3 small packs and 2 large packs

160 × 3 = 480  
£2.50 × 3 = £7.50  
3 small packs gives us 480 tea bags for £7.50

240 × 2 = 480  
£4 × 2 = £8  
2 large packs gives us 480 tea bags for £8

£7.50 < £8  
The small pack offers better value for money

To make the price the same we need to find a common factor, or a common multiple, 2.5 and 4

20 is a common multiple of 2.5 and 4

We can work out how many tea bags we would get for £20  
We need to multiply the small pack by 8 and the large pack by 5

160 × 8 = 1280  
£2.50 × 8 = £20  
With the small packs we get 1280 tea bags for £20

240 × 5 = 1200  
£4 × 5 = £20  
With the large packs we get 1200 tea bags for £20

1280 tea bags > 1200 tea bags  
The small pack offers better value for money

## Try these:

**Practice questions**

1. A 500 ml bottle costs £1.45 and a 750 ml bottle costs £2.10. Which bottle offers better value?

   - The 500 ml bottle

   - They are both the same value

   - The 750 ml bottle ✔

   - It is impossible to tell

   _Explanation:_ Compare the cost of 100 ml. The 500 ml bottle costs 29p per 100 ml, while the 750 ml bottle costs 28p per 100 ml, so the 750 ml bottle is better value.

2. A pack of 10 cereal bars costs £3.00 and has 20% extra free. What is the cost per bar?

   - 30p

   - 25p ✔

   - 24p

   - 20p

   _Explanation:_ 20% of 10 is 2, so the pack contains 12 bars. £3.00 divided by 12 is 25p per bar.

3. A shop sells 8 toilet rolls for £2.40 or 5 toilet rolls for £1.70. Which pack is better value?

   - 8 rolls for £2.40 ✔

   - 5 rolls for £1.70

   - They are the same value

   - It is impossible to tell

   _Explanation:_ The 8-roll pack costs £2.40 ÷ 8 = 30p per roll. The 5-roll pack costs £1.70 ÷ 5 = 34p per roll, so the 8-roll pack is better value.

4. A 400 g jar of coffee normally costs £4.00. Offer A gives 25% extra free for £4.00. Offer B gives 20% off the normal 400 g jar. Which offer is better value?

   - Offer A

   - Offer B

   - It is impossible to compare them

   - They are the same value ✔

   _Explanation:_ Offer A gives 500 g for £4.00, which is 80p per 100 g. Offer B costs £3.20 for 400 g, which is also 80p per 100 g, so the offers have the same value.

5. Three bags of rice are on sale: Bag A is 2 kg for £3.20, Bag B is 3 kg for £4.65, and Bag C is 5 kg for £8.00. Which is the best value?

   - Bag A

   - Bags A and C

   - Bag B ✔

   - Bag C

   _Explanation:_ Compare the cost per kilogram. Bag A costs £1.60 per kg, Bag B costs £1.55 per kg, and Bag C costs £1.60 per kg. Therefore, Bag B is the best value.

6. A supermarket sells apples in two different packs. Pack A contains 6 apples for £1.50. Pack B contains 10 apples for £2.30. Which pack is the best value?

   - Pack A

   - Pack B ✔

   - They are both the same value

   - It is impossible to tell

7. A brand of coffee is sold in two sizes. A 200g jar costs £3.80. A 350g jar costs £6.30. Which jar is better value?

   - The 200g jar

   - The 350g jar ✔

   - They are both the same value

   - More information is needed

8. Washing up liquid is sold in three different sizes. * Small: 500 ml for £1.25 * Medium: 750 ml for £1.80 * Large: 1.2 litres for £2.76 Which size is the best value?

   - Small
