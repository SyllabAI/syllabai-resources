# Spheres and Cones

Learn the key formulas and diagram skills for finding volumes and outside areas of round 3D shapes.

**Practice questions**

1. A cone has base diameter $8$ cm and perpendicular height $9$ cm. What is its volume in terms of $\pi$?

   - $24\pi$ cm³

   - $144\pi$ cm³

   - $48\pi$ cm³ ✔

   - $192\pi$ cm³

   _Explanation:_ The radius is $8\div2=4$ cm. Therefore, $V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h=\frac{1}{3}\pi\times4^2\times9=48\pi$ cm³.

2. A solid hemisphere has radius $5$ cm. What is its total surface area, including its circular base?

   - $75\pi$ cm² ✔

   - $50\pi$ cm²

   - $25\pi$ cm²

   - $100\pi$ cm²

   _Explanation:_ The curved area is $2\pi r^2$ and the base area is $\pi r^2$, so the total is $3\pi r^2=3\pi\times5^2=75\pi$ cm².

3. A solid cone has radius $5$ cm and perpendicular height $12$ cm. What is its total surface area?

   - $60\pi$ cm²

   - $65\pi$ cm²

   - $85\pi$ cm²

   - $90\pi$ cm² ✔

   _Explanation:_ The slant height is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13$ cm. The total area is $\pi rl+\pi r^2=\pi\times5\times13+\pi\times5^2=90\pi$ cm².

4. A metal sphere of radius $4$ cm is fully submerged in a rectangular tank with base dimensions $16$ cm by $10$ cm. To $3$ significant figures, how far does the water level rise?

   - $0.419$ cm

   - $1.68$ cm ✔

   - $1.60$ cm

   - $5.03$ cm

   _Explanation:_ The sphere displaces $\frac{4}{3}\pi\times4^3$ cm³ of water. Dividing this by the tank's base area, $16\times10$, gives a rise of approximately $1.68$ cm.

5. A shape consists of a cone joined to a hemisphere. Both have radius $3$ cm, and the cone has perpendicular height $4$ cm. What is the total volume?

   - $18\pi$ cm³

   - $48\pi$ cm³

   - $54\pi$ cm³

   - $30\pi$ cm³ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The cone's volume is $\frac{1}{3}\pi\times3^2\times4=12\pi$ cm³. The hemisphere's volume is $\frac{2}{3}\pi\times3^3=18\pi$ cm³, giving $12\pi+18\pi=30\pi$ cm³.
