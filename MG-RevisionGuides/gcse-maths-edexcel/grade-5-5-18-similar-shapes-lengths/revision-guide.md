# Similar Shapes (Lengths)

Use scale factors to unlock matching lengths in triangles, parallel-line diagrams, ratio problems, and rectangle similarity checks.

**Practice questions**

1. Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $9$ cm and $15$ cm. If another side of the smaller triangle is $12$ cm, what is the corresponding side of the larger triangle?

   - $7.2$ cm

   - $18$ cm

   - $20$ cm ✔

   - $24$ cm

   _Explanation:_ The scale factor from the smaller triangle to the larger triangle is $15\div9=\frac{5}{3}$. Therefore, the corresponding length is $12\times\frac{5}{3}=20$ cm.

2. In triangle $ACD$, point $B$ lies on $AC$, point $E$ lies on $AD$, and $BE$ is parallel to $CD$. Given $AB=6$ cm, $BC=3$ cm and $AE=8$ cm, find $ED$.

   - $3$ cm

   - $5.3$ cm

   - $12$ cm

   - $4$ cm ✔

   _Explanation:_ The whole length $AC$ is $6+3=9$ cm, so the scale factor from triangle $ABE$ to triangle $ACD$ is $9\div6=1.5$. Thus $AD=8\times1.5=12$ cm, giving $ED=12-8=4$ cm.

3. Triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$ are similar, with $A$ matching $P$, $B$ matching $Q$ and $C$ matching $R$. If $AB=8$ cm, $AC=14$ cm and $PQ=12$ cm, what is $PR$?

   - $21$ cm ✔

   - $18$ cm

   - $9.3$ cm

   - $24$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Since $AB$ corresponds to $PQ$, the scale factor is $12\div8=1.5$. As $AC$ corresponds to $PR$, $PR=14\times1.5=21$ cm.

4. The corresponding widths of two similar shapes are $9$ cm and $15$ cm. A length on the smaller shape is $6$ cm. What is the corresponding length on the larger shape?

   - $3.6$ cm

   - $10$ cm ✔

   - $12$ cm

   - $15$ cm

   _Explanation:_ The scale factor from the smaller shape to the larger shape is $15\div9=\frac{5}{3}$. The required length is therefore $6\times\frac{5}{3}=10$ cm.

5. Rectangle A is $12$ cm long and $5$ cm wide. Rectangle B is $18$ cm long and $8$ cm wide. Which conclusion is correct?

   - The rectangles are similar because all rectangles have equal corresponding angles.

   - The rectangles are similar because both dimensions have increased.

   - The rectangles are not similar because similar shapes must be the same size.

   - The rectangles are not similar because the corresponding scale factors are different. ✔

   _Explanation:_ For the lengths, the scale factor is $18\div12=1.5$, but for the widths it is $8\div5=1.6$. Similar shapes require one consistent scale factor for every pair of corresponding lengths.
