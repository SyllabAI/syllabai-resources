# Cylinders

Build confidence with cylinder volume, surface area, half-cylinders, missing lengths and practical unit conversions.

**Practice questions**

1. A cylinder has diameter $12$ cm and height $5$ cm. Which expression gives its volume?

   - $\pi \times 6^2 \times 5$ ✔

   - $\pi \times 12^2 \times 5$

   - $2\pi \times 6 \times 5$

   - $\pi \times 6^2$

   _Explanation:_ The radius is half the diameter, so $r=6$ cm. Using $V=\pi r^2h$ gives $V=\pi \times 6^2 \times 5$.

2. A cylinder has radius $4$ cm and height $10$ cm. What is its volume in terms of $\pi$?

   - $40\pi$ cm³

   - $80\pi$ cm³

   - $160\pi$ cm³ ✔

   - $400\pi$ cm³

   _Explanation:_ Using $V=\pi r^2h$, the volume is $\pi \times 4^2 \times 10=160\pi$ cm³.

3. A closed cylinder has radius $3$ cm and height $8$ cm. What is its total surface area?

   - $48\pi$ cm²

   - $66\pi$ cm² ✔

   - $57\pi$ cm²

   - $42\pi$ cm²

   _Explanation:_ The two circular ends have area $2\pi r^2=18\pi$, and the curved surface has area $2\pi rh=48\pi$. The total is $66\pi$ cm².

4. A cylindrical tank has radius $20$ cm and volume $12$ litres. What is its height to the nearest centimetre? Use $1$ litre $=1000$ cm³.

   - $30$ cm

   - $38$ cm

   - $191$ cm

   - $10$ cm ✔

   _Explanation:_ First convert $12$ litres to $12000$ cm³. From $12000=\pi \times 20^2 \times h$, $h=\frac{12000}{400\pi}\approx9.55$ cm, which rounds to $10$ cm.

5. A semi-cylinder has radius $4$ cm and length $10$ cm. What is its total surface area?

   - $56\pi+80$ cm² ✔

   - $56\pi$ cm²

   - $40\pi+80$ cm²

   - $112\pi+80$ cm²

   _Explanation:_ The half-curved surface is $40\pi$ cm², the two semicircular ends total $16\pi$ cm², and the rectangular face is $8\times10=80$ cm². Therefore, the total is $56\pi+80$ cm².
