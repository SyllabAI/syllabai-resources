# Enlarging with Negative Scale Factors

**Practice questions**

1. Point $A$ has coordinates $(3,-1)$. It is enlarged by scale factor $-2$ with centre $(0,0)$. What are the coordinates of its image?

   - $(6,-2)$

   - $(-6,2)$ ✔

   - $(-6,-2)$

   - $(1.5,-0.5)$

   _Explanation:_ With centre $(0,0)$, multiply both coordinates by $-2$: $(3,-1)\times -2=(-6,2)$. The negative sign places the image on the opposite side of the centre.

2. Point $P=(4,6)$ is enlarged by scale factor $-1$ about the centre $C=(1,2)$. What is the image of $P$?

   - $(3,4)$

   - $(-4,-6)$

   - $(2,4)$

   - $(-2,-2)$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The vector from $C$ to $P$ is $(4-1,6-2)=(3,4)$. Multiplying by $-1$ gives $(-3,-4)$, so the image is $(1,2)+(-3,-4)=(-2,-2)$.

3. An enlargement has centre $C=(2,1)$. Point $A=(4,2)$ maps to $A'=(-2,-1)$. What is the scale factor?

   - $-2$ ✔

   - $2$

   - $-\frac{1}{2}$

   - $\frac{1}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ From $C$ to $A$, the vector is $(2,1)$. From $C$ to $A'$, it is $(-4,-2)$, which is $-2$ times $(2,1)$. Therefore, the scale factor is $-2$.

4. A side of a shape is $8$ cm long. The shape is enlarged using scale factor $-\frac{3}{2}$. How long is the corresponding side of the image?

   - $-12$ cm

   - $\frac{16}{3}$ cm

   - $12$ cm ✔

   - $4$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Lengths are multiplied by the magnitude of the scale factor: $8\times\left|-\frac{3}{2}\right|=12$ cm. A length cannot be negative; the negative sign describes the image's position relative to the centre.

5. A point is enlarged with scale factor $-\frac{1}{2}$. Which statement correctly describes its image?

   - It is on the same side of the centre and twice as far away.

   - It is on the opposite side of the centre and half as far away. ✔

   - It is on the opposite side of the centre and twice as far away.

   - It is on the same side of the centre and half as far away.

   _Explanation:_ A negative scale factor places the image on the opposite side of the centre. Since the magnitude is $\frac{1}{2}$, the image is half as far from the centre.
