# Box Plots

Learn how box plots summarise data clearly using medians, quartiles, ranges, and comparisons.

## Box Plots

**Practice questions**

1. A box plot has a minimum of $6$, lower quartile $14$, median $22$, upper quartile $31$ and maximum $40$. What is the interquartile range?

   - $9$

   - $34$

   - $17$ ✔

   - $25$

   _Explanation:_ The interquartile range is the width of the box: $Q_3-Q_1=31-14=17$.

2. A box plot has a maximum of $67$ and a range of $48$. What is the minimum?

   - $19$ ✔

   - $26$

   - $48$

   - $115$

   _Explanation:_ Since range $=$ maximum $-$ minimum, the minimum is $67-48=19$.

3. The ordered data set is $4,6,7,9,10,12,13,15,17,18,20$. What are $Q_1$, the median and $Q_3$?

   - $8,12,16$

   - $7,10,17$

   - $4,12,20$

   - $7,12,17$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The middle value is $12$. Excluding it, the medians of the lower and upper halves are $7$ and $17$, so the quartiles are $7,12,17$.

4. Runner A has median time $56$ seconds and interquartile range $12$ seconds. Runner B has median time $58$ seconds and interquartile range $6$ seconds. Which conclusion is correct?

   - Runner B was typically faster and more consistent.

   - Runner A was typically faster and Runner B was more consistent. ✔

   - Runner A was typically faster and more consistent.

   - Runner B was typically faster and Runner A was more consistent.

   _Explanation:_ A lower median time means Runner A was typically faster. A smaller interquartile range means Runner B's times were more consistent.

5. A cumulative frequency graph represents $80$ test scores. The scores read at cumulative frequencies $20$, $40$ and $60$ are $45$, $58$ and $74$. The minimum is $7$ and the maximum is $92$. Which five-number summary should be used to draw a box plot?

   - $7,20,40,60,92$

   - $7,40,58,74,92$

   - $7,45,58,74,92$ ✔

   - $7,45,60,74,92$

   _Explanation:_ For $80$ values, the quartiles are located at cumulative frequencies $20$, $40$ and $60$. Their corresponding scores are $45$, $58$ and $74$, giving $7,45,58,74,92$.
