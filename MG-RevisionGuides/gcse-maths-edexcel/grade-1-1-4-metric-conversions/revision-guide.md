# Metric Conversions

Learn quick multiply-and-divide rules for changing between common metric units used in everyday GCSE questions.

When we have the prefix (before a word) kilo it means 1000

1 kilogram = 1000 grams

1 kilometre = 1000 metres

When we have the prefix (before a word) milli it means 1⁄1000

1000 millimetres = 1 metre

1000 millilitres = 1 litre

When we have the prefix centi it means 1⁄100

100 centimetres = 1 metre

**Practice questions**

1. A piece of string is 4 m long. What is this length in centimetres?

   - 400 cm ✔

   - 40 cm

   - 0.04 cm

   - 4000 cm

   _Explanation:_ Metres are converted to smaller centimetres by multiplying by 100: $4 \times 100 = 400$ cm.

2. A screw is 28 mm long. What is its length in centimetres?

   - 280 cm

   - 28.0 cm

   - 0.28 cm

   - 2.8 cm ✔

   _Explanation:_ There are 10 millimetres in 1 centimetre, so divide by 10: $28 \div 10 = 2.8$ cm.

3. A road sign says one park is 1.2 km away, and another is 950 m away. Which distance is longer?

   - 1.2 km, because it is 1200 m ✔

   - 950 m, because it is 9500 m

   - They are the same distance

   - 950 m, because 1.2 km is 120 m

   _Explanation:_ Convert kilometres to metres using $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$. Thus $1.2\text{ km}=1200\text{ m}$, which is longer than 950 m.
