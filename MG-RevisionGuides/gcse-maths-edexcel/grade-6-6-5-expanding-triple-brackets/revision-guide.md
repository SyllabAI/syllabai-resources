# Expanding Triple Brackets

Learn a reliable bracket-by-bracket method for turning three multiplied brackets into one simplified cubic expression.

**Practice questions**

1. Expand and simplify $(x+1)(x+2)(x+3)$.

   - $x^3+5x^2+11x+6$

   - $x^2+3x+2$

   - $x^3+6x^2+11x+6$ ✔

   - $x^3+6x^2+6x+6$

   _Explanation:_ First, $(x+1)(x+2)=x^2+3x+2$. Multiplying this by $(x+3)$ gives $x^3+6x^2+11x+6$.

2. When expanding $(x+2)(x+5)(x-1)$, what do the first two brackets simplify to?

   - $x^2+7x+10$ ✔

   - $x^2+10$

   - $x^2+5x+10$

   - $2x^2+7x+10$

   _Explanation:_ Expanding $(x+2)(x+5)$ gives $x^2+5x+2x+10=x^2+7x+10$. This can then be multiplied by the third bracket.

3. Expand and simplify $(x-3)(x+4)(x+2)$.

   - $x^3+3x^2+10x-24$

   - $x^3+3x^2-10x+24$

   - $x^3+x^2-10x-24$

   - $x^3+3x^2-10x-24$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ First, $(x-3)(x+4)=x^2+x-12$. Multiplying by $(x+2)$ gives $x^3+3x^2-10x-24$.

4. Expand and simplify $(2x-1)(x+3)(x-2)$.

   - $2x^3+3x^2-11x-6$

   - $2x^3+x^2-13x+6$ ✔

   - $2x^3+x^2-11x+6$

   - $x^3+x^2-13x+6$

   _Explanation:_ First, $(x+3)(x-2)=x^2+x-6$. Multiplying by $(2x-1)$ and collecting like terms gives $2x^3+x^2-13x+6$.

5. Expand and simplify $(x-2)^2(x+3)$.

   - $x^3+x^2-8x+12$

   - $x^3-x^2+8x+12$

   - $x^3-x^2-8x+12$ ✔

   - $x^3-4x^2+4x+12$

   _Explanation:_ First, $(x-2)^2=x^2-4x+4$. Multiplying by $(x+3)$ gives $x^3-x^2-8x+12$.
