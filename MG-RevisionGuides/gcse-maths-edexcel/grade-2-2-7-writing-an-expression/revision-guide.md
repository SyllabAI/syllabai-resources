# Writing an Expression

Learn how to turn short word problems into algebraic expressions using letters, operations, and simple totals.

We can calculate using unknown numbers by using letters in the place of the unknown numbers.

When we use letters instead of unknown numbers the answer will be an expression.

If we multiply n by 5 we get n × 5  
We don't write a times sign in algebra, so we simplify n × 5 to 5n  
(We write the number first to not mistake 5 × n for n5)

If we have 5 more than n we have n + 5

If we have 5 less than n we have n - 5

If we have n divided by 5 we have n ÷ 5 or n⁄5

**Practice questions**

1. An online game score is $x$. Sam scores 8 more points. Which expression shows Sam's score?

   - $x+8$ ✔

   - $8x$

   - $x-8$

   - $8-x$

   _Explanation:_ “8 more” means add 8 to the original score, giving $x+8$. The expression $8x$ means 8 times the score.

2. A ribbon has length $r$ cm. A shorter piece is half as long. Which expression gives the length of the shorter piece?

   - $2r$

   - $r-2$

   - $\frac{r}{2}$ ✔

   - $r+2$

   _Explanation:_ To find half of a length, divide it by 2. Therefore, the shorter piece has length $\frac{r}{2}$ cm.


**Example 1: Apples cost 30p each. Write an algebraic expression for the cost of *x* apples.**

We need to multiply 30 by *x*.

30 × *x* = 30*x*

The total cost is 30*x* pence.

**Example 2: William has m marbles. Simon has 10 more marbles than William. Write an algebraic expression for the numbers of marbles Simon has.**

We need to add 10 onto m

The answer is m + 10


**Example 3: You buy 5 cups of tea for £p Write an expression for the price of one cup of tea.**

We need divide p by 5

The answer is p⁄5

**Practice questions**

1. Ben is $n$ years old. Ava is 6 years older than Ben and Kai is 3 years younger than Ben. Which simplified expression gives the total of their ages?

   - $3n+9$

   - $3n+3$ ✔

   - $n+3$

   - $3n-3$

   _Explanation:_ Their ages are $n$, $n+6$ and $n-3$. Adding gives $n+(n+6)+(n-3)=3n+3$.

2. A pen costs 30p and a ruler costs 45p. Sami buys $m$ pens and $r$ rulers. Which formula gives the total cost, $C$ pence?

   - $C=30+m+45+r$

   - $C=45m+30r$

   - $30m+45r$

   - $C=30m+45r$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The pens cost $30m$ pence and the rulers cost $45r$ pence, so the formula is $C=30m+45r$.

3. Tickets cost £4 each. A club receives a total of £$T$ from ticket sales. Which expression gives the number of tickets sold?

   - $4T$

   - $\frac{T}{4}$ ✔

   - $T+4$

   - $4-T$

   _Explanation:_ Divide the total amount received by the price of one ticket. The number sold is therefore $\frac{T}{4}$.
