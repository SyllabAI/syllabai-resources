## The ANS button

The ANS button stores your last answer

![Calculator buttons ans](../assets/028-Calculator-buttons-ans.jpg)

If we type 3 × 5 into the calculator and then press equals:

![Calculator buttons 5](../assets/006-Calculator-buttons-5.jpg)

The answer (15) is now stored as ANS

We can then do calculations using the ANS button

![Calculator buttons ans](../assets/028-Calculator-buttons-ans.jpg)

![Calculator buttons plus](../assets/025-Calculator-buttons-plus.jpg)

![Calculator buttons 3](../assets/023-Calculator-buttons-3.jpg)

**Practice questions**

1. A calculator gives $4\times7=28$. What is the value of $\text{ANS}-9$?

   - 37

   - 19 ✔

   - 21

   - 4

   _Explanation:_ ANS stores the previous answer, which is $28$. Therefore, $\text{ANS}-9=28-9=19$.

2. A calculator gives $2.5\times8=20$. You then enter $\text{ANS}\div5$. What answer should you get?

   - 1.6

   - 2.5

   - 40

   - 4 ✔

   _Explanation:_ ANS stores $20$, so the next calculation is $20\div5=4$.

**Practice questions**

1. You calculate $2\times5$ and store the answer as $A$. What is the value of $A+7$?

   - 12

   - 17 ✔

   - 35

   - 10

   _Explanation:_ The stored value is $A=2\times5=10$. Substituting this into the calculation gives $A+7=10+7=17$.

2. You calculate $3^2$ and store the answer as $B$. What is $2B-1$?

   - 8

   - 19

   - 17 ✔

   - 81

   _Explanation:_ First, $B=3^2=9$. Therefore, $2B-1=2\times9-1=17$.
