# Simultaneous Equations

Learn how to solve two linked linear equations by eliminating one variable and checking your answers.

**Practice questions**

1. Solve the simultaneous equations $2x=8+y$ and $x+y=7$.

   - $x=1$, $y=6$

   - $x=4$, $y=3$

   - $x=5$, $y=3$

   - $x=5$, $y=2$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Rewrite the first equation as $2x-y=8$. Adding it to $x+y=7$ gives $3x=15$, so $x=5$. Substitution then gives $y=2$.

2. Solve $4x+3y=18$ and $x+3y=9$.

   - $x=6$, $y=-1$

   - $x=2$, $y=3$

   - $x=3$, $y=2$ ✔

   - $x=3$, $y=1$

   _Explanation:_ Subtracting the second equation from the first eliminates $y$: $3x=9$, so $x=3$. Then $3+3y=9$, giving $y=2$.

3. Solve $2x+y=13$ and $3x+2y=22$.

   - $x=5$, $y=3$

   - $x=4$, $y=5$ ✔

   - $x=2$, $y=9$

   - $x=4$, $y=6$

   _Explanation:_ Double the first equation to get $4x+2y=26$. Subtracting $3x+2y=22$ gives $x=4$, and substitution into $2x+y=13$ gives $y=5$.

4. Which pair satisfies both $2x+y=9$ and $x-y=3$?

   - $x=4$, $y=1$ ✔

   - $x=3$, $y=3$

   - $x=5$, $y=-1$

   - $x=4$, $y=5$

   _Explanation:_ For $x=4$ and $y=1$, $2(4)+1=9$ and $4-1=3$. The pair must make both equations true.

5. Two adult tickets and three child tickets cost £31. One adult ticket and two child tickets cost £18. What are the individual ticket prices?

   - Adult £5, child £8

   - Adult £7, child £6

   - Adult £8, child £5 ✔

   - Adult £13, child £5

   _Explanation:_ Let the prices be $a$ and $c$. The equations are $2a+3c=31$ and $a+2c=18$. Doubling the second and subtracting the first gives $c=5$, then $a=8$.
