# Averages

Learn how to find range, mode, median and mean, including missing numbers in simple GCSE data questions.

The mean, the median and the mode are three different measures of average which we can use. The range is a measure of how spread out data is.

## The Mode

The mode is the value that appears most often.

## The Median

The median is the middle number (when the numbers are in order).

## The Mean

To calculate the mean add up all the numbers and then divide by how many numbers there are.

## The Range

The range is the difference between the biggest number and the smallest number.

**Practice questions**

1. What is the mode of $3, 5, 3, 7, 8, 3, 5$?

   - $5$

   - $3$ ✔

   - $7$

   - There is no mode

   _Explanation:_ The mode is the value that appears most often. The number $3$ appears three times, more than any other value.

2. Find the median of $12, 5, 2, 9, 8$ and $10$.

   - $9$

   - $5.5$

   - $8.5$ ✔

   - $8$

   _Explanation:_ In order, the numbers are $2, 5, 8, 9, 10, 12$. The median is halfway between the two middle values: $(8+9)\div2=8.5$.

3. The lengths are $4$ cm, $8$ cm, $8$ cm, $12$ cm and $15$ cm. What is the range?

   - $19$ cm

   - $15$ cm

   - $8$ cm

   - $11$ cm ✔

   _Explanation:_ The range is the largest value minus the smallest value, so $15-4=11$ cm.


**Example 1: Here are a list of 7 numbers**

7   4   7   9   5   1   2  
**Find:  
a) The mode  
b) The median  
c) The mean  
d) The range**


a) The mode is the most common number  
The only number that appears more than once is 7  
The mode is 7

b) The median is the middle number (but only when the numbers are in order)  
We have to put the numbers in order:  
1   2   4   5   7   7   9  
We can now find the middle number:

![median1](../assets/001-median1.gif)

The median is 5

c) To find the mean we need to add up all the numbers then divide by how many numbers there are:

1 + 2 + 4 + 5 + 7 + 7 + 9 ⁄7 = 35⁄7 = 5

The mean is 5

d) To find the range we take the smallest number away from the biggest number  
9 - 1 = 8  
The range is 8

**Example 2: Here are a list of 8 numbers**

6   6   13   5   11   11   11   9  
**Find:  
a) The mode  
b) The median  
c) The mean  
d) The range**


a) The mode is the most common number  
11 appears three times, that is more than any other number  
The mode is 11

b) To find the median (the middle number) we need to put the numbers in order:  
5   6   6   9   11  11  11  13

![median2](../assets/002-median2.gif)

We can see that there is not one middle number, we have 9 and 11 in the middle.  
When this happens the median is half way between the two middle numbers, the middle of 9 and 11 is 10. We can work this out by adding 9 and 11 and then dividing by 2.

9 + 11⁄2 = 10  
The median is 10

c) To find the mean we need to add up all the numbers then divide by how many numbers there are:

5 + 6 + 6 + 9 + 11 + 11 + 11 + 13⁄8 = 72⁄8 = 9

The mean is 9

d) To find the range we take the smallest number away from the biggest number  
13 - 5 = 8  
The range is 8

## Try these:

**Practice questions**

1. Four test scores are $6$, $11$, $13$ and $10$. What is the mean score?

   - $10$ ✔

   - $40$

   - $10.5$

   - $7$

   _Explanation:_ The scores total $6+11+13+10=40$. There are four scores, so the mean is $40\div4=10$.

2. A runner has a mean distance of $12$ km over $4$ days. She runs $17$ km on the fifth day. What is her new mean distance?

   - $14.5$ km

   - $12.5$ km

   - $13$ km ✔

   - $65$ km

   _Explanation:_ The first four days total $12\times4=48$ km. The new total is $48+17=65$ km, so the new mean is $65\div5=13$ km.

3. Six cards show $1, 3, x, 6, 8$ and $y$. Their mean is $5$ and their mode is $3$. What are $x$ and $y$?

   - $4$ and $8$

   - $3$ and $9$ ✔

   - $5$ and $7$

   - $3$ and $8$

   _Explanation:_ A mean of $5$ for six cards requires a total of $5\times6=30$. The known cards total $18$, so $x+y=12$. One hidden value must be $3$ to make $3$ the mode, so the other is $9$.

4. Calculate the mean of the following numbers: 4, 6, 7, 10, 13.

   - 8 ✔

   - 7

   - 9

   - 40

5. Find the median of the following set of numbers: 15, 12, 20, 18, 10, 14.

   - 14.5 ✔

   - 14

   - 15

   - 14.8

6. The mean of four numbers is 10. Three of the numbers are 8, 12, and 9. What is the fourth number?

   - 11 ✔

   - 10

   - 9.67

   - 19
