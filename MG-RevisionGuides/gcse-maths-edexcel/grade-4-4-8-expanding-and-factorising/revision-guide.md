# Expanding and Factorising

Learn how brackets change algebra, then practise expanding, factorising, simplifying and solving simple bracket equations.

**Practice questions**

1. Expand $4(3x-2)$.

   - $12x-2$

   - $12x-8$ ✔

   - $7x-8$

   - $12x+8$

   _Explanation:_ Multiply $4$ by both terms in the bracket: $4\times 3x=12x$ and $4\times(-2)=-8$, giving $12x-8$.

2. Expand and simplify $5(y+3)-2(y-4)$.

   - $3y+7$

   - $7y+23$

   - $7y+7$

   - $3y+23$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Expanding gives $5y+15-2y+8$. The minus sign makes the final term positive, so this simplifies to $3y+23$.

3. Expand and simplify $(x+3)(x+5)$.

   - $x^2+15$

   - $x^2+8x+8$

   - $x^2+8x+15$ ✔

   - $2x^2+8x+15$

   _Explanation:_ Multiplying every term gives $x^2+5x+3x+15$. Combining the middle terms produces $x^2+8x+15$.

4. Factorise fully $15m^2+10mn$.

   - $5m(3m+2n)$ ✔

   - $5(3m^2+2mn)$

   - $5m(3m+2)$

   - $5mn(3m+2)$

   _Explanation:_ The highest common factor of both terms is $5m$. Dividing each term by $5m$ leaves $3m$ and $2n$, so the fully factorised form is $5m(3m+2n)$.

5. Factorise $x^2+7x+12$.

   - $(x+2)(x+6)$

   - $(x+3)(x+4)$ ✔

   - $(x-3)(x-4)$

   - $(x+1)(x+12)$

   _Explanation:_ The two numbers must multiply to $12$ and add to $7$. These numbers are $3$ and $4$, so $x^2+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)$.
