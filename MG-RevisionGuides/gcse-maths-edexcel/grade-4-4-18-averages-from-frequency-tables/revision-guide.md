# Averages from Frequency Tables

Practise turning frequencies into totals so you can find means, medians, modes and estimates confidently.

**Practice questions**

1. The values $0$, $1$, $2$ and $3$ have frequencies $3$, $5$, $4$ and $2$. How many values were recorded altogether?

   - $6$

   - $14$ ✔

   - $17$

   - $20$

   _Explanation:_ Add the frequencies, not the values: $3+5+4+2=14$.

2. The scores $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$ have frequencies $2$, $5$, $1$ and $2$. What is the mean score?

   - $2$

   - $2.5$

   - $10$

   - $2.3$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The total of the scores is $1(2)+2(5)+3(1)+4(2)=23$, and the total frequency is $10$. Therefore, the mean is $23\div10=2.3$.

3. The scores $0$, $1$, $2$ and $3$ have frequencies $3$, $7$, $8$ and $2$. Which pair gives the median and the mode?

   - Median $1.5$, mode $2$ ✔

   - Median $1$, mode $2$

   - Median $2$, mode $1$

   - Median $2$, mode $2$

   _Explanation:_ There are $20$ scores, so the median is the mean of the 10th and 11th scores. These are $1$ and $2$, giving a median of $1.5$. The largest frequency is $8$, so the mode is $2$.

4. The scores $0$, $1$, $2$ and $3$ have frequencies $4$, $6$, $x$ and $2$. The mean score is $1.25$. What is $x$?

   - $2$

   - $3$

   - $4$ ✔

   - $6$

   _Explanation:_ The score total is $0(4)+1(6)+2x+3(2)=12+2x$, and the total frequency is $12+x$. Hence $12+2x=1.25(12+x)$, which solves to give $x=4$.

5. Times are grouped as over $0$ up to $10$, over $10$ up to $20$, and over $20$ up to $40$ minutes, with frequencies $2$, $4$ and $5$. What are the estimated mean and the median class?

   - $17.7$ minutes; over $10$ up to $20$

   - $20$ minutes; over $10$ up to $20$ ✔

   - $20$ minutes; over $20$ up to $40$

   - $27.3$ minutes; over $20$ up to $40$

   _Explanation:_ Using midpoints $5$, $15$ and $30$, the estimated mean is $\frac{5(2)+15(4)+30(5)}{11}=20$ minutes. The median is the 6th value, which lies in the class over $10$ up to $20$.
