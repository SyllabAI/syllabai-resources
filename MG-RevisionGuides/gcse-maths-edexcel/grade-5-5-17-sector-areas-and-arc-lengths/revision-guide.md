# Sector Areas and Arc Lengths

Learn how slices of circles connect angles, areas and curved lengths in GCSE sector questions.

**Practice questions**

1. A sector has a central angle of $90°$. What fraction of a full circle is the sector?

   - $\frac{1}{2}$

   - $\frac{1}{4}$ ✔

   - $\frac{1}{3}$

   - $\frac{3}{4}$

   _Explanation:_ A full circle is $360°$, so the fraction is $\frac{90}{360}=\frac{1}{4}$.

2. A sector has radius $10$ cm and central angle $72°$. What is its arc length in terms of $π$?

   - $20π$ cm

   - $10π$ cm

   - $4π$ cm ✔

   - $2π$ cm

   _Explanation:_ The sector is $\frac{72}{360}=\frac{1}{5}$ of a circle. Its arc length is $\frac{1}{5}\times 2π\times 10=4π$ cm.

3. A major sector has radius $6$ cm and central angle $240°$. What is its area in terms of $π$?

   - $12π$ cm²

   - $18π$ cm²

   - $36π$ cm²

   - $24π$ cm² ✔

   _Explanation:_ The area is $\frac{240}{360}\times π\times 6^2=\frac{2}{3}\times 36π=24π$ cm².

4. A sector has radius $5$ cm and central angle $144°$. What is its perimeter in terms of $π$?

   - $10+4π$ cm ✔

   - $4π$ cm

   - $5+4π$ cm

   - $10π$ cm

   _Explanation:_ The arc length is $\frac{144}{360}\times 2π\times 5=4π$ cm. Adding the two radii gives a perimeter of $4π+5+5=10+4π$ cm.

5. A rectangle has area $200$ cm². A sector inside it has radius $8$ cm and arc length $5π$ cm. If the sector is unshaded, what is the shaded area?

   - $200-40π$ cm²

   - $200-20π$ cm² ✔

   - $20π$ cm²

   - $200-5π$ cm²

   _Explanation:_ Using sector area $=\frac{1}{2}rl$, the unshaded area is $\frac{1}{2}\times 8\times 5π=20π$ cm². Therefore, the shaded area is $200-20π$ cm².
