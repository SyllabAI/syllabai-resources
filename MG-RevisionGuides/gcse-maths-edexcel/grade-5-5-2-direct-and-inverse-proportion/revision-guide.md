# Direct and Inverse Proportion

Spot when quantities scale together or oppositely, then use unit rates and formulas to answer GCSE proportion questions.

**Practice questions**

1. A graph of $y$ against $x$ is a straight line through the origin and passes through the point $(2,10)$. Which formula connects $y$ and $x$?

   - $y=5x$ ✔

   - $y=10x$

   - $y=\frac{10}{x}$

   - $y=5x^2$

   _Explanation:_ Since the graph is a straight line through the origin, $y$ is directly proportional to $x$. The constant is $10\div2=5$, so $y=5x$.

2. A printer produces 150 leaflets in 5 minutes at a steady rate. How many leaflets will it produce in 12 minutes?

   - 300

   - 360 ✔

   - 375

   - 1800

   _Explanation:_ The printer produces $150\div5=30$ leaflets per minute, so in 12 minutes it produces $30\times12=360$ leaflets.

3. $y$ is inversely proportional to $x$. When $x=4$, $y=6$. Find $y$ when $x=8$.

   - 12

   - 2

   - 3 ✔

   - 48

   _Explanation:_ For inverse proportion, $xy$ is constant. Here $4\times6=24$, so when $x=8$, $y=24\div8=3$.

4. Six identical machines take 10 hours to complete a job. How long would four machines take to complete the same job?

   - $6.7$ hours

   - 10 hours

   - 24 hours

   - 15 hours ✔

   _Explanation:_ The number of machines and the time are inversely proportional. The job requires $6\times10=60$ machine-hours, so four machines take $60\div4=15$ hours.

5. A force is given by $F=\frac{80}{d^2}$. What is $F$ when $d=4$?

   - 5 ✔

   - 20

   - 16

   - 320

   _Explanation:_ Substitute $d=4$ and square it first: $F=\frac{80}{4^2}=\frac{80}{16}=5$.
