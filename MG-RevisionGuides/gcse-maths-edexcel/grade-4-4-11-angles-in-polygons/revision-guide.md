# Angles in Polygons

Master polygon angle facts so you can find missing angles and identify regular shapes.

**Practice questions**

1. What is the sum of the interior angles of a decagon?

   - $1800°$

   - $1440°$ ✔

   - $1080°$

   - $360°$

   _Explanation:_ A decagon has 10 sides, so its interior angles sum to $(10-2)\times180°=1440°$.

2. A hexagon has interior angles $110°$, $125°$, $130°$, $145°$, $x$ and $x$. What is $x$?

   - $210°$

   - $95°$

   - $105°$ ✔

   - $120°$

   _Explanation:_ The angles in a hexagon total $720°$. The known angles total $510°$, leaving $210°$ for the two equal angles, so $x=210°\div2=105°$.

3. What is the size of one exterior angle of a regular octagon?

   - $135°$

   - $60°$

   - $40°$

   - $45°$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The exterior angles of any polygon total $360°$. A regular octagon has 8 equal exterior angles, so each is $360°\div8=45°$.

4. A regular polygon has an interior angle of $156°$. How many sides does it have?

   - 15 ✔

   - 24

   - 12

   - 10

   _Explanation:_ Its exterior angle is $180°-156°=24°$. Therefore, the number of sides is $360°\div24°=15$.

5. Two regular pentagons and another regular polygon meet at a point with no gap. How many sides does the other polygon have?

   - 8

   - 10 ✔

   - 12

   - 5

   _Explanation:_ Each regular pentagon has an interior angle of $108°$, so the remaining angle is $360°-108°-108°=144°$. The other polygon's exterior angle is $180°-144°=36°$, giving $360°\div36°=10$ sides.
