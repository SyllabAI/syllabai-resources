# Venn Diagrams

Learn how diagrams organise overlapping groups so you can shade regions, fill counts, and calculate probabilities confidently.

**Practice questions**

1. 80 students were asked whether they play football or tennis. 46 play football, 35 play tennis and 12 play both. How many play neither sport?

   - 23

   - 12

   - 69

   - 11 ✔

   _Explanation:_ The number playing at least one sport is $46+35-12=69$. Therefore, $80-69=11$ students play neither sport.

2. A student chosen at random is in set $A$ with probability $0.73$. What is $P(A')$?

   - $0.27$ ✔

   - $0.73$

   - $0.37$

   - $1.73$

   _Explanation:_ $A'$ means not in $A$. Complementary probabilities total 1, so $P(A')=1-0.73=0.27$.

3. In a two-circle Venn diagram, which region represents $A\cap B'$?

   - The overlap of $A$ and $B$

   - The part of $B$ outside $A$

   - The part of $A$ outside $B$ ✔

   - The region outside both sets

   _Explanation:_ $A\cap B'$ means in $A$ and not in $B$, so it is the part of circle $A$ that does not overlap circle $B$.

4. The universal set is the integers from 1 to 8. Set $A=\{2,3,5,8\}$ and set $B=\{1,3,4,8\}$. One number is chosen at random. What is $P(A\cup B)$?

   - $\frac{1}{4}$

   - $\frac{3}{4}$ ✔

   - $\frac{1}{2}$

   - $1$

   _Explanation:_ $A\cup B=\{1,2,3,4,5,8\}$, which contains 6 of the 8 numbers. Thus $P(A\cup B)=\frac{6}{8}=\frac{3}{4}$.

5. 40 students were asked whether they study Art, Drama or Music. 5 study all three subjects. The pair totals are 14 for Art and Drama, 12 for Art and Music, and 11 for Drama and Music; these totals include those studying all three. Overall, 24 study Art, 22 study Drama and 20 study Music. How many study none of the subjects?

   - 5

   - 11

   - 6 ✔

   - 34

   _Explanation:_ Using inclusion-exclusion, the number studying at least one is $24+22+20-14-12-11+5=34$. Therefore, $40-34=6$ study none.
