# Congruent Triangles

Learn how to prove triangles are identical by matching sides, angles and clear GCSE proof reasons.

**Practice questions**

1. A diagonal $AC$ divides parallelogram $ABCD$ into triangles $ABC$ and $CDA$. Which congruence test proves that the triangles are congruent?

   - SSS ✔

   - SAS

   - ASA

   - RHS

   _Explanation:_ Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal, so $AB=CD$ and $BC=DA$. The triangles also share $AC$, giving three pairs of equal sides, so SSS applies.

2. For triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, you know that $AB=PQ$ and $AC=PR$. Which additional fact is needed to prove congruence by SAS?

   - $\angle ABC=\angle PQR$

   - $BC=QR$

   - $\angle ACB=\angle PRQ$

   - $\angle BAC=\angle QPR$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ SAS requires the angle included between the two known equal sides. The angle between $AB$ and $AC$ is $\angle BAC$, which corresponds to $\angle QPR$.

3. From point $X$, tangents $XP$ and $XQ$ touch a circle with centre $O$. Which test proves that triangles $XPO$ and $XQO$ are congruent?

   - AAA

   - RHS ✔

   - ASA

   - SSS

   _Explanation:_ A radius is perpendicular to a tangent, so both triangles are right-angled. They have equal radii $OP=OQ$ and the common hypotenuse $XO$, so RHS applies.

4. Triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$ share side $AC$. Also, $\angle BAC=\angle CAD$ and $\angle BCA=\angle ACD$. If $BC=9\text{ cm}$, what is $DC$?

   - $4.5\text{ cm}$

   - $18\text{ cm}$

   - $9\text{ cm}$ ✔

   - It cannot be determined

   _Explanation:_ The two equal angle pairs and the common included side $AC$ prove the triangles congruent by ASA. Corresponding sides $BC$ and $DC$ are therefore equal, so $DC=9\text{ cm}$.

5. Triangle $ABC$ is congruent to triangle $DFE$, with corresponding vertices written in the same order. Which side corresponds to $BC$?

   - $FE$ ✔

   - $DF$

   - $DE$

   - $AB$

   _Explanation:_ The order shows that $A$ corresponds to $D$, $B$ to $F$, and $C$ to $E$. Therefore, side $BC$ corresponds to side $FE$.
