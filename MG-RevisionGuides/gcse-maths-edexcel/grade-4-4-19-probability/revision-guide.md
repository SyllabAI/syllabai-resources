# Probability

Turn chance into predictions by filling gaps, sharing remainders, and linking probabilities to real counts.

**Practice questions**

1. A biased coin has a probability of $0.62$ of landing on heads. It is tossed 150 times. Estimate the number of times it will land on heads.

   - 93 ✔

   - 57

   - 62

   - 150

   _Explanation:_ Estimated frequency is probability multiplied by the number of trials: $0.62 \times 150 = 93$.

2. A box contains red, blue, green and white beads. The probabilities of choosing red and white are $0.30$ and $0.10$. Blue is twice as likely as green. There are 30 white beads. How many blue beads are there?

   - 60

   - 90

   - 120 ✔

   - 180

   _Explanation:_ The total number of beads is $30 \div 0.10=300$. Blue and green have probability $1-0.30-0.10=0.60$. Splitting this in the ratio $2:1$ gives blue probability $0.40$, so there are $0.40\times300=120$ blue beads.

3. Red, blue and yellow counters are in the ratio $2:3:5$. What is the probability of choosing a yellow counter?

   - $\frac{5}{8}$

   - $\frac{1}{2}$ ✔

   - $\frac{2}{5}$

   - $\frac{3}{10}$

   _Explanation:_ There are $2+3+5=10$ ratio parts altogether, of which 5 are yellow. Therefore, the probability is $\frac{5}{10}=\frac{1}{2}$.

4. A spinner can land on 1, 2, 3 or 4. The probabilities are $0.18$, $0.25$, $0.31$ and $0.26$ respectively. What is the probability that it lands on 2 or 4?

   - $0.26$

   - $0.06$

   - $0.49$

   - $0.51$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Landing on 2 and landing on 4 are mutually exclusive outcomes, so their probabilities are added: $0.25+0.26=0.51$.

5. A spinner can land only on red, blue or green. The probability of red is $0.28$ and the probability of blue is $0.45$. What is the probability of green?

   - $0.27$ ✔

   - $0.17$

   - $0.73$

   - $0.55$

   _Explanation:_ The probabilities of all possible outcomes total 1. Therefore, the probability of green is $1-0.28-0.45=0.27$.
