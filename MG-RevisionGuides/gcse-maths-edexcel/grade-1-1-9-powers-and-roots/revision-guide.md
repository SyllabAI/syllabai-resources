# Powers and Roots

Learn to read small raised numbers, find missing starting numbers, and pick special number types from lists.

## Squares

To square a number we multiply the number by itself.  
3 squared is 9 because 3 × 3 = 9  
4 squared is 16 because 4 × 4 = 16

We can write squared using a small (superscript) 2.  
5² means 5 squared  
10² means 10 squared

**Example 1: Work out the value of 9²**

To work out the value of 9 squared we need to calculate 9 × 9  
9 × 9 = 81  
9² = 81


**Example 2: Work out the value of (-8)²**

To work out the value of -8 squared we need to calculate -8 × -8  
\-8 × -8 = 64 (A negative times a negative is a positive)  
(-8)² = 64  
If you are using a calculator you must put the -8 in brackets, otherwise the calculator will get the answer wrong!


**Practice questions**

1. Which power represents $6 \times 6$?

   - $6^2$ ✔

   - $2^6$

   - $6 \times 2$

   - $6^3$

   _Explanation:_ There are two factors of 6, so $6 \times 6=6^2$.

2. Work out $(-9)^2$.

   - $-81$

   - $-18$

   - $81$ ✔

   - $18$

   _Explanation:_ The brackets mean that $-9$ is squared. Since $(-9)\times(-9)=81$, the answer is $81$.

3. Work out $12^2$.

   - $24$

   - $144$ ✔

   - $122$

   - $124$

   _Explanation:_ Squaring means multiplying the number by itself, so $12^2=12\times12=144$.

## Cubes

To cube a number we multiply the number by itself, and then multiply that answer by the number again.

We can write cubed using a small (superscript) 3.  
2³ means 2 cubed  
3³ means 3 cubed

**Example 3: Work out the value of 4³**

To work out the value of 4 cubed we need to calculate 4 × 4 × 4  
4 × 4 = 16  
16 × 4 = 64  
4³ = 64


**Example 4: Work out the value of 10³**

To work out the value of 10 cubed we need to calculate 10 × 10 × 10  
10 × 10 = 100  
100 × 10 = 1000  
10³ = 1000


**Practice questions**

1. Work out $6^3$.

   - $216$ ✔

   - $18$

   - $36$

   - $126$

   _Explanation:_ Cubing means multiplying three copies of the number: $6^3=6\times6\times6=216$.

2. Which power represents $4 \times 4 \times 4$?

   - $3^4$

   - $4^2$

   - $4 \times 3$

   - $4^3$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ There are three factors of 4, so the expression is $4^3$.

3. From the list $8, 25, 27, 36, 64, 125$, which option contains all the cube numbers?

   - $8, 25, 64$

   - $27, 36, 125$

   - $8, 27, 64, 125$ ✔

   - $25, 36, 64$

   _Explanation:_ The cube numbers are $2^3=8$, $3^3=27$, $4^3=64$ and $5^3=125$.

## Square Roots

Square rooting is the opposite of sqauring a number.  
9² = 81, so the square root of 81 is 9

Square root 100 can be written as √100  
√81 means square root 81

**Example 5: Work out the value of √144**

We are looking for the number that multiplies by itself to make 144  
12 × 12 = 144  
√144 = 12


**Example 6: Work out the value of √100**

We are looking for the number that multiplies by itself to make 100  
10 × 10 = 100  
√100 = 10


**Practice questions**

1. Find $\sqrt{169}$.

   - $84.5$

   - $13$ ✔

   - $26$

   - $14$

   _Explanation:_ The square root is the number that multiplies by itself to make 169. Since $13\times13=169$, $\sqrt{169}=13$.

2. Which calculation correctly checks that $\sqrt{225}=15$?

   - $225\div2=112.5$

   - $15+15=30$

   - $225-15=210$

   - $15\times15=225$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ A square root can be checked by squaring it. Since $15\times15=225$, $\sqrt{225}=15$.

## Cube Roots

Cube rooting is the opposite of cubing a number.  
10³ = 1000, so the cube root of 1000 is 10

Cube root 125 can be written as ∛125  
∛216 means cube root 216

**Example 7: Work out the value of ∛8**

We are looking for the number that multiplies by itself, and then by itself again, to make 8  
2 × 2 × 2 = 8  
∛8 = 2


**Practice questions**

1. Find $\sqrt[3]{343}$.

   - $49$

   - $6$

   - $7$ ✔

   - $21$

   _Explanation:_ The cube root is the number that multiplies by itself three times to make 343. Since $7\times7\times7=343$, $\sqrt[3]{343}=7$.

2. Which multiplication shows that $\sqrt[3]{216}=6$?

   - $6\times6\times6=216$ ✔

   - $6\times36\times6=1296$

   - $216\div2=108$

   - $6\times6=36$

   _Explanation:_ Cube rooting reverses cubing, and $6^3=6\times6\times6=216$. Therefore, $\sqrt[3]{216}=6$.
