# Standard Form

Learn how to handle very large and very small numbers using powers of ten and clear calculator strategies.

**Practice questions**

1. Write $0.00056$ in standard form.

   - $5.6 \times 10^4$

   - $0.56 \times 10^{-3}$

   - $56 \times 10^{-5}$

   - $5.6 \times 10^{-4}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Move the decimal point 4 places right to make $5.6$. Since the original number is less than 1, the exponent is negative, giving $5.6 \times 10^{-4}$.

2. Write $3.08 \times 10^5$ as an ordinary number.

   - $308000$ ✔

   - $30800$

   - $0.0000308$

   - $3080000$

   _Explanation:_ The positive exponent means move the decimal point 5 places right, so $3.08 \times 10^5=308000$.

3. Write $0.47 \times 10^8$ in standard form.

   - $4.7 \times 10^8$

   - $47 \times 10^7$

   - $4.7 \times 10^7$ ✔

   - $0.47 \times 10^7$

   _Explanation:_ In standard form, the first number must be at least 1 but less than 10. Multiplying $0.47$ by 10 and reducing the exponent by 1 gives $4.7 \times 10^7$.

4. A spacecraft is $6.3 \times 10^{16}$ m from Earth and travels $8 \times 10^{15}$ m towards Earth. How far from Earth is it now, in standard form?

   - $5.5 \times 10^{15}$ m

   - $5.5 \times 10^{16}$ m ✔

   - $1.7 \times 10^{16}$ m

   - $6.22 \times 10^{16}$ m

   _Explanation:_ First write $8 \times 10^{15}$ as $0.8 \times 10^{16}$. Then subtract: $(6.3-0.8)\times 10^{16}=5.5 \times 10^{16}$ m.

5. Work out $\left(4 \times 10^5\right)\left(3 \times 10^{-2}\right) \div \left(6 \times 10^1\right)$ in standard form.

   - $2 \times 10^2$ ✔

   - $2 \times 10^4$

   - $2 \times 10^{-8}$

   - $12 \times 10^2$

   _Explanation:_ Calculate the numbers using $4 \times 3 \div 6=2$. For the powers, add when multiplying and subtract when dividing: $5+(-2)-1=2$. Therefore the answer is $2 \times 10^2$.
