# Scatter Graphs

See how dots on a graph reveal trends so you can make sensible predictions from real data.

**Practice questions**

1. A scatter graph compares the age of a car with its value. The points generally slope downwards from left to right. What type of correlation does this show?

   - Positive correlation

   - No correlation

   - Negative correlation ✔

   - Perfect positive correlation

   _Explanation:_ This is negative correlation: as the age of a car increases, its value tends to decrease.

2. A scatter graph has revision time on the horizontal axis and test score on the vertical axis. What does the point $(6,24)$ represent?

   - $6$ hours of revision and a test score of $24$ ✔

   - $24$ hours of revision and a test score of $6$

   - $6$ students who each scored $24$

   - $24$ students who each revised for $6$ hours

   _Explanation:_ The first coordinate is read from the horizontal axis and the second from the vertical axis, so the point represents $6$ hours and a score of $24$.

3. Which description gives the most suitable line of best fit for a roughly linear scatter graph?

   - A zig-zag line joining every plotted point

   - A straight line passing through the lowest point

   - A straight line that must pass through the origin

   - A straight line through the middle of the points, with roughly equal numbers above and below ✔

   _Explanation:_ A line of best fit should follow the overall trend through the middle of the data, with the points reasonably balanced above and below it.

4. The data on a scatter graph covers revision times from $2$ to $18$ hours. Which estimate made using its line of best fit is likely to be more reliable?

   - An estimate for $25$ hours, because it uses a larger value

   - An estimate for $10$ hours, because it is interpolation ✔

   - An estimate for $25$ hours, because it is extrapolation

   - Both estimates are equally reliable

   _Explanation:_ An estimate at $10$ hours is interpolation because it lies within the observed range. An estimate at $25$ hours is extrapolation, so the trend may not continue that far.

5. A line of best fit is drawn with practice time on the horizontal axis and score on the vertical axis. How should you estimate the score for $10$ hours of practice?

   - Start at $10$ on the horizontal axis, go up to the line, then go across to the score axis ✔

   - Start at $10$ on the score axis, go across to the line, then go down

   - Use the score of the nearest plotted point

   - Read the highest score shown on the graph

   _Explanation:_ Begin with the given practice time on the horizontal axis, move vertically to the line of best fit, and then move horizontally to read the estimated score.
