# Indices

Learn the index laws for multiplying, dividing, brackets, zero powers and negative powers so algebra looks much less scary.

**Practice questions**

1. Simplify $y^5 \times y^3$.

   - $y^{15}$

   - $y^8$ ✔

   - $2y^8$

   - $y^2$

   _Explanation:_ When multiplying powers with the same base, add the indices: $y^5 \times y^3=y^{5+3}=y^8$.

2. Simplify $\left(3a^2b\right)^2$.

   - $9a^4b^2$ ✔

   - $6a^4b^2$

   - $9a^4b$

   - $9a^2b^2$

   _Explanation:_ Square every factor: $3^2=9$, $\left(a^2\right)^2=a^4$ and $b^2=b^2$. Therefore the answer is $9a^4b^2$.

3. Simplify $\frac{20m^7n^3}{5m^2n}$.

   - $4m^9n^4$

   - $15m^5n^2$

   - $4m^5n^2$ ✔

   - $4m^5n^3$

   _Explanation:_ Divide the coefficients and subtract indices for matching bases: $20\div5=4$, $m^{7-2}=m^5$ and $n^{3-1}=n^2$.

4. Work out $4^0 \times 2^{-3}$.

   - $0$

   - $-8$

   - $\frac{1}{2}$

   - $\frac{1}{8}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ A non-zero number to the power zero is $1$, and $2^{-3}=\frac{1}{2^3}=\frac{1}{8}$. Hence $4^0 \times 2^{-3}=1\times\frac{1}{8}=\frac{1}{8}$.

5. Given $27 \times 3^p=3^8$, find $p$.

   - $11$

   - $5$ ✔

   - $6$

   - $24$

   _Explanation:_ Rewrite $27$ as $3^3$. Then $3^3\times3^p=3^{3+p}=3^8$, so $3+p=8$ and $p=5$.
