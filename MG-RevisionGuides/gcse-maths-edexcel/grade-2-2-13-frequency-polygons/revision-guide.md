# Frequency Polygons

Learn how to turn grouped frequency tables into neat line graphs using class midpoints and frequencies.

A frequency polygon is used to display grouped data

## To draw a frequency polygon we plot the midpoint of the group against the frequency and then join the points with straight lines

Here is a frequency table showing the time, in minutes, it took for 25 students to get to school one day

| Time | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 0 ≤ t ≤ 10 | 7 |
| 10 ≤ t ≤ 20 | 9 |
| 20 ≤ t ≤ 30 | 6 |
| 30 ≤ t ≤ 40 | 3 |

To draw a frequency polygon we plot the midpoint with each group against the frequency

The midpoint of 0 and 10 is 5  
We could work this out by adding 0 and 10, and then dividing by 2: (0 + 10)⁄2 = 5

For the first point we plot (5, 7)

**Practice questions**

1. A group is $40 \leq t \leq 50$ with frequency $8$. What point should be plotted?

   - $(40, 8)$

   - $(50, 8)$

   - $(45, 8)$ ✔

   - $(8, 45)$

   _Explanation:_ The midpoint is $\frac{40+50}{2}=45$. The frequency is the y-coordinate, so the point is $(45,8)$.

2. For the group $0 \leq t \leq 10$ with frequency $7$, which coordinate is plotted on the frequency polygon?

   - $(5, 7)$ ✔

   - $(7, 5)$

   - $(0, 7)$

   - $(10, 7)$

   _Explanation:_ The midpoint of 0 and 10 is 5, and the frequency is 7. Therefore the coordinate is $(5,7)$.

**Practice questions**

1. What is the midpoint of the group $20 \leq t \leq 30$?

   - 10

   - 20

   - 50

   - 25 ✔

   _Explanation:_ Add the two class boundaries and divide by 2: $\frac{20+30}{2}=25$.

2. A group $30 \leq t \leq 40$ has frequency $3$. Which point represents it?

   - $(30, 3)$

   - $(35, 3)$ ✔

   - $(3, 35)$

   - $(40, 3)$

   _Explanation:_ The midpoint is $\frac{30+40}{2}=35$, and the frequency is 3, giving $(35,3)$.

**Practice questions**

1. Once all the midpoint-frequency points have been plotted, what should you do?

   - Join neighbouring points with straight lines ✔

   - Join every point back to $(0,0)$

   - Draw bars up to each point

   - Draw a smooth curve through the points

   _Explanation:_ A frequency polygon is made by joining neighbouring plotted points from left to right with straight line segments.

2. For a frequency polygon, what do the x-coordinate and y-coordinate represent?

   - Frequency and midpoint

   - Lower class boundary and frequency

   - Upper class boundary and midpoint

   - Midpoint and frequency ✔

   _Explanation:_ The x-coordinate is the midpoint of the group and the y-coordinate is its frequency, so the order is midpoint, frequency.
