# Bar Charts

Bar charts help you compare categories clearly, read values from scales, and draw accurate graphs from tables.

A bar chart is used to display data visually. The height of each rectangular bar shows the frequency of each category.

Here is a bar chart showing the colours of cars in a car park:

![bar charts 1](../assets/001-bar-charts-1.jpg)

The different colours are shown on the horizontal axis and the frequency is shown by the height of each bar.

Example 1: Here is a bar chart showing the number of goals scored by a football team in some matches.

![bar charts 2](../assets/002-bar-charts-2.jpg)

a) In how many games did the team score 1 goal?  
b) How many games did the team play?

a) The bar for 1 goal goes up to 8, the team scored 1 goal in 8 games

b) To find the number of games played we need to add up all of the frequencies  
The team scored 0 goals in 7 games, 1 goal in 8 games, 2 goals in 5 games and 3 goals in 3 games.  
7 + 8 + 5 + 3 = 23 games

**Practice questions**

1. A bar chart has a vertical axis marked $0, 4, 8, 12, 16$. A bar reaches halfway between $8$ and $12$. What frequency does it show?

   - $8$

   - $10$ ✔

   - $12$

   - $20$

   _Explanation:_ The scale increases in $4$s. Halfway between $8$ and $12$ is $10$, so the frequency is $10$.

2. A bar chart shows the favourite sports of some students: football has frequency $12$, tennis has frequency $7$, swimming has frequency $9$ and hockey has frequency $5$. What is the modal sport?

   - Swimming

   - $12$ students

   - Football ✔

   - Hockey

   _Explanation:_ The mode is the category with the greatest frequency. Football has the tallest bar, with a frequency of $12$.

3. A frequency bar chart shows the number of books read by students: $0$ books has frequency $3$, $1$ book has frequency $8$, $2$ books has frequency $6$ and $3$ books has frequency $5$. How many students are represented altogether?

   - $22$ ✔

   - $19$

   - $8$

   - $25$

   _Explanation:_ Add all the frequencies: $3+8+6+5=22$. This gives the total number of students, not the total number of books.

**Practice questions**

1. In a dual bar chart, Class A's bar for Monday has height $9$ and Class B's bar has height $14$. How many more does Class B have than Class A?

   - $23$

   - $9$

   - $14$

   - $5$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Compare the two bar heights by subtracting: $14-9=5$. Adding them would find the total instead.

2. A dual bar chart shows these four frequencies for one category: boys in Year 7, $6$; girls in Year 7, $11$; boys in Year 8, $8$; girls in Year 8, $14$. What is the range of the four frequencies?

   - $6$

   - $8$ ✔

   - $14$

   - $39$

   _Explanation:_ The range is the greatest frequency minus the smallest frequency: $14-6=8$.
