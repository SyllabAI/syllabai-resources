# Speed and Density

Learn how rates connect distance, mass, volume, force and time, then use them confidently in GCSE problem solving.

**Practice questions**

1. A cyclist travels 45 miles in 3 hours. What is the cyclist's average speed?

   - $15$ mph ✔

   - $42$ mph

   - $48$ mph

   - $135$ mph

   _Explanation:_ Average speed is distance divided by time, so $45 \div 3 = 15$ mph.

2. A car travels 150 miles in 2 hours 30 minutes. What is its average speed?

   - $50$ mph

   - $65.2$ mph

   - $375$ mph

   - $60$ mph ✔

   _Explanation:_ First convert 2 hours 30 minutes to $2.5$ hours. Then calculate $150 \div 2.5 = 60$ mph.

3. A van travels 80 miles in 2 hours, then 60 miles in 1 hour. What is its average speed for the whole journey, to 1 decimal place?

   - $35$ mph

   - $46.7$ mph ✔

   - $50$ mph

   - $70$ mph

   _Explanation:_ Use the total distance and total time: $(80+60) \div (2+1)=140 \div 3=46.7$ mph to 1 decimal place.

4. A metal block has a mass of $540$ g and a volume of $200$ cm³. What is its density?

   - $0.37$ g/cm³

   - $2.7$ g/cm³ ✔

   - $340$ g/cm³

   - $108000$ g/cm³

   _Explanation:_ Density is mass divided by volume, so $540 \div 200=2.7$ g/cm³.

5. $150$ cm³ of Liquid A has density $1.4$ g/cm³. It is mixed with $100$ cm³ of Liquid B. The resulting $250$ cm³ mixture has density $1.2$ g/cm³. What is the density of Liquid B?

   - $0.36$ g/cm³

   - $1.2$ g/cm³

   - $0.9$ g/cm³ ✔

   - $1.3$ g/cm³

   _Explanation:_ The mixture's mass is $250 \times 1.2=300$ g, while Liquid A's mass is $150 \times 1.4=210$ g. Liquid B therefore has mass $300-210=90$ g, so its density is $90 \div 100=0.9$ g/cm³.
