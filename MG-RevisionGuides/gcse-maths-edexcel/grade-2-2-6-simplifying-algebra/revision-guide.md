# Simplifying Algebra

Learn how to turn messy algebra into shorter, cleaner expressions without changing what they mean.

In algebra we use letters to represent unknown numbers.

When we are writing algebra we do not use a times sign.  
We can write a × b as ab  
We can write 5 × c as 5c  
We can write 6 × a × b as 6ab

When we have the same letter multiplied together we can use a power.

In the same way that we say the 5 × 5 = 5² we can say that a × a = a²  
b × b × b = b³  
p × p × p × p × p = p⁵

**Practice questions**

1. Simplify $c \times c \times c \times c$.

   - $4c$

   - $c^{4}$ ✔

   - $c^{3}$

   - $4+c$

   _Explanation:_ There are four factors of $c$ multiplied together, so this is written as $c^{4}$. Adding the $c$ terms would give $4c$, but the operation here is multiplication.

2. Write $7 \times m \times n$ without using a times sign.

   - $7mn$ ✔

   - $7+m+n$

   - $mn+7$

   - $7m+n$

   _Explanation:_ The number is written first, followed by the letters, so $7 \times m \times n$ becomes $7mn$. The multiplication is understood from the notation.


**Example 1: Simplify: 4a × 5b**

We can multiply the 4 and 5 together  
4 × 5 = 20

We now have 20 × a × b  
We don't write the times sign in algebra.  
We write 20 × a × b as 20ab

**The answer is 20ab**


**Practice questions**

1. Simplify $6p \times 4q$.

   - $10pq$

   - $24p+q$

   - $24pq$ ✔

   - $24p^{2}q^{2}$

   _Explanation:_ Multiply the coefficients: $6 \times 4=24$. The different letters remain as $pq$, giving $24pq$.

2. Simplify $4r \times 3r$.

   - $7r$

   - $12r$

   - $12r^{2}$ ✔

   - $7r^{2}$

   _Explanation:_ Multiply the numbers to get $12$, and use $r \times r=r^{2}$. Therefore the simplified expression is $12r^{2}$.


**Example 2: Simplify: 10t × 3t**

We can multiply the 10 and 3 together  
10 × 3 = 30

We now have 30 × t × t  
t × t = t²  
30 × t² = 30t²

**The answer is 30t²**


We write division as a fraction  
We write a ÷ b as a⁄b

**Example 3: Simplify 12a³⁄4a**

We can look at this question in two parts:  
We have 12 ÷ 4  
12 ÷ 4 = 3  
We also have a³ ÷ a  
a³ ÷ a = a²

**The answer is 3a²**


**Practice questions**

1. Simplify $\frac{15b^{3}}{5b}$.

   - $3b$

   - $10b^{2}$

   - $3b^{2}$ ✔

   - $3b^{3}$

   _Explanation:_ Divide the coefficients: $15 \div 5=3$. Also, $b^{3} \div b=b^{2}$, so the answer is $3b^{2}$.

2. Simplify $\frac{18x^{2}}{3}$.

   - $6x$

   - $6x^{2}$ ✔

   - $21x^{2}$

   - $18x^{2}$

   _Explanation:_ Divide the coefficient $18$ by $3$ to get $6$. The $x^{2}$ is unchanged because only the numerical part is being divided, giving $6x^{2}$.
