# Reverse Percentages

Learn how to undo percentage increases and decreases when the original value is hidden.

**Practice questions**

1. After a 25% reduction, a pair of trainers costs £36. What was the original price?

   - £27

   - £45

   - £48 ✔

   - £61

   _Explanation:_ A 25% reduction leaves 75% of the original price. Therefore, the original price was $36 \div 0.75 = £48$.

2. The price of a bicycle increased by 20% to £72. What was its price before the increase?

   - £60 ✔

   - £57.60

   - £86.40

   - £90

   _Explanation:_ After a 20% increase, the new price is 120% of the original. Divide by the multiplier: $72 \div 1.2 = £60$.

3. A gym membership fee increased by 8%. The amount of the increase was £24. What was the original fee?

   - £25.92

   - £222

   - £324

   - £300 ✔

   _Explanation:_ The £24 increase represents 8% of the original fee, so the original was $24 \div 0.08 = £300$.

4. A phone bill is now £123 after being reduced by 18%. What was the bill before the reduction?

   - £145.14

   - £150 ✔

   - £100.86

   - £141

   _Explanation:_ An 18% reduction leaves 82% of the original bill. Therefore, the original bill was $123 \div 0.82 = £150$.

5. A camera is reduced by 60%, giving a discount of £120. What is its sale price?

   - £200

   - £48

   - £80 ✔

   - £72

   _Explanation:_ The £120 discount is 60% of the original price, so the original was $120 \div 0.6 = £200$. The sale price is the remaining 40%: $0.4 \times £200 = £80$.
