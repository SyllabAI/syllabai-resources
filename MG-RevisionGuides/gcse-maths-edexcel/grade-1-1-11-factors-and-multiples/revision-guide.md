# Factors and Multiples

Learn how times tables help you spot number patterns, exact division and simple prime-number facts.

## Multiples

The multiples of a number are all the numbers in its times table.  
The multiples of 5 are all the numbers in the 5 times table.

**Example 1: Write down the first 5 multiples of 3**

We need to write down the first 5 numbers in the 3 times table.  
1 × 3 = 3  
2 × 3 = 6  
3 × 3 = 9  
4 × 3 = 12  
5 × 3 = 15

The first 5 multiples of 3 are 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15.


**Example 2: Write down the first 5 multiples of 5**

We need to write down the first 5 numbers in the 5 times table.  
1 × 5 = 5  
2 × 5 = 10  
3 × 5 = 15  
4 × 5 = 20  
5 × 5 = 25

The first 5 multiples of 5 are 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.


**Practice questions**

1. Which number is a multiple of $8$ between $30$ and $35$?

   - $30$

   - $32$ ✔

   - $35$

   - $40$

   _Explanation:_ The multiples of $8$ include $24, 32, 40, \ldots$, so $32$ is the only one between $30$ and $35$.

2. Which list shows the first four multiples of $7$?

   - $1, 7, 14, 21$

   - $7, 12, 17, 22$

   - $7, 14, 28, 35$

   - $7, 14, 21, 28$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Multiply $7$ by $1, 2, 3$ and $4$ to get $7, 14, 21$ and $28$.

3. Which number is both a square number and a multiple of $6$?

   - $18$

   - $24$

   - $36$ ✔

   - $48$

   _Explanation:_ Since $36=6\times6$, it is a multiple of $6$ and also a square number.

## Factors

The factors of a number are the numbers that multiply together to make it.  
The factors of 10 are the numbers that you can multiply together to make 10. 2 × 5 = 10, so 2 and 5 are both factors of 10 (so are 1 and 10).

**Example 1: Write down all the factors of 20**

It is helpful to check for factors in order, we can start with 1.  
1 × 20 = 20  
Next we can try 2: 2 × 10 = 20  
20 is not in the 3 times table, so 3 is not a factor.  
4 × 5 = 20  
We don't need to check any other numbers because we would only get repeats of the ones we already have.

The factors of 20 are: 1, 2, 4, 5, 10 and 20.


**Example 2: Write down all the factors of 16**

We will check in order again:  
1 × 16 = 16  
2 × 8 = 16  
16 is not in the 3 times table  
4 × 4 = 16 (16 is a square number)  
We don't need to check any other numbers because we would only get repeats of the ones we already have.

The factors of 16 are: 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16.


**Practice questions**

1. Which list gives all the factors of $18$?

   - $1, 2, 3, 6, 9$

   - $2, 3, 6, 9, 18$

   - $1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18, 36$

   - $1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The factor pairs are $1\times18$, $2\times9$ and $3\times6$, giving $1, 2, 3, 6, 9$ and $18$.

2. Which number is not a factor of $24$?

   - $10$ ✔

   - $4$

   - $6$

   - $8$

   _Explanation:_ The factor pairs of $24$ are $1\times24$, $2\times12$, $3\times8$ and $4\times6$. Therefore, $10$ is not a factor.

## Prime Numbers

A prime number is a number with exactly 2 factors.  
5 is a prime number because its only factors are 1 and 5.  
6 is not a prime number because its factors are 1, 2, 3 and 6.

**Example 1: Write down all prime numbers between 1 and 10.**

1 is not a prime number because it only has one factor. It is only 1 × 1.  
2 is a prime number - its only factors are 1 and 2.  
3 is a prime number - its only factors are 1 and 3.  
4 is not a prime number: it is in the 2 times table (so 2 is a factor as well as 1 and 4).  
5 is a prime number - its only factors are 1 and 5.  
6 is not a prime number: it is in the 2 (and 3) times table.  
7 is a prime number.  
8 is not a prime number: it is in the 2 (and 4) times table.  
9 is not a prime number: it is in the 3 times table.  
10 is not a prime number: it is in the 2 (and 5) times table.

**The prime numbers between 1 and 10 are 2, 3, 5 and 7.**


**Practice questions**

1. Which number is prime?

   - $17$ ✔

   - $15$

   - $21$

   - $27$

   _Explanation:_ The only factors of $17$ are $1$ and $17$. Each of the other numbers has factors other than $1$ and itself.

2. Which pair of prime numbers has a sum of $25$?

   - $3$ and $22$

   - $2$ and $23$ ✔

   - $5$ and $20$

   - $11$ and $14$

   _Explanation:_ Both $2$ and $23$ are prime, and $2+23=25$. Each other pair contains a number that is not prime.

3. Why is $1$ not a prime number?

   - It is an odd number.

   - It is a factor of every whole number.

   - It has only one factor. ✔

   - It has more than two factors.

   _Explanation:_ A prime number must have exactly two factors. The number $1$ has only one factor, which is itself.
