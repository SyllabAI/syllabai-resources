# Bounds

Learn how rounded measurements create upper and lower limits, then use them safely in geometry and formula questions.

**Practice questions**

1. A length is $58.0$ cm correct to the nearest millimetre. Which interval shows the possible values of the length $l$?

   - $57.5 \le l < 58.5$

   - $57.99 \le l < 58.01$

   - $57.95 \le l < 58.05$ ✔

   - $58.0 \le l < 58.1$

   _Explanation:_ One millimetre is $0.1$ cm, so the half-gap is $0.05$ cm. Therefore, $57.95 \le l < 58.05$; the upper bound is not included because it would round to $58.1$ cm.

2. A rectangle has length $12$ cm to the nearest centimetre and width $4.8$ cm to the nearest millimetre. What is the lower bound for its area?

   - $54.625$ cm² ✔

   - $57.6$ cm²

   - $60.625$ cm²

   - $54.05$ cm²

   _Explanation:_ The lower bounds are $11.5$ cm and $4.75$ cm. Multiplying them gives the lower area: $11.5 \times 4.75=54.625$ cm².

3. A distance is $8.4$ m correct to $1$ decimal place and a time is $2.6$ s correct to $1$ decimal place. For $v=\frac{s}{t}$, what is the upper bound for $v$, rounded to $2$ decimal places?

   - $3.19$ m/s

   - $3.23$ m/s

   - $3.15$ m/s

   - $3.31$ m/s ✔

   _Explanation:_ To maximise a positive fraction, divide the upper distance by the lower time: $8.45 \div 2.55=3.3137\ldots$, which rounds to $3.31$ m/s.

4. In a right-angled triangle, $b=\sqrt{c^2-a^2}$. If $a=9.8$ cm to the nearest millimetre and $c=15$ cm to the nearest centimetre, what is the lower bound for $b$, rounded to $1$ decimal place?

   - $10.7$ cm

   - $10.6$ cm ✔

   - $12.0$ cm

   - $17.5$ cm

   _Explanation:_ To minimise $b$, use the lower bound of $c$ and the upper bound of $a$: $\sqrt{14.5^2-9.85^2}=10.640\ldots$, which rounds to $10.6$ cm.

5. For $q=\frac{x}{y}$, the values $x=26.40$ and $y=5.10$ are each correct to $2$ decimal places. Which rounded value of $q$ is guaranteed by the bounds?

   - $5.17$ to $2$ d.p.

   - $5.18$ to $2$ d.p.

   - $5.2$ to $1$ d.p. ✔

   - $5.1$ to $1$ d.p.

   _Explanation:_ The bounds give $\frac{26.395}{5.105}\le q<\frac{26.405}{5.095}$, so approximately $5.170\le q<5.183$. These values do not all round to the same answer to $2$ decimal places, but they all round to $5.2$ to $1$ decimal place.
