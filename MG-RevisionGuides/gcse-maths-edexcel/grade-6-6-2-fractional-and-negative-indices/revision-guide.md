# Fractional and Negative Indices

Learn how negative and fractional powers turn into reciprocals, roots and ordinary index-law calculations.

**Practice questions**

1. Work out $125^{\frac{1}{3}}$.

   - 15

   - 25

   - 5 ✔

   - $\frac{1}{5}$

   _Explanation:_ The denominator 3 means take the cube root. Since $5^3=125$, $125^{\frac{1}{3}}=5$.

2. Work out $64^{\frac{2}{3}}$.

   - 8

   - 16 ✔

   - 32

   - $\frac{1}{16}$

   _Explanation:_ First take the cube root: $\sqrt[3]{64}=4$. Then square the result: $4^2=16$.

3. Work out $8^{-1}$.

   - $-\frac{1}{8}$

   - -8

   - 8

   - $\frac{1}{8}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ A negative index means take the reciprocal, not make the value negative. Therefore $8^{-1}=\frac{1}{8}$.

4. Work out $\left(\frac{8}{27}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$.

   - $\frac{9}{4}$ ✔

   - $\frac{4}{9}$

   - $-\frac{9}{4}$

   - $\frac{3}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ The cube root of $\frac{8}{27}$ is $\frac{2}{3}$, and squaring gives $\frac{4}{9}$. The negative index then gives the reciprocal, $\frac{9}{4}$.

5. Given that $3\sqrt{27}=3^n$, find $n$.

   - $\frac{3}{2}$

   - 5

   - $\frac{5}{2}$ ✔

   - $\frac{7}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ Since $27=3^3$, $\sqrt{27}=3^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Therefore $3\sqrt{27}=3^1\times3^{\frac{3}{2}}=3^{\frac{5}{2}}$, so $n=\frac{5}{2}$.
