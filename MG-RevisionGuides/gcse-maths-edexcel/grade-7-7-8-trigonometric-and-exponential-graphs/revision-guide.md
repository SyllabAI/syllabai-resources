# Trigonometric and Exponential Graphs

**Practice questions**

1. What is the value of $\sin 90^\circ$?

   - $0$

   - $1$ ✔

   - $-1$

   - Undefined

   _Explanation:_ The sine graph reaches its maximum value of $1$ when $x=90^\circ$, so $\sin 90^\circ=1$.

2. What is the period of the graph $y=\cos x$?

   - $90^\circ$

   - $180^\circ$

   - $270^\circ$

   - $360^\circ$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The cosine graph repeats after every $360^\circ$, so its period is $360^\circ$.

3. At which value of $x$ is $y=\tan x$ undefined?

   - $90^\circ$ ✔

   - $0^\circ$

   - $180^\circ$

   - $360^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The tangent graph has a vertical asymptote at $x=90^\circ$, so $\tan 90^\circ$ is undefined.

4. Which point lies on the graph $y=2^x$?

   - $(2,2)$

   - $(4,2)$

   - $(2,4)$ ✔

   - $(4,4)$

   _Explanation:_ When $x=2$, $y=2^2=4$. Therefore, $(2,4)$ lies on the graph.

5. What is the equation of the horizontal asymptote of $y=2^x+3$?

   - $x=3$

   - $y=3$ ✔

   - $y=0$

   - $x=0$

   _Explanation:_ The graph of $y=2^x$ has asymptote $y=0$. Adding $3$ translates it upwards by $3$, so the new asymptote is $y=3$.
