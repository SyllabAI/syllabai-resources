# Writing and Simplifying Ratio

Learn to compare amounts clearly, reduce ratios, and build ratios from fractions or “times as many” clues.

## Writing Ratio

We use ratio to compare amounts.

Here are some red squares and some blue squares.

![writing ratio 1](../assets/001-writing-ratio-1.jpg)

There are 3 red squares and 6 blue squares.  
We can say the ratio of red to blue squares is 3:6  
We read this as 3 to 6  
For every 3 red squares, there are 6 blue squares.

3 and 6 are both in the 3 times table so we can simplify this ratio by dividing both numbers by 3  
The ratio of red squares to blue squares is 1:2

If we wanted to write the ratio of blue squares to red squares we would have to write the numbers the other way around.  
The ratio of blue squares to red squares is 6:3 or simplified it is 2:1

Here are some yellow circles, some blues circles and some red circles

![writing ratio 2](../assets/002-writing-ratio-2.jpg)

There are 4 yellow circles, 2 blue circles and 6 red circles. The ratio of yellow circles to blue circles to red circles is 4:2:6

4, 2 and 6 are all in the 2 times table so we can simplify by dividing by 2  
The ratio of yellow circles to blue circles to red circles is 2:1:3

**Practice questions**

1. There are 8 apples and 12 oranges. What is the ratio of oranges to apples in its simplest form?

   - $2:3$

   - $12:8$

   - $3:2$ ✔

   - $8:12$

   _Explanation:_ Oranges must come first, so the ratio is $12:8$. Dividing both parts by $4$ gives $3:2$.

2. A bag contains 6 red counters, 9 blue counters and 3 green counters. Write the ratio of red to blue to green counters in its simplest form.

   - $2:3:1$ ✔

   - $6:9:3$

   - $3:2:1$

   - $2:1:3$

   _Explanation:_ The ratio is $6:9:3$. All three parts divide by $3$, giving $2:3:1$ in the same order.

3. Aisha has four 10p coins and three 20p coins. What is the ratio of the value of the 10p coins to the value of the 20p coins in its simplest form?

   - $4:3$

   - $3:2$

   - $40:60$

   - $2:3$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The four 10p coins are worth 40p and the three 20p coins are worth 60p. Simplifying $40:60$ gives $2:3$.

## Simplifying Ratio

We simplify ratio in the same way we simplify fractions.

## Simplify 10:15

10 and 15 are both in the 5 times table, we can simplify by dividing both 10 and 15 by 5

10 ÷ 5 = 2  
15 ÷ 5 = 3

10:15 = 2:3

## Simplify 20:8

20 and 8 are both in the 4 times table (they are also both in the 2 times table), we can simplify by dividing both 20 and 8 by 4

20 ÷ 4 = 5  
8 ÷ 4 = 2

20:8 = 5:2

If we had divided by 2 we would get 10:4  
To simplify fully we would have to divide by 2 again - we would then get the same answer 5:2

**Practice questions**

1. Simplify the ratio $42:56$ fully.

   - $21:28$

   - $3:4$ ✔

   - $4:3$

   - $6:8$

   _Explanation:_ The highest common factor of $42$ and $56$ is $14$. Dividing both parts by $14$ gives $3:4$.

2. Simplify the ratio $18:30:42$ fully.

   - $9:15:21$

   - $6:10:14$

   - $3:5:7$ ✔

   - $2:3:4$

   _Explanation:_ All three numbers divide by $6$: $18\div6=3$, $30\div6=5$ and $42\div6=7$.

3. Write the ratio of $1.5$ m to $60$ cm in its simplest form.

   - $5:2$ ✔

   - $15:6$

   - $2:5$

   - $1.5:60$

   _Explanation:_ Convert $1.5$ m to $150$ cm first. The ratio $150:60$ divides by $30$ to give $5:2$.

## Converting between a fraction and a ratio

## Example: 1⁄5 of pens in a box a green, the rest are red. Write the ratio of green pens to red pens.

A whole is 5⁄5

5⁄5 − 1⁄5 = 4⁄5

4⁄5 of the pens are red

Out of every 5 pens, 1 is green and 4 are red

The ratio of green pens to red pens is 1:4

**Example: The ratio of black pens to blue pens in a box is 3:1  
What fraction of the pens are black?**

For every 3 black pens there is 1 blue pen  
Out of every 4 pens 3 are black.

3⁄4 of pens are black

**Practice questions**

1. In a year group, $\frac{2}{7}$ of the students are left-handed and the rest are right-handed. What is the ratio of left-handed to right-handed students?

   - $2:7$

   - $5:2$

   - $7:5$

   - $2:5$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ There are $7$ equal parts altogether. If $2$ parts are left-handed, then $7-2=5$ parts are right-handed, so the ratio is $2:5$.

2. The ratio of cats to dogs at an animal shelter is $5:3$. What fraction of these animals are cats?

   - $\frac{3}{8}$

   - $\frac{5}{3}$

   - $\frac{5}{8}$ ✔

   - $\frac{3}{5}$

   _Explanation:_ There are $5+3=8$ parts altogether, and $5$ of those parts represent cats. The fraction is therefore $\frac{5}{8}$.

3. The ratio of red to blue to green beads is $2:3:5$. What fraction of the beads are blue?

   - $\frac{3}{5}$

   - $\frac{3}{10}$ ✔

   - $\frac{2}{10}$

   - $\frac{5}{10}$

   _Explanation:_ The total number of parts is $2+3+5=10$. Blue beads make up $3$ parts, so the fraction that are blue is $\frac{3}{10}$.
