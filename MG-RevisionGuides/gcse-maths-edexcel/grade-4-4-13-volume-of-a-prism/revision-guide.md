# Volume of a Prism

Learn a reliable method for finding space inside prisms, from simple cuboids to containers and packing problems.

**Practice questions**

1. A triangular prism has a right-angled cross-section with perpendicular sides of $5\text{ cm}$ and $12\text{ cm}$. The prism is $8\text{ cm}$ long. What is its volume?

   - $480\text{ cm}^3$

   - $96\text{ cm}^3$

   - $30\text{ cm}^3$

   - $240\text{ cm}^3$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The cross-sectional area is $\frac{1}{2}\times 5\times 12=30\text{ cm}^2$. Therefore, the volume is $30\times 8=240\text{ cm}^3$.

2. An L-shaped cross-section is divided into two non-overlapping rectangles measuring $7\text{ cm}$ by $2\text{ cm}$ and $3\text{ cm}$ by $5\text{ cm}$. The prism is $4\text{ cm}$ long. What is its volume?

   - $116\text{ cm}^3$ ✔

   - $29\text{ cm}^3$

   - $132\text{ cm}^3$

   - $196\text{ cm}^3$

   _Explanation:_ The cross-sectional area is $(7\times 2)+(3\times 5)=14+15=29\text{ cm}^2$. Multiplying by the prism length gives $29\times 4=116\text{ cm}^3$.

3. A prism has a rectangular cross-section measuring $4\text{ cm}$ by $7\text{ cm}$ and a length of $6\text{ cm}$. What is its volume?

   - $34\text{ cm}^3$

   - $168\text{ cm}^3$ ✔

   - $28\text{ cm}^3$

   - $17\text{ cm}^3$

   _Explanation:_ The cross-sectional area is $4\times 7=28\text{ cm}^2$. Multiply this by the length: $28\times 6=168\text{ cm}^3$.

4. A rectangular tank is $80\text{ cm}$ long, $50\text{ cm}$ wide and $45\text{ cm}$ high. A bucket holds $7$ litres. What is the minimum number of full buckets needed to fill the tank?

   - $25.7$ buckets

   - $25$ buckets

   - $26$ buckets ✔

   - $180$ buckets

   _Explanation:_ The volume is $80\times 50\times 45=180000\text{ cm}^3=180$ litres. Since $180\div 7\approx25.7$, this must be rounded up to $26$ whole buckets.

5. A prism has a trapezium cross-section with parallel sides of $4\text{ cm}$ and $8\text{ cm}$ and a perpendicular height of $5\text{ cm}$. Its volume is $300\text{ cm}^3$. What is the length of the prism?

   - $5\text{ cm}$

   - $30\text{ cm}$

   - $60\text{ cm}$

   - $10\text{ cm}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The cross-sectional area is $\frac{1}{2}(4+8)\times 5=30\text{ cm}^2$. As volume equals cross-sectional area times length, the length is $300\div 30=10\text{ cm}$.
