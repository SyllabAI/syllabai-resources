# Velocity Time Graphs

**Practice questions**

1. An object's velocity increases uniformly from $4\text{ m/s}$ to $16\text{ m/s}$ in $6$ seconds. What is its acceleration?

   - $12\text{ m/s}^2$

   - $2\text{ m/s}^2$ ✔

   - $20\text{ m/s}^2$

   - $72\text{ m/s}^2$

   _Explanation:_ Acceleration is the gradient of a velocity-time graph: $\frac{16-4}{6}=2\text{ m/s}^2$.

2. A car travels at a constant velocity of $12\text{ m/s}$ for $8$ seconds. How far does it travel?

   - $20\text{ m}$

   - $1.5\text{ m}$

   - $8\text{ m}$

   - $96\text{ m}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Distance is the area under the graph. Here it is a rectangle, so the distance is $12\times 8=96\text{ m}$.

3. A cyclist accelerates uniformly from rest to $20\text{ m/s}$ over $5$ seconds. What distance does the cyclist travel during these $5$ seconds?

   - $50\text{ m}$ ✔

   - $25\text{ m}$

   - $100\text{ m}$

   - $4\text{ m}$

   _Explanation:_ The area under the graph is a triangle, so the distance is $\frac{1}{2}\times 5\times 20=50\text{ m}$.

4. An object's velocity decreases uniformly from $18\text{ m/s}$ to $0\text{ m/s}$ in $3$ seconds. What is its acceleration?

   - $6\text{ m/s}^2$

   - $-5\text{ m/s}^2$

   - $-6\text{ m/s}^2$ ✔

   - $-54\text{ m/s}^2$

   _Explanation:_ Acceleration is the change in velocity divided by time: $\frac{0-18}{3}=-6\text{ m/s}^2$. The negative sign shows that the object is decelerating.

5. What does a horizontal line at $-4\text{ m/s}$ on a velocity-time graph represent?

   - The object is slowing down

   - The object has a constant velocity of $-4\text{ m/s}$ ✔

   - The object is stationary

   - The object has an acceleration of $-4\text{ m/s}^2$

   _Explanation:_ A horizontal line has zero gradient, so the velocity is constant. The negative velocity means the object is moving opposite to the chosen positive direction.
