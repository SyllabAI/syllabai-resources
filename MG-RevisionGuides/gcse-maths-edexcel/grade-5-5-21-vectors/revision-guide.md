# Vectors

**Practice questions**

1. A translation moves a point $7$ units to the right and $3$ units upwards. What is the column vector for this translation?

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-7\\3\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}7\\3\end{pmatrix}$ ✔

   - $\begin{pmatrix}3\\7\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}7\\-3\end{pmatrix}$

   _Explanation:_ The top number gives the horizontal movement and the bottom number gives the vertical movement. Right and up are positive, so the vector is $\begin{pmatrix}7\\3\end{pmatrix}$.

2. Calculate $\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-5\\7\end{pmatrix}$.

   - $\begin{pmatrix}8\\-9\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-5\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}2\\5\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-2\\5\end{pmatrix}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Add corresponding components: $3+(-5)=-2$ and $-2+7=5$. Therefore the answer is $\begin{pmatrix}-2\\5\end{pmatrix}$.

3. Given $\mathbf{a}=\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{b}=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\3\end{pmatrix}$, find $2\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$.

   - $\begin{pmatrix}10\\-1\end{pmatrix}$ ✔

   - $\begin{pmatrix}6\\5\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}6\\-1\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}10\\5\end{pmatrix}$

   _Explanation:_ First, $2\mathbf{a}=\begin{pmatrix}8\\2\end{pmatrix}$. Then subtract $\mathbf{b}$: $\begin{pmatrix}8-(-2)\\2-3\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}10\\-1\end{pmatrix}$.

4. The points $A$ and $B$ have coordinates $A(2,-1)$ and $B(-3,5)$. Find the vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$.

   - $\begin{pmatrix}5\\-6\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-1\\4\end{pmatrix}$

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-5\\6\end{pmatrix}$ ✔

   - $\begin{pmatrix}-5\\4\end{pmatrix}$

   _Explanation:_ To find $\overrightarrow{AB}$, subtract the coordinates of $A$ from those of $B$: $\begin{pmatrix}-3-2\\5-(-1)\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-5\\6\end{pmatrix}$.

5. Points $A$ and $B$ have position vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$. Point $P$ lies on $AB$ so that $AP:PB=2:1$. What is the position vector of $P$?

   - $\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}+\frac{1}{3}\mathbf{b}$

   - $2\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}$

   - $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}+\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{b}$

   - $\frac{1}{3}\mathbf{a}+\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{b}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Since $AP:PB=2:1$, point $P$ is two-thirds of the way from $A$ to $B$. Thus $\overrightarrow{OP}=\mathbf{a}+\frac{2}{3}(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})=\frac{1}{3}\mathbf{a}+\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{b}$.
