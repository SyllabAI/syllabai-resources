# Bearings

Learn how to read, draw and reverse compass bearings using North, clockwise angles and three-figure notation.

**Practice questions**

1. From P, the clockwise angle from North to Q is $47^\circ$. What is the bearing of Q from P?

   - $047^\circ$ ✔

   - $47^\circ$

   - $313^\circ$

   - $133^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Bearings are measured clockwise from North and written using three figures, so $47^\circ$ is written as $047^\circ$.

2. At C, D is $35^\circ$ anticlockwise from North. What is the bearing of D from C?

   - $035^\circ$

   - $145^\circ$

   - $215^\circ$

   - $325^\circ$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ A bearing is measured clockwise from North. The clockwise angle is $360^\circ-35^\circ=325^\circ$.

3. Point Q is due south-east of P. Which of these could be the bearing of Q from P?

   - $045^\circ$

   - $315^\circ$

   - $135^\circ$ ✔

   - $225^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ South-east is halfway between east ($090^\circ$) and south ($180^\circ$), giving a bearing of $135^\circ$.

4. The bearing of B from A is $068^\circ$. What is the bearing of A from B?

   - $112^\circ$

   - $248^\circ$ ✔

   - $292^\circ$

   - $022^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Reverse bearings differ by $180^\circ$. Since $68+180=248$, the bearing of A from B is $248^\circ$.

5. A boat sails from H on a bearing of $210^\circ$. In which general direction does it travel?

   - Down and to the left ✔

   - Up and to the right

   - Down and to the right

   - Up and to the left

   _Explanation:_ The bearing $210^\circ$ lies between south ($180^\circ$) and west ($270^\circ$), so the boat travels down and to the left.
