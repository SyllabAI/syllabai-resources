# 3d Pythagoras and Trigonometry

Learn how hidden right triangles unlock lengths, angles and volumes in 3D shapes.

**Practice questions**

1. A cuboid has perpendicular edge lengths $2$ cm, $3$ cm and $6$ cm. What is the length of its space diagonal?

   - $6.7$ cm

   - $7$ cm ✔

   - $11$ cm

   - $49$ cm

   _Explanation:_ Apply Pythagoras in three dimensions: $d=\sqrt{2^2+3^2+6^2}=\sqrt{49}=7$ cm.

2. A cuboid has a space diagonal of $11$ cm. Two of its perpendicular edges are $4$ cm and $6$ cm. Find the third edge to $1$ decimal place.

   - $8.3$ cm ✔

   - $9.2$ cm

   - $7.0$ cm

   - $12.6$ cm

   _Explanation:_ If the missing edge is $x$, then $x^2+4^2+6^2=11^2$. Therefore $x=\sqrt{121-16-36}=\sqrt{69}=8.3$ cm to $1$ decimal place.

3. A cuboid has a base measuring $9$ cm by $12$ cm and a height of $8$ cm. What angle does its space diagonal make with the base plane, to $1$ decimal place?

   - $33.7^\circ$

   - $61.9^\circ$

   - $41.6^\circ$

   - $28.1^\circ$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The projection of the space diagonal onto the base is the base diagonal, $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$ cm. Hence $\tan\theta=\frac{8}{15}$, so $\theta=28.1^\circ$.

4. Rectangle $ABCD$ has $AB=7$ cm and $BC=24$ cm. Point $F$ is vertically above $C$, with $CF=10$ cm. Find the angle that $AF$ makes with the plane $ABCD$, to $1$ decimal place.

   - $22.6^\circ$

   - $68.2^\circ$

   - $21.8^\circ$ ✔

   - $55.0^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The projection of $AF$ onto the plane is $AC$, where $AC=\sqrt{7^2+24^2}=25$ cm. Thus $\tan\theta=\frac{10}{25}$, giving $\theta=21.8^\circ$.

5. A square-based pyramid has a base side length of $10$ cm. Its apex $P$ is vertically above the centre $O$. The angle $PAC$ is $50^\circ$, where $AC$ is a diagonal of the base. Find the volume of the pyramid to $3$ significant figures.

   - $397$ cm$^3$

   - $281$ cm$^3$ ✔

   - $562$ cm$^3$

   - $181$ cm$^3$

   _Explanation:_ The distance from a corner to the centre is half the base diagonal: $AO=5\sqrt{2}$ cm. In right-angled triangle $AOP$, $PO=5\sqrt{2}\tan 50^\circ$. Therefore the volume is $\frac13\times10^2\times PO=281$ cm$^3$ to $3$ significant figures.
