# Quadratic Formula

Learn how to solve any quadratic by substituting carefully into one reliable formula.

**Practice questions**

1. Before using the quadratic formula to solve $3x^2=2x+8$, what are the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$?

   - $a=3$, $b=2$, $c=-8$

   - $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=8$

   - $a=3$, $b=-2$, $c=-8$ ✔

   - $a=3$, $b=2$, $c=8$

   _Explanation:_ Rearrange the equation into the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$: $3x^2-2x-8=0$. Therefore, $a=3$, $b=-2$ and $c=-8$.

2. Which is the correct substitution into the quadratic formula for $2x^2-5x-3=0$?

   - $x=\frac{5\pm\sqrt{(-5)^2-4(2)(-3)}}{4}$ ✔

   - $x=\frac{-5\pm\sqrt{(-5)^2-4(2)(-3)}}{4}$

   - $x=\frac{5\pm\sqrt{(-5)^2-4(2)(3)}}{4}$

   - $x=\frac{5\pm\sqrt{(-5)^2+4(2)(-3)}}{4}$

   _Explanation:_ Here $a=2$, $b=-5$ and $c=-3$. In $x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$, this gives $-b=5$ and $2a=4$.

3. Solve $x^2-6x+2=0$, giving your answers in exact form.

   - $x=-3\pm\sqrt{7}$

   - $x=3\pm2\sqrt{7}$

   - $x=6\pm2\sqrt{7}$

   - $x=3\pm\sqrt{7}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The formula gives $x=\frac{6\pm\sqrt{36-8}}{2}=\frac{6\pm\sqrt{28}}{2}$. Since $\sqrt{28}=2\sqrt{7}$, the solutions simplify to $x=3\pm\sqrt{7}$.

4. Solve $x^2+4x-1=0$, giving both solutions to 2 decimal places.

   - $x=4.24$ or $x=-0.24$

   - $x=0.24$ or $x=-4.24$ ✔

   - $x=0.23$ or $x=-4.23$

   - $x=0.47$ or $x=-8.47$

   _Explanation:_ Using the formula gives $x=-2\pm\sqrt{5}$. These values are approximately $0.236$ and $-4.236$, which round to $0.24$ and $-4.24$.

5. A right-angled triangle has shorter sides $x$ and $x+3$, and hypotenuse $x+5$. What is the valid value of $x$?

   - $x=2-2\sqrt{5}$

   - $x=-2+2\sqrt{5}$

   - $x=2+2\sqrt{5}$ ✔

   - $x=4+4\sqrt{5}$

   _Explanation:_ Pythagoras gives $x^2+(x+3)^2=(x+5)^2$, which simplifies to $x^2-4x-16=0$. The roots are $x=2\pm2\sqrt{5}$, but a side length must be positive, so $x=2+2\sqrt{5}$.
