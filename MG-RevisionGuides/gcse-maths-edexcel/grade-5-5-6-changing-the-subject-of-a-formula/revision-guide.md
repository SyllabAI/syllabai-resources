# Changing the Subject of a Formula

Rearranging formulas helps you isolate a chosen letter by undoing operations in the right order.

**Practice questions**

1. Make $x$ the subject of $y = 5x - 8$.

   - $x = \frac{y-8}{5}$

   - $x = \frac{y+8}{5}$ ✔

   - $x = 5y+8$

   - $x = \frac{y}{5}-8$

   _Explanation:_ Add $8$ to both sides to get $y+8=5x$, then divide by $5$. Therefore $x=\frac{y+8}{5}$.

2. Make $b$ the subject of $v = 6(b - 2)$.

   - $b = \frac{v}{6}+2$ ✔

   - $b = \frac{v}{6}-2$

   - $b = 6v+2$

   - $b = \frac{v+2}{6}$

   _Explanation:_ Divide both sides by $6$ first, giving $\frac{v}{6}=b-2$, then add $2$.

3. Make $s$ the subject of $k = \frac{r-5}{s}$, assuming $k \neq 0$.

   - $s = \frac{k}{r-5}$

   - $s = k(r-5)$

   - $s = \frac{r-5}{k}$ ✔

   - $s = \frac{r+5}{k}$

   _Explanation:_ Multiply both sides by $s$ to obtain $ks=r-5$, then divide both sides by $k$.

4. Make $p$ the subject of $C = 3q + 4p$.

   - $p = \frac{C+3q}{4}$

   - $p = C-\frac{3q}{4}$

   - $p = 4C-3q$

   - $p = \frac{C-3q}{4}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Subtract $3q$ from both sides to get $C-3q=4p$, then divide the whole of $C-3q$ by $4$.

5. Make $x$ the subject of $p = \sqrt{\frac{x^2+5}{2}}$.

   - $x = \sqrt{2p^2-5}$

   - $x = \pm\sqrt{2p^2-5}$ ✔

   - $x = \pm\sqrt{\frac{p^2-5}{2}}$

   - $x = \pm\sqrt{2p-5}$

   _Explanation:_ Square both sides, multiply by $2$, and subtract $5$ to get $x^2=2p^2-5$. Taking the square root gives both possibilities: $x=\pm\sqrt{2p^2-5}$.
