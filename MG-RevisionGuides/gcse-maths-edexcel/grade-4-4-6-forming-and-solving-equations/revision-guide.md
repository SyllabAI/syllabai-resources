# Forming and Solving Equations

Turn clues from diagrams and word problems into equations so you can find missing lengths, angles, and totals.

**Practice questions**

1. Two angles on a straight line are $5x+10$ degrees and $3x+2$ degrees. Find $x$.

   - $21$ ✔

   - $9.75$

   - $24$

   - $21.5$

   _Explanation:_ Angles on a straight line total $180°$, so $(5x+10)+(3x+2)=180$. This gives $8x+12=180$, so $8x=168$ and $x=21$.

2. A triangle has side lengths $3x-4$, $2x+7$ and $x+1$ cm. Which expression gives its perimeter?

   - $6x+12$

   - $6x+4$ ✔

   - $5x+4$

   - $6x-4$

   _Explanation:_ Add all three sides and collect like terms: $(3x-4)+(2x+7)+(x+1)=6x+4$.

3. A rectangle has length $2x+3$ cm and width $x+5$ cm. Its perimeter is $46$ cm. Which equation should be solved?

   - $3x+8=46$

   - $2x^2+13x+15=46$

   - $6x+16=46$ ✔

   - $6x+8=46$

   _Explanation:_ A rectangle has two lengths and two widths, so $2(2x+3)+2(x+5)=46$. Simplifying gives $6x+16=46$.

4. A right-angled triangle has perpendicular sides $3x$ cm and $8x$ cm. Its area is $300\text{ cm}^2$. Find $x$.

   - $25$

   - $\pm5$

   - $3.5$

   - $5$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ Using the triangle area formula gives $\frac{1}{2}\times3x\times8x=300$, so $12x^2=300$ and $x^2=25$. Since $x$ represents a length, $x=5$ rather than $-5$.

5. Asha's age is $x$, Ben's age is $2x$, and Cara's age is $2x+6$. Their total age is $66$. What is the ratio Asha : Ben : Cara in its simplest form?

   - $1:2:2$

   - $12:24:30$

   - $2:4:5$ ✔

   - $2:4:3$

   _Explanation:_ Form the equation $x+2x+(2x+6)=66$. This gives $5x=60$, so $x=12$. The ages are $12:24:30$, which simplify to $2:4:5$.
