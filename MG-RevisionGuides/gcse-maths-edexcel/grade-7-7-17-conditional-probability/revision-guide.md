# Conditional Probability

Learn how tree diagrams update probabilities after earlier choices, then combine routes for “and”, “or”, and survey questions.

**Practice questions**

1. Given that a bus is late, the probability that Mia misses registration is $0.35$. What is the probability that she does not miss registration, given that the bus is late?

   - $0.35$

   - $0.55$

   - $0.65$ ✔

   - $1.35$

   _Explanation:_ The two outcomes are complementary, so the probability is $1-0.35=0.65$.

2. A bag contains 5 red and 3 green counters. Two counters are chosen without replacement. What is the probability of choosing a red counter followed by a green counter?

   - $\frac{15}{64}$

   - $\frac{15}{56}$ ✔

   - $\frac{8}{15}$

   - $\frac{3}{7}$

   _Explanation:_ After the red counter is removed, 7 counters remain. Multiply along the route: $\frac{5}{8}\times\frac{3}{7}=\frac{15}{56}$.

3. A bag contains 6 red and 4 blue counters. Two counters are chosen without replacement. What is the probability of choosing one counter of each colour?

   - $\frac{4}{15}$

   - $\frac{12}{25}$

   - $\frac{7}{15}$

   - $\frac{8}{15}$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ One of each colour can occur in either order. Therefore, $\frac{6}{10}\times\frac{4}{9}+\frac{4}{10}\times\frac{6}{9}=\frac{8}{15}$.

4. Of 40 students, 24 study French. Of the students who study French, 15 also study Spanish. Given that a student studies French, what is the probability that they also study Spanish?

   - $\frac{5}{8}$ ✔

   - $\frac{3}{8}$

   - $\frac{15}{64}$

   - $\frac{8}{15}$

   _Explanation:_ The condition restricts the group to the 24 French students. Of these, 15 study Spanish, so the probability is $\frac{15}{24}=\frac{5}{8}$.

5. In a survey of 36 students, 19 like art, 17 like music and 8 like both. Two students are chosen without replacement. What is the probability that both students like exactly one of art and music?

   - $\frac{5}{9}$

   - $\frac{3}{5}$

   - $\frac{19}{63}$ ✔

   - $\frac{2}{45}$

   _Explanation:_ The number who like exactly one is $(19-8)+(17-8)=20$. The probability is $\frac{20}{36}\times\frac{19}{35}=\frac{19}{63}$.
