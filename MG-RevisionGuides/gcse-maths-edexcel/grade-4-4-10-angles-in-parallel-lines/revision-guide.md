# Angles in Parallel Lines

Learn how to spot equal and adding-to-180 angles when parallel lines are crossed, then combine them with triangles and parallelograms.

**Practice questions**

1. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The top-right angle at the upper intersection is $72^\circ$, and $x$ is the top-right angle at the lower intersection. What is $x$?

   - $72^\circ$ ✔

   - $108^\circ$

   - $36^\circ$

   - $118^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The two angles are in the same relative position, so they are corresponding angles. Corresponding angles are equal, giving $x=72^\circ$.

2. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One interior angle is $123^\circ$, and $x$ is the interior angle on the same side of the transversal at the other line. What is $x$?

   - $123^\circ$

   - $47^\circ$

   - $67^\circ$

   - $57^\circ$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ These are co-interior angles, so they add to $180^\circ$. Therefore, $x=180^\circ-123^\circ=57^\circ$.

3. Line $AB$ is parallel to line $CD$. A transversal makes an interior angle of $47^\circ$ below $AB$ on the left, and $x$ is the interior angle above $CD$ on the right. What is $x$?

   - $133^\circ$

   - $94^\circ$

   - $47^\circ$ ✔

   - $43^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ The angles are alternate angles because they are inside the parallel lines and on opposite sides of the transversal. Alternate angles are equal, so $x=47^\circ$.

4. $ABCD$ is a parallelogram. Point $E$ lies on the same straight line as $B$ and $C$. Angle $DAB$ is $118^\circ$ and angle $AEB$ is $35^\circ$. What is angle $BAE$?

   - $83^\circ$ ✔

   - $62^\circ$

   - $27^\circ$

   - $97^\circ$

   _Explanation:_ Since $AB$ is parallel to $CD$ and $BE$ is a transversal, the co-interior angle at $ABE$ is $180^\circ-118^\circ=62^\circ$. In triangle $ABE$, the angles add to $180^\circ$, so $\angle BAE=180^\circ-62^\circ-35^\circ=83^\circ$.
