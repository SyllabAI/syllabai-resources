# Finding the Area of Any Triangle

Learn the sine area formula so you can find triangle areas, missing angles, and unknown side expressions confidently.

**Practice questions**

1. A triangle has sides 6 cm and 10 cm, with an included angle of $40°$. Which calculation gives its area?

   - $6\times 10\times \sin 40°$

   - $\frac{1}{2}\times 6\times 40\times \sin 10°$

   - $\frac{1}{2}\times 6\times 10\times \sin 40°$ ✔

   - $\frac{1}{2}\times 6\times 10\times \sin 80°$

   _Explanation:_ Use $A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$, where $C$ is the angle between sides $a$ and $b$. Therefore the calculation is $\frac{1}{2}\times 6\times 10\times \sin 40°$.

2. A triangle has sides 11 cm and 13 cm with an included angle of $40°$. What is its area to 1 decimal place?

   - $46.0\text{ cm}^2$ ✔

   - $92.0\text{ cm}^2$

   - $45.9\text{ cm}^2$

   - $54.8\text{ cm}^2$

   _Explanation:_ $A=\frac{1}{2}\times 11\times 13\times \sin 40°=45.959\ldots$, which rounds to $46.0\text{ cm}^2$.

3. A triangle has sides 10 cm and 16 cm and an area of $40\text{ cm}^2$. The included angle $x$ is obtuse. What is $x$?

   - $30°$

   - $60°$

   - $120°$

   - $150°$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ $40=\frac{1}{2}\times 10\times 16\times \sin x$, so $\sin x=0.5$. The calculator gives $30°$, but the obtuse angle with the same sine is $180°-30°=150°$.

4. Two sides of a triangle meet at $30°$. Their lengths are in the ratio $4:3$, and the area is $96\text{ cm}^2$. What is the shorter side?

   - $16\sqrt{2}$ cm

   - $12\sqrt{2}$ cm ✔

   - $4\sqrt{2}$ cm

   - 12 cm

   _Explanation:_ Write the sides as $4k$ and $3k$. Then $96=\frac{1}{2}(4k)(3k)\sin 30°=3k^2$, so $k=4\sqrt{2}$. The shorter side is $3k=12\sqrt{2}$ cm.

5. A triangle has area $54\text{ cm}^2$. Two sides enclosing an angle of $30°$ have lengths 9 cm and $x$ cm. Find $x$.

   - 6 cm

   - 12 cm

   - 24 cm ✔

   - 27 cm

   _Explanation:_ Using $A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$ gives $54=\frac{1}{2}\times 9\times x\times \sin 30°=2.25x$. Therefore $x=54\div 2.25=24$ cm.
