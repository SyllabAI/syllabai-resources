# Writing a Ratio as a Fraction or Linear Function

Learn how to connect ratios using fractions, common multipliers, and simple algebraic links.

**Practice questions**

1. Paint is mixed in the ratio red:blue $=7:3$. What fraction of the mixture is blue paint?

   - $\frac{3}{7}$

   - $\frac{3}{10}$ ✔

   - $\frac{7}{10}$

   - $\frac{7}{3}$

   _Explanation:_ There are $7+3=10$ parts altogether, of which 3 are blue. Therefore, the fraction that is blue is $\frac{3}{10}$.

2. If $p:q=5:8$, which equation writes $q$ in terms of $p$?

   - $q=\frac{5}{8}p$

   - $p=\frac{8}{5}q$

   - $q=\frac{8}{5}p$ ✔

   - $q=5p+8$

   _Explanation:_ For every 5 parts represented by $p$, $q$ has 8 parts. Therefore, $q$ is $\frac{8}{5}$ times $p$, so $q=\frac{8}{5}p$.

3. Ali, Bea and Chen share some counters. $A:B=2:3$ and $B:C=4:5$. Chen has 14 more counters than Ali. How many counters does Bea have?

   - 14

   - 30

   - 56

   - 24 ✔

   _Explanation:_ Match Bea's parts: $A:B=8:12$ and $B:C=12:15$, so $A:B:C=8:12:15$. The difference between Chen and Ali is 7 parts, so one part is $14\div7=2$. Bea has $12\times2=24$ counters.

4. Students choose exactly one of football, tennis and cricket. The ratio football:not football is $3:5$, and the ratio cricket:not cricket is $1:3$. What is football:tennis:cricket?

   - $3:3:2$ ✔

   - $3:5:2$

   - $3:2:3$

   - $3:5:1$

   _Explanation:_ Football is $\frac{3}{8}$ of the students, while cricket is $\frac{1}{4}=\frac{2}{8}$. Tennis is the remaining $1-\frac{3}{8}-\frac{2}{8}=\frac{3}{8}$, giving $3:3:2$.

5. Points $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ lie in order on a straight line. $AB:BD=1:2$ and $AC:CD=5:4$. What is $AB:BC:CD$?

   - $5:10:12$

   - $3:2:4$ ✔

   - $5:6:4$

   - $1:2:4$

   _Explanation:_ Let $AB=x$, $BC=y$ and $CD=z$. Then $y+z=2x$ and $4(x+y)=5z$. The ratio $x:y:z=3:2:4$ satisfies both equations, so $AB:BC:CD=3:2:4$.
