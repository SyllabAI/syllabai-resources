# Time

Build confidence with minutes, hours, clock times and simple travel timetables.

**Example 1: Convert 300 minutes to hours.**

There are 60 minutes in every hour.  
To convert from minutes to hours we need to divide by 60.  
300 ÷ 60  
300 ÷ 60 is the same as 30 ÷ 6 (this is like simplifying a fraction)  
30 ÷ 6 = 5  
**There are 5 hours in 300 minutes.**


**Example 2: Convert 7 hours to minutes.**

There are 60 minutes in every hour. To convert from hours to minutes we need to multiply by 60.  
7 × 60  
7 × 60 = 420  
**There are 420 minutes in 7 hours.**


**Example 3: Convert 281 minutes to hours. Give your answer in hours and minutes.**

There are 60 minutes in every hour. We can write down the 60 times table to see how many whole hours are in 281 minutes.



4 hours = 240 minutes  
There are 4 whole hours in 281 minutes.  
We can calculate how many additional minutes there are by taking 240 away from 281  
281 - 240 = 41  
**281 minutes = 4 hours and 41 minutes.**


**Practice questions**

1. A lesson lasts $1\frac{3}{4}$ hours. How many minutes is this?

   - 75 minutes

   - 90 minutes

   - 175 minutes

   - 105 minutes ✔

   _Explanation:_ One hour is 60 minutes and $\frac{3}{4}$ of an hour is 45 minutes, so the total is $60+45=105$ minutes.

2. Convert 390 minutes into hours and minutes.

   - 6 hours 50 minutes

   - 6 hours 30 minutes ✔

   - 5 hours 30 minutes

   - 6 hours 15 minutes

   _Explanation:_ Six hours is $6\times60=360$ minutes. The remaining $390-360=30$ minutes gives 6 hours 30 minutes.

3. How many minutes are there in 2 hours and 20 minutes?

   - 120 minutes

   - 220 minutes

   - 140 minutes ✔

   - 80 minutes

   _Explanation:_ Two hours is $2\times60=120$ minutes. Adding 20 minutes gives $120+20=140$ minutes.


**Example 4: A film starts at 7:50 pm and runs for 89 minutes. Work out the time when the film ends.**

There are 60 minutes in every hour.  
We need 10 minutes to get to the next hour (8 pm). 89 - 10 = 79 (there are 79 minutes of film left)  
Another 60 minutes will get to 9 pm. 79 -60 = 19 (there are 19 minutes of the film left)



**9pm + 19 minutes = 9.19 pm**


**Practice questions**

1. A show starts at 7:45 pm and lasts 50 minutes. What time does it finish?

   - 8:25 pm

   - 8:35 pm ✔

   - 7:95 pm

   - 7:35 pm

   _Explanation:_ It takes 15 minutes to reach 8:00 pm, leaving 35 minutes. Therefore, the show finishes at 8:35 pm.

2. A train leaves at 13:48 and arrives at 15:05. How long is the journey?

   - 1 hour 17 minutes ✔

   - 1 hour 23 minutes

   - 2 hours 17 minutes

   - 57 minutes

   _Explanation:_ From 13:48 to 14:00 is 12 minutes, from 14:00 to 15:00 is 60 minutes, and another 5 minutes gives 77 minutes, or 1 hour 17 minutes.

3. Leo must be at school by 09:00. It takes 6 minutes to walk from School Stop to school and 9 minutes to walk from home to Home Stop. Buses run from Home Stop to School Stop at 08:10–08:35, 08:20–08:45 and 08:30–08:55. What is the latest time Leo can leave home?

   - 08:20

   - 08:21

   - 08:11 ✔

   - 08:30

   _Explanation:_ Leo needs to reach School Stop by 08:54, so the 08:20–08:45 bus is the latest one he can use. He must leave home 9 minutes before 08:20, at 08:11.
