**Practice questions**

1. Work out $\frac{3}{5}$ of 45.

   - 15

   - 27 ✔

   - 9

   - 135

   _Explanation:_ First find one fifth: $45 \div 5 = 9$. Then multiply by 3: $9 \times 3 = 27$.

2. A game costs £45. Its price is reduced by $\frac{2}{5}$ in a sale. What is the sale price?

   - £18

   - £36

   - £63

   - £27 ✔

   _Explanation:_ The reduction is $\frac{2}{5}$ of £45, which is £18. Subtract it from the original price: $£45-£18=£27$.

3. At a concert there are 200 people: 80 adults, 48 teenagers and the rest children. $\frac{5}{8}$ of the children are girls and the rest are boys. What is the difference between the number of girls and boys?

   - 18 ✔

   - 45

   - 27

   - 72

   _Explanation:_ There are $200-80-48=72$ children. There are $\frac{5}{8}\times72=45$ girls and $72-45=27$ boys, so the difference is $45-27=18$.
