# Perpendicular Lines and the equation of a tangent

Learn how perpendicular gradients unlock equations of lines, bisectors and tangents to circles.

**Practice questions**

1. A line has equation $y=-4x+7$. What is the gradient of a line perpendicular to it?

   - $-4$

   - $\frac{1}{4}$ ✔

   - $-\frac{1}{4}$

   - $4$

   _Explanation:_ Perpendicular gradients have a product of $-1$. The negative reciprocal of $-4$ is $\frac{1}{4}$.

2. A line passes through $(2,5)$ and is perpendicular to $y=\frac{1}{3}x-2$. What is its equation?

   - $y=3x-1$

   - $y=-\frac{1}{3}x+\frac{17}{3}$

   - $y=\frac{1}{3}x+\frac{13}{3}$

   - $y=-3x+11$ ✔

   _Explanation:_ The perpendicular gradient is $-3$. Substituting $(2,5)$ into $y=-3x+c$ gives $5=-6+c$, so $c=11$.

3. Which line is perpendicular to $4x-2y=7$?

   - $y=-\frac{1}{2}x+3$ ✔

   - $y=\frac{1}{2}x-3$

   - $y=-2x+3$

   - $y=2x-3$

   _Explanation:_ Rearranging gives $y=2x-\frac{7}{2}$, so its gradient is $2$. A perpendicular line must therefore have gradient $-\frac{1}{2}$.

4. The endpoints of a line segment are $A(2,1)$ and $B(6,9)$. What is the equation of its perpendicular bisector?

   - $y=2x-3$

   - $y=-\frac{1}{2}x+2$

   - $y=-\frac{1}{2}x+7$ ✔

   - $y=\frac{1}{2}x+3$

   _Explanation:_ The midpoint is $(4,5)$ and the gradient of $AB$ is $2$, so the perpendicular gradient is $-\frac{1}{2}$. Using $(4,5)$ gives $5=-2+c$, hence $c=7$.

5. The circle $x^2+y^2=100$ has a point $P(6,8)$ on its circumference. What is the equation of the tangent at $P$?

   - $y=\frac{4}{3}x$

   - $y=-\frac{3}{4}x+\frac{25}{2}$ ✔

   - $y=-\frac{4}{3}x+16$

   - $y=\frac{3}{4}x+\frac{7}{2}$

   _Explanation:_ The radius from $(0,0)$ to $(6,8)$ has gradient $\frac{4}{3}$, so the tangent has gradient $-\frac{3}{4}$. Substituting $(6,8)$ gives $8=-\frac{3}{4}(6)+c$, so $c=\frac{25}{2}$.
