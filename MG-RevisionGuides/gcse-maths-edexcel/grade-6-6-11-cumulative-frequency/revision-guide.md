# Cumulative Frequency

Turn grouped data into running-total graphs that reveal medians, quartiles and spread.

**Practice questions**

1. The frequencies for the intervals 0–10, 10–20, 20–30 and 30–40 are 4, 7, 11 and 6. What is the cumulative frequency up to 30?

   - 11

   - 18

   - 22 ✔

   - 28

   _Explanation:_ Up to 30 includes the first three intervals, so the cumulative frequency is $4+7+11=22$.

2. The cumulative frequency up to the class interval 30–40 is 29. Which point should be plotted on a cumulative frequency graph?

   - $(40,29)$ ✔

   - $(35,29)$

   - $(30,29)$

   - $(29,40)$

   _Explanation:_ A cumulative frequency is plotted against the upper class boundary. The upper boundary is 40, so the point is $(40,29)$.

3. The cumulative frequency up to 20 minutes is 14 and the cumulative frequency up to 30 minutes is 35. How many values are in the interval 20–30 minutes?

   - 14

   - 35

   - 49

   - 21 ✔

   _Explanation:_ Subtract consecutive cumulative frequencies to find the class frequency: $35-14=21$.

4. A cumulative frequency graph represents 80 values. At which cumulative frequencies should you read off $Q_1$, the median and $Q_3$?

   - 10, 40, 70

   - 20, 40, 60 ✔

   - 20, 60, 80

   - 40, 60, 80

   _Explanation:_ Use $N/4$, $N/2$ and $3N/4$. For $N=80$, these are 20, 40 and 60.

5. Group A has median 52, $Q_1=38$ and $Q_3=67$. Group B has median 55, $Q_1=44$ and $Q_3=64$. Which comparison is correct?

   - Group A has the higher median and smaller IQR

   - Group A has the higher median and larger IQR

   - Group B has the higher median and smaller IQR ✔

   - Group B has the higher median and larger IQR

   _Explanation:_ Group B has the higher median because $55>52$. Its IQR is $64-44=20$, which is smaller than Group A's IQR of $67-38=29$.
