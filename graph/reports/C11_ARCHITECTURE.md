## 1. Purpose & research questions

T-C10 answers: *which validated revision notes substantively teach each
SpecificationPoint?* T-C11 answers: *what concepts constitute those
SpecificationPoints, what concepts are actually required to understand them, and
what misconceptions / wrong-answer patterns / remediation relationships are

Non-goals of the pilot (explicit):

- NOT building the full 4CH1 concept graph (blocked on §16 authorization).
- NOT creating assessment-graph content (Phase 4 / T-C06 F-168 territory; question
  distractor mining is out of scope — only mark-scheme *evidence quoting* is used).
- NOT wiring the DB (T-C06); graph-as-code is authoritative (§8A.14).
- NOT minting Student-Book concepts (383-page OCR not yet run).
- NOT promoting anything to HUMAN_VALIDATED. Generation emits candidates only.
- NOT maximizing graph density. Success = precision + provenance + auditability +
  correct abstention. Every edge the system declines to draw for insufficient
  evidence, and every such declination that survives review, is a pilot success.

## 2. Provenance hierarchy (information flow)

```text
official specification (4CH1 Issue 3, PDF + pinned OCR md)
        → SpecificationPoint registry (T-C09, RULE_DERIVED)          [SP authority]
validated T-C10 mapping (209 HUMAN_VALIDATED note↔SP blocks)          [coverage evidence]
        → concept extraction (AI candidate concepts)                 [Stage C]
        → prerequisite/misconception inference (AI candidate edges)  [Stage D]
        → evidence + provenance + confidence attached                [Stage E]
        → pilot validation: adversarial 2nd-pass review + operator   [Stage F, pilot]
        → (operator command only) HUMAN_VALIDATED authoritative relations
```

Machine rule (the load-bearing one, enforced in generator + checker + negative
tests): **a concept may attach to SP X only if at least one of its evidence
anchors is (a) the official wording of X (specification_points.yaml, itself
md-line-provenanced), or (b) a verbatim quote from a note whose T-C10 mapping to X
is HUMAN_VALIDATED.** Mark-scheme anchors are admissible *only* for
misconception-family relations (assessment evidence class), never as coverage
evidence for concept attachment. This is the rule that makes 4.15 structurally
uncoverable (§11).

## 4. Edge schema (graph/concept_edges.yaml)

Relation vocabulary = V2 enum ∪ {COMMONLY_CONFUSED_WITH, WRONG_ANSWER_PATTERN}
(§8A.11 triple distinction). Semantics and direction rules — all machine-enforced:

| relation | direction | semantics | admissible source→target | constraints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PART_OF | child→parent | concept is a constituent of the SP's demanded/taught substance | CONCEPT→SPEC_POINT | edge carries `role` (CORE/SUPPORTING/ENRICHMENT); attachment rule §2 |
| REQUIRES_PREREQUISITE | dependent→prerequisite | target is genuinely needed to learn source (actual learning dependency) | CONCEPT→CONCEPT, PRACTICAL→CONCEPT | acyclic; NOT mention-order; evidence must show use-without-reteaching or definitional containment |
| RELATED_TO | either (rationale required) | evidence-backed association that is NOT a learning dependency | CONCEPT↔CONCEPT | must not duplicate a REQUIRES_PREREQUISITE pair; the "not a dependency" justification is mandatory |
| MISCONCEPTION_OF | misconception→concept | erroneous belief about the concept | MISCONCEPTION(pattern_class=ERRONEOUS_BELIEF)→CONCEPT | misconception node needs explicit named-source evidence (§8A.11) |
| WRONG_ANSWER_PATTERN | pattern→concept | documented wrong answer in assessment contexts of the concept | MISCONCEPTION(pattern_class=WRONG_ANSWER_PATTERN)→CONCEPT | mark-scheme verbatim evidence mandatory |
| EXPLAINED_BY | explained→explainer | the explainer concept grounds/derives the explained concept | CONCEPT→CONCEPT | single-source causal/explanatory teaching required (premise+consequence aggregation forbidden) |
| REMEDIATED_BY | misconception→concept | correct understanding of target remediates the error | MISCONCEPTION→CONCEPT | remediation_evidence mandatory on the node |
| COMMONLY_CONFUSED_WITH | either (symmetric) | two concepts students conflate per explicit source statement | CONCEPT↔CONCEPT | explicit confusion statement required; no paired-mention inference |

Record schema:

```yaml
- source: 4CH1-CON-REACTING-MASS
  relation: REQUIRES_PREREQUISITE
  target: 4CH1-CON-MOLE-MASS-CONV
  evidence:                     # ≥1 anchor; load-bearing for THIS edge
  - kind: NOTE
    file: <path>
    quote: <verbatim>
  provenance:
    tier: AI_SUGGESTED
    model_version: GLM (Super Z agent, z.ai)
    extraction_pass: c11-pilot-pass-1
    derivation_method: USED_WITHOUT_RETEACHING   # §6 controlled vocabulary
    derivation_notes: <why this is an actual learning dependency>
    upstream: <validated upstream chain>
    generated_date: '2026-09-11'
  confidence: high
  validation_status: SUGGESTED    # or REVIEW_REQUIRED (§7)
  ambiguity_note: null            # REVIEW_REQUIRED only: what is uncertain
  version: 1
  created_at: '2026-09-11'
```

Forbidden (machine-checked): self-edges; duplicate (source, relation, target);
unknown relation types; endpoints outside the declared node universe
(concepts.yaml ∪ specification_points.yaml ∪ practicals.yaml, pilot-scope);
REQUIRES_PREREQUISITE cycles; HUMAN_VALIDATED status from generation; 4.15
attachment (§11).

**Relation-vs-relation discipline (the instruction's core semantic rules,
operationalized):**

- REQUIRES_PREREQUISITE requires evidence of *use-without-reteaching* (the
  dependent note uses the prerequisite as a given), *definitional containment*
  (the source's definition contains the target), or *explicit taught sequence*
  within one note. "Mentioned before" alone is inadmissible.
- EXPLAINED_BY requires the causal/explanatory teaching to be present *in a single
  anchor source* ("enables us to … since …"). Premise in note A + consequence in
  note B is inadmissible — that is the 4.15 failure mode.
- MISCONCEPTION_OF / WRONG_ANSWER_PATTERN require an explicit mistake/wrong-answer
  statement in a note (Examiner Tips) or mark scheme (ACCEPT/REJECT/IGNORE/"i.e.
  failing to …"). Generic chemistry folklore is inadmissible.
- RELATED_TO is the *residual* class: only after a candidate fails the dependency /
  explanation / misconception tests but still has a real evidenced association may
  RELATED_TO be considered — and if the association is weak, the correct output is
  abstention, not RELATED_TO. Zero RELATED_TO edges is an acceptable pilot outcome.

## 5. Provenance model

Per-record provenance block (nodes and edges), aligned to §8A.12 evidence anchors:

- `tier` ∈ {AI_SUGGESTED} for all pilot records (T-C09 RULE_DERIVED data is
  referenced, never re-minted; HUMAN_VALIDATED exists only as the *target* state
  reachable by operator promotion, never from generation).
- `model_version` (who generated), `extraction_pass` (which pass; enables
  re-extraction comparability), `generated_date` (pinned constant).
- `derivation_method` — closed vocabulary (§6); free text only in
  `derivation_notes`.
- `upstream` — the validated chain this record rests on, e.g. "T-C10 HUMAN_VALIDATED
  mapping 4CH1-1.28 @ Calculating moles and mass" or "specification_points.yaml
  4CH1-1.27 official wording (md line 421)" or "CFEC2 MS Q4(a) (pinned extraction
  sha 6c63a9e02b71)".
- Evidence anchors: `kind` ∈ {NOTE, SPEC, MARK_SCHEME} + `file` + `quote`
  (byte-verified). Mark-scheme anchors carry the pinned-extraction file under
  `scripts/c11_evidence/` (extraction reproducible from the repo PDFs; sha1 of each
  extraction recorded in concepts.yaml meta).

Coverage invariants (machine-checked): 100% of nodes have ≥1 evidence anchor; 100%
of edges have ≥1 evidence anchor; 100% of records have complete provenance blocks;
100% of quotes byte-verify; 100% of misconception-family nodes have remediation
evidence.

## 6. Confidence model

Two-layer model, kept qualitative-first for auditability (T-C10 convention):

**Band** ∈ {high, medium, low} — human-readable review priority.

**Derivation-method cap** — the closed `derivation_method` vocabulary *bounds* the
admissible band (machine-enforced; a high-confidence record with a capped method is
a schema violation):

| derivation_method | meaning | max band |
|---|---|---|
| SPEC_VERBATIM | concept/edge rests directly on official SP wording | high |
| DEFINITIONAL_DEPENDENCY | source definition mathematically/semantically contains target | high |
| USED_WITHOUT_RETEACHING | dependent note uses target as a given, never re-teaches it | high |
| EXPLICIT_TEACH_SEQUENCE | one note teaches target then source in that order | high |
| SINGLE_SOURCE_CAUSAL_TEACHING | one anchor teaches the explanatory link itself | high |
| ASSESSMENT_DOCUMENTED | mark scheme documents the wrong answer / tolerance verbatim | high |
| EXAMINER_TIP_EXPLICIT | note's Examiner-Tips block names the mistake | high |
| TEACH_SEQUENCE_WITHIN_NOTE | same-note ordering evidence only | medium |
| IMPLICIT_USE | concept used implicitly (e.g. table row labeled "moles") without naming | medium |
| EXAMINER_TIP_IMPLIED | tip prescribes correct practice without naming the error | low |
| RELATED_RESIDUAL | evidenced non-dependency association | medium |

Band semantics (for review triage): high = denial would contradict the quoted
evidence; medium = well-evidenced but judgment-dependent (review should verify the
relation *class*, not just existence); low = only implied — such records are
normally HELD out of the graph rather than emitted (see §7).

## 7. Validation-state model

States (graph-as-code superset; V2 projection §13):

| state | meaning | who can set it |
|---|---|---|
| SUGGESTED | AI candidate with complete evidence+provenance; awaits review | generator (only) |
| REVIEW_REQUIRED | evidence-complete but semantically ambiguous; explicitly uncertain (§8A.13); carries `ambiguity_note` | generator (only) |
| HUMAN_VALIDATED | authoritative relation; operator-promoted on the review sheet | operator command only (staged batch, T-C10 pattern) |
| REJECTED | candidate considered and rejected — NOT a graph state; lives in the review artifacts (rejected/held lists) | review records (only) |

Machine rules: generation may emit only SUGGESTED/REVIEW_REQUIRED; the generator
hard-fails if the decision record contains any HUMAN_VALIDATED (anti-forgery);
checkers reject HUMAN_VALIDATED unless a complete validated_by/date block exists
(promotion pathway: scripts/c11_promote.py + scripts/c11_promotions.yaml, §18).

**Operator review verdicts (session 41+)**: REJECT / HOLD / PENDING decisions
are recorded as `operator_decision` blocks inside the decision record (and on
its held candidates), re-authored per the §8 operator-decision precedent — with
decided_by / decided_date / review_reference and the operator's reasons
verbatim. These blocks are review state, NOT validation state: they are never
emitted into graph/*.yaml (the generator emits only its fixed key schema), and
they can never carry HUMAN_VALIDATED — promotion remains exclusively the
promotions-file pathway (§18). An operator REJECT is applied by re-authoring:
the edge leaves the authored set and is preserved as a rejected candidate
(REJECTED is not a graph state; HELD-13 is the first instance).

Two abstention channels, deliberately distinct:

- **HELD (not in graph)** — insufficient evidence or unsafe semantics; recorded in
  the review sheet's held list with the specific failing rule. The system's correct
  "insufficient evidence" output.
- **REVIEW_REQUIRED (in graph)** — evidence complete, semantics uncertain; the
  §8A.13 explicit-uncertainty state. The pilot must emit ≥1 of each to demonstrate
  both channels.

## 8. Concept identity policy (split-first)

TODO T-C11 (authoritative): *over-splitting is recoverable, over-merging corrupts
mastery attribution; merges are human-only.*

- **Minting**: concepts are minted per demanded-substance and per distinct taught
  procedure (a definition, a law, a constant, a calculation procedure, an
  experimental method are distinct concepts). Multi-part SPs decompose into
  multiple concepts (1.25 → word equations / balanced symbol equations / state
  symbols).
- **Aliases**: every node carries `aliases`; the alias table is the merge input.
  "Mole", "amount of substance", "chemical amount" are aliases of ONE node — the
  note and spec use these interchangeably, so this is not a split, it is an alias.
- **Definition-vs-procedure split**: where a SP demands definitions (know-terms
  command kind) and the corpus also teaches a calculation procedure for the same
  objects, the definition concepts and the procedure concept are SEPARATE nodes
  (mirrors 1.32 know-terms vs 1.33 calculate — the command-kind distinction made
  structural).
- **No free-form mass minting**: all pilot concepts come from the curated decision
  record; the generator rejects any node not in it. Expansion passes add nodes via
  new decision records with their own extraction_pass id.
- **Merges**: machine-forbidden. If review finds two nodes that should be one, the
  operator records the merge in the review sheet and the decision record is
  re-authored (nodes re-versioned, never silently folded).
- **Cross-slice boundary**: concepts outside the pilot slice are NOT minted.
  Cross-slice prerequisites are representable as edges only to existing node
  universes (SP / practical nodes); boundary concepts that would need new nodes are
  recorded in the coverage-gap report instead (e.g. chemical-formulae
  interpretation, isotope abundance — outside the slice).

## 10. Deterministic generation contract

- **Single source of truth**: `scripts/c11_pilot_decisions.yaml` (the AI decision
  record, analogous to c10_decisions/S*.json). Hand-editing generated files is
  forbidden and silently reverted by re-runs (T-C10 discipline).
- **Generator**: `scripts/c11_concept_pilot.py` — validates the decision record
  (schema §3/§4, vocabularies, evidence byte-verification, T-C10 mapping
  cross-check, negative-control rules §11, confidence caps §6, acyclicity,
  uniqueness), then emits `graph/concepts.yaml`, `graph/concept_edges.yaml`,
  `graph/spec_command_kinds.yaml`. Emission is canonical: fixed key order, sorted
  record order (nodes by family then code; edges by relation then source then
  target), pinned dates, no timestamps, no randomness, no environment reads.
- **Idempotence**: a second run over an unchanged decision record must be
  byte-identical (verified by the negative-test script's positive control and by
  the pilot report's regeneration check).
- **Gates**: the generator itself hard-fails on any violation (fail-closed); it
  never "repairs" data. All failures name the record and the violated rule.
- **Reproducibility chain**: decisions (authored, reviewed) → generator (gated) →
  YAML (checked by graph_check groups 10–11) → review sheet + report (rendered by
  `scripts/c11_review_render.py` / `scripts/c11_report_render.py` from the same
  decision record, so documents cannot drift from data).

## 11. Negative-control rules (4CH1-4.15)

4.15 ("explain how the combustion of some impurities in hydrocarbon fuels results
in the formation of sulfur dioxide") is the pilot's deliberate negative control.
Machine rules (generator + checker + negative tests, fail-closed):

1. No concept/misconception node may attach to 4CH1-4.15 (no validated T-C10
   mapping exists → the §2 attachment rule cannot be satisfied → structurally
   impossible).
2. No edge may carry an evidence anchor that resolves to a note whose T-C10
   mappings do not HUMAN_VALIDATED-cover the SP the edge's endpoints attach to
   (mark-scheme anchors exempt, misconception-class only).
3. The premise/consequence pair (Definition-of-combustion note: "All these fuels
   contain carbon, hydrogen and small quantities of sulfur"; Nitrogen-Oxides-&-
   Sulfur-Dioxide note: "The sulfur dioxide produced from the combustion of fossil
   fuels dissolves in rainwater") is *known superficial evidence* — the negative
   test injects an EXPLAINED_BY / MISCONCEPTION_OF / REMEDIATED_BY candidate built
   from exactly these two notes and the checker MUST reject it (rule 2 + the
   single-source causal-teaching rule §4).
4. The coverage-gap report must record 4.15 with the corpus reason (premise +
   stated-result present, causal mechanism taught nowhere) and the remediation
   pathway (new corpus note or student-book OCR teaching the mechanism — an
   operator content decision, never a graph-side inference).

## 18. Promotion mechanism (operator-only, exact-edge-identity)

Added in the session-40 review round. The tasking properties and their

| never promote by node or relation type alone | 3-token exact spec is the only accepted syntax; partial/wildcard specs are rejected at parse (T03); PART_OF and held candidates are categorically rejected (T05/T06) |
| preserve evidence / provenance / confidence | the generator adds ONLY validation fields; T01c asserts all other edge fields byte-identical, T01d the decision record untouched |
| idempotent | re-promoting an identity is a reported no-op; entries keyed by triple; T01h |
| preserve candidate/held records | promotions live in a separate file; the decision record (including `held:`) is never mutated by promotion |
| support HUMAN_VALIDATED state | emitted as `validation_status: HUMAN_VALIDATED` + `validated_by` + `validated_date` (schema §7 extension, operator-only tier) |
| fail closed on malformed or missing evidence | tool pre-verifies every anchor quote (T-C10 norm byte-verification) before writing (T08/T09); generator G13 re-validates the record (T10–T13); checker c11.10/c11.13 re-validates against the real repo files |
| positive and negative tests | scripts/c11_promote_test.py — 25/25 (T01–T18) in a sandbox repo copy; live store untouched |
| preserve deterministic regeneration | promotion state is a pure function of (decision record, promotions file); zero promotions emits the exact pre-promotion bytes (verified live); N>0 promotions re-generate byte-identically (T01g) |

Pathway (c10_promote pattern, decisions-side):

Anti-forgery closure (three layers):

1. The AI decision record may never carry HUMAN_VALIDATED (G10; c11.13

2. The promotions record may never carry AI attribution

## 20. Ontology decisions (recorded rulings)

Session 41 (2026-09-11). Ontology decisions are identity-policy rulings that
generalize beyond a single candidate: recorded here so the §16 expansion
inherits them as generation rules, cross-referenced from the review sheet.
A ruling never changes validation state and never promotes anything; merges
remain operator-only (§8). Operator rulings are recorded verbatim.

### OD-1 — The yield triple stays split (three concept nodes)

**Question** (pass-2 flag; finding FP-2): should CON-YIELD (actual yield),
CON-THEOR-YIELD (theoretical yield) and CON-PERCENT-YIELD (percentage yield)
merge into one yield concept? The two PERCENT-YIELD → {YIELD, THEOR-YIELD}
prerequisite edges share one anchor sentence, and FP-2 noted both "vanish
under an operator merge".

**Ruling (recorded for the operator; agent-derived from the frozen identity
policy, exercising the operator's delegated session-41 instruction to resolve
the question as an ontology decision): NO MERGE — the three nodes stay
split.**

1. Minting rule (§8): the triple is two definitions + one calculation
   procedure — "a definition, a law, a constant, a calculation procedure, an
   experimental method are distinct concepts". Each node carries distinct
   taught substance: two distinct definition sentences in the 1.30-mapped
   note plus the SPEC-demanded comparison procedure.
2. Definition-vs-procedure split (§8; the 1.32 know-terms vs 1.33-calculate
   precedent) applies directly: 4CH1-1.30 demands a calculation, and the
   operand definitions and the procedure are separate node kinds by frozen
   rule.
3. Mastery attribution: the three are independently assessable (a candidate
   can define theoretical yield without executing the percentage computation
   and vice versa — this very unit separates KNOW_TERM from CALCULATE
   demands, e.g. 1.27 vs 1.28). A merge would credit definitional knowledge
   from procedural mastery and vice versa — exactly the "over-merging
   corrupts mastery attribution" failure; over-splitting is recoverable, the
   reverse is not.
4. The same-anchor edge pair is not a split artifact: "The percentage yield
   compares the actual yield to the theoretical yield" names BOTH operands
   of the one taught comparison — one sentence can ground two distinct
   dependencies (numerator object, denominator object). Edge multiplicity
   mirroring the formula's operand structure is the honest representation,
   not a defect to be normalized away.

**Generalized rule for §16** (why this is an ontology decision, not a mapping
tweak): where an SP demands a calculation procedure whose formula operates on
distinctly-defined operands, mint ONE procedure concept and ONE concept per
distinctly-taught operand definition, and emit one DEFINITIONAL_DEPENDENCY
prerequisite per operand; same-anchor multiplicity is expected and correct.
Directly applicable ahead to 1.34C (concentration vs amount/volume operands)
and every future formula-driven SP.

A future merge remains an operator-only identity decision, recorded on the
review sheet with nodes re-versioned.
