         Assessment objectives
         The assessment objectives (AOs) are:


         AO1 Knowledge and understanding
         •     Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of business concepts, terms and theories.
         •     Use business terminology.


         AO2 Application
         •     Apply knowledge and understanding of business concepts, terms and theories to a given business.


         AO3 Analysis
         •     Select, organise and interpret business data and information in written, numerical and graphical forms.
         •     Analyse business information.


         AO4 Evaluation
         •     Make justified decisions, reach conclusions and make recommendations based on evidence.


         Weighting for assessment objectives
