         Assessment objectives
         The assessment objectives (AOs) are:


         AO1 Knowledge and understanding
         •     Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of facts, terms, concepts, policies, procedures and
               techniques.
         •     Apply this knowledge and understanding to a variety of accounting situations and problems, and present
               outcomes in an appropriate form.


         AO2 Analysis
         •     Select, calculate and interpret relevant data and information.
         •     Analyse financial accounting information.
         •     Communicate outcomes in the most appropriate form.


         AO3 Evaluation
         •     Interpret and evaluate accounting information and draw conclusions.
