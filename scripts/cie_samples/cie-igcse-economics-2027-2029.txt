         Assessment objectives
         The assessment objectives (AOs) are:


         AO1 Knowledge and understanding
         •     Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of economic definitions, formulas, concepts and theories.
         •     Use economic terminology.


         AO2 Analysis
         •     Select, organise and interpret economics data and information.
         •     Apply economic analysis to written, numerical, diagrammatic and graphical data.
         •     Analyse economic issues, identifying and developing links and relationships.


         AO3 Evaluation
         •     Evaluate economic information, data and arguments, and recognise that economic decisions have
               uncertain outcomes.
