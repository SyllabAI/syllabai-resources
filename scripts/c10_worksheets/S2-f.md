## Spec points in this subsection

- **4CH1-2.29** (2.29) · DAMAGE: understand how to use the pH scale, from 0-14, can be used to classify solutions as strongly acidic(0-3), weakly acidic(4-6), neutral(7), weakly alkaline(8-10)和 strongly alkaline(11-14)
