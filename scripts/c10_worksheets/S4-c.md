# Worksheet 4CH1-S4-c — Alkanes
SME group `4-3-alkanes` · notes: 2 · spec points: 4

## Spec points in this subsection

- **4CH1-4.19** (4.19): know the general formula for alkanes
- **4CH1-4.20** (4.20): explain why alkanes are classified as saturated hydrocarbons
- **4CH1-4.21** (4.21): understand how to draw the structural and displayed formulae for alkanes with up to five carbon atoms in the molecule,and to name the unbranched-chain isomers
