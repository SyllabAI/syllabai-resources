The R- represents a varying hydrocarbon chain - The general formula of a carboxylic acid is C<sub><b>n</b></sub>H<sub><b>2n+1</b></sub>COOH which can be shortened to just RCOOH Careful: When using the general formula C<sub><b>n</b></sub>H<sub><b>2n+1</b></sub>COOH, the 'n' does not represent the total number of carbons in the molecule. It only counts the carbons in the alkyl chain. This is a common point of confusion. For example, methanoic acid (HCOOH), n = 0!
