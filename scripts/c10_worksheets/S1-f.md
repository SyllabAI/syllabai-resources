# Worksheet 4CH1-S1-f — Ionic bonding
SME group `1-6-ionic-bonding` · notes: 5 · spec points: 7

## Spec points in this subsection

- **4CH1-1.37** (1.37): understand how ions are formed by electron loss or gain
- **4CH1-1.38** (1.38) · DAMAGE: know the charges of these ions: •metals in Groups 1,2 and 3 •non-metals in Groups 5,6 and 7 •Ag+，Cu2+，Fe2+，Fe3+，Pb2+，Zn2+ •hydrogen（H+），hydroxide（OH-），ammonium（NH4+），carbonate（CO32-），nitrate（NO3-），sulfate（SO42-）
- **4CH1-1.39** (1.39): write formulae for compounds formed between the ions listed above
- **4CH1-1.40** (1.40) · DAMAGE: draw dot-and-cross diagrams to show the formation of ionic compounds by electron transfer，limited to combinations of elements from Groups1，2，3和5，6，7only outer electrons need be shown
- **4CH1-1.41** (1.41): understand ionic bonding in terms of electrostatic attractions
- **4CH1-1.42** (1.42): understand why compounds with giant ionic lattices have high melting and boiling points
- **4CH1-1.43** (1.43) · DAMAGE: know that ionic compounds do not conduct electricity when solid，but do conduct electricity when molten and in aqueous solution
