# Worksheet 4CH1-S3-c — Reversible reactions and equilibria
SME group `3-3-reversible-reactions-and-equilibria` · notes: 3 · spec points: 6

## Spec points in this subsection

- **4CH1-3.17** (3.17) · DAMAGE: know that some reactions are reversible and this is indicated by the symbol $\rightleftharpoons$ in equations
- **4CH1-3.18** (3.18): describe reversible reactions such as the dehydration of hydrated copper(II) sulfate and the effect of heat on ammonium chloride
- **4CH1-3.19C** (3.19C) [C]: know that a reversible reaction can reach dynamic equilibrium in a sealed container
- **4CH1-3.20C** (3.20C) [C]: know that the characteristics of a reaction at dynamic equilibrium are: • the forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate • the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant.
- **4CH1-3.21C** (3.21C) [C]: understand why a catalyst does not affect the position of equilibrium in a reversible reaction
- **4CH1-3.22C** (3.22C) [C] · DAMAGE: know the effect of changing either temperature or pressure on the position of equilibrium in a reversible reaction: • an increase (or decrease) in temperature shifts the position of equilibrium in the direction of the endothermic (or exothermic) reaction • an increase (or decrease) in pressure shifts the position of equilibrium in the direction that produces fewer (or more) moles of gas References to Le Chatelier's principle are not required
