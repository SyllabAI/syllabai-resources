# Worksheet 4CH1-S2-e — Extraction and uses of metals
SME group `2-5-extraction-and-uses-of-metals` · notes: 4 · spec points: 6

## Spec points in this subsection

- **4CH1-2.22C** (2.22C) [C]: know that most metals are extracted from ores found in the Earth's crust and that unreactive metals are often found as the uncombined element
- **4CH1-2.23C** (2.23C) [C]: explain how the method of extraction of a metal is related to its position in the reactivity series, illustrated by carbon extraction for iron and electrolysis for aluminium
- **4CH1-2.24C** (2.24C) [C] · DAMAGE: be able to comment on a metal extraction process, given appropriate information detailed knowledge of the processes used in the extraction of a specific metal is not required
- **4CH1-2.25C** (2.25C) [C]: explain the uses of aluminium, copper, iron and steel in terms of their properties the types of steel will be limited to low-carbon (mild), high-carbon and stainless
- **4CH1-2.26C** (2.26C) [C]: know that an alloy is a mixture of a metal and one or more elements, usually other metals or carbon
- **4CH1-2.27C** (2.27C) [C]: explain why alloys are harder than pure metals
