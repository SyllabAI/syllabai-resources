## From words to equations

![Flowchart showing choose the unknown, write expressions, form an equation, solve, and check by substitution](../assets/001-c069d70c-dd9d-456b-bf40-e2a9f907d0c3.jpeg)
Many IGCSE questions give you a story, a diagram, or a total instead of an equation. The skill is to choose a variable, usually $x$, turn the information into algebra, then solve carefully.

An expression has no equals sign, like $3x+5$. An equation has an equals sign, like $3x+5=20$, and that is what you solve.

Use this routine every time: form first, solve second, check last. Checking means substituting your answer back into the perimeter, angle total, area, or context in the question.

## Collect like terms first

Perimeter means add every side before doing anything else. For sides $4x-3$, $2x+5$, and $x+1$, start by writing the expression: $$(4x-3)+(2x+5)+(x+1)$$

Collect the $x$ terms together and the numbers together. This simplifies the expression to: $$7x+3$$

If the question gives a total, turn the expression into an equation by setting it equal to that total. For example, a perimeter of 45 would give $7x+3=45$.

Keep each sign attached to its term while you add. A common mistake is to lose the $-3$ or forget one of the $x$ terms.

## Worked example: rectangle perimeter

A rectangle has length $3x+2$ cm and width $x+6$ cm, and its perimeter is 52 cm. Because a rectangle has two lengths and two widths, form
$$
2(3x+2)+2(x+6)=52
$$

Expand the brackets, then collect like terms. This gives
$$
6x+4+2x+12=52
$$
$$
8x+16=52
$$

Now use inverse operations to isolate $x$. Subtract 16 and divide by 8, so
$$
8x=36
$$
$$
x=4.5
$$

Check by substitution before you stop. The sides are $15.5$ cm and $10.5$ cm, and $15.5+10.5+15.5+10.5=52$ cm.

## Geometry facts for equations

![Labelled rectangle with sides 3x+2 and x+6, and triangle with angles 2x+15, x+20, and x-5](../assets/002-2b5964cf-d849-466d-a21c-fafdd29e546b.jpeg)
Geometry facts often tell you what the algebra must add up to or what two expressions must equal. In the figure, the rectangle uses two lengths and two widths, while the triangle reminds you that triangle angles total $180^\circ$.

Other useful facts are that angles on a straight line total $180^\circ$, angles around a point total $360^\circ$, and opposite sides of a rectangle are equal. For example, if opposite sides are labelled $4x-7$ and $2x+9$, you can form the equation $4x-7=2x+9$.

For triangle angles $2x+15$, $x+20$, and $x-5$, write
$$(2x+15)+(x+20)+(x-5)=180$$
Then simplify the left side to get
$$4x+30=180$$

Subtract 30 and divide by 4, which gives $x=37.5$. If the question asks for the actual angles, substitute $x$ back into the original expressions to get $90^\circ$, $57.5^\circ$, and $32.5^\circ$.

## Word problems: define the variable

In word problems, begin by defining $x$ clearly. A good choice is often the smallest amount or the first amount mentioned, because the other quantities become easy to write.

Suppose Amir has $x$ marbles, so Ben has $2x$ and Cara has $2x+6$. If they have 61 marbles altogether, form the equation: $$x+2x+(2x+6)=61$$

Collect like terms and solve using inverse operations. You get: $$5x+6=61$$ $$5x=55$$ $$x=11$$

Always answer the quantity the question actually asked, not just the value of $x$. In this case, Cara has $2x+6=2(11)+6=28$ marbles.

## Stretch: area equations

Area of a triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$. If both base and height contain $x$, the equation can involve $x^2$. For a right-angled triangle with perpendicular sides $5x$ and $6x$ and area $240\text{ cm}^2$, use: $$\frac{1}{2} \times 5x \times 6x = 240$$

Simplify first before solving. This gives $$15x^2=240$$ and then $$x^2=16$$

To undo the square, take the square root. Because $x$ represents a length, use the positive root and get $x=4$.

## Check yourself

1. A triangle has side lengths $4x-3$, $2x+5$, and $x+1$. Which simplified expression is its perimeter?

   - $$7x+3$ ✔

   - $$6x+3$

   - $$7x+1$

   - $$7x-3$

   _Explanation:_ Add all three sides, so $4x+2x+x=7x$ and $-3+5+1=3$. The common wrong answer $6x+3$ comes from missing one of the $x$ terms when collecting like terms. Hint: Add first, then simplify.

2. A rectangle has length $3x+2$ cm and width $x+6$ cm. Which equation should you form if the perimeter is 52 cm?

   - $$2(3x+2)+2(x+6)=52$ ✔

   - $$(3x+2)+(x+6)=52$

   - $$2(3x+2)+(x+6)=52$

   - $$(3x+2)+(3x+2)+(x+6)=52$

   _Explanation:_ A rectangle has two lengths and two widths, so the perimeter is $2l+2w$, giving $2(3x+2)+2(x+6)=52$. The common wrong answer B only adds one length and one width, which is only halfway around the rectangle.

3. A right-angled triangle has perpendicular sides $5x$ cm and $6x$ cm and area 240 cm². What is $x$?

   - 4 ✔

   - -4

   - 16

   - 8

   _Explanation:_ Using $\frac{1}{2}\times \text{base}\times \text{height}$ gives $\frac{1}{2}\times 5x \times 6x = 240$, so $15x^2=240$ and $x^2=16$. The common wrong answer -4 forgets that $x$ is a length, so only the positive square root is valid.
