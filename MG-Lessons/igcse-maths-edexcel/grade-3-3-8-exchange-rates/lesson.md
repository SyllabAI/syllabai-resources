## What an exchange rate tells you

![Flow diagram showing exchange rate £1 = €1.18, with pounds to euros labelled multiply by 1.18 and euros to pounds labelled divide by 1.18](../assets/001-f67fc58b-2bb5-43bb-8467-4533856fd184.jpeg)
An exchange rate tells you how much one currency is worth in another currency. If the rate is written as $£1 = €1.18$, each pound can be exchanged for €1.18.

When the rate is written with $£1$ first, go from pounds to the foreign currency by multiplying. To come back to pounds, divide by the same rate, and always check which currency has the 1.

## Worked example: pounds to euros

A student changes £280 into euros at the rate £1 = €1.18. The money starts in pounds, so multiply by 1.18 to get euros.

You can think of 1.18 as euros per pound. Multiplying by the rate changes the amount into euros.

$$$280 \times 1.18 = 330.40$$

So the student receives €330.40. Because the rate is greater than 1, the euro amount should be larger than 280. This quick sense check helps you spot calculator slips.

## Worked example: euros back to pounds

To change euros back into pounds when the rate is written as $£1 = €1.17$, divide by $1.17$. We are finding how many groups of €1.17 fit into the euro amount.

For €95, the calculation is shown below: $$95 \div 1.17 = 81.19658$$ After calculating, you must round the raw result to two decimal places.

So the traveller receives £81.20. Money answers in pounds are usually given to the nearest penny, which means formatting the value to 2 decimal places.

## Read the side with 1

Sometimes the rate is written the other way round, such as $€1 = £0.86$. Then each euro is worth $£0.86$, so euros to pounds means multiply by $0.86$.

To compare prices fairly, first convert them into the same currency. Using the rate $£1 = €1.20$, the abroad price in pounds is:

$$$54 \div 1.20 = 45$$

So the abroad price is $£45$. Now both prices are in pounds, so you can compare them directly. The jacket is cheaper abroad by $£4.99$, and you should never compare $54$ with $49.99$ before converting.

## Better rates and whole notes

To choose the better rate, rewrite both offers in the same form. If City A offers £1 = €1.14 and City B offers €1 = £0.86, then City B gives:

$$$1 \div 0.86 = 1.16279$$

euros for £1, so City B is better. Some questions ask for the greatest number of notes. If £730 changes at £1 = 28000 dong, first work out the total amount of dong.

$$$730 \times 28000 = 20440000$$

So the traveller has 20440000 dong. Now divide by the value of one note to find the quantity.

$$$20440000 \div 200000 = 102.2$$

You must round down, so the greatest number of 200000 dong notes the traveller receives is 102.

## Check yourself

1. The exchange rate is £1 = €1.15. Which calculation changes £60 into euros?

   - $$60 \times 1.15$ ✔

   - $$60 \div 1.15$

   - $$1.15 \div 60$

   - $$60 - 1.15$

   _Explanation:_ Because each £1 becomes €1.15, you multiply the number of pounds by 1.15. The most common wrong answer is B, but dividing would be used to go from euros back to pounds when the rate is written with £1 first.

2. A traveller has €120 and the rate is £1 = €1.20. How many pounds do they receive?

   - £100 ✔

   - £144

   - £121.20

   - £118.80

   _Explanation:_ Since the rate is euros for £1, convert euros back to pounds by dividing: $120 \div 1.20 = 100$. The most common wrong answer is B, which comes from multiplying by 1.20 instead of dividing.

3. A traveller can spend no more than £250. Flights cost 120 US dollars and a hotel costs €165. The exchange rates are £1 = €1.10 and 1 US dollar = €0.80. Can they afford both?

   - Yes, because the total is £237.27 ✔

   - No, because the total is £261.00

   - No, because the total is £259.09

   - Yes, because the total is £287.10

   _Explanation:_ First convert the flights into euros: $120 \times 0.80 = 96$. Then add the hotel to get €261, and divide by 1.10 to change to pounds: $261 \div 1.10 = 237.27$. The budget is £250, so they can afford both. The most common wrong answer is B, which stops at euros and forgets to convert into pounds.
