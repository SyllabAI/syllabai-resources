## What a fraction of an amount means

![Bar model showing 3/4 of 160 as four equal parts of 40 with three shaded](../assets/001-d20d25b8-603e-4c01-84a1-c35e6b91be34.jpeg)

A fraction of an amount means a part of a quantity. In $\frac{3}{5}$ of 140, the amount is 140. The denominator 5 tells you to split it into 5 equal parts, and the numerator 3 tells you to take 3 of those parts.

The word "of" tells you which amount the fraction is applied to. For most questions, divide first, then multiply.

$$$\frac{a}{b} \text{ of amount} = (\text{amount} \div b) \times a$$

This formula means you should find the value of one equal part first. Then, you take as many of those parts as the numerator asks for.

## Unit fractions

A unit fraction has numerator 1, so it means one equal part. The denominator still tells you how many equal parts the whole is split into.

To find a unit fraction of a total, we must calculate the value of one equal part. This is done by dividing the amount by the denominator.

$$$96 \div 8 = 12$$

This shows that $\frac{1}{8}$ of 96 is 12. Since $\frac{1}{8}$ is less than 1, the answer should be smaller than the starting amount. That quick size check helps confirm the result.

## Non-unit fractions

A non-unit fraction has a numerator greater than 1, so you want more than one equal part. The method is still find one part first, then multiply by the numerator.

Start with one sixth.
$$84 \div 6 = 14$$
This tells you each equal part is 14.

Then take five of those parts.
$$14 \times 5 = 70$$
So $\frac{5}{6}$ of 84 is 70.

A common mistake is stopping at 14. That answer is only $\frac{1}{6}$, not $\frac{5}{6}$.

## Working backwards from a fraction

Sometimes the fraction of a number is given, and the whole number is missing. In reverse questions, the known value represents the number of parts shown by the numerator.

If $\frac{3}{4}$ of a number is 27, then 27 is the value of 3 equal parts. Find 1 part first. $$27 \div 3 = 9$$

Now rebuild the whole with 4 parts. $$9 \times 4 = 36$$ So the original number is 36.

The shortcut is divide by the numerator, then multiply by the denominator. This works because you are moving from the known parts back to the whole.

## Choose the correct whole in word problems

In worded problems, ask "a fraction of what?" The fraction may apply to a smaller group, not the total. For example, at a concert there are 850 people, with 320 adults and 410 teenagers.

The rest of the people are children, so we must first find their total count: $$850 - 320 - 410 = 120$$ This means there are 120 children in the group.

If $\frac{3}{5}$ of the children are boys, use 120 as the whole for the fraction. First find one fifth and then multiply by three: $$120 \div 5 = 24$$ $$24 \times 3 = 72$$

This calculation gives 72 boys. The girls are the children who are left, so we calculate $120 - 72 = 48$, giving a final result of 48 girls.

## Fractions, percentages, discounts, and difference

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. For example, 20% can be written as a simple fraction. $$20\% = \frac{1}{5}$$ So 20% of 75 is the same as one fifth of 75.

Find the percentage amount first. $$75 \div 5 = 15$$ So 20% of 75 is 15.

A discount is the part taken off the original price. If a jacket costs £48 and is reduced by $\frac{1}{4}$, first find the discount. $$48 \div 4 = 12$$ The sale price is £36 because $48 - 12 = 36$.

Difference means how far apart two values are. If $\frac{4}{5}$ of 30 is found by $30 \div 5 = 6$ and $6 \times 4 = 24$, then the difference between 24 and 15 is 9 because $24 - 15 = 9$.

## Check yourself

1. In the calculation "find $\frac{3}{5}$ of 140", what does the denominator 5 tell you to do first?

   - Split 140 into 5 equal parts ✔

   - Multiply 140 by 5

   - Take 3 equal parts straight away

   - Add 3 and 5

   _Explanation:_ The denominator tells you how many equal parts the whole is divided into, so you start by doing $140 \div 5$. Option C is the most common wrong answer because the numerator tells you how many parts to take only after you have found one equal part.

2. What is $\frac{7}{8}$ of 56?

   - 7

   - 49 ✔

   - 64

   - 48

   _Explanation:_ First find one eighth by doing $56 \div 8 = 7$, then multiply by 7 to get 49. Option A is the most common wrong answer because it gives only $\frac{1}{8}$ of 56, not $\frac{7}{8}$.

3. There are 120 books on a shelf. $\frac{1}{3}$ are fiction. Of the remaining books, $\frac{1}{4}$ are science. How many books are science?

   - 20 ✔

   - 30

   - 40

   - 80

   _Explanation:_ First find fiction: $\frac{1}{3}$ of 120 is 40, so 80 books remain. Then science is $\frac{1}{4}$ of 80, which is 20. Option B is the most common wrong answer because it takes $\frac{1}{4}$ of the total 120 instead of the remaining 80.
