## Percent means per hundred

![A 10 by 10 hundred-square grid with 25 squares shaded, labelled 25% and 100% whole, plus a quarter bar model below](../assets/001-70fd8177-1c0b-48cc-947b-bb91d4e8b38e.jpeg)

A percentage is a number of parts out of 100. The symbol `%` means "per hundred", so $25\% = \frac{25}{100} = 0.25$.

Useful benchmark percentages make calculations quicker. For example, $50\%$ is half, $25\%$ is a quarter, $10\%$ means divide by 10, and $1\%$ means divide by 100.

In percentage questions, "of" means multiply. So $20\%$ of 60 can be written as $0.20 \times 60$.

## Two ways to find a percentage

For any percentage, a safe method is to find $1\%$ first by dividing by 100, then scale up. A quicker method is to turn the percentage into a decimal multiplier and multiply once.

Example: find $36\%$ of 2400 using the $1\%$ method.
$$2400 \div 100 = 24 \qquad 24 \times 36 = 864$$
So $36\%$ of 2400 is 864.

The multiplier method gives the same answer because $36\% = 0.36$.
$$0.36 \times 2400 = 864$$

## Percentages over 100% and comparisons

Percentages can be bigger than $100\%$ because the amount can be more than one whole. For example, $200\%$ is double and $250\%$ is $2.5$ times the original amount.

To find $250\%$ of 140, use the multiplier $2.5$.
$$2.5 \times 140 = 350$$
So $250\%$ of 140 is 350.

When comparing two percentage amounts, calculate both actual values before deciding. For example,
$$0.30 \times 110 = 33 \qquad 0.32 \times 96 = 30.72$$
so $30\%$ of 110 is greater even though $32\%$ is the bigger percentage.

## Finding the whole from a part

![A percentage bar model split into 25% children and 75% adults labelled 45, with the full 100% total unknown](../assets/002-2eebd515-c437-4904-ba20-4de6986985a3.jpeg)

The whole amount is always $100\%$, so first work out which percentage the known group represents. If $25\%$ are children, then adults must be the remaining $75\%$.

Example: 45 adults is $75\%$ of the club. To calculate the total, divide by 3 to find $25\%$, then multiply by 4 to reach $100\%$:

$$$45 \div 3 = 15 \qquad 15 \times 4 = 60$$

So the total number of people is 60. A quicker check is to divide the amount by its decimal percentage equivalent: $45 \div 0.75 = 60$.

## Percentage increase and decrease

To calculate a percentage change, we use a multiplier. For an increase by $p\%$, multiply by $1 + \frac{p}{100}$. For a decrease by $p\%$, multiply by $1 - \frac{p}{100}$.

For example, a £48 jacket reduced by $20\%$ uses the multiplier $0.8$. 

$$48 \times 0.8 = 38.4$$

This calculation shows that the sale price is £38.40.

Likewise, a salary of £32,000 increased by $4\%$ uses the multiplier $1.04$. 

$$32000 \times 1.04 = 33280$$

So the new salary is £33,280.

## Multi-step percentage problems

Many percentage problems have an extra step after finding the percentage. In deposit questions, find the deposit, subtract it from the total cost, and then deal with the remaining balance. In shop-offer questions, compare the actual total cost for the exact number of items needed.

Example: a tablet costs £600 with a $15\%$ deposit and £30 monthly payments. $$0.15 \times 600 = 90 \qquad 600 - 90 = 510 \qquad 510 \div 30 = 17$$ So 17 payments are needed.

For simple interest, the interest each year is based on the original amount, not the new total. If £600 is invested at $1.5\%$ for 4 years, $$0.015 \times 600 = 9 \qquad 9 \times 4 = 36$$ so the total interest is £36.

## Check yourself

1. Which statement is correct?

   - $$25\% = 0.25$ ✔

   - $$25\% = 2.5$

   - $$25\% = \frac{25}{10}$

   - $$25\% = \frac{1}{25}$

   _Explanation:_ $$25\%$ means $25 \div 100 = 0.25$, so A is correct. A common wrong answer is B because it divides by 10 instead of 100.

2. Which is greater: $30\%$ of 110 or $32\%$ of 96?

   - $$30\%$ of 110 ✔

   - $$32\%$ of 96

   - They are equal

   - You cannot tell without more information

   _Explanation:_ $$30\%$ of 110 is 33, while $32\%$ of 96 is 30.72, so A is correct. A common wrong answer is B because 32% is the bigger percentage, but it is being taken from a smaller starting number.

3. Stretch: You need 6 tins of soup. Shop A sells tins for 50p each with $10\%$ off the total, and Shop B sells tins for 70p each with "buy 2 get 1 free". Which shop is cheaper?

   - Shop A ✔

   - Shop B

   - They cost the same

   - You cannot tell

   _Explanation:_ Shop A costs $6 \times 50 = 300$ pence before the discount, and $10\%$ of 300 is 30, so the total is 270 pence. Shop B gives 1 free tin in each group of 3, so for 6 tins you pay for 4 tins: $4 \times 70 = 280$ pence. A common wrong answer is B because "buy 2 get 1 free" sounds better, but the actual total is still higher.
