## What a ratio means

![Ratio bar model with one bar split into 5 equal boxes, 3 boxes for one share and 2 boxes for the other, labelled total 5 equal parts](../assets/001-4567ac80-e62f-45a6-9ccd-a84a3c479793.jpeg)
A ratio compares quantities using equal parts. The ratio $3:2$ is read as "3 to 2" and means one share has 3 equal parts while the other has 2.

There are $3+2=5$ parts altogether, so you must think in parts before you think in actual amounts. Ratios can be scaled, so $3:2$, $6:4$, and $12:8$ are equivalent, but $2:3$ is different.

## Sharing a total in a ratio

To share a total in a ratio, add the parts first. Then divide the total by that number to find the value of 1 part.

Amira and Ben share £90 in the ratio $3:2$. The total number of parts is $3+2=5$, so 1 part is worth $90 \div 5 = 18$.

Amira gets $3 \times 18 = 54$ and Ben gets $2 \times 18 = 36$. A quick check is $54+36=90$, which matches the original total.

## Ratios with three parts

Ratios can have three or more parts, and the method stays exactly the same. Add all the parts before you divide.

Noah, Isla, and Maya share 72 sweets in the ratio $4:3:2$. The total number of parts is $4+3+2=9$, so 1 part is worth $72 \div 9 = 8$.

Their shares are $4 \times 8 = 32$, $3 \times 8 = 24$, and $2 \times 8 = 16$. These values add back to 72, which confirms the sharing is correct.

## When one share is known

Sometimes you are told one person's share instead of the whole amount. In that case, divide by that person's ratio number to find 1 part.

Liam and Eva share money in the ratio $2:5$. If Eva gets £75, then 5 parts are worth 75, so 1 part is worth $75 \div 5 = 15$.

Liam has 2 parts, so he gets $2 \times 15 = 30$. Do not divide by $2+5$ here, because £75 is not the total.

## Using the difference between shares

If one share is more than another, the extra amount matches the difference in the ratio parts. So compare the ratio numbers before doing any division.

Sofia and Jack share money in the ratio $6:4$. Sofia gets £50 more, and the difference is $6-4=2$ parts, so 1 part is worth $50 \div 2 = 25$.

Jack gets $4 \times 25 = 100$ and Sofia gets $6 \times 25 = 150$. Their difference is £50, so the answer is consistent.

## Ratios on a straight line

![Straight line with points A, B, C where AB is 1 equal part and BC is 4 equal parts, with labels AB:BC = 1:4 and AC = 75 m](../assets/002-79ce88b2-345f-4ec1-ab13-7572e716ae1b.jpeg)

Ratio also appears on straight lines. If one section is four times another, we can write the shorter to longer ratio as $1:4$.

In the diagram, $AB:BC = 1:4$ and the whole length $AC$ is 75 m. There are $1 + 4 = 5$ parts altogether, so 1 part is calculated as $75 \div 5 = 15$.

Because $BC$ has 4 parts, $BC = 4 \times 15 = 60$. The same 'add, divide, multiply' method used for sharing amounts still works for geometric segments.

## Check yourself

1. A bag has red and blue counters in the ratio $3:2$. Which statement is correct?

   - For every 5 counters, 3 are red and 2 are blue ✔

   - There is always exactly 1 more red counter than blue counter

   - There are exactly 3 red counters and 2 blue counters

   - Blue counters are more common than red counters

   _Explanation:_ $$3:2$ means 3 equal parts to 2 equal parts, so any matching group can be split 3 red and 2 blue. The common wrong answer is C, but a ratio compares proportions, not exact totals.

2. 60 stickers are shared in the ratio $2:3:5$. How many stickers does the 5-part share get?

   - 30 ✔

   - 20

   - 12

   - 25

   _Explanation:_ The ratio has $2+3+5=10$ parts, so 1 part is $60 \div 10 = 6$. The 5-part share is $5 \times 6 = 30$; the common wrong answer 20 comes from using the 3-part share instead of the 5-part share.

3. Two amounts are in the ratio $7:4$. The larger amount is 36 more than the smaller amount. What is the smaller amount?

   - 48 ✔

   - 84

   - 36

   - 12

   _Explanation:_ The difference between 7 parts and 4 parts is 3 parts, so 3 parts are worth 36 and 1 part is worth 12. The smaller amount is $4 \times 12 = 48$; the common wrong answer 84 is the larger amount, not the smaller one.

4. A line is split so $AB:BC = 2:3$ and the whole length $AC$ is 40 cm. What is $BC$?

   - 16 cm

   - 24 cm ✔

   - 20 cm

   - 15 cm

   _Explanation:_ There are 5 parts altogether, so 1 part is $40 \div 5 = 8$ cm and $BC = 3 \times 8 = 24$ cm. The common wrong answer 16 cm is actually the length of $AB$, not $BC$.
