## What an equation means

![Balance scale with a box labelled $x$ and four unit blocks on the left, eleven unit blocks on the right, and arrows showing subtract 4 from both sides to get $x = 7$](../assets/001-a7fe46a4-9a1d-45ce-9eb0-99edee18f459.jpeg)

An equation says two expressions have the same value, so it helps to picture it as balanced scales. The letter is the unknown, and solving the equation means finding the value that makes the statement true.

To keep the balance, do the same operation to both sides. These matching actions are usually inverse operations, such as subtracting to undo adding or dividing to undo multiplying.

A value is a solution only if it works when you substitute it back into the original equation. Checking at the end helps you catch sign mistakes and arithmetic slips.

## One-step equations with add and subtract

When the unknown has had a number added or subtracted, undo that in one step. Work backwards from the letter and use the inverse operation on both sides. For instance, solve $x+6=18$ by subtracting 6 from both sides.

$$$x+6=18$$ $$x=18-6=12$$

Check by substitution: $12+6=18$, so $x=12$ is correct. For $p-7=11$, add 7 to both sides because subtraction is undone by addition. That gives $p=18$, so the key question is always "what has happened to the letter?"

## Multiply, divide, and negative answers

A number written next to a letter means multiply, so $8a$ means 8 multiplied by $a$. To solve these equations, undo multiplying by dividing and undo dividing by multiplying.

Solve $\frac{d}{2}=8.5$ by multiplying both sides by 2:
$$\frac{d}{2}=8.5$$
$$d=17$$
A quick check is $17 \div 2=8.5$, so the solution works.

Negative answers are normal too. In the equation $6-m=14$, subtract 6 from both sides to get $-m=8$, which means the solution is $m=-8$, not $m=8$.

## Two-step equations

In a two-step equation, the variable has been changed twice. To solve it, undo the operations in reverse order, usually by adding or subtracting first and then multiplying or dividing.

Solve $3h-5=16$ by working backwards from the letter:
$$3h-5=16$$
$$3h=21$$
$$h=7$$
You add 5 to both sides first, then divide the result by 3.

If the whole numerator of a fraction is being divided, multiply both sides first. For $\frac{d+5}{4}=6$, multiply by 4 to get $d+5=24$, then subtract 5 to reach the solution $d=19$.

## Brackets first, then inside

In $5(x-4)$, the whole bracket is one object being multiplied by 5. It is often quickest to undo the outside operation first, before touching what is inside the bracket.

Solve $5(x-4)=35$ by dividing both sides by 5 first.
$$5(x-4)=35$$
$$x-4=7$$
$$x=11$$

Now the bracket has disappeared and the equation is simpler. The same idea works for $20=4(2x-1)$. Divide by 4 first to get $5=2x-1$, then solve the smaller two-step equation.

## Unknowns on both sides

When the letter appears on both sides, collect the letter terms on one side and the number terms on the other. Moving the smaller letter term can help keep the coefficient positive.

Solve $6w+9=3w+24$ by subtracting $3w$ first. $$6w+9=3w+24$$ $$3w+9=24$$ $$3w=15$$ $$w=5$$ After the letters are together, it becomes an ordinary two-step equation.

Check by substitution to be sure. With $w=5$, the left side is $6(5)+9=39$ and the right side is $3(5)+24=39$, so the equation is true.

## Check yourself

1. To solve $x+6=18$, what should you do first?

   - Subtract 6 from both sides ✔

   - Subtract 6 from the left side only

   - Add 6 to both sides

   - Divide both sides by 6

   _Explanation:_ Subtracting 6 from both sides undoes the $+6$ without breaking the balance, so it isolates $x$. The common wrong choice is subtracting 6 from the left side only, because that changes one side but not the other and the equation stops being equal. Hint: Think about what would happen on a set of scales.

2. Solve $6-m=14$.

   - $$m=8$

   - $$m=-8$ ✔

   - $$m=20$

   - $$m=-20$

   _Explanation:_ Subtracting 6 from both sides gives $-m=8$, so $m=-8$. The common wrong choice is $m=8$, but that forgets the minus sign attached to the letter.

3. Solve $4(x-2)=28$.

   - $$x=5$

   - $$x=7$

   - $$x=9$ ✔

   - $$x=26$

   _Explanation:_ Divide by 4 first to get $x-2=7$, then add 2 to get $x=9$. The common wrong choice is $x=7$, which happens if you stop after removing the bracket and forget to solve the rest.

4. Stretch: Solve $7x-4=3x+12$.

   - $$x=2$

   - $$x=4$ ✔

   - $$x=8$

   - $$x=-4$

   _Explanation:_ Subtract $3x$ from both sides to get $4x-4=12$, then add 4 so $4x=16$ and $x=4$. The common wrong choice is $x=2$, which comes from moving the $-4$ the wrong way instead of adding 4 to both sides.
