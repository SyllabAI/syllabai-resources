## What are averages?

![Median diagram showing an unsorted list becoming an ordered list with 6 highlighted as the middle value](../assets/001-2bbb6585-38b0-470b-9efb-c9911ab7f3a6.jpeg)

A set of numbers is called data. Averages describe what is typical in the data, while the range tells you how spread out the data is.

At GCSE, the main averages are mode, median and mean. You are often asked for the range as well, even though it measures spread rather than a typical value. If the data has units such as cm, g or km, include those units in your final answer.

Putting numbers in order is essential before finding the median. In the list $9, 3, 6, 4, 8$, the ordered data is $3, 4, 6, 8, 9$, so the median is $6$.

## Range and mode

The range measures spread, not typical size. Find it by subtracting the smallest value from the largest value.

For heights 152 cm, 168 cm, 160 cm, 174 cm, 152 cm and 181 cm, the smallest is 152 and the largest is 181. The calculation is $181 - 152 = 29$, so the range is 29 cm.

The mode is the value that appears most often. In 5, 8, 8, 9, 10, 8, 11, the mode is 8 because it appears three times. A data set can have more than one mode, or no mode if nothing repeats.

## Finding the median

To find the median, write the data in order first. If there are 5 values, the median is the 3rd value because it is in the middle. Do not just pick the middle of the list as written.

For 9, 3, 6, 4, 8, the ordered list is 3, 4, 6, 8, 9. The median is 6.

If there is an even number of values, there are two middle numbers, so find their mean. For 118 g, 124 g, 126 g, 132 g, 140 g and 150 g, the median is $\frac{126+132}{2}=129$, so the median is 129 g.

## Mean = total divided by number of values

The mean uses every value in the data set. To find it, you first calculate the sum of all values and then divide that total by how many values are present.

The formula for the mean is: $$\text{mean}=\frac{\text{total of values}}{\text{number of values}}$$ This relationship shows that the mean depends on both the total sum and the number of values in the set.

For the numbers 14, 6, 10, 6, 12 and 18, the total sum is $14+6+10+6+12+18=66$. Since there are 6 numbers in the list, the mean is calculated as $66 \div 6 = 11$.

## Hidden numbers from the mean

When a mean is given, start by turning it back into a total. If 6 cards have mean 5, their total must be $5 \times 6 = 30$.

Suppose the cards are 2, 4, a hidden number, 7, 5 and another hidden number. The known numbers add to $2+4+7+5=18$, so the two hidden numbers must add up to $30-18=12$.

If the mode is 4, one hidden number must be 4 so that 4 appears most often. The other hidden number is $12-4=8$, so the hidden numbers are 4 and 8.

## Updating the mean when new data is added

Do not just average the old mean and the new value. First turn the old mean back into a total sum.

A runner has a mean distance of 12 km for 4 days, so the total for those 4 days is $12 \times 4 = 48$ km. If she then runs 17 km, the new total is $48 + 17 = 65$ km.

There are now 5 days altogether. The new mean is calculated by dividing the new total by the new count: $65 \div 5 = 13$ km.

## Check yourself

1. Which statement about the data 12, 15, 9, 20, 12 is correct?

   - The range is 11 and the mode is 12 ✔

   - The range is 20 and the mode is 12

   - The range is 11 and the mode is 20

   - The range is 8 and the mode is 9

   _Explanation:_ The largest value is 20 and the smallest is 9, so the range is $20 - 9 = 11$. The common wrong answer B uses the biggest number as the range, but the range is a difference, and 12 is the mode because it appears most often.

2. What is the mean of 6, 8, 8, 12?

   - 8.5 ✔

   - 8

   - 34

   - 12

   _Explanation:_ Add the numbers to get $34$, then divide by 4, so the mean is $8.5$. The common wrong answer 8 is the mode, not the mean, because 8 appears most often.

3. The mean of 6 numbers is 20. The mean of 2 of the numbers is 14. What is the mean of the other 4 numbers?

   - 23 ✔

   - 17

   - 92

   - 6

   _Explanation:_ To find the mean of the remaining numbers, you must work with totals. All 6 numbers total $20 \times 6 = 120$, and the 2 known numbers total $14 \times 2 = 28$. So the other 4 numbers total $120 - 28 = 92$. Their mean is $92 \div 4 = 23$. Note: 92 is the sum of the remaining numbers, while 17 results from the mistake of simply averaging the two means $(20 + 14) \div 2$.
