## Conversion factors

A unit tells you what a measurement is counted in, such as mm, cm, m, km, litres or seconds. A conversion rewrites the same amount in a different unit, so $1 \, \text{m}$ and $100 \, \text{cm}$ describe the same length.

A conversion factor is the number you multiply or divide by to change units. Useful facts are $1 \, \text{cm} = 10 \, \text{mm}$, $1 \, \text{m} = 100 \, \text{cm}$, $1 \, \text{km} = 1000 \, \text{m}$, and $1 \, \text{hour} = 3600 \, \text{s}$.

If you change to a smaller unit, the number usually gets bigger, so you multiply. If you change to a larger unit, the number usually gets smaller, so you divide.

## Reading conversion graphs

![Straight conversion graph with litres on the horizontal axis, pints on the vertical axis, a line through the origin, and guide lines showing 12 litres maps to about 21 pints](../assets/001-ccbd891c-eece-4f7b-bfc5-3e3519d88814.jpeg)

A conversion graph is usually a straight line through the origin because the two units are in direct proportion. On this graph, $20$ litres matches about $35$ pints, so doubling litres doubles pints.

If the question gives the x-axis value, start there, go vertically to the line, then horizontally to the other axis. For $12$ litres, this reads about $21$ pints.

If the question gives the y-axis value, work backwards by going horizontally to the line and then vertically down. Always check which unit is on each axis before you move.

## Why area uses bigger scale factors

![Square labelled 1 cm by 1 cm divided into a 10 by 10 grid of millimetre squares to show that 1 cm² equals 100 mm²](../assets/002-d019fe96-9c29-4108-8036-56f16309fa2f.jpeg)
Area uses squared units, so the scale factor must be squared as well. If $1 \, \text{cm} = 10 \, \text{mm}$, then we must square the conversion factor.

$$$1 \, \text{cm}^2 = 10 \times 10 = 100 \, \text{mm}^2$$
This is why you do not multiply by just $10$ for area conversions. You must use the squared relationship between the linear units.

Worked example: convert $2.8 \, \text{m}^2$ to $\text{cm}^2$. Since $1 \, \text{m} = 100 \, \text{cm}$, the area factor is $100^2 = 10000$. Now, we multiply the given area by this new factor.

$$$2.8 \times 10000 = 28000$$
So $2.8 \, \text{m}^2 = 28000 \, \text{cm}^2$. When moving from a smaller area unit to a larger one, you should divide by the squared factor instead.

## Volume uses cubed factors

Volume uses cubed units, so you cube the length conversion factor. If $1 \, \text{cm} = 10 \, \text{mm}$, then:

$$$1 \, \text{cm}^3 = 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 1000 \, \text{mm}^3$$

This is why volume conversions grow even faster than area conversions. For a worked example, to convert $24 \, \text{cm}^3$ to $\text{mm}^3$, multiply by $1000$ because the unit is getting smaller.

$$$24 \times 1000 = 24000$$ So $24 \, \text{cm}^3 = 24000 \, \text{mm}^3$. A quick check is that the number should increase when you move to the smaller unit.

## Converting km/h to m/s

Speed is a compound unit because it combines distance and time. To change from km/h to m/s, convert kilometres to metres and hours to seconds.

Here is the full conversion for $72 \, \text{km/h}$.

$$$72 \, \text{km/h} = 72 \times \frac{1000 \, \text{m}}{1 \, \text{km}} \times \frac{1 \, \text{h}}{3600 \, \text{s}} = \frac{72000}{3600} \, \text{m/s} = 20 \, \text{m/s}$$

The $\text{km}$ and $\text{h}$ units cancel, leaving $\text{m/s}$. Because $\frac{1000}{3600} = \frac{1}{3.6}$, there is a shortcut: divide by $3.6$. For example, $54 \div 3.6 = 15$, so $54 \, \text{km/h} = 15 \, \text{m/s}$.

## Check yourself

1. Write $3.7 \, \text{m}$ in centimetres.

   - 37 cm

   - 370 cm ✔

   - 3700 cm

   - 0.37 cm

   _Explanation:_ Since $1 \, \text{m} = 100 \, \text{cm}$, multiply $3.7$ by $100$ to get $370 \, \text{cm}$. The most common wrong answer is 37 cm, which comes from multiplying by 10 instead of 100.

2. A straight conversion graph shows that $20$ litres is about $35$ pints. About how many pints is $12$ litres?

   - 21 pints ✔

   - 17.5 pints

   - 24 pints

   - 35 pints

   _Explanation:_ $$12$ litres is $\frac{12}{20} = 0.6$ of $20$ litres, so the pints value is $0.6 \times 35 = 21$. The most common wrong answer is 17.5 pints, which comes from halving 35 as if the question were 10 litres, not 12 litres.

3. Which is equal to $3 \, \text{cm}^3$?

   - $$30 \, \text{mm}^3$

   - $$300 \, \text{mm}^3$

   - $$3000 \, \text{mm}^3$ ✔

   - $$0.003 \, \text{mm}^3$

   _Explanation:_ Since $1 \, \text{cm}^3 = 1000 \, \text{mm}^3$, multiply $3$ by $1000$ to get $3000 \, \text{mm}^3$. The most common wrong answer is $300 \, \text{mm}^3$, which comes from using the area factor $100$ instead of the volume factor $1000$. Hint: For volume, cube the length factor.

4. A scooter travels at 72 km/h for 15 seconds. How far does it travel?

   - 300 m ✔

   - 20 m

   - 180 m

   - 1080 m

   _Explanation:_ First convert the speed: 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Then use $d = vt$. Here, $v = 20$ m/s and $t = 15$ s, so 20 × 15 = 300 and the scooter travels 300 m. The most common wrong answer is 20 m, which is just the converted speed and ignores the 15 seconds completely.
