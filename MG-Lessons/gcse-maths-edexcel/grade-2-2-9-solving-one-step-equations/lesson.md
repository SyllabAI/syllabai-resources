## Equations are balances

![Balance scale showing $x+7$ on one side and $19$ on the other, then subtracting $7$ from both sides to leave $x=12$](../assets/001-ca398a43-9896-4caf-9244-48c73915994d.jpeg)

The diagram shows an equation as a balance: $x+7$ on one side and $19$ on the other. If you subtract $7$ from both sides, the balance stays equal and $x$ is left on its own.

This is why the equals sign means both sides have the same value. A one-step equation can be solved using just one inverse operation.

A missing-number question works the same way. In $6+\square=17$, the blank must be $11$, and a letter such as $x$ can stand for that missing number.

## Undo the operation next to the variable

Addition and subtraction are inverse operations, and multiplication and division are inverse operations. To solve a one-step equation, undo the operation next to the variable.

Solve $x+7=19$. Subtract $7$ from both sides so the equation stays balanced.

$$$x+7=19$$
$$x+7-7=19-7$$
$$x=12$$

Check by substituting the answer back in. Since $12+7=19$, the solution is correct.

## Be careful with subtraction

If the variable has a number subtracted from it, undo the operation with addition. For $m-6=10$, add $6$ to both sides of the equation to get $m=16$.

If the variable is the amount being taken away, read the equation differently. In $23-n=14$, find what was subtracted from the starting value, so $n=23-14=9$.

Do not swap the subtraction order when solving. Calculating $14-23$ gives the common wrong answer $-9$, but $23-(-9)=32$, not $14$.

## Multiplication, division and repeated letters

A number written next to a letter means multiply. For example, $7a$ means $7 \times a$, and the equation $7a=42$ is solved by dividing by $7$ to get $a=6$.

The fraction bar represents division. In the equation $\frac{d}{2}=7.5$, you must multiply by $2$ to undo the division, resulting in $d=15$.

Repeated letters can be collected first using simplification. Since $r+r+r=3r$, the equation $r+r+r=27$ simplifies to $3r=27$, which gives the solution $r=9$.

## When the letter is on the bottom

The bottom part of a fraction is called the denominator. In $\frac{30}{y}=6$, the letter $y$ is the number you are dividing by.

Read the equation as "$30$ divided by $y$ equals $6$". Since $30 \div 5=6$, the solution is $y=5$.

Do not do $30 \times 6$. That common mistake gives $180$, which does not make the original division true.

## Check every solution

After solving, substitute your answer back into the original equation. If both sides are equal, your answer works.

For $5+p=13$, solve first to get $p=8$. Then check with $5+8=13$, so the equation is true.

If the check fails, reread the operation beside the variable. Most errors come from using the wrong inverse operation or subtracting in the wrong order.

## Check yourself

1. What does the equals sign mean in an equation?

   - Both sides have the same value ✔

   - The left side is bigger than the right side

   - It is time to do the calculation from left to right

   - The answer must go on the right

   _Explanation:_ The equals sign means the two sides balance and represent the same value. Think about a balance scale. Option D is the common wrong idea, but an equation can have expressions on both sides, so $=$ does not just mean "write the answer".

2. Solve $23-n=14$.

   - $$9$ ✔

   - $$-9$

   - $$37$

   - $$14$

   _Explanation:_ $$n$ is the amount taken away from $23$, so find $23-14=9$. Option B comes from the common mistake of doing $14-23$, which reverses the subtraction and does not fit the original equation.

3. Solve $\frac{30}{y}=6$.

   - $$5$ ✔

   - $$180$

   - $$24$

   - $$36$

   _Explanation:_ The equation means "$30$ divided by $y$ equals $6$", so the missing divisor is $5$ because $30 \div 5=6$. Option B is the common wrong answer from multiplying $30 \times 6$, but that does not make the original division true.
