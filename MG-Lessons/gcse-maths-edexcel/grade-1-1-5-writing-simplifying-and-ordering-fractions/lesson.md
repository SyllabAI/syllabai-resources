## Part over total

A fraction shows part of a whole or part of a group. In $\frac{3}{8}$, the numerator is the top number and the denominator is the bottom number.

When you write a fraction from a word problem, use part over total. If 6 out of 20 counters are yellow, start with $\frac{6}{20}$, not $\frac{6}{14}$ or $\frac{20}{6}$.

## When the question says "the rest"

Sometimes a question gives one part and says "the rest". First subtract from the total to find the missing part, then write that part over the total.

Example: there are 15 pens and 6 are red, so the number of green pens is $15-6=9$. The fraction that are green is therefore $\frac{9}{15}$.

Now simplify by dividing top and bottom by 3: $$\frac{9}{15}=\frac{3}{5}$$ The denominator in the first fraction is still 15 because 15 is the total number of pens.

## Simplifying and spotting equivalence

Equivalent fractions have the same value, even if they look different. To simplify a fraction, divide the numerator and denominator by the same common factor.

For $\frac{24}{32}$, both numbers divide by 8. We use this common factor to divide both the numerator and the denominator.

$$$\frac{24}{32}=\frac{24\div 8}{32\div 8}=\frac{3}{4}$$

A fraction is in simplest form when the top and bottom have no common factor except 1. If you change only one number, the value changes and the fractions are no longer equivalent.

## Increase as a fraction of the original

An increase represents how much a price has gone up. If a question asks for this as a fraction of last year's cost, you must put the increase amount over the old price.

For example, if a bus pass rises from £30 to £42, first find the increase: $42 - 30 = 12$. The fraction of the increase relative to the original price is:
$$\frac{12}{30}$$

Now simplify the fraction by dividing both the top and bottom by 6:
$$\frac{12}{30}=\frac{2}{5}$$

Do not use 42 as the denominator in this calculation. This is because the question specifically compares the increase with last year's cost, not the new price.

## Ordering fractions with a common denominator

![Number line from 0 to 1 with 1 over 4, 3 over 10, 2 over 5 and 1 over 2 marked, plus each rewritten in twentieths.](../assets/001-3a8b8e6d-819a-4d09-bc3f-1cacb2c96a72.jpeg)

Ordering fractions is easier when they all have the same denominator. Then the numerators tell you which is smaller or larger.

A good common denominator for $\frac{1}{4}$, $\frac{3}{10}$, $\frac{2}{5}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$ is 20. Rewrite them as:

$$\frac{1}{4}=\frac{5}{20},\quad \frac{3}{10}=\frac{6}{20},\quad \frac{2}{5}=\frac{8}{20},\quad \frac{1}{2}=\frac{10}{20}$$

Now compare the numerators 5, 6, 8 and 10. From smallest to largest, the original fractions are ordered as follows:

$$\frac{1}{4},\quad \frac{3}{10},\quad \frac{2}{5},\quad \frac{1}{2}$$

Always write the original fractions in your final answer.

## Which fraction is closer?

To see which fraction is closer to a target such as 1 or $\frac{1}{2}$, find the distance from each fraction to the target. The smaller distance means the fraction is closer.

Compare $\frac{5}{4}$ and $\frac{4}{5}$ to 1. $$\frac{5}{4}-1=\frac{1}{4},\qquad 1-\frac{4}{5}=\frac{1}{5}$$ So $\frac{4}{5}$ is closer because $\frac{1}{5}<\frac{1}{4}$.

Compare $\frac{2}{5}$ and $\frac{5}{8}$ to $\frac{1}{2}$. $$\frac{1}{2}-\frac{2}{5}=\frac{1}{10},\qquad \frac{5}{8}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{8}$$ So $\frac{2}{5}$ is closer because $\frac{1}{10}<\frac{1}{8}$.

## Check yourself

1. There are 18 sweets in a jar and 7 are strawberry. Which fraction are strawberry before simplifying?

   - $$\frac{7}{18}$ ✔

   - $$\frac{11}{18}$

   - $$\frac{18}{7}$

   - $$\frac{7}{11}$

   _Explanation:_ $$\frac{7}{18}$ is correct because the part you want is 7 strawberry sweets and the total is 18 sweets. A common wrong answer is $\frac{11}{18}$, which uses the number that are not strawberry instead of the part named in the question. Think 'part over total'.

2. Which fraction is not equivalent to $\frac{2}{3}$?

   - $$\frac{8}{12}$

   - $$\frac{14}{21}$

   - $$\frac{15}{24}$ ✔

   - $$\frac{18}{27}$

   _Explanation:_ $$\frac{15}{24}$ is not equivalent because it simplifies to $\frac{5}{8}$, not $\frac{2}{3}$. A common wrong choice is $\frac{8}{12}$, but dividing top and bottom by 4 gives $\frac{2}{3}$, so it is equivalent.

3. Which list is in order from smallest to largest?

   - $$\frac{1}{3},\ \frac{2}{5},\ \frac{1}{2}$ ✔

   - $$\frac{2}{5},\ \frac{1}{3},\ \frac{1}{2}$

   - $$\frac{1}{2},\ \frac{2}{5},\ \frac{1}{3}$

   - $$\frac{1}{3},\ \frac{1}{2},\ \frac{2}{5}$

   _Explanation:_ $$\frac{1}{3}=\frac{10}{30}$, $\frac{2}{5}=\frac{12}{30}$ and $\frac{1}{2}=\frac{15}{30}$, so option A is correct. A common wrong answer is D, but $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{15}{30}$ and $\frac{2}{5}$ is $\frac{12}{30}$, so $\frac{1}{2}$ is actually larger. Hint: Use a common denominator such as 30.

4. Which fraction is closer to $\frac{1}{2}$?

   - $$\frac{7}{15}$ ✔

   - $$\frac{5}{9}$

   - They are equally close

   - It cannot be worked out without decimals

   _Explanation:_ $$\frac{1}{2} - \frac{7}{15} = \frac{1}{30}$ and $\frac{5}{9} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{18}$, so $\frac{7}{15}$ is closer because $\frac{1}{30}$ is the smaller gap. A common wrong answer is $\frac{5}{9}$, but although it looks near $\frac{1}{2}$, its distance is actually larger.
