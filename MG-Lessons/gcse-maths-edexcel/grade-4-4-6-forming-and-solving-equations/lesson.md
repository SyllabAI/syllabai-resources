## From words to algebra

A variable is a letter such as $x$ that stands for an unknown number. An expression is something like $3x+5$, while an equation says two expressions are equal, like $3x+5=20$.

When a question gives a shape, a total, or an angle fact, first write an expression from the information. Then form an equation by setting that expression equal to the known value.

To solve the equation, use inverse operations and do the same thing to both sides. Finally, check that your answer fits the original situation.

## Form an expression first

Perimeter means the total distance around the outside, so add all the side lengths. For sides $4x-2$, $x+6$, and $2x+1$, start with

$$$P=(4x-2)+(x+6)+(2x+1)$$

Collect the $x$ terms and the constants separately. Here $4x+x+2x=7x$ and $-2+6+1=5$, so the perimeter simplifies to $P=7x+5$.

Be careful with negative numbers. In $4x-2$, the $-2$ stays negative when you collect constants.

## Worked example: solving from a perimeter

![Rectangle with top and bottom labeled 3x + 2 cm, sides labeled x + 4 cm, and perimeter 44 cm](../assets/001-ef4055a9-c707-4ad2-8262-8acdb959dca0.jpeg)

A rectangle has two lengths and two widths, so its perimeter is twice the length plus twice the width. For this rectangle, write:

$$$P=2(3x+2)+2(x+4)$$

Expand and collect like terms to get $P=6x+4+2x+8=8x+12$. Because the perimeter is 44 cm, form the equation $8x+12=44$.

Subtract 12 from both sides and then divide by 8, so $8x=32$ and $x=4$. Check by substituting: the sides are 14 cm and 8 cm, and $2(14)+2(8)=44$.

## Equations from angle facts

Angle questions use the same pattern: write an expression for the total, then match it to the correct angle fact. Useful facts include angles on a straight line ($180^{\circ}$), around a point ($360^{\circ}$), a right angle ($90^{\circ}$), and internal angles of a triangle ($180^{\circ}$).

If a right angle is split into $4x$ and $2x+6$, we can form an equation to solve for the unknown. Since the total must be $90^{\circ}$, we write: $$4x+(2x+6)=90$$

Collect like terms to get $6x+6=90$, then solve to get $x=14$. In a right-angled triangle, since one angle is already $90^{\circ}$, the other two angles must add to $90^{\circ}$ rather than $180^{\circ}$ by themselves.

## Word problems: choose the variable carefully

In a word problem, decide what $x$ means before you write any algebra. A good choice is often the smallest or simplest unknown.

Consider a problem where Sam, Tia, and Ravi have counters. Let Sam have $x$ counters. Then Tia has $2x$ and Ravi has $2x+4$, so the total gives:

$$$x+2x+(2x+4)=49$$

Collect like terms and solve: $5x+4=49$, so $5x=45$ and $x=9$. But the question asks for Ravi's counters, so substitute back to get $2(9)+4=22$. A quick check is $9+18+22=49$.

## Stretch: area can create a squared term

Sometimes forming an equation from a shape fact gives a squared term. For a right-angled triangle with perpendicular sides $5x$ and $4x$ and area 360 cm², use

$$$\frac{1}{2}\times 5x \times 4x=360$$

Simplify to $10x^2=360$, then $x^2=36$. Take the positive square root to get $x=6$.

The negative root is not suitable here because lengths cannot be negative. The method is still the same: form the equation from the shape fact, solve it, and keep only answers that fit the context.

## Check yourself

1. Which of these is an equation?

   - $$3x+7=22$ ✔

   - $$3x+7$

   - $$x^2+5$

   - $$4(x-1)$

   _Explanation:_ $$3x+7=22$ is an equation because it says two expressions are equal. The most common wrong answer is B, but $3x+7$ is only an expression because it has no equals sign.

2. The angles in a triangle are $2x+5$, $x+20$, and $x-1$. Which equation should you form?

   - $$(2x+5)+(x+20)+(x-1)=180$ ✔

   - $$(2x+5)+(x+20)+(x-1)=90$

   - $$(2x+5)+(x+20)+(x-1)=360$

   - $$2x+5=x+20+x-1$

   _Explanation:_ Angles in any triangle add to $180^\circ$, so option A is the correct equation. The most common wrong answer is B, but $90^\circ$ only applies to a right angle or to the two non-right angles in a right-angled triangle.

3. A right-angled triangle has perpendicular sides $5x$ cm and $4x$ cm and area $360 \text{ cm}^2$. What is $x$?

   - 6 ✔

   - $$\pm 6$

   - 18

   - 36

   _Explanation:_ Using the area formula gives $\frac{1}{2} \times 5x \times 4x = 360$, so $10x^2 = 360$ and $x^2 = 36$. The most common wrong answer is $\pm 6$, but a length cannot be negative, so only $x = 6$ is valid. Hint: Use the triangle area formula, then think about what values a length can take.
