## Hours, minutes and duration

Time can describe a clock time, such as 7:15 pm, or a duration, such as 45 minutes. The key fact for this topic is that 1 hour is 60 minutes.

To change hours to minutes, multiply by 60. For example, 5 hours is $5 \times 60 = 300$ minutes.

To change minutes to hours, divide by 60. For example, 240 minutes is $240 \div 60 = 4$ hours.

## Fractions of an hour

Half an hour is 30 minutes. A quarter of an hour is 15 minutes, and three quarters of an hour is 45 minutes.

To change a mixed number of hours, split it into the whole hours part and the fraction part. Then convert each part to minutes.

Example: $1\frac{1}{4}$ hours is 1 hour plus a quarter of an hour. That is 60 minutes plus 15 minutes, so $60 + 15 = 75$ minutes.

## Finding the difference between durations

When you find the difference between two durations, change them into the same unit first. Minutes are usually the easiest unit to use.

Example: find the difference between 40 minutes and $1\frac{1}{4}$ hours. First change $1\frac{1}{4}$ hours into minutes: 1 hour is 60 minutes and a quarter of an hour is 15 minutes, so $60 + 15 = 75$.

Now subtract the smaller duration from the larger one. $75 - 40 = 35$, so the difference is 35 minutes.

## Adding a duration to a start time

![Time line from 9:50 am to 10:29 am with arrows showing plus 10 minutes to 10:00 am and plus 29 minutes to 10:29 am, total 39 minutes](../assets/001-53ed1c14-03d9-4b26-b6e8-08ea6c14db1f.jpeg)

When you add minutes to a clock time, it helps to reach the next hour first. This stops you writing impossible times such as 7:75.

Nina leaves at 9:50 am and spends 12 minutes walking, 8 minutes waiting, and 19 minutes walking again. First add the durations: $12 + 8 + 19 = 39$ minutes.

From 9:50 am to 10:00 am is 10 minutes, so there are 29 minutes left. Add the remaining 29 minutes to get 10:29 am.

## Reading a timetable

![Bus timetable with rows Green Road, Market, Station and columns Bus A, Bus B, Bus C, with the Bus B column highlighted to show one journey](../assets/002-c7f46898-12f6-4f05-a68f-e09b30164506.jpeg)

In a timetable, one column usually shows one whole bus or train journey. If you start with a time in one row, read down the same column to follow that service.

In this timetable, the time 0735 is in the Bus B column at Green Road. Reading down the same column shows 0747 at Market and 0805 at Station.

So a bus that leaves Green Road at 0735 arrives at Station at 0805. The journey time is 30 minutes because 07:35 to 08:05 is half an hour.

## Working backwards for the latest time

To find the latest time that still works, start with the deadline and work backwards. Subtract any walking time after the bus or train first, then choose the latest service that still arrives in time.

Use this timetable for the worked example. Once you choose a row entry, stay in the same column to find the matching time at the other stop.

| Place | Bus A | Bus B | Bus C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hill Road | $0806$ | $0816$ | $0826$ |
| Town Centre | $0836$ | $0846$ | $0856$ |

Sam must be at work by $09:00$, and the walk from Town Centre to work takes $8$ minutes. So the bus must arrive by $08:52$, which means $0856$ is too late and $0846$ is the latest arrival that works.

Bus B arrives at $0846$, so it must be the bus Sam uses. It leaves Hill Road at $0816$, and with a $12$ minute walk to the stop Sam must leave home at $08:04$.

## Check yourself

1. How many minutes are in 3 hours?

   - 180 ✔

   - 300

   - 360

   - 30

   _Explanation:_ 3 hours is 3 groups of 60 minutes, so $3 \times 60 = 180$. 300 is the common wrong answer if you wrongly think 1 hour has 100 minutes instead of 60 minutes.

2. What is the difference between 50 minutes and $1\frac{1}{2}$ hours?

   - 40 minutes ✔

   - 25 minutes

   - 90 minutes

   - 10 minutes

   _Explanation:_ Hint: Convert both durations to minutes before subtracting. $1\frac{1}{2}$ hours is 90 minutes because 1 hour is 60 minutes and half an hour is 30 minutes. Then $90 - 50 = 40$; 25 minutes is the common wrong answer if you mistakenly think half an hour is 25 minutes.

3. In the timetable shown, a bus leaves Green Road at 0800. When does it reach Market?

   - 0812 ✔

   - 0830

   - 0747

   - 0722

   _Explanation:_ 0800 is in the Bus C column at Green Road, and the Market time in that same column is 0812. 0830 is the common wrong answer if you read down to Station instead of stopping at the Market row. Find 0800, then stay in the same column.
