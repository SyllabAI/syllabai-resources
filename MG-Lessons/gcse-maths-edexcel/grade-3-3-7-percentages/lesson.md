## Percent means out of 100

![Percentage bar](../assets/001-3aa5a5ff-1884-43da-a6c5-90a56e71d300.jpeg)

A percentage compares an amount to 100. The whole starting amount is always $100\%$, so $50\%$ is half and $25\%$ is a quarter.

$$10\%$ means divide by 10, and $1\%$ means divide by 100. If your $10\%$ answer is bigger than the original amount, check your division.

If the percentage is more than $100\%$, the answer is bigger than the original amount. For example, $200\%$ means double.

## Finding easy percentages

To find $10\%$, divide by $10$. To find $50\%$, halve the amount, and to find $1\%$, divide by $100$.

For example, $86 \div 10 = 8.6$, so $10\%$ of £$86$ is £$8.60$. Also, $1280 \div 2 = 640$, so $50\%$ of $1280$ g is $640$ g.

For $1\%$, use $350 \div 100 = 3.5$. So $1\%$ of $350$ litres is $3.5$ litres.

## Build harder percentages from easy ones

Break awkward percentages into easy chunks such as 10% and 1%. Then combine the parts at the end.

To find 34% of 2500, start with $2500 \div 10 = 250$, so 30% is $3 \times 250 = 750$. Also, $2500 \div 100 = 25$, so 4% is $4 \times 25 = 100$.

Now add the parts: $750 + 100 = 850$. So 34% of 2500 is 850, not $34 \times 2500$.

## More than 100%

A percentage greater than 100% means more than the whole, so the answer will be larger than the starting amount. For example, 200% is double and 150% is one and a half times.

To find 215% of 80, split it into 200%, 10%, and 5%. That gives $2 \times 80 = 160$, $80 \div 10 = 8$, and $8 \div 2 = 4$.

Now add the parts: $160 + 8 + 4 = 172$. In comparison questions, work out both actual values before deciding which is greater.

## Working backwards to the whole

Sometimes the question gives you a part and asks for the total. First decide what percentage the known amount represents.

If 54 adults are 60% of the people, find 10% by dividing by 6: $54 \div 6 = 9$. Then find 100% by multiplying by 10: $9 \times 10 = 90$.

So there are 90 people altogether. Do not find 40% of 54, because 54 is the part, not the whole.

## Increase, reduce, and compare offers

For an increase, add the percentage amount to the original value. For a reduction, subtract the percentage amount from the original value.

A wage of £32,000 increasing by 3% goes up by $32000 \div 100 = 320$ for 1%, so 3% is $3 \times 320 = 960$. The new wage is $32000 + 960 = 32960$, so £32,960.

A coat costing £45 reduced by 20% goes down by $45 \div 10 = 4.5$ for 10%, so 20% is £9. The sale price is $45 - 9 = 36$, so £36.

For shop offers, work out the total cost for the exact number of items you need. Then compare the actual totals, not just the percentage or the slogan.

## Deposits and monthly payments

A deposit is money paid at the start. Subtract it from the total cost before you work out what is left to pay.

For a £260 tablet with a 15% deposit, 10% is £26 and 5% is £13, so the deposit is £39. The amount left is $260 - 39 = 221$.

If the rest is paid in monthly payments of £17, then $221 \div 17 = 13$. So 13 monthly payments are needed.

## Simple interest

Simple interest is worked out from the original amount each year, so the interest stays the same every year. You can also write the total interest as $I = \frac{Prt}{100}$. Here, $P$ is the original amount, $r$ is the yearly rate, and $t$ is the time in years.

For £600 at 1.5% simple interest per year, 1% is $600 \div 100 = 6$ and 0.5% is half of that, so £3. The interest for one year is £9.

Over 4 years, the total interest is $9 \times 4 = 36$. A common mistake is to give £9, which is only one year's interest.

## Check yourself

1. Which statement is correct?

   - 25% of an amount means one quarter of it ✔

   - 25% of an amount means add 25 to it

   - 25% of an amount is always greater than half

   - 25% of an amount means divide by 25

   _Explanation:_ 25% means $\frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}$, so it is one quarter of the whole. A common wrong choice is D because dividing by 25 gives $\frac{1}{25}$ of the amount, which is 4%, not 25%.

2. What is 17% of 300?

   - 51 ✔

   - 30

   - 17

   - 90

   _Explanation:_ 10% of 300 is 30 and 1% is 3, so 17% is $30 + 7 \times 3 = 51$. A common wrong choice is B because 30 is only 10%, so it misses the extra 7%.

3. What is 125% of 48?

   - 60 ✔

   - 12

   - 48

   - 72

   _Explanation:_ 125% means $100\% + 25\%$. So $48 + 12 = 60$. A common wrong choice is C because 48 is only 100%, so it misses the extra 25%.

4. A sofa costs $£450$ and the deposit is $15\%$. How much is left to pay?

   - $$£382.50$ ✔

   - $$£67.50$

   - $$£435$

   - $$£517.50$

   _Explanation:_ $$10\%$ of $£450$ is $£45$ and $5\%$ is $£22.50$, so the $15\%$ deposit is $£67.50$. The amount left is $£450 - £67.50 = £382.50$. A common wrong choice is B because it is the deposit itself, not the balance still to pay.

5. £600 is invested for 4 years at 1.5% simple interest per year. How much interest is earned altogether?

   - £36 ✔

   - £9

   - £24

   - £90

   _Explanation:_ 1.5% of £600 is £9 for one year, and simple interest stays the same each year. Over 4 years, $9 \times 4 = 36$, so the total interest is £36. A common wrong choice is B because it gives only one year's interest.
