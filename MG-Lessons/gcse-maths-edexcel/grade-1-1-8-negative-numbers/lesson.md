## Numbers below zero

![Number line from -10 to 8 with arrows showing 6 minus 11 landing at -5 and -4 plus negative 6 landing at -10, with a note that further left means smaller](../assets/001-396efb38-6aea-4e2b-a95d-c02410e715d9.jpeg)

A negative number is less than $0$, and a positive number is greater than $0$. On a number line, numbers get bigger as you move right and smaller as you move left.

This means a number further left on the number line is always smaller. For example, $-7$ is smaller than $-2$, even though the magnitude $7$ is bigger than $2$ in the positive sense.

Temperatures, bank balances, and heights below sea level often use negative numbers. If one town is $-7^\circ \text{C}$ and another is $1^\circ \text{C}$, the colder town has the smaller number.

## Adding and subtracting on a number line

When you add a positive number, you move right. When you subtract a positive number, you move left.

Adding a negative number also moves left. Starting at $-4$ and moving $6$ places left gives $$-4 + (-6) = -10$$

The total becomes more negative. Subtracting can take you past zero.

Starting at $6$ and moving $11$ places left gives $$6 - 11 = -5$$ Cross zero carefully and keep counting left.

## Subtracting a negative

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding the positive version. The second minus sign belongs to the negative number, so you must not ignore it.

$$$9 - (-4) = 9 + 4 = 13$$
Start at $9$, then move $4$ places right because you are subtracting a negative.

Whenever you see two signs together, slow down and read them as words. '$-(-4)$' means 'subtract negative $4$', not 'subtract $4$'.

## Multiplying and dividing signed numbers

For multiplication and division, first find the size of the answer. Then decide the sign: same signs give a positive answer, and different signs give a negative answer.

One negative sign gives a negative answer. For example,
$$-7 \times 5 = -35$$
The signs are different, so the product is negative.

Two negative signs give a positive answer. For example,
$$-42 \div (-6) = 7$$
The signs are the same, so the quotient is positive.

You can use inverse operations for missing numbers too. If $x \times 6 = -18$, divide by $6$ to get
$$x = -18 \div 6 = -3$$
Check by multiplying back: $6 \times (-3) = -18$.

## Sequences that go below zero

A sequence is a list of numbers in order, and each number is called a term. To continue a simple sequence, look for the change from one term to the next.

In $13, 8, 3, \ldots$, the change each time is subtract $5$. Keep using the same change, even when you cross zero.

$$$3 - 5 = -2,\quad -2 - 5 = -7$$

So the next two terms are $-2$ and $-7$. Crossing zero does not change the pattern.

## Temperatures and difference

In temperature questions, words matter. "Risen" or "increased" means add, while "fallen" or "lower" means subtract.

If a temperature is $-5^\circ \text{C}$ and it rises by $8^\circ \text{C}$, then $$-5 + 8 = 3$$ so the new temperature is $3^\circ \text{C}$. You have crossed zero from negative to positive.

The difference between two temperatures is the distance between them, so it is written as a positive number. Between $-5^\circ \text{C}$ and $6^\circ \text{C}$, the difference is $$5 + 6 = 11^\circ \text{C}$$ Count to zero, then from zero to the other temperature.

## Check yourself

1. Which temperature is lowest?

   - $$4^\circ \text{C}$

   - $$-2^\circ \text{C}$

   - $$-7^\circ \text{C}$ ✔

   - $$1^\circ \text{C}$

   _Explanation:_ $$-7^\circ \text{C}$ is the smallest value because it is furthest left on the number line. A common wrong answer is $-2^\circ \text{C}$, which is negative, but $-7$ is more negative and therefore smaller.

2. What is $5 - (-3)$?

   - $$2$

   - $$8$ ✔

   - $$-8$

   - $$-2$

   _Explanation:_ Subtracting a negative becomes addition, so $5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8$. A common wrong answer is $2$, which comes from dropping the second minus and doing $5 - 3$ instead.

3. Continue the sequence $4, 1, -2, \ldots$ What is the next term?

   - $$-5$ ✔

   - $$2$

   - $$-1$

   - $$-6$

   _Explanation:_ The sequence decreases by $3$ each time: $4 \to 1 \to -2 \to -5$. A common wrong answer is $2$, which comes from adding $3$ instead of subtracting $3$.

4. At 6 am, a temperature is $-4^\circ \text{C}$. By midday it rises $7^\circ \text{C}$, and another city is at $5^\circ \text{C}$. What are the new temperature and the difference between the two cities?

   - $$3^\circ \text{C}$ and $2^\circ \text{C}$ ✔

   - $$-11^\circ \text{C}$ and $9^\circ \text{C}$

   - $$3^\circ \text{C}$ and $8^\circ \text{C}$

   - $$-11^\circ \text{C}$ and $1^\circ \text{C}$

   _Explanation:_ Rising by $7^\circ \text{C}$ means add, so $-4 + 7 = 3$. Then compare $3^\circ \text{C}$ and $5^\circ \text{C}$, which are $2^\circ \text{C}$ apart; a common wrong answer is $3^\circ \text{C}$ and $8^\circ \text{C}$ because it adds the temperatures instead of finding the distance between them.
