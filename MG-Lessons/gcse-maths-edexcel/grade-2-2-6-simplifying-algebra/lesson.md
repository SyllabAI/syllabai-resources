## What simplifying algebra means

Simplifying algebra means rewriting an expression in a shorter, neater form without changing its value. You are not solving for the letter, just tidying the expression.

A variable is a letter that stands for a number, such as $x$ or $m$. A term is one part of an expression, and the coefficient is the number multiplying the variable.

Repeated addition can be written as one term with a coefficient. For example, $r + r + r + r$ has four lots of $r$, so it simplifies to $4r$.

## Collecting like terms

Like terms have exactly the same letter part. $7x$ and $-2x$ are like terms, but $x$ and $x^2$ are not.

Add or subtract the coefficients, then keep the letter part the same.

$$6t + 3t - 4t = (6 + 3 - 4)t = 5t$$

This gives $5t$.

If different letters appear, collect each type separately.

$$8g + 4h - 3g + 2h = (8 - 3)g + (4 + 2)h = 5g + 6h$$

The answer is $5g + 6h$.

## Constants and unlike terms

A constant is a number with no letter attached. Constants only combine with other constants, not with variable terms.

In $5a + 6 - 2a + 3$, collect the $a$ terms and the constants separately. $$5a + 6 - 2a + 3 = (5 - 2)a + (6 + 3) = 3a + 9$$ So the simplified expression is $3a + 9$.

A common mistake is turning $3a + 4b$ into $7ab$. That is wrong because the letter parts are different, so the terms are not like terms.

## Multiplying algebra terms

When multiplying algebra terms, multiply the number parts and then the letter parts. We usually omit the multiplication sign, so $4 \times x$ is written as $4x$.

For a number times a term, multiply the numbers and keep the letter.

$$5 \times 4x = 20x$$

This works because 5 times 4 is 20 and the $x$ stays.

For two algebra terms, multiply both coefficients and both letter parts.

$$3p \times 6q = 18pq$$

This is why $2a \times 5b$ becomes $10ab$, not $7ab$.

## Powers and repeated letters

![Colour-coded expression 4x squared minus 3x plus 2x squared plus 5x with x squared terms bracketed in blue, x terms bracketed in green, and arrows to 6x squared plus 2x](../assets/001-e50f10bf-f53b-4137-8730-e96ae2713b68.jpeg)

A power, or index, tells you how many times a letter is multiplied by itself. For example, $z \times z \times z = z^3$ and $x \times x = x^2$.

The image shows why $x^2$ terms must be grouped separately from $x$ terms.

$$4x^2 - 3x + 2x^2 + 5x = (4 + 2)x^2 + (-3 + 5)x = 6x^2 + 2x$$

So the simplified expression is $6x^2 + 2x$. Never mix $x$ and $x^2$ into one term. The letter may match, but the power changes the identity of the variable part.

## Dividing algebra terms

To divide a simple algebra term by a number, divide the coefficient and keep the letter part. For example, $15k$ divided by 5 becomes $3k$.

$$$\frac{15k}{5} = 3k$$

Sometimes you should simplify the numerator first, then divide. Combining like terms on top makes the division easier.

$$$\frac{8m + 4m}{4} = \frac{12m}{4} = 3m$$

If the same letter multiplies the whole term on top and bottom, it can cancel. But you cannot cancel a letter across addition in something like $\frac{6d + 2}{d}$.

$$$\frac{6d}{d} = 6$$

## Check yourself

1. Simplify $r + r + r + r$.

   - $$4r$ ✔

   - $$r^4$

   - $$r + 4$

   - $$4 + r$

   _Explanation:_ $$r + r + r + r$ is four lots of $r$, so it simplifies to $4r$. The common wrong answer $r^4$ would mean $r \times r \times r \times r$, which is repeated multiplication, not repeated addition.

2. Simplify $5a + 6 - 2a + 3$.

   - $$3a + 9$ ✔

   - $$7a + 9$

   - $$12a$

   - $$3a + 3$

   _Explanation:_ The $a$ terms combine to $3a$ and the constants combine to 9, so the result is $3a + 9$. The common wrong answer $7a + 9$ forgets that the second $a$ term is negative, so the minus sign has been lost. Hint: Keep the sign with each term when you collect it.

3. Which pair are like terms?

   - $$4x^2$ and $2x^2$ ✔

   - $$4x$ and $4x^2$

   - $$3a$ and $3b$

   - $$2xy$ and $2x$

   _Explanation:_ Like terms must have exactly the same letter part, including any powers, so $4x^2$ and $2x^2$ can be combined. The common wrong answer $4x$ and $4x^2$ looks similar, but the powers are different, so they are unlike terms.
