## Best buy means a fair comparison

![Flowchart comparing 12 rolls for £4.20 and 5 rolls for £1.85, converting to pence, finding 35p and 37p per roll, and choosing the lower unit cost](../assets/001-c8a0bc2d-80b7-48a3-9e26-85135583d3e8.jpeg)

Best buy questions ask which option gives the best value for money. To compare fairly, turn each option into the same unit, such as p per roll, p per kg, or p per 100 ml.

The best value is the option with the lowest unit cost, not always the lowest total price. A smaller pack often costs less overall but more for each item.

A useful formula is
$$\text{unit cost} = \frac{\text{total price}}{\text{quantity}}$$
If prices are in pounds, converting to pence first often makes the calculation easier.

## Worked example with items

A shop sells 12 kitchen rolls for £4.20 or 5 kitchen rolls for £1.85. Convert both prices to pence first, so the calculations use 420p and 185p.

For the 12-pack, $$420 \div 12 = 35$$ so the cost is 35p per roll. For the 5-pack, $$185 \div 5 = 37$$ so the cost is 37p per roll.

Now compare the unit costs, not the total prices. The 12-pack is better value because 35p per roll is lower than 37p per roll.

## Comparing weights and volumes

When quantities are in g, kg, or ml, choose a sensible comparison unit such as p per kg, p per 100 g, or p per 100 ml. Using per 100 often makes the numbers easier to compare.

Example: a large shampoo bottle has 800 ml for £4.80 and a small bottle has 350 ml for £2.24. Convert to pence first, so the prices are 480p and 224p.

For the large bottle,
$$\frac{480 \, \text{p}}{800 \, \text{ml}} \times 100 \, \text{ml} = 60 \, \text{p}$$
so the cost is 60p per 100 ml.

For the small bottle,
$$\frac{224 \, \text{p}}{350 \, \text{ml}} \times 100 \, \text{ml} = 64 \, \text{p}$$
so the cost is 64p per 100 ml. The large bottle is better value because 60 is lower than 64.

## Comparing three options

With three or more options, do the same comparison for each one. Sometimes a common amount is quicker than finding cost per unit value.

Example: Pack A has 180 tea bags for £4.50, Pack B has 150 tea bags for £3.60, and Pack C has 120 tea bags for £3.00. All three amounts are multiples of 30, so we can compare the cost per 30 tea bags.

Pack A has 6 groups of 30, so $450 \div 6 = 75$. Pack B has 5 groups of 30, so $360 \div 5 = 72$.

Pack C has 4 groups of 30, so $300 \div 4 = 75$. Pack B is the best value because 72p per 30 bags is the lowest cost among the options.

## Special offers change different things

Special offers change either the quantity or the price. Extra free increases the amount you get, while money off or percentage off reduces the price.

Pack A has 200 wipes plus $20\%$ extra free for £3. Since $20\%$ of 200 is $0.20 \times 200 = 40$, Pack A gives 240 wipes for 300p.

Pack B has 200 wipes with $15\%$ off the normal price of £3. Since $15\%$ of 300p is $0.15 \times 300 = 45$, Pack B costs 255p for 200 wipes.

Now find unit costs: $300 \div 240 = 1.25$ p per wipe and $255 \div 200 = 1.275$ p per wipe. Pack A is slightly better value because 1.25 is lower than 1.275. Keep full calculator values until the end when answers are close.

## Check yourself

1. In a best buy question, which option is the best value?

   - The one with the lowest unit cost ✔

   - The one with the lowest total price

   - The one with the biggest quantity

   - The one with the brightest offer label

   _Explanation:_ Best value means the cheapest cost for each equal unit, such as per item or per 100 ml. The most common wrong answer is B, but the cheapest total price can still be worse value if the pack is much smaller.

2. A $750$ ml juice bottle costs $£1.80$ and a $1$ litre bottle costs $£2.30$. Which statement is correct?

   - The $750$ ml bottle is better value because $£1.80$ is cheaper.

   - The $1$ litre bottle is better value because it costs $23$p per $100$ ml, compared with $24$p per $100$ ml for the $750$ ml bottle ✔

   - They cannot be compared because one is in ml and one is in litres.

   - The $750$ ml bottle is better value because $750$ is smaller than $1000$.

   _Explanation:_ Convert $1$ litre to $1000$ ml, then compare the same unit for both bottles. The most common wrong answer is A, but lower total price does not mean lower cost per $100$ ml.

3. A pack says "20% extra free". What changes before you work out unit cost?

   - The price decreases by 20%

   - The quantity increases by 20% ✔

   - Both the price and the quantity decrease by 20%

   - Nothing changes until the final answer

   _Explanation:_ Extra free means you get more product for the same price, so you must increase the quantity first. The most common wrong answer is A, but that would describe a discount, not extra free.

4. Stretch: Which cereal pack is better value?

   - Pack A: 500 g for £1.89, because 189p is less than 244p

   - Pack B: 650 g for £2.44, because it costs about 37.54p per 100 g, which is slightly less than Pack A ✔

   - They are exactly the same because both are about 38p per 100 g

   - You cannot tell unless both are converted to kg

   _Explanation:_ Pack A costs $189 \div 500 \times 100 = 37.8$ p per 100 g, while Pack B costs $244 \div 650 \times 100 \approx 37.54$ p per 100 g. The most common wrong answer is C, but rounding too early hides the small difference, so keep full calculator values until you compare.
