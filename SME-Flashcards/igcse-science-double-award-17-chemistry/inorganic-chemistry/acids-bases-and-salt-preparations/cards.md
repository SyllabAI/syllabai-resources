## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_hY5S2rH9KvmVHBnG`)

**FRONT**

What are the steps to prepare a pure, dry soluble salt from an insoluble base?


**BACK**

What are the steps to prepare a pure, dry soluble salt from an insoluble base?

1. Add excess **insoluble base** to acid and stir until no more dissolves
2. **Filter** to remove excess solid
3. **Evaporate** the filtrate by heating until crystals begin to form
4. Allow to cool and dry the crystals
