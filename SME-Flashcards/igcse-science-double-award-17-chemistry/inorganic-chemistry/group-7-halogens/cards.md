## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_VmGxqz4hZT5VRhQC`)

**FRONT**

Define **halogens**.


**BACK**

The **halogens** are the Group 7 non-metal elements: fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine and astatine. They are all **poisonous** and form **diatomic** molecules (pairs of atoms joined by a single covalent bond).

## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_3Hs5y6TcjJtdHMcJ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Reactivity decreases as you go down Group 7.


**BACK**

**True.**

As you descend Group 7, each halogen atom has more electron shells, so it is less able to attract an extra electron to complete its outer shell. This makes each successive halogen **less reactive**.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_8s7G2bCHDCcp255Z`)

**FRONT**

State the colour and physical state of **chlorine**, **bromine** and **iodine** at room temperature.


**BACK**

1. **Chlorine** — pale yellow-green gas
2. **Bromine** — red-brown liquid
3. **Iodine** — grey solid (sublimes to form a purple vapour)

The colours become darker going down the group.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_NbV39XG4sQCHPcVt`)

**FRONT**

What is observed when chlorine solution is added to potassium bromide solution?


**BACK**

The solution turns **orange-yellow** as **bromine** is produced. Chlorine displaces bromide ions because chlorine is more reactive, forming potassium chloride and bromine: Cl<sub>2</sub> + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br<sub>2</sub>.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_MjGnSgbrwJTxhRNJ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Bromine can displace chloride ions from potassium chloride solution.


**BACK**

**False.**

**Bromine** is less reactive than **chlorine**, so it cannot displace **chloride ions**. A halogen can only displace a halide ion of a **less reactive** halogen from solution.

## Card 8 — keyword_definition (`fl_pGSvsryFhpSZVgzq`)

**FRONT**

Define **halogen displacement reaction**.


**BACK**

A **halogen displacement reaction** occurs when a more reactive halogen displaces a less reactive halogen from an aqueous solution of its **halide** salt. It provides evidence for the trend in reactivity in Group 7.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_JGQxGn8DtpTGFkkC`)

**FRONT**

Explain whether **astatine** would displace iodide ions from potassium iodide solution.


**BACK**

**Astatine** would not displace iodide ions because it lies below iodine in Group 7 and is therefore **less reactive** than iodine. Only a more reactive halogen can displace a less reactive one.
