## Card 17 — keyword_definition (`fl_GGv42w9f9RKsSCWM`)

**FRONT**

What is **simple distillation**?


**BACK**

**Simple distillation** is a technique used to separate a liquid from a dissolved solid, or a pure liquid from a mixture of liquids with sufficiently different boiling points. The solution is heated so the liquid evaporates, then the vapour condenses in a condenser and is collected.


Spec links: `spcpt_kXdX76GTHCRfmgjJ`


## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_Tt54J379pKWXdSk2`)

**FRONT**

How does **fractional distillation** separate a mixture of ethanol and water?


**BACK**

Fractional distillation separates ethanol and water by exploiting their different **boiling points**. The mixture is heated to 78 °C, the boiling point of **ethanol**, which evaporates first, condenses in the condenser and is collected. Heating is stopped before the temperature reaches the boiling point of water (100 °C).

## Card 31 — question_and_answer (`fl_x7zJDJp2XXSyQWZQ`)

**FRONT**

Why is a **reference spot** included in a chromatography experiment?


**BACK**

A **reference spot** of a known substance is included so that unknown spots can be compared to it. If an unknown spot travels the same distance as the reference spot, the two substances are likely to be the same compound.

The Rf value of a compound is the same regardless of which solvent is used.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **Rf value** depends on the solvent used. If the solvent is changed, the Rf value changes. Rf values are only comparable when measured under the **same conditions**.

## Card 33 — question_and_answer (`fl_DRq9bdKg9mxCKyT7`)

**FRONT**

A substance travels 3 cm and the solvent travels 6 cm. What is the **Rf value** of the substance?


**BACK**

The **Rf value** = distance moved by substance ÷ distance moved by solvent = 3 ÷ 6 = **0.5**. Rf values have no units and are always less than 1.

**FRONT**

Why should sample spots be kept as **small** as possible in paper chromatography?


**BACK**

Large sample spots cause separated spots to **merge or spread**, making it difficult to measure the distance moved accurately. Keeping spots small and concentrated produces a clearer **chromatogram** and more accurate Rf values.
