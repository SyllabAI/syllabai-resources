**FRONT**

Define the **Law of Conservation of Mass**.


**BACK**

The **Law of Conservation of Mass** states that the total mass of **reactants** is always equal to the total mass of **products** in a chemical reaction. Atoms cannot be created or destroyed. They are simply rearranged.
