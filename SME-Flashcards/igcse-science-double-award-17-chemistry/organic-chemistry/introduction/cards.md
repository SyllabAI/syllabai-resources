## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_43FwKnpyCzgCtmRR`)

**FRONT**

What does a **displayed formula** show that a structural formula does not?


**BACK**

A **displayed formula** shows **every atom** and **every bond** in the molecule. A **structural formula** omits most covalent bonds and only shows key bonds such as double bonds.

## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_jdqcHQPTzyVYYNFY`)

**FRONT**

Define **homologous series**.


**BACK**

A **homologous series** is a family of organic compounds that share the same **functional group**, the same **general formula**, similar **chemical properties** and a gradation in **physical properties**. Each member differs from the next by CH<sub>2</sub>.

## Card 17 — question_and_answer (`fl_w3C7ZWGP8wF4nhsX`)

**FRONT**

How do you name the **position isomers** of an unbranched alkene?


**BACK**

Position isomers of an unbranched alkene are named by placing a **number** before the suffix **-ene** to show the carbon where the double bond starts. For example, **but-1-ene** has the double bond starting on carbon 1, while **but-2-ene** has it starting on carbon 2.

## Card 27 — question_and_answer (`fl_W7FkKfqGDDmgJKQ3`)

**FRONT**

How does incomplete combustion of a hydrocarbon differ from complete combustion in terms of products?


**BACK**

**Incomplete combustion** occurs when oxygen supply is limited. Instead of **carbon dioxide**, the products include **carbon monoxide** (and possibly soot/unburned carbon). **Water** is produced in both cases.
