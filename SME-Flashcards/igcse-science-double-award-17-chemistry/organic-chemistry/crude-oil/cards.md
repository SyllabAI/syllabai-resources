**BACK**

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Crude oil is a pure substance made up of a single hydrocarbon.


**BACK**

**False.**

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_gYvV4zBBm43Wv47F`)

**FRONT**

What property of a hydrocarbon fraction determines where it condenses in the fractionating column?


**BACK**

A fraction condenses at the point in the column where the temperature matches its **boiling point**. Fractions with **higher boiling points** (longer chains) condense lower down; those with **lower boiling points** (shorter chains) rise higher before condensing.


Spec links: `spcpt_pRGVQD76dB6Fmnfv`

**BACK**

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_CkWxG5c2pJ68Qz4S`)

**FRONT**

Why does volatility decrease as the carbon chain length of a hydrocarbon fraction increases?


**BACK**

Longer chains have stronger **intermolecular forces** between molecules. More energy is needed to overcome these forces, so the fraction is less likely to vaporise, making it less **volatile**.

## Card 30 — question_and_answer (`fl_G9qrHkG4ZjkFbtgv`)

**FRONT**

Why is cracking necessary in the oil industry?


**BACK**

**Demand** for short-chain fractions such as petrol exceeds their **supply** from fractional distillation, so cracking converts surplus long-chain fractions into more useful shorter-chain products.
