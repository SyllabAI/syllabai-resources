## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_HNYJ54QqnxytP4mg`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for the **density** of an object?


**BACK**

The equation for density is $ρ=\frac{m}{V}$

Where:

- *ρ*  = density, measured in kg / m<sup>3</sup>
- $m$ = mass, measured in kilograms (kg)
- $V$ = volume, measured in metres cubed (m<sup>3</sup>)

## Card 20 — keyword_definition (`fl_pFzczCHyy4xbnydm`)

**FRONT**

State the equation relating pressure, force and area.


**BACK**

The equation for pressure, force and area is $P=\frac{F}{A}$

Where:

- $P$ = pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $F$ = force, measured in newton's (N)
- $A$ = surface area, measured in metres squared (m<sup>2</sup>)

## Card 32 — question_and_answer (`fl_zjMVrTfBWWKq5634`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for the pressure difference of an object in a fluid.


**BACK**

The equation for the pressure difference of an object in a fluid is $\Deltap=ρg\Deltah$

Where:

- $\Deltap$ = change in pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $ρ$ = density of the liquid, measured in kilograms per metre cubed (kg/m<sup>3</sup>)
- $g$ = gravitational field strength on Earth, measured in newtons per kilogram (N/kg)
- $\Deltah$ = change in depth of the object in the liquid, measured in metres (m)
