**BACK**

**BACK**

**Salinisation** is when high **evaporation** draws groundwater to the surface; as it evaporates, **salts** are left in the topsoil, making the land **toxic to many crops**.


Spec links: `spcpt_XSBYzr69MbH6pwrT`
