## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_knvPDvQM63vkRND3`)

**FRONT**

Define **renewable energy**.


**BACK**

**Renewable energy** sources are **infinite** and will **not run out**, e.g. hydroelectric, wave/tidal, wind, solar, geothermal and biomass.

**BACK**
