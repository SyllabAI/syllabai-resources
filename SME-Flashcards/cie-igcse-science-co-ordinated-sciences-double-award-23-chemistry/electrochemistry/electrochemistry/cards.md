## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_dxQKmHTfwBBDBPNB`)

**FRONT**

Define electrolysis.


**BACK**

Electrolysis is the process in which an ionic compound, either molten or dissolved in aqueous solution, is **broken down** by passing an electric current through it.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_VWsJzSd9FRxr3TgR`)

**FRONT**

Why can solid ionic compounds not undergo electrolysis?


**BACK**

Solid ionic compounds cannot undergo electrolysis because their ions are **fixed in a lattice** and cannot move to carry charge.

Electrolysis requires free ions, which are only present when the compound is molten or dissolved in solution.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_wMB7NJQ2thWWhg6k`)

**FRONT**

How do electrons and ions each contribute to charge transfer during electrolysis?


**BACK**

**Electrons** carry charge through the external circuit (wires and electrodes), while **ions** carry charge through the electrolyte. Cations move towards the cathode and anions move towards the anode, completing the circuit.
