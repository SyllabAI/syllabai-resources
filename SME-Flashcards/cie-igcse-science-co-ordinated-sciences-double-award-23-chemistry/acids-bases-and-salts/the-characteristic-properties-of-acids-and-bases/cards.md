## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_H6pRz9qjK5cXwJzK`)

**FRONT**

Why does a solution become neutral when an acid and an alkali react in a neutralisation reaction?


**BACK**

The solution becomes neutral because **H**<sup>**+**</sup>** ions** from the acid react with **OH**<sup>**-**</sup>** ions** from the alkali to form **water**, removing both types of ion from solution.
