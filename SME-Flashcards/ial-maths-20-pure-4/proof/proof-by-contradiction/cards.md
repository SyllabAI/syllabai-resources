## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_YfNQ8Pt4wSnnzYKh`)

**FRONT**

Define **proof by contradiction**.


**BACK**

A **proof by contradiction** begins by assuming the **opposite** of the statement to be proved.

Logical steps then lead to something impossible, which rules the assumption out and leaves the original statement standing.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_P7QFRyd4WZrNWGdK`)

**FRONT**

Why does a proof by contradiction need written explanation as well as algebra?


**BACK**

Because the **logic** is the proof, not the algebra.

A reader has to be told what is being assumed and where the impossibility appears, and neither of those is visible in the working on its own.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_RbDMpzW4NHxBYwtq`)

**FRONT**

To prove that $\sqrt{5}$ is irrational you assume it is rational. How is that assumption written down?


**BACK**

As $\sqrt{5} = \frac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers with **no common factors** and $b \neq 0$.

The no-common-factors condition is the one the contradiction eventually attacks, so it has to be stated right at the start.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_KP5sbfGMX6zSFhT7`)

**FRONT**

How does the proof that $\sqrt{5}$ is irrational reach its contradiction?


**BACK**

It shows that $a$ and $b$ must **both** be multiples of $5$.

That contradicts the starting condition that they share no common factor, so $\sqrt{5}$ cannot be rational after all.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_StHm2tWCJMVS3J6c`)

**FRONT**

What does prime factorisation let you write any composite number as?


**BACK**

A **product of primes**.

It earns its place in proofs by contradiction because an assumption about a number often forces its prime factors into an impossible arrangement.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_fTmP8R6qY6gdb5zB`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A single contradiction can prove a statement about infinitely many numbers.


**BACK**

**True.**

The assumption covered every case at once, so one impossibility brings the whole of it down.

That is what lets contradiction prove statements no amount of checking examples ever could.
