## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_wG4XQqwyc8jMggH5`)

**FRONT**

Define **bioplastics**.


**BACK**

**Bioplastics** are plastics made **fully or partly** from **biological raw materials**, such as plant starch, vegetable oils or algae.

## Card 4 — true_or_false (`fl_2kkZrjTS5M7WHDT7`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

All bioplastics are biodegradable.


**BACK**

**False.**

Whether a bioplastic biodegrades depends on its **chemical structure**, so some bioplastics **do not decompose** naturally.

## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_sGfZCHxDWW8N7kts`)

**FRONT**

Define **biodegradable plastics**.


**BACK**

**Biodegradable plastics** are plastics designed to **decompose in water or soil** through natural processes.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_ycMs8wFQpDG2PGVd`)

**FRONT**

Which organisms decompose **biodegradable plastics**?


**BACK**

**Bacteria** and **fungi** decompose biodegradable plastics by breaking the chemical bonds in the plastic.


Spec links: `spcpt_H7XjvYf6F6x34Yhk`

**FRONT**

**BACK**

The decomposition rate of biodegradable plastics depends on **temperature**, **moisture** and **oxygen levels**.

**BACK**

**Non-biodegradable plastics** are plastics that **do not decompose quickly** and take hundreds to thousands of years to break down.

**Sunlight degradation** and **wave action** break down non-biodegradable plastics over very long periods.

## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_pKsxgkrfh4pW4DSm`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

Entanglement **restricts movement**, so animals may become unable to **swim, feed or surface for air**.
