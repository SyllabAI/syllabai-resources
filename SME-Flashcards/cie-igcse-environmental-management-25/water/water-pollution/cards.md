## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_QwF7n6t8wd2XqnKs`)

**FRONT**

Define **water pollution**.


**BACK**

**Water pollution** occurs when substances enter rivers, lakes, groundwater or oceans and make the water **unsafe** for drinking, farming, fishing or wildlife.
