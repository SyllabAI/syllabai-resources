## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_ckG3tyFRTY9ptYyn`)

**FRONT**

Define **igneous rock**.


**BACK**

**Igneous rock** is rock that forms when **molten rock** (magma or lava) cools and solidifies.


Spec links: `spcpt_pVT4zyP7Pk92Rgnf`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_qCNgz5WgZW3cXCYG`)

**FRONT**

How does **magma** differ from **lava**?


**BACK**

Magma is molten rock **beneath the Earth's surface**, while lava is molten rock that **reaches the surface**.


Spec links: `spcpt_pVT4zyP7Pk92Rgnf`


## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_WYJQ7N9fsTfkKRYF`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**Intrusive igneous rock** forms **below the surface** when magma cools **slowly**, so it has **large crystals**, for example granite.


Spec links: `spcpt_pVT4zyP7Pk92Rgnf`
