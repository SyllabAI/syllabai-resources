## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_Qhr3W5yVQFwGQ4hy`)

**FRONT**

Define **acid rain**.


**BACK**

**Acid rain** is precipitation that has become **acidic** because **sulfur dioxide** and **nitrogen oxides** react with water and oxygen in the atmosphere to form acids.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_65DcBz7gbkF2zDHK`)

**FRONT**

How does acid rain affect the **pH** of lakes and rivers?


**BACK**

Acid rain **lowers the pH** of lakes and rivers, causing **water acidification** that can leave them unable to support life.


Spec links: `spcpt_XzsWkvj5G697NzvX`

**FRONT**

Why does **acidification** of a lake lead to the death of larger fish?


**BACK**

Acidification kills the **microorganisms** at the base of the **food web**, so the food supply for larger fish dwindles.

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_3rM537vB2JnWN8kN`)

**FRONT**

How does acid rain affect **soil nutrients**?


**BACK**

Acid rain **lowers soil pH**, which **reduces the nutrients** in the soil and makes it harder for plants to absorb nutrients and grow.


Spec links: `spcpt_XzsWkvj5G697NzvX`


## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_QjsbpddtqTf9hbNK`)

**FRONT**

How does acid rain falling on **leaves** damage a plant?


**BACK**

Acid rain damages the leaves' **protective waxy coating**, which interrupts **water and gas exchange** and can eventually kill the plant.


Spec links: `spcpt_XzsWkvj5G697NzvX`

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_7C8BV9SfwgtPGpg3`)

**FRONT**

Why does acid rain damage **limestone** and **marble** buildings?


**BACK**

Limestone and marble contain **calcium carbonate**, which reacts with sulfuric or nitric acid, so the stone **corrodes and weakens**.

**BACK**

A **catalytic converter** is a device fitted to a vehicle's exhaust that uses **metal catalysts** to convert toxic gases, such as nitrogen oxides and carbon monoxide, into less toxic gases.
