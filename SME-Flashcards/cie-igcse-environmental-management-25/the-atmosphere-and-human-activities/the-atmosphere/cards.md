**FRONT**

**BACK**

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_gJhYVDdH8V6ysqQ7`)

**FRONT**

How does high **UV radiation** affect **phytoplankton**?


**BACK**

High UV radiation **damages DNA** and **slows photosynthesis** in phytoplankton, which reduces **primary production**.


Spec links: `spcpt_vnsJzV9bXYgKFzzH`


## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_xyhrCTqKq9dNhdgF`)

**FRONT**

What **health problems** can high UV radiation cause in humans?


**BACK**

High UV radiation can cause **skin cancer**, **cataracts**, **sunburn**, **premature skin ageing** and **damage to immune systems**.


Spec links: `spcpt_vnsJzV9bXYgKFzzH`
