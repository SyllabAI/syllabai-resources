## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_XRTc4YKjPVvpHFQp`)

**FRONT**

Define **ozone depletion**.


**BACK**

**Ozone depletion** is the gradual **thinning** of the Earth's **ozone layer**.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_C44dj8yZV2zPrTF3`)

**FRONT**

In which layer of the atmosphere is the **ozone layer** found?


**BACK**

The ozone layer is in the **stratosphere**, about 15-30 km above the Earth's surface.


Spec links: `spcpt_BHBGrGqz9nWsWnR3`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_Yr5zbcggHBR78pQT`)

**FRONT**

What does the **ozone layer** absorb?


**BACK**

The ozone layer absorbs most of the Sun's harmful **ultraviolet (UV) radiation** before it reaches the Earth's surface.


Spec links: `spcpt_BHBGrGqz9nWsWnR3`


## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_V3wJfJRRcr2BXcJ6`)

**FRONT**

Define **ozone-depleting substances**.


**BACK**

**Ozone-depleting substances** are chemicals, such as those containing **chlorine**, that break down ozone faster than natural processes can replace it.


Spec links: `spcpt_BHBGrGqz9nWsWnR3`

## Card 8 — true_or_false (`fl_cczYKXJCXFPjtcr2`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Rain washes ozone-depleting substances out of the lower atmosphere.


**BACK**

**False.**

Ozone-depleting substances are **stable** and **do not dissolve in rain**, so no natural process removes them from the lower atmosphere.

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_2T7XbmF49tYxxnyQ`)

**FRONT**

Why does ozone depletion over Antarctica speed up in **spring**?


**BACK**

Returning **sunlight** releases and reactivates the **chlorine** trapped in polar stratospheric clouds, which then rapidly destroys ozone.


Spec links: `spcpt_BHBGrGqz9nWsWnR3`


## Card 12 — true_or_false (`fl_yvvWxgcP2hjccg8q`)

Chlorine from swimming pools and sea salt is a major cause of ozone depletion.


**BACK**

**False.**

Chlorine from pools, factories, sea salt and volcanoes **does not reach the stratosphere**, so it does not deplete the ozone layer.

## Card 13 — true_or_false (`fl_hp45XQFCkPvq4b2P`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**False.**

A thinner ozone layer lets more **ultraviolet radiation** reach the surface, not more heat; ozone depletion and **global warming** are separate problems.
