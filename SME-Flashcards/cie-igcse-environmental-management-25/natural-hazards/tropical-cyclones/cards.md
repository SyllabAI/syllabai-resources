## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_Dpkm7RXCV7KrQ4zM`)

**FRONT**

Why must warm water extend to a **depth of 60 m**?


**BACK**

Deep warm water lets the storm **keep gaining energy** without **mixing with cooler water** from below.


Spec links: `spcpt_fn4RwcWVPKQqRK96`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_6mtjx35dd2JCMjm5`)

**FRONT**

How does **warm ocean water** strengthen a tropical cyclone?


**BACK**

Warm water **evaporates rapidly**, adding **heat and moisture** to the rising air; more evaporation releases more **latent heat**, which makes the cyclone stronger.


Spec links: `spcpt_fn4RwcWVPKQqRK96`

**BACK**

Tropical cyclones form when tropical oceans are **warmest**: **June-November** in the northern hemisphere and **November-April** in the southern hemisphere.
