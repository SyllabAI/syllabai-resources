# Drought

Course: cie-igcse-environmental-management-25 · Section: Natural Hazards

Source: https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/environmental-management/cie/25/flashcards/natural-hazards/drought/


## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_5dQTpYd4dv5dcmBM`)

**FRONT**

Define **drought**.


**BACK**

A **drought** is a period of dry weather that is **longer or more severe than normal** for a particular region.


Spec links: `spcpt_dQvRZqYt3vrrH5RS`


## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_SvhF2hYx6sXXpxnm`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A drought means a period with no rain at all.


**BACK**

**False.**

A drought means water levels have fallen **below what people, crops and ecosystems need**, not simply that no rain falls.


Spec links: `spcpt_dQvRZqYt3vrrH5RS`

**FRONT**

How do **high-pressure systems** cause drought?


**BACK**

High-pressure systems **block clouds** and prevent rainfall.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_tN7rHzDRNvp67spg`)

**FRONT**

How do **higher global temperatures** make droughts worse?


**BACK**

Higher temperatures increase **evaporation** from soil, plants and water bodies, so moisture is lost faster than rain can replace it.


Spec links: `spcpt_dQvRZqYt3vrrH5RS`


## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_dPtzg79fmVs3sm8q`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Climate change makes rainfall more reliable.


**BACK**

**False.**

Climate change brings **more extreme weather**, so rainfall becomes **unreliable**, with floods one year and long dry spells the next.


Spec links: `spcpt_dQvRZqYt3vrrH5RS`


## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_JVPQNh27SgstHwb5`)

**FRONT**

How do **warmer oceans** contribute to drought?


**BACK**

Warmer oceans **disrupt wind patterns and monsoons**, which can move rain-bearing winds away and reduce rainfall.

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_4KwzP7zvyW9mVCgR`)

**FRONT**

Why does drought lead to **soil erosion**?


**BACK**

Without vegetation, dry soil is **not anchored**, so wind blows it away and sudden rainstorms wash away topsoil.


Spec links: `spcpt_zj2NptgKNvbRjJMf`


## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_4z5vZF92QnnBHDtD`)

**FRONT**

Define **desertification**.


**BACK**

**Desertification** is the process by which long-term drought turns fertile land into **desert-like conditions**.

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_gnXtnfhBTkwbxPqP`)

**FRONT**

Why can drought cause **famine**?


**BACK**

**Crop failure** and **livestock losses** reduce the food supply, and food prices rise so vulnerable communities **cannot afford food**.

**FRONT**

How can a long drought affect **food chains**?


**BACK**
