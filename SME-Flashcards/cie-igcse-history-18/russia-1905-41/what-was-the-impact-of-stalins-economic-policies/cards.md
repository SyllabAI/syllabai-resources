## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_PpWzPNZnNDPZVXNk`)

**FRONT**

Define **Five-Year Plans**.


**BACK**

The **Five-Year Plans** were Stalin's economic programmes, introduced in 1928, that aimed to rapidly **industrialise** the USSR by setting economic targets over five-year intervals.
