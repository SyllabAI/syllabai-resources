E.g. $\begin{gathered} 2 - 5 + 7 - 3 - 2 \end{gathered}$

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_Xv2FBsjwWyRNfJ3T`)

**FRONT**

How can you perform a **division** by cancelling common factors in a** fraction**?

E.g. $1008\div28$


**BACK**

A division can be treated like a fraction where **common factors** from both the **numerator** and **denominator **can be found and **cancelled**.

E.g. $1008\div28$ can be written as $\frac{1008}{28}$

You can then cancel it to its simplest form by finding common factors, $\frac{1008}{28}=\frac{504}{14}=\frac{252}{7}=36$

## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_GNRkRwcVBhcqgtKW`)

**FRONT**

How can you use an** 'easier' calculation** to calculate a **decimal division problem**?

E.g**. **$2.4\div0.08$**?**


**BACK**

Turn the division into a **fraction**, e.g. $2.4\div0.08=\frac{2.4}{0.08}$

Multiply the values by a **multiple of 10** to change them to** integers**, 
e.g. $\frac{2.4\times10}{0.08\times100}=\frac{24}{8}$

**Simplify **the fraction (complete the easier calculation), e.g. $3$

**Convert the answer** by 'undoing' the initial changes, e.g. $\frac{3\times100}{10}=30$
