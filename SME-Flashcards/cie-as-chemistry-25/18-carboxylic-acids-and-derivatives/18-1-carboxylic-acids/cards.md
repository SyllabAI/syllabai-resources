## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_spBShYr6DmHxFBvc`)

**FRONT**

Define **carboxylic acid**.


**BACK**

A **carboxylic acid** is a compound containing the -COOH functional group. It is a weak acid that partially dissociates in water, giving a pH below 7.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_HgYgRK7GVzytPxzh`)

**FRONT**

Name three ways carboxylic acids can be produced.


**BACK**

1. **Oxidation** of primary alcohols or aldehydes with acidified K<sub>2</sub>Cr<sub>2</sub>O<sub>7</sub> or acidified KMnO<sub>4</sub> under reflux.
2. **Hydrolysis of nitriles** using dilute acid or dilute alkali followed by acidification.
3. **Hydrolysis of esters** using dilute acid or dilute alkali followed by acidification.

## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_9bj9JP3fSqdSj2pX`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Hydrolysis of a nitrile with dilute acid forms a carboxylic acid and an ammonium salt.


**BACK**

**True.**

Acid hydrolysis of a nitrile converts the -CN group to a -COOH group and also produces an ammonium salt.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_HQ3MmGdx4rnRBBCN`)

**FRONT**

What is observed and what gas is produced when a carboxylic acid reacts with a carbonate?


**BACK**

The carbonate **fizzes** and **carbon dioxide** gas is produced. The reaction is an acid-base reaction forming a salt and water alongside the CO<sub>2</sub>(g).

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_tVqJ9mV8xVGyRFR6`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

LiAlH<sub>4</sub> can reduce a carboxylic acid to a primary alcohol, but NaBH<sub>4</sub> cannot.


**BACK**

**True.**

LiAlH<sub>4</sub> is a stronger reducing agent than NaBH<sub>4</sub> and can reduce carboxylic acids to primary alcohols. NaBH<sub>4</sub> is not powerful enough for this reaction.

## Card 8 — keyword_definition (`fl_zHyPd4msh5wf5p8D`)

**FRONT**

Define **hydrolysis**.


**BACK**

A **hydrolysis** is the breakdown of a compound using water (or dilute acid/alkali), converting functional groups such as -CN or -COOR into -COOH.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_CNDdrpnzxfPWcmdY`)

**FRONT**

What type of reaction is esterification, and what are the reagents and conditions needed?


**BACK**

Esterification is a **condensation reaction** between a carboxylic acid and an alcohol, heated under reflux with a strong acid catalyst (H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> or H<sub>3</sub>PO<sub>4</sub>), producing an ester and water.
