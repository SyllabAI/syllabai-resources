Define **compound ion**.


**BACK**

**FRONT**

What is the formula of magnesium chloride? Explain how you determine it.


**BACK**

The formula is **MgCl**<sub>**2**</sub>. Magnesium (Group 2) has a 2+ charge and chlorine (Group 17) has a 1- charge. Two Cl<sup>-</sup> ions are needed to balance one Mg<sup>2+</sup> ion.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_dPdxgXKZZS7hbMmq`)

**FRONT**

Define **spectator ions**.


**BACK**

**Spectator ions** are ions present in solution that do not take part in a chemical reaction. They appear on both sides of the full ionic equation and are cancelled out when writing the net ionic equation.

## Card 14 — keyword_definition (`fl_gjcQQzfdMGdyDzwQ`)

**BACK**

## Card 19 — keyword_definition (`fl_xQSG2jHkykCkXsT4`)

**FRONT**

Define **empirical formula**.


**BACK**

An **empirical formula** is the simplest whole-number ratio of the elements present in one molecule or formula unit of a compound. For example, the empirical formula of ethanoic acid (C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>4</sub>O<sub>2</sub>) is CH<sub>2</sub>O.


Spec links: `spcpt_Js5fp3VP5t5SnnJY`


## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_9wm32gQ5dKY5kXyz`)

**FRONT**

How do you calculate the empirical formula from the masses of elements present in a compound?


**BACK**

Divide the mass of each element by its **relative atomic mass** to find **moles**. Then divide all values by the smallest to get the **simplest whole-number ratio**.
