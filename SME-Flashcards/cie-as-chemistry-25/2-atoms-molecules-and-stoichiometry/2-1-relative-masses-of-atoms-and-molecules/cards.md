## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_VGd8r9rHSR6bCcGS`)

**FRONT**

Define **unified atomic mass unit**.


**BACK**

A **unified atomic mass unit** is defined as one-twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. Its symbol is *u* (or Da, Dalton), and 1 *u* = 1.66 x 10<sup>-27</sup> kg.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_JwQVZBfzjtdMKQ4J`)

**FRONT**

How is the relative atomic mass (*A*<sub>r</sub>) of an element calculated from its isotopes?


**BACK**

Multiply each isotope's **mass number** by its **percentage abundance**, sum the results, then divide by 100. This gives the weighted average of the isotope masses.

## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_Fyv298WBfQBwGgQn`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Relative atomic mass has units of grams.


**BACK**

**False.**

Relative atomic mass is a ratio (mass of one atom relative to one-twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom), so the units cancel and it is dimensionless.

**BACK**

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_WCrbyTfVWBmpwdq4`)

**FRONT**

What is the relative molecular mass (*M*<sub>r</sub>) of water (H<sub>2</sub>O)? (A<sub>r</sub>: H = 1.0, O = 16.0)


**BACK**

*M*<sub>r</sub> = (2 x 1.0) + (1 x 16.0) = **18.0**. It is calculated by summing the relative atomic masses of all atoms in one molecule.

## Card 9 — keyword_definition (`fl_nTj8NMtWK4YjQGfd`)

**FRONT**

Define **relative molecular mass**.


**BACK**

**Relative molecular mass** (*M*<sub>r</sub>) is the ratio of the weighted average mass of a molecule of a molecular compound to the unified atomic mass unit. It has no units and is found by adding the relative atomic masses of all atoms in one molecule.
