## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_c2YWRjjFnPkbgVRn`)

**FRONT**

Define **the Avogadro constant**.


**BACK**

The **Avogadro constant** (*N*<sub>A</sub>) is 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup> mol<sup>-1</sup>. It is the number of particles (atoms, molecules, ions or electrons) in one mole of a substance.

## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_B9XxRGDXwR5RGXdp`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

One mole of any element has a mass equal to its relative atomic mass in grams.


**BACK**

**True.**

For example, one mole of sodium (A<sub>r</sub> = 23.0) has a mass of 23.0 g, and one mole of carbon (A<sub>r</sub> = 12.0) has a mass of 12.0 g.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_h8ggnSPsksVfkjGz`)

**FRONT**

How many molecules are in 1 mole of H<sub>2</sub>, and how many individual H atoms does this correspond to?


**BACK**

1 mole of H<sub>2</sub> contains **6.02 × 10**<sup>**23**</sup> molecules. Since each molecule has 2 H atoms, this corresponds to 2 × 6.02 × 10<sup>23</sup> = **1.204 × 10**<sup>**24**</sup> H atoms.

## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_hDnDkDrYVzTfd9w4`)

**FRONT**

Define **mole**.


**BACK**

A **mole** is the amount of substance that contains the same number of fundamental units as there are atoms in exactly 12.00 g of carbon-12. This number is 6.02 x 10<sup>23</sup>.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_rtQgpYqy5rQnHCv5`)

**FRONT**

What is the mass of 1 mole of NaCl? (A<sub>r</sub>: Na = 23.0, Cl = 35.5)


**BACK**

Mass = 23.0 + 35.5 = **58.5 g mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>. The molar mass is found by summing the relative atomic masses of all atoms in the formula unit.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_7sQcc4dFJvTYDwwF`)

**FRONT**

How many total atoms are in 1 mole of NaCl?


**BACK**

1 mole of NaCl contains **1.204 × 10**<sup>**24**</sup> atoms in total, because each formula unit has one Na atom and one Cl atom, giving 2 × 6.02 × 10<sup>23</sup> atoms.
