## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_yZbQdjdXdxjkhCDV`)

**FRONT**

Why does a beam of **electrons** deflect more than a beam of **protons** in the same electric field?


**BACK**

**Electrons** deflect more because their **mass** (~1/1836 that of a proton) is far smaller. The same electrostatic force produces a much greater acceleration on the lighter electron, causing stronger deflection.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_j34xGqgRTS6n85cR`)

**FRONT**

Why does the **nucleus** carry an overall **positive charge**?


**BACK**

The **nucleus** contains **protons** (relative charge +1) and **neutrons** (no charge). The positive charges of the protons are unopposed within the nucleus, giving it an overall **positive charge**.

**BACK**

**True.**

**Protons** and **neutrons** each have a relative mass of 1, while the **electron** mass (~1/1836) is negligible. Almost all the mass of an atom is therefore found in the **nucleus**.

**BACK**

An **atom** is the smallest particle of an element that can take part in a **chemical reaction**. It consists of a central **nucleus** containing **protons** and **neutrons**, surrounded by **electrons** in **shells**.

**BACK**

A **positive ion** forms when an atom **loses** one or more electrons. This leaves more **protons** than electrons, so the particle has more positive charge than negative charge, giving it an overall **positive charge**.

## Card 20 — keyword_definition (`fl_qZYQfrhCCYsqjvkb`)

**FRONT**

Define **atomic radius**.


**BACK**

An **atomic radius** is a measure of the size of an atom. It is defined as half the distance between the **nuclei** of two covalently bonded atoms of the **same element**.


Spec links: `spcpt_zb9mdZDfSD9sPHYQ`


## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_b5m5SXgTCC8SzvSp`)

**FRONT**

Why does **atomic radius decrease** across a period?


**BACK**

Across a period, **nuclear charge** increases as more protons are added, but electrons are added to the **same principal quantum shell**. The stronger nuclear pull draws electrons closer to the nucleus, decreasing the **atomic radius**.


Spec links: `spcpt_zb9mdZDfSD9sPHYQ`


## Card 22 — true_or_false (`fl_5s6dNh4YS67WWkDx`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Atomic **radius** increases across a period as more electrons are added to the outer **shell**.


**BACK**

**False.**

Atomic **radius decreases** across a period. Although electrons are added to the same shell, the increasing **nuclear charge** pulls electrons closer to the nucleus, reducing the radius.


Spec links: `spcpt_zb9mdZDfSD9sPHYQ`


## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_YCG8R83CFQc7w7YB`)

**FRONT**

Why does **atomic radius increase** down a group?


**BACK**

Moving down a group, each element has an additional **principal quantum shell**. Electrons in inner shells **shield** outer electrons from the nuclear charge, weakening the attraction and increasing the **atomic radius**.
