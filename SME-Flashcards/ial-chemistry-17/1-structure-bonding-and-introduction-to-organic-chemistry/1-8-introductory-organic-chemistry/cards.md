## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_ndvmNQrNxNSMqty4`)

**FRONT**

What four features do all members of a homologous series share?


**BACK**

All members of a **homologous series**:

1. Have the same functional group
2. Have the same general formula
3. Have similar chemical properties
4. Differ by CH<sub>2</sub> between successive members

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_yn73jwXpxZMdGGgm`)

**FRONT**

Why do members higher in a homologous series tend to have higher boiling points?


**BACK**

As molecules increase in size up the series, there are more electrons and greater **London dispersion forces** between molecules. More energy is required to overcome these forces, so the boiling point increases.

How does chain isomerism arise in organic molecules?

**Chain isomerism** arises through **branching** in the carbon skeleton. The same atoms are present but arranged so that the longest continuous carbon chain differs between isomers.
