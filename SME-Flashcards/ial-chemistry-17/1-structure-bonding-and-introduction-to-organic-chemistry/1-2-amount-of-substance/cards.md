## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_j9WvcBfmVFVSksYx`)

**FRONT**

Define: **concentration**


**BACK**

**Concentration** is the amount of a substance dissolved in a given volume of solution.

Molar concentration has units of mol dm<sup>-3</sup>. Mass concentration has units of g dm<sup>-3</sup>.


Spec links: `spcpt_9Hm8KN3TfCw8rfM6`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_tvB8vyCmJdgtdcfM`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for molar concentration?


**BACK**

**Molar concentration** (mol dm<sup>-3</sup>) = number of moles / volume of solution (dm<sup>3</sup>).

Equivalently: moles = concentration x volume.

## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_TFGWGZkDc277W52r`)

**FRONT**

What is **parts per million (ppm)** as a concentration unit?


**BACK**

**Parts per million (ppm)** is a unit for expressing very low concentrations. 1 ppm equals 1 mg of solute dissolved in 1 dm<sup>3</sup> (1 kg) of solution.

**FRONT**

How is the concentration of an atmospheric gas calculated in ppm?


**BACK**

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_bxKqJGsxWqkNSqpq`)

**FRONT**

What are the steps to calculate the mass of a product from the mass of a reactant?


**BACK**

1. Write the **balanced equation**.
2. Find the **molar mass** of each substance.
3. Convert the given mass to **moles**.
4. Use the **molar ratio** to find moles of the target substance.
5. Multiply moles by molar mass to get mass.

## Card 23 — keyword_definition (`fl_pTMXBnkqMcsbWz34`)

**FRONT**

Define **ideal gas**


**BACK**

An **ideal gas** is a theoretical gas whose molecules have negligible volume, no intermolecular forces, undergo elastic collisions, and whose temperature is proportional to average kinetic energy.


Spec links: `spcpt_7nwbSww24c4gCBct`


## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_38Gm4TVhgkrkN9km`)

**FRONT**

State the ideal gas equation and define each variable.


**BACK**

The **ideal gas equation** is *PV* = *nRT*, where:

*P* = pressure (Pa), *V* = volume (m<sup>3</sup>), *n* = moles of gas (mol), *R* = 8.31 J K<sup>-1</sup> mol<sup>-1</sup>, *T* = temperature (K).

## Card 31 — keyword_definition (`fl_kSRpyDdbTgjdMrJT`)

**FRONT**

Define **atom economy**


**BACK**

**Atom economy** is the molecular mass of the desired product expressed as a percentage of the total molecular mass of all reactants:

atom economy = (mass of desired product / sum of masses of all reactants) x 100
