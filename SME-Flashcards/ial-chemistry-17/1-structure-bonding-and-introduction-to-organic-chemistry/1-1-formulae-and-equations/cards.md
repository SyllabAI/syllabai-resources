Why do chemists use moles rather than counting individual particles?


**BACK**

Atoms and molecules are too small to count individually. The **mole** links the measurable mass of a substance to the number of particles it contains via the **Avogadro constant**, enabling stoichiometric calculations.

## Card 27 — question_and_answer (`fl_pK3BDvcSHxhXgvmF`)

**FRONT**

What is the method for calculating an **empirical formula** from mass data?


**BACK**

1. Find moles of each element: n = mass ÷ *A*<sub>r</sub>
2. Divide all values by the smallest to get a ratio
3. Scale up to whole numbers if needed

The ratio gives the **empirical formula**.
