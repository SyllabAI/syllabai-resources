## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_PKjmDQxx5Fq26crW`)

**FRONT**

Give two reasons why alkanes are relatively unreactive.


**BACK**

Alkanes are relatively unreactive because:

1. The C-C and C-H bonds have high **bond enthalpies**, requiring a lot of energy to break
2. The bonds have very low **polarity**, as carbon and hydrogen have similar electronegativities
