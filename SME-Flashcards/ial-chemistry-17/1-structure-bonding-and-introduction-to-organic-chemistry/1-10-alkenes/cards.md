## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_KWwbPy5gWNfrtbvQ`)

**FRONT**

Why do alkenes react in ways alkanes cannot?


**BACK**

Alkenes contain a C=C double bond, which is an area of high electron density. This acts as the **functional group** and makes alkenes susceptible to attack by electrophiles. Alkanes have no double bond and so cannot undergo these reactions.
