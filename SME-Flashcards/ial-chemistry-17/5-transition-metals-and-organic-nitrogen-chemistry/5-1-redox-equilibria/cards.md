## Card 60 — question_and_answer (`fl_BCpvFrwjdsSJvjqv`)

**FRONT**

A burette has an uncertainty of ±0.1 cm<sup>3</sup>. The initial reading is 0.0 cm<sup>3</sup> and the final reading is 5.0 cm<sup>3</sup>. What is the **percentage uncertainty** in the titre?


**BACK**

Total absolute uncertainty = 0.1 + 0.1 = 0.2 cm<sup>3</sup>

Percentage uncertainty = (0.2 / 5.0) × 100 = **4.0%**
