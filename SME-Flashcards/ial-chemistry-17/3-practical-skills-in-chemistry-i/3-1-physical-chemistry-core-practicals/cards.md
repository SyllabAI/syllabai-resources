## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_wDYXg7Tqmkm3G53k`)

**FRONT**

What equation is used to calculate the **enthalpy change** (ΔH) per mole of limiting reagent?


**BACK**

**ΔH = q ÷ n**, where q is the energy transferred (in kJ) and n is the moles of the **limiting reagent**. A temperature increase indicates an exothermic reaction, giving a negative ΔH.

## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_6ScHMpXsbbt32gz3`)

**FRONT**

State the key steps in performing a titration.


**BACK**

1. Measure a known volume (20 or 25 cm<sup>3</sup>) of one solution using a **volumetric pipette** and place in a conical flask
2. Place the other solution in the **burette** (usually filled to 0.00 cm<sup>3</sup>)
3. Add a few drops of **indicator** to the conical flask
4. Open the burette tap gradually, adding solution portion by portion
5. Near the end point, add solution **dropwise** until one drop causes a colour change
6. Repeat until **concordant** results are obtained


Spec links: `spcpt_cnCQY7KM54wRjbS5`
