## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_5BBm5TBwNzfMF5N8`)

**FRONT**

What is **hydrolysis** of a halogenoalkane?


**BACK**

**Hydrolysis** is a reaction in which the C-X bond in a halogenoalkane is broken by a nucleophile (OH<sup>-</sup> or water), replacing the halogen with a hydroxyl group.

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_TP7tjjZMTwjpNrTQ`)

**FRONT**

What are the key steps in the synthesis of 2-chloro-2-methylpropane from 2-methylpropan-2-ol?


**BACK**

1. Measure 8 cm<sup>3</sup> of 2-methylpropan-2-ol into a **separating funnel**
2. In a fume hood, add 20 cm<sup>3</sup> of **concentrated hydrochloric acid** in small portions, releasing pressure after each addition
3. Stand for 20 minutes in the fume hood, shaking every 2 minutes
4. Drain the lower aqueous layer
5. Add sodium hydrogen carbonate solution to remove acidic impurities, then drain the aqueous layer
6. Add **magnesium sulfate** to dry the organic layer, then distil and collect at 47–53°C


Spec links: `spcpt_4RvTH5SwvmH3GMjM`

## Card 25 — question_and_answer (`fl_XSKSPfmD9vjx8RGS`)

**FRONT**

State the key steps in the synthesis of propanal from propan-1-ol.


**BACK**

1. Add 20 cm<sup>3</sup> of acidified potassium dichromate(VI) solution to a pear-shaped flask and cool in an ice bath
2. Set up reflux apparatus, keeping flask cool
3. Add **anti-bumping granules**
4. Slowly add 1 cm<sup>3</sup> of propan-1-ol **dropwise** into the reflux condenser using a pipette
5. Remove the ice bath and allow to warm to room temperature
6. Heat for 20 minutes using an **electric heater or water bath**
7. Purify the product by **distillation**
