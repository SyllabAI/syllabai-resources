What is the oxidation number of nitrogen in the nitrite ion, NO<sub>2</sub><sup>–</sup>?


**BACK**

## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_WsPZkkbPk6pt5562`)

**FRONT**

What are the key steps to balance a disproportionation reaction?


**BACK**

1. Write the unbalanced equation and identify atoms that change **oxidation number**.
2. Deduce the **oxidation number changes**.
3. Balance the oxidation number changes using **coefficients**.
4. Balance the **charges**.
5. Balance the remaining **atoms**.
