**BACK**

Δ*H* represents the **enthalpy change**: the difference in chemical energy between the products and reactants.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_btWKyBqDNyFywpgf`)

**FRONT**

What is the sign of Δ*H* for an exothermic reaction, and why?


**BACK**

Δ*H* is **negative** because the products have less energy than the reactants, so the system loses energy to the surroundings.

## Card 28 — question_and_answer (`fl_tYH699CgMcXy2w5Z`)

**FRONT**

1.50 g of an organic liquid (*M*<sub>r</sub> = 58.0) is burned completely, heating 100 g of water from 20°C to 75°C. Calculate the enthalpy of combustion.


**BACK**

Step 1: *q* = 100 × 4.18 × 55 = 22 990 J = 22.99 kJ

Step 2: *n* = 1.50 / 58.0 = 0.0259 mol

Step 3: Δ*H* = -22.99 / 0.0259 = **-887 kJ mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>

The value is negative as combustion is **exothermic**.
