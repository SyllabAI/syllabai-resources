Define **Brønsted-Lowry acid**


**BACK**

A **Brønsted-Lowry acid** is a species that can **donate** a proton (H<sup>+</sup>) to another species.

For example, HCl donates H<sup>+</sup> to form Cl<sup>-</sup>.
