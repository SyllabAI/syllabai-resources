## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_jTbbgj3jvnxtdgHX`)

**FRONT**

Define **carboxyl group**


**BACK**

A **carboxyl group** is the functional group –COOH, consisting of a carbonyl (C=O) and a hydroxyl (O–H) attached to the same carbon atom. It is the defining feature of carboxylic acids.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_jZFDKFqd5yrGg7y5`)

**FRONT**

How does the solubility of carboxylic acids in water change as the hydrocarbon chain length increases?


**BACK**

**Solubility falls** as chain length increases. The longer hydrocarbon chain forces its way between water molecules, breaking **hydrogen bonds** between them, reducing overall solubility.
