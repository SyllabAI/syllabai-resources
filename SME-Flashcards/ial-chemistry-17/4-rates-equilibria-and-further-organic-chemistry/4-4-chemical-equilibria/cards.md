## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_4cSKHkKnmF4KcXF7`)

**FRONT**

Define **K****<sub>c</sub>**


**BACK**

**K**<sub>**c**</sub> is the equilibrium constant expressed in terms of **molar concentrations** at a given temperature.

For the reaction aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD:

K<sub>c</sub> = [C]<sup>c</sup>[D]<sup>d</sup> / [A]<sup>a</sup>[B]<sup>b</sup>

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_nWW4JQGsQQQBTBCz`)

**FRONT**

Which species are **excluded** from a K<sub>c</sub> expression and why?


**BACK**

**Solids** are excluded from K<sub>c</sub> expressions.

The concentration of a solid is constant and does not change, so it is incorporated into the value of K<sub>c</sub> rather than written separately.
