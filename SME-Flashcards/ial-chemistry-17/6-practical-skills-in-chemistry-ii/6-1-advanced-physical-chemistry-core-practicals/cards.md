## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_k7C4Sb8m42hVrjQh`)

**FRONT**

What is a **clock reaction** in chemistry?


**BACK**

A **clock reaction** is a reaction that shows a sharp, dramatic colour change after a period of time has elapsed. The sudden colour change marks the end of the reaction and acts as a timing device for kinetics studies.

## Card 28 — keyword_definition (`fl_9n4bzXCYcYW44chR`)

**FRONT**

What is the **half-equivalence point** in an acid–base titration?


**BACK**

The **half-equivalence point** is the point in a titration at which exactly half the acid has been neutralised. At this point, Ka = [H<sup>+</sup>], so pH = pKa.


Spec links: `spcpt_HTbFfy6Jh2wp2JfY`


## Card 29 — question_and_answer (`fl_BmMcnw7PsH7FQmn8`)

**FRONT**

Why does Ka equal [H<sup>+</sup>] at the half-equivalence point?


**BACK**

At the half-equivalence point, the concentrations of the weak acid and its conjugate base are **equal**, so they cancel in the Ka expression, leaving **Ka = [H**<sup>**+**</sup>**]**.
