## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_kjnVd7HfpnrypBRJ`)

**FRONT**

What is a **redox titration**?


**BACK**

A **redox titration** is a titration in which an oxidising agent reacts with a reducing agent, with electrons transferred between species. The concentration of one species is determined from the known concentration of the other.

**FRONT**

Why is dilute sulfuric acid used (rather than HCl or HNO<sub>3</sub>) in the KMnO<sub>4</sub> titration?

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_pwwbjBMQGBQShQpS`)

Percentage by mass = (0.1436/0.960) × 100 = **15.0%**

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_H4ZPMzqPrcXNTTxP`)

**FRONT**

What is an **iodometric titration**?


**BACK**

An **iodometric titration** is a redox titration in which an oxidising agent liberates iodine from iodide ions, and the iodine produced is then titrated against a known concentration of sodium thiosulfate solution.
