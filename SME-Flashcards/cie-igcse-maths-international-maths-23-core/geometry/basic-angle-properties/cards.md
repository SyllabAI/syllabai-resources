## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_nHQFcWC3tyHv6HD7`)

**FRONT**

What are **vertically opposite** angles, and what is true of them?


**BACK**

They are the pairs of angles **facing each other** where two straight lines cross.

Vertically opposite angles are always **equal**.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_BkGjjZxrsQP7VM2s`)

**FRONT**

Two of the angles on a straight line are 25° and 98°. What is the third angle on that line?


**BACK**

The third angle is **57°**.

The three angles must total 180°, and $180 - 25 - 98 = 57$.

## Card 5 — true_or_false (`fl_YBfG6ZngtC7vnRxQ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

When two straight lines cross, all four of the angles formed are equal.


**BACK**

**False.**

Only the pairs facing each other are equal.

Angles that sit next to each other are on a straight line, so they add to 180° instead, and they are equal only in the special case where every angle is 90°.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_DRwgf3vzfJNcd5Pz`)

**FRONT**

In an **isosceles** triangle, which two angles are the equal ones?


**BACK**

The two angles **opposite the two sides of equal length**.

It is the position relative to the equal sides that decides it, not where the triangle happens to be drawn on the page.

## Card 9 — true_or_false (`fl_bHmRv4TWcwnQBtfs`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A right-angled triangle can also be isosceles.


**BACK**

**True.**

A triangle with angles of 90°, 45° and 45° is both.

The names describe different things, one the presence of a right angle and the other a pair of equal sides, so a triangle can satisfy both at once.

**FRONT**

## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_tHCXDQdz6Mw3vb7Y`)

**FRONT**

In a **parallelogram** or a **rhombus**, which angles are equal?


**BACK**

The **opposite** angles are equal, giving **two pairs** of equal angles.

The angles next to each other are not equal unless the shape happens to be a rectangle or a square.


Spec links: `spcpt_QTbSSjMfdVzY3kb3`


## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_YW6n4BN64C27kPXP`)

**FRONT**

How many pairs of equal opposite angles does a **kite** have?


**BACK**

Just **one** pair.

The two angles between the pairs of equal sides are the equal ones, and the other two angles are generally different from each other.

## Card 16 — true_or_false (`fl_83qTNNb6Dxfnt3vB`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Every quadrilateral has at least one pair of equal angles.


**BACK**

**False.**

An irregular quadrilateral can have four angles that are all different from one another.

Equal angles come from a shape's symmetry, and an irregular quadrilateral has none.
