## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_mbhG8NhB7BYVxkcJ`)

**FRONT**

Define a **term** in algebra.


**BACK**

A **term** is a single part of an expression: a letter on its own, a letter raised to a power, a number on its own, or a number multiplied by one or more letters.

Examples are $x$, $4 x^{2}$, $20$ and $6 x y$.

## Card 9 — keyword_definition (`fl_zKywR9MscdBtxghw`)

**FRONT**

Define an algebraic **expression**.


**BACK**

An **expression** is an algebraic statement made by combining terms with $+$, $-$, $\times$ or $\div$, and it has **no equals sign**.

There is therefore nothing to solve, though an expression can often be **simplified**: $x + x + x$ simplifies to $3 x$.

## Card 11 — keyword_definition (`fl_4z88RSXnFhMm8CWk`)

**FRONT**

Define an **equation**.


**BACK**

An **equation** is an algebraic statement with an **equals sign**, where the left-hand side and the right-hand side have the same value.

An equation can be **solved**: the value of the letter that makes both sides equal is called the **solution**, so $2 x = 10$ has the solution $x = 5$.

## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_W94kJRxVHg4qwqtw`)

**FRONT**

Define a **formula**.


**BACK**

A **formula** is a rule or relationship between different quantities, written in shorthand using **letters**.

For example, weight $w$ is mass $m$ multiplied by gravitational acceleration $g$, which is written as the formula $w = m g$.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_dWTX5MfymNkFSYVq`)

**FRONT**

What does **substitution** mean in algebra?


**BACK**

**Substitution **in algebra** **is the process of replacing letters in a formula with their corresponding numerical values (inputs) in order to calculate an output of the formula.

E.g. If $v=3$ and $a=-5$, substituting them into the formula $a^{2}+2v=t$ would give `open parentheses negative 5 close parentheses plus 2 cross times 3 equals t`.



## Card 15 — true_or_false (`fl_Mw25bDqJF8wNPHc5`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

When substituting in the number $g=-3$ you should replace $g$* *with `open parentheses negative 3 close parentheses`, not $-3$.


**BACK**

**True.**

It is important to put brackets around any **negative** numbers that you substitute into an algebraic expression.

E.g. Substitute $g=-3$ into the expression  $4g^{2}$.

If you use brackets it will give you the correct answer `4 cross times open parentheses negative 3 close parentheses squared equals 4 cross times 9 equals 36`.

If you don't use brackets it will give an incorrect answer $4\times-3^{2}=4\times-9=-36$.

You can **rearrange **a formula first before substituting numbers in.
