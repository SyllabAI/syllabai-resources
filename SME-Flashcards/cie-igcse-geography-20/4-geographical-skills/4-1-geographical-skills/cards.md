## Card 1 — true_or_false (`fl_nsfwq5dtWwb9H4NH`)

**FRONT**

**True or False? **

A 4-figure grid reference locates an **exact point** within a grid square.


**BACK**

**False.**

**BACK**

**Eastings **are the horizontal grid lines on a map that indicate how far east or west a location is across the map.

## Card 18 — true_or_false (`fl_DBsBj8dn7ggw7FJF`)

**FRONT**

**True or False? **

The median is the most common value in a dataset.


**BACK**

**False. **

The median is the middle value when the data is ordered by size. The most common value is the mode.



## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_xdQxTvCR5bgRzSc6`)

**FRONT**

What are **mean, median, mode and range**?


**BACK**

- The **mean **is the average. The values of all the  items are added together and then divided by the number of items.
- The **median **is the middle value when ordered by size.
- The **mode **is the most common value.
- The **range **is the difference between the highest value and the lowest value.



## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_8Bks6sV5vnjPqpXY`)

**FRONT**

How do you calculate the **percentage change**?


**BACK**

A **percentage change** is calculated using the following equation:

$Percentagechange=\frac{finalvalue-originalvalue}{originalvalue}\times100$

A percentage change shows how much something has either increased or decreased.
