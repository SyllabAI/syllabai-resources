# Benefits of using a Local Area Network (LAN)

Course: igcse-ict-17 · Section: 2. Connectivity

Source: https://www.savemyexams.com/igcse/ict/edexcel/17/flashcards/2-connectivity/benefits-of-using-a-local-area-network-lan/


## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_jRVfCFpjKxRdRxh4`)

**FRONT**

Give four benefits of using a local area network.


**BACK**

Users can **share peripherals** such as printers, have **shared access to the internet**, **access their files from any computer** on the network, **communicate more easily**, **share files and folders**, use servers for **centralised backups, updates and security**, and use **media streaming**.

## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_c5bM6nCd657Zf7gb`)

**FRONT**

Define **peer-to-peer network**.


**BACK**

A peer-to-peer network is a network with **no server providing services**, where every computer is **equal to all other computers**.


Spec links: `spcpt_2HbsmYmcTnGbZ2hH`


## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_BykkMHzPMwmRx3HD`)

**FRONT**

Name three things each peer on a peer-to-peer network is responsible for.


**BACK**

Each peer **provides access to its own files** and is responsible for its **own backups** and its **own security**, and often has its **own printer attached**.


Spec links: `spcpt_2HbsmYmcTnGbZ2hH`
