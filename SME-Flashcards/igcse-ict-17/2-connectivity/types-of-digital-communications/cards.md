## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_6fqFg5J6jq483dny`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**Wi-Fi** connects devices to a LAN, **Bluetooth** connects two devices over very short distances, **infra-red (IR)** transmits tiny amounts of data over very short distances, **NFC** uses close proximity RFID, and **4G and 5G** provide internet access to mobile devices.

## Card 14 — keyword_definition (`fl_kjB7N6pXS4PhvMyW`)

**FRONT**

Define **local area network (LAN)**.


**BACK**

A local area network is a network covering a **small geographical area** (under 1 mile), where **all of the hardware is owned by the company, organisation or household using it**.


Spec links: `spcpt_NSVdWYsb722tS6v9`
