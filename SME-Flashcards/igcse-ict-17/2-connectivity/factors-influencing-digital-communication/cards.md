## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_zwF88GgCtSKfZnR7`)

**FRONT**

Define **buffer**.


**BACK**

A buffer is a **temporary area of memory** holding enough downloaded data to play a few seconds of streamed content.


Spec links: `spcpt_8jgDC388shK6gc5k`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_ryhmH4vCGCXpvNGM`)

**FRONT**

What condition must be met for content to stream successfully?


**BACK**

Data must be downloaded to the buffer **at a faster rate than it is being emptied**, otherwise the buffer empties and **playback will pause**.


Spec links: `spcpt_8jgDC388shK6gc5k`


## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_vrMkz7vZWpKhWJ2m`)

**FRONT**

Why does a large delay between data being sent and received increase the error rate?


**BACK**

A big delay means **more data will be on the network causing collisions**, which leads to **even more packets being sent** as the error rate increases.


Spec links: `spcpt_8jgDC388shK6gc5k`


## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_38JwZbWhfZBHYQD4`)

**FRONT**

How does high latency affect a user playing a fast paced online game?


**BACK**

High latency causes **lag**, so the game does **not respond as quickly** to the user's commands, putting them at a disadvantage against players with lower latency.


Spec links: `spcpt_8jgDC388shK6gc5k`


## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_zBr9XJBBZhYqXWxW`)

**FRONT**

How does high latency affect a video call?


**BACK**

It **increases the time it takes for data to be transferred between devices**, so voice and video become **out of sync, lagging or delayed**.


Spec links: `spcpt_8jgDC388shK6gc5k`

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_SJMYXknxX48RpXQK`)

**FRONT**

Why does distance reduce the speed of a data transfer?


**BACK**

The **strength of both wired and wireless signals reduces** as data has to travel further, so **more packets have to be requested again**.

A wireless connection can carry more frequencies than a wired one, giving it a higher bandwidth.


**BACK**

## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_qf8PWDnty3jnFcdT`)

**FRONT**

Compare wired and wireless connections on security and portability.


**BACK**

Wired has **better physical security** but its location is **limited by the physical cable**, while wireless is **less secure and easier to intercept** but its location is **only limited by range**.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_8RjvPSxchGKCC8rJ`)

**FRONT**

Give three advantages of fibre optic over copper cable.


**BACK**

Fibre transmits data at a **much higher speed** with a **much higher bandwidth**, **does not suffer from interference** so is the most secure option for sensitive data, and can **cover long distances without degradation**.


Spec links: `spcpt_gmnGrdRsfntWyPg3`


## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_cY35pKMtZxTWVrm4`)

**FRONT**

Give two drawbacks of copper cable.


**BACK**

Copper **degrades over time**, which limits its range, and it **suffers from interference**, which can disrupt data quality; it is also **slower with lower bandwidth** than fibre.
