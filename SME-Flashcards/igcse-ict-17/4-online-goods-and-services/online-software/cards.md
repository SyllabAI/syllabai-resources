## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_p8tgR453MXHG6W4y`)

**FRONT**

What is the key difference between accessing data locally and through the cloud?


**BACK**

Data and software accessed locally are **confined to the resources of the specific device**, while cloud access **relies on an internet connection**.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_yvCHk8Pss9NMPRcG`)

**FRONT**

Give four advantages of hosted software.


**BACK**

**Access from different types of device**, the ability to **save files online** when combined with online storage, features that **enable collaboration**, **updates and security managed by someone else**, and it is **cost effective** through pay monthly plans.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_STgdZdhwFsNmjQKM`)

**FRONT**

Give four disadvantages of hosted software.


**BACK**

It **can only be accessed with an internet connection**, the user experience is affected by **high latency and low bandwidth**, it has **not as many features** as locally installed software, there are **privacy concerns** over who has access to your data, and **hidden fees can add up**.

## Card 7 — keyword_definition (`fl_nJzDcMTjq9GdRXBs`)

**FRONT**

Define **local software**.


**BACK**

Local software is software **installed directly onto a device's secondary storage** that **runs from that device without needing an internet connection**.


Spec links: `spcpt_j3yvC6JKFns7mWX3`

## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_5jjN3mc56xwcwJgg`)

**FRONT**

Define **online storage**.


**BACK**

Online storage is **long-term secondary storage of data that resides in a remote location**, accessible only **via a wide area network**.


Spec links: `spcpt_TWVNQhkNkz5Mn6nJ`


## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_K8Shfx4KbVgDrn79`)

**FRONT**

Name the three types of online storage.


**BACK**

**Public cloud**, where customer and provider are different companies; **private cloud**, where they are a single organisation; and **hybrid cloud**, which combines both.


Spec links: `spcpt_TWVNQhkNkz5Mn6nJ`


## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_CTW5YyGvXj9fcZBZ`)

**FRONT**

What problem does a hybrid cloud solve?


**BACK**

It allows **sensitive data to remain private** while providing **public cloud services for less sensitive information**.


Spec links: `spcpt_TWVNQhkNkz5Mn6nJ`

Data can be **accessed from anywhere and on any device**, it can be **shared with anyone holding permissions**, capacity can be **increased or decreased as needed**, providers use **multiple servers to back data up**, they offer **advanced security features**, and there is **no need to hire specialist staff**.

## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_23p5pWZvRJhC2h2P`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

A **stable internet connection is required**, data may be **vulnerable to security breaches**, the user is **dependent on the provider** for availability and reliability, **all data may be lost if the company ceases to exist**, and the service may become **expensive over time**.

## Card 18 — true_or_false (`fl_93cv8VKkN97XvJwx`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Backing up a large music library to cloud storage is quick and free.


**BACK**

**False.**

**Uploads take a long time** because bandwidth is limited and file sizes are large, and there is an **increased cost** since data is often charged per megabyte used.
