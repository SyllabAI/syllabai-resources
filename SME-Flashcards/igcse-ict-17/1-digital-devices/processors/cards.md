**BACK**

The purpose of the Central Processing Unit is to **execute instructions**, which it achieves by completing **processor cycles**.

**FRONT**

Name the three stages of the processor cycle.


**BACK**

The three stages are **fetch**, **decode** and **execute**.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_CtHXwMWmTHVFBZw5`)

**FRONT**

What happens during the fetch stage of the processor cycle?


**BACK**

The next instruction or data is **fetched from the computer's memory (RAM)** and brought back to the CPU.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_RJNyFqjRYdknyysw`)

**FRONT**

What happens during the decode stage of the processor cycle?


**BACK**

The CPU **works out what is required** from the instruction.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_T6vp74f5FXC7yM8M`)

**FRONT**

Give two things the CPU might do during the execute stage.


**BACK**

The CPU **carries out the instruction**, which could mean **performing a calculation**, **storing a result back in main memory**, or **fetching data from a different location** in main memory.

## Card 6 — keyword_definition (`fl_cDrFSHgB3QPq2kMs`)

**FRONT**

Define **clock speed**.


**BACK**

Clock speed is the measure of a processor's speed, giving the **number of processor cycles that can take place in 1 second**.

**FRONT**

How many instructions per second can a clock speed of 3.5 GHz perform?


**BACK**

A clock speed of 3.5 GHz can perform up to **3.5 billion instructions per second**, because a gigahertz means a billion cycles per second.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_xCp6xJxnQh8XFPxt`)

**FRONT**

What is a core?


**BACK**

A core **works like it is its own CPU**, so a multi-core processor has **multiple separate processing units** that can fetch, decode and execute instructions at the same time.


Spec links: `spcpt_g6Rmv5pwnqxDRVnW`

**BACK**

4 cores × 3 GHz gives **12 billion instructions per second**.

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A slower clock speed allows more instructions to be fetched and executed per second.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **faster** the clock speed, the **more instructions** can be fetched and executed per second.

## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_rc29XzRKs7Qr9d7b`)

**FRONT**

How does a faster processor improve a user's experience when playing a game?


**BACK**

A faster processor **fetches, decodes and executes more instructions per second**, so **graphics render more quickly and at greater resolution**, making the visuals **smoother** and the environment **more realistic**, and allowing **more actions** to be carried out.
