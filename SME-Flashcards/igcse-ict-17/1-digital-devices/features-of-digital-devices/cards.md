**BACK**

## Card 12 — true_or_false (`fl_hCjS8zFk46Nx4vFm`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A smartphone has higher performance and greater expansion capability than a desktop computer.


**BACK**

**False.**

A smartphone rates **low** on performance and expansion capability, while a desktop rates **high** on both; the smartphone's advantage is **portability**.

## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_ZBs64ncgVJZqwXNf`)

**FRONT**

How does the connectivity of a desktop compare with that of a smartphone?


**BACK**

A desktop offers **multiple ports in a wide variety** (such as USB and HDMI), while a smartphone connects **wirelessly** using Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
