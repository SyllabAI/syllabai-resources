## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_9bqP3fS9prRbKvk8`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between a Braille display and a Braille printer?


**BACK**

A **Braille display** enables visually impaired users to **read digital text**, while a **Braille printer** prints a **hard copy** of a document in Braille.
