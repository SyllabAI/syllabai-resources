## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_v5VbTdKFhfqzWj35`)

**FRONT**

Define **real GDP per capita**.


**BACK**

Real GDP per capita is the **total value of goods and services produced in an economy, adjusted for inflation, divided by the population size**. It shows the average wealth per person and is useful for comparing living standards between countries.

**FRONT**

**BACK**

The main advantage of using **real GDP per capita** is that it allows for a **fairer comparison of living standards between countries** by adjusting for both population size and inflation.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_2hQXqbWTkkppBNC2`)

**FRONT**

Describe one disadvantage of using **real GDP per capita** as a measure of living standards.


**BACK**
