## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_zwPD9pQTFcbjrcxr`)

**FRONT**

What is **overpopulation**?


**BACK**

**Overpopulation** occurs when there are more people in a country or region than can be supported by its **resources and technology**, leading to a fall in the standard of living.

**BACK**
