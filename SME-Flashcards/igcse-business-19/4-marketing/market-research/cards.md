## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_ngttX6DD4DCDTGC3`)

**FRONT**

Define **market research**.


**BACK**

**Market research** is the process of **systematically gathering data from consumers**, used to **influence business decisions**.

**BACK**

To **identify customer needs and wants**, **recognise gaps in the market**, **reduce risk**, and **investigate competitors**.
