## Card 36 — question_and_answer (`fl_jPCY6bnZJ2TbNjqq`)

**FRONT**

How would you use the fact that $9\times15=135$ to work out $900\times150?$


**BACK**

You could write $900\times150$ as $9\times10\times10\times15\times10$.

Then bring the 9 and 15 together, giving  $9\times15\times10\times10\times10$.

You know that $9\times15=135$ so the number above becomes $135\times10\times10\times10$.

Multiplying by ten each time adds an extra zero, so the answer is $135000$.



## Card 37 — true_or_false (`fl_vKDwtfZtjjwcvBTW`)

**FRONT**

**True or false?**

If $3\times2=6$ then $0.3\times0.2=0.6$.


**BACK**

**False.**

$0.3\times0.2$ is the same as `open parentheses 3 divided by 10 close parentheses cross times open parentheses 2 divided by 10 close parentheses`.

This is also the same as `open parentheses 3 cross times 2 close parentheses divided by open parentheses 10 cross times 10 close parentheses`.

Alternatively, you can use fractions to see this: $\frac{3}{10}\times\frac{2}{10}=\frac{6}{100}$.

The correct answer is $0.3\times0.2=0.06$.
