## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_yVy5bGhBsHqct2t2`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for orbital speed, and what does each symbol represent?


**BACK**

$v = \frac{2πr}{T}$

Where *v* = orbital speed (m/s), *r* = average orbital radius (m), *T* = orbital period (s).
