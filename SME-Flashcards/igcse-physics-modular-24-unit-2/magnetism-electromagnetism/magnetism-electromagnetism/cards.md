**BACK**

## Card 18 — keyword_definition (`fl_477HQrHM9638Scy3`)

**FRONT**

Define **induced magnetism**.


**BACK**

**Induced magnetism** is the temporary magnetism produced in a magnetic material when it is placed in a magnetic field; the material loses most or all of its magnetism quickly once removed from the field.


Spec links: `spcpt_qwrQbzyxGk5BhDwF`


## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_tDr9fjMCmth9HZTP`)

**FRONT**

How can you test whether an unknown sample is itself a magnet, rather than just a magnetic material?


**BACK**

Bring it close to a known magnet. If it is **repelled**, it is a magnet. If it can only be **attracted** (never repelled), it is a magnetic material but not a magnet.


Spec links: `spcpt_qwrQbzyxGk5BhDwF`
