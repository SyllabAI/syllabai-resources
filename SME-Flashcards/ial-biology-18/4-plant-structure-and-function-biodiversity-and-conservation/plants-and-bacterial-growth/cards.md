## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_KWqgdvdtYNqmcKXF`)

**FRONT**

What happens during the three phases of testing a new drug on humans?


**BACK**

**Phase 1:** the drug is tested on a **small group of healthy volunteers** to check safety, side effects, and dosage.

**Phase 2:** the drug is tested on a **larger group of patients** to assess how well it works.

**Phase 3:** the drug is compared against **existing treatments** in a large trial, often using **double blind** methods.
