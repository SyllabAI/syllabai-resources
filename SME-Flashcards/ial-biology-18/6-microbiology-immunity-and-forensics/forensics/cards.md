## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_rrhvMqdqZddm57zY`)

**FRONT**

Define **annealing**.


**BACK**

**Annealing** is the PCR stage where the mixture is cooled to about **55 °C** so that **primers bind** to their complementary sequences on each separated DNA strand.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_hhwNssQJCWfSbGG8`)

**FRONT**

What are the three stages of each PCR cycle?


**BACK**

**Denaturation** (about 95 °C — the two DNA strands separate), **annealing** (about 55 °C — primers bind), and **extension** (about 72 °C — DNA polymerase builds new complementary strands).
