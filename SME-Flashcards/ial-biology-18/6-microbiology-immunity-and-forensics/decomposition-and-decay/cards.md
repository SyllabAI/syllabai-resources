**FRONT**

Define **decomposers**.


**BACK**

Decomposers are **microorganisms such as bacteria and fungi** that break down dead plants and animals.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_jnNfF43Z8DyVFHgF`)

**FRONT**

What is the role of **decomposers** in recycling nutrients?


**BACK**

Decomposers **break down dead plants and animals** and release nutrients that can be reused by living organisms.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_Y659HF97sKwkpQFc`)

**FRONT**

How is carbon dioxide absorbed and reused in the carbon cycle?


**BACK**

Carbon dioxide is **absorbed by green plants** and the carbon is fixed back into carbohydrates during **photosynthesis**.
