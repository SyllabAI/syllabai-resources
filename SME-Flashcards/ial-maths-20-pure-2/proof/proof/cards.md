**FRONT**

What is the difference between $=$ and $\equiv$?


**BACK**

$=$ means the two sides are **equal for some values**.

$\equiv$ means the two sides are **identical**, so they are equal for **all values**.

## Card 5 — true_or_false (`fl_TKCPq2746h93mjDr`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

$P \Leftrightarrow Q$ means that Q is true if and only if P is true.


**BACK**

**True.**

$P \Leftrightarrow Q$ means P and Q are **equivalent**, so each one being true forces the other to be true. This is stronger than $P \Rightarrow Q$, which only works in **one direction**.

**FRONT**

Why is it useful to know that the square of any real number is $\geq 0$?


**BACK**

Multiplying or dividing an inequality by a **positive** number keeps the inequality sign the **same way round**. Since a square is never negative, a **non-zero** squared term is guaranteed to be positive.
