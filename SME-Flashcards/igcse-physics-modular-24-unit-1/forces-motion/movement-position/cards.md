## Card 49 — question_and_answer (`fl_kvtKqmkfpjfpTN4Q`)

**FRONT**

A car accelerates uniformly from rest at 2.5 m/s<sup>2</sup> up to a speed of 16 m/s. Calculate the distance it travels while accelerating.


**BACK**

$v^{2} = u^{2} + 2 a s$
