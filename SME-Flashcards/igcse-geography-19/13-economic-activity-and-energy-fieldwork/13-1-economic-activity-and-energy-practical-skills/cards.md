## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_77kzVK2CQGdbNrgb`)

**FRONT**

Define **stratified sampling** for a questionnaire.


**BACK**

Sampling where the population selected is **representative of the whole** population, e.g. if 10% are over 65, then 10% of the sample is over 65.
