## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_CfPHQR3XtPxtDxBQ`)

**FRONT**

Describe the effect of temperature on the rate of respiration.


**BACK**

As temperature increases, the rate of respiration increases because the enzymes work faster.

Above the optimum temperature, the enzymes denature and the rate falls.

## Card 17 — question_and_answer (`fl_qpbc6JXmKtRT9ghV`)

**FRONT**

What is oxygen debt (Extended)?


**BACK**

During hard exercise, muscles respire anaerobically and lactic acid builds up.

Extra oxygen is needed afterwards to break down the lactic acid.

This extra oxygen is called the oxygen debt.
