## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_MJy5rYkJXMd2fKGX`)

**FRONT**

Why must Benedict's test be heated, but the iodine and Biuret tests are not?


**BACK**

Benedict's solution only reacts with reducing sugars when heated in a water bath.

The iodine (starch) and Biuret (protein) tests work at room temperature.
