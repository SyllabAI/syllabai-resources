## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_WcgNjj3f2zRSK4xZ`)

**FRONT**

Describe human pressures that threaten other species.


**BACK**

Human pressures that threaten other species include:

- **habitat destruction** (e.g. for farming or building)
- **pollution**
- **hunting** and **overharvesting**
- introduction of new (**invasive**) species

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_bP37XZQFHKKsfXV3`)

**FRONT**

Explain the undesirable effects of deforestation. **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The undesirable effects of deforestation include:

- loss of **habitat** and reduced **biodiversity**, which can lead to **extinction** of species
- **increased carbon dioxide** in the atmosphere, as fewer trees remove it by **photosynthesis** and burning timber releases it
- **soil erosion**, as roots no longer bind the soil
- **flooding**, as less water is absorbed and soil is washed away

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_2TRj22FKzQBNPcwb`)

**FRONT**

Give three reasons why humans destroy natural habitats.


**BACK**

Habitats are destroyed to make space for human activities, including:

- clearing land for **farming** (e.g. crops or grazing cattle)
- **building** to house a growing human **population**
- **pollution** from human economic activities

## Card 8 — true_or_false (`fl_ywM7sKvPTzjqsKSB`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Deforestation increases the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere.


**BACK**

**True.**

With fewer trees, less **carbon dioxide** is removed by **photosynthesis**, and burning the felled timber releases even more carbon dioxide.
