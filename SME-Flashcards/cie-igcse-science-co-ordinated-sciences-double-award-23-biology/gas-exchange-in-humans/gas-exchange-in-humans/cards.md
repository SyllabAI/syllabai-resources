What features do efficient gas exchange surfaces share (Extended)?


**BACK**

Thin walls (one cell thick) for a short diffusion distance.

A good blood supply to maintain a steep concentration gradient.

Good ventilation to maintain the concentration gradient.

**FRONT**

Outline how to investigate the effect of physical activity on breathing.


**BACK**

To investigate the effect of physical activity on breathing:

- count the **breathing rate** (breaths per minute) at rest
- **exercise** for a set period of time
- count the **breathing rate** immediately after exercising
- **repeat** and calculate a **mean**
