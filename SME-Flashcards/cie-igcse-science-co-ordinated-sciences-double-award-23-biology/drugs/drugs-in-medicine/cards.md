**BACK**

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_NkshR2dvHKWsTZSc`)

**FRONT**

Why can't antibiotics be used to treat viral infections?


**BACK**

Antibiotics only work on bacteria.

Viruses reproduce inside the body's own cells, so they cannot be targeted without harming the cells.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_YZvJmPVh5cFJcgzP`)

**FRONT**

How does antibiotic resistance develop (Extended)?


**BACK**

Random mutations make some bacteria resistant to the antibiotic.

When the antibiotic is used, non-resistant bacteria are killed but resistant ones survive.

The resistant bacteria reproduce and pass on the resistance, so the resistant population grows.


Spec links: `spcpt_x3RVPVdsBsxVHphB`


## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_9Sh6mctgCMfJRrQp`)

**FRONT**

How can the development of antibiotic resistance be reduced (Extended)?


**BACK**

Only use antibiotics when they are really needed (for example, not for viral infections), so that resistant bacteria are less likely to develop and spread.
