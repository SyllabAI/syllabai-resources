**BACK**

## Card 4 — true_or_false (`fl_RX2PVhH4gJwWg2YK`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Carbon monoxide is produced by the complete combustion of carbon-containing fuels.


**BACK**

**False.**

Carbon monoxide is produced by the **incomplete** combustion of carbon-containing fuels when there is insufficient oxygen. **Complete** combustion produces carbon dioxide.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_DSQDKbsJfXbpH3Jq`)

**FRONT**

Why is carbon monoxide dangerous to humans?


**BACK**

Carbon monoxide is a **toxic gas** that combines with haemoglobin in the blood. This prevents haemoglobin from carrying oxygen around the body, which can be fatal.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_SkbRJMpW4x2SbwYf`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between complete and incomplete combustion of a hydrocarbon fuel?


**BACK**

**Complete combustion** occurs in excess oxygen and produces carbon dioxide and water only. **Incomplete combustion** occurs in oxygen-deficient conditions and produces carbon monoxide, water, and sometimes carbon particulates (soot).

**BACK**

## Card 11 — keyword_definition (`fl_WyrMnqwxvWtcbcXx`)

**FRONT**

Define the **greenhouse effect**.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The **greenhouse effect** is the process by which greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane absorb thermal energy re-emitted from the Earth's surface and re-emit it in all directions, reducing the energy lost to space and keeping the Earth warm.


Spec links: `spcpt_b8kXGMmB5sMPg7Tw`


## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_QcQZdc8XjdmRxYqd`)

**FRONT**

How do greenhouse gases reduce the loss of thermal energy to space?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

Greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane **absorb** thermal energy re-emitted from the Earth's surface. They then **re-emit** this energy in all directions, including back towards Earth, so less energy escapes into space.


Spec links: `spcpt_b8kXGMmB5sMPg7Tw`


## Card 13 — true_or_false (`fl_Tt6kwqpZj8mGpzXy`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

All of the Sun's energy that reaches Earth is absorbed by the Earth's surface.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**False.**

Some of the Sun's energy is **reflected** from the Earth's surface back into space. Only some is absorbed, re-emitted as thermal energy, and then partially trapped by greenhouse gases.

What is the enhanced greenhouse effect?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The **enhanced greenhouse effect** is when increasing concentrations of greenhouse gases — due to human activity — trap more thermal energy than normal. This causes the Earth's average temperature to rise above natural levels, leading to global warming.

## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_3Bfv2RS3S83D8p6D`)

**FRONT**

Why might rising global temperatures cause the spread of disease?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

A **warmer climate** allows disease-carrying organisms (such as mosquitoes) to survive and spread into regions that were previously too cold for them. This increases the geographic range of diseases they carry.
