**BACK**

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_Q6854DYRj7CFDcjk`)

**FRONT**

What is the **swap-and-drop method** and when is it used to find an ionic formula?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The **swap-and-drop method** is used when two ions have different charges. The size of each ion's charge becomes the subscript of the other ion, giving the ratio that produces an overall neutral compound.

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_mz4SW6HkjgWkdR9B`)

**FRONT**

What is the formula of copper(II) chloride, given that copper(II) ions are Cu<sup>2+</sup> and chloride ions are Cl<sup>-</sup>?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The formula is CuCl<sub>2</sub>.

One Cu<sup>2+</sup> ion requires two Cl<sup>-</sup> ions to cancel the overall charge, giving the **ratio** 1:2.

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_Kzj7sfX8Fc9zFfq4`)

**FRONT**

Why must the swap-and-drop method give the simplest whole-number ratio when the two ions have the **same** charge?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

After swapping and dropping, **always simplify to the lowest whole-number ratio**.

When both ions carry the same charge the method gives, for example, Ca<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> — which must be simplified to **CaO**. Without that step the formula is wrong.

## Card 15 — true_or_false (`fl_ZPf2P872k2rPmxRz`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

When balancing an equation, you can change the subscript numbers in a formula.


**BACK**

**False.**

You can only change the **coefficients** (numbers in front of formulas). Changing subscripts would alter the identity of the substance.

## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_Jjs4FD5pbYGPRsg2`)

**FRONT**

Why is it useful to treat a polyatomic ion (e.g. NO<sub>3</sub><sup>–</sup>) as a single unit when balancing an equation?


**BACK**

Treating an unchanged **polyatomic ion** as a single unit simplifies the balancing process. If the group appears on both sides of the equation unchanged, counting it as one entity avoids errors from counting individual atoms separately.


Spec links: `spcpt_X6MRSqBTZkVy33bY`
