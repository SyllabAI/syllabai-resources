## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_pHFJdNXn9Ygycdt5`)

**FRONT**

Define **hydrocarbon**.


**BACK**

A **hydrocarbon** is a compound made up of **hydrogen** and **carbon** atoms only. Methane, the main component of natural gas, is an example of a hydrocarbon.

## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_6hgvBTvnzjsnp5J9`)

**FRONT**

Define **fractional distillation**.


**BACK**

**Fractional distillation** is the process used to separate crude oil into groups of hydrocarbons called **fractions** according to their **boiling points** using a fractionating column.

## Card 5 — true_or_false (`fl_XKc7tmvhjmQwxV79`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Petroleum is a pure compound of one type of hydrocarbon.


**BACK**

**False.**

Petroleum (crude oil) is a **mixture** of many different hydrocarbons. The mixture must be separated by fractional distillation into useful fractions.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_4FFkpJRtHWTZtsDV`)

**FRONT**

How does chain length affect the boiling point of a petroleum fraction?


**BACK**

Longer chain hydrocarbons have **higher boiling points** because there are stronger **attractive forces between the molecules**, so more energy is needed to separate them. Shorter chain fractions have lower boiling points and collect at the top of the column.
