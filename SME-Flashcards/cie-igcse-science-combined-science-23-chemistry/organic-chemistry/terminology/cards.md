## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_dPvb3YXX6ZDF7MhG`)

**FRONT**

Define a **saturated compound**.


**BACK**

A **saturated compound** is a molecule in which all carbon-carbon bonds are **single bonds** (C-C). Alkanes are examples of saturated compounds.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_NBQJdfWvcqmb4jSb`)

**FRONT**

What is the general formula of the alkanes and the alkenes?


**BACK**

Alkanes have the general formula **C**<sub>**n**</sub>**H**<sub>**2n+2**</sub>. Alkenes have the general formula **C**<sub>**n**</sub>**H**<sub>**2n**</sub>.


Spec links: `spcpt_zcjHMGnpDkQvZpXP`


## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_GGFxbV7W3vkWnbdg`)

**FRONT**

Define an **unsaturated compound**.


**BACK**

An **unsaturated compound** is a molecule in which one or more carbon-carbon bonds are **not** single bonds. Alkenes contain a carbon-carbon double bond (C=C) and are examples of unsaturated compounds.


Spec links: `spcpt_zcjHMGnpDkQvZpXP`


## Card 4 — true_or_false (`fl_gJ8DVns2DQf3FPfJ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Alkanes are unsaturated hydrocarbons.


**BACK**

**False.**

Alkanes are **saturated** hydrocarbons. They contain only **single** carbon-carbon bonds (C-C). It is **alkenes** that are unsaturated because they contain a C=C double bond.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_JnVXJWybSR9SbVpg`)

**FRONT**

Define **homologous series**.


**BACK**

A **homologous series** is a family of organic compounds that share the same functional group, have the same general formula, have similar chemical properties, and show a trend in physical properties.


Spec links: `spcpt_8RfvdhCPDR2XxGTK`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_4CNWQRNdzdH6HgfP`)

**FRONT**

What is the functional group of the alkenes, and what does the name ending reveal about the family?


**BACK**

The functional group of the **alkenes** is the carbon-carbon double bond (C=C). Alkene names end in **-ene**, which identifies the compound as belonging to this homologous series.


Spec links: `spcpt_8RfvdhCPDR2XxGTK`


## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_cmCXRV3Y5Ys5mwPp`)

**FRONT**

Define **functional group**.


**BACK**

A **functional group** is a group of atoms bonded in a specific arrangement that is responsible for the characteristic reactions of an organic homologous series. For example, the C=C bond is the functional group of alkenes.


Spec links: `spcpt_8RfvdhCPDR2XxGTK`

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_JqnQdgrmhYcNTCz2`)

**FRONT**

Why do members of a homologous series have similar chemical properties?


**BACK**

Members of a homologous series have similar chemical properties because they all contain the **same functional group**. The functional group is responsible for the characteristic reactions of the series.


Spec links: `spcpt_8RfvdhCPDR2XxGTK`


## Card 15 — true_or_false (`fl_FrnbMfbdP33hDdvH`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

All members of a homologous series have identical physical properties.


**BACK**

**False.**

Members of a homologous series show a **trend** in physical properties. For example, boiling point, melting point, and viscosity all increase as molecule size increases.


Spec links: `spcpt_8RfvdhCPDR2XxGTK`


## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_NGNP9PHZ6RdKxnMh`)

**FRONT**

How does the boiling point change as molecules in a homologous series get larger, and why?


**BACK**

Boiling point **increases** as molecules get larger. Larger molecules have stronger **intermolecular forces**, so more energy is needed to separate them.

## Card 18 — true_or_false (`fl_DQbZzXcNycbGmk6v`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Alkanes and alkenes belong to the same homologous series.


**BACK**

**False.**

**Alkanes** and **alkenes** are different homologous series. Alkanes have the functional group C-C (single bond) and end in -ane; alkenes have the functional group C=C (double bond) and end in -ene.
