## Card 19 — keyword_definition (`fl_h3nCJhCpW4NNk3f5`)

**FRONT**

What is the **activation energy** of a reaction?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The **activation energy** (*E*<sub>a</sub>) is the minimum energy that colliding particles must have in order to react.

Collisions with energy below the activation energy are unsuccessful.


Spec links: `spcpt_DY57jxyqYD9xwM7K`


## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_Hwcx8NDM38FXbvxt`)

**FRONT**

According to collision theory, what two conditions must be met for a reaction to occur?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

According to **collision theory**, two conditions must be met:

1. The particles must collide with each other.
2. The collision must have sufficient energy (at least equal to the activation energy) to break bonds.


Spec links: `spcpt_DY57jxyqYD9xwM7K`

**BACK**

**False.**
