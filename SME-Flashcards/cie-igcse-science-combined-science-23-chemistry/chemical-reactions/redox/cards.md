## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_xHMvknDMRSV3F5Jc`)

**FRONT**

What is **oxidation** in terms of oxygen?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**Oxidation** is the gain of oxygen by an element or compound.

For example, carbon is oxidised when it gains oxygen to form carbon dioxide.

## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_Cn4zb9gMDCZhXwbG`)

**FRONT**

What is **reduction** in terms of oxygen?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**Reduction** is the loss of oxygen from an element or compound.

For example, zinc oxide is reduced when it loses oxygen to form zinc metal.

## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_cDys7mRPRmFD4Mth`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In a redox reaction, oxidation and reduction occur simultaneously.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**True.**

A **redox** reaction always involves both oxidation and reduction happening at the same time in the same reaction. One species gains oxygen while another loses it.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_nMBh6B2cm6Yk3rcF`)

**FRONT**

What do Roman numerals in a compound's name indicate?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

Roman numerals indicate the **oxidation number** (charge) of the transition metal ion in the compound.

For example, iron(III) oxide contains Fe<sup>3+</sup> ions and iron(II) oxide contains Fe<sup>2+</sup> ions.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_JPbdcGQCDw6xDC3h`)

**FRONT**

State the formula of iron(III) oxide.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The formula of **iron(III) oxide** is Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>.

It contains iron(III) ions, Fe<sup>3+</sup>, meaning the **oxidation number** of iron is 3.

## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_HC2P5xCPPy9GMkJv`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In the reaction Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO<sub>2</sub>, carbon monoxide is reduced.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**False.**

Carbon monoxide **gains** oxygen to form carbon dioxide, so it is **oxidised**. Iron(III) oxide loses oxygen to form iron, so it is reduced.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_f5v3YK4sNJRjr5yR`)

**FRONT**

In the reaction between iron(III) oxide and carbon monoxide, identify which substance is oxidised and which is reduced.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**
