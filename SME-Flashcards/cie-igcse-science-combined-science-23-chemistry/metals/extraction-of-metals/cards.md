## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_7JFDnX9hSfJ556sV`)

**FRONT**

What is a **metal ore**?


**BACK**

A **metal ore** is a rock that contains enough metal (or metal compound) to make it economically worthwhile to extract the metal.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_tYcRF79N2GWMq5nf`)

**FRONT**

Why is the extraction of a metal from its oxide described as a reduction reaction?


**BACK**

Extraction from an oxide ore involves **removing oxygen** from the metal compound. Removal of oxygen is the definition of **reduction**, so the process is a reduction reaction.

## Card 4 — true_or_false (`fl_9X8KyhNJ4bGGSkyv`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The higher a metal is in the reactivity series, the easier it is to extract from its ore.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **higher** a metal is in the reactivity series, the **more difficult** it is to extract. Highly reactive metals require electrolysis, which is expensive, while less reactive metals can be extracted more cheaply using carbon.

**BACK**

Gold and platinum are very **unreactive** metals. Because they do not easily react with other substances, they are found as **native metals** — as pure, uncombined elements.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**Limestone** (calcium carbonate) is added to remove acidic impurities in the ore. It thermally decomposes to form **calcium oxide**, which reacts with silicon dioxide impurities to form **slag** (calcium silicate).

## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_xtYVsTSrBDWbp8pM`)

**FRONT**

Why cannot aluminium be extracted from its ore using carbon?


**BACK**

Aluminium is **more reactive than carbon**, so carbon cannot displace it from its oxide. Only a more reactive substance can displace a less reactive one, so electrolysis is needed instead.

## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_NZ9DNmGm9hDryM5c`)

**FRONT**

What is the role of molten cryolite in the extraction of aluminium?


**BACK**

**Cryolite** acts as a solvent, dissolving aluminium oxide so that it can conduct electricity and undergo **electrolysis**. It also lowers the melting point of the aluminium oxide, reducing energy costs.
