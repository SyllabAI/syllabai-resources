## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_QjFHNzSrbscjBmB6`)

**FRONT**

What is an **exothermic reaction**?


**BACK**

An **exothermic reaction** is one that transfers thermal energy to the surroundings, causing the temperature of the surroundings to increase.

**BACK**

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_3zzP9grb9W6dQDxW`)

**FRONT**

How does a reaction pathway diagram for an **endothermic** reaction differ from one for an exothermic reaction?

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

In an endothermic diagram, the products are at a **higher** energy level than the reactants, so Δ*H* is positive and the arrow showing the energy change points upwards. In an exothermic diagram, products are lower in energy and Δ*H* is negative.

## Card 18 — true_or_false (`fl_W8FtJHhTqcJX5PD6`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A reaction with a higher activation energy requires more energy to start than one with a lower activation energy.

**(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

**True.**

Activation energy is the minimum energy colliding particles must have to react. A higher *E*<sub>a</sub> means fewer collisions have **sufficient energy**, so the reaction is harder to start.

**(Extended Tier Only)**
