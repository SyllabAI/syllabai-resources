**FRONT**

How is carbon taken out of the atmosphere?


**BACK**

By plants during **photosynthesis**, which takes in **carbon dioxide** and uses the carbon to make **glucose**.

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_tH49Bw9y9mpbhHgr`)

**FRONT**

What happens to the carbon a plant takes in?


**BACK**

It is used to make **glucose**, which can be turned into **carbohydrates, fats and proteins** within the plant's **biomass**.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_hTtJfqdDT5sNwyYx`)

**FRONT**

How is carbon transferred between living organisms?


**BACK**

By **feeding** — from plants to animals when herbivores feed, and between animals when carnivores feed.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_KMqyzbZDHjF4WRrt`)

**FRONT**

How does respiration return carbon to the atmosphere?


**BACK**

**Respiration** in plants and animals releases carbon as **carbon dioxide**.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_DdnWP4KhkNVpqn2R`)

**FRONT**

How does decomposition return carbon to the atmosphere?


**BACK**

**Microorganisms** such as bacteria and fungi use **dead and waste material** to fuel their respiration, releasing carbon dioxide.


Spec links: `spcpt_vDqqYZxjM5YZQJfN`


## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_3WQMbmRCdgQDVnvC`)

**FRONT**

How are fossil fuels formed?


**BACK**

When animals and plants **die in conditions where decomposers are not present**, the carbon in their bodies is converted into **fossil fuels**.


Spec links: `spcpt_vDqqYZxjM5YZQJfN`

**BACK**

## Card 12 — true_or_false (`fl_3jVBTP46ckjm5rrN`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Only animals return carbon dioxide to the atmosphere by respiration.


**BACK**

**False.**

Respiration occurs in **plants as well as animals**, and in the **microorganisms** that decompose dead material.
