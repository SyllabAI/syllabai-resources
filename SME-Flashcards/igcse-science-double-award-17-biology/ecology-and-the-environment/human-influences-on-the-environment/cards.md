**FRONT**

Describe the four steps of the natural greenhouse effect.


**BACK**

The **Sun emits radiation** that enters the Earth's atmosphere.

The Earth's surface **absorbs** some of it and **re-emits it as heat** (infrared radiation).

Some of that heat is **reflected back into space**.

Some is **absorbed by greenhouse gases** and **trapped in the atmosphere**.

## Card 39 — question_and_answer (`fl_jjPcMnMrQT77N7DQ`)

**FRONT**

Describe the stages of eutrophication.


**BACK**

Fertilisers are **high in nitrates**, causing **overgrowth of aquatic plants and algae** at the water surface.

Plants **below the surface die** from reduced light and are **broken down by decomposers**.

Decomposers **increase in number**, and their **increased respiration uses up oxygen**.

The water no longer holds enough oxygen, so **many aquatic organisms die**.


Spec links: `spcpt_SNQY2DKhmgHhf96k`
