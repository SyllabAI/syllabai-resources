## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_w2srjWnwXv54m3xb`)

**FRONT**

Define **selective breeding**.


**BACK**

Selecting individuals with **desirable characteristics** and **breeding them together**. Also called **artificial selection**.


Spec links: `spcpt_6kfNB5G2D22mDSMq`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_MQ5wB59MHdDgksSV`)

**FRONT**

Describe the process of selective breeding.


**BACK**

Select individuals with the **desired characteristics** and **breed them together**.

Select the **offspring that show those characteristics** and breed those together.

**Repeat for many successive generations**, until all offspring reliably show the characteristics — a **new breed**.


Spec links: `spcpt_6kfNB5G2D22mDSMq`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_dPN7h34yNqPbdNYy`)

**FRONT**

Why must selective breeding be repeated over many generations?


**BACK**

Because **not all offspring** will show the desired characteristics. Only after many generations will they appear **reliably** in all offspring.

## Card 6 — keyword_definition (`fl_h79zqkBdpg7fkQ3f`)

**FRONT**

Define **inbreeding**.


**BACK**

When only the 'best' animals or plants, which are **closely related to each other**, are bred together.


Spec links: `spcpt_6kfNB5G2D22mDSMq`


## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_FhWNBcwsD6j2dNYX`)

**FRONT**

What does inbreeding do to the gene pool?


**BACK**

It causes a **reduction in the gene pool** — a reduction in the **number of alleles** present in the population.


Spec links: `spcpt_6kfNB5G2D22mDSMq`


## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_x5JVfvGy3MPTvJ45`)

**FRONT**

Give two risks of a reduced gene pool.


**BACK**

- An increased chance of inheriting **harmful genetic defects**
- Greater vulnerability to **new diseases**, since resistant alleles are less likely to be present
