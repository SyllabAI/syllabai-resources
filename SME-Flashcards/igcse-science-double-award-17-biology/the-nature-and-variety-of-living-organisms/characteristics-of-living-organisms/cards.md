**FRONT**

How does nutrition in plants differ from nutrition in animals?


**BACK**

Plants **make their own food** by photosynthesis, using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water.

Animals **consume other living organisms** and break the large molecules down by **digestion** into simpler molecules they can absorb.

## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_B5NgBwGrZGdHJtFJ`)

**FRONT**

How does sexual reproduction differ from asexual reproduction?


**BACK**

In **sexual** reproduction, male and female **gametes fuse**, so the offspring's DNA comes from **both parents**.

In **asexual** reproduction there is only **one parent**, so the offspring is a **clone** with DNA identical to the parent's.
