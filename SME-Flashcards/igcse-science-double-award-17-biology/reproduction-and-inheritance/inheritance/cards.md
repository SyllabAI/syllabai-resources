**FRONT**

Describe how antibiotic resistance increases in a bacterial population.


**BACK**

A **random mutation** gives rise to a new allele coding for **antibiotic resistance**.

When the population meets the antibiotic, bacteria **without** the allele die while those **with** it **survive**.

The survivors are **more likely to reproduce**, **passing on the resistance allele**.

Over generations the **frequency of the resistance allele increases**, producing a **resistant strain**.
