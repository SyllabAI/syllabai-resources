## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_7Z5DySFVCJY9fQs3`)

**FRONT**

Describe the three steps of enzyme action.


**BACK**

**1.** Enzymes and substrates move about **randomly** in solution.

**2.** An enzyme and its **complementary substrate collide**, an **enzyme-substrate complex** forms, and the reaction occurs.

**3.** The **products** form and are released from the active site. The enzyme is **unchanged** and goes on to catalyse further reactions.

## Card 77 — question_and_answer (`fl_XZnj6DmqPnwKKJzr`)

**FRONT**

How does a pH that is too high or too low affect an enzyme?


**BACK**

The **bonds** holding the amino acid chain together are **disrupted or destroyed**.

This **changes the shape of the active site**, so the **substrate can no longer fit**, and the rate of activity falls.
