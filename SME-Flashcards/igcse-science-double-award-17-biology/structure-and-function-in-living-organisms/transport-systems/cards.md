## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_9sFYqwfjdVSmdV6D`)

**FRONT**

Why can unicellular organisms rely on diffusion alone?


**BACK**

They have a **large surface area relative to their volume**, so the distance from the surface to the centre is **small**.

Diffusion, osmosis and active transport through the cell membrane are therefore **sufficient**.


Spec links: `spcpt_JzqXDyF2DZCZwSfg`

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_7pRKcx4hnfHrtWB4`)

**FRONT**

Why do multicellular organisms need a transport system?


**BACK**

They have **multiple cell layers**, so the distance from the surface to the centre is **too long** for diffusion alone.

Diffusion to every cell would be **too slow** to meet the organism's needs.

**BACK**
