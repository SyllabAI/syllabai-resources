## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_h9DFy6gKkqgbYyjH`)

**FRONT**

Define **organelle**.


**BACK**

A **specialised structure within a cell** that carries out a specific function.

For example, the **mitochondrion** is the organelle where aerobic respiration occurs.


Spec links: `spcpt_ShbyQ3PDxvb4gYv3`

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_5vcZrVwrXBvjdwMH`)

**FRONT**

Why are cells described as the basic building blocks of living organisms?


**BACK**

Because they are the basic **structural and functional units** of all living organisms.

**Unicellular** organisms are made from one cell; **multicellular** organisms are made from collections of cells.

## Card 9 — true_or_false (`fl_PWQRyk8rpQVQMGjn`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

All the cells in a multicellular organism are identical.


**BACK**

**False.**

In complex multicellular organisms, cells are **specialised** to carry out particular functions, and these specialised cells group into tissues.


Spec links: `spcpt_ShbyQ3PDxvb4gYv3`


## Card 10 — true_or_false (`fl_DS4Ftc7jgtkRHPWT`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A tissue is made up of different types of organ.


**BACK**

**False.**

It is the other way round — a **tissue** is a group of similar cells, and an **organ** is made up of different tissues.
