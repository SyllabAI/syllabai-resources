**BACK**

## Card 33 — question_and_answer (`fl_qTBgjSbgv9v43rmp`)

**FRONT**

Outline the limewater method for the carbon dioxide investigation.


**BACK**

Pour 2 cm<sup>3</sup> of **limewater** into three boiling tubes.

Set up three more tubes on damp cotton wool: **A** with germinating seeds, **B** with boiled seeds, **C** with glass beads.

Leave at **25–30 °C** for several hours, then observe any colour changes.

## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_krwqwjvzSY32gHGp`)

**FRONT**

Outline the vacuum flask method for the heat investigation.


**BACK**

**Sterilise** the seeds and flasks with weak bleach.

Set up **flask A** with dead seeds and **flask B** with germinating seeds, on moist cotton wool.

Insert a **thermometer** into each so the bulb sits among the seeds, and seal the tops with cotton wool.

**Invert** both flasks, record the **initial temperature**, then record the **final temperature** after 2–3 days.


Spec links: `spcpt_3pv9yByyFQjX8MxD`
