**FRONT**

**BACK**

**Oxygen**.

The rate of **photosynthesis is higher** than the rate of respiration, so **more oxygen is produced than used**.

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_ZTGWppRYxR3Rr79X`)

**FRONT**

Why is a build-up of carbon dioxide dangerous?


**BACK**

It dissolves easily in water to form an **acidic solution**, which **lowers the pH of cells** and can **reduce enzyme activity**.


Spec links: `spcpt_9qf4FgFhJKzt96T5`


## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_9XC8bWbrHjPkFxn3`)

**FRONT**

What osmotic problem can a build-up of waste cause?


**BACK**

Body fluids become **more concentrated**, so **water moves out of the cells** by osmosis and essential reactions can no longer take place.


Spec links: `spcpt_9qf4FgFhJKzt96T5`
