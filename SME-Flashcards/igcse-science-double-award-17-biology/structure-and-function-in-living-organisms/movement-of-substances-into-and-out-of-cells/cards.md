## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_WCpHyRdcfwCdb9YR`)

**FRONT**

Define **diffusion**.


**BACK**

The movement of particles from a region of **higher concentration** to a region of **lower concentration** — down a **concentration gradient**.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_7bPW5ZGBcr7dP6Nt`)

**FRONT**

Why is diffusion described as a passive process?


**BACK**

Because it needs **no energy from the cell**.

The diffusing particles have their own **kinetic energy** and spread out **randomly** from high to low concentration.

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_XFSPrg6jJ3Pymbcz`)

**FRONT**

How does dissolved solute affect the direction of osmosis?


**BACK**

The **more solute** a solution contains, the **lower** its water concentration.

**BACK**

This causes more **collisions** with the cell membrane and a faster rate of movement across it.

## Card 38 — question_and_answer (`fl_MB3J64Q6pj8xjBTF`)

**FRONT**

Outline the agar-cube diffusion method.


**BACK**

Cut cubes of pink agar to different side lengths, such as 0.5 cm, 1 cm and 2 cm.

Pour 50 ml of **dilute hydrochloric acid** into a boiling tube.

Using forceps, add one cube and **start a timer**.

**Stop the timer** when the cube has turned completely colourless, and record the time.

Repeat at least twice more per size, then repeat for the other cube sizes.
