## Card 50 — question_and_answer (`fl_m9vfVz7HwmdjKjtZ`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

When light shines **predominantly from one side**, auxin made in the tip concentrates on the **shaded side**.

Cells on the shaded side **elongate faster** than those on the sunny side.

This **unequal growth** makes the shoot **bend towards the light**.
