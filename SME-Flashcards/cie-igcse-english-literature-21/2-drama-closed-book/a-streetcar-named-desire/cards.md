## Card 5 — keyword_definition (`fl_gW6Hv6zWwnnjR433`)

**FRONT**

*Blanche, Scene 1*


**BACK**

**Analysis**

The journey works as an **allegory**: **desire** leads Blanche towards social death ('Cemeteries'). It maps out her downfall from the very first scene.
