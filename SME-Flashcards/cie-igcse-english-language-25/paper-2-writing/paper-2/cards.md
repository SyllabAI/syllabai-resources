**Question 1(b)**


**BACK**

**Timing:** ~50 minutes

**Type:** speech, letter, article or report (discursive, argumentative or persuasive), 250–350 words

**Marks:** 35 marks (10 reading + 25 writing)

**How to answer:**

- Identify the **form, audience and purpose**
- Use and develop the texts' ideas in your **own words**

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_k5vnNzRpywsm66t8`)

**FRONT**

**Section B: Composition**


**BACK**

**Timing:** ~60 minutes (15 plan + 45 write)

**Type:** one composition from four titles: descriptive (Q2 or Q3) or narrative (Q4 or Q5), 350–450 words

**Marks:** 40 marks (16 content and structure + 24 style and accuracy)

**How to answer:**

- Descriptive = **a picture, not a story**
- Narrative = **a shaped plot** with a clear ending
- Vary your sentences and proofread
