**Developing details**


**BACK**

## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_CQdDBWR7TkhFJdQ5`)

**FRONT**

**Register**


**BACK**

Adapting your **tone, language and expression** to suit the format and its audience, e.g. a letter to your parents can be less formal than a report.


Spec links: `spcpt_vcfTWWvBbhrDFks8`


## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_gv52wFDjNjcbyknP`)

**FRONT**

**Colloquialism**


**BACK**

**Informal, everyday language**, e.g. 'The exam was a doddle'; it suits, for example, a student's letter to their parents.

## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_p7QCr8npj9DcF76y`)

**FRONT**

**Narrative voice**


**BACK**

The **point of view** you write from (e.g. a student on the trip), mirroring that character's attitude and keeping it **consistent** throughout.

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_9kJFjWRGfKFTDtZQ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In a Question 4 letter, you should include an address, a date and headings.


**BACK**

**False.**

Use a suitable **greeting** ('Dear…') and **sign-off**, but you need no address, date, headings or subheadings.
