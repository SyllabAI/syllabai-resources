## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_rkptgbB6GGtrPjtF`)

**FRONT**

In 'The man stumbled into the house on his return from a night out, tripping and cursing his way up the stairs', what is a logical inference, and what would be an illogical one?


**BACK**

**Logical:** he is drunk ('night out', 'stumbled', 'tripping', 'cursing').

**Illogical:** he is an alcoholic, as there is no **evidence** in the sentence for this.
