## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_Q5pNg3mMnkZbkWwd`)

**FRONT**

Define **internal energy**.


**BACK**

**Internal energy** is the sum of the **kinetic** and **potential** energies of all the molecules within a given mass of a substance.

**FRONT**

**BACK**

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_vq5sBHgq33ZVjzfV`)

**FRONT**

What is the **potential energy** of the molecules in a substance due to?


**BACK**

The **separation** between the molecules and their **position** within the structure. Greater **intermolecular forces** give a higher potential energy.

## Card 7 — keyword_definition (`fl_gXWT9HsXhyWtf9jK`)

**FRONT**

Define **absolute zero**.


**BACK**

**Absolute zero** is the lowest temperature possible, equal to 0 K or -273.15 °C. It is the temperature at which the molecules in a substance have **zero kinetic energy**.

## Card 13 — keyword_definition (`fl_j8K7QRWpWxFrYwNh`)

**FRONT**

Define **Boyle's law**.


**BACK**

For a fixed mass of gas at **constant temperature**, the pressure is **inversely proportional** to the volume: $P_{1} V_{1} = P_{2} V_{2}$


Spec links: `spcpt_dZNyfcWTcTBwkbpb`


## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_kc2mvnh23fzVRfVN`)

**FRONT**

State **Charles's law**.


**BACK**

For a fixed mass of gas at **constant pressure**, the volume is **directly proportional** to the thermodynamic temperature: $\frac{V_{1}}{T_{1}} = \frac{V_{2}}{T_{2}}$


Spec links: `spcpt_dZNyfcWTcTBwkbpb`
