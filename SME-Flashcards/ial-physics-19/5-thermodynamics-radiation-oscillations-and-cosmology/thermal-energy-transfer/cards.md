## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_w6vphvzK4FMvRS2W`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for the change in heat energy, Δ*E*, of a substance and define each term.


**BACK**

$Δ E = m c Δ θ$

- Δ*E* = change in heat energy (J)
- *m* = mass (kg)
- *c* = specific heat capacity (J kg<sup>-1</sup> K<sup>-1</sup>)
- Δ*θ* = change in temperature (K or °C)

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_YjVWBrhMTstY67zM`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for the energy needed to change the state of a substance and define each term.


**BACK**

$Δ E = L Δ m$

- Δ*E* = energy to change state (J)
- *L* = specific latent heat of fusion or vaporisation (J kg<sup>-1</sup>)
- Δ*m* = change in mass of the substance changing state (kg)


Spec links: `spcpt_B2Zprr2kyNdR4d9w`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_ZGpkzP4t8wPbqNxx`)

**FRONT**

Why does vaporising a mass of water need roughly seven times more energy than melting the same mass of ice?


**BACK**

**Melting** only needs enough energy to increase the separation between molecules so they can flow over each other. **Vaporising** needs enough energy to completely separate the molecules and overcome all the intermolecular forces between them, which requires much more energy.
