## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_mwTxSBStP98PQ3vn`)

**FRONT**

Define **work**.


**BACK**

**Work** is the amount of energy transferred when an external force causes an object to move over a distance in the direction of the force.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_CgnWTSrvpRcjTjrb`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for work done when a force acts parallel to an object's displacement.


**BACK**

$Δ W = F Δ s$

where *F* is the force in the direction of motion (N) and *s* is the displacement (m).


Spec links: `spcpt_6xhKMyzgkpZp3ZPG`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_Rv3rKzCG3yg7B3m6`)

**FRONT**

A force acts at an angle *θ* to an object's displacement. How is the work done calculated?


**BACK**

Only the **component** of the force parallel to the displacement is used.

If *θ* is measured from the horizontal: $W = F s \mathrm{cos} θ$

If *θ* is measured from the vertical: $W = F s \mathrm{sin} θ$

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_5xHrmHJtxPsGT3BQ`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for kinetic energy.


**BACK**

where *m* is mass (kg) and *v* is speed (m s<sup>-1</sup>).

**BACK**

It **gains** an equal amount of kinetic energy, since the lost gravitational potential energy is transferred into kinetic energy.

## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_JZYZmqmtq9dDTwvd`)

**FRONT**

A body's speed increases from 12 m s<sup>-1</sup> to 45 m s<sup>-1</sup>. Does its kinetic energy increase by the same factor as its speed?

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_vxMd5bvDy7YQjMtQ`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for the change in gravitational potential energy in a uniform gravitational field.


**BACK**

$Δ E_{p} = m g Δ h$

where *m* is mass (kg), *g* is the gravitational field strength (m s<sup>-2</sup>) and Δ*h* is the change in height (m).

## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_qbMGfhvVFhr7fxpY`)
