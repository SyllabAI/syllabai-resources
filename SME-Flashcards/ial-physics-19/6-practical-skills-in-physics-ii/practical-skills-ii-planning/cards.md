## Card 44 — true_or_false (`fl_zCZ4Sz8TpNsFzhRq`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Plotting a graph of the corrected count rate, *C*, against distance, *x*, produces a straight line that confirms the inverse square law for gamma radiation.


**BACK**

**False.**

To confirm the inverse square law, a graph of *C*<sup>-1/2</sup> against *x* must be plotted; a straight line through the origin on this graph (not a plot of *C* against *x*) confirms the inverse square relationship.
