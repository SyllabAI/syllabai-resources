## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_hWG7DSTPZtXzNPb2`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

$\text{mean} = \frac{\text{sum of readings}}{\text{number of readings}}$

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_VgQsqzV8rVq9tZQq`)

**FRONT**

Explain why taking natural logs of the radioactive decay equation $N = N_{0} e^{-λt}$ produces a straight-line graph.


**BACK**

Taking ln of both sides gives:

$\mathrm{ln} N = - λ t + \mathrm{ln} N_{0}$

This matches *y* = *mx* + *c*, so plotting ln *N* against *t* gives a straight line with gradient −λ and y-intercept ln *N*<sub>0</sub>.

## Card 63 — question_and_answer (`fl_dSJZZ3793yHJnKbT`)

**FRONT**

A student records repeat values of angular frequency, *ω*: 0.154, 0.153, 0.159, 0.147, 0.152 rad s<sup>-1</sup>. Calculate the percentage uncertainty in the mean value.


**BACK**

Mean *ω* = 0.153 rad s<sup>-1</sup>.

Half the range = ½ × (0.159 − 0.147) = 0.006 rad s<sup>-1</sup>.

Percentage uncertainty = (0.006 ÷ 0.153) × 100% = **3.92%**.
