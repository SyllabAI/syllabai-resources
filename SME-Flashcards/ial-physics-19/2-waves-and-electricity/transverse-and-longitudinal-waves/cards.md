## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_pbxTG69rMkz4Mr9P`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

The distance between a point on a wave and the same point on the next cycle of the wave, e.g. two crests or two troughs.

## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_FHV76Mfk6BN2YgTs`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**False.**

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_sgbjnVgkVPW5dYdQ`)

**FRONT**

State the **wave equation** linking wave speed, frequency and wavelength.


**BACK**

$v = f λ$

where *v* = wave speed (m s<sup>-1</sup>), *f* = frequency (Hz), *λ* = wavelength (m)


Spec links: `spcpt_CtyyQS9MJmgPswrH`
