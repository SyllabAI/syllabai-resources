## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_cKzBtt3WvbbF7x9X`)

**FRONT**

State the diffraction grating equation and define each term.


**BACK**

$n λ = d \mathrm{sin} θ$

- *n* = order of the diffraction pattern
- *λ* = wavelength of light (m)
- *d* = slit separation (m)
- *θ* = angle between the normal and the maxima (°)


Spec links: `spcpt_CV5bhyNsZBmc6MBP`

## Card 32 — question_and_answer (`fl_tWzRhbpB5CTQHrTj`)

**BACK**

$λ = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{h}{p}$

- *λ* = de Broglie wavelength (m)

- *m* = mass (kg)
