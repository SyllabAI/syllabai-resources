**FRONT**

**BACK**

The equation for pressure due to contact between two surfaces is $P=\frac{F}{A}$

Where:

- $P$ = pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $F$ = force, measured in newtons (N)
- $A$ = area, measured in metres squared (m<sup>2</sup>)

## Card 44 — keyword_definition (`fl_Ww4yHVQFjtNq6FTc`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for Boyle's law.  **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for Boyle's law is $P_{1}V_{1}=P_{2}V_{2}$

Where:

- $P_{1}$ = initial pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $P_{2}$ = final pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $V_{1}$ = initial volume, measured in metres cubed (m<sup>3</sup>)
- $V_{2}$ = final volume, measured in metres cubed (m<sup>3</sup>)



## Card 45 — question_and_answer (`fl_5cMbh3x4xS9s9KNR`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for Boyle's law in terms of final volume?  **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for Boyle's law in terms of final volume is $V_{2}=\frac{P_{1}V_{1}}{P_{2}}$

Where:

- $V_{2}$ = final volume, measured in metres cubed (m<sup>3</sup>)
- $P_{1}$ = initial pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $P_{2}$ = final pressure, measured in pascals (Pa)
- $V_{1}$ = initial volume, measured in metres cubed (m<sup>3</sup>)
