## Card 22 — keyword_definition (`fl_Vpcqj7TZDWFSZ7k9`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for calculating the combined resistance of two resistors connected in parallel. **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for the combined resistance of two resistors connected in parallel is $\frac{1}{R}=\frac{1}{R_{1}}+\frac{1}{R_{2}}$

Where:

- $R$ = the total combined resistance, measured in ohms (Ω)
- $R_{1}$ = the resistance of the first resistor, measured in ohms (Ω)
- $R_{2}$ = the resistance of the second resistor, measured in ohms (Ω)

## Card 27 — keyword_definition (`fl_HW3FJpwWzJNK8Nfz`)

**FRONT**

State the potential divider equation.


**BACK**

The potential divider equation is $\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}=\frac{V_{1}}{V_{2}}$Where:

- $R_{1}$ = resistance of the first resistor, measured in ohms ($Ω$)
- $R_{2}$ = resistance of the second resistor, measured in ohms ($Ω$)
- $V_{1}$ = potential difference across the first resistor, measured in volts (V)
- $V_{2}$ = potential difference across the second resistor, measured in volts (V)
