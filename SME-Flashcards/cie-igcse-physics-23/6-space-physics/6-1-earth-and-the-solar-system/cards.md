## Card 28 — question_and_answer (`fl_rY89GGQbNFrZf999`)

**FRONT**

Mars is 227.9 million km from the Sun and the speed of light is 3 × 10<sup>8</sup> m/s. How long does it take light to travel from the Sun to Mars?


**BACK**

It takes light **760 s** or **12.6 min** to travel from the Sun to Mars

- distance from the Sun to Mars = 227.9 million km
- distance from the Sun to Mars = 227.9 × 10<sup>6</sup> km = 227.9 × 10<sup>9</sup> m
- $\mathrm{time}=\frac{\mathrm{distance}}{\mathrm{speed} \mathrm{of} \mathrm{light}}$
- $\mathrm{time}=\frac{227.9\times10^{9}}{3\times10^{8}}=759.6s$
- $\mathrm{time}=\frac{759.6}{60}=12.6\mathrm{min}$
