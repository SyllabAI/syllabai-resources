**FRONT**

**BACK**

The formula for calculating power is:

$P=\frac{W}{t}$ or $P=\frac{ΔE}{t}$

Where:

- $P$ = power, measured in watts (W)
- $W$= work done, measured in joules (J)
- $ΔE$ = energy transferred, measured in joules (J)
- $t$ = time, measured in seconds (s)
