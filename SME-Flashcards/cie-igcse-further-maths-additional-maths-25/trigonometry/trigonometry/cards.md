## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_kxRBdYkD68GgrBmG`)

**FRONT**

Why do $\mathrm{sin}$, $\mathrm{cos}$ and $\mathrm{tan}$ have no inverse unless their domain is restricted?


**BACK**

Because they are **many-to-one**: many different angles share the same output.

An inverse would have to send that one output back to several angles at once, which no function can do, so the domain is cut down until the mapping is one-to-one.

**BACK**
