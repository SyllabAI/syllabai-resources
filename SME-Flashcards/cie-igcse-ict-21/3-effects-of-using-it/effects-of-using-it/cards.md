## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_TJp9kHBg9YhzHQRh`)

**FRONT**

Why does any device fitted with a microprocessor create a security risk?


**BACK**

Any device fitted with a microprocessor is **open to cybercriminal activity**, giving hackers more opportunities, for example **hacking smart home cameras and locks**, or **monitoring smart heating patterns** to work out when people are at home.
