## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_gvmN5rfgSV56wDhJ`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between a transition and an animation?


**BACK**

A **transition** is used when **moving between slides**, while an **animation** adds **movement or emphasis to elements on a slide**.


Spec links: `spcpt_XDdh42vPHmy9gF6D`


## Card 19 — true_or_false (`fl_ZH78rC8mh48JZn9C`)

**FRONT**

An animation is the effect used when moving from one slide to the next.


**BACK**
