## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_njPKZ6jjxBDJ4Vrt`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

Virtual reality **shuts out the real world** and immerses the user in a **fully simulated environment**, using a headset with built-in screens giving a **360 degree view**.


Spec links: `spcpt_xtSyNhjf7wp8fWms`


## Card 22 — question_and_answer (`fl_KGMNtyPkHDVGR45n`)

**FRONT**

How does augmented reality differ from virtual reality?


**BACK**

Augmented reality **overlays digital information or graphics onto the real world**, so the user is **not fully immersed** and can still see and interact with what is in front of them.
