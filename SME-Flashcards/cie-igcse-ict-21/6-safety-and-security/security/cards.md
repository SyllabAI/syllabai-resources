## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_x5nPFCGbjnt2NSqS`)

**FRONT**

Define **phishing**.


**BACK**

Phishing is a form of **social engineering** in which fraudulent, legitimate-looking **emails** are sent to a large number of addresses, claiming to be from a reputable company, to gain access to a user's details.


Spec links: `spcpt_mrPqCfM7XMTRsjkf`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_972tPzMyPrnvrfQ3`)

**FRONT**

Give two ways an organisation can prevent phishing.


**BACK**

Phishing can be prevented by **anti-spam filters**, **training staff** to recognise fraudulent emails, and **user access levels** that stop staff opening file types such as **.exe** and **.bat** files.


Spec links: `spcpt_mrPqCfM7XMTRsjkf`

Smishing uses **SMS messages** and vishing uses **fraudulent phone calls**, but both are forms of phishing used to obtain personal information.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_xSVbGGxN9tyzN7Bw`)
