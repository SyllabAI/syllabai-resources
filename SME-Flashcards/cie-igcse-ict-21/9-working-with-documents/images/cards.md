## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_zgCv8Bwc4g9nT5sJ`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between brightness and contrast?


**BACK**

**Brightness** affects **all pixels equally**, making the image lighter or darker. **Contrast** adjusts the **difference between light and dark values**, making the image appear more or less detailed.
