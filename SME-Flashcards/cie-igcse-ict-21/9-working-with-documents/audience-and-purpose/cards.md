## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_CvQ9xGpyQKg76xft`)

**FRONT**

What two things must you understand before designing an ICT solution?


**BACK**

You must understand the **audience characteristics** and the **needs of the audience**.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_H6ZW7454NFD55V5W`)

**FRONT**

Name the five characteristics of an audience.


**BACK**

The five characteristics are **age**, **location**, **education and technical skill**, **interests** and **accessibility needs**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_Zcj8FWMMGNfCkTQv`)

**FRONT**

How does the age of an audience affect a design?


**BACK**

**Younger** audiences are generally **open to new interfaces**, while **older** audiences may prefer a more **intuitive design** and **require training**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_pQKZZY5mWnhWn9fB`)

**FRONT**

What three things does the location of an audience affect?


**BACK**

Location affects **cultural references**, **access to high speed internet** and **language**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_NWRmySkhVSSKD5gv`)

**FRONT**

What does the education and technical skill of an audience determine?


**BACK**

It determines the **level of complexity** the audience can handle.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_8wCCK7zfp57TbFfm`)

**FRONT**

Why should an audience's interests be considered?


**BACK**

Interests **influence design elements and the user experience**, so the solution can be made to **cater to what users enjoy**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_vHs5D8pQFTXZtbSB`)

**FRONT**

Which three accessibility needs should a solution consider?


**BACK**

A solution should consider users with **visual impairments**, users with **hearing impairments**, and users with **limited mobility**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_qhWdw5bFtXmF67xs`)

**FRONT**

Give three accessibility features an ICT solution could include.


**BACK**

Accessibility features include **screen readers**, **high contrast mode**, **closed captions**, **transcripts** and **alternative control methods** such as voice commands.


Spec links: `spcpt_3N2qyHcyPy2DCctH`


## Card 11 — true_or_false (`fl_8rkqJrvzxDXwJd8M`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Every audience should be given the same interface, whatever their age or skill level.


**BACK**

**False.**

The design must match the **audience characteristics**, because age, education and technical skill change the **level of complexity** users can handle.
