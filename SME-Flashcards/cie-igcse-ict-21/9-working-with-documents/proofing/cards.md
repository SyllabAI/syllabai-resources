**BACK**

Spell check software is **built into many word processing applications** and compares the **words typed in a document** with **those held in its dictionary**.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_79QTy6392Grfv2DF`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between verification and proofreading?


**BACK**

**Proofreading** is **checking the content of the data for errors**, while **verification** is **comparing the data with the original**.


Spec links: `spcpt_pQTRq2P9XZsypqrn`


## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_Tvm28h2HqDHy8rJM`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between verification and validation?


**BACK**

**Verification** ensures data has been **entered or copied correctly**, while **validation** makes sure the data is **sensible, reasonable and within acceptable boundaries**.


Spec links: `spcpt_pQTRq2P9XZsypqrn`


## Card 16 — true_or_false (`fl_yPrgJMP6WSgVs8yQ`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Validation checks that data is sensible, reasonable and within acceptable boundaries.


**BACK**

**True.**

That is **validation**. **Verification** is the separate check that data has been copied correctly from the original.


Spec links: `spcpt_pQTRq2P9XZsypqrn`
