## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_KjhtvMGczb28gM7x`)

**FRONT**

Define **partition coefficient (*****K*****<sub>pc</sub>**).


**BACK**

A **partition coefficient** (*K*<sub>pc</sub>) is the ratio of the concentration of a solute in an organic solvent to its concentration in water (or vice versa), at equilibrium and at a specified temperature.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_m2f9DbJK2XC3mwJ2`)

**FRONT**

State the equilibrium expression for the partition coefficient of methylamine (CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub>) distributed between an organic solvent and water.


**BACK**

CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub> (aq) ⇌ CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub> (organic solvent)

*K*<sub>pc</sub> = [CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub>(organic)] / [CH<sub>3</sub>NH<sub>2</sub>(aq)]

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_9GsvDPp67YmB9Dmg`)

**FRONT**

Explain why ammonia has a lower partition coefficient (organic / water) than expected from its molecular mass alone.


**BACK**

Ammonia and water are both polar and form **hydrogen bonds** with each other. Ammonia forms only weaker permanent dipole-induced dipole interactions with non-polar organic solvents. The stronger hydrogen bonding makes ammonia more soluble in water, lowering *K*<sub>pc</sub>.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_MmTQhmhpcT3FS7dz`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The partition coefficient changes with temperature.


**BACK**

**True.**

*K*<sub>pc</sub> depends on the solubilities of the solute in each solvent, which are temperature-dependent. A change in temperature alters the equilibrium position and therefore the value of *K*<sub>pc</sub>.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_tChsYf22M9SFgnBs`)

**FRONT**

In a partition coefficient calculation using titration, why is only part of the aqueous layer titrated rather than all of it?


**BACK**

A measured **aliquot** (e.g. 50.0 cm<sup>3</sup>) is titrated for precision and practicality. The moles found in that aliquot are then **scaled up** proportionally to find the total moles of solute in the full aqueous layer.

## Card 8 — keyword_definition (`fl_RZKcFSbZxbd7Sqm4`)

**FRONT**

Define **immiscible solvents**.


**BACK**

**Immiscible solvents** are two liquids that do not mix and form two distinct layers when combined, such as water and an organic solvent like hexane. A solute can distribute between both layers, establishing a partition equilibrium.
