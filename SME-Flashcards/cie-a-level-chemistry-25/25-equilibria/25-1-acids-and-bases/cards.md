## Card 29 — question_and_answer (`fl_wsHZxH2mHH4Hcn8Z`)

**FRONT**

Explain how an ethanoic acid / sodium ethanoate buffer resists a decrease in pH when small amounts of H<sup>+</sup> ions are added.


**BACK**

The added H<sup>+</sup> ions react with the reserve supply of **CH**<sub>**3**</sub>**COO**<sup>**-**</sup> ions from the sodium ethanoate:

The equilibrium **shifts left**, consuming the added H<sup>+</sup>. Because there is a large reservoir of CH<sub>3</sub>COO<sup>-</sup>, the pH does not change significantly.
