## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_nbWx4z23Tpsd7YVm`)

**FRONT**

Why do arenes prefer substitution reactions over addition reactions?


**BACK**

Arenes prefer substitution reactions because the delocalised π electron ring system is extremely stable. Addition reactions would disrupt this **aromatic stabilisation**, whereas substitution reactions maintain the delocalised ring.

## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_fJ2Pkm4V8VvyC8Vs`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Halogenoarenes are soluble in water due to the halogen atom attached to the ring.


**BACK**

**False.**

Halogenoarenes are insoluble in water. The non-polar hydrocarbon ring means it is not energetically favourable for halogenoarene molecules to break the hydrogen bonds between water molecules.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_362WtdXQnFyXhDYr`)

**FRONT**

Why is the –OH group in phenol more acidic than in alcohols?


**BACK**

The oxygen in phenol donates one of its **lone pairs** into the ring's π system, increasing electron density in the ring. This weakens the **O–H bond**, making the hydrogen easier to donate and phenol more acidic than an alcohol.

What structural features define an amino acid?


**BACK**

An amino acid contains both an **amine group** (–NH<sub>2</sub>) and a **carboxyl group** (–COOH). Most amino acids have a chiral centre and exhibit optical isomers, except for glycine.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_yYyZFXqfbngpgwXD`)

**FRONT**

Define **skeletal formula**.


**BACK**

A **skeletal formula** is a simplified structural representation of an organic molecule that shows the carbon skeleton as a zigzag, with functional groups shown explicitly and hydrogen atoms on carbon omitted.


Spec links: `spcpt_jWY47tYndC23Nb6v`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_vpTXWqY628WV6vBf`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between a structural formula and a displayed formula?


**BACK**

A **structural formula** shows how atoms are grouped and bonded without showing all individual bonds. A **displayed formula** shows every bond in the molecule explicitly, including all C–H bonds.


Spec links: `spcpt_jWY47tYndC23Nb6v`

A general formula gives the simplest algebraic expression for any member of a homologous series. For example, alkanes have the general formula C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n+2</sub>.

**BACK**

A **systematic nomenclature** is a standardised method of naming organic compounds based on the length of the longest carbon chain, the functional group present and the positions of substituents.

What is the first step when naming an aliphatic organic compound systematically?


**BACK**

Identify the **longest carbon chain** that contains the **principal functional group**. This gives the parent chain name and the base suffix.

**FRONT**

How is the naming of aromatic compounds similar to that of aliphatic compounds?


**BACK**

Both use the position numbering system to indicate where substituents are located. For aromatic compounds, benzene is the parent name and substituents are numbered to give the **lowest possible locants**.
