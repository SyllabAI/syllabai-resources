## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_Z77Bb6T46Rx6qVxk`)

**FRONT**

Define **catalyst**.


**BACK**

A **catalyst** is a substance that increases the rate of a chemical reaction by providing an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy. It is not consumed in the reaction.

## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_p8hrPxTWGQ3Xfg6Z`)

**FRONT**

Define **homogeneous catalyst**.


**BACK**

A **homogeneous catalyst** is one that is in the **same phase** as the reactants. For example, a catalyst dissolved in the same liquid solution as the reactants.


Spec links: `spcpt_YSQfmtB7RFM428Q6`


## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_5m2F4dTmdcTvyvZB`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A catalyst is consumed during a chemical reaction.


**BACK**

**False.**

A catalyst is **not** consumed. It provides an alternative reaction pathway but is regenerated at the end of the reaction and does not appear in the overall chemical equation.

## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_gTsyP8nDbV4Xwp7C`)

**FRONT**

Define **heterogeneous catalyst**.


**BACK**

A **heterogeneous catalyst** is one that is in a **different phase** to the reactants. A common example is a solid catalyst used with gaseous or liquid reactants.

A catalyst increases the rate of reaction by lowering the **..........**  energy, so a greater **..........**  of molecules have sufficient energy for an effective collision.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_vjz3xzDzJnzzSgYB`)

**FRONT**

How does a catalyst affect the Boltzmann distribution curve and the rate of reaction?


**BACK**

The Boltzmann distribution curve shape is **unchanged** (the temperature is the same). However, the lower *E*<sub>a</sub> means the area under the curve to the right of *E*<sub>a</sub> is larger, so a greater proportion of molecules can react and the rate increases.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_ngkygjBVwCwhz3BW`)

**FRONT**

Give one example each of a homogeneous catalyst and a heterogeneous catalyst used in industrial chemistry.


**BACK**

**Homogeneous:** concentrated H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> (acid) in esterification reactions (same liquid phase as reactants).

**Heterogeneous:** iron catalyst in the Haber process (solid catalyst with gaseous N<sub>2</sub> and H<sub>2</sub> reactants).

## Card 8 — true_or_false (`fl_9VJ8bHbSgc7HfQvb`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A catalyst changes the enthalpy change (ΔH) of a reaction.


**BACK**

**False.**

A catalyst only lowers the **activation energy** and provides an alternative pathway. The overall enthalpy change (Δ*H*) between reactants and products is **unchanged**.
