## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_kqCMxbKN92M8PVNn`)

**FRONT**

How do you determine the order of reaction with respect to a reactant from a table of experimental data?


**BACK**

Identify two experiments where **only that reactant's concentration changes** while all others remain constant. Compare the change in concentration to the change in rate to determine the order: if doubling the concentration doubles the rate → order 1; if it quadruples the rate → order 2; if no change → order 0.

Why does increasing temperature increase both the rate constant and the rate of reaction?


**BACK**

The rate of reaction depends on the rate constant *k*. At higher temperatures, more molecules exceed the activation energy, so *k* increases. Since rate = *k*[A]<sup>m</sup>[B]<sup>n</sup>, a higher *k* gives a higher rate even at the same concentrations.
