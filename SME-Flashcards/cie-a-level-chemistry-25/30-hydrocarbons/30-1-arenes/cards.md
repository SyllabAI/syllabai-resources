## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_YFysJFbSKDTJxVsy`)

**FRONT**

Define **Friedel-Crafts reaction**.


**BACK**

A **Friedel-Crafts reaction** is an electrophilic substitution reaction of a benzene ring with a halogenoalkane (alkylation) or an acyl chloride (acylation) in the presence of an anhydrous AlCl<sub>3</sub> catalyst.

**BACK**

Benzene is mixed with concentrated nitric acid (HNO<sub>3</sub>) and concentrated sulfuric acid (H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>) at a temperature between 25 and 60°C. The H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> generates the **NO**<sub>**2**</sub><sup>**+**</sup>** electrophile**.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_v4HDvRmdH8mCsTJ4`)

**FRONT**

Define **halogen carrier**.


**BACK**

A **halogen carrier** is an anhydrous Lewis acid catalyst (such as AlCl<sub>3</sub> or AlBr<sub>3</sub>) used in the halogenation of arenes. It accepts a lone pair from the halogen molecule to generate the electrophilic halogen cation (e.g. Cl<sup>+</sup> or Br<sup>+</sup>).


Spec links: `spcpt_T47tXfbF7rvThY8r`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_2YvVHts7KfT6KXXD`)

**FRONT**

What are the three steps of electrophilic substitution in arenes?


**BACK**

1. **Generation of the electrophile** (in situ from appropriate reagents).
2. **Electrophilic attack**: a pair of electrons from the benzene ring is donated to the electrophile, disrupting aromaticity.
3. **Regeneration of aromaticity**: heterolytic cleavage of the C–H bond returns electrons to the π system.


Spec links: `spcpt_T47tXfbF7rvThY8r`


## Card 12 — true_or_false (`fl_xzzCtjXfGCHQxmty`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

During electrophilic attack on benzene, the aromaticity of the ring is maintained throughout the reaction.


**BACK**

**False.**

Aromaticity is temporarily lost during electrophilic attack. The ring only has four π electrons and carries a positive charge spread over five carbons. Aromaticity is restored only in the final step by heterolytic cleavage of the C–H bond.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_Kq3CpH2HBDKyvbJs`)

**FRONT**

How is the Br<sup>+</sup> electrophile generated for the bromination of benzene?


**BACK**

Bromine forms a **dative bond** with the AlBr<sub>3</sub> halogen carrier by donating a lone pair from one of its bromine atoms into an empty 3p orbital of AlBr<sub>3</sub>. This polarises the Br–Br bond and generates **Br**<sup>**+**</sup> in situ.

**BACK**

A **heterolytic cleavage** is the breaking of a covalent bond in which both electrons in the bond go to one of the two atoms, generating ions rather than radicals.
