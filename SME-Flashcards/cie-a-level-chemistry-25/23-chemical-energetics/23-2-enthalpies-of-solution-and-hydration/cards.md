## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_4ckddNMG3psPwX9b`)

**FRONT**

Define **standard enthalpy change of solution**.


**BACK**

**Standard enthalpy change of solution** (Δ*H*<sub>sol</sub><sup>θ</sup>) is the enthalpy change when 1 mole of an ionic substance dissolves in sufficient water to form a very dilute solution under standard conditions.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_5yTG9GBkXGR7FzgM`)

**FRONT**

What type of bonds form between water molecules and ions during hydration, and which part of the water molecule is attracted to cations?


**BACK**

**Ion-dipole bonds** form during hydration. The δ<sup>-</sup> oxygen atom of water is attracted to positive ions (**cations**). The δ<sup>+</sup> hydrogen atoms are attracted to negative ions (**anions**).

## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_GckMzzxNBq8bCJKW`)

**FRONT**
