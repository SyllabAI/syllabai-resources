## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_pjQ23kkJywbJzsQG`)

**FRONT**

Define **Gibbs free energy**.


**BACK**

**Gibbs free energy** (*G*) is the energy that accounts for both the entropy change and the enthalpy change of a reaction, used to determine reaction feasibility.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_tDkM8BRYYp7tZGYk`)

**FRONT**

State the Gibbs equation and give the units of each term.


**BACK**

Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sub>reaction</sub><sup>θ</sup> - *T*Δ*S*<sub>system</sub><sup>θ</sup>

Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup>: kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>

Δ*H*<sub>reaction</sub><sup>θ</sup>: kJ mol<sup>-1</sup>

*T*: K

Δ*S*<sub>system</sub><sup>θ</sup>: J K<sup>-1</sup> mol<sup>-1</sup> (must be converted to **kJ K**<sup>**-1**</sup>** mol**<sup>**-1**</sup>)

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_RFBHWyKHhqvT3SwQ`)

**FRONT**

Calculate Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> for the reaction: 2NaHCO<sub>3</sub> (s) → Na<sub>2</sub>CO<sub>3</sub> (s) + H<sub>2</sub>O (l) + CO<sub>2</sub> (g) at 298 K.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_4dyZ8QFDxJqgvYyf`)

**FRONT**

Why is it insufficient to use only entropy change to predict whether a reaction is feasible?


**BACK**

**Feasibility** depends on both **entropy** and **enthalpy** changes. A reaction with a negative entropy change could still be feasible if it is sufficiently exothermic. The **Gibbs equation** combines both terms to give an accurate prediction.

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_3mcCGmCQcFcj7q33`)

**FRONT**

State the condition for a reaction to be feasible in terms of Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup>.


**BACK**

A reaction is feasible when Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> is **negative** (less than zero). When Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> is positive, the reaction is not feasible.

## Card 11 — true_or_false (`fl_qhTY3xQGW5Nj4Bhq`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

An exothermic reaction with a positive entropy change is feasible at all temperatures.


**BACK**

**True.**

When Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> is negative and Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> is positive, both terms in Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> = Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup> - *T*Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> are negative at any temperature, so Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> is always negative.

**FRONT**

Define **feasible reaction**.


**BACK**

A **feasible reaction** is one in which Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> is negative, indicating the reaction is thermodynamically likely to occur spontaneously.

## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_8zXdrN9f8RdNct79`)

**FRONT**

Explain why an exothermic reaction with a negative entropy change becomes less feasible at high temperatures.


**BACK**

At **high temperatures**, the -*T*Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> term becomes large and positive (since Δ*S*<sup>θ</sup> is negative). This positive term can overcome the negative Δ*H*<sup>θ</sup>, making Δ*G*<sup>θ</sup> positive and the reaction **not feasible**.
