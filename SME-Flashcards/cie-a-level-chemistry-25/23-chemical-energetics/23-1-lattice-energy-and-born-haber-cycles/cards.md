## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_3HxGY8thV6B7hWzj`)

Define **lattice energy**.


**BACK**

**Lattice energy** is the enthalpy change when 1 mole of an ionic compound is formed from its gaseous ions under standard conditions.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_RmyZ5XVz5ZDr8ydH`)

**FRONT**

Why is fluorine's first electron affinity less exothermic than chlorine's, despite fluorine having a higher nuclear charge?


**BACK**

Fluorine has a very small **atomic radius**, causing high **electron density** around the nucleus. This leads to increased repulsion between the incoming electron and existing electrons, reducing the overall attraction and making EA<sub>1</sub> less exothermic.

## Card 22 — question_and_answer (`fl_P5Tyw88trtvfMyxK`)

**FRONT**

List the steps required to construct a Born-Haber cycle for potassium chloride, KCl.


**BACK**

1. **Atomisation** of K (s) → K (g)
2. First **ionisation energy** of K (g) → K<sup>+</sup> (g)
3. **Atomisation** of ½Cl<sub>2</sub> (g) → Cl (g)
4. First **electron affinity** of Cl (g) → Cl<sup>-</sup> (g)
5. **Enthalpy of formation** of KCl (s)
6. Apply **Hess's law** to find **lattice energy**
