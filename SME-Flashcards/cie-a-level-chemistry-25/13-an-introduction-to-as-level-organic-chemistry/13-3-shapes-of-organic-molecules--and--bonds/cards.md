**BACK**

A **straight-chain organic molecule** is one in which all carbon atoms are connected to each other in one continuous, unbranched chain.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_9TTXmsFkJGpCthCq`)

**FRONT**

What distinguishes a branched organic molecule from a straight-chain molecule?


**BACK**

A **branched** organic molecule has one or more **side groups** (alkyl groups) attached to the main carbon chain, whereas a straight-chain molecule has no side branches.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_6vGYYzrkHfbTBfTz`)

**FRONT**

Define **sigma (σ) bond**.


**BACK**

A **sigma bond** is formed by the **end-on overlap** of atomic orbitals. Electron density is symmetrical about the line joining the two nuclei.


Spec links: `spcpt_qzx4VbjHZKNywYNz`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_3VC9xxF5324DyZvv`)

**FRONT**

What hybridisation state does a carbon atom have when it forms four sigma bonds, and what shape does this produce?


**BACK**

A carbon forming four σ bonds is **sp**<sup>**3**</sup>** hybridised**, giving a **tetrahedral** arrangement with bond angles of **109.5°**.


Spec links: `spcpt_QytGnRrdQn5z8m9n`


## Card 12 — true_or_false (`fl_hgdMr4nxqy5mb5qV`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A double bond consists of one sigma bond and one pi bond.


**BACK**

**True.**

A **double bond** is made up of one **σ bond** (end-on orbital overlap) and one **π bond** (sideways p orbital overlap).


Spec links: `spcpt_qzx4VbjHZKNywYNz`


## Card 13 — keyword_definition (`fl_Wp7dKrHwcrKpQznT`)

**FRONT**

Define **pi (π) bond**.


**BACK**

A **pi bond** is formed by the **sideways overlap** of p orbitals. The two electron clouds lie above and below (or in front of and behind) the plane of the bonded atoms.


Spec links: `spcpt_qzx4VbjHZKNywYNz`

## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_gGvDJ4DP2vVYrHD4`)

**FRONT**

Why does bond strength increase from single to double to triple bonds?


**BACK**

Each additional bond increases the **electron density** between the carbon atoms, making the bond **stronger** and harder to break. Bond strength order: single < double < triple.

## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_yCR5dVZnC92H8NBq`)

**FRONT**

Why does the presence of an sp<sup>2</sup> hybridised carbon force a molecule to adopt a trigonal planar shape?


**BACK**

An sp<sup>2</sup> carbon has three σ bonds that spread into a **trigonal planar** arrangement (120° bond angles) to **minimise repulsion** between bonding pairs of electrons.
