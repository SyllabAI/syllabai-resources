## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_YZP56jv3SGvZRbDV`)

**FRONT**

Define **acyl chloride**.


**BACK**

An **acyl chloride** is a carboxylic acid derivative in which the –OH group is replaced by –Cl, making it highly reactive towards nucleophilic addition-elimination reactions.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_W2xQDCxjqz5JBfTc`)

**FRONT**

What reagents convert a carboxylic acid into an acyl chloride, and what by-products does each produce?


**BACK**

Three reagents work: **PCl**<sub>**5**</sub> (solid) gives POCl<sub>3</sub> and HCl; **PCl**<sub>**3**</sub> (liquid, heat) gives H<sub>3</sub>PO<sub>3</sub>; **SOCl**<sub>**2**</sub> (liquid) gives SO<sub>2</sub> and HCl.

**FRONT**

Why is it more effective to synthesise an ester from an acyl chloride rather than directly from a carboxylic acid and alcohol?


**BACK**
