## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_jD2QgBq9n9VTSfzt`)

**FRONT**

Define **ester**.


**BACK**

An **ester** is an organic compound containing a -COOR functional group. Esters have characteristic smells and are used in perfumes, cosmetics and as solvents.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_ynrcRbYTJM7Btnch`)

**FRONT**

What are two advantages of preparing esters using acyl chlorides rather than carboxylic acids?


**BACK**

Acyl chlorides are **more reactive** so the ester forms faster, and the reaction **goes to completion** so there is no equilibrium mixture and the maximum yield of ester is obtained.


Spec links: `spcpt_h7kySjG6rT7x7yjn`


## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_4SQhQ7jZqtpK42bM`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The esterification of alcohols with carboxylic acids goes to completion, giving maximum yield.


**BACK**

**False.**

The esterification of alcohols with carboxylic acids is reversible and reaches an equilibrium, so the yield of ester is not maximised. Using acyl chlorides instead gives a complete reaction.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_n2XbD9MMfSGrDm9c`)

**FRONT**

What type of reaction occurs when an alcohol reacts with an acyl chloride to form an ester?


**BACK**

A **condensation** reaction (also described as an **addition-elimination** reaction). The alcohol adds across the C=O bond and HCl is eliminated.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_r4B54bSQ2tQH376T`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The first part of an ester's name comes from the alcohol used and the second part comes from the acyl chloride.


**BACK**

**True.**

For example, ethanol + ethanoyl chloride gives ethyl ethanoate: "ethyl" from ethanol and "ethanoate" from ethanoyl chloride.

## Card 9 — keyword_definition (`fl_ymj66YRmQRx8CyM4`)

**FRONT**

Define **esterification**.


**BACK**

**Esterification** is a condensation reaction in which an alcohol (or phenol) reacts with a carboxylic acid or acyl chloride to form an ester and a small molecule (water or HCl).
