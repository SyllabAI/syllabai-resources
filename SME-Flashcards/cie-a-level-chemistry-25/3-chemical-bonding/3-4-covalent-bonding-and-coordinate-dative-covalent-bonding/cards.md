## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_3bD6nxwD8vkvFKVC`)

**FRONT**

Define **covalent bond**.


**BACK**

A **covalent bond** is the electrostatic attraction between the nuclei of two non-metal atoms and their shared pair of electrons in the outer shell.


Spec links: `spcpt_WfbwJkmCX7gyZZqb`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_Cs4qhJHTjWwPG4Kb`)

**FRONT**

How many electrons are shared in a single, double and triple covalent bond?


**BACK**

A **single** bond shares **2** electrons (1 pair), a **double** bond shares **4** electrons (2 pairs), and a **triple** bond shares **6** electrons (3 pairs).

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_wgsj4xpwzkyhYMBD`)

**FRONT**

Define **coordinate bond**.


**BACK**

A **coordinate bond** (dative covalent bond) is a covalent bond in which both electrons in the shared pair are donated by the same atom, which must possess a lone pair.

**BACK**

**Hybridisation** is the mixing of atomic orbitals within the same shell to form new hybrid orbitals of equal energy, used to explain the bonding and geometry of covalent molecules.


Spec links: `spcpt_NbtHdZ2KrTzhrkdg`


## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_3V8t6kRd8G2WhMVp`)

**FRONT**

What orbitals combine to form sp<sup>3</sup> hybrid orbitals, and what geometry do they produce?


**BACK**

One **s orbital** and three **p orbitals** mix to form four sp<sup>3</sup> hybrid orbitals. These arrange with **tetrahedral geometry**, giving bond angles of **109.5°** (e.g. in methane, CH<sub>4</sub>).
