## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_5G8SgTycJtfVgVFT`)

**FRONT**

Define **metallic bonding**.


**BACK**

**Metallic bonding** is the strong electrostatic attraction between a lattice of positive metal ions and a surrounding sea of mobile delocalised electrons.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_jHHQJYh7ZvfbgmTs`)

**FRONT**

What happens to the outer electrons of metal atoms when they pack together to form a metallic lattice?


**BACK**

The outer electrons **delocalise**, becoming free to move throughout the structure. They are no longer associated with any single atom, forming a mobile 'sea' of electrons surrounding the positive metal ions.

## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_j2cFjXgt5kKNP5DN`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In metallic bonding, the metal atoms remain neutral and do not form ions.


**BACK**

**False.**

When outer electrons delocalise, the metal atoms become **positive ions** (cations). The structure consists of a lattice of these cations surrounded by delocalised electrons.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_K9dCg683HkH7KhWr`)

**FRONT**

Describe the arrangement of particles in a metallic lattice.


**BACK**

Metal atoms are arranged in a **regular, tightly packed lattice**. Delocalisation of outer electrons produces a lattice of positive metal ions surrounded by a mobile sea of delocalised electrons.

The delocalised electrons in metallic bonding are fixed to specific metal ions.


**BACK**

**False.**

## Card 8 — keyword_definition (`fl_hGxTBSXm9SFjdbwf`)

**FRONT**

Define **delocalised electrons**.


**BACK**

**Delocalised electrons** are electrons that are not associated with any single atom or bond but are free to move throughout a structure, as seen in metals and some covalent molecules.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_MX3CJJXPzFjZdy2R`)

**FRONT**

Why is metallic bonding described as strong?


**BACK**

Metallic bonding is strong because the **electrostatic attraction** between the lattice of positive metal ions and the surrounding delocalised electrons acts in all directions and requires a large amount of energy to overcome.
