## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_zryQMFmvX3xFjp2B`)

**FRONT**

Why are poly(alkenes) non-biodegradable?


**BACK**

Poly(alkenes) have **non-polar, saturated** carbon chains. This makes them **chemically inert**, so they cannot be broken down by biological or chemical processes under normal environmental conditions.

## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_KKwKd2mhrZwdfkvy`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Polyesters and polyamides can be broken down by hydrolysis, unlike poly(alkenes).


**BACK**

**True.**

Polyesters and polyamides contain hydrolysable ester and amide links, allowing them to be broken down by acid or alkaline hydrolysis. Poly(alkenes) lack these links and are non-biodegradable.

## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_8yhpDW2np7RjxSnz`)

**FRONT**

Define **photodegradation**.


**BACK**

A **photodegradation** is the breakdown of polymers using light energy. In polyesters and polyamides, carbonyl groups (C=O) absorb UV light, weakening and breaking the polymer chains.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_kTK2NHjmtwBj2Hbr`)

**FRONT**

What products are formed by the acid hydrolysis of a polyamide such as Kevlar?


**BACK**

Acid hydrolysis of Kevlar produces a **dicarboxylic acid** and **ammonium ions**. The amide links are broken down by water in the presence of acid.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_bdpnKM7KYBgtCg4C`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Photodegradable polymers in landfill sites may not degrade effectively because they lack access to UV light.


**BACK**

**True.**

In landfill sites, polymeric materials are buried under other waste. Without access to UV light, photodegradable polyesters and polyamides cannot absorb the energy needed to break down.

## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_wKtnZpbXRvyRHYDC`)

**FRONT**

How can the energy released by burning poly(alkenes) be used, and what are the environmental consequences?


**BACK**

Burning poly(alkenes) releases energy that can generate **electricity**. However, complete combustion produces **CO**<sub>**2**</sub>** and water**, contributing to the **greenhouse effect** and global warming.

## Card 8 — keyword_definition (`fl_kKtYXzrFStJXBqqR`)

**FRONT**

Define **biodegradable polymer**.


**BACK**

A **biodegradable polymer** is a polymer that can be broken down by biological or chemical processes in the environment. Polyesters and polyamides are biodegradable via hydrolysis; poly(alkenes) are not.
