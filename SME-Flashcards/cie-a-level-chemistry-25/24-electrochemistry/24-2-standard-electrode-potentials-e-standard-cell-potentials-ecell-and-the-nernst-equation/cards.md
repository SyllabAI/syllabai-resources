## Card 46 — keyword_definition (`fl_dcqrR4bDRdGpkPRw`)

**FRONT**

Define **standard Gibbs free energy change (Δ*****G***<sup>**ɔ**</sup>**)**.


**BACK**

A **standard Gibbs free energy change** (Δ*G*<sup>ɔ</sup>) is the maximum energy available to do useful work when a reaction occurs under standard conditions (298 K, 100 kPa, unit activity). It combines enthalpy and entropy: Δ*G*<sup>ɔ</sup> = Δ*H*<sup>ɔ</sup> − *T*Δ*S*<sup>ɔ</sup>. A negative value indicates a **spontaneous** reaction.


Spec links: `spcpt_9h2zdD3sCnvBMsK8`


## Card 47 — question_and_answer (`fl_6nP49NgGytvTzx2Z`)

**FRONT**

State the equation linking standard Gibbs free energy change to standard cell potential, defining all symbols.


**BACK**

Δ*G*<sup>ɔ</sup> = −*n* × *E*<sub>cell</sub><sup>ɔ</sup> × *F*

- Δ*G*<sup>ɔ</sup> = **standard Gibbs free energy** (J mol<sup>-1</sup>)
- *n* = **moles of electrons transferred**
- *E*<sub>cell</sub><sup>ɔ</sup> = **standard cell potential** (V)
- *F* = **Faraday constant** (96 500 C mol<sup>-1</sup>)


Spec links: `spcpt_9h2zdD3sCnvBMsK8`

Define **Faraday constant (*****F*****) in free energy calculations**.
