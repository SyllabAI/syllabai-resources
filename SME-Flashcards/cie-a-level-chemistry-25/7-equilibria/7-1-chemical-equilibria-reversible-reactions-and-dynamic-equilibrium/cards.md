**FRONT**

What two conditions must be met for a dynamic equilibrium to exist?


**BACK**

A dynamic equilibrium requires: (1) the rate of the forward reaction equals the rate of the backward reaction, and (2) the system is **closed** so no reactants or products can escape.

What are the steps to calculate *K*<sub>p</sub> when only moles of each gas and total pressure are given?


**BACK**

1. Calculate the **total moles** of all gases.
2. Find the **mole fraction** of each gas (moles / total moles).
