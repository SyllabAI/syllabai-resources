## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_ZDBT5rGsxjRMcwM3`)

**FRONT**

Define **zwitterion**.


**BACK**

A **zwitterion** is a species that carries both a positive (-NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup>) and a negative (-COO<sup>-</sup>) charge on the same molecule. Amino acids form zwitterions in aqueous solution.


Spec links: `spcpt_nkkvrrBtBQrm3jc8`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_FRDHJCxdCYx9B9sx`)

**FRONT**

Why are amino acids described as amphoteric?


**BACK**

Amino acids contain both a basic **amino group** (-NH<sub>2</sub>) and an acidic **carboxylic acid group** (-COOH). They can act as both acids and bases, which is the definition of amphoteric.


Spec links: `spcpt_nkkvrrBtBQrm3jc8`


## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_NR8XW5HqtymHFwcf`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

At low pH, the carboxylate group (-COO<sup>-</sup>) of a zwitterion accepts a proton to reform -COOH.


**BACK**

**True.**

Adding acid lowers the pH. The -COO<sup>-</sup> group accepts an H<sup>+</sup> to reform the -COOH group, causing the zwitterion to become a **positively charged ion**.

## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_65ThRcmqKbhjrrBY`)

**FRONT**

Define **isoelectric point**.


**BACK**

An **isoelectric point** is the pH at which an amino acid exists predominantly as a neutral zwitterion, with no net overall charge.


Spec links: `spcpt_nkkvrrBtBQrm3jc8`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_BJqg3j89XXVs43DM`)

**FRONT**

What happens to the -NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> group of a zwitterion when a base is added?


**BACK**

The -NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup> group **donates** a proton (H<sup>+</sup>) to the added base, reforming the -NH<sub>2</sub> group. This causes the zwitterion to become a **negatively charged ion**.

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_4mrCRBrSpdnxZM3c`)

**FRONT**

How does an amino acid solution act as a buffer?


**BACK**

The zwitterion can **accept protons** (via -COO<sup>-</sup>) when acid is added or **donate protons** (via -NH<sub>3</sub><sup>+</sup>) when base is added. This resists changes in pH, making it a buffer solution.

## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_qX55kSf2ckNJRj6p`)

**FRONT**

Define **peptide bond**.


**BACK**

A **peptide bond** is an amide bond formed between the -NH<sub>2</sub> group of one amino acid and the -COOH group of another in a condensation reaction, with H<sub>2</sub>O eliminated.


Spec links: `spcpt_G2nMVpt96f6Jvkms`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_sWZ3qNHDT6c2SrHR`)

**FRONT**

What small molecule is eliminated when two amino acids join to form a dipeptide?


**BACK**

**Water (H**<sub>**2**</sub>**O)** is eliminated. The -NH<sub>2</sub> group of one amino acid reacts with the -COOH group of another in a condensation reaction.


Spec links: `spcpt_G2nMVpt96f6Jvkms`

**BACK**

## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_7zMvmY2KSxRNTkQz`)

**FRONT**

In electrophoresis, what determines the rate at which an ion moves toward an electrode?


**BACK**

Two factors determine the rate:

1. **Size** of the ion: larger ions move more slowly.
2. **Charge** of the ion: more highly charged ions move more quickly.
