## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_PT6zXS9DNWTbtqfY`)

**FRONT**

What is a carboxylic acid?


**BACK**

A carboxylic acid is a compound containing the functional group carboxyl, -COOH.



## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_ChfR7StQMgNXKQMh`)

**FRONT**

State the general formula for carboxylic acids.


**BACK**

The general formula of a carboxylic acid is C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n+1</sub>COOH or RCOOH.

## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_HztQJS5rZNj7F85F`)

## Card 17 — keyword_definition (`fl_xYwfKHtJ2hJQf9kR`)

**FRONT**

Define **neutralisation reaction**.


**BACK**

A neutralisation reaction is a chemical reaction between an acid and a base that produces a salt and water.
