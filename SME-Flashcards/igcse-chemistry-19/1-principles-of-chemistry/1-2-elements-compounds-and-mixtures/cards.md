## Card 38 — keyword_definition (`fl_kXkhq8bCxtFf4XBP`)

**FRONT**

State the **equation **to calculate the retention factor (*R*<sub>f</sub> value).


**BACK**

The **equation **for the retention factor (*R*<sub>f</sub> value) is:

*R*<sub>f</sub> = $\frac{\mathrm{Distance} \mathrm{travelled} \mathrm{by} \mathrm{sample}}{\mathrm{Distance} \mathrm{travelled} \mathrm{by} \mathrm{solvent} \mathrm{front}}$
