## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_rVZJxgJpYqnGRCRn`)

**FRONT**

Find the centre and radius of the circle $x^{2} - 6 x + y^{2} + y - 15 = 0$.


**BACK**

Completing the square gives `\left(x - 3\right)^{2} + \left(y + \frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{97}{4}`

Centre `\left(3, -\frac{1}{2}\right)`, radius $\frac{\sqrt{97}}{2}$

## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_drWj7q442hZjRHWs`)

**FRONT**

What does the angle in a semicircle property say?


**BACK**

Any angle at the circumference in a **semicircle** is a **right angle**.

Equivalently, if a triangle is right-angled, its **hypotenuse is a diameter** of its circumcircle.

## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_bcVfhGwkKxmjZw2w`)

**FRONT**

How can you show that a triangle is right-angled?


**BACK**

Two ways, depending on what you are given.

If you know the **side lengths**, show that they satisfy **Pythagoras' theorem**.

If the three vertices lie on a circle, show that one side is a **diameter**, since the angle in a semicircle is a right angle.

## Card 26 — fill_in_the_blanks (`fl_4GCZjCBgdzN3tKwX`)

**FRONT**

The gradient of a tangent to a circle is the negative `\_\_\_\_\_\_` of the gradient of the radius at the point where the tangent touches.


**BACK**

The gradient of a tangent is the negative **reciprocal** of the gradient of the radius at that point.
