## Card 40 — keyword_definition (`fl_m2XhCgZDD4JcdhzG`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for specific heat capacity.


**BACK**

The equation for specific heat capacity is $\DeltaE=mc\Deltaθ$

Where:

- $\DeltaE$ = change in energy, measured in joules (J)
- $m$ = mass, measured in kilograms (kg)
- $c$ = specific heat capacity, measured in joules per kilogram per degree Celsius (J/kg °C)
- $\Deltaθ$ = change in temperature, measured in degrees Celsius (°C)



## Card 41 — question_and_answer (`fl_yycFxPsW9BcT53br`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for specific heat capacity when specific heat capacity is the subject of the equation?


**BACK**

The equation for specific heat capacity when specific heat capacity is the subject of the equation is: $c=\frac{\DeltaE}{m\Deltaθ}$

Where:

- $c$ = specific heat capacity, measured in joules per kilogram per degree Celsius (J/kg °C)
- $m$ = mass, measured in kilograms (kg)
- $\Deltaθ$ = change in temperature, measured in degrees Celsius (°C)
- $\DeltaE$ = change in energy, measured in joules (J)
