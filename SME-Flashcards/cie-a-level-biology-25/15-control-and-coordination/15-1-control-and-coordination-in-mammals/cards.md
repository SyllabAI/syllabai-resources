## Card 35 — question_and_answer (`fl_6ZgRFjm4DNsjrtYx`)

**FRONT**

How is the **resting potential maintained**?


**BACK**

The **sodium–potassium pump** actively pumps **3 Na^+^ out** and **2 K^+^ in**.

The membrane is more permeable to **K^+^**, which leaks back out.

This leaves the inside **negative** relative to the outside (about –70 mV).


Spec links: `spcpt_6ssDzDFZsFgMDpqD`


## Card 36 — keyword_definition (`fl_dtzCTpydWWQQfNc6`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

An **action potential** is a rapid, temporary **reversal** of the membrane potential, from about **–70 mV to +30 mV**.


Spec links: `spcpt_6ssDzDFZsFgMDpqD`


## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_324mQdXWgD4YnsvR`)

**FRONT**

Describe the **events during an action potential**.


**BACK**

**Depolarisation:** voltage-gated **Na^+^ channels** open, Na<sup>+</sup> floods in, the membrane reverses to about **+30 mV**.

**Repolarisation:** Na<sup>+</sup> channels close and voltage-gated **K^+^ channels** open, so K<sup>+</sup> leaves and the potential falls.

**Restoration:** a brief **hyperpolarisation** occurs, then the **Na^+^/K^+^ pump** re-establishes the resting potential.


Spec links: `spcpt_6ssDzDFZsFgMDpqD`

## Card 59 — question_and_answer (`fl_CVrTZYcKVwrk8fkc`)

**FRONT**

Explain how an impulse is **transmitted across a cholinergic synapse**.


**BACK**

An action potential reaches the **presynaptic membrane** and opens voltage-gated **calcium ion channels**, so Ca<sup>2+</sup> enters.

Ca<sup>2+</sup> causes vesicles to fuse with the membrane and release **acetylcholine** by **exocytosis**.

Acetylcholine diffuses across the cleft and binds **receptors** on the postsynaptic membrane, opening Na<sup>+</sup> channels.

If **threshold** is reached, a new action potential is generated in the postsynaptic neurone.

## Card 83 — question_and_answer (`fl_TkPqdP57h3jC2g5D`)

**FRONT**

Describe the **cross-bridge cycle**.


**BACK**

**Myosin heads** bind to the exposed sites on actin, forming **cross-bridges**.

The **power stroke** pulls the actin towards the centre of the sarcomere; **ADP + P~i~** are released.

**ATP** binds to the myosin head, detaching it; its hydrolysis re-cocks the head, ready to bind again.
