## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_vxFykg85yfGqqCcX`)

**FRONT**

How does the Venus flytrap **avoid closing on false alarms**?


**BACK**

A trigger hair must be stimulated **twice** (or two hairs touched) within about **20–30 seconds** before the trap closes.


Spec links: `spcpt_zc7ZZP4vXp7Kpzwc`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_tdSnXKb3cxX5kBh5`)

**FRONT**

What happens when a **trigger hair is bent**?


**BACK**

It produces a **receptor potential**.

If a second stimulus follows quickly, an **action potential** is generated and spreads rapidly across the leaf.


Spec links: `spcpt_zc7ZZP4vXp7Kpzwc`


## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_Qp5Vd74ZF3Cnq73k`)

**FRONT**

Explain how the **closure of the trap is achieved**.


**BACK**

The **action potential** causes a rapid change in **turgor pressure** and cell volume in the cells of the lobes.

This makes the leaf snap from **convex to concave**, closing the trap in a fraction of a second.

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_TgWJMWBxmb7MH3M4`)

**FRONT**

Explain how **auxin causes cell elongation**.


**BACK**

Auxin stimulates **proton (H^+^) pumps** in the cell surface membrane to pump **H^+^ into the cell wall**.

The wall becomes **acidic**, activating **expansins** that loosen bonds between cellulose microfibrils.

The wall becomes more **flexible**, so the cell takes up water by **osmosis** and **elongates**.


Spec links: `spcpt_MyCNYNJrpdPZFvT2`

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_N6wmkv7q2kG3kV6z`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

The low pH activates **expansins**, which loosen the links between **cellulose microfibrils**, making the wall more **plastic/extensible** so it can stretch.

## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_cMsGhrhtw9b52tXk`)

**FRONT**

Describe the **role of gibberellin in the germination of barley**.


**BACK**

On hydration, the **embryo** produces **gibberellin**.

Gibberellin diffuses to the **aleurone layer** and switches on the genes for **amylase**.

Amylase hydrolyses **starch** in the endosperm into **maltose/glucose**, providing the embryo with substrates for growth.


Spec links: `spcpt_y5b3cPCpXFMprgpt`
