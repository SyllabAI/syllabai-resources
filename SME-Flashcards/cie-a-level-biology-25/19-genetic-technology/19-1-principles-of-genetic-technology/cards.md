## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_pR54rwQPhj4Jj7v2`)

**FRONT**

Why is it possible to combine DNA from different species into recombinant DNA?


**BACK**

Because all organisms have the **same DNA structure**, with the same **complementary base pairing**, so DNA from different species can be joined together.

The **genetic code is universal**, so the transferred gene is then **expressed** as the same polypeptide in the host organism.

## Card 45 — question_and_answer (`fl_xNrqtfD8Q656Wbq8`)
