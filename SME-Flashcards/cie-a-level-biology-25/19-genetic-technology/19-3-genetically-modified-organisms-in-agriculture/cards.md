## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_mQMDQBvwSyr75v5z`)

**FRONT**

Define **genetically modified organism (GMO)**.


**BACK**

An organism whose **genome has been altered by genetic engineering**, e.g. by inserting a gene from another organism.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_M9mhQb2nyrMz48sZ`)

**FRONT**

How can **genetic engineering** help meet the **global demand for food**?


**BACK**

By improving the **quality and productivity** of farmed animals and crop plants — e.g. faster growth, higher yields, pest or herbicide resistance.


Spec links: `spcpt_7WNq8SjWBgf8SvMG`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_KJ8fDNxh2xfKKQyz`)

**FRONT**

How has **GM salmon** been engineered to improve productivity?


**BACK**

It carries genes that keep **growth hormone production switched on**, so it **grows to market size much faster** than normal salmon.


Spec links: `spcpt_7WNq8SjWBgf8SvMG`


## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_rD7kP2D8VmrRk8Jy`)

**FRONT**

What is the benefit of **herbicide resistance** in GM soybean?


**BACK**

Farmers can spray herbicide to kill weeds **without harming the crop**, reducing competition and increasing yield.


Spec links: `spcpt_7WNq8SjWBgf8SvMG`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_M28Cfd7vFWmvRfsb`)

**FRONT**

How does **insect-resistant GM cotton (Bt cotton)** protect the crop?


**BACK**

It contains a **Bt gene** that makes the plant produce a **toxin lethal to insect pests**, reducing crop damage and pesticide use.


Spec links: `spcpt_7WNq8SjWBgf8SvMG`

## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_5qSVmZJZcKFRZB2P`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Bt cotton is sprayed with a bacterial toxin to kill insect pests.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **Bt gene** makes the **plant itself** produce the toxin, which is why **pesticide use** falls.

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_KXYKgqSQkDDP3skm`)

**FRONT**

State two potential benefits of using GMOs in food production.


**BACK**

**Higher yields and better-quality food**, helping to feed a growing population.

**Reduced pesticide/herbicide use** and crops that can grow in poorer conditions.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_KDmNPpNftMwfskKR`)

**FRONT**

State two **environmental** concerns about GM crops.


**BACK**

Transferred genes may **spread to wild plants** (e.g. herbicide resistance passing to weeds).

Possible reduction in **biodiversity** and harm to **non-target organisms**.


Spec links: `spcpt_hYHpX6wSwBSG2nrS`


## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_MSysypYKK9SZSyzV`)

**FRONT**

State one social or economic concern about GMOs in agriculture.


**BACK**

Farmers may become **dependent on buying seed** from large biotech companies, and GM seed may be **unaffordable** for poorer farmers.


Spec links: `spcpt_hYHpX6wSwBSG2nrS`

Possible **unknown long-term health effects** or introduction of **allergens**, though no harm has been demonstrated in approved GM foods.

## Card 13 — true_or_false (`fl_VwRhZcHYYcBTHckK`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Genes from GM crops could spread to wild plant populations.


**BACK**

**True.**

**Cross-pollination** can transfer genes (e.g. herbicide resistance) to wild relatives or weeds.
