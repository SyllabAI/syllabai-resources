## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_JGdvq4Vb8gcP4x3w`)

**FRONT**

Define **extinction**.


**BACK**

The **permanent loss** of a species, when **all** of its members have died out.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_PSJb59JxY7CVPtpg`)

**FRONT**

State **four** causes by which populations and species can become **extinct**.


**BACK**

1. **Climate change**

1. **Competition**

1. **Hunting** by humans

1. **Degradation and loss of habitats**


Spec links: `spcpt_yKGpMjjHpVpZPGzF`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_WXWcsDpVhWVnZt6v`)

**FRONT**

How can **climate change** cause a species to become extinct?


**BACK**

Conditions may change **faster than the species can adapt** or migrate, and its habitat or food sources may be lost — so numbers fall until it dies out.


Spec links: `spcpt_yKGpMjjHpVpZPGzF`


## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_T5mn6tn4wjYzXBvX`)

**FRONT**

How can **competition** lead to extinction?


**BACK**

A species may be **outcompeted** for resources (e.g. by an invasive species) and so its population declines until it dies out.


Spec links: `spcpt_yKGpMjjHpVpZPGzF`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_377qpqs8PRTV5PV3`)

**FRONT**

How does **habitat loss and degradation** cause extinction?


**BACK**

It reduces available **food, shelter and breeding sites** and **fragments** populations, so numbers fall below a viable level.


Spec links: `spcpt_yKGpMjjHpVpZPGzF`


## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_3nsFCSPT2rprn5p5`)

**FRONT**

How can **hunting by humans** cause a species to become extinct?


**BACK**

Individuals are removed **faster than they can reproduce and be replaced**, so the population falls below the level needed to survive.


Spec links: `spcpt_yKGpMjjHpVpZPGzF`

**FRONT**

**BACK**

With more species and more interlinked **food webs**, the loss of any one species has **less impact**, so the ecosystem can recover more easily from change.

An **embryo** (e.g. produced by IVF) is transferred into the **uterus of a female** so that it can develop.

## Card 27 — keyword_definition (`fl_zqd5jFX2H6fKRfXm`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

An embryo is implanted into a **surrogate female** (of the same or a closely related species) who carries the pregnancy but is **not the genetic mother**.

**BACK**

Spec links: `spcpt_wpxYk2CNdKqk5R42`

**BACK**

They have **no natural predators, competitors or diseases** to keep their population in check.
