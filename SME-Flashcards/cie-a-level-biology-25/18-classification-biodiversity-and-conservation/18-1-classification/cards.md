## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_HbKHx5xpkHyq8Nbf`)

**FRONT**

Define a **species** using the **biological species concept**.


**BACK**

A group of organisms with **similar morphology and physiology** that can **interbreed** to produce **fertile offspring**, and are **reproductively isolated** from other species.


Spec links: `spcpt_yv2J99fDnzNQnd5V`


## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_768HhWqYR7sJn3Xr`)

**FRONT**

Define the **morphological species concept**.


**BACK**

Organisms are classified as the same species based on their shared **physical features** (observable **morphology** and **anatomy**).


Spec links: `spcpt_yv2J99fDnzNQnd5V`


## Card 4 — keyword_definition (`fl_hXt5hkpxYqnbYTjR`)

**FRONT**

Define the **ecological species concept**.


**BACK**

A species is defined by the **ecological niche** it occupies — the role it plays in its environment.


Spec links: `spcpt_yv2J99fDnzNQnd5V`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_WMwJBHmGXs7WTmy2`)

**FRONT**

Give one **limitation** of the **biological species concept**.


**BACK**

It **cannot** be applied to organisms that reproduce **asexually**, or to species known only from **fossils** (they cannot be tested for interbreeding).


Spec links: `spcpt_yv2J99fDnzNQnd5V`
