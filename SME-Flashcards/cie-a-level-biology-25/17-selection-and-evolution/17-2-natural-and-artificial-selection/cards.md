## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_qJxkKSMtfZy8YBnG`)

**FRONT**

Define **natural selection**.


**BACK**

**Natural selection** is the process by which organisms best adapted to their environment **survive** and **reproduce**, passing on their advantageous alleles to the next generation.


Spec links: `spcpt_NfbRShNhYKWHVFj4`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_8ssGV6pphytVY39G`)

**FRONT**

Outline the process of **natural selection**.


**BACK**

Step 1: Populations produce more **offspring** than the environment can support.

Step 2: There is **variation**, and individuals compete for resources.

Step 3: Individuals with **advantageous alleles** are better adapted and more likely to survive.

Step 4: Survivors **reproduce** and pass on the advantageous alleles.

Step 5: The **frequency** of the advantageous allele increases over generations.


Spec links: `spcpt_NfbRShNhYKWHVFj4`

**BACK**

Step 4: The resistance allele **increases in frequency** in the population.

## Card 38 — question_and_answer (`fl_84zxyxRQ5CPbW6fX`)

**FRONT**

Outline the **principles** of selective breeding.


**BACK**

Step 1: Select individuals with the **desired** characteristics.

Step 2: **Breed** these individuals together.

Step 3: Select the **offspring** with the best characteristics.

Step 4: Breed these selected offspring together.

Step 5: **Repeat** over many generations.


Spec links: `spcpt_wSPxkBCBMp86cNMd`
