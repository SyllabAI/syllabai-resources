## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_NnRXB6rFDFmhHWSp`)

**FRONT**

When is the **t-test** used?


**BACK**

To compare the **means** of **two samples** of continuous, normally distributed data, to see whether the difference between them is **significant**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Ccd47V7YjMXN9d89`


## Card 17 — keyword_definition (`fl_cHngn5GTWXt5Nqvd`)

**FRONT**

Define **null hypothesis**.


**BACK**

The **null hypothesis** states that there is **no significant difference** between the two samples, any difference being due to **chance**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Ccd47V7YjMXN9d89`


## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_khtFy7fjt3MQ6MMc`)

**FRONT**

What conditions must the data meet to use a t-test?


**BACK**

The data must be **continuous** and approximately **normally distributed**.

The two samples should have similar **variance**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Ccd47V7YjMXN9d89`


## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_9zydBrybCndJTVyK`)

**FRONT**

How are the **degrees of freedom** calculated for a t-test?


**BACK**

Degrees of freedom = **n~1~ + n~2~ − 2**

(the two sample sizes added together, minus 2).


Spec links: `spcpt_Ccd47V7YjMXN9d89`


## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_VN5W83W9t4RZJXcb`)

**FRONT**

How do you interpret the calculated t against the **critical value** (at p = 0.05)?


**BACK**

If calculated **t ≥ critical value**: reject the null hypothesis — the difference is **significant**.

If calculated **t < critical value**: accept the null hypothesis — **no significant** difference.


Spec links: `spcpt_Ccd47V7YjMXN9d89`

## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_yVHBHGsKkdWZMWBg`)

**FRONT**

Outline the **steps** to carry out a t-test.


**BACK**

Step 1: State the **null hypothesis**.

Step 2: Calculate the **mean** and **standard deviation** of each sample.

Step 3: Substitute into the formula to find **t**.

Step 4: Find the **degrees of freedom** and read off the critical value at p = 0.05.

Step 5: **Compare** and accept or reject the null hypothesis.
