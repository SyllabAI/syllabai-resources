## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_kMjFP5Mnrz4rcbmV`)

**FRONT**

Define **evolution**.


**BACK**

**Evolution** is the change in the **allele frequencies** (gene pool) of a population over many generations, which can lead to the formation of new **species**.

**BACK**

This happens as a result of changes to **gene pools** from generation to generation, driven by natural selection.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_BDc9QDyDbP7ZSPfh`)

**FRONT**

How can **DNA sequence data** show evolutionary relationships between species?


**BACK**

The more similar the **base sequences** of two species' DNA, the more **closely related** they are.

They also shared a common ancestor more **recently**.


Spec links: `spcpt_6KnTSCsYcDPKj5wj`


## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_CQ6Ww4rnMHFn5P8x`)

**FRONT**

Why is comparing **DNA sequences** more reliable than comparing physical features?


**BACK**

It directly compares the **genetic material**.

Closely related species have accumulated **fewer differences** by mutation, giving objective evidence of relatedness.

**BACK**

**Sympatric speciation** occurs **without** geographical separation, due to **ecological** or **behavioural** isolation within the same area.


Spec links: `spcpt_bVcGtBQP2HmgSMWT`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_MCdCsms3NHxdFXQd`)

**FRONT**

Outline how **allopatric speciation** occurs.


**BACK**

Step 1: A physical **barrier** geographically separates two populations.

Step 2: **Gene flow** between them stops.

Step 3: Different **selection pressures**, mutations and drift change each gene pool differently.

Step 4: The populations can no longer **interbreed** to produce fertile offspring — they are separate species.


Spec links: `spcpt_bVcGtBQP2HmgSMWT`


## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_vzfXFZGW9MPFCTTd`)

**FRONT**

Give two types of isolation that can cause **sympatric speciation**.


**BACK**

**Ecological** isolation — occupying different habitats or niches.

**Behavioural** isolation — different courtship behaviours or breeding times.
