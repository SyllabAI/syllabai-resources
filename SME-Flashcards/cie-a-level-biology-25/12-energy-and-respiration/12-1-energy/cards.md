## Card 30 — question_and_answer (`fl_syfWvVprCzgpPXMR`)

**FRONT**

In a respirometer, what is the role of **potassium hydroxide (KOH)**?


**BACK**

It **absorbs carbon dioxide**, so any change in gas volume is due only to **oxygen uptake**.


Spec links: `spcpt_qShskGsjbkRgc55F`


## Card 31 — question_and_answer (`fl_zqngfKR6zbcJd8My`)

**FRONT**

Outline how a respirometer measures the **oxygen uptake** of germinating seeds.


**BACK**

Seeds are sealed in a tube with **KOH** to absorb CO<sub>2</sub>.

As O<sub>2</sub> is used up, gas volume falls and the coloured fluid in the manometer moves **towards the seeds**.

The distance moved measures the volume of O<sub>2</sub> consumed.


Spec links: `spcpt_qShskGsjbkRgc55F`


## Card 32 — question_and_answer (`fl_rCZTNjnxk8mvn3SS`)

**FRONT**

How is a respirometer used to determine the **RQ** of an organism?


**BACK**

Measure O<sub>2</sub> uptake with CO<sub>2</sub> absorbed (KOH present).

Then repeat **without** the CO<sub>2</sub> absorbent to measure the net volume change (CO<sub>2</sub> produced − O<sub>2</sub> used).

Use both readings to calculate RQ = CO<sub>2</sub> produced ÷ O<sub>2</sub> used.


Spec links: `spcpt_qShskGsjbkRgc55F`


## Card 33 — question_and_answer (`fl_nCPWzFPtDvVPt3Mh`)

**FRONT**

Why should a respirometer be kept in a **water bath** at constant temperature?


**BACK**

Gas volume changes with temperature.

A constant temperature ensures volume changes are due to **respiration only**.
