## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_7nYJkvfvdRRzdxfV`)

**FRONT**

Why are the **ventricle** walls thicker than the **atrial** walls?


**BACK**

The **ventricles** pump blood much further, so they need more **muscle** to generate a higher **pressure**.

The **atria** only pump blood into the ventricles just below them.


Spec links: `spcpt_zXHNZ2vvNh5ShMXy`


## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_zdJvSjKB3QX5nQ5C`)

**FRONT**

Why is the **left ventricle** wall thicker than the **right**?


**BACK**

The **left ventricle** pumps blood around the **whole body**, so it must generate a much higher **pressure**.

The **right ventricle** only pumps to the **lungs**, which are close by and would be damaged by high pressure.


Spec links: `spcpt_zXHNZ2vvNh5ShMXy`

**FRONT**

## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_pg9GKhxmRTcSFTzw`)

**FRONT**

What happens during **atrial systole**?


**BACK**

The **atria contract**, pushing the remaining blood into the ventricles.

The **atrioventricular valves are open**.


Spec links: `spcpt_5z8DMsbVrxK76zg2`


## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_JDkmznc7SzYRjnPW`)

**FRONT**

What happens to the valves during **ventricular systole**?


**BACK**

Rising ventricular pressure closes the **atrioventricular valves**.

When ventricular pressure exceeds arterial pressure, the **semilunar valves open** and blood is forced out.


Spec links: `spcpt_5z8DMsbVrxK76zg2`


## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_ZNPBH3nyzzkKsycj`)

**FRONT**

What causes the **semilunar valves to close**?


**BACK**

During **diastole**, ventricular pressure falls below arterial pressure.

The higher arterial pressure pushes the semilunar valves shut, preventing backflow.


Spec links: `spcpt_5z8DMsbVrxK76zg2`

## Card 27 — question_and_answer (`fl_Gsrpdv8GGPWz4vSv`)

**FRONT**

What is the role of the **Purkyne tissue**?


**BACK**

It conducts the wave of excitation down the **septum** to the **apex** of the heart.

This makes the ventricles contract from the **apex upwards**, pushing blood up towards the arteries.
