## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_Q2yYbkdqPxqXqsq5`)

**FRONT**

Define **homeostasis**.


**BACK**

**Homeostasis** is the maintenance of a **constant internal environment**, keeping conditions within **narrow limits** despite changes in internal and external factors.


Spec links: `spcpt_xPCS7cStXwQ2n9mR`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_dcWphyk6vnrhZxqx`)

**FRONT**

Why is **homeostasis** important in mammals?


**BACK**

Cells work most efficiently in **near-optimum conditions**.

Maintaining a stable internal environment (e.g. **core temperature**, **blood glucose** and **blood water potential**) ensures **enzymes** and cells function properly.


Spec links: `spcpt_xPCS7cStXwQ2n9mR`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_SqyyNdZ6YcQfyMTq`)

**FRONT**

Outline the general sequence of a **homeostatic control** pathway.


**BACK**

**Stimulus** (change in internal or external factor)

→ **receptor** detects the change

→ **coordination system** (nervous or endocrine)

→ **effector** (muscle or gland)

→ **response** that restores the norm.

## Card 48 — question_and_answer (`fl_DfDGRhGvRz7t4ydM`)

**FRONT**

Each response **reverses** the change, returning glucose to normal.
