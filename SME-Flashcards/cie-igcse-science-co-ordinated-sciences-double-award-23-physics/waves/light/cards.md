## Card 31 — question_and_answer (`fl_B9RdSDYfTr3Xx9FW`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for refractive index as a ratio of angles. **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for the refractive index as a ratio of angles is:

$n = \frac{\mathrm{sin}i}{\mathrm{sin}r}$

Where:

- $n$ = refractive index of the material, which has no units
- $i$ = angle of incidence, measured in degrees (°)
- $r$ = angle of refraction, measured in degrees (°)
