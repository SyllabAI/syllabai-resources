## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_7WgK54RbkKVXxgpD`)

**FRONT**

Mars is 227.9 million km from the Sun and the speed of light is 3 × 10<sup>8</sup> m/s. How long does it take light to travel from the Sun to Mars?


**BACK**

It takes light **760 s** or **12.6 min** to travel from the Sun to Mars

- distance from the Sun to Mars = 227.9 million km = 227.9 × 10<sup>9</sup> m
- $t i m e = \frac{distance}{speedoflight}$
- $t i m e = \frac{227.9\times10^{9}}{3\times10^{8}} = 759 . 6 s$
- $t i m e = \frac{759.6}{60} = 12 . 6 m i n$

## Card 25 — question_and_answer (`fl_wFtRX5FgB2fHVRYY`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for orbital speed *v*, in terms of orbital radius *r* and orbital period *T*. **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for orbital speed is:

$v = \frac{2πr}{T}$

Where:

- $v$ = orbital speed, measured in m/s
- $r$ = average radius of the orbit, measured in m
- $T$ = orbital period, measured in s
