## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_JGsdrv2j3hgynGTb`)

**FRONT**

What is the equation for calculating velocity?


**BACK**

The equation for velocity is $v=\frac{s}{t}$

Where:

- $v$ = velocity, measured in metres per second (m/s)
- $s$ = displacement, measured in metres (m)
- $t$ = time, measured in seconds (s)

## Card 16 — keyword_definition (`fl_C2hWHq487K3VbpYN`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for calculating **acceleration**. **(Extended Tier Only)**


**BACK**

The equation for **acceleration** is:

$a = \frac{Δv}{Δt}$

Where:

- $a$ = acceleration, measured in metres per second squared (m/s²)
- $Δ v$ = change in velocity, measured in metres per second (m/s)
- $Δ t$ = time taken, measured in seconds (s)
