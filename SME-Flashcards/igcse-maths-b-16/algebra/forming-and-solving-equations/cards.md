## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_5XRT9jRDsyMnVqtK`)

**FRONT**

Three bags contain $x$, $x+5$ and $x+10$ marbles each.

If there are 75 marbles in total, explain how to** form **an equation.


**BACK**

Three bags contain $x$, $x+5$ and $x+10$ marbles each.

If there are 75 marbles in total, then an equation can be formed by **adding** all the quantities up and setting them **equal to** 75.

This gives `x plus open parentheses x plus 5 close parentheses plus open parentheses x plus 10 close parentheses equals 75`.

Examiners want to see this full equation (before simplifying or solving).

## Card 8 — fill_in_the_blanks (`fl_69Cz9YGNc8gYyPRd`)

**FRONT**

A rectangle has length $3 x + 1$ and width $2 x - 5$, and its perimeter is 22. Fill in the missing part of the equation.

`2 \left(3 x + 1\right) + \_\_\_\_\_\_ = 22`


**BACK**

The completed equation is:

`2 \left(3 x + 1\right) + 2 \left(2 x - 5\right) = 22`

A rectangle has **two** lengths and **two** widths, so each expression is doubled before the two are added together.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_Yxrb4V2cSgJ3j7Tt`)

**FRONT**

Why should you put brackets round an algebraic side length before substituting it?


**BACK**

Because the shape's property usually multiplies the **whole** side length, and without brackets only the first term gets multiplied.

A width of $2 x - 5$ contributes `2 \left(2 x - 5\right) = 4 x - 10` to a perimeter, not $4 x - 5$.

## Card 10 — true_or_false (`fl_7BcvbtbtSVqXjmGK`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

On an irregular shape you may assume that sides which look equal are equal.


**BACK**

**False.**

With an irregular shape, assume every angle and every length is **different** unless you are told otherwise.

Only marked equal sides, marked equal angles, or a stated property such as a line of symmetry let you treat two of them as the same.

## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_Gp9hyxGbyY323nzv`)

**FRONT**

How do you find the volume of a prism whose cross-section is a trapezium?


**BACK**

Work out the **area of the cross-section** using the trapezium formula, then multiply that area by the **length** of the prism.

A prism has a constant cross-section, which is what makes a single multiplication enough.

## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_22H5RzmQZ32CsFj7`)

**FRONT**

A prism has cross-sectional area $7 x^{2}$ and length $x + 3$, and its volume is 784. How do you reach $x^{3} + 3 x^{2} - 112 = 0$?


**BACK**

Multiply out the volume, `7 x^{2} \left(x + 3\right) = 7 x^{3} + 21 x^{2}`, and set the result equal to 784.

Dividing every term by 7 gives $x^{3} + 3 x^{2} = 112$, and subtracting 112 from both sides puts it in the form asked for.

## Card 14 — true_or_false (`fl_j88yy6QZXyhqsccz`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

You can use a '**show that**' answer from part (a) to do part (b), even if you did not complete part (a) yourself.


**BACK**

**True.**

You can use a '**show that**' answer from part (a) to do part (b), even if you did not complete part (a) yourself.

If part (a) says to show that certain information leads to the equation $x^{2}-x-6=0$, then you **can** use that equation in part (b) without having proved part (a)!



## Card 15 — keyword_definition (`fl_X68d2TP3DdN28m37`)

**FRONT**

Explain the phrase **answer the question** **in context**.


**BACK**

**Answer the question** **in context** means that your final answer should

- be related to the real-life situation in the question
- be written in sentences
- use words and phrases from the question
- state the correct units
- be rounded appropriately

The answer should **not** be, for example:

- $x=2$ (no explanation)
- "the length is bigger" (no context)
