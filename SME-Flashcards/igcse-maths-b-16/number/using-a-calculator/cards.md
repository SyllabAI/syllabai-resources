## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_V9RbxJy8HcQCfM7K`)

**FRONT**

**True of False?**

To find the **square **of -13, you would type $-13^{2}$ into your calculator.


**BACK**

**False.**

To find the **square **of -13, you would type `open parentheses negative 13 close parentheses squared` into your calculator.

Your calculator applies the order of operations so it will square before multiplying by -1 unless you use brackets.



## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_CKntCFVfwMcS3p9N`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Typing $\frac{2+5}{3-1}$into a **calculator **is the **same **as $2+5\div3-1$.


**BACK**

**False.**

Typing $\frac{2+5}{3-1}$into a **calculator **is not the **same **as $2+5\div3-1$.

Imagine invisible brackets around the numerator and denominator, giving `open parentheses 2 plus 5 close parentheses divided by open parentheses 3 minus 1 close parentheses`.



## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_Bjz9BJkw48g8yhQV`)

**FRONT**

How do you use a **calculator **to find the **cube root** of a number?


**BACK**

To find the **cube root** of a number on a **calculator**, find the button that looks like `cube root of blank`.

Be careful, this is different to typing 3 followed by the square root button, $3\sqrt{}$.



## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_zt8mrYX7J6Trzw29`)

**FRONT**

What does the **Ans **button on your calculator do?


**BACK**

The **Ans **button **recalls **the **last answer** the calculator calculated.

If you have used your calculator to work out a long calculation and then want to square the answer, you can just type **Ans**<sup>**2**</sup>.



## Card 5 — true_or_false (`fl_tvvmhcrBf3CbQbsP`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

You should **round **your answer **each time** you use your calculator to do a series of calculations in your working.


**BACK**

**False.**

You should **only round **your answer at the very **end** of your working, otherwise you may get rounding errors.

You can show you are not rounding by putting three dots at the end of a number.

For example:

sin(20)=0.3420... then 15×0.3420... means you typed in 15×Ans.



## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_dSrPRsNCTwDgj2GT`)

**FRONT**

If your calculator has given you an **exact **answer (e.g. $2π,\sqrt{3},\frac{2}{7}$), how do you **change it to a decimal**?


**BACK**

To change an **exact **answer into a **decimal**, press the $S\LeftrightarrowD$ button.

On some calculators, this might be labelled differently such as $F\LeftrightarrowD$.
