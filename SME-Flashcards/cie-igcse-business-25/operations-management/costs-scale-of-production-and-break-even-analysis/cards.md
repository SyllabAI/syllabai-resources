**FRONT**

Define **break-even point**.


**BACK**

The **break-even point** is the number of units that need to be sold for **total costs** to equal **sales revenue**.

## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_YjvWWZDJdfcQsMJ7`)

**FRONT**

**BACK**

Knowing the **break-even point** helps businesses understand the **minimum level of sales or output** needed to cover all costs, allowing managers to make informed decisions about pricing and production volumes.

## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_n7yv7hr5vRXjscQM`)

**FRONT**

What is the formula to calculate the **break-even point** in units?


**BACK**

$\mathrm{Break}-\mathrm{even} \mathrm{point}=\frac{\mathrm{Fixed} \mathrm{costs}}{\mathrm{Selling} \mathrm{price}-\mathrm{Variable} \mathrm{cost} \mathrm{per} \mathrm{unit}}$

or

$\mathrm{Break}-\mathrm{even} \mathrm{point}=\frac{\mathrm{Fixed} \mathrm{costs}}{\mathrm{Contribution} \mathrm{per} \mathrm{unit}}$
