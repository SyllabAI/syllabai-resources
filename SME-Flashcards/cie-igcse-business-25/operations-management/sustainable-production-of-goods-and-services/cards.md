## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_ymnKKQTfS4q2SYy6`)

**FRONT**

Define **sustainable production**.


**BACK**

Sustainable production means producing goods and services in ways that do not harm the **environment, society** or **future generations**.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_CMSd4M4Rfhn5wDQb`)

**FRONT**

What does **reducing resource use** mean for a business?


**BACK**

Reducing resource use means using **fewer materials, water and energy** during production to cut waste, lower costs, and protect the environment.

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**BACK**

**FRONT**

**BACK**

Environmentally friendly products are designed to **have less harm on the planet** by using natural or recycled materials, being energy efficient, and producing less waste.

## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_DyVGdW4VYzn6S9x2`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Sustainable production always increases costs for a business.


**BACK**

**False.**

Sustainable production can reduce long-term costs by using less energy and fewer materials, even though some changes are expensive at first.

**FRONT**

What is **greenwashing**?


**BACK**

Greenwashing is when a business **pretends to be environmentally friendly**, but its actions do not match its claims.
