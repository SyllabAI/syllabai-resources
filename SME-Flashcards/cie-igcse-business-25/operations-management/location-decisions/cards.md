## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_dPt4fZMN6YrDb4PD`)

**FRONT**

Which factor involves being close to those who buy a business's goods or services?


**BACK**

Proximity to the market means being close to customers, which can reduce transport costs and make the business more accessible.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_s6xyWKpCH2qFRxtx`)

**FRONT**

Why might a manufacturing business choose to locate near its suppliers?


**BACK**

Locating near suppliers, or raw materials, helps reduce transportation costs and ensures a steady supply for production.

## Card 3 — keyword_definition (`fl_RmjJ7RzZg6MNSnHP`)

**FRONT**

Define **trade barriers**.


**BACK**

Trade barriers are **tariffs, quotas, or restrictions** that make it more difficult or expensive to import and export goods between countries.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_3H2fM5Z88npbRJzx`)

**FRONT**

Give **one** reason why multinational companies choose Ireland as a business location.


**BACK**

Multinational companies choose Ireland because of its **low corporation tax rate, highly educated workforce, and access to European and US markets.**

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_Vwnb4Z3Tg2dPKcV3`)

**FRONT**

Give **one** reason a retail business might choose a high street or shopping mall location.


**BACK**

Retail businesses choose high street or shopping mall locations to benefit from high footfall, making them more accessible and attractive to customers.

## Card 6 — true_or_false (`fl_VdMDBnKhRMhYRzFz`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

All service businesses prefer central locations regardless of their customer base.


**BACK**

**False.**

Service businesses that do not rely on passing trade may prefer out-of-town locations to reduce costs and access local incentives.
