**FRONT**

Define **legal controls**.


**BACK**

## Card 2 — keyword_definition (`fl_skJMw24zKvjg3HQm`)

**FRONT**

Define **misleading promotion**.


**BACK**

Misleading promotion occurs when a business **makes false or exaggerated claims** about its products in advertisements, packaging, or promotional campaigns.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_DKrjr9vtrzXtNh4J`)

**FRONT**

Give an example of **misleading promotion**.


**BACK**

An example of misleading promotion is **advertising a discount that is not genuine, such as claiming “50% off this week only” when the product was never sold at a higher price**.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_zCgFG54g88zWfJR3`)

**FRONT**

Why must products sold to customers be **safe and fit for purpose**?


**BACK**

Products must be safe and fit for purpose because **it is illegal to sell goods that could cause harm or injury due to poor design, defects, or lack of safety instructions**.

## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_Kcy7h8Yq7ssyDbJ2`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

It is legal for a business to claim a product is waterproof when it provides no protection from the rain.


**BACK**

**False.**

Making false claims about a product, such as saying it is waterproof when it is not, is **illegal and considered misleading promotion**.
