## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_3H6tJPQ3ZCqqRh2Q`)

**FRONT**

Define **diffusion**.


**BACK**

**Diffusion** is the **net movement** of molecules or ions from a region of **higher concentration** to a region of **lower concentration**, down a **concentration gradient**.


Spec links: `spcpt_3NTMxMTvgW6SYynz`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_5RBtdJngysJFWj52`)

**FRONT**

Why is diffusion described as a **passive** process?


**BACK**

It does **not** require **ATP** from the cell.

Molecules move using their own **kinetic energy**.
