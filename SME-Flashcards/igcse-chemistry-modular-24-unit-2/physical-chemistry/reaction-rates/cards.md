## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_7DwQxThPpqNkBd5D`)

**FRONT**

What are the four factors that can affect the rate of a reaction?


**BACK**

The four factors that can affect the **rate of a reaction** are:

1. Concentration of reactants in solution (or pressure of reacting gases)
2. Temperature
3. Surface area of solid reactants
4. Presence of a **catalyst**

**FRONT**

**BACK**
