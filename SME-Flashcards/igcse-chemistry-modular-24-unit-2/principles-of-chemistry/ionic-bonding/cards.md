## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_JVKwyfFpn3bzR4gX`)

**FRONT**

Why do ionic compounds have no overall charge?


**BACK**

Ionic compounds have no overall charge because the **positive charges** from the metal ions are exactly **cancelled out** by the negative charges from the non-metal ions. The total charge across the compound equals zero.
