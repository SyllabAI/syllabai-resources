## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_WJds29D7ZFSWtmy4`)

**FRONT**

What is produced at the cathode and anode during the electrolysis of molten lead(II) bromide?


**BACK**

At the cathode, positive lead ions gain electrons to form **lead metal**, deposited on the electrode. At the anode, negative bromide ions lose electrons to form **bromine gas**, which is brown.
