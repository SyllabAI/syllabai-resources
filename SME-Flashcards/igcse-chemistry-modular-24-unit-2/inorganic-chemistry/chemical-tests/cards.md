## Card 40 — question_and_answer (`fl_kjFbVsbVpccS6wtX`)

**FRONT**

Why is checking the boiling point a physical test for the purity of water?


**BACK**

Pure water boils at exactly **100 °C**. By heating a sample and measuring its **boiling point**, you can check whether it is pure — impurities raise the boiling point above 100 °C, indicating the water is not pure.
