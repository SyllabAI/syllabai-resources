## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_NYFCFNRYHr8jwKXv`)

**FRONT**

How can burning phosphorus be used to find the percentage of oxygen in air?


**BACK**

Burning **phosphorus** can determine the percentage of oxygen because it burns inside a bell jar sitting in a trough of water, using up the available oxygen. As the oxygen is consumed, the **water level rises** inside the jar. Careful measurements of water levels before and after allow the percentage to be calculated.

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_MgNW5Jc9ybQ5M2nv`)

**FRONT**

How is the percentage of oxygen in air calculated from the burette experiment?


**BACK**

The percentage of oxygen is calculated by finding the **initial volume of air** and the **final volume of air** (after oxygen is removed). Volume of oxygen = initial volume − final volume. Percentage = (volume of oxygen ÷ initial volume) × 100.
