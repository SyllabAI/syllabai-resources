## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_BZXhfvkYJ2V5QZgm`)

**FRONT**

Define **carboxylic acid**.


**BACK**

A **carboxylic acid** is an organic compound containing the **carboxyl** (-COOH) functional group. The general formula is C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n+1</sub>COOH.

**FRONT**

How are carboxylic acids named?


**BACK**

Carboxylic acids are named using the pattern **alkan + oic acid**. For example, the two-carbon acid is called **ethanoic acid**.

## Card 6 — keyword_definition (`fl_htGjY4pwWJb8ctYf`)

**FRONT**

What is the **carboxyl group**?


**BACK**

The **carboxyl group** is the -COOH functional group found in all **carboxylic acids**. It consists of a carbonyl (C=O) and a hydroxyl (-OH) group bonded to the same carbon atom.


Spec links: `spcpt_4Qz3GhHWYT9DYVrZ`


## Card 7 — true_or_false (`fl_s43shCBpVBRqBFtp`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The general formula of a carboxylic acid can be written as RCOOH.


**BACK**

**True.**

RCOOH is a shorthand for the general formula of a **carboxylic acid**, where **R** represents the varying **hydrocarbon** chain attached to the -COOH group.
