## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_TYCkpb8vVxqVRtxK`)

**FRONT**

Define **alcohol**.


**BACK**

An **alcohol** is an organic compound containing the **hydroxyl** (-OH) functional group. Alcohols are colourless liquids that dissolve in water to form neutral solutions.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_NVmFGrVMCJ3Rs6gv`)

**FRONT**

Why are alcohols considered a homologous series?


**BACK**

Alcohols are considered a **homologous series** because they all contain the same functional group (the **-OH** group) and follow the same naming pattern, differing only by a -CH<sub>2</sub>- unit.


Spec links: `spcpt_TnxTQCM7vDVPTRW7`


## Card 3 — true_or_false (`fl_TjQb9XjRSR5qBVX8`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Alcohols dissolve in water to form acidic solutions.


**BACK**

**False.**

Alcohols dissolve in water to form **neutral** solutions, not acidic ones. The -OH group does not release H<sup>+</sup> ions like an acid does.

## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_zkQnRcX3nFFryfMR`)

**FRONT**

How are alcohols named?


**BACK**

Alcohols are named by taking the corresponding **alkane** name and replacing the final -e with **-ol**. For example, ethane becomes ethanol.

Why is ethanol heated under reflux when being oxidised with potassium dichromate?


**BACK**

**BACK**

**FRONT**

What is **fermentation**?


**BACK**

**Fermentation** is the process by which **yeast** converts **sugar** or **starch** dissolved in water into **ethanol** and **carbon dioxide**, in the absence of **oxygen**.

## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_98wdhYqqjGRQsjmw`)

**FRONT**

Why is ethanol production by fermentation described as a batch process?


**BACK**

Fermentation is a **batch process** because yeast is killed once the ethanol concentration reaches around **15%**. The reaction vessel must be emptied and the process restarted rather than running continuously.

**FRONT**

How is ethanol separated from water after hydration of ethene?


**BACK**

After hydration, the ethene is first separated in a **condenser** (it has a much lower boiling point than ethanol or water). The ethanol and water are then separated by **fractional distillation**.

## Card 25 — keyword_definition (`fl_5QDvsFnxdDqCtfy7`)

**FRONT**

What is **hydration**?


**BACK**

**Hydration** is a reaction in which water is added across a double bond. In ethanol production, a water molecule adds across the C=C double bond of **ethene** to form **ethanol**.
