## Card 6 — question_and_answer (`fl_fbNfPzPkGWjP6jKx`)

**FRONT**

What does **AND** mean for combined probabilities?


**BACK**

**AND** means **multiply** ($\times$) and is used for **independent events** to find the **probability of both events **occurring.

E.g. the probability of events A **and** B occurring is $\mathrm{P} ( A ) \\times \mathrm{P} ( B )$.



## Card 7 — question_and_answer (`fl_zBZJ2Xqdgd8cVkjC`)

**FRONT**

What does **OR** mean for combined probabilities?


**BACK**

**OR** means **add** (+) and is used for **mutually exclusive** **events** to find the **probability of one event or the other event **occurring.

E.g. the probability of either event *A* and/or event *B* occurring is $\mathrm{P} ( A ) + \mathrm{P} ( B )$.

## Card 9 — keyword_definition (`fl_QgdQS2gvBbGgT97Q`)

**FRONT**

State the** equation** for the probability of** two independent events**, $A$ and $B$, occurring together.


**BACK**

If $A$ and $B$ are **independent events**, then the probability of both events occurring is:

`P ( A and B ) = P ( A ) x P ( B )`.



## Card 10 — keyword_definition (`fl_ZMk2W4VkKp2BvfW7`)

**FRONT**

State the **equation** for the probability of one or the other of **two mutually exclusive events**, $A$ and $B$, occurring.


**BACK**

If $A$ and $B$ are **mutually exclusive events**, then the probability of one or the other of them occurring is:

$\mathrm{P} ( A \text{or} B ) = \mathrm{P} ( A ) + \mathrm{P} ( B )$.

## Card 12 — question_and_answer (`fl_j93nQ5qVj62ZpyQ9`)

## Card 16 — question_and_answer (`fl_SqhWQNxgzpwKwBxD`)

**FRONT**

What is the **difference** between $\mathrm{P} ( A \vert B )$ and $\mathrm{P} ( B \vert A )$?


**BACK**

The **difference** between $\mathrm{P} ( A \vert B )$ and $\mathrm{P} ( B \vert A )$ is this:

- $\mathrm{P} ( A \vert B )$ is the probability that $A$ happens, given that $B$ has happened.
- $\mathrm{P} ( B \vert A )$ is the probability that $B$ happens, given that $A$ has happened.

## Card 19 — true_or_false (`fl_sKBQRGnjSBBk8pHR`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In **combined conditional probability** questions, **probabilities **for subsequent events **may change** based on previous events.


**BACK**

**True.**

In **combined conditional probability** questions, **probabilities** for subsequent events **may change** based on previous events.

E.g. if there are a number of yellow sweets and green sweets in a jar and two sweets are eaten, the probability of picking a yellow sweet will be different for the first and second picks.
This is because there will be fewer sweets, and different numbers of each kind of sweet, after the first is picked.
