## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_xrVRxPc7S4tZWJgK`)

**FRONT**

Why are alkanes described as **saturated**?


**BACK**

Alkanes are described as **saturated** because they contain only **single carbon-carbon bonds**. There are no double bonds between carbon atoms, so the molecule cannot take on any more hydrogen atoms.

**FRONT**

What types of reactions can alkanes undergo?


**BACK**

**FRONT**

## Card 13 — question_and_answer (`fl_znpNY5pfNZRkcMzv`)

**FRONT**

What type of compounds are formed when alkanes react with halogens?


**BACK**

The products are called **halogenoalkanes** (or haloalkanes). These are compounds in which one or more hydrogen atoms of the alkane have been replaced by a **halogen** atom.


Spec links: `spcpt_QNyPFTfrcqrWKKZX`

Methane reacts with **chlorine** under UV light in a **substitution reaction**, producing chloromethane and hydrogen chloride.
