## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_q6W3tQrhsTnn6Nwr`)

**FRONT**

Define **organic chemistry**.


**BACK**

**Organic chemistry** is the study of the structure, properties, and reactions of compounds that contain **carbon**.

By convention, metal carbonates, carbon dioxide, and carbon monoxide are not classified as organic compounds.

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_2tX2NPs9YD83Zw2f`)

**FRONT**

What does a **structural formula** show, and how does it differ from a displayed formula?


**BACK**

A **structural formula** shows enough information to make the structure clear but omits most covalent bonds. Only important bonds such as double bonds are shown, and identical groups may be bracketed.

A **displayed formula** shows every single bond and atom in full spatial arrangement.

## Card 7 — keyword_definition (`fl_wvnbs7d93bGrdQDq`)

**FRONT**

Define **homologous series**.


**BACK**

A **homologous series** is a family of organic compounds that share the same **functional group**, the same **general formula**, similar chemical properties, and a gradation in physical properties.

Each successive member differs by CH<sub>2</sub>.

**BACK**

**FRONT**

What do the two parts of an organic compound's name tell you?


**BACK**

The name of an organic compound has two parts: the **prefix** (or stem) tells you how many carbon atoms are in the longest continuous chain, and the **suffix** tells you which **functional group** is present.

## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_th639GsH2y578zVn`)

**FRONT**

What happens in an **addition reaction**, and how many products are formed?


**BACK**

In an **addition reaction**, two or more molecules combine to form a single, larger molecule with **no other products**.

For example, bromine adds across the C=C **double bond** of ethene to form dibromoethane.

## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_hQ6Y74QjpNjQJ8j9`)

**FRONT**

What are the products of complete combustion compared to incomplete combustion of a hydrocarbon?


**BACK**

**Complete combustion** (excess oxygen) produces **carbon dioxide** and **water** only.

**Incomplete combustion** (limited oxygen) produces **carbon monoxide**, unburned carbon (soot), and **water**.
