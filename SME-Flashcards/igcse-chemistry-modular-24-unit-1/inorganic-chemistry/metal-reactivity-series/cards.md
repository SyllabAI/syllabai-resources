## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_cp8gFjKCbZFFQSF7`)

**FRONT**

What is the **reactivity series**?


**BACK**

The **reactivity series** is a list of metals ranked in **order of reactivity**, based on observations of their reactions with water and dilute acids.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_qf7QfCnTMtwkb7K5`)

**FRONT**

What products are formed when a metal reacts with a dilute acid?


**BACK**

When a metal reacts with a dilute acid, a **metal salt** and **hydrogen gas** are produced.

The general equation is: metal + acid → salt + hydrogen.
