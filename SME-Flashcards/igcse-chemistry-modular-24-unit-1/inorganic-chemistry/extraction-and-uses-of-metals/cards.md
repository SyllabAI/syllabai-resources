## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_nnYYHHdxQYmRrgcN`)

**FRONT**

Why are most metals found as ores rather than as pure elements?


**BACK**

Most metals are found as ores because they are **chemically combined** with other substances in the Earth's crust.

Only very unreactive metals, such as gold and platinum, are found as the uncombined **element**.

## Card 8 — true_or_false (`fl_vcXVmYdwKGF2j4GV`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The extraction of a metal from an oxide ore always involves reduction.


**BACK**

**True.**

When a metal is extracted from an oxide ore, **oxygen** is removed from the metal compound. Removing oxygen is the definition of **reduction**, so the process is always a reduction reaction.

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_j2D2QqQ2FZrYjn8D`)

**FRONT**

Why must metals above carbon in the reactivity series be extracted by electrolysis?


**BACK**

Metals above carbon in the reactivity series cannot be extracted by reduction because they are too **reactive** to be reduced by carbon.

Instead, **electrolysis** must be used, which requires large amounts of electricity and is therefore an expensive process.

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_29Phwvcpnt7dw4k7`)

**FRONT**

What happens in Zone 1 of the blast furnace?


**BACK**

In Zone 1 of the blast furnace, **coke** (carbon) burns in the hot air blown in at the bottom, producing **carbon dioxide**.

This reaction is exothermic and releases heat, raising the temperature inside the furnace.

## Card 13 — keyword_definition (`fl_4QkHHphVGT7kz943`)

**FRONT**

Define **slag**.


**BACK**

**Slag** is the waste product removed from a blast furnace. It forms when **calcium oxide** (from limestone) reacts with silicon dioxide impurities in the ore, producing calcium silicate, which floats on top of the molten iron.


Spec links: `spcpt_gpwDvPqZsxj2fwx7`


## Card 14 — question_and_answer (`fl_bnPb8rhq7bBGjQSb`)

**FRONT**

Why is limestone added to the blast furnace?


**BACK**

Limestone is added to the blast furnace to remove **acidic impurities** (mainly silicon dioxide) from the iron ore.

It thermally decomposes to form **calcium oxide**, which then reacts with the silicon dioxide to form slag, which is tapped off separately.


Spec links: `spcpt_gpwDvPqZsxj2fwx7`

**FRONT**

What is **cryolite** used for in the extraction of aluminium?


**BACK**

**Cryolite** is a substance in which aluminium oxide is dissolved before electrolysis. This lowers the **melting point** of the mixture, making the process less energy-intensive and more economical.

## Card 29 — question_and_answer (`fl_D8WtZyp74yvm8tq8`)

**FRONT**

Why are alloys harder than the pure metals they are made from?


**BACK**

Alloys are harder than pure metals because they contain atoms of **different sizes**. These distort the regular arrangement of atoms in the metal, making it more difficult for the layers to **slide over each other**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Wy4ZGtw52sFZDBYb`


## Card 30 — true_or_false (`fl_dvvtVSpKHR2VBCMM`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Pure iron is harder than steel because it contains no other elements.


**BACK**

**False.**

Pure iron has a regular arrangement of atoms, which allows layers to **slide** easily. Steel is an alloy in which atoms of different sizes **distort** this arrangement, making it harder than pure iron.
