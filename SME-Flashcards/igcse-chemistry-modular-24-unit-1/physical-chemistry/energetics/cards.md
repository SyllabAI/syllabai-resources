## Card 20 — keyword_definition (`fl_GxDNmbTPtHKNWfcJ`)

**FRONT**

What is **heat energy change**?


**BACK**

**Heat energy change (Q)** is the amount of heat energy released or absorbed in a reaction. It is calculated using Q = m × c × ΔT and is measured in **joules (J)**.


Spec links: `spcpt_zkhrZFdsfqVWgyJg`

**BACK**

In Q = m × c × ΔT:

- Q = **heat energy change** (J)
- m = **mass** of the substance being heated (g)
- c = **specific heat capacity** (J/g/°C)
- ΔT = **temperature change** (°C)

Spec links: `spcpt_b5YczdCTkzHM78fH`


## Card 26 — question_and_answer (`fl_CJmQhTYvpNH4f5KD`)

**FRONT**

How do you calculate the molar enthalpy change from Q?


**BACK**

To calculate the molar enthalpy change from Q: first calculate Q (in J) using Q = m × c × ΔT. Then divide by the number of **moles** (n): Δ*H* = Q ÷ n. Finally, convert Q to **kJ** by dividing by 1000 before calculating Δ*H*.
