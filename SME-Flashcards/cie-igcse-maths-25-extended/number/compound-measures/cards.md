## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_DFBdDWqK8Ds7JvFb`)

**FRONT**

Define the term **compound measure**.


**BACK**

A **compound measure** is something calculated using **more than one measurement**, e.g. speed is calculated using distance and time.



## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_3K99ZwbRWQnZ4Wwt`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

**Flow rate** is an example of a **compound measure**.


**BACK**

**True.**

**Flow rate** is an example of a **compound measure**.

It combines two measures (volume and time) by looking at the volume of fluid that moves over time, (e.g. cm<sup>3</sup> / s).

## Card 4 — true_or_false (`fl_942VZq3QyzznBw3d`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The **units** of a **compound measure** can be used to find the **equation **needed to calculate it.


**BACK**

**True.**

You **can** use the** units** of a **compound measure** to find the **equation** used to calculate it.

For example, speed is often measured in km / h. 
Km (kilometres) is a measure of distance and h (hours) is a measure of time, so the calculation for speed is:

$\mathrm{Speed}=\frac{\mathrm{Distance}}{\mathrm{Time}}$
