## Card 15 — question_and_answer (`fl_HbPRspVB488jVxkQ`)

**FRONT**

How do you use an **exchange rate** £1 = \$1.25 to **convert £ into \$**?


**BACK**

Given an **exchange rate** of £1 = \$1.25, you can **convert £ into \$** by **multiplying **your value in £ by** 1.25**.

E.g. Convert £16 into \$.
16 × 1.25 = \$20.
