## Card 36 — question_and_answer (`fl_Xv2FBsjwWyRNfJ3T`)

E.g. $1008\div28$


**BACK**

E.g. $1008\div28$ can be written as $\frac{1008}{28}$

## Card 38 — question_and_answer (`fl_GNRkRwcVBhcqgtKW`)

**FRONT**

How can you use an** 'easier' calculation** to calculate a **decimal division problem**?

E.g**. **$2.4\div0.08$**?**


**BACK**

Turn the division into a **fraction**, e.g. $2.4\div0.08=\frac{2.4}{0.08}$

Multiply the values by a **multiple of 10** to change them to** integers**, 
e.g. $\frac{2.4\times10}{0.08\times100}=\frac{24}{8}$

**Simplify **the fraction (complete the easier calculation), e.g. $3$

**Convert the answer** by 'undoing' the initial changes, e.g. $\frac{3\times100}{10}=30$
