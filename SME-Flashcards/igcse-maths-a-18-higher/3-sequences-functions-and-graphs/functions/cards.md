## Card 13 — keyword_definition (`fl_GyCy5tCYC5ZqJj23`)

**FRONT**

What does the notation `fg open parentheses x close parentheses` mean?


**BACK**

`fg open parentheses x close parentheses` is the notation for a composite function made up of functions $f$ and $g$.

It means "$f$ is applied to the output of `straight g open parentheses x close parentheses`".

I.e., **first** put $x$ into the function $g$, and **next** put the output `straight g open parentheses x close parentheses` into the function $f$.

Note that the function on the right ($g$) acts first, and the function on the left ($f$) acts second.

## Card 19 — question_and_answer (`fl_r92tdny8rG5fPwG4`)

**FRONT**

If you knew that `straight f open parentheses 3 close parentheses equals 10`, how would you find `straight f to the power of negative 1 end exponent open parentheses 10 close parentheses`?


**BACK**

An inverse function 'undoes' the operations of the original function.

So if $f$ turns 3 into 10, then $f^{-1}$ turns 10 back into 3.

I.e., if `straight f open parentheses 3 close parentheses equals 10`, then `straight f to the power of negative 1 end exponent open parentheses 10 close parentheses equals 3`.
