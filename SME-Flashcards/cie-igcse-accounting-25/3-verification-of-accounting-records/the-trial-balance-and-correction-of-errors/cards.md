# The Trial Balance & Correction of Errors

## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_Y5rnBWQSZtyjC6xS`)

**FRONT**

Define a **trial balance**.


**BACK**

A **trial balance** is a statement listing **all the accounts and their balances** on a particular date, each in either a debit or a credit column. The two column totals should be **equal**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Zh4BpcZyh8mfZ3Jd`


## Card 2 — true_or_false (`fl_CyBXpxZjkyDspGvY`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

The trial balance is part of the double entry system.


**BACK**

**False.**

It is a **statement** drawn up from the ledger balances, not a ledger account. Nothing is ever posted to it, which is why it can be redrafted without affecting the accounts themselves.


Spec links: `spcpt_Zh4BpcZyh8mfZ3Jd`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_QQr6jpXm54GHFGwy`)

**FRONT**

Give two uses of a trial balance.


**BACK**

A trial balance:

- checks for **arithmetic errors** in the ledger accounts, since a miscalculated balance will usually stop the totals agreeing
- gathers every balance **in one place**, which helps with preparing the financial statements


Spec links: `spcpt_Zh4BpcZyh8mfZ3Jd`

## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_V6DF9vcq5sFPdyYr`)

**FRONT**

Which balance in a trial balance is always the opening figure, and why?


**BACK**

The **inventory** balance is always the opening figure. The current inventory sits inside the **purchases** account until the year-end transfer, so the inventory account still shows last year's closing value.

**BACK**

Debit balances include:

- **purchases** and **sales returns**
- expenses such as rent paid, discount allowed and carriage
- **trade receivables**, the **bank**, and other assets including inventory

**BACK**

Credit balances include:

- **sales** and **purchases returns**
- other income such as rent received and discount received
- **trade payables**, a **bank overdraft**, and other liabilities such as a bank loan
- **capital**

## Card 9 — question_and_answer (`fl_TdCJKHzK2cBg9kJ9`)

**FRONT**

Name the six types of error that do not affect the trial balance.


**BACK**

The six are:

- error of **omission**
- error of **original entry**
- error of **complete reversal**
- error of **commission**
- error of **principle**
- **compensating** errors

## Card 10 — true_or_false (`fl_txmDFqjN9MVMyYxS`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A trial balance that agrees proves the ledger accounts are free of errors.


**BACK**

**False.**

Six kinds of error leave the trial balance **still agreeing**, because the debit and credit totals are unaffected. Agreement shows only that the two totals match, not that every entry is right.


Spec links: `spcpt_h47nWDqMzcpj9KFS`


## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_PfpkP7T7QCb2Fs32`)

**FRONT**

Why can an error that does not affect the trial balance still not be ignored?


**BACK**

It can misstate the **profit**, the **value of assets and liabilities**, or the balances owed to and from **other businesses**. An error that hides from the trial balance is still an error in the accounts.


Spec links: `spcpt_h47nWDqMzcpj9KFS`


## Card 12 — keyword_definition (`fl_nByKsxJ5BcHcKHd4`)

**FRONT**

Define a **posting error**.


**BACK**

A **posting error** is where **both entries** for a transaction are made on the **same side**, either both debits or both credits. Paying rent and debiting both the rent account and the bank account is an example.

## Card 14 — keyword_definition (`fl_8ZtShWj8ZR3PdzvG`)

**FRONT**

Define an **unequal posting error**.


**BACK**

An **unequal posting error** is where a transaction is entered into the two accounts using **different amounts**. Only one of the two figures can be right, so the trial balance will not agree.

**BACK**

An **addition error** is a **numerical mistake** made when balancing an account, or when totalling the debit or credit column of the trial balance. Totalling \$550 and \$450 as \$910 is an example.

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

A journal entry correcting an error must still have equal debits and credits.


**BACK**

**True.**

A correction is a journal entry like any other, so the debit total must equal the credit total. If it does not, the correction has introduced an error of its own.

## Card 24 — keyword_definition (`fl_YG9pXv9PjDSy5rWp`)

**FRONT**

Define a **suspense account**.


**BACK**

A **suspense account** is a **temporary** account opened when the trial balance totals do **not** agree. It holds the difference until the errors that caused it have been found and corrected.


Spec links: `spcpt_JvSxJbtD5wh8cNjS`


## Card 25 — question_and_answer (`fl_5TX4zTyrP8cg5nCg`)

**FRONT**

Which side of the suspense account does the difference go on?


**BACK**

The side matching the **lower total** on the trial balance, labelled **Difference on trial balance**. That is what makes the suspense account's own two sides equal to begin with.


Spec links: `spcpt_JvSxJbtD5wh8cNjS`

**FRONT**

Define an **error of original entry**.


**BACK**

An **error of original entry** is where a transaction is entered into **both** ledger accounts using the **same wrong amount**. The accounts and the sides are right; only the figure is wrong.

## Card 37 — question_and_answer (`fl_SVjMwkntK3q2HtM8`)

**FRONT**

Why does an error of original entry leave the trial balance still agreeing?


**BACK**

The **same wrong figure** has gone into the debit total and the credit total. Both sides are wrong by the same amount, so they still match each other.


Spec links: `spcpt_4sg67PFnWnNK8Ppm`


## Card 38 — question_and_answer (`fl_SrDh7jZ86JQ4TJ5F`)

**FRONT**

How do you correct an error of original entry?


**BACK**

Find the **difference** between the wrong amount and the right one, and enter that difference in both accounts. Put it on the **same side** as the original entry to increase the figure, or on the **opposite side** to decrease it.


Spec links: `spcpt_4sg67PFnWnNK8Ppm`

**BACK**

Only the **difference** between the wrong figure and the right one is entered. Entering the full amount again would record the transaction twice over.

**FRONT**

Why does an error of complete reversal leave the trial balance still agreeing?


**BACK**

There is still **one debit and one credit** of the right amount. They are simply on the wrong sides, which the trial balance cannot detect.

## Card 44 — question_and_answer (`fl_KxmsFZBGjp88yYjc`)

**FRONT**

Why must the correcting entry be double the value of the transaction?


**BACK**

The entry has to work **twice over**: once to undo the wrong entries, and once to make the right ones. A single entry would only cancel the error out and leave the transaction unrecorded.


Spec links: `spcpt_ss9vSy3kWyYSD7R2`


## Card 45 — true_or_false (`fl_VxZQTCtNTJz85mTW`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

In an error of complete reversal, the wrong accounts have been used.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **correct accounts** were used, and only the **sides** are wrong. That is what separates it from an error of commission, where the entry went into the wrong account altogether.

## Card 46 — keyword_definition (`fl_mmQCrF6NP57SGCMM`)

**FRONT**

Define an **error of commission**.


**BACK**

An **error of commission** is where one entry goes into the **wrong account**, but into an account of the **right type**. A credit sale posted to the wrong customer's account is the standard example.


Spec links: `spcpt_GqJsjWZXmRYMrWJx`


## Card 47 — question_and_answer (`fl_MDfQQKycHWFVPZc8`)

**FRONT**

How do you correct an error of commission?


**BACK**

**Undo** the entry in the wrong account by making an equal entry on the **opposite side**. Then make the entry in the **correct** account.


Spec links: `spcpt_GqJsjWZXmRYMrWJx`


## Card 48 — question_and_answer (`fl_d9f43yVswZYg96xV`)

**FRONT**

Why does an error of commission leave the trial balance still agreeing?


**BACK**

The right amount is still in the debit total and in the credit total. Only **which** account holds it is wrong, and a trial balance does not check that.


Spec links: `spcpt_GqJsjWZXmRYMrWJx`

## Card 52 — question_and_answer (`fl_9XgnzSvwYmYycVYR`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between an error of principle and an error of commission?


**BACK**

In an error of **commission** the wrong account was used but it was the **right type**, such as the wrong customer's account. In an error of **principle** the type of account is wrong too, such as an expense posted to an asset account.

How do you correct an error of principle?


**BACK**

The same way as an error of commission: **undo** the entry in the wrong account with an equal entry on the opposite side. Then make the entry in the **correct** account.

Why does an error of principle leave the trial balance still agreeing?


**BACK**

The correct amount is still in the debit total and in the credit total. A trial balance checks that the two totals match, not that the right **kind** of account was used.

## Card 57 — keyword_definition (`fl_6TMrczBTSJjQXPpb`)

**FRONT**

Define **compensating errors**.


**BACK**

**Compensating errors** are two or more unrelated errors whose effects **cancel each other out** when the debits and credits are totalled. Each error is real, but together they leave the totals agreeing.


Spec links: `spcpt_TMGMTPxH7Yq4yJ9y`


## Card 58 — question_and_answer (`fl_DNrymJyyXGKkqzm9`)

**FRONT**

How do you correct compensating errors?


**BACK**

Correct **each error separately**, as though the other had not happened. Once both are put right, the total debits and total credits will still be equal.


Spec links: `spcpt_TMGMTPxH7Yq4yJ9y`


## Card 59 — question_and_answer (`fl_5jKq8qscVm9nS7yB`)

**FRONT**

Why do compensating errors leave the trial balance still agreeing?


**BACK**

The errors work in **opposite directions** and are the **same size**, so one makes up for the other. The debit and credit totals are unaffected even though individual accounts are wrong.


Spec links: `spcpt_TMGMTPxH7Yq4yJ9y`


## Card 60 — true_or_false (`fl_XVn9T5JpYrpKFbGw`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

Because compensating errors cancel out, the accounts do not need correcting.


**BACK**

**False.**

The **totals** happen to come out right, but the **individual accounts** are both wrong. Each customer concerned would be sent a statement of account showing the wrong balance.


Spec links: `spcpt_TMGMTPxH7Yq4yJ9y`
