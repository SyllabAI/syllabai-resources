## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_fNw4kX75V2HMfrY4`)

**FRONT**

Define the **ethical framework**.


**BACK**

The **ethical framework** sets out the fundamental principles governing how people in accounting roles must behave.

It applies to every accounting function, from bookkeeping and payroll through to reporting and auditing.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_QGQ949srKMjTqFzD`)

**FRONT**

What are the five principles of professional ethics?


**BACK**

The five principles are:

- **integrity**
- **objectivity**
- **professional competence and due care**
- **confidentiality**
- **professional behaviour**


Spec links: `spcpt_PrDfHFSxqbwybFQ9`


## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_3WBYsxQySRnWHXVj`)

**FRONT**

An accountant is asked to leave information out of the financial statements so the business looks better. Which principle would that break?


**BACK**

**Integrity**.

Accountants must be honest, straightforward and trustworthy, which rules out presenting a picture they know to be incomplete.

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_D7YsbVV2WKVHQhgT`)

**FRONT**

Why can an accountant not prepare financial statements for a relative?


**BACK**

**Objectivity**.

Accountants must be fair, free from bias and able to make their own judgements, so they must not work where there is a conflict of interest.


Spec links: `spcpt_PrDfHFSxqbwybFQ9`


## Card 5 — question_and_answer (`fl_SwHJx3rGNRmb4NHT`)

**FRONT**

An accountant tells a business's employees what their colleagues are paid. Which principle has been broken?


**BACK**

**Confidentiality**.

Information must not be shared without the business's permission unless the law requires it, and must not be used for anything other than the accounting work.

## Card 17 — question_and_answer (`fl_FMMWZ6q4kgMfhHNg`)

**FRONT**

What are the advantages and drawbacks of storing records manually?


**BACK**

They need no technology, are simple to understand and **cannot be hacked**.

But they take up physical space, are slow to search, are harder to back up, and can be lost, damaged or stolen.

## Card 20 — question_and_answer (`fl_PH2jfnVtZHK6SY6k`)

**FRONT**

Why must a business store its data securely?


**BACK**

Because data can be **lost**, through accidental deletion, a forgotten save or a technical fault.

**Confidential information** can also be shared by mistake, or **accessed** by others through guessed passwords or hacking.


Spec links: `spcpt_cQdX2FThQ2Vn9WcS`


## Card 21 — question_and_answer (`fl_svqrTvJfksn6Pygz`)

**FRONT**

How can a business make its digital records more secure?


**BACK**

Install **firewalls** and **antivirus** software, use secure **passwords**, and **back the data up** to an external drive or the cloud.

Data can also be encrypted, screens locked when unattended, and computers wiped before they are disposed of.


Spec links: `spcpt_cQdX2FThQ2Vn9WcS`

## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_CX8Bcp4X5ssPP6Hb`)

**FRONT**

How does storing data sustainably differ from storing it securely?


**BACK**

Sustainability is about **reducing energy use and waste**: keeping only what is needed and disposing of it responsibly.

Security is about stopping data being lost, shared by mistake, or accessed by the wrong people.
