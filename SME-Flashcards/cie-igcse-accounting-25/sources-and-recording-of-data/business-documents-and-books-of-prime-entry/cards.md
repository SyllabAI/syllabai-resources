## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_DBDxJV3yBZM2Pjdd`)

**FRONT**

What are the four stages of the accounting process, in order?


**BACK**

The four stages are:

- a **transaction** takes place
- a **business document** is issued or received
- the amount is entered in a **book of prime entry**
- the entry is posted to a **ledger account**

**FRONT**

**BACK**

**Business documents** are the records kept of every transaction a business makes. They are the **source of information** for the books of prime entry, and can be used to **check for errors**.

## Card 8 — question_and_answer (`fl_S5dX29SD8NQh5RHb`)

**FRONT**

Give two advantages of using books of prime entry.


**BACK**

Books of prime entry:

- give an extra stage at which **errors** can be caught
- allow **control accounts** to be prepared to check the ledger's accuracy
- reduce the number of **entries** needed in some ledger accounts
- let different book-keepers take **different books**, without work being duplicated or missed

## Card 18 — question_and_answer (`fl_BHsRdhdXrqsCFwVF`)

**FRONT**

How is a trade discount treated in the books of prime entry, and how does a cash discount differ?


**BACK**

For a trade discount only the **discounted amount** is entered, the discount itself is never recorded, and it is **not part of the double entry system**. For a cash discount the amount **before** the discount is entered, and the discount is recorded separately when the customer pays early.

## Card 23 — question_and_answer (`fl_MVVVG2zQJKVdGprg`)

**FRONT**

An invoice lists 50 items at \$4 each, with a 5% trade discount. What is the total amount owed?


**BACK**

The total owed is **\$190**. The items come to 50 × \$4 = \$200, and the trade discount is 5% × \$200 = \$10, which leaves \$200 − \$10 = **\$190**.
