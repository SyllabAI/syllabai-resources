## Card 1 — question_and_answer (`fl_JCsThzxVS3KbR7nX`)

**BACK**

## Card 24 — question_and_answer (`fl_Q5K76jyhwqPH9vsr`)

**FRONT**

Why are alpha particles strongly ionising but weakly penetrating?


**BACK**

Alpha particles have a large charge (+2*e*), so they interact strongly with matter (highly ionising). This means they lose energy quickly and cannot travel far, so they are weakly penetrating and can be blocked by skin or a sheet of paper.


Spec links: `spcpt_hqmJY4MT3zxKmGNj`


## Card 25 — question_and_answer (`fl_ntsXTPnpVGwhVZfp`)

**FRONT**

What is the difference between a β⁻ particle and a β⁺ particle?


**BACK**

A **β⁻ particle** is a high energy electron, emitted by nuclei with too many neutrons.

A **β⁺ particle** is a high energy positron, emitted by nuclei with too many protons.
