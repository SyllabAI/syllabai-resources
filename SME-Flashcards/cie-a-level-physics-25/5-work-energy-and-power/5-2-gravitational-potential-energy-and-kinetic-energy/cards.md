## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_NbHpG8NRqb5SJQpB`)

**FRONT**

Define **gravitational potential energy**.


**BACK**

**Gravitational potential energy** is energy stored in a mass due to its position in a gravitational field.

## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_dPwBj5wC88MCwJkS`)

**FRONT**

Starting from the equation for work done, $W = F s$, derive the equation for the gravitational potential energy gained when a mass *m* is lifted through a height Δ*h*.


**BACK**

- The force needed to lift the mass is equal to its weight, $F = m g$
- The displacement is $s = Δ h$
- Substituting into $W = F s$ gives $W = m g Δ h$
- This work done is stored as gravitational potential energy: $Δ E_{p} = m g Δ h$

## Card 3 — question_and_answer (`fl_wgGdMCP3hKkjdpcC`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for the change in gravitational potential energy in a uniform gravitational field, and define each symbol.


**BACK**

$Δ E_{p} = m g Δ h$

- *E*<sub>p</sub> = gravitational potential energy in joules (J)
- *m* = mass in kilograms (kg)
- *g* = gravitational field strength in newtons per kilogram (N kg<sup>-1</sup>)
- Δ*h* = change in height in metres (m)

## Card 4 — question_and_answer (`fl_BGVm7t6K4xnbR7bb`)

**FRONT**

What happens to an object's gravitational potential energy as it rises, and as it falls?


**BACK**

- As it **rises**, it **gains** gravitational potential energy
- As it **falls**, it **loses** gravitational potential energy

## Card 10 — question_and_answer (`fl_CcFB6YkNWHMfcKtW`)

**FRONT**

State the equation for kinetic energy, and define each symbol.


**BACK**

$E_{k} = \frac{1}{2} m v^{2}$

- *E*<sub>k</sub> = kinetic energy in joules (J)
- *m* = mass in kilograms (kg)
- *v* = velocity in metres per second (m s<sup>-1</sup>)

## Card 11 — question_and_answer (`fl_6P9vJGdZH29zQwcV`)

**FRONT**

Starting from $W = F s$, $F = m a$ and $v^{2} = u^{2} + 2 a s$ for an object accelerating from rest, derive the equation for kinetic energy.


**BACK**

- With initial velocity *u* = 0, the suvat equation gives $a = \frac{v^{2}}{2s}$
- Substituting into $F = m a$ gives $F = \frac{mv^{2}}{2s}$
- Substituting this into $W = F s$ gives $W = \frac{1}{2} m v^{2}$
- This work done equals the kinetic energy gained: $E_{k} = \frac{1}{2} m v^{2}$

## Card 13 — true_or_false (`fl_sDxhYWtRkCybWrwK`)

**FRONT**

**True or False?**

If an object's speed doubles, its kinetic energy also doubles.


**BACK**

**False.**

Kinetic energy is proportional to the **square** of velocity, so doubling the speed **quadruples** the kinetic energy.

**FRONT**

How can the mass of an object be calculated if its kinetic energy and velocity are known?


**BACK**
