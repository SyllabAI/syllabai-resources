## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_TGbVqXGpzWhhdhwF`)

**FRONT**

Define **price elasticity of demand (PED)**.


**BACK**

**Price elasticity of demand (PED)** measures how **responsive** the quantity demanded is to a change in price.


Spec links: `spcpt_Pd2n725sMgdVz7pV`


## Card 2 — question_and_answer (`fl_yGfkkj6kfSM7XHPz`)

**FRONT**

What is the formula for PED?


**BACK**

PED is the **percentage change in quantity demanded** divided by the **percentage change in price**.


Spec links: `spcpt_Pd2n725sMgdVz7pV`
