## Card 1 — keyword_definition (`fl_h3B46jVTsjK7FQdM`)

**FRONT**

Define **rational**.


**BACK**

In classical economic theory, **rational** describes economic agents who consider the outcomes of their choices and select the option offering the **highest net benefits**.
